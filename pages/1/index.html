The Opening (Surah Al-Fatihah)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Actualizing complete servitude to Allah the Exalted.

Explanation:

Surah Al-Fatihah is called 'The Opening Chapter' because Allah begins the Quran with it. It is also referred to as Umm Al-Quran (Mother of the Quran) because it encompasses the Quran's themes, from the principles of faith to worship, among other matters. It is also called 'The Seven Oft-Repeated Verses.'

(١) بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

In the name of Allah the Exalted, I begin reading the Quran, seeking His aid and blessing by invoking His name. The basmalah consists of three of Allah's beautiful names: 1- Allah, meaning the One worthy of worship, and this is the most exclusive of names of Allah the Exalted. 2- Ar-Raḥmān, i.e., the Possessor of all-encompassing mercy, the Most Merciful in His Essence. 3- Ar-Raḥīm, i.e., the Bestower of mercy that reaches all He desires, for He bestows His Mercy upon whomever He wills from His creation, including His believing slaves.

(٢) ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Complete praise and all forms of extolment of majesty and perfection belong exclusively to Allah alone; He is the Lord of everything, its Creator, and its Manager. Al-Ālamūn (worlds) is the plural of Al-'Ālam (world) and refers to all in existence other than Allah.

(٣) ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

Further extolling Allah, the Exalted, that He is the Most Merciful and Compassionate after praising Him in the preceding verse.

(٤) مَٰلِكِ يَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ

Glorification of Allah the Transcendent that he is the Owner of everything on the Day of Resurrection when a soul will not possess anything for another. The “Day of Judgment” is the Day of Recompense and Reckoning.

(٥) إِيَّاكَ نَعۡبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسۡتَعِينُ

We single You out alone in all forms of worship and obedience, and we do not associate anyone with You. We seek help in all of our affairs from You alone, for all virtue belongs with You, and there is no helper other than You.

(٦) ٱهۡدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمَ

Guide us to the Straight Path, put us firmly upon it, and increase us in guidance. As-Sirat al-Mustaqim is the clear path in which there is no crookedness, and that is Islam, which Allah sent Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم with.

(٧) صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ غَيۡرِ ٱلۡمَغۡضُوبِ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

The path of those slaves whom You have bestowed with guidance, such as the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the pious, for they are the ones worthy of companionship. [And do not guide us] to the path of those who have earned Your anger after recognizing the Truth and not following it (i.e., the Jews) and not of those who went astray for being neglectful in seeking the Truth and being guided by it (i.e., the Christians).

Points of Benefit:

Allah starts His Book with the basmalah (In the name of Allah), demonstrating to His slaves that they should begin their actions and speech with it, seeking His aid and His guidance.
Guiding Allah's righteous slaves to begin supplication by glorifying and praising Him (the Exalted) and then proceeding to request their needs.
Warning Muslims against negligence in seeking the truth, like Christians who became misguided or failed to act on the truth after recognizing it, like the Jews who earned Allah's anger.
The surah demonstrates that faith is perfected by worshipping Allah the Transcendent sincerely and seeking His help alone and none besides Him.
