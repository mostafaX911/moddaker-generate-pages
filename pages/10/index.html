(٦٢) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱلَّذِينَ هَادُواْ وَٱلنَّصَٰرَىٰ وَٱلصَّٰبِ‍ِٔينَ مَنۡ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَلَهُمۡ أَجۡرُهُمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ وَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

Those who believe in Allah and the Final Day from this nation and the previous nations before the deputation of Muhammad, from the Jews, the Christians, and Sabians [a group of followers of one of the prophets] will have their reward with their Lord. They will not fear what awaits them in the Hereafter nor grieve over what they lost on earth.

(٦٣) وَإِذۡ أَخَذۡنَا مِيثَٰقَكُمۡ وَرَفَعۡنَا فَوۡقَكُمُ ٱلطُّورَ خُذُواْ مَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰكُم بِقُوَّةٖ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ مَا فِيهِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ

Remember when We took your firm covenant, O Children of Israel, to believe in Allah and His Messenger. We raised the mountain over you as a warning and to instill fear of the consequences of not abiding by the covenant, telling you: Take the Torah that we have revealed to you with seriousness and diligence, without taking it for granted or being lazy about it. And protect what it [the Torah] contains and ponder upon it so that you might start working to protect yourself against Allah's punishment.

(٦٤) ثُمَّ تَوَلَّيۡتُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَۖ فَلَوۡلَا فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَرَحۡمَتُهُۥ لَكُنتُم مِّنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ

Yet even after making a firm covenant, you turned away and rebelled. Had it not been for Allah's favor upon you, His tolerance towards you, and His Mercy in accepting your repentance, you would surely be among the losers for abandoning the covenant and your disobedience.

(٦٥) وَلَقَدۡ عَلِمۡتُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱعۡتَدَوۡاْ مِنكُمۡ فِي ٱلسَّبۡتِ فَقُلۡنَا لَهُمۡ كُونُواْ قِرَدَةً خَٰسِ‍ِٔينَ

You know with absolute certainty about your ancestors' story when they transgressed with their hunting on Saturday, a day on which they were prohibited from fishing. So they would cheat by setting up nets before Saturday and pulling them out on Sunday. As a result of their tricks, Allah turned these deceivers into ostracized and outcast monkeys, a consequence of their deception.

(٦٦) فَجَعَلۡنَٰهَا نَكَٰلٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهَا وَمَا خَلۡفَهَا وَمَوۡعِظَةٗ لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Allah made an example out of this transgressing village for surrounding villages and an example to those who came after so that they would not engage in the same behavior and become deserving of its consequences. And We made it a reminder for the pious who fear Allah's punishment and His retribution for transgressing His limits.

(٦٧) وَإِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأۡمُرُكُمۡ أَن تَذۡبَحُواْ بَقَرَةٗۖ قَالُوٓاْ أَتَتَّخِذُنَا هُزُوٗاۖ قَالَ أَعُوذُ بِٱللَّهِ أَنۡ أَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِينَ

From the stories of your ancestors, remember what happened between them and Moses (peace be upon him) when he told them that Allah had commanded them to sacrifice a cow among the cattle. Instead of hastening to comply, they stubbornly asked: Are you trying to make a mockery out of us?! Moses responded: I seek refuge in Allah for being among those who lie about Allah and mock people.

(٦٨) قَالُواْ ٱدۡعُ لَنَا رَبَّكَ يُبَيِّن لَّنَا مَا هِيَۚ قَالَ إِنَّهُۥ يَقُولُ إِنَّهَا بَقَرَةٞ لَّا فَارِضٞ وَلَا بِكۡرٌ عَوَانُۢ بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَۖ فَٱفۡعَلُواْ مَا تُؤۡمَرُونَ

Then they asked Moses: Call upon your Lord for us so He can clarify the characteristics of the cow that He commanded us to sacrifice. Moses responded: Allah says that it be a cow that was neither old nor young but was between the two, so hasten to comply with your Lord's command.

(٦٩) قَالُواْ ٱدۡعُ لَنَا رَبَّكَ يُبَيِّن لَّنَا مَا لَوۡنُهَاۚ قَالَ إِنَّهُۥ يَقُولُ إِنَّهَا بَقَرَةٞ صَفۡرَآءُ فَاقِعٞ لَّوۡنُهَا تَسُرُّ ٱلنَّٰظِرِينَ

They continued debating and being stubborn, telling Moses (peace be upon him): Call upon your Lord to clarify the color of the cow for us. Moses replied: Allah says that it be a yellow cow, bright in color, pleasing to everyone who sees it.

Points of Benefit:

The ruling in the first verse is for that which was before the advent of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم; after his prophethood, the religion that is acceptable to Allah is Islam, and He does not accept any other religion, as He said: "And whoever seeks a religion other than Islam, it will never be accepted of him, and in the Hereafter he will be one of the losers." [3:85]
Allah, the Exalted, may hasten the punishment of some sins in this world before the Hereafter so that it serves as a reminder for people to be reminded and so that they may be warned from opposing Allah's command.
Whoever restricts and imposes difficulty on himself regarding what is vast in the legislation may be punished if it becomes difficult.
