(١٣٥) ۞يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُونُواْ قَوَّٰمِينَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِ شُهَدَآءَ لِلَّهِ وَلَوۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَوِ ٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ وَٱلۡأَقۡرَبِينَۚ إِن يَكُنۡ غَنِيًّا أَوۡ فَقِيرٗا فَٱللَّهُ أَوۡلَىٰ بِهِمَاۖ فَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ ٱلۡهَوَىٰٓ أَن تَعۡدِلُواْۚ وَإِن تَلۡوُۥٓاْ أَوۡ تُعۡرِضُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٗا

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, stand up for justice in all circumstances: testify to the truth concerning every person, even if that requires the truth to be proven against yourself, your parents, or close relatives. Do not be motivated to give or withhold testimony due to anyone's poverty or wealth. Allah knows best what is in the interest of the poor and wealthy among you. Do not follow your whims in testimony, lest you deviate from the truth. If you falsify testimony by giving it inaccurately or refuse to provide it, then Allah is All-Aware of what you are doing.

(١٣٦) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ ءَامِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلَّذِي نَزَّلَ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِهِۦ وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ مِن قَبۡلُۚ وَمَن يَكۡفُرۡ بِٱللَّهِ وَمَلَٰٓئِكَتِهِۦ وَكُتُبِهِۦ وَرُسُلِهِۦ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ فَقَدۡ ضَلَّ ضَلَٰلَۢا بَعِيدًا

O you who believe, be firm in your faith in Allah, His Messenger, the Quran which He sent down to His Messenger, and the books He revealed to the messengers before him. Whoever disbelieves in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, and the Day of Judgment has strayed far from the straight path.

(١٣٧) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ثُمَّ كَفَرُواْ ثُمَّ ءَامَنُواْ ثُمَّ كَفَرُواْ ثُمَّ ٱزۡدَادُواْ كُفۡرٗا لَّمۡ يَكُنِ ٱللَّهُ لِيَغۡفِرَ لَهُمۡ وَلَا لِيَهۡدِيَهُمۡ سَبِيلَۢا

Those who repeatedly disbelieve after accepting faith – by entering into the faith, then leaving it, entering into it again, then leaving again, and so on – and then insist on disbelief until they die upon it, Allah will not forgive their sins and nor will He lead them to the straight path that leads to Him.

(١٣٨) بَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ بِأَنَّ لَهُمۡ عَذَابًا أَلِيمًا

O Prophet, announce to the hypocrites who manifest faith externally and hide disbelief internally that Allah has a tormenting punishment for them on the Day of Judgment.

(١٣٩) ٱلَّذِينَ يَتَّخِذُونَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ أَوۡلِيَآءَ مِن دُونِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَۚ أَيَبۡتَغُونَ عِندَهُمُ ٱلۡعِزَّةَ فَإِنَّ ٱلۡعِزَّةَ لِلَّهِ جَمِيعٗا

This punishment is because they took disbelievers as helpers and supporters instead of believers. How astonishing is that which made them take disbelievers as allies?! Were they seeking power and strength by which to advance? All power and strength belong to Allah.

(١٤٠) وَقَدۡ نَزَّلَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أَنۡ إِذَا سَمِعۡتُمۡ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ يُكۡفَرُ بِهَا وَيُسۡتَهۡزَأُ بِهَا فَلَا تَقۡعُدُواْ مَعَهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ يَخُوضُواْ فِي حَدِيثٍ غَيۡرِهِۦٓ إِنَّكُمۡ إِذٗا مِّثۡلُهُمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ جَامِعُ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ وَٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ فِي جَهَنَّمَ جَمِيعًا

Allah has already sent down to you, O believers, in the noble Quran that if you sit in a gathering and you hear someone disbelieving in Allah's verses, making fun of them, then you are obligated to get up and leave their gathering until they begin speaking about something other than disbelief in Allah's verses and making fun of it. If you sit with them after hearing them disbelieve in and mock Allah's verses, then you are like them in opposing Allah's command. This is because you disobeyed Allah by sitting with them just as they disobeyed Him by their disbelief. Surely, Allah will gather the hypocrites who exhibit Islam but hide disbelief and the disbelievers together in the Fire on the Day of Judgment.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation of justice in dispensing judgment among people and when giving testimony, even if the truth is against oneself or a close relative.
It is incumbent upon the believer to strive to perform actions that enhance his faith, encompassing both the deeds of the heart and limbs and to establish them firmly in his heart.
The great danger posed by the hypocrites to Islam and its followers is immense; thus, Allah has warned them of the severest punishment in the Hereafter.
If a believer is unable to denounce someone who disrespects the signs of Allah and His legislation, it is not permissible for him to sit with that person under such circumstances.
