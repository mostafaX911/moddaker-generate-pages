(١٤١) ٱلَّذِينَ يَتَرَبَّصُونَ بِكُمۡ فَإِن كَانَ لَكُمۡ فَتۡحٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ قَالُوٓاْ أَلَمۡ نَكُن مَّعَكُمۡ وَإِن كَانَ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ نَصِيبٞ قَالُوٓاْ أَلَمۡ نَسۡتَحۡوِذۡ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَنَمۡنَعۡكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَۚ فَٱللَّهُ يَحۡكُمُ بَيۡنَكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۗ وَلَن يَجۡعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ سَبِيلًا

Those who wait in anticipation to see whether good or evil happens to you will say if Allah gives you victory and spoils of war: Were we not alongside you, witnessing what you saw? That is to obtain some spoils of war. If the disbelievers have some success, they say to them: Did we not protect you and defend you against the believers by assisting you instead of them? Allah will judge between all of you on the Day of Judgment, rewarding the believers by admitting them into Paradise and repaying the hypocrites by entering them to the lowest depths of the Fire. Allah, by His favor, will never provide the disbelievers with proof against the believers on the Day of Judgment.

(١٤٢) إِنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ يُخَٰدِعُونَ ٱللَّهَ وَهُوَ خَٰدِعُهُمۡ وَإِذَا قَامُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ قَامُواْ كُسَالَىٰ يُرَآءُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ وَلَا يَذۡكُرُونَ ٱللَّهَ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

The hypocrites try to deceive Allah by exhibiting Islam on the outside while hiding disbelief. Allah deceives them because He protects their lives despite knowing about their disbelief. In the Afterlife, He has prepared the most severe punishment for them. When they stand for prayer, they do so lazily, with reluctance. They only remember Allah a little when they see the believers.

(١٤٣) مُّذَبۡذَبِينَ بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَ لَآ إِلَىٰ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ وَلَآ إِلَىٰ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِۚ وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَلَن تَجِدَ لَهُۥ سَبِيلٗا

These hypocrites are wavering in confusion. Outwardly and inwardly, they are neither with the believers nor the disbelievers. On the outside, they are with the believers; inside, they are with the disbelievers. You, O Messenger, will never find a way to lead out of misguidance whomever Allah misguides.

(١٤٤) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَتَّخِذُواْ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ أَوۡلِيَآءَ مِن دُونِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَۚ أَتُرِيدُونَ أَن تَجۡعَلُواْ لِلَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ سُلۡطَٰنٗا مُّبِينًا

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, do not take those who disbelieve in Allah as close friends, taking them as allies instead of believers. By doing so, do you wish to give Allah clear evidence against you, proving that you deserve punishment?!

(١٤٥) إِنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ فِي ٱلدَّرۡكِ ٱلۡأَسۡفَلِ مِنَ ٱلنَّارِ وَلَن تَجِدَ لَهُمۡ نَصِيرًا

Allah will place the hypocrites in the lowest place in the Fire on the Day of Judgment. You will never find any helper to defend them against the punishment.

(١٤٦) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ تَابُواْ وَأَصۡلَحُواْ وَٱعۡتَصَمُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَأَخۡلَصُواْ دِينَهُمۡ لِلَّهِ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ مَعَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَۖ وَسَوۡفَ يُؤۡتِ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ أَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا

Except for those who return to Allah repenting from their hypocrisy, rectifying themselves internally, holding firmly to Allah's covenant, performing their actions sincerely for Him, without showing off- those described with these characteristics will be with the believers in this worldly life and the Afterlife. Allah will give the believers a considerable reward.

(١٤٧) مَّا يَفۡعَلُ ٱللَّهُ بِعَذَابِكُمۡ إِن شَكَرۡتُمۡ وَءَامَنتُمۡۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ شَاكِرًا عَلِيمٗا

There is no need for Allah to punish you if you are thankful to Him and believe. He is the One who is Kind and Merciful. He only punishes you for your sins. If you rectify your actions, you are thankful to Him for His favors and believe in Him both outwardly and inwardly, He will never punish you. Allah is Ever-Grateful to the person who acknowledges His favors, and the reward for them is abundant. He is All-Knowing regarding the faith of His creation and will requite every person for his actions.

Points of Benefit:

Describing the characteristics of the hypocrites, which includes their eagerness to get their share, whether with the believers or the disbelievers.
One of the most robust characteristics of the hypocrites is their wavering, confusion, and uneasiness, as they do not truly belong with the believers nor with the disbelievers.
A strong prohibition against taking disbelievers as allies instead of believers.
The greatest means by which a person can protect themselves from the punishment of Allah in the Hereafter is through belief and righteous deeds.
