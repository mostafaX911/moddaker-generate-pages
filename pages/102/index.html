(١٤٨) ۞لَّا يُحِبُّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡجَهۡرَ بِٱلسُّوٓءِ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ إِلَّا مَن ظُلِمَۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ سَمِيعًا عَلِيمًا

Allah does not like evil words to be said in public. Rather, He hates it and threatens to punish it. However, if someone has been wronged, he may mention the evil words to complain about who wronged him and to supplicate against him. But it is better for the person who is wronged to be patient instead of mentioning such evil. Allah hears what you say and knows your intentions, so beware of saying or intending any evil.

(١٤٩) إِن تُبۡدُواْ خَيۡرًا أَوۡ تُخۡفُوهُ أَوۡ تَعۡفُواْ عَن سُوٓءٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَفُوّٗا قَدِيرًا

Whether you make your good deeds known, keep them secret, or overlook someone who acted negatively towards you, Allah is eternally Pardoning and Powerful. Therefore, have a pardoning character so that Allah may pardon you.

(١٥٠) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَكۡفُرُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَرُسُلِهِۦ وَيُرِيدُونَ أَن يُفَرِّقُواْ بَيۡنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرُسُلِهِۦ وَيَقُولُونَ نُؤۡمِنُ بِبَعۡضٖ وَنَكۡفُرُ بِبَعۡضٖ وَيُرِيدُونَ أَن يَتَّخِذُواْ بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَ سَبِيلًا

Indeed, there are those who disbelieve in Allah and His messengers, and who want to make a distinction between Allah and His messengers by believing in Allah yet rejecting His messengers by saying, 'We accept some messengers and reject others'. They want to take a path between disbelief and belief, but are severely deluded in thinking that this will save them.

(١٥١) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ حَقّٗاۚ وَأَعۡتَدۡنَا لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَذَابٗا مُّهِينٗا

Those who that do that are, in fact, disbelievers because whoever disbelieves in the messengers, or some of them, has actually disbelieved in Allah and all of His messengers. We have prepared a humiliating torment for disbelievers on The Day of Resurrection as a punishment for their refusal to have faith in Allah and His messengers.

Upon mentioning the recompense for the disbelievers, Allah subsequently notes the recompense for the believers, stating:

(١٥٢) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرُسُلِهِۦ وَلَمۡ يُفَرِّقُواْ بَيۡنَ أَحَدٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ سَوۡفَ يُؤۡتِيهِمۡ أُجُورَهُمۡۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا

As for those who have faith in Allah, worship Him alone, do not make any partners with Him, and believe in all His messengers without distinguishing between them as the disbelievers do, Allah will reward them tremendously for their belief and the righteous deeds they do based on that belief. Allah is always forgiving and merciful to His repenting slaves.

(١٥٣) يَسۡ‍َٔلُكَ أَهۡلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أَن تُنَزِّلَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ كِتَٰبٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِۚ فَقَدۡ سَأَلُواْ مُوسَىٰٓ أَكۡبَرَ مِن ذَٰلِكَ فَقَالُوٓاْ أَرِنَا ٱللَّهَ جَهۡرَةٗ فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلصَّٰعِقَةُ بِظُلۡمِهِمۡۚ ثُمَّ ٱتَّخَذُواْ ٱلۡعِجۡلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَتۡهُمُ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتُ فَعَفَوۡنَا عَن ذَٰلِكَۚ وَءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَىٰ سُلۡطَٰنٗا مُّبِينٗا

O Messenger, the Jews ask you to bring them a complete book from the sky, just like what happened to Moses, to prove that you are telling the truth. Do not be surprised by them, as their ancestors asked Moses for much greater than that when they asked him to show them Allah. They were then struck by a thunderbolt as a punishment for their actions and brought back to life. Then, they worshipped the calf instead of Allah even after receiving clear evidence that Allah is One, the only Lord of everything and the only One worthy of worship. Then, we forgave them and gave Moses clear evidence against his people.

(١٥٤) وَرَفَعۡنَا فَوۡقَهُمُ ٱلطُّورَ بِمِيثَٰقِهِمۡ وَقُلۡنَا لَهُمُ ٱدۡخُلُواْ ٱلۡبَابَ سُجَّدٗا وَقُلۡنَا لَهُمۡ لَا تَعۡدُواْ فِي ٱلسَّبۡتِ وَأَخَذۡنَا مِنۡهُم مِّيثَٰقًا غَلِيظٗا

To take a solemn pledge from them, frighten them, and make them carry out its stipulations, We raised the mountain over them and said to them: Enter the gate of Jerusalem prostrating by bowing your heads. However, they entered by dragging their behinds on the floor. We also told them: Do not disobey me by fishing on the Sabbath. Despite this, they rebelled and did what We commanded them not to do. We took a firm and solemn pledge from them regarding this, but they broke it.

Points of Benefit:

It is permissible for the oppressed person to speak about his injustice and his oppressor to someone who is expected to take his rights, even if he says something that does not please the oppressor.
Urging the oppressed to forgive, even if he is capable, just as the Lord [may He be Exalted] forgives with His ability to punish His slaves.
It is not permissible to differentiate between the messengers by believing in some rather than others, but one must believe in all.
