(١٥٥) فَبِمَا نَقۡضِهِم مِّيثَٰقَهُمۡ وَكُفۡرِهِم بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَقَتۡلِهِمُ ٱلۡأَنۢبِيَآءَ بِغَيۡرِ حَقّٖ وَقَوۡلِهِمۡ قُلُوبُنَا غُلۡفُۢۚ بَلۡ طَبَعَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهَا بِكُفۡرِهِمۡ فَلَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

We distanced them from Our mercy because they broke the promise that was taken from them, and because of disbelieves in Allah's signs, and their boldness in killing prophets sent to them, and for telling Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم: 'Our hearts are covered, so we cannot understand what you say.' That is not the case. Instead, Allah has sealed their hearts due to their disbelief, so no good reaches their hearts. They only have a little faith, which will not be of any use to them.

(١٥٦) وَبِكُفۡرِهِمۡ وَقَوۡلِهِمۡ عَلَىٰ مَرۡيَمَ بُهۡتَٰنًا عَظِيمٗا

We expelled them from our Mercy due to their disbelief and because they slandered Mary (peace be upon her) by falsely accusing her of fornication.

(١٥٧) وَقَوۡلِهِمۡ إِنَّا قَتَلۡنَا ٱلۡمَسِيحَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ رَسُولَ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا قَتَلُوهُ وَمَا صَلَبُوهُ وَلَٰكِن شُبِّهَ لَهُمۡۚ وَإِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱخۡتَلَفُواْ فِيهِ لَفِي شَكّٖ مِّنۡهُۚ مَا لَهُم بِهِۦ مِنۡ عِلۡمٍ إِلَّا ٱتِّبَاعَ ٱلظَّنِّۚ وَمَا قَتَلُوهُ يَقِينَۢا

I cursed them because they proudly, but falsely, said: We killed the Messiah, Jesus, son of Mary, the messenger of Allah. They did not kill him as they claimed, nor did they crucify him, but they killed and crucified a man whom Allah made to resemble Jesus, so they thought the person who was killed was Jesus. Those Jews who claimed to have killed him and those Christians who surrendered him over to them are in doubt and confusion regarding the matter. They have no knowledge, but make guesses that are of no worth against the truth. Truly, they did not kill Jesus nor crucify him.

(١٥٨) بَل رَّفَعَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِلَيۡهِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَزِيزًا حَكِيمٗا

Rather, Allah saved him from their plot and raised him up (body and soul) to Him. Allah is eternally Mighty in His sovereignty; nobody can overcome Him. He is eternally Wise in His management, decree, and legislation.

(١٥٩) وَإِن مِّنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ إِلَّا لَيُؤۡمِنَنَّ بِهِۦ قَبۡلَ مَوۡتِهِۦۖ وَيَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ يَكُونُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ شَهِيدٗا

There will be none from the People of the Book except that they will believe in Jesus (peace be upon him) before his death after he returns towards the end of time. Then, on the Day of Resurrection, Jesus (peace be upon him) will bear witness to which of their actions agreed with the legislation and which opposed it.

(١٦٠) فَبِظُلۡمٖ مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ هَادُواْ حَرَّمۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ طَيِّبَٰتٍ أُحِلَّتۡ لَهُمۡ وَبِصَدِّهِمۡ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ كَثِيرٗا

As the Jews did wrong, we prohibited some of the good foods previously permitted for them, such as animals with undivided hoofs and the fat of cows and sheep, except for the back fat. This was also because they prevented themselves and others from Allah's path to the extent that they became known for obstructing others from doing good.

(١٦١) وَأَخۡذِهِمُ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْ وَقَدۡ نُهُواْ عَنۡهُ وَأَكۡلِهِمۡ أَمۡوَٰلَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِۚ وَأَعۡتَدۡنَا لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ مِنۡهُمۡ عَذَابًا أَلِيمٗا

This was also due to them dealing with usury after Allah prohibited them from taking it and because they misappropriated people's wealth without any legal right. We have prepared an excruciating torment for the disbelievers among them.

After mentioning the shortcomings of the People of the Book, He acknowledges the believers among them, stating:

(١٦٢) لَّٰكِنِ ٱلرَّٰسِخُونَ فِي ٱلۡعِلۡمِ مِنۡهُمۡ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ مِن قَبۡلِكَۚ وَٱلۡمُقِيمِينَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَۚ وَٱلۡمُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ سَنُؤۡتِيهِمۡ أَجۡرًا عَظِيمًا

But those Jews who are firmly grounded in knowledge and the Believers believe in the Quran that Allah sent to you (O Messenger) and the books that He sent to the messengers before you, like the Torah and the Gospel. They also establish the prayer, pay Zakat (obligatory alms) from their wealth, believe that Allah is the only One worthy of worship without any partner, and believe in the Day of Resurrection. We will give a great reward to those that have such qualities.

Points of Benefit:

The consequence of disbelief is a seal on hearts. The seal on them is a reason to deprive them of understanding.
Demonstrating the Jews' hostility to the Prophet of Allah, Jesus, peace be upon him, to the point that they reached the stage of trying to kill him.
Explaining the ignorance and confusion of Christians regarding the issue of the crucifixion and their behavior towards it with corrupt suspicions.
Explaining the virtue of knowledge, for among the People of the Book are those who are proficient in knowledge until this mastery led him to believe in the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم.
