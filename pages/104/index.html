(١٦٣) ۞إِنَّآ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ كَمَآ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ نُوحٖ وَٱلنَّبِيِّ‍ۧنَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦۚ وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلَ وَإِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَ وَٱلۡأَسۡبَاطِ وَعِيسَىٰ وَأَيُّوبَ وَيُونُسَ وَهَٰرُونَ وَسُلَيۡمَٰنَۚ وَءَاتَيۡنَا دَاوُۥدَ زَبُورٗا

I have sent revelation to you, O Messenger, as I had sent revelation to the prophets before you. This revelation to you is not something strange or unexpected. I had sent revelation to Noah and to the prophets who came after him. I sent revelation to Abraham and his two sons, Ishmael and Isaac, and to Jacob, the son of Isaac, and to the Tribes (which refers to the prophets that were from the twelve tribes of the Israelites from the children of Jacob). I also gave David a book called the Psalms.

(١٦٤) وَرُسُلٗا قَدۡ قَصَصۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَيۡكَ مِن قَبۡلُ وَرُسُلٗا لَّمۡ نَقۡصُصۡهُمۡ عَلَيۡكَۚ وَكَلَّمَ ٱللَّهُ مُوسَىٰ تَكۡلِيمٗا

We have sent messengers that we have mentioned to you in the Quran, and we have also sent messengers that we have not mentioned to you. We did not tell you about them for a reason. Allah spoke directly to Moses with the revelation without any intermediary, speaking in a way that befits Him (The Exalted and Most High) to honor Moses.

(١٦٥) رُّسُلٗا مُّبَشِّرِينَ وَمُنذِرِينَ لِئَلَّا يَكُونَ لِلنَّاسِ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ حُجَّةُۢ بَعۡدَ ٱلرُّسُلِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَزِيزًا حَكِيمٗا

We sent them as bearers of glad tidings of a generous reward for those who believe in Allah and to warn those who disbelieve in Him of a painful torment. That is so that the people have no proof to use against Allah to excuse themselves after He had sent the messengers. Allah is eternally Mighty in His sovereignty and eternally Wise in his decree.

(١٦٦) لَّٰكِنِ ٱللَّهُ يَشۡهَدُ بِمَآ أَنزَلَ إِلَيۡكَۖ أَنزَلَهُۥ بِعِلۡمِهِۦۖ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ يَشۡهَدُونَۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ شَهِيدًا

Even if the Jews disbelieve in you, Allah testifies to the truth of what He sent to you, O Muhammad, from the Quran. In it, He has revealed the knowledge that He wants His slaves to know from what He loves, likes, dislikes, and condemns. The angels bear witness along with the testimony of Allah that what you have brought is true. Sufficient is Allah as a witness, for his testimony is enough without needing anyone else's.

(١٦٧) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَصَدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ قَدۡ ضَلُّواْ ضَلَٰلَۢا بَعِيدًا

Indeed, those who disbelieve in your prophethood and hinder people from embracing Islam have strayed far from the truth.

(١٦٨) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَظَلَمُواْ لَمۡ يَكُنِ ٱللَّهُ لِيَغۡفِرَ لَهُمۡ وَلَا لِيَهۡدِيَهُمۡ طَرِيقًا

Indeed, those who disbelieve in Allah and His messengers and wrong themselves by persisting in disbelief never will Allah forgive them so long as they keep disbelieving, nor will He guide them to the path that leads them to salvation from the punishment of Allah.

(١٦٩) إِلَّا طَرِيقَ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدٗاۚ وَكَانَ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرٗا

Except for the path that leads them to enter Hell, where they will abide forever. That is easy for Allah, as there is nothing that He cannot do.

(١٧٠) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَكُمُ ٱلرَّسُولُ بِٱلۡحَقِّ مِن رَّبِّكُمۡ فَ‍َٔامِنُواْ خَيۡرٗا لَّكُمۡۚ وَإِن تَكۡفُرُواْ فَإِنَّ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمٗا

O people, verily the Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم has come to you with guidance and the true religion from Allah, the Exalted. Therefore, have faith in what has been brought to you, as it is better for you both in this world and the Hereafter. But if you disbelieve in Allah, know that Allah does not need your faith, and your disbelief does not harm him, for He owns all that is in the heavens, all that is on the Earth, and everything in between. He is eternally Knowledgeable of those who deserve His guidance (and so makes it easy for them) and those who do not (and so makes them blind to it). He is eternally Wise in His speech, actions, legislation, and decree.

Points of Benefit:

The proof of prophecy and message regarding Noah, Abraham, and others among their descendants, those whom Allah mentioned and those whose news was not mentioned, for wisdom known to Him (may He be Exalted).
Affirming the attribute of Speech for Allah, the Exalted, in a manner befitting His essence and majesty. He spoke to Prophet Moses, peace be upon him.
Consoling the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم by explaining that Allah [the Exalted] bears witness to the truth of his claim to be a prophet, and so do the angels.
