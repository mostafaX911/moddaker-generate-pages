(١٧١) يَٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَا تَغۡلُواْ فِي دِينِكُمۡ وَلَا تَقُولُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلۡحَقَّۚ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمَسِيحُ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ وَكَلِمَتُهُۥٓ أَلۡقَىٰهَآ إِلَىٰ مَرۡيَمَ وَرُوحٞ مِّنۡهُۖ فَ‍َٔامِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرُسُلِهِۦۖ وَلَا تَقُولُواْ ثَلَٰثَةٌۚ ٱنتَهُواْ خَيۡرٗا لَّكُمۡۚ إِنَّمَا ٱللَّهُ إِلَٰهٞ وَٰحِدٞۖ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥٓ أَن يَكُونَ لَهُۥ وَلَدٞۘ لَّهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ وَكِيلٗا

Say, O Messenger, to the Christians who received the Gospel: Do not overstep the limits of your religion, and do not say anything but the truth about Allah concerning Jesus. The Messiah, Jesus, son of Mary, is only Allah's messenger sent with the truth. He created him by His word, which He sent with Gabriel to Mary, which was the word 'Be,' and he became. It was a breath from Allah that Gabriel blew with Allah's instruction. So have faith in Allah and all His messengers without making a distinction between them. Do not say, 'The Divine is three' (i.e., Trinity). Avoid saying this corrupt, false statement, and it will be better for you in this world and the Hereafter. Allah is One, free of any partner or child. He is Self-Sufficient. The dominion of the heavens, the Earth, and whatever is in between the two belong to Him. He is sufficient as a guardian to carry out the affairs of His creation.

(١٧٢) لَّن يَسۡتَنكِفَ ٱلۡمَسِيحُ أَن يَكُونَ عَبۡدٗا لِّلَّهِ وَلَا ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَۚ وَمَن يَسۡتَنكِفۡ عَنۡ عِبَادَتِهِۦ وَيَسۡتَكۡبِرۡ فَسَيَحۡشُرُهُمۡ إِلَيۡهِ جَمِيعٗا

Jesus, Son of Mary, will never be too proud or object to being a slave of Allah. Likewise, the angels who Allah has brought close to Him and elevated their status will not reject being slaves of Allah. So, how can you then take Jesus as a deity?! Or how can the polytheists worship the angels?! Whoever is too proud to worship Allah and looks down on that, Allah will gather them before Him on the Day of Resurrection and give each one what he deserves.

After Allah mentions that He will gather everyone together before Him, he defines their recompense, stating:

(١٧٣) فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ فَيُوَفِّيهِمۡ أُجُورَهُمۡ وَيَزِيدُهُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦۖ وَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَنكَفُواْ وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ فَيُعَذِّبُهُمۡ عَذَابًا أَلِيمٗا وَلَا يَجِدُونَ لَهُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَلِيّٗا وَلَا نَصِيرٗا

As for those who have faith in Allah, believe in His messengers, and do righteous deeds purely for His sake according to the legislation, Allah will give them their full reward for what they have done and give them even more from His bounty and kindness. But for those who were too proud to worship Allah and obey Him, and behaved arrogantly, He will punish them with excruciating torment. They will not have any ally besides Allah who can help or protect them from harm.

(١٧٤) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَكُم بُرۡهَٰنٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَأَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكُمۡ نُورٗا مُّبِينٗا

O people, verily, a decisive proof has come to you from your Lord that does not leave any excuses and removes all doubts, which is Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. We have also sent down to you a clear light, and that is the Quran.

(١٧٥) فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱعۡتَصَمُواْ بِهِۦ فَسَيُدۡخِلُهُمۡ فِي رَحۡمَةٖ مِّنۡهُ وَفَضۡلٖ وَيَهۡدِيهِمۡ إِلَيۡهِ صِرَٰطٗا مُّسۡتَقِيمٗا

As for those who believe in Allah and adhere to the Quran sent to their prophet, Allah will have mercy on them by admitting them into Paradise, increasing their reward, raising their rank, and guiding them to travel the straight path that has no bends, which is the path that leads to the Garden of Eden.

Points of Benefit:

Explaining that Christ and his mother were human. The misguided Christians went to extremism regarding Jesus, peace be upon him, and his mother until they removed them from the limit of humanity.
Explaining the invalidity of the polytheism of Christians who believe in the Trinity and the transcendence of Allah the Exalted from having a partner, similarity, or relative and explaining His uniqueness [may He be Exalted] in oneness in the self, names, and attributes.
Proving that Jesus, peace be upon him, and the angels are all created slaves who are not too arrogant to acknowledge their slavery to Allah the Exalted and obey His orders. So, how can it be justified to consider them as gods even though they are slaves to the Exalted Allah?!
There are rational arguments and proofs in religion that repel doubts. Also, there is light and guidance [in faith] that push back confusion and vain desires.
