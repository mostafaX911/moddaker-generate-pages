(٣) حُرِّمَتۡ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡمَيۡتَةُ وَٱلدَّمُ وَلَحۡمُ ٱلۡخِنزِيرِ وَمَآ أُهِلَّ لِغَيۡرِ ٱللَّهِ بِهِۦ وَٱلۡمُنۡخَنِقَةُ وَٱلۡمَوۡقُوذَةُ وَٱلۡمُتَرَدِّيَةُ وَٱلنَّطِيحَةُ وَمَآ أَكَلَ ٱلسَّبُعُ إِلَّا مَا ذَكَّيۡتُمۡ وَمَا ذُبِحَ عَلَى ٱلنُّصُبِ وَأَن تَسۡتَقۡسِمُواْ بِٱلۡأَزۡلَٰمِۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ فِسۡقٌۗ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ يَئِسَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن دِينِكُمۡ فَلَا تَخۡشَوۡهُمۡ وَٱخۡشَوۡنِۚ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ أَكۡمَلۡتُ لَكُمۡ دِينَكُمۡ وَأَتۡمَمۡتُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ نِعۡمَتِي وَرَضِيتُ لَكُمُ ٱلۡإِسۡلَٰمَ دِينٗاۚ فَمَنِ ٱضۡطُرَّ فِي مَخۡمَصَةٍ غَيۡرَ مُتَجَانِفٖ لِّإِثۡمٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Allah has forbidden you from eating those animals that die without being ritually slaughtered. He has also forbidden you from spilled blood, pig's meat, any animal on which other than the name of Allah was taken at the time of slaughter, any animal that was strangled or beaten to death, or one that fell from a high place, or any animal that was gored by another animal and any animal that was hunted by a predator, such as a lion, tiger or wolf, unless you reach any of these animals while they are alive and you slaughter them – in which case they will be lawful for you. He has forbidden you from those animals that were sacrificed for idols. He has also prohibited you from drawing lots to know whether something is destined for you or not or to use as a means of making a decision when intending to do something or not. By doing these prohibited things, a person is leaving obedience to Allah. Today, the disbelievers have lost hope that you will leave the religion of Islam because they see its strength. So do not fear them, but fear Me alone. Today I have perfected for you your religion, which is Islam. I have completed My apparent and hidden favors upon you. I have selected Islam as your religion, so I will not accept any other. If anyone is forced due to hunger to eat from an animal that was not slaughtered correctly, with no inclination to sin, there is no sin on such a person to do so. Allah is Forgiving and Compassionate.

After mentioning what He prohibited to eat, Allah mentions what He has permitted the consumption of, stating:

(٤) يَسۡ‍َٔلُونَكَ مَاذَآ أُحِلَّ لَهُمۡۖ قُلۡ أُحِلَّ لَكُمُ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتُ وَمَا عَلَّمۡتُم مِّنَ ٱلۡجَوَارِحِ مُكَلِّبِينَ تُعَلِّمُونَهُنَّ مِمَّا عَلَّمَكُمُ ٱللَّهُۖ فَكُلُواْ مِمَّآ أَمۡسَكۡنَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱسۡمَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡهِۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

Your Companions ask you: O Messenger, what has Allah allowed them to eat? Say, O Messenger: Allah has permitted all pure food, and also what was caught by your hunting animals, those that have canine teeth like dogs and cheetahs, and those that have talons like birds of prey. You trained them to hunt as Allah blessed you with knowledge on how to do that, such that they obey when ordered and leave what they are told to leave. So, eat what they catch when hunting for you, even if they kill it, and mention the name of Allah upon releasing [the hunting animal]. Be fearful of Allah by fulfilling His commands and abstaining from His prohibitions. Indeed, Allah is swift to account for your deeds.

(٥) ٱلۡيَوۡمَ أُحِلَّ لَكُمُ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتُۖ وَطَعَامُ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ حِلّٞ لَّكُمۡ وَطَعَامُكُمۡ حِلّٞ لَّهُمۡۖ وَٱلۡمُحۡصَنَٰتُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُحۡصَنَٰتُ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ إِذَآ ءَاتَيۡتُمُوهُنَّ أُجُورَهُنَّ مُحۡصِنِينَ غَيۡرَ مُسَٰفِحِينَ وَلَا مُتَّخِذِيٓ أَخۡدَانٖۗ وَمَن يَكۡفُرۡ بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ فَقَدۡ حَبِطَ عَمَلُهُۥ وَهُوَ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ

On this day, Allah has allowed you to eat wholesome food. He has also allowed you to eat slaughtered meat from the People of the Book (the Jews and the Christians) and allowed them to eat what you slaughter. He has permitted that you marry free, chaste women from the believers and from the Jews and Christians who received revelation before you, so long as you pay their dowries and were chaste, not committing immoral actions with them, nor taking them as secret lovers to fornicate with them. Whoever rejects the rulings Allah has legislated for His slaves will find his deeds nullified due to not fulfilling the prerequisite for accepting his action, which is true faith. He will be among the losers on the Day of Resurrection due to entering the Fire, where he will abide for all eternity.

Points of Benefit:

Prohibiting what died without dhakah (livestock not slaughtered according to ritual requirements), spilled blood, pork, and what is mentioned on it is a name other than the name of Allah at the time of slaughter. Likewise, every livestock that was killed by strangulation, beating, falling from a height, gored, or being eaten by a predator, except for what was caught alive and slaughtered, with legal slaughter.
Every hunt caught by a trained animal with a fang or a claw is permissible.
Permitting the sacrifices of the People of the Book and allowing the marriage of their chaste, free women.
