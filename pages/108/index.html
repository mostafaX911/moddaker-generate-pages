(٦) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا قُمۡتُمۡ إِلَى ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ فَٱغۡسِلُواْ وُجُوهَكُمۡ وَأَيۡدِيَكُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡمَرَافِقِ وَٱمۡسَحُواْ بِرُءُوسِكُمۡ وَأَرۡجُلَكُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡكَعۡبَيۡنِۚ وَإِن كُنتُمۡ جُنُبٗا فَٱطَّهَّرُواْۚ وَإِن كُنتُم مَّرۡضَىٰٓ أَوۡ عَلَىٰ سَفَرٍ أَوۡ جَآءَ أَحَدٞ مِّنكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡغَآئِطِ أَوۡ لَٰمَسۡتُمُ ٱلنِّسَآءَ فَلَمۡ تَجِدُواْ مَآءٗ فَتَيَمَّمُواْ صَعِيدٗا طَيِّبٗا فَٱمۡسَحُواْ بِوُجُوهِكُمۡ وَأَيۡدِيكُم مِّنۡهُۚ مَا يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ لِيَجۡعَلَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّنۡ حَرَجٖ وَلَٰكِن يُرِيدُ لِيُطَهِّرَكُمۡ وَلِيُتِمَّ نِعۡمَتَهُۥ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

O you who believe, when you want to establish the prayer, and you are in a state of minor impurity, then perform Wudhu (ritual ablution) by washing your faces and then your hands, including the elbows, then by wiping your heads and washing your feet together with the ankle bones that protrude from the shin. But, if you are in a state of major impurity, perform Ghusl (ritual bathing). If you are sick and you fear that the sickness will get worse or it will take longer to recover, or if you are well but on a journey, or if you are in a state of minor impurity after going to the toilet, for example, or in a state of major impurity after sexual intercourse, and you do not find water to purify yourselves with, despite having searched for it, then use the earth by striking it with your palms and then wiping over your faces and hands. Allah does not wish to make His rulings difficult by making you use water when it will harm you. So, He has legislated an alternative when you cannot use water because it is unavailable or you are sick to complete His favor upon you so that you may be thankful for what Allah blessed you with and not be ungrateful.

(٧) وَٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَمِيثَٰقَهُ ٱلَّذِي وَاثَقَكُم بِهِۦٓ إِذۡ قُلۡتُمۡ سَمِعۡنَا وَأَطَعۡنَاۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

Remember Allah's favor upon you by guiding you to Islam, and also remember the covenant He made with you when you pledged to hear and obey the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم in ease and difficulty, saying: We hear your words and obey your commands. Fear Allah by fulfilling His orders (which include His covenants) and abstaining from His prohibitions. Allah knows what is in the hearts; nothing is hidden from Him.

(٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُونُواْ قَوَّٰمِينَ لِلَّهِ شُهَدَآءَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۖ وَلَا يَجۡرِمَنَّكُمۡ شَنَ‍َٔانُ قَوۡمٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَعۡدِلُواْۚ ٱعۡدِلُواْ هُوَ أَقۡرَبُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and His Messenger, uphold Allah's rights upon you sincerely seeking His face and be witnesses justly and not oppressively. Do not let the hatred of a people cause you to abandon justice, for justice is required for friend and foe. So, be just with both of them because justice is like fearing Allah, while oppression is a kind of disrespect towards Him. Be fearful of Allah by fulfilling His commands and abstaining from His prohibitions. Indeed, Allah knows all that you do; none of your deeds are hidden from Him, and He will recompense them.

(٩) وَعَدَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٞ وَأَجۡرٌ عَظِيمٞ

Allah, who does not break any promise, has promised those who have faith in Allah and His Messenger and do righteous deeds that He will forgive their sins and give them a great reward, which is entry into Paradise.

Points of Benefit:

The basic principle of purification is to use water for ablution to remove minor impurity (a minor ritual impurity necessitates ablution only) and washing the body to remove a major impurity (a major ritual impurity requires a complete shower).
In case it is not possible to obtain water, or if it is impossible to use it due to a severe illness or severe coldness, it is authorized to perform Tayammum (dry ablution) using dirt to remove minor or major impurities.
The command is to be fair and avoid injustice, even in treating violators.
