(١٠) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَكَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

But those who disbelieve in Allah and reject His signs are the companions of the Fire; they will enter it as a punishment for their disbelief and rejection, remaining in it as a person stays with his companion.

(١١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِذۡ هَمَّ قَوۡمٌ أَن يَبۡسُطُوٓاْ إِلَيۡكُمۡ أَيۡدِيَهُمۡ فَكَفَّ أَيۡدِيَهُمۡ عَنكُمۡۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ

O you who believe, make remembrance with your hearts and tongues of what Allah blessed you with from security and striking fear in the hearts of your enemies when they wanted to lay hands on you and attack you. Allah turned them away from you and protected you from them. Therefore, fear Allah by fulfilling His commands and abstaining from His prohibitions. Let the believers rely solely on Allah to achieve their worldly and religious interests.

(١٢) ۞وَلَقَدۡ أَخَذَ ٱللَّهُ مِيثَٰقَ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ وَبَعَثۡنَا مِنۡهُمُ ٱثۡنَيۡ عَشَرَ نَقِيبٗاۖ وَقَالَ ٱللَّهُ إِنِّي مَعَكُمۡۖ لَئِنۡ أَقَمۡتُمُ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتَيۡتُمُ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَءَامَنتُم بِرُسُلِي وَعَزَّرۡتُمُوهُمۡ وَأَقۡرَضۡتُمُ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنٗا لَّأُكَفِّرَنَّ عَنكُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِكُمۡ وَلَأُدۡخِلَنَّكُمۡ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۚ فَمَن كَفَرَ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ مِنكُمۡ فَقَدۡ ضَلَّ سَوَآءَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ

Verily, Allah took a solemn pledge from the Israelites (which will be mentioned shortly) and put twelve chiefs over them. Each one would lead those under him. Then, Allah told the Israelites: I am with you with My aid and support if you fully establish the prayer, give zakat from your wealth, believe in all My Messengers without exception, honor them, aid them, and spend in good causes. If you do all of this, I will expiate the sins that you have committed, and on the Day of Resurrection, I will enter you into Paradise that has rivers flowing below its palaces. So, whoever disbelieves after taking this solemn pledge has knowingly and willingly strayed from the true path.

(١٣) فَبِمَا نَقۡضِهِم مِّيثَٰقَهُمۡ لَعَنَّٰهُمۡ وَجَعَلۡنَا قُلُوبَهُمۡ قَٰسِيَةٗۖ يُحَرِّفُونَ ٱلۡكَلِمَ عَن مَّوَاضِعِهِۦ وَنَسُواْ حَظّٗا مِّمَّا ذُكِّرُواْ بِهِۦۚ وَلَا تَزَالُ تَطَّلِعُ عَلَىٰ خَآئِنَةٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا مِّنۡهُمۡۖ فَٱعۡفُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَٱصۡفَحۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Because they broke their covenant, We distanced them from Our mercy and made their hearts so hard that they could not receive any good or admonition. They distorted [the revelation] by changing the actual words or interpreting their meaning according to their desires and abandoned some of what they were told to do. O Messenger, you will keep uncovering their treason against Allah and His believing slaves, except for some who fulfilled what the covenant required of them. Therefore, pardon them and do not call them accountable. Instead, overlook what they do as it is kind, and Allah loves those who are kind.

Points of Benefit:

One of the great blessings of Allah, may He be glorified and exalted, upon the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم and his companions, may Allah be pleased with them, is that He protected and shielded them from the hands of the people of disbelief and their harm.
Believing in the Messengers, supporting them, establishing prayer, and paying Zakat as required is a superb reason for attaining the presence of the Exalted Allah and for the causes of victory, empowerment, forgiveness, and entering Paradise.
Breaking the covenants obligating obedience to the messengers is a reason for the ruggedness and hardness of hearts.
Condemnation of the Jews' paths of altering the heavenly books revealed to them.
