(٧٠) قَالُواْ ٱدۡعُ لَنَا رَبَّكَ يُبَيِّن لَّنَا مَا هِيَ إِنَّ ٱلۡبَقَرَ تَشَٰبَهَ عَلَيۡنَا وَإِنَّآ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَمُهۡتَدُونَ

They persisted in their stubbornness, saying: Call upon your Lord to further clarify its description for us as many cows fit the current description, and therefore we cannot identify it. They insisted that they would then if Allah willed, be guided to the cow required to be sacrificed.

(٧١) قَالَ إِنَّهُۥ يَقُولُ إِنَّهَا بَقَرَةٞ لَّا ذَلُولٞ تُثِيرُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَلَا تَسۡقِي ٱلۡحَرۡثَ مُسَلَّمَةٞ لَّا شِيَةَ فِيهَاۚ قَالُواْ ٱلۡـَٰٔنَ جِئۡتَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ فَذَبَحُوهَا وَمَا كَادُواْ يَفۡعَلُونَ

So Moses replied: Allah says that the cow is not used for plowing or irrigating the earth; it is free from defects and has no other marks of color except its yellow color. They then replied: Now you have brought a detailed description that accurately identifies the cow. They sacrificed the cow after almost not having done so due to their arguing and stubbornness.

(٧٢) وَإِذۡ قَتَلۡتُمۡ نَفۡسٗا فَٱدَّٰرَٰٔتُمۡ فِيهَاۖ وَٱللَّهُ مُخۡرِجٞ مَّا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡتُمُونَ

Remember the time you murdered one of your own and defended yourselves, with each person defending themselves against the accusation of murder and, in turn, accusing the other until you started a conflict within yourselves. But Allah revealed what you were hiding regarding the murder of the innocent person.

(٧٣) فَقُلۡنَا ٱضۡرِبُوهُ بِبَعۡضِهَاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُحۡيِ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَيُرِيكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ

So We said to you: Strike the murdered man with a piece of the cow which you were commanded to sacrifice, for verily Allah will bring him back to life to inform you who the murderer was. They did this, and the dead man informed them of his murderer. And just as this man was brought back to life, Allah will bring the dead back to life on the Day of Resurrection. And He gives you clear signs of His power that perhaps you will understand and have true faith in Allah, the Exalted.

(٧٤) ثُمَّ قَسَتۡ قُلُوبُكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَ فَهِيَ كَٱلۡحِجَارَةِ أَوۡ أَشَدُّ قَسۡوَةٗۚ وَإِنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡحِجَارَةِ لَمَا يَتَفَجَّرُ مِنۡهُ ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۚ وَإِنَّ مِنۡهَا لَمَا يَشَّقَّقُ فَيَخۡرُجُ مِنۡهُ ٱلۡمَآءُۚ وَإِنَّ مِنۡهَا لَمَا يَهۡبِطُ مِنۡ خَشۡيَةِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَمَا ٱللَّهُ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Then your hearts hardened after these profound admonitions and extraordinary miracles until they became like rocks or even harder. The states of these hearts never change, whereas rocks change and transform. Verily, there are rocks from which rivers burst forth, and some of them split, and water comes out of them, gushing and flowing through the earth, from which people and animals benefit. Some of them fall from the tallest of mountains out of fear of Allah and awe. Your hearts are not like this. And Allah not heedless of what you do. Rather, He is well aware of it and will recompense you accordingly.

(٧٥) ۞أَفَتَطۡمَعُونَ أَن يُؤۡمِنُواْ لَكُمۡ وَقَدۡ كَانَ فَرِيقٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ يَسۡمَعُونَ كَلَٰمَ ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ يُحَرِّفُونَهُۥ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا عَقَلُوهُ وَهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُونَ

Do you then hope, O Believers, after knowing their true nature and obstinacy, that they will believe and respond to you?! A group of their scholars would hear the word of Allah revealed to them in the Torah and then proceed to alter its words and meanings after they had understood and become familiar with it. And they did this fully aware of the magnitude of their crime.

(٧٦) وَإِذَا لَقُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا وَإِذَا خَلَا بَعۡضُهُمۡ إِلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ قَالُوٓاْ أَتُحَدِّثُونَهُم بِمَا فَتَحَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لِيُحَآجُّوكُم بِهِۦ عِندَ رَبِّكُمۡۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

One of the contradictions of the Jews and their deviousness would occur when some of them would meet believers and confess the truth of the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم and the validity of his message, which is what the Torah testifies to. But when the Jews were alone with one another, they would blame each other because of these confessions because the Muslims would establish their confessions as evidence against them and the truth of the prophethood.

Points of Benefit:

Some people's hearts are harder than solid rocks and do not soften by admonition or remembrance.
All forms of evidence and proof -no matter how insurmountable- are of no benefit to the heart if it is not submissive and fearful of Allah.
The verses reveal the hidden reality of the souls of the Jews, as they inherited recklessness, deception, and manipulation of religion.
