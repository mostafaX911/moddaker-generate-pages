(١٤) وَمِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا نَصَٰرَىٰٓ أَخَذۡنَا مِيثَٰقَهُمۡ فَنَسُواْ حَظّٗا مِّمَّا ذُكِّرُواْ بِهِۦ فَأَغۡرَيۡنَا بَيۡنَهُمُ ٱلۡعَدَاوَةَ وَٱلۡبَغۡضَآءَ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۚ وَسَوۡفَ يُنَبِّئُهُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَصۡنَعُونَ

Just as We took a firm binding covenant from the Jews, We also did so from those who claim to follow Jesus (peace be upon him). However, they did not follow through with everything they were instructed to do, just like their predecessors, the Jews. So, We placed enmity and intense hatred between them until the Day of Resurrection, causing them to fight and kill each other, with each side declaring the other disbelievers. Allah will inform them about what they used to do and recompense them for that.

When Allah mentions all of the covenants He made with them and how they continually broke them, He commands them to believe in Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, stating:

(١٥) يَٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ قَدۡ جَآءَكُمۡ رَسُولُنَا يُبَيِّنُ لَكُمۡ كَثِيرٗا مِّمَّا كُنتُمۡ تُخۡفُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَيَعۡفُواْ عَن كَثِيرٖۚ قَدۡ جَآءَكُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ نُورٞ وَكِتَٰبٞ مُّبِينٞ

O People of the Book (the Jews of the Torah and the Christians of the Gospel), verily, Our Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم has come to you, explaining much of what you have concealed from what was revealed to you and overlooking much of what had no benefit for you except to disgrace you. Verily, the Quran has come to you as a book from Allah. It is a light to guide you and a clear book explaining everything everyone needs for the affairs of this life and the next.

(١٦) يَهۡدِي بِهِ ٱللَّهُ مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ رِضۡوَٰنَهُۥ سُبُلَ ٱلسَّلَٰمِ وَيُخۡرِجُهُم مِّنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِ بِإِذۡنِهِۦ وَيَهۡدِيهِمۡ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

Through this book, Allah guides whoever follows what pleases Him of faith and righteous deeds to the safe path away from Allah's punishment. These are the paths that lead to Paradise. He brings them out of the darkness of disbelief and disobedience into the light of faith and obedience with His permission. He guides them to the straight path, the path of Islam.

(١٧) لَّقَدۡ كَفَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡمَسِيحُ ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَۚ قُلۡ فَمَن يَمۡلِكُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡ‍ًٔا إِنۡ أَرَادَ أَن يُهۡلِكَ ٱلۡمَسِيحَ ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ وَأُمَّهُۥ وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗاۗ وَلِلَّهِ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَاۚ يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

Those Christians who say that 'Allah is the Messiah, Jesus, son of Mary' have committed disbelief. Say to them, O Messenger: Who is able to prevent Allah from destroying the Messiah, Jesus, son of Mary, as well as his mother, and all of those on Earth if He wishes to do so? If no one is able to stop Allah from this, it is a clear indication that there is no Allah but Allah and that all of those people (Jesus, son of Mary, his mother, and all of creation) are all Allah's creation. The dominion of the heavens and the Earth, and whatever is between them, is Allah's. He creates whatever He wishes, and one such creation of His was Jesus (peace be upon him), who was His slave and messenger.

Points of Benefit:

Abandoning the work of Allah's covenants and charters may lead to enmity, the spread of hatred, and disharmony. It may also lead to fighting between violators of the command of the Exalted Allah.
Responding to the Christians who say that the Exalted Allah was incarnated in Christ, peace be upon him, and explaining their disbelief and the misguidance of their words.
One of the proofs of the invalidity of Christ's divinity is that if the Exalted Allah wanted to exterminate Christ and his mother [peace be upon them] and all the people of the earth, then no one would be able to stop Him. This proves His uniqueness [may He be Exalted and Transcendent] in all matters and that there is no god other than Him.
Among the evidence of the invalidity of Christ's divinity is that the Exalted Allah reminds us that He [the Exalted] "creates whatever He wills" (Al-Ma'idah: 17). He creates from two parents, from a mother without a father like Jesus [peace be upon him], from inanimate objects like the serpent of Moses [peace be upon him], and from a man without a female like Eve from Adam [peace be upon them].
