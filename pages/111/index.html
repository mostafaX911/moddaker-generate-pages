(١٨) وَقَالَتِ ٱلۡيَهُودُ وَٱلنَّصَٰرَىٰ نَحۡنُ أَبۡنَٰٓؤُاْ ٱللَّهِ وَأَحِبَّٰٓؤُهُۥۚ قُلۡ فَلِمَ يُعَذِّبُكُم بِذُنُوبِكُمۖ بَلۡ أَنتُم بَشَرٞ مِّمَّنۡ خَلَقَۚ يَغۡفِرُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيُعَذِّبُ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَلِلَّهِ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَاۖ وَإِلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

Both the Jews and the Christians claimed to be the children and loved ones of Allah. Say, O Messenger, in response to them: Why does Allah punish you for the sins you commit?! For if you were His loved ones as you claim, He would not punish you with killing and maiming in this life and with the Fire in the Afterlife because he does not punish those He loves. Rather, you are just like any other human. He rewards those who do good with Paradise and punishes those who do evil with the Fire. Allah forgives whom He wills due to His grace and punishes whom He wills due to His justice. The dominion of the heavens, the earth, and everything in between belongs only to Him, and to Him alone is the return.

(١٩) يَٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ قَدۡ جَآءَكُمۡ رَسُولُنَا يُبَيِّنُ لَكُمۡ عَلَىٰ فَتۡرَةٖ مِّنَ ٱلرُّسُلِ أَن تَقُولُواْ مَا جَآءَنَا مِنۢ بَشِيرٖ وَلَا نَذِيرٖۖ فَقَدۡ جَآءَكُم بَشِيرٞ وَنَذِيرٞۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

O People of the Book from the Jews and Christians, Our Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم has come to you after a period without Messengers and a dire need for one to be sent so that you will not make excuses saying: No messenger came to us to give us the good news about Allah's reward and warn us about His punishment. So, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم has come to you, giving you the good news about His reward and warning about His punishment. Allah is Omnipotent over everything; nothing can hinder Him. From His Omnipotence is the ability to send messengers, the final one being Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم.

(٢٠) وَإِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوۡمِهِۦ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِذۡ جَعَلَ فِيكُمۡ أَنۢبِيَآءَ وَجَعَلَكُم مُّلُوكٗا وَءَاتَىٰكُم مَّا لَمۡ يُؤۡتِ أَحَدٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Remember, O Messenger, when Moses said to his people, the Israelites: O my people, remember with your hearts and tongues how Allah blessed you when he made prophets among you, calling you to guidance, and made you kings in charge of yourselves after you were subjugated slaves, and gave you blessings that He had not given to anyone else in the world during your time.

(٢١) يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱدۡخُلُواْ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ ٱلۡمُقَدَّسَةَ ٱلَّتِي كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ وَلَا تَرۡتَدُّواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَدۡبَارِكُمۡ فَتَنقَلِبُواْ خَٰسِرِينَ

Moses said: O my people, enter the purified land (Jerusalem and the surrounding lands), which Allah has promised that you will enter and fight the disbelievers there. Do not be weak in front of the tyrants, as this will result in loss in both this life and the next.

(٢٢) قَالُواْ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّ فِيهَا قَوۡمٗا جَبَّارِينَ وَإِنَّا لَن نَّدۡخُلَهَا حَتَّىٰ يَخۡرُجُواْ مِنۡهَا فَإِن يَخۡرُجُواْ مِنۡهَا فَإِنَّا دَٰخِلُونَ

His people responded: O Moses, there is a strong and fierce nation in the Holy Land that prevents us from entering. So we will not go in while they are there because we have no power or strength to fight them. But if they leave, we will enter.

(٢٣) قَالَ رَجُلَانِ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخَافُونَ أَنۡعَمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِمَا ٱدۡخُلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡبَابَ فَإِذَا دَخَلۡتُمُوهُ فَإِنَّكُمۡ غَٰلِبُونَۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَتَوَكَّلُوٓاْ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Two companions of Moses, who feared Allah and His punishment, and whom Allah had blessed with guidance to obey Him, urged their people to fulfill Moses' (peace be upon him) commands by saying: Enter upon those tyrants by the city gate and once you storm the gate, you will overpower them by Allah's permission. It is from the way of Allah to grant victory to those who take the means for that, such as having faith in Allah and preparing the material resources. If you truly have faith, rely on Allah alone and place your trust in Him because having faith necessitates relying on Him (may He be Exalted).

Points of Benefit:

The Exalted Allah's tormenting of the disbelievers of the Israelites through mutilation and other things mandates the invalidation of their claim to be Allah's children and loved ones.
Relying on the Exalted Allah and trusting Him is a reason for descending victory.
The verses came to beware of the bad morals that the Israelites had.
Fear of Allah is a reason for blessings to descend upon the servant, and one of the greatest of them is the blessing of obedience to Him [may He be Exalted].
