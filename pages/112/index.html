(٢٤) قَالُواْ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّا لَن نَّدۡخُلَهَآ أَبَدٗا مَّا دَامُواْ فِيهَا فَٱذۡهَبۡ أَنتَ وَرَبُّكَ فَقَٰتِلَآ إِنَّا هَٰهُنَا قَٰعِدُونَ

Those from the Israelites who persisted in disobeying the order of their prophet Moses (peace be upon him) said: We will never enter the city so long as the tyrants are still there, so you (O Moses) and your Lord can go and fight them. As for us, we will stay behind here and not fight alongside you.

(٢٥) قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي لَآ أَمۡلِكُ إِلَّا نَفۡسِي وَأَخِيۖ فَٱفۡرُقۡ بَيۡنَنَا وَبَيۡنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ

Moses said to his Lord: O my Lord, I do not have any power over anyone except myself and my brother Aaron, so separate us from those who refuse to obey You and Your messenger.

(٢٦) قَالَ فَإِنَّهَا مُحَرَّمَةٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡۛ أَرۡبَعِينَ سَنَةٗۛ يَتِيهُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ فَلَا تَأۡسَ عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ

Allah said to His prophet Moses (peace be upon him): Indeed, Allah has prohibited the Israelites from entering the Holy Land for a period of forty years. During this time, they will wander the desert, aimless and confused. So do not, O Moses, feel sorry for those who disobey Allah because they were only punished for their own disobedience and sins.

(٢٧) ۞وَٱتۡلُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ نَبَأَ ٱبۡنَيۡ ءَادَمَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ إِذۡ قَرَّبَا قُرۡبَانٗا فَتُقُبِّلَ مِنۡ أَحَدِهِمَا وَلَمۡ يُتَقَبَّلۡ مِنَ ٱلۡأٓخَرِ قَالَ لَأَقۡتُلَنَّكَۖ قَالَ إِنَّمَا يَتَقَبَّلُ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

O Messenger, tell those jealous wrongdoers from the Jews the true and undoubtable story of Adam's two sons, Cain and Abel. They offered a sacrifice to gain closeness to Allah (may He be Exalted), so Allah accepted Abel's sacrifice because he feared Allah, but He did not accept Cain's sacrifice because he did not fear Allah. Cain was upset with jealousy that Abel's sacrifice was accepted and said: I am going to kill you, Abel. Abel replied: Allah only accepts the sacrifice of those who fear Him by fulfilling His commands and abstaining from His prohibitions.

(٢٨) لَئِنۢ بَسَطتَ إِلَيَّ يَدَكَ لِتَقۡتُلَنِي مَآ أَنَا۠ بِبَاسِطٖ يَدِيَ إِلَيۡكَ لِأَقۡتُلَكَۖ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ ٱللَّهَ رَبَّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

If you stretch out your hand to kill me, I will not retaliate in the same manner, not due to cowardice but because I fear Allah, Lord of all creation.

(٢٩) إِنِّيٓ أُرِيدُ أَن تَبُوٓأَ بِإِثۡمِي وَإِثۡمِكَ فَتَكُونَ مِنۡ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلنَّارِۚ وَذَٰلِكَ جَزَٰٓؤُاْ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

He told him these terrifying words: I want the sin of murdering me to be added to your previous sins so that you become one of the people of the Fire who will enter it on the Day of Resurrection. That is the recompense for the transgressors, and I do not want to become one of them by committing the same sin.

(٣٠) فَطَوَّعَتۡ لَهُۥ نَفۡسُهُۥ قَتۡلَ أَخِيهِ فَقَتَلَهُۥ فَأَصۡبَحَ مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ

But Cain's evil self drove him to murder his brother, and he killed him. As a result, he became one of those who have caused themselves to lose out both in this world and the next.

(٣١) فَبَعَثَ ٱللَّهُ غُرَابٗا يَبۡحَثُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لِيُرِيَهُۥ كَيۡفَ يُوَٰرِي سَوۡءَةَ أَخِيهِۚ قَالَ يَٰوَيۡلَتَىٰٓ أَعَجَزۡتُ أَنۡ أَكُونَ مِثۡلَ هَٰذَا ٱلۡغُرَابِ فَأُوَٰرِيَ سَوۡءَةَ أَخِيۖ فَأَصۡبَحَ مِنَ ٱلنَّٰدِمِينَ

Allah sent a crow to dig the earth in front of him to bury another dead crow and teach him how to cover his brother's body. He then became regretful. Regret is the end result of those who commit sins.

Points of Benefit:

Opposing the messengers mandates punishment, as it happened to the Israelites. The Exalted Allah punished them with wandering (traveling aimlessly from place to place).
According to the Qur'an, the first sin that occurred on Earth was envy and transgression, which led the son of Adam to injustice and shed forbidden blood that mandates loss.
Regret is the consequence of those who commit sins.
The Sunnah of the Prophet clarified that whoever enacts an evil practice or spreads evil and encourages it will have the same sins as those who follow him.
