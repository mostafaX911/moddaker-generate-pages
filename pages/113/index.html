(٣٢) مِنۡ أَجۡلِ ذَٰلِكَ كَتَبۡنَا عَلَىٰ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ أَنَّهُۥ مَن قَتَلَ نَفۡسَۢا بِغَيۡرِ نَفۡسٍ أَوۡ فَسَادٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَكَأَنَّمَا قَتَلَ ٱلنَّاسَ جَمِيعٗا وَمَنۡ أَحۡيَاهَا فَكَأَنَّمَآ أَحۡيَا ٱلنَّاسَ جَمِيعٗاۚ وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُنَا بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ ثُمَّ إِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنۡهُم بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لَمُسۡرِفُونَ

Due to Cain killing his brother, we taught the Israelites that whoever kills anybody without a just reason, like capital punishment for causing corruption in the land through treason and warmongering or for legal retribution, then it is as if he has killed all humanity because he does not distinguish between the guilty and the innocent. But whoever refrains from killing a soul that Allah (the Exalted) has prohibited from killing, believing that it is forbidden and does not kill him, then it is as if he has saved all humanity because by doing this, he is taking care of everyone's safety. Verily, Our messengers brought clear signs and undeniable evidence to the Israelites. Yet, despite that, many of them transgressed the limits set by Allah by being disobedient and opposing their messengers.

(٣٣) إِنَّمَا جَزَٰٓؤُاْ ٱلَّذِينَ يُحَارِبُونَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَيَسۡعَوۡنَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَسَادًا أَن يُقَتَّلُوٓاْ أَوۡ يُصَلَّبُوٓاْ أَوۡ تُقَطَّعَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَأَرۡجُلُهُم مِّنۡ خِلَٰفٍ أَوۡ يُنفَوۡاْ مِنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ ذَٰلِكَ لَهُمۡ خِزۡيٞ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَلَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٌ

Those who wage war against Allah and His Messenger and show him enmity by causing corruption in the land through killing, looting, and highway robbery have no punishment except for being put to death by being crucified or by some other method or having their right hand and left leg cut off. Then, if they do it again, the left hand and the right leg, or by exiling them. That punishment is a humiliation for them in this life, and there is great torment for them in the Afterlife.

(٣٤) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ تَابُواْ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن تَقۡدِرُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡۖ فَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Except for those who repent before you (people of authority) apprehend them, then know that Allah forgives them after their repentance. He is merciful to them, and overlooking their punishment is from His mercy.

(٣٥) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱبۡتَغُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ ٱلۡوَسِيلَةَ وَجَٰهِدُواْ فِي سَبِيلِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ

O you who believe, fear Allah by obeying His commands and abstaining from his prohibitions. Seek nearness to Him by fulfilling what He has ordered you to do and staying away from what He has prohibited. Fight the disbelievers seeking His pleasure so that by doing so, you may attain what you seek and avoid what you fear.

(٣٦) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَوۡ أَنَّ لَهُم مَّا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا وَمِثۡلَهُۥ مَعَهُۥ لِيَفۡتَدُواْ بِهِۦ مِنۡ عَذَابِ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ مَا تُقُبِّلَ مِنۡهُمۡۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Had those who disbelieve in Allah and His messengers owned everything in the Earth and the likeness of it and gave all of that to ransom themselves from Allah's punishment on the Day of Resurrection, such ransom would not be accepted from them. And they will receive a painful punishment.

Points of Benefit:

The sanctity of the human soul, and whoever preserves and revives it, is as if [he or she] did that to all human beings. On the other hand, whoever destroys a human soul or harms it unjustly, it is as if [he or she] did that to all human beings.
The punishment for those who wage war against Allah and His Messenger, who cause corruption by killing, stealing money, and cutting off roads [for piracy], is killing without crucifixion or with crucifixion, cutting off limbs from edgings, or expelling them from the country. This is according to what they did.
Repentance of corrupt warriors and highway robbers before the Sultan (a Muslim sovereign) has power over them mandates pardon.
