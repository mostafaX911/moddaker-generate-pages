(٣٧) يُرِيدُونَ أَن يَخۡرُجُواْ مِنَ ٱلنَّارِ وَمَا هُم بِخَٰرِجِينَ مِنۡهَاۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ مُّقِيمٞ

They will want to escape the Fire after entering it. Do they deserve that?! They will never get out and will be tormented there for eternity.

After mentioning the ruling of those who openly take people's wealth, Allah explains the ruling of those who secretly steal, stating:

(٣٨) وَٱلسَّارِقُ وَٱلسَّارِقَةُ فَٱقۡطَعُوٓاْ أَيۡدِيَهُمَا جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَسَبَا نَكَٰلٗا مِّنَ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٞ

Cut off, O rulers, the right hand of both the male and female thief as recompense and punishment from Allah for them unjustly taking people's wealth, as well as to deter them and others from theft. Allah is Mighty; nothing can overpower Him. He is Wise in His decree and legislation.

(٣٩) فَمَن تَابَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ظُلۡمِهِۦ وَأَصۡلَحَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَتُوبُ عَلَيۡهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٌ

Whoever repents to Allah for stealing and reforms their actions, Allah will accept their repentance due to His grace. This is because Allah forgives the sins of His slaves who repent and is merciful towards them. However, repentance does not excuse them from the prescribed punishment if their case reaches the ruler.

(٤٠) أَلَمۡ تَعۡلَمۡ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ يُعَذِّبُ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَغۡفِرُ لِمَن يَشَآءُۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

Verily, O Messenger, you know that Allah owns the dominion of the heavens and the Earth, doing with them as He wills. He torments whom He wills out of His justice and forgives whom He wills due to His grace. Allah is Omnipotent over everything; nothing can hinder Him.

(٤١) ۞يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلرَّسُولُ لَا يَحۡزُنكَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡكُفۡرِ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِأَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَلَمۡ تُؤۡمِن قُلُوبُهُمۡۛ وَمِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ هَادُواْۛ سَمَّٰعُونَ لِلۡكَذِبِ سَمَّٰعُونَ لِقَوۡمٍ ءَاخَرِينَ لَمۡ يَأۡتُوكَۖ يُحَرِّفُونَ ٱلۡكَلِمَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَوَاضِعِهِۦۖ يَقُولُونَ إِنۡ أُوتِيتُمۡ هَٰذَا فَخُذُوهُ وَإِن لَّمۡ تُؤۡتَوۡهُ فَٱحۡذَرُواْۚ وَمَن يُرِدِ ٱللَّهُ فِتۡنَتَهُۥ فَلَن تَمۡلِكَ لَهُۥ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡ‍ًٔاۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يُرِدِ ٱللَّهُ أَن يُطَهِّرَ قُلُوبَهُمۡۚ لَهُمۡ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا خِزۡيٞۖ وَلَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٞ

O Messenger, do not be saddened by those who rush to display their disbelief to irritate you. These are hypocrites who pretend to be believers while hiding their disbelief. Also, do not be saddened by the Jews who listen to and believe the lies of their elders, mindlessly following their leaders who arrogantly avoid you. They replace Allah's words in the Torah with words that agree with their desires and say to their followers: If the law of Muhammad agrees with your desires, then follow it, but if not, then beware. Whoever Allah wants to leave misguided, you will not find (O Messenger) anyone who can save them from misguidance and guide them to the right way. The Jews and hypocrites who have these characteristics are the ones that Allah does not want to purify their hearts from disbelief. They are humiliated and disgraced in this life and will have a great torment in the Afterlife, which is the punishment of the Fire.

Points of Benefit:

The wisdom of punishment for theft is deterring the thief from overstepping on people's money and scaring others from falling into something similar to what he [or she] did.
Accepting the thief's repentance unless the issue has reached the authorities, and he [or she] must return what he [or she] stole. If it reaches the authorities, the punishment must be imposed and not waived by repentance.
It is better for the caller to Allah not to bear worry and distress because of the disbelief, deception, and conspiracy that happens from some people because the Exalted Allah invalidates the plots of these people.
The hypocrites were keen to tease the believers by displaying acts of disbelief while claiming Islam.
