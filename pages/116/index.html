(٤٦) وَقَفَّيۡنَا عَلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِهِم بِعِيسَى ٱبۡنِ مَرۡيَمَ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ مِنَ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلۡإِنجِيلَ فِيهِ هُدٗى وَنُورٞ وَمُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ مِنَ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِ وَهُدٗى وَمَوۡعِظَةٗ لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ

After the prophets of the Israelites, I sent Jesus, son of Mary, as a believer in the Torah, giving judgment in accordance with it. I also gave him the Gospel that contained guidance to the truth and evidence to remove doubts and solve difficult cases of rulings: it corresponded to the Torah that came before it, except in a few rulings that it replaced. I made the Gospel a guide and a means to restrain people from doing that which was prohibited.

(٤٧) وَلۡيَحۡكُمۡ أَهۡلُ ٱلۡإِنجِيلِ بِمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ فِيهِۚ وَمَن لَّمۡ يَحۡكُم بِمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ

So let the Christians believe in what Allah has revealed in the Gospel and judge according to the truth contained within it before the sending of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. Whoever does not judge by what Allah has revealed has left Allah's obedience and abandoned the truth in favor of falsehood.

After mentioning and praising the Torah and the Gospel, Allah praises the Quran, stating:

(٤٨) وَأَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ مِنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَمُهَيۡمِنًا عَلَيۡهِۖ فَٱحۡكُم بَيۡنَهُم بِمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُۖ وَلَا تَتَّبِعۡ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡ عَمَّا جَآءَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّۚ لِكُلّٖ جَعَلۡنَا مِنكُمۡ شِرۡعَةٗ وَمِنۡهَاجٗاۚ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَجَعَلَكُمۡ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ وَلَٰكِن لِّيَبۡلُوَكُمۡ فِي مَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۖ فَٱسۡتَبِقُواْ ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِۚ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ جَمِيعٗا فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ فِيهِ تَخۡتَلِفُونَ

We have revealed the Quran to you (O Messenger) with the truth without any question or doubt about it being from Allah. It confirms what was in the previously revealed books and protects them. So, whatever agrees with the Quran is the truth, and whatever contradicts it is falsehood. Therefore, judge between the people according to what Allah has revealed to you in it, and do not follow their desires that caused them to abandon what Allah revealed to you of unquestionable truth. We have made laws for every nation to practice and a clear path to guide them. If Allah had willed to unite them upon one legislation, He would have, but He made separate laws for each nation to test them all and distinguish the obedient from the sinner. So, hasten to perform good deeds and abandon evil deeds. You will all return to Allah alone on the Day of Resurrection. He will inform you about everything you used to differ and recompense you for everything you did.

(٤٩) وَأَنِ ٱحۡكُم بَيۡنَهُم بِمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ وَلَا تَتَّبِعۡ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡ وَٱحۡذَرۡهُمۡ أَن يَفۡتِنُوكَ عَنۢ بَعۡضِ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَيۡكَۖ فَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَٱعۡلَمۡ أَنَّمَا يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ أَن يُصِيبَهُم بِبَعۡضِ ذُنُوبِهِمۡۗ وَإِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَفَٰسِقُونَ

Judge between them (O Messenger) according to what Allah has revealed to you, and do not follow the opinions that stem from them following their desires. Beware of them misleading you from some of what Allah has revealed to you because they will not spare any effort in that. If they refuse to accept the judgment that is according to what Allah revealed to you, then know that Allah only wants to punish them for some of their sins in this life and all of their sins in the Afterlife. Indeed, many people do not obey Allah.

(٥٠) أَفَحُكۡمَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِيَّةِ يَبۡغُونَۚ وَمَنۡ أَحۡسَنُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ حُكۡمٗا لِّقَوۡمٖ يُوقِنُونَ

Are they turning away from your judgment, seeking the judgment of the people of ignorance who worship idols and judge according to their desires?! The people of sure faith who have understood what Allah has revealed to His messenger all consider that no one can judge better than Allah. But those people of ignorance and desires who only accept what agrees with them, even if it is falsehood, do not.

Points of Benefit:

The prophets agree on the fundamentals of religion, although some differences exist between their sacred laws in the branches.
The obligation to adhere to Allah's law and turn away from other vain desires.
Condemnation of the rulings of the people of the Age of Ignorance and their customs.
