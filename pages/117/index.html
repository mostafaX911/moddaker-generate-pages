(٥١) ۞يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَتَّخِذُواْ ٱلۡيَهُودَ وَٱلنَّصَٰرَىٰٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَۘ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ بَعۡضٖۚ وَمَن يَتَوَلَّهُم مِّنكُمۡ فَإِنَّهُۥ مِنۡهُمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

O you who believe in Allah and His Messenger, do not take supporters and close friends from the Jews and the Christians allying with them. The Jews only ally with the people of their religion, and the Christians only make alliances with other Christians. But they will both unite against you. Whoever allies with them has joined their ranks. Indeed, Allah does not guide those who have become wrongdoers by taking the disbelievers as allies.

(٥٢) فَتَرَى ٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٞ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِيهِمۡ يَقُولُونَ نَخۡشَىٰٓ أَن تُصِيبَنَا دَآئِرَةٞۚ فَعَسَى ٱللَّهُ أَن يَأۡتِيَ بِٱلۡفَتۡحِ أَوۡ أَمۡرٖ مِّنۡ عِندِهِۦ فَيُصۡبِحُواْ عَلَىٰ مَآ أَسَرُّواْ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ نَٰدِمِينَ

You will see O Messenger, the hypocrites who are weak of faith making alliances with the Jews and Christians, saying: We are afraid that they will be victorious and then cause us harm when they come into power. Perhaps Allah will grant victory to His Messenger and the believers, or an order may come from Him repelling the forces of the Jews and their allies. Then, those who rushed to join forces with them will regret the hypocrisy they hid in their hearts because the weak connections they clung to were false.

(٥٣) وَيَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَهَٰٓؤُلَآءِ ٱلَّذِينَ أَقۡسَمُواْ بِٱللَّهِ جَهۡدَ أَيۡمَٰنِهِمۡ إِنَّهُمۡ لَمَعَكُمۡۚ حَبِطَتۡ أَعۡمَٰلُهُمۡ فَأَصۡبَحُواْ خَٰسِرِينَ

The believers will express surprise at the condition of these hypocrites and say: Are these the ones who swore their most binding oaths that they are with you (O believers) in faith, support, and alliance?! Their good deeds have been nullified, and they have become losers by failing to achieve their goal and due to the torment that has been prepared for them.

(٥٤) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَن يَرۡتَدَّ مِنكُمۡ عَن دِينِهِۦ فَسَوۡفَ يَأۡتِي ٱللَّهُ بِقَوۡمٖ يُحِبُّهُمۡ وَيُحِبُّونَهُۥٓ أَذِلَّةٍ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ أَعِزَّةٍ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ يُجَٰهِدُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَا يَخَافُونَ لَوۡمَةَ لَآئِمٖۚ ذَٰلِكَ فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ يُؤۡتِيهِ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ

O you who believe, whoever from you renounces their religion for disbelief, Allah will replace them with a people who He loves, and they love Him due to their righteousness. They are merciful to the believers and harsh towards the disbelievers. They strive with their wealth and lives to make Allah's word victorious. They do not fear those who torment them because they prioritize pleasing Allah over pleasing the creation. Allah grants this to those who He wills from His slaves. Allah has vast bounty and kindness. He knows who deserves it and gives it to them, and he prohibits it from those who do not deserve it.

After prohibiting allegiance to Jews, Christians, and non-believers, Allah instructs believers on whom to ally with, stating:

(٥٥) إِنَّمَا وَلِيُّكُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَهُمۡ رَٰكِعُونَ

Neither the Christians, nor the Jews, nor other disbelievers are your allies. Rather your only allies and supporters are Allah and His Messenger, and the believers who perform all their prayers correctly, pay the Zakat (obligatory alms), and submit humbly to Allah.

(٥٦) وَمَن يَتَوَلَّ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ فَإِنَّ حِزۡبَ ٱللَّهِ هُمُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبُونَ

Whoever allies with Allah, His Messenger, and the believers by supporting them is from the party of Allah. The party of Allah will be victorious because they have Allah's support.

(٥٧) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَتَّخِذُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ دِينَكُمۡ هُزُوٗا وَلَعِبٗا مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ وَٱلۡكُفَّارَ أَوۡلِيَآءَۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

O you who believe, do not take supporters and close friends from those who mock your religion and take it as a game, neither from those who received a [revealed] book before you like the Jews and the Christians nor from the polytheists. If you truly believe in Allah and what He has revealed, then fear Him by abstaining from what he has prohibited, like taking [the disbelievers] as allies.

Points of Benefit:

Alerting to the doctrine of allegiance and disavowal. Which is summed up in loyalty and love for Allah, His Messenger, and the believers. On the other hand, hatred of the people of disbelief and avoiding loving them.
Among the characteristics of the people of hypocrisy is loyalty to the enemies of the Exalted Allah.
Inaction and negligence in supporting the religion may result in replacing the negligent person with someone else and stripping him of the honor of supporting the religion.
The warning against those who mock the religion of the Exalted Allah from the disbelievers and hypocrites and from allegiance to them.
