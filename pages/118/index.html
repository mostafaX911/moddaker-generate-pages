(٥٨) وَإِذَا نَادَيۡتُمۡ إِلَى ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ ٱتَّخَذُوهَا هُزُوٗا وَلَعِبٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَوۡمٞ لَّا يَعۡقِلُونَ

They likewise mock and play around when you call them to establish the prayer, the greatest form of worship, because they do not understand the true meaning of worshipping Allah and what the law He has legislated for the people means.

(٥٩) قُلۡ يَٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ هَلۡ تَنقِمُونَ مِنَّآ إِلَّآ أَنۡ ءَامَنَّا بِٱللَّهِ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡنَا وَمَآ أُنزِلَ مِن قَبۡلُ وَأَنَّ أَكۡثَرَكُمۡ فَٰسِقُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to those people of the book who are mocking: Are you criticizing us because we believe in Allah and what He revealed to us and revealed to those before us, and that we believe that most of you have left the obedience of Allah by abandoning faith and neglecting to fulfill His commands?! We regard what you criticize us for as a virtue, not a fault.

(٦٠) قُلۡ هَلۡ أُنَبِّئُكُم بِشَرّٖ مِّن ذَٰلِكَ مَثُوبَةً عِندَ ٱللَّهِۚ مَن لَّعَنَهُ ٱللَّهُ وَغَضِبَ عَلَيۡهِ وَجَعَلَ مِنۡهُمُ ٱلۡقِرَدَةَ وَٱلۡخَنَازِيرَ وَعَبَدَ ٱلطَّٰغُوتَۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ شَرّٞ مَّكَانٗا وَأَضَلُّ عَن سَوَآءِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ

Say, O Messenger: Shall I tell you about those who are more at fault and whose punishment is worse than these people? They are their predecessors whom Allah has removed from His mercy, transformed into monkeys and pigs, and made some of them worshippers of false deities. The Arabic word ţāghūt refers to any being that is worshipped besides Allah without coercion. Those mentioned will be in a worse position on the Day of Resurrection, and their efforts are further away from the straight path.

(٦١) وَإِذَا جَآءُوكُمۡ قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا وَقَد دَّخَلُواْ بِٱلۡكُفۡرِ وَهُمۡ قَدۡ خَرَجُواْ بِهِۦۚ وَٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡتُمُونَ

When the hypocrites from them come to you, O believers, they hypocritically display their faith to you. In reality, they were covered in disbelief when they came just as they left. Allah knows the disbelief they conceal, even if they pretend to have faith, and He will recompense them.

(٦٢) وَتَرَىٰ كَثِيرٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡعُدۡوَٰنِ وَأَكۡلِهِمُ ٱلسُّحۡتَۚ لَبِئۡسَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

You will see, O Messenger, many Jews and hypocrites rush to commit sins like lying, oppressively attacking others, and illicitly consuming people's wealth. How evil is what they do!

(٦٣) لَوۡلَا يَنۡهَىٰهُمُ ٱلرَّبَّٰنِيُّونَ وَٱلۡأَحۡبَارُ عَن قَوۡلِهِمُ ٱلۡإِثۡمَ وَأَكۡلِهِمُ ٱلسُّحۡتَۚ لَبِئۡسَ مَا كَانُواْ يَصۡنَعُونَ

Why do their leaders and scholars not prevent them from rushing towards lying, bearing false testimony, and wrongfully consuming people's wealth? How evil it is that they do not prohibit them from doing bad deeds!

(٦٤) وَقَالَتِ ٱلۡيَهُودُ يَدُ ٱللَّهِ مَغۡلُولَةٌۚ غُلَّتۡ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَلُعِنُواْ بِمَا قَالُواْۘ بَلۡ يَدَاهُ مَبۡسُوطَتَانِ يُنفِقُ كَيۡفَ يَشَآءُۚ وَلَيَزِيدَنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنۡهُم مَّآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَ طُغۡيَٰنٗا وَكُفۡرٗاۚ وَأَلۡقَيۡنَا بَيۡنَهُمُ ٱلۡعَدَٰوَةَ وَٱلۡبَغۡضَآءَ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۚ كُلَّمَآ أَوۡقَدُواْ نَارٗا لِّلۡحَرۡبِ أَطۡفَأَهَا ٱللَّهُۚ وَيَسۡعَوۡنَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَسَادٗاۚ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ

Whenever the Jews faced struggles and drought, they would say: Allah's Hand is closed, not giving out any goodness or generosity; He is keeping what he has from us. Their hands were kept from doing good or being generous, and they were flung far from Allah's mercy due to what they said. Rather, His (may He be Exalted and Transcendent) Two Hands are open-wide with goodness and generosity. He spends as He wills. He gives and withholds. No one can stop Him or force Him. What was revealed to you, O Messenger, only makes the Jews more extreme and disbelieve more because of their jealousy. We have placed hatred and enmity between the different groups of Jews. Whenever they would join forces for war, prepare for it, and make plans, Allah divided their ranks and took their strength away. They will not stop doing what causes corruption in the land by attempting to eradicate Islam and plotting against it. Allah does not like corrupt people.

Points of Benefit:

Condemning the scholar for remaining silent about the sins of his people and for not clarifying their evils and warning them about them.
The impoliteness of the Jews with the Exalted Allah because they described Him (may He be Exalted) as being tight-fisted (stingy) and withholding goodness.
Affirming the attribute of the Hands of the Exalted Allah in a way befitting His essence, majesty, and great authority. ​
Pointing out the discord, disagreement, and hostility that occurred among some Jewish sects as a result of their disbelief and inclination from the truth.
