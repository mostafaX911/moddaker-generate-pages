(٦٥) وَلَوۡ أَنَّ أَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱتَّقَوۡاْ لَكَفَّرۡنَا عَنۡهُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِهِمۡ وَلَأَدۡخَلۡنَٰهُمۡ جَنَّٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

If the Jews and Christians had believed in what Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought and feared Allah by abstaining from sins, We would have wiped out the wrongdoings that they had committed, even if they were many, and entered them into the gardens of bliss on the day of resurrection, where they would enjoy endless delights.

(٦٦) وَلَوۡ أَنَّهُمۡ أَقَامُواْ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةَ وَٱلۡإِنجِيلَ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡهِم مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡ لَأَكَلُواْ مِن فَوۡقِهِمۡ وَمِن تَحۡتِ أَرۡجُلِهِمۚ مِّنۡهُمۡ أُمَّةٞ مُّقۡتَصِدَةٞۖ وَكَثِيرٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ سَآءَ مَا يَعۡمَلُونَ

If the Jews had implemented what was in the Torah, and the Christians what was in the Gospel, and both of them had implemented what was revealed to them in the Quran, We would have made it easy for them to get provisions by sending down rain and making crops grow. Some people from the people of the book are moderate and adhere to the truth, but most of them do evil because they lack faith.

(٦٧) ۞يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلرَّسُولُ بَلِّغۡ مَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَۖ وَإِن لَّمۡ تَفۡعَلۡ فَمَا بَلَّغۡتَ رِسَالَتَهُۥۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡصِمُكَ مِنَ ٱلنَّاسِۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

O Messenger, convey everything revealed to you from your Lord, and do not hide any of it. If you hide anything from it, then it is as if you have not communicated your Lord's message (Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم did indeed convey everything that he was ordered to; whoever claims otherwise has fabricated an enormous lie against Allah). After today, Allah will protect you from the people so they will not be able to cause you any harm, so you only have to convey [the message]. Allah will not bless the disbelievers who do not want to be guided with guidance.

(٦٨) قُلۡ يَٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَسۡتُمۡ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٍ حَتَّىٰ تُقِيمُواْ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةَ وَٱلۡإِنجِيلَ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكُم مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡۗ وَلَيَزِيدَنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنۡهُم مَّآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَ طُغۡيَٰنٗا وَكُفۡرٗاۖ فَلَا تَأۡسَ عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Say, O Messenger: O Jews and Christians, you are not practicing the religion until you implement what is in the Torah and the Gospel and practice what was revealed to you in the Quran. Your faith is invalid until you believe in it and implement it. What was revealed to you from your Lord will send many of the people of the book to higher levels of transgression and disbelief because of their jealousy. So do not feel sorry for those disbelievers. The believers that follow you are more than enough.

(٦٩) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱلَّذِينَ هَادُواْ وَٱلصَّٰبِ‍ُٔونَ وَٱلنَّصَٰرَىٰ مَنۡ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

Indeed, whoever among the believers, the Jews, the Sabeans (a group from the followers of some of the prophets), and the Christians who believe in Allah, the last day, and do righteous deeds, will not fear anything from his future and not grieve anything that passed him from his share of this world.

(٧٠) لَقَدۡ أَخَذۡنَا مِيثَٰقَ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ وَأَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِمۡ رُسُلٗاۖ كُلَّمَا جَآءَهُمۡ رَسُولُۢ بِمَا لَا تَهۡوَىٰٓ أَنفُسُهُمۡ فَرِيقٗا كَذَّبُواْ وَفَرِيقٗا يَقۡتُلُونَ

I took firm promises from the Israelites that they will listen and follow. But they broke their promises and followed their desires by ignoring what their messengers brought and by disbelieving some and murdering others.

Points of Benefit:

Acting upon what the Exalted Allah has revealed is a reason for expiating sins, entering Paradise, and increasing livelihoods.
Directing preachers to the fact that the reliable conveyance that clears responsibility is that which is complete and undiminished. Furthermore, it is in light of what was contained in the revelation.
No belief is valid unless its owner proves it comes from the Exalted Allah.
