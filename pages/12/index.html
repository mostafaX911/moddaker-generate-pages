(٧٧) أَوَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ مَا يُسِرُّونَ وَمَا يُعۡلِنُونَ

These Jews follow this disgraceful path as if they are unaware that Allah knows what they conceal from their words and actions and what they reveal. But He will reveal and expose them to His slaves.

(٧٨) وَمِنۡهُمۡ أُمِّيُّونَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ إِلَّآ أَمَانِيَّ وَإِنۡ هُمۡ إِلَّا يَظُنُّونَ

Among the Jews is a group who do not know the Torah except for its recitation and do not understand what it means. These people have nothing but lies that they heard from their elders and think it is the Torah revealed by Allah.

(٧٩) فَوَيۡلٞ لِّلَّذِينَ يَكۡتُبُونَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِأَيۡدِيهِمۡ ثُمَّ يَقُولُونَ هَٰذَا مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ لِيَشۡتَرُواْ بِهِۦ ثَمَنٗا قَلِيلٗاۖ فَوَيۡلٞ لَّهُم مِّمَّا كَتَبَتۡ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَوَيۡلٞ لَّهُم مِّمَّا يَكۡسِبُونَ

Misery and great suffering await those who write the scripture with their own hands and then falsely allege that it comes from Allah. They exchange the truth and guidance for a small gain in this world, such as wealth or leadership. They will experience destruction and immense torment for what their hands have written, through which they lie about Allah. They will also attain destruction and immense torment for whatever wealth or leadership they gain through these lies.

(٨٠) وَقَالُواْ لَن تَمَسَّنَا ٱلنَّارُ إِلَّآ أَيَّامٗا مَّعۡدُودَةٗۚ قُلۡ أَتَّخَذۡتُمۡ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ عَهۡدٗا فَلَن يُخۡلِفَ ٱللَّهُ عَهۡدَهُۥٓۖ أَمۡ تَقُولُونَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

And they falsely and delusionally said: The Fire will not touch us, and we won't be in it except for a few days [even if it did]. Tell them, O Prophet: Did you have a definite promise from Allah that this will be the case? If so, then Allah never breaks his covenant. Or are you telling lies and falsehoods about Allah, speaking about that which you do not know?

(٨١) بَلَىٰۚ مَن كَسَبَ سَيِّئَةٗ وَأَحَٰطَتۡ بِهِۦ خَطِيٓ‍َٔتُهُۥ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

The matter is not as they imagine, for Allah punishes those who earn the evil of disbelief and whose sins surround them from every side. And He will recompense them with permanent admission into the Fire, where they will live eternally.

(٨٢) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

And those who have faith in Allah and His Prophet and do good will be rewarded with permanent entry into Paradise, where they will live eternally.

(٨٣) وَإِذۡ أَخَذۡنَا مِيثَٰقَ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ لَا تَعۡبُدُونَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَ وَبِٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ إِحۡسَانٗا وَذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينِ وَقُولُواْ لِلنَّاسِ حُسۡنٗا وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ ثُمَّ تَوَلَّيۡتُمۡ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا مِّنكُمۡ وَأَنتُم مُّعۡرِضُونَ

Remember, O Israelites, the firm covenant that We took from you: to worship Him alone, and not worship anyone with Him; to be kind to parents, relatives, orphans, and the needy and destitute; to say kind words to people, telling them to do what is right and forbidding evil, without harshness or coercion; to perform the prayer completely as instructed; to give Zakat to its rightful recipients with a contentful heart. But after you took this promise, you turned away from fulfilling it except for some of you whom Allah protected, and they fulfilled their promise and covenant to Allah.

Points of Benefit:

Some of the People of the Book claim knowledge of what Allah has revealed, but the truth is that they have no knowledge of what He revealed, but rather it is delusion and ignorance.
From the worst sinners among people is he who lies about Allah, the Exalted, and His Messengers and attributes to them something that is not from them.
Despite the tremendous covenants that Allah, the Exalted, imposed on the Jews and the severity of the emphasis on them, this only increased their aversion and rejection of them.
