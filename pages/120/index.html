(٧١) وَحَسِبُوٓاْ أَلَّا تَكُونَ فِتۡنَةٞ فَعَمُواْ وَصَمُّواْ ثُمَّ تَابَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ثُمَّ عَمُواْ وَصَمُّواْ كَثِيرٞ مِّنۡهُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ بَصِيرُۢ بِمَا يَعۡمَلُونَ

They thought that breaking their promises and covenants, lying, and murdering prophets would not bring about any harm. But it brought consequences that they could not even think of. They were blind and deaf to the truth and could not be guided. Then, Allah accepted their repentance due to His generosity. However, they became blind and deaf to the truth again, which happened to many of them. Allah sees all they do; nothing is hidden from Him, and He will recompense them for their actions.

(٧٢) لَقَدۡ كَفَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡمَسِيحُ ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَۖ وَقَالَ ٱلۡمَسِيحُ يَٰبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ رَبِّي وَرَبَّكُمۡۖ إِنَّهُۥ مَن يُشۡرِكۡ بِٱللَّهِ فَقَدۡ حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ وَمَأۡوَىٰهُ ٱلنَّارُۖ وَمَا لِلظَّٰلِمِينَ مِنۡ أَنصَارٖ

Verily, the Christians who claim that Allah is the Messiah, Jesus, Son of Mary, have committed disbelief by ascribing divinity to other than Allah. Meanwhile, the Messiah, Son of Mary, himself, said to them: O Israelites, worship Allah alone. He is my Lord and your Lord. We are all His slaves because Allah will prevent anyone who associated partners with Him from ever entering Paradise, and they will dwell in the Hellfire forever. They will not find any help or support before Allah, and no one can rescue them from the torment that awaits them.

(٧٣) لَّقَدۡ كَفَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ثَالِثُ ثَلَٰثَةٖۘ وَمَا مِنۡ إِلَٰهٍ إِلَّآ إِلَٰهٞ وَٰحِدٞۚ وَإِن لَّمۡ يَنتَهُواْ عَمَّا يَقُولُونَ لَيَمَسَّنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ

Verily, the Christians have committed disbelief by saying, Allah is part of a trinity; the father, the son, and the holy spirit (Allah is Transcendent and far beyond what they claim). Allah is not many; He is One God alone without any partner. If they do not cease from this heinous statement, they will undoubtedly face an excruciating torment.

(٧٤) أَفَلَا يَتُوبُونَ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَيَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَهُۥۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Will they not retract what they said in repentance to Allah and seek forgiveness from Him for the polytheism they committed?! Allah forgives whoever repents to Him, no matter what their sin is. Even if the sin was disbelief, He is merciful to the believers.

(٧٥) مَّا ٱلۡمَسِيحُ ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ إِلَّا رَسُولٞ قَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلِهِ ٱلرُّسُلُ وَأُمُّهُۥ صِدِّيقَةٞۖ كَانَا يَأۡكُلَانِ ٱلطَّعَامَۗ ٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ نُبَيِّنُ لَهُمُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ ثُمَّ ٱنظُرۡ أَنَّىٰ يُؤۡفَكُونَ

The Messiah, Jesus, Son of Mary, is only a messenger like other messengers. He will experience death like the others did. His mother, Mary (peace be upon her), was sincere and truthful. They both ate food because they needed it. How could they, therefore, be gods while they needed food?! Look, O Messenger, and ponder how we make the signs clear to them which show that Allah is one and that their exaggeration of ascribing divinity to other than Him (may He be Exalted) is falsehood, yet they reject these signs. Then, look and ponder how they are directed away from the truth despite these clear signs showing Allah's oneness.

(٧٦) قُلۡ أَتَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا يَمۡلِكُ لَكُمۡ ضَرّٗا وَلَا نَفۡعٗاۚ وَٱللَّهُ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

Say, O Messenger, as an argument against their worship of others besides Allah: Do you worship what cannot bring you any benefit nor protect you from any harm?! Those objects of worship are powerless, and Allah is high above any incapacity. Allah is the only one who hears everything you say without missing anything. He is Omniscient of your actions; none of them are hidden from Him, and He will recompense you for them.

(٧٧) قُلۡ يَٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَا تَغۡلُواْ فِي دِينِكُمۡ غَيۡرَ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُوٓاْ أَهۡوَآءَ قَوۡمٖ قَدۡ ضَلُّواْ مِن قَبۡلُ وَأَضَلُّواْ كَثِيرٗا وَضَلُّواْ عَن سَوَآءِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ

Say, O Messenger, to the Christians: Do not transgress the limits of your orders to follow the truth, and do not exaggerate in reverence of those whom you were ordered to revere, like the prophets, such that you believe they are divine like you did with Jesus, Son of Mary, because you followed your predecessors from the people of misguidance who misled many people and strayed from the true path.

Points of Benefit:

Explaining the disbelief of Christians in their claim to the divinity of Christ, peace be upon him. Also explaining its invalidity and calling for repentance from it.
Among the evidence of the humanity of Christ and his mother is their eating of food and doing what resulted from it.
The inability to prevent harm and deliver benefit is clear evidence that those who are worshiped other than Allah are not worthy of divinity because they are helpless.
Prohibiting extremism and exceeding the limit in treating the righteous people of the Exalted Allah's creation.
