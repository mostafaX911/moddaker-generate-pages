(٧٨) لُعِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنۢ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ عَلَىٰ لِسَانِ دَاوُۥدَ وَعِيسَى ٱبۡنِ مَرۡيَمَۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِمَا عَصَواْ وَّكَانُواْ يَعۡتَدُونَ

Allah, the Exalted, mentions in the book He revealed to David (the Psalms) that He distanced the disbelieving Israelites from His mercy. He also informs about it in the book (the Gospel) He revealed to Jesus, Son of Mary. He did this because they committed sins and violated what Allah had made sacred.

(٧٩) كَانُواْ لَا يَتَنَاهَوۡنَ عَن مُّنكَرٖ فَعَلُوهُۚ لَبِئۡسَ مَا كَانُواْ يَفۡعَلُونَ

They never used to forbid each other from committing sins. Instead, they would openly commit sins and evil because no one would tell them otherwise. How evil it was for them to abandon the practice of forbidding evil.

(٨٠) تَرَىٰ كَثِيرٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ يَتَوَلَّوۡنَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْۚ لَبِئۡسَ مَا قَدَّمَتۡ لَهُمۡ أَنفُسُهُمۡ أَن سَخِطَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَفِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ هُمۡ خَٰلِدُونَ

You will see, O Messenger, many of the disbelievers from among the Jews showing affection towards the Christians and inclining towards them. At the same time, they oppose you and the believers who worship Allah alone. How evil it is that they take the disbelievers as allies. For this reason, Allah is angry with them and will place them in the Fire, where they will reside eternally and never leave.

(٨١) وَلَوۡ كَانُواْ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلنَّبِيِّ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡهِ مَا ٱتَّخَذُوهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءَ وَلَٰكِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ فَٰسِقُونَ

If those Jews had genuinely believed in Allah, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, and what Allah revealed to him in the Quran, they would not have taken the polytheists as allies, loving them and inclining towards them over the believers. That is because they were prohibited from taking the disbelievers as allies. But many Jews disobey Allah and do not side with Allah and the believers.

(٨٢) ۞لَتَجِدَنَّ أَشَدَّ ٱلنَّاسِ عَدَٰوَةٗ لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱلۡيَهُودَ وَٱلَّذِينَ أَشۡرَكُواْۖ وَلَتَجِدَنَّ أَقۡرَبَهُم مَّوَدَّةٗ لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا نَصَٰرَىٰۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ مِنۡهُمۡ قِسِّيسِينَ وَرُهۡبَانٗا وَأَنَّهُمۡ لَا يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ

You will find, O Messenger, that the people with the most hostility towards those who believe in you and that which you brought are the Jews, due to their malice, jealousy, and arrogance, as well as the polytheists and others who associate partners with Allah. You will also find that the people who are closest in affection to those who believe in you and that which you brought are those who call themselves Christians. The [reason for the] affection of these Christians towards the believers is that there are scholars and worshippers among them who are humble and not arrogant. Good does not enter the heart of an arrogant person.

(٨٣) وَإِذَا سَمِعُواْ مَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَى ٱلرَّسُولِ تَرَىٰٓ أَعۡيُنَهُمۡ تَفِيضُ مِنَ ٱلدَّمۡعِ مِمَّا عَرَفُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّۖ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَآ ءَامَنَّا فَٱكۡتُبۡنَا مَعَ ٱلشَّٰهِدِينَ

And these people, such as Najashi and his companions, have soft hearts. When they listen to [any part of] the Quran that has been revealed, they cry in submission as they recognize that it is the truth due to knowing what Jesus (peace be with him) brought. They say: O Our Lord, we believe in what You have revealed to Your Messenger, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, so count us, O Our Lord, among the nation of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم who will be a proof against the people on the Day of Resurrection.

Points of Benefit:

Neglecting the enjoining of good and forbidding evil is a cause for being cursed and excluded from the mercy of Allah, the Exalted.
Among the signs of faith is the love for Allah's sake and hatred for Allah's sake.
Allying with the enemies of Allah incurs the wrath of Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, upon the one who does so.
The intense animosity of the Jews and the polytheists towards the people of Islam, and conversely, the presence of factions among the Christians who harbor goodwill towards Islam, recognizing it as the true religion.
