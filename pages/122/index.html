(٨٤) وَمَا لَنَا لَا نُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ وَمَا جَآءَنَا مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَنَطۡمَعُ أَن يُدۡخِلَنَا رَبُّنَا مَعَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

What prevents us from believing in Allah and the truth that He has revealed to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم? We hope to enter Paradise with the prophets and their followers who obeyed Allah and feared His punishment.

(٨٥) فَأَثَٰبَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِمَا قَالُواْ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ وَذَٰلِكَ جَزَآءُ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

So, Allah recompensed them for their belief and recognition of the truth with gardens that have rivers flowing beneath its palaces and trees, where they will live eternally. That is the recompense of those who do good by following the truth and submitting to it without any restriction or condition.

(٨٦) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَكَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

Those who disbelieve in Allah and His Messenger and reject the verses Allah revealed to His Messenger will remain in the blazing Fire eternally and never leave.

(٨٧) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تُحَرِّمُواْ طَيِّبَٰتِ مَآ أَحَلَّ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ وَلَا تَعۡتَدُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُعۡتَدِينَ

O you who believe, do not regard as unlawful the pleasures of food, drink, and women [that Allah has made lawful for you]. Do not treat these things as unlawful due to asceticism and devotion. Do not transgress the limits of that which Allah has declared forbidden upon you because Allah does not love those who transgress His limits; rather, He hates them.

(٨٨) وَكُلُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ حَلَٰلٗا طَيِّبٗاۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنتُم بِهِۦ مُؤۡمِنُونَ

Consume the lawful and pure things that Allah gives to you from His provision. But do not consume the unlawful, such as something that is taken wrongfully or is impure. Fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions because He is the one you believe in, and your belief in Him requires you to fear Him.

(٨٩) لَا يُؤَاخِذُكُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِٱللَّغۡوِ فِيٓ أَيۡمَٰنِكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن يُؤَاخِذُكُم بِمَا عَقَّدتُّمُ ٱلۡأَيۡمَٰنَۖ فَكَفَّٰرَتُهُۥٓ إِطۡعَامُ عَشَرَةِ مَسَٰكِينَ مِنۡ أَوۡسَطِ مَا تُطۡعِمُونَ أَهۡلِيكُمۡ أَوۡ كِسۡوَتُهُمۡ أَوۡ تَحۡرِيرُ رَقَبَةٖۖ فَمَن لَّمۡ يَجِدۡ فَصِيَامُ ثَلَٰثَةِ أَيَّامٖۚ ذَٰلِكَ كَفَّٰرَةُ أَيۡمَٰنِكُمۡ إِذَا حَلَفۡتُمۡۚ وَٱحۡفَظُوٓاْ أَيۡمَٰنَكُمۡۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

Allah will not take you to account, O believers, for the oaths that you utter unintentionally. He will only take you to account for those oaths you break after making them with a firm intention in your hearts. The sin of breaking an oath that has been made can be atoned for by one of three options: feeding ten poor people with the average food of your city by giving each poor person half a ṣā' of food, or clothing them with what society considers to be a suit of clothing, or freeing a believing slave. If the person required to make an expiation does not find any of these three things, then the needed expiation is fasting for three days. That is the expiation for your oaths, O believers, when you break them. Guard your oaths against being taken falsely in Allah's name and breaking them unless it is better to do so. If it is better to break your oath, do so and perform the expiation. Just as Allah has explained the expiation for oaths, He also explains to you His laws, which make clear what is lawful and unlawful, so that you might thank Allah for having taught you what you did not know.

(٩٠) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡخَمۡرُ وَٱلۡمَيۡسِرُ وَٱلۡأَنصَابُ وَٱلۡأَزۡلَٰمُ رِجۡسٞ مِّنۡ عَمَلِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ فَٱجۡتَنِبُوهُ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ

O you who believe, intoxicants that affect the mind, gambling with a contribution from both sides, stone alters on which the polytheists offer sacrifices to honor them or which they erect for worship, and divining arrows to seek out their share of the unseen – these are all sinful acts that the Devil has made attractive. Avoid such actions so that you successfully achieve a noble life in this world and the bliss of Paradise in the hereafter.

Points of Benefit:

The command to choose what is good from provisions and to avoid what is harmful.
Not holding oneself accountable for an oath made without a firm intention in the heart, and holding oneself accountable for what was made with a firm intention in the heart to either do or not do.
Clarification that the expiation for breaking an oath involves either feeding ten needy individuals, providing them with clothing, or freeing a believing slave. If the one making the oath is unable to fulfill any of these options, then they should expiate by fasting for three days.
His saying, the Exalted: '...Indeed, intoxicants...' is the last verse revealed regarding the prohibition of intoxicants, serving as a clear prohibition.
