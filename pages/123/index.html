(٩١) إِنَّمَا يُرِيدُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ أَن يُوقِعَ بَيۡنَكُمُ ٱلۡعَدَٰوَةَ وَٱلۡبَغۡضَآءَ فِي ٱلۡخَمۡرِ وَٱلۡمَيۡسِرِ وَيَصُدَّكُمۡ عَن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ وَعَنِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةِۖ فَهَلۡ أَنتُم مُّنتَهُونَ

By making intoxicants and gambling seem attractive, the Devil's only intention is to create animosity and hatred for each other in their hearts and to turn them away from Allah's remembrance and prayer. So will you, O believers, leave these evil acts? Without a doubt, [abandoning these acts] is the right thing to do, so avoid them.

(٩٢) وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱلرَّسُولَ وَٱحۡذَرُواْۚ فَإِن تَوَلَّيۡتُمۡ فَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا عَلَىٰ رَسُولِنَا ٱلۡبَلَٰغُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

And obey Allah and the Messenger by complying with the commands of the Sacred Law and avoiding its prohibitions. Be careful of contradicting it. If you turn away from this, know that Our Messenger's duty is only to convey to you what Allah has ordered him to, and he has already done so. If you are guided, then it is for your own good. If you do wrong, it will be to your detriment.

When the prohibition of alcohol was revealed, some believers questioned the status of those who died as Muslims before its prohibition. This led to the revelation of the following verse:

(٩٣) لَيۡسَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ جُنَاحٞ فِيمَا طَعِمُوٓاْ إِذَا مَا ٱتَّقَواْ وَّءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ ثُمَّ ٱتَّقَواْ وَّءَامَنُواْ ثُمَّ ٱتَّقَواْ وَّأَحۡسَنُواْۚ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

There is no sin on those who believe in Allah and do righteous deeds to gain closeness to Him for consuming intoxicants before it was made unlawful. As long as they now stay away from prohibited things, take heed of Allah's anger, believe in Him and do righteous deeds, and then increase their awareness of Allah until they worship Him as if they see Him. Allah loves those who worship Him as if they see Him because they know Allah is always watching them. This awareness of Allah leads believers to perfect their deeds.

(٩٤) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَيَبۡلُوَنَّكُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِشَيۡءٖ مِّنَ ٱلصَّيۡدِ تَنَالُهُۥٓ أَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَرِمَاحُكُمۡ لِيَعۡلَمَ ٱللَّهُ مَن يَخَافُهُۥ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِۚ فَمَنِ ٱعۡتَدَىٰ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ فَلَهُۥ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

O you who believe, Allah will certainly test you by giving you land game when you are in the state of Ihram (ritual sanctity). You could catch the smaller game with your hands and the bigger ones with your spears. Allah will do this to make known and hold accountable those who fear their Creator without seeing Him due to their complete belief in Allah's Knowledge. Therefore, they avoid hunting due to the fear of their Creator, from whom nothing is hidden. Whoever transgresses the limits and hunts in the state of Ihram (ritual sanctity) shall receive a painful torment on the Day of Resurrection for violating Allah's prohibition.

(٩٥) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَقۡتُلُواْ ٱلصَّيۡدَ وَأَنتُمۡ حُرُمٞۚ وَمَن قَتَلَهُۥ مِنكُم مُّتَعَمِّدٗا فَجَزَآءٞ مِّثۡلُ مَا قَتَلَ مِنَ ٱلنَّعَمِ يَحۡكُمُ بِهِۦ ذَوَا عَدۡلٖ مِّنكُمۡ هَدۡيَۢا بَٰلِغَ ٱلۡكَعۡبَةِ أَوۡ كَفَّٰرَةٞ طَعَامُ مَسَٰكِينَ أَوۡ عَدۡلُ ذَٰلِكَ صِيَامٗا لِّيَذُوقَ وَبَالَ أَمۡرِهِۦۗ عَفَا ٱللَّهُ عَمَّا سَلَفَۚ وَمَنۡ عَادَ فَيَنتَقِمُ ٱللَّهُ مِنۡهُۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَزِيزٞ ذُو ٱنتِقَامٍ

O you who believe, do not kill the land game when you are in the state of Ihram (ritual sanctity). Whoever of you does so deliberately shall be liable for recompense equivalent to the game they killed from camels, cattle, or sheep. Two men known to be upright amongst the Muslims will judge this. The animal they decide on shall be treated like a sacrificial animal and sent to Mecca to be slaughtered in the Haram (Sanctuary). Another option for the recompense is that the equivalent value should be distributed to the poor within the Haram; every poor person should receive half a sa` of grain, or the fast of one day should be kept for every half sa` of grain. All of that is so that the hunter may taste the consequences of his actions. Allah has overlooked the game of the Haram, which were killed in the past, and the person who hunted the land game in the state of Ihram before the prohibition. If anyone repeats this after the prohibition, then Allah will take retribution from them by punishing them for the same. Allah is Mighty and Powerful. Due to His Might, He can take revenge on those who disobey Him if He wishes; nothing can stop Him from doing so.

Points of Benefit:

Not holding a person accountable for what has not been explicitly prohibited or what they are not aware has been prohibited.
The prohibition of hunting for the one in a state of Ihram for Hajj or Umrah, and the explanation of the expiation for killing game.
Among the wisdom of Allah, the Almighty, in prohibition is the trial and purification of His servants, and in the expiation is deterrence and admonition.
