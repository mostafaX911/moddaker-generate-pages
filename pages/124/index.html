(٩٦) أُحِلَّ لَكُمۡ صَيۡدُ ٱلۡبَحۡرِ وَطَعَامُهُۥ مَتَٰعٗا لَّكُمۡ وَلِلسَّيَّارَةِۖ وَحُرِّمَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ صَيۡدُ ٱلۡبَرِّ مَا دُمۡتُمۡ حُرُمٗاۗ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ

Allah has permitted you to fish and permitted what the sea throws out, dead or alive, to benefit the resident or to nourish the traveler. On the other hand, He has made hunting land game unlawful as long as you are in the state of Ihram (ritual sanctity). Therefore, fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions because He is the One to whom you will return on the Day of Resurrection, and He will recompense you for your deeds accordingly.

(٩٧) ۞جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡكَعۡبَةَ ٱلۡبَيۡتَ ٱلۡحَرَامَ قِيَٰمٗا لِّلنَّاسِ وَٱلشَّهۡرَ ٱلۡحَرَامَ وَٱلۡهَدۡيَ وَٱلۡقَلَٰٓئِدَۚ ذَٰلِكَ لِتَعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٌ

Allah has made the Kaaba, the Sanctified House, an establishment for people, which secures their religious interests, such as prayer, Hajj, and Umrah, and worldly interests, such as security in the Sanctuary and the import of different fruits. He has also made the sacred months (Dhu Al-Qa'dah, Dhu Al-Hijjah, Muharram, and Rajab) an establishment for people to be safe from anyone fighting against them in these months. Then, He made the sacrificial animals and the garlands that indicate they are being taken to the Haram (Sanctuary), an establishment for people where their owners are safe from anyone harming them. All of this is Allah's favor to you so that you are aware that Allah knows everything in the heavens and the earth. His legislation of such laws that secure your interests and protect you from harm proves that He knows what is best for His slaves.

(٩٨) ٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Know, O people, that Allah's punishment for those who disobey Him is severe and that He is Forgiving and Compassionate towards those who repent.

(٩٩) مَّا عَلَى ٱلرَّسُولِ إِلَّا ٱلۡبَلَٰغُۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مَا تُبۡدُونَ وَمَا تَكۡتُمُونَ

The duty of the Messenger is only to convey what Allah has ordered him to. It is not his duty to make people accept the guidance: that is only in Allah's Hand. Allah knows what you reveal and keep hidden, whether guidance or misguidance. He will recompense you for that accordingly.

(١٠٠) قُل لَّا يَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡخَبِيثُ وَٱلطَّيِّبُ وَلَوۡ أَعۡجَبَكَ كَثۡرَةُ ٱلۡخَبِيثِۚ فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ يَٰٓأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ

Say, O Messenger: Evil is not the same as good, even if the abundance of evil impresses you. Its abundance does not prove its virtue. Therefore, fear Allah, O people of intellect, by abandoning evil and doing good so that you may successfully achieve Paradise.

(١٠١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَسۡ‍َٔلُواْ عَنۡ أَشۡيَآءَ إِن تُبۡدَ لَكُمۡ تَسُؤۡكُمۡ وَإِن تَسۡ‍َٔلُواْ عَنۡهَا حِينَ يُنَزَّلُ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ تُبۡدَ لَكُمۡ عَفَا ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهَاۗ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٌ حَلِيمٞ

O you who believe, do not ask your Messenger about things you do not need and will not help you in your religion. If such things were shown to you, they would upset you because of their difficulty. If, despite the prohibition, you ask about these things while the revelation is being sent to the Messenger, they will be made clear to you. That is easy for Allah. Allah has omitted and remained silent about certain things in the Quran, so do not ask about them. If you ask about them, you must follow the law that is then revealed concerning them. Indeed, Allah forgives the sins of His slaves when they repent. He is Forbearing not to hasten their punishment.

(١٠٢) قَدۡ سَأَلَهَا قَوۡمٞ مِّن قَبۡلِكُمۡ ثُمَّ أَصۡبَحُواْ بِهَا كَٰفِرِينَ

A people before you had asked about similar things [not initially revealed to them]. When they were then instructed to do them, they did not carry out the order; and became disbelievers as a result.

(١٠٣) مَا جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ مِنۢ بَحِيرَةٖ وَلَا سَآئِبَةٖ وَلَا وَصِيلَةٖ وَلَا حَامٖ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡكَذِبَۖ وَأَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡقِلُونَ

Allah has made livestock lawful for you [to consume]. He has not made unlawful what the polytheists declared unlawful for themselves when dedicating them to their idols, such as: bahirah, a she-camel whose ear is cut after it gives birth to a certain number, or Sa'ibah, a she-camel that is left for the idols after it reaches a certain age; or wasilah, a she-camel that gives birth to one female after another; or hami, a male camel stud that mates with other camels and produces a given number of offspring. But, the disbelievers falsely claimed that Allah had forbidden these things. Most disbelievers do not differentiate between truth and falsehood and between the lawful and the unlawful.

Points of Benefit:

The fundamental principle regarding the rituals of Allah, the Almighty, is that they have been established to realize the worldly and Hereafter benefits for His servants and to avert harm from them.
Not being impressed by quantity, as the abundance of something is not an indication of its permissibility or virtue; rather, the legal ruling is the criterion.
One of the etiquettes of the one seeking advice is to limit the question within specific boundaries. It is not appropriate to ask about something when there is no need or purpose for it
Criticism of the practices of the polytheists in what they have invented and claimed regarding the forbidden aspects of livestock, such as consecrated animals, the Sa'ibah, the Wasilah, and the Hama.
