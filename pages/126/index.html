(١٠٩) ۞يَوۡمَ يَجۡمَعُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلرُّسُلَ فَيَقُولُ مَاذَآ أُجِبۡتُمۡۖ قَالُواْ لَا عِلۡمَ لَنَآۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ عَلَّٰمُ ٱلۡغُيُوبِ

Remember, O people, the Day of Resurrection when Allah will gather all the messengers and ask them: What did your nations respond to you? They will leave the answer to that question to Allah and say: We do not know. You have all knowledge, O our Lord. You alone know all things unseen.

(١١٠) إِذۡ قَالَ ٱللَّهُ يَٰعِيسَى ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ ٱذۡكُرۡ نِعۡمَتِي عَلَيۡكَ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَتِكَ إِذۡ أَيَّدتُّكَ بِرُوحِ ٱلۡقُدُسِ تُكَلِّمُ ٱلنَّاسَ فِي ٱلۡمَهۡدِ وَكَهۡلٗاۖ وَإِذۡ عَلَّمۡتُكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةَ وَٱلۡإِنجِيلَۖ وَإِذۡ تَخۡلُقُ مِنَ ٱلطِّينِ كَهَيۡ‍َٔةِ ٱلطَّيۡرِ بِإِذۡنِي فَتَنفُخُ فِيهَا فَتَكُونُ طَيۡرَۢا بِإِذۡنِيۖ وَتُبۡرِئُ ٱلۡأَكۡمَهَ وَٱلۡأَبۡرَصَ بِإِذۡنِيۖ وَإِذۡ تُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ بِإِذۡنِيۖ وَإِذۡ كَفَفۡتُ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ عَنكَ إِذۡ جِئۡتَهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ فَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا سِحۡرٞ مُّبِينٞ

Remember when Allah will address Jesus (peace be upon him) and say to him: O Jesus, son of Mary, remember My favor upon you when I created you without a father; and My favor upon your mother, Mary (peace be upon her), when I chose her over all the women of her time; and upon you when I strengthened you with Gabriel (peace be upon him.). You used to speak to people as a breastfed baby, inviting them to Allah, and you spoke to them in adulthood, telling them what Allah had sent you with. From among my favors upon you was I taught you how to write; I taught you the Torah which I had revealed to Moses (peace be upon him); the Gospel which I revealed to you, and I taught you the secrets of the sacred law, its benefits, and wisdom. Also, another favor was that you would mold a bird from clay, blow into it, and it would become a living bird. You used to cure the person who was born blind, and you would treat the leper and heal his skin. You also used to bring the dead to life by supplicating to Allah to give them life. All of this happened with My permission. Another favor I favored you with was that I protected you from the Israelites when they intended to kill you after you brought clear miracles to them. The only thing they could do was disbelieve in them, and they said: Jesus has brought nothing but clear magic.

(١١١) وَإِذۡ أَوۡحَيۡتُ إِلَى ٱلۡحَوَارِيِّ‍ۧنَ أَنۡ ءَامِنُواْ بِي وَبِرَسُولِي قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا وَٱشۡهَدۡ بِأَنَّنَا مُسۡلِمُونَ

Another favor that I graced you with was that I facilitated for you helpers when I inspired the disciples to believe in you and Me. They submitted to that and responded: We believe. Be a witness, O our Lord, that we submit to You in devotion.

(١١٢) إِذۡ قَالَ ٱلۡحَوَارِيُّونَ يَٰعِيسَى ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ هَلۡ يَسۡتَطِيعُ رَبُّكَ أَن يُنَزِّلَ عَلَيۡنَا مَآئِدَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِۖ قَالَ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Remember when the disciples said: Can your Lord send down a table spread from the sky if you supplicate to Him? Jesus (peace be upon him) responded by instructing them to fear Allah and not to ask for such a thing, as it may prove to be a trial for them. He told them: Place your trust in your Lord in seeking provision if you are truly believers.

(١١٣) قَالُواْ نُرِيدُ أَن نَّأۡكُلَ مِنۡهَا وَتَطۡمَئِنَّ قُلُوبُنَا وَنَعۡلَمَ أَن قَدۡ صَدَقۡتَنَا وَنَكُونَ عَلَيۡهَا مِنَ ٱلشَّٰهِدِينَ

The disciples said to Jesus: We want to eat from this table spread so that our hearts can be satisfied with Allah's perfect power and that you are His Messenger, and to know for certain that you have told us the truth about what you have brought from Allah, and to be witnesses to it for those people who are not present.

Points of Benefit:

Affirmation of Allah gathering all of His creation on the Day of Resurrection, whether they are esteemed or lowly.
Affirmation of the humanity of Jesus (peace be upon him) and confirmation of his sensory miracles, such as reviving the dead and healing the blind and lepers, which Allah performed through him.
Clarifying that the miracles of the prophets aim to strengthen the believers and confound the opponents and that they are not of their own accord but come by the permission of Allah Almighty.
