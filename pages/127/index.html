(١١٤) قَالَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ ٱللَّهُمَّ رَبَّنَآ أَنزِلۡ عَلَيۡنَا مَآئِدَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ تَكُونُ لَنَا عِيدٗا لِّأَوَّلِنَا وَءَاخِرِنَا وَءَايَةٗ مِّنكَۖ وَٱرۡزُقۡنَا وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّٰزِقِينَ

Jesus gave in to their request and supplicated to Allah, saying: O our Lord, send down to us a table spread of food so that we can hold a festival on the day it comes down to honor You out of gratitude. It will also be a sign and proof of Your Oneness and the truth of what I have been sent with. So give us such provision that will help us worship You. You, O our Lord, are the best provider.

(١١٥) قَالَ ٱللَّهُ إِنِّي مُنَزِّلُهَا عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ فَمَن يَكۡفُرۡ بَعۡدُ مِنكُمۡ فَإِنِّيٓ أُعَذِّبُهُۥ عَذَابٗا لَّآ أُعَذِّبُهُۥٓ أَحَدٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Allah responded to the supplication of Jesus (peace be upon him) and said: I will send down the table spread that you have requested, but whoever disbelieves after I have sent it down should blame only themselves. I will torment them with a severe torment, unlike any other, due to them seeing an overwhelming sign and disbelieving only out of stubbornness. So Allah fulfilled His promise and sent down the table spread to them.

(١١٦) وَإِذۡ قَالَ ٱللَّهُ يَٰعِيسَى ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ ءَأَنتَ قُلۡتَ لِلنَّاسِ ٱتَّخِذُونِي وَأُمِّيَ إِلَٰهَيۡنِ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۖ قَالَ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ مَا يَكُونُ لِيٓ أَنۡ أَقُولَ مَا لَيۡسَ لِي بِحَقٍّۚ إِن كُنتُ قُلۡتُهُۥ فَقَدۡ عَلِمۡتَهُۥۚ تَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي نَفۡسِي وَلَآ أَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي نَفۡسِكَۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ عَلَّٰمُ ٱلۡغُيُوبِ

Remember, [O Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم], when Allah will address Jesus, son of Mary (peace be upon them) on the Day of Resurrection, saying: O Jesus, son of Mary, did you tell the people to worship you and your mother instead of Allah? Jesus will reply, exalting his Lord: It was not right for me to tell them anything but the truth. If I had said that, You would know it because nothing is hidden from You. You know what I keep hidden within myself, but I do not know what is with You. You alone know everything unseen, hidden, and apparent.

(١١٧) مَا قُلۡتُ لَهُمۡ إِلَّا مَآ أَمَرۡتَنِي بِهِۦٓ أَنِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ رَبِّي وَرَبَّكُمۡۚ وَكُنتُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ شَهِيدٗا مَّا دُمۡتُ فِيهِمۡۖ فَلَمَّا تَوَفَّيۡتَنِي كُنتَ أَنتَ ٱلرَّقِيبَ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ وَأَنتَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدٌ

Jesus will say to His Lord: I only told people what You ordered me to tell them: to worship You alone. I observed what they were saying as long as I remained among them. When my time with them ended, and I was raised to the sky alive, You, O Lord, were watching their deeds. You are a witness to everything, and nothing is hidden from You, so You know what I said to them and what they said after me.

(١١٨) إِن تُعَذِّبۡهُمۡ فَإِنَّهُمۡ عِبَادُكَۖ وَإِن تَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ فَإِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

If you punish them, O my Lord, they are Your slaves, and You can do with them as You wish. If You graciously forgive those of them who believe, nothing can stop You from doing so because You are the Almighty, Who cannot be overpowered, and the Wise in Your planning.

(١١٩) قَالَ ٱللَّهُ هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ يَنفَعُ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ صِدۡقُهُمۡۚ لَهُمۡ جَنَّٰتٞ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدٗاۖ رَّضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

Allah will say to Jesus (peace be upon him): this [the Day of Resurrection] is the Day on which the truthfulness of those who were truthful in their intentions, statements, and deeds will benefit them. They will receive gardens with rivers flowing beneath its palaces and trees, where they will live eternally, with death never coming to them. Allah will be pleased with them and will never become displeased with them. They will be pleased with Allah because of the everlasting bliss they have received. Such reward and pleasure are a great success, and no success comes close to it.

(١٢٠) لِلَّهِ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا فِيهِنَّۚ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرُۢ

To Allah alone belongs the dominion of the heavens, Earth, and every creation within them. He is their Creator and the One who plans their affairs. He has power over everything, and nothing can hinder Him.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's warning to everyone who persists in their disbelief and stubbornness after clear evidence has been presented against them.
The exoneration of Jesus (peace be upon him) from the Christians' claim that he informed them that he is God, the Son of God, or that he claimed divinity or lordship.
Allah, the Most High, will question the great individuals and leaders among the people on the Day of Judgment, so what about those below them in status?!
The elevated status of truthfulness, the praise of Allah, the Most High, for its people, and the clarification of the benefits of truthfulness for its people on the Day of Judgment.
