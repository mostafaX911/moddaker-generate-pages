The Cattle (Surah Al-An'am)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Affirming pure monotheism and responding to the deviations of polytheists.

Explanation:

(١) ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَجَعَلَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ وَٱلنُّورَۖ ثُمَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِرَبِّهِمۡ يَعۡدِلُونَ

Only Allah, Who created the heavens and the earth without precedent, is described, in adoration, with perfection and praised with the loftiest, most beautiful attributes. He created the night and the day, which follow one another. He created the night for darkness and the day for light. Yet, despite all this, some of His slaves disbelieve in Him, equate others with Him, and associate partners with Him.

(٢) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن طِينٖ ثُمَّ قَضَىٰٓ أَجَلٗاۖ وَأَجَلٞ مُّسَمًّى عِندَهُۥۖ ثُمَّ أَنتُمۡ تَمۡتَرُونَ

He (may He be Exalted) is the One who created you, O people, from clay when He created your father, Adam (peace be upon him) from it. Then He (may He be Exalted) fixed a period for your stay in the life of this world. He also set another term, which only He knows, when He will resurrect you back to life on the Day of Resurrection. Despite this, you still doubt His power (may He be Exalted) to resurrect you.

(٣) وَهُوَ ٱللَّهُ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَفِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَعۡلَمُ سِرَّكُمۡ وَجَهۡرَكُمۡ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا تَكۡسِبُونَ

He (may He be Exalted) is the One worthy of worship in the heavens and the earth. Nothing is hidden from him: He knows the intentions, statements, and deeds you keep hidden and those you make public, and He will recompense you accordingly.

(٤) وَمَا تَأۡتِيهِم مِّنۡ ءَايَةٖ مِّنۡ ءَايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ إِلَّا كَانُواْ عَنۡهَا مُعۡرِضِينَ

No proof from their Lord comes to the polytheists except that they ignore it without paying attention. Clear proofs and evidence of the Oneness of Allah, as well as miracles proving the truthfulness of the messengers, have now come to them; yet despite this, they turned away and paid no attention [to these signs and miracles].

(٥) فَقَدۡ كَذَّبُواْ بِٱلۡحَقِّ لَمَّا جَآءَهُمۡ فَسَوۡفَ يَأۡتِيهِمۡ أَنۢبَٰٓؤُاْ مَا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

Besides turning away from these clear proofs and evidence, they had previously turned away from something even clearer: they rejected the Quran that Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought. They will soon realize, when they see the torment on the Day of Resurrection, that what they used to poke fun at and what he brought to them was, in fact, the truth.

(٦) أَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ كَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّن قَرۡنٖ مَّكَّنَّٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَا لَمۡ نُمَكِّن لَّكُمۡ وَأَرۡسَلۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ عَلَيۡهِم مِّدۡرَارٗا وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرَ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهِمۡ فَأَهۡلَكۡنَٰهُم بِذُنُوبِهِمۡ وَأَنشَأۡنَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ قَرۡنًا ءَاخَرِينَ

Do these disbelievers not know Allah's established practice of destroying nations that do wrong? Allah has destroyed many nations before them. They were more firmly established on the Earth than these disbelievers. Allah sent down continuous rain upon them and made rivers flow beneath their homes. Yet, they disobeyed Allah, so He destroyed them because of their disobedience. Then, after them, He created other nations.

(٧) وَلَوۡ نَزَّلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ كِتَٰبٗا فِي قِرۡطَاسٖ فَلَمَسُوهُ بِأَيۡدِيهِمۡ لَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا سِحۡرٞ مُّبِينٞ

If We had sent down to you, O Messenger, a book written on paper, and they had seen it with their eyes and touched it with their hands, they would not have accepted it out of stubbornness and rejection. They would have said: What you have brought is nothing more than clear magic, and we will never believe in it.

(٨) وَقَالُواْ لَوۡلَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيۡهِ مَلَكٞۖ وَلَوۡ أَنزَلۡنَا مَلَكٗا لَّقُضِيَ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ ثُمَّ لَا يُنظَرُونَ

These disbelievers said: We will believe if Allah sends down an angel with Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم who speaks to us and bears witness that he is a messenger. If We had sent down an angel of the kind they wished, We would have destroyed them if they did not believe, and they would not be given time to repent.

Points of Benefit:

The stubbornness of disbelievers and the manifestation of their insistence on disbelief despite clear evidence.
Reflecting on the ways of Allah in dealing with the past nations to understand the reasons for their destruction and to take heed from them.
Out of Allah's mercy for His servants, He did not send them a messenger from the angels because they wouldn't be given respite for repentance if a messenger were sent to them.
