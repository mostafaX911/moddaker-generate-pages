(٩) وَلَوۡ جَعَلۡنَٰهُ مَلَكٗا لَّجَعَلۡنَٰهُ رَجُلٗا وَلَلَبَسۡنَا عَلَيۡهِم مَّا يَلۡبِسُونَ

If We had sent an angel to them, We would have made him in the form of a man so that they could listen and learn from him, as they would not be able to learn from an angel in the original form that Allah created him. If We had made him in the form of a man, they would have been confused about him.

(١٠) وَلَقَدِ ٱسۡتُهۡزِئَ بِرُسُلٖ مِّن قَبۡلِكَ فَحَاقَ بِٱلَّذِينَ سَخِرُواْ مِنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

If these people poke fun at you by asking for an angel to be sent down with you, nations in the past also poked fun at their messengers. Then, the punishment, which they used to deny and mock when warned about it, encompassed them.

(١١) قُلۡ سِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ثُمَّ ٱنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

Say, O Messenger, to these people who reject and mock you: Travel throughout Earth and then think about what happened to those who disbelieved in Allah's messengers. Allah's punishment descended upon them after they were in a position of strength and power.

(١٢) قُل لِّمَن مَّا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ قُل لِّلَّهِۚ كَتَبَ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَةَۚ لَيَجۡمَعَنَّكُمۡ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهِۚ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Say to them, O Messenger: To whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and the Earth, and whatever is between them? Say: The entire dominion belongs to Allah. Out of His Grace, He has obligated upon Himself mercy towards His slaves and will, therefore, not rush to punish them. He will gather them together for reckoning and recompense on the Day of Resurrection. There is no doubt about this. Those who have lost themselves due to their disbelief in Allah will not believe and rescue themselves from loss.

(١٣) ۞وَلَهُۥ مَا سَكَنَ فِي ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِۚ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

To Allah alone belongs the dominion of everything, all that rests in the night and the day. He is All-Hearing of His slaves' words and Omniscient of their deeds and will recompense them accordingly.

(١٤) قُلۡ أَغَيۡرَ ٱللَّهِ أَتَّخِذُ وَلِيّٗا فَاطِرِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَهُوَ يُطۡعِمُ وَلَا يُطۡعَمُۗ قُلۡ إِنِّيٓ أُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أَكُونَ أَوَّلَ مَنۡ أَسۡلَمَۖ وَلَا تَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

Say, O Messenger, to the polytheists who worship idols and other things alongside Allah: Does it make sense that I take anyone other than Allah as a helper and guardian from whom I seek help while it is He who created the heavens and the earth without precedent? There was no creator before Him. He is the One who gives provision to whichever of His slaves He wishes, and none of His slaves provide for Him. He does not need His slaves, but His slaves need Him. Say, O Messenger: My Lord (may He be Exalted) has instructed me to be the first one from this nation to submit and be humble. He has prohibited me from being from those who associate others as partners with Him.

(١٥) قُلۡ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ إِنۡ عَصَيۡتُ رَبِّي عَذَابَ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ

Say, O Messenger: If I disobey Allah by committing that which He has made unlawful, such as associating partners with Him and other than it or by abandoning what He has instructed me to do, such as believing [in Him] and other acts of obedience, then I fear that He will torment me with a great torment on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٦) مَّن يُصۡرَفۡ عَنۡهُ يَوۡمَئِذٖ فَقَدۡ رَحِمَهُۥۚ وَذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

Whoever Allah distances from the torment on the Day of Resurrection has attained success through the mercy of Allah. Such salvation from torment is a clear success that no other success comes close to.

(١٧) وَإِن يَمۡسَسۡكَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرّٖ فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهُۥٓ إِلَّا هُوَۖ وَإِن يَمۡسَسۡكَ بِخَيۡرٖ فَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

If any calamity from Allah afflicts you, O son of Adam, no one can remove it from you besides Allah. If any good from Him comes to you, no one can stop Him and withhold His grace. He has power over everything, and nothing can render Him incapable.

(١٨) وَهُوَ ٱلۡقَاهِرُ فَوۡقَ عِبَادِهِۦۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ

He is the Predominant over His slaves and the One who subdues them. He is exalted above them in all regards, and nothing can overpower or defeat Him. Everything is subservient to Him. He is above His slaves in a manner that befits His majesty; may He be Exalted. He is the Wise in His creation, planning, and legislation. He is All-Aware, and nothing is hidden from Him.

Points of Benefit:

Clarification of Allah's Wisdom in sending a messenger of the same kind as the people to whom the messenger is sent, ensuring that the message is delivered in a way that is most effective in terms of understanding and acceptance.
Invitation to reflect on the fact that the repetition of the patterns of disobedience by those who came before may be met with the repetition of the divine patterns of punishment from Allah.
The obligation of fearing disobedience and its consequences.
Afflictions that befall humans have no averting power except Allah, and the good that befalls them has no preventer except Allah. There is no repeller of His favor and no withholder of His blessings.
