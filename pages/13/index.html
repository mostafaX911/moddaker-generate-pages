(٨٤) وَإِذۡ أَخَذۡنَا مِيثَٰقَكُمۡ لَا تَسۡفِكُونَ دِمَآءَكُمۡ وَلَا تُخۡرِجُونَ أَنفُسَكُم مِّن دِيَٰرِكُمۡ ثُمَّ أَقۡرَرۡتُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ تَشۡهَدُونَ

Remember the firm covenant We took from you in the Torah: forbidding you from spilling each other's blood and forcing people to leave their homes. You then admitted that We took that covenant from you while you bore witness to its validity.

(٨٥) ثُمَّ أَنتُمۡ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ تَقۡتُلُونَ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَتُخۡرِجُونَ فَرِيقٗا مِّنكُم مِّن دِيَٰرِهِمۡ تَظَٰهَرُونَ عَلَيۡهِم بِٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡعُدۡوَٰنِ وَإِن يَأۡتُوكُمۡ أُسَٰرَىٰ تُفَٰدُوهُمۡ وَهُوَ مُحَرَّمٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِخۡرَاجُهُمۡۚ أَفَتُؤۡمِنُونَ بِبَعۡضِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَتَكۡفُرُونَ بِبَعۡضٖۚ فَمَا جَزَآءُ مَن يَفۡعَلُ ذَٰلِكَ مِنكُمۡ إِلَّا خِزۡيٞ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَيَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ يُرَدُّونَ إِلَىٰٓ أَشَدِّ ٱلۡعَذَابِۗ وَمَا ٱللَّهُ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Then you broke this covenant: you killed one another, and some of you forced a group among you to leave their homes, assisted by their enemies, oppressively and aggressively. When those among you became prisoners in the hands of enemies, you would strive to pay their ransom and rescue them from their captivity, even though forcing them out of their homes was forbidden to you. How can you believe in parts of the Torah (like the obligation to pay ransom for captives) and reject other parts (prohibitions regarding spilling blood and forcing people out of their homes)? For those among you who do that, only humiliation and disgrace await you in this life and the most severe torment in the Hereafter. Allah is not unaware of what you do. Rather, He is fully aware of it, and He will recompense you accordingly.

(٨٦) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱشۡتَرَوُاْ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ فَلَا يُخَفَّفُ عَنۡهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ وَلَا هُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ

They are the people who exchange the Hereafter for worldly life, preferring that which is temporary to that which is eternal. As a result, their punishment will not be lightened in the Hereafter, and they will have no one to help them on that day.

(٨٧) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَقَفَّيۡنَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ بِٱلرُّسُلِۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَا عِيسَى ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَأَيَّدۡنَٰهُ بِرُوحِ ٱلۡقُدُسِۗ أَفَكُلَّمَا جَآءَكُمۡ رَسُولُۢ بِمَا لَا تَهۡوَىٰٓ أَنفُسُكُمُ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرۡتُمۡ فَفَرِيقٗا كَذَّبۡتُمۡ وَفَرِيقٗا تَقۡتُلُونَ

We gave Moses the Torah and followed him with other messengers who took after him. We gave Jesus, the son of Mary, clear signs of the truth of what he came with, such as bringing the dead back to life and healing the blind and the lepers. We also strengthened him with the angel Gabriel, the Holy Spirit (peace be upon him). Yet why is it, O Israelites, that whenever a messenger from Allah comes to you with something that does not correspond to your whims, you become arrogant towards the truth, looking down on the messengers of Allah, rejecting some amongst them, and killing others?

(٨٨) وَقَالُواْ قُلُوبُنَا غُلۡفُۢۚ بَل لَّعَنَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِكُفۡرِهِمۡ فَقَلِيلٗا مَّا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

The Jews' evidence for not following Muhammad was their saying: Our hearts are covered such that nothing you say reaches them, and they do not understand. However, the matter is not as they claim. Rather, Allah has expelled them from His mercy due to their disbelief, so only a few of them believe in what Allah has sent to the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم.

Points of Benefit:

From the greatest forms of disbelief is to believe in some of what Allah revealed and to disbelieve in some of it because the one who does that has made his own desires his deity.
The great extent of the Jews' stubbornness, following their desires, and manipulating what Allah, the Exalted, revealed.
Allah's grace and mercy toward His creation, as He continued sending messengers to them and sending down scriptures to direct them to guidance.
Allah punishes those who turn away from guidance and oppose His commands by sealing their hearts and expelling them from His mercy so they are not guided to the Truth or act upon it.
