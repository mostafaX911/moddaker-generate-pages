(١٩) قُلۡ أَيُّ شَيۡءٍ أَكۡبَرُ شَهَٰدَةٗۖ قُلِ ٱللَّهُۖ شَهِيدُۢ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكُمۡۚ وَأُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ لِأُنذِرَكُم بِهِۦ وَمَنۢ بَلَغَۚ أَئِنَّكُمۡ لَتَشۡهَدُونَ أَنَّ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ ءَالِهَةً أُخۡرَىٰۚ قُل لَّآ أَشۡهَدُۚ قُلۡ إِنَّمَا هُوَ إِلَٰهٞ وَٰحِدٞ وَإِنَّنِي بَرِيٓءٞ مِّمَّا تُشۡرِكُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to the polytheists who reject you: What is greatest and most honored as a witness to my truthfulness? Say: Allah is the greatest and most honored witness to my truthfulness. He is a witness between you and me. He knows what I have brought to you and how you respond. Allah has revealed this Quran to me to warn you with it and to warn any human or jinn it reaches. You, O polytheists, believe that there are other deities worthy of worship alongside Allah. Say, O Messenger: I do not bear witness to what you have acknowledged because it is false. Allah is the only One worthy of worship: He has no partner. I am free from all that you associate as partners with Him.

(٢٠) ٱلَّذِينَ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ يَعۡرِفُونَهُۥ كَمَا يَعۡرِفُونَ أَبۡنَآءَهُمُۘ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

The Jews who were given the Torah and the Christians who were given the Gospel recognize the Prophet Muhammad completely, just as they recognize their children from other children. They are the ones who have lost themselves by causing themselves to enter The Fire, and they will not believe.

(٢١) وَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّنِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبًا أَوۡ كَذَّبَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

No one does more wrong than someone who associates a partner with Allah, worshipping it alongside Him, or rejects His verses, which He has revealed to His Messenger. Those who do wrong by associating partners with Allah and rejecting His verses will never succeed if they do not repent.

(٢٢) وَيَوۡمَ نَحۡشُرُهُمۡ جَمِيعٗا ثُمَّ نَقُولُ لِلَّذِينَ أَشۡرَكُوٓاْ أَيۡنَ شُرَكَآؤُكُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ كُنتُمۡ تَزۡعُمُونَ

Remember the Day of Resurrection when We will gather them all together, not leaving any of them out. Then, as a way of reprimand, We will say to those who worshipped others alongside Allah: Where are your partners that you falsely claimed to be Allah's partners?

(٢٣) ثُمَّ لَمۡ تَكُن فِتۡنَتُهُمۡ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُواْ وَٱللَّهِ رَبِّنَا مَا كُنَّا مُشۡرِكِينَ

After this test, they will have no excuse except to declare themselves free of what they worshipped by dishonestly saying: By Allah, our Lord, we did not associate partners with You in the worldly life. Instead, we believed in You and were monotheists who worshipped You alone.

(٢٤) ٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَذَبُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡۚ وَضَلَّ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ

Look, O Muhammad, how these people lied about themselves by denying they associated partners with Allah. The partners they set up with Allah in this worldly life have disappeared and deserted them.

(٢٥) وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَسۡتَمِعُ إِلَيۡكَۖ وَجَعَلۡنَا عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ أَكِنَّةً أَن يَفۡقَهُوهُ وَفِيٓ ءَاذَانِهِمۡ وَقۡرٗاۚ وَإِن يَرَوۡاْ كُلَّ ءَايَةٖ لَّا يُؤۡمِنُواْ بِهَاۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءُوكَ يُجَٰدِلُونَكَ يَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّآ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

Some of the polytheists listen attentively to you, O Messenger, when you recite the Quran. However, they do not benefit from listening to it because We have placed covers on their hearts that prevent them from understanding it. We did this due to their stubbornness and rejection. We have made their ears deaf, not able to listen for benefit. Therefore, no matter how many clear indications and glaring evidence they see, they still do not believe in them, such that when they come to you to debate the truth with falsehood, they say: What you have brought is taken from the books of earlier people.

(٢٦) وَهُمۡ يَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنۡهُ وَيَنۡ‍َٔوۡنَ عَنۡهُۖ وَإِن يُهۡلِكُونَ إِلَّآ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَمَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

They prevent people from believing in the Messenger while they keep their distance from him. They do not let others benefit from him just as they do not benefit. They are only destroying themselves by doing this, but they fail to realize that their actions will cause their destruction.

(٢٧) وَلَوۡ تَرَىٰٓ إِذۡ وُقِفُواْ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ فَقَالُواْ يَٰلَيۡتَنَا نُرَدُّ وَلَا نُكَذِّبَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ رَبِّنَا وَنَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

If you, O Messenger, could only see when they will be exposed to the Fire on the Day of Resurrection, you would be amazed at their bad situation. Then, they will say with regret: If only we could be sent back to the world, not reject Allah's signs/verses, and be from those who believed in Allah.

Points of Benefit:

Clarifying the wisdom behind sending the Prophet, peace be upon him, with the Quran, for the purpose of clear communication and eloquence, and most importantly, the call to the Oneness of Allah.
Denying any partner to Allah, exalted be He, and refuting the false claims of the polytheists in this regard.
Clarification of the knowledge of the Jews and Christians about the Prophet, peace be upon him, despite their denial and disbelief.
One of the manifestations of Allah's Justice is that He gathers the worshiper and the worshipped, the follower and the followed, in the open plains of the Day of Judgment so that they bear witness against each other.
Not everyone who listens to the Quran benefits from it; there may be barriers such as a sealed heart, deafness to its impact, or other hindrances.
