(٢٨) بَلۡ بَدَا لَهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يُخۡفُونَ مِن قَبۡلُۖ وَلَوۡ رُدُّواْ لَعَادُواْ لِمَا نُهُواْ عَنۡهُ وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَكَٰذِبُونَ

Their saying that they would believe if sent back is a lie. Instead, the statement they used to keep hidden, which was: "By Allah, we were not polytheists," appeared to them when their limbs testified against them. If they were supposedly sent back to this world, they would return to the disbelief and associating partners with Allah they had been prohibited from. So, they are lying in their promise that they will believe if they are sent back.

(٢٩) وَقَالُوٓاْ إِنۡ هِيَ إِلَّا حَيَاتُنَا ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمَبۡعُوثِينَ

These polytheists say: There is no life except the life we presently have, and we will not be resurrected for the reckoning.

(٣٠) وَلَوۡ تَرَىٰٓ إِذۡ وُقِفُواْ عَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡۚ قَالَ أَلَيۡسَ هَٰذَا بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ قَالُواْ بَلَىٰ وَرَبِّنَاۚ قَالَ فَذُوقُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡفُرُونَ

If you, O Messenger, could only see when those who rejected resurrection will be made to stand before their Lord, you would be amazed at their bad situation when Allah will say to them: Is the resurrection that you used to reject not the truth about which there is no doubt? They will say: We swear by our Lord who created us; it is true, and there is no doubt about it. At this juncture, Allah will then say to them: So, taste the torment which is a result of your disbelief of this Day, which you used to reject in the worldly life.

(٣١) قَدۡ خَسِرَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِلِقَآءِ ٱللَّهِۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَتۡهُمُ ٱلسَّاعَةُ بَغۡتَةٗ قَالُواْ يَٰحَسۡرَتَنَا عَلَىٰ مَا فَرَّطۡنَا فِيهَا وَهُمۡ يَحۡمِلُونَ أَوۡزَارَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ ظُهُورِهِمۡۚ أَلَا سَآءَ مَا يَزِرُونَ

Those who reject the resurrection on the Day of Judgment and consider standing before Allah farfetched will be in utter loss. Until, when the Hour comes upon them unexpectedly without prior knowledge, they will say out of extreme regret: Alas for us and what loss and disappointment, due to our negligence towards Allah by disbelieving in Him and not preparing for the Day of Resurrection. They will carry sins on their backs. How terrible are the sins that they carry?!

(٣٢) وَمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ إِلَّا لَعِبٞ وَلَهۡوٞۖ وَلَلدَّارُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرٞ لِّلَّذِينَ يَتَّقُونَۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

The worldly life you are inclined towards is nothing but play and deception for those who do not do deeds to please Allah. The abode of the Hereafter is better for those who fear Allah by doing what He has ordered, such as believing [in Allah] and obeying Him, and avoiding what He has prohibited, such as associating partners with Allah and disobedience. Do you, O polytheists, not understand this so that you can believe and do righteous deeds?

(٣٣) قَدۡ نَعۡلَمُ إِنَّهُۥ لَيَحۡزُنُكَ ٱلَّذِي يَقُولُونَۖ فَإِنَّهُمۡ لَا يُكَذِّبُونَكَ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ يَجۡحَدُونَ

We know, O Messenger, that their outward rejection grieves you. However, they do not deny you inwardly because they know your truthfulness and honesty. They do wrong by rejecting what you have brought outwardly, while inwardly, they firmly believe in it.

(٣٤) وَلَقَدۡ كُذِّبَتۡ رُسُلٞ مِّن قَبۡلِكَ فَصَبَرُواْ عَلَىٰ مَا كُذِّبُواْ وَأُوذُواْ حَتَّىٰٓ أَتَىٰهُمۡ نَصۡرُنَاۚ وَلَا مُبَدِّلَ لِكَلِمَٰتِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَكَ مِن نَّبَإِيْ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

Do not consider that such rejection only concerns what you have brought. Instead, messengers before you were also rejected and harmed by their people, but they faced this patiently, inviting towards Allah and fighting in Allah's path until His help came. No one can hold back the help of Allah if He has written and promised it to His messengers. O Messenger, information has come to you about previous messengers, their experiences with their people, and how Allah gave them victory over their enemies by destroying them.

(٣٥) وَإِن كَانَ كَبُرَ عَلَيۡكَ إِعۡرَاضُهُمۡ فَإِنِ ٱسۡتَطَعۡتَ أَن تَبۡتَغِيَ نَفَقٗا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَوۡ سُلَّمٗا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَتَأۡتِيَهُم بِ‍َٔايَةٖۚ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَجَمَعَهُمۡ عَلَى ٱلۡهُدَىٰۚ فَلَا تَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِينَ

If their rejection and turning away from the truth you have brought is difficult for you, O Messenger, then see if you can find a tunnel into the ground or a ladder into the sky so that you may bring them proof and evidence in addition to what I have already given you. If Allah willed to direct them to the guidance that you brought, He would have done so. However, He has not willed this because of His far-reaching wisdom. So [O Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم] do not be among those who are ignorant of this and lose yourself in regret over them because they do not believe.

Points of Benefit:

Clarification that even though the polytheists may outwardly deny, deep down, they acknowledge the truthfulness of the Prophet, peace be upon him.
The Prophet's (peace be upon him) consolation and reassurance by informing him that this rejection is not unique to him alone but has been the way of the polytheists in dealing with previous messengers.
