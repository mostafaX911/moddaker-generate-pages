(٣٦) ۞إِنَّمَا يَسۡتَجِيبُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡمَعُونَۘ وَٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ يَبۡعَثُهُمُ ٱللَّهُ ثُمَّ إِلَيۡهِ يُرۡجَعُونَ

Only those who listen and understand the speech will respond to what you have brought. The disbelievers are dead and useless, for their hearts have died. Allah will resurrect the dead on the Day of Resurrection, and then they will be returned to Him to be recompensed accordingly for the deeds they have sent forth.

(٣٧) وَقَالُواْ لَوۡلَا نُزِّلَ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦۚ قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَادِرٌ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يُنَزِّلَ ءَايَةٗ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

The polytheists, delaying the acceptance of belief, say stubbornly: Why has no extraordinary sign been sent down to Muhammad as proof from His Lord about the truth of what he has brought? Say, O Messenger: Allah can send down a sign as they wish, but most of these polytheists who demand that a sign be sent down do not know that signs are only sent down in accordance with Allah's wisdom and not per their demands. If He was to send it down and they still did not believe in it, He would destroy them.

(٣٨) وَمَا مِن دَآبَّةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا طَٰٓئِرٖ يَطِيرُ بِجَنَاحَيۡهِ إِلَّآ أُمَمٌ أَمۡثَالُكُمۚ مَّا فَرَّطۡنَا فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِن شَيۡءٖۚ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يُحۡشَرُونَ

There are no animals on earth or birds in the sky except that they are species like you, O children of Adam, with respect to being created and provided for. We have not left out anything from the Preserved Tablet. The knowledge of all of them is with Allah. Then, they will all be gathered to their Lord on the Day of Resurrection for the final decision, and He will recompense them accordingly.

(٣٩) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا صُمّٞ وَبُكۡمٞ فِي ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِۗ مَن يَشَإِ ٱللَّهُ يُضۡلِلۡهُ وَمَن يَشَأۡ يَجۡعَلۡهُ عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

Those who reject Our verses/signs are like the deaf who cannot hear, the mute who cannot speak. Moreover, they are in total darkness and cannot see. How can such a person be guided? Allah misguides from among the people whomever He wills and guides whomever He wills by placing them on a straight path with no crookedness.

(٤٠) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتَكُمۡ إِنۡ أَتَىٰكُمۡ عَذَابُ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡ أَتَتۡكُمُ ٱلسَّاعَةُ أَغَيۡرَ ٱللَّهِ تَدۡعُونَ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists: Tell me, if Allah's torment or the Hour that you have been promised comes to you, will you call on anyone besides Allah at that time to remove the calamity and difficulty that has descended on you? [You would] if you are truthful in your claim that those whom you worship can bring benefit or prevent harm.

(٤١) بَلۡ إِيَّاهُ تَدۡعُونَ فَيَكۡشِفُ مَا تَدۡعُونَ إِلَيۡهِ إِن شَآءَ وَتَنسَوۡنَ مَا تُشۡرِكُونَ

The truth is that when the Hour comes, you will not call on anyone besides Allah, Who created you. He removes the calamity and takes away harm as He is worthy of that and can do so if He wills. As for what you worshipped as deities, you will leave them because you know that they cannot bring benefit nor cause harm.

(٤٢) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ أُمَمٖ مِّن قَبۡلِكَ فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُم بِٱلۡبَأۡسَآءِ وَٱلضَّرَّآءِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَضَرَّعُونَ

We sent messengers to nations before you, O Messenger, but they rejected these messengers and turned away from what they brought to them. We punished them with difficulties such as poverty and illness so they could submit and humble themselves before their Lord.

(٤٣) فَلَوۡلَآ إِذۡ جَآءَهُم بَأۡسُنَا تَضَرَّعُواْ وَلَٰكِن قَسَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَزَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

If only they humbled themselves when the calamity came to them and begged Us to remove it, We would have shown them mercy, but they did not do that. Instead, their hearts became hard, and they did not reflect and take lessons. The Devil made their disbelief and disobedience attractive, so they continued with those actions.

(٤٤) فَلَمَّا نَسُواْ مَا ذُكِّرُواْ بِهِۦ فَتَحۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَبۡوَٰبَ كُلِّ شَيۡءٍ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا فَرِحُواْ بِمَآ أُوتُوٓاْ أَخَذۡنَٰهُم بَغۡتَةٗ فَإِذَا هُم مُّبۡلِسُونَ

When they ignored the warning of extreme poverty and illness and did not act on Allah's orders, We enticed them further away from the truth by opening the doors of provision to them and giving them wealth after they were poor and good health after they had suffered illness. This continued until they were steeped in their arrogance and enjoyment of life; then, Our punishment came upon them suddenly, and they were overtaken by despair and lost all hope.

Points of Benefit:

Comparing the disbelievers to the dead, as true life lies in the heart's acceptance of the truth and its adherence to the path of guidance.
Among the wisdom of Allah in trials is to send afflictions upon the dissenters, aiming to soften their hearts and bring them back to their Lord.
The presence of blessings and wealth in the hands of those in error does not indicate Allah's love for them; rather, it is a means of testing and a trial for them and others.
