(٤٥) فَقُطِعَ دَابِرُ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْۚ وَٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

The last of the disbelievers was cut off by being wiped out altogether by destroying them while the messengers were helped. All thanks and praise are only for Allah, Lord of the worlds, for destroying His enemies and giving victory to His friends.

(٤٦) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِنۡ أَخَذَ ٱللَّهُ سَمۡعَكُمۡ وَأَبۡصَٰرَكُمۡ وَخَتَمَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِكُم مَّنۡ إِلَٰهٌ غَيۡرُ ٱللَّهِ يَأۡتِيكُم بِهِۗ ٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ نُصَرِّفُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ ثُمَّ هُمۡ يَصۡدِفُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: Tell me, if Allah were to make you deaf by snatching away your hearing, make you blind you by removing your sight, and seal your hearts so that you cannot understand anything, what other being deserving worship is there that will bring all of these back to you? Think, O Messenger, about how We explain the proofs to them in various ways, yet they still turn away from it.

(٤٧) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتَكُمۡ إِنۡ أَتَىٰكُمۡ عَذَابُ ٱللَّهِ بَغۡتَةً أَوۡ جَهۡرَةً هَلۡ يُهۡلَكُ إِلَّا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

O Messenger, tell them: Tell me, if Allah's torment comes to you suddenly or openly before your eyes, no one will be punished except the wrongdoers who disbelieve in Allah and His messengers.

(٤٨) وَمَا نُرۡسِلُ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ إِلَّا مُبَشِّرِينَ وَمُنذِرِينَۖ فَمَنۡ ءَامَنَ وَأَصۡلَحَ فَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

We have sent Our messengers only to give the pleasing news to those who believe and obey [Allah] of eternal bliss that will never end and to warn those who disbelieve and disobey [Allah] of Our severe torment. So, whoever believes in the messengers and sets his deeds right will have no fear for what awaits them in the Hereafter, nor will they grieve and regret over the pleasures of this world that they missed.

(٤٩) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا يَمَسُّهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡسُقُونَ

Those who reject our signs will be punished for abandoning Allah's obedience.

(٥٠) قُل لَّآ أَقُولُ لَكُمۡ عِندِي خَزَآئِنُ ٱللَّهِ وَلَآ أَعۡلَمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبَ وَلَآ أَقُولُ لَكُمۡ إِنِّي مَلَكٌۖ إِنۡ أَتَّبِعُ إِلَّا مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّۚ قُلۡ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ وَٱلۡبَصِيرُۚ أَفَلَا تَتَفَكَّرُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: I do not say to you that I have Allah's treasures of provision with me and that I can do with them as I please. Nor do I not tell you I know the unseen besides Allah's revelation. I also do not say that I am an angel. Instead, I am Allah's Messenger and only follow what is revealed to me. I do not claim that which I do not have. Say to them, O Messenger: Is the disbeliever whose sight is blind to the truth equal to the believer who sees the truth and believes in it? O polytheists, do you not reflect using your intellect about the signs around you?

(٥١) وَأَنذِرۡ بِهِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخَافُونَ أَن يُحۡشَرُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ لَيۡسَ لَهُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ وَلِيّٞ وَلَا شَفِيعٞ لَّعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَّقُونَ

O Messenger, warn with this Quran those who fear they will be gathered before their Lord on the Day of Resurrection that they will have no ally besides Allah to bring them any benefit nor any intercessor to remove harm from them, so they may fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. These are the people who will benefit from the Quran.

(٥٢) وَلَا تَطۡرُدِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلۡغَدَوٰةِ وَٱلۡعَشِيِّ يُرِيدُونَ وَجۡهَهُۥۖ مَا عَلَيۡكَ مِنۡ حِسَابِهِم مِّن شَيۡءٖ وَمَا مِنۡ حِسَابِكَ عَلَيۡهِم مِّن شَيۡءٖ فَتَطۡرُدَهُمۡ فَتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

O Messenger, do not dismiss the poor Muslims who continuously worship Allah sincerely at the beginning and end of the day from your gathering. Do not dismiss them to win favor with the leaders of the polytheists. You are in no way accountable for these poor people. They, too, are in no way accountable for you. If you dismiss them from your gathering, you will be of those who transgress Allah's limits.

Points of Benefit:

The prophets are human, devoid of any attributes of lordship. Their mission is to convey the message, and they have no control over the universe. They do not possess knowledge of the unseen, nor do they have control over provisions or similar matters.
The preacher should pay attention to his followers, especially the weak ones who seek nothing but the truth. He should draw them closer and not distance them to please the disbelievers.
The verse indicates the importance of worship performed at the beginning and end of the day.
