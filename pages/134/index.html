(٥٣) وَكَذَٰلِكَ فَتَنَّا بَعۡضَهُم بِبَعۡضٖ لِّيَقُولُوٓاْ أَهَٰٓؤُلَآءِ مَنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِم مِّنۢ بَيۡنِنَآۗ أَلَيۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَعۡلَمَ بِٱلشَّٰكِرِينَ

So, We tested some by means of others. We made them different in relation to their worldly fortunes [some of them rich and others poor]. We tested them in this way so that the rich disbelievers say about the poor believers: Are these poor people the ones whom Allah has favored with guidance out of all of us? If belief [in Allah] were any good, they would not have beaten us to it because we are the people who come first. Does Allah not know those who show gratitude for His favors such that He guides them to belief, from those who are ungrateful for these favors, so He abandons them to disbelief? Certainly, Allah knows best about them.

(٥٤) وَإِذَا جَآءَكَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا فَقُلۡ سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ كَتَبَ رَبُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَةَ أَنَّهُۥ مَنۡ عَمِلَ مِنكُمۡ سُوٓءَۢا بِجَهَٰلَةٖ ثُمَّ تَابَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ وَأَصۡلَحَ فَأَنَّهُۥ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

O Messenger, when those who believe in our signs that indicate the truth of what you have brought come to you, then respond to their greeting of peace to honor them. Then, give them the good news of Allah's vast mercy because He has obligated mercy on Himself as a favor from Him. So, whoever of you commits a sin out of ignorance and foolishness (and this is the state of anyone who sins intentionally or unintentionally), then repents and sets their deeds right, then Allah will forgive them, for Allah is forgiving and merciful to His slaves who repent.

(٥٥) وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُفَصِّلُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ وَلِتَسۡتَبِينَ سَبِيلُ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

Just as We have explained what was previously mentioned, We present Our proofs and evidence against the people of falsehood and make clear the way and path of the criminals so that it can be avoided and steered clear from.

(٥٦) قُلۡ إِنِّي نُهِيتُ أَنۡ أَعۡبُدَ ٱلَّذِينَ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۚ قُل لَّآ أَتَّبِعُ أَهۡوَآءَكُمۡ قَدۡ ضَلَلۡتُ إِذٗا وَمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ

Say, O Messenger: Allah has prohibited me from worshipping those whom you worship instead of Allah. Say, O Messenger: I do not follow your desires in worshipping other than Allah. If I had followed your vain desires, I would have gone astray from the path of truth and not been guided to it. This is the condition of everyone following their desires without proof from Allah.

(٥٧) قُلۡ إِنِّي عَلَىٰ بَيِّنَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّي وَكَذَّبۡتُم بِهِۦۚ مَا عِندِي مَا تَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَ بِهِۦٓۚ إِنِ ٱلۡحُكۡمُ إِلَّا لِلَّهِۖ يَقُصُّ ٱلۡحَقَّۖ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡفَٰصِلِينَ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: I stand on clear evidence from my Lord, not on vain desires, yet you have rejected this evidence. I do not possess the torment and the extraordinary signs that you demand and are in such haste to bring about. That is only in Allah's hands. The decision for anything, including your requests, is only Allah's. He speaks the truth and decides accordingly. Allah (may He be Exalted) is the best who distinguishes those who are upon truth from those who are upon falsehood.

(٥٨) قُل لَّوۡ أَنَّ عِندِي مَا تَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَ بِهِۦ لَقُضِيَ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Say to them, O Messenger: If I possessed the torment you are in such haste to bring about, I would have brought it down on you. If that happens, the matter between you and me will be settled, and Allah knows best the duration of respite for the wrongdoers and when to punish them.

(٥٩) ۞وَعِندَهُۥ مَفَاتِحُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ لَا يَعۡلَمُهَآ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِۚ وَمَا تَسۡقُطُ مِن وَرَقَةٍ إِلَّا يَعۡلَمُهَا وَلَا حَبَّةٖ فِي ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا رَطۡبٖ وَلَا يَابِسٍ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٖ

The treasures of the unseen are with Allah alone: no one besides Him knows them. He knows everything created on the land, such as animals, plants, and inanimate objects. He knows whatever animals or plants there are in the sea. No leaf falls in any place, nor is there any seed of grain hidden in the earth, nor anything moist or dry, except that it is recorded in a clear book, the Preserved Tablet.

Points of Benefit:

Allah, the Exalted, makes people a trial for one another, and their levels vary in terms of sustenance, faith, and disbelief. Sustenance is not determined by one's faith or disbelief.
Among the etiquettes of a caller to Islam is having a pleasant countenance, offering greetings, showing a friendly demeanor, and expressing joy in the company of others.
It is incumbent upon the caller to Islam to avoid personal inclinations in their beliefs, methodology, and conduct.
Affirmation of Allah's unique knowledge of the unseen, with no partner, and the vastness of His Omniscience in that regard. Nothing escapes Him, and nothing is hidden from Him among His creatures, as everything is firmly recorded in minute detail with Him, the Exalted.
