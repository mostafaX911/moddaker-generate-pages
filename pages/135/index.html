(٦٠) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يَتَوَفَّىٰكُم بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا جَرَحۡتُم بِٱلنَّهَارِ ثُمَّ يَبۡعَثُكُمۡ فِيهِ لِيُقۡضَىٰٓ أَجَلٞ مُّسَمّٗىۖ ثُمَّ إِلَيۡهِ مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ ثُمَّ يُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

Allah is the One who temporarily takes away your souls at night as you sleep, and He is the One who knows the deeds you do during the day when you are active. He then restores you to life in the day after taking away your souls through sleep so that you may carry out your tasks. This will continue until the decreed period of your lives ends, as known to Allah. Then you will return to Him alone when He resurrects you on the Day of Resurrection. He will then inform you about what you used to do in the worldly life and recompense you accordingly.

(٦١) وَهُوَ ٱلۡقَاهِرُ فَوۡقَ عِبَادِهِۦۖ وَيُرۡسِلُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ حَفَظَةً حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَ أَحَدَكُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ تَوَفَّتۡهُ رُسُلُنَا وَهُمۡ لَا يُفَرِّطُونَ

He is the Predominant over His slaves and the One who subdues them. He is exalted above them in all regards. Everything is subservient to Him. He is above His slaves in a manner that befits His majesty; may He be Exalted and Transcendent. O people, He sends down to you honorable angels to record your deeds. This will continue until the person's life ends with the angel of death and his helpers taking his soul. They never fail to do what they are ordered to do.

(٦٢) ثُمَّ رُدُّوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ مَوۡلَىٰهُمُ ٱلۡحَقِّۚ أَلَا لَهُ ٱلۡحُكۡمُ وَهُوَ أَسۡرَعُ ٱلۡحَٰسِبِينَ

Then all of those whose souls were taken away will be returned to Allah, their true Master, so that He may recompense them for their deeds accordingly. To Him belongs the effectual decision and fair judgment between them. He is the swiftest of those who take account of your deeds.

(٦٣) قُلۡ مَن يُنَجِّيكُم مِّن ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ تَدۡعُونَهُۥ تَضَرُّعٗا وَخُفۡيَةٗ لَّئِنۡ أَنجَىٰنَا مِنۡ هَٰذِهِۦ لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلشَّٰكِرِينَ

O Messenger, ask these polytheists: Who rescues and delivers you from these dangerous situations you encounter in the darkness of the land and sea? You supplicate to Him alone, submissively and humbly in secret and public, saying: If our Lord delivers us from these deadly situations, we will certainly be of those who show gratitude for His favors upon us and not worship anyone besides Him.

(٦٤) قُلِ ٱللَّهُ يُنَجِّيكُم مِّنۡهَا وَمِن كُلِّ كَرۡبٖ ثُمَّ أَنتُمۡ تُشۡرِكُونَ

Say to them, O Messenger: Allah is the One who rescues you from it and delivers you from every distress. But, after that, when times are good, you associate partners with Him in worship. What can be more wrong than that which you do?!

(٦٥) قُلۡ هُوَ ٱلۡقَادِرُ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يَبۡعَثَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ عَذَابٗا مِّن فَوۡقِكُمۡ أَوۡ مِن تَحۡتِ أَرۡجُلِكُمۡ أَوۡ يَلۡبِسَكُمۡ شِيَعٗا وَيُذِيقَ بَعۡضَكُم بَأۡسَ بَعۡضٍۗ ٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ نُصَرِّفُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَفۡقَهُونَ

O Messenger, say to them: Allah is able to send upon you a torment that will come from above you, such as stones, thunderbolts, and floods, or that will come from below you, such as earthquakes and sinking into the ground. He can also cause disunity between your hearts, making each person follow their vain desire and making you fight one another. Think, O Messenger, how We diversify the evidence and proofs and explain it to them so that they understand that what you have brought to them is the truth and that what they have is false.

(٦٦) وَكَذَّبَ بِهِۦ قَوۡمُكَ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّۚ قُل لَّسۡتُ عَلَيۡكُم بِوَكِيلٖ

Your people have rejected this Quran, while it is the truth. There is no doubt that it is from Allah. O Messenger, tell them: I have not been appointed to watch over you. I am but a warner to you before a severe torment.

(٦٧) لِّكُلِّ نَبَإٖ مُّسۡتَقَرّٞۚ وَسَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ

Everything has a time in which it occurs and an endpoint which it reaches, including your end. Soon, you will know it when you are resurrected on the Day of Resurrection.

(٦٨) وَإِذَا رَأَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخُوضُونَ فِيٓ ءَايَٰتِنَا فَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنۡهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ يَخُوضُواْ فِي حَدِيثٍ غَيۡرِهِۦۚ وَإِمَّا يُنسِيَنَّكَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ فَلَا تَقۡعُدۡ بَعۡدَ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ مَعَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

When you, O Messenger, see the polytheists speaking about Our verses by mocking and poking fun at them, then distance yourself from them until they enter into a discussion free of such mockery. If the Devil makes you forget, and you sit with them, but you remember afterward, leave that gathering and do not sit with these transgressors.

Points of Benefit:

Affirmation that sleep is a form of death, where the souls are taken and returned upon awakening.
The evidence supporting Allah's entitlement to divinity through the proof of human nature. Indeed, disbelievers believe in Allah and turn to their innate disposition when faced with distress or peril, supplicating to Allah alone.
Obligating polytheists based on their behavior and establishing the evidence that their nature turns towards recognizing Allah when they seek His help alone in times of distress, yet associating partners with Him when He delivers them to safety on land.
The impermissibility of sitting in gatherings of people of falsehood and idle talk, distancing oneself from them, and not returning to them except when they abandon such behavior.
