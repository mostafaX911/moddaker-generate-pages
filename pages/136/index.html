(٦٩) وَمَا عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يَتَّقُونَ مِنۡ حِسَابِهِم مِّن شَيۡءٖ وَلَٰكِن ذِكۡرَىٰ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَّقُونَ

Those who fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions are in no way responsible for the account of these wrongdoers. They only have to prohibit them from the evil they are doing so that they may also fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

(٧٠) وَذَرِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ دِينَهُمۡ لَعِبٗا وَلَهۡوٗا وَغَرَّتۡهُمُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَذَكِّرۡ بِهِۦٓ أَن تُبۡسَلَ نَفۡسُۢ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ لَيۡسَ لَهَا مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَلِيّٞ وَلَا شَفِيعٞ وَإِن تَعۡدِلۡ كُلَّ عَدۡلٖ لَّا يُؤۡخَذۡ مِنۡهَآۗ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُبۡسِلُواْ بِمَا كَسَبُواْۖ لَهُمۡ شَرَابٞ مِّنۡ حَمِيمٖ وَعَذَابٌ أَلِيمُۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡفُرُونَ

Leave, O Messenger, these polytheists who have made their religion a thing of play and distraction and mock and poke fun at it. The life of this world, with its temporary pleasures, has deceived them. Advise people, O Messenger, with this Quran so that no soul is delivered to destruction because of the evil actions they have done. If they so act, they will have no one to help them nor any intercessor to protect them against Allah's torment on the Day of Resurrection. If they have to offer any ransom to be saved from Allah's torment, such will not be accepted. Those are the ones who have been delivered to their destruction because of the sins they committed. On the Day of Resurrection, they will have a scalding drink and a painful torment because of their disbelief.

(٧١) قُلۡ أَنَدۡعُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا يَنفَعُنَا وَلَا يَضُرُّنَا وَنُرَدُّ عَلَىٰٓ أَعۡقَابِنَا بَعۡدَ إِذۡ هَدَىٰنَا ٱللَّهُ كَٱلَّذِي ٱسۡتَهۡوَتۡهُ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ حَيۡرَانَ لَهُۥٓ أَصۡحَٰبٞ يَدۡعُونَهُۥٓ إِلَى ٱلۡهُدَى ٱئۡتِنَاۗ قُلۡ إِنَّ هُدَى ٱللَّهِ هُوَ ٱلۡهُدَىٰۖ وَأُمِرۡنَا لِنُسۡلِمَ لِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: Should we worship idols, instead of Allah, that do not possess the ability to benefit, nor can they harm us? Should we leave belief after Allah has guided us to it? If we do so, we will be similar to the one the devils have misguided, leaving him confused and without guidance. His companions on the straight path call him to the truth, but he refuses to respond to their call. Therefore, O Messenger, say to them: Allah's guidance is the true guidance, and Allah has ordered us to submit to Him (may He be Exalted and Transcendent) by devoutly sticking to singling Him out and worshiping Him alone because He is the Lord of the Worlds.

(٧٢) وَأَنۡ أَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَٱتَّقُوهُۚ وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ

Allah has ordered us to establish the prayer perfectly and to fear Him by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. He, alone, is the One to whom the slaves will be gathered on the Day of Resurrection, and He will recompense them accordingly for their deeds.

(٧٣) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ وَيَوۡمَ يَقُولُ كُن فَيَكُونُۚ قَوۡلُهُ ٱلۡحَقُّۚ وَلَهُ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ يَوۡمَ يُنفَخُ فِي ٱلصُّورِۚ عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ

He (may He be Exalted and Transcendent) is the One who created the heavens and the earth for a purpose. "On the Day that He will say to something: Be, and it will become" means: On the Day of Resurrection, when He says Rise, they will all rise. His word is the truth that will undoubtedly occur. To Him alone (may He be Exalted) belongs the dominion on the Day of Resurrection when the angel Israfil will blow the trumpet a second time. He, Allah, knows the unseen and what can be seen. He is Wise in His creation and planning. He is Aware of the hidden just as He knows the apparent, and nothing is hidden from Him.

Points of Benefit:

A preacher calling to Allah is not responsible for anyone's accountability. Instead, they are responsible for informing and reminding.
Admonition is one of the best ways of awakening the heedless and arrogant.
One of the proofs for monotheism is that the one who cannot benefit, harm, or control anything by necessity does not deserve to be a worshipped deity.
