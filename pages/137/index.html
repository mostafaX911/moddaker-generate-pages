(٧٤) ۞وَإِذۡ قَالَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمُ لِأَبِيهِ ءَازَرَ أَتَتَّخِذُ أَصۡنَامًا ءَالِهَةً إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰكَ وَقَوۡمَكَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ

Remember, O Messenger, when Abraham (peace be upon him) said to his father, Azar, a polytheist: O my dear father, do you make the idols into deities you then worship besides Allah? I think you and your people who worship idols are misguided and confused about the path of truth because you worship others instead of Allah. Allah alone (may He be Exalted) deserves worship, and no deity besides Him is worthy of worship.

(٧٥) وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُرِيٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ مَلَكُوتَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلِيَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُوقِنِينَ

In the same way that We showed him the misguidance of his father and his people, We also showed him the dominion of the heavens and the earth so that he could use that vast dominion as evidence of the Oneness of Allah and that Allah alone deserves to be worshipped and so that he be from those who certainly know that Allah is One without any partner and He is Omnipotent over everything.

(٧٦) فَلَمَّا جَنَّ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلَّيۡلُ رَءَا كَوۡكَبٗاۖ قَالَ هَٰذَا رَبِّيۖ فَلَمَّآ أَفَلَ قَالَ لَآ أُحِبُّ ٱلۡأٓفِلِينَ

So, when the darkness of the night came, he saw a star and said: This is my Lord, but when it disappeared, he said: I do not like that which disappears because the One worthy of worship is always present and never disappears.

(٧٧) فَلَمَّا رَءَا ٱلۡقَمَرَ بَازِغٗا قَالَ هَٰذَا رَبِّيۖ فَلَمَّآ أَفَلَ قَالَ لَئِن لَّمۡ يَهۡدِنِي رَبِّي لَأَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

When he saw the moon appear, he said: This is my Lord. But when it disappeared, he said: If Allah does not guide me to His Oneness and His worship, I will undoubtedly become of those who are distant from the true religion.

(٧٨) فَلَمَّا رَءَا ٱلشَّمۡسَ بَازِغَةٗ قَالَ هَٰذَا رَبِّي هَٰذَآ أَكۡبَرُۖ فَلَمَّآ أَفَلَتۡ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ إِنِّي بَرِيٓءٞ مِّمَّا تُشۡرِكُونَ

When he saw the sun rising, he said: This thing that is rising is my Lord. It is bigger than the star and the moon. But, still, when it disappeared, he said: O my people, I disassociate myself from what you associate as partners with Allah.

When they asked him what he worshiped after disavowing what they worshiped besides Allah, he replied:

(٧٩) إِنِّي وَجَّهۡتُ وَجۡهِيَ لِلَّذِي فَطَرَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ حَنِيفٗاۖ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

I have sincerely devoted my religion to Allah, Who created the heavens and the earth without precedent, inclining away from polytheism towards pure monotheism. I am not one of the polytheists who worship others besides Allah.

(٨٠) وَحَآجَّهُۥ قَوۡمُهُۥۚ قَالَ أَتُحَٰٓجُّوٓنِّي فِي ٱللَّهِ وَقَدۡ هَدَىٰنِۚ وَلَآ أَخَافُ مَا تُشۡرِكُونَ بِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ رَبِّي شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۚ وَسِعَ رَبِّي كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ عِلۡمًاۚ أَفَلَا تَتَذَكَّرُونَ

His people, who were polytheists, argued with him concerning Allah's Oneness (may He be Exalted). They threatened him with their idols. He asked them: Do you argue with me concerning Allah's Oneness and worshipping Him alone when my Lord has guided me to it? I do not fear your idols because they do not possess the ability to harm or benefit me except if Allah wills. Whatever He wills happens. Allah's knowledge encompasses everything; nothing in the earth or heavens is hidden from Him. Are you not mindful, O my people, of your disbelief and associating partners with Allah instead of believing in Him alone?

(٨١) وَكَيۡفَ أَخَافُ مَآ أَشۡرَكۡتُمۡ وَلَا تَخَافُونَ أَنَّكُمۡ أَشۡرَكۡتُم بِٱللَّهِ مَا لَمۡ يُنَزِّلۡ بِهِۦ عَلَيۡكُمۡ سُلۡطَٰنٗاۚ فَأَيُّ ٱلۡفَرِيقَيۡنِ أَحَقُّ بِٱلۡأَمۡنِۖ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

How can I fear the idols you worship besides Allah when you have no fear of associating partners with Allah with things that He created while not having any proof for doing so? Which of the two groups, the monotheists or the polytheists, is more deserving of safety and security? If you know which group is more deserving, then follow that group. The more deserving group is undoubtedly the believers who are monotheists.

Points of Benefit:

The evidence for the Lordship of Allah through contemplating the created beings is a Quranic approach.
Explicit and rational forms of evidence lead to affirming Allah's lordship.
