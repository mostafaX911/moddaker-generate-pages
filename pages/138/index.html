(٨٢) ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَلَمۡ يَلۡبِسُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنَهُم بِظُلۡمٍ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡأَمۡنُ وَهُم مُّهۡتَدُونَ

Those who believe in Allah, follow what He has legislated, and do not mix their belief with polytheism will be the only ones who will be safe and secure. They are guided. Their Lord directed them to the path of guidance.

(٨٣) وَتِلۡكَ حُجَّتُنَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦۚ نَرۡفَعُ دَرَجَٰتٖ مَّن نَّشَآءُۗ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ حَكِيمٌ عَلِيمٞ

The argument, which is the statement of Allah: "Which of the two groups is more deserving of safety and security...?" which Abraham used to defeat his people in debate, is Our argument, which We showed and gave to him to use against his people. We raise in ranks whoever of Our slaves We will in this world and the Hereafter. Your Lord, O Messenger, is Wise in His creation and planning, and He knows all about His slaves.

(٨٤) وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥٓ إِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَۚ كُلًّا هَدَيۡنَاۚ وَنُوحًا هَدَيۡنَا مِن قَبۡلُۖ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِهِۦ دَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيۡمَٰنَ وَأَيُّوبَ وَيُوسُفَ وَمُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

We provided Abraham with his son Isaac and his grandson Jacob (peace be upon them), guiding all of them to the straight path. Before them, We guided Noah to the path of truth, and from the descendants of Noah, We guided David, his son Solomon, Job, Joseph, Moses, and his brother Aaron (peace be upon them). Just as We reward the prophets for doing good, We will similarly reward all those, besides the prophets, who do good.

(٨٥) وَزَكَرِيَّا وَيَحۡيَىٰ وَعِيسَىٰ وَإِلۡيَاسَۖ كُلّٞ مِّنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

Similarly, We guided Zechariah, John, Jesus, the son of Mary, and Elijah (peace be upon them). All these prophets were of the righteous, who Allah chose to be messengers.

(٨٦) وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلَ وَٱلۡيَسَعَ وَيُونُسَ وَلُوطٗاۚ وَكُلّٗا فَضَّلۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Similarly, We guided Ishmael, Elisha, Jonah, and Lot (peace be upon them). We favored all of them, with Muhammad being the best of them, over all the people of their time.

(٨٧) وَمِنۡ ءَابَآئِهِمۡ وَذُرِّيَّٰتِهِمۡ وَإِخۡوَٰنِهِمۡۖ وَٱجۡتَبَيۡنَٰهُمۡ وَهَدَيۡنَٰهُمۡ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

We also guided some of their forefathers, descendants, and brothers, whom We had willed to guide. We selected them and guided them to tread the straight path: the path of Allah's Oneness and obedience.

(٨٨) ذَٰلِكَ هُدَى ٱللَّهِ يَهۡدِي بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦۚ وَلَوۡ أَشۡرَكُواْ لَحَبِطَ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

They received Allah's guidance, which He grants to whoever of His slaves He wills. If they had associated others as partners with Allah, their deeds would be void because associating partners with Allah nullifies righteous deeds.

(٨٩) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحُكۡمَ وَٱلنُّبُوَّةَۚ فَإِن يَكۡفُرۡ بِهَا هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ فَقَدۡ وَكَّلۡنَا بِهَا قَوۡمٗا لَّيۡسُواْ بِهَا بِكَٰفِرِينَ

Those prophets mentioned are the ones to whom We had given Books, wisdom, and prophethood. Suppose your people reject any of these things which We have given them. In that case, We have prepared other people who do not disbelieve but instead believe and hold firm onto it. They are the Muhajireen (Emigrants), the Ansar (Supporters), and all those who follow them in goodness until the Day of Recompense.

(٩٠) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ هَدَى ٱللَّهُۖ فَبِهُدَىٰهُمُ ٱقۡتَدِهۡۗ قُل لَّآ أَسۡ‍َٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ أَجۡرًاۖ إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرَىٰ لِلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Those prophets and their forefathers, descendants, and brothers mentioned are the people of guidance, so follow and imitate them. Say, O Messenger, to your people: I do not want any reward from you for conveying this Quran. The Quran is simply an admonishment for the worlds of human beings and the jinn so that they can seek guidance to the straight and correct path through it.

Points of Benefit:

One of the virtues of monotheism is that it guarantees security for the servant, especially in the Hereafter when people will be in panic.
The verses affirm that all the prophets who preceded only conveyed their message with the help of Allah, not by their own power.
The prophets all share in calling to the oneness of Allah, while there may be differences in the details of their legislations.
Emulating the prophets is a commendable practice, especially in the fundamentals of monotheism.
