(٩١) وَمَا قَدَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ حَقَّ قَدۡرِهِۦٓ إِذۡ قَالُواْ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ بَشَرٖ مِّن شَيۡءٖۗ قُلۡ مَنۡ أَنزَلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ ٱلَّذِي جَآءَ بِهِۦ مُوسَىٰ نُورٗا وَهُدٗى لِّلنَّاسِۖ تَجۡعَلُونَهُۥ قَرَاطِيسَ تُبۡدُونَهَا وَتُخۡفُونَ كَثِيرٗاۖ وَعُلِّمۡتُم مَّا لَمۡ تَعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنتُمۡ وَلَآ ءَابَآؤُكُمۡۖ قُلِ ٱللَّهُۖ ثُمَّ ذَرۡهُمۡ فِي خَوۡضِهِمۡ يَلۡعَبُونَ

The polytheists did not revere Allah as He should be revered when they said to His Messenger, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم: Allah has not sent down any revelation to any human being. Say to them, O Messenger: Who is the One who sent down the Torah to Moses as a light and guidance for his people? The Jews put it into sheets, showing what agrees with their desires and concealing what does not, such as the description of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. You, O Arabs, were taught the Quran, which neither you nor your predecessors knew. Say to them, O Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم: Allah sent it down, then leave them to their ignorance and misguidance until certainty comes to them.

(٩٢) وَهَٰذَا كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ مُبَارَكٞ مُّصَدِّقُ ٱلَّذِي بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ وَلِتُنذِرَ أُمَّ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ وَمَنۡ حَوۡلَهَاۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهِۦۖ وَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمۡ يُحَافِظُونَ

And this Quran is a book that We revealed to you, O Messenger. It is a blessed book that confirms the heavenly books that preceded it so that you could warn the people of Mecca and all the people in the East and West until they are guided. Therefore, those who believe in the Hereafter believe in this Quran, practice it, and guard their prayer by maintaining its pillars, obligatory, and recommended acts during times legislated in sacred law.

(٩٣) وَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّنِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبًا أَوۡ قَالَ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ وَلَمۡ يُوحَ إِلَيۡهِ شَيۡءٞ وَمَن قَالَ سَأُنزِلُ مِثۡلَ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُۗ وَلَوۡ تَرَىٰٓ إِذِ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ فِي غَمَرَٰتِ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ بَاسِطُوٓاْ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ أَخۡرِجُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمُۖ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ تُجۡزَوۡنَ عَذَابَ ٱلۡهُونِ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَقُولُونَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ غَيۡرَ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَكُنتُمۡ عَنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ تَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ

No one does more wrong than the one who makes up a lie against Allah by claiming that Allah has not revealed anything to any human being, or by falsely saying that Allah had sent revelation to him when Allah did not, or by saying that he will reveal the like of the Quran that Allah has revealed. O Messenger, you would see a terrible sight if you could only see these wrongdoers afflicted by the pangs of death while the angels stretch out their hands to punish, beat, and reprimand them, saying: Give up your souls because we are here to take them. Today, you will be recompensed with a torment that will disgrace and humiliate you because of your lies against Allah by claiming prophethood, revelation, or the ability to reveal what Allah has revealed, and because of your arrogance [preventing] you from believing in His signs.

(٩٤) وَلَقَدۡ جِئۡتُمُونَا فُرَٰدَىٰ كَمَا خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖ وَتَرَكۡتُم مَّا خَوَّلۡنَٰكُمۡ وَرَآءَ ظُهُورِكُمۡۖ وَمَا نَرَىٰ مَعَكُمۡ شُفَعَآءَكُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ زَعَمۡتُمۡ أَنَّهُمۡ فِيكُمۡ شُرَكَٰٓؤُاْۚ لَقَد تَّقَطَّعَ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَضَلَّ عَنكُم مَّا كُنتُمۡ تَزۡعُمُونَ

They will be told on the Day of Resurrection: Today, you have come to Us alone. Now, you do not have any wealth or leadership, and you have come to Us just as We had created you in the first place: barefoot, naked, and uncircumcised. You have reluctantly left behind what We had given you in the world. Today, We do not see with you the deities you claimed will intercede for you and that they are Allah's partners in worship. All ties between you have been cut. Your false claim of their intercession and that they are Allah's partners has let you down.

Points of Benefit:

Revealing books to the Prophets is Allah's established practice with the Messengers, and the Prophet (peace be upon him) is one of them.
The greatest falsehood and deceit is when someone lies about Allah, attributing, denying, or affirming something to Allah without valid evidence.
Everyone will be resurrected on the Day of Judgment individually, stripped of positions and titles, in a state of utter neediness, and each person will be held accountable alone.
