(٨٩) وَلَمَّا جَآءَهُمۡ كِتَٰبٞ مِّنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ مُصَدِّقٞ لِّمَا مَعَهُمۡ وَكَانُواْ مِن قَبۡلُ يَسۡتَفۡتِحُونَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُم مَّا عَرَفُواْ كَفَرُواْ بِهِۦۚ فَلَعۡنَةُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

The Quran came to them from Allah, agreeing with what the Torah and the Gospel contain. Before it was revealed, they used to say that they would be helped against the polytheists, who worship others alongside Allah and would have success over them when a prophet was sent since they would have faith in him and follow him. But they disbelieved him when the Quran and Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم came to them, according to the description they recognized and the truth they knew. Allah curses those who disbelieve Him and His Prophet.

(٩٠) بِئۡسَمَا ٱشۡتَرَوۡاْ بِهِۦٓ أَنفُسَهُمۡ أَن يَكۡفُرُواْ بِمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ بَغۡيًا أَن يُنَزِّلَ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ عَلَىٰ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦۖ فَبَآءُو بِغَضَبٍ عَلَىٰ غَضَبٖۚ وَلِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَذَابٞ مُّهِينٞ

Miserable is what they have purchased in exchange for their portion of belief in Allah and His messengers. They disbelieved in what Allah revealed and unjustly and enviously rejected His messengers because He gave prophethood and the Quran to Muhammad. So they deserved Allah's multiplied anger for disbelieving in Muhammad and distorting the Torah beforehand. Those who disbelieve in the prophethood of Muhammad will receive a humiliating torment on the Day of Resurrection.

(٩١) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمۡ ءَامِنُواْ بِمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ قَالُواْ نُؤۡمِنُ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيۡنَا وَيَكۡفُرُونَ بِمَا وَرَآءَهُۥ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا مَعَهُمۡۗ قُلۡ فَلِمَ تَقۡتُلُونَ أَنۢبِيَآءَ ٱللَّهِ مِن قَبۡلُ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

When these Jews were told to believe in the truth and guidance that Allah revealed to His Prophet, they replied: We believe in what had been revealed to our prophets, and they reject anything besides this which was revealed to Muhammad, although this Quran is the truth, confirming what Allah had already revealed to them. Had they truly believed in what had been revealed to them, they would have believed in the Quran. So, ask them then, O Prophet: Why did you kill the prophets of Allah before if you believed in the truth they brought?

(٩٢) ۞وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَكُم مُّوسَىٰ بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ ثُمَّ ٱتَّخَذۡتُمُ ٱلۡعِجۡلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ وَأَنتُمۡ ظَٰلِمُونَ

And your prophet Moses brought clear signs to you, proving his truthfulness. But after that, you made the calf into a deity and worshipped it when Moses had gone to meet his Lord. You were wrongdoers for worshipping others alongside Allah since only He is worthy of worship.

(٩٣) وَإِذۡ أَخَذۡنَا مِيثَٰقَكُمۡ وَرَفَعۡنَا فَوۡقَكُمُ ٱلطُّورَ خُذُواْ مَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰكُم بِقُوَّةٖ وَٱسۡمَعُواْۖ قَالُواْ سَمِعۡنَا وَعَصَيۡنَا وَأُشۡرِبُواْ فِي قُلُوبِهِمُ ٱلۡعِجۡلَ بِكُفۡرِهِمۡۚ قُلۡ بِئۡسَمَا يَأۡمُرُكُم بِهِۦٓ إِيمَٰنُكُمۡ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Remember when We made a binding promise with you to follow Moses and accept what he brought from Allah? We raised the mountain over you to scare you and told you: Take the Torah seriously, which We had sent to you and listen to accept and submit; otherwise, We would make the mountain fall upon you; then you said: We heard with our ears and disobeyed with our actions. The worship of the calf took hold of their hearts because of their disbelief. O Prophet, say: How evil is what this belief instructs you to do: disbelieving in Allah – if you are true believers – because true belief cannot be accompanied by disbelief.

Points of Benefit:

The Jews are the most envious of people, as their envy led them to disbelieve in Allah and reject what was revealed because the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم was not from among them.
True faith in Allah, the Exalted, necessitates belief in every book revealed and every messenger sent.
It is the greatest injustice to turn away from the Truth and its guidance after recognizing it and comprehending the validity of its evidence.
It is the custom of the Jews to break covenants and vows, and this is their practice to this day.
