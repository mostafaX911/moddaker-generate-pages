(٩٥) ۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ فَالِقُ ٱلۡحَبِّ وَٱلنَّوَىٰۖ يُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡحَيَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ وَمُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ مِنَ ٱلۡحَيِّۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُؤۡفَكُونَ

Allah alone is the One who splits open the seed, from which crops emerge, and the date stone, from which the date palm sprouts. He brings forth the living from the dead, just as man and all other animals are formed from sperm. He brings forth the dead from the living, just as sperm comes out of a human being and a chicken lays an egg. The One who does this is Allah, the One Who created you. How are you, polytheists, then misled from the truth despite witnessing His amazing creation?

(٩٦) فَالِقُ ٱلۡإِصۡبَاحِ وَجَعَلَ ٱلَّيۡلَ سَكَنٗا وَٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَ حُسۡبَانٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَقۡدِيرُ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ

He (may He be Exalted and Transcendent) splits the light of dawn from the darkness of the night. He is the One who has made the night still, in which people can rest from the tiring and hard work of the day. He is the One who has made the sun and the moon move according to a precise calculation. That amazing creation is the determination of the Almighty, whom no one can overpower, Omniscient of what is best for His creation.

(٩٧) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلنُّجُومَ لِتَهۡتَدُواْ بِهَا فِي ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِۗ قَدۡ فَصَّلۡنَا ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ

He (may He be Exalted and Transcendent) is the One who created for you, O children of Adam, the stars in the sky, so that you may find your way during your travels when the routes on land and sea are unclear. We have explained the evidence and proofs that indicate Our power for people who reflect and take benefit from them.

(٩٨) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَكُم مِّن نَّفۡسٖ وَٰحِدَةٖ فَمُسۡتَقَرّٞ وَمُسۡتَوۡدَعٞۗ قَدۡ فَصَّلۡنَا ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَفۡقَهُونَ

He (may He be Exalted and Transcendent) is the One who created you from a single soul, the soul of your father, Adam. He began your creation by creating your father, Adam, from clay, and then He created you from him. He also created for you a place to settle in, which is the wombs of your mothers, and a depository in which you are stored, which is the loins of your fathers. We have explained the signs to people who can understand Allah's speech.

(٩٩) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَخۡرَجۡنَا بِهِۦ نَبَاتَ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ فَأَخۡرَجۡنَا مِنۡهُ خَضِرٗا نُّخۡرِجُ مِنۡهُ حَبّٗا مُّتَرَاكِبٗا وَمِنَ ٱلنَّخۡلِ مِن طَلۡعِهَا قِنۡوَانٞ دَانِيَةٞ وَجَنَّٰتٖ مِّنۡ أَعۡنَابٖ وَٱلزَّيۡتُونَ وَٱلرُّمَّانَ مُشۡتَبِهٗا وَغَيۡرَ مُتَشَٰبِهٍۗ ٱنظُرُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ ثَمَرِهِۦٓ إِذَآ أَثۡمَرَ وَيَنۡعِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكُمۡ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

He (may He be Exalted and Transcendent) is the One who sent down rainwater from the sky, and then with it, We caused all types of vegetation to grow. From the vegetation, We produce crops and green trees, from which We produce grain packed one on top of the other, as it is found in spikes. From the spathe of the date palm comes clusters that are close to any person standing or sitting. We also brought forth grape orchards; and produced olives and pomegranates, the leaves of which are similar, but the fruit is different. Look, O people, at the fruit when it first appears and when it ripens. O people, in that there are clear proofs of Allah's power for a people who believe in Allah. Such people are the ones who will benefit from these proofs and evidence.

(١٠٠) وَجَعَلُواْ لِلَّهِ شُرَكَآءَ ٱلۡجِنَّ وَخَلَقَهُمۡۖ وَخَرَقُواْ لَهُۥ بَنِينَ وَبَنَٰتِۢ بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖۚ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥ وَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ

Yet the polytheists made the jinn partners with Allah in worship, believing that jinn could independently bring benefit and cause harm. Allah alone had created them, so He is most worthy of worship. They attributed sons to Allah, as the Jews did concerning Ezra, the Christians with respect to Jesus, and daughters as the polytheists did with respect to the angels. Allah is free and above what the people of falsehood claim about Him.

(١٠١) بَدِيعُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لَهُۥ وَلَدٞ وَلَمۡ تَكُن لَّهُۥ صَٰحِبَةٞۖ وَخَلَقَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٞ

He (may He be Exalted and Transcendent) is the Creator of the heavens and the earth without precedent. How can He have a child when He has no mate? He has created everything and knows everything. Nothing is hidden from Him.

Points of Benefit:

The evidence is drawn from the creation and provision (the creation and growth of plants, their transformation in shape and size, and the descent of rain) and from the evidence of movement (the movement of celestial bodies and the regularity and order in their courses). Both of these observable phenomena affirm the exclusive Lordship of Allah and His deserving of divinity.
Clarifying the misguided and feeble minds of the polytheists in their worship of the jinn.
