(١٠٢) ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمۡۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ خَٰلِقُ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ فَٱعۡبُدُوهُۚ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَكِيلٞ

O people, the One described with these divine attributes is your Lord. You have no lord besides Him, and no one is worthy of worship besides Him. He is the Creator of everything, so worship Him alone, as He is the only One deserving of worship and takes care of everything.

(١٠٣) لَّا تُدۡرِكُهُ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَهُوَ يُدۡرِكُ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَۖ وَهُوَ ٱللَّطِيفُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ

Vision cannot encompass Him, but His Vision, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, covers and encompasses all things. He is the One who is Subtle with His righteous slaves and is Aware of them.

(١٠٤) قَدۡ جَآءَكُم بَصَآئِرُ مِن رَّبِّكُمۡۖ فَمَنۡ أَبۡصَرَ فَلِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَنۡ عَمِيَ فَعَلَيۡهَاۚ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ عَلَيۡكُم بِحَفِيظٖ

Clear evidence and proofs from your Lord have come to you, O people. Whoever attempts to understand them and submits to them, then the benefit will return to them. Whoever is blind to them and does not try to understand and submit to them will only lead themselves to their own loss. It is not my duty to watch over you and record your deeds; I am only a messenger from my Lord. He is the One Who watches over you.

(١٠٥) وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُصَرِّفُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ وَلِيَقُولُواْ دَرَسۡتَ وَلِنُبَيِّنَهُۥ لِقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ

Just as We vary the evidence and proofs of Allah's Power, We also vary the verses concerning promises, threats, and admonition. However, the polytheists will say: This is not revelation; it is merely something you learned from the People of the Book before you. [And We did this] to explain the truth to the believers of the nation of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, as they are the ones who accept the truth and follow it.

(١٠٦) ٱتَّبِعۡ مَآ أُوحِيَ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ وَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنِ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

O Messenger, follow the truth that your Lord revealed to you. There is no one worthy of worship besides Him (may He be Exalted). Do not let the disbelievers and their stubbornness occupy your heart, as their affair is left to Allah.

(١٠٧) وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مَآ أَشۡرَكُواْۗ وَمَا جَعَلۡنَٰكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَفِيظٗاۖ وَمَآ أَنتَ عَلَيۡهِم بِوَكِيلٖ

If Allah had willed them not to associate anyone as a partner with Him, they would not have done so. We have not made you, O Messenger, a guardian to record their deeds, nor are you in charge of them. You are only a messenger, and your duty is only to convey the message.

(١٠٨) وَلَا تَسُبُّواْ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ فَيَسُبُّواْ ٱللَّهَ عَدۡوَۢا بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖۗ كَذَٰلِكَ زَيَّنَّا لِكُلِّ أُمَّةٍ عَمَلَهُمۡ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِم مَّرۡجِعُهُمۡ فَيُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

O believers, do not insult the idols they worship alongside Allah, even though they are insignificant and deserving of that, for then the polytheists would have the audacity to insult Allah out of ignorance of what befits Him (may He be Exalted). Just as He made their misguidance attractive to them, He made every nation's behavior, be it good or bad, seem attractive to them, so they act accordingly. Then their return on the Day of Resurrection is to their Lord, Who will inform them about what they used to do in the world, and He will recompense them accordingly.

(١٠٩) وَأَقۡسَمُواْ بِٱللَّهِ جَهۡدَ أَيۡمَٰنِهِمۡ لَئِن جَآءَتۡهُمۡ ءَايَةٞ لَّيُؤۡمِنُنَّ بِهَاۚ قُلۡ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡأٓيَٰتُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِۖ وَمَا يُشۡعِرُكُمۡ أَنَّهَآ إِذَا جَآءَتۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

The polytheists swear by Allah with their strongest oaths: If Muhammad brings them one of the signs they suggest, they will surely believe in it. Say to them, O Messenger: I do not have signs; they are only with Allah, Who sends them down as He wills. And what will make you realize, O believers, that even if these signs come, as they suggested, they still will not believe? Instead, they will persist in their stubbornness and denial because they do not desire guidance.

(١١٠) وَنُقَلِّبُ أَفۡ‍ِٔدَتَهُمۡ وَأَبۡصَٰرَهُمۡ كَمَا لَمۡ يُؤۡمِنُواْ بِهِۦٓ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖ وَنَذَرُهُمۡ فِي طُغۡيَٰنِهِمۡ يَعۡمَهُونَ

We turn away their hearts and sight by preventing them from being guided to the truth, just as We prevented them from believing in the Quran the first time because of their stubbornness. We will leave them confused and wandering in their misguidance and rebellion against their Lord.

Points of Benefit:

Exonerating Allah from the injustice ingrained in the belief of (al-Jabr) and explaining that the disbelief and polytheism of the servants occur by their own choice.
It is not within the capacity of any prophet, among the prophets, to bring forth a sign at his own discretion or whenever he wishes. Rather, this matter is attributed solely to Allah. He alone is capable of it, and He is the Wise who determines the type of sign and the time of its manifestation.
The prohibition of cursing the polytheists' idols is a warning against a greater evil, which is the grave sin of crossing the limit by insulting the Lord of the Worlds.
Allah, the Exalted, may prevent guidance from reaching a person, directing their sight and heart away from obedience as a punishment for choosing disbelief.
