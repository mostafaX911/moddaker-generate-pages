(١١١) ۞وَلَوۡ أَنَّنَا نَزَّلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةَ وَكَلَّمَهُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَحَشَرۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖ قُبُلٗا مَّا كَانُواْ لِيُؤۡمِنُوٓاْ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ يَجۡهَلُونَ

If we had responded to them by bringing what they requested and sent down the angels for them to see, made the dead speak to them and inform them of your truthfulness regarding what you brought, and gathered everything they asked for before their eyes, they would not believe in what you brought, except those whom Allah wished to guide. However, most are ignorant of this and do not turn to Allah for guidance.

(١١٢) وَكَذَٰلِكَ جَعَلۡنَا لِكُلِّ نَبِيٍّ عَدُوّٗا شَيَٰطِينَ ٱلۡإِنسِ وَٱلۡجِنِّ يُوحِي بَعۡضُهُمۡ إِلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ زُخۡرُفَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ غُرُورٗاۚ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ رَبُّكَ مَا فَعَلُوهُۖ فَذَرۡهُمۡ وَمَا يَفۡتَرُونَ

And just as we tested you with the hatred of these polytheists towards you, we tried every Prophet that came before you. So we made enemies for each one of them, from the transgressors from amongst humanity and Jinn alike, each of them whispering to the other. Thus, they beautify the falsehood to deceive them. And if Allah had willed them not to do that, they wouldn't have. But Allah willed that for them as a test. So leave them and that which they fabricate of disbelief and falsehood, and do not toil over them.

(١١٣) وَلِتَصۡغَىٰٓ إِلَيۡهِ أَفۡ‍ِٔدَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَلِيَرۡضَوۡهُ وَلِيَقۡتَرِفُواْ مَا هُم مُّقۡتَرِفُونَ

And so that the hearts of those who do not accept the Hereafter may incline towards what they whisper to one another, happily accepting such things for themselves, and persist in committing the sins and wrongs they were doing.

(١١٤) أَفَغَيۡرَ ٱللَّهِ أَبۡتَغِي حَكَمٗا وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ إِلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مُفَصَّلٗاۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ يَعۡلَمُونَ أَنَّهُۥ مُنَزَّلٞ مِّن رَّبِّكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ فَلَا تَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمُمۡتَرِينَ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists who worship others with Allah: Does it make sense for me to accept anyone other than Allah as a judge between us? For Allah is the One who has revealed the Quran, which sufficiently explains everything. The Jews, to whom I gave the Torah, and the Christians, to whom I gave the Gospel, know that the Quran has been revealed to you because the proof is found in their scriptures. So do not be of those who doubt what I revealed to you.

(١١٥) وَتَمَّتۡ كَلِمَتُ رَبِّكَ صِدۡقٗا وَعَدۡلٗاۚ لَّا مُبَدِّلَ لِكَلِمَٰتِهِۦۚ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

The Quran is the pinnacle of truthfulness in its statements and reports and the pinnacle of justice in its rulings. No one can alter His words. He is All-Hearing of His slaves' words and Omniscient; nothing is hidden from him, and He will recompense those who strive to change His words accordingly.

(١١٦) وَإِن تُطِعۡ أَكۡثَرَ مَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يُضِلُّوكَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِن يَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا ٱلظَّنَّ وَإِنۡ هُمۡ إِلَّا يَخۡرُصُونَ

If you, O Messenger, were to follow most people on earth, they would mislead you from Allah's religion. Allah's way has always been that the truth is with the minority. Most people follow nothing but speculation that has no basis, and they think that those they worship will bring them closer to Allah, whereas they are utterly wrong in believing this.

(١١٧) إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ مَن يَضِلُّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦۖ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ

Your Lord, O Messenger, knows best those people who are misled from His path, and He knows best those who are rightly guided to it. Nothing of that is hidden from Him.

(١١٨) فَكُلُواْ مِمَّا ذُكِرَ ٱسۡمُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡهِ إِن كُنتُم بِ‍َٔايَٰتِهِۦ مُؤۡمِنِينَ

Eat, O people, from that which was slaughtered in Allah's name if you truly accept His clear proofs.

Points of Benefit:

The supreme goal for a servant should be to follow the truth, seeking it through the ways elucidated by Allah, acting upon it, seeking the assistance of his Lord in following it, and not relying on his own self, abilities, or strength.
The Quran, in its fairness, attributes ignorance and misguidance to the majority of creation while acknowledging the knowledgeable and faithful minority.
It is His example in creation that enemies from among humans and jinn emerge against the prophets and their followers because the truth is known by its opposite, falsehood.
The Quran is truthful in its narrations and just in its rulings. There is no contradiction in its accounts of reality nor its judgments of the truth.
