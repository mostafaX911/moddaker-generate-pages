(١٢٥) فَمَن يُرِدِ ٱللَّهُ أَن يَهۡدِيَهُۥ يَشۡرَحۡ صَدۡرَهُۥ لِلۡإِسۡلَٰمِۖ وَمَن يُرِدۡ أَن يُضِلَّهُۥ يَجۡعَلۡ صَدۡرَهُۥ ضَيِّقًا حَرَجٗا كَأَنَّمَا يَصَّعَّدُ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَجۡعَلُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلرِّجۡسَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Whomever Allah wishes to guide to the right path, He opens their heart to Islam so they accept it willingly. Whomever He wishes to abandon and not guide, He makes their chest extremely narrow and unable to accept the truth, such that they become like a person climbing into the higher layers of the atmosphere while finding it increasingly difficult to breathe. Just as Allah made this extreme narrowness the condition of the person who goes astray, He will punish those who do not have faith in Him.

(١٢٦) وَهَٰذَا صِرَٰطُ رَبِّكَ مُسۡتَقِيمٗاۗ قَدۡ فَصَّلۡنَا ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَذَّكَّرُونَ

This religion that I have given you, O Messenger, is My straight path, in which there is no crookedness. I have made the verses and signs clear for the one who understands them.

(١٢٧) ۞لَهُمۡ دَارُ ٱلسَّلَٰمِ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡۖ وَهُوَ وَلِيُّهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

Such people will be awarded Paradise [on the Day of Judgment], where they will be safe from harm and discomfort. Allah will be their Friend, Protector, Supporter, and Guardian. This will be their reward for the good actions they used to do.

(١٢٨) وَيَوۡمَ يَحۡشُرُهُمۡ جَمِيعٗا يَٰمَعۡشَرَ ٱلۡجِنِّ قَدِ ٱسۡتَكۡثَرۡتُم مِّنَ ٱلۡإِنسِۖ وَقَالَ أَوۡلِيَآؤُهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡإِنسِ رَبَّنَا ٱسۡتَمۡتَعَ بَعۡضُنَا بِبَعۡضٖ وَبَلَغۡنَآ أَجَلَنَا ٱلَّذِيٓ أَجَّلۡتَ لَنَاۚ قَالَ ٱلنَّارُ مَثۡوَىٰكُمۡ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ إِلَّا مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُۗ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ حَكِيمٌ عَلِيمٞ

Remember, O Messenger, the day when Allah will gather both the jinns and humanity. He will address the jinns and tell them they had misled many people and prevented them from Allah's path. Their followers from amongst humanity will say in response to their Lord: O our Lord, we benefited from one another: the jinns benefited by having human beings follow them, and the human beings benefited by using the jinns to obtain their desires. And now we have reached the term You have appointed for us: the Day of Resurrection. Allah will tell them that the Fire will be their home, where they will live eternally, except for those sinful monotheists whom Allah wills to remove from it. Your Lord, O Messenger, is Wise in His planning and handling of matters; He knows which of His slaves deserve the punishment.

(١٢٩) وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُوَلِّي بَعۡضَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ بَعۡضَۢا بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ

Just as I gave some of the rebellious jinns the ability to mislead some people and we allowed them to influence some of the people to misguide them, I will give authority and power to every evil sinner so that they may influence other sinners, prompting them to do evil and turn them away from good. This is a just recompense for the sins they used to commit.

(١٣٠) يَٰمَعۡشَرَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِ أَلَمۡ يَأۡتِكُمۡ رُسُلٞ مِّنكُمۡ يَقُصُّونَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِي وَيُنذِرُونَكُمۡ لِقَآءَ يَوۡمِكُمۡ هَٰذَاۚ قَالُواْ شَهِدۡنَا عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِنَاۖ وَغَرَّتۡهُمُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَشَهِدُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ أَنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ كَٰفِرِينَ

On the Day of Resurrection, I will address the jinns and humanity and ask them if messengers from amongst them had not come to them, reciting what Allah had revealed to them and warning them that they will meet this day, the Day of Resurrection. They will say: Of course they did. Today, we confess against ourselves that Your messengers did communicate Your message to us and told us about the meeting of this day, but we had rejected Your messengers and denied the meeting of this day. The life of the world, with its fleeting beauty, decorations, and luxuries, had deceived them, and they will bear witness against themselves that they disbelieved in Allah and His messengers in the worldly life. Yet this confession and acceptance will not be of any use to them, as the time for its usefulness would have already passed.

Points of Benefit:

It is Allah's established practice in misguidance and guidance that they both come from Him, meaning through His creation and origination, and they result from the actions of the servant by his own choice after the will of Allah.
Allah's guardianship over the believers corresponds to their righteous deeds. The more righteous deeds they engage in, the greater His guardianship over them becomes, and vice versa.
It is from Allah's established practice that every oppressor is made subject to another oppressor, driving them towards evil, encouraging and enticing them in wrongdoing, and deterring them from goodness.
