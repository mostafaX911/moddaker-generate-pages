(١٣١) ذَٰلِكَ أَن لَّمۡ يَكُن رَّبُّكَ مُهۡلِكَ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ بِظُلۡمٖ وَأَهۡلُهَا غَٰفِلُونَ

The reason for sending messengers to humanity and the jinns is so that no one is punished for their crimes without a messenger being sent to warn them and the invitation reaching them. I only punished a nation after sending messengers to them.

(١٣٢) وَلِكُلّٖ دَرَجَٰتٞ مِّمَّا عَمِلُواْۚ وَمَا رَبُّكَ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا يَعۡمَلُونَ

Each one of them will have ranks in accordance with their actions. The person who did a great deal of evil will not be equal to the one who did less. The followers will not be equal to those who were followed. Similarly, the reward for those who do good will be different. Your Lord is not unaware of what they used to do. Instead, He is well aware of that. Nothing of it is hidden from Him, and He will requite them for their actions accordingly.

(١٣٣) وَرَبُّكَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ذُو ٱلرَّحۡمَةِۚ إِن يَشَأۡ يُذۡهِبۡكُمۡ وَيَسۡتَخۡلِفۡ مِنۢ بَعۡدِكُم مَّا يَشَآءُ كَمَآ أَنشَأَكُم مِّن ذُرِّيَّةِ قَوۡمٍ ءَاخَرِينَ

Your Lord, O Messenger, is Self-sufficient and is not in need of His slaves. He does not need them or their worship of Him. Their disbelief in Him does not harm Him. On the contrary, despite His not needing them, He is Merciful towards them. If He wished to destroy you, O disobedient slaves, He would destroy you with His punishment, and then after this, He would bring others who would have faith in Him and obey Him, just as He created you from the progeny of other people who had come before you.

(١٣٤) إِنَّ مَا تُوعَدُونَ لَأٓتٖۖ وَمَآ أَنتُم بِمُعۡجِزِينَ

O disbelievers, the resurrection, revival, reckoning, and punishment you are promised will most certainly happen. You cannot escape from your Lord by running away: He will seize and punish you.

(١٣٥) قُلۡ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡمَلُواْ عَلَىٰ مَكَانَتِكُمۡ إِنِّي عَامِلٞۖ فَسَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ مَن تَكُونُ لَهُۥ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلدَّارِۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

Say, O Messenger: O my people, remain on your path of disbelief and misguidance if you choose, as I have fulfilled my obligation having established the evidence against you by communicating the message to you. I am, therefore, not concerned about your disbelief and misguidance. Instead, I will remain firm on the path of truth I follow. Soon, you will come to know who will be victorious in this world and inherit the Earth and who will receive the home of the Afterlife. The polytheists will not succeed either in this world or the Afterlife. Their end result will be a loss even though they may have some enjoyment in the world.

(١٣٦) وَجَعَلُواْ لِلَّهِ مِمَّا ذَرَأَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَرۡثِ وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ نَصِيبٗا فَقَالُواْ هَٰذَا لِلَّهِ بِزَعۡمِهِمۡ وَهَٰذَا لِشُرَكَآئِنَاۖ فَمَا كَانَ لِشُرَكَآئِهِمۡ فَلَا يَصِلُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَمَا كَانَ لِلَّهِ فَهُوَ يَصِلُ إِلَىٰ شُرَكَآئِهِمۡۗ سَآءَ مَا يَحۡكُمُونَ

The polytheists innovated and made false claims in their religion concerning Allah by assigning a share of the crops and livestock that He created to Him and giving the other share to their idols. Yet the share they give to their partners does not reach those avenues which Allah requires them to be spent in, such as on the poor and needy. But what they gave to Allah reaches the idols and is spent on them. How evil their judgment and distribution are!

(١٣٧) وَكَذَٰلِكَ زَيَّنَ لِكَثِيرٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ قَتۡلَ أَوۡلَٰدِهِمۡ شُرَكَآؤُهُمۡ لِيُرۡدُوهُمۡ وَلِيَلۡبِسُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ دِينَهُمۡۖ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مَا فَعَلُوهُۖ فَذَرۡهُمۡ وَمَا يَفۡتَرُونَ

Just as the Devil made this unfair judgment fair-seeming to the polytheists, the devils – who were their partners and associates – made it seem good to them that they should kill their children out of the fear of poverty. The devils inspired them with this to destroy them by committing the act of murder, which Allah had made unlawful, and to make their religion confusing for them such that they did not know anymore what Allah had commanded or prohibited for them. If Allah had willed them not to do this, they would not have done so. However, He willed it out of His wisdom. So leave, O Messenger, these polytheists and their fabrications about Allah because that will not harm you, and entrust their matter to Allah.

Points of Benefit:

Variations in the ranks of creation exist based on their acts of obedience and disobedience, resulting in differences in their levels of punishment and reward.
Following Satan causes a person's natural disposition to the point of endorsing evil, such as the killing of children and equating their idols with Allah the Exalted and Transcendent.
