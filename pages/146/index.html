(١٣٨) وَقَالُواْ هَٰذِهِۦٓ أَنۡعَٰمٞ وَحَرۡثٌ حِجۡرٞ لَّا يَطۡعَمُهَآ إِلَّا مَن نَّشَآءُ بِزَعۡمِهِمۡ وَأَنۡعَٰمٌ حُرِّمَتۡ ظُهُورُهَا وَأَنۡعَٰمٞ لَّا يَذۡكُرُونَ ٱسۡمَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡهَا ٱفۡتِرَآءً عَلَيۡهِۚ سَيَجۡزِيهِم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ

The polytheists said that certain animals and crops were prohibited, and only certain people may eat of them, such as those who worship the idols and others, according to their flawed thinking and fabrication. They also said that the backs of certain animals were forbidden and that no one could ride or carry a load on those animals. Then they claimed that the name of Allah must not be mentioned over certain animals at the time of slaughter and the names of idols must be taken instead. They falsely attributed all of this to Allah. Allah will requite them with His punishment because of what they made up about Him.

(١٣٩) وَقَالُواْ مَا فِي بُطُونِ هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ خَالِصَةٞ لِّذُكُورِنَا وَمُحَرَّمٌ عَلَىٰٓ أَزۡوَٰجِنَاۖ وَإِن يَكُن مَّيۡتَةٗ فَهُمۡ فِيهِ شُرَكَآءُۚ سَيَجۡزِيهِمۡ وَصۡفَهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ حَكِيمٌ عَلِيمٞ

They said: The fetus in the wombs of these reserved animals, if born alive, is permitted for our males and forbidden to our women. But, if it is stillborn, the males and females will share in it. Therefore, Allah will justly requite them for this statement. He is Wise in His sacred laws and in handling the affairs of His creation, and He knows full well about them.

(١٤٠) قَدۡ خَسِرَ ٱلَّذِينَ قَتَلُوٓاْ أَوۡلَٰدَهُمۡ سَفَهَۢا بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖ وَحَرَّمُواْ مَا رَزَقَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱفۡتِرَآءً عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ قَدۡ ضَلُّواْ وَمَا كَانُواْ مُهۡتَدِينَ

Those who killed their children due to their foolishness and ignorance and made unlawful the animals that Allah provided for them, falsely attributing this to Allah, are ruined. This is because they have strayed from the straight path and are not guided to it.

(١٤١) ۞وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَ جَنَّٰتٖ مَّعۡرُوشَٰتٖ وَغَيۡرَ مَعۡرُوشَٰتٖ وَٱلنَّخۡلَ وَٱلزَّرۡعَ مُخۡتَلِفًا أُكُلُهُۥ وَٱلزَّيۡتُونَ وَٱلرُّمَّانَ مُتَشَٰبِهٗا وَغَيۡرَ مُتَشَٰبِهٖۚ كُلُواْ مِن ثَمَرِهِۦٓ إِذَآ أَثۡمَرَ وَءَاتُواْ حَقَّهُۥ يَوۡمَ حَصَادِهِۦۖ وَلَا تُسۡرِفُوٓاْۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ

Allah (may He be Exalted) is the One who created gardens raised above the ground through stems and trellises and spread out gardens on the surface of the Earth without any stems. He is the One Who made the date palm. He produced crops that differed in their shape and taste. He is the One Who created the olive and the pomegranate, the leaves of which are similar, but their fruit is different. Eat, O people, from its fruit when it bears fruit and pay its Zakat on the day of its harvest. Do not overstep the limits of the sacred law concerning eating and spending because Allah does not love those who exceed His limits in this and other respects. Instead, He hates such people. The One Who created all of this is the One Who has made it lawful for His slaves. Therefore, the polytheists have no right to declare it unlawful.

(١٤٢) وَمِنَ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ حَمُولَةٗ وَفَرۡشٗاۚ كُلُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ خُطُوَٰتِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمۡ عَدُوّٞ مُّبِينٞ

He is the One who produced some animals for you that are suitable for carrying loads (such as large camels) and other animals not suitable for that purpose (such as small camels, goats, and sheep). Eat, O people, of the permissible things that Allah has provided for you, and do not follow the footsteps of the Devil by considering as lawful what Allah has forbidden and making unlawful what Allah has permitted, as the polytheists do. O people, the Devil is your clear enemy as He wants you to oppose Allah by doing so [by making lawful that which is unlawful and vice verse].

Points of Benefit:

Allah disapproves of the polytheists for seven qualities: loss, foolishness, lack of knowledge, prohibition of what Allah has provided for them, false attribution to Allah, misguidance, and lack of guidance. Each of these is a complete reason for disapproval.
Desires are a cause for prohibiting what Allah has made lawful and permitting what Allah has forbidden.
Obligation of Zakat on crops and fruits upon harvesting, with permission to consume them before paying their Zakat, and it is not counted as part of the Zakat.
Enjoying lawful and good things while avoiding extravagance and exceeding limits in eating and spending.
