(١٤٣) ثَمَٰنِيَةَ أَزۡوَٰجٖۖ مِّنَ ٱلضَّأۡنِ ٱثۡنَيۡنِ وَمِنَ ٱلۡمَعۡزِ ٱثۡنَيۡنِۗ قُلۡ ءَآلذَّكَرَيۡنِ حَرَّمَ أَمِ ٱلۡأُنثَيَيۡنِ أَمَّا ٱشۡتَمَلَتۡ عَلَيۡهِ أَرۡحَامُ ٱلۡأُنثَيَيۡنِۖ نَبِّ‍ُٔونِي بِعِلۡمٍ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

He created eight types for you: two types of sheep, male and female, and two types of goats. Say, O Messenger, to the polytheists: Did Allah make the two males unlawful because of their being male? If they say yes, ask them: Why do you regard the females as unlawful? Or did He make the two females unlawful because of their being female? If they say yes, ask them: Why do you regard the males as unlawful? Or did He make what the wombs of the two females contain unlawful because of being contained in the womb? If they say Yes, ask them: Why do you differentiate between what the wombs contain by making the male unlawful sometimes and making the female unlawful at other times? Tell me, O polytheists, what proper knowledge do you base this on if you are truthful in your claim that the prohibition for this is from Allah?

(١٤٤) وَمِنَ ٱلۡإِبِلِ ٱثۡنَيۡنِ وَمِنَ ٱلۡبَقَرِ ٱثۡنَيۡنِۗ قُلۡ ءَآلذَّكَرَيۡنِ حَرَّمَ أَمِ ٱلۡأُنثَيَيۡنِ أَمَّا ٱشۡتَمَلَتۡ عَلَيۡهِ أَرۡحَامُ ٱلۡأُنثَيَيۡنِۖ أَمۡ كُنتُمۡ شُهَدَآءَ إِذۡ وَصَّىٰكُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِهَٰذَاۚ فَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّنِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبٗا لِّيُضِلَّ ٱلنَّاسَ بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٍۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

The remainder of the eight types are two types of camels, male and female, and two types of cattle, male and female. Say, O Messenger, to the polytheists: Did Allah make any of them unlawful due to their being male or female or due to being contained in the womb? Were you, O polytheists, present when Allah gave the instruction for the prohibition of the animals that you regard as unlawful, according to your claim? There is no greater transgression and offense than a person who makes up lies about Allah by attributing to Him the prohibition of that which He has not prohibited to mislead people from the straight path without any basis. Allah does not guide those who do wrong by making up lies about Allah to the right path.

(١٤٥) قُل لَّآ أَجِدُ فِي مَآ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ مُحَرَّمًا عَلَىٰ طَاعِمٖ يَطۡعَمُهُۥٓ إِلَّآ أَن يَكُونَ مَيۡتَةً أَوۡ دَمٗا مَّسۡفُوحًا أَوۡ لَحۡمَ خِنزِيرٖ فَإِنَّهُۥ رِجۡسٌ أَوۡ فِسۡقًا أُهِلَّ لِغَيۡرِ ٱللَّهِ بِهِۦۚ فَمَنِ ٱضۡطُرَّ غَيۡرَ بَاغٖ وَلَا عَادٖ فَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Say, O Messenger: I do not find in what Allah has revealed to me anything forbidden except that which dies without being properly slaughtered, blood that is flowing, the meat of a pig – for that is impure and unlawful – or that which is slaughtered in the name of other than Allah (such as animals slaughtered in the name of idols). Whoever is forced by necessity to eat these forbidden things due to extreme hunger, without seeking enjoyment by eating them and without exceeding the limit of need and necessity, there will be no sin on him in that. Your Lord, O Messenger, is Forgiving towards the person who is compelled to eat from that and is Compassionate towards such a person.

When Allah mentions what He has forbidden upon this nation, He mentions what He forbade for the Jews. This was to demonstrate that the polytheists forbidding livestock was not based on anything that came from Allah, but rather what Satan dictated to them, stating:

(١٤٦) وَعَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ هَادُواْ حَرَّمۡنَا كُلَّ ذِي ظُفُرٖۖ وَمِنَ ٱلۡبَقَرِ وَٱلۡغَنَمِ حَرَّمۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ شُحُومَهُمَآ إِلَّا مَا حَمَلَتۡ ظُهُورُهُمَآ أَوِ ٱلۡحَوَايَآ أَوۡ مَا ٱخۡتَلَطَ بِعَظۡمٖۚ ذَٰلِكَ جَزَيۡنَٰهُم بِبَغۡيِهِمۡۖ وَإِنَّا لَصَٰدِقُونَ

We made unlawful for the Jews animals whose toes were not separated (such as camels and ostriches). We also made the fat of cattle and sheep unlawful, except for what was attached to their backs or on their intestines or what was mixed with the bone (such as the rump or flank). We recompensed them for their disobedience by making these things unlawful for them. We are truthful in all that we relate.

Points of Benefit:

In the verses, there is evidence for the affirmation of debate in matters of knowledge and the affirmation of relying on observation and analogy.
Revelation and what is derived from it are the means to know what is permissible (halal) and what is forbidden (haram).
It is unjust for someone to issue religious verdicts (fatwas) unless they are certain that what they are stating is correct and pleasing to Allah.
As an act of mercy towards His servants, Allah permits them to consume forbidden things when facing necessity or hardship.
