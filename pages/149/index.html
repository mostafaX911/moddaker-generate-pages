(١٥٢) وَلَا تَقۡرَبُواْ مَالَ ٱلۡيَتِيمِ إِلَّا بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ حَتَّىٰ يَبۡلُغَ أَشُدَّهُۥۚ وَأَوۡفُواْ ٱلۡكَيۡلَ وَٱلۡمِيزَانَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۖ لَا نُكَلِّفُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۖ وَإِذَا قُلۡتُمۡ فَٱعۡدِلُواْ وَلَوۡ كَانَ ذَا قُرۡبَىٰۖ وَبِعَهۡدِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡفُواْۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ وَصَّىٰكُم بِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ

He has prohibited you from dealing with the wealth of orphans – those who lose their father before maturity – until they become mature and are considered to be sensible unless you do so in a manner that brings benefit and an increase in that wealth. He has also prohibited you from cheating in weights and measures; instead, you must be fair and just when taking or giving anything in a transaction. He does not burden a soul more than it can bear, and you will not be taken to task for any increase or decrease in the measure that is unintentional. He has, likewise, prohibited you from saying that which is false when relating an incident or giving testimony, displaying unfair preference to a relative or friend. He has, too, prohibited you from breaking Allah's pledge. If you make a pledge with Allah or in Allah's name, then such pacts must be fulfilled. Allah has instructed you with the above in the hope that you would ponder over the outcomes of your actions.

(١٥٣) وَأَنَّ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطِي مُسۡتَقِيمٗا فَٱتَّبِعُوهُۖ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ ٱلسُّبُلَ فَتَفَرَّقَ بِكُمۡ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ وَصَّىٰكُم بِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ

He has prohibited you from following the paths of misguidance. You are required to follow Allah's straight path, in which there is no crookedness. The path of misguidance will lead you far away from the path of truth. Allah instructs you to follow the straight path in the hope that you will become mindful of Him by complying with His commands and avoiding what He has prohibited.

(١٥٤) ثُمَّ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ تَمَامًا عَلَى ٱلَّذِيٓ أَحۡسَنَ وَتَفۡصِيلٗا لِّكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٗ لَّعَلَّهُم بِلِقَآءِ رَبِّهِمۡ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

After all this, We relate to you that We gave the Torah to Moses as a completion of our favor and a reward for his good actions, to clarify that which is required in the religion, and as a mercy in the hope that they would have faith in the meeting with their Lord on the Day of Resurrection and prepare for it with good actions.

(١٥٥) وَهَٰذَا كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ مُبَارَكٞ فَٱتَّبِعُوهُ وَٱتَّقُواْ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ

This Quran is a book that We have revealed, and it is full of blessings due to the religious and worldly benefits contained within. So follow what is revealed in it, and be mindful of going against it in the hope that you will be shown mercy.

(١٥٦) أَن تَقُولُوٓاْ إِنَّمَآ أُنزِلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ عَلَىٰ طَآئِفَتَيۡنِ مِن قَبۡلِنَا وَإِن كُنَّا عَن دِرَاسَتِهِمۡ لَغَٰفِلِينَ

This Book was revealed so that you, polytheists, do not say: Allah only revealed the Torah and the Gospel to the Jews and the Christians before us, and He did not reveal any scripture to us. I do not know how to read their scriptures as they are in their language, not ours.

(١٥٧) أَوۡ تَقُولُواْ لَوۡ أَنَّآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيۡنَا ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ لَكُنَّآ أَهۡدَىٰ مِنۡهُمۡۚ فَقَدۡ جَآءَكُم بَيِّنَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٞۚ فَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّن كَذَّبَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَصَدَفَ عَنۡهَاۗ سَنَجۡزِي ٱلَّذِينَ يَصۡدِفُونَ عَنۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا سُوٓءَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَصۡدِفُونَ

Likewise, I sent this Book so that you do not say: If Allah had revealed scripture to us, just as He did to the Jews and Christians, we would have been more upright than them. So now Allah has revealed the Book to your Prophet, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, in your language: a clear proof, guidance, and mercy for the entire nation. So then, do not offer false excuses and give invalid reasons for rejecting it. Undoubtedly, the greatest wrongdoer is the person who denies Allah's revealed verses and turns away from them. Allah will punish those who turn away from His words with severe punishment by causing them to enter the Fire as recompense for turning away and ignoring them.

Points of Benefit:

It is not permissible to handle the wealth of an orphan except within the bounds of their best interests, and their property should only be handed over to them after reaching the age of maturity.
There are numerous paths of misguidance, and only the path of Allah leads to salvation from punishment.
Following this book with knowledge and action is among the greatest means to attain the mercy of Allah.
