(٩٤) قُلۡ إِن كَانَتۡ لَكُمُ ٱلدَّارُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ خَالِصَةٗ مِّن دُونِ ٱلنَّاسِ فَتَمَنَّوُاْ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

O Prophet, say: O Jews, if Paradise in the Hereafter is just for you, and no other people will enter it, then wish for death, and ask for it, so that you reach this place quickly and can rest from the burdens and worries of the life of this world if you are truthful about what you claim.

(٩٥) وَلَن يَتَمَنَّوۡهُ أَبَدَۢا بِمَا قَدَّمَتۡ أَيۡدِيهِمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

They will never wish for death because of what they have done, such as disbelieving in Allah, rejecting His Messengers, and distorting His Books. Allah is Omniscient of the wrongdoers among them and others and will recompense them for their deeds accordingly.

(٩٦) وَلَتَجِدَنَّهُمۡ أَحۡرَصَ ٱلنَّاسِ عَلَىٰ حَيَوٰةٖ وَمِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَشۡرَكُواْۚ يَوَدُّ أَحَدُهُمۡ لَوۡ يُعَمَّرُ أَلۡفَ سَنَةٖ وَمَا هُوَ بِمُزَحۡزِحِهِۦ مِنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ أَن يُعَمَّرَۗ وَٱللَّهُ بَصِيرُۢ بِمَا يَعۡمَلُونَ

You will find, O Prophet, that the Jews are the most greedy for life despite its lowliness and insignificance. They are more greedy for it than the polytheists, who do not believe in the resurrection and the reckoning. Despite being People of the Book and believing in the resurrection and the reckoning, they would love to live a thousand years. However long a life they might be given would not distance them from Allah's torment. Allah is aware of all that they do. Nothing is hidden from Him, and He will recompense them for it accordingly.

(٩٧) قُلۡ مَن كَانَ عَدُوّٗا لِّـجِبۡرِيلَ فَإِنَّهُۥ نَزَّلَهُۥ عَلَىٰ قَلۡبِكَ بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ وَهُدٗى وَبُشۡرَىٰ لِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

O Muhammad, tell those of the Jews who claim that Gabriel is our enemy among the angels: Whoever is an enemy to Gabriel, then it is he who brought down the Quran to your (Muhammad's) heart, with Allah's permission, affirming the sacred Books that came before it, such as the Torah and the Gospel, guiding towards virtue, and bringing glad tidings to the believers of the bliss Allah has prepared for them. Thus, whoever is an enemy to an angel of such a description and duty is misguided.

(٩٨) مَن كَانَ عَدُوّٗا لِّلَّهِ وَمَلَٰٓئِكَتِهِۦ وَرُسُلِهِۦ وَجِبۡرِيلَ وَمِيكَىٰلَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَدُوّٞ لِّلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Whoever is an enemy to Allah, His angels, His messengers, and His two angels, Gabriel and Michael, then indeed Allah is an enemy to the disbelievers from among you and other than you. And whoever has Allah as an enemy, then he faces evident loss.

(٩٩) وَلَقَدۡ أَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ءَايَٰتِۢ بَيِّنَٰتٖۖ وَمَا يَكۡفُرُ بِهَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ

We have revealed to you, O Prophet, clear signs proving the truthfulness of your prophethood and revelation. Despite its clarity, the only people who reject these signs are those who have abandoned Allah's religion.

(١٠٠) أَوَ كُلَّمَا عَٰهَدُواْ عَهۡدٗا نَّبَذَهُۥ فَرِيقٞ مِّنۡهُمۚ بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Due to the corrupt state of the Jews, whenever they took a covenant upon themselves, including the duty to believe in what the Torah revealed about the prophethood of Muhammad, they would always break any commitment they made. Instead, most of them do not believe in what Allah revealed because true belief leads to fulfilling commitments and promises.

(١٠١) وَلَمَّا جَآءَهُمۡ رَسُولٞ مِّنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ مُصَدِّقٞ لِّمَا مَعَهُمۡ نَبَذَ فَرِيقٞ مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ كِتَٰبَ ٱللَّهِ وَرَآءَ ظُهُورِهِمۡ كَأَنَّهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

When Muhammad came to them as a prophet from Allah, and he fitted the Torah's description of him, a group of them turned away from their Book that showed this, throwing it behind their backs, unmindful of it, not benefitting from its truth or guidance, like an ignoramus.

Points of Benefit:

The true believer hopes for eternal bliss with Allah, so he rejoices at meeting Allah and does not fear death.
The Jews' eagerness for the worldly life, even though it is a despicable and undignified life.
Whoever is hostile to Allah's close allies is indeed hostile to Allah, the Exalted.
The Jews' turning away from the prophethood of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم and recognizing his affirmation of the books in their hands.
Whoever does not benefit from his knowledge is worthy of being described as ignorant because he resembles the ignorant person in his ignorance.
