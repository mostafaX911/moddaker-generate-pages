(١٥٨) هَلۡ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّآ أَن تَأۡتِيَهُمُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ أَوۡ يَأۡتِيَ رَبُّكَ أَوۡ يَأۡتِيَ بَعۡضُ ءَايَٰتِ رَبِّكَۗ يَوۡمَ يَأۡتِي بَعۡضُ ءَايَٰتِ رَبِّكَ لَا يَنفَعُ نَفۡسًا إِيمَٰنُهَا لَمۡ تَكُنۡ ءَامَنَتۡ مِن قَبۡلُ أَوۡ كَسَبَتۡ فِيٓ إِيمَٰنِهَا خَيۡرٗاۗ قُلِ ٱنتَظِرُوٓاْ إِنَّا مُنتَظِرُونَ

O Messenger, those who deny the truth are not waiting for anything but for the angel of death and his assistants to come to them to take away their souls in this world or for their Lord to come in the Hereafter on the Day of Separation, the day when decisions regarding reward and punishment, in order to make the final decision about them; or for some of your Lord's signs to come that indicate the approach of the Final Hour. On the day when some of your Lord's signs come – such as the rising of the sun in the West – the faith of a disbeliever will not be of any benefit to him, nor will the good action of a believer who did not do good previously be of benefit to him. Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists who deny this: Wait for one of these things; I am also waiting.

(١٥٩) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ فَرَّقُواْ دِينَهُمۡ وَكَانُواْ شِيَعٗا لَّسۡتَ مِنۡهُمۡ فِي شَيۡءٍۚ إِنَّمَآ أَمۡرُهُمۡ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ يُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡعَلُونَ

O Messenger, you have nothing to do with those Jews and Christians who divided their religion by taking a part of it and leaving out another part and dividing themselves into various sects. You are free from the misguidance that they adopt. Your duty is only to warn them. Their matter is entrusted to Allah, and then, on the Day of Judgment, He will tell them about what they used to do in the worldly life and requite them for that.

(١٦٠) مَن جَآءَ بِٱلۡحَسَنَةِ فَلَهُۥ عَشۡرُ أَمۡثَالِهَاۖ وَمَن جَآءَ بِٱلسَّيِّئَةِ فَلَا يُجۡزَىٰٓ إِلَّا مِثۡلَهَا وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ

Whoever of the believers brings a good action on the Day of Resurrection, Allah will multiply it for them ten times. Whoever brings an evil action will only be punished with its equivalent amount, and not more than that. On the Day of Resurrection, they will not be wronged by a reduction in the reward of good actions, nor by an increase in the punishment for evil actions.

(١٦١) قُلۡ إِنَّنِي هَدَىٰنِي رَبِّيٓ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ دِينٗا قِيَمٗا مِّلَّةَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ حَنِيفٗاۚ وَمَا كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists who are in denial: My Lord has guided me to a straight path: the path of the religion that maintains the interests of this world and the Afterlife. It is the faith of Abraham, who was inclined to the truth and who was never one of those who associated others with Allah.

(١٦٢) قُلۡ إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحۡيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Say, O Messenger: My prayer and sacrifice is for Allah and in His name, not for anyone else. My life and death are only for Allah, the Lord of all creation; no one else has any share in that from me.

(١٦٣) لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُۥۖ وَبِذَٰلِكَ أُمِرۡتُ وَأَنَا۠ أَوَّلُ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ

He (may He be Exalted) has no partner. There is no one else worthy of worship besides Him. Therefore, Allah has instructed me to accept this pure monotheism, and I am the first of those who acknowledge Him from amongst this nation.

(١٦٤) قُلۡ أَغَيۡرَ ٱللَّهِ أَبۡغِي رَبّٗا وَهُوَ رَبُّ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖۚ وَلَا تَكۡسِبُ كُلُّ نَفۡسٍ إِلَّا عَلَيۡهَاۚ وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةٞ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰۚ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُم مَّرۡجِعُكُمۡ فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ فِيهِ تَخۡتَلِفُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: Shall I search for someone other than Allah as a Lord when He (may He be Exalted) is the Lord of everything? He is the Lord of those things that you worship besides Him. Therefore, no innocent person will bear the sin of another. Your return will be to your Lord alone on the Day of Resurrection, and He will inform you about religious matters that you used to differ about when you were in the life of this world.

(١٦٥) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَكُمۡ خَلَٰٓئِفَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَرَفَعَ بَعۡضَكُمۡ فَوۡقَ بَعۡضٖ دَرَجَٰتٖ لِّيَبۡلُوَكُمۡ فِي مَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۗ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ وَإِنَّهُۥ لَغَفُورٞ رَّحِيمُۢ

Allah is the One who made you the successors of those who came before you on Earth so that you inhabit it after them. He has raised some of you in rank over others – in terms of physical form, livelihood, and other aspects – to test you through what He has given you. Your Lord, O Messenger, is quick in delivering justice, so everything that is approaching is close. He is also Forgiving and Compassionate towards those of His slaves who repent to Him.

Points of Benefit:

The religion commands unity and collaboration while discouraging division and discord.
In complete justice and benevolence, Allah recompenses an evil deed with its equivalent and a good deed with ten times its like, which is the least in terms of multiplication.
The true and upright religion requires harnessing all the deeds and concerns of the servant for Allah alone. The servant directs to Him alone his prayers, acts of worship, rituals, sacrifices, and all his nearness offerings and deeds during his life and what he has bequeathed after his death.
