The Heights (Surah Al-A'raf)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Triumph of truth in its struggle against falsehood, and mentioning consequences for the arrogant in this world and the Hereafter.

Explanation:

(١) الٓمٓصٓ

Alif Lām Mīm Sād – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) كِتَٰبٌ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ فَلَا يَكُن فِي صَدۡرِكَ حَرَجٞ مِّنۡهُ لِتُنذِرَ بِهِۦ وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

The noble Quran is a scripture revealed by Allah to you, O Prophet, so do not let there be any distress or doubt in your heart regarding it. He revealed it to you to warn people by it, for you to establish the proof by it, and to remind those who have faith through it because it is they who benefit from being reminded.

(٣) ٱتَّبِعُواْ مَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكُم مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَۗ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ

Follow, O people, the Scripture that your Lord has revealed to you and the traditions of your Prophet. Do not follow the desires of those whom you think are your friends and protectors amongst the devils or evil scholars, turning to them instead and leaving what was revealed to you because of your desires. How little do you remember! If you had remembered, you would not have preferred falsehood over the truth, and you would have followed what your Messenger came with, acting in obedience to it and leaving anything besides it.

(٤) وَكَم مِّن قَرۡيَةٍ أَهۡلَكۡنَٰهَا فَجَآءَهَا بَأۡسُنَا بَيَٰتًا أَوۡ هُمۡ قَآئِلُونَ

How many a city has Allah destroyed through His punishment because they persisted in their disbelief and their misguidance? Allah sent His punishment down on them in their obliviousness at night or during the day: they could not do anything to avoid the punishment, and their false gods could do nothing to protect them from it.

(٥) فَمَا كَانَ دَعۡوَىٰهُمۡ إِذۡ جَآءَهُم بَأۡسُنَآ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا كُنَّا ظَٰلِمِينَ

All they could do when the punishment came down to them was admit that they had done wrong by disbelieving in Allah.

(٦) فَلَنَسۡ‍َٔلَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أُرۡسِلَ إِلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَنَسۡ‍َٔلَنَّ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

Allah will undoubtedly question the communities He sent messengers to on the Day of Resurrection about how they received the messengers. Also, He will undoubtedly ask the messengers whether they delivered what they were instructed to deliver and how their communities responded to them.

(٧) فَلَنَقُصَّنَّ عَلَيۡهِم بِعِلۡمٖۖ وَمَا كُنَّا غَآئِبِينَ

Allah will inform everyone about what they did in the life of this world through His knowledge because He was always aware of whatever they did. Nothing is hidden from Him, and He was never, at any point, absent from them.

(٨) وَٱلۡوَزۡنُ يَوۡمَئِذٍ ٱلۡحَقُّۚ فَمَن ثَقُلَتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ

The weighing on the Day of Resurrection of what they did will be with justice, with no one being wronged in the least. Those whose good actions weigh more than their bad actions have succeeded in reaching the goal and being saved from what they feared.

(٩) وَمَنۡ خَفَّتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا يَظۡلِمُونَ

Those whose bad actions weigh more than good ones are those who have lost themselves, attaining their destruction on the Day of Resurrection and being excluded from eternal bliss because they rejected Allah's signs and verses.

(١٠) وَلَقَدۡ مَكَّنَّٰكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَجَعَلۡنَا لَكُمۡ فِيهَا مَعَٰيِشَۗ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَشۡكُرُونَ

Allah certainly established you, O children of Adam, on the earth and gave you the means of making a living. You should be thankful to Allah for that, yet you show little gratitude.

(١١) وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ ثُمَّ صَوَّرۡنَٰكُمۡ ثُمَّ قُلۡنَا لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ ٱسۡجُدُواْ لِأٓدَمَ فَسَجَدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ لَمۡ يَكُن مِّنَ ٱلسَّٰجِدِينَ

O people, Allah created your father, Adam, making him in the best form and design. Then He commanded the angels to prostrate to him, honoring him, so they did so; all of them prostrated, except for Iblis (Satan), who refused to prostrate out of arrogance and stubbornness.

Points of Benefit:

One of the purposes of revealing the Quran is to warn the disbelievers and those who resist and to remind the believers.
Allah revealed the Quran to the believers to follow and act upon. If they do so, their upbringing is perfected, blessings are completed upon them, and they are guided to the best of deeds and morals.
On the Day of Judgment, the weight of the slaves' deeds will be determined by absolute justice, with no oppression or injustice whatsoever.
Allah has prepared the Earth for the benefit of humans, enabling them to build upon it, cultivate it, and extract resources from its depths for their benefit.
