(١٢) قَالَ مَا مَنَعَكَ أَلَّا تَسۡجُدَ إِذۡ أَمَرۡتُكَۖ قَالَ أَنَا۠ خَيۡرٞ مِّنۡهُ خَلَقۡتَنِي مِن نَّارٖ وَخَلَقۡتَهُۥ مِن طِينٖ

Allah asked Satan, rebuking him: What stopped you from following My command to prostrate to Adam? Satan replied: What prevented me is that I am better than Adam as You created me from fire and Adam from clay, and fire is better than clay.

(١٣) قَالَ فَٱهۡبِطۡ مِنۡهَا فَمَا يَكُونُ لَكَ أَن تَتَكَبَّرَ فِيهَا فَٱخۡرُجۡ إِنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰغِرِينَ

Allah told Satan: Go down from Paradise because it is not appropriate for you to be arrogant in it, as it is a place for those who are good and pure-hearted. You are, O Satan, disgraced and humiliated, even though you consider yourself superior to Adam.

(١٤) قَالَ أَنظِرۡنِيٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ

Satan said to Allah: O my Lord, give me relief until the Day of Resurrection so that I can tempt and misguide whomsoever I wish from the children of Adam.

(١٥) قَالَ إِنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُنظَرِينَ

Allah told Satan: You, O Satan, are given respite till the first blast of the trumpet when all created beings will die, and the Creator alone will remain.

(١٦) قَالَ فَبِمَآ أَغۡوَيۡتَنِي لَأَقۡعُدَنَّ لَهُمۡ صِرَٰطَكَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمَ

Satan said: Because You have made me fall into error and misguidance as I did not follow Your command to prostrate to Adam, I will surely sit in wait for the children of Adam who will be upon Your straight path, to turn them away and mislead them from it, just as I was misled and misguided due to me not prostrating to Adam.

(١٧) ثُمَّ لَأٓتِيَنَّهُم مِّنۢ بَيۡنِ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِمۡ وَعَنۡ أَيۡمَٰنِهِمۡ وَعَن شَمَآئِلِهِمۡۖ وَلَا تَجِدُ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ شَٰكِرِينَ

Then, I will surely come to them from every direction, causing them to neglect the Afterlife and desire the life of this world, sowing in them doubts and making their low desires seem beautiful to them. And You will not find, O my Lord, most of them thankful to You because of the denial and ingratitude I will lead them to.

(١٨) قَالَ ٱخۡرُجۡ مِنۡهَا مَذۡءُومٗا مَّدۡحُورٗاۖ لَّمَن تَبِعَكَ مِنۡهُمۡ لَأَمۡلَأَنَّ جَهَنَّمَ مِنكُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

Allah said: Go down from Paradise, O Satan, humiliated and removed from My mercy. I will certainly fill Hell with you and all those who follow and obey you and go against My command.

(١٩) وَيَٰٓـَٔادَمُ ٱسۡكُنۡ أَنتَ وَزَوۡجُكَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ فَكُلَا مِنۡ حَيۡثُ شِئۡتُمَا وَلَا تَقۡرَبَا هَٰذِهِ ٱلشَّجَرَةَ فَتَكُونَا مِنَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Then Allah told Adam: O Adam, go and dwell you and your wife Eve in Paradise, and eat from the good things in it, but do not approach and eat from that tree, for if you both eat from that tree after I have prohibited it, you would be of those who transgress My limits.

(٢٠) فَوَسۡوَسَ لَهُمَا ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ لِيُبۡدِيَ لَهُمَا مَا وُۥرِيَ عَنۡهُمَا مِن سَوۡءَٰتِهِمَا وَقَالَ مَا نَهَىٰكُمَا رَبُّكُمَا عَنۡ هَٰذِهِ ٱلشَّجَرَةِ إِلَّآ أَن تَكُونَا مَلَكَيۡنِ أَوۡ تَكُونَا مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰلِدِينَ

Satan said to them a word in secret in order to expose that which had been covered from their Awrah (the private areas) and said: Allah has only forbidden you from eating from that tree because He does not want you to become angels or to live eternally in Paradise.

(٢١) وَقَاسَمَهُمَآ إِنِّي لَكُمَا لَمِنَ ٱلنَّٰصِحِينَ

Satan swore to them, saying: By Allah, I am to you both, O Adam and Eve, a sincere advisor in what I direct you to.

(٢٢) فَدَلَّىٰهُمَا بِغُرُورٖۚ فَلَمَّا ذَاقَا ٱلشَّجَرَةَ بَدَتۡ لَهُمَا سَوۡءَٰتُهُمَا وَطَفِقَا يَخۡصِفَانِ عَلَيۡهِمَا مِن وَرَقِ ٱلۡجَنَّةِۖ وَنَادَىٰهُمَا رَبُّهُمَآ أَلَمۡ أَنۡهَكُمَا عَن تِلۡكُمَا ٱلشَّجَرَةِ وَأَقُل لَّكُمَآ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ لَكُمَا عَدُوّٞ مُّبِينٞ

So, the Devil brought them down from their high position through trickery and deception. When they ate from the tree they had been forbidden to eat from, their private parts became exposed and visible to them. So they started fixing leaves from Paradise onto themselves to cover up. Their Lord called to them: Did I not forbid you both from eating from this tree, and did I not warn you that the Devil is your clear enemy?

Points of Benefit:

The verses indicate that whoever disobeys his Lord is indeed humiliated.
Satan declared his enmity towards the children of Adam and vowed to divert them from the straight path using various means and methods.
The danger of disobedience and its role as a cause for both worldly and hereafter punishments from Allah.
