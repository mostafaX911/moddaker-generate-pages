(٢٣) قَالَا رَبَّنَا ظَلَمۡنَآ أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمۡ تَغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَتَرۡحَمۡنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ

Adam and Eve said: O our Lord! We have wronged ourselves by doing what You forbade us from. Surely, if You do not forgive us and have mercy on us, we will be amongst the losers, losing our share of the worldly life and the Hereafter.

(٢٤) قَالَ ٱهۡبِطُواْ بَعۡضُكُمۡ لِبَعۡضٍ عَدُوّٞۖ وَلَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مُسۡتَقَرّٞ وَمَتَٰعٌ إِلَىٰ حِينٖ

Allah told Adam, Eve, and Satan: Go down from Paradise to the Earth, where some of you will be enemies of others, and where you would have a place to settle, enjoying what is on the Earth until an appointed time.

(٢٥) قَالَ فِيهَا تَحۡيَوۡنَ وَفِيهَا تَمُوتُونَ وَمِنۡهَا تُخۡرَجُونَ

Allah addressed Adam and Eve and their descendants: On this Earth, you will live for the length of time that I decree for you, and on this Earth, you will die. After being buried, you will be taken out from your graves and brought back to life.

(٢٦) يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ قَدۡ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡكُمۡ لِبَاسٗا يُوَٰرِي سَوۡءَٰتِكُمۡ وَرِيشٗاۖ وَلِبَاسُ ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرٞۚ ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَذَّكَّرُونَ

O children of Adam, Allah made for you clothing necessary for covering your state of undress, and clothing for you to adorn yourselves with, and the clothing of Taqwa (Being mindful of Allah and fearing him), which is through following what Allah instructs and staying away from what He has not allowed, which is better than the physical types of clothing. Therefore, what is mentioned here about clothing is from among the signs of Allah, showing His power over everything so that people may remember His blessings to them and be thankful for them.

(٢٧) يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ لَا يَفۡتِنَنَّكُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ كَمَآ أَخۡرَجَ أَبَوَيۡكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ يَنزِعُ عَنۡهُمَا لِبَاسَهُمَا لِيُرِيَهُمَا سَوۡءَٰتِهِمَآۚ إِنَّهُۥ يَرَىٰكُمۡ هُوَ وَقَبِيلُهُۥ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا تَرَوۡنَهُمۡۗ إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَا ٱلشَّيَٰطِينَ أَوۡلِيَآءَ لِلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

O children of Adam, do not let the Devil deceive you by making disobedience look beautiful to you, making you leave covering your state of undress with clothing, or leave the clothing of Taqwa (Being mindful of Allah and fearing him). Do not be deceived as he tricked your parents, making eating from the tree seem good to them, and the result of that was that he got them expelled from Paradise, and their nakedness became clear to them. The Devil and his tribe watch and see you, while you cannot see or watch them, so always be on your guard against him and his tribe. Allah made the devils friends and helpers of those who do not have faith in Allah. As for those who have faith and do good, the devils have no way against them.

(٢٨) وَإِذَا فَعَلُواْ فَٰحِشَةٗ قَالُواْ وَجَدۡنَا عَلَيۡهَآ ءَابَآءَنَا وَٱللَّهُ أَمَرَنَا بِهَاۗ قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَأۡمُرُ بِٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِۖ أَتَقُولُونَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

If the polytheists commit an indecent act, they defend it, saying they found their fathers doing it and Allah commanded them to do it. Tell them, O Muhammad: Indeed Allah does not command that which is wrong; rather, He forbids it, so how can you claim such a thing about Him? Or are you, O polytheists, inventing things about Allah and saying about Him that which you do not know?

(٢٩) قُلۡ أَمَرَ رَبِّي بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۖ وَأَقِيمُواْ وُجُوهَكُمۡ عِندَ كُلِّ مَسۡجِدٖ وَٱدۡعُوهُ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَۚ كَمَا بَدَأَكُمۡ تَعُودُونَ

Say O Muhammad: Indeed, my Lord commands justice, not indecency or sin. And He commands loyalty to Him in worship in general, and particularly in places of worship, that you call on Him alone, sincere in your dedication to Him. As He created you from nothing the first time, He will bring you back to life a second time. He Who is able to create you in the first place is able to bring you back to life.

(٣٠) فَرِيقًا هَدَىٰ وَفَرِيقًا حَقَّ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلضَّلَٰلَةُۚ إِنَّهُمُ ٱتَّخَذُواْ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينَ أَوۡلِيَآءَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَحۡسَبُونَ أَنَّهُم مُّهۡتَدُونَ

Allah made people into two groups: one group of them He guided, making it easy for them to find guidance and removing the barriers to it; and another He made go astray from the path of truth because they made the devils their friends and helpers instead of Allah, ignorantly becoming bound to them, considering themselves to be correctly guided to a straight path.

Points of Benefit:

Whoever resembles Adam by acknowledging their sins, seeking forgiveness, expressing remorse, and distancing themselves from sin, Allah chooses and guides them. On the other hand, whoever resembles Iblis, persisting in sin with obstinacy and defiance, gains nothing from Allah but distance.
Clothing is of two types: outward, covering the nakedness, and inward, which is the garment of piety, enduring with the servant, beautifying the heart and soul.
Many of the helpers of Satan call for the removal of outward clothing, exposing nakedness, making it easier for people to commit reprehensible acts and indulge in obscenities.
Guidance is by the grace and favor of Allah, while misguidance is a consequence of the servant turning away, in ignorance and wrongdoing, towards Satan, causing their own misguidance.
