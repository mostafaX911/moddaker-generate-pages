(٣١) ۞يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ خُذُواْ زِينَتَكُمۡ عِندَ كُلِّ مَسۡجِدٖ وَكُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ وَلَا تُسۡرِفُوٓاْۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ

O children of Adam, wear clean, pure, beautiful clothes that cover your nakedness when you pray and when you circumambulate around the Kaaba. Eat and drink whatever you like from the good things that Allah has allowed. But do not be extravagant in this, nor go beyond what is allowed. Allah does not love those who go beyond the limits.

(٣٢) قُلۡ مَنۡ حَرَّمَ زِينَةَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَخۡرَجَ لِعِبَادِهِۦ وَٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ مِنَ ٱلرِّزۡقِۚ قُلۡ هِيَ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا خَالِصَةٗ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۗ كَذَٰلِكَ نُفَصِّلُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to the polytheists who forbid what Allah has allowed of clothing and food: Who has forbidden you from wearing clothes as adornment, and who has forbidden you from eating the good things I have provided you to eat and drink? Say to them, O Messenger: These good things to eat, drink, and wear are for the believers in this world. While others may share in them in this world, they are exclusively for the believers on the Day of Resurrection when the disbelievers will not have a share in them because they have been forbidden Paradise. Allah makes His signs and verses clear in this way for people who reflect because it is they who benefit from them.

(٣٣) قُلۡ إِنَّمَا حَرَّمَ رَبِّيَ ٱلۡفَوَٰحِشَ مَا ظَهَرَ مِنۡهَا وَمَا بَطَنَ وَٱلۡإِثۡمَ وَٱلۡبَغۡيَ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَأَن تُشۡرِكُواْ بِٱللَّهِ مَا لَمۡ يُنَزِّلۡ بِهِۦ سُلۡطَٰنٗا وَأَن تَقُولُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to inform the polytheists who have forbidden what Allah has allowed: Indeed Allah has only forbidden lewd, indecent acts done openly or secretly, all forms of disobedience, and transgressing unjustly against people with regards to their selves, wealth, or honor. And He has forbidden worshipping others with Him – for which there is no proof to do so. And He has prohibited you from saying things about Him without knowledge regarding His Names, Attributes, Actions, and Sacred Law.

(٣٤) وَلِكُلِّ أُمَّةٍ أَجَلٞۖ فَإِذَا جَآءَ أَجَلُهُمۡ لَا يَسۡتَأۡخِرُونَ سَاعَةٗ وَلَا يَسۡتَقۡدِمُونَ

For every generation and people, there is an appointed time. If their decreed time arrives, they cannot delay it, even by a small amount, and they cannot move it forward either.

(٣٥) يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ إِمَّا يَأۡتِيَنَّكُمۡ رُسُلٞ مِّنكُمۡ يَقُصُّونَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِي فَمَنِ ٱتَّقَىٰ وَأَصۡلَحَ فَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

O children of Adam, if messengers from amongst you come to you from Allah, reciting what Allah has revealed from His Scripture to them, then follow them and what they bring. Those who are mindful of Allah by complying with His commands, staying away from His prohibitions, and setting their deeds right will have no fear upon them on the Day of Resurrection and will not grieve about the pleasures that passed them by in this world.

(٣٦) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ عَنۡهَآ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

As for the disbelievers who reject Allah's signs, do not have faith in them, and are too arrogant to act in accordance with what their messengers bring to them, they are the dwellers of the Fire, where they will live eternally.

(٣٧) فَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّنِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبًا أَوۡ كَذَّبَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِهِۦٓۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يَنَالُهُمۡ نَصِيبُهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُنَا يَتَوَفَّوۡنَهُمۡ قَالُوٓاْ أَيۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۖ قَالُواْ ضَلُّواْ عَنَّا وَشَهِدُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ أَنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ كَٰفِرِينَ

Who is more unjust than the person who invents a lie about Allah considering Him to have a partner, says He is deficient in some way, says something about Him that He has not said, or rejects His clear signs and verses that guide to the straight path? Those who do such things will receive the portion of worldly pleasure that is destined for them in the Preserved Tablet until the angel of death and his helpers come to them to take their souls, and they are asked: Where are the deities you used to worship besides Allah, claiming they would help you? Call on those idols if they can benefit you! The polytheists will say to the angels: The deities we used to worship have gone, and we do not know where they are! Then, they will admit that they were disbelievers, but their admission at that time will be proof against them and will not benefit them.

Points of Benefit:

A believer is commanded to honor the symbols of Allah by covering their nakedness and adorning themselves, especially when performing prayers and particularly when heading to the masjid.
Whoever interprets the Quran without knowledge, issues a fatwa without knowledge, or judges without knowledge has indeed spoken about Allah without knowledge, and this is among the gravest prohibitions.
In the verses, there is evidence that on the Day of Judgment, the believers will neither fear nor grieve, and neither terror nor dismay will befall them. And if it does, their ultimate destination will be security.
The most oppressive of people are those who obstruct the intentions of Allah from two perspectives: by invalidating what indicates His will, and by deluding people into thinking that Allah desires from them what He does not.
