(٣٨) قَالَ ٱدۡخُلُواْ فِيٓ أُمَمٖ قَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلِكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِ فِي ٱلنَّارِۖ كُلَّمَا دَخَلَتۡ أُمَّةٞ لَّعَنَتۡ أُخۡتَهَاۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا ٱدَّارَكُواْ فِيهَا جَمِيعٗا قَالَتۡ أُخۡرَىٰهُمۡ لِأُولَىٰهُمۡ رَبَّنَا هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ أَضَلُّونَا فَ‍َٔاتِهِمۡ عَذَابٗا ضِعۡفٗا مِّنَ ٱلنَّارِۖ قَالَ لِكُلّٖ ضِعۡفٞ وَلَٰكِن لَّا تَعۡلَمُونَ

The angels will say: Enter you, O polytheists, the Fire with the crowds of nations from the jinns and humanity who passed away before you in disbelief and error! Whenever a nation enters the Fire, it condemns its sister nation, which entered the Fire before it, until they all reach one another and are gathered together. The last of them to enter, who are the lowly people and followers, will ask concerning the previous nations who were their leaders and masters: O our Lord! These are the ones who led us astray from the proper guidance, so give them a double punishment, for they beautified for us the path of misguidance. Allah will reply to them: Each group of you will receive a doubled share of the punishment, but you do not perceive it.

(٣٩) وَقَالَتۡ أُولَىٰهُمۡ لِأُخۡرَىٰهُمۡ فَمَا كَانَ لَكُمۡ عَلَيۡنَا مِن فَضۡلٖ فَذُوقُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡسِبُونَ

The leaders and chiefs will exclaim: You do not have, O followers, over us any preference that makes you deserving of any lightening of the punishment, as it results from your own actions. You do not have any excuse for having followed what was false. So taste then, O followers, the punishment just as we taste it because of what you earned through your own disbelief and disobedience.

(٤٠) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ عَنۡهَا لَا تُفَتَّحُ لَهُمۡ أَبۡوَٰبُ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَلَا يَدۡخُلُونَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ حَتَّىٰ يَلِجَ ٱلۡجَمَلُ فِي سَمِّ ٱلۡخِيَاطِۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

Those who reject Allah's clear signs and verses and are too proud to follow them have lost everything because the gates of heaven will not open to their actions because of their disbelief, nor will they open for their souls when they die. Such people will never enter Paradise until the camel – one of the largest animals – goes into the eye of a needle – one of the smallest things. The aforementioned is impossible, and so is the idea that they will ever enter Paradise. In such a way, Allah recompenses those who stubbornly do wrong.

(٤١) لَهُم مِّن جَهَنَّمَ مِهَادٞ وَمِن فَوۡقِهِمۡ غَوَاشٖۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

For these people who rejected the truth because they were too arrogant, there is a bed of Hell to lie on, and above them is a covering of torment. This is how Allah recompenses those who overstep His limits by disbelieving in Him and turning away from Him.

(٤٢) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَا نُكَلِّفُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَآ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

But those who have faith in their Lord and do good actions to the best of their ability – and Allah does not burden a soul with more than it can bear – are the dwellers of Paradise, entering it and living there eternally.

(٤٣) وَنَزَعۡنَا مَا فِي صُدُورِهِم مِّنۡ غِلّٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهِمُ ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۖ وَقَالُواْ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي هَدَىٰنَا لِهَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لِنَهۡتَدِيَ لَوۡلَآ أَنۡ هَدَىٰنَا ٱللَّهُۖ لَقَدۡ جَآءَتۡ رُسُلُ رَبِّنَا بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ وَنُودُوٓاْ أَن تِلۡكُمُ ٱلۡجَنَّةُ أُورِثۡتُمُوهَا بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

Their bliss in Paradise is perfected by Allah removing whatever hatred and resentment is in their hearts, with rivers flowing beneath them. When they come to live there and settle in their homes, they will exclaim while recognizing Allah's blessings on them, praising Him: All praise and thanks belong to Allah, Who allowed us to do the good actions that brought us to this place. We would not have been able to do that if Allah had not enabled us to do so. The messengers of our Lord came with the truth about which there was no doubt, true in its promises of good for the righteous and true in its threats for the wrongdoers. It will then be announced to them: This, truly this, is the Paradise that Allah has made you inherit out of His Mercy for the good actions you used to do seeking the countenance of Allah.

Points of Benefit:

The friendship that existed among the deniers in the world will turn into enmity and curses on the Day of Judgment.
The souls of the believers will have the doors of the heavens opened for them, ascending towards Allah, rejoicing in the proximity to their Lord and the delight of His pleasure.
The souls of the deniers and those who turned away will not have the doors of the heavens opened for them. If they ascend, they seek permission but are not granted, for just as they did not ascend in the world with faith in Allah, knowledge of Him, and love for Him, likewise, they do not ascend after death. Indeed, the recompense is in accordance with their deeds.
The people of Paradise are saved from the Fire through Allah's forgiveness, admitted into Paradise by His mercy, and they share and inherit the abodes based on their righteous deeds, which are a manifestation of His mercy. Rather, it is one of the highest forms of His Mercy.
