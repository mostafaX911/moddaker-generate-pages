(٤٤) وَنَادَىٰٓ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلنَّارِ أَن قَدۡ وَجَدۡنَا مَا وَعَدَنَا رَبُّنَا حَقّٗا فَهَلۡ وَجَدتُّم مَّا وَعَدَ رَبُّكُمۡ حَقّٗاۖ قَالُواْ نَعَمۡۚ فَأَذَّنَ مُؤَذِّنُۢ بَيۡنَهُمۡ أَن لَّعۡنَةُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَى ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

The dwellers of Paradise will call unto the dwellers of the Fire when they have all entered their places, saying: Indeed we have found what our Lord promised us about Paradise to be true, as we have entered into it. Have you found what Allah promised you about the Fire to be true? The disbelievers will reply: We have found what was promised about the Fire to be true. Then, a caller will proclaim that the wrongdoers be removed from Allah's mercy because the doors of His mercy were open to them, and they turned away from them in their worldly life.

(٤٥) ٱلَّذِينَ يَصُدُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَبۡغُونَهَا عِوَجٗا وَهُم بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ كَٰفِرُونَ

These wrongdoers used to turn away from Allah's religion and used to turn others away from it, wanting to make the path of truth seem crooked so that people would not follow it. They also rejected the Afterlife, neither believing in nor preparing for it.

(٤٦) وَبَيۡنَهُمَا حِجَابٞۚ وَعَلَى ٱلۡأَعۡرَافِ رِجَالٞ يَعۡرِفُونَ كُلَّۢا بِسِيمَىٰهُمۡۚ وَنَادَوۡاْ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ أَن سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡۚ لَمۡ يَدۡخُلُوهَا وَهُمۡ يَطۡمَعُونَ

Between these two groups – the dwellers of Paradise and the dwellers of the Fire – is a partition called al-A'rāf (the Heights). On this partition are people whose good and bad actions are equal. They know the dwellers of Paradise by their signs, such as the light on their faces, and the dwellers of the Fire by their signs, such as the darkness on their faces. These people of the partition call to the companions of Paradise, honoring them, saying: Peace be upon you. They will not have entered Paradise yet but hope to enter it through Allah's mercy.

(٤٧) ۞وَإِذَا صُرِفَتۡ أَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ تِلۡقَآءَ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلنَّارِ قَالُواْ رَبَّنَا لَا تَجۡعَلۡنَا مَعَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

When their eyes turn to the people who are the people of the Fire, and they see the severe punishment they are afflicted with, they pray: O our Lord! Do not put us with the wrongdoers who disbelieved and worshipped others besides you!

(٤٨) وَنَادَىٰٓ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡأَعۡرَافِ رِجَالٗا يَعۡرِفُونَهُم بِسِيمَىٰهُمۡ قَالُواْ مَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنكُمۡ جَمۡعُكُمۡ وَمَا كُنتُمۡ تَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ

The people of the Heights will call to people in the Fire who had disbelieved, whom they recognize by their marks, such as the darkness of their faces and the blue shade of their eyes: Your gathering of wealth and men did not benefit you in the slightest, nor did arrogantly turning away from the truth.

(٤٩) أَهَٰٓؤُلَآءِ ٱلَّذِينَ أَقۡسَمۡتُمۡ لَا يَنَالُهُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِرَحۡمَةٍۚ ٱدۡخُلُواْ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ لَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَلَآ أَنتُمۡ تَحۡزَنُونَ

Allah will say, rebuking the disbelievers: Are these faithful people those you swore Allah would not show mercy to? Then, these people who have faith will be told to enter Paradise, not fearing what awaits them and not grieving over the pleasures of the world that passed them by because of the eternal bliss they find themselves in.

(٥٠) وَنَادَىٰٓ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ أَنۡ أَفِيضُواْ عَلَيۡنَا مِنَ ٱلۡمَآءِ أَوۡ مِمَّا رَزَقَكُمُ ٱللَّهُۚ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ حَرَّمَهُمَا عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

The dwellers of the Fire will call unto the dwellers of Paradise, begging them to pour some water or provide them with some of the food they have been given. The companions of Paradise will tell them that Allah has forbidden both of these for the disbelievers because of their disbelief and that they will not help them in what Allah has forbidden for them.

(٥١) ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ دِينَهُمۡ لَهۡوٗا وَلَعِبٗا وَغَرَّتۡهُمُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ نَنسَىٰهُمۡ كَمَا نَسُواْ لِقَآءَ يَوۡمِهِمۡ هَٰذَا وَمَا كَانُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا يَجۡحَدُونَ

These disbelievers are the ones who used to mock and make fun of faith and were deluded by the life of this world, being engrossed in its pleasures and luxuries. On the Day of Resurrection, Allah will forget them, and they will be left to suffer the punishment because they forgot the meeting of the Day of Resurrection, which they neglected despite having full knowledge of it. They rejected Allah's signs and proofs in condemnation despite knowing and recognizing the truth of His message.

Points of Benefit:

Disbelief in the resurrection is a direct cause for indulging in desires.
People will realize on the Day of Judgment the fulfillment of Allah's promise to the obedient and the realization of His threat to the disbelievers.
On the Day of Judgment, people will be divided into two groups: one in Paradise and the other in the Fire. In between them is a group in a balanced position, where their good deeds and bad deeds are equal, and their destiny is towards Paradise.
Those who possess wealth, status, and numerous followers should know that all of this will not avail them anything before Allah, nor will it save them from the punishment of Allah.
