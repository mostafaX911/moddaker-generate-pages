(٥٢) وَلَقَدۡ جِئۡنَٰهُم بِكِتَٰبٖ فَصَّلۡنَٰهُ عَلَىٰ عِلۡمٍ هُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

And Allah has indeed sent them this Quran, the Scripture revealed to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, explaining it and clarifying all things with His Knowledge. It guides the believers to the right path and to the truth and is a mercy for them because of what it guides them to, of goodness in this world and in the Afterlife.

(٥٣) هَلۡ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّا تَأۡوِيلَهُۥۚ يَوۡمَ يَأۡتِي تَأۡوِيلُهُۥ يَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ نَسُوهُ مِن قَبۡلُ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡ رُسُلُ رَبِّنَا بِٱلۡحَقِّ فَهَل لَّنَا مِن شُفَعَآءَ فَيَشۡفَعُواْ لَنَآ أَوۡ نُرَدُّ فَنَعۡمَلَ غَيۡرَ ٱلَّذِي كُنَّا نَعۡمَلُۚ قَدۡ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَضَلَّ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ

The disbelievers wait only for what they have been warned about, a painful punishment in the Afterlife as a result of their actions. The believers were also foretold about the reward of that day. Those who ignored the Quran in the life of this world, not acting according to what is in it, will then say: Indeed, the messengers of our Lord came with the truth, in which there was no doubt. If only there were a mediator who could now plead on our behalf so we could be excused from the punishment. Or if only we could return to the worldly life to do good actions that would save us instead of the bad we committed! The disbelievers have driven themselves to loss, earning their own ruin due to their disbelief. Those whom they used to worship beside Allah have abandoned them and can be of no help to them.

(٥٤) إِنَّ رَبَّكُمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ يُغۡشِي ٱلَّيۡلَ ٱلنَّهَارَ يَطۡلُبُهُۥ حَثِيثٗا وَٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَ وَٱلنُّجُومَ مُسَخَّرَٰتِۢ بِأَمۡرِهِۦٓۗ أَلَا لَهُ ٱلۡخَلۡقُ وَٱلۡأَمۡرُۗ تَبَارَكَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

O people, your Lord is Allah, who created the heavens and the Earth in six days, with no precedent. He established Himself on the Throne in a way befitting His Majesty. He made the darkness of the night disappear into the light of the day and the light of the day into the darkness of night, both chasing each other quickly so that neither is delayed. He also created the sun, the moon, and the stars, all subservient under His dominion. Is the whole of creation not His? Who besides Him is the creator? His alone is the command. He is High above any flaw. He is described with the attributes of splendor and perfection- the Lord of the Worlds.

(٥٥) ٱدۡعُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ تَضَرُّعٗا وَخُفۡيَةًۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُعۡتَدِينَ

O believers, call on your Lord with complete humility, silently and in private, sincere in your prayer, not showing off, and not worshipping others along with Allah in your prayers. He does not love those who overstep the limits in their prayers, and one of the worst ways someone can do this is by invoking others besides Him, as the polytheists do.

(٥٦) وَلَا تُفۡسِدُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بَعۡدَ إِصۡلَٰحِهَا وَٱدۡعُوهُ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعًاۚ إِنَّ رَحۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ قَرِيبٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Do not ruin things on Earth by committing sins after Allah has put it right by sending the messengers (peace be upon them) and establishing things in accordance with His laws. Instead, call on Allah alone, fearing His punishment and hoping for His reward. The mercy of Allah is truly close to those who do good, so be from among them.

(٥٧) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُرۡسِلُ ٱلرِّيَٰحَ بُشۡرَۢا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ رَحۡمَتِهِۦۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَقَلَّتۡ سَحَابٗا ثِقَالٗا سُقۡنَٰهُ لِبَلَدٖ مَّيِّتٖ فَأَنزَلۡنَا بِهِ ٱلۡمَآءَ فَأَخۡرَجۡنَا بِهِۦ مِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ

It is Allah who sends the winds as good news of coming rain until they carry a cloud filled with water. He drives the cloud to a barren land and sends down rain on it, bringing out all kinds of fruits and crops. The way this vegetation is brought out is similar to how the dead will be brought out of their graves and back to life. This is, O people, so that perhaps you will then reflect on the power of Allah and the wonder of His creation and that He is able to bring the dead to life.

Points of Benefit:

The Noble Quran is a book of guidance that provides detailed information that humanity needs. It is a source of mercy from Allah and guidance for those who approach it with a sincere heart.
Allah created the heavens and the earth in six days for a wisdom that He, the Exalted, intended, and had He willed, He could have simply said to it, "Be," and it would have been.
Believers must supplicate to Allah with humility and earnestness so that He responds to them with His grace.
Corruption on Earth in all its forms and manifestations is forbidden.
