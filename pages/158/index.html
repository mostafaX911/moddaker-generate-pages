(٥٨) وَٱلۡبَلَدُ ٱلطَّيِّبُ يَخۡرُجُ نَبَاتُهُۥ بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهِۦۖ وَٱلَّذِي خَبُثَ لَا يَخۡرُجُ إِلَّا نَكِدٗاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نُصَرِّفُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَشۡكُرُونَ

Good land produces vegetation in abundance, by Allah's permission. Likewise, the believer understands the teachings they are given, and they benefit from them, acting accordingly with righteousness. On the other hand, the rotten, salty land only produces vegetation in small amounts, and there is little goodness in what it produces. Likewise, the disbelievers will not benefit from the true teachings they receive and will not perform good actions that benefit them. Through such variation, Allah reveals His signs and proofs in different ways to confirm the truth to people who are thankful for Allah's blessings, do not reject them, and follow their Lord.

(٥٩) لَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ فَقَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥٓ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ عَذَابَ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ

Indeed, We sent Noah as a messenger to his people, calling them to single out Allah and leave off worshipping anything besides Him. Noah said to them: O people! Worship Allah alone; you have no deity worthy of worship except Him. I fear for you, O people, the punishment of a great day if you persist in your disbelief.

(٦٠) قَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ مِن قَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِنَّا لَنَرَىٰكَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ

The leaders and notables among his people said: O Noah! Indeed, we think you are in clear error.

(٦١) قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ لَيۡسَ بِي ضَلَٰلَةٞ وَلَٰكِنِّي رَسُولٞ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Noah told the leaders of his people: I am not in error, as you claim, but rather I am on guidance from my Lord, and I am a messenger from Him who is my Lord, your Lord, and the Lord of all creations.

(٦٢) أُبَلِّغُكُمۡ رِسَٰلَٰتِ رَبِّي وَأَنصَحُ لَكُمۡ وَأَعۡلَمُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

I only convey to you the revelation Allah has sent me with. I only want what is good for you when I encourage you to follow what Allah has instructed and to receive the reward for doing so, and I warn you of what Allah has prohibited and the punishment you would receive for disobeying Him. I know from Allah (may He be Exalted) what you do not know because of the knowledge Allah has given to me through revelation.

(٦٣) أَوَعَجِبۡتُمۡ أَن جَآءَكُمۡ ذِكۡرٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ رَجُلٖ مِّنكُمۡ لِيُنذِرَكُمۡ وَلِتَتَّقُواْ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ

Do you find it strange, O people, that revelation and good teaching has come to you from your Lord on the tongue of a man from amongst yourselves whom you know well?! I have grown up amongst you and have never been a liar or misguided, nor have I come to you from another people. I have come to warn you about the punishment of Allah so that you might comply with His commands and avoid His prohibitions, hoping that then you would receive mercy through having faith in Him.

(٦٤) فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَأَنجَيۡنَٰهُ وَٱلَّذِينَ مَعَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ وَأَغۡرَقۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمًا عَمِينَ

However, Noah's people rejected him and did not have faith in Allah but continued in their disbelief. Noah, at last, asked Allah to destroy the disbelievers. Allah saved Noah and those who were with him and had faith from drowning by enabling them to retreat upon the ark. He destroyed those who rejected His signs and continued rejecting them, drowning them in a great flood as their punishment. Indeed, they were a people whose hearts were blind to the truth.

(٦٥) ۞وَإِلَىٰ عَادٍ أَخَاهُمۡ هُودٗاۚ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥٓۚ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ

And I sent to the people of 'Ād a messenger whose name was Hūd (peace be upon him). Hūd said to them: O people! Worship Allah alone, for you have no true God except Him. Will you not then be mindful of Him by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions so that you would be saved from His punishment?

(٦٦) قَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن قَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِنَّا لَنَرَىٰكَ فِي سَفَاهَةٖ وَإِنَّا لَنَظُنُّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ

The leaders and notables among his people who disbelieved in Allah and rejected His Messenger said: Indeed we only think of you, O Hūd, as someone foolish and backward, calling us to worship Allah alone and to leave the worship of our idols. In fact, we are certain that you are a liar in claiming that Allah has sent you!

(٦٧) قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ لَيۡسَ بِي سَفَاهَةٞ وَلَٰكِنِّي رَسُولٞ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Hūd replied: O my people, I am not foolish or backward, but I am a messenger from the Lord of the Worlds.

Points of Benefit:

The fertile land serves as an example for pure hearts when revelation descends upon it, which is the substance of life. Just as rain is the substance of life for the earth, pure hearts, when touched by revelation, accept, nurture, and grow according to the purity of their essence and the goodness of their components, and vice versa.
The prophets and messengers have greater compassion for creation than the compassion of their own fathers and mothers.
It is Allah's established practice to send every messenger from among their own people and speak their language to appeal to the hearts of those whose nature remains untainted and to facilitate guidance for humanity.
Among the greatest fools is the one who confronts the truth with denial and rejection, arrogantly refuses to submit to scholars and advisers, and surrenders their heart and soul to every wayward devil.
