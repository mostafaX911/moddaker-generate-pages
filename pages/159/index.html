(٦٨) أُبَلِّغُكُمۡ رِسَٰلَٰتِ رَبِّي وَأَنَا۠ لَكُمۡ نَاصِحٌ أَمِينٌ

I am only conveying what Allah had instructed me to deliver to you, that you believe in Allah's Oneness and His sacred law. I am a trustworthy adviser to you all, delivering what I am instructed, no less, no more.

(٦٩) أَوَعَجِبۡتُمۡ أَن جَآءَكُمۡ ذِكۡرٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ رَجُلٖ مِّنكُمۡ لِيُنذِرَكُمۡۚ وَٱذۡكُرُوٓاْ إِذۡ جَعَلَكُمۡ خُلَفَآءَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ قَوۡمِ نُوحٖ وَزَادَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡخَلۡقِ بَصۜۡطَةٗۖ فَٱذۡكُرُوٓاْ ءَالَآءَ ٱللَّهِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ

Is it strange and amazing that a reminder should come to you from your Lord, from a man amongst you, and from your likeness, not through an angel or a jinn? Thank Allah and praise Him for having established you on the Earth, making you successors to the people of Noah who were destroyed because of their disbelief. Thank Allah for having favored you by making you strong in stature, and remember Allah's great blessings on you so that you may be successful, attaining what you desire and avoiding what is feared.

(٧٠) قَالُوٓاْ أَجِئۡتَنَا لِنَعۡبُدَ ٱللَّهَ وَحۡدَهُۥ وَنَذَرَ مَا كَانَ يَعۡبُدُ ءَابَآؤُنَا فَأۡتِنَا بِمَا تَعِدُنَآ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

Hūd's people said: Have you come to tell us to worship Allah alone and to leave what our parents and ancestors worshipped? Bring us the punishment you promise if you are truthful in what you claim.

(٧١) قَالَ قَدۡ وَقَعَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ رِجۡسٞ وَغَضَبٌۖ أَتُجَٰدِلُونَنِي فِيٓ أَسۡمَآءٖ سَمَّيۡتُمُوهَآ أَنتُمۡ وَءَابَآؤُكُم مَّا نَزَّلَ ٱللَّهُ بِهَا مِن سُلۡطَٰنٖۚ فَٱنتَظِرُوٓاْ إِنِّي مَعَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُنتَظِرِينَ

Hūd said: The punishment of Allah and His Anger is already on its way! Do you argue with me about idols you and your fathers have called gods when you have no basis for doing so since Allah has not sent down any proof of their divinity for you to use? Wait then for the punishment you ask for; I am also waiting, for indeed, it is on its way.

(٧٢) فَأَنجَيۡنَٰهُ وَٱلَّذِينَ مَعَهُۥ بِرَحۡمَةٖ مِّنَّا وَقَطَعۡنَا دَابِرَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَاۖ وَمَا كَانُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ

So Allah saved Hūd (peace be upon him) and those of the believers who were with him through Mercy from Him, and He removed and destroyed those who rejected His signs, did not have faith, and deserved their punishment because of their denial.

(٧٣) وَإِلَىٰ ثَمُودَ أَخَاهُمۡ صَٰلِحٗاۚ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥۖ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكُم بَيِّنَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡۖ هَٰذِهِۦ نَاقَةُ ٱللَّهِ لَكُمۡ ءَايَةٗۖ فَذَرُوهَا تَأۡكُلۡ فِيٓ أَرۡضِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَلَا تَمَسُّوهَا بِسُوٓءٖ فَيَأۡخُذَكُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Then Allah sent to the tribe of Thamūd, their brother Saleh, to call them to believe in the Oneness of Allah and to worship Him alone. Saleh said: O People! Believe in Allah alone; you have no other deity worthy of being worshipped. I have come to you with a sign proving the truth of what I have brought you: namely, a camel that will come out of a rock. It has an allocated time to drink from the water, and for you, a separate time to drink has been allocated. Allow her to eat within Allah's land, and do not harm her in any way, or a painful punishment will befall you.

Points of Benefit:

It is appropriate to adorn oneself with patience when calling to Allah, thereby emulating the prophets, peace be upon them.
One of the priorities in calling to Allah is to invite to the worship of Allah alone, rejecting any association with Him and denouncing polytheism.
Arrogance in material and physical strength diverts its possessor from obeying the commands and prohibitions of Allah.
A Prophet is of the same lineage as his people, but he is the noblest among them in lineage, the best among them in character, the most generous among them in social relations, and the highest among them in morality.
The prophets and their successors respond to the foolish with forbearance, overlook negative words with forgiveness and pardon, and exhibit patience and forgiveness.
