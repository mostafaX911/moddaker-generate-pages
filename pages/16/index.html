(١٠٢) وَٱتَّبَعُواْ مَا تَتۡلُواْ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينُ عَلَىٰ مُلۡكِ سُلَيۡمَٰنَۖ وَمَا كَفَرَ سُلَيۡمَٰنُ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينَ كَفَرُواْ يُعَلِّمُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ ٱلسِّحۡرَ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَى ٱلۡمَلَكَيۡنِ بِبَابِلَ هَٰرُوتَ وَمَٰرُوتَۚ وَمَا يُعَلِّمَانِ مِنۡ أَحَدٍ حَتَّىٰ يَقُولَآ إِنَّمَا نَحۡنُ فِتۡنَةٞ فَلَا تَكۡفُرۡۖ فَيَتَعَلَّمُونَ مِنۡهُمَا مَا يُفَرِّقُونَ بِهِۦ بَيۡنَ ٱلۡمَرۡءِ وَزَوۡجِهِۦۚ وَمَا هُم بِضَآرِّينَ بِهِۦ مِنۡ أَحَدٍ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَيَتَعَلَّمُونَ مَا يَضُرُّهُمۡ وَلَا يَنفَعُهُمۡۚ وَلَقَدۡ عَلِمُواْ لَمَنِ ٱشۡتَرَىٰهُ مَا لَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ مِنۡ خَلَٰقٖۚ وَلَبِئۡسَ مَا شَرَوۡاْ بِهِۦٓ أَنفُسَهُمۡۚ لَوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ

When they abandoned Allah's religion and instead followed the lies that the devils fabricated about Prophet Solomon's kingdom (peace be upon him), claiming that he established his kingdom through black magic, Solomon did not disbelieve by practicing black magic, as the Jews claimed. Instead, the devils disbelieved when they taught people black magic revealed to the two angels, Harut and Marut, in the city of Babylon, Iraq. These two angels would only teach black magic to someone after warning and explaining: We are simply a trial and test for people, so do not disbelieve by learning black magic. Those who did not heed their advice and learned black magic from them would, through it, separate a man and his wife by sowing hatred between them. These sorcerers could not harm anyone except with Allah's permission and will. They learned what harmed them and did not benefit them. This group of Jews knew that whoever exchanged Allah's scripture for black magic would have no portion in the Hereafter. What an evil thing they sold their souls for when they traded Allah's revelation and sacred law for black magic! If they had known what would benefit them, they would not have done something so disgraceful and clearly misguided.

(١٠٣) وَلَوۡ أَنَّهُمۡ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱتَّقَوۡاْ لَمَثُوبَةٞ مِّنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرٞۚ لَّوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ

If only the Jews truly believed in Allah and feared Him by obeying Him and not disobeying Him, Allah's reward would have been far better for them if they only knew what benefits them.

(١٠٤) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَقُولُواْ رَٰعِنَا وَقُولُواْ ٱنظُرۡنَا وَٱسۡمَعُواْۗ وَلِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Allah, the Exalted, directs the believers to be careful in choosing their words when addressing the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, telling them not to say 'rā'inā', meaning 'consider our condition/affairs' or 'shepherd us.', because the Jews would distort and address the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم with it, thereby changing its meaning to 'foolish' or 'heedless.' So, Allah prohibited them from using this phrase to shut the door to mockery. Instead, He prescribed His slaves to say: 'unẓurnā,' meaning 'wait for us to understand what you are saying', as this phrase has no negative connotation while conveying the same meaning. For the disbelievers lies an excruciating and painful torment with Allah.

(١٠٥) مَّا يَوَدُّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَلَا ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ أَن يُنَزَّلَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّنۡ خَيۡرٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَخۡتَصُّ بِرَحۡمَتِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو ٱلۡفَضۡلِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

The disbelievers, whether from the People of the Book or among the polytheists, do not like that Allah sends down any good thing, big or small, to you. Through His mercy, Allah favors who He wills of His slaves with prophethood, revelation, and belief. Allah is the possessor of great grace. No one in creation gets any good except that it is from Him, and it is from His grace that He sent His Prophet and revealed His Book to us.

Points of Benefit:

The bad manners of the Jews towards Allah's prophets, as they attributed to Solomon, peace be upon him, the use of magic, but Allah cleared him of it and declared them liars in their claim.
Magic has a reality and influence on the intellects and bodies, and the magician is a disbeliever, and his punishment is death.
Nothing of good or evil occurs within Allah's kingdom, the Exalted, except with His permission and knowledge.
Preventative measures are one of the goals of the legislation, so every word or action that suggests corrupt things must be avoided and kept away from.
Bounty is in the Hand of Allah, the Exalted, and He is the One who chooses it for whomsoever He wishes, with His mercy and wisdom.
