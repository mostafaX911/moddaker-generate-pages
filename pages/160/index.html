(٧٤) وَٱذۡكُرُوٓاْ إِذۡ جَعَلَكُمۡ خُلَفَآءَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ عَادٖ وَبَوَّأَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ تَتَّخِذُونَ مِن سُهُولِهَا قُصُورٗا وَتَنۡحِتُونَ ٱلۡجِبَالَ بُيُوتٗاۖ فَٱذۡكُرُوٓاْ ءَالَآءَ ٱللَّهِ وَلَا تَعۡثَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مُفۡسِدِينَ

Praise Allah for making you representatives on the Earth after the destruction of the people of 'Ād who had persevered in disbelief, and thank Him for establishing you in the land so that you could enjoy it and satisfy your desires. You build palaces in the plains and carve houses for yourselves out of the mountains. Remember Allah's blessings upon you and thank Him for them. Do not strive to cause corruption on Earth, and leave off your disbelief in Allah and your disobedience to Him.

(٧٥) قَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ مِن قَوۡمِهِۦ لِلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتُضۡعِفُواْ لِمَنۡ ءَامَنَ مِنۡهُمۡ أَتَعۡلَمُونَ أَنَّ صَٰلِحٗا مُّرۡسَلٞ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦۚ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا بِمَآ أُرۡسِلَ بِهِۦ مُؤۡمِنُونَ

The leaders and the important people who looked down in arrogance on the people who had faith, those they considered to be weak, asked them: Do you really know that Saleh is a messenger from Allah? The believers replied: We have sure faith in him and agree with and submit to what he has brought us.

(٧٦) قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُوٓاْ إِنَّا بِٱلَّذِيٓ ءَامَنتُم بِهِۦ كَٰفِرُونَ

The arrogant among his people said: Indeed we disbelieve in what you have faith in, O believers, and we will not have faith in it nor act in agreement with its sacred laws.

(٧٧) فَعَقَرُواْ ٱلنَّاقَةَ وَعَتَوۡاْ عَنۡ أَمۡرِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَقَالُواْ يَٰصَٰلِحُ ٱئۡتِنَا بِمَا تَعِدُنَآ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

So they killed the camel, which they had been forbidden to harm, too proud to follow Allah's command. Doubting what Saleh had promised would happen, they mocked him, saying: O Saleh! Bring the painful punishment you have told us would afflict us if you really are a messenger from Allah!

(٧٨) فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلرَّجۡفَةُ فَأَصۡبَحُواْ فِي دَارِهِمۡ جَٰثِمِينَ

When the punishment the disbelievers had asked for arrived, they were taken by a great earthquake so that they lay fallen in the morning, their faces and knees on the Earth, none escaping from the destruction.

(٧٩) فَتَوَلَّىٰ عَنۡهُمۡ وَقَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ لَقَدۡ أَبۡلَغۡتُكُمۡ رِسَالَةَ رَبِّي وَنَصَحۡتُ لَكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن لَّا تُحِبُّونَ ٱلنَّٰصِحِينَ

Saleh (peace be upon him) turned away from his people, having given up all hope for them. He said: O people, I had delivered what Allah had instructed me to deliver to you and had advised you well, encouraging you and warning you, but you were a people who did not have any love for sincere advisers who wanted to lead you to goodness and distance you from evil.

(٨٠) وَلُوطًا إِذۡ قَالَ لِقَوۡمِهِۦٓ أَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلۡفَٰحِشَةَ مَا سَبَقَكُم بِهَا مِنۡ أَحَدٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

And remember when Lot denounced his people, saying: Do you commit such a forbidden and shameful act as sodomy when no one before you has committed such a crime!

(٨١) إِنَّكُمۡ لَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلرِّجَالَ شَهۡوَةٗ مِّن دُونِ ٱلنِّسَآءِۚ بَلۡ أَنتُمۡ قَوۡمٞ مُّسۡرِفُونَ

Do you go to men to satisfy your lust instead of women, who you were supposed to go to?! Then, in that case, you have not followed any sense or any sound report or even what is natural! Rather, you have gone beyond the limits of Allah, overstepping the bounds of moderation, good sense, and the natural nobility of humanity.

Points of Benefit:

Arrogance often arises from wealth and status, while lack of wealth and status tends to lead to faith, belief, and submission.
The construction of elevated structures such as palaces is permissible, as one of the effects of blessings is the construction of beautiful buildings with gratitude to the Bestower.
It is predominant in the call of the prophets that the weak and the poor are often the ones to respond attentively to the words of truth they bring, while the wealthy and the leaders tend to rebel and resist it.
