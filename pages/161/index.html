(٨٢) وَمَا كَانَ جَوَابَ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُوٓاْ أَخۡرِجُوهُم مِّن قَرۡيَتِكُمۡۖ إِنَّهُمۡ أُنَاسٞ يَتَطَهَّرُونَ

His people, who committed this shameless act they had been forbidden from doing, only responded by turning away from the truth, saying: Drive Lot and his followers out of your city. They are a people who have kept away from this action of ours, so it is not befitting that they stay living here among us.

(٨٣) فَأَنجَيۡنَٰهُ وَأَهۡلَهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱمۡرَأَتَهُۥ كَانَتۡ مِنَ ٱلۡغَٰبِرِينَ

So Allah saved Lot and his family by instructing them to leave the city by night. His family was saved except for his wife, who remained behind in the city and suffered the same punishment as the rest.

(٨٤) وَأَمۡطَرۡنَا عَلَيۡهِم مَّطَرٗاۖ فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

And we sent down upon them a great rain of rocks of baked clay, overturning the city and turning it upside down. So consider what the end of the sinful people of Lot was. Their end was ruin and eternal disgrace.

(٨٥) وَإِلَىٰ مَدۡيَنَ أَخَاهُمۡ شُعَيۡبٗاۚ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥۖ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكُم بَيِّنَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡۖ فَأَوۡفُواْ ٱلۡكَيۡلَ وَٱلۡمِيزَانَ وَلَا تَبۡخَسُواْ ٱلنَّاسَ أَشۡيَآءَهُمۡ وَلَا تُفۡسِدُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بَعۡدَ إِصۡلَٰحِهَاۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

To the tribe of Midian, Allah sent their brother Shuayb (peace be upon him). He said: O people! Worship Allah alone, for you have no deity worthy of worship other than Him. A clear proof from Allah has come to you confirming what I bring to you from your Lord is the truth. Give people their rights by measuring and weighing things correctly, not short-changing people by saying bad things about their goods or by cheating them, and do not cause corruption on Earth through disbelief and disobedience after things have been set right through the sending of prophets. All of this is better for you and more beneficial if you are believers because then you would leave disobedience and draw close to Allah by doing what He has commanded.

(٨٦) وَلَا تَقۡعُدُواْ بِكُلِّ صِرَٰطٖ تُوعِدُونَ وَتَصُدُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ مَنۡ ءَامَنَ بِهِۦ وَتَبۡغُونَهَا عِوَجٗاۚ وَٱذۡكُرُوٓاْ إِذۡ كُنتُمۡ قَلِيلٗا فَكَثَّرَكُمۡۖ وَٱنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ

And do not sit waiting on every road seeking to threaten the people therein to steal from their possessions and to prevent those who want to be guided to the path of Allah, wanting the path of Allah to be crooked so that people would not follow it. Remember the blessings of Allah upon you so that you might be thankful, for you had been few in the land, and He increased your numbers. Reflect on what had been the end of those who caused corruption in the Earth before you since their end had been destruction and ruin!

(٨٧) وَإِن كَانَ طَآئِفَةٞ مِّنكُمۡ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱلَّذِيٓ أُرۡسِلۡتُ بِهِۦ وَطَآئِفَةٞ لَّمۡ يُؤۡمِنُواْ فَٱصۡبِرُواْ حَتَّىٰ يَحۡكُمَ ٱللَّهُ بَيۡنَنَاۚ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡحَٰكِمِينَ

And if there is a group among you who believe in what I have brought from my Lord and another group who do not believe in it, then you, O deniers, should wait to see how Allah judges between the two groups. He makes the best judgments and the most just rulings.

Points of Benefit:

Homosexuality is a heinous sin that indicates a deviation from the natural disposition, and it is fitting that their punishment corresponds to their actions, as Allah inflicted a severe punishment on the people of Lot.
The call of the prophets, including Shuayb (peace be upon him), is based on two principles: Esteeming the command of Allah, which includes acknowledging monotheism and believing in prophethood. And showing compassion to the creation of Allah, which involves refraining from oppression, corruption, and all forms of harm.
Corruption on Earth after its rectification is a social crime against humanity, as the righteousness of the land through faith and ethics is beneficial for everyone, and corruption on Earth is an aggression against people.
One of the greatest, most significant, severe, and disgraceful sins is seizing what is not rightfully taken according to Islamic law in financial positions through coercion and compulsion. It is usurpation, injustice, tyranny against people, promotion of wrongdoing, engagement, persistence, and acknowledgment of it.
