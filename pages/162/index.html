(٨٨) ۞قَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ مِن قَوۡمِهِۦ لَنُخۡرِجَنَّكَ يَٰشُعَيۡبُ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَعَكَ مِن قَرۡيَتِنَآ أَوۡ لَتَعُودُنَّ فِي مِلَّتِنَاۚ قَالَ أَوَلَوۡ كُنَّا كَٰرِهِينَ

The heads and leaders who were arrogant among Shuayb's people said to him: We will surely drive you, O Shuayb, out of this town of ours, you and those who have believed with you, or you will most certainly return to our religion. Shuayb said critically and in astonishment: Are we to follow your creed and religion, even if we hate it because we know the falsehood of what you are upon?

(٨٩) قَدِ ٱفۡتَرَيۡنَا عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبًا إِنۡ عُدۡنَا فِي مِلَّتِكُم بَعۡدَ إِذۡ نَجَّىٰنَا ٱللَّهُ مِنۡهَاۚ وَمَا يَكُونُ لَنَآ أَن نَّعُودَ فِيهَآ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّنَاۚ وَسِعَ رَبُّنَا كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ عِلۡمًاۚ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ تَوَكَّلۡنَاۚ رَبَّنَا ٱفۡتَحۡ بَيۡنَنَا وَبَيۡنَ قَوۡمِنَا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡفَٰتِحِينَ

We would have invented lies about Allah if we believed in the idolatry and disbelief you are upon after Allah has saved us from it by His grace. It would not be valid or right for us to return to your false creed unless Allah, our Lord, wills us to do so, as all accede to His Will, may He be Exalted. Our Lord surrounds us with the knowledge of everything. Nothing is hidden from Him. We rely on Allah alone to make us firm on the straight path and protect us from the roads of the Inferno. O our Lord, judge between us and our disbelieving people with the truth. So help the wronged person of truth against the obstinate wrongdoer, for You, O Lord, are the best of judges.

(٩٠) وَقَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن قَوۡمِهِۦ لَئِنِ ٱتَّبَعۡتُمۡ شُعَيۡبًا إِنَّكُمۡ إِذٗا لَّخَٰسِرُونَ

The disbelieving heads and leaders of his people rejecting the call to Tawheed (singling out) [Allah in worship and attributes] and warning against Shuayb and his religion, said: O our people, if you enter Shuayb's religion, and leave your religion and that of your ancestors, you would indeed be surely destroyed by that.

(٩١) فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلرَّجۡفَةُ فَأَصۡبَحُواْ فِي دَارِهِمۡ جَٰثِمِينَ

Then, a severe earthquake seized them, so in the morning, they were destroyed in their houses, fallen to their knees and faces, dead and still in their homes.

(٩٢) ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ شُعَيۡبٗا كَأَن لَّمۡ يَغۡنَوۡاْ فِيهَاۚ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ شُعَيۡبٗا كَانُواْ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ

Those who rejected Shuayb were all destroyed. It was as if they had never lived in their homes nor enjoyed themselves within them. Those who rejected Shuayb were the losers because they lost themselves and what they possessed. And the believers from his people were not the losers as these rejecting disbelievers claimed.

(٩٣) فَتَوَلَّىٰ عَنۡهُمۡ وَقَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ لَقَدۡ أَبۡلَغۡتُكُمۡ رِسَٰلَٰتِ رَبِّي وَنَصَحۡتُ لَكُمۡۖ فَكَيۡفَ ءَاسَىٰ عَلَىٰ قَوۡمٖ كَٰفِرِينَ

Their prophet Shuayb turned aside from them when they perished, and he addressed them, saying: O my people, I have delivered what my Lord instructed me to convey to you, and I sincerely gave you advice, so you did not take my advice nor yield to my guidance. How, then, can I grieve for people who disbelieve in Allah and are insistent on their disbelief?

(٩٤) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا فِي قَرۡيَةٖ مِّن نَّبِيٍّ إِلَّآ أَخَذۡنَآ أَهۡلَهَا بِٱلۡبَأۡسَآءِ وَٱلضَّرَّآءِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَضَّرَّعُونَ

We did not send a prophet to any town and then its inhabitants rejected and disbelieved, except that We seized them with suffering, poverty, and illness in the hope they might humble themselves to Allah and thus leave what disbelief and arrogance they were upon. This is a warning to Quraysh and all who disbelieve and reject by mentioning Allah's way with rejecting nations.

(٩٥) ثُمَّ بَدَّلۡنَا مَكَانَ ٱلسَّيِّئَةِ ٱلۡحَسَنَةَ حَتَّىٰ عَفَواْ وَّقَالُواْ قَدۡ مَسَّ ءَابَآءَنَا ٱلضَّرَّآءُ وَٱلسَّرَّآءُ فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُم بَغۡتَةٗ وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

Then We replaced them, after seizing them with suffering and illness, with goodness, well-being, and security, until they numbered many, their wealth increased, and they said: What afflicted us of good and evil is a perpetual norm. It occurred to our predecessors before. They did not recognize that what afflicted them was intended to teach them a lesson, while the good they were given was meant to draw out [their punishment]. Then Allah suddenly seized them with the torment they did not sense or expect.

Points of Benefit:

One of the manifestations of Allah's honor to His righteous servants is that He has opened for them the doors of knowledge by clarifying the truth from falsehood, explaining the salvation of the believers, and the punishment of the disbelievers.
It is Allah's established practice with His servants to give them respite, allowing them to take heed of events and to turn away from their sinful and harmful actions.
Many endure trials and hardships with patience, but only a few endure the trials of prosperity with patience.
