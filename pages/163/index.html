(٩٦) وَلَوۡ أَنَّ أَهۡلَ ٱلۡقُرَىٰٓ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱتَّقَوۡاْ لَفَتَحۡنَا عَلَيۡهِم بَرَكَٰتٖ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَٰكِن كَذَّبُواْ فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ

If the people of these towns to which We sent Our messengers had believed in what their messengers brought them and had been heedful of their Lord by abandoning disbelief and acts of disobedience and complying with His commands, then We would have opened to them the gates of goodness from every direction. But instead, they did not believe and were not heedful of Him. Instead, they rejected what their messengers came with. So We suddenly seized them with torment because of what they used to earn of sins and transgressions.

(٩٧) أَفَأَمِنَ أَهۡلُ ٱلۡقُرَىٰٓ أَن يَأۡتِيَهُم بَأۡسُنَا بَيَٰتٗا وَهُمۡ نَآئِمُونَ

So, have the people of these rejecting towns felt safe from Our torment coming to them at night while they are asleep, deep in their rest and tranquility?

(٩٨) أَوَ أَمِنَ أَهۡلُ ٱلۡقُرَىٰٓ أَن يَأۡتِيَهُم بَأۡسُنَا ضُحٗى وَهُمۡ يَلۡعَبُونَ

Or have they felt safe from Our torment coming to them at the beginning of the day while they are amused and unaware due to their preoccupation with their worldly life?

(٩٩) أَفَأَمِنُواْ مَكۡرَ ٱللَّهِۚ فَلَا يَأۡمَنُ مَكۡرَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ

Look at what Allah granted them of postponement [of torment]. By it, He blessed them with strength and abundance of provision, drawing out [punishment] for them. So, have these rejecting people among the people of those towns felt safe from Allah's designs and His subtle planning? No one feels secure from Allah's plans except the perishing people. As for the successful, then indeed, they fear His designs, so they do not become deluded with what Allah bestowed upon them. They merely recognize His favor upon them, so they thank Him.

(١٠٠) أَوَ لَمۡ يَهۡدِ لِلَّذِينَ يَرِثُونَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ أَهۡلِهَآ أَن لَّوۡ نَشَآءُ أَصَبۡنَٰهُم بِذُنُوبِهِمۡۚ وَنَطۡبَعُ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡمَعُونَ

Has it not become apparent to those who follow in the earth after the destruction of their predecessors among the nations because of their sins? Then, they do not take lessons from what befell them. Rather, they did their [very same] actions. Has it not become apparent to these [people] that if Allah wanted to strike them for their sins, He certainly would have done so, as is His Way? He seals upon their hearts so they do not accept any admonishment, and no reminder benefits them.

(١٠١) تِلۡكَ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ نَقُصُّ عَلَيۡكَ مِنۡ أَنۢبَآئِهَاۚ وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ فَمَا كَانُواْ لِيُؤۡمِنُواْ بِمَا كَذَّبُواْ مِن قَبۡلُۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَطۡبَعُ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

[Regarding] those previous nations: the townships of the people of Noah, Hūd, Saleh, Lot, and Shuayb; We recite and relate their narratives to you, O Messenger, and what they were upon of rejection and stubbornness, and of the destruction that befell them. [That is] to serve as a lesson for those who accept lessons and an admonishment to those who accept admonishment. The Messengers of the people of these towns had undoubtedly come with clear proofs of their truthfulness, so when the Messengers came, those people were not to believe; that is, in regards to what preceded from Allah's knowledge that they would reject it. In the same way that Allah sealed the hearts of the peoples of these towns who rejected their messengers, Allah seals the hearts of the disbelievers in Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم so that they are not guided to faith.

(١٠٢) وَمَا وَجَدۡنَا لِأَكۡثَرِهِم مِّنۡ عَهۡدٖۖ وَإِن وَجَدۡنَآ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَفَٰسِقِينَ

In most nations to whom the messengers were sent, We did not find fulfillment and adherence to what Allah prescribed to them, and We did not find them following His commands. We merely found most of them outside of Allah's obedience.

(١٠٣) ثُمَّ بَعَثۡنَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِم مُّوسَىٰ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَمَلَإِيْهِۦ فَظَلَمُواْ بِهَاۖ فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ

Then, after those Messengers, We sent Moses (peace be upon him) to Pharaoh and his people with Our convincing arguments and clear evidence demonstrating his truthfulness. However, they only rejected those miracles and disbelieved in them. So reflect, O Messenger, how Pharoah and his people ended up, for Allah has destroyed them by drowning them and followed them up with curses in the world and the hereafter.

(١٠٤) وَقَالَ مُوسَىٰ يَٰفِرۡعَوۡنُ إِنِّي رَسُولٞ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

When Allah sent him to Pharaoh, and he came to him, Moses said: O Pharaoh, I am sent from the Creator of all creation, their owner and determiner of all their matters.

Points of Benefit:

Faith and righteous deeds are a cause for the abundant flow of blessings and goodness from the heavens and the earth upon the Ummah (community/nation).
The close connection between the abundance of sustenance and piety is evident, and if Allah bestows His blessings upon the disbelievers, it is a lure for them and a deception.
The servant should not feel secure from the sudden punishment of Allah, which may come at any hour of the night or day.
The Quran narrates the stories of past nations to strengthen the believers and warn the disbelievers.
