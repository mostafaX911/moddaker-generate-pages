(١٠٥) حَقِيقٌ عَلَىٰٓ أَن لَّآ أَقُولَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلۡحَقَّۚ قَدۡ جِئۡتُكُم بِبَيِّنَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ فَأَرۡسِلۡ مَعِيَ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ

Moses said: Since I have been sent as a messenger from Him, it is only appropriate for me to tell the truth about Him. I have brought clear proof indicating my truthfulness and that I am indeed sent from my Lord to you. So, release the Israelites from the captivity and coercion they have been in.

(١٠٦) قَالَ إِن كُنتَ جِئۡتَ بِ‍َٔايَةٖ فَأۡتِ بِهَآ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

Pharaoh said to Moses: If you have brought a sign, as you allege, then bring it if you are truthful in your claim.

(١٠٧) فَأَلۡقَىٰ عَصَاهُ فَإِذَا هِيَ ثُعۡبَانٞ مُّبِينٞ

So Moses threw his stick, and it changed into a great snake, clear for those witnessing it.

(١٠٨) وَنَزَعَ يَدَهُۥ فَإِذَا هِيَ بَيۡضَآءُ لِلنَّٰظِرِينَ

And he took and revealed his hand from the opening in his shirt near his chest or under his underarm. So it came out white, not because of vitiligo (a skin disease), radiating brilliantly to onlookers due to its extreme whiteness.

(١٠٩) قَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ مِن قَوۡمِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَسَٰحِرٌ عَلِيمٞ

Once they witnessed the transformation of Moses's staff into a snake and his hand becoming white without vitiligo, the heads and leaders said: Moses is nothing but a sorcerer well-versed in sorcery.

(١١٠) يُرِيدُ أَن يُخۡرِجَكُم مِّنۡ أَرۡضِكُمۡۖ فَمَاذَا تَأۡمُرُونَ

By the feats he performs, he intends to drive you all out of this land of yours: Egypt. Then Pharaoh sought counsel with them regarding the matter of Moses (peace be upon him), saying to them: What opinion do you advise me with?

(١١١) قَالُوٓاْ أَرۡجِهۡ وَأَخَاهُ وَأَرۡسِلۡ فِي ٱلۡمَدَآئِنِ حَٰشِرِينَ

They said: Delay Moses and his brother Aaron and send those who would gather the sorcerers in the cities of Egypt.

(١١٢) يَأۡتُوكَ بِكُلِّ سَٰحِرٍ عَلِيمٖ

Those you send to gather the sorcerers from the cities will bring you every sorcerer skilled in sorcery and proficient in his craft.

(١١٣) وَجَآءَ ٱلسَّحَرَةُ فِرۡعَوۡنَ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّ لَنَا لَأَجۡرًا إِن كُنَّا نَحۡنُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبِينَ

So, Pharaoh dispatched individuals to gather the sorcerers. Then, when the sorcerers came to Pharaoh, they asked him: Would there be compensation for them if they were to defeat Moses with their magic and overcome him?

(١١٤) قَالَ نَعَمۡ وَإِنَّكُمۡ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبِينَ

So Pharaoh answered them, stating: Yes, indeed, [there is] compensation and reward for you, and you will be close [to me] in the ranks.

(١١٥) قَالُواْ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِمَّآ أَن تُلۡقِيَ وَإِمَّآ أَن نَّكُونَ نَحۡنُ ٱلۡمُلۡقِينَ

Trusting they would be victorious over Moses, the magicians pompously and arrogantly said: Choose, O Moses, what you wish from [either] you beginning by casting what you want, or us beginning with that.

(١١٦) قَالَ أَلۡقُواْۖ فَلَمَّآ أَلۡقَوۡاْ سَحَرُوٓاْ أَعۡيُنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَٱسۡتَرۡهَبُوهُمۡ وَجَآءُو بِسِحۡرٍ عَظِيمٖ

So Moses answered them, trusting in the victory of His Lord and not worried about them: Throw your ropes and your staffs. Then, when they threw them, they bewitched the people's eyes by distorting them from their proper perception. They terrified them and came with potent sorcery in the eyes of the onlookers.

(١١٧) ۞وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنۡ أَلۡقِ عَصَاكَۖ فَإِذَا هِيَ تَلۡقَفُ مَا يَأۡفِكُونَ

Allah inspired His prophet and conversant Moses (peace be upon him): Throw your staff, O Moses. So he threw it, then the stick changed into a snake swallowing up their ropes and staffs, which they would use to alter [appearances from] reality and deceive people that they were crawling snakes.

(١١٨) فَوَقَعَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَبَطَلَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

So the truth became apparent, and the truthfulness of what Moses (peace be upon him) came with became apparent, as well as the falsehood of the sorcery the sorcerers had performed.

(١١٩) فَغُلِبُواْ هُنَالِكَ وَٱنقَلَبُواْ صَٰغِرِينَ

So, they were defeated and routed. Moses (peace be upon him) triumphed over them in that exhibition and returned humiliated and compelled.

(١٢٠) وَأُلۡقِيَ ٱلسَّحَرَةُ سَٰجِدِينَ

When the magicians witnessed the greatness of Allah's ability and saw the clear signs, they could only fall and prostrate to Him, Exalted and Transcendent is He.

Points of Benefit:

From the wisdom and mercy of Allah is His decree that each prophet is assigned a sign that resonates with the understanding of his people, often reflecting their proficiency in a particular aspect.
Pharaoh was indeed a humiliated, contemptible, and powerless servant. Otherwise, he would not have needed to seek the assistance of magicians to counter Moses (peace be upon him).
It is indicative of the weakness of the magicians, despite their connection to the demons who fulfill their demands, that they sought rewards and prestige from Pharaoh.
