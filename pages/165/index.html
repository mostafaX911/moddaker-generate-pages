(١٢١) قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

The sorcerers said: We have believed in the Lord of all creation.

(١٢٢) رَبِّ مُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَ

The Lord of Moses and Aaron (peace be upon them), for He is worthy of worship [alone] without anyone from the alleged gods.

(١٢٣) قَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ ءَامَنتُم بِهِۦ قَبۡلَ أَنۡ ءَاذَنَ لَكُمۡۖ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَمَكۡرٞ مَّكَرۡتُمُوهُ فِي ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ لِتُخۡرِجُواْ مِنۡهَآ أَهۡلَهَاۖ فَسَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ

Pharaoh said to them, threatening them after their faith in Allah alone: You believed in Moses before I have permitted you? Indeed, your faith in him and affirmation of what Moses has come with is undoubtedly a trick and scheme you and Moses planned to drive out the city's people. Then you will know, O sorcerers, what punishment will inflict you and what penalty will strike you.

(١٢٤) لَأُقَطِّعَنَّ أَيۡدِيَكُمۡ وَأَرۡجُلَكُم مِّنۡ خِلَٰفٖ ثُمَّ لَأُصَلِّبَنَّكُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

I will surely cut off the right hand and left foot or the left hand and right foot of each of you. Then I will surely suspend you all upon palm trunks, making an example of you and terrifying whoever sees you in that state.

(١٢٥) قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا مُنقَلِبُونَ

In reply to Pharaoh's threat, the sorcerers said: Indeed we will return to our Lord alone, so we are not concerned with what you threaten with.

(١٢٦) وَمَا تَنقِمُ مِنَّآ إِلَّآ أَنۡ ءَامَنَّا بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ رَبِّنَا لَمَّا جَآءَتۡنَاۚ رَبَّنَآ أَفۡرِغۡ عَلَيۡنَا صَبۡرٗا وَتَوَفَّنَا مُسۡلِمِينَ

O Pharaoh, you are not objecting and angry with us except for our belief in the signs of our Lord when they came to us at the hands of Moses. If this can be considered a sin deserving of blame, then it is our sin. They then turned to Allah in supplication, pleading: O our Lord, pour patience upon us until it engulfs us so that we be firm on the truth. Take our lives [while we are] submitting to You, yielding to Your command, and following Your Messenger.

(١٢٧) وَقَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ مِن قَوۡمِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ أَتَذَرُ مُوسَىٰ وَقَوۡمَهُۥ لِيُفۡسِدُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَيَذَرَكَ وَءَالِهَتَكَۚ قَالَ سَنُقَتِّلُ أَبۡنَآءَهُمۡ وَنَسۡتَحۡيِۦ نِسَآءَهُمۡ وَإِنَّا فَوۡقَهُمۡ قَٰهِرُونَ

The leaders and notables among Pharaoh's people said to Pharaoh, provoking him to act against Moses and the believers with him: Will you leave Moses and his people to spread corruption in the land, abandon you and your gods, and call to worship Allah alone? Pharaoh said: We will kill the sons of the Israelites and keep their women to serve. We will overcome them with compulsion, domination, and control.

(١٢٨) قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوۡمِهِ ٱسۡتَعِينُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱصۡبِرُوٓاْۖ إِنَّ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ لِلَّهِ يُورِثُهَا مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦۖ وَٱلۡعَٰقِبَةُ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Advising his people, Moses said: O my people, ask for assistance from Allah alone to avert harm and bring benefit to you. And be patient with the trials you are facing, for indeed, the earth belongs to Allah alone, and it does not belong to Pharaoh or anyone else for him to control it. Allah exchanges [control of it] between people according to His Will. However, the noble end on earth is for the believers who comply with the commands of their Lord and avoid His prohibitions, for it is for them despite the tests and trials that inflict them.

(١٢٩) قَالُوٓاْ أُوذِينَا مِن قَبۡلِ أَن تَأۡتِيَنَا وَمِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جِئۡتَنَاۚ قَالَ عَسَىٰ رَبُّكُمۡ أَن يُهۡلِكَ عَدُوَّكُمۡ وَيَسۡتَخۡلِفَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَيَنظُرَ كَيۡفَ تَعۡمَلُونَ

Moses's people from the Israelites said to Moses (peace be upon him): O Moses, we were tried at the hands of Pharaoh with the murder of our sons and preservation of our women before you came to us and after. Advising them and giving good news of relief, Moses said to them: It is hopeful that Allah will destroy your enemy, Pharaoh, and his people and establish you in the earth after them. Then He would see what you do after that [out of] thankfulness or ingratitude.

(١٣٠) وَلَقَدۡ أَخَذۡنَآ ءَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ بِٱلسِّنِينَ وَنَقۡصٖ مِّنَ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَذَّكَّرُونَ

We had undoubtedly punished the people of Pharaoh with drought and aridity. We tested them with a shortage of fruits of the earth and its yields, hoping they would reflect and take a lesson that what happened to them from [all] that was but a punishment for their disbelief so that they would repent to Allah.

Points of Benefit:

The stance of the magicians and their bold declaration of belief unequivocally signifies that when a person strips away his desires and submits to sound reasoning and intellect, he readily embraces belief when presented with compelling evidence.
The people who believe in Allah and the Last Day are the most resolute, courageous, and patient individuals, particularly during times of crises, trials, and wars.
Those who benefit from authority incite and inflame the ruler to confront the people of faith because the preservation of the ruler ensures the continuation of their interests.
One of the reasons for the withholding of rainfall and the rise in prices is oppression and corruption.
