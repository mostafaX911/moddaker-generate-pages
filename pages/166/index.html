(١٣١) فَإِذَا جَآءَتۡهُمُ ٱلۡحَسَنَةُ قَالُواْ لَنَا هَٰذِهِۦۖ وَإِن تُصِبۡهُمۡ سَيِّئَةٞ يَطَّيَّرُواْ بِمُوسَىٰ وَمَن مَّعَهُۥٓۗ أَلَآ إِنَّمَا طَٰٓئِرُهُمۡ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

So whenever the people of Pharaoh experienced luxury, healthy produce, and low prices, they said: We have been given this because we deserve it and are privileged by it. And if a misfortune reaches or inflicts them, of drought, aridity, prevalence of disease, and other calamities, they would blame their bad fortune on Moses and the Israelites who were with him. The truth is that what struck them from all that was only by the decree of Allah, may He be Exalted. Neither they nor Moses (peace be upon him) had any part in the affair apart from Moses's supplications against them. But most do not know, so they attribute it to other than Allah.

(١٣٢) وَقَالُواْ مَهۡمَا تَأۡتِنَا بِهِۦ مِنۡ ءَايَةٖ لِّتَسۡحَرَنَا بِهَا فَمَا نَحۡنُ لَكَ بِمُؤۡمِنِينَ

The people of Pharaoh said to Moses (peace be upon them), out of their stubbornness against the truth: Whatever sign or evidence you bring to us, and whatever proof you establish to invalidate what we have, to turn us away from it and to affirm what you have come with: we will not believe in you.

(١٣٣) فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلطُّوفَانَ وَٱلۡجَرَادَ وَٱلۡقُمَّلَ وَٱلضَّفَادِعَ وَٱلدَّمَ ءَايَٰتٖ مُّفَصَّلَٰتٖ فَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ وَكَانُواْ قَوۡمٗا مُّجۡرِمِينَ

Then, as a punishment for their rejection and stubbornness, We sent abundant water, so it flooded their crops and fruits; sent locusts upon them so it ate their yields; sent small creatures upon them called lice striking their crops or harming humans in their hair; sent frogs upon them, so they filled their vessels, ruined their food and kept [people] awake in their places of sleep; and We sent the blood so the water in their wells and rivers turned into blood. We sent all of that as clear and distinct signs, one after another. Despite all that struck them of punishments, they were too pompous to have faith in Allah and believe in what Moses (peace be upon him) brought. They were people who committed wrongs, would not give up falsehood, and would not be guided to the truth.

(١٣٤) وَلَمَّا وَقَعَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلرِّجۡزُ قَالُواْ يَٰمُوسَى ٱدۡعُ لَنَا رَبَّكَ بِمَا عَهِدَ عِندَكَۖ لَئِن كَشَفۡتَ عَنَّا ٱلرِّجۡزَ لَنُؤۡمِنَنَّ لَكَ وَلَنُرۡسِلَنَّ مَعَكَ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ

And when the torment with these matters inflicted them, they sought refuge with Moses (peace be upon him), so they said to him: O Moses, by the prophethood He has privileged you with and what He pledged to of removing the torment by repentance, supplicate to your Lord for us that He uplift the torment that has inflicted on us. Then indeed, if you uplift that from us, we will certainly believe in you and send the Israelites with you and set them free.

(١٣٥) فَلَمَّا كَشَفۡنَا عَنۡهُمُ ٱلرِّجۡزَ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٍ هُم بَٰلِغُوهُ إِذَا هُمۡ يَنكُثُونَ

Then, when We lifted the torment from them for a set time, before their destruction by drowning, they reneged from what they had taken upon themselves to believe and release the Children of Israel. So they persisted in their disbelief and did not allow the Israelites to go with Moses (peace be upon him).

(١٣٦) فَٱنتَقَمۡنَا مِنۡهُمۡ فَأَغۡرَقۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ بِأَنَّهُمۡ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَكَانُواْ عَنۡهَا غَٰفِلِينَ

So when the appointed time came for their destruction, We unleashed Our punishment on them by drowning them in the sea because they rejected the signs of Allah and turned away from the indisputable truth which the signs had indicated.

(١٣٧) وَأَوۡرَثۡنَا ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَانُواْ يُسۡتَضۡعَفُونَ مَشَٰرِقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَغَٰرِبَهَا ٱلَّتِي بَٰرَكۡنَا فِيهَاۖ وَتَمَّتۡ كَلِمَتُ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ عَلَىٰ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ بِمَا صَبَرُواْۖ وَدَمَّرۡنَا مَا كَانَ يَصۡنَعُ فِرۡعَوۡنُ وَقَوۡمُهُۥ وَمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡرِشُونَ

We made the Israelites, whom Pharaoh and his people would humiliate, inherit the eastern areas of the land and the western regions of it, referring to the Levant. Allah blessed this land by making its crops and fruits grow in the most perfect fashion. The good word of your Lord was fulfilled, O Messenger, where Allah says: {We wish to grant favor to those who were weakened in the earth and make them leaders and make them the inheritors} [Al-Qasas:5]. So Allah established them in the land because of their patience in the face of the harm they had suffered from Pharaoh and his people. We destroyed the farms and homes that Pharaoh would construct and the palaces they would build.

Points of Benefit:

Good and evil, as well as deeds both virtuous and harmful, all occur by the decree and destiny of Allah; nothing transgresses beyond His control.
The state of people during times of adversity and calamities reflects their natural inclination to turn to Allah, driven by the instinctive call of innate belief.
The believer benefits from contemplating the signs of Allah and His laws in creation, reflecting on their causes and consequences.
The strength of individuals and nations fades in the face of the Almighty power of Allah, and belief in Allah is the source of all strength.
Allah, the Exalted, rewards His patient believing servants by granting them empowerment on the earth after their period of weakness.
