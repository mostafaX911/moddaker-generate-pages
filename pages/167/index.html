(١٣٨) وَجَٰوَزۡنَا بِبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَ فَأَتَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ قَوۡمٖ يَعۡكُفُونَ عَلَىٰٓ أَصۡنَامٖ لَّهُمۡۚ قَالُواْ يَٰمُوسَى ٱجۡعَل لَّنَآ إِلَٰهٗا كَمَا لَهُمۡ ءَالِهَةٞۚ قَالَ إِنَّكُمۡ قَوۡمٞ تَجۡهَلُونَ

And We made the Israelites cross the sea when Moses hit it with his staff, and then it parted. So they passed by a people who were lingering upon the worship of [some] idols of theirs, worshipping them instead of Allah. The Israelites said to Moses (peace be upon him): O Moses, make us an idol to worship as these people have idols to worship other than Allah. Moses said to them: You are a people who are ignorant of that which due to Allah from reverence and Tawheed (singling out) [in worship and attributes], and that which does not befit Him of associating partners [with Him] and worshipping other than Him.

(١٣٩) إِنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ مُتَبَّرٞ مَّا هُمۡ فِيهِ وَبَٰطِلٞ مَّا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

Indeed, [regarding] those people lingering upon worshiping their idols: destroyed is the worship of other than Him which they are upon, and invalid are all acts of worship that they would do out of their association of others in worship with Allah.

(١٤٠) قَالَ أَغَيۡرَ ٱللَّهِ أَبۡغِيكُمۡ إِلَٰهٗا وَهُوَ فَضَّلَكُمۡ عَلَى ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Moses said to his people: O my people, how can I seek a god for you to worship other than Allah when you have witnessed what you have from His great signs, and He, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, has favored you upon the [the inhabitants] of the worlds of your time with what he bestowed upon you from the destruction of your enemy, your inheritance [of control] in the land, and your established governance in it?

(١٤١) وَإِذۡ أَنجَيۡنَٰكُم مِّنۡ ءَالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ يَسُومُونَكُمۡ سُوٓءَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ يُقَتِّلُونَ أَبۡنَآءَكُمۡ وَيَسۡتَحۡيُونَ نِسَآءَكُمۡۚ وَفِي ذَٰلِكُم بَلَآءٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ عَظِيمٞ

Remember, O Israelites, when We saved you from your abasement by Pharaoh and his people when they would subject you to varieties of degradation, from killing your sons and preserving your women to serve. There [was] a great test from your Lord in your rescue from Pharaoh and his people, obliging gratitude from you.

(١٤٢) ۞وَوَٰعَدۡنَا مُوسَىٰ ثَلَٰثِينَ لَيۡلَةٗ وَأَتۡمَمۡنَٰهَا بِعَشۡرٖ فَتَمَّ مِيقَٰتُ رَبِّهِۦٓ أَرۡبَعِينَ لَيۡلَةٗۚ وَقَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِأَخِيهِ هَٰرُونَ ٱخۡلُفۡنِي فِي قَوۡمِي وَأَصۡلِحۡ وَلَا تَتَّبِعۡ سَبِيلَ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ

Allah promised His messenger Moses thirty nights for a private conversation with Him. Then Allah completed them with the addition of ten, so they became forty nights. When he wanted to leave for the private conversation with his Lord, Moses told his brother Aaron: O Aaron, be my successor within my people and make their affairs right with fine leadership and gentleness. Do not follow the path of the corrupters by engaging in acts of disobedience, and do not be a helper to the disobedient [to Allah].

(١٤٣) وَلَمَّا جَآءَ مُوسَىٰ لِمِيقَٰتِنَا وَكَلَّمَهُۥ رَبُّهُۥ قَالَ رَبِّ أَرِنِيٓ أَنظُرۡ إِلَيۡكَۚ قَالَ لَن تَرَىٰنِي وَلَٰكِنِ ٱنظُرۡ إِلَى ٱلۡجَبَلِ فَإِنِ ٱسۡتَقَرَّ مَكَانَهُۥ فَسَوۡفَ تَرَىٰنِيۚ فَلَمَّا تَجَلَّىٰ رَبُّهُۥ لِلۡجَبَلِ جَعَلَهُۥ دَكّٗا وَخَرَّ مُوسَىٰ صَعِقٗاۚ فَلَمَّآ أَفَاقَ قَالَ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ تُبۡتُ إِلَيۡكَ وَأَنَا۠ أَوَّلُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

When Moses came for the private conversation with his Lord at the appointment designated for him, which was a full forty nights, and his Lord spoke to him with commands, prohibitions, and otherwise, Moses yearned to see his Lord, so he asked to look upon Him. So, Allah, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, replied: You will not see Me in the worldly life because of your inability to endure that, but look, instead, at the mountain when I reveal Myself to it, for if it remains in its place, unaffected, then You will see Me; and if it becomes leveled to the ground, then you will not be able to see Me in the [life of this] world.Then, when Allah revealed Himself to the mountain, He made it leveled to the ground, and Moses fell unconscious. Then, when he recovered from the unconsciousness that overtook him, he said: I declare Your Perfection, O Lord, far above all that which does not befit You. Here I am, turned to You in repentance from what I asked You of seeing You in the world, and I am the first believer among my people.

Points of Benefit:

The events affirm that the Israelites were transitioning from one deviation to another despite the presence of the Prophet of Allah, Moses, among them.
One manifestation of a nation's decline is when it praises the reprehensible and devalues the virtuous based solely on opinion and whims.
Reforming the nation and closing the doors of corruption are noble goals pursued by the prophets and preachers.
Allah, the Exalted, has decreed that no one from His creation will see Him in this world, and He will honor those whom He loves by allowing them to see Him in the Hereafter.
