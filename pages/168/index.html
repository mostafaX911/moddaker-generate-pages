(١٤٤) قَالَ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنِّي ٱصۡطَفَيۡتُكَ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ بِرِسَٰلَٰتِي وَبِكَلَٰمِي فَخُذۡ مَآ ءَاتَيۡتُكَ وَكُن مِّنَ ٱلشَّٰكِرِينَ

Allah told Moses: O Moses, I have chosen you and favored you over the people with My messages when I sent you to them. I also favored you with My [direct] speech to you without any intermediary. So take the generous honor I have given you, and be among those thankful to Allah for such a great gift.

(١٤٥) وَكَتَبۡنَا لَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأَلۡوَاحِ مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ مَّوۡعِظَةٗ وَتَفۡصِيلٗا لِّكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ فَخُذۡهَا بِقُوَّةٖ وَأۡمُرۡ قَوۡمَكَ يَأۡخُذُواْ بِأَحۡسَنِهَاۚ سَأُوْرِيكُمۡ دَارَ ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ

And We wrote for Moses on the Tablets, of wood or otherwise, everything that the Israelites would need for their religious and worldly affairs, as an admonishment for those among them who would accept admonishment and elaboration for the rulings that needed explanation. So take this Torah, O Moses, with seriousness and utmost effort, and instruct your people, the Israelites, to take hold of the best within it from that whose reward is more significant, such as doing what was commanded in the most perfect way, and such as patience and pardoning. I will show you [all] the consequence of he who went against My commands and left obedience to Me and the ruin and destruction that he ends up in.

(١٤٦) سَأَصۡرِفُ عَنۡ ءَايَٰتِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَتَكَبَّرُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَإِن يَرَوۡاْ كُلَّ ءَايَةٖ لَّا يُؤۡمِنُواْ بِهَا وَإِن يَرَوۡاْ سَبِيلَ ٱلرُّشۡدِ لَا يَتَّخِذُوهُ سَبِيلٗا وَإِن يَرَوۡاْ سَبِيلَ ٱلۡغَيِّ يَتَّخِذُوهُ سَبِيلٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَكَانُواْ عَنۡهَا غَٰفِلِينَ

[Regarding] those who are unjustly pompous against the slaves of Allah and the truth, I will turn them away from taking lessons from My signs in the horizons and in themselves and from understanding the verses of My Book. Even if they see every sign, they will not believe in them due to their objection to and turning away from them and for opposing Allah and His Messenger. If they see the path of truth leading to Allah's contentment, they do not follow it or desire it. And if they see the way of misguidance and error leading to Allah's wrath, they follow it. That which inflicted them only struck them because of their rejection of Allah's great signs, which indicated the truthfulness of what the messengers came with and due to being oblivious from reflecting upon them.

(١٤٧) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَلِقَآءِ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ حَبِطَتۡ أَعۡمَٰلُهُمۡۚ هَلۡ يُجۡزَوۡنَ إِلَّا مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

Those who rejected Our signs, which indicate the truthfulness of Our messengers, and rejected meeting Allah on the Day of Resurrection, their actions that were among the acts of obedience will be invalidated, and they will not be rewarded for them because they were missing their condition, which is faith. They will not be requited on the Day of Resurrection except for what they would commit of disbelief in Allah and associating partners with Him. The requital of that is living eternally in the Fire.

(١٤٨) وَٱتَّخَذَ قَوۡمُ مُوسَىٰ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ مِنۡ حُلِيِّهِمۡ عِجۡلٗا جَسَدٗا لَّهُۥ خُوَارٌۚ أَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّهُۥ لَا يُكَلِّمُهُمۡ وَلَا يَهۡدِيهِمۡ سَبِيلًاۘ ٱتَّخَذُوهُ وَكَانُواْ ظَٰلِمِينَ

After Moses had gone to privately converse with his Lord, his people established a statue of a calf made from their ornaments; it did not possess a soul, but it made a sound. Did they not know that this calf could not speak to them nor guide them to any path of good, [be it] material or spiritual, and that it could not bring anything of benefit to them or remove any harm from them? They took it as an object of worship and wronged themselves by doing so.

(١٤٩) وَلَمَّا سُقِطَ فِيٓ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَرَأَوۡاْ أَنَّهُمۡ قَدۡ ضَلُّواْ قَالُواْ لَئِن لَّمۡ يَرۡحَمۡنَا رَبُّنَا وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ

When they regretted their actions, became bewildered, and realized that they had gone astray from the straight path by taking the calf as an object of worship alongside Allah, they said: If our Lord does not have mercy on us by granting us success to obey Him and forgive us for the worship of the calf which we engaged in, then we will undoubtedly be among those who lose their worldly life and Afterlife.

Points of Benefit:

It is incumbent upon the servant to manifest gratitude for the kindness and favor of Allah upon him, for thankfulness is associated with an increase in blessings.
It is upon the servant to adopt the best in words and actions.
The divine law must be received with determination and resolve, with a commitment to obedience and the implementation of the rectification and goodness it contains, and to prevent corruption and wrongdoing.
When the servant makes a mistake or falls short in fulfilling his duties to his Lord, it is incumbent upon him to acknowledge the gravity of the transgression he has committed. He must recognize that there is no refuge from Allah in rectifying his mistake except by turning to Him.
