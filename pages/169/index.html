(١٥٠) وَلَمَّا رَجَعَ مُوسَىٰٓ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ غَضۡبَٰنَ أَسِفٗا قَالَ بِئۡسَمَا خَلَفۡتُمُونِي مِنۢ بَعۡدِيٓۖ أَعَجِلۡتُمۡ أَمۡرَ رَبِّكُمۡۖ وَأَلۡقَى ٱلۡأَلۡوَاحَ وَأَخَذَ بِرَأۡسِ أَخِيهِ يَجُرُّهُۥٓ إِلَيۡهِۚ قَالَ ٱبۡنَ أُمَّ إِنَّ ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱسۡتَضۡعَفُونِي وَكَادُواْ يَقۡتُلُونَنِي فَلَا تُشۡمِتۡ بِيَ ٱلۡأَعۡدَآءَ وَلَا تَجۡعَلۡنِي مَعَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

When Moses returned to his people from his private conversation with His Lord, full of anger towards them and sadness upon finding them worshipping the calf, he said: What a terrible condition you have fallen into after me, O my people, for what it leads to of ruin and misery. Did you become bored of waiting for me, so you proceeded to worship the calf? Moses threw the Tablets because of the intensity of the anger and sadness that struck him and took his brother Aaron by the head and beard, dragging him towards him for remaining with them and not rectifying the worship of the calf that he saw. Aaron said to his brother Moses, in excuse and seeking clemency from him: O son of my mother, indeed the people considered me weak, so they belittled and almost killed me. So do not punish me with what would please my enemies, and do not include me, out of your anger, among the wrongdoers from the people due to their worship of other than Allah.

(١٥١) قَالَ رَبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَلِأَخِي وَأَدۡخِلۡنَا فِي رَحۡمَتِكَۖ وَأَنتَ أَرۡحَمُ ٱلرَّٰحِمِينَ

So Moses supplicated to his Lord: O my Lord, forgive me and my brother Aaron, and enter us into Your Mercy, and make it surround us from all sides. You, O our Lord, are more merciful to us than any merciful one.

(١٥٢) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ ٱلۡعِجۡلَ سَيَنَالُهُمۡ غَضَبٞ مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡ وَذِلَّةٞ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُفۡتَرِينَ

Indeed, [regarding] those who made the calf a god, worshipping it; severe anger from their Lord and humiliation will strike them in this life for angering their Lord and belittlement of Him. With the like of this recompense, We requite those who fabricate lies about Allah.

(١٥٣) وَٱلَّذِينَ عَمِلُواْ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِ ثُمَّ تَابُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهَا وَءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهَا لَغَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Those who do evil deeds from associating partners with Allah and committing acts of disobedience, and then repent by believing in Him and desisting from the acts of disobedience they were upon, [then] indeed, O Messenger, after this repentance and return from associating partners to faith, and from acts of disobedience to obedience, your Lord is certainly forgiving to them by concealment [of their sins] and pardon and is merciful to them.

(١٥٤) وَلَمَّا سَكَتَ عَن مُّوسَى ٱلۡغَضَبُ أَخَذَ ٱلۡأَلۡوَاحَۖ وَفِي نُسۡخَتِهَا هُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٞ لِّلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِرَبِّهِمۡ يَرۡهَبُونَ

When the anger of Moses (peace be upon him) had subsided and he calmed down, he picked up the Tablets which he had thrown in anger. These Tablets contained guidance from misguidance, clarification of the truth, and mercy for those who fear their Lord and are afraid of His punishment.

(١٥٥) وَٱخۡتَارَ مُوسَىٰ قَوۡمَهُۥ سَبۡعِينَ رَجُلٗا لِّمِيقَٰتِنَاۖ فَلَمَّآ أَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلرَّجۡفَةُ قَالَ رَبِّ لَوۡ شِئۡتَ أَهۡلَكۡتَهُم مِّن قَبۡلُ وَإِيَّٰيَۖ أَتُهۡلِكُنَا بِمَا فَعَلَ ٱلسُّفَهَآءُ مِنَّآۖ إِنۡ هِيَ إِلَّا فِتۡنَتُكَ تُضِلُّ بِهَا مَن تَشَآءُ وَتَهۡدِي مَن تَشَآءُۖ أَنتَ وَلِيُّنَا فَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَٱرۡحَمۡنَاۖ وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡغَٰفِرِينَ

Moses chose seventy men among the best of his people to apologize to their Lord for the worship of the calf that the fools among them had committed. Allah promised them a time and place for them to attend. Then, when they attended, they were brazen with Allah and asked Moses to let them see Allah with their eyes. So an earthquake seized them, and they were struck down by its intensity and destroyed. So Moses begged his Lord, saying: O my Lord, if You had willed to destroy them, and me with them before they had come, You would have certainly destroyed them. Would You destroy us because of what the feeble-minded amongst us did? For the worship of the calf that my people committed is only a trial and test by which You lead astray whom You will, and You guide whom You will. You are in charge of our affair, so forgive us our wrongs, and have mercy on us with Your vast Mercy. You are the best of those who forgive wrongdoing and pardon sin.

Points of Benefit:

The verses serve as evidence that when there is a clear indication in the evidence, making an error in independent reasoning (ijtihad) is not excusable for its author when judgments are passed upon him. This is what jurists refer to as a farfetched interpretation (ta'weel ba'eed).
One of the etiquettes of supplication is to begin with oneself. Thus, Moses (peace be upon him) initiated his supplication by seeking forgiveness for himself as a form of reverence toward Allah for the anger he had displayed. Then, he sought forgiveness for his brother, acknowledging the possibility of any negligence or leniency on his part in deterring the worshipers of the calf.
The warning against anger and its dominion over a person's intellect is emphasized. Therefore, Allah attributes the act of silence to anger, as if it is the commander and the prohibitor.
The necessity of guarding against the anger of Allah and fearing His punishment is evident. Consider the stance of Moses (peace be upon him) before his Lord and observe his fear of the anger of his Lord.
