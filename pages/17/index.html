(١٠٦) ۞مَا نَنسَخۡ مِنۡ ءَايَةٍ أَوۡ نُنسِهَا نَأۡتِ بِخَيۡرٖ مِّنۡهَآ أَوۡ مِثۡلِهَآۗ أَلَمۡ تَعۡلَمۡ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ

Allah, the Exalted, makes it clear that whenever He removes the ruling of a verse or its words from the Quran, and people forget them, He (may He be Exalted) replaces them with something more beneficial for the immediate moment or for later, or with what is similar. Such cases occur because of Allah's wisdom and knowledge. You know, O Prophet, that Allah is the Most Capable of everything. He does as He wills and judges as He wishes.

(١٠٧) أَلَمۡ تَعۡلَمۡ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ وَمَا لَكُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مِن وَلِيّٖ وَلَا نَصِيرٍ

You know, O Prophet, Allah is the Owner of the heavens and the earth. He judges as He wishes, instructs His slaves, forbids them from doing what He wills, establishes the sacred law, and abrogates what He wills. Your only protector is Allah, Who will take care of your affairs, and you have no other helper to protect you from harm. Allah is the protector and guardian of all and has power over everything.

(١٠٨) أَمۡ تُرِيدُونَ أَن تَسۡ‍َٔلُواْ رَسُولَكُمۡ كَمَا سُئِلَ مُوسَىٰ مِن قَبۡلُۗ وَمَن يَتَبَدَّلِ ٱلۡكُفۡرَ بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ فَقَدۡ ضَلَّ سَوَآءَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ

O believers, it is not your nature to question your Prophet out of opposition and intransigence as the people of Moses questioned him before, like when they said: {Show us Allah openly} [Al-Nisa:153]. Whoever exchanges belief for disbelief has strayed from the middle way, the straight path.

(١٠٩) وَدَّ كَثِيرٞ مِّنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَوۡ يَرُدُّونَكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ إِيمَٰنِكُمۡ كُفَّارًا حَسَدٗا مِّنۡ عِندِ أَنفُسِهِم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا تَبَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ فَٱعۡفُواْ وَٱصۡفَحُواْ حَتَّىٰ يَأۡتِيَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَمۡرِهِۦٓۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

Many Jews and Christians, out of envy, wish to turn you back to disbelief after belief, worshipping idols as you did before when it has become clear to them that what the Prophet came with is the truth from Allah. O believers, forgive them for their actions and pardon them for their ignorance and the evil in their souls until Allah's instruction concerning them comes, which has come: the disbeliever is given a choice between embracing Islam, paying the Jizyah (poll tax), or fighting [the Muslims]. Allah is Omnipotent, and they cannot hinder Him.

After instructing the believers to be patient in the face of harm, Allah commands them to remain steadfast in their religion and strengthen their faith, stating:

(١١٠) وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَۚ وَمَا تُقَدِّمُواْ لِأَنفُسِكُم مِّنۡ خَيۡرٖ تَجِدُوهُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٞ

Establish the prayer entirely with all its pillars, obligatory and recommended acts, and give Zakat from your wealth to those who deserve it. Whatever righteous deeds you do in life before death will be a treasure for yourselves: the reward you will find with your Lord on the Day of Resurrection, and He will requite you. Allah sees all that you do and will recompense everyone accordingly.

(١١١) وَقَالُواْ لَن يَدۡخُلَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ إِلَّا مَن كَانَ هُودًا أَوۡ نَصَٰرَىٰۗ تِلۡكَ أَمَانِيُّهُمۡۗ قُلۡ هَاتُواْ بُرۡهَٰنَكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

The Jews and the Christians each said: Paradise would be for them exclusively. The Jews said: Only a Jew will enter Paradise, and the Christians said: Only a Christian will enter Paradise. These are just their vain desires and imaginings. Tell them, O Prophet, in reply: Bring proof of what you claim if you are truthful.

(١١٢) بَلَىٰۚ مَنۡ أَسۡلَمَ وَجۡهَهُۥ لِلَّهِ وَهُوَ مُحۡسِنٞ فَلَهُۥٓ أَجۡرُهُۥ عِندَ رَبِّهِۦ وَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

The matter is not as they claim. Only those who are sincere in directing themselves to Allah and do good in worshipping Him by following what the Prophet brought will enter Paradise, no matter what group they come from. They will have their reward with their Lord. They will not fear what awaits them in the Hereafter nor grieve over what they lost on earth. These descriptions only apply to Muslims after the Prophet Muhammad's mission.

Points of Benefit:

The matter is entirely up to Allah, so He changes whatever of His rulings and laws He wishes and keeps whatever He wishes of them, and all of that is with His knowledge and wisdom.
The envy of many of the People of the Book towards this nation, due to what Allah specified it with from faith and following the Messenger, to the point that they wished it would return to disbelief such as it was before.
