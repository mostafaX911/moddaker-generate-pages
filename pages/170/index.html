(١٥٦) ۞وَٱكۡتُبۡ لَنَا فِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةٗ وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ إِنَّا هُدۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَۚ قَالَ عَذَابِيٓ أُصِيبُ بِهِۦ مَنۡ أَشَآءُۖ وَرَحۡمَتِي وَسِعَتۡ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖۚ فَسَأَكۡتُبُهَا لِلَّذِينَ يَتَّقُونَ وَيُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

And make us among those You have honored in this life with blessings and well-being, given success to do good works, and among Your righteous slaves for whom You have prepared Paradise in the Afterlife. Indeed, we have turned to You in repentance and turned back, admitting our deficiencies. Allah, exalted is He, said: I inflict My torment to whom I will among those who act upon the causes of misery, and My Mercy encompasses everything in the world. So there is no creation except that Allah's Mercy has reached, and His favor and benevolence [has] enshrouded. I will decree My Mercy in the Afterlife for those who heed Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, who give the zakat (obligatory alms) from their wealth to the people deserving of it, and who have faith in Our signs.

(١٥٧) ٱلَّذِينَ يَتَّبِعُونَ ٱلرَّسُولَ ٱلنَّبِيَّ ٱلۡأُمِّيَّ ٱلَّذِي يَجِدُونَهُۥ مَكۡتُوبًا عِندَهُمۡ فِي ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِ وَٱلۡإِنجِيلِ يَأۡمُرُهُم بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَيَنۡهَىٰهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَيُحِلُّ لَهُمُ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ وَيُحَرِّمُ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡخَبَٰٓئِثَ وَيَضَعُ عَنۡهُمۡ إِصۡرَهُمۡ وَٱلۡأَغۡلَٰلَ ٱلَّتِي كَانَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ فَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِهِۦ وَعَزَّرُوهُ وَنَصَرُوهُ وَٱتَّبَعُواْ ٱلنُّورَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أُنزِلَ مَعَهُۥٓ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ

Those who follow Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, the illiterate prophet who cannot read or write, His Lord merely inspires him. He is the one whom they find his name, description, and prophethood written in the Torah sent down to Moses (peace be upon him), and the Gospel sent down to Jesus (peace be upon him). He commands them with that whose good and righteousness is known, and forbids them from that whose horridness is known in right minds and sound natures; he permits for them the delightful matters which contain no harm from food, drink, and intercourse, and he forbids from them the foul amongst them; he removes the onerous burdens which they were burdened with. So those who believed in him, from among the Israelites and others, aggrandized and honored him, supported him against whoever takes him as an enemy from the disbelievers, and followed the Quran which was revealed to him: those are the successful ones who attain what they seek and are averted from what they fear.

(١٥٨) قُلۡ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنِّي رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ إِلَيۡكُمۡ جَمِيعًا ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُۖ فَ‍َٔامِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ ٱلنَّبِيِّ ٱلۡأُمِّيِّ ٱلَّذِي يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ وَكَلِمَٰتِهِۦ وَٱتَّبِعُوهُ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَهۡتَدُونَ

Say, O Messenger: O people, I am a Messenger of Allah [sent] to all of you, Arabs and non-Arabs, Who He alone owns the dominion of the heavens and the earth. There is nothing rightfully worshipped other than Him, may He be Exalted. He brings life to the dead and brings death to the living. So believe, O people, in Allah and believe in Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم His Messenger Prophet, who neither reads nor writes; he has merely come with the revelation revealed to him by his Lord. He believes in Allah and what has been revealed to him and the other Prophets before him without any differentiation. And follow him in that which he has come with from his Lord, in hopes that you would be guided to that which holds your benefit in the [life of this] world and the Afterlife.

After mentioning the Israelites' worship of the calf, Allah notes the presence of a group that opposed it, stating:

(١٥٩) وَمِن قَوۡمِ مُوسَىٰٓ أُمَّةٞ يَهۡدُونَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَبِهِۦ يَعۡدِلُونَ

A group of Moses's people from the Israelites are upright upon the true religion. They direct people to it and rule with justice so they do not oppress.

Points of Benefit:

The Torah and the Gospel contained clear evidence regarding the mission of the Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, and the authenticity of his message.
The mercy of Allah encompasses everything, yet His mercy toward His servants varies in degrees, depending on their faith and righteous deeds.
Supplication can be general or detailed depending on the circumstances, and Moses (peace be upon him) was eloquent and detailed in his supplication in this context.
One aspect of the justice of Allah, the Almighty, is His fairness to the believing minority. He highlighted the characteristics of the Israelites that were inconsistent with perfection and contrary to guidance. Someone might mistakenly assume that these traits apply to all of them, but Allah, in His wisdom, mentions that among them is a group that is upright, guided, and guiding.
