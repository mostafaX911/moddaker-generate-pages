(١٦٠) وَقَطَّعۡنَٰهُمُ ٱثۡنَتَيۡ عَشۡرَةَ أَسۡبَاطًا أُمَمٗاۚ وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ مُوسَىٰٓ إِذِ ٱسۡتَسۡقَىٰهُ قَوۡمُهُۥٓ أَنِ ٱضۡرِب بِّعَصَاكَ ٱلۡحَجَرَۖ فَٱنۢبَجَسَتۡ مِنۡهُ ٱثۡنَتَا عَشۡرَةَ عَيۡنٗاۖ قَدۡ عَلِمَ كُلُّ أُنَاسٖ مَّشۡرَبَهُمۡۚ وَظَلَّلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡغَمَٰمَ وَأَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡمَنَّ وَٱلسَّلۡوَىٰۖ كُلُواْ مِن طَيِّبَٰتِ مَا رَزَقۡنَٰكُمۡۚ وَمَا ظَلَمُونَا وَلَٰكِن كَانُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَظۡلِمُونَ

We divided the Israelites into twelve tribes. When Moses's people requested that he ask Allah to give them drink, We revealed to him: Strike, O Moses, the rock with your staff. So, Moses struck it. Then, twelve springs gushed out from it according to the number of their tribes. Every tribe knew their specific spring to drink from, so no other tribe would share it with them. We also shaded them with the clouds, making them go where they would go and stop where they would stop. And from our blessings, We sent down a drink sweet like honey and small birds with excellent meat similar to quail. We told them: Eat from the pure things We have provided you. Their wrongdoing, denial of blessings, and not giving those blessings their due appreciation did not diminish Us in any way. Instead, they wronged themselves by diminishing their allotments when they brought themselves to the roads of ruin by opposing the command of Allah and denying His blessings.

(١٦١) وَإِذۡ قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱسۡكُنُواْ هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةَ وَكُلُواْ مِنۡهَا حَيۡثُ شِئۡتُمۡ وَقُولُواْ حِطَّةٞ وَٱدۡخُلُواْ ٱلۡبَابَ سُجَّدٗا نَّغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ خَطِيٓـَٰٔتِكُمۡۚ سَنَزِيدُ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Remember, O Messenger, when Allah said to the Israelites: Enter Jerusalem and eat from the fruits of the town anywhere within it anytime you like. Ask your Lord to efface your misdeeds and enter the gate, bowing humbly to your Lord. Then, if you do that, We will overlook your wrongs and give the good-doers even more of the good of this life and the next.

(١٦٢) فَبَدَّلَ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ قَوۡلًا غَيۡرَ ٱلَّذِي قِيلَ لَهُمۡ فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ رِجۡزٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَظۡلِمُونَ

So the unjust among them altered what they were commanded to say, so they said: a grain in a hair, instead of what they were commanded with of requesting forgiveness. They manipulated the action that they were commanded with, and so they entered crawling on their behinds instead of entering humbled to Allah, raising their heads; so We sent a torment upon them from the sky because of their injustice.

(١٦٣) وَسۡ‍َٔلۡهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِ ٱلَّتِي كَانَتۡ حَاضِرَةَ ٱلۡبَحۡرِ إِذۡ يَعۡدُونَ فِي ٱلسَّبۡتِ إِذۡ تَأۡتِيهِمۡ حِيتَانُهُمۡ يَوۡمَ سَبۡتِهِمۡ شُرَّعٗا وَيَوۡمَ لَا يَسۡبِتُونَ لَا تَأۡتِيهِمۡۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَبۡلُوهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡسُقُونَ

O Messenger, ask the Jews, in reminder to them of what Allah punished their predecessors with, about the story of the town near the sea when they would transgress the bounds of Allah by fishing on Saturday after they were prohibited from that; when Allah tested them such that the fish would come to them, apparent on the sea's surface on Saturday, and on the remaining days of the week, the fish would not come. Allah tested them with that because they abandoned obedience to Allah and committed acts of disobedience. So, they used trickery, casting their nets and digging their holes so the fish would get caught in them on Saturday. Then, on Sunday, they would take them and eat them.

Points of Benefit:

Ingratitude and disbelief are reasons for being deprived of blessings.
One of the reasons for the onset of punishment and the descent of torment is the manipulation and circumvention of the divine law, as it constitutes injustice and exceeds the limits set by Allah.
