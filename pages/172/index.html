(١٦٤) وَإِذۡ قَالَتۡ أُمَّةٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ لِمَ تَعِظُونَ قَوۡمًا ٱللَّهُ مُهۡلِكُهُمۡ أَوۡ مُعَذِّبُهُمۡ عَذَابٗا شَدِيدٗاۖ قَالُواْ مَعۡذِرَةً إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُمۡ وَلَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَّقُونَ

Mention, O Messenger, when a group among them prohibited them from this objectionable act and warned them against it. Then, another group said to them: Why do you advise a group who either Allah will destroy in this life due to the sins it committed or torment it severely on the Day of Resurrection? The advising ones said: Our advice to them is an excuse to Allah by doing what we were commanded with of enjoining good and forbidding evil so that we are not taken to account for leaving that off. And so that they might benefit from the admonishment and then abstain from the acts of disobedience they are engaged in.

(١٦٥) فَلَمَّا نَسُواْ مَا ذُكِّرُواْ بِهِۦٓ أَنجَيۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ يَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلسُّوٓءِ وَأَخَذۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ بِعَذَابِۢ بَ‍ِٔيسِۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡسُقُونَ

Then, when the disobedient disregarded what the admonishers reminded them of and did not refrain, We saved those who forbade evil from the torment and seized those who did wrong with their transgression of fishing on Saturday, with a severe torment because they departed from the obedience of Allah and their insistence on the act of disobedience.

(١٦٦) فَلَمَّا عَتَوۡاْ عَن مَّا نُهُواْ عَنۡهُ قُلۡنَا لَهُمۡ كُونُواْ قِرَدَةً خَٰسِ‍ِٔينَ

So when they transgressed the limits by disobeying Allah out of arrogance and stubbornness and did not accept admonishment, We said to them: O disobedient ones, be abased monkeys. Then, they became what We willed. Our command for something, when We will it, is merely saying: Be, then it is.

(١٦٧) وَإِذۡ تَأَذَّنَ رَبُّكَ لَيَبۡعَثَنَّ عَلَيۡهِمۡ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ مَن يَسُومُهُمۡ سُوٓءَ ٱلۡعَذَابِۗ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَسَرِيعُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ وَإِنَّهُۥ لَغَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Mention, O Messenger, when Allah declared clearly, in no uncertain terms, that the Jews will certainly be ruled by those who will humiliate and belittle them in this worldly life until the Day of Resurrection. Indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, is surely swift to punish those who disobey Him, such that He might [even] hasten their punishment to be in this life. Indeed, He is surely forgiving of the sins of His slaves who repent to Him and is Merciful to them.

(١٦٨) وَقَطَّعۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أُمَمٗاۖ مِّنۡهُمُ ٱلصَّٰلِحُونَ وَمِنۡهُمۡ دُونَ ذَٰلِكَۖ وَبَلَوۡنَٰهُم بِٱلۡحَسَنَٰتِ وَٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ

We distributed them throughout the earth and divided them into sects after they were once united. Some of them were righteous, upholding the rights of Allah and the rights of His slaves. Some of them were moderate. Others were excessive upon themselves with acts of disobedience. We tested them with ease and hardship, hoping they would turn back from what they were doing.

(١٦٩) فَخَلَفَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ خَلۡفٞ وَرِثُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ يَأۡخُذُونَ عَرَضَ هَٰذَا ٱلۡأَدۡنَىٰ وَيَقُولُونَ سَيُغۡفَرُ لَنَا وَإِن يَأۡتِهِمۡ عَرَضٞ مِّثۡلُهُۥ يَأۡخُذُوهُۚ أَلَمۡ يُؤۡخَذۡ عَلَيۡهِم مِّيثَٰقُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أَن لَّا يَقُولُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلۡحَقَّ وَدَرَسُواْ مَا فِيهِۗ وَٱلدَّارُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرٞ لِّلَّذِينَ يَتَّقُونَۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

Then, after those, the people of evil came to follow. They took the Torah from their predecessors, reading it and not acting upon what it contained. They took the lowly commodities of this life as a bribe for their distortion of the book of Allah and to judge with other than what Allah sent down in it. They would also speak favorably about themselves that Allah would forgive their sins. If any meager worldly payment came to them, they would take it time and time again. Did Allah not take covenants and contracts with them that they should not say anything about Allah except the truth without making any distortions or changes? They did not unknowingly leave off acting upon the book, but rather, they knew. They read what was in it and knew it, so their sin is even worse. The abode of the hereafter and the everlasting bliss within it are better than those passing commodities for the ones who take heed of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. Will those who took such measly commodities not realize that what Allah has prepared in the hereafter for the heedful is better and longer lasting?

(١٧٠) وَٱلَّذِينَ يُمَسِّكُونَ بِٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ إِنَّا لَا نُضِيعُ أَجۡرَ ٱلۡمُصۡلِحِينَ

Those who hold tightly to the book, implement it, and establish the prayer by maintaining its stated times, prerequisites, obligations, and supererogatory elements will be recompensed by Allah according to their deeds, for Allah does not lose the reward of those whose work is righteous.

Points of Benefit:

When the punishment of Allah descends upon a people due to their sins, those who used to enjoin good and forbid evil among them are saved from it.
It is imperative to beware of the punishment of Allah, as it can be terrifying in this world, as demonstrated when a group of the Israelites were transformed into apes due to their disobedience.
Allah decreed humiliation and abasement upon the Israelites and permitted that they be afflicted with punishment periodically due to their wrongdoing and deviation.
The joys of this world, no matter how grand they may seem, are trivial and meager compared to the eternal bliss of the Hereafter.
The best deeds of a servant, after faith, are the establishment of prayer, as it is the pillar of the religion.
