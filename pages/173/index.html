(١٧١) ۞وَإِذۡ نَتَقۡنَا ٱلۡجَبَلَ فَوۡقَهُمۡ كَأَنَّهُۥ ظُلَّةٞ وَظَنُّوٓاْ أَنَّهُۥ وَاقِعُۢ بِهِمۡ خُذُواْ مَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰكُم بِقُوَّةٖ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ مَا فِيهِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ

Mention, O Muhammad, when we uprooted the mountain and raised it over the Israelites when they refused to accept what was in the Torah. The mountain became like a cloud shading their heads, and they were sure it would fall upon them. It was said to them: Take what We have given you with seriousness, diligence, and determination, and remember the rulings in it that Allah has legislated for you and do not forget them, in hopes that you will take heed of Allah if you do that.

(١٧٢) وَإِذۡ أَخَذَ رَبُّكَ مِنۢ بَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ مِن ظُهُورِهِمۡ ذُرِّيَّتَهُمۡ وَأَشۡهَدَهُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ أَلَسۡتُ بِرَبِّكُمۡۖ قَالُواْ بَلَىٰ شَهِدۡنَآۚ أَن تَقُولُواْ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ إِنَّا كُنَّا عَنۡ هَٰذَا غَٰفِلِينَ

Remember, O Muhammad, when your Lord brought out the descendants of Adam's children from their loins and had them affirm His lordship with the acknowledgment he placed in their innate nature that He is their Creator and Lord, saying to them: Am I not your Lord? They all said: Rather, You are our Lord. He said: We only tested you and took the covenant upon you so that you would not deny Allah's argument against you on the Day of Resurrection and say that you had no knowledge of that.

(١٧٣) أَوۡ تَقُولُوٓاْ إِنَّمَآ أَشۡرَكَ ءَابَآؤُنَا مِن قَبۡلُ وَكُنَّا ذُرِّيَّةٗ مِّنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡۖ أَفَتُهۡلِكُنَا بِمَا فَعَلَ ٱلۡمُبۡطِلُونَ

Or argue that your forefathers were the ones who broke the covenant, for they made partners with Allah, and that you were blindly following your forefathers in the polytheism you found them upon and then say: Do You, O our Lord, hold us accountable for the actions of our forefathers who invalidated their deeds by associating partners with Allah and thus torment us? So there is no sin on us for our ignorance and blind following of our forefathers.

(١٧٤) وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُفَصِّلُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ وَلَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ

Just as We clarified the signs regarding the destination of the disbelieving nations, We clarify them to these people in hopes that they revert from the polytheism they are upon to the Oneness of Allah and worshipping Him alone, just as is in their covenant with Allah that they committed themselves to.

(١٧٥) وَٱتۡلُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ نَبَأَ ٱلَّذِيٓ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ ءَايَٰتِنَا فَٱنسَلَخَ مِنۡهَا فَأَتۡبَعَهُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ فَكَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡغَاوِينَ

Recite, O Messenger, to the Israelites the news of a man from among them whom We gave Our signs to such that he knew them and understood the truth they indicated. But he did not act according to them. Rather, he left them and removed himself from them. Then the Devil followed him and became a close companion for him, so he became one of the misguided and ruined after he had been among the guided and saved.

(١٧٦) وَلَوۡ شِئۡنَا لَرَفَعۡنَٰهُ بِهَا وَلَٰكِنَّهُۥٓ أَخۡلَدَ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱتَّبَعَ هَوَىٰهُۚ فَمَثَلُهُۥ كَمَثَلِ ٱلۡكَلۡبِ إِن تَحۡمِلۡ عَلَيۡهِ يَلۡهَثۡ أَوۡ تَتۡرُكۡهُ يَلۡهَثۚ ذَّٰلِكَ مَثَلُ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَاۚ فَٱقۡصُصِ ٱلۡقَصَصَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ

If We wished to benefit him through these signs, We would have raised him in rank by allowing him to act according to them, thus rising in this world and the Afterlife. But he chose what would lead to his abandonment when he leaned towards the lusts of the worldly life, preferring his worldly life to his Afterlife. Thus, his example, in his extreme greed for this world, is like the example of a dog, which pants with its tongue in any circumstance, whether it is left alone or chased away. That example is the example of those misguided by their rejection of Our signs. So relate the stories to them, O Messenger, in hopes that they think, then abstain from the rejection and misguidance which they are in.

(١٧٧) سَآءَ مَثَلًا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَأَنفُسَهُمۡ كَانُواْ يَظۡلِمُونَ

None is worse than the people who rejected Our arguments and proofs and did not believe them, wronging their souls with that by bringing them to the roads of ruin.

(١٧٨) مَن يَهۡدِ ٱللَّهُ فَهُوَ ٱلۡمُهۡتَدِيۖ وَمَن يُضۡلِلۡ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ

Whomever Allah gives success of guidance to His straight path is truly the one who is guided, and whomever He distances from the straight path, those are truly the ones undermining their own fortunes, who lost themselves and their families on the Day of Resurrection. Is that not the clear loss?

Points of Benefit:

The purpose of divine scriptures is to act upon them, not just to recite them as chanting on the tongue. This would be a form of rejecting their true objective.
Allah created in humans, from the time of their formation, an inherent understanding of the evidence of His Oneness. If their nature remains sound and is not corrupted, they comprehend these proofs and act in accordance with them.
In the verses, there is a lesson for the successful ones: to act upon the signs of the Quran, recognizing the favor of Allah upon them by guiding them to implement these signs, purifying their souls.
In the verses, there is guidance for Muslims to turn to Allah, seeking guidance from Him and seeking protection from the pitfalls of misguidance.
