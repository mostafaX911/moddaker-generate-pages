(١٧٩) وَلَقَدۡ ذَرَأۡنَا لِجَهَنَّمَ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِۖ لَهُمۡ قُلُوبٞ لَّا يَفۡقَهُونَ بِهَا وَلَهُمۡ أَعۡيُنٞ لَّا يُبۡصِرُونَ بِهَا وَلَهُمۡ ءَاذَانٞ لَّا يَسۡمَعُونَ بِهَآۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ كَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ بَلۡ هُمۡ أَضَلُّۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡغَٰفِلُونَ

And We made for Hell many people from humanity and the jinn because of Our Knowledge that they would do the actions of its people. They have hearts with which they do not understand what will benefit them and [what will] harm them; eyes which they do not use to see the signs of Allah within themselves and upon the horizons so that they can learn from them; and they have ears which they do not use to listen to the verses of Allah so that they might reflect what they contain. Those described by these characteristics resemble cattle in their lack of reasoning. Rather, they are further in misguidance than the cattle. Those are the ones oblivious to faith in Allah and the Last Day.

(١٨٠) وَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡأَسۡمَآءُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ فَٱدۡعُوهُ بِهَاۖ وَذَرُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ يُلۡحِدُونَ فِيٓ أَسۡمَٰٓئِهِۦۚ سَيُجۡزَوۡنَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

To Allah (may He be Exalted) belong the most beautiful names that indicate His majesty and perfection. So use them to call on Allah when you ask for whatever you wish and praise Him with them. And leave those who turn away from the truth of these names by assigning them to others, negating them from Him, distorting their meanings, or making others similar to Him with them. We will repay these people who deviate from the truth regarding them with painful torment for what they used to do.

(١٨١) وَمِمَّنۡ خَلَقۡنَآ أُمَّةٞ يَهۡدُونَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَبِهِۦ يَعۡدِلُونَ

Among those whom We created is a group of people who are guided by the truth within themselves and call others to it so that they are [also] guided. They rule with justice so they do not oppress.

(١٨٢) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا سَنَسۡتَدۡرِجُهُم مِّنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

[As for] those who rejected Our signs and did not believe in them, but rather denied them, We will open the gates of provision for them, not to honor them, but rather to lead them on gradually such that they continue upon the misguidance they are upon. Then, Our torment will strike them at a moment of unawareness.

(١٨٣) وَأُمۡلِي لَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ كَيۡدِي مَتِينٌ

I delay punishment from them until they think they will not be punished, so they persist in their rejection and disbelief until their torment is multiplied upon them. Indeed, My Planning is strong, so I show benevolence to them while I intend to forsake them.

(١٨٤) أَوَلَمۡ يَتَفَكَّرُواْۗ مَا بِصَاحِبِهِم مِّن جِنَّةٍۚ إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا نَذِيرٞ مُّبِينٌ

Do those who reject the signs of Allah and His Messenger not ponder such that they would work their minds so it would be clear to them that Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم is not insane? He is but a messenger from Allah, Who sent him as a clear warner of Allah's torment.

(١٨٥) أَوَلَمۡ يَنظُرُواْ فِي مَلَكُوتِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ مِن شَيۡءٖ وَأَنۡ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُونَ قَدِ ٱقۡتَرَبَ أَجَلُهُمۡۖ فَبِأَيِّ حَدِيثِۢ بَعۡدَهُۥ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Do these people not look with a gaze of reflection to the dominion of Allah in the heavens and the earth, all that Allah created within them of animals and plants and other things, and at their own life spans, the end of which may be near, so that they might repent to Allah before the time has gone? If they do not have faith in the Quran and what it contains of promises and threats, then what other book will they believe in?

(١٨٦) مَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَلَا هَادِيَ لَهُۥۚ وَيَذَرُهُمۡ فِي طُغۡيَٰنِهِمۡ يَعۡمَهُونَ

Whoever Allah forsakes from guidance to the truth, sending them away from the right path, there is no guide to direct them back to guidance. Allah leaves them in their misguidance and disbelief, confused, not guided to anything.

(١٨٧) يَسۡ‍َٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلسَّاعَةِ أَيَّانَ مُرۡسَىٰهَاۖ قُلۡ إِنَّمَا عِلۡمُهَا عِندَ رَبِّيۖ لَا يُجَلِّيهَا لِوَقۡتِهَآ إِلَّا هُوَۚ ثَقُلَتۡ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ لَا تَأۡتِيكُمۡ إِلَّا بَغۡتَةٗۗ يَسۡ‍َٔلُونَكَ كَأَنَّكَ حَفِيٌّ عَنۡهَاۖ قُلۡ إِنَّمَا عِلۡمُهَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

Those who reject the truth and are stubborn ask you about the Hour: When will it occur and be known? Say to them, O Muhammad: the knowledge of it is not with me, nor anyone else. Knowledge of it is with Allah alone. None will reveal it at its decreed time except Allah. The matter of its emergence is hidden from those in the heavens and those in the earth, and it will only come upon you unexpectedly. They ask you, O Muhammad, about the Hour as if you are eager to know about it, not realizing that you did not ask about it because of the perfection of your knowledge of your Lord. Say to them, O Muhammad: The knowledge of the Hour is with Allah alone, but most people do not know that.

Points of Benefit:

Allah created for humans instruments of perception and knowledge - hearts, eyes, and ears - to attain benefits and ward off harms.
Supplicating with the beautiful names of Allah is a means to have prayers answered. One should invoke Allah using names that are fitting for the request, for example, saying, "O Allah, accept my repentance, O Who Accepts Repentance."
Reflecting on the greatness of the heavens and the earth, and arriving through this contemplation at the realization that Allah, the Almighty, is the deserving entity of divinity above all others, for He is the unique Creator.
