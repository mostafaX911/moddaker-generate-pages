(١٨٨) قُل لَّآ أَمۡلِكُ لِنَفۡسِي نَفۡعٗا وَلَا ضَرًّا إِلَّا مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ وَلَوۡ كُنتُ أَعۡلَمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبَ لَٱسۡتَكۡثَرۡتُ مِنَ ٱلۡخَيۡرِ وَمَا مَسَّنِيَ ٱلسُّوٓءُۚ إِنۡ أَنَا۠ إِلَّا نَذِيرٞ وَبَشِيرٞ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Say to them, O Muhammad: I cannot bring good to myself or remove any harm from myself, except by Allah's will. Those things only belong to Allah, and I only know what Allah taught me. Thus, I do not know the unseen. If I did know the unseen, I would have used the means that I know to bring me benefits and protect me from ruinous affairs due to my knowledge of things before they happen and what they result in. I am only a messenger from Allah, alarming of a painful punishment, and bringing the good news of Allah's generous reward for a community who believe that I am a messenger from Him (may He be Exalted) and affirm what I have come with.

(١٨٩) ۞هُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن نَّفۡسٖ وَٰحِدَةٖ وَجَعَلَ مِنۡهَا زَوۡجَهَا لِيَسۡكُنَ إِلَيۡهَاۖ فَلَمَّا تَغَشَّىٰهَا حَمَلَتۡ حَمۡلًا خَفِيفٗا فَمَرَّتۡ بِهِۦۖ فَلَمَّآ أَثۡقَلَت دَّعَوَا ٱللَّهَ رَبَّهُمَا لَئِنۡ ءَاتَيۡتَنَا صَٰلِحٗا لَّنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلشَّٰكِرِينَ

It is He who brought you into being, O men and women, from one soul, Adam (peace be upon him), and created from Adam (peace be upon him) his wife, Eve. He made her from his rib so he would find comfort and tranquility in her. When a husband had relations with his wife, she became pregnant with a light load, not noticing it because it was at the beginning of the pregnancy. She continued with this pregnancy of hers, going about her needs without heftiness. Then, when she became heavy with it upon having grown in her belly, both spouses called upon their Lord, saying: O our Lord, if You grant us a healthy child, we will surely be among the thankful for Your blessings.

(١٩٠) فَلَمَّآ ءَاتَىٰهُمَا صَٰلِحٗا جَعَلَا لَهُۥ شُرَكَآءَ فِيمَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمَاۚ فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

Then, when Allah responded to their prayer and gave them a healthy child as they asked, they made partners with Allah in what He granted them, for they made their child a slave [in name] to other than Him. Transcendent is Allah and far above [having] any partner, for He is the Singular in lordship and godship.

(١٩١) أَيُشۡرِكُونَ مَا لَا يَخۡلُقُ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَهُمۡ يُخۡلَقُونَ

Do they make these idols and other things to be partners with Allah in worship when they know that they do not create anything such that they would deserve worship? Rather, they [themselves] are created, so how do they make them partners with Allah?

(١٩٢) وَلَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ لَهُمۡ نَصۡرٗا وَلَآ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَنصُرُونَ

These objects of worship cannot help their worshippers or even help themselves, so how do they worship them?

(١٩٣) وَإِن تَدۡعُوهُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡهُدَىٰ لَا يَتَّبِعُوكُمۡۚ سَوَآءٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡ أَدَعَوۡتُمُوهُمۡ أَمۡ أَنتُمۡ صَٰمِتُونَ

And If you, O polytheists, call these idols which you take as gods instead of Allah who guides to guidance, they will not respond to what you call them to, nor will they follow you. So it is the same to them whether you call them or keep silent because they are just inanimate objects; they do not reason, hear, or speak.

(١٩٤) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ عِبَادٌ أَمۡثَالُكُمۡۖ فَٱدۡعُوهُمۡ فَلۡيَسۡتَجِيبُواْ لَكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

Indeed, those you worship, O polytheists, instead of Allah, were created for Allah and belong to Him, so they are the same as you in that way. However, you are in a better state because you are living: you speak, walk, hear, and see, while your idols are not. So call on them and let them respond to you if you are telling the truth in what you claim about them.

(١٩٥) أَلَهُمۡ أَرۡجُلٞ يَمۡشُونَ بِهَآۖ أَمۡ لَهُمۡ أَيۡدٖ يَبۡطِشُونَ بِهَآۖ أَمۡ لَهُمۡ أَعۡيُنٞ يُبۡصِرُونَ بِهَآۖ أَمۡ لَهُمۡ ءَاذَانٞ يَسۡمَعُونَ بِهَاۗ قُلِ ٱدۡعُواْ شُرَكَآءَكُمۡ ثُمَّ كِيدُونِ فَلَا تُنظِرُونِ

Do these idols, which they took as gods, have feet they walk with such that they go in pursuit of their needs? Or hands they firmly defend you with? Or do they have eyes with which they see what is unseen to you so they inform you? Or ears with which they hear what is hidden from you so they relate its knowledge to you? If they are incapable of all that, then how do you worship them, hoping to bring benefit or fend off harm? Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: Call upon those whom you have made equal with Allah. Then plot to hurt me and give me no respite.

Points of Benefit:

The verses clarify the ignorance of those who seek the Prophet, peace be upon him, and invoke him for obtaining benefits or warding off harm. Indeed, benefits are obtained through what he was sent with, namely glad tidings and warnings.
By His grace, Allah has made for a man a spouse from among his own kind so that he may find comfort in her company, not feel estranged, and find solace, thus fulfilling the divine wisdom in reproduction.
It is not fitting for the best and noblest of creatures, the human being, to engage in the worship of the lowly and inferior, such as stones, wood, and other false deities.
