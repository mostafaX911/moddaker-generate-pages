(١٩٦) إِنَّ وَلِـِّۧيَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي نَزَّلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَۖ وَهُوَ يَتَوَلَّى ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

Truly, my supporter and helper is Allah, Who keeps me safe so I do not put my hope in anyone besides Him. I fear nothing from your idols, for He is the One who sent down the Quran upon me as a guidance for the people, and it is He Who takes charge of those who do good among His slaves, so he protects and supports them.

(١٩٧) وَٱلَّذِينَ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ لَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ نَصۡرَكُمۡ وَلَآ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَنصُرُونَ

Those you call upon, O polytheists, from amongst these idols are unable to support you nor themselves, for they are utterly incapable. So, how can you call upon them instead of Allah?

(١٩٨) وَإِن تَدۡعُوهُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡهُدَىٰ لَا يَسۡمَعُواْۖ وَتَرَىٰهُمۡ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَيۡكَ وَهُمۡ لَا يُبۡصِرُونَ

If you, O polytheists, call your idols, which you worship instead of Allah, to uprightness, they will not hear you calling them. You see them facing you with fashioned eyes, inanimate objects that cannot see, for they used to make statues in the form of people or animals. They had hands, feet, and eyes but were inanimate, with no life in them nor motion.

(١٩٩) خُذِ ٱلۡعَفۡوَ وَأۡمُرۡ بِٱلۡعُرۡفِ وَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنِ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِينَ

Accept from people, O Messenger, what they are able to do, and the actions and character that are easy for them. Do not burden them with what their natures do not allow, for that will drive them away. Command with every beautiful saying and excellent action, and turn away from the ignorant so do not face them with their ignorance. So whoever harms you, do not harm them, and whoever denies you [something], do not deny them.

(٢٠٠) وَإِمَّا يَنزَغَنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ نَزۡغٞ فَٱسۡتَعِذۡ بِٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّهُۥ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ

And if you feel, O Messenger, that the Devil has inflicted you with a whisper or deterrence from doing good, then turn to Allah for security and seek refuge with Him, for He hears what you say and knows what you do when you seek protection, so He will protect you from the Devil.

(٢٠١) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْ إِذَا مَسَّهُمۡ طَٰٓئِفٞ مِّنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ تَذَكَّرُواْ فَإِذَا هُم مُّبۡصِرُونَ

Those who are heedful of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, when inflicted by a whisper from the Devil, then commit wrongs; they remember the splendor of Allah, His punishment for the disobedient, and His reward for the obedient. So they repent from their wrongs and turn back to their Lord. Then [suddenly] they have become upright upon the truth, awoken from what they were upon and desisted.

(٢٠٢) وَإِخۡوَٰنُهُمۡ يَمُدُّونَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡغَيِّ ثُمَّ لَا يُقۡصِرُونَ

And [as for] the brethren of the devils from among the wicked and disbelievers, the devils increase them in misguidance with sin after sin. They do not cease: neither the devils from deviating and misguiding nor the wicked from the humans from submitting and doing evil.

(٢٠٣) وَإِذَا لَمۡ تَأۡتِهِم بِ‍َٔايَةٖ قَالُواْ لَوۡلَا ٱجۡتَبَيۡتَهَاۚ قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَتَّبِعُ مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ مِن رَّبِّيۚ هَٰذَا بَصَآئِرُ مِن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٞ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

And if you, O Messenger, come with a sign, they reject it and turn away from it; and if you do not come to them with a sign, they say: Would you not invent a sign yourself and create it? Say to them, O Messenger: It is not for me to come with a sign from myself. I do not follow except that which Allah reveals to me. This Quran I recite to you is [a collection of] arguments and proofs from Allah, your Creator and the disposer of your affairs, and [it is] guidance and mercy for the believers among His slaves. As for those other than the believers, then they are lost and wretched ones.

(٢٠٤) وَإِذَا قُرِئَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ فَٱسۡتَمِعُواْ لَهُۥ وَأَنصِتُواْ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ

When the Quran is recited, listen to its recitation, do not speak, and do not busy yourself with other than it in hopes that Allah will have mercy on you.

(٢٠٥) وَٱذۡكُر رَّبَّكَ فِي نَفۡسِكَ تَضَرُّعٗا وَخِيفَةٗ وَدُونَ ٱلۡجَهۡرِ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ بِٱلۡغُدُوِّ وَٱلۡأٓصَالِ وَلَا تَكُن مِّنَ ٱلۡغَٰفِلِينَ

And remember, O Messenger, your Lord Allah humbly, modestly, and fearfully. Make your supplication moderate between raising the voice and lowering it at the beginning and end of the day for the virtue of these two times. And do not be among those inattentive to the remembrance of Allah Exalted.

(٢٠٦) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ عِندَ رَبِّكَ لَا يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ عَنۡ عِبَادَتِهِۦ وَيُسَبِّحُونَهُۥ وَلَهُۥ يَسۡجُدُونَۤ۩

Indeed, those with your Lord, O Messenger, from the angels, never abstain from His worship, may He be Exalted. Rather, they yield to it submissively and unceasingly. They declare the perfection of Allah, day and night, from that which does not befit Him and prostrate to Him alone.

Points of Benefit:

It is the duty of the rational person to worship Allah, as He is the One who brings about the benefits of religion by revealing the Book containing great knowledge in matters of faith. He also brings about the benefits of this world by supporting and safeguarding the righteous among His servants and granting them victory, so that enmity from those who oppose them does not harm them.
In the verses, there is a promise for the upright Muslims on the path of their Prophet, peace be upon him, that Allah will grant them victory just as He granted victory to His Prophet and his allies.
The verses encompass the collective virtues. Therefore, it is incumbent upon the servant to forgive those who wrong him, give to those who deprive him, and maintain ties with those who sever them.
When a servant is touched by an evil impulse from Satan—leading him to commit a forbidden act or neglect a duty—it is incumbent upon him to seek forgiveness from Allah and rectify any shortcomings through sincere repentance and righteous deeds.
