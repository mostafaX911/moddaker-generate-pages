Spoils of War (Surah Al-Anfal)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Gratitude for Allah's victory at Badr and enumerating the patterns of triumph and defeat.

Explanation:

(١) يَسۡ‍َٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلۡأَنفَالِۖ قُلِ ٱلۡأَنفَالُ لِلَّهِ وَٱلرَّسُولِۖ فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَصۡلِحُواْ ذَاتَ بَيۡنِكُمۡۖ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥٓ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Your companions ask you, O Messenger, about the war booty, how it should be distributed, and to whom. Say, O Messenger, in answer to their question: The spoils of war are for Allah and His Messenger, and it is for Allah and His Messenger to judge what to do with it and how to distribute it. You only have to submit and comply. So take heed of Allah, O believers, by complying with His commands and abstaining from His prohibitions. Resolve whatever is between you of severed relations and abandonment with affection, reconciliation, good character, and pardon. Adhere to obeying Allah and His Messenger if you truly believe because faith leads to obedience and staying far away from disobedience. This question was raised after the battle of Badr.

(٢) إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِرَ ٱللَّهُ وَجِلَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَإِذَا تُلِيَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥ زَادَتۡهُمۡ إِيمَٰنٗا وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ

The true believers are merely those whose hearts have fear when Allah, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, is mentioned, which leads their hearts and bodies to obedience; when Allah's verses are recited, they ponder them, which increases their faith; and they rely on their Lord alone to bring about what is good for them and to ward off what harms them.

(٣) ٱلَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ

Those who consistently offer the prayer on time in its complete form and spend from what We have provided them on what is obligatory and recommended.

(٤) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ حَقّٗاۚ لَّهُمۡ دَرَجَٰتٌ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ وَمَغۡفِرَةٞ وَرِزۡقٞ كَرِيمٞ

Those described as having these qualities are true believers because they combine the qualities of faith and open [practice of] Islam. Their recompense will be high stations with their Lord, forgiveness for their sins, and a generous provision, which is the bliss that Allah has prepared for them.

(٥) كَمَآ أَخۡرَجَكَ رَبُّكَ مِنۢ بَيۡتِكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَإِنَّ فَرِيقٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ لَكَٰرِهُونَ

Just as when Allah, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, took the distribution of the spoils of war from you all after you differed and disputed over its distribution and He made the decision for Allah and His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, Your Lord also ordered you, O Messenger, by sending revelation to you to leave Medina to meet the polytheists [in battle] while a party of the believers disdained doing that.

(٦) يُجَٰدِلُونَكَ فِي ٱلۡحَقِّ بَعۡدَ مَا تَبَيَّنَ كَأَنَّمَا يُسَاقُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡمَوۡتِ وَهُمۡ يَنظُرُونَ

This group of believers argued with you, O Messenger, about fighting the polytheists after it became clear to them that it would happen, [arguing] as if being driven to death, looking at it with their own eyes. They argued due to the severity of their disdain for going out to battle because they had not gotten ready for it nor prepared its preparations.

(٧) وَإِذۡ يَعِدُكُمُ ٱللَّهُ إِحۡدَى ٱلطَّآئِفَتَيۡنِ أَنَّهَا لَكُمۡ وَتَوَدُّونَ أَنَّ غَيۡرَ ذَاتِ ٱلشَّوۡكَةِ تَكُونُ لَكُمۡ وَيُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ أَن يُحِقَّ ٱلۡحَقَّ بِكَلِمَٰتِهِۦ وَيَقۡطَعَ دَابِرَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Remember, O arguing believers, when Allah promised you victory over one of the two parties of polytheists, either the caravan and the wealth it contained so you would take it as booty, or the squad so you would fight and defeat them. You preferred victory over the caravan due to the simplicity and ease of conquering it without fighting. However, Allah wanted the truth established by ordering you to fight to kill the polytheists' leaders and capture many of them so that the strength of Islam would become apparent.

(٨) لِيُحِقَّ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَيُبۡطِلَ ٱلۡبَٰطِلَ وَلَوۡ كَرِهَ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ

So that Allah would establish the truth by making Islam and its people victorious by demonstrating the affairs that bear witness to its truthfulness, and so that He (may He be Exalted) expose the falsehood by showing proof against it. Even if the polytheists hate that, Allah will show it.

Points of Benefit:

It is incumbent upon the servant to nurture and strengthen his faith because faith can either increase or decrease. It increases through acts of obedience and decreases through their opposites.
Argumentation is appropriate and beneficial when the truth is ambiguous and matters are unclear. However, once clarity and evidence are established, there is nothing left but submission and acknowledgment.
The matter of distributing spoils of war is left to the discretion of the Prophet, peace be upon him, and legal judgments refer back to Allah and His Messenger, not to anyone else.
The desire to achieve divine victory for the believers, to uphold the truth, and to invalidate falsehood.
