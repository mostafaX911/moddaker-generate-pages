(٩) إِذۡ تَسۡتَغِيثُونَ رَبَّكُمۡ فَٱسۡتَجَابَ لَكُمۡ أَنِّي مُمِدُّكُم بِأَلۡفٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ مُرۡدِفِينَ

Recall the day of Badr when you all asked for assistance from Allah in defeating your enemies. Then, He answered you, O believers, in that He would reinforce and assist you with one thousand successive angels, one following the other.

(١٠) وَمَا جَعَلَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا بُشۡرَىٰ وَلِتَطۡمَئِنَّ بِهِۦ قُلُوبُكُمۡۚ وَمَا ٱلنَّصۡرُ إِلَّا مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٌ

Allah only reinforced you, O believers, with angels as glad tidings of His support for you against your enemies and so that your hearts would be tranquil in the assurance of victory. Victory is not due to the strength of numbers or the supply of arms. It is only from Allah, may He be Exalted. Indeed, Allah is Mighty in His sovereignty, and no one can overpower him. He is Wise in His legislation and decree.

(١١) إِذۡ يُغَشِّيكُمُ ٱلنُّعَاسَ أَمَنَةٗ مِّنۡهُ وَيُنَزِّلُ عَلَيۡكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ لِّيُطَهِّرَكُم بِهِۦ وَيُذۡهِبَ عَنكُمۡ رِجۡزَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ وَلِيَرۡبِطَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِكُمۡ وَيُثَبِّتَ بِهِ ٱلۡأَقۡدَامَ

Remember, O believers, when Allah cast drowsiness upon you as a safeguard from the fear of your enemies which befell you and sent down rain upon you from the sky to cleanse you from impurities, remove the whisperings of the Devil, make your hearts firm so that your bodies are firm when meeting the enemy, and make your feet steady by clumping the sandy ground so that [your] feet would not slip on it.

(١٢) إِذۡ يُوحِي رَبُّكَ إِلَى ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ أَنِّي مَعَكُمۡ فَثَبِّتُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْۚ سَأُلۡقِي فِي قُلُوبِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ٱلرُّعۡبَ فَٱضۡرِبُواْ فَوۡقَ ٱلۡأَعۡنَاقِ وَٱضۡرِبُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ كُلَّ بَنَانٖ

When your Lord, O Prophet, revealed to the angels whom Allah had reinforced the believers with at Badr: I am with you, O angels, in aid and support, so strengthen the believers' resolve to fight their enemies. I will cast intense fear into the hearts of those who disbelieve. So, O believers, strike the necks of the disbelievers so that they die and strike their joints and limbs so they are incapacitated from fighting you.

(١٣) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ شَآقُّواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥۚ وَمَن يُشَاقِقِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ

That killing and striking of limbs which befell the disbelievers was because they opposed Allah and His Messenger. So, they did not fulfill what they were ordered to do and did not cease what they were forbidden from. Whoever opposes Allah and His Messenger in that, then indeed, Allah is severe in punishment to them with killing and capture in this life and with the Fire in the Afterlife.

(١٤) ذَٰلِكُمۡ فَذُوقُوهُ وَأَنَّ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ

That is the torment mentioned for you, O opposers to Allah and His Messenger, so taste it in advance in this worldly life. Then, if you die upon your disbelief and obstinance, you will have the torment of the Fire in the hereafter.

(١٥) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا لَقِيتُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ زَحۡفٗا فَلَا تُوَلُّوهُمُ ٱلۡأَدۡبَارَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, when you meet the polytheists at close quarters in battle, do not part them in defeat and turn your backs to them in retreat, but rather, stay firm facing towards them and be patient up meeting them [in battle], for Allah is with you with His aid and support.

(١٦) وَمَن يُوَلِّهِمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ دُبُرَهُۥٓ إِلَّا مُتَحَرِّفٗا لِّقِتَالٍ أَوۡ مُتَحَيِّزًا إِلَىٰ فِئَةٖ فَقَدۡ بَآءَ بِغَضَبٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَمَأۡوَىٰهُ جَهَنَّمُۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

Whoever turns his back to them, fleeing from them, not in inclination to battling them by exhibiting retreat as a trick from him while he intends to return to them, nor joining forces with a group of the Muslims present [on the battlefield] seeking support from it, then he will come back with Allah's anger and rightly deserve it. His place in the hereafter will be Hell. What a miserable fate and destination he reached.

Points of Benefit:

The verses reflect the great care of Allah for the condition of His believing servants, facilitating the means by which their faith is strengthened, their steps are affirmed, and adversity and Satanic whispers are removed from them.
Victory is in the Hand of Allah, and it comes from Him alone. It is not determined by the abundance or scarcity of numbers, despite the importance of proper preparation.
Fleeing from the battlefield without a valid excuse is among the major sins.
The verses teach the believers the principles of military combat, including obedience to Allah and the Messenger, standing firm in the face of enemies, and exercising patience during encounters.
