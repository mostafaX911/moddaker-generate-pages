(١٧) فَلَمۡ تَقۡتُلُوهُمۡ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَتَلَهُمۡۚ وَمَا رَمَيۡتَ إِذۡ رَمَيۡتَ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ رَمَىٰ وَلِيُبۡلِيَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ مِنۡهُ بَلَآءً حَسَنًاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٞ

O believers, you did not kill the polytheists on the Day of Badr with your own power and strength; rather, Allah helped you with that. And you did not throw, O Prophet, at the disbelievers when you threw; rather, Allah threw at them when He made your throw reach them. And He will certainly test the believers with victory over their enemies, which He blessed them with, despite their meager numbers and equipment, so that they thank Him. Indeed, Allah hears your supplication and speech and knows your actions and what benefits you.

(١٨) ذَٰلِكُمۡ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مُوهِنُ كَيۡدِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

The aforementioned killing of the polytheists, throwing at them until they were defeated and fled, and blessing the believers with victory over their enemy, is [all] from Allah. Allah weakens the plot of the disbelievers who plot against Islam.

(١٩) إِن تَسۡتَفۡتِحُواْ فَقَدۡ جَآءَكُمُ ٱلۡفَتۡحُۖ وَإِن تَنتَهُواْ فَهُوَ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡۖ وَإِن تَعُودُواْ نَعُدۡ وَلَن تُغۡنِيَ عَنكُمۡ فِئَتُكُمۡ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَوۡ كَثُرَتۡ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

If you, O polytheists, request that Allah inflict His torment and affliction upon the transgressing wrongdoers, then Allah has inflicted upon you what you requested, for He has brought down on you that which was a punishment for you and a lesson for the heedful. If you refrain from requesting that, then it is better for you. And if you go back to requesting it and killing the believers, we will go back to inflicting torment upon you and giving victory to the believers. Neither your faction nor your supporters will avail you, even if they were much in numbers and equipment while the believers are few, and because Allah is with them in support and aid. Whoever Allah is with, then none can defeat them.

(٢٠) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَلَا تَوَلَّوۡاْ عَنۡهُ وَأَنتُمۡ تَسۡمَعُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, obey Allah and obey His Messenger by complying with His commands and abstaining from His prohibitions. Do not turn away from Him by violating His orders and engaging in His prohibitions while you hear the verses of Allah being recited to you.

(٢١) وَلَا تَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ سَمِعۡنَا وَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡمَعُونَ

O believers, do not be like the polytheists and hypocrites who, when the verses of Allah are recited upon them, say: We have heard with our ears what has been recited to us from the Quran, while they do not listen to ponder or take admonishment, so they do not benefit from what they listen to.

(٢٢) ۞إِنَّ شَرَّ ٱلدَّوَآبِّ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلصُّمُّ ٱلۡبُكۡمُ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡقِلُونَ

Indeed, in the sight of Allah, the worst creatures that tread the earth are the deaf, who do not listen to the truth with acceptance, and the mute, who do not speak the truth, for they are the ones who do not understand what Allah commands nor what He forbids.

(٢٣) وَلَوۡ عَلِمَ ٱللَّهُ فِيهِمۡ خَيۡرٗا لَّأَسۡمَعَهُمۡۖ وَلَوۡ أَسۡمَعَهُمۡ لَتَوَلَّواْ وَّهُم مُّعۡرِضُونَ

If Allah had known that these polytheist deniers had [any] good in them, He would have surely made them hear with a hearing with which they would benefit and ponder His proof and evidence. However, He knows that there is no good in them. If He (may He be Exalted) had made them hear, hypothetically and theoretically speaking, they would have stubbornly turned away from faith, shunning [it].

(٢٤) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱسۡتَجِيبُواْ لِلَّهِ وَلِلرَّسُولِ إِذَا دَعَاكُمۡ لِمَا يُحۡيِيكُمۡۖ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَحُولُ بَيۡنَ ٱلۡمَرۡءِ وَقَلۡبِهِۦ وَأَنَّهُۥٓ إِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, answer Allah and His Messenger when they call you to the truth that gives you life by submitting to what they have commanded and avoiding what they have prohibited. Be certain that Allah can do anything so He can come between you and submission to the truth if you desire it after rejecting it. Therefore, hasten to it and be certain that you will be gathered to Allah alone on the Day of Resurrection. Then He will recompense you for your actions in the [life of this] world.

(٢٥) وَٱتَّقُواْ فِتۡنَةٗ لَّا تُصِيبَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ مِنكُمۡ خَآصَّةٗۖ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ

Beware, O believers, of a torment that will not only reach the sinners among you but rather reach them and others. That will be when there is open wrongdoing, and then it goes unchanged. Be certain that Allah is strong in punishing those who disobey Him, so beware of disobeying Him.

Points of Benefit:

Whoever has Allah with him is victorious, even if he is weak and few in number. This support is in accordance with the deeds of faith performed by the believers.
The believer is obligated to take material means, fulfill the duties assigned by Allah, then place trust in Allah, and delegate the matter to Him. As for achieving results and goals, that is left to Allah, the Mighty and Majestic.
The verses serve as evidence that Allah, the Exalted, does not prevent faith and goodness except from those in whom there is no goodness. It is from those who do not have genuine faith and do not bear fruit in His sight.
It is incumbent upon the servant to frequently supplicate, saying: "O Turner of hearts, keep my heart steadfast on Your religion," and "O Director of hearts, guide my heart towards Your obedience."
Allah has commanded the believers not to tolerate the manifest wrong among them, lest punishment befall them collectively.
