(١١٣) وَقَالَتِ ٱلۡيَهُودُ لَيۡسَتِ ٱلنَّصَٰرَىٰ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٖ وَقَالَتِ ٱلنَّصَٰرَىٰ لَيۡسَتِ ٱلۡيَهُودُ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٖ وَهُمۡ يَتۡلُونَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَۗ كَذَٰلِكَ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ مِثۡلَ قَوۡلِهِمۡۚ فَٱللَّهُ يَحۡكُمُ بَيۡنَهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فِيمَا كَانُواْ فِيهِ يَخۡتَلِفُونَ

The Jews say: The Christians are not upon the correct religion, and the Christians say: The Jews are not upon the correct religion. They say this even though both of them recite their respective Books, which Allah has revealed, and they contain the instruction to believe in all the prophets without distinction. They are similar to the ill-informed among the polytheists who reject all messengers and the Books sent with them. Hence, Allah will judge between all those who disagreed [with one another] on the Day of Resurrection with His fair judgment of which He has informed His slaves: There is no success except through belief in all that Allah is revealed.

(١١٤) وَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّن مَّنَعَ مَسَٰجِدَ ٱللَّهِ أَن يُذۡكَرَ فِيهَا ٱسۡمُهُۥ وَسَعَىٰ فِي خَرَابِهَآۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ مَا كَانَ لَهُمۡ أَن يَدۡخُلُوهَآ إِلَّا خَآئِفِينَۚ لَهُمۡ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا خِزۡيٞ وَلَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٞ

No one does more wrong than those who prevent the name of Allah from being mentioned in His Houses of worship: He prevents the prayer, Allah's remembrance, and the recitation of the Quran in them, trying to destroy and ruin them, through tearing them down or preventing people from worshipping in them. Those who try to destroy these places of worship should not enter them, except fearfully and with trembling hearts, after their disbelief and having stopped people from worshipping in them. They will have shame and humiliation in their life on earth at the hands of the believers, and in the Hereafter is a great torment for preventing people from Allah's Houses of worship.

(١١٥) وَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقُ وَٱلۡمَغۡرِبُۚ فَأَيۡنَمَا تُوَلُّواْ فَثَمَّ وَجۡهُ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٞ

To Allah belong the East and the West and whatever is between them. He instructs His slaves as He wills. Wherever you turn, you will find Allah. If He instructs you to face Jerusalem or the Kaaba, or you make a mistake in the direction of prayer, or you cannot face it, then it is not an issue because all directions belong to Allah. Allah is All-Encompassing; He encompasses His creation with mercy and ease. He knows their intentions and actions.

(١١٦) وَقَالُواْ ٱتَّخَذَ ٱللَّهُ وَلَدٗاۗ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥۖ بَل لَّهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ كُلّٞ لَّهُۥ قَٰنِتُونَ

The Jews, the Christians, and the polytheists said: Allah had taken a son. He is free and sanctified from such a thing because He does not need His creation, and no one has a son unless he is needy. To Allah, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth. All creation is His slaves, in submission to Him, and He deals with them as He wills.

(١١٧) بَدِيعُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَإِذَا قَضَىٰٓ أَمۡرٗا فَإِنَّمَا يَقُولُ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ

Allah is the Originator of the heavens, earth, and all that is in them without precedent. If He decrees and wants something, He only says to it 'Be,' and it is as He wanted it. Nothing can stop His command and His decree.

(١١٨) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ لَوۡلَا يُكَلِّمُنَا ٱللَّهُ أَوۡ تَأۡتِينَآ ءَايَةٞۗ كَذَٰلِكَ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّثۡلَ قَوۡلِهِمۡۘ تَشَٰبَهَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡۗ قَدۡ بَيَّنَّا ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يُوقِنُونَ

Those without knowledge from among the People of the Book and the polytheists asked, in their stubbornness against the truth: Why did Allah not speak to us directly, or why was a tangible miracle not sent to us? The rejecting nations said similar things to their messengers in the past; despite different times and places, their hearts are identical to those of their disbelieving, stubborn, and insolent predecessors. Allah clarifies His signs to people convinced of the truth when made apparent to them; they are not afflicted with doubt, nor do they stubbornly resist.

(١١٩) إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ بَشِيرٗا وَنَذِيرٗاۖ وَلَا تُسۡ‍َٔلُ عَنۡ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

We have sent you, O Prophet, with the religion of truth, in which there is no doubt: to bring the good news of Paradise to those who believe and to warn the disbelievers about the Fire. Allah will not ask you about the companions of the Inferno who did not believe in you.

Points of Benefit:

Disbelief is one religion, even if the races and places of its people differ, for they are similar in their disbelief and speech about Allah without knowledge.
The most severe criminals among the people and the most sinful of them are the ones who hinder others from Allah's path and prevent those who want to do good.
Allah, the Transcendent, is sanctified from having a wife and child, for He, the Exalted, does not need His creation.
