(٢٦) وَٱذۡكُرُوٓاْ إِذۡ أَنتُمۡ قَلِيلٞ مُّسۡتَضۡعَفُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ تَخَافُونَ أَن يَتَخَطَّفَكُمُ ٱلنَّاسُ فَ‍َٔاوَىٰكُمۡ وَأَيَّدَكُم بِنَصۡرِهِۦ وَرَزَقَكُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

Remember, O believers, when you were few in number in Mecca. Its residents considered you weak and overpowered you. You feared that your enemies would quickly seize you. Then, Allah took you into a shelter you could seek refuge in, which was Al-Medina, and strengthened you with victory over your enemies on the battlefields, including Badr. He provided for you with pure things; among them was the war booty you took from your enemies. [All that] so that you might be grateful to Allah for His blessings so He would give you more of them, and that you not be ungrateful for them, then He would seize it from you and torment you.

(٢٧) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَخُونُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلرَّسُولَ وَتَخُونُوٓاْ أَمَٰنَٰتِكُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, do not betray Allah and the Messenger by abandoning compliance with the commands and avoidance of the prohibitions. Also, do not betray what you have been entrusted with regarding religion and other matters, while you know what you would do is betrayal, for then you would be among the treacherous.

Given that the love of wealth and children leads a slave to betrayal, Allah informs us that they are both sources of temptation, stating:

(٢٨) وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَآ أَمۡوَٰلُكُمۡ وَأَوۡلَٰدُكُمۡ فِتۡنَةٞ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ عِندَهُۥٓ أَجۡرٌ عَظِيمٞ

Know, O believers, that your wealth and children are only a trial and test for you from Allah, for they might obstruct you from working towards the Afterlife and lead you to treachery. Know that Allah has a great reward with Him, so do not lose out on this reward by [your] tending to your wealth and children, and treachery for their sake.

(٢٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن تَتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ يَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ فُرۡقَانٗا وَيُكَفِّرۡ عَنكُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِكُمۡ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو ٱلۡفَضۡلِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, know that if you take heed of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, Allah will make for you that by which you can distinguish between truth and falsehood, so you will not be confused between them. He will also wipe away the evil deeds that you have committed and forgive you your sins. Allah is the possessor of great bounty, and from His great bounty is His Paradise that He has prepared for the heedful among His slaves.

(٣٠) وَإِذۡ يَمۡكُرُ بِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِيُثۡبِتُوكَ أَوۡ يَقۡتُلُوكَ أَوۡ يُخۡرِجُوكَۚ وَيَمۡكُرُونَ وَيَمۡكُرُ ٱللَّهُۖ وَٱللَّهُ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡمَٰكِرِينَ

Remember, O Messenger, when the polytheists joined forces against you, conspiring to capture, kill, or exile you from your land to another. They plot against you, but Allah turns their plot back against them. Allah plans, and He is the best of planners.

(٣١) وَإِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُنَا قَالُواْ قَدۡ سَمِعۡنَا لَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَقُلۡنَا مِثۡلَ هَٰذَآ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّآ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

When our verses are recited to them, they say in obstinance and arrogance to the truth: We have heard this sort of thing before. If we had wanted speech like this Quran, we would have spoken it. This Quran, which we heard, is just the lies of the ancients, so we will not believe in it.

(٣٢) وَإِذۡ قَالُواْ ٱللَّهُمَّ إِن كَانَ هَٰذَا هُوَ ٱلۡحَقَّ مِنۡ عِندِكَ فَأَمۡطِرۡ عَلَيۡنَا حِجَارَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ أَوِ ٱئۡتِنَا بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ

Remember, O Messenger, when the polytheists said: O Allah, if what Muhammad has brought is true, then drop stones from the sky upon us that would destroy us or bring us a severe torment. They said that in exaggeration of their denial and objection.

(٣٣) وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيُعَذِّبَهُمۡ وَأَنتَ فِيهِمۡۚ وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ مُعَذِّبَهُمۡ وَهُمۡ يَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ

Allah would not torment your nation, whether they are from the nation that answered [your call] or from the nation of being called, with a torment that would eradicate them while you, O Muhammad, are alive and present among them. Thus, your presence among them is a safeguard for them from torment. Also, Allah would not punish them while they seek forgiveness from Allah for their sins.

Points of Benefit:

Gratitude is a great blessing by which the favor of Allah, the Exalted, increases, and it diminishes when neglected.
Trustworthiness holds great significance in upholding the well-being of the Muslim community. It is what they establish and embody, serving as evidence of the integrity of the soul and the moderation of their actions.
The reward from Allah for restraining the self from forbidden acts is better than the benefits gained from transgressing the prohibitions for the sake of wealth and children.
The verses illustrate the foolishness of the minds of those who turn away, as they did not say: "O Allah, if this is indeed the truth from You, guide us to it."
The verses highlight the virtue and blessings of istighfar (seeking forgiveness) and emphasize that it serves as a deterrent against the occurrence of punishment.
