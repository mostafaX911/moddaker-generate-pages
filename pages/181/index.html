(٣٤) وَمَا لَهُمۡ أَلَّا يُعَذِّبَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَهُمۡ يَصُدُّونَ عَنِ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِ وَمَا كَانُوٓاْ أَوۡلِيَآءَهُۥٓۚ إِنۡ أَوۡلِيَآؤُهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡمُتَّقُونَ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

What could prevent their torment while they have committed what obliges it, including preventing people from praying and making tawaf (circumambulation) in the Sacred Mosque? The polytheists are not the allies of Allah, for Allah's allies are only the heedful who take heed of Him by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. But most polytheists are unaware when they claim that they are His allies while they are not.

(٣٥) وَمَا كَانَ صَلَاتُهُمۡ عِندَ ٱلۡبَيۡتِ إِلَّا مُكَآءٗ وَتَصۡدِيَةٗۚ فَذُوقُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡفُرُونَ

The prayer of the polytheists in Sacred Mosque was only whistling and clapping. So taste the torment, O polytheists, by being slain or captured on the day of Badr for your disbelief in Allah and your rejection of His Messenger.

(٣٦) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يُنفِقُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَهُمۡ لِيَصُدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ فَسَيُنفِقُونَهَا ثُمَّ تَكُونُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَسۡرَةٗ ثُمَّ يُغۡلَبُونَۗ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ جَهَنَّمَ يُحۡشَرُونَ

Indeed, those who disbelieve in Allah spend their wealth to prevent people from Allah's religion. So they will spend it and not accomplish what they sought. Then, the result of spending their wealth will be regret due to losing it and losing what was intended by it. Then, they will be defeated by the believers' victory over them. Those who disbelieve in Allah will be driven to Hell on the Day of Resurrection, so they will enter it and stay there for all eternity.

(٣٧) لِيَمِيزَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡخَبِيثَ مِنَ ٱلطَّيِّبِ وَيَجۡعَلَ ٱلۡخَبِيثَ بَعۡضَهُۥ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ فَيَرۡكُمَهُۥ جَمِيعٗا فَيَجۡعَلَهُۥ فِي جَهَنَّمَۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ

These disbelievers who spend their wealth to obstruct from the path of Allah will be driven to Hellfire so that Allah separates the group of filthy disbelievers from the group of pure believers and [so that] He piles up the filthy people, deeds, and wealth one atop the other, then put them in Hellfire. Those are the losers because they lost themselves and their families on the Day of Resurrection.

(٣٨) قُل لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِن يَنتَهُواْ يُغۡفَرۡ لَهُم مَّا قَدۡ سَلَفَ وَإِن يَعُودُواْ فَقَدۡ مَضَتۡ سُنَّتُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

Say, O Messenger, to those from your people who disbelieve in Allah and His Messenger, that indeed if they abstain from their disbelief in Allah and His Messenger and [abstain from] obstructing those that believe in Allah from His path, Allah will forgive them whatever preceded from their sins because Islam wipes out what preceded it. But if they go back to their disbelief, Allah's way of dealing with the ancients has preceded that, if they rejected and persisted in their disbelief, He hastened their punishment.

(٣٩) وَقَٰتِلُوهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ لَا تَكُونَ فِتۡنَةٞ وَيَكُونَ ٱلدِّينُ كُلُّهُۥ لِلَّهِۚ فَإِنِ ٱنتَهَوۡاْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِمَا يَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٞ

O believers, fight your enemies from the disbelievers until there is no more polytheism, no obstruction to the Muslims from Allah's religion, and religion and obedience be for Allah alone without any partner in them. So if the disbelievers cease the polytheism and obstruction from Allah which they are upon, then leave them [alone], for indeed, Allah sees their actions. No hidden thing is concealed from Him.

(٤٠) وَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَوۡلَىٰكُمۡۚ نِعۡمَ ٱلۡمَوۡلَىٰ وَنِعۡمَ ٱلنَّصِيرُ

And if they turn away from what they were ordered to do and from desisting from disbelief and obstruction from the way of Allah, then be certain, O believers, that Allah will support you against them. What an excellent sponsor for whom He sponsors, and what an excellent supporter for whom He supports. So whoever is sponsored by Him will triumph, and whoever is supported by Him will be victorious.

Points of Benefit:

Refraining from the Sacred Mosque is a grave crime deserving of worldly punishment before the punishment of the Hereafter.
The construction of the Sacred Mosque and its guardianship is an honor bestowed upon none but the righteous allies of Allah.
The verses serve as a warning to the disbelievers that their spending of wealth in falsehood will bring them no gain. Regret and intense remorse will befall them.
Allah's call to the disbelievers to repent and embrace faith is an open invitation to them despite their persistent defiance.
Whoever has Allah as their protector and helper has nothing to fear, while whoever makes Allah their adversary will find no honor.
