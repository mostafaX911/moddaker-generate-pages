(٤١) ۞وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا غَنِمۡتُم مِّن شَيۡءٖ فَأَنَّ لِلَّهِ خُمُسَهُۥ وَلِلرَّسُولِ وَلِذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينِ وَٱبۡنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ إِن كُنتُمۡ ءَامَنتُم بِٱللَّهِ وَمَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِنَا يَوۡمَ ٱلۡفُرۡقَانِ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡتَقَى ٱلۡجَمۡعَانِۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ

And know, O believers, that whatever you took from the disbelievers by force in Jihad (striving) for the sake of Allah shall be divided into five-fifths. Four-fifths shall be distributed to Mujahideen (those who fight for the sake of Allah), and the remaining fifth divided into five partitions: a portion for Allah and His Messenger, to be spent on the general expenses of the Muslims; a portion for the relatives of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم from the children of Hashim and the children of Al-Muttalib, a portion for orphans, a portion for the poor and the needy, and a portion for travelers who were stranded. Obey this if you believe in Allah and what We have revealed to our slave, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, on the day of the Battle of Badr, in which Allah made a distinction between truth and falsehood by giving you victory over your enemies. And Allah, who gave you victory, is able to do everything.

(٤٢) إِذۡ أَنتُم بِٱلۡعُدۡوَةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَهُم بِٱلۡعُدۡوَةِ ٱلۡقُصۡوَىٰ وَٱلرَّكۡبُ أَسۡفَلَ مِنكُمۡۚ وَلَوۡ تَوَاعَدتُّمۡ لَٱخۡتَلَفۡتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡمِيعَٰدِ وَلَٰكِن لِّيَقۡضِيَ ٱللَّهُ أَمۡرٗا كَانَ مَفۡعُولٗا لِّيَهۡلِكَ مَنۡ هَلَكَ عَنۢ بَيِّنَةٖ وَيَحۡيَىٰ مَنۡ حَيَّ عَنۢ بَيِّنَةٖۗ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَسَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ

And remember, when you were on the near side of the valley towards Medina, the polytheists were on the far side towards Mecca, and the caravan was at a spot lower than you by the Red Sea. If you and the polytheists had agreed to meet at Badr, neither of you would have missed each other. But Allah (may He be Exalted) gathered you at Badr without an agreement to complete a matter that was to pass: the victory of the believers, the desertion of the disbelievers, the honoring of His religion, and the humiliation of polytheism. [That was] so that whoever died amongst them would die after proof had been established against him through the believers' victory despite their lack of numbers and equipment, and whoever lived would live after evidence and proof that Allah made apparent to him. So, no one will have any proof to argue against Allah. Allah hears everyone's statements and knows their actions. No of those are hidden from Him, and He will compensate them based on them.

(٤٣) إِذۡ يُرِيكَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ فِي مَنَامِكَ قَلِيلٗاۖ وَلَوۡ أَرَىٰكَهُمۡ كَثِيرٗا لَّفَشِلۡتُمۡ وَلَتَنَٰزَعۡتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡرِ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَلَّمَۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

Remember, O Messenger, one of Allah's blessings upon you and the believers was when Allah showed you a dream in which the polytheists were few. So you informed the believers about this; they took it as a good omen, and their determination to face the enemy in battle was strengthened. If Allah had shown the polytheists to you to be many, the morale of your Companions would have decreased, and they would have become afraid of fighting. However, Allah saved you all from that and protected them from losing courage. He, therefore, made them seem few in the eye of His Messenger ﷺ. Indeed, he knows what the hearts contain and what the souls conceal.

(٤٤) وَإِذۡ يُرِيكُمُوهُمۡ إِذِ ٱلۡتَقَيۡتُمۡ فِيٓ أَعۡيُنِكُمۡ قَلِيلٗا وَيُقَلِّلُكُمۡ فِيٓ أَعۡيُنِهِمۡ لِيَقۡضِيَ ٱللَّهُ أَمۡرٗا كَانَ مَفۡعُولٗاۗ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ تُرۡجَعُ ٱلۡأُمُورُ

And remember, O believers, when Allah showed the polytheists to you when you met them as few and made you bravely go forward towards fighting them. He also made you seem few in their eyes, so they came forward to fight you without thinking of leaving. Moreover, Allah would bring a predetermined matter by taking revenge on the polytheists by them being killed or captured and favoring the believers with victory and defeat of their enemies. To Allah alone, things return, and He will recompense the one who does evil for wrongdoing and the one who does good for good deeds.

(٤٥) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا لَقِيتُمۡ فِئَةٗ فَٱثۡبُتُواْ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, when you fight against a group of disbelievers, be patient when you meet them and do not become cowardly. Moreover, mention Allah much and call on Him because He is the One who can help you against them. Furthermore, pray to Him to grant you what you desire and protect you from what you fear.

Points of Benefit:

The spoils belong to Allah; He allocates them as He wills, in the manner He desires. No one has a say in that.
Among the reasons for victory is Allah's arrangement for the believers, providing them with what assists them in achieving victory, along with patience, steadfastness, and abundant remembrance of Allah.
Allah's decree is effective, His wisdom is profound, and it is for the benefit of Allah's servants and the entire Ummah.
