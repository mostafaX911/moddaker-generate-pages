(٤٦) وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَلَا تَنَٰزَعُواْ فَتَفۡشَلُواْ وَتَذۡهَبَ رِيحُكُمۡۖ وَٱصۡبِرُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

Oblige Allah's obedience and the obedience of His Messenger in all of your statements, actions, and conditions. Do not differ in opinion because disagreement leads to weakness, cowardliness, and losing strength. Be patient when meeting your enemy. Indeed, Allah is with those who are patient with victory, support, and help. Whoever Allah is with him is the victor inevitably.

(٤٧) وَلَا تَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّذِينَ خَرَجُواْ مِن دِيَٰرِهِم بَطَرٗا وَرِئَآءَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَيَصُدُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا يَعۡمَلُونَ مُحِيطٞ

Do not be like the polytheists who left Mecca with arrogance and showing off to people, and they turn people away from Allah's religion and prevent them from entering it. Allah encompasses what they do; nothing of their deeds is hidden from Him, and He will recompense them accordingly.

(٤٨) وَإِذۡ زَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ وَقَالَ لَا غَالِبَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ مِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَإِنِّي جَارٞ لَّكُمۡۖ فَلَمَّا تَرَآءَتِ ٱلۡفِئَتَانِ نَكَصَ عَلَىٰ عَقِبَيۡهِ وَقَالَ إِنِّي بَرِيٓءٞ مِّنكُمۡ إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰ مَا لَا تَرَوۡنَ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ ٱللَّهَۚ وَٱللَّهُ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ

Remember, O believers, among the blessings of Allah upon you is when the Devil made the polytheists' deeds seem good to them and encouraged them to meet the Muslims and fight them. He said: No one can defeat you today because I am your helper and refuge from your enemy. When the two teams met [the team of believers who had the angels as their support and the group of polytheists with them the Devil who will let them down], the Devil turned away [escaping] and said to the polytheists: I am innocent of you. I see the angels who have come to support the believers. I fear that Allah will destroy me. Allah's punishment is severe, and no one can bear His punishment.

(٤٩) إِذۡ يَقُولُ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٌ غَرَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ دِينُهُمۡۗ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٞ

Remember when the hypocrites and those weak in faith were saying: The religion of these Muslims, which promises them victory over their enemies despite lack of numbers and feeble equipment and the significant number of their enemy and the strength of their gear, has deceived them. They have not understood that he who relies upon Allah alone and trusts in the victory that He has promised, then indeed, Allah will support him and not abandon him, however weak he may be. Allah is Mighty; no one can overcome Him. He is Wise in His predetermination and legislation.

(٥٠) وَلَوۡ تَرَىٰٓ إِذۡ يَتَوَفَّى ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ يَضۡرِبُونَ وُجُوهَهُمۡ وَأَدۡبَٰرَهُمۡ وَذُوقُواْ عَذَابَ ٱلۡحَرِيقِ

And if you watch, O Messenger, those who disbelieve in Allah and His messengers when the angels take and snatch their souls, striking their faces when they face towards them. Moreover, striking their backs when they try to run away, and they say to them: Taste, O disbelievers, the burning torment! If you watched that, you would see a severe heart-wrenching sight.

(٥١) ذَٰلِكَ بِمَا قَدَّمَتۡ أَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَيۡسَ بِظَلَّٰمٖ لِّلۡعَبِيدِ

That painful torment when your souls are taken, O disbelievers, and the burning torment in your graves and the Hereafter is caused by what your hands have earned in this world. For Allah does not wrong people, but He judges between them with justice, for He is the just judge.

(٥٢) كَدَأۡبِ ءَالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ كَفَرُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ فَأَخَذَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِذُنُوبِهِمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَوِيّٞ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ

This torment befalling those disbelievers is not exclusive to them. Instead, Allah's established way of dealing with disbelievers is what He preordained every time and place. The family of Pharaoh and the nations before them were tormented when they disbelieved in the signs of Allah (may He be Exalted). So, Allah punished them for their sins with His might and power. Indeed, Allah is Strong and cannot be overpowered or defeated, and He severely penalizes those who disobey Him.

Points of Benefit:

Arrogance is a serious disease that corrodes the formation of a person's character and hastens the destruction of the individual's essence.
Patience assists in bearing hardships and difficulties, and the benefits of patience are divine, as it is the assistance of Allah for those who patiently obey His command. This is evident in the events of life.
Disputes and differences are among the causes of the division of the Ummah, warning of defeat, retreat, and the loss of strength, victory, and authority.
Belief requires its possessor to undertake formidable tasks that even mighty armies may hesitate to approach.
