(٥٣) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَمۡ يَكُ مُغَيِّرٗا نِّعۡمَةً أَنۡعَمَهَا عَلَىٰ قَوۡمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُواْ مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمۡ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٞ

That severe punishment is because when Allah favors a people a blessing from Him, He does not take it away from them until they change themselves—from their pure state of faith, righteousness, and thanks for the blessings—to a bad state of disbelief in Allah, disobeying Him and rejecting His blessing. Verily, Allah hears the statements of His slaves and knows about their actions; nothing is hidden from Him.

(٥٤) كَدَأۡبِ ءَالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ فَأَهۡلَكۡنَٰهُم بِذُنُوبِهِمۡ وَأَغۡرَقۡنَآ ءَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنَۚ وَكُلّٞ كَانُواْ ظَٰلِمِينَ

The case of these disbelievers is like that of others who disbelieved in Allah, such as the people of Pharaoh and those who rejected Allah before them. They rejected the signs of their Lord, so Allah destroyed them because of the sins they committed. Allah destroyed the people of Pharaoh by causing them to drown in the sea. The people of Pharaoh and the nations before them were all wrongdoers because they disbelieved in Allah and their association with Him. They, therefore, deserved His punishment (may He be Exalted), so He inflicted it on them.

(٥٥) إِنَّ شَرَّ ٱلدَّوَآبِّ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فَهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

The worst of those who move on the earth are those who disbelieve in Allah and His messengers. Likewise, they do not believe even if every sign was to come to them because of their insistence on disbelief. So, their means of guidance have been disrupted, such as intellect, hearing, and sight.

(٥٦) ٱلَّذِينَ عَٰهَدتَّ مِنۡهُمۡ ثُمَّ يَنقُضُونَ عَهۡدَهُمۡ فِي كُلِّ مَرَّةٖ وَهُمۡ لَا يَتَّقُونَ

Those with whom covenants and promises [such as Banu Qurayza] were concluded. Then, they break the covenant every time you make it with them. Furthermore, they have no fear of Allah, so they do not fulfill their promises nor abide by the covenants taken from them.

(٥٧) فَإِمَّا تَثۡقَفَنَّهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَرۡبِ فَشَرِّدۡ بِهِم مَّنۡ خَلۡفَهُمۡ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَذَّكَّرُونَ

If you meet those, O Messenger, who break their covenants in war, then make a severely exemplary punishment of them so that others hear about it. They may learn from that and become afraid of fighting you and supporting your enemies against you.

(٥٨) وَإِمَّا تَخَافَنَّ مِن قَوۡمٍ خِيَانَةٗ فَٱنۢبِذۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡ عَلَىٰ سَوَآءٍۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡخَآئِنِينَ

And if you fear, O Messenger, that a people with whom you made a covenant will cheat and break the covenant with a sign that appears to you, then inform them that the covenant is canceled so that they are equal with you in the knowledge of that. And do not attack them suddenly without informing them because doing so will be a betrayal on your part. Indeed, Allah does not love the traitors, but He hates them, so beware of treachery.

(٥٩) وَلَا يَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ سَبَقُوٓاْۚ إِنَّهُمۡ لَا يُعۡجِزُونَ

Those disbelieving should not think they have skipped Allah's punishment and escaped it because they cannot do so. Instead, it will surely fall upon them.

(٦٠) وَأَعِدُّواْ لَهُم مَّا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُم مِّن قُوَّةٖ وَمِن رِّبَاطِ ٱلۡخَيۡلِ تُرۡهِبُونَ بِهِۦ عَدُوَّ ٱللَّهِ وَعَدُوَّكُمۡ وَءَاخَرِينَ مِن دُونِهِمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُهُمۡۚ وَمَا تُنفِقُواْ مِن شَيۡءٖ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ يُوَفَّ إِلَيۡكُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تُظۡلَمُونَ

Prepare, O believers, what you can prepare in terms of numbers and equipment, such as archery. Moreover, prepare for them whatever horses you have stored for the sake of Allah to terrify the enemies of Allah and your enemies from the disbelievers lurking around you. Moreover, to frighten other people with it whom you do not know, and you do not know the hostility they harbor against you. Instead, Allah alone is the One who knows them and knows what they have in their hearts. Whatever money you spend in the cause of Allah, whether small or large, Allah will reward you in this world and give you its full, undiminished reward in the Hereafter. So hasten to spend in His cause.

(٦١) ۞وَإِن جَنَحُواْ لِلسَّلۡمِ فَٱجۡنَحۡ لَهَا وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

And if they lean toward reconciliation and giving up fighting, then, O Messenger, incline toward it: make a covenant with them and place your reliance and trust in Allah because He will never let you down. He is All-Hearing of their words, Omniscient of their intentions and actions.

Points of Benefit:

One of the benefits of the prescribed punishments and boundaries set for sins is that they serve as a deterrent for those who refrain from committing sins. Additionally, they act as a prevention for those who engage in such actions from repeating them.
Among the moral traits of the believers is to fulfill their agreements with those who have treaties with them unless clear betrayal is evident from the other party.
Muslims are required to be fully prepared with all means to counteract any terrorism from the enemy, encompassing various forms of weaponry, strategies, and political awareness.
The permissibility of making peace with the enemy if it serves the interests of Muslims.
