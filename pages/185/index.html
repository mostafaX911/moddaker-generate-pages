(٦٢) وَإِن يُرِيدُوٓاْ أَن يَخۡدَعُوكَ فَإِنَّ حَسۡبَكَ ٱللَّهُۚ هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَيَّدَكَ بِنَصۡرِهِۦ وَبِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

And if, by inclining to reconciliation, they intend to deceive you, O Messenger, so that they can prepare to fight you, then Allah is sufficient for you against their scheming and deception. It is He who strengthened you with His victory and the victory of the believers among Al-Muhajireen (the Emigrants) and Al-Ansar (the Supporters).

(٦٣) وَأَلَّفَ بَيۡنَ قُلُوبِهِمۡۚ لَوۡ أَنفَقۡتَ مَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا مَّآ أَلَّفۡتَ بَيۡنَ قُلُوبِهِمۡ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَلَّفَ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٞ

Likewise, He brought together the hearts of the believers [with whom He assisted you] after it was dispersed. If you had spent all the wealth on earth to get their dispersed hearts together, you would not have been able to do so. However, Allah alone brought them together. He is Mighty in His dominion, and nothing can overpower Him. He is Wise in His decree, planning, and sacred laws.

(٦٤) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ حَسۡبُكَ ٱللَّهُ وَمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

O Prophet, Allah protects you from the evil of your enemies, and He protects the believers with you. So, trust in Allah and rely on Him.

(٦٥) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ حَرِّضِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ عَلَى ٱلۡقِتَالِۚ إِن يَكُن مِّنكُمۡ عِشۡرُونَ صَٰبِرُونَ يَغۡلِبُواْ مِاْئَتَيۡنِۚ وَإِن يَكُن مِّنكُم مِّاْئَةٞ يَغۡلِبُوٓاْ أَلۡفٗا مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَوۡمٞ لَّا يَفۡقَهُونَ

O Prophet, urge the believers to fight and encourage them to do so in a way that strengthens their determination and lifts their spirits. If there are twenty of you, O believers, who are patient in fighting the disbelievers, they will overcome two hundred of the disbelievers. Likewise, if there are one hundred who are patient among you, they will overcome one thousand of the disbelievers. That is because the disbelievers are a people who do not understand Allah's Sunnah (the way of Allah) by giving victory to His allies and defeating His enemies. They also do not realize the purpose of fighting, for they are fighting for superiority in this world.

(٦٦) ٱلۡـَٰٔنَ خَفَّفَ ٱللَّهُ عَنكُمۡ وَعَلِمَ أَنَّ فِيكُمۡ ضَعۡفٗاۚ فَإِن يَكُن مِّنكُم مِّاْئَةٞ صَابِرَةٞ يَغۡلِبُواْ مِاْئَتَيۡنِۚ وَإِن يَكُن مِّنكُمۡ أَلۡفٞ يَغۡلِبُوٓاْ أَلۡفَيۡنِ بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ مَعَ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

Allah has made it easier for you now, O believers, since He knows your weakness. Therefore, he has made it easier for you [with His kindness] and has made it obligatory that one of you remains patient against two disbelievers instead of ten. So, if there are one hundred among you who remain patient in fighting against the disbelievers, they will defeat two hundred. Likewise, if there are one thousand among you who remain patient, they will vanquish two thousand of the disbelievers by Allah's permission. Therefore, with His support and victory, Allah is with those believers who remain patient.

(٦٧) مَا كَانَ لِنَبِيٍّ أَن يَكُونَ لَهُۥٓ أَسۡرَىٰ حَتَّىٰ يُثۡخِنَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ تُرِيدُونَ عَرَضَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱللَّهُ يُرِيدُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٞ

It should not be for a prophet to have prisoners among the disbelievers who fight against him until he kills many of them. Thus, the terror enters their hearts, so they do not return to fighting. By taking captives from Badr, O believers, you want to receive ransom, but Allah desires the Hereafter [for you], which is obtained by making religion victorious and powerful. Allah is Mighty in His essence, His attributes, and His domination. No one can overcome Him. He is Wise in His decree and sacred laws.

(٦٨) لَّوۡلَا كِتَٰبٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ سَبَقَ لَمَسَّكُمۡ فِيمَآ أَخَذۡتُمۡ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٞ

Had it not been for a book from Allah that preceded his decree and destiny [in which it had already been decided] that He permitted you the spoils and allowed you to ransom the prisoners, a severe torment would have occurred to you because of what you took from the spoils of war. Likewise, the ransom you took from the prisoners [at Badr] before a revelation came down from Allah to permit that.

(٦٩) فَكُلُواْ مِمَّا غَنِمۡتُمۡ حَلَٰلٗا طَيِّبٗاۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

So consume, O believers, what you took from the disbelievers of the spoils of war, for it is lawful for you. And fear Allah by obeying His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. Indeed, Allah is forgiving to His faithful slaves, Merciful to them.

Points of Benefit:

In the verses, a promise from Allah to His believing servants of sufficiency and victory over the enemies.
Standing firm in the face of the enemy is an obligation upon Muslims with no choice unless there is an exception permitted by Sharia.
Allah loves for His servants the noble aspects of affairs and dislikes their degradation. Therefore, He encourages them to seek the everlasting rewards of the Hereafter.
Ransoming prisoners or showing kindness to them by releasing them should only occur after achieving dominance and authority over the enemies and demonstrating the dignity of the state in the face of others.
