(٧٠) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ قُل لِّمَن فِيٓ أَيۡدِيكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَسۡرَىٰٓ إِن يَعۡلَمِ ٱللَّهُ فِي قُلُوبِكُمۡ خَيۡرٗا يُؤۡتِكُمۡ خَيۡرٗا مِّمَّآ أُخِذَ مِنكُمۡ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

O Prophet, say to those who fell into your hands among the polytheist captives you captured on the day of Badr: If Allah knows good in your hearts and good intentions, He will give you better than what was taken from you as a ransom. So do not grieve over what was taken from you. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful to those of His slaves who repent, and He [also] forgives your sins. This promise of Allah was fulfilled for Al-Abbas, the uncle of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم, and for others like him who accepted Islam after being captured.

(٧١) وَإِن يُرِيدُواْ خِيَانَتَكَ فَقَدۡ خَانُواْ ٱللَّهَ مِن قَبۡلُ فَأَمۡكَنَ مِنۡهُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌ

And if they intend, O Muhammad, to betray you by the words they utter, then they have betrayed Allah before, and Allah has given you victory over them, so some of them were killed, and others were taken captive. They should, therefore, wait for something similar to happen if they return to their evil. And Allah is All-Knowing of His creation and what works for them, Wise in His planning.

(٧٢) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَهَاجَرُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَأَنفُسِهِمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَاوَواْ وَّنَصَرُوٓاْ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ بَعۡضٖۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَلَمۡ يُهَاجِرُواْ مَا لَكُم مِّن وَلَٰيَتِهِم مِّن شَيۡءٍ حَتَّىٰ يُهَاجِرُواْۚ وَإِنِ ٱسۡتَنصَرُوكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِ فَعَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلنَّصۡرُ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِۢ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَبَيۡنَهُم مِّيثَٰقٞۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٞ

Those who believed in Allah believed in His Messenger, acted upon His sacred law, migrated from the land of disbelief to the land of Islam or to a place where they could worship Allah safely, and strived by spending their money and their lives to raise the word of Allah. Likewise, those who hosted them in their homes and supported them - those emigrants and those locals who supported them are allies and friends of one another in providing support and assistance. As for those who believed in Allah but did not emigrate from the land of disbelief to the land of Islam, it is not upon you, O believers, that you help them and protect them until they migrate for the sake of Allah. If the disbelievers oppress them and they [the weak believers] seek your victory and help, then help them against their enemies unless a covenant between you and their enemy is still in place and they [the disbelievers] did not break. Allah sees everything you do. Nothing of your deeds is hidden from Him, and He will recompense them accordingly.

(٧٣) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ بَعۡضٍۚ إِلَّا تَفۡعَلُوهُ تَكُن فِتۡنَةٞ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَفَسَادٞ كَبِيرٞ

And those who disbelieve in Allah are united in their disbelief, so they support each other. A believer should not be loyal to them. Suppose you do not support the believers and stand hostile to the disbelievers. In that case, there will be a Fitnah (suffering weakness, wars, battles, polytheism, etc.) for the believers as they did not find anyone to support them from among their brothers in religion. And there will be grand corruption on earth by obstructing Allah's path.

(٧٤) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَهَاجَرُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَاوَواْ وَّنَصَرُوٓاْ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ حَقّٗاۚ لَّهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٞ وَرِزۡقٞ كَرِيمٞ

Those who believed in Allah and emigrated in His path and those who provided shelter and assistance to the emigrants for Allah's sake are the ones who can be genuinely described as having faith. Their reward from Allah is forgiveness for their sins and a generous provision from Him, which is Paradise.

(٧٥) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدُ وَهَاجَرُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ مَعَكُمۡ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ مِنكُمۡۚ وَأُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَرۡحَامِ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلَىٰ بِبَعۡضٖ فِي كِتَٰبِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمُۢ

And those who believed in Allah after the faith of those who preceded Islam from among Al-Muhajireen (the Emigrants) and Al-Ansar (the Supporters), who emigrated from the land of disbelief to the land of Islam and strived in Allah's path so that Allah's Word may be the highest and the word of the disbelievers may be the lowest – they are part of you, O believers. They will have the same rights and obligations as you have. Under Allah's final sacred inheritance laws, family members are closer to one another than in the previous Islamic inheritance system based on faith and emigration. Allah knows everything. Nothing is hidden from Him, for He knows what is best for His slaves, so He legislates it for them.

Points of Benefit:

Believers are obligated to encourage captives toward faith.
The verses contained glad tidings for the believers regarding the continuous triumph over the polytheists as long as they adhere to the material and moral means of victory.
If the Muslims are not united against the disbelievers, their strength will not manifest, and this will lead to significant corruption.
The virtue of fulfilling covenants and treaties is emphasized in the Islamic Sharia, even if it contradicts the interests of some Muslims.
