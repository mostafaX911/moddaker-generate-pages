The Repentance (Surah At-Tawbah)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Disassociating from the polytheists and hypocrites and struggling against them while keeping the door of repentance open for those who turn in penitence.

Explanation:

(١) بَرَآءَةٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦٓ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ عَٰهَدتُّم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

This is a declaration of dissociation from Allah and His Messenger. And an announcement of the termination of the covenants that you, O Muslims, had made with the polytheists in the Arabian Peninsula.

(٢) فَسِيحُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَرۡبَعَةَ أَشۡهُرٖ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّكُمۡ غَيۡرُ مُعۡجِزِي ٱللَّهِ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡزِي ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

So travel safely, O polytheists, on earth for a period of four months, after which there will be no covenant for you or protection. And be sure that you will never escape Allah's torment and punishment if you continue to disbelieve in Him. Also, be sure that Allah humiliates the disbelievers by being killed or taken captive in this world and entering The Fire on The Day of Resurrection. This includes those who broke their covenant and those who had an open covenant without any fixed period. As for those with whom you made a covenant for a limited period, their covenant will be completed until the end of the specified period, even if this is longer than four months.

(٣) وَأَذَٰنٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦٓ إِلَى ٱلنَّاسِ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡحَجِّ ٱلۡأَكۡبَرِ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَرِيٓءٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ وَرَسُولُهُۥۚ فَإِن تُبۡتُمۡ فَهُوَ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡۖ وَإِن تَوَلَّيۡتُمۡ فَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّكُمۡ غَيۡرُ مُعۡجِزِي ٱللَّهِۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ

Proclamation from Allah and proclamation from His Messenger to all people on the Day of Sacrifice that Allah (may He be Exalted) is unassociated with the polytheists as His Messenger. So if you, O polytheists, repent from your polytheism, then your repentance is better for you. If you turn away from repentance, then be certain that you will not get away from Allah and will not escape from His punishment. O Messenger, inform those who disbelieved in Allah with that which will upset them: a painful torment that awaits them.

(٤) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ عَٰهَدتُّم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَنقُصُوكُمۡ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَمۡ يُظَٰهِرُواْ عَلَيۡكُمۡ أَحَدٗا فَأَتِمُّوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِمۡ عَهۡدَهُمۡ إِلَىٰ مُدَّتِهِمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Except those of the polytheists with whom you made a covenant, and they fulfilled your covenant. Moreover, they did not shorten anything from it [they honored the covenant in full], so complete the fulfillment of their covenant until its term expires. Allah loves those who fear Him by obeying His commands, including completion of the covenant, and avoiding His prohibitions, including treason.

(٥) فَإِذَا ٱنسَلَخَ ٱلۡأَشۡهُرُ ٱلۡحُرُمُ فَٱقۡتُلُواْ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ حَيۡثُ وَجَدتُّمُوهُمۡ وَخُذُوهُمۡ وَٱحۡصُرُوهُمۡ وَٱقۡعُدُواْ لَهُمۡ كُلَّ مَرۡصَدٖۚ فَإِن تَابُواْ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتَوُاْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ فَخَلُّواْ سَبِيلَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

When the sacred months (Dhū Al-Qa'dah, Dhul-Hijjah, Muḥarram, and Rajab) during which you assure the safety of your enemies end, then kill the polytheists wherever you meet them, capture them, besiege them in their fortresses and lie in wait in watch for them on their paths. Then, if they repent to Allah from their polytheism, establish the prayer, and give the zakat (obligatory alms) from their wealth, they have become your brothers in Islam, so desist from fighting them. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful towards those of His slaves who repent to Him.

(٦) وَإِنۡ أَحَدٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ ٱسۡتَجَارَكَ فَأَجِرۡهُ حَتَّىٰ يَسۡمَعَ كَلَٰمَ ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ أَبۡلِغۡهُ مَأۡمَنَهُۥۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَوۡمٞ لَّا يَعۡلَمُونَ

And if one of the polytheists, whose blood and wealth is permissible, comes to you, O Messenger, and requests your protection, respond to his request so that he can hear the Quran. Then, take them to a place where they will be safe. That is because the disbelievers are a people who do not know the realities of this religion. So, if they learn it from hearing the recitation of the Quran, they may be guided.

Points of Benefit:

In the verses, there is a clear indication of Islam's keenness to settle external relations with adversaries on the basis of peace, security, and understanding.
Islam values treaties, obligates fulfillment of them, and considers their preservation as emanating from belief and being consistent with the fear of Allah, the Exalted.
The establishment of prayer and the payment of zakat serve as evidence of Islam, and they safeguard life and property. They entail the rights of Muslims in terms of preserving one's life and wealth, except in cases justified by Islamic law, such as committing crimes that warrant capital punishment, killing an innocent soul, committing adultery by a married individual, and apostasy, which involves turning away from faith after embracing Islam.
The legitimacy of granting asylum means that it is permissible to provide war refugees with protection if they seek it from Muslims. This action is based on hearing what indicates the correctness of Islam. It reflects generosity and honor in dealing with non-Muslims, serving as evidence of prioritizing peace.
