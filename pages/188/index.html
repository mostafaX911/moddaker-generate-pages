(٧) كَيۡفَ يَكُونُ لِلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ عَهۡدٌ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَعِندَ رَسُولِهِۦٓ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ عَٰهَدتُّمۡ عِندَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِۖ فَمَا ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ لَكُمۡ فَٱسۡتَقِيمُواْ لَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

It is not correct for those who associate partners with Allah to have a covenant and an assurance of safety from Allah and His Messenger, except for the covenant you made, O Muslims, with those polytheists at the Sacred Mosque during the al-Hudaybiyah peace treaty. As long as they uphold the covenant with you and do not break it, then you should also remain upon it and not break it. Indeed, Allah loves those of His slaves who take heed of Him by obeying His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

(٨) كَيۡفَ وَإِن يَظۡهَرُواْ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لَا يَرۡقُبُواْ فِيكُمۡ إِلّٗا وَلَا ذِمَّةٗۚ يُرۡضُونَكُم بِأَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَتَأۡبَىٰ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَأَكۡثَرُهُمۡ فَٰسِقُونَ

How can they have a covenant with you and an assurance of safety when they are your enemies? And if they win over you [and conquer you], they will not consider Allah concerning you or respect any kinship relationship or covenant. But instead, inflict severe torment on you?! They please you with the pleasing words that their tongues utter, but their hearts do not obey and agree with their tongues, so they do not fulfill what they say, and most of them are out of obedience to Allah for breaking the covenant.

(٩) ٱشۡتَرَوۡاْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ ثَمَنٗا قَلِيلٗا فَصَدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُمۡ سَآءَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

They took compensation and replaced following the verses of Allah, including the fulfillment of covenants, for a trivial worthless price from the wreckage of the world with which they reach their lusts and whims. So, they prevented themselves from following the truth, turned away from it, and prevented others from the truth. They have gone wrong with the work they were doing.

(١٠) لَا يَرۡقُبُونَ فِي مُؤۡمِنٍ إِلّٗا وَلَا ذِمَّةٗۚ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُعۡتَدُونَ

They do not respect Allah, kinship, or covenant with a believer because of their hostility. They transgress the limits of Allah because of their injustice and aggression.

(١١) فَإِن تَابُواْ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتَوُاْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ فَإِخۡوَٰنُكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِۗ وَنُفَصِّلُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ

If they repent to Allah from their disbelief, pronounce the two testimonies, establish the prayer, and give the Zakat (obligatory alms) from their wealth. They have become Muslims, and they are your brothers in religion. They then have the same rights and duties you have, and it is not permissible for you to fight them. Their acceptance of Islam protects their blood, money, and honor. Allah explains the verses clearly to people who know, as they are the ones who will benefit from them and will cause others to benefit as well.

(١٢) وَإِن نَّكَثُوٓاْ أَيۡمَٰنَهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ عَهۡدِهِمۡ وَطَعَنُواْ فِي دِينِكُمۡ فَقَٰتِلُوٓاْ أَئِمَّةَ ٱلۡكُفۡرِ إِنَّهُمۡ لَآ أَيۡمَٰنَ لَهُمۡ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَنتَهُونَ

And if those polytheists with whom you pledged not to fight for a known period break their covenants and promises and insult your religion and belittle it, then fight them, for they are the leaders of disbelief and its chiefs. Furthermore, they have no covenants nor promises to spare their blood. Fight them in the hope that they will end their disbelief, breaking of covenants, and derogation of the religion.

(١٣) أَلَا تُقَٰتِلُونَ قَوۡمٗا نَّكَثُوٓاْ أَيۡمَٰنَهُمۡ وَهَمُّواْ بِإِخۡرَاجِ ٱلرَّسُولِ وَهُم بَدَءُوكُمۡ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٍۚ أَتَخۡشَوۡنَهُمۡۚ فَٱللَّهُ أَحَقُّ أَن تَخۡشَوۡهُ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Why do you, O believers, not fight a people who have broken their covenants and treaties and who have strived in their meeting at Dār Al-Nadwah, to expel the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم from Mecca? They started the fight against you in the first place when they assisted [the Children of] Bakr, allies of Quraysh, against [the Children of] Khuzā'ah, allies of the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم. Is it because you fear to meet them in battle? Then Allah (may He be Exalted) is more rightful for you to fear if you are true believers.

Points of Benefit:

The verses indicate that the fight against the treacherous polytheists was due to various reasons, most notably their violation of the covenant.
The verses provide evidence that anyone who refrains from performing the prayer or giving Zakat is to be fought until they fulfill these obligations, as exemplified by the actions of Abu Bakr (may Allah be pleased with him).
Some scholars deduce from the verse "And revile your religion" the obligation to kill anyone who deliberately mocks and reviles the religion.
The verses indicate that the believer who fears Allah alone should be among the bravest and most courageous in facing the enemy in battle.
