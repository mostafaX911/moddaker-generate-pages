(١٤) قَٰتِلُوهُمۡ يُعَذِّبۡهُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِأَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَيُخۡزِهِمۡ وَيَنصُرۡكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَيَشۡفِ صُدُورَ قَوۡمٖ مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

O believers, fight these polytheists. If you fight them, Allah will torment them with your hands by killing and humiliating them with defeat and captivity. Also, He will grant you victory over them by making you [believers] overcome them and healing the hearts of believing people who did not witness the fighting with what happened to their enemy in terms of killing, captivity, defeat, and the victory of the believers over them.

(١٥) وَيُذۡهِبۡ غَيۡظَ قُلُوبِهِمۡۗ وَيَتُوبُ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ مَن يَشَآءُۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌ

He removes the rage from the hearts of His believing slaves because of the victory they gained over them. Allah repents to whom He wills among those who were stubborn if they repented, as happened to some of the people of Mecca on the day of the conquest. Allah knows the sincerity of those who repent. He is Wise in His creation, planning, and legislation.

(١٦) أَمۡ حَسِبۡتُمۡ أَن تُتۡرَكُواْ وَلَمَّا يَعۡلَمِ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ جَٰهَدُواْ مِنكُمۡ وَلَمۡ يَتَّخِذُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَا رَسُولِهِۦ وَلَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَلِيجَةٗۚ وَٱللَّهُ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Did you, O believers, think Allah would leave you without a test? Testing is one of Allah's established ways. You will be tested until Allah [clearly] proves those who strive with sincerity for Him, who did not take besides Allah, His Messenger, or the believers, a lining from the unbelievers to be loyal to them. Likewise, do not make intimate friends with the disbelievers. Allah knows what you do, and nothing is hidden from Him. He will recompense you accordingly.

(١٧) مَا كَانَ لِلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ أَن يَعۡمُرُواْ مَسَٰجِدَ ٱللَّهِ شَٰهِدِينَ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِم بِٱلۡكُفۡرِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ حَبِطَتۡ أَعۡمَٰلُهُمۡ وَفِي ٱلنَّارِ هُمۡ خَٰلِدُونَ

The polytheists should not attend or care for the mosques of Allah, with worship and types of obedience, while they admit themselves to disbelief in what they show of it. Those whose deeds have been invalidated because the condition of their acceptance, which is faith, has been lost. On the Day of Resurrection, they will enter the Fire to abide in it forever unless they repent of polytheism before their death.

(١٨) إِنَّمَا يَعۡمُرُ مَسَٰجِدَ ٱللَّهِ مَنۡ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَأَقَامَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتَى ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَلَمۡ يَخۡشَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَۖ فَعَسَىٰٓ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَن يَكُونُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ

Only those who deserve to sustain the mosques and fulfill their rights are those who believe in Allah alone, do not associate anyone with Him, believe in the Day of Resurrection, establish prayer, give the zakat on their wealth, and do not fear anyone except Allah the Exalted. These are the ones who are hoped to be guided to the straight path, but the polytheists are far from that.

(١٩) ۞أَجَعَلۡتُمۡ سِقَايَةَ ٱلۡحَآجِّ وَعِمَارَةَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِ كَمَنۡ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَجَٰهَدَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ لَا يَسۡتَوُۥنَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Have you, O polytheists, made those in charge of giving water to the pilgrims and taking care of the Sacred Mosque like those who believed in Allah, did not associate any partner with Him, believed in the Day of Resurrection, and strived with their lives and money so that the word of Allah would be the highest, and the word of the unbelievers the lowest? They are never equal to Allah, and Allah does not profit the oppressors with polytheism, even if they do good deeds, such as giving water to the pilgrim.

(٢٠) ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَهَاجَرُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ بِأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَأَنفُسِهِمۡ أَعۡظَمُ دَرَجَةً عِندَ ٱللَّهِۚ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡفَآئِزُونَ

Those who combine a belief in Allah with migration from the lands of disbelief to the lands of Islam and striving for Allah's sake with money and souls have the greatest rank with Allah than others, and those who are characterized by these qualities are the prizewinners of Paradise.

Points of Benefit:

The verses indicate Allah's love for His believing servants and His care for their well-being. He has even made among the legislative purposes the healing of what is in their hearts and the removal of their resentment.
Allah legislated Jihad to achieve this greatest purpose, which is that the truthful believers, who are only biased for the sake of Allah's religion, distinguish themselves from the liars who falsely claim faith.
The true builders of mosques are those described with sincere faith, performing righteous deeds rooted in prayers and almsgiving, and possessing the fear of Allah, which is the foundation of all goodness.
Jihad and belief in Allah are superior to providing water for pilgrims and building the Sacred Mosque by many degrees because belief is the foundation of religion, and jihad in the path of Allah is the pinnacle of the religion's spearhead.
