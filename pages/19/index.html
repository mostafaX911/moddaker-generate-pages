(١٢٠) وَلَن تَرۡضَىٰ عَنكَ ٱلۡيَهُودُ وَلَا ٱلنَّصَٰرَىٰ حَتَّىٰ تَتَّبِعَ مِلَّتَهُمۡۗ قُلۡ إِنَّ هُدَى ٱللَّهِ هُوَ ٱلۡهُدَىٰۗ وَلَئِنِ ٱتَّبَعۡتَ أَهۡوَآءَهُم بَعۡدَ ٱلَّذِي جَآءَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ مَا لَكَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مِن وَلِيّٖ وَلَا نَصِيرٍ

Allah addresses His Prophet, directs, and warns him: The Jews and the Christians will never be satisfied with you unless you leave Islam and follow what they are on. If you or anyone from your followers were to follow them after the clear truth came to you, you would not find any assistance or support from Allah. This explains the danger of leaving the truth and conforming to the people of falsehood. Otherwise, Allah has protected His Prophet from that, which serves as a warning for his nation after him.

(١٢١) ٱلَّذِينَ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ يَتۡلُونَهُۥ حَقَّ تِلَاوَتِهِۦٓ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهِۦۗ وَمَن يَكۡفُرۡ بِهِۦ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ

The Noble Quran speaks about a group from the People of the Book who act upon the revealed books they have been given and follow them correctly. They find signs pointing to the truth of the Prophet Muhammad in these books, and because of this, they hasten to believe in him. Another group persists in disbelief, which will result in their loss.

(١٢٢) يَٰبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتِيَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنۡعَمۡتُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَأَنِّي فَضَّلۡتُكُمۡ عَلَى ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

O Israelites, remember My favors, religious and worldly, which I gave you, and remember that I favored you over those of your time with prophethood and kingdom.

(١٢٣) وَٱتَّقُواْ يَوۡمٗا لَّا تَجۡزِي نَفۡسٌ عَن نَّفۡسٖ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا يُقۡبَلُ مِنۡهَا عَدۡلٞ وَلَا تَنفَعُهَا شَفَٰعَةٞ وَلَا هُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ

Protect yourself from the torment of the Day of Resurrection by following Allah's instructions and avoiding His prohibitions because, on that Day, no soul will be able to help another soul in any way; no ransom will be accepted from it, no matter how great; and no one interceding on its behalf will benefit it, no matter how high their position. On that Day, a soul will have no helper besides Allah.

(١٢٤) ۞وَإِذِ ٱبۡتَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمَ رَبُّهُۥ بِكَلِمَٰتٖ فَأَتَمَّهُنَّۖ قَالَ إِنِّي جَاعِلُكَ لِلنَّاسِ إِمَامٗاۖ قَالَ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِيۖ قَالَ لَا يَنَالُ عَهۡدِي ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

And remember when Allah tested Abraham (peace be upon him) by instructing him to fulfill laws and obligations. Abraham obeyed, fulfilling the orders completely. Allah told His prophet Abraham: I will make you an example in actions and character for people to follow. Abraham said: O my Lord, make my descendants leaders who people would follow. Allah replied to him: My promise to you of sacred leadership would not extend to the wrongdoers among your descendants.

(١٢٥) وَإِذۡ جَعَلۡنَا ٱلۡبَيۡتَ مَثَابَةٗ لِّلنَّاسِ وَأَمۡنٗا وَٱتَّخِذُواْ مِن مَّقَامِ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمَ مُصَلّٗىۖ وَعَهِدۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمَ وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلَ أَن طَهِّرَا بَيۡتِيَ لِلطَّآئِفِينَ وَٱلۡعَٰكِفِينَ وَٱلرُّكَّعِ ٱلسُّجُودِ

And remember when Allah made the sacred house a place for people to return to connect their hearts to it. Whenever they travel from it, they return to it. He also made it a safe place for them not to be attacked. Allah told the people: Take the place of the stone that Abraham stood on while building the Kaaba as a place of prayer. We advised Abraham and his son Ishmael to purify the sacred house of filth and idols and prepare it for those who wish to worship through Tawaf (circumambulation), seclusion, praying, and so on.

(١٢٦) وَإِذۡ قَالَ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمُ رَبِّ ٱجۡعَلۡ هَٰذَا بَلَدًا ءَامِنٗا وَٱرۡزُقۡ أَهۡلَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ مَنۡ ءَامَنَ مِنۡهُم بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۚ قَالَ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَأُمَتِّعُهُۥ قَلِيلٗا ثُمَّ أَضۡطَرُّهُۥٓ إِلَىٰ عَذَابِ ٱلنَّارِۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

Remember, O Prophet, when Abraham prayed to his Lord: O my Lord, make Mecca a safe place, where no one would be faced with evil; and provide the people who live there with all types of fruits and a special provision for those who believe in You and the Final Day. Allah said: Whoever disbelieves among them, then I will give them some enjoyment by giving them a small provision in this life; but in the Hereafter I will force them into the torment of the Fire, what a miserable destination to which they will return on the Day of Resurrection.

Points of Benefit:

No matter how much good the Muslims do for the Jews and Christians, they will not be satisfied until they expel the Muslims from their religion and follow them in their misguidance.
Leadership in the religion is not attained except with sound certainty and patience in carrying out Allah's commands, the Exalted.
The blessing of Abraham's invocation, peace be upon him, for the Sacred Land, as Allah made it a safe place for the people and favored its people with various kinds of sustenance.
