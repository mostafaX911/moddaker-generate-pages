(٢١) يُبَشِّرُهُمۡ رَبُّهُم بِرَحۡمَةٖ مِّنۡهُ وَرِضۡوَٰنٖ وَجَنَّٰتٖ لَّهُمۡ فِيهَا نَعِيمٞ مُّقِيمٌ

Allah tells them, their Lord, what makes them happy of His mercy and bestows His satisfaction so that He will never be angry with them. Likewise, by entering paradises in which they have perpetual bliss that never ceases.

(٢٢) خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدًاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عِندَهُۥٓ أَجۡرٌ عَظِيمٞ

They will remain in those paradises for an endless stay as a reward for them for the righteous deeds that they used to do in this world. Indeed, Allah greatly rewards those who obey His commands, avoid His prohibitions, and are faithful to His religion.

(٢٣) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَتَّخِذُوٓاْ ءَابَآءَكُمۡ وَإِخۡوَٰنَكُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءَ إِنِ ٱسۡتَحَبُّواْ ٱلۡكُفۡرَ عَلَى ٱلۡإِيمَٰنِۚ وَمَن يَتَوَلَّهُم مِّنكُمۡ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow what His Messenger brought, do not take your fathers and brothers in bloodlines and others from your kinship a close and trustworthy friend allying with them. Likewise, by divulging the secrets of the believers to them and consulting with them if they prefer disbelief over faith in Allah alone. Whoever makes them allies while they remain in disbelief and shows them affection has disobeyed Allah and oppressed himself by placing himself in a doom situation because of disobedience.

(٢٤) قُلۡ إِن كَانَ ءَابَآؤُكُمۡ وَأَبۡنَآؤُكُمۡ وَإِخۡوَٰنُكُمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجُكُمۡ وَعَشِيرَتُكُمۡ وَأَمۡوَٰلٌ ٱقۡتَرَفۡتُمُوهَا وَتِجَٰرَةٞ تَخۡشَوۡنَ كَسَادَهَا وَمَسَٰكِنُ تَرۡضَوۡنَهَآ أَحَبَّ إِلَيۡكُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَجِهَادٖ فِي سَبِيلِهِۦ فَتَرَبَّصُواْ حَتَّىٰ يَأۡتِيَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَمۡرِهِۦۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ

Say, O Messenger: If your fathers, O believers, your children, brothers, spouses, relatives, the wealth you have earned, the trade you love to flourish and fear to decline, and your homes where you are pleased to live. If all of these are more beloved to you than Allah, His Messenger, and striving for His sake, wait for what Allah will send down on you of torment and punishment. Allah does not help those who abandon His obedience to do what pleases Him.

(٢٥) لَقَدۡ نَصَرَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ فِي مَوَاطِنَ كَثِيرَةٖ وَيَوۡمَ حُنَيۡنٍ إِذۡ أَعۡجَبَتۡكُمۡ كَثۡرَتُكُمۡ فَلَمۡ تُغۡنِ عَنكُمۡ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَضَاقَتۡ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ بِمَا رَحُبَتۡ ثُمَّ وَلَّيۡتُم مُّدۡبِرِينَ

Allah has certainly supported you, O believers, against your enemies among the polytheists in many battles, despite your deficient numbers and weak equipment, when you relied on Allah and took the appropriate measures. You were not impressed by your abundance, for abundance was not the cause of your victory over them. And We supported you on the day of Hunayn when you were impressed by your abundance, so you said: We will not be defeated today from deficiency. Then your abundance which impressed you did not benefit you at all, so your enemy overwhelmed you, and the earth became constricted upon you, despite its vastness. Then you ran away from your enemies, fleeing in defeat.

(٢٦) ثُمَّ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ سَكِينَتَهُۥ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِهِۦ وَعَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَأَنزَلَ جُنُودٗا لَّمۡ تَرَوۡهَا وَعَذَّبَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْۚ وَذَٰلِكَ جَزَآءُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Then, after you [believers] fled from your enemy, Allah sent down tranquility upon His Messenger and sent it down to the believers, so they stood firm to fight. Moreover, He sent down angels that you did not see and punished those who disbelieved [for what happened to them] in terms of killing, captivity, taking money, and capturing offspring. Likewise, the compensation these people were rewarded is the recompense of the disbelievers who rejected their Messenger and denied what he came with.

Points of Benefit:

The ranks of the mujahideen are numerous, as they hold a superior status with Allah compared to all other ranks. They have the privilege and the highest position, being the triumphant, successful ones who are promised by their Lord with bliss.
In the verses, the greatest evidence of the obligation to love Allah and His Messenger is highlighted, emphasizing the precedence of this love over the love of everything else.
Designating the Day of Hunayn for mention among the days of wars is due to the lessons it holds regarding achieving victory through obedience to the command of Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, and experiencing defeat when prioritizing immediate gains over compliance.
The virtue of the descent of tranquility, as the tranquility of the Prophet, peace be upon him, reflects a sense of reassurance for the Muslims with him, accompanied by confidence in victory. The tranquility of the believers signifies steadfastness and courage after moments of panic and fear.
