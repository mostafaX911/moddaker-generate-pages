(٢٧) ثُمَّ يَتُوبُ ٱللَّهُ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَىٰ مَن يَشَآءُۗ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Then, whoever repents of his disbelief and misguidance after that torture, Allah repents of him and accepts his repentance. Moreover, Allah is Forgiving to those of His slaves who repent, Merciful to them, as He accepts their repentance after disbelief and committing sins.

(٢٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُشۡرِكُونَ نَجَسٞ فَلَا يَقۡرَبُواْ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدَ ٱلۡحَرَامَ بَعۡدَ عَامِهِمۡ هَٰذَاۚ وَإِنۡ خِفۡتُمۡ عَيۡلَةٗ فَسَوۡفَ يُغۡنِيكُمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦٓ إِن شَآءَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٞ

O you who believed in Allah and His Messenger and followed the sacred law that He legislated for them, the polytheists are indeed unclean because of their disbelief, injustice, reprehensible morals, and bad habits. So, they do not enter the sacred area of Mecca [including the Sacred Mosque] even if they want to perform Hajj or Umrah after this year, which is the ninth year of the Hijra. If you fear, O believers, poverty because of the interruption of what they used to bring to you of various foods and trades, then Allah will suffice you of His bounty if He wills. Indeed, Allah knows the situation that you are in, and He is wise in what He manages for you.

(٢٩) قَٰتِلُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَلَا بِٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَلَا يُحَرِّمُونَ مَا حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَلَا يَدِينُونَ دِينَ ٱلۡحَقِّ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ حَتَّىٰ يُعۡطُواْ ٱلۡجِزۡيَةَ عَن يَدٖ وَهُمۡ صَٰغِرُونَ

Fight, O believers, the disbelievers who do not have faith in Allah as their deity without any partner; do not believe in the Day of Resurrection; do not avoid what Allah and His Messenger prohibited for them of carrion, pork, intoxicants, usury, and other matters; and who do not submit to what Allah legislated, among the Jews and Christians, until they pay the Jizyah [tax] with their [own] hands, humbled and subdued.

(٣٠) وَقَالَتِ ٱلۡيَهُودُ عُزَيۡرٌ ٱبۡنُ ٱللَّهِ وَقَالَتِ ٱلنَّصَٰرَى ٱلۡمَسِيحُ ٱبۡنُ ٱللَّهِۖ ذَٰلِكَ قَوۡلُهُم بِأَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡۖ يُضَٰهِ‍ُٔونَ قَوۡلَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن قَبۡلُۚ قَٰتَلَهُمُ ٱللَّهُۖ أَنَّىٰ يُؤۡفَكُونَ

The Jews associated partners with Allah when they claimed that Uzair was the son of Allah. Likewise, the Christians associated partners with Him when they claimed that the Messiah Jesus was the son of Allah. That saying that they fabricated, they spoke with their mouths without establishing proof of it. They are similar in this saying to the polytheists saying before them who said: The angels are the daughters of Allah. Allah is Transcendent and far beyond that. May Allah destroy them. How do they turn away from the straightforward truth to falsehood?!

(٣١) ٱتَّخَذُوٓاْ أَحۡبَارَهُمۡ وَرُهۡبَٰنَهُمۡ أَرۡبَابٗا مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡمَسِيحَ ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ وَمَآ أُمِرُوٓاْ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَٰهٗا وَٰحِدٗاۖ لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

The Jews made their scholars lords instead of Allah, as did the Christians with their monks. They would permit for them what Allah prohibited them from and prohibit them from what Allah permitted for them. The Christians made the Messiah, Jesus, son of Mary, a deity with Allah. Allah did not command the Jews' scholars and Christians' monks, nor Ezra and Jesus, son of Mary, except that they worship Him alone and not associate any partners with Him, for He, may He be glorified, is one deity. There is no rightful object of worship besides Him. Exalted is He from having a partner, as these polytheists and others say.

Points of Benefit:

The verses serve as evidence that connecting the heart to a means of sustenance is permissible and does not contradict relying on Allah.
The verses indicate that sustenance is not attained through personal effort but is a favor from Allah, who manages its distribution.
Jizya is one of three options that Islam presents to its enemies, aiming to ensure that all matters are under the control of the Muslims by removing the threat posed by disbelievers.
Among the Jews is wickedness and evil that led them to dare to challenge Allah and diminish His greatness, Exalted is He.
