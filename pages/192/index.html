(٣٢) يُرِيدُونَ أَن يُطۡفِ‍ُٔواْ نُورَ ٱللَّهِ بِأَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَيَأۡبَى ٱللَّهُ إِلَّآ أَن يُتِمَّ نُورَهُۥ وَلَوۡ كَرِهَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ

These disbelievers and others who are in a sect of disbelief, with their fabrications and their denial of what Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought, want to destroy and invalidate Islam. Likewise, they want to invalidate the clear arguments and clear proofs of Allah's Oneness and that what His Messenger got is true. Allah (may He be Exalted) refuses except to complete His religion, show it, and elevate it over others. If the unbelievers hate His religion's completion, manifestation, and elevation, Allah will complete, show, and elevate it. If Allah wills something, the will of others is invalidated.

(٣٣) هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَرۡسَلَ رَسُولَهُۥ بِٱلۡهُدَىٰ وَدِينِ ٱلۡحَقِّ لِيُظۡهِرَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلدِّينِ كُلِّهِۦ وَلَوۡ كَرِهَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكُونَ

It is Allah (may He be Exalted) who sent His Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم with the Quran, which is a guide for people. Likewise, He sent him with the religion of truth (Islam's religion) to elevate it with its arguments, proofs, and rulings over other faiths, even if the polytheists hate that.

(٣٤) ۞يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡأَحۡبَارِ وَٱلرُّهۡبَانِ لَيَأۡكُلُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ وَيَصُدُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَكۡنِزُونَ ٱلذَّهَبَ وَٱلۡفِضَّةَ وَلَا يُنفِقُونَهَا فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَبَشِّرۡهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ

O you who believe in Allah and do what Allah has prescribed for them, many of the scholars of the Jews (Rabbis) and many of the worshipers of the Christians (Monks) take people's money without a legitimate right. They take it by bribery and other things, preventing people from entering Allah's religion. And those who collect gold and silver and do not pay what is required of them of its Zakat (obligatory alms), then inform them, O Messenger, of the painful torment that will afflict them on the Day of Resurrection.

(٣٥) يَوۡمَ يُحۡمَىٰ عَلَيۡهَا فِي نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ فَتُكۡوَىٰ بِهَا جِبَاهُهُمۡ وَجُنُوبُهُمۡ وَظُهُورُهُمۡۖ هَٰذَا مَا كَنَزۡتُمۡ لِأَنفُسِكُمۡ فَذُوقُواْ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡنِزُونَ

On the Day of Resurrection, what they collected and denied its right will be kindled in the Fire. And when it is incredibly heated, it will be placed on their foreheads, sides, and backs. It will be said to them as a way of reprimanding: This is the money you collected and did not fulfill its due rights, so taste the evil consequences of what you used to collect and not fulfill rights and the consequence of that.

(٣٦) إِنَّ عِدَّةَ ٱلشُّهُورِ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱثۡنَا عَشَرَ شَهۡرٗا فِي كِتَٰبِ ٱللَّهِ يَوۡمَ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِنۡهَآ أَرۡبَعَةٌ حُرُمٞۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلۡقَيِّمُۚ فَلَا تَظۡلِمُواْ فِيهِنَّ أَنفُسَكُمۡۚ وَقَٰتِلُواْ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ كَآفَّةٗ كَمَا يُقَٰتِلُونَكُمۡ كَآفَّةٗۚ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

In Allah's judgment and decree, the number of months of the year is 12 months, as Allah established in al-Lawh al-Mahfuz (the Preserved Tablet), the first thing He created the heavens and the earth. Of these 12 months, there are four months in which Allah has forbidden fighting. Three are consecutive (Dhu'l-Qa'dah, Dhu'l-Hijjah, and Muharram), and one is not (the month of Rajab). The aforementioned are the number of months in the year, and the prohibition of four is the straight religion. Do not wrong yourselves in these sacred months by fighting within them and violating their sanctity. Moreover, fight all polytheists as they fight you all. Know that Allah is with those who fear Him -by obeying what He commanded and avoiding what He prohibited- with victory and steadfastness. Whomever Allah is with, no one will defeat him.

Points of Benefit:

The religion of Allah is apparent and victorious, no matter how much its enemies strive to harm it out of envy from their own souls.
Prohibition of consuming people's wealth unlawfully and refraining from the path of Allah the Almighty.
Prohibition of hoarding wealth without fulfilling the due rights associated with it.
Diligence in maintaining God-consciousness both in private and public, especially during the confrontation with disbelievers, as the believer fears Allah in all circumstances.
