(٣٧) إِنَّمَا ٱلنَّسِيٓءُ زِيَادَةٞ فِي ٱلۡكُفۡرِۖ يُضَلُّ بِهِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يُحِلُّونَهُۥ عَامٗا وَيُحَرِّمُونَهُۥ عَامٗا لِّيُوَاطِ‍ُٔواْ عِدَّةَ مَا حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُ فَيُحِلُّواْ مَا حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُۚ زُيِّنَ لَهُمۡ سُوٓءُ أَعۡمَٰلِهِمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Delaying the sanctity of the month of Muharram to a month that is not Muharram and making it in its place [as the Arabs used to do during the Jahiliyyah (the time of ignorance, i.e., pre-Islam)] is an increase in disbelief over their disbelief in Allah. They disbelieved in His rule in the sacred months. The Devil misled those disbelieving in Allah when He enacted this evil/lousy practice. They would declare a sacred month not to be sacred for one year, exchanging it for one of the permissible months, and they keep it sacred for a year to agree with the number of months that Allah has made sacred, even if they violate its specifics. They do not make a month permissible except that they forbid a month in its place, so they make permissible what Allah has forbidden from the sacred months and violate his rule. The Devil made their evil deeds seem attractive, so they did them, including what they invented by postponing the sacred months. And Allah does not grant success to the disbelievers who insist on their disbelief.

(٣٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَا لَكُمۡ إِذَا قِيلَ لَكُمُ ٱنفِرُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ٱثَّاقَلۡتُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ أَرَضِيتُم بِٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا مِنَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ فَمَا مَتَٰعُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ إِلَّا قَلِيلٌ

O you who believed in Allah and His Messenger and did what He legislated to them. What is the matter with you if you were called to the Jihad (striving/fighting) for the sake of Allah to fight your enemy, you slowed down, and you tend to settle in your homes?! Have you been satisfied with the fleeting enjoyment of this worldly life and its intermittent pleasures instead of the everlasting bliss of the Hereafter that Allah has prepared for the Mujahideen (the fighters) for His sake?! The enjoyment of this worldly life in comparison to the Hereafter is nothing but despicable, so how can a sane person choose a mortal over an eternal and a despicable over a great?!

(٣٩) إِلَّا تَنفِرُواْ يُعَذِّبۡكُمۡ عَذَابًا أَلِيمٗا وَيَسۡتَبۡدِلۡ قَوۡمًا غَيۡرَكُمۡ وَلَا تَضُرُّوهُ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ

If you do not go out, O believers, for striving for the sake of Allah to fight your enemy, Allah will punish you with persecution, humiliation, and other things. He will replace you with people who are devoted and obedient to Allah, who rush to fight when called. And you do not harm Him [in anything] by violating His command, for He is self-sustaining of you, and you are the poor to him. Allah is Omnipotent over everything; nothing can hinder Him. He is capable of supporting His religion and His Prophet without you all.

(٤٠) إِلَّا تَنصُرُوهُ فَقَدۡ نَصَرَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِذۡ أَخۡرَجَهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ثَانِيَ ٱثۡنَيۡنِ إِذۡ هُمَا فِي ٱلۡغَارِ إِذۡ يَقُولُ لِصَٰحِبِهِۦ لَا تَحۡزَنۡ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَنَاۖ فَأَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ سَكِينَتَهُۥ عَلَيۡهِ وَأَيَّدَهُۥ بِجُنُودٖ لَّمۡ تَرَوۡهَا وَجَعَلَ كَلِمَةَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ٱلسُّفۡلَىٰۗ وَكَلِمَةُ ٱللَّهِ هِيَ ٱلۡعُلۡيَاۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٌ

If you do not help, O believers, Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم and respond to his call to strive for the sake of Allah, then Allah has already helped him without you being with him. It was when the polytheists took him and Abu-Bakr (may Allah be pleased with him) out, and there was no third with them when they were in the cave of Thawr, hiding from The disbelievers who were searching for them. The Messenger of Allah صلى الله عليه وسلم said to his companion Abu-Bakr Al-Siddiq when he feared that the polytheists would reach him: Do not grieve, for Allah is with us in His support and victory. Then, Allah sent down His tranquility upon the heart of His Messenger and sent down soldiers you do not see, which are angels supporting him. He made the word of the polytheists the lowest and the word of Allah the highest when He elevated Islam. Allah is Mighty in His Essence, power, and dominion, and no one can overpower Him. He is Wise in His planning, His decree, and His Legislation.

Points of Benefit:

Persevering in customs contrary to religious teachings without disapproval may eventually erase the ugliness of these practices from people's hearts, leading some to mistakenly believe they are virtuous customs.
Failing to participate in mobilization during a state of alert is among the major sins that incur severe punishment, given the significant harm it entails.
The virtue of tranquility is a complete blessing from Allah upon the servant in times of hardships and fears when hearts may become unsettled. It depends on the servant's knowledge of his Lord and confidence in His truthful promise, which is proportional to his faith and courage.
That sorrow may affect the qualities of the sincere servants of Allah, especially when there is fear of missing out on a public interest.
