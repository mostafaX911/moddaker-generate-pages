(٤١) ٱنفِرُواْ خِفَافٗا وَثِقَالٗا وَجَٰهِدُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِكُمۡ وَأَنفُسِكُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

March forth, O believers, for striving for the sake of Allah in hardship and ease, whether young or old, and striving with your wealth and selves. Going out and striving for the sake of Allah with money and life is more beneficial in the life of this world and the Hereafter than sitting and becoming attached to your wealth and self. If you truly know that, then be eager to obey.

(٤٢) لَوۡ كَانَ عَرَضٗا قَرِيبٗا وَسَفَرٗا قَاصِدٗا لَّٱتَّبَعُوكَ وَلَٰكِنۢ بَعُدَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلشُّقَّةُۚ وَسَيَحۡلِفُونَ بِٱللَّهِ لَوِ ٱسۡتَطَعۡنَا لَخَرَجۡنَا مَعَكُمۡ يُهۡلِكُونَ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ إِنَّهُمۡ لَكَٰذِبُونَ

If what you call upon those of the hypocrites who asked you for permission to stay behind were an easy spoil and a journey without hardship, they would have followed you, O Prophet. However, the distance you called them to travel to the enemy was too far for them, so they fell behind. Likewise, those who asked permission [from the hypocrites] to stay behind will swear by Allah when you return to them, saying: If only we could go out to strive with you, we would go out. They destroy themselves by exposing themselves to Allah's punishment because of their backwardness and this false oath. Allah knows they are liars in their claim and their oath.

(٤٣) عَفَا ٱللَّهُ عَنكَ لِمَ أَذِنتَ لَهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ يَتَبَيَّنَ لَكَ ٱلَّذِينَ صَدَقُواْ وَتَعۡلَمَ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ

May Allah forgive you, O Messenger, for your diligence in allowing them to stay behind. Why did you permit it? Was it to discern who among them was truthful in their excuses and who was lying, so that you could authorize the honest and not the liars?

(٤٤) لَا يَسۡتَ‍ٔۡذِنُكَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ أَن يُجَٰهِدُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَأَنفُسِهِمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

It is not the business of those who truly believe in Allah and the Day of Resurrection with sincere faith to ask you, O Messenger, for permission to stay behind from striving for Allah's sake by their wealth and themselves. Instead, it is their business to rally when they are called to fight and to strive by their wealth and themselves. Allah knows those among His slaves who are fearful and do not seek your permission except for excuses preventing them from going out with you.

(٤٥) إِنَّمَا يَسۡتَ‍ٔۡذِنُكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَٱرۡتَابَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡ فَهُمۡ فِي رَيۡبِهِمۡ يَتَرَدَّدُونَ

Those who ask you, O Messenger, for permission to stay behind from striving for the sake of Allah are hypocrites who do not believe in Allah or the Day of Resurrection. Their hearts were struck by doubt about Allah's religion. Likewise, they hesitate in their doubts, are confused, and are not guided to the truth in their doubts.

(٤٦) ۞وَلَوۡ أَرَادُواْ ٱلۡخُرُوجَ لَأَعَدُّواْ لَهُۥ عُدَّةٗ وَلَٰكِن كَرِهَ ٱللَّهُ ٱنۢبِعَاثَهُمۡ فَثَبَّطَهُمۡ وَقِيلَ ٱقۡعُدُواْ مَعَ ٱلۡقَٰعِدِينَ

If they were truthful in their claim that they wanted to go out with you to strive for the sake of Allah, they would have prepared for it by preparing the equipment. However, Allah hated their going out with you, so it was burdensome for them to go out until they preferred to sit in their homes with the sick, weak people, women, and children.

Allah reassured the believers that their deployment in battle was more harmful than their staying back, despite the demoralizing effect of their staying behind, stating:

(٤٧) لَوۡ خَرَجُواْ فِيكُم مَّا زَادُوكُمۡ إِلَّا خَبَالٗا وَلَأَوۡضَعُواْ خِلَٰلَكُمۡ يَبۡغُونَكُمُ ٱلۡفِتۡنَةَ وَفِيكُمۡ سَمَّٰعُونَ لَهُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

It is good that these hypocrites do not go out with you. If they go out with you, they will only increase corruption among you by demoralizing people and casting doubts. Moreover, they would hasten in your ranks to spread gossip to disperse you. And among you, O believers, who listen to what they spread of lies, accept and spread it, and then disagreement arises between you. Allah is All-Knowing of the unjust hypocrites who cast intrigues and doubts among the believers.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation of Jihad with oneself and wealth whenever the need arises.
False oaths lead to destruction.
The obligation of avoiding haste, the necessity of verification and deliberation, refraining from being deceived by the apparent aspects of matters, and the importance of thorough examination and caution.
As a manifestation of Allah's care for the believers, He restrains the hypocrites and prevents them from joining His faithful servants. This is a mercy towards the believers and a kindness to prevent those who would not benefit them but rather harm them from entering their ranks.
