(٤٨) لَقَدِ ٱبۡتَغَوُاْ ٱلۡفِتۡنَةَ مِن قَبۡلُ وَقَلَّبُواْ لَكَ ٱلۡأُمُورَ حَتَّىٰ جَآءَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَظَهَرَ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ وَهُمۡ كَٰرِهُونَ

These hypocrites were keen to corrupt you by dividing the word of the believers and dispersing their unity before the Battle of Tabuk. They diversified and diverted matters for you, O Messenger, by planning tricks. Perhaps their tricks affect your determination to fight. Until Allah's victory and support for you came, strengthened His religion, and subdued His enemies. They hated this because they wanted the victory of falsehood over the right.

(٤٩) وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَقُولُ ٱئۡذَن لِّي وَلَا تَفۡتِنِّيٓۚ أَلَا فِي ٱلۡفِتۡنَةِ سَقَطُواْۗ وَإِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ لَمُحِيطَةُۢ بِٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Among the hypocrites is the one who brings various excuses, saying: O Messenger of Allah, allow me to forsake striving [in battle against the disbelievers] and do not press me to depart with you so that I would not fall into sin due to the tribulation of the women of the enemy, the Romans, when I witness them. Have they not fallen into a tribulation greater than what they claimed: the tribulation of hypocrisy and forsaking? Indeed, on the Day of Resurrection, Hell will surely surround the disbelievers, none of them eluding it, nor will they find an escape from it.

(٥٠) إِن تُصِبۡكَ حَسَنَةٞ تَسُؤۡهُمۡۖ وَإِن تُصِبۡكَ مُصِيبَةٞ يَقُولُواْ قَدۡ أَخَذۡنَآ أَمۡرَنَا مِن قَبۡلُ وَيَتَوَلَّواْ وَّهُمۡ فَرِحُونَ

If you obtain a blessing from Allah, O Messenger of Allah, with what makes you happy regarding victory or spoil, they hate that and grieve for it. On the other hand, if a calamity befalls you due to the severity or victory of an enemy, these hypocrites say: We took precautions for ourselves. Moreover, we took firmness when we did not go out to fight as the believers did, so they [the believers] were afflicted by killing and captivity. Then, these hypocrites go back to their families, rejoicing in their safety.

(٥١) قُل لَّن يُصِيبَنَآ إِلَّا مَا كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَنَا هُوَ مَوۡلَىٰنَاۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to these hypocrites: We will only get what Allah has written for us. For He [Almighty] is our master and refuge to whom we seek shelter, and we rely on Him in our affairs. To Him alone, the believers delegate their affairs, for He is sufficient for them and the best guardian.

(٥٢) قُلۡ هَلۡ تَرَبَّصُونَ بِنَآ إِلَّآ إِحۡدَى ٱلۡحُسۡنَيَيۡنِۖ وَنَحۡنُ نَتَرَبَّصُ بِكُمۡ أَن يُصِيبَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِعَذَابٖ مِّنۡ عِندِهِۦٓ أَوۡ بِأَيۡدِينَاۖ فَتَرَبَّصُوٓاْ إِنَّا مَعَكُم مُّتَرَبِّصُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to them: Are you waiting for anything but victory or martyrdom to happen to us?! Yet we are waiting for Allah to send down on you a torment from Him that will destroy you or punish you with our own hands by killing and capturing you [if He permits us to fight you. Wait for our consequence, for we are waiting for your consequence.

(٥٣) قُلۡ أَنفِقُواْ طَوۡعًا أَوۡ كَرۡهٗا لَّن يُتَقَبَّلَ مِنكُمۡ إِنَّكُمۡ كُنتُمۡ قَوۡمٗا فَٰسِقِينَ

Say, O Messenger, to them: Spend what you spend of your money, willingly or unwillingly. What you spend on it will not be accepted by you because of your disbelief and your deviation from Allah's obedience.

(٥٤) وَمَا مَنَعَهُمۡ أَن تُقۡبَلَ مِنۡهُمۡ نَفَقَٰتُهُمۡ إِلَّآ أَنَّهُمۡ كَفَرُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَبِرَسُولِهِۦ وَلَا يَأۡتُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ إِلَّا وَهُمۡ كُسَالَىٰ وَلَا يُنفِقُونَ إِلَّا وَهُمۡ كَٰرِهُونَ

Nothing prevented them from accepting their expenses except for [three things]: their disbelief in Allah and His Messenger, their laziness and burden when they pray, and that they do not spend their money voluntarily. Instead, they spend it unwillingly and with hatred because they do not hope for a reward in their prayers or spending.

Points of Benefit:

It is the habit of the hypocrites to seek to harm the Muslims through schemes and espionage.
Neglecting jihad is a major corruption and a great trial, constituting disobedience to Allah and His Messenger.
In the verses, there is a lesson for Muslims not to be saddened by what befalls them so that they do not become disheartened and lose their strength. They are taught to be content with what Allah has decreed for them and to seek the satisfaction of their Lord. This is because they are confident that Allah intends the victory of His religion.
One of the signs of weak faith and lack of piety is negligence in performing prayers and spending without contentment and hope for reward.
