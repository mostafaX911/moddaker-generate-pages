(٥٥) فَلَا تُعۡجِبۡكَ أَمۡوَٰلُهُمۡ وَلَآ أَوۡلَٰدُهُمۡۚ إِنَّمَا يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ لِيُعَذِّبَهُم بِهَا فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَتَزۡهَقَ أَنفُسُهُمۡ وَهُمۡ كَٰفِرُونَ

O Messenger, do not admire the wealth of the hypocrites or their children or think it to be good. Yet the end of their wealth and their children is awful. Allah makes it a torment for them with toil and fatigue when collecting it and with the misfortunes that come down in it until Allah expels their souls in the state of their disbelief. Furthermore, they are tormented with eternity in the lowest depths of the Fire.

(٥٦) وَيَحۡلِفُونَ بِٱللَّهِ إِنَّهُمۡ لَمِنكُمۡ وَمَا هُم مِّنكُمۡ وَلَٰكِنَّهُمۡ قَوۡمٞ يَفۡرَقُونَ

The hypocrites swear to you, O believers, yet they are liars. They are among your group and not among you in their hearts. Even if they show that they are among you, they fear that what happened to the polytheists will happen to them regarding killing and captivity, so they show Islam as Taqiyyah (pretend to be Muslim).

(٥٧) لَوۡ يَجِدُونَ مَلۡجَ‍ًٔا أَوۡ مَغَٰرَٰتٍ أَوۡ مُدَّخَلٗا لَّوَلَّوۡاْ إِلَيۡهِ وَهُمۡ يَجۡمَحُونَ

If these hypocrites find shelter in a fortress where they protect themselves, caves in the mountains to hide in, or find a tunnel to enter, they would take refuge in it and enter it hastily.

(٥٨) وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَلۡمِزُكَ فِي ٱلصَّدَقَٰتِ فَإِنۡ أُعۡطُواْ مِنۡهَا رَضُواْ وَإِن لَّمۡ يُعۡطَوۡاْ مِنۡهَآ إِذَا هُمۡ يَسۡخَطُونَ

Among the hypocrites are those who criticize you, O Messenger, for dividing the charities when they do not get what they want from it. If you give them what they ask for, they will be satisfied with you, and if you don't give them what they ask for, they show grumbling.

(٥٩) وَلَوۡ أَنَّهُمۡ رَضُواْ مَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَقَالُواْ حَسۡبُنَا ٱللَّهُ سَيُؤۡتِينَا ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَرَسُولُهُۥٓ إِنَّآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ رَٰغِبُونَ

It would be better if those hypocrites who criticize you in the division of charities were satisfied with what Allah had imposed for them and what His Messenger gave them from it. Also, if they said Allah is sufficient for us, Allah will give us what He wills from His bounty and His Messenger from what Allah has given him, and we ask Allah alone to enrich us and give us His bounty. If they did that, it would be better for them than to criticize you.

When they criticized the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم for his distribution of charity, Allah vindicated him by explaining how it is disbursed and who its beneficiaries were, stating:

(٦٠) ۞إِنَّمَا ٱلصَّدَقَٰتُ لِلۡفُقَرَآءِ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينِ وَٱلۡعَٰمِلِينَ عَلَيۡهَا وَٱلۡمُؤَلَّفَةِ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَفِي ٱلرِّقَابِ وَٱلۡغَٰرِمِينَ وَفِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱبۡنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِۖ فَرِيضَةٗ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٞ

The Zakat (obligatory alms) must be spent on the poor, [which are the needy] who have money from a profession or a job. However, it is not enough for them, and their condition is not noticed. Also, it is for those in need who have almost nothing, and it is obvious to people because of their condition or what they tell people. Moreover, it is for the couriers [those employed] whom the imam sends to collect it. It is also for the disbelievers as a sign of friendship to bring them closer to Islam or for those of weak faith to strengthen their faith or prevent their evil. Likewise, it is to be disbursed to slaves to free them and debtors without extravagance or disobedience if they do not find fulfillment in their debt. Also, it is for equipment to be disposed of for the Mujahideen (fighters) for the sake of Allah and for the traveler whose expenses have been cut off. Restricting the disbursement of the obligatory charity to these people is an obligation from Allah, and Allah knows the interests of His slaves, Wise in His planning and His legislation.

(٦١) وَمِنۡهُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡذُونَ ٱلنَّبِيَّ وَيَقُولُونَ هُوَ أُذُنٞۚ قُلۡ أُذُنُ خَيۡرٖ لَّكُمۡ يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ وَيُؤۡمِنُ لِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَرَحۡمَةٞ لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنكُمۡۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡذُونَ رَسُولَ ٱللَّهِ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Among the hypocrites are those who hurt Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم through their words, so they say when they witness his kindness صلى الله عليه وسلم -: he hears from everyone and believes him, and does not distinguish between truth and falsehood. Say to them, O Messenger, the Messenger only hears the good. Also, he believes in Allah and what the true believers tell him and shows mercy to them. His mission is mercy for those who believe in him, and those who harm him صلى الله عليه وسلم with any harm will have a painful torment.

Points of Benefit:

Wealth and children can be a cause of punishment in this world, and they can also be a cause of punishment in the hereafter. Therefore, the servant should deal with them in a way that pleases his Master so that salvation is achieved through them.
The distribution of Zakat is entrusted to the discretion of the rulers, who allocate it according to the needs of different categories and the abundance of resources.
Harming the Prophet, peace be upon him, regarding his message constitutes disbelief, and it leads to severe punishment.
It is fitting for the servant to have an ear inclined towards goodness and not towards evil. They should listen to what brings benefit and goodness while avoiding and rejecting anything that involves harm and corruption.
