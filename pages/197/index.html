(٦٢) يَحۡلِفُونَ بِٱللَّهِ لَكُمۡ لِيُرۡضُوكُمۡ وَٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥٓ أَحَقُّ أَن يُرۡضُوهُ إِن كَانُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ

The hypocrites swear by Allah to you, O believers, that they did not say anything to hurt the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم. They do this so that you may become pleased with them, but Allah and His Messenger are more deserving of being pleased by their accepting faith and doing righteous deeds if they are indeed believers.

(٦٣) أَلَمۡ يَعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّهُۥ مَن يُحَادِدِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَأَنَّ لَهُۥ نَارَ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدٗا فِيهَاۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡخِزۡيُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

Do these hypocrites not know that by doing this, they are hostile to Allah and His Messenger and that whoever is hostile to Allah and His Messenger will enter the Fire on the Day of Resurrection, where they will stay forever?! That is an excellent indignity and humiliation.

(٦٤) يَحۡذَرُ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ أَن تُنَزَّلَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سُورَةٞ تُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا فِي قُلُوبِهِمۡۚ قُلِ ٱسۡتَهۡزِءُوٓاْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡرِجٞ مَّا تَحۡذَرُونَ

The hypocrites fear that Allah will send down a surah (chapter) to His Messenger, revealing the disbelief they conceal in their hearts to the believers. Say, O Messenger: Continue, O hypocrites, upon your mockery and denigration of the religion, for Allah will bring out what you fear by sending down a surah or by informing His Messenger about it.

(٦٥) وَلَئِن سَأَلۡتَهُمۡ لَيَقُولُنَّ إِنَّمَا كُنَّا نَخُوضُ وَنَلۡعَبُۚ قُلۡ أَبِٱللَّهِ وَءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَرَسُولِهِۦ كُنتُمۡ تَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

If you asked, O Messenger, the hypocrites about the stabbing and insulting that they said against the believers after Allah told you about it, they would say we were just joking when we said that and were not serious. Say, O Messenger: Was it Allah and His verses and Messenger that you were mocking?!

(٦٦) لَا تَعۡتَذِرُواْ قَدۡ كَفَرۡتُم بَعۡدَ إِيمَٰنِكُمۡۚ إِن نَّعۡفُ عَن طَآئِفَةٖ مِّنكُمۡ نُعَذِّبۡ طَآئِفَةَۢ بِأَنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ مُجۡرِمِينَ

Do not apologize, O hypocrites, with these false excuses, for you have shown disbelief by your mocking after you had been hiding it. If We will pardon a group of you for leaving hypocrisy, repenting from it, and being faithful to Allah, We will torment a group of you for their persistence in hypocrisy and not repenting.

(٦٧) ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقَٰتُ بَعۡضُهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡضٖۚ يَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَيَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَيَقۡبِضُونَ أَيۡدِيَهُمۡۚ نَسُواْ ٱللَّهَ فَنَسِيَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ هُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ

The hypocrites [men and women] agree in the states of hypocrisy. They are, in contrast (not like) the believers, as they enjoin evil and prohibit what is good. Moreover, they are stingy with their money, so they do not spend it for the sake of Allah. They forgot Allah and disobeyed Him, so Allah left them out of His success. The hypocrites are those [who are] out of obedience to Allah, the path of truth to His disobedience, and the way of misguidance.

(٦٨) وَعَدَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقَٰتِ وَٱلۡكُفَّارَ نَارَ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ هِيَ حَسۡبُهُمۡۚ وَلَعَنَهُمُ ٱللَّهُۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ مُّقِيمٞ

Allah has promised the hypocrites and disbelievers who did not repent that He would enter them into the Fire to stay therein forever. It is enough for them as a punishment, and Allah expelled them from His mercy. They have continuous torment.

Points of Benefit:

The despicable traits of the hypocrites are numerous, including their tendency to resort to false oaths, their opposition to Allah and His Messenger, their mockery of the Quran, the Prophet, and the believers, their fear of the revelation of a surah in the Quran exposing their deeds, and their excuses claiming they are just joking and playing, which is not an admission of guilt but a more repugnant form of excuse.
Jesting in matters of religion and its rulings is not acceptable. Engaging in falsehood regarding the Book of Allah, His messengers, and His attributes is considered an act of disbelief.
Hypocrisy is a deep-rooted ailment inherent in humans, and the carriers of this ailment are similar in every era. They engage in promoting evil, forbidding good, withholding their support and spending in the path of Allah for jihad, and neglecting their obligations and responsibilities.
The recompense is in accordance with the nature of the deed. Whoever neglects the commandments of Allah and engages in forbidden actions will be deprived of His mercy.
