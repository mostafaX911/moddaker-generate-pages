(٦٩) كَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ كَانُوٓاْ أَشَدَّ مِنكُمۡ قُوَّةٗ وَأَكۡثَرَ أَمۡوَٰلٗا وَأَوۡلَٰدٗا فَٱسۡتَمۡتَعُواْ بِخَلَٰقِهِمۡ فَٱسۡتَمۡتَعۡتُم بِخَلَٰقِكُمۡ كَمَا ٱسۡتَمۡتَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكُم بِخَلَٰقِهِمۡ وَخُضۡتُمۡ كَٱلَّذِي خَاضُوٓاْۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ حَبِطَتۡ أَعۡمَٰلُهُمۡ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ

You, O hypocrites, are in disbelief and mockery like the lying nations before you. They were more significant in strength than you and had more wealth and children. They enjoyed the share written for them from the pleasures and desires of this world, so you, O hypocrites, enjoyed your share destined for you from that like the previous lying nations enjoyed their share. Likewise, you got in denying the truth and slandering the Messenger as they denied the fact and defamed their messengers. Those characterized by these reprehensible qualities are the ones whose deeds are invalidated by their corruption with Allah by disbelief. They are the losers who have lost themselves by bringing themselves to the sources of perdition.

(٧٠) أَلَمۡ يَأۡتِهِمۡ نَبَأُ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ قَوۡمِ نُوحٖ وَعَادٖ وَثَمُودَ وَقَوۡمِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَأَصۡحَٰبِ مَدۡيَنَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡتَفِكَٰتِۚ أَتَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِۖ فَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيَظۡلِمَهُمۡ وَلَٰكِن كَانُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَظۡلِمُونَ

Has the news of what the lying nations have done not come to these hypocrites? What was done to them [the people of Noah, the people of Hūd, the people of Saleh, the people of Abraham, the inhabitants of Midian, and the villages of the people of Lot] in terms of punishment? Their messengers came to them with clear proofs and arguments so Allah would not have wronged them. Their messengers warned them, but they were wronging themselves because they disbelieved in Allah and refused His messengers.

(٧١) وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتُ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ بَعۡضٖۚ يَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَيَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَيُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَيُطِيعُونَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥٓۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ سَيَرۡحَمُهُمُ ٱللَّهُۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٞ

The believing men and women are helpers and supporters of each other because of the faith that unites them. They enjoin what is right, which is all that Almighty Allah loves in terms of obedience to Him, such as monotheism and prayer. Likewise, they forbid evil, which is all that Almighty Allah hates from disobedience, such as disbelief and usury. Moreover, they perform the prayer thoroughly in the most total way, and they obey Allah and His Messenger. For those who are characterized by these praiseworthy qualities, Allah will enter them into His mercy. Allah is Mighty. No one overpowers him, and He is Wise in His creation, planning, and legislation.

(٧٢) وَعَدَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا وَمَسَٰكِنَ طَيِّبَةٗ فِي جَنَّٰتِ عَدۡنٖۚ وَرِضۡوَٰنٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ أَكۡبَرُۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

Allah has promised the believers in Allah, both men and women who believe in Him, that He will admit them on the Day of Resurrection to gardens under which rivers flow beneath their palaces. They will remain forever, in which they will not die and whose bliss will not be interrupted. He promised them that He would admit them to suitable dwellings in gardens of residency, and the satisfaction that Allah will bestow upon them is more significant than all of that. That reward mentioned above is a great win that is unparalleled.

Points of Benefit:

The reason for the punishment for disbelievers and hypocrites is consistent throughout all ages: preferring the worldly life over the Hereafter, indulging in its pleasures, rejecting the prophets, and engaging in deceit, trickery, and betrayal against them.
The destruction of nations and communities of the past due to their disbelief and rejection of the prophets serves as a profound lesson and warning for those who reflect among the wise.
The people of faith, men and women, form a united and interconnected Ummah, collaborating and supporting one another, with hearts united in mutual love, compassion, and sympathy.
The satisfaction of the Lord of the Earth and the Heavens is greater than the delights of the gardens, as spiritual happiness surpasses physical pleasure.
