(٧٣) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ جَٰهِدِ ٱلۡكُفَّارَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ وَٱغۡلُظۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ وَمَأۡوَىٰهُمۡ جَهَنَّمُۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

O Messenger, strive against the disbelievers by fighting them with the sword and strive against the hypocrites with the tongue (well-expressed words) and argument. Be hard on both groups because this is what they deserve. Their abode on the Day of Resurrection is Hell, so this fate is their fate.

(٧٤) يَحۡلِفُونَ بِٱللَّهِ مَا قَالُواْ وَلَقَدۡ قَالُواْ كَلِمَةَ ٱلۡكُفۡرِ وَكَفَرُواْ بَعۡدَ إِسۡلَٰمِهِمۡ وَهَمُّواْ بِمَا لَمۡ يَنَالُواْۚ وَمَا نَقَمُوٓاْ إِلَّآ أَنۡ أَغۡنَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦۚ فَإِن يَتُوبُواْ يَكُ خَيۡرٗا لَّهُمۡۖ وَإِن يَتَوَلَّوۡاْ يُعَذِّبۡهُمُ ٱللَّهُ عَذَابًا أَلِيمٗا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ وَمَا لَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِن وَلِيّٖ وَلَا نَصِيرٖ

The hypocrites swear a false oath by Allah that they did not say what you were told about them, which is insulting you and faulting your religion. Yet they have said what has been reported to you about them, of what makes them unbelievers, and they have shown disbelief after they have shown faith. They had intended to assassinate the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم, but with what they could not achieve it. Moreover, they did not deny anything except something that cannot be denied: Allah favored them by enriching them from the spoils that He bestowed upon His Prophet. If they repent to Allah from their hypocrisy, then their repentance [from their hypocrisy] will be better for them than staying on it. Yet if they turn away from repentance to Allah, He will torture them with a painful torment [in this world] by killing and capturing. Furthermore, He will torture them with a painful torment in the Hereafter by the Fire. They will have no guardian to take care of them and save them from the torment and no helper to avert the torment from them.

(٧٥) ۞وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ عَٰهَدَ ٱللَّهَ لَئِنۡ ءَاتَىٰنَا مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ لَنَصَّدَّقَنَّ وَلَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

Among the hypocrites are those who made a covenant with Allah, saying: If Allah gives us [from] His bounty, we will provide charities to the needy. Moreover, we will be among the righteous whose deeds are right.

(٧٦) فَلَمَّآ ءَاتَىٰهُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦ بَخِلُواْ بِهِۦ وَتَوَلَّواْ وَّهُم مُّعۡرِضُونَ

When Allah Almighty gave them [from] His bounty, they did not fulfill what they had promised Allah. Instead, they withheld their wealth, did not give anything to charity, and turned away from faith.

(٧٧) فَأَعۡقَبَهُمۡ نِفَاقٗا فِي قُلُوبِهِمۡ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يَلۡقَوۡنَهُۥ بِمَآ أَخۡلَفُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا وَعَدُوهُ وَبِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡذِبُونَ

So He made their consequence a constant hypocrisy in their hearts until the Day of Resurrection, as a punishment for their disobedience to Allah's covenant and lying.

(٧٨) أَلَمۡ يَعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ سِرَّهُمۡ وَنَجۡوَىٰهُمۡ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَّٰمُ ٱلۡغُيُوبِ

Did the hypocrites not know that Allah knows what they conceal of plotting and cunning in their gatherings and that Allah, the Exalted, is the Omniscient of the Unseen? Nothing of their deeds is hidden from him, and He will recompense them accordingly.

(٧٩) ٱلَّذِينَ يَلۡمِزُونَ ٱلۡمُطَّوِّعِينَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ فِي ٱلصَّدَقَٰتِ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَجِدُونَ إِلَّا جُهۡدَهُمۡ فَيَسۡخَرُونَ مِنۡهُمۡ سَخِرَ ٱللَّهُ مِنۡهُمۡ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ

Those who fault the volunteers among the believers by giving small charities, who find nothing but a little that is the sum of what they are capable of. They ridicule them, saying: What is the value of their charities?! Allah mocked them as a reward for their mockery of the believers, and they will have a painful torment.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation of Jihad against disbelievers and hypocrites, with Jihad against disbelievers involving physical combat and various forms of weaponry, while Jihad against hypocrites involves presenting arguments and using speech.
The hypocrites are among the worst of people because they betray kindness with wrongdoing.
The verses indicate that breaking covenants and failing to fulfill promises lead to hypocrisy. Therefore, it is incumbent upon Muslims to be extremely cautious and avoid such behavior.
The verses praise the strength of the body and the value of labor, emphasizing that they hold a significant position akin to wealth. This underscores a fundamental principle in considering the foundations of public wealth and highlights the importance of the workforce.
