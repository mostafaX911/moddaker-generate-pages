The Cow (Surah Al-Baqarah)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Command to establish life on earth by implementing submission and servitude to Allah (i.e., Islam) and warns against the condition of the Israelites.

Explanation:

Surah Al-Baqarah is called the Chapter of the Cow because of the mention of the story about the Israelites' cow. The surah's name points to the necessity of hastening to apply Allah's law and avoid stalling as the Jews did.

(١) الٓمٓ

Alif Lām Mīm – letters like these begin certain surahs (chapters) of the Quran. Although separated letters (Alif, Baa, Taa, etc.) of the alphabet have no meaning in and of themselves, they do have wisdom and significance, as there is nothing in the Quran devoid of divine wisdom. Among the significance of these letters is their allusion to the linguistic miracle of the Quran, which comprises letters they know and speak with. Therefore, surahs beginning with letters are usually followed by the mention of the Quran, as we see in this surah.

(٢) ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ لَا رَيۡبَۛ فِيهِۛ هُدٗى لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ

This is the Mighty Quran in which there is nothing of doubt, neither in terms of its origin nor in terms of its words and meaning. It is the word of Allah, guiding those who are mindful of Allah to the path leading to Him.

(٣) ٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَيُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ

They are those who have faith in that which cannot be perceived through the senses and is hidden from us. However, Allah and His Messengers informed us about these matters, such as the Last Day. They perform the prayer in the manner that Allah legislated: its prerequisites, its pillars, its obligatory, and its highly recommended components. And they spend from that which Allah provided them as obligatory alms (Zakat) or voluntary charity (Ṣadaqah), hoping for Allah's reward. They are the ones who believe in the revelation that Allah sent down upon you, O Muhammad, just as he sent it upon the rest of the prophets (may peace be upon them) before you without prejudice. And they are those who firmly believe in the Hereafter and what it contains from reward and punishment.

(٤) وَٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ مِن قَبۡلِكَ وَبِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ يُوقِنُونَ

They are those who have faith in that which cannot be perceived through the senses and is hidden from us. However, Allah and His Messengers informed us about these matters, such as the Last Day. They perform the prayer in the manner that Allah legislated: its prerequisites, its pillars, its obligatory, and its highly recommended components. And they spend from that which Allah provided them as obligatory alms (Zakat) or voluntary charity (Ṣadaqah), hoping for Allah's reward. They are the ones who believe in the revelation that Allah sent down upon you, O Muhammad, just as he sent it upon the rest of the prophets (may peace be upon them) before you without prejudice. And they are those who firmly believe in the Hereafter and what it contains from reward and punishment.

(٥) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ عَلَىٰ هُدٗى مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ

The people who possess these characteristics are on the path of guidance and will be successful in this world and the next, attaining their wishes and being saved from what they fear.

Points of Benefit:

The complete certainty with which the Quran dispels doubt is evidence that it is from Allah, as it is not possible for a created being to claim this in their speech.
Only those who fear Allah the Transcendent and venerate Him will benefit from the great guidance in the Quran.
From the greatest levels of faith is faith in the unseen, as it includes submission to Allah, the Transcendent in everything that He alone knows of the unseen, and to His Messenger in what he has informed about from Him, the Exalted.
Allah, the Transcendent, often pairs prayer with zakat (obligatory alms), because prayer is devotion to the one worshiped, and zakat is kindness to the servants, and they are the embodiment of happiness and salvation.
Belief in Allah and righteous deeds lead to guidance and prosperity in this life, and success and victory in the Hereafter.
