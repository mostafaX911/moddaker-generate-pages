(١٢٧) وَإِذۡ يَرۡفَعُ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمُ ٱلۡقَوَاعِدَ مِنَ ٱلۡبَيۡتِ وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلُ رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلۡ مِنَّآۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

Remember, O Prophet, when Abraham and Ishmael were raising the foundations of the Kaaba. While they did so, they said, with submissiveness and humility: O Lord, accept our deeds from, among which is the building of this house, for You are the Responder to our supplications and Omniscient of our intentions and deeds.

(١٢٨) رَبَّنَا وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا مُسۡلِمَيۡنِ لَكَ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِنَآ أُمَّةٗ مُّسۡلِمَةٗ لَّكَ وَأَرِنَا مَنَاسِكَنَا وَتُبۡ عَلَيۡنَآۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلتَّوَّابُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

O our Lord, make us surrender in devotion to Your command, humble towards You, not worshipping anyone alongside You, and make our descendants a nation who also surrenders in devotion to You. Teach us how to worship You and forgive us for our faults and shortcomings in Your obedience. You are the Acceptor of repentance from those who repent among Your slaves and compassionate towards them.

(١٢٩) رَبَّنَا وَٱبۡعَثۡ فِيهِمۡ رَسُولٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ يَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِكَ وَيُعَلِّمُهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَيُزَكِّيهِمۡۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

O our Lord, send a messenger from among them from the descendants of Ishmael to recite Your revealed verses, teach them the Quran and the Sunnah, and purify them from polytheism and vice. You are the Mighty, Dominant, and Wise in Your actions and laws.

(١٣٠) وَمَن يَرۡغَبُ عَن مِّلَّةِ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمَ إِلَّا مَن سَفِهَ نَفۡسَهُۥۚ وَلَقَدِ ٱصۡطَفَيۡنَٰهُ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَإِنَّهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ لَمِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

No one turns away from the religion of Abraham to other religions except those who wrong themselves through foolishness and bad decisions to leave the truth for misguidance and are contented with humiliation. We chose him as a messenger and a friend in this world, and in the Hereafter, he will be one of the righteous people who fulfilled what God required them to do, and thus, they reached the highest levels.

(١٣١) إِذۡ قَالَ لَهُۥ رَبُّهُۥٓ أَسۡلِمۡۖ قَالَ أَسۡلَمۡتُ لِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Allah chose Abraham for his swiftness in submitting [to Allah]. When his Lord told him: Devote your worship to Me sincerely and submit to Me in obedience. Abraham answered to His Lord: I have submitted myself to Allah in devotion, the One who created His slaves, provided for them, and handled their affairs.

(١٣٢) وَوَصَّىٰ بِهَآ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمُ بَنِيهِ وَيَعۡقُوبُ يَٰبَنِيَّ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱصۡطَفَىٰ لَكُمُ ٱلدِّينَ فَلَا تَمُوتُنَّ إِلَّا وَأَنتُم مُّسۡلِمُونَ

Abraham advised his sons with the statement: 'I have submitted to the Lord of all worlds', and Jacob told his sons to do the same. They both summoned their sons and told them: Allah chose for you the religion of Islam, so hold tightly to it such that death comes to you while you submit to Allah outwardly and inwardly.

(١٣٣) أَمۡ كُنتُمۡ شُهَدَآءَ إِذۡ حَضَرَ يَعۡقُوبَ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ إِذۡ قَالَ لِبَنِيهِ مَا تَعۡبُدُونَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِيۖ قَالُواْ نَعۡبُدُ إِلَٰهَكَ وَإِلَٰهَ ءَابَآئِكَ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمَ وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلَ وَإِسۡحَٰقَ إِلَٰهٗا وَٰحِدٗا وَنَحۡنُ لَهُۥ مُسۡلِمُونَ

Were you present at the time of Jacob's death when he asked his sons: What will you worship after my death? They replied to him: We will worship your God and the God of your fathers: Abraham, Ishmael, and Isaac, One God without partners, and we will surrender in devotion alone and submit to Him.

(١٣٤) تِلۡكَ أُمَّةٞ قَدۡ خَلَتۡۖ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتۡ وَلَكُم مَّا كَسَبۡتُمۡۖ وَلَا تُسۡ‍َٔلُونَ عَمَّا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

That was a nation that has passed on like other nations. They have proceeded to the deeds they have sent forth, and they will face [the consequence of] what they earned of either good or evil, and you will face [the consequence of] what you have earned. You will not be asked about their deeds, and they will not be asked about your deeds. No one will be held accountable for the sins of another; instead, everyone will be recompensed for their own deeds; thus, the deeds of those who came before you should not distract you from paying attention to your own deeds because, after Allah's mercy, nothing will benefit a person anyone except his own righteous deeds.

Points of Benefit:

The pious believer is not deceived by his righteous deeds; rather, he fears that they will be rejected and not accepted from him. That is why he frequently asks Allah to accept them.
The blessing of the invocation of the father of the prophets, Abraham, peace be upon him, as Allah answered it and made the seal of His prophets and the best of His messengers from the people of Mecca.
The religion of Abraham, peace be upon him, is the pure monotheistic religion that conforms to the natural disposition; no one is averse to it or renounces it except the ignorant person who opposes his natural disposition.
The legitimacy of making the legacy for the offspring to follow guidance and to take a pledge from them to adhere to the Truth and adhere to it.
