(٨٠) ٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ أَوۡ لَا تَسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ إِن تَسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ سَبۡعِينَ مَرَّةٗ فَلَن يَغۡفِرَ ٱللَّهُ لَهُمۡۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ كَفَرُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ

Ask, O Messenger, forgiveness for them, or do not ask for it. If you ask for it seventy times, it will not lead to Allah's forgiveness [for them]. They are disbelievers in Allah and His Messenger, and Allah does not guide those who deliberately deviate from His sacred law and intentionally.

(٨١) فَرِحَ ٱلۡمُخَلَّفُونَ بِمَقۡعَدِهِمۡ خِلَٰفَ رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ وَكَرِهُوٓاْ أَن يُجَٰهِدُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَأَنفُسِهِمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَقَالُواْ لَا تَنفِرُواْ فِي ٱلۡحَرِّۗ قُلۡ نَارُ جَهَنَّمَ أَشَدُّ حَرّٗاۚ لَّوۡ كَانُواْ يَفۡقَهُونَ

The hypocrites who stayed behind from the Battle of Tabuk rejoiced because they abstained from striving for the sake of Allah, opposing Allah's Messenger. They hated striving with their money and themselves for Allah's sake as the believers strive. They discouraged their brothers among the hypocrites by saying: Do not march in the heat, and the Battle of Tabuk was in a time of heat. Say to them, O Messenger: The Fire that awaits the hypocrites is much hotter than the heat they ran away from if they only knew.

(٨٢) فَلۡيَضۡحَكُواْ قَلِيلٗا وَلۡيَبۡكُواْ كَثِيرٗا جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ

Let these hypocrites who lag behind Jihad (fighting in Allah's path) laugh a little in their mortal worldly life and cry a lot in their eternal Afterlife. That is a reward for what they had earned from disbelief, disobedience, and sins in this world.

(٨٣) فَإِن رَّجَعَكَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَىٰ طَآئِفَةٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ فَٱسۡتَ‍ٔۡذَنُوكَ لِلۡخُرُوجِ فَقُل لَّن تَخۡرُجُواْ مَعِيَ أَبَدٗا وَلَن تُقَٰتِلُواْ مَعِيَ عَدُوًّاۖ إِنَّكُمۡ رَضِيتُم بِٱلۡقُعُودِ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖ فَٱقۡعُدُواْ مَعَ ٱلۡخَٰلِفِينَ

If Allah returns you, O Prophet, to a group of these hypocrites who are steadfast in their hypocrisy, and they ask you for permission to go out with you on another battle, then say to them: You will never go out, you hypocrites, with me in striving for the sake of Allah, ever. This is as a punishment for you and as a cautionary measure against the evils resulting from your presence with me. You have accepted being left behind in the Battle of Tabuk, so sit back and stay with those left behind from among the sick, women and children.

(٨٤) وَلَا تُصَلِّ عَلَىٰٓ أَحَدٖ مِّنۡهُم مَّاتَ أَبَدٗا وَلَا تَقُمۡ عَلَىٰ قَبۡرِهِۦٓۖ إِنَّهُمۡ كَفَرُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَمَاتُواْ وَهُمۡ فَٰسِقُونَ

Do not perform the prayer, O Messenger, for any dead hypocrite, ever. Likewise, do not stand at his grave to pray for his forgiveness because they disbelieved in Allah and His Messenger and died while they were out of obedience to Allah. Whoever is like that is not prayed for or supplicated for him.

(٨٥) وَلَا تُعۡجِبۡكَ أَمۡوَٰلُهُمۡ وَأَوۡلَٰدُهُمۡۚ إِنَّمَا يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ أَن يُعَذِّبَهُم بِهَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَتَزۡهَقَ أَنفُسُهُمۡ وَهُمۡ كَٰفِرُونَ

Do not, O Messenger, admire the money of these hypocrites or their children. However, Allah wants to torture them with it in the life of this world. That is because of the hardships they suffer for its sake and the misfortunes they suffer in it. Moreover, their souls leave their bodies while they are in [their] disbelief.

(٨٦) وَإِذَآ أُنزِلَتۡ سُورَةٌ أَنۡ ءَامِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَجَٰهِدُواْ مَعَ رَسُولِهِ ٱسۡتَ‍ٔۡذَنَكَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلطَّوۡلِ مِنۡهُمۡ وَقَالُواْ ذَرۡنَا نَكُن مَّعَ ٱلۡقَٰعِدِينَ

When Allah sent down a surah (chapter) to His Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم that included the command to believe in Allah and strive in His way, those who are rich and wealthy asked for permission to lag behind you. They said: Leave us to lag with those with excuses, such as the powerless and sick.

Points of Benefit:

The disbeliever does not benefit from seeking forgiveness or any deeds as long as they remain in a state of disbelief.
The verses indicate the limited insight of human beings. They often focus on their current state and the present reality, neglecting to consider the future and the events that may unfold.
Neglecting to perform acts of obedience when their time comes is a reason for Allah's punishment and a hindrance to the servant from fulfilling them and gaining their virtues.
The verses are evidence of the legitimacy of offering prayers on the believers, visiting their graves, and supplicating for them after their death, as the Prophet, peace be upon him, used to do for the believers.
