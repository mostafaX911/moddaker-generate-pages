(٨٧) رَضُواْ بِأَن يَكُونُواْ مَعَ ٱلۡخَوَالِفِ وَطُبِعَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يَفۡقَهُونَ

These hypocrites accepted humiliation and shame for themselves when they accepted being left behind with those with excuses. Allah sealed their hearts because of their disbelief and hypocrisy, for they do not know what is in their best interest.

(٨٨) لَٰكِنِ ٱلرَّسُولُ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَعَهُۥ جَٰهَدُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَأَنفُسِهِمۡۚ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتُۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ

As for the Messenger and the believers with him, they did not lag in Jihad (striving) for the sake of Allah like these, but instead, they strove for the sake of Allah with their money and their lives. Their reward from Allah was getting worldly benefits, such as victory and spoils. They will be getting the benefits of the Hereafter, including entering Paradise, winning what is desired, and deliverance from what is feared.

(٨٩) أَعَدَّ ٱللَّهُ لَهُمۡ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

Allah has prepared for them [the believers] gardens with rivers flowing beneath their palaces, where they will stay forever, and no annihilation (devastation) will follow them. That reward is the great success that no success can compare to.

(٩٠) وَجَآءَ ٱلۡمُعَذِّرُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَعۡرَابِ لِيُؤۡذَنَ لَهُمۡ وَقَعَدَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَبُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥۚ سَيُصِيبُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

A group of nomads from Medina and its surroundings came to apologize to Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم so that he would permit them to lag (remain behind) from going out and striving (fighting) in Allah's path. Other people lagged and did not even apologize for going out because they did not believe in the Prophet or the promise of Allah. Because of their disbelief, they will suffer and receive painful torment.

(٩١) لَّيۡسَ عَلَى ٱلضُّعَفَآءِ وَلَا عَلَى ٱلۡمَرۡضَىٰ وَلَا عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَجِدُونَ مَا يُنفِقُونَ حَرَجٌ إِذَا نَصَحُواْ لِلَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦۚ مَا عَلَى ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ مِن سَبِيلٖۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

There is no sin on women, children, the sick, the infirm, the blind, and the poor who do not have enough money to spend on equipment. There is no sin on all these people if they fail to go out because their excuses exist. If they are sincere to Allah and His Messenger and work according to His law, there is no way for the do-gooders who have these excuses to inflict punishment on them. Allah Forgives the sins of the do-gooders and is Merciful to them.

(٩٢) وَلَا عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا مَآ أَتَوۡكَ لِتَحۡمِلَهُمۡ قُلۡتَ لَآ أَجِدُ مَآ أَحۡمِلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ تَوَلَّواْ وَّأَعۡيُنُهُمۡ تَفِيضُ مِنَ ٱلدَّمۡعِ حَزَنًا أَلَّا يَجِدُواْ مَا يُنفِقُونَ

Likewise, there is no sin on those absent from you [in the battle] if they came to you, O Messenger, asking you to give them an animal to carry them, and you replied to them: I cannot find an animal to carry you, they turned away from you with eyes pouring tears out of sorrow that they did not find what could be spent from themselves or from you.

When Allah clarified that there is no way to punish those with excuses, He mentioned those who deserve punishment and accountability, for He said:

(٩٣) ۞إِنَّمَا ٱلسَّبِيلُ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَ‍ٔۡذِنُونَكَ وَهُمۡ أَغۡنِيَآءُۚ رَضُواْ بِأَن يَكُونُواْ مَعَ ٱلۡخَوَالِفِ وَطَبَعَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

Indeed, the way of punishment and accountability is only for those who ask you, O Messenger, for permission to be absent from jihad (fighting the disbelievers for the sake of Allah) while they are capable of it by having the means. They have accepted humiliation and shame for themselves by remaining with the laggers in their homes. Allah has sealed their hearts so that they are not affected by any sermon, and because of this seal, they do not know what is in their best interest to choose and what is in their harm to avoid.

Points of Benefit:

The Mujahideen will attain goodness in this world, and if this eludes them, they will achieve success in paradise and be saved from punishment in the Hereafter.
The principle is that the one who does good to people, as an act of generosity, is not held accountable if he falls short in his kindness towards them.
That whoever intends goodness, and firmly resolves to strive within his capacity, then is unable to fulfill it, he is rewarded according to his intention.
Islam is a religion of justice and logic; therefore, it imposes punishment and blame on the hypocrites who seek permission and are wealthy and capable of participating in jihad with both wealth and themselves.
