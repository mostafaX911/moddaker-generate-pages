(٩٤) يَعۡتَذِرُونَ إِلَيۡكُمۡ إِذَا رَجَعۡتُمۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡۚ قُل لَّا تَعۡتَذِرُواْ لَن نُّؤۡمِنَ لَكُمۡ قَدۡ نَبَّأَنَا ٱللَّهُ مِنۡ أَخۡبَارِكُمۡۚ وَسَيَرَى ٱللَّهُ عَمَلَكُمۡ وَرَسُولُهُۥ ثُمَّ تُرَدُّونَ إِلَىٰ عَٰلِمِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

The hypocrites who stay behind during battle make pathetic excuses to the Muslims when they return. Allah directs His Messenger and the believers to respond by saying: Do not make false excuses; we will never believe them. Indeed, Allah has informed us what is in your souls. Allah and His Messenger will see whether you will repent so that Allah accepts your repentance or continue upon hypocrisy. Then you will return to Allah, the One who knows everything, and He will inform you of what you used to do and recompense you accordingly. So hasten in repentance and perform righteous deeds.

(٩٥) سَيَحۡلِفُونَ بِٱللَّهِ لَكُمۡ إِذَا ٱنقَلَبۡتُمۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡ لِتُعۡرِضُواْ عَنۡهُمۡۖ فَأَعۡرِضُواْ عَنۡهُمۡۖ إِنَّهُمۡ رِجۡسٞۖ وَمَأۡوَىٰهُمۡ جَهَنَّمُ جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ

O believers, when you return to them, those who stayed behind will swear by Allah to emphasize their false excuses so that you desist from blaming and reprimanding them. So leave and abandon them furiously; they are filthy and impure inside. They will take refuge in Hell as an abode, as a recompense for their hypocrisy and sins.

(٩٦) يَحۡلِفُونَ لَكُمۡ لِتَرۡضَوۡاْ عَنۡهُمۡۖ فَإِن تَرۡضَوۡاْ عَنۡهُمۡ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَرۡضَىٰ عَنِ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ

Those who stayed behind will swear to please you, O believers, so that you accept their excuses; so do not be pleased with them. If you were to do so, you would have indeed gone against [the commands] of your Lord. Indeed, Allah is not pleased with people disobeying Him through committing disbelief and hypocrisy. So Muslims, beware that you are happy with that which Allah is displeased with.

(٩٧) ٱلۡأَعۡرَابُ أَشَدُّ كُفۡرٗا وَنِفَاقٗا وَأَجۡدَرُ أَلَّا يَعۡلَمُواْ حُدُودَ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِهِۦۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٞ

Those among the Bedouins who disbelieve and commit hypocrisy are worse in disbelief and hypocrisy than the disbelievers who live within the city. Since they are more likely to be ignorant of the religion and most certainly will not know the obligatory, the supererogatory, and the legislative rulings revealed to His Messenger. Moreover, they are harsh and rough and do not mix with other people. Allah is aware of their situation; nothing is hidden from Him. He is Wise in controlling His affairs and His legislation.

(٩٨) وَمِنَ ٱلۡأَعۡرَابِ مَن يَتَّخِذُ مَا يُنفِقُ مَغۡرَمٗا وَيَتَرَبَّصُ بِكُمُ ٱلدَّوَآئِرَۚ عَلَيۡهِمۡ دَآئِرَةُ ٱلسَّوۡءِۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٞ

Some of the hypocrites from the Bedouins consider whatever wealth they spend in Allah's path to be a loss and a punishment because they think that there will be no reward for spending and that Allah will not punish them if they do not spend. Despite this, they sometimes spend to show off, hiding their real beliefs. They wait for evil to come to you, O believers, so they can get rid of you. Instead, Allah has made the evil and the turn of fortune that they wished to afflict the believers with occur on them instead. Allah hears what they say and knows what they keep hidden in their hearts.

(٩٩) وَمِنَ ٱلۡأَعۡرَابِ مَن يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَيَتَّخِذُ مَا يُنفِقُ قُرُبَٰتٍ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَصَلَوَٰتِ ٱلرَّسُولِۚ أَلَآ إِنَّهَا قُرۡبَةٞ لَّهُمۡۚ سَيُدۡخِلُهُمُ ٱللَّهُ فِي رَحۡمَتِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

However, among the Bedouins are those who believe in Allah and the Last Day and make what they spend in the path of Allah a way to gain closeness to Allah and a means of receiving the prayers of the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم and his seeking forgiveness for them. Indeed, their spending in the path of Allah and the Messenger praying for them will bring them closer to Allah. They will find the reward with Him. Allah will admit them into His vast Mercy that includes His forgiveness and Paradise. Indeed, Allah forgives His slaves when they repent and is Merciful to them.

Points of Benefit:

The field of action and responsibilities is the best witness to expose the lies of the hypocrites and reveal their true nature.
If the people of the desert were to disbelieve, their disbelief and hypocrisy would be more severe than that of the urban dwellers, due to the influence of their environment.
Encouraging expenditure in the path of Allah with sincere intention magnifies the reward for those who do so.
The virtue of knowledge, and that the one devoid of it, is closer to error.
