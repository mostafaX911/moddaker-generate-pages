(١٠٠) وَٱلسَّٰبِقُونَ ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ وَٱلۡأَنصَارِ وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُم بِإِحۡسَٰنٖ رَّضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُ وَأَعَدَّ لَهُمۡ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي تَحۡتَهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

Allah is pleased with and has accepted the obedience of the foremost in faith, the first emigrants who immigrated from their homes and lands for the sake of Allah, the helpers who helped His Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم, and those who followed them in goodness; in their creed, good speech and action. They are pleased with Him for what He will grant them of His great reward, and has prepared gardens with rivers flowing beneath its palaces: That is the utmost success.

(١٠١) وَمِمَّنۡ حَوۡلَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَعۡرَابِ مُنَٰفِقُونَۖ وَمِنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ مَرَدُواْ عَلَى ٱلنِّفَاقِ لَا تَعۡلَمُهُمۡۖ نَحۡنُ نَعۡلَمُهُمۡۚ سَنُعَذِّبُهُم مَّرَّتَيۡنِ ثُمَّ يُرَدُّونَ إِلَىٰ عَذَابٍ عَظِيمٖ

There are hypocrites among the Bedouins living close to Medina. There are also hypocrites among the people of Medina, who have remained obstinate in their hypocrisy. You do not know them, O Prophet; Allah truly knows who they are. Therefore, Allah will punish them twice: in the worldly life by exposing their hypocrisy and with execution and captivity, and then in the hereafter, with the punishment of the grave. Then, they will be returned on the Day of Resurrection for a painful punishment in the lowest depths of the Fire.

(١٠٢) وَءَاخَرُونَ ٱعۡتَرَفُواْ بِذُنُوبِهِمۡ خَلَطُواْ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا وَءَاخَرَ سَيِّئًا عَسَى ٱللَّهُ أَن يَتُوبَ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٌ

Some of the people of Medina did not participate in battle without a valid excuse. They admitted to this and did not come up with false reasons. So they have mixed obedience to Allah (by doing righteous actions, adhering to His legislation, and battling in His path) with evil, hoping Allah would accept their repentance and overlook their [evil deeds]. Indeed, Allah is most forgiving to those of His slaves who repent and is Merciful to them.

(١٠٣) خُذۡ مِنۡ أَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ صَدَقَةٗ تُطَهِّرُهُمۡ وَتُزَكِّيهِم بِهَا وَصَلِّ عَلَيۡهِمۡۖ إِنَّ صَلَوٰتَكَ سَكَنٞ لَّهُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ

In order to purify them from the impurity of disobedience and sins, [O Prophet] take alms out of their property, hoping they are replaced with good deeds, and pray for them. Indeed, your prayer is mercy and safety for them. Allah hears your prayer and knows their good actions and intentions.

(١٠٤) أَلَمۡ يَعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ يَقۡبَلُ ٱلتَّوۡبَةَ عَنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ وَيَأۡخُذُ ٱلصَّدَقَٰتِ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلتَّوَّابُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Those who did not participate in battle and repent to Allah should understand that Allah accepts the repentance of His slaves and the voluntary charity [given for His sake], while He does not need it. He will reward the one who spends in charity, and it is He (may He be Exalted) who is the Acceptor of repentance when His slaves repent and is the most Merciful to them.

(١٠٥) وَقُلِ ٱعۡمَلُواْ فَسَيَرَى ٱللَّهُ عَمَلَكُمۡ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَۖ وَسَتُرَدُّونَ إِلَىٰ عَٰلِمِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to those who had stayed behind from fighting in Allah's path and have repented from their sin: Make up for the harm caused by what you missed and be sincere in your deeds to Allah. Do what pleases Him because Allah, His Messenger, and the believers will soon see your deeds. Then you will be returned, on the Day of Resurrection, to your Lord, the One Who knows everything. He knows what you hide and what you make public, and He will inform you about what you used to do in this world and recompense you for that accordingly.

(١٠٦) وَءَاخَرُونَ مُرۡجَوۡنَ لِأَمۡرِ ٱللَّهِ إِمَّا يُعَذِّبُهُمۡ وَإِمَّا يَتُوبُ عَلَيۡهِمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٞ

There are others from among those who stayed behind from the battle of Tabuk who did not have any valid excuse. The matter of these people has been deferred to Allah's decree and decision regarding them. Allah will judge them as He wishes; either He will torment them if they do not repent to Him, or He will accept their repentance if they do. Allah knows who is deserving of His punishment and who deserves His pardon. Allah is Wise in His legislation and planning. This was referring to Murarah bin Al-Rabi, Ka'b bin Malik, and Hilal bin Umayyah.

Points of Benefit:

The virtue of hastening towards faith, emigrating in the path of Allah, supporting the religion, and following the path of the righteous predecessors.
Allah, the Almighty, exclusively possesses knowledge of the unseen, for no one knows what lies in the hearts except Allah.
Hope for those among the believers who engage in sinful acts lies in Allah's forgiveness and acceptance of their repentance, provided they turn back to Him and rectify their deeds.
The obligation of Zakat and elucidation of its virtues, as well as its impact on the growth of wealth and the purification of souls from miserliness and other moral afflictions.
