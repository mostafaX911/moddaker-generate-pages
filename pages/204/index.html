(١٠٧) وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مَسۡجِدٗا ضِرَارٗا وَكُفۡرٗا وَتَفۡرِيقَۢا بَيۡنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَإِرۡصَادٗا لِّمَنۡ حَارَبَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ مِن قَبۡلُۚ وَلَيَحۡلِفُنَّ إِنۡ أَرَدۡنَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۖ وَٱللَّهُ يَشۡهَدُ إِنَّهُمۡ لَكَٰذِبُونَ

Among the hypocrites are those who built a mosque not for Allah's obedience but to harm the Muslims, to support disbelief by strengthening the people of hypocrisy, to cause disunity among the believers, and as preparation and anticipation for those who had waged war against Allah and His Messenger before the construction of the mosque. These hypocrites swear to you: We only intended goodness for the Muslims. Allah is a witness that they are lying in this claim of theirs.

(١٠٨) لَا تَقُمۡ فِيهِ أَبَدٗاۚ لَّمَسۡجِدٌ أُسِّسَ عَلَى ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ مِنۡ أَوَّلِ يَوۡمٍ أَحَقُّ أَن تَقُومَ فِيهِۚ فِيهِ رِجَالٞ يُحِبُّونَ أَن يَتَطَهَّرُواْۚ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُطَّهِّرِينَ

O Prophet, do not accept the invitation of the hypocrites for you to pray in such a mosque. Instead, the Mosque of Quba, founded from its beginning upon fear of Allah, is more worthy of you praying in it than this mosque founded upon disbelief. In the Mosque of Quba, there are men who love to purify themselves from ritual and physical impurities with water, as well as from sins with repentance and seeking forgiveness. Allah loves those who purify themselves from ritual and physical impurities and sins.

(١٠٩) أَفَمَنۡ أَسَّسَ بُنۡيَٰنَهُۥ عَلَىٰ تَقۡوَىٰ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرِضۡوَٰنٍ خَيۡرٌ أَم مَّنۡ أَسَّسَ بُنۡيَٰنَهُۥ عَلَىٰ شَفَا جُرُفٍ هَارٖ فَٱنۡهَارَ بِهِۦ فِي نَارِ جَهَنَّمَۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Can those who founded their building upon the fear of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions and upon His pleasure by doing all forms of good be equal to those who built a mosque to cause harm to the Muslims, strengthen disbelief, and cause disunity among the believers? They can never be equal! The first group has a strong structure with a good foundation, so it will not collapse. Yet, the second group is like a person who built a structure on the edge of a pit that is about to collapse, and then it collapses with him into the depths of Hell. Allah does not guide those who do wrong by committing disbelief, hypocrisy, and other evils.

(١١٠) لَا يَزَالُ بُنۡيَٰنُهُمُ ٱلَّذِي بَنَوۡاْ رِيبَةٗ فِي قُلُوبِهِمۡ إِلَّآ أَن تَقَطَّعَ قُلُوبُهُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌ

The mosque they built to cause harm will continue to be a source of doubt and hypocrisy in their hearts until their hearts are torn apart through death or by being killed by the sword. Allah knows the deeds of His slaves, and He is Wise in the judgments He passes with respect to recompensing them for their good or evil.

After Allah exposed the disgrace of the hypocrites who neglected jihad, He mentioned the reward of those who fight for His cause, stating:

(١١١) ۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱشۡتَرَىٰ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَأَمۡوَٰلَهُم بِأَنَّ لَهُمُ ٱلۡجَنَّةَۚ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَيَقۡتُلُونَ وَيُقۡتَلُونَۖ وَعۡدًا عَلَيۡهِ حَقّٗا فِي ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِ وَٱلۡإِنجِيلِ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِۚ وَمَنۡ أَوۡفَىٰ بِعَهۡدِهِۦ مِنَ ٱللَّهِۚ فَٱسۡتَبۡشِرُواْ بِبَيۡعِكُمُ ٱلَّذِي بَايَعۡتُم بِهِۦۚ وَذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

Allah (may He be Exalted) has purchased from the believers their lives, despite them belonging to Him, out of His grace for a high price: Paradise. They will fight the disbelievers so that Allah's Word is highest, killing the disbelievers or being killed by them. Allah has made this true promise in the Torah, the Book of Moses, the Gospel, the Book of Jesus, and the Quran, the Book of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. No one is more faithful to his promise than Allah; may He be Exalted. So, rejoice, O believers, with the deal you made with Allah because you gained such a huge profit from it. That deal is a great success.

Points of Benefit:

The love of Allah is constant for those who purify themselves from physical and spiritual impurities.
Not all deeds are equal; it is the intention for the sake of Allah that distinguishes the lasting and rewarding deeds, bringing happiness to their doers. Conversely, deeds aimed at supporting disbelief and antagonizing Muslims will perish and bring misery to their doers.
The legitimacy of jihad and the encouragement towards it existed in the preceding religious laws before Islam as well.
In every situation where division among believers occurs, it is considered a sin that must be abandoned and rectified. Similarly, in every circumstance that leads to the gathering and unity of believers, it is imperative to follow and promote it, encouraging and emphasizing such unity.
