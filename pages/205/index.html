(١١٢) ٱلتَّٰٓئِبُونَ ٱلۡعَٰبِدُونَ ٱلۡحَٰمِدُونَ ٱلسَّٰٓئِحُونَ ٱلرَّٰكِعُونَ ٱلسَّٰجِدُونَ ٱلۡأٓمِرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَٱلنَّاهُونَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱلۡحَٰفِظُونَ لِحُدُودِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

These people who will receive this great reward return from what Allah dislikes and despises to what He loves and approves of; they submit out of awe and humility to Allah and obey Him; they praise their Lord in all conditions; they fast and pray; they instruct and prohibit what Allah and His Messenger instructs and prohibits; they guard Allah's instructions through following them and His prohibitions through avoiding them. O Messenger, tell the believers with these qualities the good news of what will make them happy in this world and the Hereafter.

(١١٣) مَا كَانَ لِلنَّبِيِّ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَن يَسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ لِلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ وَلَوۡ كَانُوٓاْ أُوْلِي قُرۡبَىٰ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا تَبَيَّنَ لَهُمۡ أَنَّهُمۡ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

It is not appropriate for the Prophet and the believers to seek forgiveness from Allah for the polytheists, even if they are their relatives, after it has become clear to them that they are the people of the Fire because they died on polytheism.

(١١٤) وَمَا كَانَ ٱسۡتِغۡفَارُ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ لِأَبِيهِ إِلَّا عَن مَّوۡعِدَةٖ وَعَدَهَآ إِيَّاهُ فَلَمَّا تَبَيَّنَ لَهُۥٓ أَنَّهُۥ عَدُوّٞ لِّلَّهِ تَبَرَّأَ مِنۡهُۚ إِنَّ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ لَأَوَّٰهٌ حَلِيمٞ

Abraham's seeking forgiveness for his [polytheist] father was only because of a promise he had made to do so, hoping he would submit. When it became clear to Abraham that his father was an enemy of Allah, due to the advice not being of benefit to him or due to him knowing through revelation that he would die as a disbeliever, he disassociated himself from him. He had asked forgiveness for him based on his own reasoning and not in contradiction of any law that Allah had revealed to him. Abraham (peace be with him) used to turn to Allah in humility frequently, and he used to overlook and pardon his people who were wrongdoers.

(١١٥) وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيُضِلَّ قَوۡمَۢا بَعۡدَ إِذۡ هَدَىٰهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ يُبَيِّنَ لَهُم مَّا يَتَّقُونَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٌ

Allah would never consider a people misguided after He had guided them until He clarifies to them those forbidden things they must avoid. If they do what Allah had forbidden after it has been declared forbidden and made clear to them, He will consider them misguided. Allah knows everything. Nothing is hidden from Him. He has taught you what you did not know.

(١١٦) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُۚ وَمَا لَكُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مِن وَلِيّٖ وَلَا نَصِيرٖ

The dominion of the heavens and the Earth belongs to Allah. He has no partner in this; nothing in them is hidden from Him. He gives life to whoever He wills and causes to die whoever He wills. You, O people, do not have, besides Allah, any guardian to take care of your affairs nor any helper to guard you against evil and assist you against your enemy.

(١١٧) لَّقَد تَّابَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّ وَٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ وَٱلۡأَنصَارِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُ فِي سَاعَةِ ٱلۡعُسۡرَةِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا كَادَ يَزِيغُ قُلُوبُ فَرِيقٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ ثُمَّ تَابَ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِهِمۡ رَءُوفٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Allah has forgiven the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم since he permitted the hypocrites to stay behind from the battle of Tabuk. He has also forgiven the Muhajireen and the Ansar, who did not stay behind but followed him in the battle of Tabuk despite the intense heat, the lack of means, and the strength of the enemy. This was after the hearts of a group of them who intended to abandon the expedition almost deviated because of the great difficulty they faced. Then Allah inspired them to be patient and to go out for that battle. He forgave them, for He (may He be Exalted) is Kind and Merciful towards them. Because of His mercy, He inspired them to repent to Him and accepted their repentance.

Points of Benefit:

The invalidity of objecting to the permissibility of seeking forgiveness for the polytheists based on the actions of the Prophet Ibrahim (peace be upon him).
That sins and transgressions are the cause of calamities, abandonment, and a lack of success.
That Allah is the Owner of the Dominion, He is our Protector, and there is neither protector nor helper for us besides Him.
Clarification of the virtues of the companions of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) over all other people.
