(١١٨) وَعَلَى ٱلثَّلَٰثَةِ ٱلَّذِينَ خُلِّفُواْ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا ضَاقَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ بِمَا رَحُبَتۡ وَضَاقَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَنفُسُهُمۡ وَظَنُّوٓاْ أَن لَّا مَلۡجَأَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّآ إِلَيۡهِ ثُمَّ تَابَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ لِيَتُوبُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلتَّوَّابُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Allah forgave the three, Murarah bin Al-Rabi, Ka'b bin Malik and Hilal bin Umayyah. Their repentance and acceptance were delayed after they stayed behind instead of going to Tabuk with Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم. The Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم instructed people to stay away from them, making them feel sadness and grief. The Earth seemed narrow to them despite its vastness, and their hearts felt constricted because of their loneliness. They were certain there was no place of refuge or help they could go to except Allah alone. Allah was Merciful to them by inspiring them to repent to Him and accepting their repentance. Allah is the One who accepts the repentance from His slaves and is full of mercy and compassion for them.

(١١٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَكُونُواْ مَعَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

O you who have believed in Allah, followed His Messenger, and acted according to His sacred law, fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, and be with those who are truthful in their belief, statements, and deeds, as there is no salvation except in truthfulness.

(١٢٠) مَا كَانَ لِأَهۡلِ ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ وَمَنۡ حَوۡلَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَعۡرَابِ أَن يَتَخَلَّفُواْ عَن رَّسُولِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَا يَرۡغَبُواْ بِأَنفُسِهِمۡ عَن نَّفۡسِهِۦۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ لَا يُصِيبُهُمۡ ظَمَأٞ وَلَا نَصَبٞ وَلَا مَخۡمَصَةٞ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَا يَطَ‍ُٔونَ مَوۡطِئٗا يَغِيظُ ٱلۡكُفَّارَ وَلَا يَنَالُونَ مِنۡ عَدُوّٖ نَّيۡلًا إِلَّا كُتِبَ لَهُم بِهِۦ عَمَلٞ صَٰلِحٌۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُضِيعُ أَجۡرَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

It is not for the people of Medina, or the Bedouins around them, to stay behind after [the departure of] Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم if he himself goes out to fight, nor should they be stingy and give preference to their own safety over the safety of the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم. Instead, they should sacrifice themselves for him. This is because they suffer no thirst, fatigue, or hunger in Allah's path, nor do they settle anywhere by which their presence angers the disbelievers, nor do they make a gain over their enemy, killing or capturing them, acquiring booty or defeating them, except that Allah records for them the reward of a righteous deed, which He accepts from them. Allah does not allow the reward of those who do good to be lost. Instead, He gives this reward to them in full and increases it for them.

(١٢١) وَلَا يُنفِقُونَ نَفَقَةٗ صَغِيرَةٗ وَلَا كَبِيرَةٗ وَلَا يَقۡطَعُونَ وَادِيًا إِلَّا كُتِبَ لَهُمۡ لِيَجۡزِيَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ أَحۡسَنَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

And they do not sacrifice any wealth, small or large, or cross a valley except that their sacrifice or journey is recorded so that Allah may repay them, giving them in the Hereafter the reward for the best of what they used to do.

(١٢٢) ۞وَمَا كَانَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ لِيَنفِرُواْ كَآفَّةٗۚ فَلَوۡلَا نَفَرَ مِن كُلِّ فِرۡقَةٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ طَآئِفَةٞ لِّيَتَفَقَّهُواْ فِي ٱلدِّينِ وَلِيُنذِرُواْ قَوۡمَهُمۡ إِذَا رَجَعُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِمۡ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَحۡذَرُونَ

It is not appropriate for believers to go out all together to fight lest they be wiped out if their enemy overcomes them. Why does one group not go out to fight, and another group remain behind to accompany Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم and learn the religion by listening to the Quran and sacred laws from him so they can advise their people when they return to them of what they have learned so that they are aware of Allah's torment and punishment and comply with His instructions and avoid His prohibitions? This concerned the troops that Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم would send to different areas, choosing a group from among his companions.

Points of Benefit:

The necessity of God-consciousness (Taqwa) and truthfulness, as they are the means for salvation from destruction.
The great virtue of spending in the path of Allah.
The obligation of acquiring religious knowledge is similar to that of engaging in Jihad, as both are essential for the establishment of the religion, and one cannot exist without the other.
