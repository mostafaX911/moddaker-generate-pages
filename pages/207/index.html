(١٢٣) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ قَٰتِلُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ يَلُونَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡكُفَّارِ وَلۡيَجِدُواْ فِيكُمۡ غِلۡظَةٗۚ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Allah instructed the believers to fight those disbelievers near them due to the danger they pose to the believers because of their proximity and to show strength and power to frighten them and repel their evil. Allah, the Exalted, is with the God-fearing believers with His help and support.

(١٢٤) وَإِذَا مَآ أُنزِلَتۡ سُورَةٞ فَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَقُولُ أَيُّكُمۡ زَادَتۡهُ هَٰذِهِۦٓ إِيمَٰنٗاۚ فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ فَزَادَتۡهُمۡ إِيمَٰنٗا وَهُمۡ يَسۡتَبۡشِرُونَ

When Allah revealed a surah [chapter] to the Messenger, some of the hypocrites would ask by way of ridicule and mockery: Which of you believers has increased in belief in what Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم has brought due to this surah? As for those who believed in Allah and His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, the surah's revelation had indeed increased their belief, and they were happy with the revelation because of the benefit it contained for them in this world and the Hereafter.

(١٢٥) وَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٞ فَزَادَتۡهُمۡ رِجۡسًا إِلَىٰ رِجۡسِهِمۡ وَمَاتُواْ وَهُمۡ كَٰفِرُونَ

As for the hypocrites, the revelation of the Quran, with its laws and stories, increases their sickness and wickedness because they reject what is revealed. So, the disease of their hearts increases with the revelation of the Quran because whenever something is revealed, they doubt what it contains and die in disbelief.

(١٢٦) أَوَلَا يَرَوۡنَ أَنَّهُمۡ يُفۡتَنُونَ فِي كُلِّ عَامٖ مَّرَّةً أَوۡ مَرَّتَيۡنِ ثُمَّ لَا يَتُوبُونَ وَلَا هُمۡ يَذَّكَّرُونَ

Do the hypocrites not see and learn how Allah has tested them by exposing their condition and hypocrisy once or twice a year? Then, despite knowing that Allah did this to them, they do not repent from their disbelief, abandon their hypocrisy, or consider what happened to them and that it is from Allah.

(١٢٧) وَإِذَا مَآ أُنزِلَتۡ سُورَةٞ نَّظَرَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ إِلَىٰ بَعۡضٍ هَلۡ يَرَىٰكُم مِّنۡ أَحَدٖ ثُمَّ ٱنصَرَفُواْۚ صَرَفَ ٱللَّهُ قُلُوبَهُم بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَوۡمٞ لَّا يَفۡقَهُونَ

Whenever Allah reveals a surah [chapter] to His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, mentioning the condition of the hypocrites, the hypocrites would look at one another, saying: Is anyone watching you? If no one is watching them, they leave the gathering. Behold! Allah turned their hearts away from guidance and goodness and deserted them because they are a people who do not understand.

(١٢٨) لَقَدۡ جَآءَكُمۡ رَسُولٞ مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ عَزِيزٌ عَلَيۡهِ مَا عَنِتُّمۡ حَرِيصٌ عَلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رَءُوفٞ رَّحِيمٞ

There has come to you, O Arabs, a Messenger from among yourselves, who is an Arab like you. What is difficult for you is concerning to him. He is greatly concerned for your guidance and is kind and compassionate with the believers.

(١٢٩) فَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَقُلۡ حَسۡبِيَ ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ عَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡتُۖ وَهُوَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

If they turn away from you, O Messenger, not believing in what you brought, then say: Allah is sufficient for me; none is worthy of worship except Him. I rely on Him alone, and He (may He be Exalted) is the Lord of the Great Throne.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation to initiate combat with the nearest disbelievers when the scope of Islam expands and there is a need for it.
Clarification of the condition of the hypocrites when the Quran was revealed to them, characterized by anticipation and anxiety.
Explanation of the Prophet's (peace be upon him) compassion for the believers and his keenness on their well-being.
In these verses, there is evidence that belief can increase and decrease. It is incumbent upon the believer to introspect his belief, renew and nurture it, ensuring that it remains in a state of ascension.
