Jonah (Surah Yunus)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Affirming prophethood through evidence, inviting rejectors to faith, and threatening them with divine torment.

Explanation:

(١) الٓرۚ تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ

Alif Lām Rā – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah. The verses recited in this surah are from the verses of a clear and perfect Quran containing wisdom and laws.

(٢) أَكَانَ لِلنَّاسِ عَجَبًا أَنۡ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ رَجُلٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ أَنۡ أَنذِرِ ٱلنَّاسَ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَنَّ لَهُمۡ قَدَمَ صِدۡقٍ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡۗ قَالَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَسَٰحِرٞ مُّبِينٌ

Are the people amazed that We have sent revelation to a man from among themselves, instructing him to warn them about Allah's torment and to inform those who believe in Allah with news that will make them happy; that they will receive a high rank with their Lord (may He be Exalted) as a reward for the righteous deeds they did? But the disbelievers said that the man who brought these verses was clearly a magician.

(٣) إِنَّ رَبَّكُمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ يُدَبِّرُ ٱلۡأَمۡرَۖ مَا مِن شَفِيعٍ إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ إِذۡنِهِۦۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمۡ فَٱعۡبُدُوهُۚ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ

O those who are amazed, surely, your Lord is Allah, who created the heavens despite their greatness and the Earth despite its vastness in six days and then ascended and rose above the Throne. Why are they amazed that Allah has sent a man from among you? Allah alone determines and decrees in His vast dominion, and none can intercede with Him on behalf of anything unless Allah permits and is pleased with the one who intercedes. That is Allah, your Lord, so sincerely worship Him alone. Do you not heed all these proofs and evidence of His Oneness? Whoever heeds this reminder will know that and believe in Him.

(٤) إِلَيۡهِ مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ جَمِيعٗاۖ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقًّاۚ إِنَّهُۥ يَبۡدَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَلۡقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُۥ لِيَجۡزِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَهُمۡ شَرَابٞ مِّنۡ حَمِيمٖ وَعَذَابٌ أَلِيمُۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡفُرُونَ

To Him alone is your return on the Day of Resurrection to recompense you for your deeds. Allah has made a truthful promise to humanity that He will not break, and He is well capable of that. He initiated creation without precedent. He will bring them back again after their death to recompense justly those who believed in Allah and did righteous deeds, neither decreasing their good actions nor increasing their bad actions. As for those who disbelieve in Allah and His Messengers, they will have a drink of scalding water that will cut their intestines and will have a painful torment because of their disbelief.

(٥) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ ضِيَآءٗ وَٱلۡقَمَرَ نُورٗا وَقَدَّرَهُۥ مَنَازِلَ لِتَعۡلَمُواْ عَدَدَ ٱلسِّنِينَ وَٱلۡحِسَابَۚ مَا خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ ذَٰلِكَ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ يُفَصِّلُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ

It is He Who made the sun radiate light and the moon reflect it, determining its progression through twenty-eight phases, a phase being the change it goes through every twenty-four hours so that you, O people, would know the number of days through the sun and the number of months and years through the moon. Allah did not create the heavens, the Earth, and what they contain except with the truth, revealing His power and greatness to people. Allah makes clear these obvious signs and evidence of His Oneness for people who know how to draw conclusions from such facts.

(٦) إِنَّ فِي ٱخۡتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ وَمَا خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَّقُونَ

Indeed, in the alternation of the night and day, the darkness and light that accompany this, the shortness of one and length of the other, and the creation in the heavens and the Earth, are signs showing the power of Allah for a people who fear Him by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

Points of Benefit:

Affirming the Prophethood of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم and that his mission was reasonable and unsurprising.
The creation of the heavens and the earth and all that is within them, the orchestration of affairs, the determination of times, and the alternation of night and day—all are magnificent signs indicative of the divinity of Allah, the Most High.
Intercession on the Day of Resurrection is granted only to those whom Allah permits and who are pleased with His words and deeds.
Allah, the Almighty, determining the movement of the sun and the stages of the moon, aids in the regulation of time, days, and years.
