(٧) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَرۡجُونَ لِقَآءَنَا وَرَضُواْ بِٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱطۡمَأَنُّواْ بِهَا وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَنۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا غَٰفِلُونَ

The disbelievers who do not expect the meeting with Allah, thus not fearing or desiring it, who are satisfied with the fleeting life of this world instead of the eternal life of the Hereafter, feeling content in this world, and who are heedless and turn away from the signs and proofs of Allah,

(٨) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ مَأۡوَىٰهُمُ ٱلنَّارُ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ

Their dwelling place on the Day of Resurrection will be the Fire because of what they earned through their disbelief and rejection of the Day of Resurrection.

(٩) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ يَهۡدِيهِمۡ رَبُّهُم بِإِيمَٰنِهِمۡۖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهِمُ ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ فِي جَنَّٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

Those who believe in Allah and do righteous deeds, Allah will guide them, because of their belief, to do more righteous deeds that lead to His Pleasure. Then, on the Day of Resurrection, Allah will admit them into gardens of eternal bliss with rivers flowing beneath them.

(١٠) دَعۡوَىٰهُمۡ فِيهَا سُبۡحَٰنَكَ ٱللَّهُمَّ وَتَحِيَّتُهُمۡ فِيهَا سَلَٰمٞۚ وَءَاخِرُ دَعۡوَىٰهُمۡ أَنِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Their supplication in Paradise will be the exaltation of Allah. Allah's greeting to them, the angels, and their greeting to each other will be: Peace, and the end of their supplication will be the praise of Allah, the Lord of all creation.

(١١) ۞وَلَوۡ يُعَجِّلُ ٱللَّهُ لِلنَّاسِ ٱلشَّرَّ ٱسۡتِعۡجَالَهُم بِٱلۡخَيۡرِ لَقُضِيَ إِلَيۡهِمۡ أَجَلُهُمۡۖ فَنَذَرُ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَرۡجُونَ لِقَآءَنَا فِي طُغۡيَٰنِهِمۡ يَعۡمَهُونَ

If Allah were to hasten in responding to people who supplicate for evil against themselves, their children, or their wealth when they are angry, in the way He responds to them when they pray for good, they would have been destroyed. But Allah gives them time. He abandons those who do not expect to meet Him because they do not fear torment or hope for a reward, leaving them wavering, confused, and doubtful about the Day of Resurrection.

(١٢) وَإِذَا مَسَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ ٱلضُّرُّ دَعَانَا لِجَنۢبِهِۦٓ أَوۡ قَاعِدًا أَوۡ قَآئِمٗا فَلَمَّا كَشَفۡنَا عَنۡهُ ضُرَّهُۥ مَرَّ كَأَن لَّمۡ يَدۡعُنَآ إِلَىٰ ضُرّٖ مَّسَّهُۥۚ كَذَٰلِكَ زُيِّنَ لِلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

And whenever an affliction such as illness or misfortune touches the man who transgresses against himself, he supplicates to Us, humbly and submissively, whether lying on his side, sitting or standing, hoping for the affliction to be removed from him. However, when We respond to his supplication and remove the affliction, he continues doing what he had been doing before, as if he had never supplicated to Us to remove the affliction. Just as continuing in misguidance is made attractive to this person who turns away, the disbelief and sins of those who transgress the limits are made attractive to them, so they do not give them up.

(١٣) وَلَقَدۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا ٱلۡقُرُونَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ لَمَّا ظَلَمُواْ وَجَآءَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَمَا كَانُواْ لِيُؤۡمِنُواْۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

And We destroyed nations before you, O polytheists, because they rejected the messengers of Allah and committed sins. Though their messengers came to them with clear proofs proving the truthfulness of what they brought from their Lord, they were not keen to believe, so Allah deserted them and did not guide them. Just as We recompensed those previous wrongdoing nations, We recompense those who are like them in all times and places.

(١٤) ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَٰكُمۡ خَلَٰٓئِفَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ لِنَنظُرَ كَيۡفَ تَعۡمَلُونَ

Then We made you successors to these nations who rejected the truth and were destroyed, to see what you would do and whether you would do good, for which you would be rewarded; or do evil, for which you would be tormented.

Points of Benefit:

The kindness of Allah, the Almighty, towards His servants by not answering their prayers against themselves and their children with harm.
Clarification of the state of a person in supplication during adversity, turning away in prosperity, and the warning against adopting such behavior.
The destruction of previous nations was a result of their indulgence in sins and oppression.
