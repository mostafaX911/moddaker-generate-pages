(١٣٥) وَقَالُواْ كُونُواْ هُودًا أَوۡ نَصَٰرَىٰ تَهۡتَدُواْۗ قُلۡ بَلۡ مِلَّةَ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمَ حَنِيفٗاۖ وَمَا كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

The Jews told this nation: Become Jews, you will tread the path of guidance, and the Christians said to them: Become Christians, you will tread the path of guidance. O Prophet, respond to them: Rather, We follow the religion of Abraham, who turned away from other false religions to the religion of truth. He did not worship others alongside Allah.

(١٣٦) قُولُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِٱللَّهِ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡنَا وَمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمَ وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلَ وَإِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَ وَٱلۡأَسۡبَاطِ وَمَآ أُوتِيَ مُوسَىٰ وَعِيسَىٰ وَمَآ أُوتِيَ ٱلنَّبِيُّونَ مِن رَّبِّهِمۡ لَا نُفَرِّقُ بَيۡنَ أَحَدٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ وَنَحۡنُ لَهُۥ مُسۡلِمُونَ

O Believers tell the Jews and Christians who make these false claims: We believe in Allah and the Quran, which was revealed to us, and in what was revealed to Abraham and his sons Ishmael and Isaac, and Jacob, and in what was revealed to the prophets from the descendants of Jacob. We believe in the Torah, which Allah gave to Moses, and the Gospel, which He gave to Jesus, and in all the books that He gave to every one of the prophets, with no distinction between them, believing in all of them, not only believing in some and rejecting others. We humbly submit to Him alone; may He be Exalted.

(١٣٧) فَإِنۡ ءَامَنُواْ بِمِثۡلِ مَآ ءَامَنتُم بِهِۦ فَقَدِ ٱهۡتَدَواْۖ وَّإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَإِنَّمَا هُمۡ فِي شِقَاقٖۖ فَسَيَكۡفِيكَهُمُ ٱللَّهُۚ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

If the Jews, Christians, and other disbelievers believe in the same way you do, then they have been guided to the straight path that Allah is satisfied with. Yet if they turn away from belief because they reject all the prophets or some of them, then they are in disagreement and are against you. Do not grieve, O Prophet, because Allah will protect you from their harm and evil and give you victory over them. Allah is All-Hearing of their statements and Omniscient of their intentions and actions.

(١٣٨) صِبۡغَةَ ٱللَّهِ وَمَنۡ أَحۡسَنُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ صِبۡغَةٗۖ وَنَحۡنُ لَهُۥ عَٰبِدُونَ

Hold tightly to Allah's religion, upon which He has created you externally and internally. This religion agrees with the natural disposition, benefiting us in every way and preventing harm from us. And say: We worship Allah alone and do not worship anything alongside Him.

(١٣٩) قُلۡ أَتُحَآجُّونَنَا فِي ٱللَّهِ وَهُوَ رَبُّنَا وَرَبُّكُمۡ وَلَنَآ أَعۡمَٰلُنَا وَلَكُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلُكُمۡ وَنَحۡنُ لَهُۥ مُخۡلِصُونَ

O Prophet, say to them: Do you argue with us, O People of the Book, that you have more right to Allah and His religion than we do because your religion is older and your Book came before ours, then it will not benefit you. Allah is our Lord, and He does not exclusively belong to You. We have our deeds which you will not be asked about, and you have your deeds which we will not be asked about. Everyone will be recompensed accordingly for their deeds. We are sincerely devoted to Allah in worship and obedience and do not associate any partner with Him.

(١٤٠) أَمۡ تَقُولُونَ إِنَّ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمَ وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلَ وَإِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَ وَٱلۡأَسۡبَاطَ كَانُواْ هُودًا أَوۡ نَصَٰرَىٰۗ قُلۡ ءَأَنتُمۡ أَعۡلَمُ أَمِ ٱللَّهُۗ وَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّن كَتَمَ شَهَٰدَةً عِندَهُۥ مِنَ ٱللَّهِۗ وَمَا ٱللَّهُ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Or do you say, O People of the Book, that Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob, and the prophets from Jacob's descendants were on Judaism or Christianity? Ask, O Prophet, in reply to them: Are you more knowledgeable or Allah? If they claim that these prophets were Jews or Christians, they have lied because they were sent and died before the revelation of the Torah and the Gospel, respectively. This shows that what they say is a lie against Allah and His messengers and that they conceal the truth of what was revealed to them. No one does more wrong than someone who hides firm testimony that they have from Allah, as some of the People of the Book did. Allah is not unaware of your deeds, and He will recompense you all accordingly.

(١٤١) تِلۡكَ أُمَّةٞ قَدۡ خَلَتۡۖ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتۡ وَلَكُم مَّا كَسَبۡتُمۡۖ وَلَا تُسۡ‍َٔلُونَ عَمَّا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

That was a nation that has passed on before you. They have proceeded to the deeds they have sent forth, and they will face the consequence thereof, and you will face [the consequence of] what you have earned. You will not be asked about their deeds, and they will not be asked about your deeds. No one is held accountable for the sins of another. Only belief and fear of Allah will be of benefit; instead, everyone will be recompensed for their own deeds.

Points of Benefit:

The claim of the People of the Book that they are upon the Truth does not benefit them, as they disbelieve in what Allah revealed to His Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم.
The religion was called dye because its deeds and manners appear on the Muslim, just as the effects of dye appear on a garment.
Allah, the Exalted, has built the acknowledgment of His lordship and divinity into the natural disposition of all His creation, but Satan and his helpers mislead them from it.
