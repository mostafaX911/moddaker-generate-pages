(١٥) وَإِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَاتُنَا بَيِّنَٰتٖ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَرۡجُونَ لِقَآءَنَا ٱئۡتِ بِقُرۡءَانٍ غَيۡرِ هَٰذَآ أَوۡ بَدِّلۡهُۚ قُلۡ مَا يَكُونُ لِيٓ أَنۡ أُبَدِّلَهُۥ مِن تِلۡقَآيِٕ نَفۡسِيٓۖ إِنۡ أَتَّبِعُ إِلَّا مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّۖ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ إِنۡ عَصَيۡتُ رَبِّي عَذَابَ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ

When the clear verses of the Quran are recited to them proving the Oneness of Allah, those who deny the resurrection, do not hope for a reward, and do not fear torment, say: O Muhammad, bring a Quran other than this Quran that insults the worshipping of idols or change it by abrogating some or all of it according to what agrees with our desires. O Messenger, tell them: It is not up to me to change it, let alone bring something other than it. Instead, it is Allah alone Who changes what He wills of it, and I only follow what Allah reveals to me. I fear that if I disobey Allah by responding to your request, I will be punished on a great Day, the Day of Resurrection.

(١٦) قُل لَّوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مَا تَلَوۡتُهُۥ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَلَآ أَدۡرَىٰكُم بِهِۦۖ فَقَدۡ لَبِثۡتُ فِيكُمۡ عُمُرٗا مِّن قَبۡلِهِۦٓۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

O Messenger, say to them: If Allah had willed, I would not have recited the Quran nor conveyed it to you. And if Allah had willed, He would not have made the Quran known to you through my tongue. For I have lived among you for a long time, for forty years, not reading or writing, and I did not seek this type of knowledge nor search for it. Do you not realize that what I have brought you is from Allah and not from myself?

(١٧) فَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّنِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبًا أَوۡ كَذَّبَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ

There is no greater wrongdoer than he who fabricates a lie against Allah, so how could I change the Quran, fabricating against Him? The matter is such that those who transgress the limits of Allah by fabricating lies against Him will not succeed.

(١٨) وَيَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا يَضُرُّهُمۡ وَلَا يَنفَعُهُمۡ وَيَقُولُونَ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ شُفَعَٰٓؤُنَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِۚ قُلۡ أَتُنَبِّ‍ُٔونَ ٱللَّهَ بِمَا لَا يَعۡلَمُ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَلَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥ وَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

The polytheists worship what they claim to be deities instead of Allah, which cannot benefit or harm them. But the One deserving of worship can benefit and harm whenever He wills. They say about their deities: These are our intercessors who will intercede for us with Allah, so He will not torment us because of our sins. O Messenger, tell them: Are you informing Allah, the Omniscient, that He has a partner while He is unaware that He has a partner in the heavens or the Earth? Sanctified and Exalted is Allah above the falsehood and lies of the polytheists.

(١٩) وَمَا كَانَ ٱلنَّاسُ إِلَّآ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ فَٱخۡتَلَفُواْۚ وَلَوۡلَا كَلِمَةٞ سَبَقَتۡ مِن رَّبِّكَ لَقُضِيَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ فِيمَا فِيهِ يَخۡتَلِفُونَ

And humanity was just one nation believing in Allah's Oneness; then they differed: some remained upon belief in Allah, while others disbelieved. Had it not been for Allah's prior decree that He would not judge between them in what they differed about in this world and that He would only judge between them on the Day of Resurrection, He would have judged between them in what they differed about in this world by saving the believers and destroying the rejecting disbelievers.

(٢٠) وَيَقُولُونَ لَوۡلَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦۖ فَقُلۡ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡغَيۡبُ لِلَّهِ فَٱنتَظِرُوٓاْ إِنِّي مَعَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُنتَظِرِينَ

The polytheists say: Why has no sign been sent to Muhammad from his Lord to prove the truth of what he says? O Messenger, say to them: The sending down of signs is [of the] unseen, which Allah alone knows, so wait for the physical signs you ask for, as I am also waiting along with you for them.

Points of Benefit:

The enormity of slandering and lying about Allah, as well as distorting His words, as the Jews did with the Torah.
Benefit and harm are solely in the Hand of Allah, the Almighty, without any other entity having control over them.
The invalidity of the polytheists' claim that their deities intercede for them with Allah.
Following personal desires and discord over religion are causes of division.
