(٢١) وَإِذَآ أَذَقۡنَا ٱلنَّاسَ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ ضَرَّآءَ مَسَّتۡهُمۡ إِذَا لَهُم مَّكۡرٞ فِيٓ ءَايَاتِنَاۚ قُلِ ٱللَّهُ أَسۡرَعُ مَكۡرًاۚ إِنَّ رُسُلَنَا يَكۡتُبُونَ مَا تَمۡكُرُونَ

When We give the polytheists the taste of a blessing, such as rain and abundance after drought and suffering, they mock and reject Our signs. O Messenger, tell these polytheists: Allah is quicker at plotting, enticing, and luring you and quicker in tormenting. The angels record what you plan in the way of scheming, and nothing escapes them, so how could it escape their Creator? Allah will recompense you for your scheming.

(٢٢) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُسَيِّرُكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا كُنتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ وَجَرَيۡنَ بِهِم بِرِيحٖ طَيِّبَةٖ وَفَرِحُواْ بِهَا جَآءَتۡهَا رِيحٌ عَاصِفٞ وَجَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡجُ مِن كُلِّ مَكَانٖ وَظَنُّوٓاْ أَنَّهُمۡ أُحِيطَ بِهِمۡ دَعَوُاْ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ لَئِنۡ أَنجَيۡتَنَا مِنۡ هَٰذِهِۦ لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلشَّٰكِرِينَ

Allah is the One who enables you, O humanity, to travel by land, foot, riding, or aboard ships on the sea. To the passengers' delight, they sail in them with good winds. Suddenly, a gale wind comes to them, and the waves of the sea come to them from every side until they are certain they are doomed; they start to supplicate to Allah alone, not associating anything with Him, saying: If You save us from this deadly situation we will be truly thankful for what You have favored us with.

(٢٣) فَلَمَّآ أَنجَىٰهُمۡ إِذَا هُمۡ يَبۡغُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّۗ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّمَا بَغۡيُكُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِكُمۖ مَّتَٰعَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ ثُمَّ إِلَيۡنَا مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ فَنُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

But when He answers their supplication and saves them from their distress, they cause corruption upon the Earth, disbelieving, committing wrongs and sins. Wake up and realize, O People! The evil consequences of your sins and transgression only fall upon yourselves, and do not harm Allah in any way. You enjoy it in the life of this world, which is fleeting. Then you will return to Us on the day of Resurrection, and We will inform you of the wrongdoing you used to do and recompense you for it.

(٢٤) إِنَّمَا مَثَلُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا كَمَآءٍ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَٱخۡتَلَطَ بِهِۦ نَبَاتُ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِمَّا يَأۡكُلُ ٱلنَّاسُ وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمُ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَخَذَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ زُخۡرُفَهَا وَٱزَّيَّنَتۡ وَظَنَّ أَهۡلُهَآ أَنَّهُمۡ قَٰدِرُونَ عَلَيۡهَآ أَتَىٰهَآ أَمۡرُنَا لَيۡلًا أَوۡ نَهَارٗا فَجَعَلۡنَٰهَا حَصِيدٗا كَأَن لَّمۡ تَغۡنَ بِٱلۡأَمۡسِۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نُفَصِّلُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ

The example of the life of this world that you enjoy and how quickly it comes to an end is like that of the rain, which is absorbed by the plants of the earth, from which people eat grains and fruits, and animals benefit from its vegetation. When the earth has taken on its bright color and looks beautiful because of the different plants growing on it, and the people think that they will be able to harvest and gather what has grown, Our decree comes to destroy it, and We make it as if it had been harvested and not been full of anything such as trees and plants a short while ago. Just as We have clarified the nature of the world and how quickly it ends, We clarify the evidence and proofs for those who reflect and take lessons.

(٢٥) وَٱللَّهُ يَدۡعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ دَارِ ٱلسَّلَٰمِ وَيَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

And Allah invites all people to His Paradise, the Home of Peace, where people will be safe from difficulties, worries, and death. Allah enables whom He wills of His slaves to come to Islam, leading them to this Home of Peace.

Points of Benefit:

Allah is swifter in strategy against those who plot against His believing servants.
Human transgression rebounds upon oneself and harms none but the transgressor.
Explanation of the reality of the world in its swift passing and fading away, and that which it contains of blessings is ephemeral.
Paradise is the ultimate abode of the believer, given the blessings and protection from afflictions and sorrows it contains.
