(٢٦) ۞لِّلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ وَزِيَادَةٞۖ وَلَا يَرۡهَقُ وُجُوهَهُمۡ قَتَرٞ وَلَا ذِلَّةٌۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

Those who do good, including that which Allah made obligatory on them, and avoid the sins that Allah has prohibited will receive the best reward, Paradise, and even more, which is to see the noble face of Allah. Neither dust, humiliation, nor disgrace will cover their faces. Such people who do good are the companions of Paradise; they will remain therein eternally.

(٢٧) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَسَبُواْ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِ جَزَآءُ سَيِّئَةِۢ بِمِثۡلِهَا وَتَرۡهَقُهُمۡ ذِلَّةٞۖ مَّا لَهُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ مِنۡ عَاصِمٖۖ كَأَنَّمَآ أُغۡشِيَتۡ وُجُوهُهُمۡ قِطَعٗا مِّنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ مُظۡلِمًاۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

Those who do evil deeds such as disbelief and sins will receive Allah's torment as an equal recompense in the Hereafter for the sins they committed in this life. Their faces will be covered in humiliation and disgrace, and no one will save them from the torment Allah will send upon them. It will be as if their faces have been covered by the blackness of the night because of the significant amount of smoke and blackness that will reach them from the Fire. Such people are the companions of the Fire, wherein they will live eternally.

(٢٨) وَيَوۡمَ نَحۡشُرُهُمۡ جَمِيعٗا ثُمَّ نَقُولُ لِلَّذِينَ أَشۡرَكُواْ مَكَانَكُمۡ أَنتُمۡ وَشُرَكَآؤُكُمۡۚ فَزَيَّلۡنَا بَيۡنَهُمۡۖ وَقَالَ شُرَكَآؤُهُم مَّا كُنتُمۡ إِيَّانَا تَعۡبُدُونَ

And remember, O Messenger, the Day of Resurrection, when We will gather together all of creation and tell those who associated partners with Allah in the worldly life: O polytheists, stay in your place, you and the deities you used to worship instead of Allah. Then We will separate the deities from their worshippers, and those deities will disassociate themselves from their worshippers, saying: It was not us that you used to worship.

(٢٩) فَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ شَهِيدَۢا بَيۡنَنَا وَبَيۡنَكُمۡ إِن كُنَّا عَنۡ عِبَادَتِكُمۡ لَغَٰفِلِينَ

Their so-called gods, which they used to worship, will disassociate themselves from them, saying: Allah is sufficient as a witness that We were not happy being worshipped by you, nor did we instruct you to do so, nor were we aware of your worship!

(٣٠) هُنَالِكَ تَبۡلُواْ كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ مَّآ أَسۡلَفَتۡۚ وَرُدُّوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ مَوۡلَىٰهُمُ ٱلۡحَقِّۖ وَضَلَّ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ

In that great place of gathering, each soul will be informed of what it did in the life of this world. Then the polytheists will be returned to their true Lord, Allah, Who will carry out their reckoning, and the intercession of the idols they made up will fail them.

(٣١) قُلۡ مَن يَرۡزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَمَّن يَمۡلِكُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَمَن يُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡحَيَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ وَيُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡمَيِّتَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَيِّ وَمَن يُدَبِّرُ ٱلۡأَمۡرَۚ فَسَيَقُولُونَ ٱللَّهُۚ فَقُلۡ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: Who is it that provides for you from the sky, sending down rain; who provides for you from the earth, with the plants that grow in it and what it contains of metals and minerals; and who owns the hearing and sight that you enjoy; and who brings out the living from the dead, like the human from a drop of semen and the bird from the egg; and who brings out the dead from the living, like semen from animals, and the egg from the bird? Who governs the entire creation in the heavens and earth, and what is in them? They will reply that Allah is the One who does all of this. Say to them: Will you not know that and then fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions?!

(٣٢) فَذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ فَمَاذَا بَعۡدَ ٱلۡحَقِّ إِلَّا ٱلضَّلَٰلُۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُصۡرَفُونَ

The One who does all that, O people, is Allah, the Truth, your Creator, and the Planner of your affairs. So what is beyond the truth except misguidance? Where are your minds wandering away to from this clear truth?

(٣٣) كَذَٰلِكَ حَقَّتۡ كَلِمَتُ رَبِّكَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ فَسَقُوٓاْ أَنَّهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Just as the true Lordship of Allah is established, O Messenger, His Decree that those who abandoned the truth out of stubbornness will never believe has become binding.

Points of Benefit:

The greatest bliss that a believer aspires to is beholding the countenance of Allah, the Transcendent.
Manifestation of the power of Allah and that He is capable over all things.
The oneness in lordship and associating partners in divinity is false; therefore, it is essential to unify both aspects.
If Allah decrees the disbelief of a people due to their transgressions, then they will not believe.
