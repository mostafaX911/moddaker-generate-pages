(٣٤) قُلۡ هَلۡ مِن شُرَكَآئِكُم مَّن يَبۡدَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَلۡقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُۥۚ قُلِ ٱللَّهُ يَبۡدَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَلۡقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُۥۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُؤۡفَكُونَ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists: Can any of your partners you worship instead of Allah originate creation without precedent and then bring it back to life after death? Tell them: Allah originates creation without precedent and then brings it back to life after death, so how can you, O polytheists, be deluded from the truth to falsehood?

(٣٥) قُلۡ هَلۡ مِن شُرَكَآئِكُم مَّن يَهۡدِيٓ إِلَى ٱلۡحَقِّۚ قُلِ ٱللَّهُ يَهۡدِي لِلۡحَقِّۗ أَفَمَن يَهۡدِيٓ إِلَى ٱلۡحَقِّ أَحَقُّ أَن يُتَّبَعَ أَمَّن لَّا يَهِدِّيٓ إِلَّآ أَن يُهۡدَىٰۖ فَمَا لَكُمۡ كَيۡفَ تَحۡكُمُونَ

Tell them, O Messenger: Are there from among your partners who you worship instead of Allah those who guide to the truth? Say to them: Allah alone guides to the truth. So, is He who guides people to the truth and invites them to it more deserving of being followed, or your deities who cannot guide themselves except if others guide them? So, how do you judge with falsehood by claiming they are Allah's partners? Allah is Transcendent and far beyond what they say.

(٣٦) وَمَا يَتَّبِعُ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ إِلَّا ظَنًّاۚ إِنَّ ٱلظَّنَّ لَا يُغۡنِي مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّ شَيۡ‍ًٔاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمُۢ بِمَا يَفۡعَلُونَ

Most of the polytheists follow matters they do not know about, only guessing and conjecturing. Mere conjecture cannot replace knowledge and is useless against it. Allah knows everything they do; nothing of what they do is hidden from Him, and He will recompense them accordingly.

(٣٧) وَمَا كَانَ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ أَن يُفۡتَرَىٰ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَٰكِن تَصۡدِيقَ ٱلَّذِي بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ وَتَفۡصِيلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهِ مِن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

This Quran could not be fabricated and attributed to anyone but Allah because people cannot come up with anything similar to it. It confirms the books that were revealed before it and clarifies the laws in them. Thus, there is no doubt that the Lord of the creation revealed it (may He be Exalted and Transcendent).

(٣٨) أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ ٱفۡتَرَىٰهُۖ قُلۡ فَأۡتُواْ بِسُورَةٖ مِّثۡلِهِۦ وَٱدۡعُواْ مَنِ ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

Or do these polytheists say that Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم made up the Quran himself and then attributed it to Allah? O Messenger, say to them: If I, as a human being like you, have made it up, then bring a chapter like it and invite whoever you can to assist you if you are telling the truth in claiming that the Quran is a made-up lie. However, you will not be able to do so. Your inability, even though you are masters of the Arabic language and eloquent in it, proves that Allah has revealed the Quran.

(٣٩) بَلۡ كَذَّبُواْ بِمَا لَمۡ يُحِيطُواْ بِعِلۡمِهِۦ وَلَمَّا يَأۡتِهِمۡ تَأۡوِيلُهُۥۚ كَذَٰلِكَ كَذَّبَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۖ فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Yet they did not respond [to these challenges]; instead, they hastened to reject the Quran before even understanding or reflecting upon it, and the punishment about which they have been warned has not yet come to them, although it is near! In the same way, previous nations rejected, and then the torment came down upon them. So reflect upon the end of these nations who rejected the truth, for Allah destroyed them.

(٤٠) وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يُؤۡمِنُ بِهِۦ وَمِنۡهُم مَّن لَّا يُؤۡمِنُ بِهِۦۚ وَرَبُّكَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ

Some polytheists will eventually believe in the Quran before their death, while others will not out of stubbornness and arrogance until they die. O Messenger, your Lord Allah knows best those who persist in their disbelief and will recompense them for it.

(٤١) وَإِن كَذَّبُوكَ فَقُل لِّي عَمَلِي وَلَكُمۡ عَمَلُكُمۡۖ أَنتُم بَرِيٓ‍ُٔونَ مِمَّآ أَعۡمَلُ وَأَنَا۠ بَرِيٓءٞ مِّمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ

If your people reject you, O Messenger, then say to them: I will be rewarded for my deeds and take responsibility for the consequences thereof, and you will be rewarded for your deeds and face the consequences thereof. You are free from the consequences of what I do, and I am free from what you all do.

(٤٢) وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَسۡتَمِعُونَ إِلَيۡكَۚ أَفَأَنتَ تُسۡمِعُ ٱلصُّمَّ وَلَوۡ كَانُواْ لَا يَعۡقِلُونَ

And some polytheists listen when you, O Messenger, recite the Quran, not wanting to accept it and submit to it. Are you able to make deaf people hear? Likewise, you cannot guide those deaf to the truth, as they will not understand it.

Points of Benefit:

The guide to the truth, the guidance of success, is solely Allah, without any other.
Encouragement to seek evidence, proofs, and guidance in the pursuit of knowledge and truth while abandoning illusions and conjectures.
It is not within anyone's capacity to bring anything close to the like of the Noble Quran, not even a single verse, until the Day of Judgment.
The foolishness of the polytheists lies in their denial of what they do not understand and contemplate.
