(٤٣) وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَنظُرُ إِلَيۡكَۚ أَفَأَنتَ تَهۡدِي ٱلۡعُمۡيَ وَلَوۡ كَانُواْ لَا يُبۡصِرُونَ

And some of them look at you, O Messenger, with their normal eyesight but not with insight. Is it possible for you to make the blind see? You will not be able to do so; likewise, you cannot guide those lacking insight.

(٤٤) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَظۡلِمُ ٱلنَّاسَ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱلنَّاسَ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَظۡلِمُونَ

Allah is free from wronging His slaves; He does not wrong them by even an atom's weight. Instead, they wrong themselves by bringing about their own destruction by clinging to falsehood, arrogance, and stubbornness.

(٤٥) وَيَوۡمَ يَحۡشُرُهُمۡ كَأَن لَّمۡ يَلۡبَثُوٓاْ إِلَّا سَاعَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلنَّهَارِ يَتَعَارَفُونَ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ قَدۡ خَسِرَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِلِقَآءِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا كَانُواْ مُهۡتَدِينَ

On the Day of Resurrection, when Allah will gather people to reckon them, it will be as if they had lived in the world and the Barzakh [Transitory Stage] for only an hour and no more. They will recognize each other on that Day, but then their acquaintance will be cut off because of the intensity of the horrors they witness on the Day of Resurrection. The losers will be those who rejected the meeting with their Lord on the Day of Resurrection and, in the worldly life, did not believe in the Day of Revival so that they could safeguard themselves from loss.

(٤٦) وَإِمَّا نُرِيَنَّكَ بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي نَعِدُهُمۡ أَوۡ نَتَوَفَّيَنَّكَ فَإِلَيۡنَا مَرۡجِعُهُمۡ ثُمَّ ٱللَّهُ شَهِيدٌ عَلَىٰ مَا يَفۡعَلُونَ

Whether We show you, O Messenger, some of the torment We have promised them before your death or cause you to die before that, in both scenarios, you will return to Us on the Day of Resurrection. Moreover, Allah is aware of what they used to do; none of it is hidden from Him, and He will recompense them accordingly for their deeds.

(٤٧) وَلِكُلِّ أُمَّةٖ رَّسُولٞۖ فَإِذَا جَآءَ رَسُولُهُمۡ قُضِيَ بَيۡنَهُم بِٱلۡقِسۡطِ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ

Every previous nation had a messenger sent to them. When the messenger delivered to them what he was instructed to, and they rejected him, it was judged justly between him and them. Allah saved the messenger through His grace and destroyed them through His justice, and they were not wronged in any way in the recompense for what they did.

(٤٨) وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

The disbelievers say stubbornly and challengingly: When will the torment you promised us occur if you are truthful in your claim?

(٤٩) قُل لَّآ أَمۡلِكُ لِنَفۡسِي ضَرّٗا وَلَا نَفۡعًا إِلَّا مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُۗ لِكُلِّ أُمَّةٍ أَجَلٌۚ إِذَا جَآءَ أَجَلُهُمۡ فَلَا يَسۡتَ‍ٔۡخِرُونَ سَاعَةٗ وَلَا يَسۡتَقۡدِمُونَ

O Messenger, say to them: I have no power to benefit or harm myself, so how can I benefit or harm other people besides what Allah wills? How, then, can I know the unseen? Allah has appointed a set time for the destruction of every nation, but only Allah knows the time. If the time of their destruction arrives, it cannot be brought forward or delayed by even a moment.

(٥٠) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِنۡ أَتَىٰكُمۡ عَذَابُهُۥ بَيَٰتًا أَوۡ نَهَارٗا مَّاذَا يَسۡتَعۡجِلُ مِنۡهُ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ

O Messenger, tell these people who wish to hasten the torment: Inform me if Allah's torment were to come to you at any time of the night or day, what is it of this torment precisely that you are hastening?!

(٥١) أَثُمَّ إِذَا مَا وَقَعَ ءَامَنتُم بِهِۦٓۚ ءَآلۡـَٰٔنَ وَقَدۡ كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَ

Or will you believe after the promised torment has come down upon you when belief will not benefit anyone who had not previously believed? Is it now that you will believe, whereas before, you wanted to hasten the torment because you rejected it?!

(٥٢) ثُمَّ قِيلَ لِلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ ذُوقُواْ عَذَابَ ٱلۡخُلۡدِ هَلۡ تُجۡزَوۡنَ إِلَّا بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡسِبُونَ

Then, after being put in the torment and requesting to be removed from it, it will be said to them: Taste the everlasting torment of the Hereafter. Are you not being rewarded except for your disbelief and sins?

(٥٣) ۞وَيَسۡتَنۢبِ‍ُٔونَكَ أَحَقٌّ هُوَۖ قُلۡ إِي وَرَبِّيٓ إِنَّهُۥ لَحَقّٞۖ وَمَآ أَنتُم بِمُعۡجِزِينَ

The polytheists ask you, O Messenger: Is this torment which we have been promised true? Tell them: By Allah! It is the truth, and you cannot escape it.

Points of Benefit:

It is the individual who brings about their own sources of destruction, for Allah is free from any injustice.
The mission of the Messenger is to convey the message to those he is sent to, and it is Allah who takes charge of their accountability and punishment with His wisdom. He may hasten it during the lifetime of the Messenger or delay it after his passing.
Benefit and harm are in the Hand of Allah, the Almighty. No creature possesses the power to bring harm or benefit to themselves or others.
Belief does not avail its possessor when facing the certainty of death.
