(٥٤) وَلَوۡ أَنَّ لِكُلِّ نَفۡسٖ ظَلَمَتۡ مَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لَٱفۡتَدَتۡ بِهِۦۗ وَأَسَرُّواْ ٱلنَّدَامَةَ لَمَّا رَأَوُاْ ٱلۡعَذَابَۖ وَقُضِيَ بَيۡنَهُم بِٱلۡقِسۡطِ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ

If every polytheist were to possess all the valuable wealth on Earth, they would offer it as ransom from Allah's torment if they were allowed to do so. The polytheists will hide their regret for their disbelief when they see the torment on the Day of Resurrection. Allah will judge between them with justice, and they will not be wronged; they will only be recompensed accordingly for their deeds.

(٥٥) أَلَآ إِنَّ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَلَآ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقّٞ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

Behold! The dominion of whatever is in the heavens and Earth is only Allah's. Allah's promise of torment for the disbelievers will undoubtedly occur, but most do not know that and are therefore in doubt.

(٥٦) هُوَ يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

He (may He be Exalted) resurrects the dead and causes the living to die. You will be returned to Him alone on the Day of Resurrection. He will then recompense you for your deeds accordingly.

(٥٧) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكُم مَّوۡعِظَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَشِفَآءٞ لِّمَا فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٞ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

O people, the Quran has come to you, a powerful reminder, encouragement, and warning. It is a cure for the illnesses of doubt and uncertainty that are in the hearts. It contains guidance to the path of truth and is a mercy for the believers, who are the ones who benefit from it.

(٥٨) قُلۡ بِفَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَبِرَحۡمَتِهِۦ فَبِذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَفۡرَحُواْ هُوَ خَيۡرٞ مِّمَّا يَجۡمَعُونَ

O Messenger, say to the people: The Quran I brought you is Allah's grace and mercy. So rejoice in Allah's grace and mercy to you through the revelation of this Quran, and not in anything besides this. What Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought to them from His Lord is better than what they gather from the trivial things of this temporary world.

(٥٩) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُم مَّآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُم مِّن رِّزۡقٖ فَجَعَلۡتُم مِّنۡهُ حَرَامٗا وَحَلَٰلٗا قُلۡ ءَآللَّهُ أَذِنَ لَكُمۡۖ أَمۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ تَفۡتَرُونَ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists: Inform me about Allah's favor of the provision that He sent down for you, of which you acted according to your desires by making some unlawful and some lawful. Say to them: Did Allah permit you to declare those things lawful or unlawful, or are you making up lies against Allah?

(٦٠) وَمَا ظَنُّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَفۡتَرُونَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡكَذِبَ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَذُو فَضۡلٍ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡكُرُونَ

What do those who make up lies against Allah think will happen to them on the Day of Resurrection? Do they think they will be forgiven? Never! Allah is Bountiful to people by giving them respite and not punishing them immediately, but most people deny Allah's favors and are not grateful for them.

(٦١) وَمَا تَكُونُ فِي شَأۡنٖ وَمَا تَتۡلُواْ مِنۡهُ مِن قُرۡءَانٖ وَلَا تَعۡمَلُونَ مِنۡ عَمَلٍ إِلَّا كُنَّا عَلَيۡكُمۡ شُهُودًا إِذۡ تُفِيضُونَ فِيهِۚ وَمَا يَعۡزُبُ عَن رَّبِّكَ مِن مِّثۡقَالِ ذَرَّةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَلَآ أَصۡغَرَ مِن ذَٰلِكَ وَلَآ أَكۡبَرَ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٍ

You, O Messenger, are not engaged in any matter, do not recite a portion of the Quran, nor do you, O believers, do any deed, except that We see you, knowing when you are about to act and hear you. An atom's weight, in the sky or on Earth, is not hidden from the knowledge of your Lord. Nor is there anything lesser or more in weight, except that it is recorded in a clear book that does not omit anything, no matter how small or big it may be.

Points of Benefit:

Immense is the punishment awaiting the polytheists from Allah, to the extent that they wish to ransom themselves with everything on Earth, yet it will not be accepted from them.
The Quran is a healing for the believers from the ailments of desires and the diseases of doubts, containing guidance and intellectual and textual evidence.
It is fitting for the believer to rejoice in the blessings of Islam and faith above all else in the debris of worldly matters.
The precision of Allah's observation of His servants, their actions, thoughts, and intentions.
