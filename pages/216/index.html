(٦٢) أَلَآ إِنَّ أَوۡلِيَآءَ ٱللَّهِ لَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

Behold! The friends of Allah will not fear the terrors to come on the Day of Resurrection, and they will not grieve over the pleasures of this world that they missed.

(٦٣) ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَكَانُواْ يَتَّقُونَ

These friends of Allah are described as those who believe in Allah and His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم and fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

(٦٤) لَهُمُ ٱلۡبُشۡرَىٰ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ لَا تَبۡدِيلَ لِكَلِمَٰتِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

They have good news from their Lord in this worldly life, which will make them happy, such as righteous dreams or people praising them. They will also receive good news from the angels when their souls are taken, after their death, and at the Hashr [gathering]. There is no change to the promise of Allah. That reward is a great success because it entails achieving what they desire and salvation from what they fear.

(٦٥) وَلَا يَحۡزُنكَ قَوۡلُهُمۡۘ إِنَّ ٱلۡعِزَّةَ لِلَّهِ جَمِيعًاۚ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

Do not grieve, O Messenger, over what these people say in an attempt to attack or slander your religion. Control and domination belong to Allah; nothing can hinder Him. He is All-Hearing of their words, Omniscient of their deeds, and will recompense them accordingly.

(٦٦) أَلَآ إِنَّ لِلَّهِ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ وَمَا يَتَّبِعُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ شُرَكَآءَۚ إِن يَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا ٱلظَّنَّ وَإِنۡ هُمۡ إِلَّا يَخۡرُصُونَ

The dominion of whoever is in the heavens and Earth belongs to Allah. What is it that the polytheists who worship partners instead of Allah are following? In reality, they are only following doubt and are lying when they attribute partners to Allah. Allah is Transcendent and far beyond their statements.

(٦٧) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلَّيۡلَ لِتَسۡكُنُواْ فِيهِ وَٱلنَّهَارَ مُبۡصِرًاۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَسۡمَعُونَ

It is He alone Who has made the night for you, O people, to take rest from tiredness. He has brightened the day for you to work and earn your livelihood. In that, there are clear indications for people who listen intending to take lessons and accept them.

(٦٨) قَالُواْ ٱتَّخَذَ ٱللَّهُ وَلَدٗاۗ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥۖ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّۖ لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ إِنۡ عِندَكُم مِّن سُلۡطَٰنِۢ بِهَٰذَآۚ أَتَقُولُونَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

Some of the polytheists said: Allah has taken the angels as daughters. Allah is free of their statement because He (may He be Exalted) is Self-sufficient and does not need any of His creation. The dominion of whatever is in the heavens and the Earth belongs to Him. You, O polytheists, do not have any proof for your statement. Are you making such a severe statement about Allah by attributing a child to Him, even though you do not know the reality and have no proof?

(٦٩) قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَفۡتَرُونَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡكَذِبَ لَا يُفۡلِحُونَ

O Messenger, say to them: Those who make up lies against Allah by attributing a child to Him will not achieve what they desire and will not be saved from what they fear.

(٧٠) مَتَٰعٞ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا ثُمَّ إِلَيۡنَا مَرۡجِعُهُمۡ ثُمَّ نُذِيقُهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابَ ٱلشَّدِيدَ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡفُرُونَ

They should not be deceived by the luxuries of this world that they enjoy. This is a brief and temporary enjoyment, and then their return will be to Us on the Day of Resurrection. We will then make them taste the severe torment because they disbelieved in Allah and rejected His Messenger.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's guardianship is granted to those who believe in Him, comply with His commands, avoid His prohibitions, and follow His Messenger (peace be upon him). The allies of Allah are the believers who will have complete safety on the Day of Judgment. They are also granted glad tidings in this world through righteous visions or at the time of death.
All glory belongs to Allah alone, as He is the Owner of all Dominion, and what is worshipped besides Allah has no true existence.
Encouragement to contemplate the creation of Allah, as it leads to belief in Him and His Oneness.
The prohibition of lying about Allah, the Almighty, and the inevitable failure of its perpetrator. Among the greatest falsehoods is attributing a son to Him, the Exalted.
