(٧١) ۞وَٱتۡلُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ نَبَأَ نُوحٍ إِذۡ قَالَ لِقَوۡمِهِۦ يَٰقَوۡمِ إِن كَانَ كَبُرَ عَلَيۡكُم مَّقَامِي وَتَذۡكِيرِي بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ فَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ تَوَكَّلۡتُ فَأَجۡمِعُوٓاْ أَمۡرَكُمۡ وَشُرَكَآءَكُمۡ ثُمَّ لَا يَكُنۡ أَمۡرُكُمۡ عَلَيۡكُمۡ غُمَّةٗ ثُمَّ ٱقۡضُوٓاْ إِلَيَّ وَلَا تُنظِرُونِ

Relate, O Messenger, to these rejecting polytheists the story of Noah (peace be upon him) when he said to his people: O my people, if my presence among you is unbearable, and my reminders to you of Allah's signs and advising you is too much for you, and you have decided to kill me, then I have placed my trust in Allah alone to make your plots fail. So make a firm decision about your matter; destroy me and call upon your deities by seeking their help, and do not let your plot be an obscure secret. After your plan to kill me, carry out what you secretly intend, and do not give me a moment of respite.

(٧٢) فَإِن تَوَلَّيۡتُمۡ فَمَا سَأَلۡتُكُم مِّنۡ أَجۡرٍۖ إِنۡ أَجۡرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَأُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ

If you turn away from my invitation, then you know that I have not asked for any reward from you for conveying the message of my Lord to you. My reward is only from Allah, whether you believe in me or not. Allah has instructed me to be of those who submit to Him with obedience and righteous deeds.

(٧٣) فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَمَن مَّعَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ خَلَٰٓئِفَ وَأَغۡرَقۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَاۖ فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُنذَرِينَ

Noah's people rejected and did not accept him, so We saved him and those believers with him on the ship. We made them the successors of those before them and destroyed through the Flood those who rejected the signs and evidence he brought. So reflect, O Messenger, over the fate of the people Noah warned (peace be upon him) but did not believe.

(٧٤) ثُمَّ بَعَثۡنَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ رُسُلًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِمۡ فَجَآءُوهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ فَمَا كَانُواْ لِيُؤۡمِنُواْ بِمَا كَذَّبُواْ بِهِۦ مِن قَبۡلُۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَطۡبَعُ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِ ٱلۡمُعۡتَدِينَ

Then, some time after Noah, We sent messengers to their people. The messengers came to their nations with signs and proofs. But they had no desire to believe due to their previous persistence in rejecting the messengers. Allah, therefore, sealed their hearts. Just as We sealed the hearts of the followers of the earlier messengers, We will seal the hearts of the disbelievers of every time and place who transgress Allah's limits by committing disbelief.

(٧٥) ثُمَّ بَعَثۡنَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِم مُّوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَمَلَإِيْهِۦ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا فَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ وَكَانُواْ قَوۡمٗا مُّجۡرِمِينَ

Some time after these messengers, We sent Moses and his brother, Aaron, to Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, and the nobles from his people. We sent them with signs indicating their truthfulness, but Pharaoh and his people were too arrogant to believe in what these messengers brought. They were criminals because they disbelieved in Allah and rejected His messengers.

(٧٦) فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِنۡ عِندِنَا قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَسِحۡرٞ مُّبِينٞ

When the religion brought by Moses and Aaron (peace be upon them) came to Pharaoh and the nobles from his people, they said about the signs indicating the truthfulness of what Moses brought: it is clear magic and not the truth.

(٧٧) قَالَ مُوسَىٰٓ أَتَقُولُونَ لِلۡحَقِّ لَمَّا جَآءَكُمۡۖ أَسِحۡرٌ هَٰذَا وَلَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلسَّٰحِرُونَ

Moses said to them in disapproval: After the truth has come to you, are you calling it magic? No way! This is not magic. I know a magician can never succeed, so why would I practice magic?

(٧٨) قَالُوٓاْ أَجِئۡتَنَا لِتَلۡفِتَنَا عَمَّا وَجَدۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ ءَابَآءَنَا وَتَكُونَ لَكُمَا ٱلۡكِبۡرِيَآءُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا نَحۡنُ لَكُمَا بِمُؤۡمِنِينَ

The people of Pharaoh responded to Moses: Have you brought this magic to turn us away from the religion we found our fathers following so that you and your brother can gain control? We do not believe the two of you are messengers sent to us.

Points of Benefit:

The weapon of the believer in facing their enemies is reliance on Allah.
Insisting on disbelief and rejecting the messengers leads to the sealing of hearts, causing them never to believe.
The state of the enemies of the messengers is consistent—they often label guidance as sorcery or falsehood.
Indeed, the sorcerer will never succeed.
