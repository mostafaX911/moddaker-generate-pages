(٧٩) وَقَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ ٱئۡتُونِي بِكُلِّ سَٰحِرٍ عَلِيمٖ

Pharaoh said to his people: Bring me every person knowledgeable and skilled in magic.

(٨٠) فَلَمَّا جَآءَ ٱلسَّحَرَةُ قَالَ لَهُم مُّوسَىٰٓ أَلۡقُواْ مَآ أَنتُم مُّلۡقُونَ

When the magicians were brought to Pharaoh, Moses told them with complete confidence that he would defeat them: Throw down, O magicians, whatever you will throw.

(٨١) فَلَمَّآ أَلۡقَوۡاْ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ مَا جِئۡتُم بِهِ ٱلسِّحۡرُۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَيُبۡطِلُهُۥٓ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُصۡلِحُ عَمَلَ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ

When they cast their magic, Moses told them: What you have produced is magic. Allah will make it useless and of no effect. You cause corruption on Earth through your magic, and Allah does not set right the action of a person who causes corruption.

(٨٢) وَيُحِقُّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡحَقَّ بِكَلِمَٰتِهِۦ وَلَوۡ كَرِهَ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ

Allah establishes the truth and consolidates it with His words of decree and with the proofs and evidence contained in His legislative words, even though the sinful disbelievers from the people of Pharaoh dislike it.

(٨٣) فَمَآ ءَامَنَ لِمُوسَىٰٓ إِلَّا ذُرِّيَّةٞ مِّن قَوۡمِهِۦ عَلَىٰ خَوۡفٖ مِّن فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَمَلَإِيْهِمۡ أَن يَفۡتِنَهُمۡۚ وَإِنَّ فِرۡعَوۡنَ لَعَالٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَإِنَّهُۥ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ

The people were determined to turn away, and none accepted Moses (peace be upon him) despite the open miracles and clear proofs he brought, all except a few youths of his people, the Israelites, while fearing that Pharaoh and the nobles of his people would turn them away from their belief by having them taste torment if their affair became exposed. Pharaoh was tyrannical in his authority over Egypt and its people, and he transgressed the limits by disbelieving in Allah and killing and torturing the Israelites.

(٨٤) وَقَالَ مُوسَىٰ يَٰقَوۡمِ إِن كُنتُمۡ ءَامَنتُم بِٱللَّهِ فَعَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلُوٓاْ إِن كُنتُم مُّسۡلِمِينَ

Moses (peace be upon him) said to his people: O my people, if you truly believe in Allah and are Muslims [who submit to Him], then place your trust in Him alone. Placing your trust in Allah will protect you from harm and bring you good.

(٨٥) فَقَالُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ تَوَكَّلۡنَا رَبَّنَا لَا تَجۡعَلۡنَا فِتۡنَةٗ لِّلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

They responded to Moses (peace be upon him) and said: In Allah alone, we have placed our trust. O our Lord, do not appoint the wrongdoers over us because they will turn us away from our religion by torturing us, killing us, and tempting us.

(٨٦) وَنَجِّنَا بِرَحۡمَتِكَ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Save us through Your mercy, O our Lord, from the hands of the disbelieving people of Pharaoh because they have made us into slaves and have harmed us by torturing and killing us.

(٨٧) وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ مُوسَىٰ وَأَخِيهِ أَن تَبَوَّءَا لِقَوۡمِكُمَا بِمِصۡرَ بُيُوتٗا وَٱجۡعَلُواْ بُيُوتَكُمۡ قِبۡلَةٗ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

We revealed to Moses and his brother, Aaron (peace be upon them): Select and make homes for your people to worship Allah alone; and make your homes face the direction of the qiblah (which was then Jerusalem); and then perform the prayer with perfection. O Moses, inform the believers of news that will please them: the news of Allah's assistance and support for them, the destruction of their enemy, and making them successors on Earth.

(٨٨) وَقَالَ مُوسَىٰ رَبَّنَآ إِنَّكَ ءَاتَيۡتَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَمَلَأَهُۥ زِينَةٗ وَأَمۡوَٰلٗا فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا رَبَّنَا لِيُضِلُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِكَۖ رَبَّنَا ٱطۡمِسۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَٱشۡدُدۡ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ فَلَا يُؤۡمِنُواْ حَتَّىٰ يَرَوُاْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ ٱلۡأَلِيمَ

Moses (peace be upon him) said: O our Lord, You have given Pharaoh and the nobles of his people the glitz and glamour of this world. You have also given them wealth in this worldly life, but they were not grateful to You for what You gave them. Instead, they used that to mislead others from Your path. O our Lord, utterly destroy their wealth. Make their hearts hard so that they do not believe unless they see the painful torment at a time when their belief will not benefit them.

Points of Benefit:

Trust in Allah, confidence in His victory, and reliance upon Him should be qualities of a strong believer.
Explanation of the importance of supplication and its significance as a characteristic of those who rely on Allah.
Emphasizing the importance of prayer and the obligation to establish it in all divine messages and under all circumstances.
The legitimacy of supplicating against the oppressor.
