(٨٩) قَالَ قَدۡ أُجِيبَت دَّعۡوَتُكُمَا فَٱسۡتَقِيمَا وَلَا تَتَّبِعَآنِّ سَبِيلَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

Allah said: Your prayer, O Moses and Aaron, against Pharaoh and the nobles of his people has been responded to. So, remain firm on your religion and do not deviate from it by following the path of the ignorant ones who do not know the path of truth.

(٩٠) ۞وَجَٰوَزۡنَا بِبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَ فَأَتۡبَعَهُمۡ فِرۡعَوۡنُ وَجُنُودُهُۥ بَغۡيٗا وَعَدۡوًاۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَدۡرَكَهُ ٱلۡغَرَقُ قَالَ ءَامَنتُ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِيٓ ءَامَنَتۡ بِهِۦ بَنُوٓاْ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ وَأَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ

We made it easy for the Israelites to cross the sea safely after We split it open. Pharaoh and his soldiers pursued them unjustly and oppressively. Then, when the sea closed on him, bringing him to the point of drowning, he lost all hope of being saved and said: I believe that there is none worthy of worship except the One whom the Israelites believed in, and I am one of those who submit to Allah in obedience.

Since facing death impedes the acceptance of repentance, Allah the Transcendent states:

(٩١) ءَآلۡـَٰٔنَ وَقَدۡ عَصَيۡتَ قَبۡلُ وَكُنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ

Do you believe now after despairing of life? You, O Pharaoh, had disobeyed Allah before the torment came down upon you by disbelieving in Him and preventing others from His path, and you were one of those who spread corruption because of your own misguidance and misguiding others.

(٩٢) فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ نُنَجِّيكَ بِبَدَنِكَ لِتَكُونَ لِمَنۡ خَلۡفَكَ ءَايَةٗۚ وَإِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِ عَنۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا لَغَٰفِلُونَ

So today, We will take you out, O Pharaoh, from the sea and place you on high ground so that those who come after you can take a lesson from you. But many people are unmindful of Our proofs and evidence indicating Our power, and they do not reflect over them.

(٩٣) وَلَقَدۡ بَوَّأۡنَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ مُبَوَّأَ صِدۡقٖ وَرَزَقۡنَٰهُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ فَمَا ٱخۡتَلَفُواْ حَتَّىٰ جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡعِلۡمُۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ يَقۡضِي بَيۡنَهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فِيمَا كَانُواْ فِيهِ يَخۡتَلِفُونَ

We settled the Israelites in a commendable place in the blessed lands of the Levant, and We provided them with pure and lawful provision. They did not differ in the matter of religion until the Quran came to them, confirming what they read in the Torah about the description of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. When they denied the revelation, their homelands were taken away from them. Your Lord, O Messenger, will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection regarding the things they differed about; He will recompense those who were on truth and those who were on falsehood with what they deserve.

(٩٤) فَإِن كُنتَ فِي شَكّٖ مِّمَّآ أَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ فَسۡ‍َٔلِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَقۡرَءُونَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِن قَبۡلِكَۚ لَقَدۡ جَآءَكَ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكَ فَلَا تَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمُمۡتَرِينَ

If you, O Messenger, are in doubt or confused about the reality of the Quran that We have revealed to you, ask those Jews who read the Torah and the Christians who read the Gospel to confirm it. They will tell you that what was revealed to you is the truth because they have the description of it in their books. The truth, about which there is no doubt, has come to you from your Lord, so do not be of those who have doubt.

(٩٥) وَلَا تَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ فَتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ

Do not be of those who reject Allah's proofs and evidence because that will make you one of those who have lost themselves by bringing themselves to the point of destruction because of their disbelief. All these warnings are meant to show the seriousness of doubt and rejection, even though the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم is protected from such things.

(٩٦) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ حَقَّتۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ كَلِمَتُ رَبِّكَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Those against whom Allah's decree is established will die as disbelievers due to their persistence in disbelief, and they will never believe.

(٩٧) وَلَوۡ جَآءَتۡهُمۡ كُلُّ ءَايَةٍ حَتَّىٰ يَرَوُاْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ ٱلۡأَلِيمَ

Even if every sign in the creation or verse were to come to them until they witness the painful torment and believe at a time when their belief will not benefit them.

Points of Benefit:

The necessity of steadfastness in religion and not following the path of the wrongdoers.
Repentance is not accepted from one whose soul is reaching the throat or who witnesses the punishment.
The Jews and Christians knew the characteristics of the Prophet (peace be upon him), but pride and obstinacy prevented them from embracing faith.
