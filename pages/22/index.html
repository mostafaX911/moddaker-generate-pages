(١٤٢) ۞سَيَقُولُ ٱلسُّفَهَآءُ مِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَا وَلَّىٰهُمۡ عَن قِبۡلَتِهِمُ ٱلَّتِي كَانُواْ عَلَيۡهَاۚ قُل لِّلَّهِ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقُ وَٱلۡمَغۡرِبُۚ يَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

The weak-minded, ignorant ones from the Jews and those like them from the hypocrites will say: What caused the Muslims to turn away from their Qiblah (direction of prayer), Jerusalem, that they used to pray towards?! O Prophet, say in response to them: Allah alone owns the dominion of the lands to the East and the West and in every direction. He turns who He wills from His slaves to any direction He wills. He (may He be Exalted) guides whom He wills from His slaves to the straight path free of crookedness and deviation.

(١٤٣) وَكَذَٰلِكَ جَعَلۡنَٰكُمۡ أُمَّةٗ وَسَطٗا لِّتَكُونُواْ شُهَدَآءَ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ وَيَكُونَ ٱلرَّسُولُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ شَهِيدٗاۗ وَمَا جَعَلۡنَا ٱلۡقِبۡلَةَ ٱلَّتِي كُنتَ عَلَيۡهَآ إِلَّا لِنَعۡلَمَ مَن يَتَّبِعُ ٱلرَّسُولَ مِمَّن يَنقَلِبُ عَلَىٰ عَقِبَيۡهِۚ وَإِن كَانَتۡ لَكَبِيرَةً إِلَّا عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ هَدَى ٱللَّهُۗ وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيُضِيعَ إِيمَٰنَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِٱلنَّاسِ لَرَءُوفٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Just as We made a direction of prayer that We approved of for you, we made you the finest, most just and balanced nation among all other nations in matters of belief, acts of worship, and dealings. This is so that you may be witnesses for the messengers of Allah on the Day of Resurrection, to confirm that they delivered the messages Allah instructed them to deliver to their people, and so that the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم will also witness that he delivered the message he was sent to deliver to you. We [Allah] did not change the direction of prayer that you used to face (Jerusalem) except to reveal---and recompense---who was content with Allah had decreed and submitted to it by following the Messenger; to reveal who would renounce their religion to follow their own desires and not submit to what Allah had decreed. The change of the first direction of prayer was a significant issue, except for those to whom Allah granted faith that whatever He decrees for His slaves has profound, far-reaching wisdom behind it. Allah will not make you lose your faith in Him or the prayers you prayed before the change in prayer direction. Allah is kind and compassionate with people; He does not overburden them or cause the rewards of their good deeds to go to waste.

(١٤٤) قَدۡ نَرَىٰ تَقَلُّبَ وَجۡهِكَ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِۖ فَلَنُوَلِّيَنَّكَ قِبۡلَةٗ تَرۡضَىٰهَاۚ فَوَلِّ وَجۡهَكَ شَطۡرَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِۚ وَحَيۡثُ مَا كُنتُمۡ فَوَلُّواْ وُجُوهَكُمۡ شَطۡرَهُۥۗ وَإِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ لَيَعۡلَمُونَ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّهِمۡۗ وَمَا ٱللَّهُ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا يَعۡمَلُونَ

We have seen you, O Prophet, turning your face and sight towards the sky, anticipating revelation regarding the direction of prayer and changing it to the direction you love. So, we will turn you to a direction of prayer that you love and are pleased with, the Sacred House of Allah, instead of Jerusalem. So, turn your face toward the Sacred House of Allah in Mecca. Wherever you are, O believers, face its direction to perform the prayer. Those who have been given the book from the Jews and the Christians know that changing the direction of prayer is the truth sent down from their Creator and the One who manages their affairs because it is mentioned in their book. Allah is not unaware of what those who turn away from the truth do. He (may He be Exalted) is aware of that and will recompense them accordingly.

(١٤٥) وَلَئِنۡ أَتَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِكُلِّ ءَايَةٖ مَّا تَبِعُواْ قِبۡلَتَكَۚ وَمَآ أَنتَ بِتَابِعٖ قِبۡلَتَهُمۡۚ وَمَا بَعۡضُهُم بِتَابِعٖ قِبۡلَةَ بَعۡضٖۚ وَلَئِنِ ٱتَّبَعۡتَ أَهۡوَآءَهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ إِنَّكَ إِذٗا لَّمِنَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

By Allah, if you, O Prophet, were to bring every sign and proof to the Jews and Christians that changing the direction of the prayer is truth, they would not face your prayer direction out of stubbornness towards what you have brought and being too arrogant to follow the truth. Also, you would not face their prayer direction after Allah has turned you away from it. Likewise, they do not face each other's prayer direction because they consider each other disbelievers. If you were to follow their desires in matters of the prayer direction and other laws and rulings, after authentic knowledge which has no doubt has come to you, then you would be from the wrongdoers for leaving guidance and following desires.This verse was addressed to the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم to show the enormity of following them even though Allah has protected His Prophet from ever doing that, and it is a warning for his nation after him.

Points of Benefit:

Opposing Allah's rulings and legislation and heedlessness of their objectives is evidence of foolishness and a deficient intellect.
The virtue and honor of this nation as Allah praised it and described it as a moderate nation compared to others.
Warning from following the desires of the people of the book because they opposed the truth after knowing it.
The permissibility in Islam of abrogating prior legislative rulings upon newer revelation, as facing Jerusalem was abrogated in favor of facing Al-Masjid Al-Haram.
