(٩٨) فَلَوۡلَا كَانَتۡ قَرۡيَةٌ ءَامَنَتۡ فَنَفَعَهَآ إِيمَٰنُهَآ إِلَّا قَوۡمَ يُونُسَ لَمَّآ ءَامَنُواْ كَشَفۡنَا عَنۡهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡخِزۡيِ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَمَتَّعۡنَٰهُمۡ إِلَىٰ حِينٖ

No town to which We sent messengers benefitted from their belief upon seeing the torment because they did not believe until seeing it, except the people of Jonah. When they truly believed, We removed the torment of disgrace and humiliation from them in the worldly life and allowed them enjoyment until the end of their lives.

(٩٩) وَلَوۡ شَآءَ رَبُّكَ لَأٓمَنَ مَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كُلُّهُمۡ جَمِيعًاۚ أَفَأَنتَ تُكۡرِهُ ٱلنَّاسَ حَتَّىٰ يَكُونُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ

Had your Lord, O Messenger, willed for everyone on Earth to believe, they would have done so. However, He did not will it to be so based on wisdom. He misguides whoever He wills through His justice and guides whoever He wills through His grace. You cannot force people to become believers: their guidance to belief lies only in Allah's Hand.

(١٠٠) وَمَا كَانَ لِنَفۡسٍ أَن تُؤۡمِنَ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَيَجۡعَلُ ٱلرِّجۡسَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡقِلُونَ

It is not possible for any soul to believe by itself unless Allah permits it. Belief only happens with His will, so do not lose yourself in grief over them. Allah places torment and disgrace on those who do not apply their minds to His proofs, commands, and prohibitions.

(١٠١) قُلِ ٱنظُرُواْ مَاذَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَمَا تُغۡنِي ٱلۡأٓيَٰتُ وَٱلنُّذُرُ عَن قَوۡمٖ لَّا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to the polytheists who ask you for signs: Ponder upon the signs in the heavens and the Earth, which indicate Allah's Oneness and power. However, sending down signs, proofs, and messengers is useless for people who are not prepared to believe because of their persistence in disbelief.

(١٠٢) فَهَلۡ يَنتَظِرُونَ إِلَّا مِثۡلَ أَيَّامِ ٱلَّذِينَ خَلَوۡاْ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ قُلۡ فَٱنتَظِرُوٓاْ إِنِّي مَعَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُنتَظِرِينَ

Are these rejectors waiting for something similar to what Allah imposed on the previous rejecting nations? Say to them, O Messenger: Wait for Allah's torment. I, too, am waiting with you for my Lord's promise.

(١٠٣) ثُمَّ نُنَجِّي رُسُلَنَا وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْۚ كَذَٰلِكَ حَقًّا عَلَيۡنَا نُنجِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Then We will send the punishment upon them and save Our messengers and those who believe with them so that they are not afflicted by what afflicted their people. Just as We saved those messengers and the believers with them, We will most certainly save Allah's Messenger and the believers with him, which is Our duty to them.

(١٠٤) قُلۡ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِن كُنتُمۡ فِي شَكّٖ مِّن دِينِي فَلَآ أَعۡبُدُ ٱلَّذِينَ تَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَٰكِنۡ أَعۡبُدُ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِي يَتَوَفَّىٰكُمۡۖ وَأُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Say, O Messenger: O people, if you are in doubt about my religion that I invite you towards, the religion of monotheism, then I am sure of your religion being false, so I will not follow it. I will not worship those you worship instead of Allah. I will only worship Allah, the One who will cause you to die. He has instructed me to be of those who are sincere to Him in religion.

(١٠٥) وَأَنۡ أَقِمۡ وَجۡهَكَ لِلدِّينِ حَنِيفٗا وَلَا تَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

He has also instructed me to remain firm on the true religion, inclining away from all other religions, and He has prohibited me from being one of those who associate partners with Him.

(١٠٦) وَلَا تَدۡعُ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا يَنفَعُكَ وَلَا يَضُرُّكَۖ فَإِن فَعَلۡتَ فَإِنَّكَ إِذٗا مِّنَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

And, O Messenger, do not worship, besides Allah, the idols and false deities that cannot benefit you, nor can they inflict harm on you. If you worship them, you will be one of the wrongdoers who transgress against Allah's right and their right.

Points of Benefit:

Faith is the means of elevating its possessor to the highest ranks and enjoying prosperity in this worldly life.
No one has the ability to compel another to believe, as this is solely determined by the will of Allah alone.
Signs and warnings do not benefit those who persist in disbelief and persistently adhere to it.
The obligation of steadfastness on the true religion, and the necessity of distancing oneself completely from polytheism and false religions.
