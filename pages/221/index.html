(١٠٧) وَإِن يَمۡسَسۡكَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرّٖ فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهُۥٓ إِلَّا هُوَۖ وَإِن يُرِدۡكَ بِخَيۡرٖ فَلَا رَآدَّ لِفَضۡلِهِۦۚ يُصِيبُ بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

If Allah afflicts you, O Messenger, with a calamity and you seek to remove it, then know that no one can remove it except Him; may He be Exalted. If He wants ease for you, no one can repel His grace. He makes His grace reach whomever He wills of His slaves. No one can compel Him. He is Forgiving and Merciful to those of His slaves who repent to Him.

(١٠٨) قُلۡ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَكُمُ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكُمۡۖ فَمَنِ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ فَإِنَّمَا يَهۡتَدِي لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن ضَلَّ فَإِنَّمَا يَضِلُّ عَلَيۡهَاۖ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ عَلَيۡكُم بِوَكِيلٖ

Say, O Messenger: O people, the Quran has come to you as a revelation from your Lord. Whoever chooses to be guided and believes in it will be for his own benefit because Allah does not need the obedience of His slaves. Yet, whoever is misguided only harms himself with his misguidance because Allah is not affected by the disobedience of His slaves. I am not a guardian over you, to watch over your deeds and to reckon you for them.

(١٠٩) وَٱتَّبِعۡ مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيۡكَ وَٱصۡبِرۡ حَتَّىٰ يَحۡكُمَ ٱللَّهُۚ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡحَٰكِمِينَ

Follow, O Messenger, what your Lord reveals to you and act on it. Be patient with the harm caused by those who oppose you from your people while you convey the message as instructed. Remain firm on this path until Allah passes judgment concerning them by giving you victory over them in this world and punishing them in the Hereafter if they die as disbelievers.

Hud (Surah Hud)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Strengthening the faith of the Prophet and the believers by recounting the stories of the past prophets while also emphasizing the consequences of disbelief.

Explanation:

(١) الٓرۚ كِتَٰبٌ أُحۡكِمَتۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥ ثُمَّ فُصِّلَتۡ مِن لَّدُنۡ حَكِيمٍ خَبِيرٍ

Alif Lām Rā – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah. The Quran is a book whose verses are perfect in arrangement and meaning, in which you will see no flaw or deficiency. Then, it was detailed by mentioning the lawful and unlawful commands and prohibitions, promises and threats, narrations, etc. It is from the One who is Wise in His planning and legislation and Who is Aware of the condition of His slaves and what is in their best interests.

(٢) أَلَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّنِي لَكُم مِّنۡهُ نَذِيرٞ وَبَشِيرٞ

The content of these verses revealed to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم prohibits the slaves from worshiping anyone alongside Allah. I, O people, warn you of Allah's torment if you disbelieve in Him and disobey Him, and I give you good news of His reward if you believe in Him and practice His sacred law.

(٣) وَأَنِ ٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ ثُمَّ تُوبُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ يُمَتِّعۡكُم مَّتَٰعًا حَسَنًا إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗى وَيُؤۡتِ كُلَّ ذِي فَضۡلٖ فَضۡلَهُۥۖ وَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَإِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ عَذَابَ يَوۡمٖ كَبِيرٍ

O people, seek forgiveness for your sins from your Lord and repent with remorse over your neglect of Him. He will give you good provision in the worldly life until your fixed lifespan ends. He will give every person who has merit, in obeying Him and doing good, a full reward for such behavior without any decrease. If you turn away from believing in what I have brought from my Lord, then I fear for you the torment of a Day whose horrors are intense, which is the Day of Resurrection.

(٤) إِلَى ٱللَّهِ مَرۡجِعُكُمۡۖ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ

To Allah alone is your return, O people, on the Day of Resurrection, and He (may He be Exalted) is Omnipotent over everything; nothing can hinder Him. He is not incapable of bringing you back to life and taking you into account after reviving you from your death.

(٥) أَلَآ إِنَّهُمۡ يَثۡنُونَ صُدُورَهُمۡ لِيَسۡتَخۡفُواْ مِنۡهُۚ أَلَا حِينَ يَسۡتَغۡشُونَ ثِيَابَهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُ مَا يُسِرُّونَ وَمَا يُعۡلِنُونَۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

These polytheists lean down to conceal their doubts about Allah due to their ignorance of Him. Allah knows what they conceal and reveal when they cover their heads with their clothing. He knows what their chests hide.

Points of Benefit:

Good and evil, benefit and harm, are in the Hand of Allah alone, without any other.
The obligation of following the Book and the Sunnah, enduring harm, and patiently awaiting relief from Allah.
The verses of the Quran are precise, containing no flaw or error, and the rulings therein are elaborated in complete detail.
The necessity of hastening towards repentance, regretting sins to achieve the desired outcome, and attaining salvation from what is feared.
