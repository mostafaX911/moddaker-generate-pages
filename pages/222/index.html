(٦) ۞وَمَا مِن دَآبَّةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱللَّهِ رِزۡقُهَا وَيَعۡلَمُ مُسۡتَقَرَّهَا وَمُسۡتَوۡدَعَهَاۚ كُلّٞ فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٖ

No creature walks on the face of the Earth, wherever it may be, except that Allah has undertaken, through His grace, to provide for it. He (may He be Exalted) knows where each creature will settle on Earth, and He knows where it will die. So, the provision, place of living, and final resting place are all written in a clear book, the Preserved Tablet.

(٧) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ وَكَانَ عَرۡشُهُۥ عَلَى ٱلۡمَآءِ لِيَبۡلُوَكُمۡ أَيُّكُمۡ أَحۡسَنُ عَمَلٗاۗ وَلَئِن قُلۡتَ إِنَّكُم مَّبۡعُوثُونَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ لَيَقُولَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا سِحۡرٞ مُّبِينٞ

Allah (may He be Exalted) is the One who created the heavens and the Earth in their magnificent form and all that exists between them in six days. Before He made them, His Throne was on water. All of this He created to see which of you, O people, will be best in doing actions that please Allah and which of you will do actions that cause His anger. He will compensate each group according to what they deserve. O Messenger, If you say to them: O people, indeed you will be resurrected after you die and held to account, then those who disbelieve in Allah and reject the resurrection will surely say: This Quran that you recite is nothing but magic, and so clearly false.

(٨) وَلَئِنۡ أَخَّرۡنَا عَنۡهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابَ إِلَىٰٓ أُمَّةٖ مَّعۡدُودَةٖ لَّيَقُولُنَّ مَا يَحۡبِسُهُۥٓۗ أَلَا يَوۡمَ يَأۡتِيهِمۡ لَيۡسَ مَصۡرُوفًا عَنۡهُمۡ وَحَاقَ بِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

If We were to delay the polytheists from receiving the punishment they deserve in this world for a few days, they would mock the punishment by asking for it to come quickly, saying: What is it that is holding back the punishment from us? But, of course, the punishment they deserve has a fixed time with Allah, and on the day when it comes to them, they will not find a way to avert it from themselves. So, instead, the punishment they used to mock openly by asking for it to come quicker will fall upon them, engulfing them from all sides.

(٩) وَلَئِنۡ أَذَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِنَّا رَحۡمَةٗ ثُمَّ نَزَعۡنَٰهَا مِنۡهُ إِنَّهُۥ لَيَ‍ُٔوسٞ كَفُورٞ

If We give a man a blessing from Us, such as health or wealth, and then We take that blessing away from him, he loses all hope of the mercy of Allah and becomes hugely ungrateful for His blessings, and forgets them as soon as Allah takes them away from him.

(١٠) وَلَئِنۡ أَذَقۡنَٰهُ نَعۡمَآءَ بَعۡدَ ضَرَّآءَ مَسَّتۡهُ لَيَقُولَنَّ ذَهَبَ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتُ عَنِّيٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَفَرِحٞ فَخُورٌ

If We open up many provisions and good health for such a person after he endured poverty and illness, he will say: My troubles have gone, and the crisis is over. However, he does not show gratefulness to Allah for such blessings; instead, he becomes boastful and self-conceited, holding his head high and looking down on people because of what Allah has blessed him with.

(١١) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ صَبَرُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٞ وَأَجۡرٞ كَبِيرٞ

Except those who are patient during hard times, continue to do acts of obedience, and avoid sins. Such good people are different from the arrogant ones described previously, as they do not lose hope in times of adversity, nor are they ungrateful for Allah's blessings or arrogant. So, they will receive forgiveness from their Lord and a great reward in the Afterlife.

(١٢) فَلَعَلَّكَ تَارِكُۢ بَعۡضَ مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيۡكَ وَضَآئِقُۢ بِهِۦ صَدۡرُكَ أَن يَقُولُواْ لَوۡلَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيۡهِ كَنزٌ أَوۡ جَآءَ مَعَهُۥ مَلَكٌۚ إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ نَذِيرٞۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَكِيلٌ

Perhaps you, O Messenger, might leave off conveying some of what Allah has ordered you to convey to them from what is difficult for them to implement due to their disbelief, stubbornness, and demand for signs. Perhaps your chest is strained in conveying it lest they say: Why has no treasure been sent down to him to enrich him or an angel to verify his truthfulness? Do not leave some of what is revealed to you because of that; you are only a warner who conveys what Allah has instructed you to convey. It is not upon you to bring the signs that they request; Allah is the Guardian over all things.

Points of Benefit:

The verses show how vast Allah's knowledge is and that He has taken it upon Himself to provide for all His creation, including human beings, animals, and others.
The purpose of creation is explained here: to test the servants' obedience to Allah's instructions and avoidance of His prohibitions.
No one should be deceived by Allah's granting respite to those who sin: He may seize them suddenly while they are unaware.
Man's condition in both prosperity and hardship and praise of the patience and gratefulness the believer shows.
