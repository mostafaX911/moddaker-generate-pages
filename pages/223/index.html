(١٣) أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ ٱفۡتَرَىٰهُۖ قُلۡ فَأۡتُواْ بِعَشۡرِ سُوَرٖ مِّثۡلِهِۦ مُفۡتَرَيَٰتٖ وَٱدۡعُواْ مَنِ ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

Rather, do the polytheists say that Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم forged the Quran and that it is not a revelation from Allah? Say, O Messenger, challenging them: Bring ten forged chapters similar to the Quran without maintaining truthfulness in it, just like the Quran which you claim is forged. Call upon whomsoever you can call upon in order to seek assistance by that in this (pursuit) if you are truthful in your claim that the Quran is forged!

(١٤) فَإِلَّمۡ يَسۡتَجِيبُواْ لَكُمۡ فَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَآ أُنزِلَ بِعِلۡمِ ٱللَّهِ وَأَن لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ فَهَلۡ أَنتُم مُّسۡلِمُونَ

If they do not bring what you have asked them to due to their lack of ability, then know with certainty, O believers, that the Quran is none other than what Allah sent down to His Messenger with His complete knowledge, that it is not forged, and that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah. Then will you not surrender to Him in devotion after these conclusive proofs (and become from those who submit to Islam and become Muslims)?

(١٥) مَن كَانَ يُرِيدُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَزِينَتَهَا نُوَفِّ إِلَيۡهِمۡ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ فِيهَا وَهُمۡ فِيهَا لَا يُبۡخَسُونَ

Whoever seeks the life of this world and its fleeting pleasures with their actions and does not seek the reward of the Hereafter with them, We will compensate them for their actions in this worldly life: good health, safety, and vastness in provision, not decreasing in the reward for their actions in any way.

(١٦) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَيۡسَ لَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ إِلَّا ٱلنَّارُۖ وَحَبِطَ مَا صَنَعُواْ فِيهَا وَبَٰطِلٞ مَّا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

Those people with this blameworthy trait will not be rewarded on the Day of Resurrection, except for the Fire, which they will enter. The reward of their actions will disappear and be useless because they were not based on faith or good intention, and they did not desire the face of Allah and the abode of the Hereafter through their actions.

(١٧) أَفَمَن كَانَ عَلَىٰ بَيِّنَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ وَيَتۡلُوهُ شَاهِدٞ مِّنۡهُ وَمِن قَبۡلِهِۦ كِتَٰبُ مُوسَىٰٓ إِمَامٗا وَرَحۡمَةًۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهِۦۚ وَمَن يَكۡفُرۡ بِهِۦ مِنَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابِ فَٱلنَّارُ مَوۡعِدُهُۥۚ فَلَا تَكُ فِي مِرۡيَةٖ مِّنۡهُۚ إِنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكَ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

The disbelievers are not equal to the believers who believe in the Quran and the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, who has clear proof from His Lord, the Exalted. A witness from Allah (Jibreel) who follows him, and the Torah revealed to Moses (peace be upon him) also testify to the truthfulness of his prophethood. He was sent as a role model and mercy to people. Therefore, he and those who believe in him are not the same as those who stray in error. For those who disbelieve in it from the different religions, the Fire will be their promised destination on the Day of Resurrection. So, O Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, do not doubt the Quran and their destination because it is the truth in which there is no doubt. However, despite the many clear signs and evidence, most people do not have faith.

(١٨) وَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّنِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبًاۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يُعۡرَضُونَ عَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ وَيَقُولُ ٱلۡأَشۡهَٰدُ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَبُواْ عَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡۚ أَلَا لَعۡنَةُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَى ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

No one is a worse transgressor than someone who makes up a lie about Allah, associating a partner or a son with Him. Those who make up lies about Allah will be brought before their Lord on the Day of Resurrection to be asked about their actions. The witnesses from the angels and the messengers will say: These were the ones who lied about Allah by attributing a partner or son to Him. Allah will deprive them of His mercy due to the lies they spread about Him.

(١٩) ٱلَّذِينَ يَصُدُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَبۡغُونَهَا عِوَجٗا وَهُم بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ كَٰفِرُونَ

Those who prevent people from Allah's straight path seek to make it appear crooked so that no one follows it, and they reject the belief in the Afterlife.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's challenge to the idolaters is to produce ten chapters like the Quran, and it is explained that they will be unable to do this.
The disbeliever is rewarded for his actions, but he will have nothing except the Fire in the Hereafter.
The enormity of the sin of someone who makes up a lie about Allah and the magnitude of its punishment on the Day of Resurrection.
