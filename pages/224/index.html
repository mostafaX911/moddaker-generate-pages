(٢٠) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَمۡ يَكُونُواْ مُعۡجِزِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا كَانَ لَهُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مِنۡ أَوۡلِيَآءَۘ يُضَٰعَفُ لَهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُۚ مَا كَانُواْ يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَمَا كَانُواْ يُبۡصِرُونَ

People such as this will not be able to escape Allah's punishment in this life, and they have no allies or protectors besides Allah who can stop the punishment of Allah from reaching them. So, their punishment on the Day of Resurrection will be increased because they turned away and turned others away from Allah's path. Thus, in the life of this world, they were not able to hear the truth and guidance in a way that led them to accept it, nor were they able to see Allah's signs throughout the universe in a way that benefited them because of their total disregard of the truth.

(٢١) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَضَلَّ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ

These people have incurred great loss upon themselves because they took the path of destruction by associating partners with Allah. The partners and intercessors they invented will all abandon them.

(٢٢) لَا جَرَمَ أَنَّهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمُ ٱلۡأَخۡسَرُونَ

On the Day of Resurrection, they will undoubtedly be the greatest losers because they exchanged faith for disbelief, the next life for this life, and mercy for punishment.

(٢٣) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَأَخۡبَتُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

Those who believe in Allah and His messengers and do good actions are humble and fear Allah. They are the people of Paradise and will live therein eternally.

(٢٤) ۞مَثَلُ ٱلۡفَرِيقَيۡنِ كَٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ وَٱلۡأَصَمِّ وَٱلۡبَصِيرِ وَٱلسَّمِيعِۚ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِيَانِ مَثَلًاۚ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ

The example of the two groups, the disbelievers and the believers, is like a group of blind and deaf people who neither see nor hear and another group that can hear and see. The disbelievers do not listen in a way that leads them to accept the truth and do not see in a beneficial way. Meanwhile, the believers both hear and see. Are these two groups the same in their state and depiction? Of course, they are not the same. Will you then not consider how truly different they are to each other?

When it became apparent that the polytheists were turning away from belief, Allah consoled the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم by reminding him that he was not the first to be rejected. He then recounted the stories of previous prophets, stating:

(٢٥) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِنِّي لَكُمۡ نَذِيرٞ مُّبِينٌ

Indeed, We sent Noah (peace be upon him) as a messenger to his people. Noah said to them: O my people, I have been sent to warn you of Allah's punishment and make the message I have been sent with very clear to you.

(٢٦) أَن لَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَۖ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ عَذَابَ يَوۡمٍ أَلِيمٖ

I call you to worship Allah alone; therefore, do not worship anyone besides Him. Indeed, I fear for you the punishment of a painful day.

(٢٧) فَقَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن قَوۡمِهِۦ مَا نَرَىٰكَ إِلَّا بَشَرٗا مِّثۡلَنَا وَمَا نَرَىٰكَ ٱتَّبَعَكَ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ أَرَاذِلُنَا بَادِيَ ٱلرَّأۡيِ وَمَا نَرَىٰ لَكُمۡ عَلَيۡنَا مِن فَضۡلِۢ بَلۡ نَظُنُّكُمۡ كَٰذِبِينَ

Those who disbelieved from his people among the notables and leaders said: We will not respond to your call, as you have no distinction over us. Rather, you are a man like us. We only see those who appear lowly to us following you. Also, you do not have wealth, rank, or status above us that qualifies you to be followed by us. In fact, we think that you are not truthful in what you are calling us to.

(٢٨) قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِن كُنتُ عَلَىٰ بَيِّنَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّي وَءَاتَىٰنِي رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنۡ عِندِهِۦ فَعُمِّيَتۡ عَلَيۡكُمۡ أَنُلۡزِمُكُمُوهَا وَأَنتُمۡ لَهَا كَٰرِهُونَ

Noah asked his people: O people, tell me, if I were upon evidence from my Lord that proved my truthfulness, and you had to believe in me, and [my Lord] showed me mercy, in the form of prophethood and the messengership, but due to your ignorance, it was hidden from you; would we force you to believe and place faith in your hearts? We would never be able to do that because Allah alone grants people the ability to believe.

Points of Benefit:

The disbeliever does not benefit from his hearing or sight in a way that would lead him to believe, so it is as if he does not have them at all, but that is not the case with the believer.
Allah's established practice with the followers of the Messengers is that they are mainly poor and weak, while their adversaries are notable people and leaders.
In most cases, the leaders and elite class are arrogant and look down on those who are beneath them,
