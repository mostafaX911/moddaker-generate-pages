(٢٩) وَيَٰقَوۡمِ لَآ أَسۡ‍َٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مَالًاۖ إِنۡ أَجۡرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ بِطَارِدِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْۚ إِنَّهُم مُّلَٰقُواْ رَبِّهِمۡ وَلَٰكِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰكُمۡ قَوۡمٗا تَجۡهَلُونَ

My people, I do not ask for your wealth in exchange for bringing you the message, as my reward is with Allah. Therefore, I will not push away the poor believers like you have asked me to. Indeed, they will meet their Lord on the Day of Resurrection, and Allah will reward them for their faith. I see you as a nation that does not understand the reality of this call when you ask me to push away the weak believers.

(٣٠) وَيَٰقَوۡمِ مَن يَنصُرُنِي مِنَ ٱللَّهِ إِن طَرَدتُّهُمۡۚ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ

Noah said: My people, who would repel Allah's punishment from me if I unjustly drove away these poor believers for no crime? Will you not reflect and strive for what is best and most beneficial to you?

(٣١) وَلَآ أَقُولُ لَكُمۡ عِندِي خَزَآئِنُ ٱللَّهِ وَلَآ أَعۡلَمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبَ وَلَآ أَقُولُ إِنِّي مَلَكٞ وَلَآ أَقُولُ لِلَّذِينَ تَزۡدَرِيٓ أَعۡيُنُكُمۡ لَن يُؤۡتِيَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ خَيۡرًاۖ ٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ إِنِّيٓ إِذٗا لَّمِنَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

My people, I do not claim that I possess the treasures of Allah, which I can spend on you if you believe. Nor do I claim to know the unseen nor that I am an angel; instead, I am a man like you. I also do not say about these weak people, whom you see as inferiors or hold in such disregard, that Allah will not give them success and guidance. Allah knows best their intentions and their circumstances. If I did make such a claim, I would undoubtedly be among those wrongdoers who deserve the punishment of Allah.

(٣٢) قَالُواْ يَٰنُوحُ قَدۡ جَٰدَلۡتَنَا فَأَكۡثَرۡتَ جِدَٰلَنَا فَأۡتِنَا بِمَا تَعِدُنَآ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

Noah's people said to him, out of stubbornness and pride: O Noah, you have debated and disputed with us long enough; bring on the punishment you are threatening us with if you are truthful in what you claim.

(٣٣) قَالَ إِنَّمَا يَأۡتِيكُم بِهِ ٱللَّهُ إِن شَآءَ وَمَآ أَنتُم بِمُعۡجِزِينَ

Noah said to them: I cannot bring the punishment upon you; only Allah can do that if He wishes to, and you would not be able to escape from the punishment of Allah if He decides to punish you.

(٣٤) وَلَا يَنفَعُكُمۡ نُصۡحِيٓ إِنۡ أَرَدتُّ أَنۡ أَنصَحَ لَكُمۡ إِن كَانَ ٱللَّهُ يُرِيدُ أَن يُغۡوِيَكُمۡۚ هُوَ رَبُّكُمۡ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

Noah told them that his advice and counsel would not benefit them if Allah wills that they are led astray from the straight path and if He wills to leave them without guidance because of their stubbornness. He is their Lord and has control over their lives, so He can lead them astray if He wills. To Him alone, they will be returned on the Day of Resurrection, when they will be recompensed for their actions.

(٣٥) أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ ٱفۡتَرَىٰهُۖ قُلۡ إِنِ ٱفۡتَرَيۡتُهُۥ فَعَلَيَّ إِجۡرَامِي وَأَنَا۠ بَرِيٓءٞ مِّمَّا تُجۡرِمُونَ

The reason for the people of Noah disbelieving [in the message] is that they claimed that Noah had lied about the religion he came with, so Allah ordered His Prophet to tell them that if he had invented it, he would have been a wrongdoer. Therefore, the punishment for his crime would fall upon him alone. But, on the other hand, if they rejected the truth that he had brought them out of stubbornness and pride, they were the wrongdoers, and he was not responsible for their crime in any way. Instead, he was free from it.

(٣٦) وَأُوحِيَ إِلَىٰ نُوحٍ أَنَّهُۥ لَن يُؤۡمِنَ مِن قَوۡمِكَ إِلَّا مَن قَدۡ ءَامَنَ فَلَا تَبۡتَئِسۡ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡعَلُونَ

Allah revealed to Noah that none of his people would believe except those who had already believed. He told him not to be saddened by their rejection and mockery of him over the many years [he had been calling them].

(٣٧) وَٱصۡنَعِ ٱلۡفُلۡكَ بِأَعۡيُنِنَا وَوَحۡيِنَا وَلَا تُخَٰطِبۡنِي فِي ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوٓاْ إِنَّهُم مُّغۡرَقُونَ

Build [O Noah] the Ark under Our observation and protection. Learn how to make it with Our revelation to you. Do not address Me requesting to give time to those who wronged themselves through their disbelief. No doubt, they will be drowned in the Flood as a consequence of persisting in their disbelief.

Points of Benefit:

The caller to Allah should be virtuous, hoping for reward from Him alone.
It is forbidden to drive away poor believers; rather, they must be treated with generosity and honor.
Knowledge of the unseen is exclusively Allah's alone.
The legitimacy of debating and arguing with those who deny the truth.
