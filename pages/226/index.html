(٣٨) وَيَصۡنَعُ ٱلۡفُلۡكَ وَكُلَّمَا مَرَّ عَلَيۡهِ مَلَأٞ مِّن قَوۡمِهِۦ سَخِرُواْ مِنۡهُۚ قَالَ إِن تَسۡخَرُواْ مِنَّا فَإِنَّا نَسۡخَرُ مِنكُمۡ كَمَا تَسۡخَرُونَ

Noah followed the command of his Lord and immediately started building the Ark. Whenever the leaders and noblemen among his people passed by, they made fun of him because he was building the Ark as there was no water or rivers around. When they continued to mock him, he said to them: If you, O noblemen, mock us today as we build the Ark, then we also mock you for being ignorant of the fact that you will drown.

(٣٩) فَسَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ مَن يَأۡتِيهِ عَذَابٞ يُخۡزِيهِ وَيَحِلُّ عَلَيۡهِ عَذَابٞ مُّقِيمٌ

[Noah told them] You will come to know who will be punished in a disgraceful and humiliating way in this world and who the eternal punishment will descend upon on the Day of Resurrection.

(٤٠) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَ أَمۡرُنَا وَفَارَ ٱلتَّنُّورُ قُلۡنَا ٱحۡمِلۡ فِيهَا مِن كُلّٖ زَوۡجَيۡنِ ٱثۡنَيۡنِ وَأَهۡلَكَ إِلَّا مَن سَبَقَ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ وَمَنۡ ءَامَنَۚ وَمَآ ءَامَنَ مَعَهُۥٓ إِلَّا قَلِيلٞ

Noah (peace be upon him) finished building the Ark that Allah had commanded him to build. Then Our command to destroy them came, and the water boiled up from the furnace they used to bake in, signaling the beginning of the Flood. Allah told Noah (peace be upon him) to place onboard the Ark a pair of every type of animal on Earth, a male and a female, and to take his family, except for those who had already been determined to drown because they did not believe. He was also told to bring those who believed in him from among his people. But unfortunately, only a small number of his people believed in him, despite the long time he had remained among them, calling them to have faith in Allah.

(٤١) ۞وَقَالَ ٱرۡكَبُواْ فِيهَا بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ مَجۡرٜىٰهَا وَمُرۡسَىٰهَآۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي لَغَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Noah said to those who believed from his family and his people: Board the Ark, and its sailing and landing will be in the name of Allah. My Lord is Forgiving of the sins of those of His slaves who repent to Him and is Compassionate with them. His mercy on the believers is that He saved them from perishing.

(٤٢) وَهِيَ تَجۡرِي بِهِمۡ فِي مَوۡجٖ كَٱلۡجِبَالِ وَنَادَىٰ نُوحٌ ٱبۡنَهُۥ وَكَانَ فِي مَعۡزِلٖ يَٰبُنَيَّ ٱرۡكَب مَّعَنَا وَلَا تَكُن مَّعَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

The Ark sailed with the people and animals through great waves like mountains. Noah (peace be upon him), with the affection of a father, called to his son, who did not believe and was in a place isolated from his father and his people. Noah told him to get onto the Ark with them so that he could be saved from the Flood, and he told him not to be with the disbelievers, or he would be drowned as they would be.

(٤٣) قَالَ سَ‍َٔاوِيٓ إِلَىٰ جَبَلٖ يَعۡصِمُنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمَآءِۚ قَالَ لَا عَاصِمَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ مِنۡ أَمۡرِ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا مَن رَّحِمَۚ وَحَالَ بَيۡنَهُمَا ٱلۡمَوۡجُ فَكَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُغۡرَقِينَ

Noah's son said to his father: I will take refuge on a high mountain so the water will not reach me. Noah told his son: Nothing will save anyone from Allah's punishment of being drowned by the Flood, except those whom Allah saves with His mercy. Then, a wave came between Noah and his disbelieving son, and his son was drowned by the Flood because of his disbelief.

(٤٤) وَقِيلَ يَٰٓأَرۡضُ ٱبۡلَعِي مَآءَكِ وَيَٰسَمَآءُ أَقۡلِعِي وَغِيضَ ٱلۡمَآءُ وَقُضِيَ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ وَٱسۡتَوَتۡ عَلَى ٱلۡجُودِيِّۖ وَقِيلَ بُعۡدٗا لِّلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

After the Flood, Allah said: O Earth, absorb the water, and O sky, hold back and do not send down rain. The water decreased until the land became dry. By then, Allah had destroyed the disbelievers. The Ark stopped on Mount Jūdī. It was said: Be gone and ruined, O people who had transgressed the limits of Allah through disbelief.

(٤٥) وَنَادَىٰ نُوحٞ رَّبَّهُۥ فَقَالَ رَبِّ إِنَّ ٱبۡنِي مِنۡ أَهۡلِي وَإِنَّ وَعۡدَكَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَأَنتَ أَحۡكَمُ ٱلۡحَٰكِمِينَ

Noah (peace be upon him) called upon his Lord, seeking his aid, saying: My Lord, indeed my son is from my family that you promised to save. Your promise is always true; you are the most just and knowledgeable.

Points of Benefit:

The habit of the idolaters in mocking and sneering at the prophets and their followers.
Allah's established practice with people is that most do not believe.
There is no refuge from Allah, except in Him, and there is no protector from His command except Him.
