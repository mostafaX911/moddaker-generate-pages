(٤٦) قَالَ يَٰنُوحُ إِنَّهُۥ لَيۡسَ مِنۡ أَهۡلِكَۖ إِنَّهُۥ عَمَلٌ غَيۡرُ صَٰلِحٖۖ فَلَا تَسۡ‍َٔلۡنِ مَا لَيۡسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمٌۖ إِنِّيٓ أَعِظُكَ أَن تَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِينَ

Allah said to Noah: Indeed, your son that you asked me to save is not from your family because he is a disbeliever. Your question, O Noah, is not a befitting action for someone of your status. Therefore, do not ask me something you have no knowledge of. I warn you from being among the ignorant folk by questioning [a decision] you do not know about.

(٤٧) قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّيٓ أَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنۡ أَسۡ‍َٔلَكَ مَا لَيۡسَ لِي بِهِۦ عِلۡمٞۖ وَإِلَّا تَغۡفِرۡ لِي وَتَرۡحَمۡنِيٓ أَكُن مِّنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ

Noah (peace be upon him) said: My Lord, prevent me from asking You about something I do not know about. And if You do not forgive my sins and have mercy on me, I will be among the losers who lose their share of the Afterlife.

(٤٨) قِيلَ يَٰنُوحُ ٱهۡبِطۡ بِسَلَٰمٖ مِّنَّا وَبَرَكَٰتٍ عَلَيۡكَ وَعَلَىٰٓ أُمَمٖ مِّمَّن مَّعَكَۚ وَأُمَمٞ سَنُمَتِّعُهُمۡ ثُمَّ يَمَسُّهُم مِّنَّا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Allah said to Noah (peace be upon him): O Noah, disembark and go on land in safety and security, with many blessings from Allah upon you and on the believing descendants of those with you on the Ark who will come after you. Unfortunately, there will also be others from their descendants who will disbelieve. For a time, Allah will give them enjoyment and livelihood in the life of this world, but then a painful punishment from Him will come to them.

(٤٩) تِلۡكَ مِنۡ أَنۢبَآءِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ نُوحِيهَآ إِلَيۡكَۖ مَا كُنتَ تَعۡلَمُهَآ أَنتَ وَلَا قَوۡمُكَ مِن قَبۡلِ هَٰذَاۖ فَٱصۡبِرۡۖ إِنَّ ٱلۡعَٰقِبَةَ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ

This story of Noah is from the news of the unseen, which you, O Messenger, or your people did not know beforehand until We revealed it to you. Therefore, be patient with the harm you suffer from the rejection of your people, just as Noah صلى الله عليه وسلم was patient. Help and victory are for those who comply with Allah's commands and avoid what He has prohibited.

(٥٠) وَإِلَىٰ عَادٍ أَخَاهُمۡ هُودٗاۚ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥٓۖ إِنۡ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا مُفۡتَرُونَ

We sent to the people of 'Ād their brother Hūd (peace be upon him). He said to them: O my people, worship Allah alone and do not associate anyone with Him; you do not have a deity that deserves to be worshipped except Him, may He be Exalted; you are not telling the truth in claiming he has a partner.

(٥١) يَٰقَوۡمِ لَآ أَسۡ‍َٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ أَجۡرًاۖ إِنۡ أَجۡرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَنِيٓۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

[Hūd said] O my people, I do not seek from you reward for what I have conveyed from my Lord and my calling you to Him. My reward is only from Allah, Who created me. Will you not think about this and respond to what I am calling you to?

(٥٢) وَيَٰقَوۡمِ ٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ ثُمَّ تُوبُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ يُرۡسِلِ ٱلسَّمَآءَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّدۡرَارٗا وَيَزِدۡكُمۡ قُوَّةً إِلَىٰ قُوَّتِكُمۡ وَلَا تَتَوَلَّوۡاْ مُجۡرِمِينَ

[Hūd said] O my people, ask for forgiveness from Allah and repent to Him from your sins, the greatest of which is associating others with Him. He will reward you by sending down abundant rain and will strengthen you by increasing your descendants and wealth. Therefore, do not turn away from what I am calling you to because you will become wrongdoers due to turning away from my call and your disbelief in Allah and rejection of what I have brought.

(٥٣) قَالُواْ يَٰهُودُ مَا جِئۡتَنَا بِبَيِّنَةٖ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِتَارِكِيٓ ءَالِهَتِنَا عَن قَوۡلِكَ وَمَا نَحۡنُ لَكَ بِمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Hūd's people said: O Hūd, you have not brought us unmistakable proof to make us believe you. We will not abandon our gods because of what you are saying without evidence. We do not believe your claim that you are a messenger.

Points of Benefit:

The prophets are not able to intercede for the one who disbelieves, even if it is their own children.
The caller who abstains and avoids what people possess is more likely to be accepted by him.
There is great virtue in asking for forgiveness and turning to Allah, which are reasons for rain being sent down and for an increase in descendants and wealth.
