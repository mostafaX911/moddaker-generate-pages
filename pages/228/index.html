(٥٤) إِن نَّقُولُ إِلَّا ٱعۡتَرَىٰكَ بَعۡضُ ءَالِهَتِنَا بِسُوٓءٖۗ قَالَ إِنِّيٓ أُشۡهِدُ ٱللَّهَ وَٱشۡهَدُوٓاْ أَنِّي بَرِيٓءٞ مِّمَّا تُشۡرِكُونَ

Hūd's people said to him that their gods had inflicted him with madness because he had told them not to worship them. So Hūd said: I call upon Allah to bear witness and that you too should bear witness, that I am innocent of worshipping any gods you worship besides Allah. So, go ahead and plot against me with your gods, which you claim have inflicted me with madness, and do not give me any respite.

(٥٥) مِن دُونِهِۦۖ فَكِيدُونِي جَمِيعٗا ثُمَّ لَا تُنظِرُونِ

Hūd's people said to him that their gods had inflicted him with madness because he had told them not to worship them. So Hūd said: I call upon Allah to bear witness and that you too should bear witness, that I am innocent of worshipping any gods you worship besides Allah. So, go ahead and plot against me with your gods, which you claim have inflicted me with madness, and do not give me any respite.

(٥٦) إِنِّي تَوَكَّلۡتُ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ رَبِّي وَرَبِّكُمۚ مَّا مِن دَآبَّةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ ءَاخِذُۢ بِنَاصِيَتِهَآۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

I trust in Allah and rely upon Him alone in my affairs because He is my Lord and yours. Nothing walks on the Earth except that it is under His power and control, and He deals with it as He wills. My Lord is upon truth and justice and will not give you power over me since I stand for the truth and you stand for falsehood.

(٥٧) فَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَقَدۡ أَبۡلَغۡتُكُم مَّآ أُرۡسِلۡتُ بِهِۦٓ إِلَيۡكُمۡۚ وَيَسۡتَخۡلِفُ رَبِّي قَوۡمًا غَيۡرَكُمۡ وَلَا تَضُرُّونَهُۥ شَيۡ‍ًٔاۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٍ حَفِيظٞ

Should you continue to turn away from what I brought you, I have no responsibility to do more than convey the message. I have conveyed everything Allah has sent me and instructed me to convey. The proof has been established against you. My Lord will destroy you and bring other people to succeed you. You will not harm Allah in the slightest by rejecting and turning away because He does not need His creation. My Lord watches over everything and protects me from the evil you plot against me.

(٥٨) وَلَمَّا جَآءَ أَمۡرُنَا نَجَّيۡنَا هُودٗا وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَعَهُۥ بِرَحۡمَةٖ مِّنَّا وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُم مِّنۡ عَذَابٍ غَلِيظٖ

When Our command came to destroy them, We saved Hūd and those who believed in him as a mercy from Us, and We saved them from a stern punishment given to the disbelievers among his people.

(٥٩) وَتِلۡكَ عَادٞۖ جَحَدُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَعَصَوۡاْ رُسُلَهُۥ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓاْ أَمۡرَ كُلِّ جَبَّارٍ عَنِيدٖ

Such were the people of 'Ād. They disbelieved in the signs of Allah, their Lord, and went against His Messenger Hūd. They followed the instruction of every arrogant tyrant against the truth, not accepting it or dedicating themselves to it.

(٦٠) وَأُتۡبِعُواْ فِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لَعۡنَةٗ وَيَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۗ أَلَآ إِنَّ عَادٗا كَفَرُواْ رَبَّهُمۡۗ أَلَا بُعۡدٗا لِّعَادٖ قَوۡمِ هُودٖ

They were then cursed with humiliation and removed from Allah's mercy in this world. On the Day of Resurrection, they will also be without Allah's mercy. Due to their disbelief in Allah, the Exalted, He distances them from every good and allows them to draw closer to every evil.

(٦١) ۞وَإِلَىٰ ثَمُودَ أَخَاهُمۡ صَٰلِحٗاۚ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥۖ هُوَ أَنشَأَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱسۡتَعۡمَرَكُمۡ فِيهَا فَٱسۡتَغۡفِرُوهُ ثُمَّ تُوبُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي قَرِيبٞ مُّجِيبٞ

We sent to the people of Thamūd their brother Saleh. He said: O my people worship Allah alone; you have no god worthy of worship other than Him. He created you from the Earth's soil when He created their father Adam from it and made them settle in it. He told them to ask for forgiveness from Him and that his Lord was Near to those who devoted themselves to worshipping Him, responding to their supplications.

(٦٢) قَالُواْ يَٰصَٰلِحُ قَدۡ كُنتَ فِينَا مَرۡجُوّٗا قَبۡلَ هَٰذَآۖ أَتَنۡهَىٰنَآ أَن نَّعۡبُدَ مَا يَعۡبُدُ ءَابَآؤُنَا وَإِنَّنَا لَفِي شَكّٖ مِّمَّا تَدۡعُونَآ إِلَيۡهِ مُرِيبٖ

Saleh's people told him: O Saleh, you had a high position among us before this, and we had hoped you would be wise, a person of advice and sound counsel. Do you prevent us from worshipping what our forefathers used to worship? Indeed, we have doubts about the worship of Allah alone that you are calling us to; we even suspect you are making things up about Allah.

Points of Benefit:

One way the idolaters turn people away from the messengers is by accusing them of having low intellect and insanity.
The weakness of the plot of the idolaters and their hostility. They are submissive to Allah and subjugated to His command.
Proofs of Allah's Lordship through creating, which necessitates worshipping Allah alone and leaving all else besides Allah.
