(٦٣) قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِن كُنتُ عَلَىٰ بَيِّنَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّي وَءَاتَىٰنِي مِنۡهُ رَحۡمَةٗ فَمَن يَنصُرُنِي مِنَ ٱللَّهِ إِنۡ عَصَيۡتُهُۥۖ فَمَا تَزِيدُونَنِي غَيۡرَ تَخۡسِيرٖ

In reply, Saleh asked his people: O my people, tell me if I am upon a clear proof from my Lord, and He has given me mercy, which is prophethood, who would protect me from His punishment if I disobeyed Allah by not delivering what He instructed me to convey to you? You would only increase me in error, distancing me from Allah's pleasure.

(٦٤) وَيَٰقَوۡمِ هَٰذِهِۦ نَاقَةُ ٱللَّهِ لَكُمۡ ءَايَةٗۖ فَذَرُوهَا تَأۡكُلۡ فِيٓ أَرۡضِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَلَا تَمَسُّوهَا بِسُوٓءٖ فَيَأۡخُذَكُمۡ عَذَابٞ قَرِيبٞ

O my people, this she-camel of Allah is a sign of my truthfulness. So please leave it to graze on Allah's land and do not harm it; else punishment will come to you as soon as you cause it harm.

(٦٥) فَعَقَرُوهَا فَقَالَ تَمَتَّعُواْ فِي دَارِكُمۡ ثَلَٰثَةَ أَيَّامٖۖ ذَٰلِكَ وَعۡدٌ غَيۡرُ مَكۡذُوبٖ

They slaughtered it, further increasing their denial. So Saleh told them: Take joy in your land for the next three days. After that, the punishment of Allah will come to you, and the coming of His punishment was a real threat that could not be denied, but rather, it was a truthful promise.

(٦٦) فَلَمَّا جَآءَ أَمۡرُنَا نَجَّيۡنَا صَٰلِحٗا وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَعَهُۥ بِرَحۡمَةٖ مِّنَّا وَمِنۡ خِزۡيِ يَوۡمِئِذٍۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ ٱلۡقَوِيُّ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ

When Our command came to destroy them, We saved Saleh and those who believed with him out of mercy from Us, and We saved them from the disgrace and humiliation of that day. Therefore, O Messenger, without doubt, your Lord is the most powerful, the Mighty Whom no one can overcome. Consequently, He destroyed the nations that disbelieved.

(٦٧) وَأَخَذَ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ ٱلصَّيۡحَةُ فَأَصۡبَحُواْ فِي دِيَٰرِهِمۡ جَٰثِمِينَ

The great scream overtook the people of Thamūd, so they perished from its sheer intensity. When morning time came, they lay face down on the ground.

(٦٨) كَأَن لَّمۡ يَغۡنَوۡاْ فِيهَآۗ أَلَآ إِنَّ ثَمُودَاْ كَفَرُواْ رَبَّهُمۡۗ أَلَا بُعۡدٗا لِّثَمُودَ

It was as if they had never lived in their land in comfort and ease. Indeed, Thamūd disbelieved in Allah, their Lord, so they remained far removed from His mercy.

(٦٩) وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَتۡ رُسُلُنَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ بِٱلۡبُشۡرَىٰ قَالُواْ سَلَٰمٗاۖ قَالَ سَلَٰمٞۖ فَمَا لَبِثَ أَن جَآءَ بِعِجۡلٍ حَنِيذٖ

Then the angels came to Abraham (peace be upon him) in the form of men, giving good news to him and his wife of Isaac and Jacob. The angels said: Peace, so he said: Peace. Then he quickly brought them a roasted calf to eat, thinking they were men.

(٧٠) فَلَمَّا رَءَآ أَيۡدِيَهُمۡ لَا تَصِلُ إِلَيۡهِ نَكِرَهُمۡ وَأَوۡجَسَ مِنۡهُمۡ خِيفَةٗۚ قَالُواْ لَا تَخَفۡ إِنَّآ أُرۡسِلۡنَآ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِ لُوطٖ

But when Abraham saw that their hands were not reaching for the calf and that they were not eating it, he was confused and frightened by them. When the angels saw his fear of them, they told him not to be afraid and that Allah had sent them to the people of Lot to punish them.

(٧١) وَٱمۡرَأَتُهُۥ قَآئِمَةٞ فَضَحِكَتۡ فَبَشَّرۡنَٰهَا بِإِسۡحَٰقَ وَمِن وَرَآءِ إِسۡحَٰقَ يَعۡقُوبَ

Abraham's wife, Sarah, was standing and laughed in amazement at what she heard. Then, they told her something that greatly pleased her: that she would bear Isaac and that Isaac would have Jacob.

Points of Benefit:

The arrogance and stubbornness of the idolaters who did not believe in the sign of Saleh (peace be upon him), which was one of the most significant signs.
It is highly desirable to give the believers news that brings them joy.
It is permissible to say 'Peace' when entering someone's house and for the hosts to reply in the same way.
The permissibility of honoring the guest.
