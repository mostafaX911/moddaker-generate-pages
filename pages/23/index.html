(١٤٦) ٱلَّذِينَ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ يَعۡرِفُونَهُۥ كَمَا يَعۡرِفُونَ أَبۡنَآءَهُمۡۖ وَإِنَّ فَرِيقٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ لَيَكۡتُمُونَ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُونَ

Those who have been given the book from the scholars of the Jews and the Christians know that changing the prayer direction is one of the signs of Muhammad's صلى الله عليه وسلم prophethood with them. They recognize this as well as they recognize their own children and can distinguish them from others. Despite this, a group of them hide the truth he brought due to their envy. They do that even though they know that it is the truth.

(١٤٧) ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكَ فَلَا تَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمُمۡتَرِينَ

This is the truth from your Lord. So, O Messenger, do not be from those who doubt its validity.

(١٤٨) وَلِكُلّٖ وِجۡهَةٌ هُوَ مُوَلِّيهَاۖ فَٱسۡتَبِقُواْ ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِۚ أَيۡنَ مَا تَكُونُواْ يَأۡتِ بِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ جَمِيعًاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

Every nation has somewhere to turn to, either physically or spiritually. Part of that is that different nations have different prayer directions and different legislations from Allah. Their various directions cause no harm if they are due to the commands and legislation of Allah. So, O believers, rush to do the good deeds that you have been ordered to do. Allah will gather you all together on the Day of Resurrection from wherever you may be to recompense your deeds. Allah is indeed the Most Capable of everything; He is not incapable of gathering you and recompensing you.

(١٤٩) وَمِنۡ حَيۡثُ خَرَجۡتَ فَوَلِّ وَجۡهَكَ شَطۡرَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِۖ وَإِنَّهُۥ لَلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكَۗ وَمَا ٱللَّهُ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Wherever you leave from and wherever you are, O Prophet, you and your followers are to face towards the Sacred Mosque (the Kaaba in Mecca) when you want to pray. It is the truth that has been revealed to you by your Lord, for Allah is not unaware of what you do. Rather, He is Well-Acquainted with all you do and will recompense you accordingly.

(١٥٠) وَمِنۡ حَيۡثُ خَرَجۡتَ فَوَلِّ وَجۡهَكَ شَطۡرَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِۚ وَحَيۡثُ مَا كُنتُمۡ فَوَلُّواْ وُجُوهَكُمۡ شَطۡرَهُۥ لِئَلَّا يَكُونَ لِلنَّاسِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ حُجَّةٌ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ فَلَا تَخۡشَوۡهُمۡ وَٱخۡشَوۡنِي وَلِأُتِمَّ نِعۡمَتِي عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَهۡتَدُونَ

Wherever you leave from, O Prophet, when you want to pray, turn towards the direction of the Sacred Mosque; wherever you may be, O believers, turn your faces towards it if you want to pray. This is so that people have no argument or proof to use against you, except those who are unjust from amongst them; they will remain stubborn, arguing against you with the weakest of arguments. Do not fear them, but rather fear your Lord alone, following His commands and avoiding what He has prohibited. Allah prescribed you to face the Kaaba to perfect His blessings upon you, distinguishing you from all other nations and guiding you to the most honorable direction of prayer for people.

(١٥١) كَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا فِيكُمۡ رَسُولٗا مِّنكُمۡ يَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا وَيُزَكِّيكُمۡ وَيُعَلِّمُكُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَيُعَلِّمُكُم مَّا لَمۡ تَكُونُواْ تَعۡلَمُونَ

Likewise, we gave you another blessing by sending a messenger from among you. He recites Our verses unto you and purifies you by ordering you with virtuous and good deeds and prohibiting you from evil and wrong actions. He teaches you the Quran and Sunnah and teaches you what you did not know before about your religious and worldly lives.

(١٥٢) فَٱذۡكُرُونِيٓ أَذۡكُرۡكُمۡ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لِي وَلَا تَكۡفُرُونِ

So, remember Me with your hearts and limbs, and I will remember you with praise and protection because the recompense for deeds matches the nature of the deeds. Also, show gratitude to Me for The favors I have given you, and do not show ingratitude by denying them or using them in prohibited ways.

(١٥٣) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱسۡتَعِينُواْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ وَٱلصَّلَوٰةِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

O you who believe, seek assistance through patience and prayer in carrying out My obedience and submission to My instructions. Allah is with those who are patient. He guides and assists them.

Points of Benefit:

Changing the qibla (direction of prayer) was mentioned at length due to it being evidence for the prophethood of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم.
Abandoning argumentation, busying oneself with acts of obedience, and hastening to Allah are considered more beneficial for the believers by their Lord on the Day of Resurrection.
That the righteous actions to reach Allah are varied and numerous. The believer should race to perform them, seeking reward from Allah, the Exalted.
The magnitude of making remembrance of Allah (the Revered and Exalted) as its reward is the slave being mentioned amongst the highest assembly [of angels].
