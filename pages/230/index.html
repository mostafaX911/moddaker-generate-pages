(٧٢) قَالَتۡ يَٰوَيۡلَتَىٰٓ ءَأَلِدُ وَأَنَا۠ عَجُوزٞ وَهَٰذَا بَعۡلِي شَيۡخًاۖ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَشَيۡءٌ عَجِيبٞ

When the angels gave Sarah this good news, she asked: How can I have a child when I am an old woman with no hope of having children, and my husband is elderly?! Having a child at such an age is extraordinary and not normal at all.

(٧٣) قَالُوٓاْ أَتَعۡجَبِينَ مِنۡ أَمۡرِ ٱللَّهِۖ رَحۡمَتُ ٱللَّهِ وَبَرَكَٰتُهُۥ عَلَيۡكُمۡ أَهۡلَ ٱلۡبَيۡتِۚ إِنَّهُۥ حَمِيدٞ مَّجِيدٞ

When Sarah was amazed at the news, the angels asked her: Are you amazed by Allah's decree and command? People like you know that Allah can do such things. Allah's mercy and blessings be upon you, O people of the house of Abraham! Allah is Praiseworthy in His attributes and actions, the Possessor of Glory and Grandeur.

(٧٤) فَلَمَّا ذَهَبَ عَنۡ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ ٱلرَّوۡعُ وَجَآءَتۡهُ ٱلۡبُشۡرَىٰ يُجَٰدِلُنَا فِي قَوۡمِ لُوطٍ

Once the fear had abated from Abraham (peace be upon him) that was brought on by his guests not eating his food and the discovery that the guests were angels, then, receiving the joyous news that he would have Isaac, who would have Jacob, he started to debate with Our messengers about the people of Lot, asking that the punishment be delayed and that Lot and his family be saved.

(٧٥) إِنَّ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ لَحَلِيمٌ أَوَّٰهٞ مُّنِيبٞ

Abraham was forbearing by wanting to delay the punishment, often praying to his Lord, calling upon Him, and turning to Him.

(٧٦) يَٰٓإِبۡرَٰهِيمُ أَعۡرِضۡ عَنۡ هَٰذَآۖ إِنَّهُۥ قَدۡ جَآءَ أَمۡرُ رَبِّكَۖ وَإِنَّهُمۡ ءَاتِيهِمۡ عَذَابٌ غَيۡرُ مَرۡدُودٖ

The angels said: Turn away from this discussion, O Abraham, about the people of Lot. The command of your Lord has come. They will receive the punishment He has decreed for them. A great punishment is coming to the people of Lot, which no debate or supplication can prevent.

(٧٧) وَلَمَّا جَآءَتۡ رُسُلُنَا لُوطٗا سِيٓءَ بِهِمۡ وَضَاقَ بِهِمۡ ذَرۡعٗا وَقَالَ هَٰذَا يَوۡمٌ عَصِيبٞ

When the angels came to Lot in the form of humans, he was distressed by their coming to him, and he became anxious and feared for their well-being because his people would approach men with desires instead of women. So Lot said it was a terrible day because he thought his people would force themselves on his guests.

(٧٨) وَجَآءَهُۥ قَوۡمُهُۥ يُهۡرَعُونَ إِلَيۡهِ وَمِن قَبۡلُ كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِۚ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ بَنَاتِي هُنَّ أَطۡهَرُ لَكُمۡۖ فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَلَا تُخۡزُونِ فِي ضَيۡفِيٓۖ أَلَيۡسَ مِنكُمۡ رَجُلٞ رَّشِيدٞ

The people of Lot came rushing to him, intending to commit lewd acts with his guests. They were used to going to men with desire instead of women. To put his people off his guests, Lot told them: These are my daughters; you can marry them because they are purer for you than the evil acts you are trying to commit. So fear Allah, and do not disgrace me concerning my guests. Is there not a single right-minded man among you who can tell you not to commit such a shameful act?

(٧٩) قَالُواْ لَقَدۡ عَلِمۡتَ مَا لَنَا فِي بَنَاتِكَ مِنۡ حَقّٖ وَإِنَّكَ لَتَعۡلَمُ مَا نُرِيدُ

His people told him: You know we have no need or desire for your daughters or the women among your people. You know very well what we want; we desire men only.

(٨٠) قَالَ لَوۡ أَنَّ لِي بِكُمۡ قُوَّةً أَوۡ ءَاوِيٓ إِلَىٰ رُكۡنٖ شَدِيدٖ

Lot said: If only I had the strength to use against you or people to support me, I would have come between you and my guests.

(٨١) قَالُواْ يَٰلُوطُ إِنَّا رُسُلُ رَبِّكَ لَن يَصِلُوٓاْ إِلَيۡكَۖ فَأَسۡرِ بِأَهۡلِكَ بِقِطۡعٖ مِّنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَلَا يَلۡتَفِتۡ مِنكُمۡ أَحَدٌ إِلَّا ٱمۡرَأَتَكَۖ إِنَّهُۥ مُصِيبُهَا مَآ أَصَابَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ مَوۡعِدَهُمُ ٱلصُّبۡحُۚ أَلَيۡسَ ٱلصُّبۡحُ بِقَرِيبٖ

The angels said to Lot (peace be upon him): O Lot, we are messengers sent by Allah, and your people will not be able to touch you with their evil. Leave town tonight, when it is dark, with your family. Let no one turn to look at what is behind them except for your wife, who will turn back wrongly because the punishment that will befall your people will also come upon her. The appointed time for their destruction will be the morning, which is very near.

Points of Benefit:

The virtue and status of the family of Abraham are clearly demonstrated.
The legitimacy of debating with those who have the potential to believe before their case is decided by Allah.
The story also shows the despicable and shamefulness of the actions of the people of Lot.
