(٨٢) فَلَمَّا جَآءَ أَمۡرُنَا جَعَلۡنَا عَٰلِيَهَا سَافِلَهَا وَأَمۡطَرۡنَا عَلَيۡهَا حِجَارَةٗ مِّن سِجِّيلٖ مَّنضُودٖ

When Our command to destroy the people of Lot came, We turned their towns upside down by raising and turning them over together with the people. We rained down on them stones of hard-layered baked clay that came cascading down.

(٨٣) مُّسَوَّمَةً عِندَ رَبِّكَۖ وَمَا هِيَ مِنَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ بِبَعِيدٖ

These stones were sent by Allah and came with specific markings, and they are not too far away from the oppressors of Quraysh. Rather, they are near and will come down on them when Allah decrees it.

(٨٤) ۞وَإِلَىٰ مَدۡيَنَ أَخَاهُمۡ شُعَيۡبٗاۚ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥۖ وَلَا تَنقُصُواْ ٱلۡمِكۡيَالَ وَٱلۡمِيزَانَۖ إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰكُم بِخَيۡرٖ وَإِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ عَذَابَ يَوۡمٖ مُّحِيطٖ

We sent to Midian their brother, Shuayb, who said: O my people, worship Allah alone. You have no other deity that deserves to be worshipped besides Him. Do not give people short measure or weight when you deal with them. I see you in prosperity and with blessings, so do not change Allah's favor to you by committing sins. I fear for you the punishment of an encompassing day that will reach every one of you and from which you will not find any place to run to or seek refuge in.

(٨٥) وَيَٰقَوۡمِ أَوۡفُواْ ٱلۡمِكۡيَالَ وَٱلۡمِيزَانَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۖ وَلَا تَبۡخَسُواْ ٱلنَّاسَ أَشۡيَآءَهُمۡ وَلَا تَعۡثَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مُفۡسِدِينَ

He said: O my people, give complete and just measure and weight when you measure and weigh for others. Do not cheat people of their rights by giving short measures and deceiving them. Do not spread corruption on Earth by killing and committing other sins.

(٨٦) بَقِيَّتُ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَۚ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ عَلَيۡكُم بِحَفِيظٖ

[He further said] The lawful that Allah leaves for you after you give people their rights in full and with fairness is of more benefit and contains more blessing than the extra obtained through using short measures and spreading corruption on Earth. If you are indeed believers, then be content with what remains. It is not my duty to watch over you, record your actions, and hold you to account for them. The One who watches over you is the One Who knows all secrets and private conversations.

(٨٧) قَالُواْ يَٰشُعَيۡبُ أَصَلَوٰتُكَ تَأۡمُرُكَ أَن نَّتۡرُكَ مَا يَعۡبُدُ ءَابَآؤُنَآ أَوۡ أَن نَّفۡعَلَ فِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِنَا مَا نَشَٰٓؤُاْۖ إِنَّكَ لَأَنتَ ٱلۡحَلِيمُ ٱلرَّشِيدُ

The people of Shuayb said to him: O Shuayb, does your prayer that you pray to Allah instruct you [to tell us] to leave the worship of the idols our fathers used to worship? Does it instruct you [to tell us] to stop dealing and investing with our wealth as we wish? You are indeed a sensible and wise person. We knew you as someone with intellect and wisdom before this call [you are making]. What happened to you?

(٨٨) قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِن كُنتُ عَلَىٰ بَيِّنَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّي وَرَزَقَنِي مِنۡهُ رِزۡقًا حَسَنٗاۚ وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَنۡ أُخَالِفَكُمۡ إِلَىٰ مَآ أَنۡهَىٰكُمۡ عَنۡهُۚ إِنۡ أُرِيدُ إِلَّا ٱلۡإِصۡلَٰحَ مَا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُۚ وَمَا تَوۡفِيقِيٓ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ عَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡتُ وَإِلَيۡهِ أُنِيبُ

Shuayb said to his people: O my people, tell me what will be your condition if I am on clear proof and insight from my Lord, and He has given me lawful provision from Him, as well as prophethood? I do not wish to prohibit you from something and go against you and then do it myself. I just want to set your matter right, to the best of my ability, by inviting you to declare your Lord's Oneness and obey Him. My success in achieving this is only with Allah; may He be Exalted! Therefore, I trust Him alone and turn to Him in all my affairs.

Points of Benefit:

It is from Allah's divine practice to destroy the wrongdoers with the most horrific and severe punishment.
Prohibition of shortchanging in measurement or weight and giving people less than their due right.
The obligation of being satisfied with what is lawful, even if it is not a lot.
The verses indicate the virtue of enjoining good and forbidding evil and the obligation to implement what Allah instructs and abstain from what He prohibits.
