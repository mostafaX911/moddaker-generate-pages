(٨٩) وَيَٰقَوۡمِ لَا يَجۡرِمَنَّكُمۡ شِقَاقِيٓ أَن يُصِيبَكُم مِّثۡلُ مَآ أَصَابَ قَوۡمَ نُوحٍ أَوۡ قَوۡمَ هُودٍ أَوۡ قَوۡمَ صَٰلِحٖۚ وَمَا قَوۡمُ لُوطٖ مِّنكُم بِبَعِيدٖ

[He continued] O my people, do not let your hatred for me make you deny what I have brought. [On the contrary, you should] be afraid that you will suffer like the people of Noah, Hūd, or Saleh. The people of Lot are not far from you in terms of time or place. You know what happened to them, so take heed.

(٩٠) وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ ثُمَّ تُوبُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي رَحِيمٞ وَدُودٞ

Seek forgiveness from your Lord. Then repent to Him from your sins. My Lord is Compassionate to those who repent and is Most Loving to those who turn to Him.

(٩١) قَالُواْ يَٰشُعَيۡبُ مَا نَفۡقَهُ كَثِيرٗا مِّمَّا تَقُولُ وَإِنَّا لَنَرَىٰكَ فِينَا ضَعِيفٗاۖ وَلَوۡلَا رَهۡطُكَ لَرَجَمۡنَٰكَۖ وَمَآ أَنتَ عَلَيۡنَا بِعَزِيزٖ

The people of Shuayb said to him: O Shuayb, we do not understand much of what you have brought. We regard you as weak and not one of the notables or leaders. If it were not for your clan following our religious beliefs, we would have stoned you to death. You are not mighty enough for us to be afraid of killing you. The only reason we have not killed you is out of respect for your clan.

(٩٢) قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ أَرَهۡطِيٓ أَعَزُّ عَلَيۡكُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَٱتَّخَذۡتُمُوهُ وَرَآءَكُمۡ ظِهۡرِيًّاۖ إِنَّ رَبِّي بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ مُحِيطٞ

Shuayb told his people: O my people, is my clan more respectable and honorable to you than Allah, your Lord? You rejected Allah when you did not believe in the Prophet He sent to you. My Lord knows well what you do. No action of yours is hidden from Him. He will recompense you this life with destruction and in the Afterlife with punishment.

(٩٣) وَيَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡمَلُواْ عَلَىٰ مَكَانَتِكُمۡ إِنِّي عَٰمِلٞۖ سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ مَن يَأۡتِيهِ عَذَابٞ يُخۡزِيهِ وَمَنۡ هُوَ كَٰذِبٞۖ وَٱرۡتَقِبُوٓاْ إِنِّي مَعَكُمۡ رَقِيبٞ

O my people, do what you can in the manner you wish, and I, too, will do what I can in the manner I wish. Soon, you will know which of us will receive a humiliating punishment and who is lying in their claim. So wait for Allah's decision, and I will also wait with you.

(٩٤) وَلَمَّا جَآءَ أَمۡرُنَا نَجَّيۡنَا شُعَيۡبٗا وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَعَهُۥ بِرَحۡمَةٖ مِّنَّا وَأَخَذَتِ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ ٱلصَّيۡحَةُ فَأَصۡبَحُواْ فِي دِيَٰرِهِمۡ جَٰثِمِينَ

When Our command to destroy the people of Shuayb came, We rescued Shuayb and the people of faith with him through mercy from Us. However, those of his people who did wrong were afflicted with the intense sound and died. In the morning, they were found lying on the ground with their faces in the dust.

(٩٥) كَأَن لَّمۡ يَغۡنَوۡاْ فِيهَآۗ أَلَا بُعۡدٗا لِّمَدۡيَنَ كَمَا بَعِدَتۡ ثَمُودُ

It was as if they had never resided there previously. Without a doubt, Midian was distanced from Allah's mercy when His punishment fell on them, just as Thamūd was driven away from it by Allah sending down His anger on them.

(٩٦) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مُوسَىٰ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٍ

Indeed, We sent Moses with Our signs that indicated the Oneness of Allah and with clear evidence that proved the truth of what he brought.

(٩٧) إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَمَلَإِيْهِۦ فَٱتَّبَعُوٓاْ أَمۡرَ فِرۡعَوۡنَۖ وَمَآ أَمۡرُ فِرۡعَوۡنَ بِرَشِيدٖ

We sent Moses to Pharaoh and the notable people among them. These notable people followed Pharaoh's command to disbelieve in Allah. The command of Pharaoh was not right for it to be followed.

Points of Benefit:

The condemnation of those ignorant people who do not understand the signs brought by the prophets.
The condemnation and foolishness of those who follow the orders of people while ignoring the orders of Allah.
The verses demonstrate the role of the family in supporting the call and the callers to Allah.
The idolaters are driven away from Allah's mercy.
