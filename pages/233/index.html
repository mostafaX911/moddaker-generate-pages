(٩٨) يَقۡدُمُ قَوۡمَهُۥ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فَأَوۡرَدَهُمُ ٱلنَّارَۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡوِرۡدُ ٱلۡمَوۡرُودُ

Pharoah will precede his people into the Fire on the Day of Resurrection until he enters them into it. What an evil place he will lead them to!

(٩٩) وَأُتۡبِعُواْ فِي هَٰذِهِۦ لَعۡنَةٗ وَيَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۚ بِئۡسَ ٱلرِّفۡدُ ٱلۡمَرۡفُودُ

With the destruction and drowning that afflicted them, Allah followed that with a curse and their being driven away and far-removed from His mercy in this world and on the Day of Resurrection. What an evil thing that happened to them: they became the subject of two successive curses and punishment in this life and the Afterlife!

(١٠٠) ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ أَنۢبَآءِ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ نَقُصُّهُۥ عَلَيۡكَۖ مِنۡهَا قَآئِمٞ وَحَصِيدٞ

We inform you, O Messenger, of the tales of the towns mentioned in this Surah. The landmarks of some of these towns still exist, and the landmarks of others have been wiped out without any trace.

(١٠١) وَمَا ظَلَمۡنَٰهُمۡ وَلَٰكِن ظَلَمُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡۖ فَمَآ أَغۡنَتۡ عَنۡهُمۡ ءَالِهَتُهُمُ ٱلَّتِي يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مِن شَيۡءٖ لَّمَّا جَآءَ أَمۡرُ رَبِّكَۖ وَمَا زَادُوهُمۡ غَيۡرَ تَتۡبِيبٖ

O Messenger, We did not wrong them by afflicting them with destruction. However, they wronged their souls by bringing destruction upon themselves when they disbelieved in Allah. The gods they used to worship besides Allah did not repel the punishment that came down on them when the command of your Lord came for their destruction. On the contrary, these gods of theirs only increased them in loss and destruction.

(١٠٢) وَكَذَٰلِكَ أَخۡذُ رَبِّكَ إِذَآ أَخَذَ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ وَهِيَ ظَٰلِمَةٌۚ إِنَّ أَخۡذَهُۥٓ أَلِيمٞ شَدِيدٌ

Such is the grip and annihilation with which Allah seizes the towns that deny in every time and place [across history]. His grip on such disobedient towns is painful and severe.

(١٠٣) إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّمَنۡ خَافَ عَذَابَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمٞ مَّجۡمُوعٞ لَّهُ ٱلنَّاسُ وَذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمٞ مَّشۡهُودٞ

In Allah's severe punishment of those disobedient towns, there is a lesson and warning for the one who fears the punishment of the Day of Resurrection. Allah will gather people on that day to take them to account, and all people on the plains of the resurrection will witness it.

(١٠٤) وَمَا نُؤَخِّرُهُۥٓ إِلَّا لِأَجَلٖ مَّعۡدُودٖ

We only postpone that witnessed day until a specific time.

(١٠٥) يَوۡمَ يَأۡتِ لَا تَكَلَّمُ نَفۡسٌ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِهِۦۚ فَمِنۡهُمۡ شَقِيّٞ وَسَعِيدٞ

No soul can present any argument or intercession when that Day comes except after He gives permission. People on that Day will be of two types: a wretched person who will enter the Fire and a delighted person who will enter Paradise.

(١٠٦) فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ شَقُواْ فَفِي ٱلنَّارِ لَهُمۡ فِيهَا زَفِيرٞ وَشَهِيقٌ

Because of their disbelief and corrupt actions, those who are wretched will enter the Fire, in which their voices and breath will be raised due to the severity of the blazing Fire they will suffer from.

(١٠٧) خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا مَا دَامَتِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ إِلَّا مَا شَآءَ رَبُّكَۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ فَعَّالٞ لِّمَا يُرِيدُ

They will live in it eternally and not come out of it for as long as the heavens and the Earth last, except for those sinful monotheists whom Allah wills to remove. Your Lord, O Messenger, is the One who does whatever He wishes, and nothing can force Him to do something.

(١٠٨) ۞وَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ سُعِدُواْ فَفِي ٱلۡجَنَّةِ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا مَا دَامَتِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ إِلَّا مَا شَآءَ رَبُّكَۖ عَطَآءً غَيۡرَ مَجۡذُوذٖ

Their delight from Allah preceded those who were delighted because of their faith and good actions. Therefore, they will live in Paradise eternally for as long as the heavens and the Earth last, except for those sinful monotheists whom Allah wills to enter into the Fire for a time. Therefore, the delight Allah will provide for the people of Paradise will never be cut off from them.

Points of Benefit:

The verses warn against following the leaders of evil and corruption and the misfortune that results in both this life and the next because of following them.
Allah the Exalted is free from being accused of injustice when He destroys those who associate partners with Him and commit sin.
The idols worshipped by the polytheists will not benefit their devotees on the Day of Resurrection, nor will they avert the punishment from them.
People will be divided into two groups on the Day of Judgment: the fortunate, who will live eternally in Paradise, and the wretched, who will live eternally in flames.
