(١٠٩) فَلَا تَكُ فِي مِرۡيَةٖ مِّمَّا يَعۡبُدُ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِۚ مَا يَعۡبُدُونَ إِلَّا كَمَا يَعۡبُدُ ءَابَآؤُهُم مِّن قَبۡلُۚ وَإِنَّا لَمُوَفُّوهُمۡ نَصِيبَهُمۡ غَيۡرَ مَنقُوصٖ

So, O Messenger, do not doubt the invalidity of what these polytheists worship. They have no proof based on logic or any proof from the sacred law to justify such worship. The only thing that makes them worship other than Allah is their blind following of their forefathers. Therefore, We will give them their full portion of the punishment without any reduction.

(١١٠) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ فَٱخۡتُلِفَ فِيهِۚ وَلَوۡلَا كَلِمَةٞ سَبَقَتۡ مِن رَّبِّكَ لَقُضِيَ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَفِي شَكّٖ مِّنۡهُ مُرِيبٖ

We had given Moses the Torah, but people disagreed: some believed, while others did not. Had it not been for Allah's prior decree that the punishment would not come quickly but be postponed until the Day of Resurrection for a reason, the punishment they deserve would have come down on them in this world. Instead, the disbelieving Jews and polytheists are in doubt about the Quran, which creates uncertainty in them.

(١١١) وَإِنَّ كُلّٗا لَّمَّا لَيُوَفِّيَنَّهُمۡ رَبُّكَ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِمَا يَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٞ

And for everyone who is mentioned among the dissenters, your Lord desires for them, O Messenger, the appropriate recompense for their deeds. So what was good within the deeds, their reward will be good, and what was evil within them, their reward will be evil. Indeed, Allah is All-Knowing of the most minute aspects of what they do, and nothing from their actions is ever hidden from Him.

(١١٢) فَٱسۡتَقِمۡ كَمَآ أُمِرۡتَ وَمَن تَابَ مَعَكَ وَلَا تَطۡغَوۡاْۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٞ

So remain patient, O Messenger, as Allah has instructed you. Fulfill His instructions and stay away from His prohibitions. Let those believers who have repented with you also remain patient. Do not overstep the limits by committing sins because He sees what you do. No action of yours is hidden from Him, and He will recompense you for what you do.

(١١٣) وَلَا تَرۡكَنُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ فَتَمَسَّكُمُ ٱلنَّارُ وَمَا لَكُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مِنۡ أَوۡلِيَآءَ ثُمَّ لَا تُنصَرُونَ

Do not incline toward the disbelievers who do wrong by being kind to or showing them love, or the Fire will afflict you because of such inclination. You will not have any friends besides Allah to save you from it. Then you will not find anyone to help you.

(١١٤) وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ طَرَفَيِ ٱلنَّهَارِ وَزُلَفٗا مِّنَ ٱلَّيۡلِۚ إِنَّ ٱلۡحَسَنَٰتِ يُذۡهِبۡنَ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِۚ ذَٰلِكَ ذِكۡرَىٰ لِلذَّٰكِرِينَ

Maintain, O Messenger, the prayer in the best manner at both ends of the day. Also, maintain the prayer during the hours of the night. Good actions wipe out minor sins. This is a warning for those who pay attention and a lesson for those who take heed.

(١١٥) وَٱصۡبِرۡ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُضِيعُ أَجۡرَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Be patient in doing what you have been instructed to do concerning being upright and avoiding the things you have been prohibited from, such as transgression and inclining towards the wrongdoers. Allah will not cause the reward of those who do good to be wasted. Instead, He will accept from them the best of what they did and give them their reward according to their best actions.

(١١٦) فَلَوۡلَا كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرُونِ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ أُوْلُواْ بَقِيَّةٖ يَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡفَسَادِ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا مِّمَّنۡ أَنجَيۡنَا مِنۡهُمۡۗ وَٱتَّبَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ مَآ أُتۡرِفُواْ فِيهِ وَكَانُواْ مُجۡرِمِينَ

If only there were, among the communities who were punished before you, remnants of the people of virtue and piety who prohibited those communities from disbelief and spreading corruption throughout the Earth with sins. But there were no such remnants among them, except for a few who used to prohibit corruption and whom We saved when We destroyed their people who did wrong. So, the wrongdoers from their people pursued the luxuries they had and were wrong in doing so.

(١١٧) وَمَا كَانَ رَبُّكَ لِيُهۡلِكَ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ بِظُلۡمٖ وَأَهۡلُهَا مُصۡلِحُونَ

Your Lord, O Messenger, will not destroy any town if its inhabitants act correctly on Earth. He will only ruin it if the inhabitants are causing corruption through disbelief, disobedience, and sins.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation to remain firm on Allah's religion.
The verses warn against opening up towards the disbelievers who do wrong by being kind to them or showing them love.
The verses clarify Allah's established practice of having evil actions wiped out by good actions.
It is encouraged to create a group consisting of people of virtue who instruct good and prohibit corruption and evil, and that they will be protected from the punishment of Allah.
