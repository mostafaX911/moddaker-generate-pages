(١١٨) وَلَوۡ شَآءَ رَبُّكَ لَجَعَلَ ٱلنَّاسَ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗۖ وَلَا يَزَالُونَ مُخۡتَلِفِينَ

If your Lord, O Messenger, wanted to make everyone into one nation upon the truth, He would have done so. However, He did not do that, so they continue to differ because they follow their desires and commit transgressions.

(١١٩) إِلَّا مَن رَّحِمَ رَبُّكَۚ وَلِذَٰلِكَ خَلَقَهُمۡۗ وَتَمَّتۡ كَلِمَةُ رَبِّكَ لَأَمۡلَأَنَّ جَهَنَّمَ مِنَ ٱلۡجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ أَجۡمَعِينَ

Except for those whom Allah shows mercy upon by guiding them. They do not differ concerning His Oneness; may He be Exalted. Allah created them to test them in that disagreement; some wretched, and others delighted. The word of your Lord, O Messenger, in which He decreed to fill Hell with jinn and humans who follow the Devil will be fulfilled.

(١٢٠) وَكُلّٗا نَّقُصُّ عَلَيۡكَ مِنۡ أَنۢبَآءِ ٱلرُّسُلِ مَا نُثَبِّتُ بِهِۦ فُؤَادَكَۚ وَجَآءَكَ فِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَمَوۡعِظَةٞ وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Every incident We relate to you, O Messenger, from the lives of the messengers before you is to make your heart firm on the truth and to strengthen it. In this chapter, the truth, about which there is no doubt, has come to you. There has been a warning for the disbelievers and a reminder for the believers (who benefit from such a reminder).

(١٢١) وَقُل لِّلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱعۡمَلُواْ عَلَىٰ مَكَانَتِكُمۡ إِنَّا عَٰمِلُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to those who do not have faith in Allah and do not accept His Oneness: Act according to your way of ignoring the truth and preventing others from it. We will also act according to our way of remaining firm on the truth, inviting towards it, and being patient with it.

(١٢٢) وَٱنتَظِرُوٓاْ إِنَّا مُنتَظِرُونَ

Wait to see what descends on us. I am also waiting to see what descends on you.

(١٢٣) وَلِلَّهِ غَيۡبُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَإِلَيۡهِ يُرۡجَعُ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ كُلُّهُۥ فَٱعۡبُدۡهُ وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَيۡهِۚ وَمَا رَبُّكَ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ

To Allah alone belongs the knowledge of the unseen in the heavens and the Earth. Nothing is hidden from Him. The entire matter will return to Him alone on the Day of Resurrection. So worship Him alone, O Messenger, and rely upon Him for all your affairs. Your Lord is not unaware of what you do. Instead, He knows about it and will recompense each person for what they did.

Joseph (Surah Yusuf)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Recognizing Allah's kindness in managing His allies and establishing them towards a noble end.

Explanation:

(١) الٓرۚ تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ

Alif Lām Rā – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah. The verses revealed in this surah are from the verses of the Quran, which are clear in their meaning.

(٢) إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ قُرۡءَٰنًا عَرَبِيّٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ

We have revealed the Quran in the language of the Arabs so that you, O Arabs, can understand its meanings.

(٣) نَحۡنُ نَقُصُّ عَلَيۡكَ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلۡقَصَصِ بِمَآ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ وَإِن كُنتَ مِن قَبۡلِهِۦ لَمِنَ ٱلۡغَٰفِلِينَ

O Messenger, by revealing this Quran to you, We narrate the best of stories due to their accuracy, the soundness of their words, and their eloquence. Before its revelation, you were unaware of these stories, without any knowledge of them.

(٤) إِذۡ قَالَ يُوسُفُ لِأَبِيهِ يَٰٓأَبَتِ إِنِّي رَأَيۡتُ أَحَدَ عَشَرَ كَوۡكَبٗا وَٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَ رَأَيۡتُهُمۡ لِي سَٰجِدِينَ

We inform you, O Messenger, about when Joseph told his father, Jacob: O my father, I dreamt of eleven stars and saw the sun and the moon. I saw all of them prostrating to me. This dream was an early sign of good news for Joseph (peace be upon him).

Points of Benefit:

The explanation of the wisdom of Quranic stories is to make the heart of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم firm and to admonish the believers.
Knowledge of the unseen is exclusive to Allah, and no one shares this with Him.
The reason for the Quran being revealed in Arabic was for the Arabs to understand it and convey it to others.
The Quran encompasses the finest of narratives.
