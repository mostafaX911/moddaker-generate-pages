(٥) قَالَ يَٰبُنَيَّ لَا تَقۡصُصۡ رُءۡيَاكَ عَلَىٰٓ إِخۡوَتِكَ فَيَكِيدُواْ لَكَ كَيۡدًاۖ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ عَدُوّٞ مُّبِينٞ

Jacob told his son, Joseph: O my son, do not mention your dream to your brothers because they will understand it and become jealous of you. They will then plot against you because of their envy. The Devil is a clear enemy of man.

(٦) وَكَذَٰلِكَ يَجۡتَبِيكَ رَبُّكَ وَيُعَلِّمُكَ مِن تَأۡوِيلِ ٱلۡأَحَادِيثِ وَيُتِمُّ نِعۡمَتَهُۥ عَلَيۡكَ وَعَلَىٰٓ ءَالِ يَعۡقُوبَ كَمَآ أَتَمَّهَا عَلَىٰٓ أَبَوَيۡكَ مِن قَبۡلُ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَإِسۡحَٰقَۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٞ

Just as you have seen that dream, your Lord will choose you, O Joseph, and teach you the interpretation of dreams. He will perfect His favor on you by giving you prophethood, just as He completed His blessing on your two forefathers before you, Abraham and Isaac. Your Lord knows all about His creation and is the most Wise in His planning.

(٧) ۞لَّقَدۡ كَانَ فِي يُوسُفَ وَإِخۡوَتِهِۦٓ ءَايَٰتٞ لِّلسَّآئِلِينَ

In the story of Joseph and his brothers, there are lessons and admonitions for those who ask about the event.

(٨) إِذۡ قَالُواْ لَيُوسُفُ وَأَخُوهُ أَحَبُّ إِلَىٰٓ أَبِينَا مِنَّا وَنَحۡنُ عُصۡبَةٌ إِنَّ أَبَانَا لَفِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٍ

At that time, the brothers said among themselves: Joseph and his full brother are more beloved to our father than we are, whereas we are a large group. So why does he prefer them over us? We think our father is mistaken in doing so without an apparent reason.

(٩) ٱقۡتُلُواْ يُوسُفَ أَوِ ٱطۡرَحُوهُ أَرۡضٗا يَخۡلُ لَكُمۡ وَجۡهُ أَبِيكُمۡ وَتَكُونُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ قَوۡمٗا صَٰلِحِينَ

[It was further said] Kill Joseph or expel him to a far-off land, and your father's attention will be all yours, and he will love you completely. Then, after killing him and expelling him, you can be righteous when you repent from your sin.

(١٠) قَالَ قَآئِلٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ لَا تَقۡتُلُواْ يُوسُفَ وَأَلۡقُوهُ فِي غَيَٰبَتِ ٱلۡجُبِّ يَلۡتَقِطۡهُ بَعۡضُ ٱلسَّيَّارَةِ إِن كُنتُمۡ فَٰعِلِينَ

One of the brothers said: Do not kill Joseph, rather throw him into a well, and some passers-by may find him and take him; this is a lesser evil if you want to get rid of him.

(١١) قَالُواْ يَٰٓأَبَانَا مَالَكَ لَا تَأۡمَ۬نَّا عَلَىٰ يُوسُفَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَنَٰصِحُونَ

When they agreed to get rid of him, they said to their father, Jacob: O our father, why will you not trust us with Joseph? We have compassion for him and will take care of him against anything that might harm him. We want good for him, so we will protect and look after him until he comes back to you safely. So, what stops you from sending him with us?

(١٢) أَرۡسِلۡهُ مَعَنَا غَدٗا يَرۡتَعۡ وَيَلۡعَبۡ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ

Let us take him with us tomorrow so he can have a good time and play with us, and we will protect him from any harm that may come to him.

(١٣) قَالَ إِنِّي لَيَحۡزُنُنِيٓ أَن تَذۡهَبُواْ بِهِۦ وَأَخَافُ أَن يَأۡكُلَهُ ٱلذِّئۡبُ وَأَنتُمۡ عَنۡهُ غَٰفِلُونَ

Jacob said to his sons: It saddens me that you take him because I cannot bear to be away from him. I also fear that a wolf may eat him when you are not paying attention to him, distracted by your games and idle play.

(١٤) قَالُواْ لَئِنۡ أَكَلَهُ ٱلذِّئۡبُ وَنَحۡنُ عُصۡبَةٌ إِنَّآ إِذٗا لَّخَٰسِرُونَ

They said to their father: If a wolf were to eat Joseph while there are so many of us, then, in such a case, we would have no good left in us. Therefore, we would be losers if we were unable to stop a wolf from eating him.

Points of Benefit:

Dreams are an established legal reality and permissible to be interpreted.
The legitimacy of withholding some facts if revealing them may cause harm.
The verses show the virtue of the children of the family of Abraham and that they were chosen over other people for prophethood.
