(١٥) فَلَمَّا ذَهَبُواْ بِهِۦ وَأَجۡمَعُوٓاْ أَن يَجۡعَلُوهُ فِي غَيَٰبَتِ ٱلۡجُبِّۚ وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِ لَتُنَبِّئَنَّهُم بِأَمۡرِهِمۡ هَٰذَا وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

So Jacob sent Joseph with his brothers. They took him a long distance and were determined to throw him to the bottom of a well. Then, We sent revelation to Joseph saying: You will inform them of this act of theirs at a time when they will not realize that it is you.

(١٦) وَجَآءُوٓ أَبَاهُمۡ عِشَآءٗ يَبۡكُونَ

The brothers of Joseph came to their father in the evening, pretending to cry for him to believe their plot.

(١٧) قَالُواْ يَٰٓأَبَانَآ إِنَّا ذَهَبۡنَا نَسۡتَبِقُ وَتَرَكۡنَا يُوسُفَ عِندَ مَتَٰعِنَا فَأَكَلَهُ ٱلذِّئۡبُۖ وَمَآ أَنتَ بِمُؤۡمِنٖ لَّنَا وَلَوۡ كُنَّا صَٰدِقِينَ

They said: O our father, we went racing with one another and shooting arrows, and we left Joseph to look after our clothes and provisions. Then, a wolf ate him. Will you not believe us, even though we are truthful in what we are saying?

(١٨) وَجَآءُو عَلَىٰ قَمِيصِهِۦ بِدَمٖ كَذِبٖۚ قَالَ بَلۡ سَوَّلَتۡ لَكُمۡ أَنفُسُكُمۡ أَمۡرٗاۖ فَصَبۡرٞ جَمِيلٞۖ وَٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُسۡتَعَانُ عَلَىٰ مَا تَصِفُونَ

They confirmed their story with false evidence. They brought the shirt of Joseph stained with the blood of something else, giving the impression that it was a sign that a wolf had eaten him. Jacob realized that they were lying because the shirt was not torn, and he told them: The matter is not as you have said. Instead, your souls have made an evil action you committed fair-seeming. Therefore, I will exercise beautiful patience without any complaint. Allah is the One from Whom aid is requested against what you mention about Joseph.

(١٩) وَجَآءَتۡ سَيَّارَةٞ فَأَرۡسَلُواْ وَارِدَهُمۡ فَأَدۡلَىٰ دَلۡوَهُۥۖ قَالَ يَٰبُشۡرَىٰ هَٰذَا غُلَٰمٞۚ وَأَسَرُّوهُ بِضَٰعَةٗۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِمَا يَعۡمَلُونَ

A passing caravan came by, and they sent someone to draw water for them. He lowered his bucket into the well, and Joseph caught hold of the rope. When the person who had lowered the bucket saw him, he happily said: Good news! Here is a boy! The water drawer and a few others hid him from the rest of the caravan, regarding him as merchandise they could trade. Allah knew what they were doing with Joseph when they treated him as an item of sale. No action of theirs was hidden from Him.

(٢٠) وَشَرَوۡهُ بِثَمَنِۢ بَخۡسٖ دَرَٰهِمَ مَعۡدُودَةٖ وَكَانُواْ فِيهِ مِنَ ٱلزَّٰهِدِينَ

The water drawer and his companions sold him in Egypt for an insignificant amount of a few silver coins that could be easily counted due to the small quantity. They sold him cheaply because they wanted to get rid of him quickly. Moreover, they realized from his condition that he was not a slave, and they were afraid of his family. Therefore, Allah was Merciful to him by not keeping him with them for very long.

(٢١) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِي ٱشۡتَرَىٰهُ مِن مِّصۡرَ لِٱمۡرَأَتِهِۦٓ أَكۡرِمِي مَثۡوَىٰهُ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَنفَعَنَآ أَوۡ نَتَّخِذَهُۥ وَلَدٗاۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ مَكَّنَّا لِيُوسُفَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلِنُعَلِّمَهُۥ مِن تَأۡوِيلِ ٱلۡأَحَادِيثِۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَالِبٌ عَلَىٰٓ أَمۡرِهِۦ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

The man from Egypt who had purchased him said to his wife: Be good to him and look after him well. He may benefit us by doing what we need, or we may adopt him as a child. Just as We saved Joseph from being killed, removed him from the well, and made the minister's heart soft towards him, We established him in Egypt to teach him the interpretation of dreams. Allah is in complete control of His affair, and His affair will come into effect. No one can compel Him; may He be Exalted, but most people, who are disbelievers, do not know that.

(٢٢) وَلَمَّا بَلَغَ أَشُدَّهُۥٓ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ حُكۡمٗا وَعِلۡمٗاۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

When Joseph reached the height of his spiritual and physical strength, We gave him understanding and knowledge. Just as We gave him this reward, We reward those who do good by worshipping Allah.

Points of Benefit:

Clarification of the danger of envy, which led the brothers of Joseph to plot against him and conspire to kill him.
The legitimacy of acting on circumstantial evidence to establish a legal case.
These verses show that plan Allah had for Joseph (peace be upon him) and His kindness towards him, by placing in the heart of the minister of Egypt the meaning of fatherhood after Satan had come between him and his brothers and what it means to be brothers.
