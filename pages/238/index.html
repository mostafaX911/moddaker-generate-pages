(٢٣) وَرَٰوَدَتۡهُ ٱلَّتِي هُوَ فِي بَيۡتِهَا عَن نَّفۡسِهِۦ وَغَلَّقَتِ ٱلۡأَبۡوَٰبَ وَقَالَتۡ هَيۡتَ لَكَۚ قَالَ مَعَاذَ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهُۥ رَبِّيٓ أَحۡسَنَ مَثۡوَايَۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

The minister's wife tried to seduce Joseph (peace be upon him) by tricking him into committing an indecent act. She shut the doors tight to ensure they were alone and said to him: Come towards me! Joseph said: I seek protection in Allah from what you are calling me to. My master has cared for me during my stay here, and I will never betray him. If I betray him, I will be a wrongdoer, and those who do wrong do not succeed.

(٢٤) وَلَقَدۡ هَمَّتۡ بِهِۦۖ وَهَمَّ بِهَا لَوۡلَآ أَن رَّءَا بُرۡهَٰنَ رَبِّهِۦۚ كَذَٰلِكَ لِنَصۡرِفَ عَنۡهُ ٱلسُّوٓءَ وَٱلۡفَحۡشَآءَۚ إِنَّهُۥ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ

She had desired to commit the indecent act, and the same thing would have crossed his mind. Had he not seen Allah's signs, he would not have been prevented from the act or distanced himself from it. We showed him Our proof to keep him pure from evil and distance him from unlawful sexual relations and betrayal. Joseph was one of Our slaves whom We chose for [conveying] the message and prophethood.

(٢٥) وَٱسۡتَبَقَا ٱلۡبَابَ وَقَدَّتۡ قَمِيصَهُۥ مِن دُبُرٖ وَأَلۡفَيَا سَيِّدَهَا لَدَا ٱلۡبَابِۚ قَالَتۡ مَا جَزَآءُ مَنۡ أَرَادَ بِأَهۡلِكَ سُوٓءًا إِلَّآ أَن يُسۡجَنَ أَوۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

They raced for the door: Joseph to save himself, and she to prevent him from leaving. She caught hold of his shirt to stop him from leaving and tore it from behind. At the door, they found her husband. The minister's wife deceptively said to him: Is not the punishment for the person who intended to commit an indecent act with your wife, O minister, prison, or that he be given a painful punishment?

(٢٦) قَالَ هِيَ رَٰوَدَتۡنِي عَن نَّفۡسِيۚ وَشَهِدَ شَاهِدٞ مِّنۡ أَهۡلِهَآ إِن كَانَ قَمِيصُهُۥ قُدَّ مِن قُبُلٖ فَصَدَقَتۡ وَهُوَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ

Joseph (peace be upon him) said: It was she who tried to get me to commit an indecent act. I did not intend it from her. Allah then made a child from her family speak in the cradle. It testified, saying: If Joseph's shirt is torn from the front, then it indicates that she is truthful because she was trying to stop him from coming on to her, and then he will be lying.

(٢٧) وَإِن كَانَ قَمِيصُهُۥ قُدَّ مِن دُبُرٖ فَكَذَبَتۡ وَهُوَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

But if his shirt is torn from behind, that indicates that he is truthful because she was trying to seduce him while he was running away, and she will be lying.

(٢٨) فَلَمَّا رَءَا قَمِيصَهُۥ قُدَّ مِن دُبُرٖ قَالَ إِنَّهُۥ مِن كَيۡدِكُنَّۖ إِنَّ كَيۡدَكُنَّ عَظِيمٞ

When the minister saw that Joseph's shirt was torn from behind, he realized Joseph was telling the truth. So he said: This accusation you cast on him is part of your deceit, O women. Your deception is undoubtedly strong.

(٢٩) يُوسُفُ أَعۡرِضۡ عَنۡ هَٰذَاۚ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرِي لِذَنۢبِكِۖ إِنَّكِ كُنتِ مِنَ ٱلۡخَاطِ‍ِٔينَ

He told Joseph: O Joseph, ignore this issue and do not mention it to anyone. And you, woman, ask forgiveness for your sin, for you committed a sin by trying to seduce Joseph.

(٣٠) ۞وَقَالَ نِسۡوَةٞ فِي ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ ٱمۡرَأَتُ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ تُرَٰوِدُ فَتَىٰهَا عَن نَّفۡسِهِۦۖ قَدۡ شَغَفَهَا حُبًّاۖ إِنَّا لَنَرَىٰهَا فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ

The news spread in the city, and a group of women disapprovingly said: The minister's wife is trying to seduce her slave. Passion for him has penetrated her heart. Therefore, we think she is misguided in her effort to seduce and have an intense desire for him while he is her slave; this is a clear error [of judgment].

Points of Benefit:

It is wicked to betray the family and wealth of someone who shows you kindness. This was what Joseph mentioned as one of the reasons for his refusal to commit the lewd act.
The verses show the innocence of the prophets and that Allah has protected them from falling into evil and indecency.
The obligation to repel indecency and escape it to rid oneself of it.
The legitimacy of using circumstantial evidence in rulings.
