(٣١) فَلَمَّا سَمِعَتۡ بِمَكۡرِهِنَّ أَرۡسَلَتۡ إِلَيۡهِنَّ وَأَعۡتَدَتۡ لَهُنَّ مُتَّكَ‍ٔٗا وَءَاتَتۡ كُلَّ وَٰحِدَةٖ مِّنۡهُنَّ سِكِّينٗا وَقَالَتِ ٱخۡرُجۡ عَلَيۡهِنَّۖ فَلَمَّا رَأَيۡنَهُۥٓ أَكۡبَرۡنَهُۥ وَقَطَّعۡنَ أَيۡدِيَهُنَّ وَقُلۡنَ حَٰشَ لِلَّهِ مَا هَٰذَا بَشَرًا إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا مَلَكٞ كَرِيمٞ

When the minister's wife heard of their disapproval and gossip about her, she invited them to come and see Joseph so that they would excuse her. She prepared for them a place with cushions and pillows and gave each one of the women a knife to cut food. She then told Joseph (peace be upon him): Go out to them! When they saw him, they were taken aback by his beauty and stunned by his handsomeness. As a result, they cut their hands with knives meant for cutting food and said: Allah is free of faults! This boy is not human. The beauty he possesses is not found in human beings. He can only be one of the noble angels.

(٣٢) قَالَتۡ فَذَٰلِكُنَّ ٱلَّذِي لُمۡتُنَّنِي فِيهِۖ وَلَقَدۡ رَٰوَدتُّهُۥ عَن نَّفۡسِهِۦ فَٱسۡتَعۡصَمَۖ وَلَئِن لَّمۡ يَفۡعَلۡ مَآ ءَامُرُهُۥ لَيُسۡجَنَنَّ وَلَيَكُونٗا مِّنَ ٱلصَّٰغِرِينَ

The minister's wife said to those women when she saw what happened to them: This is the slave whom you blamed me for loving. I desired and tried to entice him, but he remained pure and refused. If he does not, in the future, do what I desire, he will go to prison and will become one of those who are disgraced.

(٣٣) قَالَ رَبِّ ٱلسِّجۡنُ أَحَبُّ إِلَيَّ مِمَّا يَدۡعُونَنِيٓ إِلَيۡهِۖ وَإِلَّا تَصۡرِفۡ عَنِّي كَيۡدَهُنَّ أَصۡبُ إِلَيۡهِنَّ وَأَكُن مِّنَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِينَ

Joseph (peace be upon him) supplicated to his Lord, saying: O my Lord, prison, which they threaten me with, is preferable to me than the shameful act they call me towards. But, if You do not remove their scheme from me, I may incline to them, and I will become one of the ignorant ones if I give in to them and do what they want me to do.

(٣٤) فَٱسۡتَجَابَ لَهُۥ رَبُّهُۥ فَصَرَفَ عَنۡهُ كَيۡدَهُنَّۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

Allah answered his supplication and revealed the scheme of the minister's wife and the city's women. He (may He be Exalted) is Omniscient of Joseph's supplication and the supplication of any who call upon Him. He is Omniscient of Joseph's condition and anyone else.

(٣٥) ثُمَّ بَدَا لَهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا رَأَوُاْ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لَيَسۡجُنُنَّهُۥ حَتَّىٰ حِينٖ

Then, the view of the minister and his people, after they had seen evidence of his innocence, was that they should put him in prison for an unknown period so that the scandal would not be exposed.

(٣٦) وَدَخَلَ مَعَهُ ٱلسِّجۡنَ فَتَيَانِۖ قَالَ أَحَدُهُمَآ إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰنِيٓ أَعۡصِرُ خَمۡرٗاۖ وَقَالَ ٱلۡأٓخَرُ إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰنِيٓ أَحۡمِلُ فَوۡقَ رَأۡسِي خُبۡزٗا تَأۡكُلُ ٱلطَّيۡرُ مِنۡهُۖ نَبِّئۡنَا بِتَأۡوِيلِهِۦٓۖ إِنَّا نَرَىٰكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

They put him in prison, and two youths entered with him. One of these youths told Joseph: I saw in my dream that I was pressing grapes to make wine.The other said: I saw that I was carrying bread on my head, from which birds were eating. Tell us, O Joseph, what is the interpretation of what we saw? We regard you as one of those who do good, so be good to us by interpreting our dreams.

(٣٧) قَالَ لَا يَأۡتِيكُمَا طَعَامٞ تُرۡزَقَانِهِۦٓ إِلَّا نَبَّأۡتُكُمَا بِتَأۡوِيلِهِۦ قَبۡلَ أَن يَأۡتِيَكُمَاۚ ذَٰلِكُمَا مِمَّا عَلَّمَنِي رَبِّيٓۚ إِنِّي تَرَكۡتُ مِلَّةَ قَوۡمٖ لَّا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَهُم بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ كَٰفِرُونَ

Joseph (peace be upon him) said: No food allocated to you by the king or anyone else will come to you, except that I will explain its essence and form before it arrives. The interpretation I give you is what my Lord has taught me. It is not soothsaying or astrology. I have left the religion of a people who do not have faith in Allah and denounce the Afterlife.

Points of Benefit:

A manifestation of the beauty of Joseph (peace be upon him), who was the reason why the women were put to trial.
This verse shows that Joseph (peace be upon him) preferred prison over disobedience to Allah.
Included in the planning of Allah in favor of Joseph (peace be upon him) is that from His kindness towards him, He taught him to interpret dreams, which was the reason he got out of the ordeal of prison.
