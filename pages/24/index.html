(١٥٤) وَلَا تَقُولُواْ لِمَن يُقۡتَلُ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ أَمۡوَٰتُۢۚ بَلۡ أَحۡيَآءٞ وَلَٰكِن لَّا تَشۡعُرُونَ

Do not say, O believers, about those who are killed while fighting in Allah's path: They are dead just as others are; instead, they are alive with their Lord, but you do not realize and understand their life because it is a unique life which can only be understood through revelation from Allah, the Exalted.

(١٥٥) وَلَنَبۡلُوَنَّكُم بِشَيۡءٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡخَوۡفِ وَٱلۡجُوعِ وَنَقۡصٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰلِ وَٱلۡأَنفُسِ وَٱلثَّمَرَٰتِۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

We will surely test you with different types of hardships: some with fear of your enemy; hunger because of lack of food; lack of wealth because of losing it or difficulty in gaining it; loss of lives due to death from diseases and tragedies that kill people, or being martyred in Allah's path; and a lack of produce from the earth. Give good news, O Prophet, to those who are patient in the face of these hardships, of what will make them happy in this world and the Hereafter.

(١٥٦) ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَآ أَصَٰبَتۡهُم مُّصِيبَةٞ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّآ إِلَيۡهِ رَٰجِعُونَ

The patient ones are those who, when they are afflicted by one of these hardships, say with satisfaction and submission: We belong to Allah, He deals with us as He wills, and we will return to Him on the Day of Resurrection, and it is He Who created us and showers us with different favors; so, to Him is our return and end.

(١٥٧) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ صَلَوَٰتٞ مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡ وَرَحۡمَةٞۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُهۡتَدُونَ

Those who possess this quality are praised by Allah in the Highest Assembly of angels, and mercy descends on them. They are the ones guided to the path of truth.

(١٥٨) ۞إِنَّ ٱلصَّفَا وَٱلۡمَرۡوَةَ مِن شَعَآئِرِ ٱللَّهِۖ فَمَنۡ حَجَّ ٱلۡبَيۡتَ أَوِ ٱعۡتَمَرَ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡهِ أَن يَطَّوَّفَ بِهِمَاۚ وَمَن تَطَوَّعَ خَيۡرٗا فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ شَاكِرٌ عَلِيمٌ

The two mountains known as Safa and Marwah near the Kaaba are clear landmarks of the sacred law. So, whoever goes to the Kaaba to perform the ritual of Hajj or Umrah, there is no sin in walking between the two mountains. By clarifying that there was no sin in doing this action, Allah was setting the minds of those Muslims at ease who avoided walking between the mountains because they feared it was an unacceptable action from the Days of Ignorance (i.e., pre-Islam); Allah made it clear that walking between the mountains was from the rituals of Hajj. And whoever sincerely does what is recommended of voluntary acts, Allah is Appreciative, accepts it from them, and will recompense them for it. He is Omniscient of those who do good and deserve to be rewarded.

(١٥٩) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَكۡتُمُونَ مَآ أَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَٱلۡهُدَىٰ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا بَيَّنَّٰهُ لِلنَّاسِ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يَلۡعَنُهُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَيَلۡعَنُهُمُ ٱللَّٰعِنُونَ

Those who hide the clear signs We have revealed that prove the truth of the Prophet and what he brought, from among the Jews and Christians, after We exposed it to the people in their Books, Allah will expel them from His mercy, and the angels, prophets and all of the rest of humanity will supplicate against them to remove them from Allah's mercy.

(١٦٠) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ تَابُواْ وَأَصۡلَحُواْ وَبَيَّنُواْ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَتُوبُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَأَنَا ٱلتَّوَّابُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Except for those who return to Allah in regret for concealing those clear signs, rectifying their hidden and apparent deeds, and clarifying the truth and guidance they concealed, I will accept their return to My obedience. I am Acceptor of repentance from those who repent among My slaves and compassionate towards them.

(١٦١) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَمَاتُواْ وَهُمۡ كُفَّارٌ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ لَعۡنَةُ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ أَجۡمَعِينَ

Those who disbelieve and die in disbelief, not having repented, are cursed by Allah by being removed from His mercy, and the angels and all of humanity supplicate for them to be removed and excluded from Allah's Mercy.

(١٦٢) خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا لَا يُخَفَّفُ عَنۡهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ وَلَا هُمۡ يُنظَرُونَ

They will always be cursed, their torment will not be lightened, not even for a day, and they will not be given any respite on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٦٣) وَإِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهٞ وَٰحِدٞۖ لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

O people, your true God is One and unique in His essence and attributes. There is none worthy of worship except [Allah] the Merciful, the Possessor of expansive mercy. He is Compassionate with His slaves, bestowing them with innumerable favors.

Points of Benefit:

Testing His slaves is one of the established practices of Allah, the Exalted, and He has promised those who are patient upon those tests the greatest reward and most honorable abode.
The legitimacy of Sa'i (going between Safa and Marwa) for those performing Hajj or Umrah.
Keeping the truth that Allah has revealed secret is from the greatest sins and those most severely punished. It also deceives people and directs them away from the guidance that the Messenger brought.
