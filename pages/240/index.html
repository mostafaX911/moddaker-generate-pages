(٣٨) وَٱتَّبَعۡتُ مِلَّةَ ءَابَآءِيٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَإِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَۚ مَا كَانَ لَنَآ أَن نُّشۡرِكَ بِٱللَّهِ مِن شَيۡءٖۚ ذَٰلِكَ مِن فَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡنَا وَعَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَشۡكُرُونَ

I follow the path of my fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob: worshipping Allah alone. It is wrong for us to associate anything with Allah, Who is alone in His Oneness. The monotheism and belief that I and my fathers are upon is from the grace of Allah upon us. It was also from the grace of Allah upon all people since He sent the prophets to them with that message. Yet, most people are not thankful to Allah and are ungrateful for His blessings; instead, they disbelieve in Him.

(٣٩) يَٰصَٰحِبَيِ ٱلسِّجۡنِ ءَأَرۡبَابٞ مُّتَفَرِّقُونَ خَيۡرٌ أَمِ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡوَٰحِدُ ٱلۡقَهَّارُ

Joseph then addressed the two men in the prison: Is worshipping many gods better or worshipping Allah, the One, Who has no partner, the Prevailing over all else, Whom none can prevail over?

(٤٠) مَا تَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَسۡمَآءٗ سَمَّيۡتُمُوهَآ أَنتُمۡ وَءَابَآؤُكُم مَّآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ بِهَا مِن سُلۡطَٰنٍۚ إِنِ ٱلۡحُكۡمُ إِلَّا لِلَّهِ أَمَرَ أَلَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِيَّاهُۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلۡقَيِّمُ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

What you worship besides Allah are names you made up, which you and your ancestors named as gods, while they have no share in divinity, and Allah did not designate or send any proof showing that these names are correct. The command in all creation is Allah's alone and does not belong to these names, which you and your ancestors had come up with. Allah (may He be Exalted) has instructed that you worship Him alone and has forbidden you to associate others with Him. Worshipping Allah alone is the straight path, which has no deviation in it. Most people do not know this; therefore, they associate other things with Allah, worshipping some of what Allah has created.

(٤١) يَٰصَٰحِبَيِ ٱلسِّجۡنِ أَمَّآ أَحَدُكُمَا فَيَسۡقِي رَبَّهُۥ خَمۡرٗاۖ وَأَمَّا ٱلۡأٓخَرُ فَيُصۡلَبُ فَتَأۡكُلُ ٱلطَّيۡرُ مِن رَّأۡسِهِۦۚ قُضِيَ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ ٱلَّذِي فِيهِ تَسۡتَفۡتِيَانِ

Joseph told his two companions in prison that he would interpret their dreams for them. He told them that the one who dreamed he was pressing grapes to make wine would get out of prison, return to work, and serve the king. As for the one who dreamt that he was carrying bread on his head that the birds were eating from, he would be killed and crucified, and the birds would eat from his head. The matter they asked for an opinion on was settled and would come to pass. It was an inevitable reality.

(٤٢) وَقَالَ لِلَّذِي ظَنَّ أَنَّهُۥ نَاجٖ مِّنۡهُمَا ٱذۡكُرۡنِي عِندَ رَبِّكَ فَأَنسَىٰهُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ ذِكۡرَ رَبِّهِۦ فَلَبِثَ فِي ٱلسِّجۡنِ بِضۡعَ سِنِينَ

Joseph asked the one he thought would be saved of the two, the wine bearer of the king: mention my story and situation to the king so that he might release me from prison. But unfortunately, the Devil made the man forget to mention Joseph to the king, so he was imprisoned for several years afterward.

(٤٣) وَقَالَ ٱلۡمَلِكُ إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰ سَبۡعَ بَقَرَٰتٖ سِمَانٖ يَأۡكُلُهُنَّ سَبۡعٌ عِجَافٞ وَسَبۡعَ سُنۢبُلَٰتٍ خُضۡرٖ وَأُخَرَ يَابِسَٰتٖۖ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمَلَأُ أَفۡتُونِي فِي رُءۡيَٰيَ إِن كُنتُمۡ لِلرُّءۡيَا تَعۡبُرُونَ

One day, the king said: I had a dream in which I saw seven fat cows being eaten by seven thin ones, seven green ears of corn, and seven dry ears of corn. O chiefs and noble ones, tell me the meaning of this dream if you know how to interpret dreams.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation to follow the religion of Abraham and free oneself from polytheism and its people.
All gods that are worshipped besides Allah are only names that have no share in divinity.
Taking advantage of special occasions to call to Allah, as Joseph (peace be upon him) did in prison.
