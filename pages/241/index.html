(٤٤) قَالُوٓاْ أَضۡغَٰثُ أَحۡلَٰمٖۖ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِتَأۡوِيلِ ٱلۡأَحۡلَٰمِ بِعَٰلِمِينَ

They said: Your dream is a mixture of visions, and because of this, it can not be interpreted. We are not knowledgeable about interpreting such dreams.

(٤٥) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِي نَجَا مِنۡهُمَا وَٱدَّكَرَ بَعۡدَ أُمَّةٍ أَنَا۠ أُنَبِّئُكُم بِتَأۡوِيلِهِۦ فَأَرۡسِلُونِ

The wine bearer, who was the one saved from the two men who had been imprisoned, then remembered Joseph (peace be upon him) after some time and that he was able to interpret dreams, so he said: I will tell you the interpretation of the king's vision after asking someone who knows how to interpret it. Send me. O King, to Joseph, so that he can interpret your dream.

(٤٦) يُوسُفُ أَيُّهَا ٱلصِّدِّيقُ أَفۡتِنَا فِي سَبۡعِ بَقَرَٰتٖ سِمَانٖ يَأۡكُلُهُنَّ سَبۡعٌ عِجَافٞ وَسَبۡعِ سُنۢبُلَٰتٍ خُضۡرٖ وَأُخَرَ يَابِسَٰتٖ لَّعَلِّيٓ أَرۡجِعُ إِلَى ٱلنَّاسِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُونَ

When the man who had been saved reached Joseph, he asked: O Joseph, the truthful one, inform us of the interpretation of someone dreaming about seven fat cows being eaten by seven thin cows, and seven green ears of corn and seven dry ears of corn, so that I might return to the king and his entourage, and so they can know the interpretation of the king's dream and also know your virtue and rank.

(٤٧) قَالَ تَزۡرَعُونَ سَبۡعَ سِنِينَ دَأَبٗا فَمَا حَصَدتُّمۡ فَذَرُوهُ فِي سُنۢبُلِهِۦٓ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا مِّمَّا تَأۡكُلُونَ

Joseph explained this dream and said: plant well for seven consecutive years. And what you harvest in each of these seven years, you should leave in its ears so that it will not rot, except for the few grains you need to eat.

(٤٨) ثُمَّ يَأۡتِي مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَ سَبۡعٞ شِدَادٞ يَأۡكُلۡنَ مَا قَدَّمۡتُمۡ لَهُنَّ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا مِّمَّا تُحۡصِنُونَ

Then, after these seven fertile years in which they successfully grow food, seven barren years will come, when the people will eat all they had harvested in the fertile years, except for a little of what they had stored, which will be used as seeds for sowing.

(٤٩) ثُمَّ يَأۡتِي مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَ عَامٞ فِيهِ يُغَاثُ ٱلنَّاسُ وَفِيهِ يَعۡصِرُونَ

Then after these years of drought, a year will come in which there will be rainfall, the crops will grow, and the people will press those things that need pressing, such as grapes, olives, and sugar cane.

(٥٠) وَقَالَ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱئۡتُونِي بِهِۦۖ فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُ ٱلرَّسُولُ قَالَ ٱرۡجِعۡ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَسۡ‍َٔلۡهُ مَا بَالُ ٱلنِّسۡوَةِ ٱلَّٰتِي قَطَّعۡنَ أَيۡدِيَهُنَّۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي بِكَيۡدِهِنَّ عَلِيمٞ

When Joseph's explanation of his dream reached him, the king told his slaves: Release Joseph from prison and bring him to me. When the king's messenger came to Joseph, he told him to go back to his master, the king, and ask him about the story of the women who cut their hands so that his innocence would be made clear before he left the prison. Joseph said: My Lord knows how they tried to seduce me, as nothing is hidden from Him.

(٥١) قَالَ مَا خَطۡبُكُنَّ إِذۡ رَٰوَدتُّنَّ يُوسُفَ عَن نَّفۡسِهِۦۚ قُلۡنَ حَٰشَ لِلَّهِ مَا عَلِمۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ مِن سُوٓءٖۚ قَالَتِ ٱمۡرَأَتُ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡـَٰٔنَ حَصۡحَصَ ٱلۡحَقُّ أَنَا۠ رَٰوَدتُّهُۥ عَن نَّفۡسِهِۦ وَإِنَّهُۥ لَمِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

The king, addressing the women, asked what happened when they called Joseph through trickery. Was it to commit immorality with them? The women replied: Allah forbid that Yusuf be accused! By Allah, we do not know any evil from him! Then the wife of the minister confessed what she had done and said: Now the truth has become clear; I tried to seduce him, and he did not try to seduce me. He was truthful in claiming that he was innocent of what I had accused him of.

(٥٢) ذَٰلِكَ لِيَعۡلَمَ أَنِّي لَمۡ أَخُنۡهُ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي كَيۡدَ ٱلۡخَآئِنِينَ

The wife of the minister said: This disclosure was so that Joseph would know through my confession that it was me who had tried to seduce him and that he was truthful and that I had not lied about him in his absence, as it had become clear to me from what had happened that Allah does not give success to those who lie and hatch plans.

Points of Benefit:

From the completeness of Joseph's character is that he referred to the event with the women but not to what happened with the wife of the minister.
From the completeness of Joseph's character (peace be upon him) is that he referred to the event with the women but not to what happened with the wife of the Aziz.
The legitimacy of freeing yourself from any oppression ascribed to you and for the facts to be made clear to establish the truth of the matter.
The virtue of speaking the truth, even if it is against oneself.
