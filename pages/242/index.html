(٥٣) ۞وَمَآ أُبَرِّئُ نَفۡسِيٓۚ إِنَّ ٱلنَّفۡسَ لَأَمَّارَةُۢ بِٱلسُّوٓءِ إِلَّا مَا رَحِمَ رَبِّيٓۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

The minister's wife continued speaking, saying, I am not free of blame for wanting evil, and I was not trying to justify myself; the human self often commands evil. This is because of its inclination towards what it desires and the difficulty of refraining from it, except for those souls whom Allah shows mercy, preventing their egos from commanding them to evil. Allah is Forgiving and Compassionate towards those of His slaves who repent to Him.

(٥٤) وَقَالَ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱئۡتُونِي بِهِۦٓ أَسۡتَخۡلِصۡهُ لِنَفۡسِيۖ فَلَمَّا كَلَّمَهُۥ قَالَ إِنَّكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ لَدَيۡنَا مَكِينٌ أَمِينٞ

The king said when Joseph's innocence became evident and apparent to him: Bring him; I will make him exclusively for me. So they brought Joseph to him, and his knowledge and intellect became clear when Joseph spoke to him. So the king said to him: Verily you, O Joseph, from this day, you are someone of rank, status, and trust.

(٥٥) قَالَ ٱجۡعَلۡنِي عَلَىٰ خَزَآئِنِ ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ إِنِّي حَفِيظٌ عَلِيمٞ

Joseph told the king: Put me in charge of maintaining the storehouses of wealth and provision in the land of Egypt, for I am a trustworthy guardian with knowledge and insight of what I take charge of.

(٥٦) وَكَذَٰلِكَ مَكَّنَّا لِيُوسُفَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَتَبَوَّأُ مِنۡهَا حَيۡثُ يَشَآءُۚ نُصِيبُ بِرَحۡمَتِنَا مَن نَّشَآءُۖ وَلَا نُضِيعُ أَجۡرَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Just as Allah proved Joseph's innocence and released him from prison, He also established him in Egypt so that he could go and live anywhere he wished. So likewise, Allah gives His mercy to whomever of His slaves He wills in this world and does not allow the reward of those who do good to be lost, giving it to them in total and nothing less.

(٥٧) وَلَأَجۡرُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ خَيۡرٞ لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَكَانُواْ يَتَّقُونَ

Allah's reward, which He has prepared in the hereafter, is undoubtedly better than the reward of this worldly life for those who have faith in Allah and who take heed of Him, following what He has commanded and refraining from what He has prohibited.

(٥٨) وَجَآءَ إِخۡوَةُ يُوسُفَ فَدَخَلُواْ عَلَيۡهِ فَعَرَفَهُمۡ وَهُمۡ لَهُۥ مُنكِرُونَ

The brothers of Joseph came to Egypt with their merchandise and entered upon him. He recognized that they were his brothers, but they did not realize that he was their brother. This was because of the time that had passed and how much his appearance had changed. After all, he was only a boy when they threw him in the well.

(٥٩) وَلَمَّا جَهَّزَهُم بِجَهَازِهِمۡ قَالَ ٱئۡتُونِي بِأَخٖ لَّكُم مِّنۡ أَبِيكُمۡۚ أَلَا تَرَوۡنَ أَنِّيٓ أُوفِي ٱلۡكَيۡلَ وَأَنَا۠ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡمُنزِلِينَ

When Joseph had given them the supplies and provisions they asked for, and after they had informed him that they had a brother on their paternal side who they had left behind with their father, Joseph asked them to bring their brother on their paternal side and that he, in return, would give them an extra camel-load of supplies. He asked them if they had yet to see that he had given them full measure, not subtracting from it, and that he was the best of hosts.

(٦٠) فَإِن لَّمۡ تَأۡتُونِي بِهِۦ فَلَا كَيۡلَ لَكُمۡ عِندِي وَلَا تَقۡرَبُونِ

If you do not bring the brother I asked for, you will be clearly lying about your claim that you have a paternal brother. Therefore, I will not give you a measure of provision and do not come close to my country.

(٦١) قَالُواْ سَنُرَٰوِدُ عَنۡهُ أَبَاهُ وَإِنَّا لَفَٰعِلُونَ

So his brothers answered him, saying, we will request him from his father and strive to do so. We will do what you commanded of us without fail.

(٦٢) وَقَالَ لِفِتۡيَٰنِهِ ٱجۡعَلُواْ بِضَٰعَتَهُمۡ فِي رِحَالِهِمۡ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَعۡرِفُونَهَآ إِذَا ٱنقَلَبُوٓاْ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِمۡ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ

Joseph told his slaves to return the merchandise of those people so that they would realize on their return that they did not do trade with it; this would force them to return with their brother to prove their honesty to Joseph so that he would receive their merchandise from them.

(٦٣) فَلَمَّا رَجَعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰٓ أَبِيهِمۡ قَالُواْ يَٰٓأَبَانَا مُنِعَ مِنَّا ٱلۡكَيۡلُ فَأَرۡسِلۡ مَعَنَآ أَخَانَا نَكۡتَلۡ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ

When they returned to their father and told him about the generosity of Joseph to them, they said, O father, the measure of merchandise had been denied to us unless you send our brother with us, so send him with us. If you send him with us, we will be given a measure of provision; we take an oath to you to protect and return him safely.

Points of Benefit:

One of the enemies of the believer is his own self that lies within his two sides; therefore, it is incumbent upon him to watch it carefully and rectify its deviations.
The stipulation of knowledge and trustworthiness for those who assume positions responsible for public affairs.
Clarification that the favors of Allah in the Hereafter are only good, lasting, and better for the people of belief.
It is permissible for a man in a position to seek it and praise himself if necessity calls for it, and he is seeking goodness and righteousness.
