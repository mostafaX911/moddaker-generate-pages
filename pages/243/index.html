(٦٤) قَالَ هَلۡ ءَامَنُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ إِلَّا كَمَآ أَمِنتُكُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَخِيهِ مِن قَبۡلُ فَٱللَّهُ خَيۡرٌ حَٰفِظٗاۖ وَهُوَ أَرۡحَمُ ٱلرَّٰحِمِينَ

Their father responded: Should I entrust you with him except as I entrusted you with Joseph's full brother before? For I had entrusted you with keeping him safe, and you swore to protect him, yet you did not fulfill your oath. I have no confidence in your oath to protect him. Rather, my confidence is in Allah, for He is the best Guardian for those He wants to guard, and He is the Most Merciful to whom He wants to have mercy on.

(٦٥) وَلَمَّا فَتَحُواْ مَتَٰعَهُمۡ وَجَدُواْ بِضَٰعَتَهُمۡ رُدَّتۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡۖ قَالُواْ يَٰٓأَبَانَا مَا نَبۡغِيۖ هَٰذِهِۦ بِضَٰعَتُنَا رُدَّتۡ إِلَيۡنَاۖ وَنَمِيرُ أَهۡلَنَا وَنَحۡفَظُ أَخَانَا وَنَزۡدَادُ كَيۡلَ بَعِيرٖۖ ذَٰلِكَ كَيۡلٞ يَسِيرٞ

And when they opened the baggage containing the food they had brought, they found what they had used to pay for it was also returned to them. So they told their father, what more can we ask of this minister [Joseph] after all of this generosity? The minister returned the price we used to pay for the provision as a show of generosity. So we will get food for our family, protect our brother from what you had feared might happen to him, and get an extra camel-load of merchandise because of our brother accompanying us; after all, an extra camel-load is an easy matter for the minister.

(٦٦) قَالَ لَنۡ أُرۡسِلَهُۥ مَعَكُمۡ حَتَّىٰ تُؤۡتُونِ مَوۡثِقٗا مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ لَتَأۡتُنَّنِي بِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن يُحَاطَ بِكُمۡۖ فَلَمَّآ ءَاتَوۡهُ مَوۡثِقَهُمۡ قَالَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ مَا نَقُولُ وَكِيلٞ

Their father told them: I will not send him with you until you swear an oath by Allah promising to bring him back to me lest you be surrounded by a source of destruction that will encompass you all such that none of you remain and nor can you repel or return from it. So when they gave him the oath of Allah on their promise, he [Jacob] said: Allah is a witness over what we say. His witnessing is sufficient for us.

(٦٧) وَقَالَ يَٰبَنِيَّ لَا تَدۡخُلُواْ مِنۢ بَابٖ وَٰحِدٖ وَٱدۡخُلُواْ مِنۡ أَبۡوَٰبٖ مُّتَفَرِّقَةٖۖ وَمَآ أُغۡنِي عَنكُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ مِن شَيۡءٍۖ إِنِ ٱلۡحُكۡمُ إِلَّا لِلَّهِۖ عَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡتُۖ وَعَلَيۡهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُتَوَكِّلُونَ

And their father advised them saying, do not enter Egypt from one gate all together, but enter from different gates. This would be safer in terms of someone harming all of you if they wished to do so. I do not tell you this to repel harm from you that Allah may have willed for you nor to gain you benefit that Allah had not willed. The decree is none but the decree of Allah, and the matter is none but by His command; upon Him alone I rely in all my affairs, and upon Him alone may the people rely on in their affairs.

(٦٨) وَلَمَّا دَخَلُواْ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ أَمَرَهُمۡ أَبُوهُم مَّا كَانَ يُغۡنِي عَنۡهُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ مِن شَيۡءٍ إِلَّا حَاجَةٗ فِي نَفۡسِ يَعۡقُوبَ قَضَىٰهَاۚ وَإِنَّهُۥ لَذُو عِلۡمٖ لِّمَا عَلَّمۡنَٰهُ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

So they journeyed with Joseph's full brother. When they entered through different gates, as their father had instructed them, it did not stop what Allah had decreed for them in any way. Instead, it was to show Jacob's compassion for his children. He advised them, knowing that the decree is for none but Allah alone. He knew what Allah had taught him regarding faith in the divine decree and taking precautions, but most people do not know this.

(٦٩) وَلَمَّا دَخَلُواْ عَلَىٰ يُوسُفَ ءَاوَىٰٓ إِلَيۡهِ أَخَاهُۖ قَالَ إِنِّيٓ أَنَا۠ أَخُوكَ فَلَا تَبۡتَئِسۡ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

When the brothers of Joseph came before him, together with his full brother, he drew his full brother close to him. He said to him privately that he was his brother Joseph and he should not feel sad about the reckless behavior of his other brothers, hurting and resenting the two of them and throwing Joseph in the well.

Points of Benefit:

Enjoining precaution and vigilance against those who may betray, as it is authentically reported: "The believer is not stung from the same hole twice." [Narrated by Bukhari and Muslim]
Aspects of precaution include ensuring the observance of confirmed covenants by oath and allowing a person who is feared to be entrusted with the safekeeping of trusts.
It is permissible for the one seeking an oath to exempt certain matters that he deems beyond the ability of the one taking the oath.
Taking precautions is a means of averting potential harms.
