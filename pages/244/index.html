(٧٠) فَلَمَّا جَهَّزَهُم بِجَهَازِهِمۡ جَعَلَ ٱلسِّقَايَةَ فِي رَحۡلِ أَخِيهِ ثُمَّ أَذَّنَ مُؤَذِّنٌ أَيَّتُهَا ٱلۡعِيرُ إِنَّكُمۡ لَسَٰرِقُونَ

So when Joseph instructed his slaves to load his brothers' camels with food, he placed the king's golden measuring bowl to measure out provisions into the bag of his full brother without their knowledge; this was to get his brother to stay with him. When they set out to travel back to their family, a caller called out after them, saying, O owners of the camels loaded with provision, you are thieves.

(٧١) قَالُواْ وَأَقۡبَلُواْ عَلَيۡهِم مَّاذَا تَفۡقِدُونَ

Joseph's brothers turned towards the caller and those with him and asked, what has gone missing so that you would accuse us of stealing?

(٧٢) قَالُواْ نَفۡقِدُ صُوَاعَ ٱلۡمَلِكِ وَلِمَن جَآءَ بِهِۦ حِمۡلُ بَعِيرٖ وَأَنَا۠ بِهِۦ زَعِيمٞ

The caller and those with him told Joseph's brothers: The king's golden measuring bowl used to measure provision has gone missing, and whoever produces it before we search will get a reward, a camel load. I am a guarantor for that.

(٧٣) قَالُواْ تَٱللَّهِ لَقَدۡ عَلِمۡتُم مَّا جِئۡنَا لِنُفۡسِدَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا كُنَّا سَٰرِقِينَ

Joseph's brothers answered him, saying: By Allah, as you have seen our situation, you know of our integrity and our innocence of this crime. And we have not come to the land of Egypt to be corrupt therein, and we have never in our lives been thieves.

(٧٤) قَالُواْ فَمَا جَزَٰٓؤُهُۥٓ إِن كُنتُمۡ كَٰذِبِينَ

The caller and those with him asked, then what is the punishment of theft among you if you are proven to be liars in your claim of innocence?

(٧٥) قَالُواْ جَزَٰٓؤُهُۥ مَن وُجِدَ فِي رَحۡلِهِۦ فَهُوَ جَزَٰٓؤُهُۥۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Joseph's brothers said: The punishment for any thief among us is that if the stolen item is found in his bag, he will be surrendered to the person he stole from and made to become his slave. To be made a slave is how we punish thieves.

(٧٦) فَبَدَأَ بِأَوۡعِيَتِهِمۡ قَبۡلَ وِعَآءِ أَخِيهِ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَخۡرَجَهَا مِن وِعَآءِ أَخِيهِۚ كَذَٰلِكَ كِدۡنَا لِيُوسُفَۖ مَا كَانَ لِيَأۡخُذَ أَخَاهُ فِي دِينِ ٱلۡمَلِكِ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ نَرۡفَعُ دَرَجَٰتٖ مَّن نَّشَآءُۗ وَفَوۡقَ كُلِّ ذِي عِلۡمٍ عَلِيمٞ

So they returned them to Joseph to search their bags. To conceal Joseph's plot, he started by searching the bags of his half-brothers before the bag of his full brother. He then searched the bag of his full brother and retrieved the king's golden measuring bowl from it. As Allah planned things for Joseph by arranging to have the bowl placed into the bag of his brother, He also arranged – for the benefit of Joseph – that his brothers would be given the punishment of their country, which was the enslavement of the thief. This would not have been achieved if it had been according to the king's punishment for thieves: beating and fining. Therefore, except by the will of Allah, another arrangement had been achieved, and He is able to achieve it; We raise in rank whom We will of Our slaves as We have raised the status of Joseph. And above every possessor of knowledge is someone more knowledgeable, and above the knowledge of everyone is the knowledge of Allah, Who knows all things.

(٧٧) ۞قَالُوٓاْ إِن يَسۡرِقۡ فَقَدۡ سَرَقَ أَخٞ لَّهُۥ مِن قَبۡلُۚ فَأَسَرَّهَا يُوسُفُ فِي نَفۡسِهِۦ وَلَمۡ يُبۡدِهَا لَهُمۡۚ قَالَ أَنتُمۡ شَرّٞ مَّكَانٗاۖ وَٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا تَصِفُونَ

The brothers of Joseph said: If he stole, then it is not surprising, for his full-brother -meaning Joseph (peace be upon him- had stolen even before him. Joseph concealed his hurt at what they said. He said to himself: In this circumstance, what you have preceded in wrongdoing and envy is in and of itself evil. Allah the Exalted knows this defamation you have committed.

(٧٨) قَالُواْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡعَزِيزُ إِنَّ لَهُۥٓ أَبٗا شَيۡخٗا كَبِيرٗا فَخُذۡ أَحَدَنَا مَكَانَهُۥٓۖ إِنَّا نَرَىٰكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Joseph's brothers addressed him, O minister, he has an elderly father who is of the utmost age, who loves him very much; take one of us instead of him; we see that you are a doer of good in how you have treated us and how you have treated other than us, be good to us in that.

Points of Benefit:

The permissibility of employing a stratagem to establish the truth, with the condition of not causing harm to others.
It is permissible for someone who has lost something or is in dire need to offer a "reward," specifying its amount and description for anyone who assists in its return.
Overlooking harm and keeping it to oneself are among the virtues of good character.
