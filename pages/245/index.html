(٧٩) قَالَ مَعَاذَ ٱللَّهِ أَن نَّأۡخُذَ إِلَّا مَن وَجَدۡنَا مَتَٰعَنَا عِندَهُۥٓ إِنَّآ إِذٗا لَّظَٰلِمُونَ

Joseph (peace be upon him) said, we seek refuge in Allah that we may take an innocent soul, and we leave the one whom we found the king's measuring bowl in his bag. If we were to do this, we would be wrongdoers, for we have punished an innocent soul and let a guilty one go free.

(٨٠) فَلَمَّا ٱسۡتَيۡ‍َٔسُواْ مِنۡهُ خَلَصُواْ نَجِيّٗاۖ قَالَ كَبِيرُهُمۡ أَلَمۡ تَعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ أَبَاكُمۡ قَدۡ أَخَذَ عَلَيۡكُم مَّوۡثِقٗا مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَمِن قَبۡلُ مَا فَرَّطتُمۡ فِي يُوسُفَۖ فَلَنۡ أَبۡرَحَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ حَتَّىٰ يَأۡذَنَ لِيٓ أَبِيٓ أَوۡ يَحۡكُمَ ٱللَّهُ لِيۖ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡحَٰكِمِينَ

When they despaired of Joseph responding to their request, they isolated themselves from the people to consult each other. Finally, their eldest brother said, I remind you that your father had taken a firm oath affirming that you will return his son to him, except that you be overwhelmed with something you cannot repel. He reminded them also, and before this, your neglect of Joseph, you never fulfilled your oath to your father in it. Therefore, I will not leave the land of Egypt until my father permits me to return to him or Allah decrees that I take my brother back, for Allah is the Best of Judges, judging with truth and justice.

(٨١) ٱرۡجِعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰٓ أَبِيكُمۡ فَقُولُواْ يَٰٓأَبَانَآ إِنَّ ٱبۡنَكَ سَرَقَ وَمَا شَهِدۡنَآ إِلَّا بِمَا عَلِمۡنَا وَمَا كُنَّا لِلۡغَيۡبِ حَٰفِظِينَ

The eldest brother said, return to your father, and tell him that his son had stolen, and the minister of Egypt has taken him as a slave as a punishment for his theft, and we only relate to you what we have knowingly witnessed; the golden measuring bowl was retrieved from his bag, we had no knowledge that he had stolen if we had known, we would have never taken an oath to return him.

(٨٢) وَسۡ‍َٔلِ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةَ ٱلَّتِي كُنَّا فِيهَا وَٱلۡعِيرَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَقۡبَلۡنَا فِيهَاۖ وَإِنَّا لَصَٰدِقُونَ

To confirm we are telling the truth, ask - O our Father - the people of Egypt where we were, and ask the caravan that we came with; they will inform you what we have informed you; we are telling the truth in what we have informed you of his theft.

(٨٣) قَالَ بَلۡ سَوَّلَتۡ لَكُمۡ أَنفُسُكُمۡ أَمۡرٗاۖ فَصَبۡرٞ جَمِيلٌۖ عَسَى ٱللَّهُ أَن يَأۡتِيَنِي بِهِمۡ جَمِيعًاۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

Their father said: The matter is not how you describe it in that he had stolen; instead, your evil-inclined selves led you to plot against him just as you plotted before against his brother Joseph. My patience is a beautiful patience; there is no complaint in it except to Allah. Perhaps Allah will return them all to me: Joseph, his full brother, and their eldest brother. He, the Exalted, is Omniscient of my condition and All-Wise in managing my affair.

(٨٤) وَتَوَلَّىٰ عَنۡهُمۡ وَقَالَ يَٰٓأَسَفَىٰ عَلَىٰ يُوسُفَ وَٱبۡيَضَّتۡ عَيۡنَاهُ مِنَ ٱلۡحُزۡنِ فَهُوَ كَظِيمٞ

He turned away from them and said: O, my dire sorrow over Joseph. The blackness of his eyes became whitened from how much he cried over him. He was filled with sadness and worry but hid his sadness from the people.

(٨٥) قَالُواْ تَٱللَّهِ تَفۡتَؤُاْ تَذۡكُرُ يُوسُفَ حَتَّىٰ تَكُونَ حَرَضًا أَوۡ تَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡهَٰلِكِينَ

Joseph's brothers told their father, by Allah, you continue - O our father - remembering Joseph, grieving over him, until sickness will overtake you or you will die outright.

(٨٦) قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أَشۡكُواْ بَثِّي وَحُزۡنِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَأَعۡلَمُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

Their father told them: I only complain about the grief and worry I've been afflicted with to Allah alone. And I know of Allah's gentleness, His kindness, His response to those in need, and His reward to those afflicted more than any of you will ever know.

Points of Benefit:

It is not permissible to take an innocent person in place of a criminal; someone else should not be taken in lieu of the wrongdoer.
Beautiful patience is when complaints are directed solely to Allah.
The believer should have complete certainty that Allah will relieve his distress.
