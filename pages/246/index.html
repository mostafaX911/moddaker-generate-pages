(٨٧) يَٰبَنِيَّ ٱذۡهَبُواْ فَتَحَسَّسُواْ مِن يُوسُفَ وَأَخِيهِ وَلَا تَاْيۡ‍َٔسُواْ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يَاْيۡ‍َٔسُ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ

Their father told them: O my sons, go and find out news of Joseph and his brother, and do not despair that Allah may alleviate and bring relief to His slaves. Indeed, no one despairs from receiving relief and alleviation from Allah except disbelieving people, for they are ignorant of His Ability and Favor upon His slaves.

(٨٨) فَلَمَّا دَخَلُواْ عَلَيۡهِ قَالُواْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡعَزِيزُ مَسَّنَا وَأَهۡلَنَا ٱلضُّرُّ وَجِئۡنَا بِبِضَٰعَةٖ مُّزۡجَىٰةٖ فَأَوۡفِ لَنَا ٱلۡكَيۡلَ وَتَصَدَّقۡ عَلَيۡنَآۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُتَصَدِّقِينَ

So they followed their father's command and went out searching for Joseph and his brother. When they entered upon Joseph, they told him, great poverty and hardship have afflicted us, and we have come with poor and inferior goods, so weigh us out a sufficient measure as you had done for us before; be charitable to us by increasing our measure or by overlooking our poor merchandise. Indeed, Allah rewards those who are charitable with the best rewards.

(٨٩) قَالَ هَلۡ عَلِمۡتُم مَّا فَعَلۡتُم بِيُوسُفَ وَأَخِيهِ إِذۡ أَنتُمۡ جَٰهِلُونَ

So when he heard their words, he felt kind to them as a mercy to them, and he made himself known to them, telling them, you know what you had done with Joseph and his brother when you were ignorant of the consequences of what you did to them!

(٩٠) قَالُوٓاْ أَءِنَّكَ لَأَنتَ يُوسُفُۖ قَالَ أَنَا۠ يُوسُفُ وَهَٰذَآ أَخِيۖ قَدۡ مَنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡنَآۖ إِنَّهُۥ مَن يَتَّقِ وَيَصۡبِرۡ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُضِيعُ أَجۡرَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

So they were surprised and asked if he was Joseph. He told them: Yes, I am Joseph, and this person you see with me is my full brother; Allah has favored us by saving us from our situation and raising our rank. For whoever fears Allah by fulfilling His commands, avoiding His prohibitions, and being patient with tribulation, that person's actions are righteous. Allah does not allow the rewards of those who do good to be lost; rather, He preserves them for them.

(٩١) قَالُواْ تَٱللَّهِ لَقَدۡ ءَاثَرَكَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡنَا وَإِن كُنَّا لَخَٰطِ‍ِٔينَ

His brothers, apologizing for what they had done to him, said, by Allah, Allah has favored you over us by what He has given you of the traits of perfection. We were wrong and oppressive in what we did to you.

(٩٢) قَالَ لَا تَثۡرِيبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۖ يَغۡفِرُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۖ وَهُوَ أَرۡحَمُ ٱلرَّٰحِمِينَ

So Joseph accepted their apology and said today, no blame on you necessitates your punishment or rebuke. I ask Allah to forgive you; He is the Most Merciful of those who show mercy.

(٩٣) ٱذۡهَبُواْ بِقَمِيصِي هَٰذَا فَأَلۡقُوهُ عَلَىٰ وَجۡهِ أَبِي يَأۡتِ بَصِيرٗا وَأۡتُونِي بِأَهۡلِكُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

So Joseph gave them his shirt when they informed him of what had happened to his father's eyesight and said: Lay this on my father's face, and his eyesight will be restored, and bring all of your family back to me.

(٩٤) وَلَمَّا فَصَلَتِ ٱلۡعِيرُ قَالَ أَبُوهُمۡ إِنِّي لَأَجِدُ رِيحَ يُوسُفَۖ لَوۡلَآ أَن تُفَنِّدُونِ

And when the caravan set out of Egypt and left the inhabited area, Jacob (peace be upon him) said to his sons and those who lived with him: I smell the scent of Joseph, although you might make me out to be a fool and consider me senile, by saying: he is a senile old man, he does not know what he is saying.

(٩٥) قَالُواْ تَٱللَّهِ إِنَّكَ لَفِي ضَلَٰلِكَ ٱلۡقَدِيمِ

Those with him of his sons said, by Allah, you are still in a state of your previous delusion concerning Joseph's importance to you and the possibility of seeing him again.

Points of Benefit:

The great knowledge of Prophet Jacob, peace be upon him, is evident in the fact that his good opinion of Allah did not waver despite the succession of trials and the passage of years.
A sincere apology involves seeking repentance from Allah, admitting to oneself, and requesting forgiveness from those who have been harmed.
Through piety and patience, one attains the highest ranks in both this world and the Hereafter.
Accepting the apology of the wrongdoer and refraining from revenge, especially when the opportunity arises, and refraining from blaming them for their past actions.
