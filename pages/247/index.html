(٩٦) فَلَمَّآ أَن جَآءَ ٱلۡبَشِيرُ أَلۡقَىٰهُ عَلَىٰ وَجۡهِهِۦ فَٱرۡتَدَّ بَصِيرٗاۖ قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُل لَّكُمۡ إِنِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

So when the person bearing good news for Jacob came, he laid Joseph's shirt on his face, and he could see again. At that moment, he told his sons: Did I not tell you that I know of Allah's Gentleness and Kindness that which you know not?

(٩٧) قَالُواْ يَٰٓأَبَانَا ٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَآ إِنَّا كُنَّا خَٰطِ‍ِٔينَ

His sons said, apologizing to their father Jacob, peace be upon him for what they did to Joseph and his brother: O father, seek forgiveness from Allah for our previous sins. We were sinners and wrongdoers for what we did to Joseph and his brother.

(٩٨) قَالَ سَوۡفَ أَسۡتَغۡفِرُ لَكُمۡ رَبِّيٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Their father told them: I will seek forgiveness from my Lord for you. He is the One who forgives the sins of those who repent among His slaves. He is Merciful to them.

(٩٩) فَلَمَّا دَخَلُواْ عَلَىٰ يُوسُفَ ءَاوَىٰٓ إِلَيۡهِ أَبَوَيۡهِ وَقَالَ ٱدۡخُلُواْ مِصۡرَ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ ءَامِنِينَ

Jacob and his family set out from their land, intending to Joseph in Egypt. When they entered upon him, he embraced his mother and father closely and told his brothers: Enter Egypt by Allah's will safely; nothing will harm you.

(١٠٠) وَرَفَعَ أَبَوَيۡهِ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِ وَخَرُّواْ لَهُۥ سُجَّدٗاۖ وَقَالَ يَٰٓأَبَتِ هَٰذَا تَأۡوِيلُ رُءۡيَٰيَ مِن قَبۡلُ قَدۡ جَعَلَهَا رَبِّي حَقّٗاۖ وَقَدۡ أَحۡسَنَ بِيٓ إِذۡ أَخۡرَجَنِي مِنَ ٱلسِّجۡنِ وَجَآءَ بِكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡبَدۡوِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ أَن نَّزَغَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَ إِخۡوَتِيٓۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي لَطِيفٞ لِّمَا يَشَآءُۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

He had his parents sit on the throne he would sit at, and his parents and eleven brothers greeted him by prostrating to him. This prostration was done to honor and not for worship; this was the realization of the dream as decreed by Allah, which is why Yusuf said to his father, this greeting with prostration to me is the explanation of the dream that I saw and narrated to you before. My Lord has made it a reality through its occurrence. My Lord bestowed grace upon me when He took me out of prison and brought you all from the desert after the Devil spoiled things between my brothers and me. Indeed my Lord is delicate in managing what He wills to occur, Omniscient regarding the conditions of His slaves, All-Wise in His management.

(١٠١) ۞رَبِّ قَدۡ ءَاتَيۡتَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُلۡكِ وَعَلَّمۡتَنِي مِن تَأۡوِيلِ ٱلۡأَحَادِيثِۚ فَاطِرَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَنتَ وَلِيِّۦ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ تَوَفَّنِي مُسۡلِمٗا وَأَلۡحِقۡنِي بِٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

Then Joseph invoked his Lord, saying, O Lord, You have given me something of the sovereignty of Egypt, and You have taught me something of dream interpretations, O Creator and Innovator of the heavens and the earth like nothing that came before them, You are the protector of my affairs in this worldly life, and You are the protector of all of my affairs in the hereafter, take my soul as a Muslim at the end of my life, and join me with the prophets and the pious from my father and others in Al-Firdaws, the highest garden of Paradise.

(١٠٢) ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ أَنۢبَآءِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ نُوحِيهِ إِلَيۡكَۖ وَمَا كُنتَ لَدَيۡهِمۡ إِذۡ أَجۡمَعُوٓاْ أَمۡرَهُمۡ وَهُمۡ يَمۡكُرُونَ

That which was mentioned from the story of Joseph and his brothers in what We revealed to you, O Messenger, you did not know of it, as you were not present with the brothers of Joseph when they decided to throw him in the bottom of the well and conceived what they did of their plot. Rather, We revealed that to you.

(١٠٣) وَمَآ أَكۡثَرُ ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَوۡ حَرَصۡتَ بِمُؤۡمِنِينَ

And most people are not believers, even if you strive, O Messenger, very hard that they believe, so do not lose yourself in grief over them.

Points of Benefit:

Kindness to parents, honoring and respecting them, is a duty. This includes hastening to share good news with them that brings joy to their hearts.
Warning against the whispers of Satan and those who seek to sow discord among loved ones to create division among them.
Regardless of how high a person may rise in their religious or worldly status, it all traces back to the favor and blessings of Allah.
Asking Allah for a good ending, safety, success on the Day of Resurrection, and joining the company of the righteous in Paradise.
One of the favors of Allah is that He, Transcendent in His grace, may inform His prophets about some matters of the unseen for specific purposes and wisdom.
That the caller to Allah cannot control the slaves' hearts and force them to perform acts of obedience, and that most of the creation are not from the people of guidance.
