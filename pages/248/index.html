(١٠٤) وَمَا تَسۡ‍َٔلُهُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرٍۚ إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرٞ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

If they had any sense, they would believe in you because you did not - O Messenger - ask of them any reward for the Quran nor for what you call them to. The Quran is only a reminder for all the people.

(١٠٥) وَكَأَيِّن مِّنۡ ءَايَةٖ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَمُرُّونَ عَلَيۡهَا وَهُمۡ عَنۡهَا مُعۡرِضُونَ

And many signs that are evidence of His Oneness, Exalted is He, are transmitted in the heavens and the earth. The disbelievers pass by them, yet they turn away from contemplating and considering them. They pay no attention to them.

(١٠٦) وَمَا يُؤۡمِنُ أَكۡثَرُهُم بِٱللَّهِ إِلَّا وَهُم مُّشۡرِكُونَ

And most people do not believe that Allah is the Creator, the Sustainer, the Giver of Life, or the Causer of Death, except that they worship alongside Him idols and false deities, and they claim that He has a son, far Exalted is He.

(١٠٧) أَفَأَمِنُوٓاْ أَن تَأۡتِيَهُمۡ غَٰشِيَةٞ مِّنۡ عَذَابِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡ تَأۡتِيَهُمُ ٱلسَّاعَةُ بَغۡتَةٗ وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

Do the polytheists feel safe from a punishment in this world enveloping and overwhelming them so that they cannot repel it, or from the Hour coming to them suddenly, when they are unaware so they might prepare for it? Is it because of this that they do not believe?

(١٠٨) قُلۡ هَٰذِهِۦ سَبِيلِيٓ أَدۡعُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۚ عَلَىٰ بَصِيرَةٍ أَنَا۠ وَمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَنِيۖ وَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

Say, O Messenger, to those whom you call [unto the way of Allah]: This is my path, which I invite people to follow based on clear evidence; I call to this path, and so do those who follow me. Adhere to my guidance and follow my Sunnah (way). Exalted be Allah in His Perfection from the falsehoods attributed to Him that do not befit His Majesty, or contradict His Perfection, for I am not among the polytheists who ascribe partners to Allah the Exalted, but rather a monotheist who worships Him alone.

(١٠٩) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ إِلَّا رِجَالٗا نُّوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِم مِّنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡقُرَىٰٓۗ أَفَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَيَنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۗ وَلَدَارُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ خَيۡرٞ لِّلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

And We have not sent before you - O Messenger- except men, in human form, not angels. We reveal to them as We have revealed to you from the people of the towns and not from the people of the deserts; their people denied them, so We destroyed them. Therefore, will not those who reject you walk the earth, contemplate the result of those deniers before them, and thus be admonished? And what is in the abode of the hereafter from bliss is better for those who fear Allah in this worldly life. Do you not realize that it is better so you would fear Allah and fulfill His commands - the greatest of which is belief - and avoid His Prohibitions, the greatest of which is associating partners with Allah?

(١١٠) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا ٱسۡتَيۡ‍َٔسَ ٱلرُّسُلُ وَظَنُّوٓاْ أَنَّهُمۡ قَدۡ كُذِبُواْ جَآءَهُمۡ نَصۡرُنَا فَنُجِّيَ مَن نَّشَآءُۖ وَلَا يُرَدُّ بَأۡسُنَا عَنِ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

We give respite to the enemies of those Messengers We send; We lure them gradually by not bringing their punishment on quickly. So when their destruction is overdue, and Messengers feel the despair of it, and the disbelievers think that the promise of doom and salvation for the believers was nothing but a lie, Our aid comes to Our Messengers. We save the Messengers and the believers from the destruction that befalls the deniers. Our punishment is never turned away from criminal people when We bring it down on them.

(١١١) لَقَدۡ كَانَ فِي قَصَصِهِمۡ عِبۡرَةٞ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِۗ مَا كَانَ حَدِيثٗا يُفۡتَرَىٰ وَلَٰكِن تَصۡدِيقَ ٱلَّذِي بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ وَتَفۡصِيلَ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

The stories of the Messengers and their people, as in the story of Joseph and his brothers, are lessons that people of sound minds can learn from. The Quran, which contains this, is not made up words and fabrications against Allah. Rather, it affirms the heavenly scriptures sent down from Allah. It is a detailed explanation of everything that needs elaborating from the rulings and legislations, a guide to all good, and a mercy to a people believing in it; they are the ones that can truly benefit from what is in it.

Points of Benefit:

Condemnation of those who turn away from the cosmic signs of Allah and the evidence of His Oneness spread across the pages of the universe.
This verse, "Say, 'This is my way...'" includes mention of some pillars of the call, among them: a. Existence of a method: "I invite to Allah." b. The method is based on knowledge: "Upon clear knowledge." c. Existence of a caller: "I invite." d. Presence of invitees: "And those who follow me."
