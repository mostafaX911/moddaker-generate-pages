The Thunder (Surah Ar-Ra'd)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Responding to those who deny revelation and prophethood by enumerating the manifestations of Allah's greatness.

Explanation:

(١) الٓمٓرۚ تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِۗ وَٱلَّذِيٓ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Alif Lām Mīm Rā – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah. These sublime verses in this surah (chapter) and the Quran that Allah has revealed upon you, O Messenger, is the absolute truth in which there is no uncertainty. There is no doubt that it is from Allah. However, most people do not believe in it out of stubbornness and arrogance.

(٢) ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي رَفَعَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ بِغَيۡرِ عَمَدٖ تَرَوۡنَهَاۖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ وَسَخَّرَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَۖ كُلّٞ يَجۡرِي لِأَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۚ يُدَبِّرُ ٱلۡأَمۡرَ يُفَصِّلُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لَعَلَّكُم بِلِقَآءِ رَبِّكُمۡ تُوقِنُونَ

Allah is the One who has created the heavens high without any support structures that you can see. He then ascended and rose above the Throne in an ascension befitting of His Majesty; Exalted is He, without modality or likening. He subjugated the sun and the moon for the benefit of His creation. Both the sun and the moon run along a specified period in the knowledge of Allah. He, the Exalted, disposes the affairs of the heavens and the earth as He wills. He makes clear the signs that are proof of His ability in the hope that you will be certain of meeting your Lord on the Day of Judgment so that you will prepare for it by righteous deeds.

(٣) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي مَدَّ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَجَعَلَ فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَأَنۡهَٰرٗاۖ وَمِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ جَعَلَ فِيهَا زَوۡجَيۡنِ ٱثۡنَيۡنِۖ يُغۡشِي ٱلَّيۡلَ ٱلنَّهَارَۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ

He (may He be Exalted) is the One who spread out the earth and created on it firm mountains so that it does not cause the people to become unsettled. He made two kinds from all types of fruit, like male and female animals. He draws the veil of night over the day so that it becomes dark after being bright. In that which is mentioned, there are signs and proof for people who reflect and ponder over Allah's creation, for they are the ones who benefit from such signs and proof.

(٤) وَفِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ قِطَعٞ مُّتَجَٰوِرَٰتٞ وَجَنَّٰتٞ مِّنۡ أَعۡنَٰبٖ وَزَرۡعٞ وَنَخِيلٞ صِنۡوَانٞ وَغَيۡرُ صِنۡوَانٖ يُسۡقَىٰ بِمَآءٖ وَٰحِدٖ وَنُفَضِّلُ بَعۡضَهَا عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ فِي ٱلۡأُكُلِۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ

On Earth, there are plots close to one another; in them are gardens of vineyards, plants, and palms sharing one origin and other palms with different sources. These gardens, palms, and plants are irrigated with the same water. Yet, despite their proximity and irrigation with the same water, we make some of them better than others in taste and other benefits. In that are signs and proofs for a people of reckoning, for they are the ones who pay heed to it.

(٥) ۞وَإِن تَعۡجَبۡ فَعَجَبٞ قَوۡلُهُمۡ أَءِذَا كُنَّا تُرَٰبًا أَءِنَّا لَفِي خَلۡقٖ جَدِيدٍۗ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِرَبِّهِمۡۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلۡأَغۡلَٰلُ فِيٓ أَعۡنَاقِهِمۡۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

If you are amazed, O Messenger, at anything, then you should surely be amazed at their denial of resurrection and their statement of proof for their denial: And when we die and become dust and decayed and decomposed bones, shall we be resurrected and restored to life? Those who reject the resurrection after death are the ones who disbelieve in their Lord, and as a result, they deny His Power to resurrect the dead. They are the ones around whose necks chains of fire will be placed on the Day of Judgment. They will be the inhabitants of the Fire, and they will remain there forever, without ceasing to exist and without their punishment coming to an end.

Points of Benefit:

Affirming Allah's Omnipotence and marveling at His creation of the heavens without visible pillars to support them despite their vastness and magnificence.
Affirming Allah's Omnipotence and Complete Lordship through the proof of creation, where massive plants grow from tiny seeds. And despite being watered with the same water, the sizes, colors, and tastes of their fruits vary.
Highlighting that Allah brings forth large trees from small seeds, which were initially non-existent, serves as a response to polytheists who deny the Resurrection. Bringing together scattered and decomposed remains in the earth and resurrecting them after they were non-existent is easier than bringing forth what was absent from a small seed.
