(١٦٤) إِنَّ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱخۡتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ وَٱلۡفُلۡكِ ٱلَّتِي تَجۡرِي فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِمَا يَنفَعُ ٱلنَّاسَ وَمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مِن مَّآءٖ فَأَحۡيَا بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَا وَبَثَّ فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ دَآبَّةٖ وَتَصۡرِيفِ ٱلرِّيَٰحِ وَٱلسَّحَابِ ٱلۡمُسَخَّرِ بَيۡنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ

The following are clear proofs of the Oneness of Allah, the Exalted, for those who comprehend and understand the evidence and proofs: the creation of the heavens and the earth, and the wonders of creation within them; the succession of night and day; the ships that sail through the waters of the seas carrying food, clothing, trade and other things that people need and are useful; water that Allah sends down, bringing the earth to life with the agriculture and pasture that grows in it; the living creatures He spread out across the earth; the changing of the winds from one direction to another; and the clouds, suspended between the sky and the earth.

(١٦٥) وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَتَّخِذُ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَندَادٗا يُحِبُّونَهُمۡ كَحُبِّ ٱللَّهِۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَشَدُّ حُبّٗا لِّلَّهِۗ وَلَوۡ يَرَى ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوٓاْ إِذۡ يَرَوۡنَ ٱلۡعَذَابَ أَنَّ ٱلۡقُوَّةَ لِلَّهِ جَمِيعٗا وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعَذَابِ

Even with these clear signs, some take other deities [to worship] besides Allah, making them out to be rivals to Allah the Exalted, loving them as they love Allah. However, the love for Allah within those who truly believe him is more than the love that these people have towards their falsely worshipped deities. This is because they do not worship others besides Allah; they love Him in times of ease and hardship. As for the others, they only love their false deities in times of ease, but in times of hardship, they don't call to anyone besides Allah. If the transgressors were to see their condition upon witnessing the punishment that awaits in the Afterlife for those who engage in idolatry and commit sins, then they would know that the sole bearer of all power is Allah and that He is severe in punishing those who rebel against him. If they were to see this, then they would not worship anyone besides Allah.

(١٦٦) إِذۡ تَبَرَّأَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتُّبِعُواْ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُواْ وَرَأَوُاْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ وَتَقَطَّعَتۡ بِهِمُ ٱلۡأَسۡبَابُ

Leaders will dissociate themselves from their weak followers when they bear witness to the terrors and adversities of the Day of Resurrection and when all ties and means of escape will be cut off from them.

(١٦٧) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُواْ لَوۡ أَنَّ لَنَا كَرَّةٗ فَنَتَبَرَّأَ مِنۡهُمۡ كَمَا تَبَرَّءُواْ مِنَّاۗ كَذَٰلِكَ يُرِيهِمُ ٱللَّهُ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ حَسَرَٰتٍ عَلَيۡهِمۡۖ وَمَا هُم بِخَٰرِجِينَ مِنَ ٱلنَّارِ

The weak and the followers will say, 'If only we could go back to the earth, we would dissociate ourselves from our leaders the way they dissociated themselves from us.' And just as Allah showed them the severe punishment in the Afterlife, He will show them the consequence of following their leaders in falsehood, accompanied by regrets and sorrow, and they will never leave the Fire.

(١٦٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ كُلُواْ مِمَّا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ حَلَٰلٗا طَيِّبٗا وَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ خُطُوَٰتِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمۡ عَدُوّٞ مُّبِينٌ

O people: eat from what is on the earth from meat, vegetation, and trees, from what was lawfully earned, and from what is pure and not harmful. Do not follow the paths of the Devil that he promotes; he is an enemy to you, harboring a clear animosity. It is not allowed for a rational person to follow his enemy, who is eager to harm and mislead him.

(١٦٩) إِنَّمَا يَأۡمُرُكُم بِٱلسُّوٓءِ وَٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِ وَأَن تَقُولُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

The Devil only commands you to do the worst sins and greatest offenses, inciting you to speak about Allah with regard to faith and sacred laws without knowledge that came to you from Allah or his messengers.

Points of Benefit:

Those who believe truly in Allah are those from the creation who have the greatest love for Allah because they obey Him in all circumstances, whether in difficulty or ease and do not ascribe any partners to Him.
On the Day of Resurrection, all ties will be severed, every person will free himself from his closest friend, and nothing will remain except that which was sincerely for Allah, the Exalted.
Warning from Satan's plots as their methods are numerous, subtle, and close to what the soul desires.
