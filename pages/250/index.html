(٦) وَيَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَكَ بِٱلسَّيِّئَةِ قَبۡلَ ٱلۡحَسَنَةِ وَقَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلِهِمُ ٱلۡمَثُلَٰتُۗ وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَذُو مَغۡفِرَةٖ لِّلنَّاسِ عَلَىٰ ظُلۡمِهِمۡۖ وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَشَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ

The polytheists ask you, O Messenger, to bring on the punishment, and they enquire why it is taking so long to come on them before they enjoy entirely the favors Allah has decreed for them. Many such punishments had come before on people like them from the nations who disbelieved. Why do they not take a lesson from them? Your Lord, O Messenger, pardons people despite their wrongdoing. He, therefore, does not rush to take retribution from them, giving them a chance to repent to Allah. On the contrary, he severely punishes those who persist in denial if they do not repent.

(٧) وَيَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَوۡلَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦٓۗ إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مُنذِرٞۖ وَلِكُلِّ قَوۡمٍ هَادٍ

Those who disbelieved in Allah - persisting in their rejection and obstinance - say: Why has no sign been sent down to Muhammad from his Lord, just as was sent down to Moses and Jesus? You, O Messenger, are only a warner who warns people of Allah's punishment. You only have signs that Allah has given you. Every nation has a prophet who shows and guides them to the path of truth.

(٨) ٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مَا تَحۡمِلُ كُلُّ أُنثَىٰ وَمَا تَغِيضُ ٱلۡأَرۡحَامُ وَمَا تَزۡدَادُۚ وَكُلُّ شَيۡءٍ عِندَهُۥ بِمِقۡدَارٍ

Allah knows what every female bears in her womb. He knows everything about it. He knows about any shrinking, swelling, health, or illness in the womb. By Him, Exalted is He; everything is determined with a measure that will neither decrease nor increase.

(٩) عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ ٱلۡمُتَعَالِ

It is because He, the Exalted, knows about everything not perceived by His creation, as well as everything that they perceive. He is the Majestic in His Attributes, Names, and Actions; The Supreme above all His creation, by His Essence and Attributes.

(١٠) سَوَآءٞ مِّنكُم مَّنۡ أَسَرَّ ٱلۡقَوۡلَ وَمَن جَهَرَ بِهِۦ وَمَنۡ هُوَ مُسۡتَخۡفِۢ بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَسَارِبُۢ بِٱلنَّهَارِ

He knows what is secret and what is even more hidden. Whether you, O people, hide your statements or proclaim them, it is the same in His knowledge. A person who hides from the eyes of people under the darkness of the night and one who acts openly in the light of day are equally known by Him.

(١١) لَهُۥ مُعَقِّبَٰتٞ مِّنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦ يَحۡفَظُونَهُۥ مِنۡ أَمۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوۡمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُواْ مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمۡۗ وَإِذَآ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ بِقَوۡمٖ سُوٓءٗا فَلَا مَرَدَّ لَهُۥۚ وَمَا لَهُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ مِن وَالٍ

He, the Exalted, has angels that are successive, one after another, upon humans. Some of them come at night, and some during the day. They encompass a person on all sides. Allah's command protects them from many decrees that Allah has written to be withheld from them. They record a person's statements and actions. Allah does not change the good condition of a people for the worse unless they change the state of gratitude within themselves. When Allah, Exalted is He, wills destruction for a people, no one can ward off what He wills. And you - O humanity - do not have anyone apart from Him who will take charge of your affairs and to whom you can resort to ward off the calamity that afflicts you.

(١٢) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُرِيكُمُ ٱلۡبَرۡقَ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعٗا وَيُنشِئُ ٱلسَّحَابَ ٱلثِّقَالَ

He shows you, O humanity, the lightning, inspiring both fear of the lightning bolts and the hope of rain. It is He who forms heavy clouds laden with abundant rainwater.

(١٣) وَيُسَبِّحُ ٱلرَّعۡدُ بِحَمۡدِهِۦ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ مِنۡ خِيفَتِهِۦ وَيُرۡسِلُ ٱلصَّوَٰعِقَ فَيُصِيبُ بِهَا مَن يَشَآءُ وَهُمۡ يُجَٰدِلُونَ فِي ٱللَّهِ وَهُوَ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡمِحَالِ

The thunder exalts and praises for its Lord the Exalted. The angels exalt their Lord in awe of Him and out of reverence and honor of Him. He sends thunderbolts to strike and eliminate whichever of His creation He wills. Yet the disbelievers still dispute Allah's Oneness. Allah is mighty in power and strength. He does not will anything except that He does it.

Points of Benefit:

The immense forgiveness and forbearance of Allah for the sins of the children of Adam, despite their arrogance and defiance of His messengers and prophets. Despite this, He provides for them, grants them health, and overlooks their faults.
The extensive knowledge of Allah about what is in the darkness of the womb, as He knows the state of the sperm in the womb, its transformation into a male or female, its health and affliction, its provision and lifespan, and whether it will be miserable or happy. His knowledge about it is all-encompassing.
The great care of Allah for the children of Adam and the confirmation of the existence of angels who guard and protect them, among others, such as the guardians.
That Allah changes the state of a servant for the better whenever He sees from them a commitment to the causes of guidance, as guidance and success are contingent upon following the guidance provided.
