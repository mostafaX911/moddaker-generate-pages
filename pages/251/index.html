(١٤) لَهُۥ دَعۡوَةُ ٱلۡحَقِّۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ لَا يَسۡتَجِيبُونَ لَهُم بِشَيۡءٍ إِلَّا كَبَٰسِطِ كَفَّيۡهِ إِلَى ٱلۡمَآءِ لِيَبۡلُغَ فَاهُ وَمَا هُوَ بِبَٰلِغِهِۦۚ وَمَا دُعَآءُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ إِلَّا فِي ضَلَٰلٖ

The calling of Oneness is for Allah alone, and no one shares this with Him. The idols that the polytheists invoke besides Allah do not answer the invocations of those who call upon them in any matter at all. Their invocations to them are nothing more than a thirsty person stretching his hand to water to reach his mouth so that he may drink from it, yet the water will never reach his mouth. The supplications of the disbelievers to their idols are nothing but misguidance and a departure from correctness because they cannot bring benefit to them nor repel harm.

(١٥) وَلِلَّهِۤ يَسۡجُدُۤ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ طَوۡعٗا وَكَرۡهٗا وَظِلَٰلُهُم بِٱلۡغُدُوِّ وَٱلۡأٓصَالِ۩

All in the heavens and on earth submit themselves in prostration to Allah alone. A believer and disbeliever are equal, except that a believer submits and prostrates to Him willingly. In contrast, a disbeliever unwillingly submits even though his natural disposition dictates that he should do so willingly. The shadow of every creature that has a shadow submits to Him at the beginning and the end of the day.

(١٦) قُلۡ مَن رَّبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ قُلِ ٱللَّهُۚ قُلۡ أَفَٱتَّخَذۡتُم مِّن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَ لَا يَمۡلِكُونَ لِأَنفُسِهِمۡ نَفۡعٗا وَلَا ضَرّٗاۚ قُلۡ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ وَٱلۡبَصِيرُ أَمۡ هَلۡ تَسۡتَوِي ٱلظُّلُمَٰتُ وَٱلنُّورُۗ أَمۡ جَعَلُواْ لِلَّهِ شُرَكَآءَ خَلَقُواْ كَخَلۡقِهِۦ فَتَشَٰبَهَ ٱلۡخَلۡقُ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ قُلِ ٱللَّهُ خَٰلِقُ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡوَٰحِدُ ٱلۡقَهَّٰرُ

Say, O Messenger, to the disbelievers who worship others with Allah: Who is the Creator and the controller of the heavens and the Earth? Say, O Messenger, Allah is their Creator and Controller, and you acknowledge this. Say, O Messenger, to them: Have you taken for yourselves protectors other than Allah who are helpless! They cannot draw any benefit for themselves nor remove any harm. How, then, will they be able to do so for others? Say to them, O Messenger, is the disbeliever, who has no insight, equal to the believer who can see and is guided? And is disbelief, which is layers of darkness, equal to faith, which is light? Have the partners associated with Allah (may He be Exalted) created anything like Allah's creation so that their creation is distinguishable from His? Say to them, O Messenger, Allah alone is the Creator of all things. He has no partner in creation. He is the Only deity who solely deserves to be worshipped, and He is the One who is Dominant over everything.

(١٧) أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَسَالَتۡ أَوۡدِيَةُۢ بِقَدَرِهَا فَٱحۡتَمَلَ ٱلسَّيۡلُ زَبَدٗا رَّابِيٗاۖ وَمِمَّا يُوقِدُونَ عَلَيۡهِ فِي ٱلنَّارِ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ حِلۡيَةٍ أَوۡ مَتَٰعٖ زَبَدٞ مِّثۡلُهُۥۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَٱلۡبَٰطِلَۚ فَأَمَّا ٱلزَّبَدُ فَيَذۡهَبُ جُفَآءٗۖ وَأَمَّا مَا يَنفَعُ ٱلنَّاسَ فَيَمۡكُثُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَالَ

Allah gives an example of the erosion of falsehood and the survival of truth with rainwater that comes down from the sky and causes riverbeds to flow, each according to its size, whether big or small. The overflowing water carries surface scum and foam that rise above the water. He then gives another example of falsehood and truth with some of the metals that people put into a furnace to melt them down and make ornaments for people. Falsehood is like the scum and foam that floats above the water and the slag expelled by smelting the metal. Truth is like the pure water that can be drunk, which causes fruits, grass, and vegetation to grow, and like the metal that remains after smelting, which benefits people. Just as Allah has given these two examples, He gives many examples to people so that truth may be distinguished from falsehood.

(١٨) لِلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَجَابُواْ لِرَبِّهِمُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يَسۡتَجِيبُواْ لَهُۥ لَوۡ أَنَّ لَهُم مَّا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا وَمِثۡلَهُۥ مَعَهُۥ لَٱفۡتَدَوۡاْ بِهِۦٓۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ سُوٓءُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ وَمَأۡوَىٰهُمۡ جَهَنَّمُۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمِهَادُ

Those believers who responded to their Lord when he called them towards His Oneness and obedience will have the best reward, which is Paradise. But those disbelievers who did not respond to His call towards His Oneness and obedience, even if they possessed all the different types of wealth on earth and twice as much, would offer all that to ransom themselves from the punishment. Those who did not respond to His call will be taken to account for all their wrong deeds, and the home in which they will reside will be Hell— What a miserable abode the Fire is to rest and settle in.

Points of Benefit:

Clarification of the misguidance of the polytheists in their supplication and seeking help from other than Allah, and likening their situation to that of someone who desires to drink but extends their hand to the water without actually taking it, as they have not adopted the proper means for it.
That one of the means of clarification in the Quran is through the use of parables, which bring the rational closer to the perceptible, providing a mental image that aids in understanding the intended meaning.
Affirmation that all beings prostrate to Allah, willingly or unwillingly, in accordance with the innate disposition that dictates submission to Him.
