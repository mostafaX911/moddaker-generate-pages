(١٩) ۞أَفَمَن يَعۡلَمُ أَنَّمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَ ٱلۡحَقُّ كَمَنۡ هُوَ أَعۡمَىٰٓۚ إِنَّمَا يَتَذَكَّرُ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

The one who knows what Allah has revealed to you, O Messenger, which is undoubtedly the Truth from your Lord, who has faith in and responds to Allah, cannot be equal to someone who is blind, disbelieves in Allah, and does not respond to Him. Only those with sound understanding will pay heed to and take a lesson from this.

(٢٠) ٱلَّذِينَ يُوفُونَ بِعَهۡدِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَا يَنقُضُونَ ٱلۡمِيثَٰقَ

Those who respond to Allah are the ones who fulfill the pledges they made to Allah or the commitments they made to His slaves. They are the ones that do not break their covenants with Allah or others.

(٢١) وَٱلَّذِينَ يَصِلُونَ مَآ أَمَرَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦٓ أَن يُوصَلَ وَيَخۡشَوۡنَ رَبَّهُمۡ وَيَخَافُونَ سُوٓءَ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

They are the ones who maintain everything that Allah commanded them to maintain, like keeping the ties of kinship. They are in awe of Allah, inspiring them to fulfill His commands and avoid His prohibitions. They fear Allah will hold them accountable for what they have done in sin, for any person scrutinized in their reckoning will perish.

(٢٢) وَٱلَّذِينَ صَبَرُواْ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ وَجۡهِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَنفَقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ سِرّٗا وَعَلَانِيَةٗ وَيَدۡرَءُونَ بِٱلۡحَسَنَةِ ٱلسَّيِّئَةَ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عُقۡبَى ٱلدَّارِ

They remained steadfast in obeying Allah and in what Allah decreed upon them of hardship and ease. And they remained firm in refraining from sinning against Him, thus seeking His pleasure. They kept up the prayer perfectly, discharged their obligations from the wealth that We gave them, and willingly gave from that wealth, secretly to avoid ostentation and openly so that others could follow their example. They repel the evil of those who harm them by doing good to them. These people with these qualities will have a praiseworthy outcome on the Day of Judgment.

(٢٣) جَنَّٰتُ عَدۡنٖ يَدۡخُلُونَهَا وَمَن صَلَحَ مِنۡ ءَابَآئِهِمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجِهِمۡ وَذُرِّيَّٰتِهِمۡۖ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ يَدۡخُلُونَ عَلَيۡهِم مِّن كُلِّ بَابٖ

This praiseworthy outcome is gardens in which they will permanently reside in bliss. To complete the favor, those of their fathers, mothers, wives, and children who were upright will enter these gardens together to make their stay more enjoyable by meeting them. In addition, the angels will visit and congratulate them from every door of their homes in Paradise.

(٢٤) سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكُم بِمَا صَبَرۡتُمۡۚ فَنِعۡمَ عُقۡبَى ٱلدَّارِ

The angels will greet them whenever they visit them, saying: Peace be on you, i.e., You are safe from calamities because of your being steadfast in following Allah and in difficult times and being firm in not going against Him. What a wonderful final abode your outcome is!

Upon noting the characteristics of the believers, Allah mentions the traits of the disbelievers who turned away, stating:

(٢٥) وَٱلَّذِينَ يَنقُضُونَ عَهۡدَ ٱللَّهِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مِيثَٰقِهِۦ وَيَقۡطَعُونَ مَآ أَمَرَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦٓ أَن يُوصَلَ وَيُفۡسِدُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمُ ٱللَّعۡنَةُ وَلَهُمۡ سُوٓءُ ٱلدَّارِ

Those who break their pledge with Allah after it has been made binding and cut off the ties that Allah has commanded to keep are the unfortunate and wretched ones who will be distanced from Allah's mercy, and they will have a dreadful outcome in the Fire.

(٢٦) ٱللَّهُ يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَقۡدِرُۚ وَفَرِحُواْ بِٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ إِلَّا مَتَٰعٞ

Allah expands provision for anyone He wills and restricts it for any of His slaves that He wills. Being given abundant provision is not a sign of being fortunate or having attained Allah's love. Similarly, restriction of provision is not a sign of being wretched. The disbelievers rejoiced in the life of this world and were inclined to it, becoming comfortable therein. Yet the life of this world, compared to the Hereafter, is only a brief enjoyment that will soon pass.

(٢٧) وَيَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَوۡلَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦۚ قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُضِلُّ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَهۡدِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ مَنۡ أَنَابَ

Those who disbelieved in Allah and His verses say: If only a physical sign were sent down to Muhammad from his Lord proving his truthfulness, then we would believe. Say - O Messenger - to those who suggest this: Allah, through His justice, leaves whoever He will to go astray, and through His grace, He guides to Himself those who turn towards Him in repentance. Guidance is not in their hands, so they could link it to the sending down of signs.

(٢٨) ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَطۡمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ أَلَا بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطۡمَئِنُّ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ

These people whom Allah guides are those who have faith and whose hearts find comfort in the remembrance of Allah, in His exaltation, praise, and in reciting and listening to His Book. Indeed it is only natural that in the remembrance of Allah, hearts find comfort.

Points of Benefit:

Encouragement towards a set of positive moral virtues that lead to Paradise, including maintaining good relations, fearing Allah, fulfilling covenants, practicing patience and generosity, responding to evil with goodness, and warning against the opposite.
Emphasizing that the keys to sustenance are in the Hand of Allah, the Exalted and Transcendent, and the expansion or contraction of one's provision should not necessarily be a cause for joy or sorrow. It is not an indication of Allah's satisfaction or displeasure with the servant.
Highlighting that guidance is not necessarily tied to the revelation of signs and miracles that the disbelievers suggested should be shown.
One of the effects of the Quran on the believing servant is that it instills contentment in their heart.
