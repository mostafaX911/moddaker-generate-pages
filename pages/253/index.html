(٢٩) ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ طُوبَىٰ لَهُمۡ وَحُسۡنُ مَ‍َٔابٖ

These people who have faith in Allah and do righteous deeds that bring them closer to Allah will have a pleasant life in the Afterlife and a beautiful outcome in Paradise.

(٣٠) كَذَٰلِكَ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ فِيٓ أُمَّةٖ قَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلِهَآ أُمَمٞ لِّتَتۡلُوَاْ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ وَهُمۡ يَكۡفُرُونَ بِٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِۚ قُلۡ هُوَ رَبِّي لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ عَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡتُ وَإِلَيۡهِ مَتَابِ

Just as we sent previous messengers to their nations, We sent you - O Messenger -to your nation so that you may recite the Quran We revealed. It is sufficient proof for your truthfulness, yet the condition of your people is that they reject this miracle because they deny the Benevolent One as they associate others with Him as partners. Say, O Messenger, The Benevolent One, with whom you associate others as partners, He is my Lord; no one deserves to be worshipped but He; in Him, I put my trust for all my affairs, and to Him, I turn in repentance.

(٣١) وَلَوۡ أَنَّ قُرۡءَانٗا سُيِّرَتۡ بِهِ ٱلۡجِبَالُ أَوۡ قُطِّعَتۡ بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ أَوۡ كُلِّمَ بِهِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰۗ بَل لِّلَّهِ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ جَمِيعًاۗ أَفَلَمۡ يَاْيۡ‍َٔسِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَن لَّوۡ يَشَآءُ ٱللَّهُ لَهَدَى ٱلنَّاسَ جَمِيعٗاۗ وَلَا يَزَالُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ تُصِيبُهُم بِمَا صَنَعُواْ قَارِعَةٌ أَوۡ تَحُلُّ قَرِيبٗا مِّن دَارِهِمۡ حَتَّىٰ يَأۡتِيَ وَعۡدُ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُخۡلِفُ ٱلۡمِيعَادَ

If any of the divine scriptures had the quality of being able to make the mountains move from their places or the earth split and turn into rivers and springs, or be recited to the dead who would then come to life, it would have been this Quran that is revealed to you - O Messenger - for its evidence is clear. Its effect would be profound if only their hearts were mindful, but they rejected it. Instead, sending down miracles and other things is entirely in Allah's control. Do those who believe in Allah not know that if Allah willed to guide all the people together without sending down any miracles, He would have done so? But He did not will that. Those who reject Allah continue to be struck with a severe disaster that shakes them, or such disaster falls close to their homes until Allah's promise of endless punishment comes to pass. Allah does not fail to accomplish what He promised when the specified time for it comes.

(٣٢) وَلَقَدِ ٱسۡتُهۡزِئَ بِرُسُلٖ مِّن قَبۡلِكَ فَأَمۡلَيۡتُ لِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ثُمَّ أَخَذۡتُهُمۡۖ فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عِقَابِ

You are not the first messenger to be rejected and mocked by his people. The nations before you - O Messenger - mocked and rejected their messengers. But I gave respite to those who rejected their messengers until they thought I would not destroy them. Then, I seized them after that respite, with various forms of punishment. Have you not seen My punishment of them? It was a severe wrath.

(٣٣) أَفَمَنۡ هُوَ قَآئِمٌ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ نَفۡسِۢ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡۗ وَجَعَلُواْ لِلَّهِ شُرَكَآءَ قُلۡ سَمُّوهُمۡۚ أَمۡ تُنَبِّ‍ُٔونَهُۥ بِمَا لَا يَعۡلَمُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَم بِظَٰهِرٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِۗ بَلۡ زُيِّنَ لِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مَكۡرُهُمۡ وَصُدُّواْ عَنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِۗ وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِنۡ هَادٖ

Is the One who watches over the actions of every soul and who recompenses it for its deeds more deserving of worship, or these idols who do not have that right? The disbelievers have unjustly and falsely made them partners to Allah. Say to them - O Messenger -: Tell us the names of the partners you worship with Allah if you are genuine in your claim. Or are you informing Allah of partners on earth that He does not know of? Or are you informing Him with a show of words that have no reality? Instead, the Devil has beautified the evil designs of the disbelievers. Whomever Allah misguides from the path of righteousness has no guidance to be guided by.

(٣٤) لَّهُمۡ عَذَابٞ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَلَعَذَابُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ أَشَقُّۖ وَمَا لَهُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ مِن وَاقٖ

There is a punishment for them in this world in the form of being killed and imprisoned at the hands of the believers. Still, the punishment of the Hereafter that awaits them is heavily more severe than the punishment of this world because of its never-ending severity and continuity. They will have no one to protect them from Allah's punishment on the Day of Judgment.

Points of Benefit:

The fundamental principle regarding every revealed book is that it came for guidance, not merely for the descent of the verses. This is a matter determined by Allah, whenever and however He wills.
Allah, in comforting the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم, encompassed him with the knowledge that the paths of denial taken by the disbelievers were also faced by previous prophets.
Satan extends his influence in leading some worshipers astray by adorning for them the sins and corruption they engage in.
