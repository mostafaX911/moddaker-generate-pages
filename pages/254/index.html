(٣٥) ۞مَّثَلُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلَّتِي وُعِدَ ٱلۡمُتَّقُونَۖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۖ أُكُلُهَا دَآئِمٞ وَظِلُّهَاۚ تِلۡكَ عُقۡبَى ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَواْۚ وَّعُقۡبَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ ٱلنَّارُ

The description of Paradise for those mindful of Allah by fulfilling His commands and avoiding His prohibitions is that rivers flow under its trees and palaces. Its fruits are everlasting and never cease, unlike this world's fruit. Its shade lasts without ever retracting. That outcome awaits those mindful of Allah by fulfilling His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. The outcome of the disbelievers is the Fire, which they will enter to remain there forever.

(٣٦) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ يَفۡرَحُونَ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَۖ وَمِنَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابِ مَن يُنكِرُ بَعۡضَهُۥۚ قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أَعۡبُدَ ٱللَّهَ وَلَآ أُشۡرِكَ بِهِۦٓۚ إِلَيۡهِ أَدۡعُواْ وَإِلَيۡهِ مَ‍َٔابِ

Those Jews to whom We gave the Torah and those Christians to whom We gave the Gospel, rejoice in what has been revealed to you - O Messenger - because it conforms to some of what was revealed to them; this is the condition of those who believe from the people of scripture. There are sects from among the Jews and Christians who deny parts of what has been revealed to you because it does not conform to their desires or it describes them as having altered or distorted their religion. Tell them - O Messenger - Allah has commanded me to worship Him alone; I do not associate any partners with Him. He alone I call upon, and I do not call anyone but Him. To Him alone is my return. This is what the Torah and Gospel came with.

(٣٧) وَكَذَٰلِكَ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ حُكۡمًا عَرَبِيّٗاۚ وَلَئِنِ ٱتَّبَعۡتَ أَهۡوَآءَهُم بَعۡدَ مَا جَآءَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ مَا لَكَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مِن وَلِيّٖ وَلَا وَاقٖ

Just as We sent down the previous scriptures in the language of their people, We have sent down to you, O Messenger, the Quran as a decisive word indicating the truth in the Arabic language. If you, O Messenger, follow the desires of the People of the Scripture, in their request to you to delete what does not conform to their desires, after you have received the knowledge that Allah has taught you, you will not have anyone to take charge of your affairs and to assist you against your enemies, and you will not have anyone to protect you from Allah's punishment.

(٣٨) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا رُسُلٗا مِّن قَبۡلِكَ وَجَعَلۡنَا لَهُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا وَذُرِّيَّةٗۚ وَمَا كَانَ لِرَسُولٍ أَن يَأۡتِيَ بِ‍َٔايَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۗ لِكُلِّ أَجَلٖ كِتَابٞ

And We have sent messengers before you, O Messenger, who were human, so you are not an innovation amongst the messengers. We gave them wives and children like other human beings. We did not make them angels who do not marry and have children. You are one of those messengers who is human, marries, and has children. So why are the polytheists surprised that you are that way? It is not befitting that a messenger comes up with a verse from himself except that Allah permits him to bring it forth. In every matter Allah has decreed, there is a book that mentions it and a prescribed time that is not delayed or prolonged.

(٣٩) يَمۡحُواْ ٱللَّهُ مَا يَشَآءُ وَيُثۡبِتُۖ وَعِندَهُۥٓ أُمُّ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ

Allah removes whatever good, evil, fortune, or misfortune He wills to remove and confirms whatever He wills. With Him is the Preserved Tablet, which is the source of all of that, and any erasure or confirmation that appears conforms to what is in it.

(٤٠) وَإِن مَّا نُرِيَنَّكَ بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي نَعِدُهُمۡ أَوۡ نَتَوَفَّيَنَّكَ فَإِنَّمَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡبَلَٰغُ وَعَلَيۡنَا ٱلۡحِسَابُ

And if We were to show you - O Prophet - some of the punishment that We promise them before your death, then that is for Us. If We were to cause you to die before showing you that, then you only must convey what We have commanded you to convey. It is not your duty to recompense them or take them to account - that is for Us.

(٤١) أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّا نَأۡتِي ٱلۡأَرۡضَ نَنقُصُهَا مِنۡ أَطۡرَافِهَاۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَحۡكُمُ لَا مُعَقِّبَ لِحُكۡمِهِۦۚ وَهُوَ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

Have not the disbelievers seen that We come to the land of disbelief, diminishing it from its sides by spreading Islam and the Muslims conquering it? Allah judges and decrees whatever He wills amongst His slaves. No one reverses, amends, or alters His decision. He (may He be Exalted) is swift in the reckoning, and He will take the earlier people and the later ones to account within one day.

(٤٢) وَقَدۡ مَكَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡمَكۡرُ جَمِيعٗاۖ يَعۡلَمُ مَا تَكۡسِبُ كُلُّ نَفۡسٖۗ وَسَيَعۡلَمُ ٱلۡكُفَّٰرُ لِمَنۡ عُقۡبَى ٱلدَّارِ

The previous nations had plotted and devised schemes against their prophets, and they rejected what they brought to them. What did they manage to do with such plots? Nothing! This is because the effective plan is only that of Allah and no other. May He be Exalted, is the One Who knows what their souls earn, and He will recompense them for it. Then they will know how wrong they were in not believing in Allah and how truthful the believers were as they would have acquired Paradise and the best outcome.

Points of Benefit:

Emphasizing the desirability of Paradise by elucidating its characteristics, including flowing rivers, continuous provisions, and perpetual shade.
Warning against following one's desires after acquiring knowledge and that it is among the reasons for Allah's punishment.
Clarifying that the messengers are human, having spouses and offspring, and our Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم is not an innovation among them, as he was similar in this aspect.
