(٤٣) وَيَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَسۡتَ مُرۡسَلٗاۚ قُلۡ كَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ شَهِيدَۢا بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكُمۡ وَمَنۡ عِندَهُۥ عِلۡمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ

Those disbelieving say: O Muhammad - Allah has not sent you. Tell them - O Messenger- Allah is sufficient as a witness between you and me to the fact that I have been sent from my Lord to you. And those who know the heavenly scriptures that contain my description. The one for whom Allah is a witness to his truthfulness is not harmed by the rejection of those who deny Him.

Abraham (Surah Ibrahim)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Affirming the messengers' duty to convey a clear message and warn of punishment for those who turn away from following them.

Explanation:

(١) الٓرۚ كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَيۡكَ لِتُخۡرِجَ ٱلنَّاسَ مِنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِ بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهِمۡ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَمِيدِ

Alif Lām Rā – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah. This Quran is a book that We revealed to you, O Messenger, so that you may remove people from disbelief, ignorance, and misguidance and bring them to faith, knowledge, and guidance (referring to the religion of Islam), which is the way of Allah, the Mighty whom no one can overpower, the One who deserves praise in everything.

(٢) ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ وَوَيۡلٞ لِّلۡكَٰفِرِينَ مِنۡ عَذَابٖ شَدِيدٍ

Allah, to whom alone belongs whatever is in the heavens, and to whom alone belongs whatever is on Earth. He alone is the One who deserves to be worshipped and that nothing of His creation be associated as a partner to Him. Those who disbelieve in Allah will receive a severe punishment.

(٣) ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَحِبُّونَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا عَلَى ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَيَصُدُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَبۡغُونَهَا عِوَجًاۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ فِي ضَلَٰلِۢ بَعِيدٖ

Those who disbelieve in Allah prefer the life of the world and its temporary pleasures over the Afterlife with its eternal bliss. They turn people away from Allah's path and seek to distort it. Making it diverge from the truth and moving away from uprightness is so no one will tread it. Those described with such attributes are in error, far from the truth and righteousness.

(٤) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا بِلِسَانِ قَوۡمِهِۦ لِيُبَيِّنَ لَهُمۡۖ فَيُضِلُّ ٱللَّهُ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

We have not sent any messenger except that We sent him speaking his people's language so they could easily understand what he brought from Allah. We have not sent him to force them to believe in Allah. Allah causes misguidance to whoever He wills out of His justice and guides whoever He wills through His grace. He is the Mighty, whom no one can overpower, and the Wise in His creation and planning.

(٥) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مُوسَىٰ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآ أَنۡ أَخۡرِجۡ قَوۡمَكَ مِنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِ وَذَكِّرۡهُم بِأَيَّىٰمِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّكُلِّ صَبَّارٖ شَكُورٖ

We have sent Moses and supported him with miracles indicating his truthfulness and that His Lord sent him. We commanded him to exit his people from disbelief and ignorance to belief and knowledge. We commanded him to remind them of the days of Allah in which He blessed them with His favors. Those days are clear signs of Allah's Oneness and His Great decrees and blessings upon the believers. This is something that will be of benefit to those who are steadfast in following Allah and who constantly show gratitude for His favors and approbations.

Points of Benefit:

The purpose of revealing the Quran is guidance, leading people out of the darkness of falsehood into the light of truth.
Sending messengers is done in the language and dialect of their people because it is more effective in understanding them, making it more likely to be accepted and followed.
The function of the messengers is summarized in guiding people and leading them out of darkness into the light.
