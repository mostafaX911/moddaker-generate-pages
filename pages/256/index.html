(٦) وَإِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوۡمِهِ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِذۡ أَنجَىٰكُم مِّنۡ ءَالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ يَسُومُونَكُمۡ سُوٓءَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ وَيُذَبِّحُونَ أَبۡنَآءَكُمۡ وَيَسۡتَحۡيُونَ نِسَآءَكُمۡۚ وَفِي ذَٰلِكُم بَلَآءٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ عَظِيمٞ

Remember, O Messenger, when Moses fulfilled the command of his Lord and said to his people, the Israelites, reminding them of Allah's blessings on them: O my people, remember Allah's favor on you when He saved you from the people of Pharaoh and gave you safety from their affliction. They were making you suffer greatly, killing your sons so that no one would be born amongst you to take over Pharaoh's kingdom, and letting your women live to humiliate and disgrace them. In their doing so was a great test of patience for you. Allah then rewarded you for your patience in this trial by saving you from the affliction of the people of Pharaoh.

(٧) وَإِذۡ تَأَذَّنَ رَبُّكُمۡ لَئِن شَكَرۡتُمۡ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمۡۖ وَلَئِن كَفَرۡتُمۡ إِنَّ عَذَابِي لَشَدِيدٞ

And Moses told them: Remember when your Lord clearly announced to you: If you are grateful to Allah for the aforementioned favors that He has blessed you with, then He will give you more of His favor and grace. But, if you deny His blessings to you and do not show gratitude for them, His punishment is severe for those who reject His favors and are not grateful for them.

(٨) وَقَالَ مُوسَىٰٓ إِن تَكۡفُرُوٓاْ أَنتُمۡ وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَغَنِيٌّ حَمِيدٌ

And Moses told his people: O my people: if you and all the people on earth reject the truth, then the harm of your rejection will only be on you. Allah is self-sufficient and deserving of praise by Himself. The faith of the believers does not benefit Him, and the denial of the disbelievers does not harm Him.

(٩) أَلَمۡ يَأۡتِكُمۡ نَبَؤُاْ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ قَوۡمِ نُوحٖ وَعَادٖ وَثَمُودَ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُهُمۡ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُۚ جَآءَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ فَرَدُّوٓاْ أَيۡدِيَهُمۡ فِيٓ أَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا كَفَرۡنَا بِمَآ أُرۡسِلۡتُم بِهِۦ وَإِنَّا لَفِي شَكّٖ مِّمَّا تَدۡعُونَنَآ إِلَيۡهِ مُرِيبٖ

Has it not come to you - O disbelievers - the news of the demise of the denying nations before you? The people of Noah, the 'Ād, the people of Hūd, Thamūd - the people of Saleh - and the nations that came after them, which are so numerous that their actual number is known only to Allah? Their messengers came to them with clear signs as well as miracles. But they returned their hands to their mouths and bit their fingertips in rage against the messengers. Telling their messengers: We reject what you have been sent with. We have disturbing doubts about what you are calling us to.

(١٠) ۞قَالَتۡ رُسُلُهُمۡ أَفِي ٱللَّهِ شَكّٞ فَاطِرِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ يَدۡعُوكُمۡ لِيَغۡفِرَ لَكُم مِّن ذُنُوبِكُمۡ وَيُؤَخِّرَكُمۡ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۚ قَالُوٓاْ إِنۡ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا بَشَرٞ مِّثۡلُنَا تُرِيدُونَ أَن تَصُدُّونَا عَمَّا كَانَ يَعۡبُدُ ءَابَآؤُنَا فَأۡتُونَا بِسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٖ

Their messengers responded: Can there be any doubt about Allah's Oneness and about worshipping Him alone when He is the Creator of the heavens and the earth, who brought them into existence without any precedent? He calls you to believe in Him so that He can wipe clean your previous sins and defer you until you can complete your fixed period of life in your worldly lives. Their people said to them: You are only human like us. You have no merit over us. You want to turn us away from what our forefathers used to worship; bring forth clear proof indicating the truthfulness of your claim that you are messengers from Allah to us.

Points of Benefit:

One of the means of calling people is to remind them of Allah's blessings upon them, especially when it is connected to a significant favor, such as victory over enemies or deliverance from harm.
From Allah's favor is His promise to reward His servants for their gratitude with even more blessings. Conversely, His warning is severe for those who disbelieve in Him.
The disbelief of the servants does not harm Allah at all, just as their belief does not add anything to Him. He is self-sufficient and praiseworthy in His essence.
