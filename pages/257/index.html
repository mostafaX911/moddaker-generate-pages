(١١) قَالَتۡ لَهُمۡ رُسُلُهُمۡ إِن نَّحۡنُ إِلَّا بَشَرٞ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَمُنُّ عَلَىٰ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦۖ وَمَا كَانَ لَنَآ أَن نَّأۡتِيَكُم بِسُلۡطَٰنٍ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ

In response, their messengers said: We are people like you, so we are not blaming you for imitating your forefathers in that respect. Just because you are people, it does not mean that you should imitate them in everything. Amongst His slaves, Allah bestows unique favors on whomever He wishes, so He will choose some to be messengers to humanity. We are forbidden to grant you whatever evidence you desire, except if Allah so chooses. It is not within our power to bring you such evidence; Allah alone is the One capable of doing so. Those who entrust all of their affairs to Allah will have trust in Him.

(١٢) وَمَا لَنَآ أَلَّا نَتَوَكَّلَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ وَقَدۡ هَدَىٰنَا سُبُلَنَاۚ وَلَنَصۡبِرَنَّ عَلَىٰ مَآ ءَاذَيۡتُمُونَاۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُتَوَكِّلُونَ

What sort of objection or excuse would cause us to cease trusting Him? He has guided us to the clearest and most upright path. We will be patient against the harm you caused by belying and mocking us. Those who trust Allah will entrust all of their affairs to Him alone.

(١٣) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِرُسُلِهِمۡ لَنُخۡرِجَنَّكُم مِّنۡ أَرۡضِنَآ أَوۡ لَتَعُودُنَّ فِي مِلَّتِنَاۖ فَأَوۡحَىٰٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ لَنُهۡلِكَنَّ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

When those who disbelieved in Allah, from amongst the people to whom the messengers were sent, could not respond to the messengers' arguments, they said: "We will drive you out from our town, or you will have to reject your religion and return to ours." Allah then sent revelation to His messengers to strengthen them: "We will surely destroy the wrongdoers who disbelieved in Allah and His Messengers."

(١٤) وَلَنُسۡكِنَنَّكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡۚ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَنۡ خَافَ مَقَامِي وَخَافَ وَعِيدِ

And We will surely make you dwell - O Messengers and those who followed you - on the earth after the rejecting disbelievers' destruction. The believers and their messengers dwelling in the land after their demise is for those who reflect on My Majesty and observance of it and those who fear My warning of punishment.

(١٥) وَٱسۡتَفۡتَحُواْ وَخَابَ كُلُّ جَبَّارٍ عَنِيدٖ

The Messengers asked their Lord to make them victorious over their enemies. Every arrogant and stubborn person will lose as they did not follow it when it was made clear to them.

(١٦) مِّن وَرَآئِهِۦ جَهَنَّمُ وَيُسۡقَىٰ مِن مَّآءٖ صَدِيدٖ

In front of this arrogant person on the Day of Judgment is Hell. It is lying in wait for him; he will be made to the puss that oozes from the people of the Fire. It does not quench his thirst. Instead, he will continue to be punished by being thirsty, among other forms of punishment.

(١٧) يَتَجَرَّعُهُۥ وَلَا يَكَادُ يُسِيغُهُۥ وَيَأۡتِيهِ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ مِن كُلِّ مَكَانٖ وَمَا هُوَ بِمَيِّتٖۖ وَمِن وَرَآئِهِۦ عَذَابٌ غَلِيظٞ

He will feel burdened to drink it repeatedly because of how bitter, rancid, and hot it is. He will not be able to swallow it. Death overcomes him from all directions because of the severity of what he faces of torment. He will not be allowed to die to rest; he is kept alive, suffering the torture. In front of him is an even more extreme punishment awaiting him.

(١٨) مَّثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِرَبِّهِمۡۖ أَعۡمَٰلُهُمۡ كَرَمَادٍ ٱشۡتَدَّتۡ بِهِ ٱلرِّيحُ فِي يَوۡمٍ عَاصِفٖۖ لَّا يَقۡدِرُونَ مِمَّا كَسَبُواْ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٖۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلضَّلَٰلُ ٱلۡبَعِيدُ

The parable of what the disbelievers put forth of righteous deeds such as charity, kindness, and mercy to the weak is like ash that the wind has taken hold of on a windy day. It is carried away by the wind and scattered all over the place till no trace of it remains. Likewise, the deeds of the disbelievers have been blown away by their disbelief. It will not benefit them on the Day of Judgment. Deeds not established on firm belief are nothing but misguidance far away from the path of truth.

Points of Benefit:

The prophets and messengers are human beings from the descendants of Adam, but Allah, in His grace, chose them to bear the message and selected them for it among the children of Adam.
The Islamic caller, if intending change, should anticipate various challenges, including expulsion, exile, and verbal as well as physical harm.
The callers and the righteous are promised victory and succession on the earth.
Clarification that the righteous deeds of disbelievers are nullified, and they are not considered due to their disbelief.
