(١٩) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ إِن يَشَأۡ يُذۡهِبۡكُمۡ وَيَأۡتِ بِخَلۡقٖ جَدِيدٖ

Do you not know - O humanity - that Allah created the heavens and the earth with truth, and He did not create them in vain? If He so wills, He can remove you - O humanity - and bring forth a new creation that will worship and obey Him in your place. It is easy and effortless for Him.

(٢٠) وَمَا ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ بِعَزِيزٖ

Destroying and replacing you with a different creation is not difficult for Him; may He be Exalted. Allah is Omnipotent over everything; nothing is beyond His capability.

(٢١) وَبَرَزُواْ لِلَّهِ جَمِيعٗا فَقَالَ ٱلضُّعَفَٰٓؤُاْ لِلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُوٓاْ إِنَّا كُنَّا لَكُمۡ تَبَعٗا فَهَلۡ أَنتُم مُّغۡنُونَ عَنَّا مِنۡ عَذَابِ ٱللَّهِ مِن شَيۡءٖۚ قَالُواْ لَوۡ هَدَىٰنَا ٱللَّهُ لَهَدَيۡنَٰكُمۡۖ سَوَآءٌ عَلَيۡنَآ أَجَزِعۡنَآ أَمۡ صَبَرۡنَا مَا لَنَا مِن مَّحِيصٖ

Creation emerged from their graves towards Allah on the Day of Appointment. The weak followers said to their eminent leaders: "O masters, we were followers to you. We were commanded by your command and forbidden by your prohibition. Will you then protect us against any of Allah's punishments? The eminent leaders said: "If Allah had directed us towards guidance, we would have ushered you towards it. Then, we would all have been saved from His punishment. However, we were misguided, and we led you astray. It is the same for both of us; whether we are too weak to bear the punishment or are patient, there is no escaping the punishment for us".

(٢٢) وَقَالَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ لَمَّا قُضِيَ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَعَدَكُمۡ وَعۡدَ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَوَعَدتُّكُمۡ فَأَخۡلَفۡتُكُمۡۖ وَمَا كَانَ لِيَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّن سُلۡطَٰنٍ إِلَّآ أَن دَعَوۡتُكُمۡ فَٱسۡتَجَبۡتُمۡ لِيۖ فَلَا تَلُومُونِي وَلُومُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۖ مَّآ أَنَا۠ بِمُصۡرِخِكُمۡ وَمَآ أَنتُم بِمُصۡرِخِيَّ إِنِّي كَفَرۡتُ بِمَآ أَشۡرَكۡتُمُونِ مِن قَبۡلُۗ إِنَّ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

When the people of Paradise entered Paradise, and the people of Hell entered Hell, Iblis said: "Indeed Allah gave you a true promise, and he carried out his promise to you. I gave you a false promise, and I failed to fulfill my promise to you. And with whatever strength I had, I compelled you in the worldly life towards disbelief and misguidance. I invited you towards disbelief and made sins attractive to you, so you were quick to follow me. Do not blame me for what befell you of misguidance, but blame yourselves because you are more deserving of blame. I will not help you in averting your penalty, and you will not help me in averting my penalty. I denounce that you made me a partner with Allah in your worship. Those who do wrong by associating partners with Allah in the worldly life and disbelieving in Him have a painful penalty that awaits them on the Day of Judgment.

Upon mentioning the fate of the disbelievers on the Day of Resurrection to instill fear, Allah mentions the fate of the believers as a source of motivation, stating:

(٢٣) وَأُدۡخِلَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهِمۡۖ تَحِيَّتُهُمۡ فِيهَا سَلَٰمٌ

In contrast to the fate of the wrongdoers, Allah caused those who believed and did righteous deeds to enter Paradise with rivers flowing under its palaces and trees, dwelling therein eternally by the permission and power of their Lord. They greet one another, the angels greet them, and their Lord, exalted is He, greets them with peace.

(٢٤) أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ ضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا كَلِمَةٗ طَيِّبَةٗ كَشَجَرَةٖ طَيِّبَةٍ أَصۡلُهَا ثَابِتٞ وَفَرۡعُهَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ

O Messenger, do you not know how Allah drew a comparison for the statement of monotheism, "There is no deity worthy of worship except Allah"? He compared it to a good tree, the date palm; its trunk is firmly rooted in the earth, it drinks water with its good roots, and its branches reach out into the sky, drinking from the dew and inhaling good air.

Points of Benefit:

Explanation of the dire consequences for the follower and the followed if they unite upon falsehood.
Clarification that Satan is the greatest enemy of humanity, a lying and weakened deceiver who possesses nothing for himself or his followers on the Day of Judgment.
Acknowledgment by Satan that the promise of Allah is true, while his own promise is purely deceitful.
Comparison of the word of monotheism to a good tree with abundant, high branches and firm roots.
