(٢٥) تُؤۡتِيٓ أُكُلَهَا كُلَّ حِينِۢ بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهَاۗ وَيَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَالَ لِلنَّاسِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ

This good tree gives forth its good fruit at all times by the command of its Lord. Allah, Exalted is He, draws parables for the people so that they may reflect.

(٢٦) وَمَثَلُ كَلِمَةٍ خَبِيثَةٖ كَشَجَرَةٍ خَبِيثَةٍ ٱجۡتُثَّتۡ مِن فَوۡقِ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَا لَهَا مِن قَرَارٖ

And the parable of the word of wicked polytheism is like that of a wicked tree, the bitter apple; it is removed from its foundation without any firmness in the earth. It does not extend into the sky. It dies, and the wind scatters it. The word of disbelief is bound for ruin; its owner's good deeds will not ascend to Allah.

(٢٧) يُثَبِّتُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱلۡقَوۡلِ ٱلثَّابِتِ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ وَيُضِلُّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَۚ وَيَفۡعَلُ ٱللَّهُ مَا يَشَآءُ

Allah keeps the believers steadfast through the firm word of monotheism, believing it in this worldly life and dying upon it. They are also made firm in their graves during the Transitory Stage. He keeps them firm on the Day of Resurrection. Allah causes the wrongdoers to go astray by associating partners with Him and their disbelief, which is far from the correct rationale. Allah does what he wills through His justice, from causing misguidance to whom He wills to misguide and guiding by His grace whom He wills to guide the Exalted.

(٢٨) ۞أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ بَدَّلُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ كُفۡرٗا وَأَحَلُّواْ قَوۡمَهُمۡ دَارَ ٱلۡبَوَارِ

You had seen the condition of the Quraysh, who disbelieved in Allah and His Messenger when they rejected Allah's favor of keeping them safe in the Noble Sanctuary and sending Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم to them. Their rejection was to show ingratitude to the blessing of Allah when they denied what the Messenger brought from his Lord. Thus, they have descended their followers into disbelief and to the abode of ruin.

(٢٩) جَهَنَّمَ يَصۡلَوۡنَهَاۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡقَرَارُ

The abode of ruin is entering Hell. They will suffer its heat, and the worst dwellings will be theirs.

(٣٠) وَجَعَلُواْ لِلَّهِ أَندَادٗا لِّيُضِلُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦۗ قُلۡ تَمَتَّعُواْ فَإِنَّ مَصِيرَكُمۡ إِلَى ٱلنَّارِ

The polytheists made idols and counterparts to Allah so that they would lead their followers away from Allah's path after they had been led away from it. O Messenger, say to them: Enjoy the desires you find yourselves in and the spreading of doubt in the worldly life. On the Day of Judgment, your return will be the Fire, and you will not have any other return except to it.

(٣١) قُل لِّعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ يُقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُنفِقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ سِرّٗا وَعَلَانِيَةٗ مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَ يَوۡمٞ لَّا بَيۡعٞ فِيهِ وَلَا خِلَٰلٌ

Say, O Messenger, to the believers, perfectly perform your prayers and spend in the way of your Lord from the provisions We have given you for both mandatory and voluntary charity. Spend secretly, fearing ostentation, and openly so others may take you as examples. Do this before a day comes to you in which there will be no trade or ransom to redeem yourselves from Allah's punishment. There will be no friendship so that a friend could intercede for another.

(٣٢) ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَأَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَخۡرَجَ بِهِۦ مِنَ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ رِزۡقٗا لَّكُمۡۖ وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡفُلۡكَ لِتَجۡرِيَ فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِأَمۡرِهِۦۖ وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرَ

Allah has constructed the heavens and the earth in an unprecedented manner. He has brought down rain from the heavens, and by that water - O humanity - He has brought forth various crops as a sustenance for you. According to His decree, He has subjugated ships that float on the water for you. He has subjugated rivers from which you drink water and irrigate your crops and livestock.

(٣٣) وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ ٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَ دَآئِبَيۡنِۖ وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ

He has subjugated the sun and the moon for you to run their course continuously. He has subjugated the night and the day that alternate. The night is for your sleep and rest, while the day is for striving and activity.

Points of Benefit:

Comparison of the word of disbelief to a creeping thorny shrub, as it neither rises, produces goodness, nor endures.
The connection between the commandment of prayer and paying alms, along with mention of the Hereafter, signifies that they are means of salvation on that day.
Listing some of the great blessings serves as an indication of the magnitude of disbelief by some of humanity and their denial of the blessings of Allah, the Exalted and Transcendent.
