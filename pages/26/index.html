(١٧٠) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱتَّبِعُواْ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ قَالُواْ بَلۡ نَتَّبِعُ مَآ أَلۡفَيۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ ءَابَآءَنَآۚ أَوَلَوۡ كَانَ ءَابَآؤُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡقِلُونَ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا يَهۡتَدُونَ

If these disbelievers are told to follow the guidance and light which Allah revealed, they stubbornly respond: 'Rather, we will follow the beliefs and traditions we found our forefathers upon.' Do they follow their forefathers even though they don't try to consider anything from guidance or the light, and therefore not guided to the truth that Allah is pleased with?

(١٧١) وَمَثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ كَمَثَلِ ٱلَّذِي يَنۡعِقُ بِمَا لَا يَسۡمَعُ إِلَّا دُعَآءٗ وَنِدَآءٗۚ صُمُّۢ بُكۡمٌ عُمۡيٞ فَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡقِلُونَ

The example of those who disbelieve by following their forefathers is like the example of the shepherd who shouts, calling to his livestock. They hear his voice but don't understand what he says. They are, therefore, deaf to hearing the truth in a way that they can benefit, dumb as their tongues have become mute in speaking the truth, and blind to seeing the truth as such. Because of this, they do not understand the guidance you call them toward.

(١٧٢) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُلُواْ مِن طَيِّبَٰتِ مَا رَزَقۡنَٰكُمۡ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لِلَّهِ إِن كُنتُمۡ إِيَّاهُ تَعۡبُدُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, eat from the good things that Allah has provided and allowed for you [to eat]; and thank Allah, outwardly and inwardly, for the blessings He has graciously bestowed upon you. Part of being thankful to Him is to obey Him and avoid disobeying him if you truly worship Him alone and do not associate any partners with Him.

(١٧٣) إِنَّمَا حَرَّمَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡمَيۡتَةَ وَٱلدَّمَ وَلَحۡمَ ٱلۡخِنزِيرِ وَمَآ أُهِلَّ بِهِۦ لِغَيۡرِ ٱللَّهِۖ فَمَنِ ٱضۡطُرَّ غَيۡرَ بَاغٖ وَلَا عَادٖ فَلَآ إِثۡمَ عَلَيۡهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٌ

Allah has only forbidden you from food which has died without being sacrificed in accordance with the sacred law, from flowing blood, from pork, and from that which had other than Allah's name mentioned over it when it was sacrificed. However, if someone is forced to eat something of these forbidden types of food, and he is not seeking to transgress by eating from it without dire need, and he does consume it over the bounds of necessity, then there is no sin upon him, and nor will he be punished. Verily, Allah is Most-Forgiving and Most-Merciful to whoever repents to Him from His slaves, and it is from His mercy that He overlooks the eating of these forbidden things in times of dire need.

(١٧٤) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَكۡتُمُونَ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَيَشۡتَرُونَ بِهِۦ ثَمَنٗا قَلِيلًا أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ مَا يَأۡكُلُونَ فِي بُطُونِهِمۡ إِلَّا ٱلنَّارَ وَلَا يُكَلِّمُهُمُ ٱللَّهُ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ وَلَا يُزَكِّيهِمۡ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ

Those who hide what Allah revealed in the scriptures and the signs of the truth and prophethood of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم – as the Jews and Christians did, in exchange for getting something menial in return for what they hide, such as leadership, status or money – they only eat into their bellies that which will cause them to suffer in the Fire. And on the Day of Resurrection, Allah will not speak to them with what they will like, but rather what will cause them grief; He will not praise them nor purify them [of their misdeeds], and they will receive a painful punishment.

(١٧٥) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱشۡتَرَوُاْ ٱلضَّلَٰلَةَ بِٱلۡهُدَىٰ وَٱلۡعَذَابَ بِٱلۡمَغۡفِرَةِۚ فَمَآ أَصۡبَرَهُمۡ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ

They are the ones who are described and those who hide the truth that people are in need of. They are the ones who exchanged guidance for misguidance, as they hid true knowledge, thereby exchanging the forgiveness of Allah for His punishment. How enduring they are to do what will lead them into the Fire as if they do not care the punishment it contains [for them] given their indulgence!

(١٧٦) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ نَزَّلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۗ وَإِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱخۡتَلَفُواْ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَفِي شِقَاقِۢ بَعِيدٖ

That is the recompense for hiding knowledge and guidance. This is because Allah revealed the sacred scriptures with the truth, which necessitates that it [the truth] must be made clear and not concealed. And those who disagree with the sacred scriptures, believing some of it and hiding some of it, oppose the truth and have departed from it.

Points of Benefit:

Most of the creation's misguidance is due to not using the intellect, following their ancestors in their misguidance, and unconsciously imitating them.
Not benefiting from the blessings that Allah has given him of intellect, hearing, and sight makes the person like the one who does not have these blessings.
On the Day of Resurrection, those who conceal the knowledge that Allah revealed and the guidance that His Messenger brought will be from those most severely punished.
From the blessings that Allah, the Exalted, has bestowed on His believing slaves, He made the impermissible things few and limited, while the permissible things are many and unlimited.
