(٣٤) وَءَاتَىٰكُم مِّن كُلِّ مَا سَأَلۡتُمُوهُۚ وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَظَلُومٞ كَفَّارٞ

He has granted you all that you have asked for and all that you have not asked for. If you were to count the blessings of Allah, you would not be able to enumerate them because of their plentitude and variety. What was mentioned to you were examples of them. People are oppressive to themselves and are very ungrateful for the blessings of Allah; Exalted is He.

(٣٥) وَإِذۡ قَالَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمُ رَبِّ ٱجۡعَلۡ هَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدَ ءَامِنٗا وَٱجۡنُبۡنِي وَبَنِيَّ أَن نَّعۡبُدَ ٱلۡأَصۡنَامَ

And recall, O Messenger, when Abraham said, after relocating his son Ishmael and Ishmael's mother, Hajar, to the valley of Mecca: O Lord! Make this city, Mecca, where I have inhabited my family, a city of peace wherein no blood is shed and no person oppresses another. Distance myself and my children from the worship of idols.

(٣٦) رَبِّ إِنَّهُنَّ أَضۡلَلۡنَ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِۖ فَمَن تَبِعَنِي فَإِنَّهُۥ مِنِّيۖ وَمَنۡ عَصَانِي فَإِنَّكَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

O Lord! The idols misguided many people when they believed they would intercede for them. They were allured to the idols and worshipped them instead of Allah. Whoever follows me in claiming Allah as One and obeying Him, then he is among my party and followers. Whoever disobeys me and does not follow me in claiming Allah as One and obeying Him, then You, O Lord, are Forgiving of the sins of whomever You wish, and You are Merciful towards them.

(٣٧) رَّبَّنَآ إِنِّيٓ أَسۡكَنتُ مِن ذُرِّيَّتِي بِوَادٍ غَيۡرِ ذِي زَرۡعٍ عِندَ بَيۡتِكَ ٱلۡمُحَرَّمِ رَبَّنَا لِيُقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ فَٱجۡعَلۡ أَفۡ‍ِٔدَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِ تَهۡوِيٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡ وَٱرۡزُقۡهُم مِّنَ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَشۡكُرُونَ

O our Lord, I have placed some of my progeny, my son Ishmael and his children, in a valley (Mecca) with no crops or water beside your Sacred House. O our Lord, I have inhabited them next to it so that they will establish prayer in it, so incline compassionately - O Lord - people's hearts towards them, and to this city, and sustain them with crops in the hope that they will be grateful for Your blessings upon them.

(٣٨) رَبَّنَآ إِنَّكَ تَعۡلَمُ مَا نُخۡفِي وَمَا نُعۡلِنُۗ وَمَا يَخۡفَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ مِن شَيۡءٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ

O our Lord, You know all that we keep secret and make known. Nothing on Earth or in the heavens remains hidden from Allah. He knows of it, so our need and dependence on Him are not unknown to Him.

(٣٩) ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي وَهَبَ لِي عَلَى ٱلۡكِبَرِ إِسۡمَٰعِيلَ وَإِسۡحَٰقَۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي لَسَمِيعُ ٱلدُّعَآءِ

Thanks and praise to Allah, the Exalted, who answered my call to grant me from the righteous. So He has given me, in my old age, Ishmael from Hager and Isaac from Sarah. My Lord, the Exalted, hears the call of those who call on Him.

(٤٠) رَبِّ ٱجۡعَلۡنِي مُقِيمَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِيۚ رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلۡ دُعَآءِ

O Lord, make me able to perform my prayer perfectly and make my progeny do that. O Our Lord, answer my prayer and make it accepted by you.

(٤١) رَبَّنَا ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيَّ وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ يَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلۡحِسَابُ

O our Lord, forgive me my sins, and forgive the sins of my father (he said that before he knew that he was an enemy of Allah, and when it became clear to him that his father was Allah's enemy, he disavowed him). And forgive the sins of the believers on the day that humanity goes forth to be taken to account in front of their Lord."

(٤٢) وَلَا تَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَٰفِلًا عَمَّا يَعۡمَلُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَۚ إِنَّمَا يُؤَخِّرُهُمۡ لِيَوۡمٖ تَشۡخَصُ فِيهِ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ

O Messenger, do not think that if Allah delays the wrongdoers' punishment, He neglected their acts of lying, obstructing the path to Allah and other such deeds. Allah is aware of these things, and nothing remains hidden from Him. Indeed, Allah delays their punishment until the Day of Resurrection, when people's eyes stare in horror at the terror they will witness.

Points of Benefit:

Clarification of the virtue of Mecca, which Prophet Abraham (peace be upon him) prayed for.
That no matter how elevated a person is in the ranks of obedience and servitude, they should fear for themselves and their offspring from the grave sin of associating partners with Allah.
The prayer of Abraham (peace be upon him) indicates that no matter how elevated a person's status, they remain dependent on Allah and in need of Him.
Among the methods of upbringing is to pray for the peity, good beliefs, and success in observing religious rituals for one's children.
