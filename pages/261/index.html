(٤٣) مُهۡطِعِينَ مُقۡنِعِي رُءُوسِهِمۡ لَا يَرۡتَدُّ إِلَيۡهِمۡ طَرۡفُهُمۡۖ وَأَفۡ‍ِٔدَتُهُمۡ هَوَآءٞ

When people rise from their graves, hurrying towards the caller, their heads raised and anxiously gazing at the sky. Their gaze does not return to them but remains fixed because of the horror they witness. Their hearts are void and are unable to comprehend, and they have no sense of understanding due to the terror they witness.

(٤٤) وَأَنذِرِ ٱلنَّاسَ يَوۡمَ يَأۡتِيهِمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ فَيَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ رَبَّنَآ أَخِّرۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ قَرِيبٖ نُّجِبۡ دَعۡوَتَكَ وَنَتَّبِعِ ٱلرُّسُلَۗ أَوَ لَمۡ تَكُونُوٓاْ أَقۡسَمۡتُم مِّن قَبۡلُ مَا لَكُم مِّن زَوَالٖ

And make your people fear, O Messenger, the torment of the Day of Resurrection. Those who wronged themselves by disbelieving in Allah and associating partners with Him will say: O Our Lord, give us respite, and delay our punishment. Return us to the world briefly so we may believe in You and follow the Messengers You sent us. They will be given a reprimanding response: Did you not swear, while you were on earth, that you would not be transferred from the world to the Afterlife, and you disbelieved in the resurrection after death?

(٤٥) وَسَكَنتُمۡ فِي مَسَٰكِنِ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَتَبَيَّنَ لَكُمۡ كَيۡفَ فَعَلۡنَا بِهِمۡ وَضَرَبۡنَا لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَالَ

You took residence in the living spaces of previous nations, who before you had wronged themselves by disbelieving in Allah, such as the nations of Hūd and Saleh. The destruction I caused to befall them was made clear to you. We also gave you examples in the book of Allah so that you would be warned, but you did not take them as a warning.

(٤٦) وَقَدۡ مَكَرُواْ مَكۡرَهُمۡ وَعِندَ ٱللَّهِ مَكۡرُهُمۡ وَإِن كَانَ مَكۡرُهُمۡ لِتَزُولَ مِنۡهُ ٱلۡجِبَالُ

Those who took up residence in the living spaces of the wrongful nations arranged a plot to kill the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم and end his mission. Allah knows of their plans, and nothing remains hidden from Him. Their plan was weak, for it could not move the mountains or anything else, contrary to Allah's plan against them.

(٤٧) فَلَا تَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِفَ وَعۡدِهِۦ رُسُلَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٞ ذُو ٱنتِقَامٖ

Do not think, O Messenger, that Allah, Who promised His messengers' victory and the establishment of the religion, has given up on His promise to His Messengers. Allah is Exalted in Might, and nothing defeats Him. He will strengthen and support His allies while reserving severe vengeance for His enemies and the enemies of His Messengers.

(٤٨) يَوۡمَ تُبَدَّلُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ غَيۡرَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُۖ وَبَرَزُواْ لِلَّهِ ٱلۡوَٰحِدِ ٱلۡقَهَّارِ

This revenge against the disbelievers will occur on the Day of Resurrection. On that day, this earth will be exchanged for a barren and dustless plain, and the skies will be exchanged for different skies. People will exit their graves with their bodies and deeds to stand before Allah, Who alone is the Possessor and Owner of Dominion and Majesty. He subdues and is never subdued, and He defeats and is never defeated.

(٤٩) وَتَرَى ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ يَوۡمَئِذٖ مُّقَرَّنِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَصۡفَادِ

On the day that the Earth will become a different Earth and the heavens will transform, you will see, O Messenger, the disbelievers and polytheists bound to each other in fetters. Their arms and legs will be chained to their necks. Their garments will be made from pitch (a highly flammable substance), and the fire will cover their grim faces.

(٥٠) سَرَابِيلُهُم مِّن قَطِرَانٖ وَتَغۡشَىٰ وُجُوهَهُمُ ٱلنَّارُ

On the day that the Earth will become a different Earth and the heavens will transform, you will see, O Messenger, the disbelievers and polytheists bound to each other in fetters. Their arms and legs will be chained to their necks. Their garments will be made from pitch (a highly flammable substance), and the fire will cover their grim faces.

(٥١) لِيَجۡزِيَ ٱللَّهُ كُلَّ نَفۡسٖ مَّا كَسَبَتۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

Allah will recompense each soul for the good and evil it did. Allah is swift in recompensing deeds.

(٥٢) هَٰذَا بَلَٰغٞ لِّلنَّاسِ وَلِيُنذَرُواْ بِهِۦ وَلِيَعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا هُوَ إِلَٰهٞ وَٰحِدٞ وَلِيَذَّكَّرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

This Quran, which was revealed to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, is a forewarning from Allah to humanity so that they may take heed of the warnings and severe threats found therein. So they may know that the One who is obeyed in truth is Allah alone, so they will worship Him and not associate any partners with Him. Also, those with sound intellects will be admonished and warned by it, as they are the ones who will benefit from admonitions and warnings.

Points of Benefit:

Depicting scenes of the Day of Judgment, showing the panic, fear, weakness, and awe of creation, and the transformation of the earth and the heavens.
Describing the severity of the punishment and the humiliation that befalls those engaged in sin and disbelief on the Day of Judgment.
That a person, despite having ease in their affairs in this worldly life, should strive in obedience, as Allah does not provide another opportunity for them when He resurrects them on the Day of Judgment.
