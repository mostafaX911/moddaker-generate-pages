The Rocky Tract (Surah Al-Hijr)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Threatening those who mock the Quran and promises to preserve it in a way that supports and validates the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم.

Explanation:

(١) الٓرۚ تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَقُرۡءَانٖ مُّبِينٖ

Alif Lām Rā – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah. These verses hold great significance, indicating they were revealed by Allah. They are verses of the Quran that clarify monotheism and the sacred law.

(٢) رُّبَمَا يَوَدُّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَوۡ كَانُواْ مُسۡلِمِينَ

On the Day of Resurrection, when things become clear, the disbelievers will wish they were Muslim. The falsity of their ways in this world, such as their disbelief, will be exposed.

(٣) ذَرۡهُمۡ يَأۡكُلُواْ وَيَتَمَتَّعُواْ وَيُلۡهِهِمُ ٱلۡأَمَلُۖ فَسَوۡفَ يَعۡلَمُونَ

O Messenger, leave these rejectors to eat as livestock eat, let them enjoy themselves with the world's fleeting pleasures, and let their [false] hopes distract them from believing in Allah and working righteous deeds. They will soon come to know the loss they are in when they are returned to Allah on the Day of Resurrection.

(٤) وَمَآ أَهۡلَكۡنَا مِن قَرۡيَةٍ إِلَّا وَلَهَا كِتَابٞ مَّعۡلُومٞ

We did not send down destruction upon a wrongdoing town except it had an appointed time according to Allah's Knowledge. Therefore, the destruction will neither come before nor after its time.

(٥) مَّا تَسۡبِقُ مِنۡ أُمَّةٍ أَجَلَهَا وَمَا يَسۡتَ‍ٔۡخِرُونَ

A nation will not be destroyed before its appointed time, and its destruction will not be delayed when its time arrives. Therefore, the wrongdoers should not be deceived [to think] that Allah is deferring their fate.

(٦) وَقَالُواْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِي نُزِّلَ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلذِّكۡرُ إِنَّكَ لَمَجۡنُونٞ

The disbelievers of Mecca said to the Messenger: O you to whom the remembrance was revealed, as you claim, your claims make you out to be one who is insane, and you behave as the insane people do.

(٧) لَّوۡ مَا تَأۡتِينَا بِٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

Why do you not bring the angels to testify on your behalf that you are a prophet sent [by Allah] and that the torment will descend upon us if you are truthful?

(٨) مَا نُنَزِّلُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةَ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَمَا كَانُوٓاْ إِذٗا مُّنظَرِينَ

Allah said, refuting their request to bring the angels: We will not send the angels except according to divine wisdom when your time comes to be punished. When We bring the angels, and they do not believe, they will not be given any respite; instead, they will be punished immediately.

(٩) إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ

We alone revealed this Quran upon the heart of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم as a reminder for people. Therefore, we will guard the Quran against anything being added to it, removed from it, changed, or distorted.

(١٠) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ فِي شِيَعِ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

O Messenger, We sent messengers before you to previous groups of disbelievers, and they rejected them. So, being rejected by your nation is not something exclusive to you as one of the messengers.

(١١) وَمَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

Previous groups of disbelievers did not receive a messenger except that they rejected and ridiculed him.

(١٢) كَذَٰلِكَ نَسۡلُكُهُۥ فِي قُلُوبِ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

As We instilled rejection into the hearts of those nations, We will also instill it into the hearts of the polytheists of Mecca because of their turning away and stubbornness.

(١٣) لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهِۦ وَقَدۡ خَلَتۡ سُنَّةُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

They do not believe in this Quran revealed upon Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. But, at the same time, Allah's established way of destroying those who reject what their messengers brought has occurred before. Therefore, those who reject you should consider that.

(١٤) وَلَوۡ فَتَحۡنَا عَلَيۡهِم بَابٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَظَلُّواْ فِيهِ يَعۡرُجُونَ

These rejectors will remain stubborn even if clear proof makes the truth evident. Even if We opened a door for them from the sky and they proceeded to ascend through it,

(١٥) لَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّمَا سُكِّرَتۡ أَبۡصَٰرُنَا بَلۡ نَحۡنُ قَوۡمٞ مَّسۡحُورُونَ

They would still not believe and would rather say: Our eyes have been dazzled! Or rather, we saw the effects of magic, so we have been bewitched.

Points of Benefit:

The Noble Quran combines the characteristics of perfection in everything, clarity, and clarification.
The disbelievers are usually concerned with material things. So, you see them indulging in lusts and desires, deceived by false hopes, and preoccupied with this worldly life instead of the Hereafter.
The nations' destruction is on a predetermined date and at a set time. It cannot be delayed nor brought forward, and it is not hastened for the sake of anyone.
Allah, the Exalted, ensures the preservation of the Noble Quran from alteration, replacement, addition, and omission until the Day of Resurrection.
