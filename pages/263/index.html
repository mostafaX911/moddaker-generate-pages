(١٦) وَلَقَدۡ جَعَلۡنَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ بُرُوجٗا وَزَيَّنَّٰهَا لِلنَّٰظِرِينَ

We placed massive stars in the sky to guide people on their travels in the darkness of the land and the sea. We also beautified them for those who behold and look at them so that they would use them as evidence indicative of Allah's Power; may He be Exalted.

(١٧) وَحَفِظۡنَٰهَا مِن كُلِّ شَيۡطَٰنٖ رَّجِيمٍ

We protected the sky against every devil far removed from Allah's mercy,

(١٨) إِلَّا مَنِ ٱسۡتَرَقَ ٱلسَّمۡعَ فَأَتۡبَعَهُۥ شِهَابٞ مُّبِينٞ

except for those who try to listen to the highest assembly secretly, they will be struck with a flaming meteor that will burn them.

(١٩) وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ مَدَدۡنَٰهَا وَأَلۡقَيۡنَا فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ مَّوۡزُونٖ

We flattened the earth so that people would dwell on it. We placed sturdy mountains on it so that it would not shake the people, and We made various crops grow in due proportion, determined by wisdom.

(٢٠) وَجَعَلۡنَا لَكُمۡ فِيهَا مَعَٰيِشَ وَمَن لَّسۡتُمۡ لَهُۥ بِرَٰزِقِينَ

O people, We have placed foods and drinks on the earth for you that would sustain you so long as you are in the worldly life. Likewise, We have placed sustenance for other people and animals for whom you do not provide.

(٢١) وَإِن مِّن شَيۡءٍ إِلَّا عِندَنَا خَزَآئِنُهُۥ وَمَا نُنَزِّلُهُۥٓ إِلَّا بِقَدَرٖ مَّعۡلُومٖ

There is nothing that benefits humanity or animals except that We can produce it and bring benefit to humanity by it. Nevertheless, We do not provide such things except according to a specific quantity determined by Our Wisdom and Will.

(٢٢) وَأَرۡسَلۡنَا ٱلرِّيَٰحَ لَوَٰقِحَ فَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَسۡقَيۡنَٰكُمُوهُ وَمَآ أَنتُمۡ لَهُۥ بِخَٰزِنِينَ

We sent the winds to fertilize the clouds, then the rain down from the fertilized clouds, and nourish you with that rainwater. O people, you do not store this water on earth in springs and wells; instead, Allah is the One who keeps the water in them.

(٢٣) وَإِنَّا لَنَحۡنُ نُحۡيِۦ وَنُمِيتُ وَنَحۡنُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثُونَ

We bring life to the dead by creating them out of nothing and resurrecting them after death. And We cause the living to die when they have completed their time while We will remain and inherit the earth and whatever is upon it.

(٢٤) وَلَقَدۡ عَلِمۡنَا ٱلۡمُسۡتَقۡدِمِينَ مِنكُمۡ وَلَقَدۡ عَلِمۡنَا ٱلۡمُسۡتَ‍ٔۡخِرِينَ

We certainly know those who were born and have gone before you and those who will be born and die after [you]. None of that is hidden from Us.

(٢٥) وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ يَحۡشُرُهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ حَكِيمٌ عَلِيمٞ

O Messenger, your Lord will gather them all on the Day of Resurrection to recompense the doers of good for their good and the doers of evil for their sins. He is Wise in His planning and knowledgeable; therefore, nothing is hidden from Him.

(٢٦) وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِن صَلۡصَٰلٖ مِّنۡ حَمَإٖ مَّسۡنُونٖ

We created Adam from dried clay; if it is struck, it makes a sound. The clay he was created from is black and of an altered scent because it has been left for a lengthy duration.

(٢٧) وَٱلۡجَآنَّ خَلَقۡنَٰهُ مِن قَبۡلُ مِن نَّارِ ٱلسَّمُومِ

We created the father of the jinn from scalding fire before We created Adam (peace be upon him).

(٢٨) وَإِذۡ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ إِنِّي خَٰلِقُۢ بَشَرٗا مِّن صَلۡصَٰلٖ مِّنۡ حَمَإٖ مَّسۡنُونٖ

O Messenger, remember when Allah told the angels and Iblis, who was among them: I will create a human being from dried clay; if it is struck, it makes a sound; it is black and of an altered scent.

(٢٩) فَإِذَا سَوَّيۡتُهُۥ وَنَفَخۡتُ فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِي فَقَعُواْ لَهُۥ سَٰجِدِينَ

When I fashion his image and perfect his creation, then prostrate to him in obedience to My instruction and as a greeting to him.

(٣٠) فَسَجَدَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ كُلُّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعُونَ

The angels obeyed, so they all prostrated as their Lord commanded them.

(٣١) إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ أَبَىٰٓ أَن يَكُونَ مَعَ ٱلسَّٰجِدِينَ

But Iblis, who was with the angels but was not one himself, refused to prostrate to Adam along with the angels.

Points of Benefit:

The slave should contemplate and look at the sky and its adornment and use it as proof for its creator.
No one except Allah owns all and every type of provision. The stores of them are in the Hand of Allah; He gives them to whomever He wills and withholds them from whomever He wills according to His wisdom and mercy.
The Earth was created smooth, flat, and suitable for humans. It is kept stable with solid mountains so it does not shake its inhabitants. It has various crops in fixed quantities according to wisdom and [the creation's] benefit.
The order for the angels to prostrate to Adam is an honor to the human race.
