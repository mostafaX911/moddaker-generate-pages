(٣٢) قَالَ يَٰٓإِبۡلِيسُ مَا لَكَ أَلَّا تَكُونَ مَعَ ٱلسَّٰجِدِينَ

After Iblis refused to prostrate to Adam, Allah asked him: What kept and prevented you from prostrating with the angels, who prostrated out of obedience to My command?

(٣٣) قَالَ لَمۡ أَكُن لِّأَسۡجُدَ لِبَشَرٍ خَلَقۡتَهُۥ مِن صَلۡصَٰلٖ مِّنۡ حَمَإٖ مَّسۡنُونٖ

Iblis arrogantly said: It is not proper for me to prostrate to a human being who You created out of dried clay that used to be black, altered clay.

(٣٤) قَالَ فَٱخۡرُجۡ مِنۡهَا فَإِنَّكَ رَجِيمٞ

Allah said to Iblis: Exit from Paradise, for you are banished.

(٣٥) وَإِنَّ عَلَيۡكَ ٱللَّعۡنَةَ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ

My curse is upon you, and you are banished from My mercy until the Day of Resurrection.

(٣٦) قَالَ رَبِّ فَأَنظِرۡنِيٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ

Iblis said: O My Lord, grant me respite and do not cause me to die until the day the creation will be resurrected.

(٣٧) قَالَ فَإِنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُنظَرِينَ

Allah said: You are of those granted respite whose end is delayed.

(٣٨) إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡوَقۡتِ ٱلۡمَعۡلُومِ

Until the time when all created things will die upon the first blow of the Horn.

(٣٩) قَالَ رَبِّ بِمَآ أَغۡوَيۡتَنِي لَأُزَيِّنَنَّ لَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَأُغۡوِيَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

Iblis said: O My Lord, because You misguided me, I will make [Your] disobedience seem attractive to them on Earth, and I will lead them all astray from the straight path.

(٤٠) إِلَّا عِبَادَكَ مِنۡهُمُ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ

Except for Your slaves whom You selected for your worship.

(٤١) قَالَ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٌ عَلَيَّ مُسۡتَقِيمٌ

Allah said: This is a straight path that leads to Me.

(٤٢) إِنَّ عِبَادِي لَيۡسَ لَكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سُلۡطَٰنٌ إِلَّا مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡغَاوِينَ

You have no power or sway in misleading My sincere slaves except those who follow you from the misguided ones.

(٤٣) وَإِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ لَمَوۡعِدُهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

Hell is the promised abode of Iblis and all those who follow him who have been led astray.

(٤٤) لَهَا سَبۡعَةُ أَبۡوَٰبٖ لِّكُلِّ بَابٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ جُزۡءٞ مَّقۡسُومٌ

Hell has seven gates through which they will enter. For each of its gates, there is a known amount of the followers of Iblis who will enter through it.

(٤٥) إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّٰتٖ وَعُيُونٍ

Those who fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions will have gardens and springs.

(٤٦) ٱدۡخُلُوهَا بِسَلَٰمٍ ءَامِنِينَ

When they enter, it will be said to them: Enter it with safety from any misfortune and security from any fears.

(٤٧) وَنَزَعۡنَا مَا فِي صُدُورِهِم مِّنۡ غِلٍّ إِخۡوَٰنًا عَلَىٰ سُرُرٖ مُّتَقَٰبِلِينَ

We will remove whatever hatred and animosity they had in their hearts. Instead, they will be brothers who love one another, sitting upon couches and looking at each other.

(٤٨) لَا يَمَسُّهُمۡ فِيهَا نَصَبٞ وَمَا هُم مِّنۡهَا بِمُخۡرَجِينَ

Fatigue will not affect them in it, and they will not be made to leave it. Instead, they will remain in it eternally.

(٤٩) ۞نَبِّئۡ عِبَادِيٓ أَنِّيٓ أَنَا ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

O Messenger, inform My slaves that I will forgive whoever among them repents and show mercy to them.

(٥٠) وَأَنَّ عَذَابِي هُوَ ٱلۡعَذَابُ ٱلۡأَلِيمُ

And inform them that My torment is painful, so they should repent to Me to receive My forgiveness and be safe from My torment.

(٥١) وَنَبِّئۡهُمۡ عَن ضَيۡفِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ

And inform them of the story of Abraham's guests (peace be upon him), who were angels. They brought him the good news of a child and informed him of the destruction of the people of Lot.

Points of Benefit:

In the verses, there is proof that the heedful will visit each other in Paradise, meet with each other, and treat each other appropriately as they will face each other directly and not face their backs to them.
The slave's heart should always be between fear and hope, longing and dread.
The angels all performed a prostration of greeting and honor to Adam together, except Iblis, who refused and rejected.
Iblis has no power to cast those whom Allah has guided, selected, and chosen into a sin that would prevent Allah from pardoning them.
