(٥٢) إِذۡ دَخَلُواْ عَلَيۡهِ فَقَالُواْ سَلَٰمٗا قَالَ إِنَّا مِنكُمۡ وَجِلُونَ

When they came to him, they said: Salam (peace on you). He responded to them with a greeting that was better than theirs and presented them with a roasted calf because he thought they were human. When they did not eat from it, he said: We are afraid of you.

(٥٣) قَالُواْ لَا تَوۡجَلۡ إِنَّا نُبَشِّرُكَ بِغُلَٰمٍ عَلِيمٖ

The messengers, who were angels, said: Do not be afraid! We will tell you something that will make you happy. You are going to have a very knowledgeable son.

(٥٤) قَالَ أَبَشَّرۡتُمُونِي عَلَىٰٓ أَن مَّسَّنِيَ ٱلۡكِبَرُ فَبِمَ تُبَشِّرُونَ

Surprised at the good news of a son, Abraham said: Do you bring me this good news despite the fact that I have been afflicted with old age? What kind of good news are you bringing me?

(٥٥) قَالُواْ بَشَّرۡنَٰكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ فَلَا تَكُن مِّنَ ٱلۡقَٰنِطِينَ

The messengers, who were angels, said to Abraham: We bring you good news of the truth in which there is no doubt, so do not be among those who despair from the good news we bring you.

(٥٦) قَالَ وَمَن يَقۡنَطُ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ رَبِّهِۦٓ إِلَّا ٱلضَّآلُّونَ

Abraham said: And who despairs from the mercy of his Lord except he who has deviated from Allah's straight path?!

(٥٧) قَالَ فَمَا خَطۡبُكُمۡ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ

Abraham said: What is the matter that has brought you, O messengers, from Allah?

(٥٨) قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّآ أُرۡسِلۡنَآ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمٖ مُّجۡرِمِينَ

The messengers, who were angels, said: Allah has sent us to destroy the people of Lot who are guilty of great corruption and evil.

(٥٩) إِلَّآ ءَالَ لُوطٍ إِنَّا لَمُنَجُّوهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

Except for the family of Lot and the believers that follow him, for the destruction will not encompass them, and We will save them all from it.

(٦٠) إِلَّا ٱمۡرَأَتَهُۥ قَدَّرۡنَآ إِنَّهَا لَمِنَ ٱلۡغَٰبِرِينَ

Except for his wife, We have decreed her to be one of those who remain behind, included in the destruction.

(٦١) فَلَمَّا جَآءَ ءَالَ لُوطٍ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ

So, the messengers, who were angels, came to the family of Lot in the form of men.

(٦٢) قَالَ إِنَّكُمۡ قَوۡمٞ مُّنكَرُونَ

Lot (peace be with him) said to them: You are an unfamiliar people.

(٦٣) قَالُواْ بَلۡ جِئۡنَٰكَ بِمَا كَانُواْ فِيهِ يَمۡتَرُونَ

The messengers, who were angels, said to Lot: Do not fear! We have brought to you, O Lot, the destructive torment your people used to doubt

(٦٤) وَأَتَيۡنَٰكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَإِنَّا لَصَٰدِقُونَ

We have brought to you the truth in which there is no joke, and we are truthful in the information we give you.

(٦٥) فَأَسۡرِ بِأَهۡلِكَ بِقِطۡعٖ مِّنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱتَّبِعۡ أَدۡبَٰرَهُمۡ وَلَا يَلۡتَفِتۡ مِنكُمۡ أَحَدٞ وَٱمۡضُواْ حَيۡثُ تُؤۡمَرُونَ

So, travel with your family after some of the night has passed. Travel behind them, and no one should turn back to see what happens to them. Instead, proceed to where Allah has ordered you to go.

(٦٦) وَقَضَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡأَمۡرَ أَنَّ دَابِرَ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ مَقۡطُوعٞ مُّصۡبِحِينَ

We informed Lot through revelation this matter that We had decreed. Those people will be annihilated by the last of them being destroyed when morning comes.

(٦٧) وَجَآءَ أَهۡلُ ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ يَسۡتَبۡشِرُونَ

The people of Sodom came rejoicing at the guests of Lot with the desire to commit indecency.

(٦٨) قَالَ إِنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ ضَيۡفِي فَلَا تَفۡضَحُونِ

Lot told them: These people are my guests, so do not disgrace me by what you want [to do] with them.

(٦٩) وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَلَا تُخۡزُونِ

Fear Allah by abandoning this indecent behavior, and do not humiliate me by committing your heinous act.

(٧٠) قَالُوٓاْ أَوَ لَمۡ نَنۡهَكَ عَنِ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

His people told him: Did we not forbid you from hosting anyone?

Points of Benefit:

Teaching the guest the etiquette of greeting and giving salutations when coming to others.
Whoever Allah has blessed with guidance and great knowledge has no way to despair of Allah's mercy.
Allah forbade Lot and his followers from paying any attention when the torment descended on Lot's people so they would not feel pity for them.
The determination of Lot's people to commit obscene acts with those guests is evidence of their destroyed nature and severe indecency.
