(٧١) قَالَ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ بَنَاتِيٓ إِن كُنتُمۡ فَٰعِلِينَ

Lot (peace be with him) said, offering an excuse for himself in front of his guests: These are the women of my people, who are like daughters to me. Therefore, marry them if you want to satisfy your desires.

(٧٢) لَعَمۡرُكَ إِنَّهُمۡ لَفِي سَكۡرَتِهِمۡ يَعۡمَهُونَ

By your life, O Messenger, the people of Lot wandered mindlessly in pursuit of their lusts.

(٧٣) فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلصَّيۡحَةُ مُشۡرِقِينَ

Then, they were seized by a severe destructive cry at sunrise.

(٧٤) فَجَعَلۡنَا عَٰلِيَهَا سَافِلَهَا وَأَمۡطَرۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ حِجَارَةٗ مِّن سِجِّيلٍ

We turned their towns upside down and caused stones of hard clay to rain down on them.

(٧٥) إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّلۡمُتَوَسِّمِينَ

In the incident of the punishment that descended on the people of Lot, there are signs for those who ponder.

(٧٦) وَإِنَّهَا لَبِسَبِيلٖ مُّقِيمٍ

The towns of the people of Lot are on an established road and can be seen by travelers who pass by.

(٧٧) إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

That incident has a sign in it for the believers to reflect on.

(٧٨) وَإِن كَانَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡأَيۡكَةِ لَظَٰلِمِينَ

The people of Shuayb, who lived in a town surrounded by intertwined trees, were wrongdoers because they rejected Allah and His Messenger, Shuayb (peace be upon him).

(٧٩) فَٱنتَقَمۡنَا مِنۡهُمۡ وَإِنَّهُمَا لَبِإِمَامٖ مُّبِينٖ

So We took retribution from them, and the punishment seized them. The towns of Lot's people and the homes of Shuayb's people are on a road clear to those who pass by.

(٨٠) وَلَقَدۡ كَذَّبَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡحِجۡرِ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

Thamūd, the people of Hijr [a place between Hijaz and the Levant], had, in effect, rejected all the messengers when they rejected their prophet, Saleh (peace be upon him).

(٨١) وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا فَكَانُواْ عَنۡهَا مُعۡرِضِينَ

We gave them proofs and signs to indicate his truthfulness in what he brought from his Lord. One of these proofs was the she-camel. But they did not take lessons from those signs and paid no attention to them.

(٨٢) وَكَانُواْ يَنۡحِتُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡجِبَالِ بُيُوتًا ءَامِنِينَ

They used to carve houses from the mountains where they lived, safe from what they feared.

(٨٣) فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلصَّيۡحَةُ مُصۡبِحِينَ

Then, the bolt of the torment seized them in the morning.

(٨٤) فَمَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ

The wealth and homes they had accumulated did not repel Allah's torment from them.

(٨٥) وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّۗ وَإِنَّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ لَأٓتِيَةٞۖ فَٱصۡفَحِ ٱلصَّفۡحَ ٱلۡجَمِيلَ

We did not create the heavens, the Earth, and everything between them in vain and devoid of wisdom. We created all of it with a true purpose. The Hour will certainly come. So, O Messenger, turn away from those who reject you and pardon them graciously.

(٨٦) إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ ٱلۡخَلَّٰقُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

Your Lord, O Messenger, is the Creator and Omniscient of everything.

(٨٧) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَٰكَ سَبۡعٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمَثَانِي وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ ٱلۡعَظِيمَ

We have given you the Opening Chapter, Surah Al-Fatihah, which consists of seven verses and it is the Great Quran.

(٨٨) لَا تَمُدَّنَّ عَيۡنَيۡكَ إِلَىٰ مَا مَتَّعۡنَا بِهِۦٓ أَزۡوَٰجٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ وَلَا تَحۡزَنۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَٱخۡفِضۡ جَنَاحَكَ لِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Do not look longingly to the temporary enjoyments We have given to some classes of disbelievers; do not grieve over their rejection and be humble with the believers.

(٨٩) وَقُلۡ إِنِّيٓ أَنَا ٱلنَّذِيرُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

Say, O Messenger: I am a warner of Allah's torment, clear in my warning.

(٩٠) كَمَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡمُقۡتَسِمِينَ

I warn you of being afflicted by a torment similar to what Allah sent down on those who divided Allah's books into parts, believing in some and rejecting others.

Points of Benefit:

When Allah wants to destroy a town, their evil and oppression will increase. Then, when it is over, He inflicts upon them the punishment they deserve.
Lessons should be taken from Allah's, the Exalted's, destruction of the disbelieving nations, and the places they were tormented.
The believer should not look at this world's embellishments and beauty but what Allah has to give.
The believer should be distant from the polytheists and not grieve when they do not believe, and he should be close to the believers, humble with them, and love them even if they are poor.
