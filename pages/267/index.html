(٩١) ٱلَّذِينَ جَعَلُواْ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ عِضِينَ

Those who divided the Quran into parts and then say: It is magic, soothsaying, or poetry.

(٩٢) فَوَرَبِّكَ لَنَسۡ‍َٔلَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

By your Lord, O Messenger, We will question all who divide the Quran into parts on the Day of Resurrection.

(٩٣) عَمَّا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

We will question them for their disbelief and sins in the world.

(٩٤) فَٱصۡدَعۡ بِمَا تُؤۡمَرُ وَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنِ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

So announce, O Messenger, the invitation towards Allah that He has instructed you to do and do not pay any attention to what the polytheists say and do.

(٩٥) إِنَّا كَفَيۡنَٰكَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَهۡزِءِينَ

Do not be afraid of them, for We are enough for you against the plot of those leaders of disbelief from the Quraysh who mock you.

(٩٦) ٱلَّذِينَ يَجۡعَلُونَ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَۚ فَسَوۡفَ يَعۡلَمُونَ

Those who take another deity along with Allah will soon know the evil outcome of associating partners with Allah.

(٩٧) وَلَقَدۡ نَعۡلَمُ أَنَّكَ يَضِيقُ صَدۡرُكَ بِمَا يَقُولُونَ

O Messenger, We know your heart is distressed by the rejection and mockery that emanates from them.

(٩٨) فَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ وَكُن مِّنَ ٱلسَّٰجِدِينَ

So, resort to Allah by declaring His transcendence from everything inappropriate for Him, praising Him with attributes of perfection, and be of those who worship and pray to Him. In that, there is a cure for the distress of your heart.

(٩٩) وَٱعۡبُدۡ رَبَّكَ حَتَّىٰ يَأۡتِيَكَ ٱلۡيَقِينُ

Continue worshipping your Lord as long as you are alive until death comes to you while in that state.

The Bee (Surah Al-Nahl)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

A reminder of the blessings that demonstrate the generosity of our Benefactor, Allah, the Exalted and Transcendent.

Explanation:

(١) أَتَىٰٓ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ فَلَا تَسۡتَعۡجِلُوهُۚ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥ وَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

The torment Allah has decreed for you, O disbelievers, is close. So do not ask for it to come sooner than its time. Allah is free from and exalted above all that the polytheists associate as partners with Him.

(٢) يُنَزِّلُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةَ بِٱلرُّوحِ مِنۡ أَمۡرِهِۦ عَلَىٰ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦٓ أَنۡ أَنذِرُوٓاْ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنَا۠ فَٱتَّقُونِ

Allah, through His decree, sends down the angels to whoever of His Messengers He wills with revelation [saying]: Warn, O messengers, the people against associating partners with Allah, for none is deserving of worship besides Me. Therefore, fear Me, O People, by complying with My commands and avoiding My prohibitions.

(٣) خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ تَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

Allah created the heavens and the Earth without precedent for an actual purpose. He did not create them in vain but to serve as a sign of His greatness. He is free from what they ascribe as partners to Him.

(٤) خَلَقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ فَإِذَا هُوَ خَصِيمٞ مُّبِينٞ

He created man from a despised drop of fluid, who then progressed from one form to another. Then suddenly, he became a harsh disputer using falsehood to attempt to invalidate the truth, being a clear opponent in his disputation.

(٥) وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمَ خَلَقَهَاۖ لَكُمۡ فِيهَا دِفۡءٞ وَمَنَٰفِعُ وَمِنۡهَا تَأۡكُلُونَ

He also created livestock such as camels, cattle, and goats for your benefit, O People. Some benefits include deriving warmth from their wool and hair and other benefits from their milk, skin, and backs. In addition, some of them serve as food that you eat.

(٦) وَلَكُمۡ فِيهَا جَمَالٌ حِينَ تُرِيحُونَ وَحِينَ تَسۡرَحُونَ

There is [also] beauty in them for you in the evening and in the morning when you take them out to graze.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's care and concern for preserving the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم and protecting him from the harm of the polytheists.
Tasbeeh (praising Allah by saying Subhan-Allah), tahmid (praising Allah by saying Alhamdulillah), and Prayer are the cure for worries and grief and the way out of crises, dilemmas, and afflictions.
Ideally, a Muslim is required to perform worship, like Prayer and the like, constantly until death comes to him, so long as he has not lost his consciousness or memory.
Allah called the revelation a spirit because it gives life to the souls.
Allah, the Exalted, gave us ownership of cattle and animals and made them subservient to us. He made it permissible for us to use them and benefit from them as a mercy from Him, the Exalted, to us.
