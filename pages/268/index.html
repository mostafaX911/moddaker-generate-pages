(٧) وَتَحۡمِلُ أَثۡقَالَكُمۡ إِلَىٰ بَلَدٖ لَّمۡ تَكُونُواْ بَٰلِغِيهِ إِلَّا بِشِقِّ ٱلۡأَنفُسِۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكُمۡ لَرَءُوفٞ رَّحِيمٞ

This livestock We created for you carries your heavy goods when you travel to places you can only reach after bearing great hardship on yourselves. Your Lord, O People, is Kind and Merciful to you as He has subjugated this livestock for you.

(٨) وَٱلۡخَيۡلَ وَٱلۡبِغَالَ وَٱلۡحَمِيرَ لِتَرۡكَبُوهَا وَزِينَةٗۚ وَيَخۡلُقُ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

He created horses, mules, and donkeys for you to ride and carry your goods on and for them to be an adornment for you that you can beautify yourselves with when among people. He creates that which you do not know of as He wishes.

(٩) وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ قَصۡدُ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَمِنۡهَا جَآئِرٞۚ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ لَهَدَىٰكُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

It is upon Allah alone to clearly show the straight path leading to His pleasure, Islam. Some paths are the paths of the Devil that deviate away from the truth. Any path other than the path of Islam deviates. Had Allah been willing to guide you all to believe [in Him], He would have done so.

(١٠) هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗۖ لَّكُم مِّنۡهُ شَرَابٞ وَمِنۡهُ شَجَرٞ فِيهِ تُسِيمُونَ

He (may He be Exalted) is the One who sent down water from the clouds for you. From this water, you have what you and your livestock can drink. You also get vegetation from it, which you can use to graze your livestock.

(١١) يُنۢبِتُ لَكُم بِهِ ٱلزَّرۡعَ وَٱلزَّيۡتُونَ وَٱلنَّخِيلَ وَٱلۡأَعۡنَٰبَ وَمِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ

With that water, Allah causes crops to grow for you to eat. He also causes olives, date palms, grapes to grow, and other types of fruit. The water and the things it produces are a sign of Allah's power for people who ponder His creation and use it as a sign of His greatness; may He be Exalted.

(١٢) وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ وَٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَۖ وَٱلنُّجُومُ مُسَخَّرَٰتُۢ بِأَمۡرِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ

Allah has subjugated the night for you so that you can find tranquility and rest in it and the day so that you can earn your livelihood in it. He subjugated the sun and made it bright and the moon a source of light. The stars are subjugated for you by His decree, which you use for navigation in the darkness of the sea and land, and by which you come to know the time etc. The subjugation of all these things is clearly indicative of Allah's power for people who apply their minds, for they are the ones who will grasp the wisdom in them.

(١٣) وَمَا ذَرَأَ لَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مُخۡتَلِفًا أَلۡوَٰنُهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَذَّكَّرُونَ

He (may He be Exalted) also subjugated for you the things on Earth that He created, having diverse colors such as minerals, animals, plants, and crops. These things that He has created and subjugated for you are clearly indicative of Allah's power (may He be Exalted) for people who reflect and realize that Allah is Powerful and a Benefactor.

(١٤) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي سَخَّرَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَ لِتَأۡكُلُواْ مِنۡهُ لَحۡمٗا طَرِيّٗا وَتَسۡتَخۡرِجُواْ مِنۡهُ حِلۡيَةٗ تَلۡبَسُونَهَاۖ وَتَرَى ٱلۡفُلۡكَ مَوَاخِرَ فِيهِ وَلِتَبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

He (may He be Exalted) is the One who subjugated the sea for you and enabled you to travel on it and to extract what is in it so that you can eat the fresh and soft meat of the fish that you catch and that you can extract from it jewelry that you and your wives can wear such as pearls. You see the ships splitting the waves of the ocean, and you travel on these ships in search of Allah's bounty in the form of profit, which you gain from trade. All of this is for you to be grateful to Allah for the favors He has given you and to worship Him alone.

Points of Benefit:

From Allah's greatness is that He creates what the whole of mankind does not even know about at whatever time He, the Transcendent, wants.
Allah created the stars to beautify the sky, for guidance in the darkness of the land and sea, and for you to know and calculate the time.
Praise and thanks to Allah, who has blessed us with what improves our lives and helps us have the best living.
Allah, the Transcendent, has blessed us by subjugating the sea so that we can eat its meat (fish), extract its pearls and coral, sail upon it, trade, and other interests and benefits.
