(١٥) وَأَلۡقَىٰ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَوَٰسِيَ أَن تَمِيدَ بِكُمۡ وَأَنۡهَٰرٗا وَسُبُلٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَهۡتَدُونَ

He placed mountains on Earth to keep it firm so that it does not shake and cause you to become unsettled. He caused rivers to flow so that you could drink from them and give water to your livestock and crops. He carved pathways in it for you to tread and reach your destinations without losing your way.

(١٦) وَعَلَٰمَٰتٖۚ وَبِٱلنَّجۡمِ هُمۡ يَهۡتَدُونَ

He made clear landmarks on Earth for you to be guided by when traveling during the day. He also made stars for you in the sky so that you may find your way.

(١٧) أَفَمَن يَخۡلُقُ كَمَن لَّا يَخۡلُقُۚ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ

Is the One who created all these things like the One who does not create anything?! Are you not reminded of Allah's greatness, who created everything, and then worship Him alone without associating those things that cannot create anything as partners with Him?

(١٨) وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَغَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

If you, O people, attempt to count and list Allah's many favors that He has favored you with, you will not be able to do so because they are so numerous and diverse. Allah is Forgiving, as He did not take you to task for being neglectful of showing thanks to Him, and Merciful, as he did not stop these favors from you because of your sins and failure to thank Him.

(١٩) وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مَا تُسِرُّونَ وَمَا تُعۡلِنُونَ

Allah knows the deeds that you, O slaves, hide and those that you reveal. None of those deeds are hidden from Him, and He will recompense you for them accordingly.

(٢٠) وَٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ لَا يَخۡلُقُونَ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَهُمۡ يُخۡلَقُونَ

Those whom the polytheists worship instead of Allah cannot create anything, no matter how insignificant. Those who worship them instead of Allah are the ones who make them. How do they worship idols instead of Allah, which they make with their own hands?!

(٢١) أَمۡوَٰتٌ غَيۡرُ أَحۡيَآءٖۖ وَمَا يَشۡعُرُونَ أَيَّانَ يُبۡعَثُونَ

In addition to their worshippers making them with their hands, they are also inanimate things without life or knowledge. Therefore, they do not know when they will be resurrected with their worshippers on the Day of Resurrection, to be thrown together with them into the Fire.

(٢٢) إِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهٞ وَٰحِدٞۚ فَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ قُلُوبُهُم مُّنكِرَةٞ وَهُم مُّسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ

The one worthy of your worship is One without any partner, and that is Allah. The hearts of those who do not believe in the resurrection and the recompense reject the Oneness of Allah because they lack fear [of Allah]. Therefore, they do not believe there will be any reckoning or punishment. Consequently, they are arrogant and refuse to accept and submit to the truth.

(٢٣) لَا جَرَمَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ مَا يُسِرُّونَ وَمَا يُعۡلِنُونَۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُسۡتَكۡبِرِينَ

Truly, Allah knows the deeds these people hide, and He knows the deeds they reveal. Nothing is hidden from Him, and He will recompense them for that accordingly. He (may He be Exalted) does not love those who are too arrogant to worship and submit to Him. Instead, he detests them intensely.

(٢٤) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُم مَّاذَآ أَنزَلَ رَبُّكُمۡ قَالُوٓاْ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

When these people who deny the Oneness of the Creator and reject the resurrection are asked: What did Allah reveal to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم? They say: Allah has not revealed anything to him. He has only brought stories and fables of the earlier people.

(٢٥) لِيَحۡمِلُوٓاْ أَوۡزَارَهُمۡ كَامِلَةٗ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ وَمِنۡ أَوۡزَارِ ٱلَّذِينَ يُضِلُّونَهُم بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٍۗ أَلَا سَآءَ مَا يَزِرُونَ

So their outcome will be that they will carry their sins without any reduction, and they will also carry some of the sins of those they misled from Islam on account of ignorance and blind imitation. How terrible will it be that they will bear some of their sins and the sins of their followers?

(٢٦) قَدۡ مَكَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ فَأَتَى ٱللَّهُ بُنۡيَٰنَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡقَوَاعِدِ فَخَرَّ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلسَّقۡفُ مِن فَوۡقِهِمۡ وَأَتَىٰهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

The disbelievers before these people plotted against their messengers. Allah then destroyed their buildings from the foundations, so their roofs fell on them from above. So the torment came to them from where they did not expect as they had expected their buildings to protect them, but instead, they were destroyed by them.

Points of Benefit:

In the verses, there is something great from the types of Allah's blessing upon His slaves, general and detailed, that Allah uses to call the slaves to establish gratitude, remembrance, and supplication to Him.
Equating the one who misguides with the misguided one in terms of the crime of misguidance, For if not for his misguidance, he would have been guided by investigating or asking sincere advisors.
Allah seizes the criminals all of a sudden and most severely because this is extremely terrifying, unlike if it is done gradually.
