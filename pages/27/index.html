(١٧٧) ۞لَّيۡسَ ٱلۡبِرَّ أَن تُوَلُّواْ وُجُوهَكُمۡ قِبَلَ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقِ وَٱلۡمَغۡرِبِ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱلۡبِرَّ مَنۡ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَٱلنَّبِيِّ‍ۧنَ وَءَاتَى ٱلۡمَالَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِۦ ذَوِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينَ وَٱبۡنَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَٱلسَّآئِلِينَ وَفِي ٱلرِّقَابِ وَأَقَامَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتَى ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَٱلۡمُوفُونَ بِعَهۡدِهِمۡ إِذَا عَٰهَدُواْۖ وَٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ فِي ٱلۡبَأۡسَآءِ وَٱلضَّرَّآءِ وَحِينَ ٱلۡبَأۡسِۗ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ صَدَقُواْۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُتَّقُونَ

Doing good that pleases Allah does not just mean turning towards the east or the west or differing over it. Rather, all virtue is in the one who has faith in Allah as the sole deity, the Day of Resurrection, all of the angels, all of the revealed scriptures, and all of the prophets without discrimination. He is the one who spends in charity, despite his love and desire for it, towards his relatives, the orphan (i.e. the one who lost his father before the age of puberty), the poor, the stranger who is cut off from his family and homeland while traveling, those encounter a need that causes them to ask people for help, and to free slaves from enslavement and captivity. He is the one who establishes the prayer and performs it completely as Allah instructed and pays the Zakat that is obligatory on him. They uphold their promises if they make one, have patience through poverty, hardship, illness, and do not flee in the heat of battle. The people described with these characteristics are the ones who are true to Allah in their faith and their actions. They are the ones who are truly pious in that they follow what Allah has commanded them to do and refrain from what Allah has prohibited for them.

(١٧٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُتِبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡقِصَاصُ فِي ٱلۡقَتۡلَىۖ ٱلۡحُرُّ بِٱلۡحُرِّ وَٱلۡعَبۡدُ بِٱلۡعَبۡدِ وَٱلۡأُنثَىٰ بِٱلۡأُنثَىٰۚ فَمَنۡ عُفِيَ لَهُۥ مِنۡ أَخِيهِ شَيۡءٞ فَٱتِّبَاعُۢ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَأَدَآءٌ إِلَيۡهِ بِإِحۡسَٰنٖۗ ذَٰلِكَ تَخۡفِيفٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَرَحۡمَةٞۗ فَمَنِ ٱعۡتَدَىٰ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ فَلَهُۥ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, it is decreed for you that in cases where someone deliberately attacks and kills someone, the punishment for the murderer should be the same as their crime: the freeman is to be killed for the freeman; the slave for the slave; and the female for the female. If the person killed had forgiven before his death, or his guardian has forgiven in return for the payment of blood money (a sum of money paid by the murderer in return for being pardoned), then the person who forgives should ask for the sum of money in a good way, without reminding them of your favors or harm them. The murderer should also pay the sum of money in a good way, without hesitation or delay. Forgiveness and taking blood money instead is a relief and mercy for this Muslim nation. So whoever attacks the murderer after his pardon and the acceptance of the blood money will receive a painful punishment from Allah.

(١٧٩) وَلَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡقِصَاصِ حَيَوٰةٞ يَٰٓأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ

And there is life for you in what Allah has decreed from legal retribution because it spares your blood and prevents further transgressions from occurring between you. Those with foresight and sound judgment will understand this: the ones who are mindful of Allah, keeping to His sacred law and doing as He instructs.

(١٨٠) كُتِبَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِذَا حَضَرَ أَحَدَكُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ إِن تَرَكَ خَيۡرًا ٱلۡوَصِيَّةُ لِلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ وَٱلۡأَقۡرَبِينَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِۖ حَقًّا عَلَى ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

It has been decreed for you that if one of you is showing signs or causes of death and is leaving a large amount of wealth behind, then they should make a will to distribute it to parents and close relatives per the sacred law, which is not to exceed one-third of the wealth (the rest of the wealth would be distributed according to the laws of inheritance). Making this will is a firm duty upon those who are heedful of Allah. This ruling was established before verses about inheritance were revealed. When the verses about inheritance were revealed, they made it clear who was eligible to inherit and how much they could inherit.

(١٨١) فَمَنۢ بَدَّلَهُۥ بَعۡدَ مَا سَمِعَهُۥ فَإِنَّمَآ إِثۡمُهُۥ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يُبَدِّلُونَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٞ

Whoever then changes a will, whether adding to it, subtracting from it, or preventing something after knowing what the will contains, the sin of it will be upon those who altered it and not the owner of the will. Indeed, Allah hears all that His slaves say and knows all that they do, and He is aware of every detail of the situations they are in.

Points of Benefit:

The piety that Allah loves is by realizing faith and righteous deeds. As for just holding onto what can be observed, this is not enough according to Allah, the Exalted.
From the greatest preservers of life and prevention of abuse and oppression is applying the principle of legal retribution that Allah has legislated for taking a life and lesser transgressions.
The great importance of making a will, especially for the one who has something to bequeath, and the sin of the one who alters the will of the deceased and replaces its contents.
