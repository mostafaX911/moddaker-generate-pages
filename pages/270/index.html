(٢٧) ثُمَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ يُخۡزِيهِمۡ وَيَقُولُ أَيۡنَ شُرَكَآءِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ كُنتُمۡ تُشَٰٓقُّونَ فِيهِمۡۚ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ إِنَّ ٱلۡخِزۡيَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ وَٱلسُّوٓءَ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Then, on the Day of Resurrection, Allah will humiliate and disgrace them with the torment. He will ask them: Where are my partners whom you used to associate with me in worship and because of which you used to oppose the prophets and the believers? The righteous scholars will respond: Indeed! On the Day of Resurrection, Humiliation and punishment will befall the disbelievers.

(٢٨) ٱلَّذِينَ تَتَوَفَّىٰهُمُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ ظَالِمِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡۖ فَأَلۡقَوُاْ ٱلسَّلَمَ مَا كُنَّا نَعۡمَلُ مِن سُوٓءِۢۚ بَلَىٰٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمُۢ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

Those whose souls are taken by the angel of death and his helpers while wronging themselves by disbelieving in Allah will submit with total surrender when death comes to them. They will deny the disbelief and sins they used to do, thinking that such denial will benefit them. It will be said to them: You have lied. You were disbelievers who used to commit disobedience. Allah knows everything that you used to do in this world. Nothing is hidden from Him, and He will recompense you for that accordingly.

(٢٩) فَٱدۡخُلُوٓاْ أَبۡوَٰبَ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۖ فَلَبِئۡسَ مَثۡوَى ٱلۡمُتَكَبِّرِينَ

It will be said to them: Enter, based on your deeds, through the doors of Hell to live therein eternally. What an evil place of residence for those too arrogant to believe in Allah and worship Him alone.

(٣٠) ۞وَقِيلَ لِلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْ مَاذَآ أَنزَلَ رَبُّكُمۡۚ قَالُواْ خَيۡرٗاۗ لِّلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ فِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةٞۚ وَلَدَارُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ خَيۡرٞۚ وَلَنِعۡمَ دَارُ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Those who feared their Lord by obeying His commands and avoiding His prohibitions will be asked: What did your Lord send down to your Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم? They will reply: Allah has sent down to him great good. For those who worshipped Allah well and treated His creation well in this worldly life, there is an excellent reward, which includes victory and abundance of provision. And the reward Allah has prepared for them in the Hereafter will be better than what He already gave them in this world. Therefore, what an excellent abode is the abode of the Hereafter for those who fear their Lord by obeying His commands and avoiding His prohibitions!

(٣١) جَنَّٰتُ عَدۡنٖ يَدۡخُلُونَهَا تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۖ لَهُمۡ فِيهَا مَا يَشَآءُونَۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَجۡزِي ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

They will enter gardens of residence and permanence with rivers flowing below their palaces and trees. In these gardens, they will have every type of food, drink, and other pleasures that they desire. This type of reward, which Allah gives to those who fear Him from the nation of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, He also gives to those who feared Him from the previous nations.

(٣٢) ٱلَّذِينَ تَتَوَفَّىٰهُمُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ طَيِّبِينَ يَقُولُونَ سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱدۡخُلُواْ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

Those whose souls are taken by the angel of death and his helpers while their hearts are pure from disbelief, the angels will address them saying: Peace be on you. You are safe from every difficulty. Enter paradise because of the correct belief and righteous deeds you used to do in this world.

(٣٣) هَلۡ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّآ أَن تَأۡتِيَهُمُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ أَوۡ يَأۡتِيَ أَمۡرُ رَبِّكَۚ كَذَٰلِكَ فَعَلَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ وَمَا ظَلَمَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَلَٰكِن كَانُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَظۡلِمُونَ

Are these rejecting polytheists waiting for anything but the angel of death and his helpers to take their souls and strike their faces and backs, or [are they waiting] for Allah's command to eradicate them through torment in this world? Similar to those polytheists who came before who were destroyed because of their polytheism, the polytheists of Mecca may perhaps meet the same fate. Allah did not wrong them when He destroyed them, but they wronged themselves by bringing themselves to the places of destruction because they disbelieved in Allah.

(٣٤) فَأَصَابَهُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتُ مَا عَمِلُواْ وَحَاقَ بِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

So, the consequences of the deeds they used to do came down on them, and they were surrounded by the torment they used to mock whenever they were reminded of it.

Points of Benefit:

The virtue of the people of knowledge is that they speak the Truth in this life and on the Day of bearing witness, and that Allah and His creation consider their words.
From the etiquette of the angels with Allah is that they ascribe knowledge to Allah without saying: We knew what you used to do. They also do this to show that they only knew that because Allah, the Exalted, taught them.
From Allah's bounty and generosity, He gives the people of Paradise everything they wish. He even reminds them of blessings their hearts never considered.
Actions are the reason and basis for entering Paradise and being saved from the Fire, and that occurs due to Allah's mercy and grace upon the believers, not due to their power and strength.
