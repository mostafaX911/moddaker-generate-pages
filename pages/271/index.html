(٣٥) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَشۡرَكُواْ لَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مَا عَبَدۡنَا مِن دُونِهِۦ مِن شَيۡءٖ نَّحۡنُ وَلَآ ءَابَآؤُنَا وَلَا حَرَّمۡنَا مِن دُونِهِۦ مِن شَيۡءٖۚ كَذَٰلِكَ فَعَلَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ فَهَلۡ عَلَى ٱلرُّسُلِ إِلَّا ٱلۡبَلَٰغُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

Those who associated others with Allah in worship said: Had Allah willed for us to worship Him alone and not to associate any partners with Him, we would not have worshipped anything besides Him, neither us nor our forefathers before us. Had He willed for us not to regard anything unlawful, we would not have done so. The disbelievers of the past also mentioned such false arguments. The duty of the messengers is only to convey what they have been ordered to clearly, and they have done so. The disbelievers cannot use the decree of Allah as an argument after Allah has given them free will and choice and sent His messengers to them.

(٣٦) وَلَقَدۡ بَعَثۡنَا فِي كُلِّ أُمَّةٖ رَّسُولًا أَنِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱجۡتَنِبُواْ ٱلطَّٰغُوتَۖ فَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ هَدَى ٱللَّهُ وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ حَقَّتۡ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلضَّلَٰلَةُۚ فَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَٱنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

We had sent to every previous nation a messenger to command his nation to worship Allah alone and abandon the worship of others besides Him, such as idols, devils, etc. Allah guided some of them; they believed in Him and followed what His messenger brought. Others rejected Allah and went against the messenger. Thus, He did not assist them, and misguidance befell them. So travel through the earth to see for yourselves what the result of those who rejected [Allah] was after the torment and destruction came upon them.

(٣٧) إِن تَحۡرِصۡ عَلَىٰ هُدَىٰهُمۡ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي مَن يُضِلُّۖ وَمَا لَهُم مِّن نَّٰصِرِينَ

Even though, O Messenger, you try everything to invite these people and are eager for them to be guided, Allah will not direct those whom He misguides to guidance. They will have no one besides Allah to assist them in averting the torment away from them.

(٣٨) وَأَقۡسَمُواْ بِٱللَّهِ جَهۡدَ أَيۡمَٰنِهِمۡ لَا يَبۡعَثُ ٱللَّهُ مَن يَمُوتُۚ بَلَىٰ وَعۡدًا عَلَيۡهِ حَقّٗا وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

These people who rejected the resurrection swore their most solemn oaths: Allah will not resurrect anyone who dies. At the same time, they have no proof supporting their claim. Yes, Allah will resurrect everyone who dies, this being a binding promise. However, most people do not know that Allah will resurrect the dead, and they, therefore, deny the resurrection.

(٣٩) لِيُبَيِّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي يَخۡتَلِفُونَ فِيهِ وَلِيَعۡلَمَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ كَٰذِبِينَ

Allah will resurrect them all on the Day of Resurrection to make clear to them the reality of the things they differed about, such as His Oneness, resurrection, and prophethood, and so that the disbelievers may realize that they were lying in their claim that Allah has partners and their denial of the resurrection.

(٤٠) إِنَّمَا قَوۡلُنَا لِشَيۡءٍ إِذَآ أَرَدۡنَٰهُ أَن نَّقُولَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ

When We want to bring the dead to life and resurrect them, nothing will prevent Us from doing so. When We want something to happen, all We say is: Be! And it is!

(٤١) وَٱلَّذِينَ هَاجَرُواْ فِي ٱللَّهِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا ظُلِمُواْ لَنُبَوِّئَنَّهُمۡ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةٗۖ وَلَأَجۡرُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ أَكۡبَرُۚ لَوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ

Those who left their homes, families, and wealth and emigrated from the land of disbelief to the land of Islam in search of Allah's pleasure after having been persecuted and punished by the disbelievers, We will make them settle in an abode in this world in which they will be honored. However, the reward of the Hereafter is greater because it includes Paradise. If those who remained behind knew the reward of those who emigrated, they would not have stayed behind.

(٤٢) ٱلَّذِينَ صَبَرُواْ وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ

These emigrants in the path of Allah are the ones who were patient when harmed by their people and separated from their families and lands. They were patient in Allah's obedience. They rely only on their Lord in all their affairs, and Allah gave them this great recompense.

Points of Benefit:

The wise person considers and learns from what happened to the misguided and the deniers and how their condition led to ruin, devastation, torment, and destruction.
The wisdom of the resurrection and the return is for Allah to expose the Truth regarding the resurrection and everything that the people differed about.
The virtue of patience and reliance upon Allah (tawakkul). As for patience, it is due to the self-control it involves. Meanwhile, the virtue of tawakkul is that it requires trust in Allah, the Exalted, and being attached to Him.
Al-Muhajireen (the Emigrants) who left their homes and wealth, were patient upon harm, and relied upon their Lord are rewarded with a better home, good status, a content life, excellent and plentiful sustenance, support against their enemies, and leadership over the lands and the people.
