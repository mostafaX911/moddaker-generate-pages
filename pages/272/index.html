(٤٣) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ إِلَّا رِجَالٗا نُّوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡۖ فَسۡ‍َٔلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلذِّكۡرِ إِن كُنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

We did not send before you, O Messenger, except men who were human beings, to whom We sent revelation. We did not send messengers that were angels. This is Our constant way. If you [people] deny that, ask the people of the previous books, and they will tell you if you did not know that messengers were only human beings; they were not angels.

(٤٤) بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَٱلزُّبُرِۗ وَأَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلذِّكۡرَ لِتُبَيِّنَ لِلنَّاسِ مَا نُزِّلَ إِلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ

We sent these human messengers with clear signs and revealed books. We have revealed to you, O Messenger, the Quran so that you can clarify to the people that which requires clarification and so that they can apply their minds and take lessons from its contents.

(٤٥) أَفَأَمِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ مَكَرُواْ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِ أَن يَخۡسِفَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ أَوۡ يَأۡتِيَهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

Do those who make plots to prevent people from Allah's path feel secure that Allah will not cause the Earth to cave in with them as He caused it to cave in with Qarun, or that the torment will not come to them from where they least expect?

(٤٦) أَوۡ يَأۡخُذَهُمۡ فِي تَقَلُّبِهِمۡ فَمَا هُم بِمُعۡجِزِينَ

Or [do they feel secure] that the torment will not afflict them while they go about their journeys and quest for earnings? They will not escape nor protect themselves from it.

(٤٧) أَوۡ يَأۡخُذَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ تَخَوُّفٖ فَإِنَّ رَبَّكُمۡ لَرَءُوفٞ رَّحِيمٌ

Or do they feel secure that Allah's torment will not come to them while they fear it? Allah can torment them in all conditions. Your Lord is compassionate and merciful. He does not rush to punish His slaves; perhaps they will repent to Him.

(٤٨) أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ مَا خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ مِن شَيۡءٖ يَتَفَيَّؤُاْ ظِلَٰلُهُۥ عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ وَٱلشَّمَآئِلِ سُجَّدٗا لِّلَّهِ وَهُمۡ دَٰخِرُونَ

Do these people who reject not look with contemplation at Allah's creation? Their shadows incline to the right and the left, following the sun's movement in the day and that of the moon at night, in humility and literal prostration to their Lord while they are submissive to Him.

(٤٩) وَلِلَّهِۤ يَسۡجُدُۤ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِن دَآبَّةٖ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ

It is to Allah alone that all creatures in the heavens and on Earth prostrate. It is to Him alone that the angels prostrate. They are not too proud to worship Allah and obey Him.

(٥٠) يَخَافُونَ رَبَّهُم مِّن فَوۡقِهِمۡ وَيَفۡعَلُونَ مَا يُؤۡمَرُونَ۩

Despite their constant worship and obedience, they fear their Lord (who is above them in His essence, Dominance, and Authority) and carry out acts of obedience their Lord commands them to do.

(٥١) ۞وَقَالَ ٱللَّهُ لَا تَتَّخِذُوٓاْ إِلَٰهَيۡنِ ٱثۡنَيۡنِۖ إِنَّمَا هُوَ إِلَٰهٞ وَٰحِدٞ فَإِيَّٰيَ فَٱرۡهَبُونِ

Allah (may He be Exalted) said to all His slaves: Do not take two beings of worship. The being deserving of worship is only One who has no second nor any partner. So fear only Me and do not fear others besides Me.

(٥٢) وَلَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَهُ ٱلدِّينُ وَاصِبًاۚ أَفَغَيۡرَ ٱللَّهِ تَتَّقُونَ

To Him alone belongs everything in the heavens and on Earth; He created, owns, and plans everything. To Him alone, obedience, submission, and sincerity are always due. Will you, then, fear anyone other than Allah? No, instead, fear Him alone!

(٥٣) وَمَا بِكُم مِّن نِّعۡمَةٖ فَمِنَ ٱللَّهِۖ ثُمَّ إِذَا مَسَّكُمُ ٱلضُّرُّ فَإِلَيۡهِ تَجۡ‍َٔرُونَ

Whatever blessing you possess, O people, whether religious or worldly, is from Allah (may He be Exalted) and not from anyone else. Then, when some calamity, sickness, or poverty afflicts you, it is to Him alone that you plead in supplication to remove what has afflicted you. Thus, the One who grants favors and removes difficulties is the One Who deserves to be worshipped alone.

(٥٤) ثُمَّ إِذَا كَشَفَ ٱلضُّرَّ عَنكُمۡ إِذَا فَرِيقٞ مِّنكُم بِرَبِّهِمۡ يُشۡرِكُونَ

When He answers your supplications and removes harm from you, a group of you suddenly begin associating partners with their Lord and worshipping others with Him. What unjust behavior is this?

Points of Benefit:

The wrongdoers should be shy from their Lord because the blessings of Allah are coming down to them at every moment while their sins are ascending to their Lord all the time.
The people of disbelief, rejection, and all types of sin should fear that Allah, the Exalted, may seize them by surprise to torment them while they are unaware.
All blessings are from Allah, the Exalted, whether material like sustenance, safety, and health or abstract like security, honor, prestige, etc.
Man can find no refuge to remove harm at times of hardship except Allah, the Exalted. So, he is full of supplications to Him because he knows that no one can remove distress besides Him.
