(٥٥) لِيَكۡفُرُواْ بِمَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمۡۚ فَتَمَتَّعُواْ فَسَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ

Their associating partners with Allah meant that they rejected Allah's favors upon them, including Allah removing their distress. It was said to them: Enjoy the favors you have been given until Allah's torment comes to you sooner or later.

(٥٦) وَيَجۡعَلُونَ لِمَا لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ نَصِيبٗا مِّمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡۗ تَٱللَّهِ لَتُسۡ‍َٔلُنَّ عَمَّا كُنتُمۡ تَفۡتَرُونَ

The polytheists allocate to their idols, which do not know anything as they are lifeless objects that can neither benefit nor harm, a portion of their wealth that We have provided for them as a means of seeking closeness to those idols. By Allah, O polytheists, you will be questioned on the Day of Resurrection about your false claims that these idols are deities and that they are entitled to a portion of your wealth.

(٥٧) وَيَجۡعَلُونَ لِلَّهِ ٱلۡبَنَٰتِ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥ وَلَهُم مَّا يَشۡتَهُونَ

The polytheists attribute daughters to Allah, believing that these are the angels. Thus, they attribute lineage to Allah and ascribe to him what they do not like for themselves. He is Pure, Exalted, and Sanctified from what they ascribe to Him. Then, they ascribe to themselves the male offspring that they desire. What crime is greater than this?!

(٥٨) وَإِذَا بُشِّرَ أَحَدُهُم بِٱلۡأُنثَىٰ ظَلَّ وَجۡهُهُۥ مُسۡوَدّٗا وَهُوَ كَظِيمٞ

When one of these polytheists is given the news of the birth of a female, his face darkens due to the extreme dislike of such news, and his heart is filled with worry and grief. Then, he attributes to Allah what he does not like for himself?!

(٥٩) يَتَوَٰرَىٰ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ مِن سُوٓءِ مَا بُشِّرَ بِهِۦٓۚ أَيُمۡسِكُهُۥ عَلَىٰ هُونٍ أَمۡ يَدُسُّهُۥ فِي ٱلتُّرَابِۗ أَلَا سَآءَ مَا يَحۡكُمُونَ

He hides from his people because of the bad news of the birth of a girl that was given to him. He asks himself: Shall he keep this girl he was given news of with humiliation and dejection, or shall he bury her alive and hide her in the sand? How evil the polytheists' judgment is when they assigned to their Lord what they disliked for themselves.

(٦٠) لِلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ مَثَلُ ٱلسَّوۡءِۖ وَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡمَثَلُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

The disbelievers who do not believe in the Hereafter have evil attributes, such as ignorance, disbelief, and the need to have offspring. Allah has lofty, praiseworthy attributes of majesty, perfection, independence, and knowledge. He (may He be Exalted) is Mighty in His dominion, and nothing can overpower Him. He is Wise in His creation, planning, and legislation.

(٦١) وَلَوۡ يُؤَاخِذُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلنَّاسَ بِظُلۡمِهِم مَّا تَرَكَ عَلَيۡهَا مِن دَآبَّةٖ وَلَٰكِن يُؤَخِّرُهُمۡ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۖ فَإِذَا جَآءَ أَجَلُهُمۡ لَا يَسۡتَ‍ٔۡخِرُونَ سَاعَةٗ وَلَا يَسۡتَقۡدِمُونَ

If Allah (may He be Exalted) were to punish people for their wrongs and rejection of Him, He would not leave behind a single human or animal on Earth that walks on its surface. But He (may He be Exalted) delays them until a fixed time per His knowledge. Then, when that appointed time, per His knowledge, comes, they will not be able to delay it nor advance it for even a short while.

(٦٢) وَيَجۡعَلُونَ لِلَّهِ مَا يَكۡرَهُونَۚ وَتَصِفُ أَلۡسِنَتُهُمُ ٱلۡكَذِبَ أَنَّ لَهُمُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۚ لَا جَرَمَ أَنَّ لَهُمُ ٱلنَّارَ وَأَنَّهُم مُّفۡرَطُونَ

They assign females to Allah (may He be Exalted) females, which they dislike to attribute to themselves. They falsely claim that if they are resurrected, they will have the best rank with Allah. Truly, for them is the Fire, and they will be left therein, never to come out of it.

(٦٣) تَٱللَّهِ لَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ أُمَمٖ مِّن قَبۡلِكَ فَزَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ فَهُوَ وَلِيُّهُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

By Allah, We have sent messengers to nations before you, O Messenger. However, the Devil made their evil deeds of polytheism, disbelief, and sins attractive to them. He is their so-called helper on the Day of Judgment, so let them ask him for help. They will have a painful torment on the Day of Resurrection.

(٦٤) وَمَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ إِلَّا لِتُبَيِّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي ٱخۡتَلَفُواْ فِيهِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

We did not reveal the Quran to you, O Messenger, except for you to clarify to all people what they differ about in respect of Allah's Oneness, resurrection, and the details of sacred law, and for the Quran to be a guidance and mercy for those who believe in Allah, His messengers and what the Quran has brought, for they are the ones who will benefit from the truth.

Points of Benefit:

From the ignorant practices of the polytheists is ascribing daughters to Allah, the Exalted, while ascribing sons to themselves. As well as their anguish from having girls and their faces changing out of sadness and grief over having them. One of them would hide away and not meet any of his people due to the severe grief, disgrace, dishonor, and shame that he would experience due to having a girl.
One of Allah's divine practices is to give respite to the disbeliever and not punish him to give him a chance to believe and repent.
The main duty of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم is to clarify what has come in the Quran and to explain the aspects of religion and rulings that the people of desires and different religions differed about so that the proof can be established upon them by that clarification.
