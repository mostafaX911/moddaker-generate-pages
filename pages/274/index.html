(٦٥) وَٱللَّهُ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَحۡيَا بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَآۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَسۡمَعُونَ

Allah sends down rain from the sky, with which He revives the Earth by bringing forth vegetation from it after it has been dry and arid. The sending down of the rain from the sky and bringing forth the vegetation of the Earth through it is a clear indication of Allah's power for people who listen to the Speech of Allah and ponder over it.

(٦٦) وَإِنَّ لَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ لَعِبۡرَةٗۖ نُّسۡقِيكُم مِّمَّا فِي بُطُونِهِۦ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ فَرۡثٖ وَدَمٖ لَّبَنًا خَالِصٗا سَآئِغٗا لِّلشَّٰرِبِينَ

There is a lesson for you to take, O people, in camels, cows, and goats: We give you milk to drink from their udders that comes out between the waste that the stomach contains and the blood in the body. But, despite this, it comes out as pure milk that is tasty and pleasant to those who drink it.

(٦٧) وَمِن ثَمَرَٰتِ ٱلنَّخِيلِ وَٱلۡأَعۡنَٰبِ تَتَّخِذُونَ مِنۡهُ سَكَرٗا وَرِزۡقًا حَسَنًاۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ

There is a lesson for you in date palm trees and grapes that We provide for you. From them, you make intoxicants that affect the mind, which is not good, and you also take from them a good provision that benefits you, such as dates, raisins, vinegar, and molasses. In this, there truly is an indication of Allah's power and favor upon His slaves for people who use their reason, for they are the ones who reflect.

(٦٨) وَأَوۡحَىٰ رَبُّكَ إِلَى ٱلنَّحۡلِ أَنِ ٱتَّخِذِي مِنَ ٱلۡجِبَالِ بُيُوتٗا وَمِنَ ٱلشَّجَرِ وَمِمَّا يَعۡرِشُونَ

Your Lord, O Messenger, inspired the bees and guided them [saying]: Make homes for yourselves in the mountains, the trees, and the structures and shelters that people build.

(٦٩) ثُمَّ كُلِي مِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ فَٱسۡلُكِي سُبُلَ رَبِّكِ ذُلُلٗاۚ يَخۡرُجُ مِنۢ بُطُونِهَا شَرَابٞ مُّخۡتَلِفٌ أَلۡوَٰنُهُۥ فِيهِ شِفَآءٞ لِّلنَّاسِۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ

[Addressing the bees]: Then eat the fruits you desire, and go along the pathways your Lord inspires you to take that are made subservient for you. From the bellies of those bees emerges honey of varying colors, such as white and yellow, among others, containing a cure for people they use to treat diseases. Such inspiration of bees and the honey that emerges from their bellies indicate Allah's power and planning His creation's affairs for people who think, for they are the ones who derive lessons.

(٧٠) وَٱللَّهُ خَلَقَكُمۡ ثُمَّ يَتَوَفَّىٰكُمۡۚ وَمِنكُم مَّن يُرَدُّ إِلَىٰٓ أَرۡذَلِ ٱلۡعُمُرِ لِكَيۡ لَا يَعۡلَمَ بَعۡدَ عِلۡمٖ شَيۡ‍ًٔاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمٞ قَدِيرٞ

Allah created you without precedent. He will then cause you to die when your life's term ends. The lifespan of some of you will be extended to the worst stage of life, which is severe old age when a person will no longer know anything about what he used to know. Allah is Omniscient, and no deed of His slaves is hidden from Him. He is Omnipotent, and nothing can overpower Him.

(٧١) وَٱللَّهُ فَضَّلَ بَعۡضَكُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ فِي ٱلرِّزۡقِۚ فَمَا ٱلَّذِينَ فُضِّلُواْ بِرَآدِّي رِزۡقِهِمۡ عَلَىٰ مَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُهُمۡ فَهُمۡ فِيهِ سَوَآءٌۚ أَفَبِنِعۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِ يَجۡحَدُونَ

Allah, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, has preferred some of you over others in the provision that He has granted you, making some among you rich and others poor, some of you leaders and others subjects. However, those to whom Allah has given preference in provision are not willing to pass on what Allah has given them to their slaves so that these slaves become equal partners in their ownership. How, then, are they pleased for Allah to have partners from among His slaves when they are not pleased with it for themselves to have partners from amongst their slaves who will be equal to them? What injustice is this, and what rejection of Allah's favors can be worse than this?!

(٧٢) وَٱللَّهُ جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا وَجَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِكُم بَنِينَ وَحَفَدَةٗ وَرَزَقَكُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِۚ أَفَبِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ يُؤۡمِنُونَ وَبِنِعۡمَتِ ٱللَّهِ هُمۡ يَكۡفُرُونَ

Allah has made for you, O people, spouses from the same species as you whose company you can enjoy. He has made for you from your spouses, children, and grandchildren; and has provided you with wholesome foods such as meat, grains, and fruit. Will they then falsely believe in idols and statues, reject Allah's innumerable favors, and not be grateful to Allah through belief in Him alone?!

Points of Benefit:

He, the Exalted, has made the fruits of palm trees and vines beneficial for the slaves. He has placed benefits in the different types of sustenance that the slaves eat: fresh, ripe, available, stored, food and drink.
In the creation of the tiny bee from whose belly comes delicious honey of different colors according to its land and what it eats from is evidence of Allah's perfected care and complete kindness for His slaves and that no one should be worshipped or called upon except Him.
From Allah's great blessings upon His slaves, He made wives for them to find comfort in, and from those wives made children to be the coolness of their eyes, serve them, fulfill their needs, and that they can benefit from in many ways.
