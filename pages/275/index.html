(٧٣) وَيَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا يَمۡلِكُ لَهُمۡ رِزۡقٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ

These polytheists worship idols besides Allah, who have no power to provide them with any provision from the heavens or the Earth. They will never be able to do so because they are inanimate objects with no life or knowledge.

(٧٤) فَلَا تَضۡرِبُواْ لِلَّهِ ٱلۡأَمۡثَالَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

So, O People, do not make Allah similar to these idols that do neither benefit nor harm. Allah has no likeness for you to associate others with Him in worship. Allah knows the attributes of Majesty and Perfection that He possesses. You do not know that, so you associate partners with Him and claim that your idols are similar to Him.

(٧٥) ۞ضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلًا عَبۡدٗا مَّمۡلُوكٗا لَّا يَقۡدِرُ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٖ وَمَن رَّزَقۡنَٰهُ مِنَّا رِزۡقًا حَسَنٗا فَهُوَ يُنفِقُ مِنۡهُ سِرّٗا وَجَهۡرًاۖ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوُۥنَۚ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِۚ بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

Allah (may He be Exalted) presents an example to refute the polytheists: A bonded slave that is unable to do anything and who has nothing to spend compared to a free person to whom We have given lawful wealth that he uses as he wills and spends from it secretly and openly as he wills. These two men are not equal. How, then, do you make Allah, Who is the Owner of everything, who does what He wills in His dominion, similar to those powerless idols? Praise be to Allah, Who is deserving of all praise. However, most polytheists do not recognize Allah's Oneness in His divinity and that He deserves to be worshipped alone.

(٧٦) وَضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا رَّجُلَيۡنِ أَحَدُهُمَآ أَبۡكَمُ لَا يَقۡدِرُ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٖ وَهُوَ كَلٌّ عَلَىٰ مَوۡلَىٰهُ أَيۡنَمَا يُوَجِّههُّ لَا يَأۡتِ بِخَيۡرٍ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِي هُوَ وَمَن يَأۡمُرُ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

Allah (may He be Exalted) presents another example to refute them, which is the example of two men: One of them is mute and cannot hear, speak, or understand due to his deafness and muteness. He is unable to benefit himself or others. He is a heavy burden on the one in charge of him who looks after him. Whichever direction he sends him, he brings no good and does not attain the objective. Can the person whose condition is like this be equal to one who is sound of hearing and speech and who benefits others? He instructs people to be just, and he is upright. He is on a clear path that has no confusion or crookedness. How then do you, O polytheist, equate Allah, Who possesses the attributes of majesty and perfection, with your idols who cannot hear or speak and who cannot bring any benefit or remove any harm?!

(٧٧) وَلِلَّهِ غَيۡبُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَمَآ أَمۡرُ ٱلسَّاعَةِ إِلَّا كَلَمۡحِ ٱلۡبَصَرِ أَوۡ هُوَ أَقۡرَبُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

To Allah alone belongs the knowledge of the unseen of the heavens and the Earth. He has the exclusive knowledge of that as opposed to anyone of His creation. The coming of the Hour is only known to Allah; when He wills, it will come quicker than in the blink of an eye, or rather, it is even closer than that. Allah has power over everything. Nothing can overpower Him. When He intends something, He says: Be, and it is.

(٧٨) وَٱللَّهُ أَخۡرَجَكُم مِّنۢ بُطُونِ أُمَّهَٰتِكُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَجَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَٱلۡأَفۡ‍ِٔدَةَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

Allah brought you, O people, out of your mothers' wombs after the completion of the term of pregnancy as babies unable to comprehend anything. And He gave you hearing to hear with, eyesight to see with, and hearts to understand, with the hope that you may be grateful to Him for these favors that He has given you.

(٧٩) أَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ إِلَى ٱلطَّيۡرِ مُسَخَّرَٰتٖ فِي جَوِّ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَا يُمۡسِكُهُنَّ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Have the polytheists not seen the birds that are subdued and designed to fly in the air because of the wings that Allah granted them, along with the lightness and delicate nature of the air? He inspired them to flap their wings. No one keeps them from falling out of the sky except Allah, the Powerful. This subjugation and holding them there from falling are indications for people who believe in Allah because they are the ones who benefit from such signs and lessons.

Points of Benefit:

Allah, the Exalted, has great wisdom in dividing provision between His slaves, as he makes them rich, poor, and in the middle so that the universe can be integrated, people can coexist, and some of them can serve others.
The two examples in the verses show the misguidance of the polytheists and the falsehood of worshipping idols because the deity that is worshipped should be a capable sovereign able to dispose of things, benefit those who worship him, and command good and justice.
Some of His, the Exalted's blessings and demonstrations of His capability is that He created mankind in their mother's belly, not knowing anything. Then, He gives them the means to know and learn, which are hearing, sight, and hearts, through which they know and realize.
