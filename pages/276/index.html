(٨٠) وَٱللَّهُ جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنۢ بُيُوتِكُمۡ سَكَنٗا وَجَعَلَ لَكُم مِّن جُلُودِ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ بُيُوتٗا تَسۡتَخِفُّونَهَا يَوۡمَ ظَعۡنِكُمۡ وَيَوۡمَ إِقَامَتِكُمۡ وَمِنۡ أَصۡوَافِهَا وَأَوۡبَارِهَا وَأَشۡعَارِهَآ أَثَٰثٗا وَمَتَٰعًا إِلَىٰ حِينٖ

Allah (may He be Exalted) has made for you the homes you build out of stone and other items to settle and rest therein. He has made tents and canopies for you in the desert from the skins of camels, cows, and goats that are like urban homes. These are light for you to carry when you travel from one place to another and easy to erect when you camp. He has made for you, from the wool of sheep and the hair of camels and goats, furnishings for your homes, garments, and coverings that you can enjoy for a limited time.

(٨١) وَٱللَّهُ جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّمَّا خَلَقَ ظِلَٰلٗا وَجَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡجِبَالِ أَكۡنَٰنٗا وَجَعَلَ لَكُمۡ سَرَٰبِيلَ تَقِيكُمُ ٱلۡحَرَّ وَسَرَٰبِيلَ تَقِيكُم بَأۡسَكُمۡۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُتِمُّ نِعۡمَتَهُۥ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُسۡلِمُونَ

Allah has made the trees and buildings you use as shade from the heat. He has made for you tunnels, caverns, and caves in the mountains that you use as a cover from the cold, heat, and your enemies. He has made shirts and robes for you from cotton and other materials that protect you from the heat and cold. He has made armor that protects you from the strikes of others in war so that their weapons do not pierce your bodies. Just as Allah has given you the above favors, He completes His favors upon you in the hope that you will submit to Him alone and not associate anything as partners with Him.

(٨٢) فَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَإِنَّمَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡبَلَٰغُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

If they turn away from believing in you and accepting what you have brought, then your responsibility, O Messenger, is only to clearly convey what you have been ordered. It is not your duty to guide.

(٨٣) يَعۡرِفُونَ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ يُنكِرُونَهَا وَأَكۡثَرُهُمُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ

The polytheists recognize the favors Allah has given them, such as sending the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم. Still, they then deny His favors by not giving thanks to Him and rejecting His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم. Most of them reject His favors; may He be Exalted.

(٨٤) وَيَوۡمَ نَبۡعَثُ مِن كُلِّ أُمَّةٖ شَهِيدٗا ثُمَّ لَا يُؤۡذَنُ لِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَلَا هُمۡ يُسۡتَعۡتَبُونَ

Remember, O Messenger, the Day when Allah will resurrect the messenger sent to every nation to testify of the belief of those who believed and the disbelief of those who disbelieved. After that, those who disbelieved will not be allowed to present an excuse for their rejection, and they will not be returned to the world to do deeds pleasing to their Lord, as the Hereafter is the place of reckoning and not the place of deeds.

(٨٥) وَإِذَا رَءَا ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ فَلَا يُخَفَّفُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يُنظَرُونَ

When the wrongdoing polytheists see the torment, it will not be lightened for them, nor will they be given respite by it being delayed. Instead, they will enter it to live therein eternally.

(٨٦) وَإِذَا رَءَا ٱلَّذِينَ أَشۡرَكُواْ شُرَكَآءَهُمۡ قَالُواْ رَبَّنَا هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ شُرَكَآؤُنَا ٱلَّذِينَ كُنَّا نَدۡعُواْ مِن دُونِكَۖ فَأَلۡقَوۡاْ إِلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقَوۡلَ إِنَّكُمۡ لَكَٰذِبُونَ

When the polytheists see the false deities they used to worship besides Allah, in the Hereafter, they will say: O our Lord! These are our partners that we used to worship besides You. They will say this to make them carry their burdens. Allah will cause their deities to speak, and they will respond to them by saying: You, O polytheists, were liars in worshipping a partner with Allah, for He has no partner deserving of worship.

(٨٧) وَأَلۡقَوۡاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ يَوۡمَئِذٍ ٱلسَّلَمَۖ وَضَلَّ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ

The polytheists will surrender and submit to Allah alone, and all the fabricated claims that their idols will intercede for them before Allah will disappear.

Points of Benefit:

The verses show the permissibility of using wool, furs, and hair in any situation, including using them for housing and furniture.
Having abundant blessings is a reason to bring about more gratitude and praise for Allah, the Exalted, from the slave.
The one who can bear witness against every nation is the purest and most just witness. They are the Messengers who, after their testimony, the ruling against their people will be final.
In His, the Exalted's, statement: (and garments to protect you from your [enemy in] battle) is evidence for the slaves taking up equipment for Jihad to use them to fight their enemies.
