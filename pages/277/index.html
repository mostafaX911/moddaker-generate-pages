(٨٨) ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَصَدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ زِدۡنَٰهُمۡ عَذَابٗا فَوۡقَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ بِمَا كَانُواْ يُفۡسِدُونَ

For those who disbelieved and turned others away from the path of Allah, We will increase their torment above and beyond what was due to them because of their disbelief, corruption, and corrupting others by misleading them.

(٨٩) وَيَوۡمَ نَبۡعَثُ فِي كُلِّ أُمَّةٖ شَهِيدًا عَلَيۡهِم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِهِمۡۖ وَجِئۡنَا بِكَ شَهِيدًا عَلَىٰ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِۚ وَنَزَّلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ تِبۡيَٰنٗا لِّكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٗ وَبُشۡرَىٰ لِلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ

Remember, O Messenger, the Day We will raise a messenger from every nation who will testify against them about their rejection or belief. This messenger will be from them and speak their language. We have brought you, O Messenger, as a witness against all nations. We revealed the Quran to you to clarify everything that requires clarification of the lawful and unlawful, reward and punishment, etc. We also revealed it as a guidance to the truth for people, as a mercy to those who believe in it and practice what it contains, and as the good news of everlasting bliss that awaits those who believe in Allah.

(٩٠) ۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأۡمُرُ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِ وَٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنِ وَإِيتَآيِٕ ذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَيَنۡهَىٰ عَنِ ٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِ وَٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱلۡبَغۡيِۚ يَعِظُكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ

Allah orders His slaves to be just by discharging Allah's rights and the rights of the slaves of Allah and by not giving preference in judgment to anyone unless with a right that demands such preference. He commands them to be good by doing more than what is mandatory, such as voluntarily spending and forgiving the oppressor. He instructs them to see to the needs of their relatives. He forbids everything vile, in words, such as obscene speech, or deeds, such as zina (illicit sexual relations). He forbids oppression and arrogance over people. Allah advises you with the commands and prohibitions in this verse, hoping you will heed His advice.

(٩١) وَأَوۡفُواْ بِعَهۡدِ ٱللَّهِ إِذَا عَٰهَدتُّمۡ وَلَا تَنقُضُواْ ٱلۡأَيۡمَٰنَ بَعۡدَ تَوۡكِيدِهَا وَقَدۡ جَعَلۡتُمُ ٱللَّهَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ كَفِيلًاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ مَا تَفۡعَلُونَ

Fulfill every pledge that you have made with Allah or with people. Do not break oaths after binding them by swearing by Allah when you have made Allah a witness to the oath you swore. Allah knows what you do. Nothing is hidden from Him, and He will recompense you for that accordingly.

(٩٢) وَلَا تَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّتِي نَقَضَتۡ غَزۡلَهَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ قُوَّةٍ أَنكَٰثٗا تَتَّخِذُونَ أَيۡمَٰنَكُمۡ دَخَلَۢا بَيۡنَكُمۡ أَن تَكُونَ أُمَّةٌ هِيَ أَرۡبَىٰ مِنۡ أُمَّةٍۚ إِنَّمَا يَبۡلُوكُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦۚ وَلَيُبَيِّنَنَّ لَكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ مَا كُنتُمۡ فِيهِ تَخۡتَلِفُونَ

Do not be foolish and dim-witted by breaking your pledges, like a lady who tired herself by spinning her wool or cotton. After it was firmly spun, she broke it open and unraveled it! She exhausted herself by spinning and then breaking it and did not obtain the desired result. You make your oaths as a means of deception to deceive one another so that your nation can be more in number and stronger than the nation of your enemies. Allah only tests you with fulfilling your pledges; will you fulfill or break them? Allah will make clear to you on the Day of Resurrection those things you differed about in this world. He will distinguish those who were on truth from those who were on falsehood and the truthful from the liars.

(٩٣) وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَجَعَلَكُمۡ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ وَلَٰكِن يُضِلُّ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَلَتُسۡ‍َٔلُنَّ عَمَّا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

Had Allah willed, He would have made you one nation in agreement on the truth, but He (may He be Exalted) leads astray, through His justice, whom He wills from the truth and from fulfilling their oaths. He guides, through His grace, whom He wills. You will surely be questioned on the Day of Resurrection about what you used to do in this world.

Points of Benefit:

The disbelievers who block others from Allah's path will have multiplied torment due to corrupting the Earth with disbelief and disobedience.
The Earth is not devoid of people of righteousness and knowledge. They are the leaders of guidance, the inheritors of the Prophets. These scholars preserve the legislation of the Prophets.
These verses define the foundations of Muslim society, in private and public life, for the individual, the community, and the nation.
The prohibition of bribery and taking money to break a covenant.
