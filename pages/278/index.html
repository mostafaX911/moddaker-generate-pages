(٩٤) وَلَا تَتَّخِذُوٓاْ أَيۡمَٰنَكُمۡ دَخَلَۢا بَيۡنَكُمۡ فَتَزِلَّ قَدَمُۢ بَعۡدَ ثُبُوتِهَا وَتَذُوقُواْ ٱلسُّوٓءَ بِمَا صَدَدتُّمۡ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَكُمۡ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٞ

Do not make your oaths a means of deception, allowing you to trick and deceive one another, following your desires - breaking them whenever you wish and fulfilling them whenever you wish. If you do so, your feet will slip from the straight path after being firm on it. You will taste the torment because of your deviation from Allah's path and misleading others, and you will receive torment that has been multiplied.

(٩٥) وَلَا تَشۡتَرُواْ بِعَهۡدِ ٱللَّهِ ثَمَنٗا قَلِيلًاۚ إِنَّمَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ هُوَ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

Do not exchange the pledge you took in Allah's name for a small amount, failing to abide by it. The victory and booty that Allah has in this world and the everlasting bliss that He has in the Hereafter are better for you than the small amount that you take for breaking your pledge; if only you realized that.

(٩٦) مَا عِندَكُمۡ يَنفَدُ وَمَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ بَاقٖۗ وَلَنَجۡزِيَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ صَبَرُوٓاْ أَجۡرَهُم بِأَحۡسَنِ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

The wealth, pleasures, and luxuries you have, O people, will end no matter how much it may be. But the reward with Allah will remain. How, then, do you prefer that which is fleeting over that which is lasting? We will certainly reward those who remain steadfast on their pledges and do not break them with a reward according to the best of what they used to do by multiplying it by ten to seven hundred times or even more.

(٩٧) مَنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا مِّن ذَكَرٍ أَوۡ أُنثَىٰ وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنٞ فَلَنُحۡيِيَنَّهُۥ حَيَوٰةٗ طَيِّبَةٗۖ وَلَنَجۡزِيَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡرَهُم بِأَحۡسَنِ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

Whoever does righteous deeds in accordance with sacred law, whether male or female, while believing in Allah, We will grant them a good life in this world. They will be pleased with Allah's decree, content, and guided towards obedience. And in the Hereafter, We will reward them according to the best virtuous deeds they used to do in this world.

(٩٨) فَإِذَا قَرَأۡتَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ فَٱسۡتَعِذۡ بِٱللَّهِ مِنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ ٱلرَّجِيمِ

When you intend to recite the Quran, O believer, ask Allah to protect you from the whispering of the Devil, who is far removed from Allah's mercy.

(٩٩) إِنَّهُۥ لَيۡسَ لَهُۥ سُلۡطَٰنٌ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ

The Devil has no authority over those who believe in Allah and rely only on their Lord in all their affairs.

(١٠٠) إِنَّمَا سُلۡطَٰنُهُۥ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يَتَوَلَّوۡنَهُۥ وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِهِۦ مُشۡرِكُونَ

His authority is only through whispering to those who take him as an ally and obey him in his tempting. Because of this temptation, they associate partners with Allah, whom they worship along with Him.

(١٠١) وَإِذَا بَدَّلۡنَآ ءَايَةٗ مَّكَانَ ءَايَةٖ وَٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يُنَزِّلُ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مُفۡتَرِۢۚ بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

When We abrogate the ruling of a verse of the Quran with another verse, and Allah knows well about the part of the Quran that is abrogated for a reason and the part that is not abrogated, they say: You, O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, are only a liar making up lies about Allah. But most of them do not know that abrogation occurs for a divine reason with far-reaching wisdom.

(١٠٢) قُلۡ نَزَّلَهُۥ رُوحُ ٱلۡقُدُسِ مِن رَّبِّكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ لِيُثَبِّتَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَهُدٗى وَبُشۡرَىٰ لِلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ

Say to them, O Messenger: This Quran has been brought down by Gabriel (peace be with him) from Allah (may He be Exalted) with the truth that contains no error, alteration or distortion to keep those who believe in Allah firm on their belief whenever new verses are revealed, and a part thereof is abrogated. It also guides them to the truth and gives good news to the Muslims about the generous reward they will receive.

Points of Benefit:

Righteous deeds coupled with belief make life good.
The way to be safe from the evil of Satan is to flee to Allah and take refuge in Him from Satan's evil.
The believers make the Quran their guide. So, they are educated by its knowledge, adopt its manners, and are illuminated by its light. Then, their religious and worldly affairs will be set straight.
Abrogating rulings occurred in the Quran at the time of revelation for a wisdom. It took into account the best interests, the situations, and the human conditions.
