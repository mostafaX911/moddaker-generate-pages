(١٠٣) وَلَقَدۡ نَعۡلَمُ أَنَّهُمۡ يَقُولُونَ إِنَّمَا يُعَلِّمُهُۥ بَشَرٞۗ لِّسَانُ ٱلَّذِي يُلۡحِدُونَ إِلَيۡهِ أَعۡجَمِيّٞ وَهَٰذَا لِسَانٌ عَرَبِيّٞ مُّبِينٌ

We know very well that the polytheists say that it is a human teaching Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم the Quran. They are lying in their claim. The language of the person they claim is teaching him is not Arabic. In contrast, this Quran has been revealed in clear and highly eloquent Arabic. How, then, do they claim that he received it from a non-Arab?!

(١٠٤) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ لَا يَهۡدِيهِمُ ٱللَّهُ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ

Those who do not believe that the verses of Allah are, in fact, from Him (may He be Exalted) will not be directed by Allah to guidance if they persist on that. They will receive a painful torment for rejecting Allah and His verses.

(١٠٥) إِنَّمَا يَفۡتَرِي ٱلۡكَذِبَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡكَٰذِبُونَ

Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم is not lying about what he brought from His Lord. Lies are made up by those who do not believe in Allah's verses because they do not fear any torment or hope for any reward. Those described as disbelievers are liars, as lying is a habit they are accustomed to.

(١٠٦) مَن كَفَرَ بِٱللَّهِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ إِيمَٰنِهِۦٓ إِلَّا مَنۡ أُكۡرِهَ وَقَلۡبُهُۥ مُطۡمَئِنُّۢ بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ وَلَٰكِن مَّن شَرَحَ بِٱلۡكُفۡرِ صَدۡرٗا فَعَلَيۡهِمۡ غَضَبٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٞ

Whoever disbelieves in Allah after believing in Him - not those who are forced to disbelieve and utter words of disbelief while their hearts are at peace with their belief [in Allah] - but rather those who embrace disbelief wholeheartedly and choose it over belief [in Allah] and utter it voluntarily then such people are apostates from Islam. The anger of Allah is on them, and in the Hereafter, they will receive a great punishment.

(١٠٧) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمُ ٱسۡتَحَبُّواْ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا عَلَى ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Such apostasy from Islam is because they preferred the trivial things of this world as compensation for their disbelief instead of the Hereafter and because Allah does not guide people who disbelieve to believe. Instead, He abandons them.

(١٠٨) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ طَبَعَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ وَسَمۡعِهِمۡ وَأَبۡصَٰرِهِمۡۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡغَٰفِلُونَ

Those who are described as being apostates after believing are the ones who Allah has sealed their hearts so they do not understand the admonition, and sealed their hearing so they do not hear anything that benefits them. He has also sealed their sight so they do not see the signs indicating belief. They are the ones who are unaware of that which brings happiness and wretchedness [in the Hereafter] and the punishment that Allah has prepared for them.

(١٠٩) لَا جَرَمَ أَنَّهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ

Truly, they will be the losers on the Day of Resurrection, for they have lost themselves because of disbelieving after believing. Had they held onto their belief, they would have entered Paradise.

(١١٠) ثُمَّ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ لِلَّذِينَ هَاجَرُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا فُتِنُواْ ثُمَّ جَٰهَدُواْ وَصَبَرُوٓاْ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهَا لَغَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Then your Lord, O Messenger, is Forgiving and Merciful towards those vulnerable believers who emigrated from Mecca to Medina after being persecuted and tested by the polytheists to the extent that they uttered words of disbelief while their hearts were at peace with their belief [in Allah]. Then they strove in Allah's path to make the Word of Allah supreme and the word of the disbelievers lowest, and they were patient upon its difficulties. Therefore, after the persecution they underwent, your Lord is Forgiving and Merciful towards them because they only uttered words of disbelief under duress.

Points of Benefit:

Permission for the one coerced to make an apparent statement of disbelief while his heart is settled upon belief.
The apostates deserve the wrath and torment of Allah because they preferred the worldly life to the Hereafter. So, Allah's guidance was prohibited from them, and Allah placed a seal over their hearts, hearing, and sight and made them heedless of the severe torment intended for them on the Day of Resurrection.
Allah has written forgiveness and mercy for those who believed, emigrated after being put to trial, and were patient upon Jihad.
