(١٨٢) فَمَنۡ خَافَ مِن مُّوصٖ جَنَفًا أَوۡ إِثۡمٗا فَأَصۡلَحَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ فَلَآ إِثۡمَ عَلَيۡهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Whoever knows that there is an error in judgment or injustices committed by the person who made the will and rectifies the matter by advising the owner of the will or reconciles between parties who disagree about the will, then there is no blame on them. Rather, this person is rewarded for rectifying these matters. Indeed, Allah is Most Forgiving and Most Compassionate towards those among His slaves who repent to him.

(١٨٣) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُتِبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, fasting has been decreed upon you by your Lord, as He decreed it on those before you, so that you may become mindful of Allah, protecting yourself from His punishment through righteous acts, and fasting is one of the greatest of them.

(١٨٤) أَيَّامٗا مَّعۡدُودَٰتٖۚ فَمَن كَانَ مِنكُم مَّرِيضًا أَوۡ عَلَىٰ سَفَرٖ فَعِدَّةٞ مِّنۡ أَيَّامٍ أُخَرَۚ وَعَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يُطِيقُونَهُۥ فِدۡيَةٞ طَعَامُ مِسۡكِينٖۖ فَمَن تَطَوَّعَ خَيۡرٗا فَهُوَ خَيۡرٞ لَّهُۥۚ وَأَن تَصُومُواْ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

The prescribed fast is for a small number of days from the year. But whoever among you is ill with an illness that makes fasting difficult or traveling, then they do not need to fast, and they can make up for these days by fasting the same number of other days. Those who can fast but do not should compensate by feeding a poor person every day they did not fast. Whoever feeds more than one poor person per day or feeds and makes up the fast as well, then that is better. Fasting is better for you than not fasting and feeding a poor person instead if you only knew the distinction of fasting. This was the first of Allah's decreed laws about fasting: whoever wanted to fast could do so, and whoever did not want to fast was permitted not to do so and instead feed a poor person. Afterward, Allah made fasting a mandatory obligation for every able person who has reached puberty.

(١٨٥) شَهۡرُ رَمَضَانَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أُنزِلَ فِيهِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ هُدٗى لِّلنَّاسِ وَبَيِّنَٰتٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡهُدَىٰ وَٱلۡفُرۡقَانِۚ فَمَن شَهِدَ مِنكُمُ ٱلشَّهۡرَ فَلۡيَصُمۡهُۖ وَمَن كَانَ مَرِيضًا أَوۡ عَلَىٰ سَفَرٖ فَعِدَّةٞ مِّنۡ أَيَّامٍ أُخَرَۗ يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ بِكُمُ ٱلۡيُسۡرَ وَلَا يُرِيدُ بِكُمُ ٱلۡعُسۡرَ وَلِتُكۡمِلُواْ ٱلۡعِدَّةَ وَلِتُكَبِّرُواْ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ مَا هَدَىٰكُمۡ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

The Quran was first revealed to the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم on the Night of Decree in the month of Ramadan. Allah revealed it as a guide for people, with clear proof of its guidance and the criteria to distinguish truth from falsehood. Whoever reaches the month of Ramadan and is in sound health is required to fast. Whoever is ill such that fasting is difficult or is traveling is allowed not to fast. If they do not fast, then they must make up these fasts for the days they missed at another time. Allah intends to have you traverse the path of ease and not hardship in what He decrees for you. He wants you to complete fasting all days of the month and glorify Him upon the completion of Ramadan, on the day of Eid, as He aided you and made it possible for you to fast. This is so you thank Allah for guiding you to this religion, which He has approved for you.

(١٨٦) وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌۖ أُجِيبُ دَعۡوَةَ ٱلدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِۖ فَلۡيَسۡتَجِيبُواْ لِي وَلۡيُؤۡمِنُواْ بِي لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡشُدُونَ

If they ask you, O Prophet, about how close Allah is and about His answering of prayers, Allah is close to them and knows everything about them. He hears their prayer, and they do not need intermediaries to raise their voices. Allah responds to the call of whoever calls Him sincerely, praying to Him; so, let them be devoted to Him and His sacred law, firm in their faith, as that is the best route to Allah's response – perhaps in that way, they might be rightly guided in worldly and sacred matters.

Points of Benefit:

Allah has given the month of Ramadan virtue by making it the month of fasting and revealing the Quran within it. So, it is the month of the Quran. This is why the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم would review the Quran with Gabriel during Ramadan and exert himself [in worship] during it more than in other months.
The Islamic legislation, with its foundations and branches, is established upon ease and removing difficulty. So, Allah did not make any difficulty for us in the Religion.
Allah's (the Exalted) closeness to His slaves, understanding of them, and complete knowledge of their situation. For this reason, He hears their supplications and answers their requests.
