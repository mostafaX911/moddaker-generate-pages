(١١١) ۞يَوۡمَ تَأۡتِي كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ تُجَٰدِلُ عَن نَّفۡسِهَا وَتُوَفَّىٰ كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ مَّا عَمِلَتۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ

Remember, O Messenger, the Day when every person will come arguing on behalf of himself. He will not argue on anyone else's behalf due to the situation's magnitude. Every soul will be given in full the recompense for what it had done, whether good or bad, and they will not be wronged by any deduction from their good actions or addition to their evil actions.

(١١٢) وَضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا قَرۡيَةٗ كَانَتۡ ءَامِنَةٗ مُّطۡمَئِنَّةٗ يَأۡتِيهَا رِزۡقُهَا رَغَدٗا مِّن كُلِّ مَكَانٖ فَكَفَرَتۡ بِأَنۡعُمِ ٱللَّهِ فَأَذَٰقَهَا ٱللَّهُ لِبَاسَ ٱلۡجُوعِ وَٱلۡخَوۡفِ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَصۡنَعُونَ

Allah presents the example of a town, Mecca, that was secure and stable, with its people not living in fear, while people around it lived in fear. Their provisions were coming to them abundantly and easily from every direction. But then its people became unappreciative of Allah's favors upon them and did not thank Him. Allah then recompensed them with hunger and intense fear that was visible in the panic [on their faces] and the emaciation on their bodies to the extent that this became like a garment for them due to their disbelief and rejection.

(١١٣) وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَهُمۡ رَسُولٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَأَخَذَهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ وَهُمۡ ظَٰلِمُونَ

And a Messenger from among them, whose honesty and truthfulness they knew, who was Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, had come to the people of Mecca. Still, they rejected what Allah had revealed to him. As a result, Allah's torment in the form of hunger and fear came on them while they were wronging themselves by taking themselves to the means of destruction: associating partners with Allah and rejecting His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم.

(١١٤) فَكُلُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ حَلَٰلٗا طَيِّبٗا وَٱشۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ إِن كُنتُمۡ إِيَّاهُ تَعۡبُدُونَ

So eat, O slaves of Allah, of the lawful and wholesome things that Allah (may He be Exalted) has provided for you, and be thankful for the favors Allah has given you by acknowledging them and using them in a way pleasing to Allah if it is Him that you worship alone without associating partners with Him.

(١١٥) إِنَّمَا حَرَّمَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡمَيۡتَةَ وَٱلدَّمَ وَلَحۡمَ ٱلۡخِنزِيرِ وَمَآ أُهِلَّ لِغَيۡرِ ٱللَّهِ بِهِۦۖ فَمَنِ ٱضۡطُرَّ غَيۡرَ بَاغٖ وَلَا عَادٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Allah has made it unlawful for you to consume that which died without being slaughtered according to the sacred law, [to consume] blood that spills forth from an animal at the time of slaughter, all parts of the pig, and what was slaughtered by a person as an offering to other than Allah. This prohibition applies at the time when a person has a choice. Suppose someone is forced to eat any of these forbidden types and does so without desiring the forbidden thing itself and without going beyond the limit of what is necessary. In that case, there is no sin on them. Allah is Forgiving and will forgive them for what they consume. He is Merciful in that He permitted that when it is necessary.

(١١٦) وَلَا تَقُولُواْ لِمَا تَصِفُ أَلۡسِنَتُكُمُ ٱلۡكَذِبَ هَٰذَا حَلَٰلٞ وَهَٰذَا حَرَامٞ لِّتَفۡتَرُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡكَذِبَۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَفۡتَرُونَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡكَذِبَ لَا يُفۡلِحُونَ

Do not say, O polytheists, about what your tongues describe falsely about Allah: This thing is lawful, and this thing is unlawful, to make up lies about Allah concerning making unlawful what Allah did not make unlawful, or making lawful what Allah did not make lawful. Those who make up lies about Allah will not succeed in attaining their objective and will not be saved from what they fear.

(١١٧) مَتَٰعٞ قَلِيلٞ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

They will have a brief and insignificant enjoyment by following their desires in this world, and on the Day of Resurrection, they will receive a painful torment.

Upon mentioning the foods He has forbidden for this nation, Allah mentions what He forbade for the Jews, stating:

(١١٨) وَعَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ هَادُواْ حَرَّمۡنَا مَا قَصَصۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ مِن قَبۡلُۖ وَمَا ظَلَمۡنَٰهُمۡ وَلَٰكِن كَانُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَظۡلِمُونَ

We had made unlawful for the Jews what We had told you about in verse 146 of Surah Al-An'am. We did not wrong them by making that unlawful, but they wronged themselves when they committed those deeds that caused Allah's punishment to come. We thus recompensed them for their transgression, and We made that unlawful for them as a punishment upon them.

Points of Benefit:

Recompense is according to what was done. So when the people of the town denied their blessings, it was replaced with its opposite, so it was destroyed and lost. They were starving after being full, in fear and panic after being in safety and peace, lacking the means to live after having enough.
The obligation to believe in Allah and the Messengers, to worship Allah alone, to be grateful for His grace and many blessings. Also, the divine punishment will befall everyone who disbelieves in Allah, disobeys Him, and denies Allah's blessing upon them.
Allah, the Exalted, has not prohibited anything for us except evil things as a favor from Him and to protect us from every filthy thing.
