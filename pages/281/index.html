(١١٩) ثُمَّ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ لِلَّذِينَ عَمِلُواْ ٱلسُّوٓءَ بِجَهَٰلَةٖ ثُمَّ تَابُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَ وَأَصۡلَحُوٓاْ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهَا لَغَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٌ

Then your Lord, O Messenger, after their repentance, forgives the sins and is merciful towards those who do evil actions out of ignorance of the consequences even though they do so deliberately but then repent to Allah afterward and rectify their corrupt acts. Indeed, your Lord, after they repented, forgives their sins and is merciful towards them.

When the polytheists claimed to follow Abraham's religion, Allah rejected their claim, stating:

(١٢٠) إِنَّ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ كَانَ أُمَّةٗ قَانِتٗا لِّلَّهِ حَنِيفٗا وَلَمۡ يَكُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

Indeed, Abraham (peace be upon him) possessed all the attributes of goodness, always obeyed His Lord, and turned away from all other religions to the religion of Islam. He was never a polytheist.

(١٢١) شَاكِرٗا لِّأَنۡعُمِهِۚ ٱجۡتَبَىٰهُ وَهَدَىٰهُ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

He was thankful for the favors that Allah had given him. Allah had selected Him to be a prophet and had guided him to the true religion of Islam.

(١٢٢) وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةٗۖ وَإِنَّهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ لَمِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

We gave him prophethood, praiseworthy mention, and pious children in this world. In the Hereafter, he will be from the righteous for whom Allah has prepared the highest ranks in Paradise.

(١٢٣) ثُمَّ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ أَنِ ٱتَّبِعۡ مِلَّةَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ حَنِيفٗاۖ وَمَا كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

Then, We revealed to you, O Messenger: Follow the religion of Abraham in terms of monotheism, disassociation from the polytheists, inviting towards Allah, and practicing Allah's sacred law. He turned away from all other religions to the religion of Islam. He was never a polytheist, as the polytheists claim, but he was a man of pure monotheism.

(١٢٤) إِنَّمَا جُعِلَ ٱلسَّبۡتُ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ٱخۡتَلَفُواْ فِيهِۚ وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَيَحۡكُمُ بَيۡنَهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فِيمَا كَانُواْ فِيهِ يَخۡتَلِفُونَ

Honoring the Sabbath was only made obligatory for the Jews, who differed about it, so that they could free themselves on that day from their occupations for the sake of worship. This was after they had strayed from the day of Friday in which they were ordered to free themselves [for worship]. Your Lord, O Messenger, will judge between these people, who differ, on the Day of Resurrection about what they used to differ in. He will recompense each person with what they deserve.

(١٢٥) ٱدۡعُ إِلَىٰ سَبِيلِ رَبِّكَ بِٱلۡحِكۡمَةِ وَٱلۡمَوۡعِظَةِ ٱلۡحَسَنَةِۖ وَجَٰدِلۡهُم بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ

Invite, O Messenger, to the religion of Islam and the believers who follow you in a manner appropriate for the person being invited and with admonition that contains encouragement and caution. Argue with them in the best manner in terms of speech, thought, and politeness, for it is not your duty to guide people. You are only required to convey the message to them. Your Lord knows best who has strayed from Islam and knows best those rightly guided to it, so do not lose yourself in grieving over them.

(١٢٦) وَإِنۡ عَاقَبۡتُمۡ فَعَاقِبُواْ بِمِثۡلِ مَا عُوقِبۡتُم بِهِۦۖ وَلَئِن صَبَرۡتُمۡ لَهُوَ خَيۡرٞ لِّلصَّٰبِرِينَ

If you want to retaliate against your enemy, retaliate to the same degree that they harmed you and not more. But suppose you are patient and do not retaliate despite having the ability to do so. In that case, that is better for the patient amongst you than taking revenge by retaliating against them.

(١٢٧) وَٱصۡبِرۡ وَمَا صَبۡرُكَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ وَلَا تَحۡزَنۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا تَكُ فِي ضَيۡقٖ مِّمَّا يَمۡكُرُونَ

So be patient, O Messenger, upon the harm they afflict you. Your ability to be patient is only by Allah's guidance. Do not grieve over the disbelievers turning away from you, and do not let your heart be distressed by the schemes and plots they carry out.

(١٢٨) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَواْ وَّٱلَّذِينَ هُم مُّحۡسِنُونَ

Allah is with those who fear Him by abandoning sin and those who do good by doing acts of obedience and fulfilling what they have been commanded to do. Allah's help and support are with them.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's mercy requires that He accepts the repentance of and forgives His slaves who did evil deeds of disbelief and disobedience, then repented to Him and rectified their actions.
It is good for the Muslim to take Abraham as his role model.
The caller to Allah's religion should follow these three methods: Wisdom, good admonition, and arguing in the best way.
Punishment should be in kind without being increased, so the oppressed one is prohibited from increasing the punishment of the oppressor.
