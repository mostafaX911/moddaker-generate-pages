The Night Journey (Surah Al-Isra)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Allah's confirmation and support for His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم with clear signs and the glad tidings of victory and steadfastness.

Explanation:

(١) سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَسۡرَىٰ بِعَبۡدِهِۦ لَيۡلٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِ إِلَى ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡأَقۡصَا ٱلَّذِي بَٰرَكۡنَا حَوۡلَهُۥ لِنُرِيَهُۥ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِنَآۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡبَصِيرُ

Allah (may He be Exalted) is Exalted from everything that does not befit him as He can do what none other than He can do. It is He who took His slave, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, on the night journey from the sacred masjid in Mecca to the masjid of Al Aqsa in Jerusalem, whose surroundings We have blessed with fruit, crops, and dwellings of prophets (peace be upon them), for him to see some of Our signs which show Allah's power and ability, may He be Exalted. He is the One who hears everything; no sound escapes Him, and the One who sees everything; nothing is hidden from Him.

(٢) وَءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ هُدٗى لِّبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ أَلَّا تَتَّخِذُواْ مِن دُونِي وَكِيلٗا

We also gave Moses (peace be upon him) the Torah, and We made it a guide and a manual for the Israelites. Therefore, We said to them: Do not take any guardian besides me to whom you entrust your affairs. Instead, place your reliance on Me alone.

(٣) ذُرِّيَّةَ مَنۡ حَمَلۡنَا مَعَ نُوحٍۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ عَبۡدٗا شَكُورٗا

You are from the progeny of those whom We had favored with Noah (peace be upon him) by saving them from drowning in the flood. So remember this favor and thank Allah by worshipping and following Him alone. In this, follow the example of Noah, for he was very grateful to Allah.

(٤) وَقَضَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَتُفۡسِدُنَّ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَرَّتَيۡنِ وَلَتَعۡلُنَّ عُلُوّٗا كَبِيرٗا

And We told the Israelites and informed them in the Torah that indeed they will be guilty of corruption on Earth by their sin and arrogance twice, and they will rise over people with injustice and oppression, transgressing the limits in their rise.

(٥) فَإِذَا جَآءَ وَعۡدُ أُولَىٰهُمَا بَعَثۡنَا عَلَيۡكُمۡ عِبَادٗا لَّنَآ أُوْلِي بَأۡسٖ شَدِيدٖ فَجَاسُواْ خِلَٰلَ ٱلدِّيَارِۚ وَكَانَ وَعۡدٗا مَّفۡعُولٗا

When the first corruption occurred, We set against them slaves of Ours, possessing great might and power, who killed and displaced them. They ransacked their homes, destroying everything they passed by. Allah's promise was indeed fulfilled.

(٦) ثُمَّ رَدَدۡنَا لَكُمُ ٱلۡكَرَّةَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَأَمۡدَدۡنَٰكُم بِأَمۡوَٰلٖ وَبَنِينَ وَجَعَلۡنَٰكُمۡ أَكۡثَرَ نَفِيرًا

Then, once again, I gave you, O Israelites, the upper hand and dominance over those who were set against you when you repented to Allah. I supplied you with wealth after your wealth had been stolen and children after your children were taken as captives, and I made you a force outnumbering your enemies.

(٧) إِنۡ أَحۡسَنتُمۡ أَحۡسَنتُمۡ لِأَنفُسِكُمۡۖ وَإِنۡ أَسَأۡتُمۡ فَلَهَاۚ فَإِذَا جَآءَ وَعۡدُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ لِيَسُ‍ُٔواْ وُجُوهَكُمۡ وَلِيَدۡخُلُواْ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدَ كَمَا دَخَلُوهُ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖ وَلِيُتَبِّرُواْ مَا عَلَوۡاْ تَتۡبِيرًا

If you do good actions, O Israelites, in the manner that is required of you, then the compensation of that will be to your benefit, for Allah is in no need of your actions. If you do bad actions, the consequences of that will be to your detriment. Your good actions do not benefit Allah, and nor do your bad actions harm Him. When the second corruption occurs, I will set your enemies against you to humiliate you and make shame appear on your faces due to the various types of disgrace they will make you taste. They will enter Jerusalem, destroy it just as they entered and destroyed it the first time, and completely ruin all the lands they conquer.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's statement, "Masjid al-Aqsa," signifies the mosque's inclusion under Islamic jurisdiction as a place of worship for Muslims.
Clarification of the virtue of gratitude and the emulation of the thankful among the prophets and messengers.
From Allah's wisdom and practice related to the mischief-makers is to send to them those who prevent them from corruption, in order to actualize Allah's wisdom in reform.
A warning to this nation against committing sins so that they do not suffer what happened to the Israelites, as Allah's established practice is one and does not change or alter.
