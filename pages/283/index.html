(٨) عَسَىٰ رَبُّكُمۡ أَن يَرۡحَمَكُمۡۚ وَإِنۡ عُدتُّمۡ عُدۡنَاۚ وَجَعَلۡنَا جَهَنَّمَ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ حَصِيرًا

Perhaps your Lord - O Israelites - will be merciful to you after this severe reprisal if you repent to Him and do good actions. But if you revert to causing corruption a third time or more than that, I will revert to taking reprisals from you. I have made Hell a prison for the disbelievers, from which they will not find any release.

(٩) إِنَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ يَهۡدِي لِلَّتِي هِيَ أَقۡوَمُ وَيُبَشِّرُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡمَلُونَ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ أَنَّ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرٗا كَبِيرٗا

This Quran that is revealed to Muhammad (peace be on him) guides to the best way, which is the way of Islam, and it informs those who have faith in Allah and who do good deeds of that which will please them, which is that they will receive a great reward from Allah.

(١٠) وَأَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ أَعۡتَدۡنَا لَهُمۡ عَذَابًا أَلِيمٗا

It informs those who do not have faith in the Day of Resurrection of what will displease them, which is that I have prepared a painful punishment for them on the Day of Resurrection.

(١١) وَيَدۡعُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ بِٱلشَّرِّ دُعَآءَهُۥ بِٱلۡخَيۡرِۖ وَكَانَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ عَجُولٗا

Because of his ignorance, man prays for evil against himself, his children, and his wealth when angry, just as he prays for good for himself. If we had to answer his prayer for evil, he would be ruined, along with his children and wealth. Human beings are naturally hasty and, therefore, may hastily bring on something that is harmful.

(١٢) وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ ءَايَتَيۡنِۖ فَمَحَوۡنَآ ءَايَةَ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَجَعَلۡنَآ ءَايَةَ ٱلنَّهَارِ مُبۡصِرَةٗ لِّتَبۡتَغُواْ فَضۡلٗا مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَلِتَعۡلَمُواْ عَدَدَ ٱلسِّنِينَ وَٱلۡحِسَابَۚ وَكُلَّ شَيۡءٖ فَصَّلۡنَٰهُ تَفۡصِيلٗا

I have created the night and the day as two signs indicating Allah's oneness and power due to their differences in terms of being long or short, hot or cold. I made the night dark for rest and sleep and the day bright so people could see and work for their livelihood. This was so that by the alternation of day and night, you could know the number of years and calculate the months, days, and hours that you require. I have explained everything in detail so that things become clear and to distinguish the truthful from the liars.

(١٣) وَكُلَّ إِنسَٰنٍ أَلۡزَمۡنَٰهُ طَٰٓئِرَهُۥ فِي عُنُقِهِۦۖ وَنُخۡرِجُ لَهُۥ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ كِتَٰبٗا يَلۡقَىٰهُ مَنشُورًا

We have bound every person's destiny to their neck (Every action a person does is attached to him just as a necklace is attached to a neck). They (the person's actions) will not be detached from him until he is taken to account for them. On the Day of Resurrection, I will bring out a book for him that contains every good or bad deed that he did, which he will find spread open before him.

(١٤) ٱقۡرَأۡ كِتَٰبَكَ كَفَىٰ بِنَفۡسِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ عَلَيۡكَ حَسِيبٗا

I will say to him on that day: O human! Read your book and take an account of your soul for your deeds. You are enough to take an account of yourself on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٥) مَّنِ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ فَإِنَّمَا يَهۡتَدِي لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن ضَلَّ فَإِنَّمَا يَضِلُّ عَلَيۡهَاۚ وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةٞ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰۗ وَمَا كُنَّا مُعَذِّبِينَ حَتَّىٰ نَبۡعَثَ رَسُولٗا

Whoever is guided to faith, then the reward of his being guided is for his own good; and whoever goes astray, the punishment for his going astray is to his detriment. No soul carries the sin of another soul, nor do I ever punish a people until I establish proof against them by sending Messengers to them.

(١٦) وَإِذَآ أَرَدۡنَآ أَن نُّهۡلِكَ قَرۡيَةً أَمَرۡنَا مُتۡرَفِيهَا فَفَسَقُواْ فِيهَا فَحَقَّ عَلَيۡهَا ٱلۡقَوۡلُ فَدَمَّرۡنَٰهَا تَدۡمِيرٗا

When We decided to destroy a town because of its wrongdoing, We instructed those who became reckless because of affluence to obey Allah, but they did not comply. Instead, they disobeyed Him and transgressed, so the word of the decimating punishment became justified against them (meaning that Allah's sentence against them was passed), and We destroyed them completely.

(١٧) وَكَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا مِنَ ٱلۡقُرُونِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ نُوحٖۗ وَكَفَىٰ بِرَبِّكَ بِذُنُوبِ عِبَادِهِۦ خَبِيرَۢا بَصِيرٗا

We have destroyed many generations after Noah, such as 'Ād and Thamūd (from the nations and people of the past). Your Lord - O Messenger - is sufficient as a Knower and Seer of the sins of His slaves. Nothing is hidden from Him, and He will requite them for the same.

Points of Benefit:

Whoever follows the guidance of the Quran is the most complete, upright, and well-guided among people in all matters.
A warning against invoking for evil upon oneself and one's children.
The alternation of night and day with their increase and decrease, the succession of light and darkness—all are evidence of the oneness, existence, knowledge, and power of Allah.
These verses establish the principle of individual accountability, out of justice from Allah and mercy to His servants.
