(١٨) مَّن كَانَ يُرِيدُ ٱلۡعَاجِلَةَ عَجَّلۡنَا لَهُۥ فِيهَا مَا نَشَآءُ لِمَن نُّرِيدُ ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَا لَهُۥ جَهَنَّمَ يَصۡلَىٰهَا مَذۡمُومٗا مَّدۡحُورٗا

Whoever desires, by doing acts of goodness, the life of this world and does not have faith in the Afterlife nor pays any attention to it, I expedite for him the luxury that I want to give him, not what he wants. Then I have made Hell for him to enter on the Day of Resurrection to suffer from its heat, being condemned for choosing this world and denying the Hereafter, banished from Allah's mercy.

(١٩) وَمَنۡ أَرَادَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَسَعَىٰ لَهَا سَعۡيَهَا وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنٞ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ كَانَ سَعۡيُهُم مَّشۡكُورٗا

But whoever desires, by doing acts of goodness, the reward of the Hereafter, and pursues it as it should be pursued, without showing off and seeking fame, while he has faith in that which Allah has made faith obligatory in; these people who have these attributes - their effort will be accepted by Allah, and He will recompense them for it.

(٢٠) كُلّٗا نُّمِدُّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ وَهَٰٓؤُلَآءِ مِنۡ عَطَآءِ رَبِّكَۚ وَمَا كَانَ عَطَآءُ رَبِّكَ مَحۡظُورًا

To both these groups, the sinners and the dutiful, we freely extend the provision of your Lord, O Messenger. The provision of your Lord in this world is not withheld from anyone, whether sinner or dutiful.

(٢١) ٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ فَضَّلۡنَا بَعۡضَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖۚ وَلَلۡأٓخِرَةُ أَكۡبَرُ دَرَجَٰتٖ وَأَكۡبَرُ تَفۡضِيلٗا

O Messenger, reflect on how we favored some over others with provisions and rank in this world. Yet the Hereafter is greater in the disparity between the ranks of luxury than that of the world and greater in favors, so a believer should be eager for that.

(٢٢) لَّا تَجۡعَلۡ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ فَتَقۡعُدَ مَذۡمُومٗا مَّخۡذُولٗا

O slave, do not set up another deity that you worship alongside Allah, or you will become condemned in the sight of Allah and His righteous slaves, and you will be forsaken by Him.

(٢٣) ۞وَقَضَىٰ رَبُّكَ أَلَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِيَّاهُ وَبِٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ إِحۡسَٰنًاۚ إِمَّا يَبۡلُغَنَّ عِندَكَ ٱلۡكِبَرَ أَحَدُهُمَآ أَوۡ كِلَاهُمَا فَلَا تَقُل لَّهُمَآ أُفّٖ وَلَا تَنۡهَرۡهُمَا وَقُل لَّهُمَا قَوۡلٗا كَرِيمٗا

Your Lord, O slave, has instructed and made obligatory that you worship none but Him, and He has instructed that you be good to your parents, especially when they become old. If either of them or both of them reach old age with you, do not become annoyed with them by uttering words that indicate the same, do not scold them, and do not be harsh when speaking to them, but say to them kind words that are soft and courteous.

(٢٤) وَٱخۡفِضۡ لَهُمَا جَنَاحَ ٱلذُّلِّ مِنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَةِ وَقُل رَّبِّ ٱرۡحَمۡهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرٗا

Humble yourself before them in humility and out of compassion, and say: O My Lord, be merciful to them as they have brought me up in my childhood.

(٢٥) رَّبُّكُمۡ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا فِي نُفُوسِكُمۡۚ إِن تَكُونُواْ صَٰلِحِينَ فَإِنَّهُۥ كَانَ لِلۡأَوَّٰبِينَ غَفُورٗا

Your Lord, O people, knows best what is within you of the sincere devotion to Him in worship, acts of goodness, and being dutiful to parents. If the intention in your acts of worship, treatment of your parents, and other acts is righteous, then He (may He be Exalted) is Forgiving of those who frequently turn to Him in repentance. If anyone repents from his prior failure to be dutiful to his Lord or his parents, Allah will forgive him.

(٢٦) وَءَاتِ ذَا ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ حَقَّهُۥ وَٱلۡمِسۡكِينَ وَٱبۡنَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَلَا تُبَذِّرۡ تَبۡذِيرًا

O believer, give the relative his rights by keeping family ties, give to the poor and needy, and give to the traveler who is stranded. Do not spend your wealth in sin or in a wasteful manner.

(٢٧) إِنَّ ٱلۡمُبَذِّرِينَ كَانُوٓاْ إِخۡوَٰنَ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِۖ وَكَانَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ لِرَبِّهِۦ كَفُورٗا

Those who spend their wealth in sin and those who are wasteful in their spending are the brothers of the devils; they follow their instructions of being wasteful and extravagant, and the Devil is ever ungrateful towards His Lord, and he only does things that are sinful and only instructs that which displeases His Lord.

Points of Benefit:

It is befitting for a person to do good deeds within their capacity and to intend to do what they are unable to do in order to be rewarded for it.
The blessings in this world should not be used as an indication of Allah's pleasure (for His slaves) - as they may occur for disbelievers - and their ultimate destiny could lead to Allah's punishment.
Excelling in kindness towards one's parents is a mandatory and obligatory duty, and Allah has linked gratitude to them with gratitude to Him due to their tremendous virtue.
Islam prohibits extravagance, which is to spend wealth in other than its rightful place.
