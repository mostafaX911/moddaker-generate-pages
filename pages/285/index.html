(٢٨) وَإِمَّا تُعۡرِضَنَّ عَنۡهُمُ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ رَحۡمَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكَ تَرۡجُوهَا فَقُل لَّهُمۡ قَوۡلٗا مَّيۡسُورٗا

But if you refrain from giving to these people, due to not having anything to give them, while waiting for Allah to bless you with provision, then say to them gentle and polite words such as praying for them to have abundant provision or promising to give them if Allah grants you wealth.

(٢٩) وَلَا تَجۡعَلۡ يَدَكَ مَغۡلُولَةً إِلَىٰ عُنُقِكَ وَلَا تَبۡسُطۡهَا كُلَّ ٱلۡبَسۡطِ فَتَقۡعُدَ مَلُومٗا مَّحۡسُورًا

Do not withhold your hand from spending, and do not be wasteful when spending; if you do, you will be blameworthy amongst the people for your miserliness, or you will become unable to spend due to your extravagant expenditure.

(٣٠) إِنَّ رَبَّكَ يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَقۡدِرُۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ بِعِبَادِهِۦ خَبِيرَۢا بَصِيرٗا

Your Lord expands the provision for whomever He wills and restricts it for whoever He wills based on far-reaching wisdom. He is All-Aware and Observant of His slaves. Nothing of theirs is hidden from Him, and He deals with respect to them however He wills.

(٣١) وَلَا تَقۡتُلُوٓاْ أَوۡلَٰدَكُمۡ خَشۡيَةَ إِمۡلَٰقٖۖ نَّحۡنُ نَرۡزُقُهُمۡ وَإِيَّاكُمۡۚ إِنَّ قَتۡلَهُمۡ كَانَ خِطۡ‍ٔٗا كَبِيرٗا

Do not kill your children for fear of facing poverty in the future by spending on them; I take charge of providing for them and for you too. Killing them is a major sin, as they have done nothing wrong, and no reason necessitates them to be killed.

(٣٢) وَلَا تَقۡرَبُواْ ٱلزِّنَىٰٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ فَٰحِشَةٗ وَسَآءَ سَبِيلٗا

Beware of fornication and stay away from things that prompt it. It is an extremely detestable and evil path to traverse as it leads to the mixing of lineages and punishment from Allah.

(٣٣) وَلَا تَقۡتُلُواْ ٱلنَّفۡسَ ٱلَّتِي حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّۗ وَمَن قُتِلَ مَظۡلُومٗا فَقَدۡ جَعَلۡنَا لِوَلِيِّهِۦ سُلۡطَٰنٗا فَلَا يُسۡرِف فِّي ٱلۡقَتۡلِۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ مَنصُورٗا

Do not kill the soul whose life Allah has protected through faith or a pledge of security, except if the killing is merited on the basis of treason or legal retribution. If someone is killed unjustly, without a valid reason permitting his being killed, I have given his successor, who takes charge of his affairs, certain authority over the killer. He may demand that the killer be killed in retribution, forgive him without asking for anything in return, or forgive him and take the blood money. But he should not mutilate the killer, nor kill him with something he did not use to kill, nor kill someone other than the killer, even if he was a helper and supporter.

(٣٤) وَلَا تَقۡرَبُواْ مَالَ ٱلۡيَتِيمِ إِلَّا بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ حَتَّىٰ يَبۡلُغَ أَشُدَّهُۥۚ وَأَوۡفُواْ بِٱلۡعَهۡدِۖ إِنَّ ٱلۡعَهۡدَ كَانَ مَسۡ‍ُٔولٗا

Do not transact in the property of a child whose father has passed away, except in his best interests, such as investing or preserving it until he reaches the age of mental and prudential maturity. Fulfill any pledge between yourselves and Allah, or between yourselves and His slaves, without breaking or falling short in them. Allah will question the one who made a pledge on the Day of Resurrection. Did he fulfill it? In which case, He will reward him. Or did he not fulfill it? In which case, He will punish him.

(٣٥) وَأَوۡفُواْ ٱلۡكَيۡلَ إِذَا كِلۡتُمۡ وَزِنُواْ بِٱلۡقِسۡطَاسِ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمِۚ ذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرٞ وَأَحۡسَنُ تَأۡوِيلٗا

Give full measure when you measure for others, and do not cheat them. Weigh with an accurate scale that does not diminish or undervalue anything. Giving full weight and measure is better for you in this world and the Hereafter than giving short measure or weight.

(٣٦) وَلَا تَقۡفُ مَا لَيۡسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمٌۚ إِنَّ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡبَصَرَ وَٱلۡفُؤَادَ كُلُّ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ كَانَ عَنۡهُ مَسۡ‍ُٔولٗا

Do not follow, O son of Adam, that which you have no knowledge of by following suspicions and conjecture. A person will be questioned about the good or bad that he used his hearing, sight, and heart for, and he will be rewarded for the good and punished for the bad.

(٣٧) وَلَا تَمۡشِ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَرَحًاۖ إِنَّكَ لَن تَخۡرِقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَلَن تَبۡلُغَ ٱلۡجِبَالَ طُولٗا

Do not walk on the Earth with pride and arrogance. If you walk haughtily on Earth, you will not be able to split the Earth, nor will your stature reach the mountains in height and elevation. Why, then, are you so proud?!

(٣٨) كُلُّ ذَٰلِكَ كَانَ سَيِّئُهُۥ عِندَ رَبِّكَ مَكۡرُوهٗا

O humanity, the evil of all that has been mentioned above is prohibited by your Lord. Allah is not pleased with those who perpetrate this; rather, He detests them.

Points of Benefit:

Exemplary manners involve responding to close relatives with kindness, promising them and fulfilling the promise of maintaining kinship when it is feasible, and apologizing to them in an acceptable manner.
Allah is more merciful to children than their parents; He has prohibited parents from killing their children out of fear of poverty and has taken upon Himself to provide for everyone.
The verses indicate that the right to seek retribution lies with the guardian, and it cannot be pursued without their permission; if they choose to forgive, the case is dropped.
From Allah's kindness and mercy towards orphans is His commanding their guardians to protect, preserve, reform, and nurture them until they reach maturity.
