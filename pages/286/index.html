(٣٩) ذَٰلِكَ مِمَّآ أَوۡحَىٰٓ إِلَيۡكَ رَبُّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡحِكۡمَةِۗ وَلَا تَجۡعَلۡ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ فَتُلۡقَىٰ فِي جَهَنَّمَ مَلُومٗا مَّدۡحُورًا

Those instructions, prohibitions, and laws that we have explained to you are part of what your Lord has revealed to you. Do not take, O humanity, another deity together with Allah, for you will be thrown into Hell on the Day of Resurrection, blamed by yourself and by people, and banished from every good.

(٤٠) أَفَأَصۡفَىٰكُمۡ رَبُّكُم بِٱلۡبَنِينَ وَٱتَّخَذَ مِنَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ إِنَٰثًاۚ إِنَّكُمۡ لَتَقُولُونَ قَوۡلًا عَظِيمٗا

O you who claim that the angels are Allah's daughters! Has your Lord chosen you, O polytheists, to have male children while taking daughters from among the angels for Himself? Allah transcends far beyond what you are saying. You are saying a terrible thing about Allah by ascribing children to him and claiming that He has daughters، so persisting in your disbelief.

(٤١) وَلَقَدۡ صَرَّفۡنَا فِي هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ لِيَذَّكَّرُواْ وَمَا يَزِيدُهُمۡ إِلَّا نُفُورٗا

We have made clear in this Quran rulings, exhortations, and parables so the people may learn from them, then follow what benefits them and leave off what harms them. However, the situation of some of them whose nature has taken a step back is that it does not increase them in anything except distance from the Truth and hatred of it.

(٤٢) قُل لَّوۡ كَانَ مَعَهُۥٓ ءَالِهَةٞ كَمَا يَقُولُونَ إِذٗا لَّٱبۡتَغَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ ذِي ٱلۡعَرۡشِ سَبِيلٗا

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: If there were other deities along with Allah, as they say as a fabrication and lie, then those supposed deities would have sought a way to Allah, the Owner of the Throne, to try to defeat and contest Him over His dominion.

(٤٣) سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥ وَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يَقُولُونَ عُلُوّٗا كَبِيرٗا

Allah (may He be Exalted) is pure and sanctified from what the polytheists attribute to Him, and He is far above what they say.

(٤٤) تُسَبِّحُ لَهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ ٱلسَّبۡعُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمَن فِيهِنَّۚ وَإِن مِّن شَيۡءٍ إِلَّا يُسَبِّحُ بِحَمۡدِهِۦ وَلَٰكِن لَّا تَفۡقَهُونَ تَسۡبِيحَهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ حَلِيمًا غَفُورٗا

The heavens exalt Allah. The Earth exalts Allah. Every creation in the heavens and the Earth also exalts Allah. There is nothing except that it declares His purity together with His praise, but you do not comprehend their manner of exalting Him. This is because you only understand the exaltation of those who do so in your language. He is Forbearing and not quick to punish. And He forgives those who repent to Him.

(٤٥) وَإِذَا قَرَأۡتَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ جَعَلۡنَا بَيۡنَكَ وَبَيۡنَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ حِجَابٗا مَّسۡتُورٗا

When you, O Messenger, recite the Quran and they hear the warnings and admonitions it contains, I place a barrier between you and those who do not have faith in the Day of Resurrection that prevents them from understanding the Quran as a punishment for their turning away from it.

(٤٦) وَجَعَلۡنَا عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ أَكِنَّةً أَن يَفۡقَهُوهُ وَفِيٓ ءَاذَانِهِمۡ وَقۡرٗاۚ وَإِذَا ذَكَرۡتَ رَبَّكَ فِي ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ وَحۡدَهُۥ وَلَّوۡاْ عَلَىٰٓ أَدۡبَٰرِهِمۡ نُفُورٗا

We have made covers on their hearts so that they cannot understand the Quran and heaviness in their ears so that they cannot listen to it in a beneficial manner. When you mention your Lord alone in the Quran and do not mention their so-called gods, they turn on their heels, distancing themselves from accepting Allah's pure Oneness.

(٤٧) نَّحۡنُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يَسۡتَمِعُونَ بِهِۦٓ إِذۡ يَسۡتَمِعُونَ إِلَيۡكَ وَإِذۡ هُمۡ نَجۡوَىٰٓ إِذۡ يَقُولُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ إِن تَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا رَجُلٗا مَّسۡحُورًا

I know best the way their leaders listen to the Quran. They do not want to take guidance from it, but they want to mock it and disrupt your recitation. I also know best what they secretly confer about with respect to their denial of and preventing others from it, when these people who wrong themselves by their denial say: You, O People, are only following a man who is bewitched and confused.

(٤٨) ٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ ضَرَبُواْ لَكَ ٱلۡأَمۡثَالَ فَضَلُّواْ فَلَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ سَبِيلٗا

O Messenger, consider and be surprised at the various blameworthy attributes they describe you with. They have deviated from the truth and are confused. As a result, they cannot find the true path.

(٤٩) وَقَالُوٓاْ أَءِذَا كُنَّا عِظَٰمٗا وَرُفَٰتًا أَءِنَّا لَمَبۡعُوثُونَ خَلۡقٗا جَدِيدٗا

The polytheists said in denial of resurrection: When we die and become bones and our bodies decompose, will we be resurrected again? This is impossible.

Points of Benefit:

Claiming that the angels are the daughters of Allah is a great fabrication and a severe sin in the sight of Allah, the Almighty.
Allah's signs only increase most people in aversion due to their hatred of the truth and their love for the falsehood they are upon.
Every creature in the heavens and the earth glorifies and praises Allah. It is fitting for the servant not to let the creatures surpass him in glorifying Allah.
Among Allah's kindness towards His servants is that He does not immediately punish them for their negligence and evil deeds; His mercy precedes His anger.
