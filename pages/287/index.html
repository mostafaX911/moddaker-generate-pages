(٥٠) ۞قُلۡ كُونُواْ حِجَارَةً أَوۡ حَدِيدًا

Say, O Messenger: Become, if you can, O polytheists, as hard as stones or as strong as iron, but you will never be able to do this.

(٥١) أَوۡ خَلۡقٗا مِّمَّا يَكۡبُرُ فِي صُدُورِكُمۡۚ فَسَيَقُولُونَ مَن يُعِيدُنَاۖ قُلِ ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَكُمۡ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖۚ فَسَيُنۡغِضُونَ إِلَيۡكَ رُءُوسَهُمۡ وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هُوَۖ قُلۡ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُونَ قَرِيبٗا

Or become some other creation greater than them, which is considered great in your hearts. Allah shall restore you just as He initiated you, and He shall bring you back to life just as He created you in the first instance. These opponents will say: Who will bring us back to life after we die? Say to them: He who created you in the first instance without any precedent shall restore you. Then they will shake their heads, mocking your response to them, and say unbelievingly: When will this restoration be? Say to them: Perhaps it is near, as everything that is coming will soon arrive.

(٥٢) يَوۡمَ يَدۡعُوكُمۡ فَتَسۡتَجِيبُونَ بِحَمۡدِهِۦ وَتَظُنُّونَ إِن لَّبِثۡتُمۡ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

Allah will restore you on the day when He shall call you toward the plain of resurrection. You will then respond to Him, complying with His command and praising Him, and you will think you have not stayed in the grave except for a while.

(٥٣) وَقُل لِّعِبَادِي يَقُولُواْ ٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُۚ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ يَنزَغُ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ كَانَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ عَدُوّٗا مُّبِينٗا

Say, O Messenger, to my believing slaves that they should say good words when speaking and refrain from bad, off-putting words because the Devil exploits them to spread between them what will spoil their worldly life and their Hereafter. Surely, the Devil is an open enemy, so it is necessary to be wary of him.

(٥٤) رَّبُّكُمۡ أَعۡلَمُ بِكُمۡۖ إِن يَشَأۡ يَرۡحَمۡكُمۡ أَوۡ إِن يَشَأۡ يُعَذِّبۡكُمۡۚ وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَكِيلٗا

Your Lord, O people, knows you best. Nothing of yours is hidden from Him. If He wishes to have mercy on you, He will grace you with having faith and doing good deeds. And if He wishes to punish you, He can punish you by depriving you of faith, leaving you to die in disbelief. He has not sent the Messenger to be a deputy over people, to force them to believe, to stop them from disbelief, or to record their deeds. The Messenger is only a conveyor of what Allah has ordered him to convey on His behalf.

(٥٥) وَرَبُّكَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ وَلَقَدۡ فَضَّلۡنَا بَعۡضَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ‍ۧنَ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَا دَاوُۥدَ زَبُورٗا

Your Lord, O Messenger, has full knowledge of all who are in the heavens and Earth, and He fully knows their conditions and what they deserve. I preferred some prophets over others with their many followers and revealing books to them. And I granted David my book, the Psalms.

(٥٦) قُلِ ٱدۡعُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ زَعَمۡتُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ فَلَا يَمۡلِكُونَ كَشۡفَ ٱلضُّرِّ عَنكُمۡ وَلَا تَحۡوِيلًا

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: O polytheists, call upon those you claim to be gods besides Allah when some harm befalls you. They will neither be able to remove the harm from you nor can they transfer it to someone else due to their inability. Anything incapable cannot be a god.

(٥٧) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ يَبۡتَغُونَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمُ ٱلۡوَسِيلَةَ أَيُّهُمۡ أَقۡرَبُ وَيَرۡجُونَ رَحۡمَتَهُۥ وَيَخَافُونَ عَذَابَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ عَذَابَ رَبِّكَ كَانَ مَحۡذُورٗا

Those angels and others whom they call upon are themselves seeking good actions to bring them closer to Allah and competing to see who is closest to Him by obeying Him. They hope He has mercy on them, and they fear His punishment. The punishment of your Lord, O Messenger, is what should be feared.

(٥٨) وَإِن مِّن قَرۡيَةٍ إِلَّا نَحۡنُ مُهۡلِكُوهَا قَبۡلَ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ أَوۡ مُعَذِّبُوهَا عَذَابٗا شَدِيدٗاۚ كَانَ ذَٰلِكَ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مَسۡطُورٗا

And there is no town or city except I will send upon it punishment and destruction in this worldly life, or afflict it with a mighty punishment such as being killed, due to their disbelief. This destruction and punishment is a divine decree written in the Preserved Tablet.

Points of Benefit:

Speaking kindly calls for every beautiful characteristic and righteous deed. Indeed, whoever controls their tongue controls all aspects of their life.
Allah has distinguished between the prophets with His knowledge and wisdom.
Allah intends nothing but goodness for His servants and commands them with only what is in their best interest.
A sign of love for Allah is when a servant strives in every action to draw nearer to Allah, competes in sincerity in all deeds for the sake of Allah, and advises others to do the same.
