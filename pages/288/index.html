(٥٩) وَمَا مَنَعَنَآ أَن نُّرۡسِلَ بِٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ إِلَّآ أَن كَذَّبَ بِهَا ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَۚ وَءَاتَيۡنَا ثَمُودَ ٱلنَّاقَةَ مُبۡصِرَةٗ فَظَلَمُواْ بِهَاۚ وَمَا نُرۡسِلُ بِٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ إِلَّا تَخۡوِيفٗا

I did not stop sending tangible miracles that the polytheists requested, such as reviving the dead, except due to having sent them to previous nations, but they rejected them. I had given Thamūd a great, clear sign, namely the camel, but they rejected it, so I hastened their punishment. I do not send miracles at the hands of the Messengers except as a warning for their people so that they believe.

(٦٠) وَإِذۡ قُلۡنَا لَكَ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ أَحَاطَ بِٱلنَّاسِۚ وَمَا جَعَلۡنَا ٱلرُّءۡيَا ٱلَّتِيٓ أَرَيۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا فِتۡنَةٗ لِّلنَّاسِ وَٱلشَّجَرَةَ ٱلۡمَلۡعُونَةَ فِي ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِۚ وَنُخَوِّفُهُمۡ فَمَا يَزِيدُهُمۡ إِلَّا طُغۡيَٰنٗا كَبِيرٗا

And remember, O Messenger, when I said to you: Your Lord has encompassed the people in His power, and they are in His control. Allah will protect you from them, so convey what you have been ordered to convey. I did not make what I clearly showed you on the Night Journey except as a test for the people; do they believe it or reject it? And I did not make the tree of Zaqqum, which is mentioned in the Quran as growing from the bottom of the Inferno, except as a test for them. Thus, if they do not believe in these signs, they will never believe in anything else. I frighten them by sending signs, but they do not increase through them except in disbelief and descending further into misguidance.

(٦١) وَإِذۡ قُلۡنَا لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ ٱسۡجُدُواْ لِأٓدَمَ فَسَجَدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ قَالَ ءَأَسۡجُدُ لِمَنۡ خَلَقۡتَ طِينٗا

And remember, O Messenger, when I said to the angels: Prostrate to Adam, as a prostration of greeting, not worship, so they complied and all prostrated to him. However, Iblis refused to prostrate to him out of arrogance, saying: Should I prostrate to the one You created out of clay, whereas You created me out of Fire? I am better than him.

(٦٢) قَالَ أَرَءَيۡتَكَ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي كَرَّمۡتَ عَلَيَّ لَئِنۡ أَخَّرۡتَنِ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ لَأَحۡتَنِكَنَّ ذُرِّيَّتَهُۥٓ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

Iblis said to his Lord: Do you see this creature You have honored above me by ordering me to prostrate to him? If You keep me alive until the end of this worldly life, I shall allure his children and misguide them from Your straight path, except for a few whom You have saved and are Your chosen slaves.

(٦٣) قَالَ ٱذۡهَبۡ فَمَن تَبِعَكَ مِنۡهُمۡ فَإِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ جَزَآؤُكُمۡ جَزَآءٗ مَّوۡفُورٗا

His Lord said to him: Go with those amongst them who obey you because Hell is your recompense and their recompense; a complete and full compensation for your actions.

(٦٤) وَٱسۡتَفۡزِزۡ مَنِ ٱسۡتَطَعۡتَ مِنۡهُم بِصَوۡتِكَ وَأَجۡلِبۡ عَلَيۡهِم بِخَيۡلِكَ وَرَجِلِكَ وَشَارِكۡهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰلِ وَٱلۡأَوۡلَٰدِ وَعِدۡهُمۡۚ وَمَا يَعِدُهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ إِلَّا غُرُورًا

And mislead those whom you can mislead through your voice, which calls to disobedience. Call out to them through your horsemen and foot soldiers who invite them towards your obedience. Become a partner to them in their wealth by making every action that opposes the sacred law attractive, and become a partner to them in their children by falsely making a claim to them, attaining them through adultery, and making them worship others besides Allah when naming them. Make attractive to them false promises and futile aspirations; the Devil only makes false promises that deceive them.

(٦٥) إِنَّ عِبَادِي لَيۡسَ لَكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سُلۡطَٰنٞۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِرَبِّكَ وَكِيلٗا

You have no control, O Iblis, over My believing slaves who act in My obedience because Allah has repelled your evil from them. Allah is sufficient as a guardian for one who relies upon Him in his affairs.

(٦٦) رَّبُّكُمُ ٱلَّذِي يُزۡجِي لَكُمُ ٱلۡفُلۡكَ فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ لِتَبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ بِكُمۡ رَحِيمٗا

Your Lord, O people, is the One who makes the ships sail for you in the sea in the hope that you seek your sustenance through the profits from trade and the like. He is Merciful to you as He facilitated these means for you.

Points of Benefit:

Out of His mercy, Allah did not send down the specific signs demanded by the deniers so that they would not be hastened with punishment in case they denied them.
Allah has tested His servants with Satan, who invites them to disobey Allah through his words and deeds.
Examples of Satan's participation in human affairs related to wealth and children: leaving off the mention of Allah while eating, drinking, and during times of intimacy, as well as not disciplining children.
