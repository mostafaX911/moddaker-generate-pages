(٦٧) وَإِذَا مَسَّكُمُ ٱلضُّرُّ فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ ضَلَّ مَن تَدۡعُونَ إِلَّآ إِيَّاهُۖ فَلَمَّا نَجَّىٰكُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡبَرِّ أَعۡرَضۡتُمۡۚ وَكَانَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ كَفُورًا

And when you are afflicted, O polytheists, by some calamity or misfortune at sea such that you fear destruction, those you worship besides Allah leave your thoughts, and you only remember Allah and seek His assistance. Then, when He assists you and saves you from your fears, and you return to land, you turn away from His Oneness and from supplicating to Him alone, returning to your idols. Humanity is constantly rejecting the blessings of Allah.

(٦٨) أَفَأَمِنتُمۡ أَن يَخۡسِفَ بِكُمۡ جَانِبَ ٱلۡبَرِّ أَوۡ يُرۡسِلَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ حَاصِبٗا ثُمَّ لَا تَجِدُواْ لَكُمۡ وَكِيلًا

So do you feel secure, O polytheists, when Allah has delivered you safely to land that He will not make it collapse beneath you? Or do you feel secure that He will not make stones rain down on you as He did with the people of Lot? Then, you will not find any protector to protect you nor any helper to save you from being destroyed.

(٦٩) أَمۡ أَمِنتُمۡ أَن يُعِيدَكُمۡ فِيهِ تَارَةً أُخۡرَىٰ فَيُرۡسِلَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ قَاصِفٗا مِّنَ ٱلرِّيحِ فَيُغۡرِقَكُم بِمَا كَفَرۡتُمۡ ثُمَّ لَا تَجِدُواْ لَكُمۡ عَلَيۡنَا بِهِۦ تَبِيعٗا

Or do you feel secure that Allah will not return you to sea again and send a strong wind upon you, drowning you because you are ungrateful for Allah's blessings after He saved you the first time? Then, you will not find any claimant against Him for what He did to you to defend you.

(٧٠) ۞وَلَقَدۡ كَرَّمۡنَا بَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ وَحَمَلۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ وَرَزَقۡنَٰهُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ وَفَضَّلۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَىٰ كَثِيرٖ مِّمَّنۡ خَلَقۡنَا تَفۡضِيلٗا

And I have honored the children of Adam through intellect, making the angels prostrate to their father, and I subjugated for them the animals and vehicles that carry them on land and the ships that carry them by sea. I provided them with good food, drink, spouses, and other things and granted them much virtue over many of My creation. Therefore, they must be grateful for Allah's favors upon them.

(٧١) يَوۡمَ نَدۡعُواْ كُلَّ أُنَاسِۢ بِإِمَٰمِهِمۡۖ فَمَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يَقۡرَءُونَ كِتَٰبَهُمۡ وَلَا يُظۡلَمُونَ فَتِيلٗا

And remember, O Messenger, when every group will be called out by their leader whom they used to follow in life. So whoever is given his book of deeds in his right hand will read their books happily, and nothing will be decreased from their reward, even if it is as tiny as the thread on a date seed.

(٧٢) وَمَن كَانَ فِي هَٰذِهِۦٓ أَعۡمَىٰ فَهُوَ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ أَعۡمَىٰ وَأَضَلُّ سَبِيلٗا

And whoever's heart is blind to accepting and submitting to the truth in this worldly life will be more blind on the Day of Resurrection, not finding guidance to the path of paradise and straying from the path of guidance. The reward will be appropriate for the deed.

(٧٣) وَإِن كَادُواْ لَيَفۡتِنُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ لِتَفۡتَرِيَ عَلَيۡنَا غَيۡرَهُۥۖ وَإِذٗا لَّٱتَّخَذُوكَ خَلِيلٗا

And the polytheists were about to turn you away, O Messenger, from the Quran I revealed to you so that you invent something else in my name which conforms to their desires. And if you did what they wanted, they would have chosen you as their beloved friend.

(٧٤) وَلَوۡلَآ أَن ثَبَّتۡنَٰكَ لَقَدۡ كِدتَّ تَرۡكَنُ إِلَيۡهِمۡ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا قَلِيلًا

And had I not favored you by making you firm on the truth, you were about to incline towards them slightly and then agree with them in what they requested of you due to their severe deception and scheming, alongside your extreme eagerness for them to have faith. However, I protected you from inclining towards them.

(٧٥) إِذٗا لَّأَذَقۡنَٰكَ ضِعۡفَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ وَضِعۡفَ ٱلۡمَمَاتِ ثُمَّ لَا تَجِدُ لَكَ عَلَيۡنَا نَصِيرٗا

And had you inclined towards them in what they requested you, I would have given you multiplied punishment in this life and the Hereafter, then you would not find any helper to assist you against Me and avert the punishment from you.

Points of Benefit:

Humankind is generally ungrateful for blessings except for those guided by Allah.
Every nation is called to follow its religion and scripture. Have they acted upon it or not? Allah does not punish anyone until the proof has been established upon him and he has opposed it.
The enmity of the criminals and deniers towards the messengers and their successors is clearly due to the truth they carry and not due to their own selves.
Allah has protected the Prophet from the means of evil as well as humans. He strengthened and guided him on the straight path. Similarly, his successors receive such protection based on their adherence to it.
