(١٨٧) أُحِلَّ لَكُمۡ لَيۡلَةَ ٱلصِّيَامِ ٱلرَّفَثُ إِلَىٰ نِسَآئِكُمۡۚ هُنَّ لِبَاسٞ لَّكُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ لِبَاسٞ لَّهُنَّۗ عَلِمَ ٱللَّهُ أَنَّكُمۡ كُنتُمۡ تَخۡتَانُونَ أَنفُسَكُمۡ فَتَابَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَعَفَا عَنكُمۡۖ فَٱلۡـَٰٔنَ بَٰشِرُوهُنَّ وَٱبۡتَغُواْ مَا كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۚ وَكُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ حَتَّىٰ يَتَبَيَّنَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡخَيۡطُ ٱلۡأَبۡيَضُ مِنَ ٱلۡخَيۡطِ ٱلۡأَسۡوَدِ مِنَ ٱلۡفَجۡرِۖ ثُمَّ أَتِمُّواْ ٱلصِّيَامَ إِلَى ٱلَّيۡلِۚ وَلَا تُبَٰشِرُوهُنَّ وَأَنتُمۡ عَٰكِفُونَ فِي ٱلۡمَسَٰجِدِۗ تِلۡكَ حُدُودُ ٱللَّهِ فَلَا تَقۡرَبُوهَاۗ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ لِلنَّاسِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَّقُونَ

At first, a man was not allowed to eat or approach his wife if he had gone to sleep on the night of a fast and then woke up before dawn. Allah replaced this ruling, allowing you, O believers, to have relations with your wives during the nights of fasting. They are a protection for you, keeping you chaste, and you are a cover for them, keeping them chaste. You both need each other. Allah knew that you were betraying yourselves by doing what you were not allowed to do, so He showed mercy, turned to you when you asked for forgiveness and made things easier for you. So, have relations with them, and seek the children Allah has decreed for you, and eat and drink as you please during the whole night until the break of true dawn appears with the light of dawn separated from the darkness of the night. Then, complete your fast by abstaining from anything that breaks the fast from the break of dawn until sunset. And do not have relations with your wives while you are in a spiritual retreat in the mosques because that invalidates the retreat. These laws are Allah's limits: between what is allowed and what is forbidden– so do not ever go near them. Whoever goes near the limits of Allah comes close to falling into that which is prohibited. By setting out a clear explanation of these laws, Allah makes His verses obvious to people so that they may be mindful of Him by doing what He instructs and leaving what He has prohibited.

(١٨٨) وَلَا تَأۡكُلُوٓاْ أَمۡوَٰلَكُم بَيۡنَكُم بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ وَتُدۡلُواْ بِهَآ إِلَى ٱلۡحُكَّامِ لِتَأۡكُلُواْ فَرِيقٗا مِّنۡ أَمۡوَٰلِ ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَأَنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

Do not unlawfully take each other's wealth, such as by stealing it, taking it by force, or by cheating. Do not create legal disputes with rulers to obtain others' wealth wrongfully while you know that Allah has prohibited you from doing this. Sinning while knowing of its prohibition is more shameful and greater in punishment.

(١٨٩) ۞يَسۡ‍َٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلۡأَهِلَّةِۖ قُلۡ هِيَ مَوَٰقِيتُ لِلنَّاسِ وَٱلۡحَجِّۗ وَلَيۡسَ ٱلۡبِرُّ بِأَن تَأۡتُواْ ٱلۡبُيُوتَ مِن ظُهُورِهَا وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱلۡبِرَّ مَنِ ٱتَّقَىٰۗ وَأۡتُواْ ٱلۡبُيُوتَ مِنۡ أَبۡوَٰبِهَاۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ

They ask you, O Messenger, about the formation of the new moons and their changing states. Tell them in reply about the wisdom behind this: that they are a way for people to measure time so they know when to perform their acts of worship, such as the months of Hajj and fasting and the completion of the year for Zakat. It is also so that they know the time for their transactions, such as the deadlines for paying blood money or debts. It is not righteousness or piety that you come to houses from the rear wearing your iḥrām for Hajj or Umrah, as you used to claim pre-Islam. Rather, true righteousness lies in those mindful of Allah, outwardly and inwardly. Entering houses from their correct doors is easier for you and less troublesome. Allah does not overburden you with difficulty and hardship, so create a barrier from the punishment of Allah through good deeds, so perhaps you may be successful in achieving what you desire, and attaining salvation from what you fear.

(١٩٠) وَقَٰتِلُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِينَ يُقَٰتِلُونَكُمۡ وَلَا تَعۡتَدُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُعۡتَدِينَ

Fight with the desire to raise the word of Allah against those among the disbelievers who fight you to turn you away from the religion of Allah. But do not overstep Allah's limits by killing children, women, the elderly, or by mutilating the dead, and so on. Allah does not love those who overstep the limits He has established and made sacred.

Points of Benefit:

The legitimacy of i'tikaf (spiritual retreat), which is to devote oneself to worshipping inside the masjid. So, everything that contradicts the objective of i'tikaf is prohibited, including being intimate with women.
The forbidding of unjustly consuming the wealth of others and the prohibition of any means and practices that lead to that, including bribery.
The forbidding and prohibiting of abuse because the Religion is established upon justice and good treatment.
