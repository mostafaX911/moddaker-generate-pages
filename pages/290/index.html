(٧٦) وَإِن كَادُواْ لَيَسۡتَفِزُّونَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لِيُخۡرِجُوكَ مِنۡهَاۖ وَإِذٗا لَّا يَلۡبَثُونَ خِلَٰفَكَ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

And the disbelievers were close to troubling you, due to their enmity towards you, by exiling you from Mecca. However, Allah stopped them from exiling you until you yourself migrated on the command of your Lord. And had they exiled you, they would not have remained after exiling you except for a short while.

(٧٧) سُنَّةَ مَن قَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا قَبۡلَكَ مِن رُّسُلِنَاۖ وَلَا تَجِدُ لِسُنَّتِنَا تَحۡوِيلًا

The issue of their not remaining after you except for a short while is Allah's established practice with respect to the Messengers before you, namely that whenever a Messenger was exiled by his people, Allah sent punishment upon them. And you will not find, O Messenger, any change in my practice, but rather, you will find it to be established and constant.

(٧٨) أَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ لِدُلُوكِ ٱلشَّمۡسِ إِلَىٰ غَسَقِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَقُرۡءَانَ ٱلۡفَجۡرِۖ إِنَّ قُرۡءَانَ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ كَانَ مَشۡهُودٗا

Establish the prayer by performing it in a complete manner in its times, which are the declining of the sun from the middle of the sky (this includes the Dhuhr and Asr prayers) until the darkness of night (this includes the Maghrib and Isha prayers). And establish the Fajr prayer, lengthening the recitation therein, as the Fajr prayer is attended by the angels of the night and the angels of day.

(٧٩) وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَتَهَجَّدۡ بِهِۦ نَافِلَةٗ لَّكَ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَبۡعَثَكَ رَبُّكَ مَقَامٗا مَّحۡمُودٗا

And in part of the night, O Messenger, stand and pray, so that your prayer increases your rank, seeking for your Lord to raise you on the Day of Resurrection as an intercessor for the people from the horrors of the Day of Resurrection, and so that you attain the position of the major intercession, for which all from the first to the last will praise you.

(٨٠) وَقُل رَّبِّ أَدۡخِلۡنِي مُدۡخَلَ صِدۡقٖ وَأَخۡرِجۡنِي مُخۡرَجَ صِدۡقٖ وَٱجۡعَل لِّي مِن لَّدُنكَ سُلۡطَٰنٗا نَّصِيرٗا

Say, O Messenger: O my Lord, make all my entrances and exits in Your obedience and for Your pleasure, and give me from Yourself a clear proof through which You help me against my enemy.

(٨١) وَقُلۡ جَآءَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَزَهَقَ ٱلۡبَٰطِلُۚ إِنَّ ٱلۡبَٰطِلَ كَانَ زَهُوقٗا

And say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: Islam has come, the help has come as Allah promised, and idolatry and disbelief have gone. Surely, falsehood is going and will disappear, unable to remain in the face of truth.

(٨٢) وَنُنَزِّلُ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مَا هُوَ شِفَآءٞ وَرَحۡمَةٞ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَلَا يَزِيدُ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا خَسَارٗا

I reveal in the Quran that which cures the hearts from ignorance, disbelief, and doubt, and cures the bodies when treated through it, and that which is a mercy for the believers who act upon it. And this Quran only increases the disbelievers in destruction, as listening to it angers them and makes them deny and turn away from it even more.

(٨٣) وَإِذَآ أَنۡعَمۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ أَعۡرَضَ وَنَ‍َٔا بِجَانِبِهِۦ وَإِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلشَّرُّ كَانَ يَ‍ُٔوسٗا

And when I grant man a blessing such as health and wealth, he turns away from thanking Allah and obeying Him and moves further away out of pride. But when illness, poverty, or the like befalls him, he becomes extremely despondent and loses hope of Allah's mercy.

(٨٤) قُلۡ كُلّٞ يَعۡمَلُ عَلَىٰ شَاكِلَتِهِۦ فَرَبُّكُمۡ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَنۡ هُوَ أَهۡدَىٰ سَبِيلٗا

Say, O Messenger: Every man acts on the path that resembles his condition in either guidance or misguidance. But your Lord knows best who is most guided towards the truth.

(٨٥) وَيَسۡ‍َٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلرُّوحِۖ قُلِ ٱلرُّوحُ مِنۡ أَمۡرِ رَبِّي وَمَآ أُوتِيتُم مِّنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

O Messenger, the disbelievers among the People of the Book ask you regarding the reality of the soul. Say to them: The soul is from the command of my Lord. And the knowledge you and all the creation of Allah have been granted is little in comparison to the knowledge of Allah.

(٨٦) وَلَئِن شِئۡنَا لَنَذۡهَبَنَّ بِٱلَّذِيٓ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ثُمَّ لَا تَجِدُ لَكَ بِهِۦ عَلَيۡنَا وَكِيلًا

By Allah, if We wanted to take away, O Messenger, the revelation We sent to you by erasing it from the hearts and books, We could take it away, then you would not find anyone to help you and take the responsibility to bring it back.

Points of Benefit:

In these verses, there is evidence of the servant's extreme need for Allah to keep him firm, and it is fitting for him not to cease turning to his Lord to grant him firmness in his faith.
When the truth appears, falsehood dissipates, and falsehood does not prevail except in times and places where the people of truth are negligent.
The healing promised in the Quran is general, addressing the healing of the hearts from doubts, ignorance, corrupt opinions, evil deviations, and bad intentions.
In these verses, there is evidence that when a person is asked about a matter that is not in the questioner's best interest, it is preferable for them to refrain from answering directly, guide them to what benefits them, and direct them to what they need.
