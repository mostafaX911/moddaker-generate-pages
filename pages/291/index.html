(٨٧) إِلَّا رَحۡمَةٗ مِّن رَّبِّكَۚ إِنَّ فَضۡلَهُۥ كَانَ عَلَيۡكَ كَبِيرٗا

However, We did not take it away by way of mercy from your Lord, and We left it preserved. Surely, your Lord's grace on you is great, as He made you a Messenger, sealed the prophets upon you, and revealed the Quran to you.

When the polytheists used the excuse that this Quran is similar to human speech and proposed changing it, Allah challenged them to come up with something like it, stating:

(٨٨) قُل لَّئِنِ ٱجۡتَمَعَتِ ٱلۡإِنسُ وَٱلۡجِنُّ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يَأۡتُواْ بِمِثۡلِ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ لَا يَأۡتُونَ بِمِثۡلِهِۦ وَلَوۡ كَانَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ لِبَعۡضٖ ظَهِيرٗا

Say, O Messenger: If all of humanity and jinn united to bring the like of this Quran which was revealed to you, in its eloquence, excellent sequence, and pure style, they would never be able to bring anything like it, even if they were to all assist and help one another.

(٨٩) وَلَقَدۡ صَرَّفۡنَا لِلنَّاسِ فِي هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مِن كُلِّ مَثَلٖ فَأَبَىٰٓ أَكۡثَرُ ٱلنَّاسِ إِلَّا كُفُورٗا

And I have explained to people in this Quran and brought all types of advice, reminders, commands, prohibitions, and stories they can take lessons from, in the hope they will believe. But most people have refused all except to reject and deny this Quran.

When they were unable to do so, they began proposing what they considered impossible, suggesting the following:

(٩٠) وَقَالُواْ لَن نُّؤۡمِنَ لَكَ حَتَّىٰ تَفۡجُرَ لَنَا مِنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَنۢبُوعًا

The polytheists said: We will never believe you until you extract a spring from the ground of Mecca for us that does not stop flowing.

(٩١) أَوۡ تَكُونَ لَكَ جَنَّةٞ مِّن نَّخِيلٖ وَعِنَبٖ فَتُفَجِّرَ ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرَ خِلَٰلَهَا تَفۡجِيرًا

Or you have a garden with many flowers in which you make rivers flow profusely.

(٩٢) أَوۡ تُسۡقِطَ ٱلسَّمَآءَ كَمَا زَعَمۡتَ عَلَيۡنَا كِسَفًا أَوۡ تَأۡتِيَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ قَبِيلًا

Or you make the sky fall upon us, as you mentioned, as fragments of punishment, or you bring Allah and the angels visibly before us so they can testify to the truth of what you claim.

(٩٣) أَوۡ يَكُونَ لَكَ بَيۡتٞ مِّن زُخۡرُفٍ أَوۡ تَرۡقَىٰ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَلَن نُّؤۡمِنَ لِرُقِيِّكَ حَتَّىٰ تُنَزِّلَ عَلَيۡنَا كِتَٰبٗا نَّقۡرَؤُهُۥۗ قُلۡ سُبۡحَانَ رَبِّي هَلۡ كُنتُ إِلَّا بَشَرٗا رَّسُولٗا

Or you have a house adorned with gold and other things, or you ascend to the sky. And if you do ascend to it, we will never believe you have been sent unless you come down with a book written by Allah in which we can read that you are Allah's Messenger. Say, O Messenger: My Lord is pure; I am only a human Messenger like all other Messengers. I do not have the ability to bring anything, so how can I bring what you have requested?

(٩٤) وَمَا مَنَعَ ٱلنَّاسَ أَن يُؤۡمِنُوٓاْ إِذۡ جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡهُدَىٰٓ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُوٓاْ أَبَعَثَ ٱللَّهُ بَشَرٗا رَّسُولٗا

The only thing that stopped the disbelievers from believing in Allah and His Messengers and acting upon what the Messenger brought was their refusal to accept that a Messenger can be from humanity, as they said denyingly: Did Allah send a Messenger from humanity?

(٩٥) قُل لَّوۡ كَانَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَلَٰٓئِكَةٞ يَمۡشُونَ مُطۡمَئِنِّينَ لَنَزَّلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَلَكٗا رَّسُولٗا

Say, O Messenger, in response to them: Had there been angels on Earth who reside therein and walk about securely, as is your condition, we would have sent an angel Messenger to them from their own kind because it is he who can make them understand what he has been sent with. It would not be wise to send them a Messenger from humanity; likewise this is the case with you.

(٩٦) قُلۡ كَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ شَهِيدَۢا بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ بِعِبَادِهِۦ خَبِيرَۢا بَصِيرٗا

Say, O Messenger: Allah is sufficient as a witness between you and me that I am a Messenger who has been sent to you, and I have conveyed what I was sent with to you. Surely, He encompasses and is fully aware of the conditions of His slaves, nothing of them being hidden from Him, and He sees all the secrets of their selves.

Points of Benefit:

In the Quran, Allah makes various admonitions, lessons, commands, prohibitions, and stories clear for the people so that they may believe.
The Quran is the speech of Allah, the everlasting miracle of the Prophet, and no one can produce anything like it.
Out of Allah's mercy for His servants, He sent human messengers to them from among themselves, as they are not capable of receiving directly from angels.
Allah's testimony to His Messenger is reinforced by the signs and victories granted to him, as well as the support against those who oppose and are hostile towards him.
