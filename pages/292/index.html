(٩٧) وَمَن يَهۡدِ ٱللَّهُ فَهُوَ ٱلۡمُهۡتَدِۖ وَمَن يُضۡلِلۡ فَلَن تَجِدَ لَهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءَ مِن دُونِهِۦۖ وَنَحۡشُرُهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ عَلَىٰ وُجُوهِهِمۡ عُمۡيٗا وَبُكۡمٗا وَصُمّٗاۖ مَّأۡوَىٰهُمۡ جَهَنَّمُۖ كُلَّمَا خَبَتۡ زِدۡنَٰهُمۡ سَعِيرٗا

And whoever Allah grants guidance to is truly guided. Whoever He removes from it and leads astray, you will never find for them, O Messenger, protectors to guide them to the truth, repel harm from them, and bring them benefit. I will gather them on the Day of Resurrection, being dragged on their faces, not seeing, speaking, or hearing. Their abode towards which they will go is Hell. Whenever the flames begin to subside, I will make them burn even more.

(٩٨) ذَٰلِكَ جَزَآؤُهُم بِأَنَّهُمۡ كَفَرُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَقَالُوٓاْ أَءِذَا كُنَّا عِظَٰمٗا وَرُفَٰتًا أَءِنَّا لَمَبۡعُوثُونَ خَلۡقٗا جَدِيدًا

That punishment which they will meet is their recompense due to their denial of my signs which were sent down upon (given to) my Messengers, and because they said, considering resurrection to be improbable: When we die and turn into decomposed bones and scattered particles, will we be resurrected after this as a new creation?

When they mentioned what they obstinately cling to in order to deny the Resurrection, Allah responded to them by stating:

(٩٩) ۞أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ قَادِرٌ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يَخۡلُقَ مِثۡلَهُمۡ وَجَعَلَ لَهُمۡ أَجَلٗا لَّا رَيۡبَ فِيهِ فَأَبَى ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ إِلَّا كُفُورٗا

Do these deniers of the resurrection not know that Allah, who created the heavens and who created the Earth despite their greatness, has the power to create the likes of them? So, whoever has the power to create something great has the power to create something smaller. And Allah has given them limited time in this world in which their lives will come to an end, and He has made a time for their resurrection in which there is no doubt. But despite the proofs for resurrection being clear, the polytheists refuse all except to reject the resurrection.

(١٠٠) قُل لَّوۡ أَنتُمۡ تَمۡلِكُونَ خَزَآئِنَ رَحۡمَةِ رَبِّيٓ إِذٗا لَّأَمۡسَكۡتُمۡ خَشۡيَةَ ٱلۡإِنفَاقِۚ وَكَانَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ قَتُورٗا

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: If you possessed my Lord's treasures of mercy which never deplete or finish, you would abstain from spending them, fearing they would run out, making you turn poor. And it is human nature to be miserly, except if one is a believer who spends hoping for Allah's reward.

When the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم faced the rejection that he faced from the polytheists, his solace came through the story of Moses with Pharoah and his people. Allah states:

(١٠١) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَىٰ تِسۡعَ ءَايَٰتِۢ بَيِّنَٰتٖۖ فَسۡ‍َٔلۡ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ إِذۡ جَآءَهُمۡ فَقَالَ لَهُۥ فِرۡعَوۡنُ إِنِّي لَأَظُنُّكَ يَٰمُوسَىٰ مَسۡحُورٗا

I granted Moses nine clear proofs, which bore testimony for him. They are the staff, the hand, the famine, the decrease in fruits, the hurricane, locusts, lice, frogs, and blood. O Messenger, ask the Jews about when Moses brought these miracles to their predecessors, and Pharaoh said: I consider you, O Moses, a man afflicted by magic, as you bring strange things.

(١٠٢) قَالَ لَقَدۡ عَلِمۡتَ مَآ أَنزَلَ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ إِلَّا رَبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ بَصَآئِرَ وَإِنِّي لَأَظُنُّكَ يَٰفِرۡعَوۡنُ مَثۡبُورٗا

Moses said in response to him: O Pharaoh, you know for sure that these signs have not been sent except by Allah, the Lord of the heavens and the Earth. He has sent them as proofs of His power and the truthfulness of His Messenger. But you have rejected them. And I know, O Pharaoh, that you are going to be a destroyed loser.

(١٠٣) فَأَرَادَ أَن يَسۡتَفِزَّهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَأَغۡرَقۡنَٰهُ وَمَن مَّعَهُۥ جَمِيعٗا

So Pharaoh intended to punish Moses (peace be upon him) and his people by exiling them from Egypt, so I destroyed him and all the armies with him by drowning them.

(١٠٤) وَقُلۡنَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ لِبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ ٱسۡكُنُواْ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ فَإِذَا جَآءَ وَعۡدُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ جِئۡنَا بِكُمۡ لَفِيفٗا

And I said to the Israelites after destroying Pharaoh and his armies: Settle in the land of the Levant. Then, when the Day of Resurrection comes, I shall bring you all to the plain of gathering for the reckoning.

Points of Benefit:

Allah, the Transcendent, is the only one who guides and lets astray; whoever He guides is truly guided, and whoever He sends astray and abandons has no guide.
The abode and residence of the disbelievers is Hell, and as its fire intensifies, Allah adds more blazing flames to it.
It is obligatory to hold firmly to Allah when faced with the threats of tyrants and oppressors.
Tyrants and oppressors resort to using power and force when confronted by people of the truth because they are unable to face them with arguments and evidence.
