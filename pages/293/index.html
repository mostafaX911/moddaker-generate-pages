(١٠٥) وَبِٱلۡحَقِّ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ وَبِٱلۡحَقِّ نَزَلَۗ وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا مُبَشِّرٗا وَنَذِيرٗا

With truth, I revealed the Quran to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, and with truth, it came down upon him without any change or tampering. And I did not send you, O Messenger, except to give glad tidings of paradise to the God-fearing and to warn the disbelievers and those who are sinful of the Fire.

(١٠٦) وَقُرۡءَانٗا فَرَقۡنَٰهُ لِتَقۡرَأَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ عَلَىٰ مُكۡثٖ وَنَزَّلۡنَٰهُ تَنزِيلٗا

I sent it as a Quran, which I have divided into parts and explained in the hope that you read it to people slowly and carefully, as this is more effective for understanding and reflecting. And I revealed it part by part according to incidents and conditions.

(١٠٧) قُلۡ ءَامِنُواْ بِهِۦٓ أَوۡ لَا تُؤۡمِنُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ مِن قَبۡلِهِۦٓ إِذَا يُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ يَخِرُّونَۤ لِلۡأَذۡقَانِۤ سُجَّدٗاۤ

Say, O Messenger: Believe in it, for your belief will not increase it in any way (will not benefit it), or do not believe in it, for your disbelief will not decrease it in any way (harm it in any way). Surely, those who read the previous divine books and understand the revelation and prophecy when the Quran is recited to them fall on their faces, prostrating to Allah out of gratitude.

(١٠٨) وَيَقُولُونَ سُبۡحَٰنَ رَبِّنَآ إِن كَانَ وَعۡدُ رَبِّنَا لَمَفۡعُولٗا

And they say in their prostration: Our Lord will never break His promise. The promise He made of sending Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم is going to happen. Surely, our Lord's promise of this and other matters will definitely occur.

(١٠٩) وَيَخِرُّونَ لِلۡأَذۡقَانِ يَبۡكُونَ وَيَزِيدُهُمۡ خُشُوعٗا۩

They fall on their faces, prostrating to Allah and crying in awe of Him. Listening to the Quran and contemplating its meanings increases their submission to Allah and awe of Him.

(١١٠) قُلِ ٱدۡعُواْ ٱللَّهَ أَوِ ٱدۡعُواْ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنَۖ أَيّٗا مَّا تَدۡعُواْ فَلَهُ ٱلۡأَسۡمَآءُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۚ وَلَا تَجۡهَرۡ بِصَلَاتِكَ وَلَا تُخَافِتۡ بِهَا وَٱبۡتَغِ بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَ سَبِيلٗا

Say, O Messenger, to those who criticize you for supplicating by saying, 'O Allah, O Merciful': Allah and the Merciful are names of His, so supplicate to Him with either of them or other names of His. For Him are all beautiful names, and these two are amongst them, so supplicate to Him through these two or His other beautiful names. Do not recite loudly in your prayer in case the polytheists hear you, and do not recite so quietly that the believers cannot hear you, but rather choose a middle path between the two.

(١١١) وَقُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي لَمۡ يَتَّخِذۡ وَلَدٗا وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ شَرِيكٞ فِي ٱلۡمُلۡكِ وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ وَلِيّٞ مِّنَ ٱلذُّلِّۖ وَكَبِّرۡهُ تَكۡبِيرَۢا

Say - O Messenger: All praise is for Allah, who deserves all types of praise and who is exalted from having a child or a partner; thus, He has no partner in His kingdom. And He is not afflicted by disgrace or weakness; hence, He has no need for anyone to help and support Him. So exalt Him abundantly, and do not attribute any child or partner to Him nor any helper or supporter.

The Cave (Surah Al-Kahf)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Explaining the manner of dealing with trials and tribulations.

Explanation:

(١) ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِهِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَلَمۡ يَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ عِوَجَاۜ

Praise - with the attributes of perfection and majesty and for the apparent and hidden favors - is for Allah alone, who revealed the Quran to His slave and Messenger and did not put any crookedness or diversion from the truth in this Quran.

(٢) قَيِّمٗا لِّيُنذِرَ بَأۡسٗا شَدِيدٗا مِّن لَّدُنۡهُ وَيُبَشِّرَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡمَلُونَ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ أَنَّ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرًا حَسَنٗا

Rather, He made it upright without any contradiction or discrepancy in it to frighten those who deny the truth of a severe punishment that awaits them from Allah and to tell the believers who do good actions that which will please them, namely that there is a good reward for them which no other reward compares to.

(٣) مَّٰكِثِينَ فِيهِ أَبَدٗا

(The believers will be) Living in this reward forever, without it being cut off from them.

(٤) وَيُنذِرَ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ ٱتَّخَذَ ٱللَّهُ وَلَدٗا

And it frightens the Jews, Christians, and some of the polytheists who said: Allah has taken a son.

Points of Benefit:

Allah revealed the Quran, encompassing truth, justice, divine law, and exemplary rulings.
The permissibility of crying in prayer out of fearing Allah, the Transcendent.
Supplication or recitation in prayer should be done in a moderate manner, between audible and silent.
The Noble Quran has encompassed every righteous deed that uplifts the spirits and brings joy to the souls.
