(٥) مَّا لَهُم بِهِۦ مِنۡ عِلۡمٖ وَلَا لِأٓبَآئِهِمۡۚ كَبُرَتۡ كَلِمَةٗ تَخۡرُجُ مِنۡ أَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡۚ إِن يَقُولُونَ إِلَّا كَذِبٗا

These fabricators have no knowledge nor evidence for their claim that Allah has a son, and nor did their fathers whom they followed in this regard have any knowledge. This statement, which has escaped their mouths without any understanding, is heinous. They only speak a lie that has no basis or support.

(٦) فَلَعَلَّكَ بَٰخِعٞ نَّفۡسَكَ عَلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِهِمۡ إِن لَّمۡ يُؤۡمِنُواْ بِهَٰذَا ٱلۡحَدِيثِ أَسَفًا

Then perhaps, O Messenger, you will end up destroying yourself out of grief and remorse if they do not believe this Quran. But do not do this, as making them accept guidance is not your responsibility; you are only responsible for conveying it.

(٧) إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَا مَا عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ زِينَةٗ لَّهَا لِنَبۡلُوَهُمۡ أَيُّهُمۡ أَحۡسَنُ عَمَلٗا

Surely, I have made the creation on the face of this Earth an adornment for it, so I can test them to see which of them does the best actions, which please Allah, and which of them is worst in actions, so that I may reward everyone with what they deserve.

(٨) وَإِنَّا لَجَٰعِلُونَ مَا عَلَيۡهَا صَعِيدٗا جُرُزًا

And I am going to turn the creation on the face of this Earth into dust free from vegetation, and this will be after the lifespan of the creation on Earth expires, so let them take heed of this.

(٩) أَمۡ حَسِبۡتَ أَنَّ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡكَهۡفِ وَٱلرَّقِيمِ كَانُواْ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا عَجَبًا

Do not think, O Messenger, that the story of the people of the cave and the tablet on which their names were written are from our amazing signs. In fact, other signs, such as creating the heavens and Earth, are more amazing.

(١٠) إِذۡ أَوَى ٱلۡفِتۡيَةُ إِلَى ٱلۡكَهۡفِ فَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةٗ وَهَيِّئۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَمۡرِنَا رَشَدٗا

Remember, O Messenger, when the believing youngsters took refuge in the cave, fleeing with their religion. Then they said in their supplication to their Lord: O Our Lord, give us mercy from Yourself by forgiving our sins and saving us from our enemies and grant us guidance towards the right path and correctness in the matter of migrating from the disbelievers and having faith.

(١١) فَضَرَبۡنَا عَلَىٰٓ ءَاذَانِهِمۡ فِي ٱلۡكَهۡفِ سِنِينَ عَدَدٗا

Then after they went and took refuge in the cave, We put a barrier on their ears from hearing, and We put them to sleep for many years.

(١٢) ثُمَّ بَعَثۡنَٰهُمۡ لِنَعۡلَمَ أَيُّ ٱلۡحِزۡبَيۡنِ أَحۡصَىٰ لِمَا لَبِثُوٓاْ أَمَدٗا

Then, after their lengthy sleep, We woke them up to demonstrate which of the two groups disputing their duration of stay in the cave knows this duration best.

(١٣) نَّحۡنُ نَقُصُّ عَلَيۡكَ نَبَأَهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ إِنَّهُمۡ فِتۡيَةٌ ءَامَنُواْ بِرَبِّهِمۡ وَزِدۡنَٰهُمۡ هُدٗى

We are informing you, O Messenger, of their affair truthfully and without any doubt. Surely, they were youngsters who believed in their Lord and obeyed Him, and We increased them in guidance and firmness on the truth.

(١٤) وَرَبَطۡنَا عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ إِذۡ قَامُواْ فَقَالُواْ رَبُّنَا رَبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ لَن نَّدۡعُوَاْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ إِلَٰهٗاۖ لَّقَدۡ قُلۡنَآ إِذٗا شَطَطًا

And We strengthened their hearts with faith and firmness on it, and to be patient in leaving one's homeland for its sake, when they stood to proclaim their faith in Allah alone in front of the disbelieving king, and they said: The Lord we believe in and worship is the Lord of the heavens and Earth. We will never worship the gods you have falsely claimed besides Him. We will have said an unjust and far-off statement if we worship others besides Him.

(١٥) هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ قَوۡمُنَا ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ ءَالِهَةٗۖ لَّوۡلَا يَأۡتُونَ عَلَيۡهِم بِسُلۡطَٰنِۢ بَيِّنٖۖ فَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّنِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبٗا

Then they turned to each other, saying: These are our people who have taken deities which they worship besides Allah. They do not have any clear proof for worshipping them, so there is no greater oppressor than one who falsely attributes a partner to Allah.

Points of Benefit:

The caller to Allah should convey the message and exert effort to the best of his ability while relying on Allah in this endeavor. If people are guided through it, it is a success and blessing; otherwise, one should neither grieve nor despair.
In knowing the duration the companions of the cave stayed, there is a precise measure, an account set straight, and an understanding of the perfect capability, wisdom, and mercy of Allah, the Transcendent.
These verses unequivocally endorse finding solace in the religion and undertaking migration, leaving behind family, children, relatives, friends, homelands, and wealth, out of fearing tribulations.
Focusing on the youth's upbringing is imperative as they possess purer hearts, clearer minds, are more sensitive and the revival of nations depends on them.
