(٢١) وَكَذَٰلِكَ أَعۡثَرۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ لِيَعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقّٞ وَأَنَّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهَآ إِذۡ يَتَنَٰزَعُونَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ أَمۡرَهُمۡۖ فَقَالُواْ ٱبۡنُواْ عَلَيۡهِم بُنۡيَٰنٗاۖ رَّبُّهُمۡ أَعۡلَمُ بِهِمۡۚ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ غَلَبُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَمۡرِهِمۡ لَنَتَّخِذَنَّ عَلَيۡهِم مَّسۡجِدٗا

Just as I did those wondrous acts of putting them to sleep and awakening them after many years, which shows Our Power, We caused them to be discovered by the people of their city so that those people would know that the promise of Allah to help the believers and of resurrection [from the graves] is true and that the [Day of] Resurrection is coming without a doubt. After the matter of the people of the cave was exposed, and they had passed away, those who discovered them differed: What should they do with regard to them? One group said: Build over the entrance of the cave a building that will conceal and preserve them. Their Lord knows best about their condition, and their condition requires that they have some special status with Him. The influential people who did not have knowledge or a proper call said: We will set up over them a place of worship in their honor and as a memoir of their location.

(٢٢) سَيَقُولُونَ ثَلَٰثَةٞ رَّابِعُهُمۡ كَلۡبُهُمۡ وَيَقُولُونَ خَمۡسَةٞ سَادِسُهُمۡ كَلۡبُهُمۡ رَجۡمَۢا بِٱلۡغَيۡبِۖ وَيَقُولُونَ سَبۡعَةٞ وَثَامِنُهُمۡ كَلۡبُهُمۡۚ قُل رَّبِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ بِعِدَّتِهِم مَّا يَعۡلَمُهُمۡ إِلَّا قَلِيلٞۗ فَلَا تُمَارِ فِيهِمۡ إِلَّا مِرَآءٗ ظَٰهِرٗا وَلَا تَسۡتَفۡتِ فِيهِم مِّنۡهُمۡ أَحَدٗا

Some of those who delve into their incident will say about their number: They were three, and their dog was the fourth among them. Others will say: They were five, and their dog was the sixth amongst them. Both groups only said this as a guess without any proof. Some of them will say: They were seven, and their dog was the eighth among them. Say, O Messenger: Your Lord knows best about their number. No one knows their number except for a few people whom Allah has informed about their number. So do not dispute with regard to their number or any other matter of theirs with the people of the scripture or with others except with an obvious argument and without delving into the matter, limiting it to the revelation that has come down to you with respect to them. Do not ask any of them about the details of their matter because they do not know about it.

(٢٣) وَلَا تَقُولَنَّ لِشَاْيۡءٍ إِنِّي فَاعِلٞ ذَٰلِكَ غَدًا

Do not say, O Prophet, about anything that you want to do tomorrow, "I am going to do this tomorrow," because you do not know whether you will do it or you will be prevented from doing so. This is a directive for every Muslim.

(٢٤) إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ وَٱذۡكُر رَّبَّكَ إِذَا نَسِيتَ وَقُلۡ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَهۡدِيَنِ رَبِّي لِأَقۡرَبَ مِنۡ هَٰذَا رَشَدٗا

Unless you suspend doing so on Allah's will by saying: I will do it - if Allah wills - tomorrow. Remember your Lord by saying: "If Allah wills" - if you forget to say it - and say: I have hope that my Lord will guide me to something closer than this in guidance and success.

(٢٥) وَلَبِثُواْ فِي كَهۡفِهِمۡ ثَلَٰثَ مِاْئَةٖ سِنِينَ وَٱزۡدَادُواْ تِسۡعٗا

The people of the cave remained in their cave for three hundred and nine years.

(٢٦) قُلِ ٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا لَبِثُواْۖ لَهُۥ غَيۡبُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ أَبۡصِرۡ بِهِۦ وَأَسۡمِعۡۚ مَا لَهُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ مِن وَلِيّٖ وَلَا يُشۡرِكُ فِي حُكۡمِهِۦٓ أَحَدٗا

Say, O Messenger: Allah knows best about how long they remained in the cave, and He has told us about the period they remained there. No statement can be considered after Allah's; May He be Exalted. To Him alone (may He be Exalted) belongs everything hidden in the heavens and on Earth. He created and knows all of it. How well He sees! May He be Exalted! He sees everything. How well He hears! He hears everything. They have no one to take charge of their matter besides Him. He does not share His rule with anyone. He alone passes judgment.

After mentioning that dominion belongs to Allah alone, He commands His messenger to recite what was revealed to him about Allah's sovereignty and the obligation to follow Him, stating:

(٢٧) وَٱتۡلُ مَآ أُوحِيَ إِلَيۡكَ مِن كِتَابِ رَبِّكَۖ لَا مُبَدِّلَ لِكَلِمَٰتِهِۦ وَلَن تَجِدَ مِن دُونِهِۦ مُلۡتَحَدٗا

Read, O Messenger, and implement what Allah has revealed to you in the Quran. Nothing can change His words because they are completely true and just. You will not find any refuge to resort to or place of protection to take cover in beside Him.

Points of Benefit:

Taking mosques built upon graves, praying in them, and constructing over them is not permissible in our legislation, as it has been prohibited in the sacred Sunnah.
The story establishes the argument for Allah's ability to gather, resurrect, raise bodies from their graves, and take them to account.
These verses indicate that praiseworthy disputation and debate involve engaging in discussions with what is better.
Both the Sunnah and the legal etiquette necessitate surrendering future matters to the will of Allah, the Transcendent.
