(٢٨) وَٱصۡبِرۡ نَفۡسَكَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلۡغَدَوٰةِ وَٱلۡعَشِيِّ يُرِيدُونَ وَجۡهَهُۥۖ وَلَا تَعۡدُ عَيۡنَاكَ عَنۡهُمۡ تُرِيدُ زِينَةَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَلَا تُطِعۡ مَنۡ أَغۡفَلۡنَا قَلۡبَهُۥ عَن ذِكۡرِنَا وَٱتَّبَعَ هَوَىٰهُ وَكَانَ أَمۡرُهُۥ فُرُطٗا

Remain close to your poor companions who sincerely call on their Lord in worship and supplication at the beginning and the end of the day. Do not let your eyes overlook them, intending to sit with people of affluence and prestige. Do not follow the one whose heart I have made neglectful of Our remembrance by sealing it and who instructed you to remove the poor from your gathering, giving preference to following his desire over following His Lord. His actions and deeds are thus foolish, excessive, and in vain.

(٢٩) وَقُلِ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكُمۡۖ فَمَن شَآءَ فَلۡيُؤۡمِن وَمَن شَآءَ فَلۡيَكۡفُرۡۚ إِنَّآ أَعۡتَدۡنَا لِلظَّٰلِمِينَ نَارًا أَحَاطَ بِهِمۡ سُرَادِقُهَاۚ وَإِن يَسۡتَغِيثُواْ يُغَاثُواْ بِمَآءٖ كَٱلۡمُهۡلِ يَشۡوِي ٱلۡوُجُوهَۚ بِئۡسَ ٱلشَّرَابُ وَسَآءَتۡ مُرۡتَفَقًا

Say, O Messenger, to those who neglect Allah's remembrance due to the heedlessness of their hearts: What I have brought to you is the truth. It is from Allah and not from me. I cannot accede to your request to chase away the believers. Whoever amongst you wishes to have faith in this truth, then let him do so, for he will be pleased with the reward. Whoever amongst you wants to reject it, let him do so, for he will be grieved by the punishment that awaits him. We have prepared for those who wrong themselves by choosing disbelief, a huge fire whose walls will surround them, and as a result, they will not be able to escape from it. If they were to seek water as relief from the severe thirst they experience, they would be given water like murky oil that will be extremely hot and scald their faces due to its heat. What a terrible drink this drink is that will be provided for their relief! It will not quench their thirst but will make it worse, and it will not put out the flames burning their faces. What a miserable destination and place of residence is the Fire!

When Allah mentions the punishment that He has prepared for the transgressors, He mentions the generous reward that He has prepared for the believers, stating:

(٣٠) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ إِنَّا لَا نُضِيعُ أَجۡرَ مَنۡ أَحۡسَنَ عَمَلًا

Those who believe in Allah and do righteous actions have done well in what they have done and will receive a huge reward. We do not let the reward of those who do good go to waste. Instead, we give them their reward in full without any reduction.

(٣١) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ جَنَّٰتُ عَدۡنٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهِمُ ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ يُحَلَّوۡنَ فِيهَا مِنۡ أَسَاوِرَ مِن ذَهَبٖ وَيَلۡبَسُونَ ثِيَابًا خُضۡرٗا مِّن سُندُسٖ وَإِسۡتَبۡرَقٖ مُّتَّكِ‍ِٔينَ فِيهَا عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِۚ نِعۡمَ ٱلثَّوَابُ وَحَسُنَتۡ مُرۡتَفَقٗا

Those who are described as having faith and doing righteous actions will receive gardens to reside in, with the sweet rivers of paradise flowing beneath their homes. They will be adorned with bracelets of gold and wear green garments of fine and thick silk. They will recline on couches decorated with beautiful coverings. Theirs will be an excellent reward, and paradise will be an excellent home and place for them to reside in.

Upon mentioning the recompense of the transgressors and believers, He gives a parable for both, stating:

(٣٢) ۞وَٱضۡرِبۡ لَهُم مَّثَلٗا رَّجُلَيۡنِ جَعَلۡنَا لِأَحَدِهِمَا جَنَّتَيۡنِ مِنۡ أَعۡنَٰبٖ وَحَفَفۡنَٰهُمَا بِنَخۡلٖ وَجَعَلۡنَا بَيۡنَهُمَا زَرۡعٗا

O Messenger, give an example of two men: a disbeliever and a believer. We gave the disbeliever two gardens of grapes, surrounding them with date palms, and in the vacant parts of the land, we caused crops to grow.

(٣٣) كِلۡتَا ٱلۡجَنَّتَيۡنِ ءَاتَتۡ أُكُلَهَا وَلَمۡ تَظۡلِم مِّنۡهُ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۚ وَفَجَّرۡنَا خِلَٰلَهُمَا نَهَرٗا

Each garden produced its fruit of dates, grapes, and crops and did not fall short thereof in anything but bore it in full. We caused a river to flow between the two gardens so that they could be irrigated easily.

(٣٤) وَكَانَ لَهُۥ ثَمَرٞ فَقَالَ لِصَٰحِبِهِۦ وَهُوَ يُحَاوِرُهُۥٓ أَنَا۠ أَكۡثَرُ مِنكَ مَالٗا وَأَعَزُّ نَفَرٗا

The owner of the two gardens had other wealth and fruit, and he said to his friend, as he spoke to him in conceit to make an impact on him: I have more wealth than you, more support, and a stronger family.

Points of Benefit:

The virtue of companionship with the righteous and striving to be in their company, even if they are poor, carries countless benefits; there are invaluable advantages in associating with them.
Abundant remembrance with a present heart is a cause for blessings in lifespan and time.
The two principles of reward and the fundamental basis for salvation is: faith coupled with righteous deeds, as Allah has ordained reward for them in this world and the Hereafter.
