(٣٥) وَدَخَلَ جَنَّتَهُۥ وَهُوَ ظَالِمٞ لِّنَفۡسِهِۦ قَالَ مَآ أَظُنُّ أَن تَبِيدَ هَٰذِهِۦٓ أَبَدٗا

The disbeliever entered his garden along with the believer to show it to him whilst he was wronging himself by his disbelief and conceit. The disbeliever said: I do not think that this garden that you see will ever perish because I have taken all measures for its survival.

(٣٦) وَمَآ أَظُنُّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ قَآئِمَةٗ وَلَئِن رُّدِدتُّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّي لَأَجِدَنَّ خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهَا مُنقَلَبٗا

I also do not think that the resurrection will occur. Life will simply continue. If it does occur and I happen to be resurrected and returned to my Lord, then I will find that what I will go back to after resurrection will be better than this garden of mine. My being rich in this world means that I will be rich after the resurrection as well.

(٣٧) قَالَ لَهُۥ صَاحِبُهُۥ وَهُوَ يُحَاوِرُهُۥٓ أَكَفَرۡتَ بِٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكَ مِن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ ثُمَّ سَوَّىٰكَ رَجُلٗا

His believing friend said to him whilst having this conversation: Do you reject the One who created your father, Adam, from dust? Then He created you from sperm. Then He made you into a male human being and proportioned your limbs, making you complete? The One who has the power to do all of that is able to resurrect you.

(٣٨) لَّٰكِنَّا۠ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ رَبِّي وَلَآ أُشۡرِكُ بِرَبِّيٓ أَحَدٗا

But I do not profess this statement of yours. I simply say: He, Allah (may He be Exalted), is my Lord, who has showered us with His blessings, and I do not associate anyone with Him in worship.

(٣٩) وَلَوۡلَآ إِذۡ دَخَلۡتَ جَنَّتَكَ قُلۡتَ مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ إِن تَرَنِ أَنَا۠ أَقَلَّ مِنكَ مَالٗا وَوَلَدٗا

If only, when you entered your garden, you had said: Whatever Allah wills. No one has any power except through Allah. He is the One who does as He pleases, and He is the One of strength. If you regard me as someone poorer than you and one with fewer children,

(٤٠) فَعَسَىٰ رَبِّيٓ أَن يُؤۡتِيَنِ خَيۡرٗا مِّن جَنَّتِكَ وَيُرۡسِلَ عَلَيۡهَا حُسۡبَانٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَتُصۡبِحَ صَعِيدٗا زَلَقًا

I anticipate that Allah will give me something better than your garden and that He will send upon your garden a punishment from the sky, making it a barren piece of land on which feet will slip due to it being so smooth.

(٤١) أَوۡ يُصۡبِحَ مَآؤُهَا غَوۡرٗا فَلَن تَسۡتَطِيعَ لَهُۥ طَلَبٗا

Or its water may sink so deep into the ground that you will never be able to reach it again in any way. Once the water sinks deep, the garden will not survive.

(٤٢) وَأُحِيطَ بِثَمَرِهِۦ فَأَصۡبَحَ يُقَلِّبُ كَفَّيۡهِ عَلَىٰ مَآ أَنفَقَ فِيهَا وَهِيَ خَاوِيَةٌ عَلَىٰ عُرُوشِهَا وَيَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي لَمۡ أُشۡرِكۡ بِرَبِّيٓ أَحَدٗا

What the believer anticipated occurred. Destruction engulfed the fruit of the disbeliever's garden. The disbeliever began wringing his hands on account of extreme regret and remorse over the money that he had spent on its maintenance and upkeep. As the garden had fallen over the supports on which the grapevines were spread out, he said: I wish I had believed in my Lord alone, and I had not associated anyone with Him in worship.

(٤٣) وَلَمۡ تَكُن لَّهُۥ فِئَةٞ يَنصُرُونَهُۥ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا كَانَ مُنتَصِرًا

This disbeliever had no group to protect him from the punishment that fell on him, whereas he was the one who used to boast about his group. He himself, too, could not stop Allah from destroying his garden.

(٤٤) هُنَالِكَ ٱلۡوَلَٰيَةُ لِلَّهِ ٱلۡحَقِّۚ هُوَ خَيۡرٞ ثَوَابٗا وَخَيۡرٌ عُقۡبٗا

In that situation, victory is for Allah alone. He (may He be Exalted) is best in rewarding His allies who have faith, as He multiplies the reward for them, and He is best in outcome for them.

(٤٥) وَٱضۡرِبۡ لَهُم مَّثَلَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا كَمَآءٍ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَٱخۡتَلَطَ بِهِۦ نَبَاتُ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَأَصۡبَحَ هَشِيمٗا تَذۡرُوهُ ٱلرِّيَٰحُۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ مُّقۡتَدِرًا

O Messenger, give an example for those who are deceived by the world. In its temporality and fleeting existence, it is similar to rainwater that We sent down from the sky. On account of this water, the vegetation of the Earth grew and ripened. Then this vegetation is broken down into pieces, the wind carries it away to far-lying places, and the Earth once again becomes as it was previously. Allah has full power over all of that. Nothing is outside His ability. He gives life to whatever He wishes and causes whatever He wishes to perish.

Points of Benefit:

It is incumbent upon the believer not to be subdued by the arrogance of the wealthy disbeliever. It is his duty to advise and guide him towards faith in Allah, acknowledgment of His Oneness, and gratitude for His blessings and favors upon him.
It is fitting for anyone who is pleased with something from his wealth or children to ascribe the blessing to its giver by saying: Ma sha Allah, la quwwata illa billah (What Allah wills [will happen]; there is no power except with Allah).
When Allah wills good for His servant, He expedites the punishment for him in this world.
It is permissible to supplicate for the destruction of the wealth of one whose wealth is a cause of his tyranny, disbelief, and ultimate loss.
