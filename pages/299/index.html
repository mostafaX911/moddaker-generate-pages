(٤٦) ٱلۡمَالُ وَٱلۡبَنُونَ زِينَةُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَٱلۡبَٰقِيَٰتُ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتُ خَيۡرٌ عِندَ رَبِّكَ ثَوَابٗا وَخَيۡرٌ أَمَلٗا

Wealth and children are things of adornment in this worldly life. Wealth will only be of benefit in the Afterlife if it is spent on that which pleases Allah. Actions and statements that are pleasing to Allah are better in reward than all the beauty in the world. That is the best thing a person can have hope in because the beauty of the world will perish while the reward for actions and statements that are pleasing to Allah will last.

(٤٧) وَيَوۡمَ نُسَيِّرُ ٱلۡجِبَالَ وَتَرَى ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَارِزَةٗ وَحَشَرۡنَٰهُمۡ فَلَمۡ نُغَادِرۡ مِنۡهُمۡ أَحَدٗا

Remember the day when I will remove the mountains from their places, and you will see the Earth as an open plain as all the mountains, trees, and buildings on it will be gone. I will gather all created things together and resurrect them, leaving no one.

(٤٨) وَعُرِضُواْ عَلَىٰ رَبِّكَ صَفّٗا لَّقَدۡ جِئۡتُمُونَا كَمَا خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةِۢۚ بَلۡ زَعَمۡتُمۡ أَلَّن نَّجۡعَلَ لَكُم مَّوۡعِدٗا

People will be presented to your Lord in rows, and He will hold them to account. It will be said to them: Now you have come to Us individually, barefoot and naked, as We created you the first time, although you claimed that you would never be resurrected and that We will not set for you a time and place in which We will recompense you for your actions.

(٤٩) وَوُضِعَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ فَتَرَى ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ مُشۡفِقِينَ مِمَّا فِيهِ وَيَقُولُونَ يَٰوَيۡلَتَنَا مَالِ هَٰذَا ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَا يُغَادِرُ صَغِيرَةٗ وَلَا كَبِيرَةً إِلَّآ أَحۡصَىٰهَاۚ وَوَجَدُواْ مَا عَمِلُواْ حَاضِرٗاۗ وَلَا يَظۡلِمُ رَبُّكَ أَحَدٗا

The book of deeds will be placed. Some people will take the book in their right hand, and some will take it in their left hand. You, O man, will see the disbelievers fearful of its contents because they are aware of the disbelief and sins that they sent forward. They will say: May we be destroyed! What is with this book that leaves no action of ours, small or big, but it has recorded and enumerated it? They will find all the sins they had done in their worldly life written and recorded. Your Lord, O Messenger, does not wrong anyone. He does not punish anyone for no reason, and He does not reduce the reward of those who follow Him.

(٥٠) وَإِذۡ قُلۡنَا لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ ٱسۡجُدُواْ لِأٓدَمَ فَسَجَدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَفَسَقَ عَنۡ أَمۡرِ رَبِّهِۦٓۗ أَفَتَتَّخِذُونَهُۥ وَذُرِّيَّتَهُۥٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَ مِن دُونِي وَهُمۡ لَكُمۡ عَدُوُّۢۚ بِئۡسَ لِلظَّٰلِمِينَ بَدَلٗا

Remember, O Messenger, when We told the angels to prostrate to Adam as a salutation. So all of them prostrated to him in fulfillment of the command of their Lord except Iblis. He was one of the Jinn and was not an angel. He refused and was too proud to prostrate. Thus he went against following His Lord. Will you, O people, then take him and his children as friends with whom you ally yourselves instead of Me while they are your enemies? How do you take your enemies to be your friends? How bad and ugly is the act of the wrongdoers who make the Devil their friend instead of befriending Allah?!

(٥١) ۞مَّآ أَشۡهَدتُّهُمۡ خَلۡقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا خَلۡقَ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَمَا كُنتُ مُتَّخِذَ ٱلۡمُضِلِّينَ عَضُدٗا

Those you have taken as friends instead of Me are all slaves like you. I did not make them witness the creation of the heavens and the Earth when I created them. In fact, they did not even exist then. I did not make some of them witness the creation of others. I am alone in creation and planning. I would not take those human and jinn devils who lead others astray as My assistants, as I am in no need of any assistants.

(٥٢) وَيَوۡمَ يَقُولُ نَادُواْ شُرَكَآءِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ زَعَمۡتُمۡ فَدَعَوۡهُمۡ فَلَمۡ يَسۡتَجِيبُواْ لَهُمۡ وَجَعَلۡنَا بَيۡنَهُم مَّوۡبِقٗا

Mention to them, O Messenger, the Day of Resurrection, when Allah will say to those who had associated partners with Him in the worldly life: Call My partners whom you claimed were partners to Me so that they can help you. They will call them, but they will not answer their call, and they will not come to their assistance. We will place between the worshippers and those whom they worshipped a pit of destruction, which will be for both groups, and this is the Fire.

(٥٣) وَرَءَا ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ ٱلنَّارَ فَظَنُّوٓاْ أَنَّهُم مُّوَاقِعُوهَا وَلَمۡ يَجِدُواْ عَنۡهَا مَصۡرِفٗا

The polytheists will see the Fire, and they will be completely certain that they are going to fall into it. They will not find any place to escape from it.

Points of Benefit:

It is incumbent upon the servant to increase in righteous deeds that last - which is encompassing of every virtuous action - whether in speech or conduct, that remains for the Hereafter.
The servant should contemplate the horrors of the Day of Resurrection, diligently working for that Day to escape its tribulations and attain Allah's Gardens and His pleasure.
Allah, the Transcendent, honored our father Adam, upon him be peace, and the entire human race by commanding the angels to prostrate to him at the inception of creation—a prostration of greeting and reverence.
These verses emphasize making Satan an adversary, urging the believers to take him as an enemy.
