When Allah describes the characteristics of the pious believers who are inwardly and outwardly sound, He mentions the characteristics of a group of disbelievers who are inwardly and outwardly corrupted, stating:

(٦) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ سَوَآءٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَأَنذَرۡتَهُمۡ أَمۡ لَمۡ تُنذِرۡهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Those upon whom the decree of Allah to disbelieve manifested will continue in their misguidance and stubbornness, so whether you warn them or not, it's the same.

(٧) خَتَمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ وَعَلَىٰ سَمۡعِهِمۡۖ وَعَلَىٰٓ أَبۡصَٰرِهِمۡ غِشَٰوَةٞۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٞ

This is because Allah has sealed their hearts, locking their falsehood inside them. He has sealed their hearing, so they do not listen to or accept the truth. He has covered their eyes so they do not see the truth despite its clarity. For them, a great torment awaits in the Hereafter.

After Allah describes the characteristics of the disbelievers who are inwardly and outwardly corrupt, He describes the characteristics of the hypocrites who are inwardly corrupt and appear outwardly sound to people, stating:

(٨) وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَقُولُ ءَامَنَّا بِٱللَّهِ وَبِٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَمَا هُم بِمُؤۡمِنِينَ

There is a group of people who claim faith, saying it with their tongues out of fear for their lives and wealth, but inwardly, they remain disbelievers.

(٩) يُخَٰدِعُونَ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَمَا يَخۡدَعُونَ إِلَّآ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَمَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

They ignorantly believe they can deceive Allah and the believers by outwardly showing faith while hiding their disbelief inside. However, in reality, they are unwittingly deceiving themselves; Allah knows all that is secret and hidden, and He has informed the believers about their attributes and characteristics.

(١٠) فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٞ فَزَادَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ مَرَضٗاۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمُۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡذِبُونَ

This is because their hearts contain uncertainty, so Allah increases their doubtfulness by adding further hesitation to their uncertainty; hence, the punishment befits the crime. They will suffer painful torment in the lowest depths of the Fire for their lies against Allah and the people and for rejecting the message brought by Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم.

(١١) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمۡ لَا تُفۡسِدُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّمَا نَحۡنُ مُصۡلِحُونَ

When they are told not to prevent corruption on earth through disbelief, disobedience, and the like, they deny doing so, claiming to be people of righteousness and reform.

(١٢) أَلَآ إِنَّهُمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدُونَ وَلَٰكِن لَّا يَشۡعُرُونَ

But in reality, they are people of corruption, but they don't realize it, nor feel that their actions are the essence of corruption.

(١٣) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمۡ ءَامِنُواْ كَمَآ ءَامَنَ ٱلنَّاسُ قَالُوٓاْ أَنُؤۡمِنُ كَمَآ ءَامَنَ ٱلسُّفَهَآءُۗ أَلَآ إِنَّهُمۡ هُمُ ٱلسُّفَهَآءُ وَلَٰكِن لَّا يَعۡلَمُونَ

When they are commanded to have faith as the companions of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم did, they reply with deprecation and mockery, proclaiming: Should we have faith like the simple-minded had faith? On the contrary, it is they who are the fools, but they are ignorant of their foolishness.

(١٤) وَإِذَا لَقُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا وَإِذَا خَلَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ شَيَٰطِينِهِمۡ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا مَعَكُمۡ إِنَّمَا نَحۡنُ مُسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

And when the hypocrites meet the believers, they say, 'We affirm what you believe,' out of fear of the believers. But when they are alone with their leaders, they assert with steadfastness, 'We are actually with you and upon your path, but we only agree with the believers as a form of mockery and sarcasm.'

(١٥) ٱللَّهُ يَسۡتَهۡزِئُ بِهِمۡ وَيَمُدُّهُمۡ فِي طُغۡيَٰنِهِمۡ يَعۡمَهُونَ

Allah mocks them for their mocking of the believers, and He will repay them in equal measure for their behavior. They are treated as believers in this world but will be repaid for their disbelief and hypocrisy in the next life. He grants them respite so they become increasingly lost and tyrannical, leaving them further confounded and perturbed.

(١٦) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱشۡتَرَوُاْ ٱلضَّلَٰلَةَ بِٱلۡهُدَىٰ فَمَا رَبِحَت تِّجَٰرَتُهُمۡ وَمَا كَانُواْ مُهۡتَدِينَ

Those hypocrites who are described with these qualities are the ones who exchanged faith for disbelief. But their trade did not yield a profit; they lost their faith in Allah and did not attain guidance to the truth.

Points of Benefit:

Whoever's heart Allah has sealed because of their stubbornness and denial, the signs - no matter how great - will not benefit them.
Allah's respite for the unjust deniers is not due to negligence or inability but rather to increase their sin so that their punishment increases in severity.
