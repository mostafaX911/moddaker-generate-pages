(١٩١) وَٱقۡتُلُوهُمۡ حَيۡثُ ثَقِفۡتُمُوهُمۡ وَأَخۡرِجُوهُم مِّنۡ حَيۡثُ أَخۡرَجُوكُمۡۚ وَٱلۡفِتۡنَةُ أَشَدُّ مِنَ ٱلۡقَتۡلِۚ وَلَا تُقَٰتِلُوهُمۡ عِندَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِ حَتَّىٰ يُقَٰتِلُوكُمۡ فِيهِۖ فَإِن قَٰتَلُوكُمۡ فَٱقۡتُلُوهُمۡۗ كَذَٰلِكَ جَزَآءُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

And kill them where you come across them and expel them from the place they expelled you from - which is Mecca. The calamity that results from preventing those who believe from worship and returning them to disbelief is worse than killing. And do not initiate fights within the Sacred Mosque out of veneration for it unless they start fighting you there. If they initiate fighting in the Sacred Mosque, then kill them. A disbeliever's death is the recompense if they attack within the Sacred Mosque.

(١٩٢) فَإِنِ ٱنتَهَوۡاْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

If they cease fighting you and stop disbelieving, then stop fighting them. Allah forgives whoever repents to Him and does not hold them to account for their past sins. He is Compassionate with them and does not rush to punish them.

(١٩٣) وَقَٰتِلُوهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ لَا تَكُونَ فِتۡنَةٞ وَيَكُونَ ٱلدِّينُ لِلَّهِۖ فَإِنِ ٱنتَهَوۡاْ فَلَا عُدۡوَٰنَ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Fight the disbelievers until they cease preventing people from Allah's path and there is no polytheism or disbelief within them, and until the apparent way of life is the religion of Allah. But if they cease disbelieving and stop deterring others from the path of Allah, then stop fighting them. There is no enmity except against transgressors who disbelieve and deter others from the path of Allah.

(١٩٤) ٱلشَّهۡرُ ٱلۡحَرَامُ بِٱلشَّهۡرِ ٱلۡحَرَامِ وَٱلۡحُرُمَٰتُ قِصَاصٞۚ فَمَنِ ٱعۡتَدَىٰ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فَٱعۡتَدُواْ عَلَيۡهِ بِمِثۡلِ مَا ٱعۡتَدَىٰ عَلَيۡكُمۡۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

The sacred month in which Allah enabled you to enter the Sacred Mosque and perform Umrah (Year 7 AH) is to compensate for the sacred month in which the polytheists prevented you from entering the Sacred Mosque (in Year 6 AH). The Law of Legal Retribution applies when the limits are overstepped concerning sacred things, such as the sacred land, the sacred month, and being in a state of ritual consecration (Ihram) for pilgrimage to the Sacred Mosque. When someone wrongs you in any of these, do unto them, as they did unto you, but do not go beyond what they have done: Allah does not love those who overstep His limits. And fear Allah in going beyond what He has permitted for you. Know that Allah is with those who are mindful of Him by granting them success and supporting them.

(١٩٥) وَأَنفِقُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَا تُلۡقُواْ بِأَيۡدِيكُمۡ إِلَى ٱلتَّهۡلُكَةِ وَأَحۡسِنُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Spend your wealth in obedience to Allah, in striving for His sake, and do not throw yourselves into destruction by leaving the struggle and spending for His sake or by throwing yourselves into something that will lead to your demise. Behave with excellence and kindness in your worship, transactions, and behavior. Allah loves those who strive toward excellence in all of their affairs, giving them a greater reward and leading them to true guidance.

(١٩٦) وَأَتِمُّواْ ٱلۡحَجَّ وَٱلۡعُمۡرَةَ لِلَّهِۚ فَإِنۡ أُحۡصِرۡتُمۡ فَمَا ٱسۡتَيۡسَرَ مِنَ ٱلۡهَدۡيِۖ وَلَا تَحۡلِقُواْ رُءُوسَكُمۡ حَتَّىٰ يَبۡلُغَ ٱلۡهَدۡيُ مَحِلَّهُۥۚ فَمَن كَانَ مِنكُم مَّرِيضًا أَوۡ بِهِۦٓ أَذٗى مِّن رَّأۡسِهِۦ فَفِدۡيَةٞ مِّن صِيَامٍ أَوۡ صَدَقَةٍ أَوۡ نُسُكٖۚ فَإِذَآ أَمِنتُمۡ فَمَن تَمَتَّعَ بِٱلۡعُمۡرَةِ إِلَى ٱلۡحَجِّ فَمَا ٱسۡتَيۡسَرَ مِنَ ٱلۡهَدۡيِۚ فَمَن لَّمۡ يَجِدۡ فَصِيَامُ ثَلَٰثَةِ أَيَّامٖ فِي ٱلۡحَجِۡ وَسَبۡعَةٍ إِذَا رَجَعۡتُمۡۗ تِلۡكَ عَشَرَةٞ كَامِلَةٞۗ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَن لَّمۡ يَكُنۡ أَهۡلُهُۥ حَاضِرِي ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ

Perform Hajj and Umrah completely, seeking only Allah's pleasure. If you are prevented from completing them by illness or an enemy, then you should sacrifice what is easily available of sacrificial animals, such as camels, cows, or sheep, before you take off your Ihram, and do not shave your heads or cut your hair until the sacrificial animals have reached the place where slaughtering them is permissible. If you are prevented from entering the Sacred Sanctuary, then perform the slaughter (of the sacrificial animal) wherever you have stopped; yet if you can enter the Sacred Sanctuary, perform it on the Day of Sacrifice (10th of Dhul-Hijjah) or within the following days of tashriq (11th, 12th and 13th of Dhul-Hijjah). Whoever among you is ill or has a problem with his hair or head, such as lice or the like, and shaves his head for that reason, then that is not a problem. He should, however, fast for three days, or feed six poor people from the area of the Sanctuary, or sacrifice a sheep to be distributed among the poor within the Sanctuary. If you are living in times of peace, then whoever takes the opportunity to perform Umrah in the months of Hajj, enjoying in between those things that are not allowed when performing the pilgrimages, should sacrifice whatever is easily available: a sheep or a seventh of a camel or cow. If they cannot make a sacrifice, they must fast for three days during the days of Hajj to make up for it and seven more days when they return home, making ten days altogether. This practice of having a break between Umrah and Hajj and sacrificing an animal or fasting for those unable to make a sacrifice is only for people who do not live within or close to the Sanctuary of the Sacred Mosque. As for those who live within the Sanctuary or close to it, they do not do this because they do not need to due to their constant presence and enjoyment of being able to make Umrah or Tawaf freely any other time of the year. Be mindful of Allah by following what He makes sacred law, respecting His limits, and understanding that Allah is firm in punishing those who go against what He instructs.

The purpose and objective of Jihad is to make judgment for Allah and to remove any impediments to people hearing the Truth and accepting it.
Abandoning and abstaining from Jihad is one of the reasons for the Islamic nation to be destroyed because it leads to it being weak and the enemy coveting it.
The obligation of whoever has begun Hajj or Umrah to complete it, and the permissibility for those prevented from entering the Haram (sanctuary) to cancel it by slaughtering a sacrificial animal.
