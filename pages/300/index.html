(٥٤) وَلَقَدۡ صَرَّفۡنَا فِي هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ لِلنَّاسِ مِن كُلِّ مَثَلٖۚ وَكَانَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ أَكۡثَرَ شَيۡءٖ جَدَلٗا

We have explained in different ways, in this Quran that was revealed to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, many types of examples so that they will take a lesson and pay heed. But the most apparent thing in man, especially the disbeliever, is to argue wrongfully.

(٥٥) وَمَا مَنَعَ ٱلنَّاسَ أَن يُؤۡمِنُوٓاْ إِذۡ جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡهُدَىٰ وَيَسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ رَبَّهُمۡ إِلَّآ أَن تَأۡتِيَهُمۡ سُنَّةُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ أَوۡ يَأۡتِيَهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ قُبُلٗا

Nothing prevented the obstinate disbelievers from having faith in what Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought from his Lord. Lack of explanation did not prevent them from seeking Allah's forgiveness for their sins, for many examples were given to them in the Quran, and clear proof came to them. The only thing that prevented them was their stubborn request for the punishment of the previous communities to be imposed on them and for them to see the punishment that they were promised.

(٥٦) وَمَا نُرۡسِلُ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ إِلَّا مُبَشِّرِينَ وَمُنذِرِينَۚ وَيُجَٰدِلُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ لِيُدۡحِضُواْ بِهِ ٱلۡحَقَّۖ وَٱتَّخَذُوٓاْ ءَايَٰتِي وَمَآ أُنذِرُواْ هُزُوٗا

We do not send any of Our Messengers except to give good news to the people of faith and obedience and to warn the people of disbelief and disobedience. They do not have the authority to make hearts accept the guidance. Those who disbelieved in Allah dispute with the Messengers despite their having clear proof in an attempt to use their falsehood to remove the truth that was sent down to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. They took the Quran and what they were warned of as a joke and mocked it.

(٥٧) وَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّن ذُكِّرَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِۦ فَأَعۡرَضَ عَنۡهَا وَنَسِيَ مَا قَدَّمَتۡ يَدَاهُۚ إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَا عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ أَكِنَّةً أَن يَفۡقَهُوهُ وَفِيٓ ءَاذَانِهِمۡ وَقۡرٗاۖ وَإِن تَدۡعُهُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡهُدَىٰ فَلَن يَهۡتَدُوٓاْ إِذًا أَبَدٗا

There is no one more oppressive than the person who was reminded of the verses of his Lord, did not pay any attention to the threat of punishment contained therein, and turned away from taking a lesson, forgetting the disbelief and sins he had done in his worldly life without repenting. We have placed covers on the hearts of people with this description that prevent them from understanding the Quran and deafness in their ears so that they cannot listen to it acceptingly. If you call them to faith, they will never respond to what you call them towards as long as there are covers on their hearts and deafness in their ears.

(٥٨) وَرَبُّكَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ذُو ٱلرَّحۡمَةِۖ لَوۡ يُؤَاخِذُهُم بِمَا كَسَبُواْ لَعَجَّلَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابَۚ بَل لَّهُم مَّوۡعِدٞ لَّن يَجِدُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦ مَوۡئِلٗا

To avoid keeping the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم in anticipation of his deniers being treated with a punishment, Allah told him: By your Lord, O Messenger, the Forgiver of the sins of His repentant slaves, the One whose Mercy encompasses everything, it is from His Mercy that He gives the sinners respite so that perhaps they may repent to Him. If he were to inflict punishment upon those who turned away, he would have punished them immediately in this world. But because He is The Most Forbearing and The Most Merciful, he has delayed their torment so that they repent. Rather, they are designated a specific place and time in which they will be disciplined for their disbelief and their shunning [of Allah's religion] if they do not repent. They will not find a refuge where they can seek shelter from what will befall them.

(٥٩) وَتِلۡكَ ٱلۡقُرَىٰٓ أَهۡلَكۡنَٰهُمۡ لَمَّا ظَلَمُواْ وَجَعَلۡنَا لِمَهۡلِكِهِم مَّوۡعِدٗا

We destroyed those disbelieving towns close to you, such as the towns of the people of Hūd, Saleh, and Shuayb, when they wronged themselves by disbelief and sins, and We set an appointed time for their destruction.

(٦٠) وَإِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِفَتَىٰهُ لَآ أَبۡرَحُ حَتَّىٰٓ أَبۡلُغَ مَجۡمَعَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ أَوۡ أَمۡضِيَ حُقُبٗا

Remember, O Messenger, when Moses (peace be upon him) said to his slave, Joshua, the son of Nun: I will continue traveling until I reach the place where the two seas meet, or I will travel until such time that I meet the righteous slave and I learn from him.

(٦١) فَلَمَّا بَلَغَا مَجۡمَعَ بَيۡنِهِمَا نَسِيَا حُوتَهُمَا فَٱتَّخَذَ سَبِيلَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ سَرَبٗا

So they both traveled and forgot the fish they had carried as provision when they reached the place where the two seas meet. Allah gave life to that fish, and it made a pathway in the sea like a tunnel, such that the water did not come together.

Points of Benefit:

The greatness, majesty, and universality of the Quran lie in it being a comprehensive guide leading to beneficial knowledge, eternal happiness, and every path that safeguards against evil.
From the wisdom and mercy of Allah is allowing the futile arguments of those who distort the truth with falsehood to be one of the greatest means of clarifying the truth and exposing falsehood and its corruption.
In the verses, there is a warning for those who abandon the truth after knowing it that a barrier may be placed between them and the truth and that they will not be able to attain it after that, which is among the most intimidating and deterring consequences of doing so.
The virtue of seeking knowledge and embarking on the journey to acquire it, seizing the opportunity to meet the virtuous and scholars even if they are distant.
The term "whale" is used in the Quran to refer to both small and large fish, and the term "fish" is not explicitly mentioned. Instead, "whale," "Nun," and "tender meat" are mentioned.
