(٦٢) فَلَمَّا جَاوَزَا قَالَ لِفَتَىٰهُ ءَاتِنَا غَدَآءَنَا لَقَدۡ لَقِينَا مِن سَفَرِنَا هَٰذَا نَصَبٗا

When they passed that place, Moses (peace be upon him) said to his slave: Bring us our lunch; we have experienced extreme tiredness in this journey.

(٦٣) قَالَ أَرَءَيۡتَ إِذۡ أَوَيۡنَآ إِلَى ٱلصَّخۡرَةِ فَإِنِّي نَسِيتُ ٱلۡحُوتَ وَمَآ أَنسَىٰنِيهُ إِلَّا ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ أَنۡ أَذۡكُرَهُۥۚ وَٱتَّخَذَ سَبِيلَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ عَجَبٗا

The boy said: Do you remember what happened when we rested by the rock? I forgot to tell you about the fish. It was only the Devil who made me forget to mention it to you. The fish came to life, and amazingly, it found its way into the sea.

(٦٤) قَالَ ذَٰلِكَ مَا كُنَّا نَبۡغِۚ فَٱرۡتَدَّا عَلَىٰٓ ءَاثَارِهِمَا قَصَصٗا

Moses (peace be upon him) said to his slave: That is what we were looking for, for that is the sign of the place of the righteous slave. So they turned back, retracing their steps, so that they would not lose the way until they reached the rock, and from there, they reached the entry point of the fish.

(٦٥) فَوَجَدَا عَبۡدٗا مِّنۡ عِبَادِنَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنۡ عِندِنَا وَعَلَّمۡنَٰهُ مِن لَّدُنَّا عِلۡمٗا

When they reached the place at which the fish was lost, they found there one of Our righteous slaves (who was Khidr, peace be upon him), to whom We had given mercy and to whom We had taught knowledge that people were not aware of, which is what is contained in this story.

(٦٦) قَالَ لَهُۥ مُوسَىٰ هَلۡ أَتَّبِعُكَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن تُعَلِّمَنِ مِمَّا عُلِّمۡتَ رُشۡدٗا

Moses humbly and politely said to him: May I follow you so that you can teach me some of the knowledge that Allah has taught you, which constitutes guidance to the truth?

(٦٧) قَالَ إِنَّكَ لَن تَسۡتَطِيعَ مَعِيَ صَبۡرٗا

Khidr said: Indeed, you will not be able to be patient with what you see of my knowledge because it will not correspond with your knowledge.

(٦٨) وَكَيۡفَ تَصۡبِرُ عَلَىٰ مَا لَمۡ تُحِطۡ بِهِۦ خُبۡرٗا

How will you bear patience over the actions that you see for which you do not know the proper reason because you judge them according to the limit of your knowledge?!

(٦٩) قَالَ سَتَجِدُنِيٓ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ صَابِرٗا وَلَآ أَعۡصِي لَكَ أَمۡرٗا

Moses said: You will find me, Allah willing, patient over those actions of yours that I see and committed to following you. I will not go against any instruction that you give me.

(٧٠) قَالَ فَإِنِ ٱتَّبَعۡتَنِي فَلَا تَسۡ‍َٔلۡنِي عَن شَيۡءٍ حَتَّىٰٓ أُحۡدِثَ لَكَ مِنۡهُ ذِكۡرٗا

Khidr said to Moses: If you follow me, then do not ask me about anything that you see me doing until I myself tell you the reason behind it.

(٧١) فَٱنطَلَقَا حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا رَكِبَا فِي ٱلسَّفِينَةِ خَرَقَهَاۖ قَالَ أَخَرَقۡتَهَا لِتُغۡرِقَ أَهۡلَهَا لَقَدۡ جِئۡتَ شَيۡ‍ًٔا إِمۡرٗا

When they agreed to that, they set out to the seashore, where they came across a ship, which they boarded for free as an honor to Khidr. Khidr then made a hole in the ship by removing one of its planks. Moses said to him: Did you make a hole in the ship whose owners let us board for free in the hope that they may drown? You have done something confusing and puzzling.

(٧٢) قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُلۡ إِنَّكَ لَن تَسۡتَطِيعَ مَعِيَ صَبۡرٗا

Khidr said to Moses: Did I not say: Indeed, you will not be able to be patient with me in what you will see from me?

(٧٣) قَالَ لَا تُؤَاخِذۡنِي بِمَا نَسِيتُ وَلَا تُرۡهِقۡنِي مِنۡ أَمۡرِي عُسۡرٗا

Moses (peace be upon him) said to Khidr: Do not take me to task on account of my breaking the pledge forgetfully, and do not make it difficult and hard for me to accompany you.

(٧٤) فَٱنطَلَقَا حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا لَقِيَا غُلَٰمٗا فَقَتَلَهُۥ قَالَ أَقَتَلۡتَ نَفۡسٗا زَكِيَّةَۢ بِغَيۡرِ نَفۡسٖ لَّقَدۡ جِئۡتَ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا نُّكۡرٗا

After they disembarked from the ship, they set out walking along the seashore when they saw a young boy who was still not an adult playing with the other boys. Khidr killed him, and Moses said to him: Did you kill a pure soul that is still a minor for no sin? You have really done something evil!!

Points of Benefit:

It is commendable for a person's servant to be intelligent, discerning, and wise, facilitating the fulfillment of his desired tasks.
Assistance descends upon a servant in accordance with the fulfillment of the duties assigned to him. Those who align with the command of Allah receive support beyond what others receive.
Displaying proper manners with the teacher, and addressing the student with the gentlest discourse.
Forgetfulness does not necessitate rebuke, nor does it fall under the realm of legal responsibility.
A knowledgeable and virtuous person learns from the knowledge in which others have excelled, even if they are at a much lower level of knowledge than them.
Attributing knowledge and other virtues to Allah, acknowledging it, and expressing gratitude to Allah for them.
