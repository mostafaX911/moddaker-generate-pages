(٨٤) إِنَّا مَكَّنَّا لَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ سَبَبٗا

Indeed, We gave him a foothold on earth and granted him a path in everything relevant to his goals, allowing him to reach his objectives.

(٨٥) فَأَتۡبَعَ سَبَبًا

So he used the means and paths We granted him to reach his objectives, so he headed West.

(٨٦) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا بَلَغَ مَغۡرِبَ ٱلشَّمۡسِ وَجَدَهَا تَغۡرُبُ فِي عَيۡنٍ حَمِئَةٖ وَوَجَدَ عِندَهَا قَوۡمٗاۖ قُلۡنَا يَٰذَا ٱلۡقَرۡنَيۡنِ إِمَّآ أَن تُعَذِّبَ وَإِمَّآ أَن تَتَّخِذَ فِيهِمۡ حُسۡنٗا

And he walked the earth until he reached the end of the land towards the sunset, in the eye of the beholder. He saw it as though it was setting in a hot spring of black mud. And he found a community of disbelievers near the setting place of the sun. We said to him, by way of giving discretion: O Dhul-Qarnayn, either you torment these people with killing or other means, or you be good to them.

(٨٧) قَالَ أَمَّا مَن ظَلَمَ فَسَوۡفَ نُعَذِّبُهُۥ ثُمَّ يُرَدُّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ فَيُعَذِّبُهُۥ عَذَابٗا نُّكۡرٗا

Dhul-Qarnayn said: As for the one who associates partners with Allah and persists in that after our call to him to worship Allah, then we shall punish him by killing [him] in this life. Then he will return to his Lord on the Day of Resurrection, and He shall then torment him with a terrible torment.

(٨٨) وَأَمَّا مَنۡ ءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَلَهُۥ جَزَآءً ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۖ وَسَنَقُولُ لَهُۥ مِنۡ أَمۡرِنَا يُسۡرٗا

And as for who among them believes in Allah and does good deeds, for him is Paradise, as a reward from his Lord for his faith and good deeds. And We shall say to him from Our command that which contains gentleness and lenience.

(٨٩) ثُمَّ أَتۡبَعَ سَبَبًا

Then, he took a different path than his first one, heading towards the sunrise.

(٩٠) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا بَلَغَ مَطۡلِعَ ٱلشَّمۡسِ وَجَدَهَا تَطۡلُعُ عَلَىٰ قَوۡمٖ لَّمۡ نَجۡعَل لَّهُم مِّن دُونِهَا سِتۡرٗا

And he walked until when he reached the place where the sun rises, in the eye of the beholder, he found the sun rising on a people whom We had not given protection [to stand] before the sun by means of houses or the shade of trees.

(٩١) كَذَٰلِكَۖ وَقَدۡ أَحَطۡنَا بِمَا لَدَيۡهِ خُبۡرٗا

Such was the matter of Dhul-Qarnayn. Our knowledge has encompassed what he possessed of power and authority in detail.

(٩٢) ثُمَّ أَتۡبَعَ سَبَبًا

Then, he followed a path different from the first two, standing between the East and West.

(٩٣) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا بَلَغَ بَيۡنَ ٱلسَّدَّيۡنِ وَجَدَ مِن دُونِهِمَا قَوۡمٗا لَّا يَكَادُونَ يَفۡقَهُونَ قَوۡلٗا

He walked until he reached a pass between two mountains, where he came across a group of people who could barely understand the speech of outsiders.

(٩٤) قَالُواْ يَٰذَا ٱلۡقَرۡنَيۡنِ إِنَّ يَأۡجُوجَ وَمَأۡجُوجَ مُفۡسِدُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَهَلۡ نَجۡعَلُ لَكَ خَرۡجًا عَلَىٰٓ أَن تَجۡعَلَ بَيۡنَنَا وَبَيۡنَهُمۡ سَدّٗا

They said: O Dhul-Qarnayn, indeed Gog and Magog (referring to two immense nations from the Children of Adam) cause corruption on the earth by the killing and other matters they engage in, so should we give you wealth so that you can make a barrier between us and them?

(٩٥) قَالَ مَا مَكَّنِّي فِيهِ رَبِّي خَيۡرٞ فَأَعِينُونِي بِقُوَّةٍ أَجۡعَلۡ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَبَيۡنَهُمۡ رَدۡمًا

Dhul-Qarnayn said: The dominion and authority my Lord has given me is better than any wealth you would me, so assist me with men and tools so that I can construct a barrier between you and them.

(٩٦) ءَاتُونِي زُبَرَ ٱلۡحَدِيدِۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا سَاوَىٰ بَيۡنَ ٱلصَّدَفَيۡنِ قَالَ ٱنفُخُواْۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَعَلَهُۥ نَارٗا قَالَ ءَاتُونِيٓ أُفۡرِغۡ عَلَيۡهِ قِطۡرٗا

Bring pieces of iron. So they brought them to him. Then he began building with them between the two mountains until he made it level between the two. He said to the workers: Ignite fire upon these pieces [of iron]. [They did so] until when the iron pieces turned red, he said: Bring copper for me to pour on it.

(٩٧) فَمَا ٱسۡطَٰعُوٓاْ أَن يَظۡهَرُوهُ وَمَا ٱسۡتَطَٰعُواْ لَهُۥ نَقۡبٗا

So Gog and Magog could not surmount it due to its height, nor could they pierce it from beneath due to its solidity.

Points of Benefit:

Dhul-Qarnayn was one of the believing kings who ruled the world and had power over its people. Allah gave him a vast kingdom and granted him wisdom, authority, and beneficial knowledge.
The king or ruler is obliged to protect the creation by preserving their homes and closing their financial gaps.
People of rectification and sincerity are keen to complete deeds seeking the Face of Allah.
