(٩٨) قَالَ هَٰذَا رَحۡمَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّيۖ فَإِذَا جَآءَ وَعۡدُ رَبِّي جَعَلَهُۥ دَكَّآءَۖ وَكَانَ وَعۡدُ رَبِّي حَقّٗا

Dhul-Qarnayn said: This barricade is a mercy from my Lord. It comes between Gog and Magog and corruption on the earth, preventing them from it. So when the time Allah has appointed for their release comes before the Hour, He will level it to the ground. And the promise of Allah to raze it to the ground and release Gog and Magog is established and will not be broken.

(٩٩) ۞وَتَرَكۡنَا بَعۡضَهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَمُوجُ فِي بَعۡضٖۖ وَنُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَجَمَعۡنَٰهُمۡ جَمۡعٗا

We left some of the creation of the end of times to agitate and mix with one another. The Trumpet will be blown. Then, We will gather all of creation for reckoning and recompense.

(١٠٠) وَعَرَضۡنَا جَهَنَّمَ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَرۡضًا

And We shall reveal Hell to the disbelievers without obscurity so they visually witness it.

(١٠١) ٱلَّذِينَ كَانَتۡ أَعۡيُنُهُمۡ فِي غِطَآءٍ عَن ذِكۡرِي وَكَانُواْ لَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ سَمۡعًا

We shall reveal it to the disbelievers who were blind to the remembrance of Allah in the world due to the obstructive veil on their eyes, which barred them from that. They could not hear the verses of Allah in an accepting way.

(١٠٢) أَفَحَسِبَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَن يَتَّخِذُواْ عِبَادِي مِن دُونِيٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَۚ إِنَّآ أَعۡتَدۡنَا جَهَنَّمَ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ نُزُلٗا

So did those disbelieving in Allah think to make My slaves of angels, messengers, devils, and others to be worshipped besides me? Indeed, We have prepared Hell as a residing place for the disbelievers.

(١٠٣) قُلۡ هَلۡ نُنَبِّئُكُم بِٱلۡأَخۡسَرِينَ أَعۡمَٰلًا

Say, O Messenger: Should we inform you, O people, who will be the most significant loser regarding his action?

(١٠٤) ٱلَّذِينَ ضَلَّ سَعۡيُهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَهُمۡ يَحۡسَبُونَ أَنَّهُمۡ يُحۡسِنُونَ صُنۡعًا

Those who, on the Day of Resurrection, will see that the effort they used to make in this world has been lost, while they thought they were doing good in their efforts and would benefit from their actions. The reality will be in contrast to that.

(١٠٥) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَلِقَآئِهِۦ فَحَبِطَتۡ أَعۡمَٰلُهُمۡ فَلَا نُقِيمُ لَهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ وَزۡنٗا

Those are the ones who disbelieved in the signs of their Lord that indicate His oneness and disbelieved in meeting Him. Hence, their actions became void due to their denial of those matters, so they have no value with Allah on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٠٦) ذَٰلِكَ جَزَآؤُهُمۡ جَهَنَّمُ بِمَا كَفَرُواْ وَٱتَّخَذُوٓاْ ءَايَٰتِي وَرُسُلِي هُزُوًا

That recompense prepared for them is Hell due to their disbelief in Allah and their making mockery of My messengers and revealed verses.

After mentioning the recompense of the disbelievers, Allah mentions the recompense of the believers, stating:

(١٠٧) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ كَانَتۡ لَهُمۡ جَنَّٰتُ ٱلۡفِرۡدَوۡسِ نُزُلًا

Indeed, those who believe in Allah and do good actions will have the highest gardens as a place in honor of them.

(١٠٨) خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا لَا يَبۡغُونَ عَنۡهَا حِوَلٗا

Staying therein forever, not asking to move from it, as no reward comes close to it.

(١٠٩) قُل لَّوۡ كَانَ ٱلۡبَحۡرُ مِدَادٗا لِّكَلِمَٰتِ رَبِّي لَنَفِدَ ٱلۡبَحۡرُ قَبۡلَ أَن تَنفَدَ كَلِمَٰتُ رَبِّي وَلَوۡ جِئۡنَا بِمِثۡلِهِۦ مَدَدٗا

Say, O Messenger: Indeed, the words of my Lord are plentiful. So if the sea were ink for them to write with, the water in the sea would deplete before His words would end, may He be glorified. And if We were to bring more seas, they would also deplete.

(١١٠) قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ بَشَرٞ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ أَنَّمَآ إِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهٞ وَٰحِدٞۖ فَمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ لِقَآءَ رَبِّهِۦ فَلۡيَعۡمَلۡ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا وَلَا يُشۡرِكۡ بِعِبَادَةِ رَبِّهِۦٓ أَحَدَۢا

Say, O Messenger: I am only human like you, [except that] it is revealed to me that your rightful object of worship is one, who has no partner, and that is Allah. Therefore, whoever fears meeting his Lord should do actions that conform to His legislation, be sincere to his Lord in them, and not associate any partners with the worship of his Lord.

Points of Benefit:

Establishing the Resurrection and the Gathering by assembling the Jinn and humans on the plains of Resurrection with the second blowing of the horn.
That the people to lose the most on the Day of Resurrection are those whose efforts were in vain in the worldly life while they thought that they had done well by worshiping others besides Allah.
It is not possible to encompass the words, knowledge, wisdom, and secrets of Allah, the Exalted, even if the seas, oceans, etc., were ink to be written with.
