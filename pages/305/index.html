Mary (Surah Maryam)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Declaring the Christians' and polytheists' creed of ascribing a child to Allah as baseless and elucidating the extent of Allah's mercy toward His slaves.

Explanation:

(١) كٓهيعٓصٓ

Kāf Hā Yā 'Ayn Sād – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) ذِكۡرُ رَحۡمَتِ رَبِّكَ عَبۡدَهُۥ زَكَرِيَّآ

This is a mention of your Lord's mercy on His slave Zechariah (peace be upon him). We relate it to you to take lessons from.

(٣) إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥ نِدَآءً خَفِيّٗا

When he supplicated to his Lord, may He be glorified, secretly so that it would more likely be accepted.

(٤) قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي وَهَنَ ٱلۡعَظۡمُ مِنِّي وَٱشۡتَعَلَ ٱلرَّأۡسُ شَيۡبٗا وَلَمۡ أَكُنۢ بِدُعَآئِكَ رَبِّ شَقِيّٗا

He said: O my Lord, indeed, my bones have become weak, the white hairs on my head have become plentiful, and I have not been unsuccessful in supplicating to You. Rather, You responded to me every time I supplicated.

(٥) وَإِنِّي خِفۡتُ ٱلۡمَوَٰلِيَ مِن وَرَآءِي وَكَانَتِ ٱمۡرَأَتِي عَاقِرٗا فَهَبۡ لِي مِن لَّدُنكَ وَلِيّٗا

And I fear that my relatives will not be able to fulfill the rights of the religion after my death because they are preoccupied with this worldly life. And my wife is barren and unbearing, so grant me from Yourself a child [who would be a] supporter.

(٦) يَرِثُنِي وَيَرِثُ مِنۡ ءَالِ يَعۡقُوبَۖ وَٱجۡعَلۡهُ رَبِّ رَضِيّٗا

Inheriting prophethood from me and the family of Jacob (peace be upon him), and O Lord, make him satisfactory in his religion, character and knowledge.

(٧) يَٰزَكَرِيَّآ إِنَّا نُبَشِّرُكَ بِغُلَٰمٍ ٱسۡمُهُۥ يَحۡيَىٰ لَمۡ نَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ مِن قَبۡلُ سَمِيّٗا

So Allah responded to his supplication and called out to him: O Zechariah, indeed, We are informing you of what will please you, for We have accepted your supplication and granted you a boy named John. We have not given this name to anyone before him.

(٨) قَالَ رَبِّ أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لِي غُلَٰمٞ وَكَانَتِ ٱمۡرَأَتِي عَاقِرٗا وَقَدۡ بَلَغۡتُ مِنَ ٱلۡكِبَرِ عِتِيّٗا

Zechariah said, in amazement at the power of Allah: How will a child be born to me when my wife is barren and unbearing, and I have reached the limit of old age and the weakness of bones?

(٩) قَالَ كَذَٰلِكَ قَالَ رَبُّكَ هُوَ عَلَيَّ هَيِّنٞ وَقَدۡ خَلَقۡتُكَ مِن قَبۡلُ وَلَمۡ تَكُ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا

The angel said: The matter is as you said, that your wife does not bear and that you indeed have reached the limit of old age and the weakness of bones. However, your Lord said: You're Lord's creation of John from a barren mother and father at the limit of old age is easy. And I had created you, O Zechariah, before that and you were nothing to be mentioned because you were non-existent.

(١٠) قَالَ رَبِّ ٱجۡعَل لِّيٓ ءَايَةٗۖ قَالَ ءَايَتُكَ أَلَّا تُكَلِّمَ ٱلنَّاسَ ثَلَٰثَ لَيَالٖ سَوِيّٗا

Zechariah (peace be upon him) said: O my Lord, give me a sign I can feel reassured with, indicating that the glad tidings the angels gave me will be attained. He said: Your sign of attaining what you were given glad tidings of is that you will not be able to speak to people for three nights without any illness, but rather you will be healthy and sound.

(١١) فَخَرَجَ عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ مِنَ ٱلۡمِحۡرَابِ فَأَوۡحَىٰٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡ أَن سَبِّحُواْ بُكۡرَةٗ وَعَشِيّٗا

Then Zechariah went out to his people from his place of prayer and gestured to them without speaking to exalt Allah at the beginning and end of the day.

Points of Benefit:

Weakness and incapability are some of the most beloved ways to have one's request reach Allah because it shows the renunciation of any power and might while the heart is attached to the power and might of Allah.
When supplicating, it is recommended for the person to mention Allah's (the Exalted) blessings upon him and whatever is befitting with humility.
Emphasizing and giving precedence to the interests of the religion over that of anything else.
It is recommended to use names that have good meanings.
