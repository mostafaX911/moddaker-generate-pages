(١٢) يَٰيَحۡيَىٰ خُذِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِقُوَّةٖۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلۡحُكۡمَ صَبِيّٗا

So John was born to him. Then, when he reached an age where he could be addressed, We said to him: O John, take hold of the Torah with resolve and utmost diligence. And We granted him understanding, knowledge, resolution, and determination while still at the age of a child.

(١٣) وَحَنَانٗا مِّن لَّدُنَّا وَزَكَوٰةٗۖ وَكَانَ تَقِيّٗا

And We granted him mercy from Us and kept him pure from sins. And he was heedful, abiding by the commands of Allah and avoiding His prohibitions.

(١٤) وَبَرَّۢا بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ وَلَمۡ يَكُن جَبَّارًا عَصِيّٗا

And he was dutiful to his parents, gentle and benevolent to them. And he was not arrogantly against obedience to His Lord nor them, and was not disobedient to his Lord nor them.

(١٥) وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡهِ يَوۡمَ وُلِدَ وَيَوۡمَ يَمُوتُ وَيَوۡمَ يُبۡعَثُ حَيّٗا

And peace and safety from Allah be on him on the day he was born, the day he dies and leaves this life, and the day he will be revived to life on the Day of Resurrection. These three points are the most desolate a person goes through, so if he is safe in them, he shall not have fear in other situations.

(١٦) وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مَرۡيَمَ إِذِ ٱنتَبَذَتۡ مِنۡ أَهۡلِهَا مَكَانٗا شَرۡقِيّٗا

And mention, O Messenger, in the Quran revealed to you, the story of Mary (peace be upon her) when she retreated from her family and stayed separately in a place towards the east of them.

(١٧) فَٱتَّخَذَتۡ مِن دُونِهِمۡ حِجَابٗا فَأَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهَا رُوحَنَا فَتَمَثَّلَ لَهَا بَشَرٗا سَوِيّٗا

Then she placed a screen between herself and her people concealing her so they could not see her when she worshipped her Lord. Then We sent Gabriel (peace be upon him) to her, so he appeared to her in the form of a perfectly-formed human. So she thought he had ill intentions towards her.

(١٨) قَالَتۡ إِنِّيٓ أَعُوذُ بِٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ مِنكَ إِن كُنتَ تَقِيّٗا

Thus, when she saw him in the form of a perfectly-formed human heading towards her, she said: I seek the protection of the Merciful from you that any harm should come to me from you; if you are heedful, fearing Allah.

(١٩) قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ رَسُولُ رَبِّكِ لِأَهَبَ لَكِ غُلَٰمٗا زَكِيّٗا

Gabriel (peace be upon him) said: I am not a human. I am merely a messenger from your Lord. He sent me to you to grant you a wholesome and pure child.

(٢٠) قَالَتۡ أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لِي غُلَٰمٞ وَلَمۡ يَمۡسَسۡنِي بَشَرٞ وَلَمۡ أَكُ بَغِيّٗا

In amazement, Mary said, How can I have a child when neither a husband nor anyone else has come near me, nor am I fornicator for me to have a child?

(٢١) قَالَ كَذَٰلِكِ قَالَ رَبُّكِ هُوَ عَلَيَّ هَيِّنٞۖ وَلِنَجۡعَلَهُۥٓ ءَايَةٗ لِّلنَّاسِ وَرَحۡمَةٗ مِّنَّاۚ وَكَانَ أَمۡرٗا مَّقۡضِيّٗا

Gabriel told her: The matter is as you mentioned, that no husband or anyone else has touched you, nor are you a fornicator. However, your Lord said: Creating a child without a father is easy for Me. And it is so that the child bestowed on you will be a sign to the people of the power of Allah, and mercy from Us to you and whoever believes in him. The creation of this child of yours is a fixed decree from Allah, written in the Preserved Tablet.

(٢٢) ۞فَحَمَلَتۡهُ فَٱنتَبَذَتۡ بِهِۦ مَكَانٗا قَصِيّٗا

So she conceived him after the angel blew [the soul], and then she withdrew with him to a place far from people.

(٢٣) فَأَجَآءَهَا ٱلۡمَخَاضُ إِلَىٰ جِذۡعِ ٱلنَّخۡلَةِ قَالَتۡ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي مِتُّ قَبۡلَ هَٰذَا وَكُنتُ نَسۡيٗا مَّنسِيّٗا

Then her labor pains started and caused her to take refuge by the trunk of a date palm tree. Mary (peace be upon her) said: If only I had died before this day, and I was something unmentioned so that people would not think bad of me.

(٢٤) فَنَادَىٰهَا مِن تَحۡتِهَآ أَلَّا تَحۡزَنِي قَدۡ جَعَلَ رَبُّكِ تَحۡتَكِ سَرِيّٗا

Then Jesus called to her from beneath her feet: Do not grieve. Your Lord has made a stream of water beneath you from which you can drink.

(٢٥) وَهُزِّيٓ إِلَيۡكِ بِجِذۡعِ ٱلنَّخۡلَةِ تُسَٰقِطۡ عَلَيۡكِ رُطَبٗا جَنِيّٗا

And get hold of the date palm trunk and shake it; it will drop soft fresh dates on you, harvested at that moment.

Points of Benefit:

Being patient with upholding all the required religious obligations.
The high status and position that good treatment of parents has with Allah, as Allah mentioned it along with gratitude to Him.
Although Allah has complete capability when showing Mary His remarkable signs, He made her do the means to obtain the fruit of the palm tree.
