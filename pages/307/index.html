(٢٦) فَكُلِي وَٱشۡرَبِي وَقَرِّي عَيۡنٗاۖ فَإِمَّا تَرَيِنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡبَشَرِ أَحَدٗا فَقُولِيٓ إِنِّي نَذَرۡتُ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ صَوۡمٗا فَلَنۡ أُكَلِّمَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ إِنسِيّٗا

So eat of the fresh dates, drink from the water, be pleased with your child, and do not grieve. And if you see anyone among the people asking you regarding the matter of the child, tell him: I have bound myself to silence from speech for my Lord's sake, so I will not speak to anyone today.

(٢٧) فَأَتَتۡ بِهِۦ قَوۡمَهَا تَحۡمِلُهُۥۖ قَالُواْ يَٰمَرۡيَمُ لَقَدۡ جِئۡتِ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا فَرِيّٗا

Then Mary came to her people carrying her son. Her people said to her, in objection: O Mary, you have come with a tremendous matter of falsehood, in that you have come with a child without a father.

(٢٨) يَٰٓأُخۡتَ هَٰرُونَ مَا كَانَ أَبُوكِ ٱمۡرَأَ سَوۡءٖ وَمَا كَانَتۡ أُمُّكِ بَغِيّٗا

You who resembles Aaron (a righteous man) in worship, your father was not an adulterer, nor was your mother. Thus, you are from a pure household known to be righteous, so how can you come with a child without a father?

(٢٩) فَأَشَارَتۡ إِلَيۡهِۖ قَالُواْ كَيۡفَ نُكَلِّمُ مَن كَانَ فِي ٱلۡمَهۡدِ صَبِيّٗا

So she pointed towards her son Jesus (peace be upon him), who was in a cradle. So the people said in bemusement: How can we talk to a child while he is [so young that he is still] in the cradle?

(٣٠) قَالَ إِنِّي عَبۡدُ ٱللَّهِ ءَاتَىٰنِيَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَجَعَلَنِي نَبِيّٗا

Jesus (peace be upon him) said: Indeed, I am the slave of Allah. He gave me the Gospel and appointed me as one of His prophets.

(٣١) وَجَعَلَنِي مُبَارَكًا أَيۡنَ مَا كُنتُ وَأَوۡصَٰنِي بِٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَٱلزَّكَوٰةِ مَا دُمۡتُ حَيّٗا

And He made me a source of great benefit for the slaves [of Allah] wherever I go. And He ordered me to perform prayer and give zakat (obligatory alms) for as long as I live.

(٣٢) وَبَرَّۢا بِوَٰلِدَتِي وَلَمۡ يَجۡعَلۡنِي جَبَّارٗا شَقِيّٗا

And He made me benevolent to my mother. And He did not make me arrogant against the obedience of my Lord, nor did He make me disobedient to Him.

(٣٣) وَٱلسَّلَٰمُ عَلَيَّ يَوۡمَ وُلِدتُّ وَيَوۡمَ أَمُوتُ وَيَوۡمَ أُبۡعَثُ حَيّٗا

And security from the Devil and his helpers is upon me on the day of my birth, the day of my death, and the day I shall be revived to life on the Day of Resurrection. So the Devil will not corrupt me in these three desolate situations.

(٣٤) ذَٰلِكَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَۖ قَوۡلَ ٱلۡحَقِّ ٱلَّذِي فِيهِ يَمۡتَرُونَ

The one described with those qualities is Jesus, son of Mary. This speech is the statement of truth regarding him, unlike what is said by misguided people who are filled with doubt about him and differ.

(٣٥) مَا كَانَ لِلَّهِ أَن يَتَّخِذَ مِن وَلَدٖۖ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥٓۚ إِذَا قَضَىٰٓ أَمۡرٗا فَإِنَّمَا يَقُولُ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ

It does not befit Allah to have a son, sanctified and hallowed He is from that. When He intends something, it is enough for Him to say regarding that matter: Be, then it undoubtedly is. So He who is like that is hallowed from having a son.

(٣٦) وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ رَبِّي وَرَبُّكُمۡ فَٱعۡبُدُوهُۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٞ مُّسۡتَقِيمٞ

And indeed Allah, may He be glorified, is both my Lord and your Lord, so make worship sincere for Him alone. This, which I have mentioned to you, is the straight path that leads to the pleasure of Allah.

(٣٧) فَٱخۡتَلَفَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِهِمۡۖ فَوَيۡلٞ لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن مَّشۡهَدِ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٍ

So the differing people disagreed regarding the matter of Jesus (peace be upon him), and then became separate factions among his people. Thus, some of them believed in him and said: He is a Messenger.Some disbelieved in him, such as the Jews, just as some groups exaggerated his status so they said: He is Allah.And some of them said: He is the son of Allah.Exalted is Allah from that. So woe to those who differ regarding his matter from witnessing the Day of Resurrection due to what it holds of scenes [of dread], reckoning, and punishment.

(٣٨) أَسۡمِعۡ بِهِمۡ وَأَبۡصِرۡ يَوۡمَ يَأۡتُونَنَاۖ لَٰكِنِ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ

How hearing and seeing they will be on that day. They will hear when hearing will not benefit them, and they will see when seeing will not benefit them. Instead, the unjust in the worldly life are in clear misguidance from the straight path. Therefore, they will not prepare for the Afterlife until it comes to them abruptly while they are upon their injustice.

Points of Benefit:

Ordering Mary not to speak is evidence of the virtue of staying silent in some situations.
Taking a vow of silence was permissible in the legislation of those before us. As for in our legislation, it is prohibited according to the Sunnah.
That is what the Quran informs us about how Jesus was created is the absolute truth; which has no doubt about it, and all other claims are baseless and not befitting the Messengers.
In the worldly life, the disbeliever is deaf and blind to the truth. But in the Hereafter, when he sees the torment, he will see and hear, but it will not benefit him.
