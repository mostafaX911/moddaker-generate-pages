(٣٩) وَأَنذِرۡهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡحَسۡرَةِ إِذۡ قُضِيَ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ وَهُمۡ فِي غَفۡلَةٖ وَهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

And warn the people, O Messenger, of the Day of Regret when the evildoer will regret his evil-doing, as will the virtuous for not gathering many acts of obedience, when the scrolls of the slaves [of Allah] will be rolled up, their reckoning is finished, and each draws towards what he put forth. During their worldly life, they are deceived by it and distracted from the hereafter and do not believe in the Day of Resurrection.

(٤٠) إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَرِثُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَنۡ عَلَيۡهَا وَإِلَيۡنَا يُرۡجَعُونَ

Indeed, We will be who remains after the creation has perished, We inherit the earth and all those therein because they would have all perished, and We will remain after them, and We own them, do with them as We wish and to the Us alone shall they return to on the Day of Resurrection for reckoning and recompense.

(٤١) وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ صِدِّيقٗا نَّبِيًّا

And mention, O Messenger, in the Quran which has been revealed to you, the story of Abraham (peace be upon him). Indeed, he was very truthful and affirming of the signs of Allah, and was a prophet [sent] from Allah.

(٤٢) إِذۡ قَالَ لِأَبِيهِ يَٰٓأَبَتِ لِمَ تَعۡبُدُ مَا لَا يَسۡمَعُ وَلَا يُبۡصِرُ وَلَا يُغۡنِي عَنكَ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا

When he said to his father Azar: O father, why, instead of Allah, do you worship an idol which does not hear your supplication if you supplicate to it, does not see your worship if you worship it, does not remove harm from you, and does not bring you benefit?

(٤٣) يَٰٓأَبَتِ إِنِّي قَدۡ جَآءَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ مَا لَمۡ يَأۡتِكَ فَٱتَّبِعۡنِيٓ أَهۡدِكَ صِرَٰطٗا سَوِيّٗا

O my father, indeed knowledge has come to me through revelation which has not come to you, so follow me, and I shall guide you to the straight path.

(٤٤) يَٰٓأَبَتِ لَا تَعۡبُدِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَۖ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ كَانَ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ عَصِيّٗا

O my father, do not worship the Devil by obeying him. Indeed, the Devil was disobedient to the Merciful when He ordered him to prostrate to Adam, and then did not prostrate.

(٤٥) يَٰٓأَبَتِ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ أَن يَمَسَّكَ عَذَابٞ مِّنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ فَتَكُونَ لِلشَّيۡطَٰنِ وَلِيّٗا

O my father, indeed I fear that a torment from the Merciful will afflict you if you die upon your disbelief, so you will be a companion to the Devil in torment due to your alliance to him.

(٤٦) قَالَ أَرَاغِبٌ أَنتَ عَنۡ ءَالِهَتِي يَٰٓإِبۡرَٰهِيمُۖ لَئِن لَّمۡ تَنتَهِ لَأَرۡجُمَنَّكَۖ وَٱهۡجُرۡنِي مَلِيّٗا

Azar said to his son Abraham (peace be upon him): Are you turning away from my idols which I worship, O Abraham? I shall certainly pelt you with stones if you do not stop cursing my idols. Stay away from me for a long time, and do not gather with me.

(٤٧) قَالَ سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكَۖ سَأَسۡتَغۡفِرُ لَكَ رَبِّيٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ بِي حَفِيّٗا

Abraham (peace be upon him) said to his father: [Greetings of] peace be upon you from me. You will not face from me what you dislike. I shall seek forgiveness and guidance from my Lord for you. Indeed, He is very gentle with me, may He be glorified.

(٤٨) وَأَعۡتَزِلُكُمۡ وَمَا تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَأَدۡعُواْ رَبِّي عَسَىٰٓ أَلَّآ أَكُونَ بِدُعَآءِ رَبِّي شَقِيّٗا

And I shall leave you [all] and your objects of worship which you worship instead of Allah, and supplicate to my Lord alone, without associating any partners with Him. Perhaps if I supplicate to Him, He will not deprive me such that I would be wretched in the supplication to Him.

(٤٩) فَلَمَّا ٱعۡتَزَلَهُمۡ وَمَا يَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥٓ إِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَۖ وَكُلّٗا جَعَلۡنَا نَبِيّٗا

So when he abandoned them and their gods which they used to worship besides Allah, We compensated his loss of family, for We bestowed him his son Isaac and his grandson Jacob. And We made each one of them a prophet.

(٥٠) وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُم مِّن رَّحۡمَتِنَا وَجَعَلۡنَا لَهُمۡ لِسَانَ صِدۡقٍ عَلِيّٗا

And out of Our mercy, We gave them much good alongside prophethood. And We established for them good praise remaining on the tongues of [Allah's] slaves.

(٥١) وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مُوسَىٰٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ مُخۡلَصٗا وَكَانَ رَسُولٗا نَّبِيّٗا

And mention O Messenger, in the Quran revealed upon you, the story of Moses (peace be upon him). He was specially chosen, and was a messenger and a prophet.

Points of Benefit:

As Abraham was with Sarah when he left his people, mentioning the child and the grandchild they were granted together was appropriate. Then Ishmael is mentioned separately because Allah gave him to Abraham before Isaac.
Etiquette, politeness, and kindness when speaking with parents. Also, choosing the best names to call them by.
Disobedience prevents the slave from Allah's mercy and closes the gates to it, just as obedience is the biggest reason to obtain His mercy.
Allah promised every doer of good that He would spread his truthful praises according to the good he had done, and Abraham (peace be upon him) and his offspring are from the leaders of doing good.
