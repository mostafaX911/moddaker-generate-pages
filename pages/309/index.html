(٥٢) وَنَٰدَيۡنَٰهُ مِن جَانِبِ ٱلطُّورِ ٱلۡأَيۡمَنِ وَقَرَّبۡنَٰهُ نَجِيّٗا

We called him from the right side of the mountain in relation to where Moses (peace be upon him) was, and We brought him near in communion, where Allah let him hear His speech.

(٥٣) وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥ مِن رَّحۡمَتِنَآ أَخَاهُ هَٰرُونَ نَبِيّٗا

We gave him, out of Our mercy and favor, his brother Aaron (peace be upon him) as a prophet in answer to his prayer when he asked his Lord for that.

(٥٤) وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ إِسۡمَٰعِيلَۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ صَادِقَ ٱلۡوَعۡدِ وَكَانَ رَسُولٗا نَّبِيّٗا

And mention, O Messenger, in the Quran revealed to you, the story of Ishmael (peace be upon him). He was true to his promise; whenever he made a promise, he fulfilled it and was a messenger and prophet.

(٥٥) وَكَانَ يَأۡمُرُ أَهۡلَهُۥ بِٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَٱلزَّكَوٰةِ وَكَانَ عِندَ رَبِّهِۦ مَرۡضِيّٗا

He used to instruct his household to establish the prayer and give zakat, and his Lord was well pleased with him.

(٥٦) وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ إِدۡرِيسَۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ صِدِّيقٗا نَّبِيّٗا

And mention, O Messenger, in the Quran which has been revealed to you, the story of Idris (peace be upon him). He was very truthful and affirming of the signs of his Lord, and he was one of the prophets of Allah.

(٥٧) وَرَفَعۡنَٰهُ مَكَانًا عَلِيًّا

We raised his mention by the prophethood We bestowed upon him; therefore, he was of high rank.

(٥٨) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنۡعَمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِم مِّنَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ‍ۧنَ مِن ذُرِّيَّةِ ءَادَمَ وَمِمَّنۡ حَمَلۡنَا مَعَ نُوحٖ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّةِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَإِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ وَمِمَّنۡ هَدَيۡنَا وَٱجۡتَبَيۡنَآۚ إِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ خَرُّواْۤ سُجَّدٗاۤ وَبُكِيّٗا۩

Those who were mentioned in this surah (chapter), beginning with Zechariah and ending with Idris (peace be upon them), are the ones whom Allah blessed with prophethood, from the children of Adam (peace be upon him), and from the children of those whom I carried in the Ark with Noah (peace be upon him), and from the children of Abraham and the children of Jacob (peace be upon them), and from those whom We granted success to the guidance of Islam and whom We chose and made prophets. When they heard the verses of Allah being recited, they prostrated to Allah, weeping out of fear of Him.

(٥٩) ۞فَخَلَفَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ خَلۡفٌ أَضَاعُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَٱتَّبَعُواْ ٱلشَّهَوَٰتِۖ فَسَوۡفَ يَلۡقَوۡنَ غَيًّا

Then after these chosen prophets, there came followers of evil and deviation. They neglected the prayer by not offering it in the manner that was called for. They perpetrated sins their souls craved such as fornication. So those people will face evil and loss in the Hell.

(٦٠) إِلَّا مَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يَدۡخُلُونَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ وَلَا يُظۡلَمُونَ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا

Except those who repented from their shortcomings and transgressions, believed in Allah, and did righteous deeds, for those who possess such attributes will enter Paradise, and the reward of their actions will not be diminished, even if it were little.

(٦١) جَنَّٰتِ عَدۡنٍ ٱلَّتِي وَعَدَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ عِبَادَهُۥ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ وَعۡدُهُۥ مَأۡتِيّٗا

Gardens to live and settle therein [permanently], which the Most-Merciful promised His righteous slaves in the unseen that He enter them into. They did not see it, then they believed in it. So Allah's promise of Paradise, even though it is hidden, will undoubtedly come to pass.

(٦٢) لَّا يَسۡمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغۡوًا إِلَّا سَلَٰمٗاۖ وَلَهُمۡ رِزۡقُهُمۡ فِيهَا بُكۡرَةٗ وَعَشِيّٗا

There, they will not hear any futile or obscene talk. Instead, they will listen to the [greeting of] peace from one another and the [greeting of] peace from the angels to them. They will be given whatever food they desire at any time they wish.

(٦٣) تِلۡكَ ٱلۡجَنَّةُ ٱلَّتِي نُورِثُ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا مَن كَانَ تَقِيّٗا

This Paradise described with the above attributes is that which We will pass on to be inherited by Our slaves who complied with the commands and avoided the prohibitions.

After mentioning the reward of the heedful, Allah notes that true heedfulness means not acting except by His command, stating:

(٦٤) وَمَا نَتَنَزَّلُ إِلَّا بِأَمۡرِ رَبِّكَۖ لَهُۥ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِينَا وَمَا خَلۡفَنَا وَمَا بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَۚ وَمَا كَانَ رَبُّكَ نَسِيّٗا

And say, O Gabriel, to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم: The angels do not descent of their own accord. They only descend at Allah's command. Everything ahead of us in the hereafter, everything behind us in this world, and everything in between belongs to Allah. O Messenger, Your Lord does not forget anything.

Points of Benefit:

The one who calls to Allah always needs supporters to help him with his mission.
Affirming the attribute of Speech for Allah, the Exalted.
Sincerely promising is praiseworthy and is one of the characteristics of the Prophets and Messengers. But its opposite, which is breaking promises, is blameworthy.
The angels whom Allah sends with the revelation do not descend upon any human prophet or messenger except by the command of Allah.
