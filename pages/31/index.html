(١٩٧) ٱلۡحَجُّ أَشۡهُرٞ مَّعۡلُومَٰتٞۚ فَمَن فَرَضَ فِيهِنَّ ٱلۡحَجَّ فَلَا رَفَثَ وَلَا فُسُوقَ وَلَا جِدَالَ فِي ٱلۡحَجِّۗ وَمَا تَفۡعَلُواْ مِنۡ خَيۡرٖ يَعۡلَمۡهُ ٱللَّهُۗ وَتَزَوَّدُواْ فَإِنَّ خَيۡرَ ٱلزَّادِ ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ وَٱتَّقُونِ يَٰٓأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

The time of the Hajj is within the well-known months, starting with the month of Shawwal and ending on the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah. Sexual relations and things that lead to it are prohibited for any person who decides to perform the Hajj in these months once he enters the state of iḥrām. In addition, the prohibition of going against Allah by committing sins becomes even more emphasized at this time due to the sacredness of the time and place. The pilgrim may not quarrel with anyone, causing them to become angry and leading to a fight. If you do any good, Allah knows and will reward you for it. To perform the Hajj, take an adequate amount of food and drink, but remember that the best provision for all of your affairs is being always mindful of Allah. O people of sound mind, fear Me (Allah) by fulfilling My instructions and avoiding My prohibitions.

(١٩٨) لَيۡسَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ جُنَاحٌ أَن تَبۡتَغُواْ فَضۡلٗا مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡۚ فَإِذَآ أَفَضۡتُم مِّنۡ عَرَفَٰتٖ فَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ عِندَ ٱلۡمَشۡعَرِ ٱلۡحَرَامِۖ وَٱذۡكُرُوهُ كَمَا هَدَىٰكُمۡ وَإِن كُنتُم مِّن قَبۡلِهِۦ لَمِنَ ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

There is no sin in seeking lawful provision by trading or other ways during the Hajj. When you leave Arafat after having spent the day there on the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah, and when you head towards Muzdalifah that night of the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah, remember Allah by exalting and praising Him and by supplicating to Him at Al-Mashʿar Al-Ḥarām (The Sacred Grove) in Muzdalifah. Remember that Allah has guided you to the teachings of His path and the sacred rites of Hajj to His House, for you were unaware of His Sacred Law before this.

(١٩٩) ثُمَّ أَفِيضُواْ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ أَفَاضَ ٱلنَّاسُ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Then leave Arafat just as the people who followed Abraham (peace be upon him) used to do, and do not leave out stopping at Arafat as the People of Ignorance (i.e., pre-Islam) used to do. Seek forgiveness from Allah for any shortcomings and defects in performing the rites that He ordained upon you. Indeed, Allah forgives those of His slaves who repent, and He is Merciful towards them.

(٢٠٠) فَإِذَا قَضَيۡتُم مَّنَٰسِكَكُمۡ فَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَذِكۡرِكُمۡ ءَابَآءَكُمۡ أَوۡ أَشَدَّ ذِكۡرٗاۗ فَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَقُولُ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَمَا لَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ مِنۡ خَلَٰقٖ

When you have completed the rites of Hajj, remember Allah and increase your praise of Him, just as you exalt and praise your forefathers – or even more than that, for every favor you enjoy comes from Him. People are different: some are disbelieving polytheists who only believe in this worldly life. Such people will only ask Allah for the blessing and adornments of this world in the form of health, wealth, and children. However, they will not receive anything of what Allah has prepared for His faithful slaves in the Afterlife. This is due to their desire for this world and neglect of the Afterlife.

(٢٠١) وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَقُولُ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةٗ وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ حَسَنَةٗ وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ

Yet there is another group of people who have faith in Allah and the Afterlife who ask their Lord for the blessings of this world and the ability to do righteous deeds, just as they ask Him to receive entry into Paradise and safety from the punishment of the Fire.

(٢٠٢) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ نَصِيبٞ مِّمَّا كَسَبُواْۚ وَٱللَّهُ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

Those who pray for the good of this world and the Afterlife will receive their share of the great reward for their good actions in the world, for Allah is Swift in taking account of actions.

Points of Benefit:

The believer should make provisions for the travel of this life and the next. So, Allah mentioned that the best provision is to be Heedful.
The legitimacy of making plentiful remembrance of Allah, the Exalted, upon completing the rights of Hajj.
People have differing objectives; some are only concerned with this life and do not ask their Lord for anything other than it, while others ask for the best of both this life and the next, and they are those granted success.
