(٦٥) رَّبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا فَٱعۡبُدۡهُ وَٱصۡطَبِرۡ لِعِبَٰدَتِهِۦۚ هَلۡ تَعۡلَمُ لَهُۥ سَمِيّٗا

He is the Creator and possessor of the heavens and the earth, and He controls the affairs of both. He is also the Creator, Owner, and Controller of what is between them, so worship Him alone, for He alone deserves worship. Remain firm in worshipping Him, for He has no match or peer who have a share in His worship.

(٦٦) وَيَقُولُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ أَءِذَا مَا مِتُّ لَسَوۡفَ أُخۡرَجُ حَيًّا

The disbeliever, who denies the Resurrection, mockingly says: When I die, will I come out of my grave alive for a second time? This seems certainly far-fetched.

(٦٧) أَوَ لَا يَذۡكُرُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ أَنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰهُ مِن قَبۡلُ وَلَمۡ يَكُ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا

Does the person rejecting the Resurrection not remember that We created him previously when he was nothing? Then the first creation can be used as evidence for the second creation, though the second creation is easier and simpler.

(٦٨) فَوَرَبِّكَ لَنَحۡشُرَنَّهُمۡ وَٱلشَّيَٰطِينَ ثُمَّ لَنُحۡضِرَنَّهُمۡ حَوۡلَ جَهَنَّمَ جِثِيّٗا

So by your Lord, O Messenger, We will certainly take them out of their graves to the place of gathering along with the devils who had misled them. Then We will bring them outside Hell, humiliated and on their knees.

(٦٩) ثُمَّ لَنَنزِعَنَّ مِن كُلِّ شِيعَةٍ أَيُّهُمۡ أَشَدُّ عَلَى ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ عِتِيّٗا

Then We shall severely and aggressively extract from each of the groups of deviation those who were most disobedient and those are their leaders.

(٧٠) ثُمَّ لَنَحۡنُ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ أَوۡلَىٰ بِهَا صِلِيّٗا

Then We surely know best those who most deserve to enter the Fire and endure and suffer its heat.

(٧١) وَإِن مِّنكُمۡ إِلَّا وَارِدُهَاۚ كَانَ عَلَىٰ رَبِّكَ حَتۡمٗا مَّقۡضِيّٗا

O people, all of you, without exception, will cross over the bridge that stretches over Hell. Your Lord has decreed this as a resolute ruling, thus no one can push back His decree.

(٧٢) ثُمَّ نُنَجِّي ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَواْ وَّنَذَرُ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ فِيهَا جِثِيّٗا

Then after crossing the bridge, We will deliver those who were heedful of their Lord by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. We will leave the wrongdoers on their knees without being able to escape from it.

(٧٣) وَإِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُنَا بَيِّنَٰتٖ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَيُّ ٱلۡفَرِيقَيۡنِ خَيۡرٞ مَّقَامٗا وَأَحۡسَنُ نَدِيّٗا

When Our clear verses sent down to Our Messenger are recited to people, the disbelievers say to the believers: Which of our two parties is better in residence and dwelling and superior in assembly and gathering: Our party or your party?

(٧٤) وَكَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا قَبۡلَهُم مِّن قَرۡنٍ هُمۡ أَحۡسَنُ أَثَٰثٗا وَرِءۡيٗا

How many a nation have We destroyed prior to these disbelievers who take pride in their material superiority! [Those nations] surpassed them in riches and outward appearance due to the luxury of their clothing and gratification of their bodies.

(٧٥) قُلۡ مَن كَانَ فِي ٱلضَّلَٰلَةِ فَلۡيَمۡدُدۡ لَهُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ مَدًّاۚ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا رَأَوۡاْ مَا يُوعَدُونَ إِمَّا ٱلۡعَذَابَ وَإِمَّا ٱلسَّاعَةَ فَسَيَعۡلَمُونَ مَنۡ هُوَ شَرّٞ مَّكَانٗا وَأَضۡعَفُ جُندٗا

Say, O Messenger: The Merciful will grant respite to those treading in their misguidance so that they fall further into it until when they see what they had been threatened with, from [either] the hastened punishment in the world or the postponed [one] on the Day of Resurrection, then they will realize who is in a worse position and who has fewer supporters. Is it their party or the party of the believers?

(٧٦) وَيَزِيدُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱهۡتَدَوۡاْ هُدٗىۗ وَٱلۡبَٰقِيَٰتُ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتُ خَيۡرٌ عِندَ رَبِّكَ ثَوَابٗا وَخَيۡرٞ مَّرَدًّا

In contrast to the deferring [of punishment] for those people so that they increase in their misguidance, Allah gives more faith and obedience to those who are guided. Righteous deeds that lead to eternal bliss are more beneficial, in the sight of your Lord, O Messenger, in terms of their recompense and better in outcome.

Points of Benefit:

The believers should work on what they have been ordered to do and persist with it as much as possible.
All of creation will pass over the Fire, i.e., they will traverse the bridge over it, not enter it. This is something inevitable.
The standards and correct understanding of the religion differ from what the ignorant and general public perceive.
Whoever is immersed in misguidance rooted in disbelief, Allah will leave him in the depths of his ignorance and disbelief, extending his complacency to intensify his punishment.
Allah keeps the believers firm upon guidance, gives them more success and victory, and sends signs that cause their certainty to become further, all as recompense for them.
