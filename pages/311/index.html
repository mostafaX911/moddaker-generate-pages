(٧٧) أَفَرَءَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِي كَفَرَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَقَالَ لَأُوتَيَنَّ مَالٗا وَوَلَدًا

Have you, O messenger, seen the man who disbelieves in Our proofs, rejects Our threat and says: If I die and am resurrected, I will undoubtedly be given much wealth and children.

(٧٨) أَطَّلَعَ ٱلۡغَيۡبَ أَمِ ٱتَّخَذَ عِندَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ عَهۡدٗا

Does he know the unseen and therefore speaks based on evidence? Or does he have a pledge from his Lord to enter him into Paradise and to give him wealth and children?

(٧٩) كَلَّاۚ سَنَكۡتُبُ مَا يَقُولُ وَنَمُدُّ لَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ مَدّٗا

The matter is not as he claims. We will undoubtedly record what he says and does, and We will punish him even more because of the falsehood that he claims.

(٨٠) وَنَرِثُهُۥ مَا يَقُولُ وَيَأۡتِينَا فَرۡدٗا

We shall inherit the wealth and children he leaves behind after We destroy him. He will come to Us on the Day of Resurrection all alone, deprived of the wealth and prestige he used to delight in.

(٨١) وَٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ ءَالِهَةٗ لِّيَكُونُواْ لَهُمۡ عِزّٗا

The polytheists worship things other than Allah so that they may be supporters and helpers to attain victory with.

(٨٢) كَلَّاۚ سَيَكۡفُرُونَ بِعِبَادَتِهِمۡ وَيَكُونُونَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ضِدًّا

The matter is not as they claim. These things that they worship besides Allah will deny the polytheists' worship of them on the Day of Resurrection. They will disassociate themselves from them and become their enemies.

(٨٣) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا ٱلشَّيَٰطِينَ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ تَؤُزُّهُمۡ أَزّٗا

Have you, O Messenger, not seen that We sent the devils and gave them authority over the disbelievers inciting them to commit sins and prevent others from Allah's religion?

(٨٤) فَلَا تَعۡجَلۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡۖ إِنَّمَا نَعُدُّ لَهُمۡ عَدّٗا

Do not be hasty, O Messenger, in asking Allah to punish them quickly. We merely tally their ages until when their respite ends, We will punish them with what they deserve.

(٨٥) يَوۡمَ نَحۡشُرُ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ إِلَى ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ وَفۡدٗا

Mention, O Messenger, the Day of Resurrection, the Day We shall gather the heedful of their Lord, by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, to their Lord as an honored, dignified delegation.

(٨٦) وَنَسُوقُ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ إِلَىٰ جَهَنَّمَ وِرۡدٗا

And We will drag the disbelievers to Hell while they are thirsty.

(٨٧) لَّا يَمۡلِكُونَ ٱلشَّفَٰعَةَ إِلَّا مَنِ ٱتَّخَذَ عِندَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ عَهۡدٗا

These disbelievers will not have the power to intercede for one another, except for those who had taken a pledge with Allah in the world by having faith in Him and His messengers.

(٨٨) وَقَالُواْ ٱتَّخَذَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وَلَدٗا

The Jews, the Christians, and some polytheists said: The Merciful had a child.

(٨٩) لَّقَدۡ جِئۡتُمۡ شَيۡ‍ًٔا إِدّٗا

You who say this, have indeed come with something tremendous.

(٩٠) تَكَادُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ يَتَفَطَّرۡنَ مِنۡهُ وَتَنشَقُّ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَتَخِرُّ ٱلۡجِبَالُ هَدًّا

The heavens are on the verge of splitting because of this objectionable statement, the earth is about to fracture, and the mountains almost falling into ruin.

(٩١) أَن دَعَوۡاْ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ وَلَدٗا

All of this is because they have attributed a son to the Merciful. Allah is vastly exalted above that.

(٩٢) وَمَا يَنۢبَغِي لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ أَن يَتَّخِذَ وَلَدًا

It is not befitting of the Merciful to take a child due to His sanctity from that.

(٩٣) إِن كُلُّ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّآ ءَاتِي ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ عَبۡدٗا

Everyone in the heavens and the earth, from the angels, men, and jinn, shall be brought before their Lord on the Day of Resurrection, in submission.

(٩٤) لَّقَدۡ أَحۡصَىٰهُمۡ وَعَدَّهُمۡ عَدّٗا

He has full knowledge of them and has numbered them exactly. Nothing of theirs is hidden from Him.

(٩٥) وَكُلُّهُمۡ ءَاتِيهِ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فَرۡدًا

Each of them will come to Him alone on the Day of Resurrection, without any helper or wealth.

Points of Benefit:

The verses show the absurdity of the disbeliever and his naivety. He has the sweetest hopes but will find the complete opposite in the Hereafter.
Allah gave the devils power to tempt the disbelievers towards evil and to abandon obedience for disobedience.
The people of virtue, knowledge, and righteousness will intercede on the Day of Resurrection by Allah's permission.
