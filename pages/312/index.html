(٩٦) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ سَيَجۡعَلُ لَهُمُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وُدّٗا

Those who believe in Allah and do righteous actions that Allah is pleased with, Allah will create for them love by His loving them and by making them beloved to His slaves.

(٩٧) فَإِنَّمَا يَسَّرۡنَٰهُ بِلِسَانِكَ لِتُبَشِّرَ بِهِ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ وَتُنذِرَ بِهِۦ قَوۡمٗا لُّدّٗا

O Messenger, We have merely made this Quran easy by revealing it in your language so that you may bring glad news to the heedful who comply with My commands and avoid My prohibitions, and [so that] you may warn people who are severe in argumentation and arrogance to yielding to the truth.

(٩٨) وَكَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا قَبۡلَهُم مِّن قَرۡنٍ هَلۡ تُحِسُّ مِنۡهُم مِّنۡ أَحَدٍ أَوۡ تَسۡمَعُ لَهُمۡ رِكۡزَۢا

How many a nation have We destroyed before your people! Do you perceive a single one of those nations today or hear any soft sound from them? What afflicted them could afflict others when Allah permits.

Ta-Ha (Surah Taha)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

The joy of following the Quran's guidance and carrying its message, and the misery that results in violating it.

Explanation:

(١) طه

Tā Hā – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) مَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِتَشۡقَىٰٓ

We have not revealed the Quran to you, O Messenger, to cause you self-exhaustion in grief over your people turning away from belief in you.

(٣) إِلَّا تَذۡكِرَةٗ لِّمَن يَخۡشَىٰ

We only revealed it to be a reminder for those whom Allah has given success to fear Him.

(٤) تَنزِيلٗا مِّمَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ ٱلۡعُلَى

It was sent down by Allah, Who created the earth and the high heavens. It is thus a great Quran, as it was sent down by one who is Great.

(٥) ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ

The Merciful who ascended and rose over the Throne in a manner that is befitting for His Majesty, may He be glorified and exalted.

(٦) لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا وَمَا تَحۡتَ ٱلثَّرَىٰ

Everything in the heavens and the earth, what lies between them, and beneath the soil belongs to Him alone, may He be glorified, in creation, dominion, and determination.

(٧) وَإِن تَجۡهَرۡ بِٱلۡقَوۡلِ فَإِنَّهُۥ يَعۡلَمُ ٱلسِّرَّ وَأَخۡفَى

If you, O Messenger, proclaim or conceal your speech, may He be glorified, knows all of that. He knows the secrets and what is even more hidden, such as someone's thoughts. Nothing is of that is hidden from Him.

(٨) ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ لَهُ ٱلۡأَسۡمَآءُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ

There is no rightful object of worship besides Allah. To Him alone belong Names that are the very pinnacle of perfection and excellence.

When the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم suffered rejection from his people, his consolation came in the story of Moses (peace be upon him). The Exalted states:

(٩) وَهَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ مُوسَىٰٓ

The story of Moses, son of 'Imran (peace be upon him), has surely come to you, O Messenger.

(١٠) إِذۡ رَءَا نَارٗا فَقَالَ لِأَهۡلِهِ ٱمۡكُثُوٓاْ إِنِّيٓ ءَانَسۡتُ نَارٗا لَّعَلِّيٓ ءَاتِيكُم مِّنۡهَا بِقَبَسٍ أَوۡ أَجِدُ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ هُدٗى

When he saw a fire while traveling, he told his family: Stay here. I saw a fire. Perhaps I will bring you a torch from it or find somebody to guide me to the path.

(١١) فَلَمَّآ أَتَىٰهَا نُودِيَ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ

When he came to the Fire, Allah (may He be Exalted) called out to him, saying: O Moses.

(١٢) إِنِّيٓ أَنَا۠ رَبُّكَ فَٱخۡلَعۡ نَعۡلَيۡكَ إِنَّكَ بِٱلۡوَادِ ٱلۡمُقَدَّسِ طُوٗى

Indeed I am your Lord. Remove your shoes in preparation for your conversation with me. You are in the purified valley of Tuwa.

Points of Benefit:

The Quran was not revealed to wear out the soul with worship or to make it taste grave hardship. It is only a book of admonishment that those who revere their Lord can benefit from.
Allah relates creating with commanding. So, just as nothing is created without wisdom, He does not command or forbid except with what is just and wise.
The husband must spend upon his family (wife) for their food, clothing, accommodation, and heating when it is cold.
