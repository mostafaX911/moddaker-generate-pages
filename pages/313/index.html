(١٣) وَأَنَا ٱخۡتَرۡتُكَ فَٱسۡتَمِعۡ لِمَا يُوحَىٰٓ

And I have chosen you, O Moses, to convey My message, so listen to what I reveal.

(١٤) إِنَّنِيٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنَا۠ فَٱعۡبُدۡنِي وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ لِذِكۡرِيٓ

Indeed, I am Allah; there is no rightful object of worship but Me. So worship Me alone and perform the prayer in the most perfect fashion so that you may remember Me in it.

(١٥) إِنَّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ ءَاتِيَةٌ أَكَادُ أُخۡفِيهَا لِتُجۡزَىٰ كُلُّ نَفۡسِۢ بِمَا تَسۡعَىٰ

Indeed, the Hour is most definitely coming and occurring. I almost keep it hidden for no creation knows its time, though they are aware of its signs by the information given to them by the prophet sent to them; so that each soul is rewarded for what it did, whether good or evil.

(١٦) فَلَا يَصُدَّنَّكَ عَنۡهَا مَن لَّا يُؤۡمِنُ بِهَا وَٱتَّبَعَ هَوَىٰهُ فَتَرۡدَىٰ

Do not let the disbelievers, who do not believe in it and follow their unlawful desires, turn you away from believing in it and preparing for it with righteous actions; for you would be destroyed on account of that.

(١٧) وَمَا تِلۡكَ بِيَمِينِكَ يَٰمُوسَىٰ

What is that in your right hand, O Moses?

(١٨) قَالَ هِيَ عَصَايَ أَتَوَكَّؤُاْ عَلَيۡهَا وَأَهُشُّ بِهَا عَلَىٰ غَنَمِي وَلِيَ فِيهَا مَ‍َٔارِبُ أُخۡرَىٰ

Moses (peace be upon him) said: It is my staff: I lean on it when walking, I beat down tree leaves with it for my sheep and goats [to eat], and it has other uses besides those I mentioned.

(١٩) قَالَ أَلۡقِهَا يَٰمُوسَىٰ

Allah said: Throw it, O Moses.

(٢٠) فَأَلۡقَىٰهَا فَإِذَا هِيَ حَيَّةٞ تَسۡعَىٰ

Moses threw it, and it turned into a snake moving swiftly and lightly.

(٢١) قَالَ خُذۡهَا وَلَا تَخَفۡۖ سَنُعِيدُهَا سِيرَتَهَا ٱلۡأُولَىٰ

Allah said to Moses (peace be upon him): Take hold of the staff and do not be frightened when it changes into a snake. We will return it to its former state when you take hold of it.

(٢٢) وَٱضۡمُمۡ يَدَكَ إِلَىٰ جَنَاحِكَ تَخۡرُجۡ بَيۡضَآءَ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ سُوٓءٍ ءَايَةً أُخۡرَىٰ

And hold your hand to your side, and it will come out white, without vitiligo (a skin disease); a second sign for you.

(٢٣) لِنُرِيَكَ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا ٱلۡكُبۡرَى

We have shown you these two signs, O Moses, to show you some of Our most significant signs that indicate Our power and that you are indeed a messenger [sent] from Allah.

(٢٤) ٱذۡهَبۡ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّهُۥ طَغَىٰ

Go, O Moses, to Pharaoh, for he has overstepped the bounds in disbelief and rebellion against Allah.

(٢٥) قَالَ رَبِّ ٱشۡرَحۡ لِي صَدۡرِي

Moses (peace be upon him) said: Lord, expand my breast for me so that I can bear the harm.

(٢٦) وَيَسِّرۡ لِيٓ أَمۡرِي

And make my task easy.

(٢٧) وَٱحۡلُلۡ عُقۡدَةٗ مِّن لِّسَانِي

Give me the ability to speak eloquently.

(٢٨) يَفۡقَهُواْ قَوۡلِي

So that they understand my speech when I convey Your message to them.

(٢٩) وَٱجۡعَل لِّي وَزِيرٗا مِّنۡ أَهۡلِي

Grant me a helper from my family to assist me in my affairs.

(٣٠) هَٰرُونَ أَخِي

[Let it be] Aaron, the son of Imran, my brother.

(٣١) ٱشۡدُدۡ بِهِۦٓ أَزۡرِي

Strengthen my by back by him.

(٣٢) وَأَشۡرِكۡهُ فِيٓ أَمۡرِي

And make him my partner in the message.

(٣٣) كَيۡ نُسَبِّحَكَ كَثِيرٗا

So that we can glorify You much.

(٣٤) وَنَذۡكُرَكَ كَثِيرًا

And remember You much.

(٣٥) إِنَّكَ كُنتَ بِنَا بَصِيرٗا

Indeed, You are watching over us. Nothing of our matter is hidden from You.

(٣٦) قَالَ قَدۡ أُوتِيتَ سُؤۡلَكَ يَٰمُوسَىٰ

Allah said: O Moses, I have granted you your request.

(٣٧) وَلَقَدۡ مَنَنَّا عَلَيۡكَ مَرَّةً أُخۡرَىٰٓ

And I have certainly favored you another time.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation to listen well to important matters, the most important being the revelation sent from Allah.
The first revelation to Moses comprised of two foundations from the creed: affirming the oneness of Allah and believing in the Hour (of Resurrection), as well as the most important obligation after belief, the Prayer.
Cooperation between those calling to Allah is essential to achieve the objective, as Allah made Moses' brother Aaron a prophet to support him in performing his mission.
The importance of the caller to Allah having the skill of making those he calls understand.
