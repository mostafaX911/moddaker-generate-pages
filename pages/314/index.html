(٣٨) إِذۡ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّكَ مَا يُوحَىٰٓ

When We inspired your mother with that which Allah protected you from Pharaoh's plot.

(٣٩) أَنِ ٱقۡذِفِيهِ فِي ٱلتَّابُوتِ فَٱقۡذِفِيهِ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ فَلۡيُلۡقِهِ ٱلۡيَمُّ بِٱلسَّاحِلِ يَأۡخُذۡهُ عَدُوّٞ لِّي وَعَدُوّٞ لَّهُۥۚ وَأَلۡقَيۡتُ عَلَيۡكَ مَحَبَّةٗ مِّنِّي وَلِتُصۡنَعَ عَلَىٰ عَيۡنِيٓ

For We had instructed her when We inspired her: Throw your child, after he is born, into a box and then cast it into the Nile River. The river will cast him onto the bank by Our command, and he will be taken in by an enemy of Mine and his, who is Pharaoh. I placed love upon you from Me, so people loved you, and so that you would be reared under My eye, protection, and care.

(٤٠) إِذۡ تَمۡشِيٓ أُخۡتُكَ فَتَقُولُ هَلۡ أَدُلُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَن يَكۡفُلُهُۥۖ فَرَجَعۡنَٰكَ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّكَ كَيۡ تَقَرَّ عَيۡنُهَا وَلَا تَحۡزَنَۚ وَقَتَلۡتَ نَفۡسٗا فَنَجَّيۡنَٰكَ مِنَ ٱلۡغَمِّ وَفَتَنَّٰكَ فُتُونٗاۚ فَلَبِثۡتَ سِنِينَ فِيٓ أَهۡلِ مَدۡيَنَ ثُمَّ جِئۡتَ عَلَىٰ قَدَرٖ يَٰمُوسَىٰ

When your sister went out following the chest, moving whenever it moved, then said to those who took it: Shall I guide you to who will care for him, nurse him and rear him? So We granted favor upon you by returning you to your mother so that she could rejoice with your return to her and not grieve over you. Then You killed the Copt you punched, so We granted favor upon you by saving you from the punishment. And we saved you time and again from every test that you faced. You then left and stayed among the people of Midian for years, and then you came at the time that was specified for you to come to be spoken to, O Moses.

(٤١) وَٱصۡطَنَعۡتُكَ لِنَفۡسِي

I have chosen you as My messenger to convey to people the what I reveal to you.

(٤٢) ٱذۡهَبۡ أَنتَ وَأَخُوكَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِي وَلَا تَنِيَا فِي ذِكۡرِي

O Moses, you and your brother Aaron go with Our signs that show Allah's power and oneness. Do not slacken in calling towards Me and in remembering Me.

(٤٣) ٱذۡهَبَآ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّهُۥ طَغَىٰ

Go to Pharaoh, for indeed he has overstepped the boundaries in disbelief and rebellion against Allah.

(٤٤) فَقُولَا لَهُۥ قَوۡلٗا لَّيِّنٗا لَّعَلَّهُۥ يَتَذَكَّرُ أَوۡ يَخۡشَىٰ

Then speak to him gently without harshness, in hopes that he may remember and fear Allah, then repent.

(٤٥) قَالَا رَبَّنَآ إِنَّنَا نَخَافُ أَن يَفۡرُطَ عَلَيۡنَآ أَوۡ أَن يَطۡغَىٰ

Moses and Aaron (peace be upon them) said: We fear that he will hasten to punish us before we can fully deliver the call to him or that he will transgress the limit in oppressing us with killing or otherwise.

(٤٦) قَالَ لَا تَخَافَآۖ إِنَّنِي مَعَكُمَآ أَسۡمَعُ وَأَرَىٰ

Allah said to them: Do not be afraid; Indeed, I am with you in support and assistance. I hear and see what occurs between you and him.

(٤٧) فَأۡتِيَاهُ فَقُولَآ إِنَّا رَسُولَا رَبِّكَ فَأَرۡسِلۡ مَعَنَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ وَلَا تُعَذِّبۡهُمۡۖ قَدۡ جِئۡنَٰكَ بِ‍َٔايَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكَۖ وَٱلسَّلَٰمُ عَلَىٰ مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ ٱلۡهُدَىٰٓ

So go to him and tell him: We are the messengers of your Lord, O Pharaoh, so send the Israelites with us and do not punish them by killing their sons and letting their women live. We have come to you a with proof from your Lord regarding our truthfulness. Security from Allah's torment is for those who have faith and follow Allah's guidance.

(٤٨) إِنَّا قَدۡ أُوحِيَ إِلَيۡنَآ أَنَّ ٱلۡعَذَابَ عَلَىٰ مَن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ

Allah has revealed to us that the torment in this world and the Afterlife falls on whoever rejects the signs of Allah and turns away from what the messengers brought.

(٤٩) قَالَ فَمَن رَّبُّكُمَا يَٰمُوسَىٰ

In denial of what they brought, Pharaoh said: Who is your Lord, that you claim has sent you to me, O Moses?

(٥٠) قَالَ رَبُّنَا ٱلَّذِيٓ أَعۡطَىٰ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلۡقَهُۥ ثُمَّ هَدَىٰ

Moses said: Our Lord is the One who gave everything the shape and form suitable for it, and then He guided all created things to what they were created for.

(٥١) قَالَ فَمَا بَالُ ٱلۡقُرُونِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ

Pharaoh said: What about the previous nations who had disbelieved?

Points of Benefit:

Allah taking complete care of the one He spoke directly to, Moses (peace be upon him), and the other Prophets and Messengers. Their descendants also have a portion of this care according to their situation with Allah.
One of the ways the creation is guided generally is that you find every created thing pursuing the beneficial things created for it and warding off harm from itself.
Clarifying the virtue of commanding good and forbidding evil and that it should be with wisdom, gentleness, and lenience.
Only Allah knows the unseen matters of the past, present, and future.
