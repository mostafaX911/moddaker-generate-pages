(٥٢) قَالَ عِلۡمُهَا عِندَ رَبِّي فِي كِتَٰبٖۖ لَّا يَضِلُّ رَبِّي وَلَا يَنسَى

Moses (peace be upon him) said to Pharaoh: The knowledge of what those nations did is with my Lord, recorded in the Preserved Tablet. My Lord does not err in His knowledge of them nor does he forget what he knows of them.

(٥٣) ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مَهۡدٗا وَسَلَكَ لَكُمۡ فِيهَا سُبُلٗا وَأَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَخۡرَجۡنَا بِهِۦٓ أَزۡوَٰجٗا مِّن نَّبَاتٖ شَتَّىٰ

[That knowledge is] with My Lord Who spread the earth for you to live on and made pathways for you to travel on. He sent down rainwater from the sky then brought forth various kinds of plant with that water.

(٥٤) كُلُواْ وَٱرۡعَوۡاْ أَنۡعَٰمَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلنُّهَىٰ

Eat, O people, of the pure things We brought forth for you, and graze your cattle. Indeed, those mentioned favors are evidences of Allah's power and oneness for the people of intellect.

(٥٥) ۞مِنۡهَا خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ وَفِيهَا نُعِيدُكُمۡ وَمِنۡهَا نُخۡرِجُكُمۡ تَارَةً أُخۡرَىٰ

From the soil of the earth, We created your father, Adam (peace be upon him). Into it, We shall return you when you are being buried therein when you pass away, and from it, We shall take you out again for revival on the Day of Resurrection.

(٥٦) وَلَقَدۡ أَرَيۡنَٰهُ ءَايَٰتِنَا كُلَّهَا فَكَذَّبَ وَأَبَىٰ

We had certainly showed Pharaoh all nine of Our signs. He witnessed them all then rejected them and refused to respond accepting [the call] to faith in Allah.

(٥٧) قَالَ أَجِئۡتَنَا لِتُخۡرِجَنَا مِنۡ أَرۡضِنَا بِسِحۡرِكَ يَٰمُوسَىٰ

Pharaoh said: Have you come to remove us from Egypt with the sorcery you have brought, O Moses, so you can reign over it?

(٥٨) فَلَنَأۡتِيَنَّكَ بِسِحۡرٖ مِّثۡلِهِۦ فَٱجۡعَلۡ بَيۡنَنَا وَبَيۡنَكَ مَوۡعِدٗا لَّا نُخۡلِفُهُۥ نَحۡنُ وَلَآ أَنتَ مَكَانٗا سُوٗى

We will undoubtedly bring, O Moses, sorcery similar to yours, so make an appointed time between you and us at a known time and specified place, which neither of us will fail to keep. Let the place be between the two sides and level.

(٥٩) قَالَ مَوۡعِدُكُمۡ يَوۡمُ ٱلزِّينَةِ وَأَن يُحۡشَرَ ٱلنَّاسُ ضُحٗى

Moses (peace be upon him) said to Pharaoh: The appointment between you and us will be on the day of the festival when people will gather to celebrate their festival in the forenoon.

(٦٠) فَتَوَلَّىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنُ فَجَمَعَ كَيۡدَهُۥ ثُمَّ أَتَىٰ

Pharaoh went back and gathered his plots and tricks. Then he came at the specified time and place for the contest.

(٦١) قَالَ لَهُم مُّوسَىٰ وَيۡلَكُمۡ لَا تَفۡتَرُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبٗا فَيُسۡحِتَكُم بِعَذَابٖۖ وَقَدۡ خَابَ مَنِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ

Moses advised Pharaoh's sorcerers: Beware, do not invent lies against Allah by using sorcery to deceive people, or He will eliminate you with a torment from Him. Whoever invents lies against Allah has lost.

(٦٢) فَتَنَٰزَعُوٓاْ أَمۡرَهُم بَيۡنَهُمۡ وَأَسَرُّواْ ٱلنَّجۡوَىٰ

The sorcerers debated among themselves when they heard what Moses (peace be upon him) had to say, and they conferred secretly.

(٦٣) قَالُوٓاْ إِنۡ هَٰذَٰنِ لَسَٰحِرَٰنِ يُرِيدَانِ أَن يُخۡرِجَاكُم مِّنۡ أَرۡضِكُم بِسِحۡرِهِمَا وَيَذۡهَبَا بِطَرِيقَتِكُمُ ٱلۡمُثۡلَىٰ

The sorcerers said to one another secretly: Moses and Aaron are sorcerers. They intend to drive you out of Egypt with the sorcery they brought and do away with your exalted way of life and lofty school of thought.

(٦٤) فَأَجۡمِعُواْ كَيۡدَكُمۡ ثُمَّ ٱئۡتُواْ صَفّٗاۚ وَقَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ مَنِ ٱسۡتَعۡلَىٰ

So make a firm decision and do not differ therein. Then go forward in rows and throw what you have all at once. Whoever defeats his opponent today has attained his objective.

Points of Benefit:

All of the different types of crops coming out of the Earth with their various colors are clear proof of the capability of Allah, the Exalted, and the existence of the Creator.
The verses mention two clear intellectual proofs for the Resurrection: Crops growing from the Earth after they die and people being brought out and created from it.
Pharoah's disbelief was that of obstinance because he actually saw the signs and was not just informed about them, and deep down, he was convinced of them.
Moses chose the festival day to make Allah's Word prevail, show his religion, and overcome disbelief so that it would all be in front of all the people in a public gathering so that the news would spread.
