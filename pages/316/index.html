(٦٥) قَالُواْ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِمَّآ أَن تُلۡقِيَ وَإِمَّآ أَن نَّكُونَ أَوَّلَ مَنۡ أَلۡقَىٰ

The sorcerers said to Moses (peace be upon him): O Moses, you have one of two options: Either you will be the first to throw down the magic you have, or we will be the first to do so.

(٦٦) قَالَ بَلۡ أَلۡقُواْۖ فَإِذَا حِبَالُهُمۡ وَعِصِيُّهُمۡ يُخَيَّلُ إِلَيۡهِ مِن سِحۡرِهِمۡ أَنَّهَا تَسۡعَىٰ

Moses (peace be upon him) said: Rather, you throw what you have first. So they threw what they had, and it appeared to Moses that their ropes and sticks were like snakes moving quickly on the ground due to the sorcery.

(٦٧) فَأَوۡجَسَ فِي نَفۡسِهِۦ خِيفَةٗ مُّوسَىٰ

Moses hid the fear he was feeling from what they did.

(٦٨) قُلۡنَا لَا تَخَفۡ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ

Allah reassured Moses (peace be upon him), saying: Do not fear the illusion. You, O Moses, will overcome them in victory and triumph.

(٦٩) وَأَلۡقِ مَا فِي يَمِينِكَ تَلۡقَفۡ مَا صَنَعُوٓاْۖ إِنَّمَا صَنَعُواْ كَيۡدُ سَٰحِرٖۖ وَلَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلسَّاحِرُ حَيۡثُ أَتَىٰ

Throw down the stick in your right hand: it will turn into a snake and swallow up the sorcery they have produced. They have only produced a plot of sorcery; a sorcerer will never be victorious wherever he is.

(٧٠) فَأُلۡقِيَ ٱلسَّحَرَةُ سُجَّدٗا قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِرَبِّ هَٰرُونَ وَمُوسَىٰ

So Moses threw down his stick, which turned into a snake, and swallowed up what the sorcerers had produced. The sorcerers prostrated to Allah once they learned that what Moses had was not sorcery. It was only from Allah. They said: We believe in the Lord of Moses and Aaron, the Lord of all created things.

(٧١) قَالَ ءَامَنتُمۡ لَهُۥ قَبۡلَ أَنۡ ءَاذَنَ لَكُمۡۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَكَبِيرُكُمُ ٱلَّذِي عَلَّمَكُمُ ٱلسِّحۡرَۖ فَلَأُقَطِّعَنَّ أَيۡدِيَكُمۡ وَأَرۡجُلَكُم مِّنۡ خِلَٰفٖ وَلَأُصَلِّبَنَّكُمۡ فِي جُذُوعِ ٱلنَّخۡلِ وَلَتَعۡلَمُنَّ أَيُّنَآ أَشَدُّ عَذَابٗا وَأَبۡقَىٰ

Pharaoh said in objection to the sorcerers' faith and as a threat to them: Have you believed in Moses before I permitted you to do so? Indeed, Moses must be your leader, O sorcerers, who taught you sorcery. So I shall certainly cut off from each of you one hand and foot from opposite sides and I will surely crucify your bodies on the trunks of palm trees until you die. You will become a lesson for others, and then you will certainly know which of us has a more intense and lasting torment: I or the Lord of Moses?

(٧٢) قَالُواْ لَن نُّؤۡثِرَكَ عَلَىٰ مَا جَآءَنَا مِنَ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَٱلَّذِي فَطَرَنَاۖ فَٱقۡضِ مَآ أَنتَ قَاضٍۖ إِنَّمَا تَقۡضِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ

The sorcerers said to Pharaoh: We shall not prefer following you, O Pharaoh, over following the clear signs that have come to us. We will not prefer you to Allah, Who created us. So do whatever you want to us. You only have authority over us in this temporary life, and your authority will vanish.

(٧٣) إِنَّآ ءَامَنَّا بِرَبِّنَا لِيَغۡفِرَ لَنَا خَطَٰيَٰنَا وَمَآ أَكۡرَهۡتَنَا عَلَيۡهِ مِنَ ٱلسِّحۡرِۗ وَٱللَّهُ خَيۡرٞ وَأَبۡقَىٰٓ

We believe in our Lord, with the hope that He will erase from us our past sins of disbelief and other vices and that He will erase from us the evil of sorcery that you forced us to learn, practice and challenge Moses with. Allah has a better reward than what you promise us, and His torment lasts longer than the torment you threaten us with.

(٧٤) إِنَّهُۥ مَن يَأۡتِ رَبَّهُۥ مُجۡرِمٗا فَإِنَّ لَهُۥ جَهَنَّمَ لَا يَمُوتُ فِيهَا وَلَا يَحۡيَىٰ

The gist of the matter is that those who come to their Lord on the Day of Resurrection as disbelievers will enter the Fire to remain there forever. Therein, they will not die and be relieved of its torment, nor will they live a pleasant life.

(٧٥) وَمَن يَأۡتِهِۦ مُؤۡمِنٗا قَدۡ عَمِلَ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمُ ٱلدَّرَجَٰتُ ٱلۡعُلَىٰ

Those who come to their Lord on the Day of Resurrection believing in Him, having done righteous actions, then the ones described with those great qualities will be rewarded with lofty abodes and high ranks.

(٧٦) جَنَّٰتُ عَدۡنٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ وَذَٰلِكَ جَزَآءُ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ

Those ranks are Gardens of settlement with rivers flowing under their palaces, remaining therein forever. That is the reward of those who purify themselves from disbelief and acts of disobedience.

Points of Benefit:

A sorcerer will never succeed or win no matter where he comes from or whatever tricks he employs, nor will he achieve his objective, whether good or evil, by using sorcery.
Belief performs miracles, and the belief of the sorcerers was more stable than the mountains because the torment of this worldly life was nothing to them, and they did not care about Pharoah's threat.
The oppressors have a habit of threatening the people of truth with severe punishment and follow that up with insult and humiliation.
