(٧٧) وَلَقَدۡ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنۡ أَسۡرِ بِعِبَادِي فَٱضۡرِبۡ لَهُمۡ طَرِيقٗا فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ يَبَسٗا لَّا تَخَٰفُ دَرَكٗا وَلَا تَخۡشَىٰ

And We certainly revealed to Moses: go with My slaves by night from Egypt so that nobody notices them, and make for them a dry path in the sea after striking the sea with the staff, without fear of Pharaoh and his elites catching up with you nor fear of drowning in the ocean.

(٧٨) فَأَتۡبَعَهُمۡ فِرۡعَوۡنُ بِجُنُودِهِۦ فَغَشِيَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡيَمِّ مَا غَشِيَهُمۡ

Then Pharoah, accompanied by his armies, followed them until they were all engulfed by the sea [as the water crashed down over them], the reality of which only Allah knows. They all drowned and perished, while Moses and those with him were saved.

(٧٩) وَأَضَلَّ فِرۡعَوۡنُ قَوۡمَهُۥ وَمَا هَدَىٰ

And Pharaoh misguided his people by making disbelief appear good to them and by deceiving them through falsehood. He did not direct them toward the path of guidance.

(٨٠) يَٰبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ قَدۡ أَنجَيۡنَٰكُم مِّنۡ عَدُوِّكُمۡ وَوَٰعَدۡنَٰكُمۡ جَانِبَ ٱلطُّورِ ٱلۡأَيۡمَنَ وَنَزَّلۡنَا عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡمَنَّ وَٱلسَّلۡوَىٰ

And We said to the Israelites after saving them from Pharaoh and his armies: O Children of Israel, We saved you from your enemy, and We promised you that We will speak to Moses to the right of the valley next to Mount Sinai, and We sent down to you, while you were aimlessly wandering, food you can eat which is sweet like honey, and [the flesh of] birds which resemble quails.

(٨١) كُلُواْ مِن طَيِّبَٰتِ مَا رَزَقۡنَٰكُمۡ وَلَا تَطۡغَوۡاْ فِيهِ فَيَحِلَّ عَلَيۡكُمۡ غَضَبِيۖ وَمَن يَحۡلِلۡ عَلَيۡهِ غَضَبِي فَقَدۡ هَوَىٰ

Consume from the lawful wholesome foods We have provided you with, and do not go beyond what We have made lawful, lest you consume what We have made forbidden. Otherwise, My anger will fall on you. Whomever My anger falls upon will be destroyed in this life and the next.

(٨٢) وَإِنِّي لَغَفَّارٞ لِّمَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا ثُمَّ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ

And I am extremely forgiving and pardoning to one who repents to Me, does good deeds, and is firm on the truth.

(٨٣) ۞وَمَآ أَعۡجَلَكَ عَن قَوۡمِكَ يَٰمُوسَىٰ

And what has made you hurry [here and depart from] your people, O Moses, leaving them behind?

(٨٤) قَالَ هُمۡ أُوْلَآءِ عَلَىٰٓ أَثَرِي وَعَجِلۡتُ إِلَيۡكَ رَبِّ لِتَرۡضَىٰ

Moses (peace be upon him) said: Here they are behind me, and shall catch up with me. I preceded my people in coming to You so that You become pleased with me for coming towards You quickly.

(٨٥) قَالَ فَإِنَّا قَدۡ فَتَنَّا قَوۡمَكَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِكَ وَأَضَلَّهُمُ ٱلسَّامِرِيُّ

Allah said: We have tested your people whom you left behind through worshipping the calf. Samiri called them to worship it and misguided them through that.

(٨٦) فَرَجَعَ مُوسَىٰٓ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ غَضۡبَٰنَ أَسِفٗاۚ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ أَلَمۡ يَعِدۡكُمۡ رَبُّكُمۡ وَعۡدًا حَسَنًاۚ أَفَطَالَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡعَهۡدُ أَمۡ أَرَدتُّمۡ أَن يَحِلَّ عَلَيۡكُمۡ غَضَبٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ فَأَخۡلَفۡتُم مَّوۡعِدِي

Moses returned to his people, angered by their worship of the calf and grieving over them. Moses (peace be upon him) said: My people, did Allah not make you a good promise that He will send the Torah to you and enter you into paradise? Had a long time passed upon you that you forgot? Or did you intend through this action of yours that your Lord's anger should descend upon you and His punishment befall you, and this is why you broke your promise to me to stay firm on obedience until I return to you?

(٨٧) قَالُواْ مَآ أَخۡلَفۡنَا مَوۡعِدَكَ بِمَلۡكِنَا وَلَٰكِنَّا حُمِّلۡنَآ أَوۡزَارٗا مِّن زِينَةِ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ فَقَذَفۡنَٰهَا فَكَذَٰلِكَ أَلۡقَى ٱلسَّامِرِيُّ

The people of Moses said: We did not break your promise, O Moses, willingly, but rather by compulsion. We carried large weights of the jewelry of Pharaoh's people and threw them into a ditch to get rid of them. But as we threw them into the ditch, the Samiri threw into it some dust he had from the hoof of the horse of Gabriel (peace be upon him).

Points of Benefit:

It is from Allah's established practice to take revenge upon the wrongdoers in a way that cures the hearts of the believers, cools their eyes, and removes the rage from their hearts.
The tyrant is evil for himself and his people because he misguides them from the right way and does not lead them to good or success.
Blessings should be preserved and thanked for so that they be increased. Denying them brings Allah's anger and the effects of that.
Allah is constantly forgiving those who repent from polytheism, disbelief, and sins, believe in Him, and work righteous deeds, then hold fast to that until they die upon it.
Although rushing is generally blameworthy, it is praiseworthy in some situations, like racing to do good deeds.
