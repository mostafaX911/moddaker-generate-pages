(٨٨) فَأَخۡرَجَ لَهُمۡ عِجۡلٗا جَسَدٗا لَّهُۥ خُوَارٞ فَقَالُواْ هَٰذَآ إِلَٰهُكُمۡ وَإِلَٰهُ مُوسَىٰ فَنَسِيَ

So Samiri brought out, from that jewelry of the Israelites, a lifeless body of a calf, which made a noise like that of a cow. Those amongst them whom the Samiri's action had tempted said: This is your object of worship and that of Moses. He forgot it and left it here.

(٨٩) أَفَلَا يَرَوۡنَ أَلَّا يَرۡجِعُ إِلَيۡهِمۡ قَوۡلٗا وَلَا يَمۡلِكُ لَهُمۡ ضَرّٗا وَلَا نَفۡعٗا

Do these people who were tempted by the calf and then worshipped it not see that the calf does not speak or respond to them, nor does it have the ability to remove harm from them or others or benefit itself or others?

(٩٠) وَلَقَدۡ قَالَ لَهُمۡ هَٰرُونُ مِن قَبۡلُ يَٰقَوۡمِ إِنَّمَا فُتِنتُم بِهِۦۖ وَإِنَّ رَبَّكُمُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ فَٱتَّبِعُونِي وَأَطِيعُوٓاْ أَمۡرِي

Aaron had said to them before Moses returned: The making of the calf from gold and its mooing is only to test you, to distinguish the believer from the disbeliever. And your Lord, O people, is Allah, so follow me by worshipping Him alone and obey my command to leave worshipping others besides Him.

(٩١) قَالُواْ لَن نَّبۡرَحَ عَلَيۡهِ عَٰكِفِينَ حَتَّىٰ يَرۡجِعَ إِلَيۡنَا مُوسَىٰ

Those who were put to trial through worship of the calf said: We will continue worshipping it until Moses returns to us.

(٩٢) قَالَ يَٰهَٰرُونُ مَا مَنَعَكَ إِذۡ رَأَيۡتَهُمۡ ضَلُّوٓاْ

Moses said to his brother Aaron: What prevented you when you saw them go astray by worshipping the calf beside Allah, from leaving them and joining me? Did you disobey my order when I left you in charge of them?

(٩٣) أَلَّا تَتَّبِعَنِۖ أَفَعَصَيۡتَ أَمۡرِي

Moses said to his brother Aaron: What prevented you when you saw them go astray by worshipping the calf beside Allah, from leaving them and joining me? Did you disobey my order when I left you in charge of them?

(٩٤) قَالَ يَبۡنَؤُمَّ لَا تَأۡخُذۡ بِلِحۡيَتِي وَلَا بِرَأۡسِيٓۖ إِنِّي خَشِيتُ أَن تَقُولَ فَرَّقۡتَ بَيۡنَ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ وَلَمۡ تَرۡقُبۡ قَوۡلِي

When Moses grabbed his brother by his beard and head, dragging and scolding him for what he did, Aaron said to him, seeking his compassion: Do not hold my beard and the hair on my head, for I have a reason for staying with them. I feared that if I left them alone, they would have dispersed. Then you would have said I split them up and did not maintain your advice regarding them.

(٩٥) قَالَ فَمَا خَطۡبُكَ يَٰسَٰمِرِيُّ

Moses (peace be upon him) said to Samiri: What is the matter with you, O Samiri? What drove you to do what you did?

(٩٦) قَالَ بَصُرۡتُ بِمَا لَمۡ يَبۡصُرُواْ بِهِۦ فَقَبَضۡتُ قَبۡضَةٗ مِّنۡ أَثَرِ ٱلرَّسُولِ فَنَبَذۡتُهَا وَكَذَٰلِكَ سَوَّلَتۡ لِي نَفۡسِي

Samiri said to Moses (peace be upon him): I saw what they did not see: I saw Gabriel on a horse, so I took a handful of soil from the footsteps of his horse and threw it on melted jewelry, which was molded into the form of a calf. From this, the body of a calf with a mooing sound was formed. And this is how my soul made what I did appealing to me.

(٩٧) قَالَ فَٱذۡهَبۡ فَإِنَّ لَكَ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ أَن تَقُولَ لَا مِسَاسَۖ وَإِنَّ لَكَ مَوۡعِدٗا لَّن تُخۡلَفَهُۥۖ وَٱنظُرۡ إِلَىٰٓ إِلَٰهِكَ ٱلَّذِي ظَلۡتَ عَلَيۡهِ عَاكِفٗاۖ لَّنُحَرِّقَنَّهُۥ ثُمَّ لَنَنسِفَنَّهُۥ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ نَسۡفًا

Moses (peace be upon him) said to the Samiri: Go from here, for it is written for you that you will say as long as you live: I am not to touch or be touched. You will live as an outcast and have a promised time on the Day of Resurrection in which you will be taken to account and punished. Allah will never break this promise with you. And look at the calf, which you took as something to worship, and continued to worship besides Allah. We shall burn it in a fire until it melts, then we shall scatter it into the sea until no trace of it remains.

(٩٨) إِنَّمَآ إِلَٰهُكُمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَسِعَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ عِلۡمٗا

Your rightful object of worship, O people, is Allah. There is no rightful deity of worship besides Him. He has encompassed everything in His knowledge; only He knows everything, and nothing escapes His knowledge.

Points of Benefit:

Deceiving people by faking reality is from the ways of the people of misguidance.
When the prohibitions of Allah are violated, then anger is praiseworthy.
The verses contain the principle of condemning the people of innovation and disobedience, abandoning them, and not mixing with them.
The verses show the obligation of thinking about knowing Allah, the Exalted, through His effect on the universe.
