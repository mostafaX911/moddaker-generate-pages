(٢٠٣) ۞وَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ فِيٓ أَيَّامٖ مَّعۡدُودَٰتٖۚ فَمَن تَعَجَّلَ فِي يَوۡمَيۡنِ فَلَآ إِثۡمَ عَلَيۡهِ وَمَن تَأَخَّرَ فَلَآ إِثۡمَ عَلَيۡهِۖ لِمَنِ ٱتَّقَىٰۗ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّكُمۡ إِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ

Remember Allah by saying, 'Allahu Akbar' (Allah is Greatest!), and, 'Lā ilāha illā Allah' (There is no deity except Allah) on the 11th, 12th, and 13th of Dhul-Hijjah. If anyone is in a hurry, he may leave Minā after pelting stones on the 12th of Dhul-Hijjah – and there will be no sin as Allah has granted that permission. It is also acceptable if someone stays to pelt stones on the 13th of Dhul-Hijjah – this is better as it was the practice of the Prophet (peace be upon him). All of this applies to those who are mindful of Allah during their Hajj and carry it out following Allah's instruction. Be mindful of Allah by fulfilling His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. Realize that you will return to Him alone, and He will recompense you for your actions.

(٢٠٤) وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يُعۡجِبُكَ قَوۡلُهُۥ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَيُشۡهِدُ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ مَا فِي قَلۡبِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَلَدُّ ٱلۡخِصَامِ

There is a type of person who is a Hypocrite, but his speech concerning the life of this world impresses you, O Prophet, which may lead you to consider him to be sound of mind and even honest and sincere because of his words. However, he only means to protect himself and his financial interests. He calls Allah to witness that he has faith and goodness in his heart while he is, in fact, lying; he bears great hostility and hatred for the Muslims.

(٢٠٥) وَإِذَا تَوَلَّىٰ سَعَىٰ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لِيُفۡسِدَ فِيهَا وَيُهۡلِكَ ٱلۡحَرۡثَ وَٱلنَّسۡلَۚ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡفَسَادَ

When this Hypocrite leaves you, he works hard to cause corruption in the world by committing sins, and he destroys crops and kills animals. Allah does not love corruption in the world, and He does not love those that cause it.

(٢٠٦) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُ ٱتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ أَخَذَتۡهُ ٱلۡعِزَّةُ بِٱلۡإِثۡمِۚ فَحَسۡبُهُۥ جَهَنَّمُۖ وَلَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمِهَادُ

When this person who causes corruption is advised to be mindful of Allah by respecting His limits and avoiding His prohibitions, his pride and arrogance prevent him from repenting, and he continues to sin. The only appropriate reward for him is entry into Hell. What a terrible place of residence for its inhabitants!

(٢٠٧) وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَشۡرِي نَفۡسَهُ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ مَرۡضَاتِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱللَّهُ رَءُوفُۢ بِٱلۡعِبَادِ

There is another type of person who is a believer who will forfeit his soul to follow his Lord and to strive in His path, seeking His pleasure. Allah's mercy for His slaves is vast, and He is Kind to them.

(٢٠٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱدۡخُلُواْ فِي ٱلسِّلۡمِ كَآفَّةٗ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ خُطُوَٰتِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمۡ عَدُوّٞ مُّبِينٞ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, enter Islam completely and do not leave out any part of it as the People of the Scripture do by believing in some parts of the book and disbelieving in other parts. Do not follow the paths of the Devil because he has made it clear that he is your enemy.

(٢٠٩) فَإِن زَلَلۡتُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَتۡكُمُ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتُ فَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٌ

If you slip up or go astray after clear proofs have come to you in which there is no ambiguity, then know that Allah is Mighty in His ability and power, Wise in His planning and establishing of sacred laws. So, fear and glorify Him.

(٢١٠) هَلۡ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَأۡتِيَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ فِي ظُلَلٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡغَمَامِ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَقُضِيَ ٱلۡأَمۡرُۚ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ تُرۡجَعُ ٱلۡأُمُورُ

These people who follow the path of the Devil instead of the path of truth are only waiting for Allah to come to them, as befits His majesty, on the Day of Resurrection in the shadows of clouds to judge them and for the angels to come and surround them from all sides. At that time, Allah will judge them once and for all. To Allah alone shall all affairs relating to creation return.

Points of Benefit:

Being heedful cannot be truly achieved by plenty of deeds alone; it must be by following and adhering to the legislation's guidance.
People are judged not only by their appearances and statements but by the reality of their actions that show what their chests conceal.
Corrupting the Earth, in all its forms, is from the characteristics of the arrogant, and Allah does not love corruption and the people that do it.
A person has not truly submitted to Allah, the Exalted, until he submits completely to this Religion and accepts it openly and inwardly.
