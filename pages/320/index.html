(١١٤) فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡحَقُّۗ وَلَا تَعۡجَلۡ بِٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يُقۡضَىٰٓ إِلَيۡكَ وَحۡيُهُۥۖ وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدۡنِي عِلۡمٗا

Exalted, sanctified, and majestic be Allah. He is the King, to whom belongs the dominion of everything, Who is true and speaks the truth. Exalted is He from what the polytheists ascribe to Him. Do not hasten, O Messenger, in reciting the Quran with Gabriel before it is fully conveyed to you. Say: O Lord, increase my knowledge on top of what You have already taught me.

When Allah narrates the story of Moses, including Pharaoh's denial and the Israelites' neglectfulness, He cites the story of Adam (peace be upon him) as an example to encourage the heedless to return to Allah's obedience, stating:

(١١٥) وَلَقَدۡ عَهِدۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ ءَادَمَ مِن قَبۡلُ فَنَسِيَ وَلَمۡ نَجِدۡ لَهُۥ عَزۡمٗا

And We enjoined Adam beforehand not to eat of the tree, and We forbade him from this and explained to him its consequences. But he forgot the instruction and ended up eating from the tree and could not resist it. And We did not see in him the resolve to observe what We enjoined him with.

(١١٦) وَإِذۡ قُلۡنَا لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ ٱسۡجُدُواْ لِأٓدَمَ فَسَجَدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ أَبَىٰ

And remember, O Messenger, when I said to the angels: Prostrate to Adam as a greeting. All of them prostrated besides Iblis (Satan), who was with them but not from them. He refused to prostrate out of arrogance.

(١١٧) فَقُلۡنَا يَٰٓـَٔادَمُ إِنَّ هَٰذَا عَدُوّٞ لَّكَ وَلِزَوۡجِكَ فَلَا يُخۡرِجَنَّكُمَا مِنَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ فَتَشۡقَىٰٓ

So We said: O Adam, Iblis is your enemy and your wife's. Let him not take you and your wife out of Paradise by obeying him in what he whispers; otherwise, you will suffer difficulties.

(١١٨) إِنَّ لَكَ أَلَّا تَجُوعَ فِيهَا وَلَا تَعۡرَىٰ

It is a right that Allah has given you that He will feed you, so you will not get hungry, and clothe you, so you will not be unclothed.

(١١٩) وَأَنَّكَ لَا تَظۡمَؤُاْ فِيهَا وَلَا تَضۡحَىٰ

And to give you water so you do not get thirsty, and to shade you so that the sun's heat does not affect you.

(١٢٠) فَوَسۡوَسَ إِلَيۡهِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ قَالَ يَٰٓـَٔادَمُ هَلۡ أَدُلُّكَ عَلَىٰ شَجَرَةِ ٱلۡخُلۡدِ وَمُلۡكٖ لَّا يَبۡلَىٰ

But the Devil whispered to Adam: Shall I show you a tree that whoever eats from it will live forever and possess an everlasting kingdom that will never fall into decay or come to an end?

(١٢١) فَأَكَلَا مِنۡهَا فَبَدَتۡ لَهُمَا سَوۡءَٰتُهُمَا وَطَفِقَا يَخۡصِفَانِ عَلَيۡهِمَا مِن وَرَقِ ٱلۡجَنَّةِۚ وَعَصَىٰٓ ءَادَمُ رَبَّهُۥ فَغَوَىٰ

Then Adam and Eve ate from the forbidden tree. Their private areas that were previously covered became exposed, and they began to pluck the leaves of the trees of Paradise and cover themselves. Adam opposed the command of his Lord, as he did not comply with His order to prevent himself from eating from the tree. So, he transgressed the limit and did what was not permissible for him to do.

(١٢٢) ثُمَّ ٱجۡتَبَٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَتَابَ عَلَيۡهِ وَهَدَىٰ

Then Allah chose him, accepted his repentance, and gave him success to guidance.

(١٢٣) قَالَ ٱهۡبِطَا مِنۡهَا جَمِيعَۢاۖ بَعۡضُكُمۡ لِبَعۡضٍ عَدُوّٞۖ فَإِمَّا يَأۡتِيَنَّكُم مِّنِّي هُدٗى فَمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ هُدَايَ فَلَا يَضِلُّ وَلَا يَشۡقَىٰ

Allah said to Adam and Eve: Both of you and Iblis descend from Paradise because he is your enemy and you are his. If there comes to you from Me enlightenment of My path, then whoever amongst you follows it, acts accordingly, and does not divert from it, he will not go astray from the truth, nor will he suffer the misfortune of being punished. Instead, Allah will enter him into Paradise.

(١٢٤) وَمَنۡ أَعۡرَضَ عَن ذِكۡرِي فَإِنَّ لَهُۥ مَعِيشَةٗ ضَنكٗا وَنَحۡشُرُهُۥ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ أَعۡمَىٰ

And whoever turns away from My reminder and does not accept or respond to it shall have a constricted and miserable life in this world and in the Barzakh (the life between this world and the hereafter). I shall lead him towards the gathering place on the Day of Resurrection without eyesight or argument [to defend himself].

(١٢٥) قَالَ رَبِّ لِمَ حَشَرۡتَنِيٓ أَعۡمَىٰ وَقَدۡ كُنتُ بَصِيرٗا

This person will say: O my Lord, why did you gather me [being] blind today when I could see in the world?

Points of Benefit:

Etiquette when studying, and the one listening to knowledge should wait and be patient until the reader or teacher completes everything they are saying.
Adam forgot, and his offspring forget. He did not stay firm in his resolve, and they do not. He repented immediately, so Allah forgave him, and whoever resembles his father will not be wronged.
The virtue of repentance because Adam (peach be upon him) was better after repentance than he was before it.
The people of disbelief and misguidance will have a miserable life in this life, the barzakh (while in the grave), and the Hereafter.
