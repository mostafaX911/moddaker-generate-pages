(١٢٦) قَالَ كَذَٰلِكَ أَتَتۡكَ ءَايَٰتُنَا فَنَسِيتَهَاۖ وَكَذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ تُنسَىٰ

Allah refuted him by saying: This is like what you did in the world; when Our signs came to you, you turned away from them and abandoned them. Likewise, you will be abandoned today in torment.

(١٢٧) وَكَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي مَنۡ أَسۡرَفَ وَلَمۡ يُؤۡمِنۢ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِۦۚ وَلَعَذَابُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ أَشَدُّ وَأَبۡقَىٰٓ

And this is the kind of recompense We give to the one engrossed in unlawful desires and who turns away from believing in the clear proofs from his Lord. And the punishment of Allah in the Hereafter is even more horrible, severe, and lasting than the life they will have in this world and the Barzakh.

(١٢٨) أَفَلَمۡ يَهۡدِ لَهُمۡ كَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا قَبۡلَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡقُرُونِ يَمۡشُونَ فِي مَسَٰكِنِهِمۡۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلنُّهَىٰ

Is it not apparent to the polytheists how many nations We destroyed before them? They walk into the homes of these ruined nations and see the effects of what befell them. The destruction and demise in these many nations are lessons for people of intellect.

(١٢٩) وَلَوۡلَا كَلِمَةٞ سَبَقَتۡ مِن رَّبِّكَ لَكَانَ لِزَامٗا وَأَجَلٞ مُّسَمّٗى

O Messenger, had a word not preceded from your Lord that He will not punish anyone before establishing the proof against him, and had there not been a timespan that He determined for them, the punishment would have surely come to them quickly because they deserved it.

(١٣٠) فَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ قَبۡلَ طُلُوعِ ٱلشَّمۡسِ وَقَبۡلَ غُرُوبِهَاۖ وَمِنۡ ءَانَآيِٕ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَسَبِّحۡ وَأَطۡرَافَ ٱلنَّهَارِ لَعَلَّكَ تَرۡضَىٰ

So be patient, O Messenger, with the false things those who disbelieve in you say about you. Exalt and praise your Lord at Fajr time before sunrise; at 'Asr before sunset; in the hours of the night during Maghrib and 'Isha prayers; and at Dhuhr just after the zenith, which is the end of the first part of the day and the beginning of the second part; in the hope of attaining reward with Allah which you will be pleased with.

(١٣١) وَلَا تَمُدَّنَّ عَيۡنَيۡكَ إِلَىٰ مَا مَتَّعۡنَا بِهِۦٓ أَزۡوَٰجٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ زَهۡرَةَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لِنَفۡتِنَهُمۡ فِيهِۚ وَرِزۡقُ رَبِّكَ خَيۡرٞ وَأَبۡقَىٰ

And do not look on to what We made a delight for various groups of the rejectors to enjoy from the glitters of the worldly life so that We may test them. For indeed, what We have given them from that will disappear. And the reward of your Lord, which He has promised you so that you will be pleased, is better and more enduring than what He provided as comfort in the world from disappearing comforts, because it does not cease.

(١٣٢) وَأۡمُرۡ أَهۡلَكَ بِٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَٱصۡطَبِرۡ عَلَيۡهَاۖ لَا نَسۡ‍َٔلُكَ رِزۡقٗاۖ نَّحۡنُ نَرۡزُقُكَۗ وَٱلۡعَٰقِبَةُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰ

O Messenger, order your family to perform the prayer and remain committed to it yourself. We do not ask you for your sustenance or that of others; We will take care of your sustenance. The commendable outcome in this world and the Afterlife is for the pious who fear Allah, [those who] carry out His commands and refrain from His prohibitions.

(١٣٣) وَقَالُواْ لَوۡلَا يَأۡتِينَا بِ‍َٔايَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦٓۚ أَوَ لَمۡ تَأۡتِهِم بَيِّنَةُ مَا فِي ٱلصُّحُفِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ

These disbelievers who reject the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم said: Why does Muhammad not bring us a sign from his Lord which proves his truthfulness and that he is a Messenger? Has the Quran, which confirms the previous divine books, not come to these rejecters?

(١٣٤) وَلَوۡ أَنَّآ أَهۡلَكۡنَٰهُم بِعَذَابٖ مِّن قَبۡلِهِۦ لَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَا لَوۡلَآ أَرۡسَلۡتَ إِلَيۡنَا رَسُولٗا فَنَتَّبِعَ ءَايَٰتِكَ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن نَّذِلَّ وَنَخۡزَىٰ

And if We were to destroy these rejectors of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم by sending punishment on them due to their disbelief and obstinacy, before sending a messenger to them and revealing a book to them, they would say on the Day of Resurrection, as an excuse for their disbelief: O Lord, if only You had sent a messenger to us in the world, so that we could have believed in him and followed the verses that he brought before disgrace and humiliation befell us through Your torment.

(١٣٥) قُلۡ كُلّٞ مُّتَرَبِّصٞ فَتَرَبَّصُواْۖ فَسَتَعۡلَمُونَ مَنۡ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلصِّرَٰطِ ٱلسَّوِيِّ وَمَنِ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ

O Messenger, tell these rejectors: All of us await what Allah will carry out, so you, too, wait. You will most certainly come to know who the people of the straight path are and who are rightly guided: you or us?

Points of Benefit:

One of the ways that help one bear the harm of the rejectors is to use the virtuous times to glorify Allah's praises.
When the slave sees himself inclined and drawn towards the embellishments of this life, he should weigh up its fleeting embellishment with the everlasting bliss of the Hereafter.
The slave should establish Prayer as it should be established, and when something becomes difficult for him, he should pray, order his family to pray, and be patient with them, following the example of the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم.
The praiseworthy beautiful outcome is Paradise for the heedful people.
