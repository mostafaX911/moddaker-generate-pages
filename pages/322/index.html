The Prophets (Surah Al-Anbiya)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Establishing the mission and demonstrating the unity in the Prophets' objectives and Allah's care for them.

Explanation:

(١) ٱقۡتَرَبَ لِلنَّاسِ حِسَابُهُمۡ وَهُمۡ فِي غَفۡلَةٖ مُّعۡرِضُونَ

The time for humanity's reckoning of their deeds on the Day of Resurrection has drawn near. Still, they are turning away from the Hereafter in their heedlessness due to preoccupation with this world.

(٢) مَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن ذِكۡرٖ مِّن رَّبِّهِم مُّحۡدَثٍ إِلَّا ٱسۡتَمَعُوهُ وَهُمۡ يَلۡعَبُونَ

And no newly revealed part of the Quran comes to them except they listen to it without benefiting from it. Instead, they listen playfully, without caring about its contents.

(٣) لَاهِيَةٗ قُلُوبُهُمۡۗ وَأَسَرُّواْ ٱلنَّجۡوَى ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ هَلۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا بَشَرٞ مِّثۡلُكُمۡۖ أَفَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلسِّحۡرَ وَأَنتُمۡ تُبۡصِرُونَ

They listened to it while their hearts were heedless of it. Those who do wrong by disbelieving concealed their private conversation where they said: Is this person who claims to be a messenger but a man like you, without any distinction over you? What he has brought is magic. So will you follow him knowing that he is a man like you and what he has brought is magic?

(٤) قَالَ رَبِّي يَعۡلَمُ ٱلۡقَوۡلَ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

The Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم said: My Lord knows the conversation you concealed, for He knows everything said by anyone in the heavens and the earth. He is All-Hearing of His slaves' words and Omniscient of their deeds, and He will recompense His slaves for them.

(٥) بَلۡ قَالُوٓاْ أَضۡغَٰثُ أَحۡلَٰمِۢ بَلِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰهُ بَلۡ هُوَ شَاعِرٞ فَلۡيَأۡتِنَا بِ‍َٔايَةٖ كَمَآ أُرۡسِلَ ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ

Rather, they are confused regarding what Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم has brought. Sometimes, they would say: [It is] confused dreams that have no meaning. Other times, they said: No, in fact, he made it up without any basis. And sometimes they said: He is a poet. If he was truthful in his claim, he should bring us a miracle like the previous messengers, as they brought miracles like the staff of Moses and the she-camel of Saleh.

(٦) مَآ ءَامَنَتۡ قَبۡلَهُم مِّن قَرۡيَةٍ أَهۡلَكۡنَٰهَآۖ أَفَهُمۡ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Not a single town before these [polytheists] who are making requests [for miracles] ever requested divine signs to be sent down was then granted them as requested and believed. In fact, they denied them, so We destroyed them. So, will these people believe?

(٧) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا قَبۡلَكَ إِلَّا رِجَالٗا نُّوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡۖ فَسۡ‍َٔلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلذِّكۡرِ إِن كُنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

And We did not send before you, O Messenger Allah, except men from humanity to whom We would send revelation. We did not send them as angels, so ask the People of the Book before you if you do not know this.

(٨) وَمَا جَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ جَسَدٗا لَّا يَأۡكُلُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ وَمَا كَانُواْ خَٰلِدِينَ

And We did not make the messengers We sent as possessors of bodies who did not need food, nor were they immortal. They used to eat as others ate and were not to stay in this world forever without dying.

(٩) ثُمَّ صَدَقۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡوَعۡدَ فَأَنجَيۡنَٰهُمۡ وَمَن نَّشَآءُ وَأَهۡلَكۡنَا ٱلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ

Then We fulfilled what We promised Our messengers, as We saved them and whoever We wished among the believers from destruction. And We destroyed those who transgressed the limits through disbelieving in Allah and committing sin.

(١٠) لَقَدۡ أَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكُمۡ كِتَٰبٗا فِيهِ ذِكۡرُكُمۡۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

We have revealed to you the Quran in which lies your honor and pride if you believe in and act according to its contents. Will you not understand this so that you hasten towards believing in it and practicing what it contains?

Points of Benefit:

The nearness of the Day of Judgment necessitates preparation for it.
Preoccupation with frivolities diverts hearts from the truth.
Allah's knowledge encompasses everything that emanates from His servants, whether in speech or action.
The disagreement among the polytheists regarding their stance towards the Prophet (peace be upon him) indicates their confusion and disturbance.
That Allah is with His messengers and the believers, providing them support and assistance against their enemies.
The Quran is an honor and glory for those who believe in it and act upon it.
