(١١) وَكَمۡ قَصَمۡنَا مِن قَرۡيَةٖ كَانَتۡ ظَالِمَةٗ وَأَنشَأۡنَا بَعۡدَهَا قَوۡمًا ءَاخَرِينَ

There are many towns which We destroyed due to their wrongdoing by disbelieving [in Allah]! Then, We created other people after them.

(١٢) فَلَمَّآ أَحَسُّواْ بَأۡسَنَآ إِذَا هُم مِّنۡهَا يَرۡكُضُونَ

Then, when those who were destroyed saw Our annihilating punishment, they began to run from their towns to flee the destruction.

(١٣) لَا تَرۡكُضُواْ وَٱرۡجِعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَآ أُتۡرِفۡتُمۡ فِيهِ وَمَسَٰكِنِكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُسۡ‍َٔلُونَ

They were asked mockingly: Do not flee, but return to the luxuries you enjoyed and your homes so that you can be questioned a little regarding your worldly possessions.

(١٤) قَالُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَآ إِنَّا كُنَّا ظَٰلِمِينَ

These wrongdoers confessing their sins said: Woe to us, and may we be destroyed. We were wrongdoers due to our disbelief in Allah.

(١٥) فَمَا زَالَت تِّلۡكَ دَعۡوَىٰهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ جَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ حَصِيدًا خَٰمِدِينَ

So they continued confessing to their sins and praying for their own destruction until We made them like harvested crops, dead and lifeless.

(١٦) وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا لَٰعِبِينَ

And We did not create the heavens and the Earth, and whatever is in between in vain. Instead, We created them to prove Our power.

(١٧) لَوۡ أَرَدۡنَآ أَن نَّتَّخِذَ لَهۡوٗا لَّٱتَّخَذۡنَٰهُ مِن لَّدُنَّآ إِن كُنَّا فَٰعِلِينَ

If We wanted to take a partner or child, We would have done so on Our part. But, We did not do this as We are free from this.

(١٨) بَلۡ نَقۡذِفُ بِٱلۡحَقِّ عَلَى ٱلۡبَٰطِلِ فَيَدۡمَغُهُۥ فَإِذَا هُوَ زَاهِقٞۚ وَلَكُمُ ٱلۡوَيۡلُ مِمَّا تَصِفُونَ

Instead, We throw the truth which We reveal to Our messengers at the falsehood of the disbelievers, and [the truth] destroys it, so their falsehood goes and disappears. And for you, O you who say Allah has taken a wife or son, is destruction, due to you describing Him with that which does not befit Him.

After mentioning how the taking of a spouse or child indicates insufficiency, Allah, the Exalted and Transcendent, explains that He is the Owner of this universe, stating:

(١٩) وَلَهُۥ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَمَنۡ عِندَهُۥ لَا يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ عَنۡ عِبَادَتِهِۦ وَلَا يَسۡتَحۡسِرُونَ

And to Him alone belongs the dominion of the heavens and Earth. And the angels with Him are neither too arrogant to worship Him, nor do they tire.

(٢٠) يُسَبِّحُونَ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ لَا يَفۡتُرُونَ

They constantly exalt Allah and are not bored of doing so.

(٢١) أَمِ ٱتَّخَذُوٓاْ ءَالِهَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ هُمۡ يُنشِرُونَ

But, the polytheists took deities besides Allah, which cannot revive the dead, so how do they worship something unable to do this?

(٢٢) لَوۡ كَانَ فِيهِمَآ ءَالِهَةٌ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ لَفَسَدَتَاۚ فَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ

If there were numerous deities in the heavens and the Earth, they would have been ruined due to the deities disputing their sovereignty. But the reality is not like this. Allah, Lord of the Throne, is free from the lie the polytheists describe Him with, namely that He has partners.

(٢٣) لَا يُسۡ‍َٔلُ عَمَّا يَفۡعَلُ وَهُمۡ يُسۡ‍َٔلُونَ

And Allah is unique in His dominion and decree. Nobody can question Him regarding what He has decreed and ordered, while He will ask His slaves about their deeds and recompense them accordingly.

(٢٤) أَمِ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ ءَالِهَةٗۖ قُلۡ هَاتُواْ بُرۡهَٰنَكُمۡۖ هَٰذَا ذِكۡرُ مَن مَّعِيَ وَذِكۡرُ مَن قَبۡلِيۚ بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ ٱلۡحَقَّۖ فَهُم مُّعۡرِضُونَ

But they took other deities besides Allah. O Messenger, tell these polytheists: Bring your proof that these deities are worthy of worship. This book which has been revealed to me and the books revealed to the [previous] messengers contain no proof of that. Instead, most polytheists only rely on ignorance and blind following. They turn away from accepting the truth.

Points of Benefit:

Oppression is a cause of destruction at the individual and collective levels.
Allah did not create anything in vain, for He is gloriously free from any act of purposelessness.
The triumph of truth and the defeat of falsehood is an established divine practice.
Invalidating the belief in polytheism with the evidence of the righteousness and orderliness in this universe, which lacks any defect, flaw, opposition, or contradiction. This indicates that there is one Creator, one Lord, and one God, the Transcendent and Exalted.
