(٢٥) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ مِن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا نُوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنَا۠ فَٱعۡبُدُونِ

And We have not sent any messenger before you, O Messenger, except that We have revealed to him that there is no true deity except Me, so, worship Me alone and do not associate any partner with Me.

(٢٦) وَقَالُواْ ٱتَّخَذَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وَلَدٗاۗ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥۚ بَلۡ عِبَادٞ مُّكۡرَمُونَ

The polytheists said that Allah has taken the angels as daughters. He (may He be Exalted) is free and sanctified from the lie they tell. The angels are the slaves of Allah, honored by Him and close to Him.

(٢٧) لَا يَسۡبِقُونَهُۥ بِٱلۡقَوۡلِ وَهُم بِأَمۡرِهِۦ يَعۡمَلُونَ

They do not precede their Lord in speech, so they do not speak until He orders them, and they act according to His command and do not oppose Him in any command.

(٢٨) يَعۡلَمُ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُمۡ وَلَا يَشۡفَعُونَ إِلَّا لِمَنِ ٱرۡتَضَىٰ وَهُم مِّنۡ خَشۡيَتِهِۦ مُشۡفِقُونَ

He knows their past and future deeds. They do not ask to intercede except with His permission for those He approves intercession. They are wary out of their fear of Him, the Exalted. Thus, they do not oppose Him in any command or prohibition.

(٢٩) ۞وَمَن يَقُلۡ مِنۡهُمۡ إِنِّيٓ إِلَٰهٞ مِّن دُونِهِۦ فَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِيهِ جَهَنَّمَۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Hypothetically, if any of the angels were to say that he is worthy of worship instead of Allah, We would punish him for saying this with the torment of Hell on the Day of Resurrection. He will remain therein forever. And with this torment, We punish those who do wrong by disbelieving and associating partners with Allah.

(٣٠) أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ كَانَتَا رَتۡقٗا فَفَتَقۡنَٰهُمَاۖ وَجَعَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلۡمَآءِ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ حَيٍّۚ أَفَلَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Do those who disbelieved in Allah not know that the heavens and the Earth were joined without any gap between them, such that no rain could come down from the sky, then We split them apart? And We made every animal and plant from the water that comes down from the sky. Do they not consider this and believe in Allah alone?

(٣١) وَجَعَلۡنَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَوَٰسِيَ أَن تَمِيدَ بِهِمۡ وَجَعَلۡنَا فِيهَا فِجَاجٗا سُبُلٗا لَّعَلَّهُمۡ يَهۡتَدُونَ

And We created firm mountains on the Earth so that it does not shake those on its surface. We have made wide paths and ways through it so that they may find their way in their journeys towards their destinations.

(٣٢) وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ سَقۡفٗا مَّحۡفُوظٗاۖ وَهُمۡ عَنۡ ءَايَٰتِهَا مُعۡرِضُونَ

And We made the sky a roof protected from falling without any pillars and protected from eavesdropping. But the polytheists turn away and do not reflect over the signs in the sky, such as the sun and moon.

(٣٣) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ وَٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَۖ كُلّٞ فِي فَلَكٖ يَسۡبَحُونَ

Allah alone is the One who created the night for relaxation, the day for earning one's livelihood, the sun as a sign of the day, and the moon as a sign of night. The sun and moon run in specific orbits without moving away or deviating.

(٣٤) وَمَا جَعَلۡنَا لِبَشَرٖ مِّن قَبۡلِكَ ٱلۡخُلۡدَۖ أَفَإِيْن مِّتَّ فَهُمُ ٱلۡخَٰلِدُونَ

O Messenger, We have not given immortality in this life to any human before you. So, if your term ends in this life and you pass away, will they live forever after you? Never.

(٣٥) كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ ذَآئِقَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۗ وَنَبۡلُوكُم بِٱلشَّرِّ وَٱلۡخَيۡرِ فِتۡنَةٗۖ وَإِلَيۡنَا تُرۡجَعُونَ

Every believing and disbelieving soul will taste death in this world. And We will test you, O people, in this worldly life through duties, favors, and difficulties. Then, after your death, you shall be returned to Us alone and nobody else. Then, We shall recompense you for your deeds accordingly.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's transcendence from having a son.
The status of the angels in the sight of Allah is that they are His servants created for His obedience. They are not characterized by masculinity or femininity but are honored servants.
The heavens and the Earth were created according to the divine pattern of gradual development; they were initially created closely joined and then later separated.
Trials, just as they can come in the form of adversity, can also come in the form of goodness.
