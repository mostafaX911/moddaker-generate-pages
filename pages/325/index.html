(٣٦) وَإِذَا رَءَاكَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِن يَتَّخِذُونَكَ إِلَّا هُزُوًا أَهَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي يَذۡكُرُ ءَالِهَتَكُمۡ وَهُم بِذِكۡرِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ هُمۡ كَٰفِرُونَ

O Messenger, when these polytheists see you, they only mock you, making [you] repulsive to their followers by saying: Is this the one who insults the idols you worship? At the same time, they reject the Quran that Allah revealed to them and the favors He favored them with, together with mocking you. So, they are more entitled to be criticized due to their accumulating every evil.

(٣٧) خُلِقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مِنۡ عَجَلٖۚ سَأُوْرِيكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِي فَلَا تَسۡتَعۡجِلُونِ

Man was created intrinsically to be hasty, so he wants things to happen before their due time. The polytheists asking for torment is a result of that nature. O you who are seeking My torment in haste! I shall show you precisely what you hastily seek, so there is no need to ask for it.

(٣٨) وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

The disbelievers who reject resurrection say in haste: O Muslims! If you are truthful in what you claim, when will this resurrection happen that you promise us?

(٣٩) لَوۡ يَعۡلَمُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ حِينَ لَا يَكُفُّونَ عَن وُجُوهِهِمُ ٱلنَّارَ وَلَا عَن ظُهُورِهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ

If only these disbelieving rejecters of the Resurrection were to know of the time when they will not be able to repel the Fire away from their faces and backs, and that there will be no one to help relieve them of the torment, and were convinced of that, they would not be so hasty.

(٤٠) بَلۡ تَأۡتِيهِم بَغۡتَةٗ فَتَبۡهَتُهُمۡ فَلَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ رَدَّهَا وَلَا هُمۡ يُنظَرُونَ

This Fire they shall be punished with will come upon them suddenly, without them anticipating it. They will not be able to repel it or be given respite to repent and be granted mercy.

When Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم suffered from his people's mockery of him and their disbelief in him, Allah consoled him with His words:

(٤١) وَلَقَدِ ٱسۡتُهۡزِئَ بِرُسُلٖ مِّن قَبۡلِكَ فَحَاقَ بِٱلَّذِينَ سَخِرُواْ مِنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

O Messenger, even if your people mock you, you are not unique because many messengers before you were also mocked. After their messengers warned them of it, the very torment the disbelievers made a mockery of engulfed them in this world.

(٤٢) قُلۡ مَن يَكۡلَؤُكُم بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ مِنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ عَن ذِكۡرِ رَبِّهِم مُّعۡرِضُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to these people hastily seeking the torment: Who will protect you through the night and day from the torment and destruction The Merciful intends to send upon you? Instead, they are turning away from the admonition and proofs of their Lord. They foolishly and ignorantly do not ponder over any of it.

(٤٣) أَمۡ لَهُمۡ ءَالِهَةٞ تَمۡنَعُهُم مِّن دُونِنَاۚ لَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ نَصۡرَ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَلَا هُم مِّنَّا يُصۡحَبُونَ

Do they have deities that will protect them from Our torment? They are not capable of helping even themselves by removing any harm or acquiring any benefit! Whoever cannot help himself, how can he help others? Nor will they be protected from Our torment.

(٤٤) بَلۡ مَتَّعۡنَا هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ وَءَابَآءَهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ طَالَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡعُمُرُۗ أَفَلَا يَرَوۡنَ أَنَّا نَأۡتِي ٱلۡأَرۡضَ نَنقُصُهَا مِنۡ أَطۡرَافِهَآۚ أَفَهُمُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبُونَ

Instead, We have allowed these disbelievers and their forefathers to enjoy all Our favors with which We favored them. As time passes, they become deceived by these favors and remain firm in their disbelief, gradually leading themselves to destruction. Do these people, deceived by Our favors and hastily seeking Our torment, not see that We are reducing the land down from its borders by Our subdual of its inhabitants and conquering them? Do they not take lessons from that, lest the same not happen to them? These people will not be victorious; instead, they will be defeated.

Points of Benefit:

Clarification of the disbelief of those who mock the Prophet, whether through speech, action, or indication.
Haste is a trait inherent in humans, while deliberation is a virtuous character.
None can be saved from the punishment of Allah except by Allah.
The destiny of falsehood is disappearance, and the destiny of truth is permanence.
