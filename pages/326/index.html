(٤٥) قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أُنذِرُكُم بِٱلۡوَحۡيِۚ وَلَا يَسۡمَعُ ٱلصُّمُّ ٱلدُّعَآءَ إِذَا مَا يُنذَرُونَ

Say, O Messenger: O people, I only warn you of Allah's torment through divine revelation that my Lord reveals to me. When those deaf to the truth are warned of Allah's torment, they do not listen to it [with the intention to] accept.

(٤٦) وَلَئِن مَّسَّتۡهُمۡ نَفۡحَةٞ مِّنۡ عَذَابِ رَبِّكَ لَيَقُولُنَّ يَٰوَيۡلَنَآ إِنَّا كُنَّا ظَٰلِمِينَ

O Messenger, if some of your Lord's torment were to afflict these people seeking it hastily, they would say: Woe to us may we be destroyed! We were wrong in ascribing partners to Allah and rejecting what Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought to us!

(٤٧) وَنَضَعُ ٱلۡمَوَٰزِينَ ٱلۡقِسۡطَ لِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فَلَا تُظۡلَمُ نَفۡسٞ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۖ وَإِن كَانَ مِثۡقَالَ حَبَّةٖ مِّنۡ خَرۡدَلٍ أَتَيۡنَا بِهَاۗ وَكَفَىٰ بِنَا حَٰسِبِينَ

We will set up the scales of justice for the people [on the Day] of Resurrection to weigh their deeds. On that Day, no soul will be wronged by having its good actions decreased, or evil actions increased. Even if the weighed action is minute, like the weight equal to a mustard seed, We shall include it. And sufficient are We as a Reckoner.

(٤٨) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَ ٱلۡفُرۡقَانَ وَضِيَآءٗ وَذِكۡرٗا لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ

We gave Moses and Aaron (peace be upon them) the Torah, which distinguished between the lawful and the unlawful; it was a means of guidance for those who believed in it and a reminder for those who feared their Lord.

(٤٩) ٱلَّذِينَ يَخۡشَوۡنَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَهُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّاعَةِ مُشۡفِقُونَ

Those who fear their Lord's punishment, Who they believe in, despite having not seen Him, and are fearful of the Hour.

(٥٠) وَهَٰذَا ذِكۡرٞ مُّبَارَكٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُۚ أَفَأَنتُمۡ لَهُۥ مُنكِرُونَ

This Quran, revealed to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, is a reminder and an admonition for those who want to heed it. It contains an abundance of benefits and goodness. Despite all that, do you still reject it? Do you not acknowledge its contents, and will you not practice it?

(٥١) ۞وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ رُشۡدَهُۥ مِن قَبۡلُ وَكُنَّا بِهِۦ عَٰلِمِينَ

And We gave Abraham, in his childhood, proof against his people, and We knew him well. We gave him the proof he deserved according to Our knowledge.

(٥٢) إِذۡ قَالَ لِأَبِيهِ وَقَوۡمِهِۦ مَا هَٰذِهِ ٱلتَّمَاثِيلُ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنتُمۡ لَهَا عَٰكِفُونَ

Remember when he told his father Azar and his people: What are these idols that you have made with your own hands and which you constantly worship?

(٥٣) قَالُواْ وَجَدۡنَآ ءَابَآءَنَا لَهَا عَٰبِدِينَ

His people told him: We found our forefathers worshipping them, so we also worship them following their example.

(٥٤) قَالَ لَقَدۡ كُنتُمۡ أَنتُمۡ وَءَابَآؤُكُمۡ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ

Abraham said to them: O you followers, you and your forefathers who are followed are in clear deviance from the path of truth!

(٥٥) قَالُوٓاْ أَجِئۡتَنَا بِٱلۡحَقِّ أَمۡ أَنتَ مِنَ ٱللَّٰعِبِينَ

His people asked him: Are you serious about what you said, or are you joking?

(٥٦) قَالَ بَل رَّبُّكُمۡ رَبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَهُنَّ وَأَنَا۠ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكُم مِّنَ ٱلشَّٰهِدِينَ

Abraham said: No, I am serious! Your Lord is the Lord of the heavens and the Earth. He created them without precedent. I am from those who testify that He is your Lord and the Lord of the heavens and the Earth and that your idols have no share in that!

(٥٧) وَتَٱللَّهِ لَأَكِيدَنَّ أَصۡنَٰمَكُم بَعۡدَ أَن تُوَلُّواْ مُدۡبِرِينَ

And Abraham said, in a manner inaudible to his people: By Allah! Once you have left them (the idols) to go to your festival, I will surely plot against them [in a way that] you will hate.

Points of Benefit:

The benefit of confessing to a sin is contingent upon accompanying it with repentance before its opportunity passes.
Affirming Allah's Justice and absolving Him of any injustice.
The importance of the strength of the argument in inviting to Allah.
The harm of blind following.
Gradual change in addressing wrongdoing, starting with the easiest and most accessible, as exemplified by Abraham, who began by verbally opposing the wrongdoing of his people and presenting logical arguments, then progressed to practical actions.
