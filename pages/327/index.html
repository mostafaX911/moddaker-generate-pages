(٥٨) فَجَعَلَهُمۡ جُذَٰذًا إِلَّا كَبِيرٗا لَّهُمۡ لَعَلَّهُمۡ إِلَيۡهِ يَرۡجِعُونَ

So Abraham smashed their idols into small pieces and left the largest idol, hoping the people would return to it to ask who smashed the others.

(٥٩) قَالُواْ مَن فَعَلَ هَٰذَا بِ‍َٔالِهَتِنَآ إِنَّهُۥ لَمِنَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

So, when they returned and found their idols smashed into pieces, they asked one another: Who smashed our idols? Indeed, the one who has smashed them is from the wrongdoers because he has degraded that which deserves respect and reverence.

(٦٠) قَالُواْ سَمِعۡنَا فَتٗى يَذۡكُرُهُمۡ يُقَالُ لَهُۥٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمُ

Some said: We heard a young man speaking badly about them and finding fault with them. He is called Abraham; perhaps he smashed them.

(٦١) قَالُواْ فَأۡتُواْ بِهِۦ عَلَىٰٓ أَعۡيُنِ ٱلنَّاسِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَشۡهَدُونَ

Their chiefs said: Bring Abraham in front of the people so they may witness his confession to what he did, and his confession will be your evidence against him.

(٦٢) قَالُوٓاْ ءَأَنتَ فَعَلۡتَ هَٰذَا بِ‍َٔالِهَتِنَا يَٰٓإِبۡرَٰهِيمُ

So they brought Abraham (peace be upon him) and questioned him, blaming him: Was it you who did this heinous act to our idols, O Abraham?!

(٦٣) قَالَ بَلۡ فَعَلَهُۥ كَبِيرُهُمۡ هَٰذَا فَسۡ‍َٔلُوهُمۡ إِن كَانُواْ يَنطِقُونَ

Abraham sarcastically exposed the idols' inability to the people, saying: I did not do it; rather, the biggest idol did. Ask your idols if, indeed, they are capable of speaking!

(٦٤) فَرَجَعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ فَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّكُمۡ أَنتُمُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

So they turned to one another, contemplating and considering. It then became clear to them that their idols do not benefit or harm in any way, so they were wrong in worshipping them instead of Allah.

(٦٥) ثُمَّ نُكِسُواْ عَلَىٰ رُءُوسِهِمۡ لَقَدۡ عَلِمۡتَ مَا هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ يَنطِقُونَ

They then returned to their stubbornness and denial, saying: O Abraham! Surely, you know these idols cannot speak, so how can you tell us to ask them? They intended it to be evidence in their favor, but it was evidence against them.

(٦٦) قَالَ أَفَتَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا يَنفَعُكُمۡ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا يَضُرُّكُمۡ

Abraham rebuked them: Do you then worship, instead of Allah, idols that do not benefit nor harm you; and are unable to remove harm or bring benefit to even themselves?

(٦٧) أُفّٖ لَّكُمۡ وَلِمَا تَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

Shame on you, and shame on these idols you worship instead of Allah, which neither benefit nor harm! Do you not understand that? Will you not abandon their worship?

(٦٨) قَالُواْ حَرِّقُوهُ وَٱنصُرُوٓاْ ءَالِهَتَكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ فَٰعِلِينَ

They resorted to force when they found themselves incapable of countering Abraham with proof. They said: If you are going to give Abraham a deterring punishment and seek revenge for your idols that he broke and destroyed, then burn him in a fire.

(٦٩) قُلۡنَا يَٰنَارُ كُونِي بَرۡدٗا وَسَلَٰمًا عَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ

They lit a fire and threw Abraham into it, so We said: O fire! Become cool and peaceful for Abraham! And so it became such, and no harm came to him.

(٧٠) وَأَرَادُواْ بِهِۦ كَيۡدٗا فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡأَخۡسَرِينَ

Abraham's (peace be upon him) people planned to burn him, and We foiled their plan. Instead, We made them the ones who were destroyed and defeated.

(٧١) وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَلُوطًا إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلَّتِي بَٰرَكۡنَا فِيهَا لِلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

We saved Abraham and Lot and relocated them to the Levant that We had blessed by having sent many prophets to it and by scattering therein many forms of goodness for the creation.

(٧٢) وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥٓ إِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَ نَافِلَةٗۖ وَكُلّٗا جَعَلۡنَا صَٰلِحِينَ

And We gifted Abraham with Isaac when he supplicated to his Lord to grant him a child. In addition, We also blessed him with Jacob. We made each of the three (Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob) pious and obedient to Allah.

Points of Benefit:

The permissibility of using clever tactics to manifest the truth and refute falsehood.
The followers of falsehood cling to arguments they think support them while those arguments are against them.
Verbal rebuke as a means of addressing wrongdoing is effective if it does not result in greater harm.
Resorting to the use of force is evidence of an inability to confront the argument.
Allah's support for His believing slaves and His rescue of them from trials in ways they do not anticipate.
