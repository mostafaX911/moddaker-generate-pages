(٧٣) وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ أَئِمَّةٗ يَهۡدُونَ بِأَمۡرِنَا وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِمۡ فِعۡلَ ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ وَإِقَامَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَإِيتَآءَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةِۖ وَكَانُواْ لَنَا عَٰبِدِينَ

We made them into leaders whom the people followed in goodness. They would invite people to worship Allah alone with His, the Exalted's, permission. We also revealed to them: Do good actions, perform prayer perfectly, and give Zakat. They were obedient to Us.

(٧٤) وَلُوطًا ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ حُكۡمٗا وَعِلۡمٗا وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِ ٱلَّتِي كَانَت تَّعۡمَلُ ٱلۡخَبَٰٓئِثَۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمَ سَوۡءٖ فَٰسِقِينَ

And to Lot, We granted the ability to judge fairly between disputing parties, together with sound knowledge of his religion. We also saved him from the torment We sent upon the people of his town (Sodom) who were involved in a shameful act. Indeed, they were corrupt people who abandoned the worship of their Lord.

(٧٥) وَأَدۡخَلۡنَٰهُ فِي رَحۡمَتِنَآۖ إِنَّهُۥ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

And We admitted him into Our Mercy when We saved him from the torment that befell his people. Indeed, he was of those who obeyed Our commands and avoided Our prohibitions.

(٧٦) وَنُوحًا إِذۡ نَادَىٰ مِن قَبۡلُ فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ فَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَأَهۡلَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلۡكَرۡبِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

O Messenger, remember the story of Noah when he called upon Allah before the time of Abraham and Lot. So We responded to him by granting him what he asked for. We saved him and his believing family members from great distress.

(٧٧) وَنَصَرۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمَ سَوۡءٖ فَأَغۡرَقۡنَٰهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

We saved him from the plot of those of his people who rejected the signs that We supported him with, those signs that pointed to his truthfulness. Indeed, they were evil, corrupt people, so We destroyed them all by drowning them.

(٧٨) وَدَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيۡمَٰنَ إِذۡ يَحۡكُمَانِ فِي ٱلۡحَرۡثِ إِذۡ نَفَشَتۡ فِيهِ غَنَمُ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ وَكُنَّا لِحُكۡمِهِمۡ شَٰهِدِينَ

O Messenger, remember the story of David and his son Solomon (peace be upon them) when they were passing judgment in the case brought to them regarding the two people who disputed. One of them had a herd of sheep that went into the field of the other at night and ruined the crops. We witnessed the judgment of David and Solomon; no part of their judgment evaded Our knowledge.

(٧٩) فَفَهَّمۡنَٰهَا سُلَيۡمَٰنَۚ وَكُلًّا ءَاتَيۡنَا حُكۡمٗا وَعِلۡمٗاۚ وَسَخَّرۡنَا مَعَ دَاوُۥدَ ٱلۡجِبَالَ يُسَبِّحۡنَ وَٱلطَّيۡرَۚ وَكُنَّا فَٰعِلِينَ

We gave Solomon the complete understanding of the case but not his father David, yet We granted each one with messengership and knowledge of the sacred law, not exclusively Solomon only. We subjugated the mountains for David, which would exalt [Us] along with him. We also subjugated the birds for him. We were the One Who gave the understanding, the ability to judge fairly, knowledge, and subjugation [of the mountains and birds].

(٨٠) وَعَلَّمۡنَٰهُ صَنۡعَةَ لَبُوسٖ لَّكُمۡ لِتُحۡصِنَكُم مِّنۢ بَأۡسِكُمۡۖ فَهَلۡ أَنتُمۡ شَٰكِرُونَ

We exclusively taught David, and not Solomon, the art of making body armor to protect you from anyone who tries to attack you with weapons. O people, will you show gratitude for this favor We [Allah] favored you with?

(٨١) وَلِسُلَيۡمَٰنَ ٱلرِّيحَ عَاصِفَةٗ تَجۡرِي بِأَمۡرِهِۦٓ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلَّتِي بَٰرَكۡنَا فِيهَاۚ وَكُنَّا بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَٰلِمِينَ

And We subjugated the raging wind for Solomon. At his command, whenever he would command it to, it would blow to the Levant, which We had blessed by sending prophets and the goodness We showered within it. We know everything; nothing is hidden from Us.

Points of Benefit:

Practicing goodness, performing prayers, and giving charity, as agreed upon by the divine legislations.
Committing obscenities is a cause for the occurrence of utter punishment.
Righteousness is a cause for entering into the mercy of Allah.
Supplication is a means of salvation from distress.
