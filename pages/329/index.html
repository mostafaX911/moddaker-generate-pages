(٨٢) وَمِنَ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِ مَن يَغُوصُونَ لَهُۥ وَيَعۡمَلُونَ عَمَلٗا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَۖ وَكُنَّا لَهُمۡ حَٰفِظِينَ

We subjugated some devils who would dive into the oceans for him and extract pearls, and do other things like construction. We supervised their numbers and work; nothing escapes Us.

(٨٣) ۞وَأَيُّوبَ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّي مَسَّنِيَ ٱلضُّرُّ وَأَنتَ أَرۡحَمُ ٱلرَّٰحِمِينَ

O Messenger, remember the story of Job (peace be upon him), when he supplicated to his Lord, the Exalted, at the time of affliction, saying: O my Lord! I have been afflicted with an illness and loss of family, yet You are the Most Merciful of all those who show mercy, so remove the afflictions that have befallen me.

(٨٤) فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ فَكَشَفۡنَا مَا بِهِۦ مِن ضُرّٖۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ أَهۡلَهُۥ وَمِثۡلَهُم مَّعَهُمۡ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنۡ عِندِنَا وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِلۡعَٰبِدِينَ

And so We responded to his supplication and removed the harm that had befallen him. We granted him the family he had lost and more along with them. We did all that out of Our Mercy so that it serves as a reminder for everyone who is submissive in worshipping Allah so that they may exercise patience just as Job did.

(٨٥) وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلَ وَإِدۡرِيسَ وَذَا ٱلۡكِفۡلِۖ كُلّٞ مِّنَ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

O Messenger, remember Ishmael, Idris, and Dhul-Kifl (peace be upon them). Each of them was from the patient ones during times of affliction and [patient] in carrying out whatever responsibility Allah placed on them.

(٨٦) وَأَدۡخَلۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي رَحۡمَتِنَآۖ إِنَّهُم مِّنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

We admitted them into Our mercy, made them prophets, and admitted them into Paradise. Indeed, they were among the pious slaves of Allah who acted on Allah's obedience and whose private and public lives were ones of piety.

(٨٧) وَذَا ٱلنُّونِ إِذ ذَّهَبَ مُغَٰضِبٗا فَظَنَّ أَن لَّن نَّقۡدِرَ عَلَيۡهِ فَنَادَىٰ فِي ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ أَن لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنتَ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Remember, O Messenger, the story of the companion of the whale, Jonah (peace be upon him), when he left [his people] without His Lord's permission, angry with his people for their insistence on sin. He thought We would not constrain him by punishing him for leaving, but then he was afflicted with a severe form of constraint and imprisonment when the whale swallowed him. He then supplicated in the veils of the darkness of the whale's belly, the ocean, and the night, acknowledging his sin and repenting to Allah for it, saying: None is worthy of worship except You. You are pure and sanctified. Indeed, I am one of the wrongdoers.

(٨٨) فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡغَمِّۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُ‍ۨجِي ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

So, We responded to his supplication and saved him from severe distress by removing him from the veils of darkness and the belly of the whale. As We saved Jonah from this distress, We will save the believers when they fall into distress and supplicate to Allah.

(٨٩) وَزَكَرِيَّآ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥ رَبِّ لَا تَذَرۡنِي فَرۡدٗا وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثِينَ

Also, remember, O Messenger, the story of Zechariah (peace be upon him), when he supplicated to his Lord, saying: O my Lord! Do not leave me alone, childless. You are the best of those who remain, so grant me a child that remains after me.

(٩٠) فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥ يَحۡيَىٰ وَأَصۡلَحۡنَا لَهُۥ زَوۡجَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ وَيَدۡعُونَنَا رَغَبٗا وَرَهَبٗاۖ وَكَانُواْ لَنَا خَٰشِعِينَ

We responded to his supplication and granted him a child, John. [In the process], We also cured his wife, and she became fertile after she had been barren up until that point. Indeed, Zechariah, his wife, and their son were quick to do righteous deeds and would supplicate to Us, eager for Our reward and fearful of Our punishment, totally humbling themselves before Us.

Points of Benefit:

Righteousness is a cause for mercy.
Turning to Allah is a means to dispel distress.
The virtue of seeking righteous offspring to carry on one's legacy after death.
The acknowledgment of one's sins, the feeling of necessity towards Allah, complaining about one's condition to Him, and obedience to Allah in times of prosperity are among the causes for the acceptance of supplications and the alleviation of distress.
