(٩١) وَٱلَّتِيٓ أَحۡصَنَتۡ فَرۡجَهَا فَنَفَخۡنَا فِيهَا مِن رُّوحِنَا وَجَعَلۡنَٰهَا وَٱبۡنَهَآ ءَايَةٗ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Also, remember O Messenger, the story of Mary (peace be upon her), who guarded her private part against fornication. Allah sent Gabriel (peace be upon him) to her, and he blew into her. Consequently, she became pregnant with Jesus (peace be upon him). They were both signs for the people of Allah's Power and that nothing can render Allah incapable, as He created Jesus without a father.

(٩٢) إِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦٓ أُمَّتُكُمۡ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ وَأَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمۡ فَٱعۡبُدُونِ

O people, this is your creed, the only creed: monotheism, the religion of Islam. I am your Lord, so only worship Me.

(٩٣) وَتَقَطَّعُوٓاْ أَمۡرَهُم بَيۡنَهُمۡۖ كُلٌّ إِلَيۡنَا رَٰجِعُونَ

The people differed: some were monotheists, others were polytheists, some were disbelievers, and others were believers. All these different types of people will return to Us alone on the Day of Resurrection, and We will recompense them for their deeds accordingly.

(٩٤) فَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنٞ فَلَا كُفۡرَانَ لِسَعۡيِهِۦ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ كَٰتِبُونَ

So whoever from among them does righteous deeds while believing in Allah, His messengers, and in the Last Day, will not be denied the due of his righteous deeds. Instead, Allah will express gratitude and then multiply his reward for him. He will find it recorded in his book of deeds on the Day he is resurrected, and he will be pleased.

(٩٥) وَحَرَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ قَرۡيَةٍ أَهۡلَكۡنَٰهَآ أَنَّهُمۡ لَا يَرۡجِعُونَ

It is impossible for the people of a town that We destroyed due to their disbelief to return to this world to repent and for that repentance to be accepted.

(٩٦) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا فُتِحَتۡ يَأۡجُوجُ وَمَأۡجُوجُ وَهُم مِّن كُلِّ حَدَبٖ يَنسِلُونَ

[They will never return], even once the barrier of Gog and Magog is opened, and they, on that Day, emerge hurriedly descending from every high piece of land.

(٩٧) وَٱقۡتَرَبَ ٱلۡوَعۡدُ ٱلۡحَقُّ فَإِذَا هِيَ شَٰخِصَةٌ أَبۡصَٰرُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَا قَدۡ كُنَّا فِي غَفۡلَةٖ مِّنۡ هَٰذَا بَلۡ كُنَّا ظَٰلِمِينَ

[The Day of] Resurrection is close to their emergence, and the horror and difficulties of the Day of Resurrection will be on display. [At this time], the disbelievers will be left wide-eyed because of its horror, saying: Oh, may we be destroyed! We were indulged in futility, preoccupied instead of preparing for this great day. Instead, we were wrongdoers by disbelieving and committing sins.

(٩٨) إِنَّكُمۡ وَمَا تَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ حَصَبُ جَهَنَّمَ أَنتُمۡ لَهَا وَٰرِدُونَ

All of you, O polytheists, the idols you worship, and the people and jinn who are content with you worshipping them instead of Allah shall be the fuel of Hell. You and everything you worship will surely enter it.

(٩٩) لَوۡ كَانَ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ ءَالِهَةٗ مَّا وَرَدُوهَاۖ وَكُلّٞ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

If these things that are worshipped were, in reality, gods worthy of being worshipped, they would not be entering the Fire with their worshippers. Yet they will all be entering it, having to remain in it forever, never to come out.

(١٠٠) لَهُمۡ فِيهَا زَفِيرٞ وَهُمۡ فِيهَا لَا يَسۡمَعُونَ

They will find it hard to breathe due to the severity of the pain they will experience. They will be unable to hear anything due to the immense and dreadful horror that will afflict them.

(١٠١) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ سَبَقَتۡ لَهُم مِّنَّا ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰٓ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ عَنۡهَا مُبۡعَدُونَ

When the polytheists said that indeed, Jesus and the angels who were worshipped would therefore enter the Fire, Allah said: Verily, those regarding whom it was decreed in the Knowledge of Allah that they would be from the people of happiness [in the Hereafter], such as Jesus (peace be upon him), will be distanced from the Fire.

Points of Benefit:

Emphasizing chastity and elucidating its virtues.
The agreement of divine messages on the concept of monotheism and the foundations of worship.
The opening of the barrier of Gog and Magog is among the major signs of the Hour.
Neglecting preparation for the Day of Judgment is a cause for experiencing its horrors.
