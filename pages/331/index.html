(١٠٢) لَا يَسۡمَعُونَ حَسِيسَهَاۖ وَهُمۡ فِي مَا ٱشۡتَهَتۡ أَنفُسُهُمۡ خَٰلِدُونَ

The sound of Hell will not reach their ears, while they shall reside among whatever luxuries and enjoyment they desire. Their bliss will never end.

(١٠٣) لَا يَحۡزُنُهُمُ ٱلۡفَزَعُ ٱلۡأَكۡبَرُ وَتَتَلَقَّىٰهُمُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ هَٰذَا يَوۡمُكُمُ ٱلَّذِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ

When it covers its inhabitants, the great horror of the Fire shall not scare the people of happiness. The angels will welcome them with glad tidings, saying: This is your day that you were promised in the worldly life and given glad tidings of its bliss.

(١٠٤) يَوۡمَ نَطۡوِي ٱلسَّمَآءَ كَطَيِّ ٱلسِّجِلِّ لِلۡكُتُبِۚ كَمَا بَدَأۡنَآ أَوَّلَ خَلۡقٖ نُّعِيدُهُۥۚ وَعۡدًا عَلَيۡنَآۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا فَٰعِلِينَ

On that Day, We will roll up the heavens like the rolling up of a scroll and gather the creation in the state they were created in the first instance. We have promised that it will happen, a promise that there is no going back on. We shall indeed fulfill Our promise.

(١٠٥) وَلَقَدۡ كَتَبۡنَا فِي ٱلزَّبُورِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ٱلذِّكۡرِ أَنَّ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ يَرِثُهَا عِبَادِيَ ٱلصَّٰلِحُونَ

Indeed, We had written in the books that We revealed to the messengers after We had written it in the Preserved Tablet that the Earth would be inherited by the pious, obedient slaves of Allah, the nation of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم.

(١٠٦) إِنَّ فِي هَٰذَا لَبَلَٰغٗا لِّقَوۡمٍ عَٰبِدِينَ

Verily, in the admonition We revealed, there is a message for people who worship their Lord according to what He has legislated for them. It is they who truly benefit from it.

(١٠٧) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا رَحۡمَةٗ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

O Muhammad, We have not sent you as a messenger except to be a mercy to all creation because you are eager to guide people and save them from Allah's torment.

(١٠٨) قُلۡ إِنَّمَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ أَنَّمَآ إِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهٞ وَٰحِدٞۖ فَهَلۡ أَنتُم مُّسۡلِمُونَ

Say, O Messenger: It is only revealed to me from my Lord that your true deity is only One; He has no partner, and He is Allah. So submit by believing in Him and performing acts of obedience to Him.

(١٠٩) فَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَقُلۡ ءَاذَنتُكُمۡ عَلَىٰ سَوَآءٖۖ وَإِنۡ أَدۡرِيٓ أَقَرِيبٌ أَم بَعِيدٞ مَّا تُوعَدُونَ

O Messenger, if these people turn away from what you have brought to them, then tell them: I have informed you that we are entirely dissociated from each other, and I do not know when Allah's promised torment will descend upon you.

(١١٠) إِنَّهُۥ يَعۡلَمُ ٱلۡجَهۡرَ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا تَكۡتُمُونَ

Indeed, Allah knows of the speech you disclose and that which you keep secret. Nothing from that evades Him, and He will recompense you for it accordingly.

(١١١) وَإِنۡ أَدۡرِي لَعَلَّهُۥ فِتۡنَةٞ لَّكُمۡ وَمَتَٰعٌ إِلَىٰ حِينٖ

I do not know whether your respite from punishment is a test for you or whether it is to lure you further [into destruction] and let you enjoy yourself by persisting in disbelief and misguidance until a time decreed that only Allah knows.

(١١٢) قَٰلَ رَبِّ ٱحۡكُم بِٱلۡحَقِّۗ وَرَبُّنَا ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ ٱلۡمُسۡتَعَانُ عَلَىٰ مَا تَصِفُونَ

Supplicating to his Lord, Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم said: O my Lord! Judge well between us and those of our people who have persisted in disbelief. We seek help solely from our Merciful Lord against your statements of disbelief and rejection.

Points of Benefit:

Righteousness is a cause for empowerment on the Earth.
The mission of the Prophet, peace be upon him, along with his legislation and Sunnah, is a mercy to the worlds.
The Messenger, peace be upon him, does not have knowledge of the unseen.
Allah has knowledge of what emanates from His servants in speech.
