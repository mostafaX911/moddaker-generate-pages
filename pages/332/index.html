The Pilgrimage (Surah Al-Hajj)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Glorifying Allah, Exalted and Transcendent, and venerating His symbols, and submitting to His command.

Explanation:

(١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمۡۚ إِنَّ زَلۡزَلَةَ ٱلسَّاعَةِ شَيۡءٌ عَظِيمٞ

O people, fear your Lord by complying with what He has ordered you with and desisting from what He has forbidden you. The earthquake and other horrors accompanying the Day of Resurrection are dreadful. They must be prepared for through deeds that please Allah.

(٢) يَوۡمَ تَرَوۡنَهَا تَذۡهَلُ كُلُّ مُرۡضِعَةٍ عَمَّآ أَرۡضَعَتۡ وَتَضَعُ كُلُّ ذَاتِ حَمۡلٍ حَمۡلَهَا وَتَرَى ٱلنَّاسَ سُكَٰرَىٰ وَمَا هُم بِسُكَٰرَىٰ وَلَٰكِنَّ عَذَابَ ٱللَّهِ شَدِيدٞ

On that Day, you will witness every nursing mother neglecting her nursing child, and every pregnant being will abort her pregnancy from intense fear. You shall see people losing themselves, like drunkards, due to the extreme horror of the situation, and not from alcohol. Rather, the severity of Allah's punishment will cause them to lose their minds.

After mentioning the horrors that will accompany the establishment of the Day of Judgment, Allah responds to those who deny the Revival and Resurrection, stating:

(٣) وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يُجَٰدِلُ فِي ٱللَّهِ بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖ وَيَتَّبِعُ كُلَّ شَيۡطَٰنٖ مَّرِيدٖ

Amongst people is he who argues regarding Allah's power to resurrect the dead, without any knowledge to take support from, and who in belief and word follows every rebel against His Lord from among the devils and leaders of misguidance.

(٤) كُتِبَ عَلَيۡهِ أَنَّهُۥ مَن تَوَلَّاهُ فَأَنَّهُۥ يُضِلُّهُۥ وَيَهۡدِيهِ إِلَىٰ عَذَابِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

It has been decreed regarding such rebellious devils among humans and jinn that whoever follows them and believes in them will be misguided by them from the path of truth. They will send these people towards the torment of Hell through disbelief and acts of disobedience that lead towards it.

(٥) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِن كُنتُمۡ فِي رَيۡبٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡبَعۡثِ فَإِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ ثُمَّ مِنۡ عَلَقَةٖ ثُمَّ مِن مُّضۡغَةٖ مُّخَلَّقَةٖ وَغَيۡرِ مُخَلَّقَةٖ لِّنُبَيِّنَ لَكُمۡۚ وَنُقِرُّ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡحَامِ مَا نَشَآءُ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗى ثُمَّ نُخۡرِجُكُمۡ طِفۡلٗا ثُمَّ لِتَبۡلُغُوٓاْ أَشُدَّكُمۡۖ وَمِنكُم مَّن يُتَوَفَّىٰ وَمِنكُم مَّن يُرَدُّ إِلَىٰٓ أَرۡذَلِ ٱلۡعُمُرِ لِكَيۡلَا يَعۡلَمَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ عِلۡمٖ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۚ وَتَرَى ٱلۡأَرۡضَ هَامِدَةٗ فَإِذَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهَا ٱلۡمَآءَ ٱهۡتَزَّتۡ وَرَبَتۡ وَأَنۢبَتَتۡ مِن كُلِّ زَوۡجِۢ بَهِيجٖ

O people, if you have any doubt regarding Our power to resurrect you after death, think about your creation: We created your father, Adam, from soil; then We created his children from sperm which the man ejaculates into the woman's womb; then the sperm turns into solid blood; then the solid blood turns into a lump of flesh; then the lump of flesh turns into either a fully formed creation which remains in the womb until it comes out alive as a child or into an unformed creation which the womb aborts, to show Our power by creating you in stages. We then settle in the wombs, whatever fetus We wish until it is born in a fixed period of nine months. Then, We bring you out of your mothers' wombs as infants so that you reach your full strength and intelligence. Amongst you are some who die before this, and amongst you are those who reach old age where strength and intelligence weaken to the extent that they become worse than a child, no longer knowing anything they used to know. And you will see the Earth dry without any plants on it. Then, when We send down rainwater on it, it opens up with plants, rises due to the plants growing, and brings forth every type of beautiful plant.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation of preparing for the Day of Resurrection with the provision of taqwa.
The severity of the horrors of the Day of Judgment, where a nursing mother forgets her child, a pregnant woman miscarries, and people's minds are confounded.
The gradual development in creation is an established divine practice.
The existence of the initial creation indicates the possibility of resurrection.
The phenomenon of rain and the subsequent growth of the earth is tangible evidence of the resurrection of the dead.
