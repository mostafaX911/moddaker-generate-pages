(٦) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَأَنَّهُۥ يُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَأَنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

That which We have mentioned to you; namely the beginning of your creation, its stages, and the conditions of those born amongst you, is so that you believe that Allah Who created you is the Truth in which there is no doubt, as opposed to the idols you worship and so that you believe He will revive the dead, and that He is Omnipotent over everything; nothing is beyond His capability.

(٧) وَأَنَّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ ءَاتِيَةٞ لَّا رَيۡبَ فِيهَا وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَبۡعَثُ مَن فِي ٱلۡقُبُورِ

And so that you believe the Hour is to come, without any doubt, and that Allah will resurrect the dead from their graves to recompense them for their deeds accordingly.

When Allah the Exalted mentions the condition of misguidedness resulting from blind following in the third verse, He cites the state of the misguidance of the leaders of disbelief in this verse, stating:

(٨) وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يُجَٰدِلُ فِي ٱللَّهِ بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖ وَلَا هُدٗى وَلَا كِتَٰبٖ مُّنِيرٖ

Among the disbelievers is he who argues regarding the Oneness of Allah without any proof to reach the truth, nor a guide to direct them to it, nor an illuminating book revealed by Allah to guide them to it.

(٩) ثَانِيَ عِطۡفِهِۦ لِيُضِلَّ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۖ لَهُۥ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا خِزۡيٞۖ وَنُذِيقُهُۥ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ عَذَابَ ٱلۡحَرِيقِ

He turns his face away in arrogance to direct people away from believing and entering the religion of Allah. He who fits this description shall have disgrace in this world due to the punishment which will befall him, and We shall make him taste the torment of the burning Fire in the Hereafter.

(١٠) ذَٰلِكَ بِمَا قَدَّمَتۡ يَدَاكَ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَيۡسَ بِظَلَّٰمٖ لِّلۡعَبِيدِ

He will be told: That torment you tasted is due to the disbelief and the acts of disobedience you committed. Allah does not punish any of His creation except due to a sin.

(١١) وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَعۡبُدُ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ حَرۡفٖۖ فَإِنۡ أَصَابَهُۥ خَيۡرٌ ٱطۡمَأَنَّ بِهِۦۖ وَإِنۡ أَصَابَتۡهُ فِتۡنَةٌ ٱنقَلَبَ عَلَىٰ وَجۡهِهِۦ خَسِرَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةَۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡخُسۡرَانُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

Amongst people is a conflicted person who worships Allah in doubt. If good, such as health and wealth, come to him, he remains on his belief and worship of Allah. But, if an affliction like illness or poverty afflicts him, he perceives his religion as a bad omen and then renounces it. He has lost this world because his disbelief will never increase him in a share of the world not written for him, and he has lost his Hereafter due to Allah's torment, which will meet him. That is the clearest loss.

(١٢) يَدۡعُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا يَضُرُّهُۥ وَمَا لَا يَنفَعُهُۥۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلضَّلَٰلُ ٱلۡبَعِيدُ

He worships idols instead of Allah, which cannot harm him if he disobeys them, nor can they benefit him if he obeys them. Supplication to idols that can neither harm nor benefit is misguidance far from the truth.

(١٣) يَدۡعُواْ لَمَن ضَرُّهُۥٓ أَقۡرَبُ مِن نَّفۡعِهِۦۚ لَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَوۡلَىٰ وَلَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡعَشِيرُ

This disbeliever who worships idols calls upon idols whose harm is more likely than their benefit. Evil is the deity whose harm is more likely than its benefit. How bad it is as a helper to the one who seeks its help and as a companion to those who accompany it.

(١٤) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُدۡخِلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَفۡعَلُ مَا يُرِيدُ

Allah will admit those who believe in Him and do righteous deeds into gardens with rivers flowing beneath its palaces. Allah does what He pleases by showing mercy to whom He wants to show mercy and punishing whom He wants to punish. Nothing can compel Him; may He be Exalted.

(١٥) مَن كَانَ يَظُنُّ أَن لَّن يَنصُرَهُ ٱللَّهُ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ فَلۡيَمۡدُدۡ بِسَبَبٍ إِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ ثُمَّ لۡيَقۡطَعۡ فَلۡيَنظُرۡ هَلۡ يُذۡهِبَنَّ كَيۡدُهُۥ مَا يَغِيظُ

Whoever thinks Allah will not help His Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم in this world and the Hereafter should extend a rope to the ceiling of his house and then strangle himself by hanging himself above the ground. Then, he should see if that removes the anger he feels in his heart. Allah is the Helper of His Prophet, whether his opponents like it or not.

Points of Benefit:

The reasons for guidance are either knowledge that leads to the truth, a guide who directs towards it, or a book that authenticates and guides to it.
Arrogance is a trait that hinders success in attaining the truth.
From the justice of Allah is that He does not punish except for a committed sin.
Allah is the supporter of His Prophet and His religion, even if the disbelievers hate it.
