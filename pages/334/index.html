(١٦) وَكَذَٰلِكَ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ ءَايَٰتِۢ بَيِّنَٰتٖ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَهۡدِي مَن يُرِيدُ

Just as We clarified the clear proofs for resurrection, We revealed the Quran to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم as clear verses. Allah directs whomever He wishes through His grace to the path of guidance and righteousness.

(١٧) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱلَّذِينَ هَادُواْ وَٱلصَّٰبِ‍ِٔينَ وَٱلنَّصَٰرَىٰ وَٱلۡمَجُوسَ وَٱلَّذِينَ أَشۡرَكُوٓاْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَفۡصِلُ بَيۡنَهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدٌ

Those who believe in Allah from this nation, the Jews, the Sabians (a group of followers of one of the prophets), the Christians, the Magians (fire-worshippers), the polytheists; Allah will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection, then He will admit the believers amongst them into Paradise and admit the others into the Fire. Allah is a Witness to all the sayings and deeds of His slaves, with nothing hidden from Him, and He shall recompense them for these deeds accordingly.

(١٨) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَسۡجُدُۤ لَهُۥۤ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلشَّمۡسُ وَٱلۡقَمَرُ وَٱلنُّجُومُ وَٱلۡجِبَالُ وَٱلشَّجَرُ وَٱلدَّوَآبُّ وَكَثِيرٞ مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِۖ وَكَثِيرٌ حَقَّ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡعَذَابُۗ وَمَن يُهِنِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن مُّكۡرِمٍۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَفۡعَلُ مَا يَشَآءُ۩

O Messenger, do you not know that the angels in the heavens and the believing humans and jinn on Earth prostrate to Allah out of obedience? The sun, moon, stars in the sky, mountains, trees, and creatures on Earth prostrate to Him compliantly. As do many people prostrate to Him out of obedience. But many refuse to prostrate to Him out of obedience; thus, Allah's torment became justified for them due to their disbelief. Whoever Allah decrees disgrace and shame for, due to his disbelief, has nobody to honor him. Allah does what He wishes. None can compel Him; may He be Exalted.

After mentioning those who prostrate to Him in obedience and those who refrain, Allah the Exalted and Transcendent proceeds to comment on their respective destinations, stating:

(١٩) ۞هَٰذَانِ خَصۡمَانِ ٱخۡتَصَمُواْ فِي رَبِّهِمۡۖ فَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ قُطِّعَتۡ لَهُمۡ ثِيَابٞ مِّن نَّارٖ يُصَبُّ مِن فَوۡقِ رُءُوسِهِمُ ٱلۡحَمِيمُ

These are two groups arguing regarding their Lord as to which of them is right: The group of believers and the group of disbelievers. The Fire will encompass the disbelievers, just as clothes cover the person wearing them, and boiling water will be poured on their heads.

(٢٠) يُصۡهَرُ بِهِۦ مَا فِي بُطُونِهِمۡ وَٱلۡجُلُودُ

The intestines in their abdomens will melt due to its intense heat, which will reach their skin and melt it.

(٢١) وَلَهُم مَّقَٰمِعُ مِنۡ حَدِيدٖ

Iron hammers are in the Fire for them, with which the angels will hit their heads.

(٢٢) كُلَّمَآ أَرَادُوٓاْ أَن يَخۡرُجُواْ مِنۡهَا مِنۡ غَمٍّ أُعِيدُواْ فِيهَا وَذُوقُواْ عَذَابَ ٱلۡحَرِيقِ

Whenever they try to leave the Fire, due to the severe distress they will face, they will be returned to it and told: Taste the punishment of the burning Fire.

(٢٣) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُدۡخِلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ يُحَلَّوۡنَ فِيهَا مِنۡ أَسَاوِرَ مِن ذَهَبٖ وَلُؤۡلُؤٗاۖ وَلِبَاسُهُمۡ فِيهَا حَرِيرٞ

Allah will admit the party of believers who believed in Allah and did righteous deeds into gardens with rivers flowing beneath its palaces and trees. Allah will adorn them with gold bracelets, embellish them with pearls, and their clothes therein will be silk.

Points of Benefit:

Guidance is in the Hand of Allah; He bestows it upon whom He wills among His servants.
Allah's watchful oversight encompasses everything, from the actions of His servants to their conditions.
The submission of all creatures to Allah is inevitable, while the submission of believers to Him is an act of obedience.
Punishment descends upon the people of disbelief and disobedience, while mercy is steadfast for the people of faith and obedience.
