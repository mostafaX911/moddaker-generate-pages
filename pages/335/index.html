(٢٤) وَهُدُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱلطَّيِّبِ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ وَهُدُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطِ ٱلۡحَمِيدِ

Allah guided them in this worldly life towards the best of speech, such as [saying the] testimony that there is no one worthy of worship besides Allah, takbir (proclaiming Allah's greatness), and tahmid (praising Allah). He guided them toward the praiseworthy path of Islam.

(٢٥) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَيَصُدُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلۡنَٰهُ لِلنَّاسِ سَوَآءً ٱلۡعَٰكِفُ فِيهِ وَٱلۡبَادِۚ وَمَن يُرِدۡ فِيهِ بِإِلۡحَادِۢ بِظُلۡمٖ نُّذِقۡهُ مِنۡ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ

Those who disbelieved in Allah, turn others away from entering Islam and prevent people from the Sacred Mosque, such as what the polytheists did in the year of Hudaybiyah; we will cause them to taste a painful punishment. That (the Sacred Mosque) is the mosque which We have made the direction for people in their prayers and one of the rites of Hajj and Umrah, and in which the resident of Mecca and one who comes there who is not from the people Mecca are equal. Should anyone intend to deviate by purposefully doing an act of disobedience therein, We will cause him to taste a painful punishment.

(٢٦) وَإِذۡ بَوَّأۡنَا لِإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ مَكَانَ ٱلۡبَيۡتِ أَن لَّا تُشۡرِكۡ بِي شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَطَهِّرۡ بَيۡتِيَ لِلطَّآئِفِينَ وَٱلۡقَآئِمِينَ وَٱلرُّكَّعِ ٱلسُّجُودِ

Remember, O Messenger, when We clarified to Abraham (peace be upon him) the place of The House and its boundaries after it was unknown. We revealed to him: Do not associate anything with Me in worship but rather worship Me alone. And purify My House from physical and abstract impurities for those who circumambulate it and perform prayer therein.

(٢٧) وَأَذِّن فِي ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡحَجِّ يَأۡتُوكَ رِجَالٗا وَعَلَىٰ كُلِّ ضَامِرٖ يَأۡتِينَ مِن كُلِّ فَجٍّ عَمِيقٖ

And proclaim amongst the people inviting them towards pilgrimage of this House We ordered you to build. They will come to you walking and riding on every lean camel due to the difficulty it endured from traveling. The camels will bring them, carrying them from every distant path.

(٢٨) لِّيَشۡهَدُواْ مَنَٰفِعَ لَهُمۡ وَيَذۡكُرُواْ ٱسۡمَ ٱللَّهِ فِيٓ أَيَّامٖ مَّعۡلُومَٰتٍ عَلَىٰ مَا رَزَقَهُم مِّنۢ بَهِيمَةِ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِۖ فَكُلُواْ مِنۡهَا وَأَطۡعِمُواْ ٱلۡبَآئِسَ ٱلۡفَقِيرَ

So that they come to what will bring them benefit such as forgiveness of sins, attaining reward, unity, and so forth; and so that they mention the Name of Allah on the sacrificial animals they slaughter on appointed days, which are: the tenth of Dhul-Hijjah and three days after it, in gratitude to Allah for the camels, cows, and sheep He granted them. So eat of these sacrificial animals and feed from them those who are extremely poor.

(٢٩) ثُمَّ لۡيَقۡضُواْ تَفَثَهُمۡ وَلۡيُوفُواْ نُذُورَهُمۡ وَلۡيَطَّوَّفُواْ بِٱلۡبَيۡتِ ٱلۡعَتِيقِ

Then, they should complete what remains of their Hajj rites, coming out of the state of Ihram (ritual sanctity) by shaving their heads, clipping their nails, and removing the dirt that accumulated on them due to the state of Ihram. They should then fulfill the Hajj, Umrah, or sacrifice of an animal that they made binding on themselves. They should perform the Tawaf al-Ifadah (obligatory circumambulation) of the House, which Allah freed from the control of tyrants.

(٣٠) ذَٰلِكَۖ وَمَن يُعَظِّمۡ حُرُمَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ خَيۡرٞ لَّهُۥ عِندَ رَبِّهِۦۗ وَأُحِلَّتۡ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمُ إِلَّا مَا يُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ فَٱجۡتَنِبُواْ ٱلرِّجۡسَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَوۡثَٰنِ وَٱجۡتَنِبُواْ قَوۡلَ ٱلزُّورِ

That which you have been ordered with, i.e., coming out of the state of Ihram (ritual sanctity) by shaving the head, clipping the nails, removing dirt, fulfilling the vow, and circumambulating the House, is what Allah has made obligatory on you. So respect what Allah has made obligatory on you. Whoever avoids what Allah has ordered him to avoid during the state of Ihram (ritual sanctity), out of respect for not transgressing the limits of Allah and not making lawful its prohibitions, then this is better for him in this world and the Hereafter in the sight of his Lord, may He be Exalted. O people! Camels, cows, and sheep amongst livestock have been made lawful for you. Allah has not made unlawful for you hami, bahirah, or wasilah. He only made what you find in the Quran unlawful for you, such as carrion, blood, etc. Stay far away from filth, such as idols and false speech, which includes lying against Allah or His creation.

Points of Benefit:

The sanctity of the Sacred House necessitates greater caution against sins within it than elsewhere.
Allah's Sacred House is the beloved destination of the yearning hearts of believers in every time and place.
The benefits of Hajj yield returns to people, whether they are worldly or pertaining to the Hereafter.
Gratefulness for blessings necessitates kindness toward the weak.
