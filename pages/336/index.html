(٣١) حُنَفَآءَ لِلَّهِ غَيۡرَ مُشۡرِكِينَ بِهِۦۚ وَمَن يُشۡرِكۡ بِٱللَّهِ فَكَأَنَّمَا خَرَّ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَتَخۡطَفُهُ ٱلطَّيۡرُ أَوۡ تَهۡوِي بِهِ ٱلرِّيحُ فِي مَكَانٖ سَحِيقٖ

Refrain from this, turning away from every religion besides the religion He is pleased with, without associating any partner with Him in worship. Whoever associates a partner with Allah, it is as though He has fallen from the sky and is either snatched away by birds or swept by the wind to a remote place.

(٣٢) ذَٰلِكَۖ وَمَن يُعَظِّمۡ شَعَٰٓئِرَ ٱللَّهِ فَإِنَّهَا مِن تَقۡوَى ٱلۡقُلُوبِ

This is from the monotheism, sincerity to Allah, abandonment of idols, and refraining from false speech that Allah has ordered. Whoever honors the signs of the religion, amongst them sacrificial animals and the rites of Hajj, then such honoring is part of the hearts fearing their Lord.

(٣٣) لَكُمۡ فِيهَا مَنَٰفِعُ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗى ثُمَّ مَحِلُّهَآ إِلَى ٱلۡبَيۡتِ ٱلۡعَتِيقِ

There are benefits for you in the sacrificial animals you slaughter at the House. For example, riding [it], wool, breeding, and milk, up until the time appointed for its slaughter when you are near the House of Allah that He freed from the control of tyrants.

(٣٤) وَلِكُلِّ أُمَّةٖ جَعَلۡنَا مَنسَكٗا لِّيَذۡكُرُواْ ٱسۡمَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَىٰ مَا رَزَقَهُم مِّنۢ بَهِيمَةِ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِۗ فَإِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهٞ وَٰحِدٞ فَلَهُۥٓ أَسۡلِمُواْۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُخۡبِتِينَ

For every previous nation, We made rites to shed blood [of a sacrificial animal] as a means of closeness to Allah, in the hope they will mention Allah's name at the time of slaughtering these sacrificial animals in gratitude to Allah for the camels, cows, and sheep He granted them. The one deserving of worship, O people, is One God who has no partner, so submit to Him alone through submission and obedience. And O Messenger, tell those who are humbly submissive and sincere what will please them.

(٣٥) ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِرَ ٱللَّهُ وَجِلَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ عَلَىٰ مَآ أَصَابَهُمۡ وَٱلۡمُقِيمِي ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ

Those who, when they remember Allah, fear His punishment and thus distance [themselves] from opposing His command, perform their prayer completely, exercise patience if any calamity befalls them, and spend in the different avenues of righteousness from what Allah has given them.

(٣٦) وَٱلۡبُدۡنَ جَعَلۡنَٰهَا لَكُم مِّن شَعَٰٓئِرِ ٱللَّهِ لَكُمۡ فِيهَا خَيۡرٞۖ فَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱسۡمَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡهَا صَوَآفَّۖ فَإِذَا وَجَبَتۡ جُنُوبُهَا فَكُلُواْ مِنۡهَا وَأَطۡعِمُواْ ٱلۡقَانِعَ وَٱلۡمُعۡتَرَّۚ كَذَٰلِكَ سَخَّرۡنَٰهَا لَكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

And We have made the camels and cows taken to the House amongst the sacred signs and symbols of the religion. In them are worldly and religious benefits for you, so say "in the name of Allah" when slaughtering them after their feet have been lined up while standing and one of their feet has been tied so they do not flee. Then, when they fall on their sides after being slaughtered, eat of them, O those who are sacrificing, and give of them to those poor who desist from begging and the poor who come forward to be granted a share of them. Just as We made them submissive to you so that you place loads on them and ride them, We have also made them submissive to you so they are led to the place you slaughter them in, as a means of closeness to Allah, so that you may be grateful to Allah for the favor of making them submissive to you.

(٣٧) لَن يَنَالَ ٱللَّهَ لُحُومُهَا وَلَا دِمَآؤُهَا وَلَٰكِن يَنَالُهُ ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ مِنكُمۡۚ كَذَٰلِكَ سَخَّرَهَا لَكُمۡ لِتُكَبِّرُواْ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ مَا هَدَىٰكُمۡۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Neither the meat of the sacrificial animals you offer nor their blood ever reach Allah, nor is it raised towards Him. Instead, your fear of Allah therein is raised towards Him through being sincere to Him in your obedience, seeking closeness to Him through it. In that way, Allah made them submissive to you so that you proclaim Allah's greatness (takbir), thanking Him for the truth He has directed you to. O Messenger, inform those who excel in worshipping their Lord and dealing with His creation of what will please them.

(٣٨) ۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُدَٰفِعُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ خَوَّانٖ كَفُورٍ

Allah defends those who believe in Him from the evil of their enemies. Allah does not love anyone who is treacherous in his trust and who is ungrateful for the favors of Allah by not showing thanks to Him for it; in fact, Allah despises him.

Points of Benefit:

Employing parables to illustrate moral concepts by embodying them in tangible narratives serves a profound educational purpose.
The virtue of humility.
Benevolence is a cause for happiness.
Belief is a cause for Allah's defense and care for His servant.
