Allah the Exalted and Transcendent mentions that He defends the believers such that their hearts become reassured. He then gives them permission to fight the disbelievers, stating:

(٣٩) أُذِنَ لِلَّذِينَ يُقَٰتَلُونَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ ظُلِمُواْۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ نَصۡرِهِمۡ لَقَدِيرٌ

Allah has permitted the believers to fight the polytheists who fought them because their enemies oppressed them. Allah has the power to help the believers against their enemies without fighting. However, the wisdom of Allah required that the believers be tested by fighting the disbelievers.

(٤٠) ٱلَّذِينَ أُخۡرِجُواْ مِن دِيَٰرِهِم بِغَيۡرِ حَقٍّ إِلَّآ أَن يَقُولُواْ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُۗ وَلَوۡلَا دَفۡعُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلنَّاسَ بَعۡضَهُم بِبَعۡضٖ لَّهُدِّمَتۡ صَوَٰمِعُ وَبِيَعٞ وَصَلَوَٰتٞ وَمَسَٰجِدُ يُذۡكَرُ فِيهَا ٱسۡمُ ٱللَّهِ كَثِيرٗاۗ وَلَيَنصُرَنَّ ٱللَّهُ مَن يَنصُرُهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَقَوِيٌّ عَزِيزٌ

Those whom the disbelievers unjustly exiled from their homes, not due to any crime they committed but because they said: "Our Lord is Allah; we have no Lord besides Him." Had Allah not legislated for the prophets and believers fighting against their enemies, they would have transgressed against the places of worship, destroying the monasteries of the monks, Christian churches, Jewish synagogues, and the mosques of the Muslims which have been made for prayer and in which the Muslims remember Allah abundantly. Allah will help those who help His religion and Prophet. Allah has the power to help whoever helps His religion and is Almighty, with nobody able to overcome Him.

(٤١) ٱلَّذِينَ إِن مَّكَّنَّٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتَوُاْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَمَرُواْ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَنَهَوۡاْ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِۗ وَلِلَّهِ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡأُمُورِ

These people who have been promised help are those who, if We establish them on Earth by helping them against their enemies, would perform prayer perfectly, give Zakat of their wealth, command what the sacred law has commanded, and forbid what it has forbidden. To Allah alone is where matters return, with respect to reward and punishment.

(٤٢) وَإِن يُكَذِّبُوكَ فَقَدۡ كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٖ وَعَادٞ وَثَمُودُ

O Messenger, if your people reject you, be patient. You are not the first messenger to be rejected by his people. Before your people, the people of Noah rejected Noah, Ād rejected Hūd, and Thamūd rejected Saleh.

(٤٣) وَقَوۡمُ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَقَوۡمُ لُوطٖ

Abraham's people rejected him, and the people of Lot rejected Lot.

(٤٤) وَأَصۡحَٰبُ مَدۡيَنَۖ وَكُذِّبَ مُوسَىٰۖ فَأَمۡلَيۡتُ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ ثُمَّ أَخَذۡتُهُمۡۖ فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ نَكِيرِ

The people of Midian rejected Shuayb, and Pharaoh and his people rejected Moses. I delayed the punishment of their people, gradually leading them to destruction. I then seized them with the torment. So contemplate how I reproached them; I destroyed them due to their disbelief.

(٤٥) فَكَأَيِّن مِّن قَرۡيَةٍ أَهۡلَكۡنَٰهَا وَهِيَ ظَالِمَةٞ فَهِيَ خَاوِيَةٌ عَلَىٰ عُرُوشِهَا وَبِئۡرٖ مُّعَطَّلَةٖ وَقَصۡرٖ مَّشِيدٍ

How many towns that did wrong because of their disbelief have We destroyed with an obliterating punishment? Their homes are ruined and empty of inhabitants. How many wells are there with nobody to drink from due to being destroyed? How many lofty decorated palaces are there which could not save their residents from the torment?

(٤٦) أَفَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَتَكُونَ لَهُمۡ قُلُوبٞ يَعۡقِلُونَ بِهَآ أَوۡ ءَاذَانٞ يَسۡمَعُونَ بِهَاۖ فَإِنَّهَا لَا تَعۡمَى ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَلَٰكِن تَعۡمَى ٱلۡقُلُوبُ ٱلَّتِي فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ

Did these people who reject what the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم brought not travel throughout the land to see the remains of those destroyed towns so that they would reflect with their minds to take heed and listen to their stories to learn from them? Blindness is not the blindness of eyesight, but rather destructive blindness is the blindness of insight, where such a person does not reflect or take lessons.

Points of Benefit:

Affirming the attributes of Strength and Might for Allah.
Affirming the legitimacy of Jihad to safeguard spaces of worship.
Upholding the religion is a cause for Allah's victory for His believing servants.
Blindness of the hearts is an obstacle to contemplating the signs of Allah.
