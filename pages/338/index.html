(٤٧) وَيَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَكَ بِٱلۡعَذَابِ وَلَن يُخۡلِفَ ٱللَّهُ وَعۡدَهُۥۚ وَإِنَّ يَوۡمًا عِندَ رَبِّكَ كَأَلۡفِ سَنَةٖ مِّمَّا تَعُدُّونَ

O Messenger, the disbelievers amongst your people hastened to seek immediate punishment in this world and deferred punishment in the Hereafter when they were warned of them [coming]. Allah will never break what he has promised them. Amongst the immediate punishment is what befell them on the day of Badr. And one day of torment in the Hereafter is equivalent to a thousand years of what you count in worldly years due to the suffering it contains.

(٤٨) وَكَأَيِّن مِّن قَرۡيَةٍ أَمۡلَيۡتُ لَهَا وَهِيَ ظَالِمَةٞ ثُمَّ أَخَذۡتُهَا وَإِلَيَّ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

How many a town of transgressing disbelievers did I give respite to, did not punish them instantly to bring them around? Then, I punished them with an obliterating punishment. To Me alone is their return on the Day of Resurrection, and then I will recompense them for their disbelief with everlasting torment.

(٤٩) قُلۡ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ لَكُمۡ نَذِيرٞ مُّبِينٞ

O humanity, I am only a warner to you, conveying to you what I have been sent with and clear in my warning.

(٥٠) فَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٞ وَرِزۡقٞ كَرِيمٞ

So those who believe in Allah and did righteous deeds will be forgiven of their sins by their Lord, and they will receive a noble provision in Paradise that will never end.

(٥١) وَٱلَّذِينَ سَعَوۡاْ فِيٓ ءَايَٰتِنَا مُعَٰجِزِينَ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

Those who strive to reject Our verses, assuming they will be able to render Allah powerless and escape Him so that He cannot punish them, are the companions of the Inferno, constantly remaining in it just as companions remain with one another.

(٥٢) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ مِن رَّسُولٖ وَلَا نَبِيٍّ إِلَّآ إِذَا تَمَنَّىٰٓ أَلۡقَى ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ فِيٓ أُمۡنِيَّتِهِۦ فَيَنسَخُ ٱللَّهُ مَا يُلۡقِي ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ ثُمَّ يُحۡكِمُ ٱللَّهُ ءَايَٰتِهِۦۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٞ

O Messenger, We did not send any messenger or prophet before you except that when he read the Book of Allah, the Devil cast in his recitation that which confuses the people [to think] that it is revelation. Then Allah would nullify what the Devil cast and affirm His verses. Allah knows everything. Nothing is hidden from Him. He is Wise in His creating, decreeing, and planning.

(٥٣) لِّيَجۡعَلَ مَا يُلۡقِي ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ فِتۡنَةٗ لِّلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٞ وَٱلۡقَاسِيَةِ قُلُوبُهُمۡۗ وَإِنَّ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ لَفِي شِقَاقِۢ بَعِيدٖ

The Devil casting [confusion] in the recitation of the Prophet is so that Allah makes what he casts as a test for the hypocrites and those polytheists whose hearts have hardened. The wrongdoers amongst the hypocrites and polytheists show enmity towards Allah and His Messenger and are distant from the truth and guidance.

(٥٤) وَلِيَعۡلَمَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكَ فَيُؤۡمِنُواْ بِهِۦ فَتُخۡبِتَ لَهُۥ قُلُوبُهُمۡۗ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَهَادِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

And so that those whom Allah gave knowledge are convinced that the Quran revealed to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم is the truth Allah revealed to you, O Messenger, thus increasing their faith in it and so that their hearts submit and humble themselves before it. Allah is the Guide of those who have faith toward the straight path of truth, which contains no crookedness, as a reward for submitting to it.

(٥٥) وَلَا يَزَالُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فِي مِرۡيَةٖ مِّنۡهُ حَتَّىٰ تَأۡتِيَهُمُ ٱلسَّاعَةُ بَغۡتَةً أَوۡ يَأۡتِيَهُمۡ عَذَابُ يَوۡمٍ عَقِيمٍ

Those who disbelieve in Allah and reject His Messenger will remain in doubt about the Quran Allah revealed to you. They will continue upon that until the Hour comes to them suddenly or the punishment of a Day comes to them in which there is no mercy or good for them, which is the Day of Resurrection.

Points of Benefit:

The luring of the oppressor until they persist in their wrongdoing is an established divine practice.
Allah's preservation of His book from alteration and distortion, deflecting the plots of the devil's allies.
Hypocrisy and hardness of hearts are two deadly diseases.
Belief is the fruit of knowledge, and humility and submission to Allah's commands are the fruits of belief.
