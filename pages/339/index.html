(٥٦) ٱلۡمُلۡكُ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلَّهِ يَحۡكُمُ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ فَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ فِي جَنَّٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

Dominion on the Day of Resurrection - when the punishment they were promised shall come to them - will only be for Allah alone. He will have no competitor for it. He (may He be Exalted) will judge between the believers and the disbelievers. He will judge each of them as they deserve: those who believed and did righteous deeds will be granted a great reward in gardens of everlasting bliss that will never end.

(٥٧) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَكَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ مُّهِينٞ

And those who disbelieved in Allah and rejected the signs/verses He revealed to His Messenger will be humiliated with a humiliating punishment in Hell.

(٥٨) وَٱلَّذِينَ هَاجَرُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ قُتِلُوٓاْ أَوۡ مَاتُواْ لَيَرۡزُقَنَّهُمُ ٱللَّهُ رِزۡقًا حَسَنٗاۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّٰزِقِينَ

And those who left their homes and homelands seeking Allah's pleasure and in honor of His religion and were killed while striving in His path or died, Allah shall grant them an everlasting provision in Paradise. Indeed, Allah (may He be Exalted) is the Best of providers.

(٥٩) لَيُدۡخِلَنَّهُم مُّدۡخَلٗا يَرۡضَوۡنَهُۥۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلِيمٌ حَلِيمٞ

Allah will admit them into a place they shall be pleased with (i.e., Paradise). Allah knows their deeds and intentions; He is forbearing in that He did not hasten to punish them for their negligence.

(٦٠) ۞ذَٰلِكَۖ وَمَنۡ عَاقَبَ بِمِثۡلِ مَا عُوقِبَ بِهِۦ ثُمَّ بُغِيَ عَلَيۡهِ لَيَنصُرَنَّهُ ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَعَفُوٌّ غَفُورٞ

Regarding what was mentioned about those who emigrate in the path of Allah being admitted into Paradise and permission for the oppressed to retaliate justly without incurring sin, if the transgressor returns to his transgression, Allah helps the one subjected to transgression. Allah pardons the sins of the believers and forgives them.

(٦١) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُولِجُ ٱلَّيۡلَ فِي ٱلنَّهَارِ وَيُولِجُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فِي ٱلَّيۡلِ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعُۢ بَصِيرٞ

Helping the one subject to transgression is because Allah is capable of doing as He wishes. His capabilities include merging the night into the day and the day into the night by increasing one and decreasing the other. Allah also hears the statements of His slaves and knows their deeds; nothing is hidden from Him, and He shall recompense them for it accordingly.

(٦٢) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَأَنَّ مَا يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ هُوَ ٱلۡبَٰطِلُ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ

What was mentioned about merging the night into the day and the day into the night is because Allah is the Truth. Consequently, His religion is true, His promise is true, and His helping of the believers is also true. The idols that the polytheists worship instead of Allah are false and have no basis. And Allah is the Most High, above His creation, in essence, power, and subjugation. Allah is the Grand to Whom all majesty, grandeur, and exaltedness belong.

(٦٣) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَتُصۡبِحُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ مُخۡضَرَّةًۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَطِيفٌ خَبِيرٞ

O Messenger, do you not see that Allah sends the rain from the sky, and so the Earth becomes green owing to the vegetation that grows upon it after the rain has fallen? Allah is kind to His slaves by sending rain to them and making the Earth grow crops. He is also aware of their requirements; nothing is hidden from Him.

(٦٤) لَّهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ

To Him alone belongs the dominion of the heavens and the Earth. He is The Independent who never needs His creation; the Praiseworthy in all conditions.

Points of Benefit:

The significance of migration in Islam and an elucidation of its virtues.
The permissibility of retribution in kind.
Allah's support for the one wronged will come either in this world or the Hereafter.
Affirming Allah's sublime attributes in a manner befitting His Majesty, such as Omniscience, Hearing, Sight, and Transcendence.
