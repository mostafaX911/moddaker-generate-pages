(٢١٦) كُتِبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡقِتَالُ وَهُوَ كُرۡهٞ لَّكُمۡۖ وَعَسَىٰٓ أَن تَكۡرَهُواْ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَهُوَ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡۖ وَعَسَىٰٓ أَن تُحِبُّواْ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَهُوَ شَرّٞ لَّكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

Fighting in Allah's path has been chosen for believers even though it is something that is naturally disliked because it means risking one's life and wealth. However, you may dislike something when, in reality, it is good and beneficial. An example of this is fighting in Allah's path. In addition to the great reward, you attain victory against the enemy and promote Allah's word. On the other hand, you may like something when, in reality, it is bad and harmful to you, such as staying behind from fighting: this will result in you being defeated and the enemy gaining authority over you. Allah knows full well what is good and what is not, whereas you do not. Therefore, follow His commands, for that is better for you.

(٢١٧) يَسۡ‍َٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلشَّهۡرِ ٱلۡحَرَامِ قِتَالٖ فِيهِۖ قُلۡ قِتَالٞ فِيهِ كَبِيرٞۚ وَصَدٌّ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَكُفۡرُۢ بِهِۦ وَٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِ وَإِخۡرَاجُ أَهۡلِهِۦ مِنۡهُ أَكۡبَرُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱلۡفِتۡنَةُ أَكۡبَرُ مِنَ ٱلۡقَتۡلِۗ وَلَا يَزَالُونَ يُقَٰتِلُونَكُمۡ حَتَّىٰ يَرُدُّوكُمۡ عَن دِينِكُمۡ إِنِ ٱسۡتَطَٰعُواْۚ وَمَن يَرۡتَدِدۡ مِنكُمۡ عَن دِينِهِۦ فَيَمُتۡ وَهُوَ كَافِرٞ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ حَبِطَتۡ أَعۡمَٰلُهُمۡ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

The people ask you, O Prophet, about the law regarding fighting in the sacred months of Dhū Al-Qa'dah, Dhul-Hijjah, Muḥarram, and Rajab. Say to them: Fighting in these months is a serious offense in the sight of Allah and is detested, just as the polytheists' preventing from the path of Allah is also disapproved. Blocking the believers' access to the Sacred Mosque and removing the people of the Sacred Mosque from it is worse than fighting in a sacred month in the sight of Allah. The idolatry that they are engulfed in is worse than killing. O believers, the polytheists will continue their oppression by fighting you until they turn you away from your religion of truth towards their false religion if they can. If anyone turns back from his religion and dies upon disbelief in Allah, his good deeds will become void, and his outcome in the Afterlife will be the Fire, wherein he will live eternally.

(٢١٨) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱلَّذِينَ هَاجَرُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يَرۡجُونَ رَحۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Those who believe in Allah and His Messenger, leave their homes as emigrants towards Allah and His Messenger, and fight to promote and ensure Allah's word reigns supreme are the ones who are eager for Allah's mercy and forgiveness. Allah is Most Forgiving of the sins of His slaves and is All-Merciful towards them.

(٢١٩) ۞يَسۡ‍َٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلۡخَمۡرِ وَٱلۡمَيۡسِرِۖ قُلۡ فِيهِمَآ إِثۡمٞ كَبِيرٞ وَمَنَٰفِعُ لِلنَّاسِ وَإِثۡمُهُمَآ أَكۡبَرُ مِن نَّفۡعِهِمَاۗ وَيَسۡ‍َٔلُونَكَ مَاذَا يُنفِقُونَۖ قُلِ ٱلۡعَفۡوَۗ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَفَكَّرُونَ

O Prophet, your Companions ask you about wine (which refers to anything that impairs the mind or intoxicates it), specifically concerning the legality of its drinking, selling, and purchasing. They also ask you about gambling (which refers to wealth taken in a competition between two people in which both contribute to the winning total). In response to them, say: They both have many spiritual and worldly harms, such as loss of intellect and wealth and breeding hatred and enmity. On the other hand, there are a few benefits, such as a financial reward for the winner of a bet. However, the harm and sin resulting from them outweigh the benefits, so a logical person should avoid such actions. This statement of Allah lays the foundation for the prohibition of wine. They also ask you, O Prophet, how much they should spend on charity. Say in reply to them: Spend whatever is easy and in excess of your needs. With such clear explanations, Allah describes the sacred law to you so that you may reflect. Initially, this was the law regarding charity, but later, Allah established the obligation of Zakat, which requires paying a set amount on certain possessions if one has owned such possessions for a particular period of time.

Points of Benefit:

Ignorance of the consequences can lead a person to hate what benefits him and love what harms him. One should ask Allah for guidance.
Islam puts importance on sacred things and prohibits infringing upon them. One of the greatest infringements is to prevent people from the path of Allah, the Exalted.
The disbelievers will never cease waging war against Islam and its people until they make them leave their Religion if only they could. But Allah renders the plots of the disbelievers useless.
Believing in Allah, the Exalted, migrating to Him, and striving in His path are among the greatest means for a person to attain the Mercy and Forgiveness of Allah.
In the best interests of the slaves, the legislation prohibits anything that is primarily harmful, even if it has some benefit.
