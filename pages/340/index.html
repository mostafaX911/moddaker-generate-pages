(٦٥) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَخَّرَ لَكُم مَّا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلۡفُلۡكَ تَجۡرِي فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِأَمۡرِهِۦ وَيُمۡسِكُ ٱلسَّمَآءَ أَن تَقَعَ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِٱلنَّاسِ لَرَءُوفٞ رَّحِيمٞ

O Messenger, do you not see that Allah has made the animals and other objects on Earth subservient to you and people's needs and benefits? He also made subservient for you the ships that sail the oceans with His command and subjugation, sailing from land to land. He also holds back the sky so it does not fall onto Earth except with His order. If He allowed it to fall, it would. Allah is Kind and Merciful, so He subjugated these things for them despite their wrongdoing.

(٦٦) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَحۡيَاكُمۡ ثُمَّ يُمِيتُكُمۡ ثُمَّ يُحۡيِيكُمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَكَفُورٞ

Allah is the only One Who gave you life because He brought you into existence when you were nonexistent. He will then cause you to die when your lives expire and then revive you after death to hold you accountable for your deeds and recompense you for them accordingly. Verily, man is ever in denial of the favors of Allah by worshipping other than Allah, despite those favors being so apparent.

(٦٧) لِّكُلِّ أُمَّةٖ جَعَلۡنَا مَنسَكًا هُمۡ نَاسِكُوهُۖ فَلَا يُنَٰزِعُنَّكَ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡرِۚ وَٱدۡعُ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَۖ إِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ هُدٗى مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

I have made a set of laws for people of every creed. O Messenger, do not let the polytheists and people of other religions dispute your law with you because you are more deserving of the truth than them, as they are the people of falsehood. Invite the people towards sincerely proclaiming the oneness of Allah; indeed, you are on a straight path in which there is no deviation.

(٦٨) وَإِن جَٰدَلُوكَ فَقُلِ ٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ

If they insist on arguing with you after the evidence has become clear, leave their matter to Allah and warn them: Allah knows the deeds you do, none of your deeds are hidden from Him, and He will recompense you for them accordingly.

(٦٩) ٱللَّهُ يَحۡكُمُ بَيۡنَكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فِيمَا كُنتُمۡ فِيهِ تَخۡتَلِفُونَ

Allah will judge between the believers and the disbelievers from among His slaves on the Day of Resurrection in the matters of religion they differed over in this world.

(٧٠) أَلَمۡ تَعۡلَمۡ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ فِي كِتَٰبٍۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرٞ

O Messenger, do you not know that Allah knows all that is in the heavens and on Earth; nothing in them is hidden from Him, and His knowledge of it is recorded in the Preserved Tablet. Knowing all of that is easy for Allah.

(٧١) وَيَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَمۡ يُنَزِّلۡ بِهِۦ سُلۡطَٰنٗا وَمَا لَيۡسَ لَهُم بِهِۦ عِلۡمٞۗ وَمَا لِلظَّٰلِمِينَ مِن نَّصِيرٖ

The polytheists worship idols instead of Allah; idols which Allah did not reveal any proof of their worship [being acceptable] in His books. Neither do they have any evidence of knowledge for it; their only support for that is blind following of their forefathers. The wrongdoers will have no helper that will protect them from Allah's torment that will befall them.

(٧٢) وَإِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُنَا بَيِّنَٰتٖ تَعۡرِفُ فِي وُجُوهِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ٱلۡمُنكَرَۖ يَكَادُونَ يَسۡطُونَ بِٱلَّذِينَ يَتۡلُونَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِنَاۗ قُلۡ أَفَأُنَبِّئُكُم بِشَرّٖ مِّن ذَٰلِكُمُۚ ٱلنَّارُ وَعَدَهَا ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

When you recite Our clear verses of the Quran to them, you will recognize the objecting frowns on the faces of the disbelievers when they hear them. It is as if their extreme anger would make them violently assault those who recite Our verses to them. O Messenger, say to them: Shall I inform you of something worse than this anger and frowning? It is the Fire that Allah has promised to admit the disbelievers into. How terrible their abode is!

Points of Benefit:

One of Allah's blessings upon people is the subjugation of what is in the heavens and the earth for their benefit.
Affirming the attributes of Compassion and Mercy for Allah, the Exalted.
Allah's comprehensive knowledge of what is in the heavens and the earth and everything between them.
Blind conformity is a cause for polytheists clinging to their association of partners with Allah.
