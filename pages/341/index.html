(٧٣) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ضُرِبَ مَثَلٞ فَٱسۡتَمِعُواْ لَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ لَن يَخۡلُقُواْ ذُبَابٗا وَلَوِ ٱجۡتَمَعُواْ لَهُۥۖ وَإِن يَسۡلُبۡهُمُ ٱلذُّبَابُ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا لَّا يَسۡتَنقِذُوهُ مِنۡهُۚ ضَعُفَ ٱلطَّالِبُ وَٱلۡمَطۡلُوبُ

O people, a parable is about to be presented, so listen carefully and take a lesson from it. Indeed, those idols and other things you worship instead of Allah cannot even create a fly despite its small size because of its inability. Even if they were to convene to try to create it, they would not be able to achieve it. And if a fly were to take anything away from them, such as perfume, etc., they would not have the power to reclaim it. Their inability to create a fly and reclaim anything from it makes their inability to do anything greater than that evident. How can they worship these idols instead of Allah, despite their inability? This pursuer - i.e., the worshipped idol incapable of reclaiming whatever the fly has taken from it- and the pursued, the fly, are both weak.

(٧٤) مَا قَدَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ حَقَّ قَدۡرِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَقَوِيٌّ عَزِيزٌ

They did not revere Allah as He deserves to be revered when they worshipped some of His creation along with worshipping Him. Indeed, Allah is Strong, and He created the heavens, the Earth, and everything within them due to this strength and power. He is the Almighty; no one can overpower Him, unlike the idols of the polytheists, which are weak and powerless [unable] to create anything.

(٧٥) ٱللَّهُ يَصۡطَفِي مِنَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ رُسُلٗا وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعُۢ بَصِيرٞ

Allah (may He be Exalted) selects messengers from the angels and humans. He sends some of the angels to the prophets, like Gabriel, whom He sent to the human messengers, and He also sends human messengers to humanity. Allah hears what the polytheists say regarding His messengers and is insightful of who He selects to convey His message.

(٧٦) يَعۡلَمُ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُمۡۚ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ تُرۡجَعُ ٱلۡأُمُورُ

Allah (may He be Exalted) knows the affairs of His messengers, from both the angels and humans, even before their creation and after their death. To Allah alone shall all matters return on the Day of Resurrection, when He will resurrect His slaves and recompense them accordingly for the deeds they sent forth.

(٧٧) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱرۡكَعُواْ وَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ وَٱعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ وَٱفۡعَلُواْ ٱلۡخَيۡرَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ۩

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, bow down and prostrate to Him alone in your prayer; do good through charity and joining family ties, etc., hoping you succeed in your objective and achieve salvation from that which you fear.

(٧٨) وَجَٰهِدُواْ فِي ٱللَّهِ حَقَّ جِهَادِهِۦۚ هُوَ ٱجۡتَبَىٰكُمۡ وَمَا جَعَلَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِ مِنۡ حَرَجٖۚ مِّلَّةَ أَبِيكُمۡ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَۚ هُوَ سَمَّىٰكُمُ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ مِن قَبۡلُ وَفِي هَٰذَا لِيَكُونَ ٱلرَّسُولُ شَهِيدًا عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَتَكُونُواْ شُهَدَآءَ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِۚ فَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَٱعۡتَصِمُواْ بِٱللَّهِ هُوَ مَوۡلَىٰكُمۡۖ فَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡمَوۡلَىٰ وَنِعۡمَ ٱلنَّصِيرُ

And strive in the path of Allah sincerely for His sake. He has selected you and made your religion a religion of ease, in which there is no constriction and difficulty. This easy creed is the creed of your forefather Abraham (peace be upon him). Allah has named you 'Muslims' in the previous books and in the Quran, so that the Messenger can be a witness against you in that he conveyed the message he was commanded to convey, and so that you can be witnesses against the previous nations in that their messengers also conveyed their messages respectively. So show gratitude to Allah for that by performing prayer as perfectly as possible, giving Zakat from your wealth, seeking refuge in Allah, and relying upon Him in all your matters, for He is the best protector (may He be Exalted) for those believers who He aids, and the best of helpers for those of them who seek His help. So seek His aid, He will aid you, and seek His help, He will help you.

Points of Benefit:

The importance of using parables to clarify meanings, a noble educational method.
The incapacity of idols to create the slightest thing is evidence of their inability to create anything else.
Associating partners with Allah is caused by not venerating Allah.
Affirming the attributes of Strength and Might for Allah, and the importance for the believer to contemplate the meanings of these attributes.
