The Believers (Surah Al-Mu'minun)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Detailing the believers' success and the non-believers' loss.

Explanation:

(١) قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ

The believers in Allah who implemented His legislation have succeeded by attaining what they seek and being saved from what they dread.

(٢) ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ فِي صَلَاتِهِمۡ خَٰشِعُونَ

Those who, when in prayer, are humble, their limbs have become tranquil therein, and their hearts are free of distractions.

(٣) وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَنِ ٱللَّغۡوِ مُعۡرِضُونَ

Those who turned away from falsehood, futility, and sinful speech and actions.

(٤) وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِلزَّكَوٰةِ فَٰعِلُونَ

Those who purified themselves of evil characteristics and purified their wealth by paying their Zakat (obligatory alms).

(٥) وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِفُرُوجِهِمۡ حَٰفِظُونَ

Those who safeguarded their private parts by keeping them far from fornication, homosexuality, and shameful actions. Thus, they are pure and chaste.

(٦) إِلَّا عَلَىٰٓ أَزۡوَٰجِهِمۡ أَوۡ مَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُهُمۡ فَإِنَّهُمۡ غَيۡرُ مَلُومِينَ

Except with their wives and what they own of female slaves, they are blameless for enjoying them through sexual intercourse and the like.

(٧) فَمَنِ ٱبۡتَغَىٰ وَرَآءَ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡعَادُونَ

So whosoever seeks pleasure from other than his wives and female slaves that he possesses, he is transgressing the limits of Allah by delving into that which Allah has forbidden, without keeping to that which He has kept lawful.

(٨) وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِأَمَٰنَٰتِهِمۡ وَعَهۡدِهِمۡ رَٰعُونَ

And those who fulfill and do not neglect the trusts Allah has entrusted them with or any of His slaves have entrusted them with. Instead, they are loyal to fulfilling them.

(٩) وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَوَٰتِهِمۡ يُحَافِظُونَ

And those who consistently keep their prayers and perform them punctually with all its pillars, obligations, and recommended acts.

(١٠) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثُونَ

Those possessing these characteristics are the true inheritors.

(١١) ٱلَّذِينَ يَرِثُونَ ٱلۡفِرۡدَوۡسَ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

They will inherit the loftiest level of paradise, wherein they will abide forever. Their bounties in it will never finish.

(١٢) وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِن سُلَٰلَةٖ مِّن طِينٖ

And We have created Adam, the father of humanity, from clay, which was extracted from water mixed with the soil of the Earth.

(١٣) ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَٰهُ نُطۡفَةٗ فِي قَرَارٖ مَّكِينٖ

Then, We created his progeny, who procreate through a drop of sperm that settles in the womb until birth.

(١٤) ثُمَّ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلنُّطۡفَةَ عَلَقَةٗ فَخَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡعَلَقَةَ مُضۡغَةٗ فَخَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡمُضۡغَةَ عِظَٰمٗا فَكَسَوۡنَا ٱلۡعِظَٰمَ لَحۡمٗا ثُمَّ أَنشَأۡنَٰهُ خَلۡقًا ءَاخَرَۚ فَتَبَارَكَ ٱللَّهُ أَحۡسَنُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقِينَ

Then, We made the settled drop of sperm into a clingy, red blood clot. Then, We made that clingy, red blood clot into a masticated piece of flesh. We then made the piece of flesh into solid bones. We then covered the bones with flesh. We then made it into a different creation by breathing the soul into it and bringing it to life. So Blessed is Allah, the Best of Creators.

(١٥) ثُمَّ إِنَّكُم بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ لَمَيِّتُونَ

Then you, O Humanity, after developing through all those stages, will die when your appointed times end.

(١٦) ثُمَّ إِنَّكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ تُبۡعَثُونَ

Then, after your death, you shall be resurrected from your graves on the Day of Resurrection so that you may be held to account for whatever deeds you put forward.

(١٧) وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا فَوۡقَكُمۡ سَبۡعَ طَرَآئِقَ وَمَا كُنَّا عَنِ ٱلۡخَلۡقِ غَٰفِلِينَ

Indeed, We have created above you, O Humanity, seven heavens, one above the other. We were never neglectful of Our creation nor forgetful of it.

Points of Benefit:

Success has diverse reasons that are beneficial to understand and prioritize.
The gradual development in creation and divine legislation is an established divine practice.
Allah's comprehensive knowledge of His creations.
