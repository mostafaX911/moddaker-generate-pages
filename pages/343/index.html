(١٨) وَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءَۢ بِقَدَرٖ فَأَسۡكَنَّٰهُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَإِنَّا عَلَىٰ ذَهَابِۢ بِهِۦ لَقَٰدِرُونَ

And We have sent rainwater down from the sky as needed, not an excessive amount to cause ruin, nor in scarcity that is not enough. We have caused it to settle on earth so that humans and animals may benefit from it. We can take it away so you will not benefit from it.

(١٩) فَأَنشَأۡنَا لَكُم بِهِۦ جَنَّٰتٖ مِّن نَّخِيلٖ وَأَعۡنَٰبٖ لَّكُمۡ فِيهَا فَوَٰكِهُ كَثِيرَةٞ وَمِنۡهَا تَأۡكُلُونَ

And with that water, we assembled orchards of palms and vineyards for you. In them, you have various fruits of different shapes and colors, like figs, pomegranates, and apples, and from them, you eat.

(٢٠) وَشَجَرَةٗ تَخۡرُجُ مِن طُورِ سَيۡنَآءَ تَنۢبُتُ بِٱلدُّهۡنِ وَصِبۡغٖ لِّلۡأٓكِلِينَ

And We created the olive tree, which originates from Mount Sinai. It produces oil, which is extracted from its fruit. It is used as an ointment and is eaten.

(٢١) وَإِنَّ لَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ لَعِبۡرَةٗۖ نُّسۡقِيكُم مِّمَّا فِي بُطُونِهَا وَلَكُمۡ فِيهَا مَنَٰفِعُ كَثِيرَةٞ وَمِنۡهَا تَأۡكُلُونَ

And indeed, O humanity, you have a lesson to take from grazing livestock (camels, cows, and sheep) in that you can find evidence of the power of Allah and His kindness to you. We give you drink from their bellies; pure milk, palatable to those who drink it. You get many other benefits from them, like conveyance, wool, fur, and bristles, and you eat from their meat.

(٢٢) وَعَلَيۡهَا وَعَلَى ٱلۡفُلۡكِ تُحۡمَلُونَ

You are carried on the camels across land and the ships at sea.

(٢٣) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ فَقَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥٓۚ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ

Indeed, We sent Noah (peace be upon him) to his people, calling them to Allah. He said to them: O my people, worship Allah alone; you have no one worthy of worship but Him, may He be Exalted. Will you not fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions?

(٢٤) فَقَالَ ٱلۡمَلَؤُاْ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن قَوۡمِهِۦ مَا هَٰذَآ إِلَّا بَشَرٞ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ يُرِيدُ أَن يَتَفَضَّلَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَأَنزَلَ مَلَٰٓئِكَةٗ مَّا سَمِعۡنَا بِهَٰذَا فِيٓ ءَابَآئِنَا ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

So the noblemen and leaders from his people who disbelieved in Allah said to their followers and the masses: This man that claims to be a messenger is only a human like yourselves; he only desires leadership and rule over you. If Allah wished to send a messenger to you, He would have sent one from the angels, not from the humans. We have not heard anything like what he claims from our predecessors!

(٢٥) إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا رَجُلُۢ بِهِۦ جِنَّةٞ فَتَرَبَّصُواْ بِهِۦ حَتَّىٰ حِينٖ

He [The messenger] is only a madman. He does not understand what he is saying. Wait and see when his true reality becomes apparent to the people.

(٢٦) قَالَ رَبِّ ٱنصُرۡنِي بِمَا كَذَّبُونِ

Noah (peace be upon him) said: O my Lord, grant me victory against them by avenging me for their rejection and defiance.

(٢٧) فَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِ أَنِ ٱصۡنَعِ ٱلۡفُلۡكَ بِأَعۡيُنِنَا وَوَحۡيِنَا فَإِذَا جَآءَ أَمۡرُنَا وَفَارَ ٱلتَّنُّورُ فَٱسۡلُكۡ فِيهَا مِن كُلّٖ زَوۡجَيۡنِ ٱثۡنَيۡنِ وَأَهۡلَكَ إِلَّا مَن سَبَقَ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ مِنۡهُمۡۖ وَلَا تُخَٰطِبۡنِي فِي ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوٓاْ إِنَّهُم مُّغۡرَقُونَ

So We revealed to him: Construct a ship under Our direction and Our tutorship to you on how to build it. And if Our command comes to destroy them, and the water gushes out from the place We told you it would then enter upon the ship a male and female from all living creatures so procreation will continue. Then join your family except those who preceded the decree of destruction, like your wife and son. Do not address me concerning those disbelieving wrongdoers by asking that they be saved and not destroyed. They will be destroyed, without a doubt, by drowning in the great flood.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's kindness towards His slaves is apparent in His sending down rain and making it easy for them to benefit from it.
Mention of the olive tree's status,
The polytheists' belief in the divinity of rocks, along with their denying prophethood from mankind, is proof of the foolishness of their intellects.
Allah's Help for His Messengers is affirmed when their nations deny them.
