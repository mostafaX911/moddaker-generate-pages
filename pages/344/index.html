(٢٨) فَإِذَا ٱسۡتَوَيۡتَ أَنتَ وَمَن مَّعَكَ عَلَى ٱلۡفُلۡكِ فَقُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي نَجَّىٰنَا مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

When you board the ship with the believers achieving salvation, say: All praise is for Allah, Who saved us from the disbelieving people and destroyed them.

(٢٩) وَقُل رَّبِّ أَنزِلۡنِي مُنزَلٗا مُّبَارَكٗا وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡمُنزِلِينَ

And say: O my Lord! Cause me to disembark in a blessed place, for You are the best of those who cause to disembark.

(٣٠) إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ وَإِن كُنَّا لَمُبۡتَلِينَ

Indeed, what was mentioned from the saving of Noah and the believers with him and the destruction of the disbelievers is clear evidence of Our Power in Aiding Our messengers and destroying their rejectors. We had indeed tested the people of Noah by sending him to make the believer apparent from the disbeliever and the obedient one from the sinner.

(٣١) ثُمَّ أَنشَأۡنَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ قَرۡنًا ءَاخَرِينَ

Then, We brought into existence another nation after the destruction of the people of Noah.

(٣٢) فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا فِيهِمۡ رَسُولٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ أَنِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥٓۚ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ

We sent them a messenger from amongst them calling them to Allah. He told them: Worship Allah alone; you have no one worthy of worship but Him; may He be Exalted. Will you not fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions?

(٣٣) وَقَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ مِن قَوۡمِهِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَكَذَّبُواْ بِلِقَآءِ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَأَتۡرَفۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا مَا هَٰذَآ إِلَّا بَشَرٞ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ يَأۡكُلُ مِمَّا تَأۡكُلُونَ مِنۡهُ وَيَشۡرَبُ مِمَّا تَشۡرَبُونَ

The noblemen and leaders from his people who disbelieved in Allah denied the hereafter and what it has of reward and punishment and transgressed against the vastness of the worldly blessings we gave them, told their followers and the masses: He is nothing but a human like you, he eats what you eat and drinks what you drink, he has no virtue over you to be sent as a messenger to you.

(٣٤) وَلَئِنۡ أَطَعۡتُم بَشَرٗا مِّثۡلَكُمۡ إِنَّكُمۡ إِذٗا لَّخَٰسِرُونَ

And if you were to obey another human like you, you would be losers as you would not benefit from obeying him by leaving your deities and following someone with no virtue over you.

(٣٥) أَيَعِدُكُمۡ أَنَّكُمۡ إِذَا مِتُّمۡ وَكُنتُمۡ تُرَابٗا وَعِظَٰمًا أَنَّكُم مُّخۡرَجُونَ

Does this person who claims to be a messenger promise you that if you were to die and become dust and bones, you would somehow come out of your grave alive? Is this conceivable?

(٣٦) ۞هَيۡهَاتَ هَيۡهَاتَ لِمَا تُوعَدُونَ

The promise that you will return to life after being dead and dusty bones in your grave is far-fetched.

(٣٧) إِنۡ هِيَ إِلَّا حَيَاتُنَا ٱلدُّنۡيَا نَمُوتُ وَنَحۡيَا وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمَبۡعُوثِينَ

Life is only this worldly life, and there is no Afterlife. Our living will die and will never live again. Others born after us live on. We will never come out of our graves after death to be held to account on the Day of Resurrection.

(٣٨) إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا رَجُلٌ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبٗا وَمَا نَحۡنُ لَهُۥ بِمُؤۡمِنِينَ

This person who claims he is a messenger to you is nothing but a man who fabricated a lie against Allah by this claim. We will never believe him.

(٣٩) قَالَ رَبِّ ٱنصُرۡنِي بِمَا كَذَّبُونِ

The Messenger said: O Lord, grant me victory over them by avenging me for their rejection of me.

(٤٠) قَالَ عَمَّا قَلِيلٖ لَّيُصۡبِحُنَّ نَٰدِمِينَ

So Allah answered him, saying: Very soon, the ones who have rejected what you came with will be regretful for what they did.

(٤١) فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلصَّيۡحَةُ بِٱلۡحَقِّ فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ غُثَآءٗۚ فَبُعۡدٗا لِّلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

So they were seized by a deafening shriek for what they deserved as punishment for their defiance. It killed them all, rendering them like the foam of floodwater. Destruction is for the wrongdoing people.

(٤٢) ثُمَّ أَنشَأۡنَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ قُرُونًا ءَاخَرِينَ

Then after their destruction, We created other peoples and nations, such as the people of Lot, the people of Shuayb, and the people of Jonah.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation of praising Allah for His blessings.
Luxury in this life leads to heedlessness and arrogance regarding the Truth.
The disbeliever's outcome is regret and loss.
Oppression is a means of becoming distanced from Allah's Mercy.
