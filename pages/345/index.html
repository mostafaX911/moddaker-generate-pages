(٤٣) مَا تَسۡبِقُ مِنۡ أُمَّةٍ أَجَلَهَا وَمَا يَسۡتَ‍ٔۡخِرُونَ

None of these rejecting nations will precede the time fixed for their destruction, nor will they be delayed from it, regardless of whatever means they have at their disposal.

(٤٤) ثُمَّ أَرۡسَلۡنَا رُسُلَنَا تَتۡرَاۖ كُلَّ مَا جَآءَ أُمَّةٗ رَّسُولُهَا كَذَّبُوهُۖ فَأَتۡبَعۡنَا بَعۡضَهُم بَعۡضٗا وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ أَحَادِيثَۚ فَبُعۡدٗا لِّقَوۡمٖ لَّا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

We then sent Our Messengers in succession, one after the other. Each time a messenger came to his nation, they rejected him, so We successively destroyed them, leaving nothing but the tales of people regarding them. So destruction be to the nation that does not believe in what their messengers came with from their Lord.

(٤٥) ثُمَّ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مُوسَىٰ وَأَخَاهُ هَٰرُونَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٍ

We then sent Moses and his brother Aaron with Our nine signs (the staff, the hand, locusts, lice, frogs, blood, floods, droughts, and a shortage of crops) along with a clear argument.

(٤٦) إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَمَلَإِيْهِۦ فَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ وَكَانُواْ قَوۡمًا عَالِينَ

We sent both of them to Pharaoh and the noblemen of his people, but they displayed arrogance and did not submit to believing in them. They imposed themselves on the people by subduing and oppressing them.

(٤٧) فَقَالُوٓاْ أَنُؤۡمِنُ لِبَشَرَيۡنِ مِثۡلِنَا وَقَوۡمُهُمَا لَنَا عَٰبِدُونَ

They said: Why should we believe in two humans like us, who have no virtue over us, while their tribe (the Israelites) are subservient and subdued to us?

(٤٨) فَكَذَّبُوهُمَا فَكَانُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡمُهۡلَكِينَ

So they rejected whatever message they brought to them from Allah. And because of their rejection, they were destroyed by drowning.

(٤٩) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَهۡتَدُونَ

And Indeed, We gave Moses the Torah in the hope that his people would be guided to the truth by it and act upon it.

(٥٠) وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ وَأُمَّهُۥٓ ءَايَةٗ وَءَاوَيۡنَٰهُمَآ إِلَىٰ رَبۡوَةٖ ذَاتِ قَرَارٖ وَمَعِينٖ

And We made Jesus, the son of Mary, and his mother Mary, signs evident of Our capability, as she conceived him without a father. We sheltered them on high, level ground suitable for their shelter, having fresh, flowing water.

(٥١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلرُّسُلُ كُلُواْ مِنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ وَٱعۡمَلُواْ صَٰلِحًاۖ إِنِّي بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ عَلِيمٞ

O Messengers, eat appealingly from what I have made lawful for you. Do righteous deeds in accordance with the law; I am Ever-Knowing of what you do; none of your actions are hidden from Me.

(٥٢) وَإِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦٓ أُمَّتُكُمۡ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ وَأَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمۡ فَٱتَّقُونِ

And your religion, O Messengers, is one religion, and that is Islam. And I am your Lord; you have no other Lord but Me. So fear Me by fulfilling My commands and avoiding My prohibitions.

(٥٣) فَتَقَطَّعُوٓاْ أَمۡرَهُم بَيۡنَهُمۡ زُبُرٗاۖ كُلُّ حِزۡبِۢ بِمَا لَدَيۡهِمۡ فَرِحُونَ

After they passed away, their followers were divided in religion. They became sects and groups, with each group conceited in believing that whatever it believed in was the true acceptable religion according to Allah, disregarding the views of anyone else.

(٥٤) فَذَرۡهُمۡ فِي غَمۡرَتِهِمۡ حَتَّىٰ حِينٍ

So leave them, O Messenger, in whatever they are in of ignorance and confusion until the punishment descends upon them.

(٥٥) أَيَحۡسَبُونَ أَنَّمَا نُمِدُّهُم بِهِۦ مِن مَّالٖ وَبَنِينَ

Do these conceited groups think that whatever wealth and offspring We bestow upon them is a prelude to the good they deserve? It is not as they think; we only give it to them as respite, gradually leading them into destruction, but they do not realize it.

(٥٦) نُسَارِعُ لَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِۚ بَل لَّا يَشۡعُرُونَ

Do these conceited groups think that whatever wealth and offspring We bestow upon them is a prelude to the good they deserve? It is not as they think; we only give it to them as respite, gradually leading them into destruction, but they do not realize it.

(٥٧) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ هُم مِّنۡ خَشۡيَةِ رَبِّهِم مُّشۡفِقُونَ

Those who, despite their belief and righteousness, are still fearful of their Lord.

(٥٨) وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

And those who believe in the verses of His book.

(٥٩) وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِرَبِّهِمۡ لَا يُشۡرِكُونَ

And those who single out their Lord in worship and do not associate anything with Him.

Points of Benefit:

Arrogance prevents divine success towards the Truth.
Purity of food has an effect in rectifying the heart and actions.
Tawheed (monotheism) is the religion of every prophet and their call.
Bestowal of blessings on the sinner is not meant to honor, but rather a gradual lure towards destruction.
