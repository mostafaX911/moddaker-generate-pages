(٦٠) وَٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡتُونَ مَآ ءَاتَواْ وَّقُلُوبُهُمۡ وَجِلَةٌ أَنَّهُمۡ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ رَٰجِعُونَ

And those who strive in acts of benevolence and get closer to Allah by righteous deeds while they are fearful that Allah will not accept their spending and good deeds when they return to Him on the Day of Resurrection.

(٦١) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ وَهُمۡ لَهَا سَٰبِقُونَ

Those who are described by these great attributes hasten to righteous deeds and are first to do them; it is for those attributes they surpass everyone else.

(٦٢) وَلَا نُكَلِّفُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۚ وَلَدَيۡنَا كِتَٰبٞ يَنطِقُ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ

And We do not charge a soul except with what it can do. We have a book that records the deeds of every doer. It speaks the truth in which there is no doubt. They will never be wronged by having their good deeds decreased or their bad deeds increased.

(٦٣) بَلۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡ فِي غَمۡرَةٖ مِّنۡ هَٰذَا وَلَهُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلٞ مِّن دُونِ ذَٰلِكَ هُمۡ لَهَا عَٰمِلُونَ

Rather, the hearts of the disbelievers are heedless to this Book, which states the truth, and also, the Book which was revealed to them. They have evil deeds besides the disbelief they are upon.

(٦٤) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَخَذۡنَا مُتۡرَفِيهِم بِٱلۡعَذَابِ إِذَا هُمۡ يَجۡ‍َٔرُونَ

Until the affluent ones in this worldly life are punished on the Day of Resurrection, it is then that they will raise their voices seeking help.

(٦٥) لَا تَجۡ‍َٔرُواْ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۖ إِنَّكُم مِّنَّا لَا تُنصَرُونَ

It will be told to them in despair of Allah's mercy: Do not scream or seek help on this day. Verily, no helper for you will prevent Allah's punishment from you.

(٦٦) قَدۡ كَانَتۡ ءَايَٰتِي تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فَكُنتُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَعۡقَٰبِكُمۡ تَنكِصُونَ

Allah's verses were recited to you in the worldly life, and you would turn away from listening to them because of your hatred of them.

(٦٧) مُسۡتَكۡبِرِينَ بِهِۦ سَٰمِرٗا تَهۡجُرُونَ

You did that in arrogance over the people because you thought you were the people of the Haram (sanctuary) while you were not; the genuine people of the Haram are the ones who fear Allah. You also stayed up nights in idle talk near it and did not sanctify it.

(٦٨) أَفَلَمۡ يَدَّبَّرُواْ ٱلۡقَوۡلَ أَمۡ جَآءَهُم مَّا لَمۡ يَأۡتِ ءَابَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

Have not those polytheists contemplated what Allah has revealed of the Quran to believe in and act upon it? Or is it that something has come to them that did not come to their predecessors so they turned away from it and rejected it?

(٦٩) أَمۡ لَمۡ يَعۡرِفُواْ رَسُولَهُمۡ فَهُمۡ لَهُۥ مُنكِرُونَ

Or is it that they do not recognize Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم whom Allah has sent to them, and so they reject him? Indeed, they recognize him, his truthfulness, and his honesty.

(٧٠) أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ بِهِۦ جِنَّةُۢۚ بَلۡ جَآءَهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَأَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لِلۡحَقِّ كَٰرِهُونَ

Instead, do they say that he is insane? Indeed, they have lied. Rather, the truth has come to them, indisputably from Allah. The majority of them dislike the truth, hating it due to their jealousy within themselves and fanaticism over their falsehood.

(٧١) وَلَوِ ٱتَّبَعَ ٱلۡحَقُّ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡ لَفَسَدَتِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمَن فِيهِنَّۚ بَلۡ أَتَيۡنَٰهُم بِذِكۡرِهِمۡ فَهُمۡ عَن ذِكۡرِهِم مُّعۡرِضُونَ

If Allah were to have let matters run and arrange them according to their desires, the heavens, Earth, and all their inhabitants would fall into ruin due to their ignorance of the outcome of matters and their inability to differentiate between good and evil plans. Instead, we have brought them what gives them honor and might, yet they turn away.

(٧٢) أَمۡ تَسۡ‍َٔلُهُمۡ خَرۡجٗا فَخَرَاجُ رَبِّكَ خَيۡرٞۖ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّٰزِقِينَ

Have you, O Messenger, asked for compensation from them for what you brought? And is that why they rejected your calling? This did not occur from you. The reward and recompense of your Lord are far greater than their reward and the reward of others. He, The Exalted, is the Best of Providers.

(٧٣) وَإِنَّكَ لَتَدۡعُوهُمۡ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

And indeed you, O Messenger, call those people and others towards a straight path that has no deviation; it is the path of Islam.

(٧٤) وَإِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ عَنِ ٱلصِّرَٰطِ لَنَٰكِبُونَ

And indeed, those who do not believe in the Hereafter, accountability, punishment, and reward by the path of Islam are inclined towards other crooked paths that lead to the Fire.

Points of Benefit:

The believer's fear of not having his righteous deeds accepted.
Dropping the accountability for what one is unable to do out of mercy for the slaves.
Luxury is from the preventative factors of steadfastness and from the means of destruction.
Man's intellectual shortcomings in understanding many of the beneficial things.
