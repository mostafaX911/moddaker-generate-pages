(٧٥) ۞وَلَوۡ رَحِمۡنَٰهُمۡ وَكَشَفۡنَا مَا بِهِم مِّن ضُرّٖ لَّلَجُّواْ فِي طُغۡيَٰنِهِمۡ يَعۡمَهُونَ

And if We were to have mercy on them and lift their famine and hunger, they would continue being misguided from the truth, confused and stumbling.

(٧٦) وَلَقَدۡ أَخَذۡنَٰهُم بِٱلۡعَذَابِ فَمَا ٱسۡتَكَانُواْ لِرَبِّهِمۡ وَمَا يَتَضَرَّعُونَ

And We have tested them with various calamities, yet they did not humble themselves to their Lord, nor submit to Him. They did not invoke Him submissively to relieve the calamities from them after they befell them.

(٧٧) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا فَتَحۡنَا عَلَيۡهِم بَابٗا ذَا عَذَابٖ شَدِيدٍ إِذَا هُمۡ فِيهِ مُبۡلِسُونَ

Until We opened a door of severe punishment upon them, they showed despair to every relief and good.

Since denial of the Resurrection should not result from someone who benefits from their hearing, sight, and mind, Allah reminds them of what He has bestowed upon them, stating:

(٧٨) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَٱلۡأَفۡ‍ِٔدَةَۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَشۡكُرُونَ

And it is Allah (may He be Exalted) who created for you, O deniers of the resurrection, hearing that you hear with, eyesight that you see with, and hearts to comprehend with. Despite all of those blessings, you are not grateful except slightly.

(٧٩) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي ذَرَأَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ

And He created you, O People, on Earth, and you will be gathered before Him alone on the Day of Resurrection for the recompense and the reckoning.

(٨٠) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُ وَلَهُ ٱخۡتِلَٰفُ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

And He alone (may He be Exalted) can give life; there is no giver of life except Him. He alone can cause death; none causes death except Him. To Him alone belongs the decree of the alternation of the night and day in terms of their darkness and light, length and shortness. You cannot fathom His power and uniqueness in creating and planning.

(٨١) بَلۡ قَالُواْ مِثۡلَ مَا قَالَ ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ

Rather, they said similar to what their forefathers and predecessors in disbelief said.

(٨٢) قَالُوٓاْ أَءِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابٗا وَعِظَٰمًا أَءِنَّا لَمَبۡعُوثُونَ

They said, unbelievingly and denyingly: When we die and turn into dust and decomposed bones, will we be raised alive to be held to account?

(٨٣) لَقَدۡ وُعِدۡنَا نَحۡنُ وَءَابَآؤُنَا هَٰذَا مِن قَبۡلُ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّآ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

We were promised this, which is resurrection after death, and so were our predecessors, but we did not see this promise fulfilled. These are only the false tales and lies of the olden people.

(٨٤) قُل لِّمَنِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمَن فِيهَآ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to those disbelievers who deny the resurrection: To whom do this Earth and those upon it belong, if you should know?

(٨٥) سَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّهِۚ قُلۡ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ

They will say the Earth and those upon it belong to Allah. Then say to them: Do you not ponder that the One who the Earth and those upon it belongs to can bring you back to life after death?

(٨٦) قُلۡ مَن رَّبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ ٱلسَّبۡعِ وَرَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

Tell them: Who is the Lord of the seven heavens? Who is the Lord of the Great Throne, which no creation is greater than it?

(٨٧) سَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّهِۚ قُلۡ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ

They will say the seven heavens and the Great Throne belong to the ownership of Allah, then tell them: Will you not fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions so that you may be saved from His punishment?

(٨٨) قُلۡ مَنۢ بِيَدِهِۦ مَلَكُوتُ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَهُوَ يُجِيرُ وَلَا يُجَارُ عَلَيۡهِ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

Tell them: In whose Hand is the realm of all things while nothing escapes His dominion; He aids whomever He wills of His slaves, and no one can stop any harm He wills to afflict anyone, thus repelling punishment; if you have any knowledge?

(٨٩) سَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّهِۚ قُلۡ فَأَنَّىٰ تُسۡحَرُونَ

They will say the ownership of everything is in His Hand; may He be Exalted. Then tell them: If you acknowledge that, how do your minds wonder and worship others besides Him?

Points of Benefit:

The disbelievers' failure to take heed of the blessings and calamities that befall them is proof of the corruption of their natural dispositions.
Ungratefulness for blessings is from the characteristics of disbelievers.
Adherence to senseless blind following prevents one from reaching the Truth.
A person's affirming Allah's lordship, if not accompanied by affirming His oneness in worship, will not save him.
