(٩٠) بَلۡ أَتَيۡنَٰهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَكَٰذِبُونَ

The matter is not as they claim. But instead, We have brought them the truth in which there is no doubt. They are lying in their claim that Allah has a partner and child. Allah is Transcendent and far beyond from what they say.

(٩١) مَا ٱتَّخَذَ ٱللَّهُ مِن وَلَدٖ وَمَا كَانَ مَعَهُۥ مِنۡ إِلَٰهٍۚ إِذٗا لَّذَهَبَ كُلُّ إِلَٰهِۢ بِمَا خَلَقَ وَلَعَلَا بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖۚ سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ

Allah has not taken a child as the disbelievers claim, nor is there any truly worshiped deity alongside Him. Hypothetically, if there were to be any true deity alongside Him, then every deity would take their share of the creation they made, and they would dominate one another, causing the order of the universe to become corrupt. The reality is that none of this has occurred, proving that the only truly worshiped god is Allah alone. He is Pure and Holy of what the polytheists describe Him with, namely partners and children, which are unbefitting.

(٩٢) عَٰلِمِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ فَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

He knows all that is hidden from His creation. He knows all things that are witnessed and perceived by the senses. Nothing of that is hidden from Him. Far Exalted and Exalted is He above having a partner.

(٩٣) قُل رَّبِّ إِمَّا تُرِيَنِّي مَا يُوعَدُونَ

Say, O Messenger, My Lord, if You should show me that which those polytheists are promised from the punishment.

(٩٤) رَبِّ فَلَا تَجۡعَلۡنِي فِي ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

My Lord, if You punish them and I witness that, then do not make me amongst them so that I would be afflicted with the punishment that afflicts them.

(٩٥) وَإِنَّا عَلَىٰٓ أَن نُّرِيَكَ مَا نَعِدُهُمۡ لَقَٰدِرُونَ

And We have the power to make you witness and see the punishment We have promised them. We are not powerless to do this or anything else.

(٩٦) ٱدۡفَعۡ بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ ٱلسَّيِّئَةَۚ نَحۡنُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يَصِفُونَ

Repel, O Messenger, by the character of what is best, any wrongdoing towards you, by forgiveness and being patient towards harm. We know best of their description of polytheism and denial. We also know best what unbecoming things they describe you with, such as insanity and sorcery.

(٩٧) وَقُل رَّبِّ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنۡ هَمَزَٰتِ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِ

And say: My Lord, I seek refuge in You from the incitements of the devils and their whisperings.

(٩٨) وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ رَبِّ أَن يَحۡضُرُونِ

And I seek refuge in You, my Lord, that they are present with me in my affairs.

(٩٩) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَ أَحَدَهُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ قَالَ رَبِّ ٱرۡجِعُونِ

When death comes to one of these polytheists, and he sees what will befall him, he says while regretting his life that has escaped him and his deficiency regarding Allah: O Lord, return me to the worldly life.

(١٠٠) لَعَلِّيٓ أَعۡمَلُ صَٰلِحٗا فِيمَا تَرَكۡتُۚ كَلَّآۚ إِنَّهَا كَلِمَةٌ هُوَ قَآئِلُهَاۖ وَمِن وَرَآئِهِم بَرۡزَخٌ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ

I may do good actions when I return to it. Never! The matter is different from what you requested. It is only a statement he is uttering. If he were to return to worldly life, he would not fulfill what he promised. These people who have died will remain in a barrier between the world and the Afterlife until the Day of Resurrection. Thus, they will not return from it to the world to make up for what they lost and to correct what they spoiled.

(١٠١) فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَلَآ أَنسَابَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ وَلَا يَتَسَآءَلُونَ

Then when the angel appointed to blow the Trumpet will blow the second time, which will announce the Day of Resurrection, there will be no relations between them to boast through due to their being engrossed in the horrors of the Hereafter, and they will not question one another due to their being absorbed in what concerns them.

(١٠٢) فَمَن ثَقُلَتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ

So whoever's scales are made heavy by their good deeds outweighing their bad ones are the winners who have attained what they desired and been saved from what they feared.

(١٠٣) وَمَنۡ خَفَّتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ فِي جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدُونَ

And whose scales were made light by bad deeds outweighing good ones, then they are the ones who have lost their soul by doing what harms it, abandoning what benefits it from belief and righteous deeds. They will live in the Hellfire and will not exit it.

(١٠٤) تَلۡفَحُ وُجُوهَهُمُ ٱلنَّارُ وَهُمۡ فِيهَا كَٰلِحُونَ

The Fire will burn their faces, and the resulting frowning will cause their upper and lower lips to shrivel up, exposing their teeth.

Points of Benefit:

Using the stability of the creation's system as proof for the oneness of Allah.
Allah's knowledge encompasses everything.
Treating the wrongdoer with kindness is a lofty Islamic etiquette that has a great influence on the opponent.
The necessity of seeking refuge in Allah from Satan's whisperings and temptations.
