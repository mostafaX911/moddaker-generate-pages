(١٠٥) أَلَمۡ تَكُنۡ ءَايَٰتِي تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فَكُنتُم بِهَا تُكَذِّبُونَ

And it will be said to them, chastising them: Were not the verses of the Quran recited to you in the worldly life, yet you denied them?

(١٠٦) قَالُواْ رَبَّنَا غَلَبَتۡ عَلَيۡنَا شِقۡوَتُنَا وَكُنَّا قَوۡمٗا ضَآلِّينَ

They will say: O Our Lord, our wretchedness which You already know of overcame us, and we were a people astray from the truth.

(١٠٧) رَبَّنَآ أَخۡرِجۡنَا مِنۡهَا فَإِنۡ عُدۡنَا فَإِنَّا ظَٰلِمُونَ

O our Lord, take us out of the Fire, and if we go back to what we were upon of disbelief and misguidance, we will have truly wronged ourselves and will have no excuses.

(١٠٨) قَالَ ٱخۡسَ‍ُٔواْ فِيهَا وَلَا تُكَلِّمُونِ

Allah will say: Live in the Fire, disgraced and humiliated, and do not speak to Me.

(١٠٩) إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ فَرِيقٞ مِّنۡ عِبَادِي يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَآ ءَامَنَّا فَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَٱرۡحَمۡنَا وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّٰحِمِينَ

There was a group of My slaves who believed in Me, saying: Our Lord, we have believed in You, so forgive our sins and grant us Your mercy, and You are the best of those who show mercy.

(١١٠) فَٱتَّخَذۡتُمُوهُمۡ سِخۡرِيًّا حَتَّىٰٓ أَنسَوۡكُمۡ ذِكۡرِي وَكُنتُم مِّنۡهُمۡ تَضۡحَكُونَ

So you took those believers who called on their Lord as a joke, mocking and ridiculing them. That ridicule made you forget the remembrance of Allah. You would taunt and laugh at them mockingly.

(١١١) إِنِّي جَزَيۡتُهُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ بِمَا صَبَرُوٓاْ أَنَّهُمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡفَآئِزُونَ

And I have rewarded those believers by granting them Paradise on the Day of Resurrection for being patient in obeying Allah and with the harm they would receive from you.

When they ask to return to this world to correct what they had corrupted, Allah reminds them of what they had done in it, enabling them to repent if they wanted to.

(١١٢) قَٰلَ كَمۡ لَبِثۡتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ عَدَدَ سِنِينَ

He will say: How many years did you spend on Earth? And how much time did you waste therein?

(١١٣) قَالُواْ لَبِثۡنَا يَوۡمًا أَوۡ بَعۡضَ يَوۡمٖ فَسۡ‍َٔلِ ٱلۡعَآدِّينَ

They will respond by saying: We only spent a day there or a part of a day, so ask those who pay attention to counting days and months.

(١١٤) قَٰلَ إِن لَّبِثۡتُمۡ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗاۖ لَّوۡ أَنَّكُمۡ كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

He will say: You only spent a short time in the world, which should have made patience and obedience easy for you if you only knew the length of your stay.

(١١٥) أَفَحَسِبۡتُمۡ أَنَّمَا خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ عَبَثٗا وَأَنَّكُمۡ إِلَيۡنَا لَا تُرۡجَعُونَ

So do you think, O humanity, that We created you uselessly without wisdom, with no reward and punishment like animals, and that you would not return to Us on the Day of Resurrection for account and recompense?

(١١٦) فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ

So Exalted is Allah, the King, who does as He wishes with His creation. It is He who is Truth and whose promise and statement are true. No one is worthy of worship, but He, Lord of the Noble Throne, the greatest of creation, and who is a Lord of the greatest of creation, is a Lord of all of creation.

(١١٧) وَمَن يَدۡعُ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ لَا بُرۡهَٰنَ لَهُۥ بِهِۦ فَإِنَّمَا حِسَابُهُۥ عِندَ رَبِّهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ

Whoever calls upon another god besides Allah for which he has no proof for worship (and this is the case with every god besides Allah), the recompense of his bad action is with his Lord (may He be Exalted), as He is the One who will hold him to account by punishing him. The disbelievers will not attain what they desire or be saved from what they fear.

(١١٨) وَقُل رَّبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ وَٱرۡحَمۡ وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّٰحِمِينَ

And say, O Messenger: My Lord, forgive my sins and have mercy on me. You are the best in showing mercy toward the one with sins, and then you accept his repentance.

Points of Benefit:

The disbeliever is despicable and disgraced in the sight of Allah.
Mocking righteous people is a grave sin whose perpetrator deserves punishment.
Wasting one's life is a necessary consequence of disbelief.
Praising Allah is a manifestation of good manners in supplication.
When Allah, the Exalted, began the chapter by mentioning the characteristics of the successful believers, it was appropriate for the chapter to end by mentioning the great loss of the disbelievers and their lack of success.
