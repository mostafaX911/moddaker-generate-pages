The Light (Surah An-Nur)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Calling to chastity and protecting one's honor.

Explanation:

(١) سُورَةٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهَا وَفَرَضۡنَٰهَا وَأَنزَلۡنَا فِيهَآ ءَايَٰتِۢ بَيِّنَٰتٖ لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ

This is a chapter (surah) that We have revealed. We have made acting upon its rulings mandatory and brought down clear verses in it in the hope that you be reminded of what is in it of commands and act upon them.

(٢) ٱلزَّانِيَةُ وَٱلزَّانِي فَٱجۡلِدُواْ كُلَّ وَٰحِدٖ مِّنۡهُمَا مِاْئَةَ جَلۡدَةٖۖ وَلَا تَأۡخُذۡكُم بِهِمَا رَأۡفَةٞ فِي دِينِ ٱللَّهِ إِن كُنتُمۡ تُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۖ وَلۡيَشۡهَدۡ عَذَابَهُمَا طَآئِفَةٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

The unmarried fornicating man and woman flog each one with a hundred lashes and let no softness or mercy overtake you that you would not establish the prescribed penalty or lessen it on them; this is if you believe in Allah and the Last Day. Establishing the prescribed punishments should be witnessed by a group of believers as a means to publicize the crime and as a deterrent to them and others.

(٣) ٱلزَّانِي لَا يَنكِحُ إِلَّا زَانِيَةً أَوۡ مُشۡرِكَةٗ وَٱلزَّانِيَةُ لَا يَنكِحُهَآ إِلَّا زَانٍ أَوۡ مُشۡرِكٞۚ وَحُرِّمَ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

To show the gravity of fornication, Allah mentions that those inclined to it do not wish to marry except someone like them; another female fornicator, or a polytheist who does not refrain from fornication though marrying them is prohibited. A female fornicator inclined to fornication does not want to marry except another male fornicator or a polytheist inclined to it, even though marriage to them is prohibited. It has been made unlawful for believers to marry male and female fornicators.

(٤) وَٱلَّذِينَ يَرۡمُونَ ٱلۡمُحۡصَنَٰتِ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَأۡتُواْ بِأَرۡبَعَةِ شُهَدَآءَ فَٱجۡلِدُوهُمۡ ثَمَٰنِينَ جَلۡدَةٗ وَلَا تَقۡبَلُواْ لَهُمۡ شَهَٰدَةً أَبَدٗاۚ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ

Those who accuse chaste women of adultery (and chaste men likewise) and do not then bring four witnesses to testify to the adultery they have accused them of, flog them, O judges, with eighty lashes, and never accept their testimony. Those who accuse chaste women have exited the obedience of Allah.

(٥) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ تَابُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَ وَأَصۡلَحُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Except those who repent to Allah after what they did and rectify their actions, for Allah will accept their repentance and testimony. Allah forgives those of His slaves who repent and is Merciful to them.

(٦) وَٱلَّذِينَ يَرۡمُونَ أَزۡوَٰجَهُمۡ وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُمۡ شُهَدَآءُ إِلَّآ أَنفُسُهُمۡ فَشَهَٰدَةُ أَحَدِهِمۡ أَرۡبَعُ شَهَٰدَٰتِۢ بِٱللَّهِ إِنَّهُۥ لَمِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

And those men who accuse their wives but do not have witnesses to testify to the authenticity of what they have accused them of besides themselves, one amongst them will testify by Allah four times that he is truthful in the accusation of adultery he has made against his wife.

(٧) وَٱلۡخَٰمِسَةُ أَنَّ لَعۡنَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡهِ إِن كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ

Then, on his fifth testimony, he will add that he deserves a curse if he is lying about what he has accused her of.

(٨) وَيَدۡرَؤُاْ عَنۡهَا ٱلۡعَذَابَ أَن تَشۡهَدَ أَرۡبَعَ شَهَٰدَٰتِۢ بِٱللَّهِ إِنَّهُۥ لَمِنَ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ

She deserves, through this, the prescribed punishment of adultery. The punishment could be repelled from her if she were to testify by Allah four times that he is lying in what he has accused her of.

(٩) وَٱلۡخَٰمِسَةَ أَنَّ غَضَبَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡهَآ إِن كَانَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

Then, on her fifth testimony, she will add a supplication upon herself that the anger of Allah be on her if he is truthful in what he has accused her of.

(١٠) وَلَوۡلَا فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَرَحۡمَتُهُۥ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ تَوَّابٌ حَكِيمٌ

Had it not been for Allah's grace on you, O people, and His mercy on you, and that He accepts those who repent amongst His slaves and is Wise in His managing and legislating, He would have quickly punished you for your sins and disgraced you through them.

Points of Benefit:

Preparing to talk about great matters in a way that indicates their greatness.
The adulterer loses respect and mercy in the Muslim community.
The social siege on adulterers is a means of protecting society from them and a deterrent for them from committing adultery.
Diversifying the punishment for the slanderer into a tangible punishment (prescribed punishment) and an intangible punishment (rejecting his testimony and ruling him to be immoral) is evidence of the seriousness of this act.
Adultery is only proven with evidence, and claiming it without that is slander.
