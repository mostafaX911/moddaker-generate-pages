(١١) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ جَآءُو بِٱلۡإِفۡكِ عُصۡبَةٞ مِّنكُمۡۚ لَا تَحۡسَبُوهُ شَرّٗا لَّكُمۖ بَلۡ هُوَ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡۚ لِكُلِّ ٱمۡرِيٕٖ مِّنۡهُم مَّا ٱكۡتَسَبَ مِنَ ٱلۡإِثۡمِۚ وَٱلَّذِي تَوَلَّىٰ كِبۡرَهُۥ مِنۡهُمۡ لَهُۥ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٞ

Those who brought the slander—accusing the Mother of the Believers Aishah (may Allah be pleased with her) of adultery—are a group that affiliates themselves to you, O believers. Do not consider the slander they made as bad for you. Rather, it is good due to the reward and expiation for the believers and the clearing of the name of the Mother of the Believers it brings with it. For each person who shared in accusing her of adultery is the sin they earned by uttering the slander. And there is a great punishment for the one who bore most of the slander by initiating it. The one meant by that is the leader of the hypocrites, Abdullah bin Ubayy bin Salul.

(١٢) لَّوۡلَآ إِذۡ سَمِعۡتُمُوهُ ظَنَّ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتُ بِأَنفُسِهِمۡ خَيۡرٗا وَقَالُواْ هَٰذَآ إِفۡكٞ مُّبِينٞ

When hearing this great slander, should not the believing men and women think well of their falsely accused believing brethren and say this is a clear lie?

(١٣) لَّوۡلَا جَآءُو عَلَيۡهِ بِأَرۡبَعَةِ شُهَدَآءَۚ فَإِذۡ لَمۡ يَأۡتُواْ بِٱلشُّهَدَآءِ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ هُمُ ٱلۡكَٰذِبُونَ

Should not those who falsely accused the Mother of the Believers Aishah, may Allah be pleased with her, bring four witnesses upon their grave accusation to testify to the authenticity of what they attributed to her? But if they do not bring four witnesses on that, and they will never be able to bring them, they are liars in the judgment of Allah.

(١٤) وَلَوۡلَا فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَرَحۡمَتُهُۥ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ لَمَسَّكُمۡ فِي مَآ أَفَضۡتُمۡ فِيهِ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٌ

Had it not been for Allah's grace on you, O believers, and His mercy on you, as He did not quickly punish you and He accepted the repentance of those amongst you who repented, a great punishment would have afflicted you, due to the lie and accusation against the Mother of the Believers you delved into.

(١٥) إِذۡ تَلَقَّوۡنَهُۥ بِأَلۡسِنَتِكُمۡ وَتَقُولُونَ بِأَفۡوَاهِكُم مَّا لَيۡسَ لَكُم بِهِۦ عِلۡمٞ وَتَحۡسَبُونَهُۥ هَيِّنٗا وَهُوَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ عَظِيمٞ

You were relating it to one another and passing it to one another through word of mouth, despite its falseness and you not knowing anything about it. You thought that was an easy, insignificant thing to do while it was grievous with Allah because it contains lies and accusing an innocent person.

(١٦) وَلَوۡلَآ إِذۡ سَمِعۡتُمُوهُ قُلۡتُم مَّا يَكُونُ لَنَآ أَن نَّتَكَلَّمَ بِهَٰذَا سُبۡحَٰنَكَ هَٰذَا بُهۡتَٰنٌ عَظِيمٞ

Should you not have said to yourself when you heard this slander, it is not right for us to speak about such a grievous issue? Purity belongs to You, our Lord. The slander they accused the Mother of the Believers of is a great lie.

(١٧) يَعِظُكُمُ ٱللَّهُ أَن تَعُودُواْ لِمِثۡلِهِۦٓ أَبَدًا إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

If you truly believe in Allah, Allah reminds and advises you never to return to this kind of slander that you accuse an innocent person of adultery.

(١٨) وَيُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌ

Allah makes clear for you the verses which comprise His rulings and admonitions. Allah is all Knowing of what you do, and nothing is hidden from Him. He will recompense you accordingly. He is the all Wise in His disposition and legislation.

(١٩) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُحِبُّونَ أَن تَشِيعَ ٱلۡفَٰحِشَةُ فِي ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

Those who love for immoral acts to spread amongst the believers, one of them being to defame with fornication, will have, by establishing the penalty of defamation, a painful punishment in this worldly life. And in the hereafter, they will have the punishment of hell. Allah knows their lies and the outcome of His slaves' affairs. He knows what benefits them, while you do not know that.

(٢٠) وَلَوۡلَا فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَرَحۡمَتُهُۥ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ رَءُوفٞ رَّحِيمٞ

If it were not for Allah's grace and mercy upon you, O those who fell into this slander, and if it were not for Allah's mercy and compassion with you, He would have punished you quickly.

Points of Benefit:

The hypocrites' focus on destroying trusted centers in the Muslim community by spreading false accusations.
The hypocrites may lure some believers into participating in their actions.
Honoring the Mother of the Believers, Aisha, may Allah be pleased with her, by declaring her innocence from above the seven heavens.
The importance of verifying rumors.
