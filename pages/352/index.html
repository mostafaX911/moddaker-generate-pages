(٢١) ۞يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَتَّبِعُواْ خُطُوَٰتِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۚ وَمَن يَتَّبِعۡ خُطُوَٰتِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ فَإِنَّهُۥ يَأۡمُرُ بِٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِ وَٱلۡمُنكَرِۚ وَلَوۡلَا فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَرَحۡمَتُهُۥ مَا زَكَىٰ مِنكُم مِّنۡ أَحَدٍ أَبَدٗا وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُزَكِّي مَن يَشَآءُۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٞ

O you who believe in Allah and act upon His sacred law, do not follow the paths of the Devil in the way they are adorned in falsehood. Whoever follows his paths, then he commands hideous sayings and actions and condemns the divine legislation. And if it were not for the grace of Allah upon you, O believers, He would not have purified any of you by accepting your repentance.

(٢٢) وَلَا يَأۡتَلِ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡفَضۡلِ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلسَّعَةِ أَن يُؤۡتُوٓاْ أُوْلِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينَ وَٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَلۡيَعۡفُواْ وَلۡيَصۡفَحُوٓاْۗ أَلَا تُحِبُّونَ أَن يَغۡفِرَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٌ

The people of virtue in religion and abundance in wealth should not swear an oath to stop giving their needy (due to their poverty) relatives amongst the migrants in Allah's way because of a sin they committed. They should pardon them and overlook them. Do you not love that Allah should forgive your sins when you pardon and overlook them? Allah forgives those who repent amongst His slaves and is Merciful to them, so His slaves should follow Him. This verse was revealed regarding Abu Bakr Siddiq (may Allah be pleased with him) when he swore an oath to stop spending on Mistah due to his joining in the slander.

(٢٣) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَرۡمُونَ ٱلۡمُحۡصَنَٰتِ ٱلۡغَٰفِلَٰتِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ لُعِنُواْ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٞ

Those who accuse chaste, unaware, and believing women of adultery, while those women are not inclined to it, are removed from the mercy of Allah in this world and the next, and they will be punished severely in the Hereafter.

(٢٤) يَوۡمَ تَشۡهَدُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَلۡسِنَتُهُمۡ وَأَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَأَرۡجُلُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

They will get that punishment on the Day of Resurrection, when their tongues will testify against them as to the falsehood they spoke, and their hands and feet will testify against them as to what they used to do.

(٢٥) يَوۡمَئِذٖ يُوَفِّيهِمُ ٱللَّهُ دِينَهُمُ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَيَعۡلَمُونَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

On that day, Allah will justly give them their full recompense, and they will know Allah (may He be Exalted) is the Truth. Every piece of information, promise, or warning that comes from Him is true without any doubt in it.

(٢٦) ٱلۡخَبِيثَٰتُ لِلۡخَبِيثِينَ وَٱلۡخَبِيثُونَ لِلۡخَبِيثَٰتِۖ وَٱلطَّيِّبَٰتُ لِلطَّيِّبِينَ وَٱلطَّيِّبُونَ لِلطَّيِّبَٰتِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ مُبَرَّءُونَ مِمَّا يَقُولُونَۖ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٞ وَرِزۡقٞ كَرِيمٞ

Every evil man, woman, saying, and action is suited to what is evil and conforms to it, whereas every good of those mentioned is suited and conforms to good. Those good men and women are innocent of what the evil men and women say about them. They will have forgiveness from Allah, who will pardon their sins, and generous sustenance, which is Paradise.

Since looking at private parts can arouse desire that leads to committing adultery, which is mentioned at the beginning of the Surah, Allah commands seeking permission to enter homes to protect one's gaze from seeing private parts, stating:

(٢٧) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَدۡخُلُواْ بُيُوتًا غَيۡرَ بُيُوتِكُمۡ حَتَّىٰ تَسۡتَأۡنِسُواْ وَتُسَلِّمُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهَاۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and act upon His sacred law, do not enter houses besides your own without first taking permission from their inhabitants to enter upon them. Greet them peacefully and seek consent by saying: peace be unto you, may I enter? The command to seek permission is better for you than suddenly entering upon them. Perhaps you will remember what I commanded you and act on it.

Points of Benefit:

Satan's temptations and whispers lead to committing sins, so the believer must beware of them.
Divine success to repentance and do good deeds is from Allah, not the servant.
Forgiving and pardoning the wrongdoer is a reason for the forgiveness of sins.
Slandering chaste women is a major sin.
The legislated nature of asking permission (to enter homes) as it protects from others seeing inside, and preserves the sanctity of the home.
