(٢٨) فَإِن لَّمۡ تَجِدُواْ فِيهَآ أَحَدٗا فَلَا تَدۡخُلُوهَا حَتَّىٰ يُؤۡذَنَ لَكُمۡۖ وَإِن قِيلَ لَكُمُ ٱرۡجِعُواْ فَٱرۡجِعُواْۖ هُوَ أَزۡكَىٰ لَكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ عَلِيمٞ

But if you do not find anyone in those houses, enter them once permitted by those with the authority to give permission. And if their owners tell you to return, return and do not enter them, as this is purer for you in the sight of Allah. Allah knows what you do; none of your actions are hidden from Him, and He shall reward you accordingly.

(٢٩) لَّيۡسَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ جُنَاحٌ أَن تَدۡخُلُواْ بُيُوتًا غَيۡرَ مَسۡكُونَةٖ فِيهَا مَتَٰعٞ لَّكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مَا تُبۡدُونَ وَمَا تَكۡتُمُونَ

There is no blame on you for entering public places that are not specific to anyone without asking permission. These places, such as libraries and shops in the marketplace, are prepared for the general public's benefit. Allah knows the actions and conditions you display and hide; none of that is hidden from Him, and He shall reward you for them.

Since looking is a precursor to adultery, Allah commanded averting one's gaze as a protection from it, stating:

(٣٠) قُل لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ يَغُضُّواْ مِنۡ أَبۡصَٰرِهِمۡ وَيَحۡفَظُواْ فُرُوجَهُمۡۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَزۡكَىٰ لَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا يَصۡنَعُونَ

O Messenger, tell the believing men to stop their eyes from looking at women and private areas that are unlawful for them and to protect their private parts from indulging in the unlawful and from exposing them. Refraining from looking at what Allah has made unlawful is purer for them in the sight of Allah. Allah is Aware of what you do.

(٣١) وَقُل لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ يَغۡضُضۡنَ مِنۡ أَبۡصَٰرِهِنَّ وَيَحۡفَظۡنَ فُرُوجَهُنَّ وَلَا يُبۡدِينَ زِينَتَهُنَّ إِلَّا مَا ظَهَرَ مِنۡهَاۖ وَلۡيَضۡرِبۡنَ بِخُمُرِهِنَّ عَلَىٰ جُيُوبِهِنَّۖ وَلَا يُبۡدِينَ زِينَتَهُنَّ إِلَّا لِبُعُولَتِهِنَّ أَوۡ ءَابَآئِهِنَّ أَوۡ ءَابَآءِ بُعُولَتِهِنَّ أَوۡ أَبۡنَآئِهِنَّ أَوۡ أَبۡنَآءِ بُعُولَتِهِنَّ أَوۡ إِخۡوَٰنِهِنَّ أَوۡ بَنِيٓ إِخۡوَٰنِهِنَّ أَوۡ بَنِيٓ أَخَوَٰتِهِنَّ أَوۡ نِسَآئِهِنَّ أَوۡ مَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُهُنَّ أَوِ ٱلتَّٰبِعِينَ غَيۡرِ أُوْلِي ٱلۡإِرۡبَةِ مِنَ ٱلرِّجَالِ أَوِ ٱلطِّفۡلِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يَظۡهَرُواْ عَلَىٰ عَوۡرَٰتِ ٱلنِّسَآءِۖ وَلَا يَضۡرِبۡنَ بِأَرۡجُلِهِنَّ لِيُعۡلَمَ مَا يُخۡفِينَ مِن زِينَتِهِنَّۚ وَتُوبُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعًا أَيُّهَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ

And tell the believing women to stop their eyes from looking at the private parts Allah has made unlawful for them to look at and protect their private parts by staying far from indecency and covering themselves. And they should not display their beauty to strangers except that which is apparent and cannot be hidden, such as clothing. They should draw their coverings over the openings at the top of their clothes to cover their hair, faces, and necks. They should not expose their hidden beauty except to their husbands, or their fathers, or their husbands' fathers, or their sons, or their husbands' sons, or their brothers, or their brother's sons, or their sister's sons, or their women who are believers, be they Muslim or disbelievers, or the slaves they own, be they male or female, or male attendants who have no desire for women, or children who have not become aware of the private aspects of women due to their young age. Women should not strike their feet to make known the adornment they are hiding, such as anklets. And all of you repent to Allah, O believers, for the gaze and so forth which affects you, in the hope that you are successful in attaining the goal and are saved from what is feared.

Points of Benefit:

Permission to enter public buildings without seeking permission.
The obligation of lowering the gaze from what is impermissible for both men and women alike.
The obligation of wearing the hijab for women.
Preventing the use of any means which provoke arousal.
