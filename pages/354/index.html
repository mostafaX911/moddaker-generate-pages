As spinsterhood is one of the reasons for the spread of adultery, Allah commands helping young women to marry, stating:

(٣٢) وَأَنكِحُواْ ٱلۡأَيَٰمَىٰ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ مِنۡ عِبَادِكُمۡ وَإِمَآئِكُمۡۚ إِن يَكُونُواْ فُقَرَآءَ يُغۡنِهِمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦۗ وَٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٞ

O believers, marry off the unmarried free men and women amongst you and marry off the believers amongst your male and female slaves. Allah will make them wealthy through His vast grace if they are poor. Allah is vast in sustenance, which does not decrease by making any of you rich. He knows the condition of His slaves.

Upon commanding the believers to marry unmarried women, Allah commands unmarried men to abstain from fornication if they do not have the means to marry, stating:

(٣٣) وَلۡيَسۡتَعۡفِفِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَجِدُونَ نِكَاحًا حَتَّىٰ يُغۡنِيَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦۗ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَبۡتَغُونَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِمَّا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُكُمۡ فَكَاتِبُوهُمۡ إِنۡ عَلِمۡتُمۡ فِيهِمۡ خَيۡرٗاۖ وَءَاتُوهُم مِّن مَّالِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۚ وَلَا تُكۡرِهُواْ فَتَيَٰتِكُمۡ عَلَى ٱلۡبِغَآءِ إِنۡ أَرَدۡنَ تَحَصُّنٗا لِّتَبۡتَغُواْ عَرَضَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَمَن يُكۡرِههُّنَّ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ إِكۡرَٰهِهِنَّ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Those who cannot marry due to poverty should seek chastity from fornication until Allah makes them wealthy through His vast grace. Those slaves who wish to make a contract with their masters to pay money so that they can be free, then their masters should accept that from them if they know they can fulfill it and are upright in religion. Their masters should give their slaves the wealth Allah gave them to help pay off a part of their contract of bondage. And do not force your female slaves into fornication for money as Abdullah bin Ubay did with his two female slaves when they asked to be chaste and distant from fornication. And whoever among you forces them to do that, know that Allah is forgiving and merciful to them after they have been forced. They were under duress, and the sin was on the ones who forced them.

(٣٤) وَلَقَدۡ أَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكُمۡ ءَايَٰتٖ مُّبَيِّنَٰتٖ وَمَثَلٗا مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَلَوۡاْ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ وَمَوۡعِظَةٗ لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ

And We have sent down to you, O people, clear verses detailing the truth from falsehood. We have also sent down to you an example from those who lived before you, from among the believers and unbelievers. We sent down to you a warning to be contemplated by those who fear their Lord by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

(٣٥) ۞ٱللَّهُ نُورُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ مَثَلُ نُورِهِۦ كَمِشۡكَوٰةٖ فِيهَا مِصۡبَاحٌۖ ٱلۡمِصۡبَاحُ فِي زُجَاجَةٍۖ ٱلزُّجَاجَةُ كَأَنَّهَا كَوۡكَبٞ دُرِّيّٞ يُوقَدُ مِن شَجَرَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٖ زَيۡتُونَةٖ لَّا شَرۡقِيَّةٖ وَلَا غَرۡبِيَّةٖ يَكَادُ زَيۡتُهَا يُضِيٓءُ وَلَوۡ لَمۡ تَمۡسَسۡهُ نَارٞۚ نُّورٌ عَلَىٰ نُورٖۚ يَهۡدِي ٱللَّهُ لِنُورِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَيَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلَ لِلنَّاسِۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٞ

Allah is the Light of the heavens and the Earth and the Guide of all those therein. The parable of His light in the heart of a believer is like a small niche in an unpenetrated wall that contains a lantern. The lantern is in a bright glass, as though it is a shining star like a pearl. The oil of a blessed tree lights the lantern, the olive tree. Nothing conceals the tree from the sun, neither in the morning nor evening. The oil almost glows due to its purity, even though a fire has not touched it, so imagine how it will be when it touches it. The light of the lantern is on the light of the glass. Likewise is the believer's heart when the light of guidance shines in it. Allah enables whomever He wishes amongst His slaves to follow the Quran. And Allah makes things clear through their likenesses by giving examples. Allah knows everything; nothing is hidden from Him.

(٣٦) فِي بُيُوتٍ أَذِنَ ٱللَّهُ أَن تُرۡفَعَ وَيُذۡكَرَ فِيهَا ٱسۡمُهُۥ يُسَبِّحُ لَهُۥ فِيهَا بِٱلۡغُدُوِّ وَٱلۡأٓصَالِ

This lantern is lit in the mosques Allah commanded to be built and revered. His name is mentioned therein by the call to prayer, remembrance, and the prayer itself. People pray in these mosques, morning and evening, seeking the pleasure of Allah.

Points of Benefit:

Allah limited the means of slavery (by war) and increased the means of liberation while encouraging it.
Liberation from slavery by means of manumission and aiding slaves financially to become free so that they do not become a lower class prone to lewdness.
The heart of the believer is illuminated by the light of the natural disposition as well as the light of divine guidance.
Mosques are Allah's houses on Earth, created for Him to be worshipped therein, so it is obligatory to protect them from tangible and intangible filth.
From Allah's Beautiful Names is An-Nur (the Light), which includes His Attribute of light, the Exalted.
