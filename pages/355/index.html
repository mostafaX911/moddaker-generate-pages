(٣٧) رِجَالٞ لَّا تُلۡهِيهِمۡ تِجَٰرَةٞ وَلَا بَيۡعٌ عَن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ وَإِقَامِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَإِيتَآءِ ٱلزَّكَوٰةِ يَخَافُونَ يَوۡمٗا تَتَقَلَّبُ فِيهِ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ

Men who neither commerce nor trade stop them from remembering Allah (may He be Exalted), praying in the most perfect manner, and giving Zakat (obligatory alms) where applicable. They fear the Day of Resurrection, when their hearts will fluctuate between the hope of salvation and the fear of punishment. Eyes will also fluctuate, anxiously wandering in all directions.

(٣٨) لِيَجۡزِيَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ أَحۡسَنَ مَا عَمِلُواْ وَيَزِيدَهُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَرۡزُقُ مَن يَشَآءُ بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ

They do that so that Allah may reward them upon the best of their actions and increase their reward through His grace. Allah provides without account for whomsoever He wishes, according to their deeds. Rather, He grants them in multitudes over the value of their deeds.

(٣٩) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَعۡمَٰلُهُمۡ كَسَرَابِۢ بِقِيعَةٖ يَحۡسَبُهُ ٱلظَّمۡ‍َٔانُ مَآءً حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَهُۥ لَمۡ يَجِدۡهُ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَوَجَدَ ٱللَّهَ عِندَهُۥ فَوَفَّىٰهُ حِسَابَهُۥۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

There is no reward for the deeds of those who disbelieve in Allah; it is like a mirage that a thirsty person sees on a low-lying piece of land and assumes it to be water. He walks towards it until he reaches it and finds no water. Likewise is a disbeliever: he thinks his deeds shall benefit him until he dies and is resurrected but does not find their reward. Instead, he finds his Lord in front of him, who takes him to account fully for his deeds; and Allah is swift in taking account.

(٤٠) أَوۡ كَظُلُمَٰتٖ فِي بَحۡرٖ لُّجِّيّٖ يَغۡشَىٰهُ مَوۡجٞ مِّن فَوۡقِهِۦ مَوۡجٞ مِّن فَوۡقِهِۦ سَحَابٞۚ ظُلُمَٰتُۢ بَعۡضُهَا فَوۡقَ بَعۡضٍ إِذَآ أَخۡرَجَ يَدَهُۥ لَمۡ يَكَدۡ يَرَىٰهَاۗ وَمَن لَّمۡ يَجۡعَلِ ٱللَّهُ لَهُۥ نُورٗا فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن نُّورٍ

Or their deeds are like darkness in the depth of the ocean, over which is a wave, over which is another wave, over which is a cloud covering the navigating stars. Compounded layers of darkness, one above the other; if the one in such darkness were to place his hand in front of him, he would barely see it due to the extent of that darkness. Likewise is a disbeliever: the darkness of ignorance, doubt, confusion, and a sealed heart have compounded over him. Whoever Allah does not grant guidance over misguidance and knowledge of His book has no guidance to be guided by nor a book to illuminate his path.

(٤١) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُسَبِّحُ لَهُۥ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلطَّيۡرُ صَٰٓفَّٰتٖۖ كُلّٞ قَدۡ عَلِمَ صَلَاتَهُۥ وَتَسۡبِيحَهُۥۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِمَا يَفۡعَلُونَ

O Messenger, do you not know that all the inhabitants of the heavens and Earth from the creation of Allah exalt Him? The birds that fly in formation also praise Him. Allah knows the prayer of each one of those creatures. He knows their exaltation of Him, such as the birds. Allah knows everything they do; nothing of their actions is hidden from Him.

(٤٢) وَلِلَّهِ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

To Allah alone belongs the dominion of the heavens and the Earth, and to Him alone is the return on the Day of Resurrection to be taken to account and recompensed.

(٤٣) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُزۡجِي سَحَابٗا ثُمَّ يُؤَلِّفُ بَيۡنَهُۥ ثُمَّ يَجۡعَلُهُۥ رُكَامٗا فَتَرَى ٱلۡوَدۡقَ يَخۡرُجُ مِنۡ خِلَٰلِهِۦ وَيُنَزِّلُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مِن جِبَالٖ فِيهَا مِنۢ بَرَدٖ فَيُصِيبُ بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَصۡرِفُهُۥ عَن مَّن يَشَآءُۖ يَكَادُ سَنَا بَرۡقِهِۦ يَذۡهَبُ بِٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرِ

O Messenger, do you not know that Allah drives the clouds, then joins parts of them together, then makes them into layers, one above the other? You then see the rain coming out from the clouds and falling from the sky from parts of cloud-like mountains, falling as hail. That hail reaches whoever Allah wills from His slaves, and He turns it away from whomsoever He wills. The intense light from bolts of lightning almost blinds the eyesight.

Points of Benefit:

A believer's balancing between worldly preoccupations and deeds for his Hereafter is a necessity.
The disbeliever's deeds are invalid due to the absence of faith, which is a condition for them to be accepted.
The disbeliever is a dissonance from Allah's creation, which praises and obeys Him.
All stages of rain are from Allah's creation and measure.
