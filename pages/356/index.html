(٤٤) يُقَلِّبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَعِبۡرَةٗ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرِ

Allah alternates between the length of the day and night. Indeed, that alternation is a lesson in the power of Allah and His Oneness for those with foresight.

(٤٥) وَٱللَّهُ خَلَقَ كُلَّ دَآبَّةٖ مِّن مَّآءٖۖ فَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَمۡشِي عَلَىٰ بَطۡنِهِۦ وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَمۡشِي عَلَىٰ رِجۡلَيۡنِ وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَمۡشِي عَلَىٰٓ أَرۡبَعٖۚ يَخۡلُقُ ٱللَّهُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

And Allah has created all living things that appear on earth from a drop of semen. Some, like snakes, crawl on their bellies. Others walk on two feet like birds and humans, while others walk on four like animals. Allah creates what He wills from what was mentioned and what was not. Allah is Omnipotent over everything; nothing is beyond His capability.

(٤٦) لَّقَدۡ أَنزَلۡنَآ ءَايَٰتٖ مُّبَيِّنَٰتٖۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

Indeed, I revealed clear verses unto Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, wherein there is no ambiguity. Allah guides whomever He wishes to the straight path without deviance, leading him to Paradise.

(٤٧) وَيَقُولُونَ ءَامَنَّا بِٱللَّهِ وَبِٱلرَّسُولِ وَأَطَعۡنَا ثُمَّ يَتَوَلَّىٰ فَرِيقٞ مِّنۡهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَۚ وَمَآ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

The hypocrites say: We have believed in Allah and His Messenger, and obeyed Allah and His Messenger, then a group of them turn away, disobeying Allah and His Messenger in the matter of jihad in Allah's cause and the like. In truth, those who turn away from the obedience of Allah and His Messenger are not believers, despite claiming to be.

(٤٨) وَإِذَا دُعُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ لِيَحۡكُمَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ إِذَا فَرِيقٞ مِّنۡهُم مُّعۡرِضُونَ

If these hypocrites are called to Allah and the Messenger so that the Messenger can judge between them in matters they dispute among themselves, they reject his judgment due to their hypocrisy.

(٤٩) وَإِن يَكُن لَّهُمُ ٱلۡحَقُّ يَأۡتُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ مُذۡعِنِينَ

Yet if they know the truth to be in their favor and that he will judge in their favor, they come to him submissive and humble.

(٥٠) أَفِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٌ أَمِ ٱرۡتَابُوٓاْ أَمۡ يَخَافُونَ أَن يَحِيفَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَرَسُولُهُۥۚ بَلۡ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

Is there a chronic illness in the hearts of these people, or is it that they are in doubt as to whether he is Allah's Messenger? Or do they fear Allah or His Messenger will wrong them in judgment? No, it is none of the aforementioned; rather, it is because of a defect within their souls due to their turning away from his judgment and their opposition to him.

When discussing the negative stance of hypocrites towards Allah and His Messenger's rules, Allah highlights the positive attitude of the believers who are satisfied with it, stating:

(٥١) إِنَّمَا كَانَ قَوۡلَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ إِذَا دُعُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ لِيَحۡكُمَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ أَن يَقُولُواْ سَمِعۡنَا وَأَطَعۡنَاۚ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ

When the believers are called towards Allah and His Messenger for him to judge between them, their only reaction is that they say, we have heard his statement and obeyed his order. Those who possess this characteristic are the ones who are successful in this world and the Hereafter.

(٥٢) وَمَن يُطِعِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَيَخۡشَ ٱللَّهَ وَيَتَّقۡهِ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡفَآئِزُونَ

Whoever obeys Allah and obeys His Messenger, fears the outcome of sins, and prevents the punishment of Allah upon themselves by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions; only they are the ones who achieve success in both this world and the Hereafter.

(٥٣) ۞وَأَقۡسَمُواْ بِٱللَّهِ جَهۡدَ أَيۡمَٰنِهِمۡ لَئِنۡ أَمَرۡتَهُمۡ لَيَخۡرُجُنَّۖ قُل لَّا تُقۡسِمُواْۖ طَاعَةٞ مَّعۡرُوفَةٌۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ

The hypocrites take their strongest and most vigorous oaths possible upon Allah that they will go forth if they are called to go out for jihad. Tell them, O Messenger, do not take oaths, for your dishonesty and fake obedience are known. Allah knows what you do; nothing of your actions is hidden from Him, no matter how much you try to conceal them.

Points of Benefit:

The creation's diversity is evidence of Allah's power.
Among the characteristics of the hypocrites is turning away from Allah's judgment unless it is in their favor, and among their characteristics is having a diseased heart with doubts and evil thoughts about Allah.
Obedience to Allah and His Messenger, along with fearing Allah, are from the means of success in both worlds (the worldly life and the Hereafter).
Taking oaths for lies is a well-known way of the hypocrites.
