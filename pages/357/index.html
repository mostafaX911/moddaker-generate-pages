(٥٤) قُلۡ أَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱلرَّسُولَۖ فَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَإِنَّمَا عَلَيۡهِ مَا حُمِّلَ وَعَلَيۡكُم مَّا حُمِّلۡتُمۡۖ وَإِن تُطِيعُوهُ تَهۡتَدُواْۚ وَمَا عَلَى ٱلرَّسُولِ إِلَّا ٱلۡبَلَٰغُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

Say, O Messenger, to those hypocrites, obey Allah and His Messenger both outwardly and inwardly. If you turn away from obeying Allah and His Messenger, then the Messenger has fulfilled what he was tasked with, and upon you is what you have been tasked with of obedience and acting on what he came with. If you obey him in what he has commanded you and avoid what he has prohibited, you will be guided to the truth. Only clear conveyance is upon the Messenger; he cannot carry you to guidance or force it upon you.

(٥٥) وَعَدَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنكُمۡ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَيَسۡتَخۡلِفَنَّهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كَمَا ٱسۡتَخۡلَفَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ وَلَيُمَكِّنَنَّ لَهُمۡ دِينَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي ٱرۡتَضَىٰ لَهُمۡ وَلَيُبَدِّلَنَّهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ خَوۡفِهِمۡ أَمۡنٗاۚ يَعۡبُدُونَنِي لَا يُشۡرِكُونَ بِي شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۚ وَمَن كَفَرَ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ

Allah has promised those who believe in Him and perform righteous deeds that He will aid them against their enemies and make them viceroys on Earth, just like He enabled the believers before them to be viceroys on Earth. He has promised to make their religion, Islam, the religion He preferred for them, dominant and triumphant. He has vowed to replace their fear with security. They worship Me alone and do not associate any partners with Me. Whoever disbelieves after all those blessings has indeed exited the obedience of Allah.

(٥٦) وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱلرَّسُولَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ

And perform prayer to perfection, give Zakat (charity) on your wealth, and obey the Messenger in whatever he commands you with, together with leaving whatever he prevents you from, in the hope that you shall be safe from your fears and be successful in your objectives.

(٥٧) لَا تَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مُعۡجِزِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَمَأۡوَىٰهُمُ ٱلنَّارُۖ وَلَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

Do not think, O Messenger, that those who disbelieve in Allah will escape Me if I bring down punishment upon them. Their destination will be hell on the Day of Resurrection, and what an evil abode hell is for those destined to it.

Allah previously mentions the rules for free adults seeking permission before entering. He then mentions regulations pertaining to slaves, free children pre-puberty, and children post-puberty who wish to enter, stating:

(٥٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لِيَسۡتَ‍ٔۡذِنكُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُكُمۡ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يَبۡلُغُواْ ٱلۡحُلُمَ مِنكُمۡ ثَلَٰثَ مَرَّٰتٖۚ مِّن قَبۡلِ صَلَوٰةِ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ وَحِينَ تَضَعُونَ ثِيَابَكُم مِّنَ ٱلظَّهِيرَةِ وَمِنۢ بَعۡدِ صَلَوٰةِ ٱلۡعِشَآءِۚ ثَلَٰثُ عَوۡرَٰتٖ لَّكُمۡۚ لَيۡسَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَلَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ جُنَاحُۢ بَعۡدَهُنَّۚ طَوَّٰفُونَ عَلَيۡكُم بَعۡضُكُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٞ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, your free adolescent children, male slaves, and female slaves should seek permission from you thrice, at three times: before the morning prayer at the time of changing from nightwear into daily clothes, in the afternoon when changing clothes for the siesta and after the night prayer because it is the time when you remove your daywear and change into your nightwear. These are three times of privacy for you; they should not enter your rooms except after taking your permission. Apart from these times, there is no harm in them entering your rooms or you entering theirs because you frequently have to move around one another. So, it would be inconvenient to prevent them from entering except with permission. Just as Allah has clarified for you the rulings of seeking permission, He also clarifies the verses that show the rulings He has laid down for you. Allah is All-Aware of the requirements of His slaves, Wise in whatever laws He passes for them.

Points of Benefit:

Following the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم is a sign of guidance.
The preacher must put effort into calling, and the results are in Allah's Hand.
Faith and righteous deeds are the reason for empowerment and security on Earth.
Training slaves and children to seek permission for entry at the times of privacy.
