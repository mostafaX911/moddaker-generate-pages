(٥٩) وَإِذَا بَلَغَ ٱلۡأَطۡفَٰلُ مِنكُمُ ٱلۡحُلُمَ فَلۡيَسۡتَ‍ٔۡذِنُواْ كَمَا ٱسۡتَ‍ٔۡذَنَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٞ

When your children reach the age of puberty, they should seek permission at all times before entering your homes, as has been mentioned regarding adults previously. Just as Allah clarifies the rulings of seeking permission for you, He also clarifies His verses. And Allah is All-Aware of the requirements of His slaves, Wise in whatever laws He passes for them.

(٦٠) وَٱلۡقَوَٰعِدُ مِنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ ٱلَّٰتِي لَا يَرۡجُونَ نِكَاحٗا فَلَيۡسَ عَلَيۡهِنَّ جُنَاحٌ أَن يَضَعۡنَ ثِيَابَهُنَّ غَيۡرَ مُتَبَرِّجَٰتِۢ بِزِينَةٖۖ وَأَن يَسۡتَعۡفِفۡنَ خَيۡرٞ لَّهُنَّۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٞ

And the elderly women that no longer menstruate, can no longer give birth, and have no desire to marry again, there is no sin upon them if they do not wear some of their outer garments, i.e., the cloak over the robe and the veil over the headscarf, as long as they do not expose the beauty they have been commanded to conceal. However, for them to wear those garments is better for them than to discard them, enhancing veiling and chastity. Allah is all-hearing of your statements; none are hidden from Him, and He shall requite you for them.

(٦١) لَّيۡسَ عَلَى ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ حَرَجٞ وَلَا عَلَى ٱلۡأَعۡرَجِ حَرَجٞ وَلَا عَلَى ٱلۡمَرِيضِ حَرَجٞ وَلَا عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَن تَأۡكُلُواْ مِنۢ بُيُوتِكُمۡ أَوۡ بُيُوتِ ءَابَآئِكُمۡ أَوۡ بُيُوتِ أُمَّهَٰتِكُمۡ أَوۡ بُيُوتِ إِخۡوَٰنِكُمۡ أَوۡ بُيُوتِ أَخَوَٰتِكُمۡ أَوۡ بُيُوتِ أَعۡمَٰمِكُمۡ أَوۡ بُيُوتِ عَمَّٰتِكُمۡ أَوۡ بُيُوتِ أَخۡوَٰلِكُمۡ أَوۡ بُيُوتِ خَٰلَٰتِكُمۡ أَوۡ مَا مَلَكۡتُم مَّفَاتِحَهُۥٓ أَوۡ صَدِيقِكُمۡۚ لَيۡسَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ جُنَاحٌ أَن تَأۡكُلُواْ جَمِيعًا أَوۡ أَشۡتَاتٗاۚ فَإِذَا دَخَلۡتُم بُيُوتٗا فَسَلِّمُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ تَحِيَّةٗ مِّنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ مُبَٰرَكَةٗ طَيِّبَةٗۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ

There is no sin upon the blind who has lost his eyesight, the cripple, or the sick if they leave those responsibilities they can no longer fulfill, like jihad in the way of Allah. O believers! There is no sin upon you in eating from your own homes or the homes of your sons, nor in eating from the homes of your fathers, mothers, brothers, sisters, paternal uncles and aunts, nor maternal uncles and aunts, nor those houses which you have been entrusted with safeguarding, e.g., garden-keeper. There is also no sin in eating at your friend's house as is customary to please him. It is fine whether you eat alone or together in groups. So when you do enter any places like the ones mentioned above or any others, greet those within them with peace by saying: "Peace be upon you," and if there is no one inside them, then greet yourselves by saying: "Peace be upon the pious slaves of Allah and us." This is a blessed greeting from Allah; He has legislated it for you because of the love and affection it spreads among you. It is also a pleasant greeting in that it pleases the soul of the one who hears it. Just like the aforementioned clarity in this Surah, Allah clearly explains His verses in the hope that you will implement whatever they contain.

Points of Benefit:

It is permissible for older women to leave off some of their clothes due to the lack of suspicion in that.
Being cautious in religion is the state of the righteous.
Acceptable excuses are a means of easing responsibility.
The Muslim community is a community of solidarity, cooperation, and brotherhood.
