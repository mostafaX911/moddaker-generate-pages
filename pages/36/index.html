(٢٢٥) لَّا يُؤَاخِذُكُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِٱللَّغۡوِ فِيٓ أَيۡمَٰنِكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن يُؤَاخِذُكُم بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ قُلُوبُكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٌ حَلِيمٞ

Allah will not take you to account for those oaths that you swear unintentionally, such as if someone carelessly says, 'No, by Allah!' or 'Yes, by Allah!' For such oaths, there is no need to make any expiation nor any further consequences. However, He will take you to account for intentional oaths. Allah forgives and tolerates the sins of His slaves and does not hasten to send punishment.

(٢٢٦) لِّلَّذِينَ يُؤۡلُونَ مِن نِّسَآئِهِمۡ تَرَبُّصُ أَرۡبَعَةِ أَشۡهُرٖۖ فَإِن فَآءُو فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

For those who swear an oath not to have sexual intercourse with their wives, there is a waiting period not longer than four months, starting from the date of the oath. This is known as al-Elaa (Oath of Abstinence). If he abandons the oath and engages in sexual intercourse after having sworn not to, and this happens within the four months, then Allah forgives them. Allah is merciful and has established expiation as a way out from such an oath.

(٢٢٧) وَإِنۡ عَزَمُواْ ٱلطَّلَٰقَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٞ

If such a man is determined to continue his oath and passes the four-month period, intending by a divorce, then Allah hears their statements, including the divorce, and He knows their states and intentions and will recompense them accordingly.

(٢٢٨) وَٱلۡمُطَلَّقَٰتُ يَتَرَبَّصۡنَ بِأَنفُسِهِنَّ ثَلَٰثَةَ قُرُوٓءٖۚ وَلَا يَحِلُّ لَهُنَّ أَن يَكۡتُمۡنَ مَا خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ فِيٓ أَرۡحَامِهِنَّ إِن كُنَّ يُؤۡمِنَّ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۚ وَبُعُولَتُهُنَّ أَحَقُّ بِرَدِّهِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ إِنۡ أَرَادُوٓاْ إِصۡلَٰحٗاۚ وَلَهُنَّ مِثۡلُ ٱلَّذِي عَلَيۡهِنَّ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِۚ وَلِلرِّجَالِ عَلَيۡهِنَّ دَرَجَةٞۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٌ

Divorced women must not remarry after a divorce for three menstrual periods. If they are true in their faith in Allah and the Last Day, they may not hide the fetus that Allah has created in their wombs. Their husbands, who issued the divorce, have the first right to recall them during the waiting period if they intend to live well with them and to cancel what has occurred because of the divorce. Wives have rights and obligations like husbands in a manner that people are accustomed to. Men have a higher rank than them regarding responsibility and divorce matters. Allah is Mighty, and nothing can overcome His decree. He is Wise in His legislation and planning.

(٢٢٩) ٱلطَّلَٰقُ مَرَّتَانِۖ فَإِمۡسَاكُۢ بِمَعۡرُوفٍ أَوۡ تَسۡرِيحُۢ بِإِحۡسَٰنٖۗ وَلَا يَحِلُّ لَكُمۡ أَن تَأۡخُذُواْ مِمَّآ ءَاتَيۡتُمُوهُنَّ شَيۡ‍ًٔا إِلَّآ أَن يَخَافَآ أَلَّا يُقِيمَا حُدُودَ ٱللَّهِۖ فَإِنۡ خِفۡتُمۡ أَلَّا يُقِيمَا حُدُودَ ٱللَّهِ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡهِمَا فِيمَا ٱفۡتَدَتۡ بِهِۦۗ تِلۡكَ حُدُودُ ٱللَّهِ فَلَا تَعۡتَدُوهَاۚ وَمَن يَتَعَدَّ حُدُودَ ٱللَّهِ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

The divorce, after which a husband may recall his wife, can only happen twice. He may divorce her the first time, recall her, divorce her again, and recall her again. After two divorces, he must either keep her in his marriage, living in a good way with her or divorce her for the third time, being good to her by fulfilling her rights. At the time of divorce, husbands may not take any part of the dowry that they had given their wives. However, if a woman is unhappy with her husband because of his character or looks, and the couple is of the view that, because of this, they will not be able to fulfill the rights of marriage, then they should present the matter to a person related to them or someone familiar with them. If they feel that the couple will not be able to fulfill their marital rights, then there is no harm if the woman offers to give something to the husband in return for a divorce. These laws of the Shariah are the criterion for the lawful and unlawful, so do not overstep them. Whoever violates the limits of Allah with regard to the lawful and the unlawful, then such people are wronging their souls, bringing themselves to destruction, and exposing themselves to Allah's anger and punishment.

(٢٣٠) فَإِن طَلَّقَهَا فَلَا تَحِلُّ لَهُۥ مِنۢ بَعۡدُ حَتَّىٰ تَنكِحَ زَوۡجًا غَيۡرَهُۥۗ فَإِن طَلَّقَهَا فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡهِمَآ أَن يَتَرَاجَعَآ إِن ظَنَّآ أَن يُقِيمَا حُدُودَ ٱللَّهِۗ وَتِلۡكَ حُدُودُ ٱللَّهِ يُبَيِّنُهَا لِقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ

If the husband divorces her for the third time, he may not remarry her until she gets married to another man in a valid marriage that is genuine and not merely contracted to make her lawful for the original husband and where sexual intercourse has taken place. Should the second husband divorce her or pass away, then there is no sin upon the woman and her first husband for returning to one another with a new marriage contract and dowry if they think they will likely be able to fulfill one another's rights in accordance with sacred law. These laws are made clear by Allah for people who know His laws and limits, as they are the ones who benefit from them.

Points of Benefit:

Allah explains the rulings of marriage and divorce entirely so that people may know the limits of what is permissible and impermissible. He prohibits exceeding those limits.
Allah made marriage a major issue and forbade using statements to play around with it. So, He made those statements count and abolished making many divorces and revoking them by limiting them to two divorces that can be revoked. Then, if he divorces her a third time, she is not permissible to him except if she marries a different husband, then he divorces her or dies, making her a widow.
The marital relationship should be with kindness. If you cannot do that, then there is no issue with divorce and no harm if either of the spouses requests it.
