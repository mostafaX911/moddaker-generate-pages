(١٢) إِذَا رَأَتۡهُم مِّن مَّكَانِۢ بَعِيدٖ سَمِعُواْ لَهَا تَغَيُّظٗا وَزَفِيرٗا

When the Fire sees the disbelievers being brought towards it from a distance, they will hear an unsettling, severe boiling sound emitting from it due to its anger upon them.

(١٣) وَإِذَآ أُلۡقُواْ مِنۡهَا مَكَانٗا ضَيِّقٗا مُّقَرَّنِينَ دَعَوۡاْ هُنَالِكَ ثُبُورٗا

And when these disbelievers are thrown into hell from a tight place, with their hands chained to their necks, they will invoke destruction upon themselves, hoping to escape from hell.

(١٤) لَّا تَدۡعُواْ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ ثُبُورٗا وَٰحِدٗا وَٱدۡعُواْ ثُبُورٗا كَثِيرٗا

O disbelievers, do not invoke one type of destruction for yourselves today; invoke many types, and it will make no difference; you will not be granted what you seek. Instead, you will remain in painful punishment forever.

(١٥) قُلۡ أَذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرٌ أَمۡ جَنَّةُ ٱلۡخُلۡدِ ٱلَّتِي وُعِدَ ٱلۡمُتَّقُونَۚ كَانَتۡ لَهُمۡ جَزَآءٗ وَمَصِيرٗا

O Messenger, say to them: Is the punishment described for you better, or the everlasting Paradise with its eternal blessings that never cease? It is the same one that Allah promised His pious slaves as a reward and abode they will return to on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٦) لَّهُمۡ فِيهَا مَا يَشَآءُونَ خَٰلِدِينَۚ كَانَ عَلَىٰ رَبِّكَ وَعۡدٗا مَّسۡ‍ُٔولٗا

In Paradise, they will have whatever blessings they desire; that is a promise from Allah which His pious slaves will ask Him for. And the promise of Allah will be fulfilled because He never breaks promises.

(١٧) وَيَوۡمَ يَحۡشُرُهُمۡ وَمَا يَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ فَيَقُولُ ءَأَنتُمۡ أَضۡلَلۡتُمۡ عِبَادِي هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ أَمۡ هُمۡ ضَلُّواْ ٱلسَّبِيلَ

On the Day when the rejecting polytheists will be gathered, together with whatever they worshipped instead of Allah, He will say to those worshipped, rebuking those who worshipped them: Did you lead My slaves astray by commanding them to worship you, or did they go astray of their own accord?

(١٨) قَالُواْ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ مَا كَانَ يَنۢبَغِي لَنَآ أَن نَّتَّخِذَ مِن دُونِكَ مِنۡ أَوۡلِيَآءَ وَلَٰكِن مَّتَّعۡتَهُمۡ وَءَابَآءَهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ نَسُواْ ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَكَانُواْ قَوۡمَۢا بُورٗا

The worshipped ones will say: Our Lord, You are exalted from having any partner. It was not becoming for us to take any guardians instead of You, so how could we call Your slaves to worship us instead of You? Our Lord, You granted these polytheists and their forefathers enjoyment through the world's pleasures, gradually leading them into their destruction until they forgot Your remembrance and worshipped others with You. They were people doomed due to their wretchedness.

(١٩) فَقَدۡ كَذَّبُوكُم بِمَا تَقُولُونَ فَمَا تَسۡتَطِيعُونَ صَرۡفٗا وَلَا نَصۡرٗاۚ وَمَن يَظۡلِم مِّنكُمۡ نُذِقۡهُ عَذَابٗا كَبِيرٗا

O polytheists, indeed, those you worshipped other than Allah have belied you in your claims against them, so you cannot fend off the punishment from yourselves nor help them due to your incapability. O believers, whoever oppresses from amongst you by ascribing partners to Allah, We shall make him suffer a great punishment, just like We will make the ones mentioned suffer.

When the polytheists criticized the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم for eating food and walking in the markets, Allah responded to them, stating:

(٢٠) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا قَبۡلَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ إِلَّآ إِنَّهُمۡ لَيَأۡكُلُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ وَيَمۡشُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَسۡوَاقِۗ وَجَعَلۡنَا بَعۡضَكُمۡ لِبَعۡضٖ فِتۡنَةً أَتَصۡبِرُونَۗ وَكَانَ رَبُّكَ بَصِيرٗا

O Messenger, all the messengers We sent before you were humans who would eat food and walk in the markets; you are not a unique messenger. O, people, I have made you trials for each other by granting you varying degrees of affluence, poverty, health, and sickness; throughout these different circumstances, will you bear whatever you are afflicted with so that Allah may reward you upon your patience? Your Lord is ever-aware of those who are patient and those who are not, those who obey Him and those who disobey Him.

Points of Benefit:

Combining fear of Allah's punishment and encouragement of His reward.
The pleasures of the world make one forget the remembrance of Allah.
The fact that the messengers were human beings is a blessing from Allah to the people because of the ease of dealing with them.
People's differences in blessings and calamities are a divine test for His servants.
