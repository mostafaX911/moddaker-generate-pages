(٢١) ۞وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَرۡجُونَ لِقَآءَنَا لَوۡلَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيۡنَا ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ أَوۡ نَرَىٰ رَبَّنَاۗ لَقَدِ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَعَتَوۡ عُتُوّٗا كَبِيرٗا

The disbelievers who do not wish to meet Us or fear Our punishment said: Why doesn't Allah send the angels down to us to tell us about Muhammad's truthfulness? Or Why can't we see our Lord with our own eyes so He can inform us about it? They became so conceited, to the point that it prevented them from having faith. They surpassed the limit of disbelief and transgression with their statements.

(٢٢) يَوۡمَ يَرَوۡنَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةَ لَا بُشۡرَىٰ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ وَيَقُولُونَ حِجۡرٗا مَّحۡجُورٗا

On the Day these people see the angels at the time of their death, when they are in the Barzakh, when they are resurrected, when they are led to their judgment, and when they enter the Fire, there will not be any glad tidings for them on these occasions. In contrast to the believers, the angels will say to them: Glad tidings from Allah are forbidden for you.

(٢٣) وَقَدِمۡنَآ إِلَىٰ مَا عَمِلُواْ مِنۡ عَمَلٖ فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ هَبَآءٗ مَّنثُورًا

We shall turn to the righteous deeds that the disbelievers did in their lives and make them like the scattered particles of dust that are visible in the light rays that pass through a window. This is due to their falsehood and lack of benefit because of their disbelief.

(٢٤) أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ يَوۡمَئِذٍ خَيۡرٞ مُّسۡتَقَرّٗا وَأَحۡسَنُ مَقِيلٗا

On that Day, the believers, the people of Paradise, will have a better abode and shelter than these disbelievers. This is due to their faith in Allah and their righteous deeds.

(٢٥) وَيَوۡمَ تَشَقَّقُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ بِٱلۡغَمَٰمِ وَنُزِّلَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ تَنزِيلًا

Mention, O Messenger, the Day when the sky will be rent asunder to reveal thin white clouds, and the angels will be sent down to the plains of the Gathering in immense numbers.

(٢٦) ٱلۡمُلۡكُ يَوۡمَئِذٍ ٱلۡحَقُّ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِۚ وَكَانَ يَوۡمًا عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَسِيرٗا

The true, established Kingdom on the Day of Resurrection will be for the Merciful, the Exalted. That will be a difficult Day for the disbelievers, in contrast to the believers, for whom it will be easy.

(٢٧) وَيَوۡمَ يَعَضُّ ٱلظَّالِمُ عَلَىٰ يَدَيۡهِ يَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي ٱتَّخَذۡتُ مَعَ ٱلرَّسُولِ سَبِيلٗا

Mention, O Messenger, the Day the transgressor will bite down on his hand in regret due to not following the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, and say: If only I had followed the Messenger in what he brought from his Lord, and taken a path with him to salvation.

(٢٨) يَٰوَيۡلَتَىٰ لَيۡتَنِي لَمۡ أَتَّخِذۡ فُلَانًا خَلِيلٗا

Due to extreme grief, he will curse himself, saying: Woe to me! If only I had not taken that disbeliever as a friend!

(٢٩) لَّقَدۡ أَضَلَّنِي عَنِ ٱلذِّكۡرِ بَعۡدَ إِذۡ جَآءَنِيۗ وَكَانَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ خَذُولٗا

That friend led me away from the Quran after it had been conveyed to me through the Messenger. Indeed, the Devil has ever been a betrayer to humanity, and when a calamity befalls humanity, he renounces him.

(٣٠) وَقَالَ ٱلرَّسُولُ يَٰرَبِّ إِنَّ قَوۡمِي ٱتَّخَذُواْ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ مَهۡجُورٗا

On that Day, the Messenger will complain about the condition of his people: O my Lord, my people whom You sent me to have abandoned this Quran and turned away from it.

(٣١) وَكَذَٰلِكَ جَعَلۡنَا لِكُلِّ نَبِيٍّ عَدُوّٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَۗ وَكَفَىٰ بِرَبِّكَ هَادِيٗا وَنَصِيرٗا

Similar to your experience with your people, O Messenger, regarding the harm and the prevention of others from following your path, We had assigned to every Prophet before you an enemy from the criminals of his people. Your Lord is a sufficient guide to the truth and a sufficient supporter against your enemies.

(٣٢) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَوۡلَا نُزِّلَ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ جُمۡلَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗۚ كَذَٰلِكَ لِنُثَبِّتَ بِهِۦ فُؤَادَكَۖ وَرَتَّلۡنَٰهُ تَرۡتِيلٗا

Those who disbelieved in Allah said: Shouldn't the Quran have been revealed to him in one go instead of intermittently? We revealed the Quran intermittently to make your heart firm, O Messenger, by revealing it in portions gradually. We revealed it to you progressively to make it easy to understand and memorize.

Points of Benefit:

Disbelief prevents the acceptance of righteous deeds.
The danger of associating with those of ill repute.
The harm of neglecting the Quran.
One of the wisdoms of revealing the Quran in portions is to reassure the Prophet, peace be upon him, and facilitate its understanding, memorization, and implementation.
