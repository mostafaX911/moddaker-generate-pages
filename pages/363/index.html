(٣٣) وَلَا يَأۡتُونَكَ بِمَثَلٍ إِلَّا جِئۡنَٰكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَأَحۡسَنَ تَفۡسِيرًا

The polytheists do not bring any argument to you, O Messenger, except that We give you a true and firm response to it and a better explanation.

(٣٤) ٱلَّذِينَ يُحۡشَرُونَ عَلَىٰ وُجُوهِهِمۡ إِلَىٰ جَهَنَّمَ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ شَرّٞ مَّكَانٗا وَأَضَلُّ سَبِيلٗا

Those who will be dragged on their faces to Hell on the Day of Resurrection, it is they who will be in the worst situation. Because their abode will be Hell, and it is the furthest path from the truth because their path is the path of disbelief and deviation.

(٣٥) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَجَعَلۡنَا مَعَهُۥٓ أَخَاهُ هَٰرُونَ وَزِيرٗا

And We Indeed gave Moses the Torah and appointed his brother, Aaron, as a Messenger to support him.

(٣٦) فَقُلۡنَا ٱذۡهَبَآ إِلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا فَدَمَّرۡنَٰهُمۡ تَدۡمِيرٗا

We then said to them: Go to Pharaoh and his people, those who denied Our signs. They fulfilled Our order, went to them, and invited them to Allah's Oneness. However, they rejected them, and so We completely destroyed them.

(٣٧) وَقَوۡمَ نُوحٖ لَّمَّا كَذَّبُواْ ٱلرُّسُلَ أَغۡرَقۡنَٰهُمۡ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ لِلنَّاسِ ءَايَةٗۖ وَأَعۡتَدۡنَا لِلظَّٰلِمِينَ عَذَابًا أَلِيمٗا

Likewise were the people of Noah when they rejected the Messengers by rejecting Noah (peace be upon him). We destroyed them by drowning them in the flood, and We made their destruction a sign of Our Ability to utterly eliminate the transgressors. And We have prepared a painful punishment on the Day of Resurrection for the transgressors.

(٣٨) وَعَادٗا وَثَمُودَاْ وَأَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلرَّسِّ وَقُرُونَۢا بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَ كَثِيرٗا

We also destroyed 'Ād, the people of Hūd, as well as Thamūd, the people of Saleh. We destroyed the people of the Well and many a nation between them.

(٣٩) وَكُلّٗا ضَرَبۡنَا لَهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلَۖ وَكُلّٗا تَبَّرۡنَا تَتۡبِيرٗا

To all of those who were destroyed, We described the destruction of the previous nations before them as well as the reasons behind their destruction so that they may take heed. And each of them We utterly destroyed because of their disbelief and obstinacy.

(٤٠) وَلَقَدۡ أَتَوۡاْ عَلَى ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِ ٱلَّتِيٓ أُمۡطِرَتۡ مَطَرَ ٱلسَّوۡءِۚ أَفَلَمۡ يَكُونُواْ يَرَوۡنَهَاۚ بَلۡ كَانُواْ لَا يَرۡجُونَ نُشُورٗا

On their journey to the Levant, those who rejected the truth from your people passed by the town of the people of Lot. The town which had rocks rained down upon them as a punishment for their immoral behavior so that they may take heed. Were they blinded to this town and did not see it? No! Rather, they did not believe they would be resurrected and taken to account thereafter.

(٤١) وَإِذَا رَأَوۡكَ إِن يَتَّخِذُونَكَ إِلَّا هُزُوًا أَهَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي بَعَثَ ٱللَّهُ رَسُولًا

Whenever these deniers of truth meet you, O Messenger, they ridicule you by mockingly saying in denial; Is this the one Allah sent as a Messenger to us?!

(٤٢) إِن كَادَ لَيُضِلُّنَا عَنۡ ءَالِهَتِنَا لَوۡلَآ أَن صَبَرۡنَا عَلَيۡهَاۚ وَسَوۡفَ يَعۡلَمُونَ حِينَ يَرَوۡنَ ٱلۡعَذَابَ مَنۡ أَضَلُّ سَبِيلًا

He almost led us away from worshiping our gods. Were it not that we remained steadfastly worshiping them, he would have turned us away from them with his clear proofs and evidence. When they see the punishment with their own eyes in their graves and on the Day of Resurrection, they will come to know who is really on the stray path. Is it them or him? And they will come to know which of the two is astray.

(٤٣) أَرَءَيۡتَ مَنِ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَٰهَهُۥ هَوَىٰهُ أَفَأَنتَ تَكُونُ عَلَيۡهِ وَكِيلًا

Have you not seen, O Messenger, those who have turned their desires into a god and obeyed them? Are you the one who is going to be a guardian over them, bringing them back to faith and preventing them from disbelief?!

Points of Benefit:

Disbelief in Allah and rejecting His signs is a cause for the destruction of nations.
The absence of belief in resurrection is a cause for the failure to heed admonition.
Ridiculing the people of truth is akin to the behavior of disbelievers.
The danger of following one's desires.
