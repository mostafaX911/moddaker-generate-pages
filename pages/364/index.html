(٤٤) أَمۡ تَحۡسَبُ أَنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ يَسۡمَعُونَ أَوۡ يَعۡقِلُونَۚ إِنۡ هُمۡ إِلَّا كَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ بَلۡ هُمۡ أَضَلُّ سَبِيلًا

Rather, O Messenger, do you think most of those whom you call to Allah's Oneness and obedience listen acceptingly or understand the evidence and proofs? They are only like cattle regarding their hearing, thinking, and understanding. Rather, they are even more astray from the path than cattle.

(٤٥) أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَيۡفَ مَدَّ ٱلظِّلَّ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ لَجَعَلَهُۥ سَاكِنٗا ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَا ٱلشَّمۡسَ عَلَيۡهِ دَلِيلٗا

Do you not see, O Messenger, the effects of Allah's creation? How your Lord extends shade over the surface of the Earth. If He had willed to make it still, without moving, He could have made it so. Then, We made the sun as an indicator for shade, extending and shortening in accordance with it.

(٤٦) ثُمَّ قَبَضۡنَٰهُ إِلَيۡنَا قَبۡضٗا يَسِيرٗا

Then, We retract the shade, reducing it gradually, little by little, in accordance with the rising sun.

(٤٧) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلَّيۡلَ لِبَاسٗا وَٱلنَّوۡمَ سُبَاتٗا وَجَعَلَ ٱلنَّهَارَ نُشُورٗا

It is Allah who rendered the night as a garment that covers you and all other things. It is He who made sleep a rest for you from work. It is He who made the day for you to go out and work.

(٤٨) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَرۡسَلَ ٱلرِّيَٰحَ بُشۡرَۢا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ رَحۡمَتِهِۦۚ وَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ طَهُورٗا

It is He who sends the winds as heralds of the good news of rain, a Mercy to His slaves. We sent down pure rainwater from above, which they use for purification.

(٤٩) لِّنُحۡـِۧيَ بِهِۦ بَلۡدَةٗ مَّيۡتٗا وَنُسۡقِيَهُۥ مِمَّا خَلَقۡنَآ أَنۡعَٰمٗا وَأَنَاسِيَّ كَثِيرٗا

With this falling rainwater, We revive barren land void of vegetation, causing various plants to sprout, spreading vegetation. We also provide drinking water for the many people and animals we created.

(٥٠) وَلَقَدۡ صَرَّفۡنَٰهُ بَيۡنَهُمۡ لِيَذَّكَّرُواْ فَأَبَىٰٓ أَكۡثَرُ ٱلنَّاسِ إِلَّا كُفُورٗا

In the Quran, we have clarified evidence and clear proof in various ways so that they might take heed. But most people persist in their rejection and denial of the truth.

(٥١) وَلَوۡ شِئۡنَا لَبَعَثۡنَا فِي كُلِّ قَرۡيَةٖ نَّذِيرٗا

If We wished, We would have sent a Messenger to every village to warn them of Allah's punishment. But We did not will it. We sent Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم as a Messenger to all people.

(٥٢) فَلَا تُطِعِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ وَجَٰهِدۡهُم بِهِۦ جِهَادٗا كَبِيرٗا

So, do not follow the disbelievers' demands for concessions and proposals. Strive against them with this Quran that was sent down to you by being patient over harm and tolerating difficulties in calling them to Allah.

(٥٣) ۞وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي مَرَجَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ هَٰذَا عَذۡبٞ فُرَاتٞ وَهَٰذَا مِلۡحٌ أُجَاجٞ وَجَعَلَ بَيۡنَهُمَا بَرۡزَخٗا وَحِجۡرٗا مَّحۡجُورٗا

It is Allah, the Exalted, who merged the water of the two seas, one sweet and the other salty, and put a barrier, dividing and preventing them from mixing.

(٥٤) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ مِنَ ٱلۡمَآءِ بَشَرٗا فَجَعَلَهُۥ نَسَبٗا وَصِهۡرٗاۗ وَكَانَ رَبُّكَ قَدِيرٗا

It is He who created humanity from the discharge of a man and woman. From the creation of humanity, He established kinship by blood and inlaws through marriage. Your Lord, O Messenger, is all-powerful; nothing is beyond His ability. Through His Power, He created humanity from the discharge of a man and woman.

(٥٥) وَيَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا يَنفَعُهُمۡ وَلَا يَضُرُّهُمۡۗ وَكَانَ ٱلۡكَافِرُ عَلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ ظَهِيرٗا

The disbelievers worship idols instead of Allah, which can neither benefit them if they obey them, nor harm them if they disobey them. The disbeliever has always followed the Devil in that which angers Allah, the Exalted.

Points of Benefit:

The degradation of the disbeliever to a level below that of animals is due to his disbelief in Allah.
The phenomenon of shadows is a sign among Allah's signs indicative of His power.
Diversifying arguments and evidence is a successful educational method.
Inviting with the Quran is one of the forms of Jihad in the path of Allah.
