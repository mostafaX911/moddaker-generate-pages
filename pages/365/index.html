(٥٦) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا مُبَشِّرٗا وَنَذِيرٗا

And we have not sent you, O Messenger, except as a bearer of glad tidings for those who obey Allah, by having faith and performing righteous actions, and as a warner to those who disobey Him, by committing disbelief and sin.

(٥٧) قُلۡ مَآ أَسۡ‍َٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرٍ إِلَّا مَن شَآءَ أَن يَتَّخِذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ سَبِيلٗا

Say, O Messenger, I do not ask you for any reward for conveying the message, except that those of you who wish can take a path to please Allah by spending in His cause.

(٥٨) وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱلۡحَيِّ ٱلَّذِي لَا يَمُوتُ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِهِۦۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِهِۦ بِذُنُوبِ عِبَادِهِۦ خَبِيرًا

And rely, O Messenger, in all your affairs on Allah, the Ever-Living, the Everlasting who never dies, and exalt Him, the Exalted. Sufficient is He in knowing the sins of His slaves. None of their sins are hidden from Him, which He will recompense them for.

(٥٩) ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ فَسۡ‍َٔلۡ بِهِۦ خَبِيرٗا

The One who created the heavens and Earth and everything between them in six days and then rose above the Throne in a manner that befits His Majesty. He is the Merciful, so ask about Him, O Messenger, one who is well informed. He is Allah, the One who knows everything and from Whom nothing is hidden.

(٦٠) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ قَالُواْ وَمَا ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ أَنَسۡجُدُ لِمَا تَأۡمُرُنَا وَزَادَهُمۡ نُفُورٗا۩

And when it is said to the disbelievers, Prostrate to the Merciful, they say: And who is the Merciful? We do not know him, nor do we recognize him. Should we prostrate to whomever you are ordering us while not knowing him? His order to prostrate to the Merciful only increased their aversion to faith in Allah.

(٦١) تَبَارَكَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ بُرُوجٗا وَجَعَلَ فِيهَا سِرَٰجٗا وَقَمَرٗا مُّنِيرٗا

Blessed is He who made places in the heavens for the planets and orbiting stars. He placed therein a sun that radiates light and a luminous moon that illuminates the Earth with the light reflected from the sun.

(٦٢) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ خِلۡفَةٗ لِّمَنۡ أَرَادَ أَن يَذَّكَّرَ أَوۡ أَرَادَ شُكُورٗا

It is Allah who has rendered the night and day in succession, each one following the other, for whoever desires to contemplate the signs of Allah and attain guidance or desires to be grateful to Allah for His favors.

When Allah mentions in this Surah the disbelievers who reject faith and obedience to Him, He mentions the characteristics of His righteous slaves who are willingly obedient to Him, stating:

(٦٣) وَعِبَادُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَمۡشُونَ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ هَوۡنٗا وَإِذَا خَاطَبَهُمُ ٱلۡجَٰهِلُونَ قَالُواْ سَلَٰمٗا

The slaves of the Merciful are the believers who walk with humility and dignity upon the Earth. When the foolish address them, they do not retaliate in a similar manner. Rather, they speak to them cordially without being rude.

(٦٤) وَٱلَّذِينَ يَبِيتُونَ لِرَبِّهِمۡ سُجَّدٗا وَقِيَٰمٗا

Those who spend their nights prostrating on their foreheads and standing on their feet in prayer to Allah.

(٦٥) وَٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا ٱصۡرِفۡ عَنَّا عَذَابَ جَهَنَّمَۖ إِنَّ عَذَابَهَا كَانَ غَرَامًا

Those who say in their prayers to their Lord: Our Lord, distance us from Hell's punishment. For indeed, the punishment of Hell is perpetual and everlasting for those who die as disbelievers.

(٦٦) إِنَّهَا سَآءَتۡ مُسۡتَقَرّٗا وَمُقَامٗا

It is indeed a terrible place of settlement for those who will settle therein and a terrible residence for those who will reside therein.

(٦٧) وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَآ أَنفَقُواْ لَمۡ يُسۡرِفُواْ وَلَمۡ يَقۡتُرُواْ وَكَانَ بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَ قَوَامٗا

Those who spend their wealth without being exorbitant or restrictive with those they are responsible for providing for, whether it is themselves or others. Their spending is just and balanced, midway between exorbitance and miserliness.

Points of Benefit:

The one who calls to Allah seeks no reward from people.
Affirming the attribute of istiwā (ascension) for Allah in a manner befitting Him, the Exalted and Transcendent
Ar-Rahman is a name exclusive to Allah, indicating one of His attributes, which is mercy.
The servant is aided by the alternation of night and day to make up for what he missed in obedience in either of them.
Among the characteristics of the servants of the Most Merciful are humility and forbearance, obedience to Allah in the negligence of people, fear of Allah, and adhering to moderation in spending and other matters.
