(٦٨) وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَدۡعُونَ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ وَلَا يَقۡتُلُونَ ٱلنَّفۡسَ ٱلَّتِي حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا يَزۡنُونَۚ وَمَن يَفۡعَلۡ ذَٰلِكَ يَلۡقَ أَثَامٗا

Those who do not call on any other deity besides Allah, the Exalted, nor do they take the life of anyone whose life Allah has prohibited, except for what Allah has allowed (such as the killing of a murderer, one who has forsaken his religion, or a married adulterer), and those who do not fornicate. And whoever commits these major sins will face punishment for their sins on the Day of Resurrection.

(٦٩) يُضَٰعَفۡ لَهُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ وَيَخۡلُدۡ فِيهِۦ مُهَانًا

The punishment will be doubled for them on the Day of Resurrection, and they will remain in the punishment in disgrace and humiliation for eternity.

(٧٠) إِلَّا مَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يُبَدِّلُ ٱللَّهُ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِهِمۡ حَسَنَٰتٖۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا

But those who repent to Allah, believe, and practice righteous deeds that prove the sincerity of their repentance, Allah will turn their sins into righteous deeds. Allah is ever forgiving towards the sins of His slaves who repent to Him. He is ever Merciful towards them.

(٧١) وَمَن تَابَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَإِنَّهُۥ يَتُوبُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ مَتَابٗا

And whoever repents to Allah and proves the sincerity of their repentance by observing righteous deeds and forsaking sin, indeed, their repentance is accepted.

(٧٢) وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَشۡهَدُونَ ٱلزُّورَ وَإِذَا مَرُّواْ بِٱللَّغۡوِ مَرُّواْ كِرَامٗا

Those who do not attend falsehood, such as places of sin or prohibited forms of entertainment, and when they come across vain speech and behavior, they hurry, maintaining their dignity by avoiding them.

(٧٣) وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِّرُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ لَمۡ يَخِرُّواْ عَلَيۡهَا صُمّٗا وَعُمۡيَانٗا

Those who, when reminded of Allah's signs, which are heard or witnessed, do not close their ears to the signs that are heard, nor shut their eyes to the signs that are witnessed.

(٧٤) وَٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا هَبۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّٰتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعۡيُنٖ وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا

Those who say in their prayers to their Lord: Our Lord, make our spouses and offspring the apples of our eyes due to their acceptance and adherence to the truth. Make us leaders of truth who would be taken as examples by the righteous.

(٧٥) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يُجۡزَوۡنَ ٱلۡغُرۡفَةَ بِمَا صَبَرُواْ وَيُلَقَّوۡنَ فِيهَا تَحِيَّةٗ وَسَلَٰمًا

Those who possess these attributes will be rewarded with lofty chambers in the highest level of Paradise, Al-Firdaws, due to their steadfastness in obedience to Allah. There, they will be greeted by the angels with peace, and they will be safe and secure from all difficulties.

(٧٦) خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ حَسُنَتۡ مُسۡتَقَرّٗا وَمُقَامٗا

They will abide therein for eternity; a wonderful abode and residence for them to abide and reside in.

(٧٧) قُلۡ مَا يَعۡبَؤُاْ بِكُمۡ رَبِّي لَوۡلَا دُعَآؤُكُمۡۖ فَقَدۡ كَذَّبۡتُمۡ فَسَوۡفَ يَكُونُ لِزَامَۢا

Say, O Messenger, to the disbelievers who persist in their disbelief: My Lord does not pay attention to you because of any benefit that He receives from your obedience. Were it not that He had slaves who pray to Him in praise and supplication, He would not have any concern for you. Indeed, you have rejected the message that the Messenger brought to you from your Lord. Therefore, the recompense of this rejection will be inseparable from you.

Points of Benefit:

Characteristics of the servants of the Most Merciful include: avoiding polytheism, refraining from unjust killing, avoiding adultery, staying away from falsehood, contemplating the signs of Allah, and supplication.
Sincere repentance requires abandoning sin and performing acts of obedience.
Patience is a means to enter Al-Firdous, the highest level of Paradise.
Allah is self-sufficient and has no need for the faith of the disbelievers.
