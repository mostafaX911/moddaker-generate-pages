The Poets (Surah Al-Shu'arah)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Demonstrating Allah's signs of supporting the messengers and destroying the rejectors.

Explanation:

(١) طسٓمٓ

Tā Sīn Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ

These are the verses of the Quran that clarify truth from falsehood.

(٣) لَعَلَّكَ بَٰخِعٞ نَّفۡسَكَ أَلَّا يَكُونُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ

Perhaps due to your eagerness, O Messenger, you are killing yourself with sorrow and keenness for their guidance because of the disbelievers rejecting you and not believing in your message.

(٤) إِن نَّشَأۡ نُنَزِّلۡ عَلَيۡهِم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ ءَايَةٗ فَظَلَّتۡ أَعۡنَٰقُهُمۡ لَهَا خَٰضِعِينَ

If We wished to send down a sign from the heavens that would compel them to believe, We would send it to them. Their heads would remain bowed in utter humility before it. But We do not wish that to test whether they would believe in the Unseen or not.

(٥) وَمَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن ذِكۡرٖ مِّنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ مُحۡدَثٍ إِلَّا كَانُواْ عَنۡهُ مُعۡرِضِينَ

A new reminder that proves His Oneness and the truthfulness of His Prophet is not revealed by the Merciful to these polytheists, except that they turn away from listening and believing in it.

(٦) فَقَدۡ كَذَّبُواْ فَسَيَأۡتِيهِمۡ أَنۢبَٰٓؤُاْ مَا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

They have rejected what their Messenger brought them. So, the reality of what they used to mock will befall them, and the punishment will take them.

(٧) أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كَمۡ أَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوۡجٖ كَرِيمٍ

These people remained persistent in their disbelief. They did not reflect over the Earth. How many different types of vegetation, beautiful to look at and full of benefits, did we cause to grow on it?!

(٨) إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

The varying types of vegetation made to grow on Earth clearly indicate that the One who has caused them to grow has the power to revive the dead. However, most of them are not believers.

(٩) وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, is the Prevailing One who can never be overpowered, the Merciful to His slaves.

(١٠) وَإِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبُّكَ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنِ ٱئۡتِ ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Remember, O Messenger, when your Lord called upon Moses, instructing him to go to the transgressors who disbelieved in Allah and enslaved the people of Moses.

(١١) قَوۡمَ فِرۡعَوۡنَۚ أَلَا يَتَّقُونَ

They were Pharaoh's people. He was to gently instruct them to be mindful of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

(١٢) قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ أَن يُكَذِّبُونِ

Moses (peace be upon him) said: I fear that they will reject the message I convey to them about you.

(١٣) وَيَضِيقُ صَدۡرِي وَلَا يَنطَلِقُ لِسَانِي فَأَرۡسِلۡ إِلَىٰ هَٰرُونَ

And that I will be distressed due to their rejection and not be able to speak. So send Gabriel (peace be upon him) to my brother Aaron so that he may support me.

(١٤) وَلَهُمۡ عَلَيَّ ذَنۢبٞ فَأَخَافُ أَن يَقۡتُلُونِ

They also have a charge against me because I had killed the Copt, and I fear they may kill me.

(١٥) قَالَ كَلَّاۖ فَٱذۡهَبَا بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآۖ إِنَّا مَعَكُم مُّسۡتَمِعُونَ

Allah said to Moses (peace be upon him): No! They will never be able to kill you. Go, you and your brother Aaron, with Our signs that prove your truthfulness. We shall be with you with Our support, listening to what you say and what is said to you. We will miss nothing of that.

(١٦) فَأۡتِيَا فِرۡعَوۡنَ فَقُولَآ إِنَّا رَسُولُ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Go to Pharaoh and tell him: We are two Messengers sent to you by the Lord of all created things.

(١٧) أَنۡ أَرۡسِلۡ مَعَنَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ

That you send the Israelites with us.

(١٨) قَالَ أَلَمۡ نُرَبِّكَ فِينَا وَلِيدٗا وَلَبِثۡتَ فِينَا مِنۡ عُمُرِكَ سِنِينَ

Pharaoh said to Moses (peace be upon him: Did we not raise you as a child among us? Did you not live with us for many years of your life? So what has now made you claim prophethood?

(١٩) وَفَعَلۡتَ فَعۡلَتَكَ ٱلَّتِي فَعَلۡتَ وَأَنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

You committed a serious crime by killing the Copt while defending one of your people. You deny my favors to you.

Points of Benefit:

The Prophet, peace be upon him, eagerly sought the guidance of people.
Affirmation of the attributes of Majesty and Mercy for Allah.
The importance of having an open heart and eloquence for the caller.
The calls of the prophets aim at liberating people from servitude to other than Allah.
Pharaoh objected to Moses' message by citing the occurrence of killing attributed to him (Moses, peace be upon him). Moses acknowledged the act, suggesting that it wasn't a valid argument for Pharaoh's denial.
