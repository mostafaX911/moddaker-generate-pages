(٢٠) قَالَ فَعَلۡتُهَآ إِذٗا وَأَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

Moses (peace be upon him) said, admitting to Pharaoh: I killed that man while I was ignorant, before I received revelation.

(٢١) فَفَرَرۡتُ مِنكُمۡ لَمَّا خِفۡتُكُمۡ فَوَهَبَ لِي رَبِّي حُكۡمٗا وَجَعَلَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

After killing him, I fled from you to Midian, fearing that you would kill me. Then, my Lord bestowed me with knowledge and made me one of His Messengers that He sends to people.

(٢٢) وَتِلۡكَ نِعۡمَةٞ تَمُنُّهَا عَلَيَّ أَنۡ عَبَّدتَّ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ

Raising me without enslaving me while enslaving the Israelites is truly a reminder of your favor upon me. But that does not prevent me from calling you to the truth.

(٢٣) قَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ وَمَا رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Pharaoh said to Moses (peace be upon him): What is the Lord of all created things, Whose Messenger you claim to be?

(٢٤) قَالَ رَبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآۖ إِن كُنتُم مُّوقِنِينَ

Moses responded: The Lord of all created things is the Lord of the heavens and the Earth. The Lord of everything between them if you have sure faith. Indeed, He is their Lord, so worship Him alone.

(٢٥) قَالَ لِمَنۡ حَوۡلَهُۥٓ أَلَا تَسۡتَمِعُونَ

Pharaoh asked the noble of his people around him: Do you hear Moses's response and the false claims it contains?

(٢٦) قَالَ رَبُّكُمۡ وَرَبُّ ءَابَآئِكُمُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

Moses said to them: Allah is your Lord and the Lord of your forefathers.

(٢٧) قَالَ إِنَّ رَسُولَكُمُ ٱلَّذِيٓ أُرۡسِلَ إِلَيۡكُمۡ لَمَجۡنُونٞ

Pharaoh said: This man who claims to be your Messenger is insane. He does not know how to respond and says things that do not make sense.

(٢٨) قَالَ رَبُّ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقِ وَٱلۡمَغۡرِبِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآۖ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ

Moses said; Allah, the One I call you to, is the Lord of the East and the West, the Lord of everything between them. If only you had the intellect to reflect.

(٢٩) قَالَ لَئِنِ ٱتَّخَذۡتَ إِلَٰهًا غَيۡرِي لَأَجۡعَلَنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمَسۡجُونِينَ

Unable to debate, Pharaoh said to Moses: If you worship any god other than Me, I will make you a prisoner.

(٣٠) قَالَ أَوَلَوۡ جِئۡتُكَ بِشَيۡءٖ مُّبِينٖ

Moses (peace be upon him) said to Pharaoh: Would you imprison me even if I brought a clear sign of the truthfulness of the message I bring to you from Allah?

(٣١) قَالَ فَأۡتِ بِهِۦٓ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

He said: Bring what you claim will prove your truthfulness if you are telling the truth!

(٣٢) فَأَلۡقَىٰ عَصَاهُ فَإِذَا هِيَ ثُعۡبَانٞ مُّبِينٞ

So Moses cast his staff on the ground, and it suddenly turned into a snake that could be clearly seen.

(٣٣) وَنَزَعَ يَدَهُۥ فَإِذَا هِيَ بَيۡضَآءُ لِلنَّٰظِرِينَ

He put his ordinary-looking hand into an opening of his garment, and he then drew it out a radiant white, not the white of vitiligo, for everyone to see.

(٣٤) قَالَ لِلۡمَلَإِ حَوۡلَهُۥٓ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَسَٰحِرٌ عَلِيمٞ

Pharaoh said to the noble of his people around him: This man is a well-versed magician.

(٣٥) يُرِيدُ أَن يُخۡرِجَكُم مِّنۡ أَرۡضِكُم بِسِحۡرِهِۦ فَمَاذَا تَأۡمُرُونَ

He means to drive you out of your land with his magic! So what do you suggest we do with him?

(٣٦) قَالُوٓاْ أَرۡجِهۡ وَأَخَاهُ وَٱبۡعَثۡ فِي ٱلۡمَدَآئِنِ حَٰشِرِينَ

They said to him: Delay him and his brother and do not rush to punish them. Send people to the cities of Egypt who will gather the magicians.

(٣٧) يَأۡتُوكَ بِكُلِّ سَحَّارٍ عَلِيمٖ

They will bring you every well-versed magician.

(٣٨) فَجُمِعَ ٱلسَّحَرَةُ لِمِيقَٰتِ يَوۡمٖ مَّعۡلُومٖ

Pharaoh assembled his magicians to challenge Moses at an appointed place and time.

(٣٩) وَقِيلَ لِلنَّاسِ هَلۡ أَنتُم مُّجۡتَمِعُونَ

The people were told: Will you not gather to see who triumphs: Moses or the magicians?

Points of Benefit:

Previous mistakes of the caller and the blessings upon him do not mean he cannot call those who wronged him or those upon whom blessings were bestowed.
Taking precautions against the enemy does not contradict belief and reliance on Allah.
The creations of Allah indicate His Lordship and Oneness.
Weakness in argumentation is one of the reasons for resorting to violence.
Inciting the public against the people of religion is a tactic used by tyrants.
