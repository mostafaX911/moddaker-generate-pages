(٤٠) لَعَلَّنَا نَتَّبِعُ ٱلسَّحَرَةَ إِن كَانُواْ هُمُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبِينَ

Let us follow the magician's religion if they triumph over Moses.

(٤١) فَلَمَّا جَآءَ ٱلسَّحَرَةُ قَالُواْ لِفِرۡعَوۡنَ أَئِنَّ لَنَا لَأَجۡرًا إِن كُنَّا نَحۡنُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبِينَ

So when the magicians came before Pharaoh to challenge Moses, they asked him: Will we have a tangible or intangible reward if we triumph over Moses?

(٤٢) قَالَ نَعَمۡ وَإِنَّكُمۡ إِذٗا لَّمِنَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبِينَ

Pharaoh said to them: Yes, you shall have a reward. If you triumph over Moses, you shall become close to me by being appointed to lofty positions.

(٤٣) قَالَ لَهُم مُّوسَىٰٓ أَلۡقُواْ مَآ أَنتُم مُّلۡقُونَ

With confidence in Allah's support and clarifying what he possessed was not sorcery, Moses said to them; Cast whatever ropes and staffs you have.

(٤٤) فَأَلۡقَوۡاْ حِبَالَهُمۡ وَعِصِيَّهُمۡ وَقَالُواْ بِعِزَّةِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّا لَنَحۡنُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبُونَ

So they cast their ropes and staffs while saying: By Pharaoh's greatness, we shall surely triumph, and Moses is the one who will be defeated!

(٤٥) فَأَلۡقَىٰ مُوسَىٰ عَصَاهُ فَإِذَا هِيَ تَلۡقَفُ مَا يَأۡفِكُونَ

So Moses cast his staff, which turned into a snake, swallowing the illusions they released over the people with their magic.

(٤٦) فَأُلۡقِيَ ٱلسَّحَرَةُ سَٰجِدِينَ

When the magicians saw Moses's staff swallowing up what they had cast, they fell into prostration.

(٤٧) قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

They said: We believe in the Lord of all creation.

(٤٨) رَبِّ مُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَ

The Lord of Moses and Aaron (peace be upon them).

(٤٩) قَالَ ءَامَنتُمۡ لَهُۥ قَبۡلَ أَنۡ ءَاذَنَ لَكُمۡۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَكَبِيرُكُمُ ٱلَّذِي عَلَّمَكُمُ ٱلسِّحۡرَ فَلَسَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَۚ لَأُقَطِّعَنَّ أَيۡدِيَكُمۡ وَأَرۡجُلَكُم مِّنۡ خِلَٰفٖ وَلَأُصَلِّبَنَّكُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

In condemnation of the magicians' faith, Pharaoh said to them: Have you believed in Moses before I have permitted? Indeed, Moses is your mentor who has taught you sorcery. You have all collaborated to exile the people of Egypt from their lands, and you will soon come to know the punishment I shall inflict upon you. I shall cut off the right foot and left hand of each of you from opposite sides or vice versa and crucify you on the trunks of palm trees. I shall spare none of you.

(٥٠) قَالُواْ لَا ضَيۡرَۖ إِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا مُنقَلِبُونَ

The magicians said to Pharaoh: There is no harm in the maiming and crucifixion you threaten us with in this worldly life because your punishment will end. And we shall return to our Lord who shall admit us into His everlasting mercy.

(٥١) إِنَّا نَطۡمَعُ أَن يَغۡفِرَ لَنَا رَبُّنَا خَطَٰيَٰنَآ أَن كُنَّآ أَوَّلَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

We indeed hope that Allah erases our past sins due to being the first to believe in Moses.

(٥٢) ۞وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنۡ أَسۡرِ بِعِبَادِيٓ إِنَّكُم مُّتَّبَعُونَ

And We revealed to Moses, commanding him to lead the Israelites out by night because Pharaoh and his people would pursue them and force them back.

(٥٣) فَأَرۡسَلَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ فِي ٱلۡمَدَآئِنِ حَٰشِرِينَ

When Pharaoh learned of the Israelites' escape, he sent some soldiers to the cities to rally and gather the armies to bring them back.

(٥٤) إِنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ لَشِرۡذِمَةٞ قَلِيلُونَ

Pharaoh said in belittlement of the Israelites: Indeed, they are a small group.

(٥٥) وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَنَا لَغَآئِظُونَ

And they are doing what angers us.

(٥٦) وَإِنَّا لَجَمِيعٌ حَٰذِرُونَ

And indeed, we are prepared for them and alert.

(٥٧) فَأَخۡرَجۡنَٰهُم مِّن جَنَّٰتٖ وَعُيُونٖ

So We expelled Pharaoh and his people from Egypt, a wealthy land with abundant gardens and flowing streams.

(٥٨) وَكُنُوزٖ وَمَقَامٖ كَرِيمٖ

A land with treasures of wealth and beautiful dwellings.

(٥٩) كَذَٰلِكَۖ وَأَوۡرَثۡنَٰهَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ

And just as We expelled Pharaoh and his people from these favors, We granted their likes to the Israelites in the Levant.

(٦٠) فَأَتۡبَعُوهُم مُّشۡرِقِينَ

Pharaoh and his people followed the trail of the Israelites at sunrise.

Points of Benefit:

The relationship among the people of falsehood is based on material interests.
Moses' confidence in triumphing over the sorcerers is a confirmation of his Lord's promise.
The belief of the sorcerers is evidence that Allah is the One who directs hearts, guiding them as He wills.
Tyranny and oppression are among the reasons for the downfall of kingdoms.
