(٢٣١) وَإِذَا طَلَّقۡتُمُ ٱلنِّسَآءَ فَبَلَغۡنَ أَجَلَهُنَّ فَأَمۡسِكُوهُنَّ بِمَعۡرُوفٍ أَوۡ سَرِّحُوهُنَّ بِمَعۡرُوفٖۚ وَلَا تُمۡسِكُوهُنَّ ضِرَارٗا لِّتَعۡتَدُواْۚ وَمَن يَفۡعَلۡ ذَٰلِكَ فَقَدۡ ظَلَمَ نَفۡسَهُۥۚ وَلَا تَتَّخِذُوٓاْ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ هُزُوٗاۚ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَمَآ أَنزَلَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةِ يَعِظُكُم بِهِۦۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٞ

When you divorce a woman, and they approach the end of their waiting period, you have the right to take them back or to leave them in a good way until their waiting period is over. Do not take them back to oppress and harm them, as was the practice in pre-Islamic times. Whoever acts intending to cause harm to them has wronged himself by exposing himself to sin and punishment. Do not make Allah's words the object of mockery and play out of insolence. Remember Allah's favors upon you, the most important of which is the revelation you have received in the form of the Quran and Sunnah, through which He reminds you through encouragement and warning. Fear Allah by fulfilling His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. Know that Allah knows everything, and nothing is hidden from Him, and he will compensate you for your actions accordingly.

(٢٣٢) وَإِذَا طَلَّقۡتُمُ ٱلنِّسَآءَ فَبَلَغۡنَ أَجَلَهُنَّ فَلَا تَعۡضُلُوهُنَّ أَن يَنكِحۡنَ أَزۡوَٰجَهُنَّ إِذَا تَرَٰضَوۡاْ بَيۡنَهُم بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِۗ ذَٰلِكَ يُوعَظُ بِهِۦ مَن كَانَ مِنكُمۡ يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۗ ذَٰلِكُمۡ أَزۡكَىٰ لَكُمۡ وَأَطۡهَرُۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

When you divorce women less than three times and their waiting period ends, do not stop them from returning to their husbands with a new marriage contract if they wish to do so in mutual agreement with their husbands. This law prohibiting them from being prevented is a reminder for those among you who have faith in Allah and the Last Day. If you obey, this will bring you greater growth in goodness and is better at keeping your honor and deeds free of impurities. Allah knows the reality and outcome of matters, whereas you do not.

(٢٣٣) ۞وَٱلۡوَٰلِدَٰتُ يُرۡضِعۡنَ أَوۡلَٰدَهُنَّ حَوۡلَيۡنِ كَامِلَيۡنِۖ لِمَنۡ أَرَادَ أَن يُتِمَّ ٱلرَّضَاعَةَۚ وَعَلَى ٱلۡمَوۡلُودِ لَهُۥ رِزۡقُهُنَّ وَكِسۡوَتُهُنَّ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِۚ لَا تُكَلَّفُ نَفۡسٌ إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۚ لَا تُضَآرَّ وَٰلِدَةُۢ بِوَلَدِهَا وَلَا مَوۡلُودٞ لَّهُۥ بِوَلَدِهِۦۚ وَعَلَى ٱلۡوَارِثِ مِثۡلُ ذَٰلِكَۗ فَإِنۡ أَرَادَا فِصَالًا عَن تَرَاضٖ مِّنۡهُمَا وَتَشَاوُرٖ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡهِمَاۗ وَإِنۡ أَرَدتُّمۡ أَن تَسۡتَرۡضِعُوٓاْ أَوۡلَٰدَكُمۡ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِذَا سَلَّمۡتُم مَّآ ءَاتَيۡتُم بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِۗ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٞ

Mothers who intend to complete the breastfeeding period should nurse their children for two full years. The father of a child is responsible for the maintenance and clothing of a divorced mother who breastfeeds her child in accordance with the customs of society so long as they do not contradict sacred law. No soul is burdened beyond what it can handle. No parent is allowed to use the child as a means to harm the other. The heir (who would inherit from the child if the child were to pass away leaving behind wealth) shall be responsible, if the father dies without leaving any wealth, for the same obligations as the father's. If the parents decide to wean the child before two years finish, then there is no sin on them if this is done after mutual consultation and agreement that it is indeed in the child's best interests. If you wish to hire wet nurses for your children instead of their mothers, there is no sin in doing so, provided you pay the agreed wages of such wet nurses without any shortchanging or delay. Be mindful of Allah by fulfilling His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, and know that Allah sees everything you do and nothing is hidden from Him. He will recompense you for any deed you do.

Points of Benefit:

Men are prohibited from oppressing women, whether by preventing those under their care from marriage or forcing them to do what they do not want.
The legislation has protected the right of the mother to breastfeed, even if she is divorced from her husband, and he must provide for her so long as she breastfeeds his child.
Allah, the Exalted, has forbidden the spouses from using the children as a means to harm each other.
All affairs related to marital life are encouraged to be based upon consultation and consensus between the spouses.
