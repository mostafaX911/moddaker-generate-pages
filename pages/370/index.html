(٦١) فَلَمَّا تَرَٰٓءَا ٱلۡجَمۡعَانِ قَالَ أَصۡحَٰبُ مُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّا لَمُدۡرَكُونَ

So when Pharaoh and his people reached Moses and his people, whereas they were in sight of each other, Moses's people said: Pharaoh and his people will surely catch up to us, and we are powerless against them.

(٦٢) قَالَ كَلَّآۖ إِنَّ مَعِيَ رَبِّي سَيَهۡدِينِ

Moses said to his people: It is not as you imagine, for indeed, my Lord is with me with His support and aid. He shall guide and show me the path to salvation.

(٦٣) فَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنِ ٱضۡرِب بِّعَصَاكَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَۖ فَٱنفَلَقَ فَكَانَ كُلُّ فِرۡقٖ كَٱلطَّوۡدِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

We revealed to Moses, commanding him to strike the sea with his staff, and so he did. The sea parted into twelve paths, the number of the tribes of the Israelites. Each path cut from the sea was like a tremendous and stable mountain from which water does not leak.

(٦٤) وَأَزۡلَفۡنَا ثَمَّ ٱلۡأٓخَرِينَ

We allowed Pharaoh and his people to draw close until they entered the sea, thinking that the path was traversable.

(٦٥) وَأَنجَيۡنَا مُوسَىٰ وَمَن مَّعَهُۥٓ أَجۡمَعِينَ

And We saved Moses and those with him from the Israelites; none amongst them perished.

(٦٦) ثُمَّ أَغۡرَقۡنَا ٱلۡأٓخَرِينَ

We then destroyed Pharaoh and his people by drowning them in the sea.

(٦٧) إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Indeed, the parting of the sea for Moses, his salvation, and Pharaoh's destruction is a sign proving the truthfulness of Moses. And the majority of those with Pharaoh were not believers.

(٦٨) وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

And Indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, is the Mighty who takes retribution from His enemies, the Merciful to those of them who repent.

(٦٩) وَٱتۡلُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ نَبَأَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ

O Messenger, recite unto them Abraham's story.

(٧٠) إِذۡ قَالَ لِأَبِيهِ وَقَوۡمِهِۦ مَا تَعۡبُدُونَ

When he said to his father Azar and his people: What is it you worship instead of Allah?

(٧١) قَالُواْ نَعۡبُدُ أَصۡنَامٗا فَنَظَلُّ لَهَا عَٰكِفِينَ

His people said to him: We worship idols, and we remain steadfast and consistent in their worship.

(٧٢) قَالَ هَلۡ يَسۡمَعُونَكُمۡ إِذۡ تَدۡعُونَ

Abraham said to them: Do the idols hear your prayers when you pray to them?

(٧٣) أَوۡ يَنفَعُونَكُمۡ أَوۡ يَضُرُّونَ

Or do they benefit you if you feed them, or harm you if you disobey them?

(٧٤) قَالُواْ بَلۡ وَجَدۡنَآ ءَابَآءَنَا كَذَٰلِكَ يَفۡعَلُونَ

They responded: They do not hear us when we supplicate to them, nor do they benefit us when we feed them, nor do they harm us if we disobey them. However, the reality is that we found our forefathers doing that, so we imitate them.

(٧٥) قَالَ أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡبُدُونَ

Abraham said: Have you contemplated and looked at the idols you worship instead of Allah?

(٧٦) أَنتُمۡ وَءَابَآؤُكُمُ ٱلۡأَقۡدَمُونَ

And what your forefathers worshiped?

(٧٧) فَإِنَّهُمۡ عَدُوّٞ لِّيٓ إِلَّا رَبَّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Indeed, they are all enemies to me because they are all false except for the Lord of all creation.

(٧٨) ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَنِي فَهُوَ يَهۡدِينِ

The One who created me and therefore guides me to the best of this world and the Hereafter.

(٧٩) وَٱلَّذِي هُوَ يُطۡعِمُنِي وَيَسۡقِينِ

It is He alone who feeds me when I am hungry and gives me to drink when I am thirsty.

(٨٠) وَإِذَا مَرِضۡتُ فَهُوَ يَشۡفِينِ

And when I am ill, He alone cures me; there is no healer for me except Him.

(٨١) وَٱلَّذِي يُمِيتُنِي ثُمَّ يُحۡيِينِ

It is He alone who will cause me to die when my lifespan expires and resurrect me after my death.

(٨٢) وَٱلَّذِيٓ أَطۡمَعُ أَن يَغۡفِرَ لِي خَطِيٓ‍َٔتِي يَوۡمَ ٱلدِّينِ

It is He alone Whom I hope will forgive my sins on the day of requital.

(٨٣) رَبِّ هَبۡ لِي حُكۡمٗا وَأَلۡحِقۡنِي بِٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

Abraham, supplicating to his Lord, said: O my Lord, grant me a deep understanding of religion and unite me with the righteous prophets who came before me by admitting me into Paradise with them.

Points of Benefit:

Allah is with His believing servants with victory, support, and deliverance from hardships.
Affirmation of the attributes of might and mercy for Allah the Exalted.
The danger of blind conformity.
The hope of the believer in their Lord is profound.
