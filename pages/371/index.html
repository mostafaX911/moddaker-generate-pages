(٨٤) وَٱجۡعَل لِّي لِسَانَ صِدۡقٖ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ

And grant me a beautiful and praiseworthy account with the generations after me.

(٨٥) وَٱجۡعَلۡنِي مِن وَرَثَةِ جَنَّةِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

And make me among those who inherit the dwellings of Paradise where your believing slaves shall reside in luxury; and grant me residence in them.

(٨٦) وَٱغۡفِرۡ لِأَبِيٓ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

And forgive my father. Indeed, he was amongst those who deviated from the truth due to polytheism. Abraham supplicated for his father before it became apparent to him that he was from the people of the Inferno. After it became clear to him, he disassociated from him and no longer supplicated for him.

(٨٧) وَلَا تُخۡزِنِي يَوۡمَ يُبۡعَثُونَ

Do not humiliate me by punishing me on the Day people are resurrected to be taken to account.

(٨٨) يَوۡمَ لَا يَنفَعُ مَالٞ وَلَا بَنُونَ

The Day when the wealth humanity accumulated in this world shall be of no benefit, nor would the offspring that used to support them.

(٨٩) إِلَّا مَنۡ أَتَى ٱللَّهَ بِقَلۡبٖ سَلِيمٖ

Except those who come with a sound heart, free of polytheism, hypocrisy, ostentation, and arrogance. For indeed, they would benefit from the wealth they spent in the path of Allah, and the offspring who prayed for them.

(٩٠) وَأُزۡلِفَتِ ٱلۡجَنَّةُ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Paradise will be brought close to the righteous who complied with His commands and avoided His prohibitions.

(٩١) وَبُرِّزَتِ ٱلۡجَحِيمُ لِلۡغَاوِينَ

And the Fire will be made visible on the plains of resurrection to those who went astray and deviated from the true religion.

(٩٢) وَقِيلَ لَهُمۡ أَيۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡبُدُونَ

It will be said to them in rebuke: Where are the idols you used to worship? Are they going to support you by preventing the punishment of Allah from you, or are they going to support themselves?

(٩٣) مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ هَلۡ يَنصُرُونَكُمۡ أَوۡ يَنتَصِرُونَ

It will be said to them in rebuke: Where are the idols you used to worship? Are they going to support you by preventing the punishment of Allah from you, or are they going to support themselves?

(٩٤) فَكُبۡكِبُواْ فِيهَا هُمۡ وَٱلۡغَاوُۥنَ

They will be thrown into the Inferno one on top of the other, together with whoever they led astray.

(٩٥) وَجُنُودُ إِبۡلِيسَ أَجۡمَعُونَ

Iblees's helpers are all devils without exception.

(٩٦) قَالُواْ وَهُمۡ فِيهَا يَخۡتَصِمُونَ

The polytheists who used to worship other than Allah, taking them as partners to Him, will say while arguing with those they used to worship,

(٩٧) تَٱللَّهِ إِن كُنَّا لَفِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٍ

By Allah, we were indeed clearly astray from the truth.

(٩٨) إِذۡ نُسَوِّيكُم بِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Since we held you on par with the Lord of all creatures, and so worshipped you as we worship Him.

(٩٩) وَمَآ أَضَلَّنَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ

And no one led us astray from the true path except the criminals who called us towards worshipping them instead of Allah.

(١٠٠) فَمَا لَنَا مِن شَٰفِعِينَ

So we have no intercessors today who will intercede for us before Allah to save us from His punishment.

(١٠١) وَلَا صَدِيقٍ حَمِيمٖ

Nor do we have a friend who sincerely loves us to defend and intercede for us.

(١٠٢) فَلَوۡ أَنَّ لَنَا كَرَّةٗ فَنَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

If only we could return to our worldly life so that we could be amongst those who believed in Allah.

(١٠٣) إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Indeed, in what was mentioned, in the story of Abraham (peace be upon him) and the ultimate destination of the deniers is a lesson for those who take heed. Although most of them are not believers.

(١٠٤) وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

And Indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, is the Mighty who takes retribution from His enemies, the Merciful to those of them who repent.

(١٠٥) كَذَّبَتۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٍ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

The people of Noah rejected all the messengers when they rejected Noah (peace be upon him).

(١٠٦) إِذۡ قَالَ لَهُمۡ أَخُوهُمۡ نُوحٌ أَلَا تَتَّقُونَ

When Noah said to them: Will you not be mindful of Allah and leave the worship of other than Him out of fear for Him?

(١٠٧) إِنِّي لَكُمۡ رَسُولٌ أَمِينٞ

Indeed, I am a messenger sent by Allah to you. I am trustworthy and will not add or remove anything from what has been revealed to me.

(١٠٨) فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ

So fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. And obey me in what I command you to do and what I forbid you from.

(١٠٩) وَمَآ أَسۡ‍َٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرٍۖ إِنۡ أَجۡرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

I do not seek any reward from you for what I convey to you from my Lord. My reward is only from Allah, the Lord of all creation, not from anyone else.

(١١٠) فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ

So fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. And obey me in what I command you to do and what I forbid you from.

(١١١) ۞قَالُوٓاْ أَنُؤۡمِنُ لَكَ وَٱتَّبَعَكَ ٱلۡأَرۡذَلُونَ

His people said to him: O Noah, are we to believe in you, follow what you have brought, and apply it while your followers are the lowliest? There aren't any leaders or noblemen among them.

Points of Benefit:

The importance of keeping the heart safe from diseases such as envy, ostentation, and self-admiration.
Transferring responsibility for misguidance to the misguided does not benefit those who are astray.
Denying the Messenger of Allah is equivalent to denying all messengers.
Skillful storytelling in the narrative of Abraham, avoiding unnecessary details about the Day of Resurrection and returning to the conclusion of the story.
