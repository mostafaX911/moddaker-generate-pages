(١١٢) قَالَ وَمَا عِلۡمِي بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

Noah (peace be upon him) said to them: What do I know of what these believers used to do? I am not an overseer over them to account for their deeds.

(١١٣) إِنۡ حِسَابُهُمۡ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ رَبِّيۖ لَوۡ تَشۡعُرُونَ

Their account is for Allah, who knows their secret and public affairs, not me. If you comprehended what you said, you would not have said it.

(١١٤) وَمَآ أَنَا۠ بِطَارِدِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

I am not going to throw out these believers from my gatherings to accommodate your requests, for you to believe.

(١١٥) إِنۡ أَنَا۠ إِلَّا نَذِيرٞ مُّبِينٞ

I am only a clear warner warning you of the punishment of Allah.

(١١٦) قَالُواْ لَئِن لَّمۡ تَنتَهِ يَٰنُوحُ لَتَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمَرۡجُومِينَ

His people said to him: If you do not stop calling to what you are calling us to, you will surely be vilified and stoned to death.

(١١٧) قَالَ رَبِّ إِنَّ قَوۡمِي كَذَّبُونِ

Supplicating to his Lord, Noah said: My Lord, indeed, my nation has rejected me. They have not believed in what I brought from you.

(١١٨) فَٱفۡتَحۡ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَهُمۡ فَتۡحٗا وَنَجِّنِي وَمَن مَّعِيَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

So judge between us such that they are destroyed due to their insistence on falsehood, and save me and the believers who are with me from the punishment you will destroy the disbelievers from my people with.

(١١٩) فَأَنجَيۡنَٰهُ وَمَن مَّعَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ ٱلۡمَشۡحُونِ

So We responded to his supplication and saved him and the believers with him in the Ark that was full of people and animals.

(١٢٠) ثُمَّ أَغۡرَقۡنَا بَعۡدُ ٱلۡبَاقِينَ

We then drowned the rest, who were the people of Noah.

(١٢١) إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Indeed, in the aforementioned story of Noah and his people, Noah's salvation and those with him, along with the destruction of the disbelievers, is a lesson for those who take heed. However, most of them are not believers.

(١٢٢) وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

And Indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, is the Mighty who takes retribution from His enemies, the Merciful to those of them who repent.

(١٢٣) كَذَّبَتۡ عَادٌ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

The people of 'Ād rejected all the messengers when they rejected their messenger, Hūd (peace be upon him).

(١٢٤) إِذۡ قَالَ لَهُمۡ أَخُوهُمۡ هُودٌ أَلَا تَتَّقُونَ

Mention when their brother, the prophet Hūd, said to them: Will you not be mindful of Allah and leave the worship of other than Him out of fear for Him?

(١٢٥) إِنِّي لَكُمۡ رَسُولٌ أَمِينٞ

Indeed, I am a messenger sent by Allah to you. I am trustworthy and will not add or remove anything from what Allah ordered me to relay.

(١٢٦) فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ

So fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. And obey me in what I command you to do and what I forbid you from.

(١٢٧) وَمَآ أَسۡ‍َٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرٍۖ إِنۡ أَجۡرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

I do not seek any reward from you for what I convey to you from my Lord. My reward is only from Allah, the Lord of all creation, not from anyone else.

(١٢٨) أَتَبۡنُونَ بِكُلِّ رِيعٍ ءَايَةٗ تَعۡبَثُونَ

Do you unnecessarily build a landmark in every elevated site without any benefit for you in this world or the Afterlife?

(١٢٩) وَتَتَّخِذُونَ مَصَانِعَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَخۡلُدُونَ

And you build forts and palaces as if you were to live eternally in this world, never departing from it?

(١٣٠) وَإِذَا بَطَشۡتُم بَطَشۡتُمۡ جَبَّارِينَ

And when you attack or battle, you attack like tyrants, showing no kindness or mercy.

(١٣١) فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ

So fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. And obey me in what I command you to do and what I forbid you from.

(١٣٢) وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَمَدَّكُم بِمَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

And fear the wrath of Allah who has granted you favors you know well.

(١٣٣) أَمَدَّكُم بِأَنۡعَٰمٖ وَبَنِينَ

He has given you domestic animals and given you offspring.

(١٣٤) وَجَنَّٰتٖ وَعُيُونٍ

He has given you gardens and flowing streams.

(١٣٥) إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ عَذَابَ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ

O my people, I fear for you, the punishment of a tremendous day, the Day of Resurrection.

(١٣٦) قَالُواْ سَوَآءٌ عَلَيۡنَآ أَوَعَظۡتَ أَمۡ لَمۡ تَكُن مِّنَ ٱلۡوَٰعِظِينَ

His people said to him; It is the same to us whether you warn us or not, for we shall never believe in you and will never turn away from what we are upon.

Points of Benefit:

The precedence of the people of Eman (faith), even if they are poor or weak.
The destruction of the wrongdoers and the deliverance of the believers is an established divine practice.
The danger of inclining towards the worldly life.
The stubbornness of the people of falsehood and their insistence on it.
