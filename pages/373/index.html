(١٣٧) إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا خُلُقُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

This is only the religion, customs, and character of the people of the past.

(١٣٨) وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمُعَذَّبِينَ

And we are not going to be punished.

(١٣٩) فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَأَهۡلَكۡنَٰهُمۡۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

They continued to reject their prophet Hūd (peace be upon him), so We destroyed them due to their rejection by a barren wind. Indeed, there is a lesson in their destruction for those who take heed. And most of them were not believers.

(١٤٠) وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

And indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, is the Mighty who takes retribution from His enemies, the Merciful to those of His slaves who repent.

(١٤١) كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

Thamūd rejected all the messengers when they rejected Saleh (peace be upon him).

(١٤٢) إِذۡ قَالَ لَهُمۡ أَخُوهُمۡ صَٰلِحٌ أَلَا تَتَّقُونَ

When their brother Saleh asked them; Will you not fear Allah by forsaking worshiping others?

(١٤٣) إِنِّي لَكُمۡ رَسُولٌ أَمِينٞ

Indeed, I am a messenger sent by Allah to you. I am trustworthy and will not add or remove anything from what has been revealed to me.

(١٤٤) فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ

So fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. And obey me in what I command you to do and what I forbid you from.

(١٤٥) وَمَآ أَسۡ‍َٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرٍۖ إِنۡ أَجۡرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

I do not seek any reward from you for what I convey to you from my Lord. My reward is only from Allah, the Lord of all creation, not from anyone else.

(١٤٦) أَتُتۡرَكُونَ فِي مَا هَٰهُنَآ ءَامِنِينَ

Do you expect to be left alone in safety in the goodness and favors you enjoy without fear?

(١٤٧) فِي جَنَّٰتٖ وَعُيُونٖ

In gardens and flowing springs.

(١٤٨) وَزُرُوعٖ وَنَخۡلٖ طَلۡعُهَا هَضِيمٞ

Among crops and palms with tender and ripe fruit.

(١٤٩) وَتَنۡحِتُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡجِبَالِ بُيُوتٗا فَٰرِهِينَ

And you skillfully carve out houses from the mountains to live in.

(١٥٠) فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ

So fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. Obey me in what I command and prohibit.

(١٥١) وَلَا تُطِيعُوٓاْ أَمۡرَ ٱلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ

And do not follow the commands of those who are excessive on themselves by committing sins.

(١٥٢) ٱلَّذِينَ يُفۡسِدُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا يُصۡلِحُونَ

Those who cause corruption on Earth by spreading sins and do not rectify themselves by steadfastly obeying Allah.

(١٥٣) قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُسَحَّرِينَ

His people said to him; You are surely one who has been bewitched repeatedly until the sorcery has overcome your intellect and driven you mad.

(١٥٤) مَآ أَنتَ إِلَّا بَشَرٞ مِّثۡلُنَا فَأۡتِ بِ‍َٔايَةٍ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

You are nothing but a human like us; there is nothing special about you compared to us to make you a Messenger. So bring proof that you are a Messenger if you are indeed truthful in your claim.

(١٥٥) قَالَ هَٰذِهِۦ نَاقَةٞ لَّهَا شِرۡبٞ وَلَكُمۡ شِرۡبُ يَوۡمٖ مَّعۡلُومٖ

After Allah had granted Saleh proof in the form of the she-camel, which Allah extracted from the rock, he said to them: This is a she-camel that is visible and tangible. It has a share of the water, as you also have a designated share. It will not drink on the day designated to you, nor shall you let your livestock drink on its appointed day.

(١٥٦) وَلَا تَمَسُّوهَا بِسُوٓءٖ فَيَأۡخُذَكُمۡ عَذَابُ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ

Do not harm it by hitting or slaughtering it, lest the punishment of Allah comes upon you and destroys you on a dreadful day due to the afflictions coming down upon you.

(١٥٧) فَعَقَرُوهَا فَأَصۡبَحُواْ نَٰدِمِينَ

So they agreed upon slaughtering it, and the most wretched one amongst them did it. They regretted what they did when they realized that the punishment was going to befall them inevitably. However, regret after seeing the punishment is of no benefit.

(١٥٨) فَأَخَذَهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

So the punishment that they were promised seized them: it was an earthquake accompanied by a scream. Indeed, in the aforementioned, i.e., the story of Saleh and his people, is a lesson for those who take heed. Although most of them do not believe.

(١٥٩) وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

And Indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, is the Mighty who takes retribution from His enemies, the Merciful to those of His slaves who repent.

Points of Benefit:

The successive blessings alongside disbelief are a lure towards destruction.
Reminding of blessings is hoped to inspire belief and lead the servant back to Allah.
Disobedience is a cause of corruption on the Earth.
