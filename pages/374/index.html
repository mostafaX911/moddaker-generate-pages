(١٦٠) كَذَّبَتۡ قَوۡمُ لُوطٍ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

Lot's people rejected all the messengers by rejecting their prophet Lot (peace be upon him).

(١٦١) إِذۡ قَالَ لَهُمۡ أَخُوهُمۡ لُوطٌ أَلَا تَتَّقُونَ

Remember when their brother in lineage Lot said to them: Will you not be mindful of Allah by leaving idolatry out of fear of Him?

(١٦٢) إِنِّي لَكُمۡ رَسُولٌ أَمِينٞ

Indeed, I am a messenger sent by Allah to you. I am trustworthy in what I convey to you from him and will not add or remove anything from what has been revealed to me.

(١٦٣) فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ

So fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. And obey me in what I command you to do and what I forbid you from.

(١٦٤) وَمَآ أَسۡ‍َٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرٍۖ إِنۡ أَجۡرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

I do not seek any reward from you for what I convey to you from my Lord. My reward is only from Allah, the Lord of all creation, not from anyone else.

(١٦٥) أَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلذُّكۡرَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Do you approach men [sexually] from their rears?!

(١٦٦) وَتَذَرُونَ مَا خَلَقَ لَكُمۡ رَبُّكُم مِّنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِكُمۚ بَلۡ أَنتُمۡ قَوۡمٌ عَادُونَ

And leave the private parts of your wives that Allah has created for you to fulfill your desires? You have indeed transgressed the limits of Allah by this detestable deviance.

(١٦٧) قَالُواْ لَئِن لَّمۡ تَنتَهِ يَٰلُوطُ لَتَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمُخۡرَجِينَ

His people said to him: O Lot, if you do not stop forbidding us from this act and rebuking us over it, you and those with you shall be exiled from our city.

(١٦٨) قَالَ إِنِّي لِعَمَلِكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡقَالِينَ

Lot said to them: Indeed, I abhor this act that you engage in.

(١٦٩) رَبِّ نَجِّنِي وَأَهۡلِي مِمَّا يَعۡمَلُونَ

Supplicating to his Lord, he said: My Lord, save me and my family from the punishment that will afflict these people because of the evil they commit.

(١٧٠) فَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَأَهۡلَهُۥٓ أَجۡمَعِينَ

So We accepted his supplication and saved him and his whole family.

(١٧١) إِلَّا عَجُوزٗا فِي ٱلۡغَٰبِرِينَ

Except for his wife, for she was a disbeliever. So she was amongst the destroyed who perished.

(١٧٢) ثُمَّ دَمَّرۡنَا ٱلۡأٓخَرِينَ

After Lot and his family left the city of Sodom, We severely destroyed who remained from his people.

(١٧٣) وَأَمۡطَرۡنَا عَلَيۡهِم مَّطَرٗاۖ فَسَآءَ مَطَرُ ٱلۡمُنذَرِينَ

We rained stones from the sky down on them, just like rain. Disgraceful was the rain that befell those whom Lot used to warn of Allah's punishment if they continued committing their evil acts.

(١٧٤) إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Indeed, in the aforementioned punishment that descended on the people of Lot due to their committing lewd acts is a lesson for those who take heed. And most of them were not believers.

(١٧٥) وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

And indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, is the Mighty who takes retribution from His enemies, the Merciful to those of His slaves who repent.

(١٧٦) كَذَّبَ أَصۡحَٰبُ لۡ‍َٔيۡكَةِ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

The People of the Thicket close to Midian rejected the Messengers when they rejected their prophet Shuayb (peace be upon him).

(١٧٧) إِذۡ قَالَ لَهُمۡ شُعَيۡبٌ أَلَا تَتَّقُونَ

Their brother Shuayb said to them: Will you not fear Allah by forsaking worshiping others?

(١٧٨) إِنِّي لَكُمۡ رَسُولٌ أَمِينٞ

Indeed, I am a messenger sent by Allah to you. I am trustworthy in what I convey from him and will not add or remove anything from what has been revealed to me.

(١٧٩) فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ

So fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. And obey me in what I command you to do and what I forbid you from.

(١٨٠) وَمَآ أَسۡ‍َٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرٍۖ إِنۡ أَجۡرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

I do not seek any reward from you for what I convey to you from my Lord. My reward is only from Allah, the Lord of all creation, not from anyone else.

(١٨١) ۞أَوۡفُواْ ٱلۡكَيۡلَ وَلَا تَكُونُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡمُخۡسِرِينَ

When you sell to the people, measure for them properly. Do not be people who reduce the measurement when selling to people.

(١٨٢) وَزِنُواْ بِٱلۡقِسۡطَاسِ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمِ

Measure justly when you sell to others.

(١٨٣) وَلَا تَبۡخَسُواْ ٱلنَّاسَ أَشۡيَآءَهُمۡ وَلَا تَعۡثَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مُفۡسِدِينَ

And do not violate people's rights and excessively cause corruption on Earth by committing sins.

Points of Benefit:

Homosexuality is a deviation from natural disposition and a grave wrongdoing.
It is a trial for the caller if his household comprises disbelievers or transgressors.
Worldly relationships, without accompanied belief, do not benefit the possessor when punishment descends.
The obligation of fulfilling measures and the prohibition of deception.
