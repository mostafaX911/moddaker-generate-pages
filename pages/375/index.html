(١٨٤) وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُمۡ وَٱلۡجِبِلَّةَ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

And be fearful of the One who created you and past nations, fearing He sends down His punishment upon you.

(١٨٥) قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُسَحَّرِينَ

Shuayb's people said to him: You are the only one who has been repeatedly afflicted with sorcery until it has overcome your intelligence and driven you mad.

(١٨٦) وَمَآ أَنتَ إِلَّا بَشَرٞ مِّثۡلُنَا وَإِن نَّظُنُّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ

You are nothing but a human like us; there is nothing special about you. So, how can you be a messenger? We believe you are lying about your claims of being a Messenger.

(١٨٧) فَأَسۡقِطۡ عَلَيۡنَا كِسَفٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

So, cause a piece of sky to fall upon us if you are truthful in your claim.

(١٨٨) قَالَ رَبِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Shuayb said to them: My Lord knows the polytheism and sins you commit; none of your actions are hidden from Him.

(١٨٩) فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَأَخَذَهُمۡ عَذَابُ يَوۡمِ ٱلظُّلَّةِۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ عَذَابَ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٍ

They continued to reject him, and so a terrible punishment befell them. A cloud shaded them after a severely hot day; it rained fire upon them, burning them. The day they were destroyed was extremely horrific.

(١٩٠) إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Indeed, in the aforementioned destruction of Shuayb's people is a lesson for those who take heed. And most of them were not believers.

(١٩١) وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

And indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, is the Mighty who takes retribution from His enemies, the Merciful to those of His slaves who repent.

(١٩٢) وَإِنَّهُۥ لَتَنزِيلُ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Indeed, this Quran that was revealed to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم was revealed by the Lord of creation.

(١٩٣) نَزَلَ بِهِ ٱلرُّوحُ ٱلۡأَمِينُ

It was brought down by the trustworthy Gabriel (peace be upon him).

(١٩٤) عَلَىٰ قَلۡبِكَ لِتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُنذِرِينَ

He brought it down to your heart, O Messenger, so that you become of the Messengers who warn people and make them fearful of Allah's punishment.

(١٩٥) بِلِسَانٍ عَرَبِيّٖ مُّبِينٖ

He brought it down in a clear Arabic language.

(١٩٦) وَإِنَّهُۥ لَفِي زُبُرِ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

And indeed, this Quran has been mentioned in the previous Books; previous divine books have given glad tidings of it.

(١٩٧) أَوَ لَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُمۡ ءَايَةً أَن يَعۡلَمَهُۥ عُلَمَٰٓؤُاْ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ

Was it not a sign of your truthfulness for these people who reject you that the scholars of the Israelites, like Abdullah bin Salām, knew the reality of what has been revealed to you?

(١٩٨) وَلَوۡ نَزَّلۡنَٰهُ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضِ ٱلۡأَعۡجَمِينَ

Had we revealed this Quran to non-Arabs who do not speak Arabic,

(١٩٩) فَقَرَأَهُۥ عَلَيۡهِم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ مُؤۡمِنِينَ

And it was read to them; they would not become believers because they would say: We do not understand it! So they should praise Allah that it was revealed in their language.

(٢٠٠) كَذَٰلِكَ سَلَكۡنَٰهُ فِي قُلُوبِ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

Likewise, we placed rejection and disbelief in the hearts of criminals.

(٢٠١) لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهِۦ حَتَّىٰ يَرَوُاْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ ٱلۡأَلِيمَ

They will not change their state of disbelief and will not believe until they see the painful punishment.

(٢٠٢) فَيَأۡتِيَهُم بَغۡتَةٗ وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

This punishment will come to them suddenly; they will not know of its approach until it befalls them.

(٢٠٣) فَيَقُولُواْ هَلۡ نَحۡنُ مُنظَرُونَ

So when the punishment will come upon them suddenly, they will say out of immense grief: Will we be given respite to repent to Allah?

(٢٠٤) أَفَبِعَذَابِنَا يَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَ

Do these disbelievers hastily anticipate our punishment, saying: We will never believe in you until you cause the sky to fall upon us in pieces, as you claim.

(٢٠٥) أَفَرَءَيۡتَ إِن مَّتَّعۡنَٰهُمۡ سِنِينَ

So tell Us, O Messenger, if We let these dismissive disbelievers enjoy the bounties for a long time,

(٢٠٦) ثُمَّ جَآءَهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يُوعَدُونَ

Then the punishment they were promised came to them after that period of enjoyment,

Points of Benefit:

The more a Muslim delves into the Arabic language, the more capable they become of understanding the Quran.
Proclaiming to the polytheists what is acknowledged by the fair-minded among the People of the Book, affirming that the Quran is from Allah.
Whatever the disbelievers attain from the worldly blessings is a lure, not an honor.
