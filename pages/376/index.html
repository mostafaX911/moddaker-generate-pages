(٢٠٧) مَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يُمَتَّعُونَ

What benefit would that worldly enjoyment be? Those bounties would surely have ended and would not be of any use.

(٢٠٨) وَمَآ أَهۡلَكۡنَا مِن قَرۡيَةٍ إِلَّا لَهَا مُنذِرُونَ

We have not destroyed any nation except after they have been warned by sending messengers and revealing books to them.

(٢٠٩) ذِكۡرَىٰ وَمَا كُنَّا ظَٰلِمِينَ

Which served as admonition and warnings for them. We were not oppressive by punishing them after sending them Messengers and Books as excuses for them.

(٢١٠) وَمَا تَنَزَّلَتۡ بِهِ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينُ

The devils did not come down with this Quran upon the heart of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم;

(٢١١) وَمَا يَنۢبَغِي لَهُمۡ وَمَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ

It is not permissible that they come down with it onto his heart. They are not even capable of doing that.

(٢١٢) إِنَّهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلسَّمۡعِ لَمَعۡزُولُونَ

They cannot do so because they have been barred from its area in the heavens, so how would they reach it to bring it down?

(٢١٣) فَلَا تَدۡعُ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ فَتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُعَذَّبِينَ

So do not worship any deity with Allah, making it a partner to Him, lest you become amongst those who shall be punished.

(٢١٤) وَأَنذِرۡ عَشِيرَتَكَ ٱلۡأَقۡرَبِينَ

And warn, O Messenger, your closest relatives, then those after them, so Allah does not punish them due to them remaining upon polytheism.

(٢١٥) وَٱخۡفِضۡ جَنَاحَكَ لِمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

And be lenient, in action and speech, to those believers who follow you, out of mercy and gentleness.

(٢١٦) فَإِنۡ عَصَوۡكَ فَقُلۡ إِنِّي بَرِيٓءٞ مِّمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ

If they disobey you and reject your commandments to single out Allah in worship and obey Him, then say to them: I am innocent of the polytheism and sins you commit.

(٢١٧) وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

And rely on the Almighty for all your affairs; the One who takes retribution from His enemies and is merciful to those who repent from amongst them.

(٢١٨) ٱلَّذِي يَرَىٰكَ حِينَ تَقُومُ

The Exalted and Transcendent one, who sees you when you stand for prayer.

(٢١٩) وَتَقَلُّبَكَ فِي ٱلسَّٰجِدِينَ

And sees you changing postures with those who pray. Nothing of what you, or other than you do, is hidden from Him.

(٢٢٠) إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

Indeed, He is the All-Hearing who hears your recitation of the Quran and your supplications in your prayer, Omniscient of your intentions.

When they alleged that the devils came down with the Quran and that Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم was a poet, Allah responded to their false claim, stating:

(٢٢١) هَلۡ أُنَبِّئُكُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَن تَنَزَّلُ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينُ

Shall I inform you of whom the devils reveal to? Those whom you claimed brought down the Quran.

(٢٢٢) تَنَزَّلُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ أَفَّاكٍ أَثِيمٖ

They reveal to every perpetual liar and sinner amongst the fortune-tellers.

(٢٢٣) يُلۡقُونَ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَأَكۡثَرُهُمۡ كَٰذِبُونَ

The devils eavesdrop on the exalted assembly of angels and then convey that information to their associates amongst fortune-tellers. The majority of fortune-tellers are liars; if they are truthful in one statement, they mix it with a hundred lies.

(٢٢٤) وَٱلشُّعَرَآءُ يَتَّبِعُهُمُ ٱلۡغَاوُۥنَ

And as for the poets that you claim Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم is from, those who are astray from the path of guidance and steadfastness follow them and relate their poetry.

(٢٢٥) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّهُمۡ فِي كُلِّ وَادٖ يَهِيمُونَ

Do you not see, O Messenger, that one of the manifestations of their errors is that they wander around, lost in every valley? At times engaged in praise, at others in criticism, and other than that!

(٢٢٦) وَأَنَّهُمۡ يَقُولُونَ مَا لَا يَفۡعَلُونَ

And they indeed lie, saying they did something while they did not.

(٢٢٧) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَذَكَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا وَٱنتَصَرُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا ظُلِمُواْۗ وَسَيَعۡلَمُ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوٓاْ أَيَّ مُنقَلَبٖ يَنقَلِبُونَ

Except for poets who have faith, perform righteous deeds, abundantly remember Allah, and take retribution from Allah's enemies after they have been oppressed by them, like Hassan bin Thabit (may Allah be pleased with him). Those who committed oppression by ascribing partners to Allah and showing hostility to His slaves will soon know which place they will return to; they will return to a tremendous standing and meticulous accounting.

Points of Benefit:

Affirming Allah's Justice and absolving Him of any injustice.
Exempting the Quran from the proximity of devils.
The importance of gentleness and kindness in inviting to Allah.
Poetry is either beautiful, and its beauty is beautiful, or it is ugly, and its ugliness is ugly.
