The Ant (Surah An-Naml)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Bestowing the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم with the blessing of the Quran, expressing gratitude for it, and perseverance in conveying its message.

Explanation:

(١) طسٓۚ تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ وَكِتَابٖ مُّبِينٍ

Tā Sīn – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) هُدٗى وَبُشۡرَىٰ لِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

These verses guide to the truth and give glad tidings to those who have faith in Allah and His Messengers.

(٣) ٱلَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَهُم بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ يُوقِنُونَ

Those who perform the prayer in its most complete manner, give the Zakat of their wealth to be spent on its rightful recipients and have a firm conviction of the reward and punishment in the Afterlife.

(٤) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ زَيَّنَّا لَهُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ فَهُمۡ يَعۡمَهُونَ

Indeed, the disbelievers who do not have faith in the Afterlife and the reward and punishment therein - We beautified their evil deeds for them, so they continued doing them. Thus, they are confused and unable to find the correct or sound path.

(٥) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَهُمۡ سُوٓءُ ٱلۡعَذَابِ وَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمُ ٱلۡأَخۡسَرُونَ

People with these descriptions will suffer in the worldly life through trials and humiliation. And in the Hereafter, they will be the greatest losers, losing themselves and their families on the Day of Resurrection by entering and remaining in the Fire forever.

(٦) وَإِنَّكَ لَتُلَقَّى ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ مِن لَّدُنۡ حَكِيمٍ عَلِيمٍ

And you, O Messenger, receive this Quran, sent down to you from One who is Wise in His creation, His decree, and His legislation. He is all-Knowing; nothing of the interests of His slaves is hidden from Him.

(٧) إِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِأَهۡلِهِۦٓ إِنِّيٓ ءَانَسۡتُ نَارٗا سَ‍َٔاتِيكُم مِّنۡهَا بِخَبَرٍ أَوۡ ءَاتِيكُم بِشِهَابٖ قَبَسٖ لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَصۡطَلُونَ

Mention, O Messenger, when Moses said to his family: I have seen a fire. I shall bring information from the person who lit it to show us the way. Or bring a flame from it so you may light a fire and keep warm.

(٨) فَلَمَّا جَآءَهَا نُودِيَ أَنۢ بُورِكَ مَن فِي ٱلنَّارِ وَمَنۡ حَوۡلَهَا وَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

When he reached the place where he saw the Fire, Allah called unto him that the one in the Fire and the angels surrounding it are blessed. Praised is the Lord of creation from the unbefitting attributes the misguided ascribe to him.

(٩) يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

Allah said to him: O Moses, it is I, Allah, the Mighty who cannot be overpowered, the Wise in My creation, decree, and legislation.

(١٠) وَأَلۡقِ عَصَاكَۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهَا تَهۡتَزُّ كَأَنَّهَا جَآنّٞ وَلَّىٰ مُدۡبِرٗا وَلَمۡ يُعَقِّبۡۚ يَٰمُوسَىٰ لَا تَخَفۡ إِنِّي لَا يَخَافُ لَدَيَّ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ

And throw down your staff. Moses obeyed. Then, when Moses saw it moving and shaking like a snake, he fled and did not return. So Allah said to him: Do not be afraid of it. For indeed, the Messengers do not fear snakes or anything else in my presence.

(١١) إِلَّا مَن ظَلَمَ ثُمَّ بَدَّلَ حُسۡنَۢا بَعۡدَ سُوٓءٖ فَإِنِّي غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

However, whoever wrongs himself by sinning then repents; I am Forgiving and Merciful to him.

(١٢) وَأَدۡخِلۡ يَدَكَ فِي جَيۡبِكَ تَخۡرُجۡ بَيۡضَآءَ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ سُوٓءٖۖ فِي تِسۡعِ ءَايَٰتٍ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَقَوۡمِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمٗا فَٰسِقِينَ

And put your hand in the opening under the neck of your garment; it will emerge white like snow without being due to vitiligo, as one of nine signs which testify to your truthfulness. Along with the hand, they are the staff, famine, deficiency of fruit, floods, locusts, lice, frogs, and blood. [Signs] for Pharaoh and his people, they were indeed a people outside the fold of obedience of Allah due to disbelieving in him.

(١٣) فَلَمَّا جَآءَتۡهُمۡ ءَايَٰتُنَا مُبۡصِرَةٗ قَالُواْ هَٰذَا سِحۡرٞ مُّبِينٞ

So when Our clear and apparent signs with which We aided Moses reached them, they said: What Moses has brought is clear sorcery.

Points of Benefit:

The Quran is guidance and glad tidings for the believers.
Disbelief in Allah is a cause for following falsehood in actions and words, confusion, and disturbance.
Allah's protection and preservation of His messengers, Exalted is He, from all harm.
