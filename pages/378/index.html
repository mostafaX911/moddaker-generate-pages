(١٤) وَجَحَدُواْ بِهَا وَٱسۡتَيۡقَنَتۡهَآ أَنفُسُهُمۡ ظُلۡمٗا وَعُلُوّٗاۚ فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ

They disbelieved in these clear miracles and did not accept them despite being convinced they were from Allah. This is due to their oppression and their arrogance from accepting the truth. So contemplate, O Messenger, the outcome of those who cause corruption on Earth with their disbelief and disobedience; We have indeed destroyed and eradicated all of them.

(١٥) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا دَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيۡمَٰنَ عِلۡمٗاۖ وَقَالَا ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي فَضَّلَنَا عَلَىٰ كَثِيرٖ مِّنۡ عِبَادِهِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

I granted David and his son Solomon knowledge, part of which was the language of birds. David and Solomon said, in gratitude to Allah: All praise is for Allah who has given us virtue over many of His believing slaves through prophethood and subjugation of the Jinn.

(١٦) وَوَرِثَ سُلَيۡمَٰنُ دَاوُۥدَۖ وَقَالَ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ عُلِّمۡنَا مَنطِقَ ٱلطَّيۡرِ وَأُوتِينَا مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٍۖ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلۡفَضۡلُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

And Solomon inherited prophethood, knowledge, and dominion from his father. Citing Allah's favor upon him and his father, he said: O people, Allah has taught us the language of birds and given us everything He gave prophets and kings. What He has given us is indeed a clear and apparent favor.

(١٧) وَحُشِرَ لِسُلَيۡمَٰنَ جُنُودُهُۥ مِنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِ وَٱلطَّيۡرِ فَهُمۡ يُوزَعُونَ

And Solomon's troops of men, Jinn, and birds were gathered for him and led orderly.

(١٨) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَتَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ وَادِ ٱلنَّمۡلِ قَالَتۡ نَمۡلَةٞ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّمۡلُ ٱدۡخُلُواْ مَسَٰكِنَكُمۡ لَا يَحۡطِمَنَّكُمۡ سُلَيۡمَٰنُ وَجُنُودُهُۥ وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

They continued to be led until they came to the valley of ants (a place in the Levant); an ant said: O ants, get into your homes so that Solomon and his armies do not destroy you, being unaware of your presence. If they were aware, they would not trample you.

(١٩) فَتَبَسَّمَ ضَاحِكٗا مِّن قَوۡلِهَا وَقَالَ رَبِّ أَوۡزِعۡنِيٓ أَنۡ أَشۡكُرَ نِعۡمَتَكَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَيَّ وَأَنۡ أَعۡمَلَ صَٰلِحٗا تَرۡضَىٰهُ وَأَدۡخِلۡنِي بِرَحۡمَتِكَ فِي عِبَادِكَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

When Solomon heard it, he smiled at its statement and called unto his Lord: O Lord, enable and inspire me to be grateful for Your favor which You have bestowed upon me and My parents, and allow me to practice righteous deeds which please you, and allow me to be amongst Your righteous slaves.

(٢٠) وَتَفَقَّدَ ٱلطَّيۡرَ فَقَالَ مَا لِيَ لَآ أَرَى ٱلۡهُدۡهُدَ أَمۡ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡغَآئِبِينَ

And Solomon inspected the birds but did not see the hoopoe, so he said: Why do I not see the hoopoe? Is there something preventing me from seeing it, or is it absent?

(٢١) لَأُعَذِّبَنَّهُۥ عَذَابٗا شَدِيدًا أَوۡ لَأَاْذۡبَحَنَّهُۥٓ أَوۡ لَيَأۡتِيَنِّي بِسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٖ

When its absence became clear to him, he said: I shall punish it severely or slaughter it as a punishment for its absence unless it brings clear proof for its excuse.

(٢٢) فَمَكَثَ غَيۡرَ بَعِيدٖ فَقَالَ أَحَطتُ بِمَا لَمۡ تُحِطۡ بِهِۦ وَجِئۡتُكَ مِن سَبَإِۢ بِنَبَإٖ يَقِينٍ

The hoopoe remained absent for a short period of time. When it arrived, it said to Solomon (peace be upon him): I discovered something that you are unaware of, and I have brought truthful news about the people of Sheba in which there is no doubt.

Points of Benefit:

Smiling is the laughter of people of dignity.
Expressing gratitude for blessings is the etiquette of the prophets and the righteous with their Lord.
Apologizing to the righteous in private.
The policy of governance involves administering punishment to those deserving it and accepting excuses from those with valid reasons.
There may be knowledge among the juniors that is not present among the seniors.
