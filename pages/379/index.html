(٢٣) إِنِّي وَجَدتُّ ٱمۡرَأَةٗ تَمۡلِكُهُمۡ وَأُوتِيَتۡ مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَلَهَا عَرۡشٌ عَظِيمٞ

Indeed, I found [there] a woman ruling them, and she has been given of all things and has a great throne.

(٢٤) وَجَدتُّهَا وَقَوۡمَهَا يَسۡجُدُونَ لِلشَّمۡسِ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَزَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ فَصَدَّهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ فَهُمۡ لَا يَهۡتَدُونَ

I found her and her people prostrating to the sun instead of Allah, the Exalted. The Devil made their polytheism and sins seem pleasing to them and averted them from the correct path; thus, they are not guided to it.

(٢٥) أَلَّاۤ يَسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي يُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡخَبۡءَ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا تُخۡفُونَ وَمَا تُعۡلِنُونَ

The Devil has beautified their polytheism and sins for them so that they do not prostrate to Allah alone, the One who brings the rain which He has hidden in the skies and the plants on the Earth. He knows the actions you conceal as well as those you reveal. None of it is hidden from Him.

(٢٦) ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ۩

Allah, no deity is worthy of worship other than Him, Lord of the magnificent Throne.

(٢٧) ۞قَالَ سَنَنظُرُ أَصَدَقۡتَ أَمۡ كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ

Solomon (peace be upon him) said to the hoopoe: I shall see whether you are truthful in what you claim or a liar.

(٢٨) ٱذۡهَب بِّكِتَٰبِي هَٰذَا فَأَلۡقِهۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡ ثُمَّ تَوَلَّ عَنۡهُمۡ فَٱنظُرۡ مَاذَا يَرۡجِعُونَ

So Solomon wrote a letter, gave it to the hoopoe, and said: Take this letter of mine and give it to the people of Sheba. Then, remain close by so you can hear what they say regarding it.

(٢٩) قَالَتۡ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمَلَؤُاْ إِنِّيٓ أُلۡقِيَ إِلَيَّ كِتَٰبٞ كَرِيمٌ

The queen received the letter and said: O noble ones, a noble and magnificent letter has been given to me.

(٣٠) إِنَّهُۥ مِن سُلَيۡمَٰنَ وَإِنَّهُۥ بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

The content of this letter sent by Solomon begins with: In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

(٣١) أَلَّا تَعۡلُواْ عَلَيَّ وَأۡتُونِي مُسۡلِمِينَ

Do not be arrogant; come to me in submission to the monotheism I call to, and forsake the polytheism you are upon of worshiping the sun alongside Him.

(٣٢) قَالَتۡ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمَلَؤُاْ أَفۡتُونِي فِيٓ أَمۡرِي مَا كُنتُ قَاطِعَةً أَمۡرًا حَتَّىٰ تَشۡهَدُونِ

The queen said to the nobles and leaders: Advise me regarding the right decision in this affair. I do not make any decisions in a matter until you come and tell me your opinions regarding it.

(٣٣) قَالُواْ نَحۡنُ أُوْلُواْ قُوَّةٖ وَأُوْلُواْ بَأۡسٖ شَدِيدٖ وَٱلۡأَمۡرُ إِلَيۡكِ فَٱنظُرِي مَاذَا تَأۡمُرِينَ

The nobles of her people said: We are mighty and forceful at war, but the decision is yours. So think about what you want to do, and we will be able to execute it.

(٣٤) قَالَتۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡمُلُوكَ إِذَا دَخَلُواْ قَرۡيَةً أَفۡسَدُوهَا وَجَعَلُوٓاْ أَعِزَّةَ أَهۡلِهَآ أَذِلَّةٗۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ يَفۡعَلُونَ

The queen said: When kings attack a city, they destroy it by killing, seizing, and looting. They turn its leaders and nobles into the lowly after having honor and security. This is what kings always do when they conquer a city to sow awe and fear in the hearts of the masses.

(٣٥) وَإِنِّي مُرۡسِلَةٌ إِلَيۡهِم بِهَدِيَّةٖ فَنَاظِرَةُۢ بِمَ يَرۡجِعُ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ

I am sending a gift to the sender of the letter and his people, and I shall see what the messengers bring after sending this gift.

Points of Benefit:

The hoopoe's denial to the people of Sheba regarding their state of polytheism and disbelief is evidence that faith is innate among creatures.
Investigating the accused and verifying their arguments.
The legitimacy of disclosing information about the enemies.
Among the etiquettes of messages is to begin them with the phrase "In the name of Allah."
Manifesting the dignity of the believer in the face of falsehood is a commendable matter.
