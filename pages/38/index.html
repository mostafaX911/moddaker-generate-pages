(٢٣٤) وَٱلَّذِينَ يُتَوَفَّوۡنَ مِنكُمۡ وَيَذَرُونَ أَزۡوَٰجٗا يَتَرَبَّصۡنَ بِأَنفُسِهِنَّ أَرۡبَعَةَ أَشۡهُرٖ وَعَشۡرٗاۖ فَإِذَا بَلَغۡنَ أَجَلَهُنَّ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فِيمَا فَعَلۡنَ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِنَّ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٞ

For those who pass away and leave behind women who are not pregnant, the widow must wait for a period of four months and ten days, during which she must not leave the husband's home, beautify herself, or get married. Once this period ends, there is no blame on their guardians if these women do these actions, which were previously prohibited during the waiting period, as long as it is done in accordance with societal norms and sacred law. Allah is well aware of what you do. None of your private or public actions are concealed from Him, and He will compensate you accordingly.

(٢٣٥) وَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فِيمَا عَرَّضۡتُم بِهِۦ مِنۡ خِطۡبَةِ ٱلنِّسَآءِ أَوۡ أَكۡنَنتُمۡ فِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡۚ عَلِمَ ٱللَّهُ أَنَّكُمۡ سَتَذۡكُرُونَهُنَّ وَلَٰكِن لَّا تُوَاعِدُوهُنَّ سِرًّا إِلَّآ أَن تَقُولُواْ قَوۡلٗا مَّعۡرُوفٗاۚ وَلَا تَعۡزِمُواْ عُقۡدَةَ ٱلنِّكَاحِ حَتَّىٰ يَبۡلُغَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ أَجَلَهُۥۚ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ مَا فِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ فَٱحۡذَرُوهُۚ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٌ حَلِيمٞ

There is no sin on you if you hint at your desire to propose to a woman who is still in her waiting period after a divorce or after the death of her husband without explicitly declaring it. For example, a person may say, 'When your waiting period is over, let me know'. There is also no sin on you if you keep the desire to marry a woman who is in her waiting period to yourselves. Allah knows that you will mention them because of your strong desire for them, so He has allowed you to give a hint without being explicit. Beware of secretly making any promise to marry them while they are still in their waiting period, except in an acceptable manner (i.e., giving a hint to them). Do not ratify the marriage contract during the waiting period. Know that Allah knows what you are concealing, so be mindful of Him and do not go against His command. Know that Allah Forgives the person who repents to Him and that He is Tolerant and does not hasten to punish.

(٢٣٦) لَّا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِن طَلَّقۡتُمُ ٱلنِّسَآءَ مَا لَمۡ تَمَسُّوهُنَّ أَوۡ تَفۡرِضُواْ لَهُنَّ فَرِيضَةٗۚ وَمَتِّعُوهُنَّ عَلَى ٱلۡمُوسِعِ قَدَرُهُۥ وَعَلَى ٱلۡمُقۡتِرِ قَدَرُهُۥ مَتَٰعَۢا بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِۖ حَقًّا عَلَى ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

There is no sin on you if you divorce your wives with whom you have entered into marriage before you have sexual intercourse with them and before you fix a dowry for them. If you must divorce them in such a situation, they will not be entitled to any dowry from you, but they must be given something that they will enjoy to make it easy on their feelings. This must be done by both a rich and a poor man in accordance with their financial abilities. This gift is binding on those who wish to excel in their actions and dealings.

(٢٣٧) وَإِن طَلَّقۡتُمُوهُنَّ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن تَمَسُّوهُنَّ وَقَدۡ فَرَضۡتُمۡ لَهُنَّ فَرِيضَةٗ فَنِصۡفُ مَا فَرَضۡتُمۡ إِلَّآ أَن يَعۡفُونَ أَوۡ يَعۡفُوَاْ ٱلَّذِي بِيَدِهِۦ عُقۡدَةُ ٱلنِّكَاحِۚ وَأَن تَعۡفُوٓاْ أَقۡرَبُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰۚ وَلَا تَنسَوُاْ ٱلۡفَضۡلَ بَيۡنَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ

If you divorce your wives with whom you have entered into marriage before having sexual intercourse with them and you had fixed the amount of the dowry, you will have to pay half of this amount to them unless they give up this right of theirs or the husband wishes to pay the full dowry. To give up your right is closer to fearing Allah and obeying Him. Do not forget to show kindness and compassion to one another regarding rights. Allah sees what you do, so try to do what is best to earn His reward.

Points of Benefit:

The legitimacy of the waiting period of the widow, such that she can not beautify herself or marry for four months and ten days.
The believer being aware of Allah watching him leads him to be wary of Him, the Exalted, and not go beyond the boundaries He has set.
Encouragement to treat wives and relatives kindly and to make pardoning and tolerance the foundation of their treatment of each other.
