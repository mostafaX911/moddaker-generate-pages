(٣٦) فَلَمَّا جَآءَ سُلَيۡمَٰنَ قَالَ أَتُمِدُّونَنِ بِمَالٖ فَمَآ ءَاتَىٰنِۦَ ٱللَّهُ خَيۡرٞ مِّمَّآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۚ بَلۡ أَنتُم بِهَدِيَّتِكُمۡ تَفۡرَحُونَ

So when her messenger and his aides came carrying the gift to Solomon, he criticized them for sending the present, saying: Are you giving me wealth to turn me away from you? The prophethood, kingdom, and wealth Allah has given me is better than what He has given you. Rather, it is you who rejoices over the worldly riches gifted to you.

(٣٧) ٱرۡجِعۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡ فَلَنَأۡتِيَنَّهُم بِجُنُودٖ لَّا قِبَلَ لَهُم بِهَا وَلَنُخۡرِجَنَّهُم مِّنۡهَآ أَذِلَّةٗ وَهُمۡ صَٰغِرُونَ

Solomon (peace be upon him) said to her messenger: Return to them with the present you brought, for we will go to her and her people with armies they will not have the power to face. We will remove them from Sheba disgracefully after being honorable if they do not come to me obediently.

(٣٨) قَالَ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمَلَؤُاْ أَيُّكُمۡ يَأۡتِينِي بِعَرۡشِهَا قَبۡلَ أَن يَأۡتُونِي مُسۡلِمِينَ

Solomon (peace be upon him) addressed the people of his kingdom; Which of you will bring me her royal throne before they come to me obediently?

(٣٩) قَالَ عِفۡرِيتٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ أَنَا۠ ءَاتِيكَ بِهِۦ قَبۡلَ أَن تَقُومَ مِن مَّقَامِكَۖ وَإِنِّي عَلَيۡهِ لَقَوِيٌّ أَمِينٞ

A rebellious Jinn replied: I will bring you her throne before you leave this gathering of yours. I have the strength to carry it and can be trusted with its contents; I will not take anything from it.

(٤٠) قَالَ ٱلَّذِي عِندَهُۥ عِلۡمٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أَنَا۠ ءَاتِيكَ بِهِۦ قَبۡلَ أَن يَرۡتَدَّ إِلَيۡكَ طَرۡفُكَۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهُ مُسۡتَقِرًّا عِندَهُۥ قَالَ هَٰذَا مِن فَضۡلِ رَبِّي لِيَبۡلُوَنِيٓ ءَأَشۡكُرُ أَمۡ أَكۡفُرُۖ وَمَن شَكَرَ فَإِنَّمَا يَشۡكُرُ لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ رَبِّي غَنِيّٞ كَرِيمٞ

A righteous and knowledgeable man who had knowledge of the scripture, of which was Allah's greatest name, which He responds to when called with, said in the presence of Solomon: I will bring her throne to you in the blink of an eye, by praying to Allah to bring it. So he prayed, and Allah responded to his prayer. When Solomon saw her throne before him, he said: This is from my Lord, the Exalted's, favor upon me, to test whether I am grateful for his favors or not? Whoever is grateful to Allah, the benefit of his gratefulness will only return to him, for Allah is self-sufficient, in need of nothing. The gratefulness of His slaves does not increase Him in anything. And whoever doesn't show gratitude and denies Allah's favors, my Lord is self-sufficient and not in need of gratitude. [He is] Generous, and His generosity grants blessings to those who deny them.

(٤١) قَالَ نَكِّرُواْ لَهَا عَرۡشَهَا نَنظُرۡ أَتَهۡتَدِيٓ أَمۡ تَكُونُ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَهۡتَدُونَ

Solomon (peace be upon him) said: Change her royal throne from its original form. We shall see if she is guided to recognize it as her throne or if she will be from those who are not guided to recognize their own things.

(٤٢) فَلَمَّا جَآءَتۡ قِيلَ أَهَٰكَذَا عَرۡشُكِۖ قَالَتۡ كَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَۚ وَأُوتِينَا ٱلۡعِلۡمَ مِن قَبۡلِهَا وَكُنَّا مُسۡلِمِينَ

So when the queen of Sheba came to Solomon, as a test, it was said to her; Does this resemble your throne? She answered accordingly; It is as if it is [my throne]. Solomon said; Allah granted us knowledge before her because of His ability to do such things, and we submitted to Allah's order and obeyed Him.

(٤٣) وَصَدَّهَا مَا كَانَت تَّعۡبُدُ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهَا كَانَتۡ مِن قَوۡمٖ كَٰفِرِينَ

And worshiping others besides Allah turned her away from the Oneness of Allah, following and blindly imitating her people. Indeed, she was from a people who disbelieved in Allah, so she was a disbeliever like them.

(٤٤) قِيلَ لَهَا ٱدۡخُلِي ٱلصَّرۡحَۖ فَلَمَّا رَأَتۡهُ حَسِبَتۡهُ لُجَّةٗ وَكَشَفَتۡ عَن سَاقَيۡهَاۚ قَالَ إِنَّهُۥ صَرۡحٞ مُّمَرَّدٞ مِّن قَوَارِيرَۗ قَالَتۡ رَبِّ إِنِّي ظَلَمۡتُ نَفۡسِي وَأَسۡلَمۡتُ مَعَ سُلَيۡمَٰنَ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

It was said to her: Enter the palace. It was like a plane surface. When she saw it, she thought it was water, so she uncovered her shins to walk through it. Solomon (peace be upon him) said: It is a palace made of smooth glass. He called her to Islam, and she accepted, saying: O Lord, I wronged myself by worshipping others besides You along with You, and I have submitted with Solomon to Allah, Lord of all created things.

Points of Benefit:

The dignity of belief shields the believer from being affected by the debris of this world.
Rejoicing excessively in material possessions and relying on them is a characteristic of disbelievers.
The vigilant awareness of the believer towards the blessings of Allah.
Testing the intelligence of the opponent to deal with them appropriately.
Highlighting superiority over the opponent to influence them.
