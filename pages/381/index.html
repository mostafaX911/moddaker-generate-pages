(٤٥) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَىٰ ثَمُودَ أَخَاهُمۡ صَٰلِحًا أَنِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ فَإِذَا هُمۡ فَرِيقَانِ يَخۡتَصِمُونَ

And we sent to Thamūd, their brother in lineage, Saleh (peace be upon him), telling them to worship Allah alone. But they split into two groups after he called them; one group was believers, and the other group was disbelievers arguing over who was on the truth.

(٤٦) قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ لِمَ تَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَ بِٱلسَّيِّئَةِ قَبۡلَ ٱلۡحَسَنَةِۖ لَوۡلَا تَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ

Saleh (peace be upon him) said: Why do you seek to hasten the punishment before seeking Allah's mercy? Why do you not seek Allah's forgiveness for your sins in the hope that He will forgive you?

(٤٧) قَالُواْ ٱطَّيَّرۡنَا بِكَ وَبِمَن مَّعَكَۚ قَالَ طَٰٓئِرُكُمۡ عِندَ ٱللَّهِۖ بَلۡ أَنتُمۡ قَوۡمٞ تُفۡتَنُونَ

His people said to him, stubbornly rejecting the truth: We consider you and the believers with you a bad omen. Saleh (peace be upon him) said: The difficulties which have afflicted you are known to Allah; nothing of it is hidden from Him. Rather, you are a people being tested with the good you are granted and the bad that afflicts you.

(٤٨) وَكَانَ فِي ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ تِسۡعَةُ رَهۡطٖ يُفۡسِدُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا يُصۡلِحُونَ

There were nine men in the city of Hijr who caused corruption on Earth through disbelief and sin, and they would not cause rectification in it through faith and righteous actions.

(٤٩) قَالُواْ تَقَاسَمُواْ بِٱللَّهِ لَنُبَيِّتَنَّهُۥ وَأَهۡلَهُۥ ثُمَّ لَنَقُولَنَّ لِوَلِيِّهِۦ مَا شَهِدۡنَا مَهۡلِكَ أَهۡلِهِۦ وَإِنَّا لَصَٰدِقُونَ

They said to one another: Each one of you should swear by Allah that we will go to him and the believers with him at night, then we will kill them and then say to the one responsible for avenging his blood: 'We were not present at the murder of Saleh or the murder of his followers, and we are truthful in what we are saying.'

(٥٠) وَمَكَرُواْ مَكۡرٗا وَمَكَرۡنَا مَكۡرٗا وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

And they secretly plotted to destroy Saleh and his followers from the believers. We also plotted to support and save him from their scheme and destroy the disbelievers from his people while they were unaware.

(٥١) فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ مَكۡرِهِمۡ أَنَّا دَمَّرۡنَٰهُمۡ وَقَوۡمَهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

So contemplate O Messenger, the result of their schemes. The result was that We uprooted them with Our punishment, so they were all destroyed.

(٥٢) فَتِلۡكَ بُيُوتُهُمۡ خَاوِيَةَۢ بِمَا ظَلَمُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ

So these are their homes whose walls have fallen on their roofs and remained empty of inhabitants due to their wrongdoing. Indeed, in the punishment which has afflicted them due to their wrongdoing is a lesson for people who have knowledge; it is the believers who learn from the signs.

(٥٣) وَأَنجَيۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَكَانُواْ يَتَّقُونَ

And We saved the believers from the people of Saleh (peace be upon him). They used to fear Allah by complying with His orders and avoiding His prohibitions.

(٥٤) وَلُوطًا إِذۡ قَالَ لِقَوۡمِهِۦٓ أَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلۡفَٰحِشَةَ وَأَنتُمۡ تُبۡصِرُونَ

And mention Lot, O Messenger, when he reprimanded and criticized his people, saying: Do you openly engage in the indecent act of sodomy in your gatherings while watching one another?

(٥٥) أَئِنَّكُمۡ لَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلرِّجَالَ شَهۡوَةٗ مِّن دُونِ ٱلنِّسَآءِۚ بَلۡ أَنتُمۡ قَوۡمٞ تَجۡهَلُونَ

Do you approach men lustfully instead of women? You do not intend chastity or children, only to fulfill your animalistic lust? Rather, you are ignorant of your obligations of faith, purity, and refraining from sin.

Points of Benefit:

Seeking forgiveness for sins is a means for Allah's mercy.
Pessimism towards people and things is not a trait of believers.
The consequence of indulging in evil and scheming against the people of truth is harmful.
Publicizing wrongdoing is worse than concealing it.
Denouncing those engaged in immorality and wickedness is an obligation.
