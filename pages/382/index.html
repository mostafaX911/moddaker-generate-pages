(٥٦) ۞فَمَا كَانَ جَوَابَ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُوٓاْ أَخۡرِجُوٓاْ ءَالَ لُوطٖ مِّن قَرۡيَتِكُمۡۖ إِنَّهُمۡ أُنَاسٞ يَتَطَهَّرُونَ

His people had no answer except: Expel Lot's family from your village. They are a people who purify themselves from dirt and filth. They said this to mock Lot's family, who did not participate in the indecent act they would commit. Instead, they forbade them from it.

(٥٧) فَأَنجَيۡنَٰهُ وَأَهۡلَهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱمۡرَأَتَهُۥ قَدَّرۡنَٰهَا مِنَ ٱلۡغَٰبِرِينَ

So, we saved him and his family, all except his wife. We ruled that she should remain to be tormented along with those who were destroyed.

(٥٨) وَأَمۡطَرۡنَا عَلَيۡهِم مَّطَرٗاۖ فَسَآءَ مَطَرُ ٱلۡمُنذَرِينَ

We sent down a rain of stones from the sky upon them. It was a terrible and devastating rain for the ones warned of the torment but did not respond.

(٥٩) قُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ عِبَادِهِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱصۡطَفَىٰٓۗ ءَآللَّهُ خَيۡرٌ أَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

Say, O Messenger: All praise is for Allah for His favors and for protecting the Prophet's صلى الله عليه وسلم companions from His torment that He inflicted upon the people of Lot and Saleh. Who is better? Allah, who is worthy of worship and in His Hand is the dominion of everything, or the idols that the polytheists worship, which cannot bring about any benefit or harm!?

(٦٠) أَمَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَأَنزَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا بِهِۦ حَدَآئِقَ ذَاتَ بَهۡجَةٖ مَّا كَانَ لَكُمۡ أَن تُنۢبِتُواْ شَجَرَهَآۗ أَءِلَٰهٞ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ قَوۡمٞ يَعۡدِلُونَ

Is He [not better] Who created the skies and the earth from nothing, sent you, O people, rainwater from the sky, and caused beautiful gardens to grow while you cannot even make the trees grow in those gardens, but Allah can? Does any false idol do this alongside Allah?! No, but they have deviated from the truth by wrongfully making the Creator equal to the creation.

(٦١) أَمَّن جَعَلَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ قَرَارٗا وَجَعَلَ خِلَٰلَهَآ أَنۡهَٰرٗا وَجَعَلَ لَهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَجَعَلَ بَيۡنَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ حَاجِزًاۗ أَءِلَٰهٞ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

Is He [not better] Who made the Earth firm and stable, not being shaken, and made rivers flow through it, placed solid mountains on it, and made a barrier between the two oceans (salty and fresh), preventing the saltwater from mixing with and spoiling the freshwater and making it undrinkable. Did any of these false idols do this alongside Allah? Instead, most of them do not know. They would not have made any of the creations a partner besides Allah if they knew.

(٦٢) أَمَّن يُجِيبُ ٱلۡمُضۡطَرَّ إِذَا دَعَاهُ وَيَكۡشِفُ ٱلسُّوٓءَ وَيَجۡعَلُكُمۡ خُلَفَآءَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَءِلَٰهٞ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ

Is He [not better] who answers who calls upon Him while in hardship, relieves people from sickness, poverty, and other [troubles], and makes you successors in the Earth succeeding one another, generation after generation? Does any false idol do this alongside Allah?! How little do you take heed and consider!

(٦٣) أَمَّن يَهۡدِيكُمۡ فِي ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ وَمَن يُرۡسِلُ ٱلرِّيَٰحَ بُشۡرَۢا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ رَحۡمَتِهِۦٓۗ أَءِلَٰهٞ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ تَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

Is He [not better] Who guides you through the darkness of the land and the sea using landmarks and stars that He has placed for you and sends winds as a sign of the coming rain as His mercy on His slaves? Does any false idol do this alongside Allah?! Allah is sanctified and far removed from the creatures they worship alongside Him.

Points of Benefit:

The people of falsehood resort to violence when surrounded by proof of the truth.
The marital bond without belief will be of no benefit in the Hereafter.
Cementing the creed of monotheism by mentioning the blessings of Allah.
Allah has promised to respond to the supplication of every distressed person, whether believer or disbeliever.
