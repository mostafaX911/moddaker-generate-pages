(٧٧) وَإِنَّهُۥ لَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٞ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Indeed, it (the Quran) is guidance and mercy to the believers who practice what is in it.

(٧٨) إِنَّ رَبَّكَ يَقۡضِي بَيۡنَهُم بِحُكۡمِهِۦۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

Indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, will judge between the people; believers and disbelievers; on the Day of Resurrection, according to His just law. He will have mercy on the believer and torment the disbeliever. He is the Most-Mighty who retaliates against His enemies, and no one can overpower Him. He is the Omniscient who does not confuse the truthful with the liar.

(٧٩) فَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَى ٱلۡحَقِّ ٱلۡمُبِينِ

So, trust in Allah and rely on Him in all your matters. Indeed, you are upon clear truth.

(٨٠) إِنَّكَ لَا تُسۡمِعُ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَلَا تُسۡمِعُ ٱلصُّمَّ ٱلدُّعَآءَ إِذَا وَلَّوۡاْ مُدۡبِرِينَ

O Messenger, you cannot make the dead whose hearts have died to their disbelief in Allah hear, nor can you make those who have lost their hearing hear what you are calling them towards when they return turning away from you.

(٨١) وَمَآ أَنتَ بِهَٰدِي ٱلۡعُمۡيِ عَن ضَلَٰلَتِهِمۡۖ إِن تُسۡمِعُ إِلَّا مَن يُؤۡمِنُ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا فَهُم مُّسۡلِمُونَ

And you are not one to guide those whose eyes are blind to the truth, so do not grieve over them and tire yourself. Only those who believe in our verses will listen to your call because they submit to the orders of Allah.

(٨٢) ۞وَإِذَا وَقَعَ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَخۡرَجۡنَا لَهُمۡ دَآبَّةٗ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ تُكَلِّمُهُمۡ أَنَّ ٱلنَّاسَ كَانُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا لَا يُوقِنُونَ

When the final hour is near, and only the evilest people remain, the torment will become binding and be established upon them due to their persisting in disbelief and sin. Then, We will bring out to them one of the major signs of the hour; a creature from the Earth that will tell them in words that they understand that the people used to disbelieve in what was revealed to Our Prophet.

(٨٣) وَيَوۡمَ نَحۡشُرُ مِن كُلِّ أُمَّةٖ فَوۡجٗا مِّمَّن يُكَذِّبُ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا فَهُمۡ يُوزَعُونَ

Remember, O Messenger, the day when we will gather a group of leaders from each nation that used to reject Our signs. The first to the last of them will be answerable, and then they will be led to the reckoning.

(٨٤) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءُو قَالَ أَكَذَّبۡتُم بِ‍َٔايَٰتِي وَلَمۡ تُحِيطُواْ بِهَا عِلۡمًا أَمَّاذَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

They will continue to be led until they arrive at the place of their reckoning. Allah will rebuke them, saying: Did you disbelieve in My signs that show that I am One and contain my legislation? You had no knowledge to say they were false, and so had no right to reject them! What were you doing, believing, or rejecting?

(٨٥) وَوَقَعَ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ عَلَيۡهِم بِمَا ظَلَمُواْ فَهُمۡ لَا يَنطِقُونَ

They will be tormented due to their wrongdoings of disbelieving in Allah and rejecting His signs. So, they will not speak to defend themselves because they will be unable to, and their arguments are false.

When they rejected the concept of resurrection, Allah drew their attention to the states that demonstrate it in their own lives: their sleeping, which resembles death, and their waking up, which resembles resurrection:

(٨٦) أَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّا جَعَلۡنَا ٱلَّيۡلَ لِيَسۡكُنُواْ فِيهِ وَٱلنَّهَارَ مُبۡصِرًاۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Do those who reject the resurrection not see that We made the night for them to rest by sleeping and the day bright so that they can see and hasten to do their work? Indeed, that recurring death and resurrection contain clear signs for those who believe.

(٨٧) وَيَوۡمَ يُنفَخُ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَفَزِعَ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّا مَن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ وَكُلٌّ أَتَوۡهُ دَٰخِرِينَ

Remember, O Messenger, there will be a day when the horn will be blown a second time by the angel entrusted with it, and everyone in the heavens and the Earth will be terrified except those who Allah has exempted as a favor from Him. All Allah's creation will come to Him obedient and humble on that day.

(٨٨) وَتَرَى ٱلۡجِبَالَ تَحۡسَبُهَا جَامِدَةٗ وَهِيَ تَمُرُّ مَرَّ ٱلسَّحَابِۚ صُنۡعَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَتۡقَنَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍۚ إِنَّهُۥ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَفۡعَلُونَ

On that day, although you may consider the mountains still and motionless, they will actually move swiftly like clouds. This is the doing of Allah. He is the One who will move them. He is aware of what you do, nothing from your deeds is hidden from Him, and He will recompense you accordingly.

Points of Benefit:

The importance of relying on Allah.
Commending the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم for being upon the clear truth.
Guidance and success are in Allah's Hand, not in the hands of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم.
Sleeping connotes death, and waking up connotes resurrection.
