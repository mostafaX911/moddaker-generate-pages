(٨٩) مَن جَآءَ بِٱلۡحَسَنَةِ فَلَهُۥ خَيۡرٞ مِّنۡهَا وَهُم مِّن فَزَعٖ يَوۡمَئِذٍ ءَامِنُونَ

Whoever comes on the Day of Resurrection with faith and righteous deeds will enter paradise. They will be safe from the horrors of the Day of Resurrection due to Allah protecting them.

(٩٠) وَمَن جَآءَ بِٱلسَّيِّئَةِ فَكُبَّتۡ وُجُوهُهُمۡ فِي ٱلنَّارِ هَلۡ تُجۡزَوۡنَ إِلَّا مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

But whoever comes with disbelief and sins will be flung face-first into the Fire. They will be rebuked and humiliated by being told: Are you not being recompensed only for the disbelief and sins you used to commit in the worldly life?

(٩١) إِنَّمَآ أُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أَعۡبُدَ رَبَّ هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡبَلۡدَةِ ٱلَّذِي حَرَّمَهَا وَلَهُۥ كُلُّ شَيۡءٖۖ وَأُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ

Say to them, O Messenger: I have only been ordered to worship the Lord of Mecca, the city He has sanctified; no blood should be shed there, nobody should be oppressed there, none of its game should be hunted, and none of its trees should be cut down. Everything belongs to Him, the Exalted. I have also been ordered to be from those who submit to Allah and obey Him.

(٩٢) وَأَنۡ أَتۡلُوَاْ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَۖ فَمَنِ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ فَإِنَّمَا يَهۡتَدِي لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن ضَلَّ فَقُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُنذِرِينَ

I have been ordered to recite the Quran to the people. Whoever is guided by it and implements what is in it, then his guidance will benefit him. But whoever is misguided, deviates from what is in it, rejects it, and does not implement it, then say: I am only one of the warners warning you about Allah's torment, and your guidance is not in my hands.

(٩٣) وَقُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ سَيُرِيكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ فَتَعۡرِفُونَهَاۚ وَمَا رَبُّكَ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Say, O Messenger, all praise is due to Allah for His innumerable blessings. Allah will show you His signs in yourselves, the sky, the Earth, and your sustenance. Your recognition of them will guide you to the truth and clarify falsehood to you. Your Lord is not unaware of your actions. He sees them all, none of them are hidden from Him, and He will recompense you accordingly.

The Stories (Surah Al-Qasas)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Demonstrating that Allah's established practice is to empower the oppressed believers and destroy the arrogant tyrants.

Explanation:

(١) طسٓمٓ

Tā Sīn Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ

These are the verses of the clear Quran.

(٣) نَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡكَ مِن نَّبَإِ مُوسَىٰ وَفِرۡعَوۡنَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ لِقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

We will read the story of Moses and Pharoah truthfully to you. The story that the believers do not doubt because they are the ones who benefit from what is in it.

(٤) إِنَّ فِرۡعَوۡنَ عَلَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَجَعَلَ أَهۡلَهَا شِيَعٗا يَسۡتَضۡعِفُ طَآئِفَةٗ مِّنۡهُمۡ يُذَبِّحُ أَبۡنَآءَهُمۡ وَيَسۡتَحۡيِۦ نِسَآءَهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ

Indeed, Pharoah was a tyrannical oppressor in the land of Egypt. He divided its people into groups, oppressing one of them, the Israelites, by killing their male children and enslaving their females to humiliate them. He used to corrupt the Earth with oppression, tyranny, and arrogance.

(٥) وَنُرِيدُ أَن نَّمُنَّ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتُضۡعِفُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَنَجۡعَلَهُمۡ أَئِمَّةٗ وَنَجۡعَلَهُمُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثِينَ

And I wanted to bestow a favor upon the Israelites who had been oppressed by the Pharaoh in Egypt, by destroying their enemy, liberating them from the oppression, making them leaders, to be followed by those who seek the truth and making them inheritors of Pharaoh's kingdom in Egypt after I drowned him and his armies.

Points of Benefit:

Belief and righteous deeds are a means to salvation from the terror of the Day of Resurrection.
Disbelief and disobedience are reasons to enter the Fire.
The prohibition of killing, oppression, and hunting in the haram (sanctuary).
Victory and empowerment is the outcome for the believers.
