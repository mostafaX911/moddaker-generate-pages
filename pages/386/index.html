(٦) وَنُمَكِّنَ لَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَنُرِيَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَهَٰمَٰنَ وَجُنُودَهُمَا مِنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَحۡذَرُونَ

We wanted to empower them in the land by making them rule it, thereby demonstrating to Pharoah, his chief advisor Haman, and their army that enforced their rule what they feared most, which was losing power at the hands of one of the male offspring from the Israelites.

Upon mentioning the fate of Pharaoh's kingdom and how Moses and his people would be honored, Allah recalls the upbringing of Moses as Allah's messenger, stating:

(٧) وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنۡ أَرۡضِعِيهِۖ فَإِذَا خِفۡتِ عَلَيۡهِ فَأَلۡقِيهِ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ وَلَا تَخَافِي وَلَا تَحۡزَنِيٓۖ إِنَّا رَآدُّوهُ إِلَيۡكِ وَجَاعِلُوهُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

We inspired Moses' (peace be upon him) mother: Suckle him until you fear that Pharoah and his people will kill him, then place him in a basket and cast it into the Nile. Do not be afraid of him drowning or being harmed by Pharoah, and do not grieve over his separation from you. We will return him to you alive and make him one of Allah's messengers sent to His creation.

(٨) فَٱلۡتَقَطَهُۥٓ ءَالُ فِرۡعَوۡنَ لِيَكُونَ لَهُمۡ عَدُوّٗا وَحَزَنًاۗ إِنَّ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَهَٰمَٰنَ وَجُنُودَهُمَا كَانُواْ خَٰطِ‍ِٔينَ

So, she did as she had been inspired and placed him in a basket, casting him into the river. Then, Pharoah's family discovered him. Thus Allah's will was realized by Moses becoming Pharoah's enemy stripping him of his kingdom and bringing about his people's grief. Indeed, Pharoah, his minister Haman, and their aides used to sin by disbelieving, oppressing, and causing corruption in the land.

(٩) وَقَالَتِ ٱمۡرَأَتُ فِرۡعَوۡنَ قُرَّتُ عَيۡنٖ لِّي وَلَكَۖ لَا تَقۡتُلُوهُ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَنفَعَنَآ أَوۡ نَتَّخِذَهُۥ وَلَدٗا وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

When Pharoah wanted to kill Moses, Pharoah's wife said to him: This boy is a source of joy for both of us, so do not kill him. Maybe, he could serve us, or we could adopt him as our son. But they did not know what would happen to their kingdom due to him.

(١٠) وَأَصۡبَحَ فُؤَادُ أُمِّ مُوسَىٰ فَٰرِغًاۖ إِن كَادَتۡ لَتُبۡدِي بِهِۦ لَوۡلَآ أَن رَّبَطۡنَا عَلَىٰ قَلۡبِهَا لِتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

The heart of Moses' (peace be upon him) mother did not care about anything from the affairs of this world except Moses. She could hardly maintain her patience. To the point where her fierce attachment to him almost led her to reveal that he was her son, but we strengthened her heart and enabled her to persevere as a believer who relies on her Lord and is patient with His decrees.

(١١) وَقَالَتۡ لِأُخۡتِهِۦ قُصِّيهِۖ فَبَصُرَتۡ بِهِۦ عَن جُنُبٖ وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

And after she had cast him into the sea, Moses's mother said to his sister, "Follow him so that you know what happens to him", and so she observed him from a distance so that she was not implicated. Pharaoh and his people were not aware that she was his sister and that she was keeping an eye on him.

(١٢) ۞وَحَرَّمۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَرَاضِعَ مِن قَبۡلُ فَقَالَتۡ هَلۡ أَدُلُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِ بَيۡتٖ يَكۡفُلُونَهُۥ لَكُمۡ وَهُمۡ لَهُۥ نَٰصِحُونَ

By the decree of Allah, Moses refused to breastfeed from the women. When his sister saw their eagerness to feed him, she said to them, "Shall I tell you of a household who shall feed him and look after him, who will also treat him with goodwill?"

(١٣) فَرَدَدۡنَٰهُ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّهِۦ كَيۡ تَقَرَّ عَيۡنُهَا وَلَا تَحۡزَنَ وَلِتَعۡلَمَ أَنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقّٞ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

We returned Moses to his mother so that her eyes, which had longed for him, could become joyful and at rest. This was to prevent her from being grieved by separation from him and to make her realize that Allah's promise to return him to her was undoubtedly true. However, most people were unaware of this promise, and no one knew she was his mother.

Points of Benefit:

Allah takes care of His righteous slaves by protecting them from the plots of their enemies.
The planning of the oppressor leads to his destruction.
The strong emotions that mothers have for their children.
The permissibility of using legitimate ploys to rid the oppression of the oppressor.
The fulfillment of Allah's promise is an inevitable reality.
