Upon mentioning Moses's childhood, Allah recalls the days of his youth, stating:

(١٤) وَلَمَّا بَلَغَ أَشُدَّهُۥ وَٱسۡتَوَىٰٓ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ حُكۡمٗا وَعِلۡمٗاۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

When he grew up and reached his prime strength, We granted him understanding and knowledge of the Israelites' religion before he was sent as a prophet. Just as Moses was rewarded for his obedience, We reward all those who do good in every time and place.

(١٥) وَدَخَلَ ٱلۡمَدِينَةَ عَلَىٰ حِينِ غَفۡلَةٖ مِّنۡ أَهۡلِهَا فَوَجَدَ فِيهَا رَجُلَيۡنِ يَقۡتَتِلَانِ هَٰذَا مِن شِيعَتِهِۦ وَهَٰذَا مِنۡ عَدُوِّهِۦۖ فَٱسۡتَغَٰثَهُ ٱلَّذِي مِن شِيعَتِهِۦ عَلَى ٱلَّذِي مِنۡ عَدُوِّهِۦ فَوَكَزَهُۥ مُوسَىٰ فَقَضَىٰ عَلَيۡهِۖ قَالَ هَٰذَا مِنۡ عَمَلِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۖ إِنَّهُۥ عَدُوّٞ مُّضِلّٞ مُّبِينٞ

Moses entered the city when the people were resting in their homes and found two men arguing and fighting. One was an Israelite from Moses' people, and the other was a Copt from the people of Pharaoh, who were Moses' enemies. The one from his people, the Israelite, asked Moses to help him against the one from his enemies, the Copt. Then, Moses punched the Copt so hard that he killed him. Moses then said: This is from the temptation and deception of the Devil. He is a clear enemy who misguides those who follow him. Whatever happened was due to the Devil's enmity and misguidance, and he intended to misguide me.

(١٦) قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي ظَلَمۡتُ نَفۡسِي فَٱغۡفِرۡ لِي فَغَفَرَ لَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Knowing what he had done, Moses called upon His lord and said: O my Lord, I have wronged myself by killing this Copt, so forgive me for my sin. Allah showed us that He forgave Moses. Indeed, He forgives His slaves who repent and is merciful towards them.

(١٧) قَالَ رَبِّ بِمَآ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيَّ فَلَنۡ أَكُونَ ظَهِيرٗا لِّلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

Then, [In this ayah] Allah continues to inform us about Moses' supplication. Moses said: O my Lord, I will not support the criminals in their crimes because You have blessed me with strength, wisdom, and knowledge.

(١٨) فَأَصۡبَحَ فِي ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ خَآئِفٗا يَتَرَقَّبُ فَإِذَا ٱلَّذِي ٱسۡتَنصَرَهُۥ بِٱلۡأَمۡسِ يَسۡتَصۡرِخُهُۥۚ قَالَ لَهُۥ مُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّكَ لَغَوِيّٞ مُّبِينٞ

After the killing of the Copt occurred, Moses arose the following day in the city, afraid of what might happen. Suddenly, the Israelite who requested his aid and support against his Coptic enemy the day before sought his help against another Copt. Moses told him: You are a clear misguider and troublemaker.

(١٩) فَلَمَّآ أَنۡ أَرَادَ أَن يَبۡطِشَ بِٱلَّذِي هُوَ عَدُوّٞ لَّهُمَا قَالَ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ أَتُرِيدُ أَن تَقۡتُلَنِي كَمَا قَتَلۡتَ نَفۡسَۢا بِٱلۡأَمۡسِۖ إِن تُرِيدُ إِلَّآ أَن تَكُونَ جَبَّارٗا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا تُرِيدُ أَن تَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُصۡلِحِينَ

When Moses (peace be upon him) wanted to attack his and the Israelite's enemy, the Copt, the Israelite thought he intended to attack him because he heard him say: Indeed, you are a clear troublemaker. So, he said to Moses: Do you want to kill me like you killed someone yesterday? You just want to be a tyrant in the land, killing and oppressing people, and you do not wish to reconcile people in dispute.

(٢٠) وَجَآءَ رَجُلٞ مِّنۡ أَقۡصَا ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ يَسۡعَىٰ قَالَ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّ ٱلۡمَلَأَ يَأۡتَمِرُونَ بِكَ لِيَقۡتُلُوكَ فَٱخۡرُجۡ إِنِّي لَكَ مِنَ ٱلنَّٰصِحِينَ

Once word got out, a man rushed over to Moses from the other end of the city, fearing that Moses would be persecuted. He said: O Moses, the leaders of Pharoah's people are discussing killing you, so escape the city. I offer you sincere advice and do not want them to catch and kill you.

(٢١) فَخَرَجَ مِنۡهَا خَآئِفٗا يَتَرَقَّبُۖ قَالَ رَبِّ نَجِّنِي مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

So Moses accepted the man's advice and fled the country, fearing what may happen to him. He called upon his Lord, saying: O my Lord, save me from the oppressive nation so they cannot harm me.

Points of Benefit:

Admitting one's sin is from the etiquette of supplicating.
Praiseworthy gratitude leads the slave to the obedience of his Lord and distances him from disobeying Him.
The importance of rushing to give advice, especially if it results in rescuing a believer from destruction.
The obligation of taking the means to salvation and seeking refuge in Allah through supplication.
