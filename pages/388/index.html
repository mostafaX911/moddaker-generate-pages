(٢٢) وَلَمَّا تَوَجَّهَ تِلۡقَآءَ مَدۡيَنَ قَالَ عَسَىٰ رَبِّيٓ أَن يَهۡدِيَنِي سَوَآءَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ

As he started heading towards Midian, he said: I hope my Lord will guide me to the best route so I will not stray from it.

(٢٣) وَلَمَّا وَرَدَ مَآءَ مَدۡيَنَ وَجَدَ عَلَيۡهِ أُمَّةٗ مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِ يَسۡقُونَ وَوَجَدَ مِن دُونِهِمُ ٱمۡرَأَتَيۡنِ تَذُودَانِۖ قَالَ مَا خَطۡبُكُمَاۖ قَالَتَا لَا نَسۡقِي حَتَّىٰ يُصۡدِرَ ٱلرِّعَآءُۖ وَأَبُونَا شَيۡخٞ كَبِيرٞ

When he reached the watering hole of Midian, he found a gathering of people watering their animals. Away from them, two women kept their sheep back from the water until the rest of the people had finished. Moses (peace be upon him) asked them: Why do you not water [your sheep] with everyone else? They replied: We usually wait to give them water until after the herdsmen leave so that we do not mix with them. Also, our father is an elderly man who cannot do it himself, so we must water them.

(٢٤) فَسَقَىٰ لَهُمَا ثُمَّ تَوَلَّىٰٓ إِلَى ٱلظِّلِّ فَقَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَآ أَنزَلۡتَ إِلَيَّ مِنۡ خَيۡرٖ فَقِيرٞ

So, he was kind to them and watered their sheep himself. Then, he went to some shade to rest and called upon His Lord, alluding to what he needed saying: O my Lord, whatever good you choose to send to me, that is what I need.

(٢٥) فَجَآءَتۡهُ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا تَمۡشِي عَلَى ٱسۡتِحۡيَآءٖ قَالَتۡ إِنَّ أَبِي يَدۡعُوكَ لِيَجۡزِيَكَ أَجۡرَ مَا سَقَيۡتَ لَنَاۚ فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُۥ وَقَصَّ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡقَصَصَ قَالَ لَا تَخَفۡۖ نَجَوۡتَ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

When they went back, they informed their father of Moses, so he sent one of them to him, inviting him. She approached him, walking modestly, saying, "Indeed, my father is inviting you to meet him, intending to reward you for watering our livestock for us." When Moses came to their father and told him of his situation, he reassuringly told him, "Do not fear, for you have escaped from an oppressive nation: Pharaoh and his crowd. They have no authority over Midian, so they will not be able to harm you."

(٢٦) قَالَتۡ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا يَٰٓأَبَتِ ٱسۡتَ‍ٔۡجِرۡهُۖ إِنَّ خَيۡرَ مَنِ ٱسۡتَ‍ٔۡجَرۡتَ ٱلۡقَوِيُّ ٱلۡأَمِينُ

One of his daughters said: O father, hire him to shepherd our sheep as he deserves that because he is both strong and trustworthy. His strength means he can fulfill his duties, and his trustworthiness means he will look after what he is entrusted with.

(٢٧) قَالَ إِنِّيٓ أُرِيدُ أَنۡ أُنكِحَكَ إِحۡدَى ٱبۡنَتَيَّ هَٰتَيۡنِ عَلَىٰٓ أَن تَأۡجُرَنِي ثَمَٰنِيَ حِجَجٖۖ فَإِنۡ أَتۡمَمۡتَ عَشۡرٗا فَمِنۡ عِندِكَۖ وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَنۡ أَشُقَّ عَلَيۡكَۚ سَتَجِدُنِيٓ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

Their father addressed Moses, saying: I would like to marry one of these two daughters of mine to you, and her dowry will be for you to shepherd our flock for eight years. If you wish, you can extend the period to ten years as a favor from yourself, as the agreement is only for eight years. Whatever is over that is optional. I do not want to obligate something that would put you in hardship. You will find me, God willing, from the righteous people who fulfill their contracts and do not break them.

(٢٨) قَالَ ذَٰلِكَ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكَۖ أَيَّمَا ٱلۡأَجَلَيۡنِ قَضَيۡتُ فَلَا عُدۡوَٰنَ عَلَيَّۖ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ مَا نَقُولُ وَكِيلٞ

Moses (peace be upon him) said: What we have agreed is confirmed. Whichever duration I work for you, eight or ten years, I will have fulfilled whatever is upon me, so do not ask for more. Allah witnesses what we have agreed upon and watches over it.

Points of Benefit:

Taking refuge with Allah is the path to salvation in this life and the next.
The modesty of the Muslim woman is the reason for her honor and high status.
The woman sharing her opinion and her opinion being followed when correct is something praiseworthy.
Strength and trustworthiness are two characteristics of someone who is given a responsibility to be successful.
It is permissible for the dowry to be something beneficial.
