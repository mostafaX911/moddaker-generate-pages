(٢٩) ۞فَلَمَّا قَضَىٰ مُوسَى ٱلۡأَجَلَ وَسَارَ بِأَهۡلِهِۦٓ ءَانَسَ مِن جَانِبِ ٱلطُّورِ نَارٗاۖ قَالَ لِأَهۡلِهِ ٱمۡكُثُوٓاْ إِنِّيٓ ءَانَسۡتُ نَارٗا لَّعَلِّيٓ ءَاتِيكُم مِّنۡهَا بِخَبَرٍ أَوۡ جَذۡوَةٖ مِّنَ ٱلنَّارِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَصۡطَلُونَ

When Moses completed the (longer) ten-year term and took his family from Midian to Egypt, he saw a fire at the side of Mount Tur. He told his family: Stay here. I have seen a fire. I could get news from there or bring embers to start your fire and ward off the cold.

(٣٠) فَلَمَّآ أَتَىٰهَا نُودِيَ مِن شَٰطِيِٕ ٱلۡوَادِ ٱلۡأَيۡمَنِ فِي ٱلۡبُقۡعَةِ ٱلۡمُبَٰرَكَةِ مِنَ ٱلشَّجَرَةِ أَن يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنِّيٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

The Exalted one, who sees you when you stand for prayer.

(٣١) وَأَنۡ أَلۡقِ عَصَاكَۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهَا تَهۡتَزُّ كَأَنَّهَا جَآنّٞ وَلَّىٰ مُدۡبِرٗا وَلَمۡ يُعَقِّبۡۚ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ أَقۡبِلۡ وَلَا تَخَفۡۖ إِنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأٓمِنِينَ

[Allah said]: Throw down your staff. So, Moses did as he was commanded. Then, when he saw it move and slither like a snake, he turned and fled, frightened. He did not return until His Lord called out: O Moses, come back and do not be afraid of it. You are from those who are safe from it and the other things you may fear.

(٣٢) ٱسۡلُكۡ يَدَكَ فِي جَيۡبِكَ تَخۡرُجۡ بَيۡضَآءَ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ سُوٓءٖ وَٱضۡمُمۡ إِلَيۡكَ جَنَاحَكَ مِنَ ٱلرَّهۡبِۖ فَذَٰنِكَ بُرۡهَٰنَانِ مِن رَّبِّكَ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَمَلَإِيْهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمٗا فَٰسِقِينَ

Put your right hand into your shirt opening below the collar, and it will come out white (but not like vitiligo). So Moses did that, and his hand came out white as snow. [Allah said]: Draw your hand close to you to settle your fear. So Moses did so, and he was no longer afraid. These two things mentioned, the staff and hand, are proofs sent from your Lord to Pharoah and the leaders of his nation. They were a nation that would rebel against the obedience of Allah by disbelieving and committing sins.

(٣٣) قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي قَتَلۡتُ مِنۡهُمۡ نَفۡسٗا فَأَخَافُ أَن يَقۡتُلُونِ

Moses pleaded to his Lord: I killed one of them before, so I am afraid they will kill me if I come to them conveying what you sent me with.

(٣٤) وَأَخِي هَٰرُونُ هُوَ أَفۡصَحُ مِنِّي لِسَانٗا فَأَرۡسِلۡهُ مَعِيَ رِدۡءٗا يُصَدِّقُنِيٓۖ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ أَن يُكَذِّبُونِ

My brother Aaron speaks clearer than I do, so send him with me as assistance to agree with what I say should Pharoah and his nation not believe me. I fear they might reject me as the previous nations who had messengers sent to them used to do.

(٣٥) قَالَ سَنَشُدُّ عَضُدَكَ بِأَخِيكَ وَنَجۡعَلُ لَكُمَا سُلۡطَٰنٗا فَلَا يَصِلُونَ إِلَيۡكُمَا بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآۚ أَنتُمَا وَمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَكُمَا ٱلۡغَٰلِبُونَ

Allah answered Moses' supplication: We will strengthen you, O Moses, by sending your brother as an aide and messenger. We will give you proof and support so the evil you detest will not reach you. Due to the signs we sent you, you and whoever follows you will be victorious believers.

Points of Benefit:

Fulfilling contracts is a concern of the believers.
Allah speaking to Moses (peace be upon him) is understood literally.
The caller to Allah needs someone to support him.
Eloquence is important for the callers to Allah.
