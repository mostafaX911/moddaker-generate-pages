(٢٣٨) حَٰفِظُواْ عَلَى ٱلصَّلَوَٰتِ وَٱلصَّلَوٰةِ ٱلۡوُسۡطَىٰ وَقُومُواْ لِلَّهِ قَٰنِتِينَ

Guard your prayers by fulfilling them completely as Allah has instructed. In particular, guard the middle prayer, which is the 'Asr (afternoon) prayer, and stand before Allah in submission and devotion.

(٢٣٩) فَإِنۡ خِفۡتُمۡ فَرِجَالًا أَوۡ رُكۡبَانٗاۖ فَإِذَآ أَمِنتُمۡ فَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَمَا عَلَّمَكُم مَّا لَمۡ تَكُونُواْ تَعۡلَمُونَ

If you fear danger from an enemy or the like, and you are unable to perform the prayer in its full manner, then pray whilst walking or riding on camels, horses, or in any way you can. If the fear of danger has subsided, remember Allah in all the ways of remembrance He has taught you, and from them is performing prayer in its entirety. He has taught you what you did not know from light and guidance.

(٢٤٠) وَٱلَّذِينَ يُتَوَفَّوۡنَ مِنكُمۡ وَيَذَرُونَ أَزۡوَٰجٗا وَصِيَّةٗ لِّأَزۡوَٰجِهِم مَّتَٰعًا إِلَى ٱلۡحَوۡلِ غَيۡرَ إِخۡرَاجٖۚ فَإِنۡ خَرَجۡنَ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فِي مَا فَعَلۡنَ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِنَّ مِن مَّعۡرُوفٖۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٞ

Those of you who pass away leaving behind wives must make a bequest for them to be provided with accommodation and maintenance for one year in which your heirs may not remove them. This is to make up for the loss they have suffered and out of respect for the deceased. If they leave before the end of the year by their own choice, there will be no sin on either you or them for what they do with themselves in terms of beautification and making themselves pleasant. Allah is Mighty, and no one can overpower Him. He is Wise in His planning, legislation, and decree. It should be noted that the majority of Quranic exegetes are of the opinion that the law contained in this verse has been abrogated by the verse: {When someone passes away and leaves behind a wife who is not pregnant, these women must wait for a period of four months and ten days} [Baqarah:234]

(٢٤١) وَلِلۡمُطَلَّقَٰتِ مَتَٰعُۢ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِۖ حَقًّا عَلَى ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Divorced women should be gifted something such as clothing, money, or the like. This is to help make up for their emotional suffering because of the divorce. Such provision must fall within the husband's financial ability, and it is the duty of those who are mindful of Allah to fulfill His instructions and avoid His prohibitions.

(٢٤٢) كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ

As with the previous explanation, Allah explains these verses – containing His limits and laws – to the believers so that they understand and practice them to achieve the good of this world and the Hereafter.

(٢٤٣) ۞أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ خَرَجُواْ مِن دِيَٰرِهِمۡ وَهُمۡ أُلُوفٌ حَذَرَ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ فَقَالَ لَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ مُوتُواْ ثُمَّ أَحۡيَٰهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَذُو فَضۡلٍ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَشۡكُرُونَ

Do you not know, O Prophet, about those who left their homes in huge numbers, fearing death due to plague or the like? This refers to a group of people from the Israelites. Allah said to them, 'Die!' and they did. Then Allah brought them back to life to show them that all matters are in His hands and that they have no control over any benefit or harm that may befall them. Allah is Gracious and Bountiful to people, but most people are not thankful to Allah for His favors.

(٢٤٤) وَقَٰتِلُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٞ

Fight, O believers, the enemies of Allah, in support of His religion and to elevate His Word. Know that Allah hears your statements and knows your intentions and actions and that He will compensate you for them accordingly.

(٢٤٥) مَّن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يُقۡرِضُ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنٗا فَيُضَٰعِفَهُۥ لَهُۥٓ أَضۡعَافٗا كَثِيرَةٗۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَقۡبِضُ وَيَبۡصُۜطُ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

Who will act as a lender and spend his wealth in the path of Allah willingly with a good heart and intention so that this may come back to him multiplied? Allah restricts and widens provision and health in accordance with His wisdom and justice. In the Afterlife, you will return to Him alone, and He will recompense you for your actions accordingly.

Points of Benefit:

Encouragement to maintain the Salah (prayer) and offer it with its complete pillars and conditions. Then, if it becomes difficult, it can be prayed to the best of one's ability.
Allah (the Exalted) manifested Mercy upon His slaves as He gave them a complete explanation of His verses so that they could benefit from them.
Allah, the Exalted, may test His slaves by restricting their provision and test others by expanding it. There is profound wisdom in that.
