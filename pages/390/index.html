(٣٦) فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُم مُّوسَىٰ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا بَيِّنَٰتٖ قَالُواْ مَا هَٰذَآ إِلَّا سِحۡرٞ مُّفۡتَرٗى وَمَا سَمِعۡنَا بِهَٰذَا فِيٓ ءَابَآئِنَا ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

When Moses (peace be upon him) brought Our clear signs to them, they said: This is nothing but lies that Moses has fabricated. We never heard of anything like this from our forefathers.

(٣٧) وَقَالَ مُوسَىٰ رَبِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن جَآءَ بِٱلۡهُدَىٰ مِنۡ عِندِهِۦ وَمَن تَكُونُ لَهُۥ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلدَّارِۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

Moses told Pharoah: My Lord knows who is the truthful one who has brought guidance from Him (may He be Exalted) and knows who will have a praiseworthy end in the hereafter. The wrongdoers will never reach their goal, nor will they be saved from what they fear.

(٣٨) وَقَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمَلَأُ مَا عَلِمۡتُ لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرِي فَأَوۡقِدۡ لِي يَٰهَٰمَٰنُ عَلَى ٱلطِّينِ فَٱجۡعَل لِّي صَرۡحٗا لَّعَلِّيٓ أَطَّلِعُ إِلَىٰٓ إِلَٰهِ مُوسَىٰ وَإِنِّي لَأَظُنُّهُۥ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ

Pharaoh said to the leaders of his nation: O chiefs, I do not know of you worshipping other than me. O Haman, fire the clay [bricks] until they become set. Then, build a towering building for me to look at who Moses worships and find out about Him. However, I think that Moses' claim that he has been sent from Allah to me and my nation is a lie.

(٣٩) وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرَ هُوَ وَجُنُودُهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَظَنُّوٓاْ أَنَّهُمۡ إِلَيۡنَا لَا يُرۡجَعُونَ

Pharaoh and his forces became so arrogant and sought to dominate the land of Egypt without any right. They denied the resurrection and thought they would not return to Us on the Day of Resurrection for reckoning and punishment.

(٤٠) فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُ وَجُنُودَهُۥ فَنَبَذۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّۖ فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

We seized him and his soldiers and flung them into the sea to drown them to death. O Messenger, reflect on the fate of the wrongdoers and their ultimate end, for indeed, it was only destruction.

(٤١) وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ أَئِمَّةٗ يَدۡعُونَ إِلَى ٱلنَّارِۖ وَيَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ لَا يُنصَرُونَ

We made them an example for all the tyrants and deviants that call to the Fire by spreading disbelief and misguidance. On the Day of Resurrection, no one will help and rescue them from the torment. Instead, the torment will be multiplied due to them establishing evil ways and calling to misguidance. They will be punished for their actions and the actions of those that followed them.

(٤٢) وَأَتۡبَعۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لَعۡنَةٗۖ وَيَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ هُم مِّنَ ٱلۡمَقۡبُوحِينَ

We piled upon them increasing punishment of humiliation and exclusion from the mercy of Allah in this life. They will be vilified and far from Allah's mercy on the day of resurrection.

(٤٣) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَآ أَهۡلَكۡنَا ٱلۡقُرُونَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ بَصَآئِرَ لِلنَّاسِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٗ لَّعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ

We sent Our messengers to the previous nations, but they rejected them, so we destroyed them. Afterward, We gave Moses the Torah. It contains what causes the people to see what benefits them so they can implement it and what harms them so they can abandon it. It also guides them to goodness and mercy from the good found in this world and the next so that they might remember Allah's favors upon them, thank Him for that and believe in Him.

Points of Benefit:

The people of tyranny reject the truth with false analogies.
Arrogance prevents one from following the truth.
The established practice of the Lord all creation is to give the arrogant an evil end.
Falsehood has leaders, callers, and different forms and appearances.
