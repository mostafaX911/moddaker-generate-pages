(٤٤) وَمَا كُنتَ بِجَانِبِ ٱلۡغَرۡبِيِّ إِذۡ قَضَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ مُوسَى ٱلۡأَمۡرَ وَمَا كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلشَّٰهِدِينَ

O Messenger, you were not there at the mountain's western side (relative to Moses) when We concluded the matter by sending Moses to Pharaoh and his chiefs. Likewise, you were not there to witness the event and tell it to the people. Whatever you tell them is a revelation that Allah revealed to you.

(٤٥) وَلَٰكِنَّآ أَنشَأۡنَا قُرُونٗا فَتَطَاوَلَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡعُمُرُۚ وَمَا كُنتَ ثَاوِيٗا فِيٓ أَهۡلِ مَدۡيَنَ تَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا وَلَٰكِنَّا كُنَّا مُرۡسِلِينَ

We made nations and peoples after Moses. Then, so much time passed that they forgot Allah's covenants. You did not stay with the people of Midian, reciting our verses to them. But, We sent you as Our messenger and revealed the story of Moses and his stay in Midian to you, which you informed the people about.

(٤٦) وَمَا كُنتَ بِجَانِبِ ٱلطُّورِ إِذۡ نَادَيۡنَا وَلَٰكِن رَّحۡمَةٗ مِّن رَّبِّكَ لِتُنذِرَ قَوۡمٗا مَّآ أَتَىٰهُم مِّن نَّذِيرٖ مِّن قَبۡلِكَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ

When we called out to Moses and revealed what we revealed, you were not there at the side of Mount Tur so that you may tell the people about it. But we sent you as a mercy to the people from your Lord and revealed that incident to you so you could warn a nation that had not been warned before by any messenger. We did this so that they might take heed and believe in what you have brought to them from Allah; may He be Exalted.

(٤٧) وَلَوۡلَآ أَن تُصِيبَهُم مُّصِيبَةُۢ بِمَا قَدَّمَتۡ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ فَيَقُولُواْ رَبَّنَا لَوۡلَآ أَرۡسَلۡتَ إِلَيۡنَا رَسُولٗا فَنَتَّبِعَ ءَايَٰتِكَ وَنَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

If they had been punished for their disbelief and sins, they would have argued that a messenger was not sent to them, saying: Why did You not send us a messenger so we could follow Your verses and implement them? Then, we would have been from the believers who fulfill their Lord's commands. If it were not for that, We would have hastened their punishment. Instead, We delayed it so We could be excused by sending a messenger to them.

(٤٨) فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِنۡ عِندِنَا قَالُواْ لَوۡلَآ أُوتِيَ مِثۡلَ مَآ أُوتِيَ مُوسَىٰٓۚ أَوَ لَمۡ يَكۡفُرُواْ بِمَآ أُوتِيَ مُوسَىٰ مِن قَبۡلُۖ قَالُواْ سِحۡرَانِ تَظَٰهَرَا وَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا بِكُلّٖ كَٰفِرُونَ

Then, when Muhammad brought the message from his Lord to the Quraysh, the Jews asked about him. So the Quraysh showed them this evidence, and they replied: Has Muhammad not been given signs that show he has been sent from his Lord like Moses had, such as the hand and the staff? Say, O Messenger, in refutation of them: Did the Jews not disbelieve in what Moses was given before? Did they not say that the Quran and Torah are magic supporting each other and that they disbelieve in both the Quran and the Torah?!

(٤٩) قُلۡ فَأۡتُواْ بِكِتَٰبٖ مِّنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ هُوَ أَهۡدَىٰ مِنۡهُمَآ أَتَّبِعۡهُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

Say to them, O Messenger: If you are honest in your claim that the Quran and Torah are magic, then bring a book revealed from Allah that contains better guidance than them. If you can do so, I will follow it.

(٥٠) فَإِن لَّمۡ يَسۡتَجِيبُواْ لَكَ فَٱعۡلَمۡ أَنَّمَا يَتَّبِعُونَ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡۚ وَمَنۡ أَضَلُّ مِمَّنِ ٱتَّبَعَ هَوَىٰهُ بِغَيۡرِ هُدٗى مِّنَ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

If the Quraysh do not answer your challenge to bring a book with better guidance than the Torah and the Quran, then you can be sure their rejection of them is unfounded. It is only based on following their desires, and no one is more astray than those who follow their desires without any guidance from Allah (may He be Exalted). Indeed, Allah does not grant guidance to people who have wronged themselves by disbelieving in Him.

Points of Benefit:

Negating that the Messenger of Allah صلى الله عليه وسلم has knowledge of the unseen except that which Allah has shown Him.
Knowledge is lost over long periods of time.
Challenging the disbelievers to bring better guidance than Allah's revelation to His Messenger.
The disbelievers' misguidance is due to following desires, not due to following the evidence.
