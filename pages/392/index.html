(٥١) ۞وَلَقَدۡ وَصَّلۡنَا لَهُمُ ٱلۡقَوۡلَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ

Indeed, We have made the polytheists and Jews from the Israelites aware of what happened to the previous nations. We did not punish them for rejecting Our messengers in the hopes that they might take heed, believe, and thus avoid being afflicted by what befell those before them.

(٥٢) ٱلَّذِينَ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِن قَبۡلِهِۦ هُم بِهِۦ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Those who had firm faith in the Torah before the Quran was revealed believe in the Quran because they can find out about it and its description in their books.

(٥٣) وَإِذَا يُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦٓ إِنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّنَآ إِنَّا كُنَّا مِن قَبۡلِهِۦ مُسۡلِمِينَ

When it was recited to them, they said: We have believed in it. Indeed, it is the unquestionable truth that our Lord has revealed. Before this Quran came, we were already Muslims due to believing in what the messengers brought before it.

(٥٤) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يُؤۡتَوۡنَ أَجۡرَهُم مَّرَّتَيۡنِ بِمَا صَبَرُواْ وَيَدۡرَءُونَ بِٱلۡحَسَنَةِ ٱلسَّيِّئَةَ وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ

Those who possess the mentioned qualities will be rewarded twice by Allah because they persevered in belief in their book and then believed in Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم when he was sent. They warded off what sins they had earned by working righteous good deeds and spending from what we gave them on good causes.

(٥٥) وَإِذَا سَمِعُواْ ٱللَّغۡوَ أَعۡرَضُواْ عَنۡهُ وَقَالُواْ لَنَآ أَعۡمَٰلُنَا وَلَكُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلُكُمۡ سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لَا نَبۡتَغِي ٱلۡجَٰهِلِينَ

When the believers from the people of the book hear falsehood, they ignore it and do not pay it any attention. They tell those who speak it: We will be recompensed for our deeds, and so will you. You are safe from us insulting and abusing you. We are not interested in staying with ignorant people as that causes harm and injury to both our religious and worldly life.

(٥٦) إِنَّكَ لَا تَهۡدِي مَنۡ أَحۡبَبۡتَ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ

You, O Messenger, cannot guide those you love, like Abu Talib and others, to have faith in their hearts. But Allah is the One who guides who He wills. He is more aware of who He already knew will be guided to the straight path.

(٥٧) وَقَالُوٓاْ إِن نَّتَّبِعِ ٱلۡهُدَىٰ مَعَكَ نُتَخَطَّفۡ مِنۡ أَرۡضِنَآۚ أَوَ لَمۡ نُمَكِّن لَّهُمۡ حَرَمًا ءَامِنٗا يُجۡبَىٰٓ إِلَيۡهِ ثَمَرَٰتُ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ رِّزۡقٗا مِّن لَّدُنَّا وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

The polytheists of Mecca excuse themselves from following Islam and believing in it by saying: If we follow this Islam that you have brought, our enemies will immediately strip us of our land.Did we not make a sanctuary for these polytheists where spilling blood and oppression is forbidden? Did we not keep them safe from others invading them and send them all types of fruits as sustenance from Us?! However, most of them are unaware of Allah's blessings upon them and do not thank Him.

(٥٨) وَكَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا مِن قَرۡيَةِۢ بَطِرَتۡ مَعِيشَتَهَاۖ فَتِلۡكَ مَسَٰكِنُهُمۡ لَمۡ تُسۡكَن مِّنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗاۖ وَكُنَّا نَحۡنُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثِينَ

And how many were the towns ungrateful for Allah's blessings upon them and went overboard in sins and acts of disobedience? We punished them until they were destroyed. These are their homes now lying in ruins that people pass by. No one stayed in them after their owners were destroyed except a few travelers, for We alone will inherit the heavens, the earth, and everything they contain.

(٥٩) وَمَا كَانَ رَبُّكَ مُهۡلِكَ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ حَتَّىٰ يَبۡعَثَ فِيٓ أُمِّهَا رَسُولٗا يَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِنَاۚ وَمَا كُنَّا مُهۡلِكِي ٱلۡقُرَىٰٓ إِلَّا وَأَهۡلُهَا ظَٰلِمُونَ

Your Lord, O Messenger, does not destroy the towns until He sends a messenger to their major city so that they have no excuse, just like you were sent to the mother of all cities, Mecca. Likewise, We do not destroy their population while they are upright upon the truth. We only destroy them if they have done wrong by disbelieving and committing sins.

Points of Benefit:

The virtue of those from the People of the Book who believe in the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم and that they have two rewards.
Being guided to success is in the Hand of Allah, not in the hands of any messenger or anyone else.
Following the truth is a means to security, not a source of fear, as the polytheists claim.
The danger of luxury on the individual and society.
From Allah's mercy, no people are destroyed until they have been given a chance by sending messengers to them.
