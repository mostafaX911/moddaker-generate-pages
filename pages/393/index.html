When the polytheists used the difficulties of war and the cutting off of trade as excuses for not following the Truth, Allah responded to them by stating:

(٦٠) وَمَآ أُوتِيتُم مِّن شَيۡءٖ فَمَتَٰعُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَزِينَتُهَاۚ وَمَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرٞ وَأَبۡقَىٰٓۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

Whatever your Lord has given you is from the enjoyments and embellishments of the worldly life. They will eventually end. The great reward with Allah in the Afterlife is better and more lasting than any enjoyment or embellishment in this world. Will you not understand so that you prefer what lasts over what ends?!

(٦١) أَفَمَن وَعَدۡنَٰهُ وَعۡدًا حَسَنٗا فَهُوَ لَٰقِيهِ كَمَن مَّتَّعۡنَٰهُ مَتَٰعَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا ثُمَّ هُوَ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ مِنَ ٱلۡمُحۡضَرِينَ

Is the one I have promised paradise in the Afterlife and the everlasting pleasure it contains like the one I have given wealth and adornment to enjoy in the worldly life, then he will be on the Day of Judgment amongst those who will be presented to the Fire?

(٦٢) وَيَوۡمَ يُنَادِيهِمۡ فَيَقُولُ أَيۡنَ شُرَكَآءِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ كُنتُمۡ تَزۡعُمُونَ

[Remember] the Day when your Lord (may He be Exalted and Transcendent) will call out to them saying: Where are the ones you used to worship besides Me claiming that they were My partners?

(٦٣) قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ حَقَّ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ رَبَّنَا هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ ٱلَّذِينَ أَغۡوَيۡنَآ أَغۡوَيۡنَٰهُمۡ كَمَا غَوَيۡنَاۖ تَبَرَّأۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَۖ مَا كَانُوٓاْ إِيَّانَا يَعۡبُدُونَ

The callers to disbelief who must be tormented will say: Our Lord! Those that we misguided, we only misguided them just as we were misguided. We disassociate ourselves from them before you. They never used to worship us. Rather, they were worshipping the devils.

(٦٤) وَقِيلَ ٱدۡعُواْ شُرَكَآءَكُمۡ فَدَعَوۡهُمۡ فَلَمۡ يَسۡتَجِيبُواْ لَهُمۡ وَرَأَوُاْ ٱلۡعَذَابَۚ لَوۡ أَنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ يَهۡتَدُونَ

They will be told: Call upon your [so-called] partners to save you from the disgrace you are in. So, they will call upon them, but their call will not be answered. Then, they will see the punishment that was prepared for them and wish that when they lived, they were from those who were guided to the truth.

(٦٥) وَيَوۡمَ يُنَادِيهِمۡ فَيَقُولُ مَاذَآ أَجَبۡتُمُ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

[Remember] the Day when your Lord will call them saying: How did you respond to My messengers that I sent to you?

(٦٦) فَعَمِيَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡأَنۢبَآءُ يَوۡمَئِذٖ فَهُمۡ لَا يَتَسَآءَلُونَ

They will not be able to come up with any argument, so they will not mention anything. Neither will they ask each other due to the shock of them being sure they are heading for the torment.

(٦٧) فَأَمَّا مَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَعَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحِينَ

But those polytheists who repent from their disbelief, believe in Allah and His messengers, and work righteous deeds will reach what they seek and be saved from what they fear.

(٦٨) وَرَبُّكَ يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُ وَيَخۡتَارُۗ مَا كَانَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡخِيَرَةُۚ سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ وَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

Your Lord, O Messenger, creates what He wills to create and chooses who He wills to be obedient and receive prophethood. The polytheists cannot decide to object to Allah. He (may He be Exalted) is entirely free of and holier than the partners that they worship alongside Him.

(٦٩) وَرَبُّكَ يَعۡلَمُ مَا تُكِنُّ صُدُورُهُمۡ وَمَا يُعۡلِنُونَ

Your Lord knows what their hearts conceal and what they make known. Nothing of that is hidden from Him, and He will recompense them for it.

(٧٠) وَهُوَ ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ لَهُ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ فِي ٱلۡأُولَىٰ وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ وَلَهُ ٱلۡحُكۡمُ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

He is Allah (may He be Exalted). Nothing has the right to be worshipped except Him. He, alone, deserves all praise in this life and the next. His decrees are inevitable and always fulfilled. To Him alone, you will be returned for reckoning and recompense on the Day of Resurrection.

Points of Benefit:

The rational person prefers what lasts over what perishes.
Repentance erases what came before it.
The choice belongs to Allah, not His slaves, and the slaves have no right to object to it.
Allah's knowledge encompasses all the slaves' deeds, apparent or hidden.
