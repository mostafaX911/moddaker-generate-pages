(٧١) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِن جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلَّيۡلَ سَرۡمَدًا إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ مَنۡ إِلَٰهٌ غَيۡرُ ٱللَّهِ يَأۡتِيكُم بِضِيَآءٍۚ أَفَلَا تَسۡمَعُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: Tell me, if Allah makes an endless nighttime that continues uninterrupted until the Day of Resurrection, which of those worshipped besides Allah will be able to bring you light like the light of the day?! Will you not hear these proofs and realize that no God brings you that [light] except Allah?!

(٧٢) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِن جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلنَّهَارَ سَرۡمَدًا إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ مَنۡ إِلَٰهٌ غَيۡرُ ٱللَّهِ يَأۡتِيكُم بِلَيۡلٖ تَسۡكُنُونَ فِيهِۚ أَفَلَا تُبۡصِرُونَ

Say to them, O Messenger: Tell me, if Allah were to make the day last forever continuously until the Day of Resurrection, which of those worshipped besides Allah will bring you night so you can rest and relax from the hard work of the day?! Will you not observe these signs and recognize that no god except Allah brings all that to you?!

(٧٣) وَمِن رَّحۡمَتِهِۦ جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ لِتَسۡكُنُواْ فِيهِ وَلِتَبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

From His (may He be Exalted) mercy to you, O people, He made the night dark for you so you can rest after getting tired from the day's work, and He made the day bright for you so you can go and earn your living in it. He does all this so that you may be thankful for Allah's favors upon you and not be ungrateful for them.

(٧٤) وَيَوۡمَ يُنَادِيهِمۡ فَيَقُولُ أَيۡنَ شُرَكَآءِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ كُنتُمۡ تَزۡعُمُونَ

[Warn them of] the Day when their Lord, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, will call out to them: Where are My [so-called] partners that you used to worship besides Me and claim they were My partners?

(٧٥) وَنَزَعۡنَا مِن كُلِّ أُمَّةٖ شَهِيدٗا فَقُلۡنَا هَاتُواْ بُرۡهَٰنَكُمۡ فَعَلِمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱلۡحَقَّ لِلَّهِ وَضَلَّ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ

We will bring the prophet of every nation to testify against the disbelief and rejection that their nations were upon. Then we will say to the disbelievers of those nations: Bring forth your proofs and evidence supporting your disbelief and rejection. So, their proofs will be exhausted, and they will be sure that the truth in which there is no doubt is for Allah. The partners that they used to invent for Allah will all have vanished.

After mentioning Pharaoh's arrogance on earth due to his power, Allah mentions Qarun's tyranny due to his wealth, stating:

(٧٦) ۞إِنَّ قَٰرُونَ كَانَ مِن قَوۡمِ مُوسَىٰ فَبَغَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡكُنُوزِ مَآ إِنَّ مَفَاتِحَهُۥ لَتَنُوٓأُ بِٱلۡعُصۡبَةِ أُوْلِي ٱلۡقُوَّةِ إِذۡ قَالَ لَهُۥ قَوۡمُهُۥ لَا تَفۡرَحۡۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡفَرِحِينَ

Indeed, Qarun was from Moses' (peace be upon him) people but held his nose up to them. We gave him such vast wealth that even a strong group of people would find the keys of his treasure heavy to carry. So, his people said to him: Do not rejoice arrogantly. Allah does not love those who rejoice arrogantly but despises them and will torment them for that.

(٧٧) وَٱبۡتَغِ فِيمَآ ءَاتَىٰكَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلدَّارَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَۖ وَلَا تَنسَ نَصِيبَكَ مِنَ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَأَحۡسِن كَمَآ أَحۡسَنَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَيۡكَۖ وَلَا تَبۡغِ ٱلۡفَسَادَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ

[Qarun's people continued:] Seek the reward of the Afterlife in what wealth Allah has given you by spending it charitably. At the same time, do not forget your share of food, drink, clothing, and other blessings, without being wasteful or arrogant. Deal well with your Lord and His slaves as He (may He be Exalted) does so with you. Do not try to cause corruption on Earth by committing sins and abandoning obedience. Allah does not love those who corrupt what is on Earth by doing that. Instead, he despises them.

Points of Benefit:

The alternation of the night and day is one of the blessings of Allah that we should thank Him for.
Just as tyranny can occur through leadership and sovereignty, it can manifest through wealth.
Expressing joy with contempt is a sin that Allah detests.
It is necessary to advise those who may be tested.
Allah's hatred for those who spread corruption through the Earth.
