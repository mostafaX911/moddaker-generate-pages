(٧٨) قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أُوتِيتُهُۥ عَلَىٰ عِلۡمٍ عِندِيٓۚ أَوَ لَمۡ يَعۡلَمۡ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَدۡ أَهۡلَكَ مِن قَبۡلِهِۦ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرُونِ مَنۡ هُوَ أَشَدُّ مِنۡهُ قُوَّةٗ وَأَكۡثَرُ جَمۡعٗاۚ وَلَا يُسۡ‍َٔلُ عَن ذُنُوبِهِمُ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ

Qarun said: I was only given this wealth due to my knowledge and capability, so I deserve it. Did Qarun not know that Allah destroyed powerful nations before him that had accumulated far more wealth?! Their power and riches did not benefit them. The criminals will not be asked about their sins on the day of resurrection, as Allah already knows about them. Any questioning of theirs will be to convict and rebuke them.

(٧٩) فَخَرَجَ عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ فِي زِينَتِهِۦۖ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُرِيدُونَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا يَٰلَيۡتَ لَنَا مِثۡلَ مَآ أُوتِيَ قَٰرُونُ إِنَّهُۥ لَذُو حَظٍّ عَظِيمٖ

Qarun went out [one day] in his adornments, showing off his glamour. Those companions of Qarun who desired the embellishments of this worldly life said: If only we were given some of the decoration of this life that was given to Qarun. Qarun possesses a great fortune, indeed.

(٨٠) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ وَيۡلَكُمۡ ثَوَابُ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرٞ لِّمَنۡ ءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗاۚ وَلَا يُلَقَّىٰهَآ إِلَّا ٱلصَّٰبِرُونَ

When those who were given knowledge saw Qarun in his adornments and heard the wishes of his companions, they said: Woe to you! The reward of Allah is in the Afterlife. The paradise He has prepared for those who believe and work righteous deeds is better than the glitter of this life that Qarun has been given. None can say this and act upon what it requires except those who patiently await the reward with Allah instead of the temporary provision of this life.

(٨١) فَخَسَفۡنَا بِهِۦ وَبِدَارِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ فَمَا كَانَ لَهُۥ مِن فِئَةٖ يَنصُرُونَهُۥ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُنتَصِرِينَ

So we caused him, his home, and everything in it to sink into the Earth as retribution for his evil. No group could help him against Allah, nor could he save himself.

(٨٢) وَأَصۡبَحَ ٱلَّذِينَ تَمَنَّوۡاْ مَكَانَهُۥ بِٱلۡأَمۡسِ يَقُولُونَ وَيۡكَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ وَيَقۡدِرُۖ لَوۡلَآ أَن مَّنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡنَا لَخَسَفَ بِنَاۖ وَيۡكَأَنَّهُۥ لَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ

Those who wished for the wealth and adornment that Qarun had before the Earth swallowed him up began to take heed and say: Did we not know that Allah extends provision to who He wills from His slaves and restricts it from who He wills?! If Allah had not favored us by not punishing us for what we said, He would have caused the Earth to swallow us up as He did to Qarun. Indeed, the disbelievers are never successful in this life or the next. Instead, their fate and outcome is to lose in both.

(٨٣) تِلۡكَ ٱلدَّارُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ نَجۡعَلُهَا لِلَّذِينَ لَا يُرِيدُونَ عُلُوّٗا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فَسَادٗاۚ وَٱلۡعَٰقِبَةُ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ

This is the abode of the hereafter. We have made it a place of bliss and honor for those who, while on the Earth, were not too arrogant to believe and act upon the truth, nor did they intend any corruption in it. The praiseworthy conclusion is the enjoyment of paradise and the pleasure of Allah therein. [Such reward] is for those who feared their Lord by fulfilling His commands and abstaining from His prohibitions.

(٨٤) مَن جَآءَ بِٱلۡحَسَنَةِ فَلَهُۥ خَيۡرٞ مِّنۡهَاۖ وَمَن جَآءَ بِٱلسَّيِّئَةِ فَلَا يُجۡزَى ٱلَّذِينَ عَمِلُواْ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِ إِلَّا مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

Whoever brings a good deed on the Day of Resurrection (like Salah, zakat, Fasting, etc.) will be recompensed with better than his good deed, as the reward will be increased tenfold. However, whoever brings an evil deed on the Day of Resurrection (like disbelief, consuming usury, fornication, etc.) will not be recompensed except for what he did, without any increase.

Points of Benefit:

All the good and blessings in a person are from Allah, who created and apportioned them.
The people of knowledge are the people of wisdom and salvation from trials, because knowledge guides the knowledgeable to the right path.
Arrogance and spreading corruption on earth lead to destruction and loss.
The vast mercy of Allah and His justice include multiplying the good deeds for the believer and not multiplying the sins for the disbeliever.
