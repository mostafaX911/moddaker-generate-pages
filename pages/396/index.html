(٨٥) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِي فَرَضَ عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لَرَآدُّكَ إِلَىٰ مَعَادٖۚ قُل رَّبِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ مَن جَآءَ بِٱلۡهُدَىٰ وَمَنۡ هُوَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ

Indeed, the One who sent this Quran to you and obliged you to convey it and implement what is in it will send you back to Mecca victoriously. Say, O Messenger, to the polytheists: My Lord knows best who has brought the Guidance and who is undoubtedly astray from the Guidance and the truth.

(٨٦) وَمَا كُنتَ تَرۡجُوٓاْ أَن يُلۡقَىٰٓ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ إِلَّا رَحۡمَةٗ مِّن رَّبِّكَۖ فَلَا تَكُونَنَّ ظَهِيرٗا لِّلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Before prophethood, O Messenger, you did not expect Allah to reveal the Quran to you. His mercy (may He be Exalted) required it be sent to you. So do not assist the disbelievers in their misguidance.

(٨٧) وَلَا يَصُدُّنَّكَ عَنۡ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ بَعۡدَ إِذۡ أُنزِلَتۡ إِلَيۡكَۖ وَٱدۡعُ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَۖ وَلَا تَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

Do not let the polytheists turn you away from Allah's verses after they have been revealed to you, such that you abandon reciting and conveying them. Call the people to believe in Allah, single Him out in worship, and implement His legislation. Do not be from the polytheists who worship others alongside Allah. Instead, be from the monotheists who do not worship anything except Allah alone.

(٨٨) وَلَا تَدۡعُ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَۘ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ كُلُّ شَيۡءٍ هَالِكٌ إِلَّا وَجۡهَهُۥۚ لَهُ ٱلۡحُكۡمُ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

Do not worship anything alongside Allah; nothing deserves worship besides Him. Everything will perish except His face (may He be Exalted). Judgment belongs to Him alone; He judges as He wills. To Him alone, you will be returned for reckoning and recompense on the Day of Resurrection.

The Spider (Surah Al-'Ankabut)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Commanding perseverance and steadfastness during times of trial and tribulation and describing the positive outcome result of it.

Explanation:

(١) الٓمٓ

Alif Lām Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) أَحَسِبَ ٱلنَّاسُ أَن يُتۡرَكُوٓاْ أَن يَقُولُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا وَهُمۡ لَا يُفۡتَنُونَ

Do the people think that by saying 'We have believed in Allah,' they will be left without any test to clarify the reality of what they said and whether they are true believers?! The situation is not as they think.

(٣) وَلَقَدۡ فَتَنَّا ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۖ فَلَيَعۡلَمَنَّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ صَدَقُواْ وَلَيَعۡلَمَنَّ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ

Indeed, We tested those before them, so Allah will certainly make apparent those who have true faith and expose those who are lying about it.

(٤) أَمۡ حَسِبَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡمَلُونَ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِ أَن يَسۡبِقُونَاۚ سَآءَ مَا يَحۡكُمُونَ

Moreover, do those who commit sins like polytheism think they can overpower Us and be rescued from Our punishment? How disgusting their judgment is! No one can overcome Allah nor be saved from His punishment if they die as disbelievers.

(٥) مَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ لِقَآءَ ٱللَّهِ فَإِنَّ أَجَلَ ٱللَّهِ لَأٓتٖۚ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

Whoever hopes to meet Allah on the Day of Resurrection to be rewarded should know that the time appointed by Allah is coming soon. He is All-Hearing of His slaves' words and Omniscient of their actions; nothing escapes Him, and He will recompense His slaves accordingly.

(٦) وَمَن جَٰهَدَ فَإِنَّمَا يُجَٰهِدُ لِنَفۡسِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَغَنِيٌّ عَنِ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Whoever pushes Himself to be obedient [to Allah] and stay far away from sins, and strives in the way of Allah, then he strives for himself because the benefit of that will be for him. Allah does not need any of His creation. Their obedience does not give Him anything, and their sins do not take anything away from Him.

Points of Benefit:

The prohibition of helping the people of misguidance.
The command to adhere to singling out Allah [in worship] and distancing oneself from associating partners with Him.
Testing and putting the believers to trial is an established divine practice.
Allah does not need the obedience of His slaves.
