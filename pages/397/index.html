(٧) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَنُكَفِّرَنَّ عَنۡهُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِهِمۡ وَلَنَجۡزِيَنَّهُمۡ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلَّذِي كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

Those who believe, who have patience when We test them, and who work righteous deeds, We will wipe out their sins due to the good deeds they did, and We will reward them in the hereafter according to the best of what they used to do in the worldly life.

(٨) وَوَصَّيۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ حُسۡنٗاۖ وَإِن جَٰهَدَاكَ لِتُشۡرِكَ بِي مَا لَيۡسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمٞ فَلَا تُطِعۡهُمَآۚ إِلَيَّ مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ فَأُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

We have commanded humanity to be good to their parents and treat them well. If your parents, O humanity, push you to ascribe a partner with Me that you know nothing of (as the mother of Sa'd bin Abi Waqas, may Allah be pleased with him, did), then do not obey them in that because there is no obedience to the creation if it involves disobedience to the Creator. To Me alone, you will return on the Day of Resurrection. Then, I will inform you of what you used to do in the worldly life and recompense you accordingly.

(٩) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَنُدۡخِلَنَّهُمۡ فِي ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

Those who believe in Allah and work righteous deeds, We will surely admit them among the righteous on the Day of Resurrection, raise them with them, and reward them as they are rewarded.

(١٠) وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَقُولُ ءَامَنَّا بِٱللَّهِ فَإِذَآ أُوذِيَ فِي ٱللَّهِ جَعَلَ فِتۡنَةَ ٱلنَّاسِ كَعَذَابِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَلَئِن جَآءَ نَصۡرٞ مِّن رَّبِّكَ لَيَقُولُنَّ إِنَّا كُنَّا مَعَكُمۡۚ أَوَ لَيۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَعۡلَمَ بِمَا فِي صُدُورِ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Some people say that they believe in Allah. Then, when the disbelievers harm them for their belief, they consider the torment of the disbelievers to be like the torment of Allah and leave the faith agreeing with them. But if you, O Messenger, are supported by your Lord, they will say: Indeed, we believe with you, O believers. Is Allah not fully aware of what is in the people's hearts?! Nothing of their faith or disbelief is hidden from Him. So how could they inform Allah about what is in their hearts while He knows what they contain better than they do?!

(١١) وَلَيَعۡلَمَنَّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَلَيَعۡلَمَنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ

Indeed, Allah will make clear who has true faith and expose the hypocrites who pretend to believe while hiding their disbelief.

(١٢) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّبِعُواْ سَبِيلَنَا وَلۡنَحۡمِلۡ خَطَٰيَٰكُمۡ وَمَا هُم بِحَٰمِلِينَ مِنۡ خَطَٰيَٰهُم مِّن شَيۡءٍۖ إِنَّهُمۡ لَكَٰذِبُونَ

Those who disbelieved said to those who believed: Follow our religion and what we are upon. We will bear your sins and be recompensed for them instead of you. But they will not carry any of their sins and were speaking untruthfully.

It may be understood that disbelievers who invite others to go astray do not commit an additional sin. Allah removes this misconception by stating:

(١٣) وَلَيَحۡمِلُنَّ أَثۡقَالَهُمۡ وَأَثۡقَالٗا مَّعَ أَثۡقَالِهِمۡۖ وَلَيُسۡ‍َٔلُنَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ عَمَّا كَانُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ

Those polytheists who call to their falsehood will undoubtedly carry the sins that they committed themselves. They will also bear the sins of those who followed their call without diminishing anything from the sins of their followers. Indeed, they will be questioned on the Day of Resurrection about the falsehood that they used to invent in this worldly life.

(١٤) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ فَلَبِثَ فِيهِمۡ أَلۡفَ سَنَةٍ إِلَّا خَمۡسِينَ عَامٗا فَأَخَذَهُمُ ٱلطُّوفَانُ وَهُمۡ ظَٰلِمُونَ

Indeed, We sent Noah as a messenger to his people. He remained with them for 950 years, calling them to the Oneness of Allah. They rejected him and persisted in their disbelief. So, the flood seized them while they were doing wrong by disbelieving in Allah and rejecting His messengers, then they were destroyed by drowning.

Points of Benefit:

Allah expiates sins with righteous deeds.
Emphasizing the obligation of treating parents well.
Believing in Allah requires patience with the harm faced in His path.
Whoever establishes an evil practice will bear the sin of it and the sin of whoever does it without diminishing from their sins.
