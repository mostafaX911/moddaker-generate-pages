(١٥) فَأَنجَيۡنَٰهُ وَأَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلسَّفِينَةِ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهَآ ءَايَةٗ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

So, We saved Noah and the believers with him on the Ark from destruction by drowning, and We made the Ark a lesson for the people to heed.

(١٦) وَإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ إِذۡ قَالَ لِقَوۡمِهِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱتَّقُوهُۖ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

O Messenger, remember Abraham's story when he told his people: Worship Allah alone and fear His punishment by fulfilling His commands and abstaining from His prohibitions. What is being ordered is better for you if you could only know.

(١٧) إِنَّمَا تَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡثَٰنٗا وَتَخۡلُقُونَ إِفۡكًاۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ تَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ لَا يَمۡلِكُونَ لَكُمۡ رِزۡقٗا فَٱبۡتَغُواْ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرِّزۡقَ وَٱعۡبُدُوهُ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لَهُۥٓۖ إِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

O polytheists, what you worship are but idols that can do no benefit nor harm. You make up lies when you claim they deserve to be worshipped. Those that you worship besides Allah have no power to provide you with sustenance. So, seek your provisions from Allah as He is the Provider. Worship Him alone and thank Him for the provisions He has blessed you with. You will be returned to Him alone for reckoning and recompense on the Day of Resurrection and not to your idols.

(١٨) وَإِن تُكَذِّبُواْ فَقَدۡ كَذَّبَ أُمَمٞ مِّن قَبۡلِكُمۡۖ وَمَا عَلَى ٱلرَّسُولِ إِلَّا ٱلۡبَلَٰغُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

O polytheists, if you reject what Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم has brought, then so did the nations before you, like the people of Noah, 'Ād, and Thamūd. The Messenger only has to convey [the revelation] clearly, and He has communicated what his Lord has commanded him to inform you.

(١٩) أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ كَيۡفَ يُبۡدِئُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡخَلۡقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرٞ

Do these deniers not see how Allah created the creation initially, then He shall restore it after it has finished? This is easy for Allah, as He is All-Powerful and nothing is beyond His ability.

(٢٠) قُلۡ سِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَٱنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ بَدَأَ ٱلۡخَلۡقَۚ ثُمَّ ٱللَّهُ يُنشِئُ ٱلنَّشۡأَةَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

Say, O Messenger, to those who deny the resurrection: Travel through the land and think about how Allah creates. Then, after they have died, He gives life to the people for the resurrection and reckoning. Indeed, Allah can do anything; there is nothing He is incapable of. He is not unable to resurrect the people, just as He is not incapable of creating them in the first instance.

(٢١) يُعَذِّبُ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَرۡحَمُ مَن يَشَآءُۖ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُقۡلَبُونَ

He torments who He wills from His creation due to His justice and has mercy on who He wills from them due to His grace. To Him alone, you will be returned on the Day of Resurrection for reckoning when you are brought out alive from your graves.

(٢٢) وَمَآ أَنتُم بِمُعۡجِزِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِۖ وَمَا لَكُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مِن وَلِيّٖ وَلَا نَصِيرٖ

You can not escape your Lord and will not be able to flee His punishment, not in the heavens nor the earth. You do not have an ally besides Allah to take care of your affairs, nor do you have any supporter besides Allah who can lift His torment from you.

(٢٣) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَلِقَآئِهِۦٓ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يَئِسُواْ مِن رَّحۡمَتِي وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Those who disbelieve in Allah's (may He be Exalted) signs and do not believe they will meet Him on the Day of Resurrection have no hope in My mercy. They will never enter paradise due to their disbelief, and an excruciating torment awaits them in the Afterlife.

Points of Benefit:

The idols cannot provide anything and so do not deserve any worship.
Provision should only be sought from Allah, who possesses that provision.
Creating in the first instance is evidence for the resurrection.
It is not permissible for those who died upon disbelief to enter Paradise.
