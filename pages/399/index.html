(٢٤) فَمَا كَانَ جَوَابَ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُواْ ٱقۡتُلُوهُ أَوۡ حَرِّقُوهُ فَأَنجَىٰهُ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلنَّارِۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

After Abraham ordered his people to worship Allah alone and abandon the worship of idols besides Him, their only response was to say: Kill him or throw him in the Fire in support of your gods. So, Allah rescued him from the Fire. Indeed, Allah saving him from the Fire after he was thrown in it is a lesson for those who believe because only those who believe can benefit from such lessons.

(٢٥) وَقَالَ إِنَّمَا ٱتَّخَذۡتُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡثَٰنٗا مَّوَدَّةَ بَيۡنِكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ ثُمَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ يَكۡفُرُ بَعۡضُكُم بِبَعۡضٖ وَيَلۡعَنُ بَعۡضُكُم بَعۡضٗا وَمَأۡوَىٰكُمُ ٱلنَّارُ وَمَا لَكُم مِّن نَّٰصِرِينَ

Abraham (peace be upon him) said to his people: You only took these idols as gods to worship due to your acquaintances and mutual affection upon their worship in this worldly life. But on the Day of Resurrection, any affection you had will cease. When you see the torment, you will free yourselves from each other and curse one another. The Fire is the place where you will abide. You will have no supporter to defend you from the torment of Allah, not from the idols you used to worship besides Allah, nor from anyone else.

(٢٦) ۞فَ‍َٔامَنَ لَهُۥ لُوطٞۘ وَقَالَ إِنِّي مُهَاجِرٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّيٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

Lot (peace be upon him) believed in Abraham, and Abraham said: I am migrating to my Lord and the blessed lands of the Levant. Indeed, He is the Most Mighty who can never be overcome. He does not humiliate the one who migrates to Him. He is Wise in His decree and management.

(٢٧) وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥٓ إِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَ وَجَعَلۡنَا فِي ذُرِّيَّتِهِ ٱلنُّبُوَّةَ وَٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ أَجۡرَهُۥ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَإِنَّهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ لَمِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

We gave Abraham Isaac and Isaac's son, Jacob. Then we placed prophethood and the revealed books from Allah among his progeny. We rewarded him in this worldly life for being steadfast upon the truth by giving him righteous offspring and beautiful praise. In the hereafter, he will be given the reward of the righteous. What he was given in this life will not decrease the generous recompense prepared for him in the Afterlife.

(٢٨) وَلُوطًا إِذۡ قَالَ لِقَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِنَّكُمۡ لَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلۡفَٰحِشَةَ مَا سَبَقَكُم بِهَا مِنۡ أَحَدٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Mention, O Messenger, when Lot told his people: You are committing a disgusting sin never perpetrated by anyone from all creation before you. You are the first to invent this sin which defies common sense.

(٢٩) أَئِنَّكُمۡ لَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلرِّجَالَ وَتَقۡطَعُونَ ٱلسَّبِيلَ وَتَأۡتُونَ فِي نَادِيكُمُ ٱلۡمُنكَرَۖ فَمَا كَانَ جَوَابَ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُواْ ٱئۡتِنَا بِعَذَابِ ٱللَّهِ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

Do you fulfill your desires with men's backsides and block the road so that travelers cannot pass you for fear of the sin that you commit? Do you commit evil actions in your gatherings, like nudity, and harm whoever passes by verbally and physically? The only response of his people after he forbade them from evil actions was to tell him: Bring Allah's torment that you are threatening us with if you are truthful in what you claim.

(٣٠) قَالَ رَبِّ ٱنصُرۡنِي عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ

After the obstinance of Lot's people and they disregarded the torment by requesting it to be sent upon them, Lot called out to his Lord: Oh my Lord, help me against these people that corrupt the Earth by propagating disbelief and despicable sins.

Points of Benefit:

Allah takes care of His righteous slaves as He saves them from the plots of their enemies
The virtue of migrating to Allah.
The great position that Abraham and his family have with Allah.
Postponing some reward in this life does not mean it will be decreased in the Hereafter.
The abomination of engaging in evil acts in public gatherings.
