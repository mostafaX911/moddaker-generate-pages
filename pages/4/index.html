(١٧) مَثَلُهُمۡ كَمَثَلِ ٱلَّذِي ٱسۡتَوۡقَدَ نَارٗا فَلَمَّآ أَضَآءَتۡ مَا حَوۡلَهُۥ ذَهَبَ ٱللَّهُ بِنُورِهِمۡ وَتَرَكَهُمۡ فِي ظُلُمَٰتٖ لَّا يُبۡصِرُونَ

Allah gives two examples for the hypocrites: one with fire and one with water. In the example with fire, they are like the ones who lit a flame to light up their surroundings. However, just when they started to benefit from the light emanating from the fire, its flame died down, its radiance leaving them with its burning. As a result, the people who lit it find themselves in complete darkness, unable to see anything and unable to find a path out from it.

(١٨) صُمُّۢ بُكۡمٌ عُمۡيٞ فَهُمۡ لَا يَرۡجِعُونَ

They are deaf: unable to hear or accept the truth. They are dumb: unable to speak the truth. They are blind: unable to see the truth. Because of this, they do not turn back from their misguidance.

(١٩) أَوۡ كَصَيِّبٖ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ فِيهِ ظُلُمَٰتٞ وَرَعۡدٞ وَبَرۡقٞ يَجۡعَلُونَ أَصَٰبِعَهُمۡ فِيٓ ءَاذَانِهِم مِّنَ ٱلصَّوَٰعِقِ حَذَرَ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۚ وَٱللَّهُ مُحِيطُۢ بِٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

In the example with water: They are like a heavy rain consisting of thick clouds of layered darkness, thunder, and lightning, descending upon a people and afflicting them with tremendous panic. The intensity of the thunderclaps and the fear of death that results causes them to plug their ears with the tips of their fingers. And Allah is All-Encompassing of the disbelievers, and they do not escape Him.

(٢٠) يَكَادُ ٱلۡبَرۡقُ يَخۡطَفُ أَبۡصَٰرَهُمۡۖ كُلَّمَآ أَضَآءَ لَهُم مَّشَوۡاْ فِيهِ وَإِذَآ أَظۡلَمَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ قَامُواْۚ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَذَهَبَ بِسَمۡعِهِمۡ وَأَبۡصَٰرِهِمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

The brightness of the lightning almost blinds them. Whenever it flashes, lighting up the surroundings, they proceed forward, and when there is no light, they remain in darkness, unable to move. If Allah willed, He could take away their hearing and their sight, in His absolute power over all things, and not return it to them due to their turning away from the truth. The rain in the example refers to the Quran. The enveloping darkness refers to the hypocrites' doubts and misconceptions regarding the Quran. The thunderclaps refer to the warnings and the lightning to the truth that sometimes becomes apparent to them. The plugging of their ears from the thunderclaps is likened to their turning away from the truth and not responding to it. The hypocrites are similar to the people in these examples because they receive no benefit from their surroundings. In the example of the fire, all they receive is darkness and a smoldering fire. In the example of the rainstorm, all they receive is terror and panic from the thunder and lightning. Such are the hypocrites who see nothing in Islam except difficulty and harshness.

After mentioning the believers, the disbelievers, and the hypocrites, Allah calls upon everyone to serve Him alone, stating:

(٢١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّكُمُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُمۡ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ

O people, worship no one other than your Lord, for He created you and nations that preceded you so that you may place protection between you and His punishment by following His commands and avoiding what He has prohibited.

(٢٢) ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ فِرَٰشٗا وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بِنَآءٗ وَأَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَخۡرَجَ بِهِۦ مِنَ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ رِزۡقٗا لَّكُمۡۖ فَلَا تَجۡعَلُواْ لِلَّهِ أَندَادٗا وَأَنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

It is He Who made the Earth like a carpet spread out in front of you, and it is He Who firmly constructed the sky over you. Through His Grace, rain falls, producing a variety of fruits from the Earth as sustenance for you. Hence, do not make others partners and equals to Allah when you realize that there is no Creator except for Allah, Exalted and Sublime be He.

(٢٣) وَإِن كُنتُمۡ فِي رَيۡبٖ مِّمَّا نَزَّلۡنَا عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِنَا فَأۡتُواْ بِسُورَةٖ مِّن مِّثۡلِهِۦ وَٱدۡعُواْ شُهَدَآءَكُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

And if you, O Stubborn Disbelievers, are skeptical of the Quran sent down to Our slave Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, then We challenge you to bring one surah (chapter) akin to it, even if it is akin to its shortest surah, and call whoever you can from your supporters if you are truthful in what you claim.

(٢٤) فَإِن لَّمۡ تَفۡعَلُواْ وَلَن تَفۡعَلُواْ فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱلنَّارَ ٱلَّتِي وَقُودُهَا ٱلنَّاسُ وَٱلۡحِجَارَةُۖ أُعِدَّتۡ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

And if you can't do that---and you will never be able to---then fear the Fire fueled by the people who deserve punishment and by the types of stones, including those they used to worship. Allah has prepared this Fire for the disbelievers.

Points of Benefit:

Allah, the Exalted, abandons the hypocrites in their most needy and intense situations as a recompense for their hypocrisy and turning away from guidance.
One of the greatest proofs of the necessity of singling out Allah in worship is the fact that He, the Most High, created for us everything in the universe and made it subservient to us.
The inability of creation to come up with anything similar to a chapter from the Holy Quran indicates that it is a revelation from the All-Wise, All-Knowing.
