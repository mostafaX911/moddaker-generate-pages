(٢٤٦) أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلۡمَلَإِ مِنۢ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مُوسَىٰٓ إِذۡ قَالُواْ لِنَبِيّٖ لَّهُمُ ٱبۡعَثۡ لَنَا مَلِكٗا نُّقَٰتِلۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۖ قَالَ هَلۡ عَسَيۡتُمۡ إِن كُتِبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡقِتَالُ أَلَّا تُقَٰتِلُواْۖ قَالُواْ وَمَا لَنَآ أَلَّا نُقَٰتِلَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَقَدۡ أُخۡرِجۡنَا مِن دِيَٰرِنَا وَأَبۡنَآئِنَاۖ فَلَمَّا كُتِبَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقِتَالُ تَوَلَّوۡاْ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا مِّنۡهُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Do you not know, O Prophet, about those nobles from the Israelites after the time of Moses who said to a prophet of theirs, 'Appoint for us a king, and we will fight alongside him in Allah's path'. The prophet replied: 'It may be that if Allah makes fighting obligatory, you will not fight!'. They denied his suspicion that this would happen and insisted, 'What would stop us from fighting in Allah's path when our enemies have expelled us from our homes and have taken our children as prisoners? We will fight to restore our homeland and free our prisoners!' Yet, when Allah made fighting obligatory on them, they all turned away without remaining loyal to what they had promised - except for a few. Allah knows the wrongdoers who broke their promise and turned away from what He commanded, and He will recompense them for that.

(٢٤٧) وَقَالَ لَهُمۡ نَبِيُّهُمۡ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَدۡ بَعَثَ لَكُمۡ طَالُوتَ مَلِكٗاۚ قَالُوٓاْ أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لَهُ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ عَلَيۡنَا وَنَحۡنُ أَحَقُّ بِٱلۡمُلۡكِ مِنۡهُ وَلَمۡ يُؤۡتَ سَعَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلۡمَالِۚ قَالَ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱصۡطَفَىٰهُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَزَادَهُۥ بَسۡطَةٗ فِي ٱلۡعِلۡمِ وَٱلۡجِسۡمِۖ وَٱللَّهُ يُؤۡتِي مُلۡكَهُۥ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٞ

Their prophet told them: Allah has appointed Saul as your king, under whose banner you must fight. Their leaders defiantly objected to this choice and said: How can he rule over us when we have a greater right to rule than he does since he is not from a royal progeny and has not been given vast wealth which he can use in his rule! Their prophet replied: Allah has selected him over you and has given him great knowledge and physical strength. Allah grants authority to whomever He wishes under His wisdom and mercy. Allah is Bountiful and grants to whomever He wishes. He knows which among His creations is most deserving.

(٢٤٨) وَقَالَ لَهُمۡ نَبِيُّهُمۡ إِنَّ ءَايَةَ مُلۡكِهِۦٓ أَن يَأۡتِيَكُمُ ٱلتَّابُوتُ فِيهِ سَكِينَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَبَقِيَّةٞ مِّمَّا تَرَكَ ءَالُ مُوسَىٰ وَءَالُ هَٰرُونَ تَحۡمِلُهُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Their prophet told them that the mark of his being truly chosen as their king was that Allah would return to them the Ark. This was a chest revered by the Israelites, which was taken away from them. In it, there was tranquility, and it contained relics left behind by the family of Moses and Aaron, such as the staff and a few tablets. In that, there is a clear sign if you truly are believers.

Points of Benefit:

Bringing attention to the most important characteristics of a leader that qualify him to lead people, which is knowledge about what he is a leader of and the strength to do it.
Guidance to whoever is entrusted to lead the people not to be deceived by what they say until he tests them and examines what they do after speaking.
What is commonly considered by the people to be the measure to judge people by may not be the right measure according to Allah, the Exalted. On the contrary, Allah selects whoever He wills from His creation according to His wisdom and knowledge.
