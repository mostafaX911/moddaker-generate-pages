(٣١) وَلَمَّا جَآءَتۡ رُسُلُنَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ بِٱلۡبُشۡرَىٰ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا مُهۡلِكُوٓاْ أَهۡلِ هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِۖ إِنَّ أَهۡلَهَا كَانُواْ ظَٰلِمِينَ

When the angels we sent to give the glad tidings of Isaac and his son Jacob came to Abraham, they told him: We will destroy the people of Sodom, the town of Lot's people. The people there did wrong by committing obscene actions.

(٣٢) قَالَ إِنَّ فِيهَا لُوطٗاۚ قَالُواْ نَحۡنُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن فِيهَاۖ لَنُنَجِّيَنَّهُۥ وَأَهۡلَهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱمۡرَأَتَهُۥ كَانَتۡ مِنَ ٱلۡغَٰبِرِينَ

Abraham (peace be upon him) said to the angels: Lot is in this town that you want to destroy the people of, and he is not one of the wrongdoers. The angels said: We know very well who is there. We will save him and his family from the destruction that will be sent upon the people of his town, except his wife, who remained with those to be destroyed. We will destroy her with them.

(٣٣) وَلَمَّآ أَن جَآءَتۡ رُسُلُنَا لُوطٗا سِيٓءَ بِهِمۡ وَضَاقَ بِهِمۡ ذَرۡعٗاۖ وَقَالُواْ لَا تَخَفۡ وَلَا تَحۡزَنۡ إِنَّا مُنَجُّوكَ وَأَهۡلَكَ إِلَّا ٱمۡرَأَتَكَ كَانَتۡ مِنَ ٱلۡغَٰبِرِينَ

When the angels that we sent to destroy the people of Lot's town came to Lot, he was troubled and upset by them coming. He feared the wickedness of his people because the angels came in the form of men, and his people lusted after men instead of women. The angels told him: Do not be afraid; your people will not harm you, and do not be sad about the destruction we informed you about. We will save you and your family from destruction, except your wife, who stayed with them. So, we will destroy her with them.

(٣٤) إِنَّا مُنزِلُونَ عَلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِ هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِ رِجۡزٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡسُقُونَ

We will rain down stones of baked clay as torment from the sky upon the people of this town who used to commit wickedness; a punishment for them abandoning obedience to Allah by committing that disgusting, immoral act of fulfilling their lusts with men instead of women.

(٣٥) وَلَقَد تَّرَكۡنَا مِنۡهَآ ءَايَةَۢ بَيِّنَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ

Indeed We left a clear sign from this town that we destroyed for those who think because they are the ones who take heed of these signs.

(٣٦) وَإِلَىٰ مَدۡيَنَ أَخَاهُمۡ شُعَيۡبٗا فَقَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱرۡجُواْ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَ وَلَا تَعۡثَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مُفۡسِدِينَ

We sent their relative, Shuayb (peace be upon him), to Midian, who said: O my people, worship Allah alone and hope for the recompense on the last day by worshipping him. Do not corrupt the Earth by doing evil deeds and propagating them.

(٣٧) فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلرَّجۡفَةُ فَأَصۡبَحُواْ فِي دَارِهِمۡ جَٰثِمِينَ

His people rejected him, so an earthquake struck them. They collapsed to the ground on their faces, where they remained motionless, their faces stuck to the debris of their homes.

(٣٨) وَعَادٗا وَثَمُودَاْ وَقَد تَّبَيَّنَ لَكُم مِّن مَّسَٰكِنِهِمۡۖ وَزَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ فَصَدَّهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَكَانُواْ مُسۡتَبۡصِرِينَ

And We also destroyed the peoples of the prophet Hūd and Thamūd, the people of the prophet Saleh. It was made evident to you, O people of Mecca, their dwellings in Ash-Shihr, from Hadramawt and Al-Hijr. Their destruction was made evident through their vacant dwellings that bear testament to their demise. The Devil beautified their disbelief and sinful acts that they were upon in their eyes, leading them astray from the Straight Path. They were clearly able to differentiate between truth and misguidance and righteousness and transgression because of their messengers' teachings. But they chose to follow their desires over following guidance.

Points of Benefit:

His (the Exalted's) statement, "and it has become clear to you," shows that the Arabs knew about their homes and what happened to them.
Human relationships are of no benefit without belief.
Ensuring the security of guests and their safety from being assaulted.
The homes of those destroyed by the torment are a lesson for those who reflect.
Knowing the Truth is useless if one follows his desires and prefers them over guidance.
