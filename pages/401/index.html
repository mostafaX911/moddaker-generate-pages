(٣٩) وَقَٰرُونَ وَفِرۡعَوۡنَ وَهَٰمَٰنَۖ وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَهُم مُّوسَىٰ بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ فَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا كَانُواْ سَٰبِقِينَ

We destroyed Qarun by the Earth swallowing him and his home up when he oppressed the people of Moses. We also destroyed Pharoah and his minister, Haman, by drowning them in the sea. Indeed, Moses brought them clear signs testifying to his truthfulness, but they were too arrogant in the land of Egypt to believe in him. They could not escape from Us and save themselves from Our torment.

(٤٠) فَكُلًّا أَخَذۡنَا بِذَنۢبِهِۦۖ فَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ حَاصِبٗا وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ أَخَذَتۡهُ ٱلصَّيۡحَةُ وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ خَسَفۡنَا بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ أَغۡرَقۡنَاۚ وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيَظۡلِمَهُمۡ وَلَٰكِن كَانُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَظۡلِمُونَ

We seized each of those previously mentioned with Our destructive torment. There were the people of Lot who We sent down upon them stones of baked clay. The people of Saleh and Shuayb were seized by the blast (a tremendous cry from the sky). From them was Qarun, who the Earth swallowed up along with his home. From them were the people of Noah, Pharoah, and Haman, who We destroyed by drowning. Allah would not wrong them by destroying them for no sin. Rather, they wronged themselves by committing sins and so deserved the torment.

(٤١) مَثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡلِيَآءَ كَمَثَلِ ٱلۡعَنكَبُوتِ ٱتَّخَذَتۡ بَيۡتٗاۖ وَإِنَّ أَوۡهَنَ ٱلۡبُيُوتِ لَبَيۡتُ ٱلۡعَنكَبُوتِۚ لَوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ

The similitude of the polytheists who worship idols besides Allah, hoping they will bring them some benefit or intercede for them, is that of the spider. It makes its home to protect it from being attacked, while the weakest home is that of the spider, as it cannot protect it from any enemy. Likewise, their idols can neither benefit nor harm nor intercede. If the polytheists knew that, they would not have worshiped idols besides Allah.

(٤٢) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ مَا يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ مِن شَيۡءٖۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

Allah (may He be Exalted) knows what they worship besides Him. Nothing is hidden from Him. He is the Most Mighty (Al-Aziz); nothing can overpower him. He is the Most Wise (Al-Hakim) in His creation, decree, and management.

(٤٣) وَتِلۡكَ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلُ نَضۡرِبُهَا لِلنَّاسِۖ وَمَا يَعۡقِلُهَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡعَٰلِمُونَ

These examples that I draw for people, to awaken them and make them see the truth and guide them to it, can only be grasped in the desired manner by those who have knowledge of Allah's law and rule.

(٤٤) خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Allah, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, created the heavens and the Earth for a true purpose. He did not create them out of falsehood or in vain. That creation is a clear indication of Allah's power for the believers because they are the ones who will see Allah's created things as evidence of the Creator (may He be Exalted). As for the disbelievers, they come across these signs upon the horizon and in themselves without paying any attention to the might and power of the Creator (may He be Exalted).

(٤٥) ٱتۡلُ مَآ أُوحِيَ إِلَيۡكَ مِنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَۖ إِنَّ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ تَنۡهَىٰ عَنِ ٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِ وَٱلۡمُنكَرِۗ وَلَذِكۡرُ ٱللَّهِ أَكۡبَرُۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مَا تَصۡنَعُونَ

Read, O Messenger, what Allah has revealed to you from the Quran to the people and offer the prayer in a complete manner. When the prayer is offered most completely, it prevents the person from falling into sins and evil deeds because it places a light in the heart that prevents the committing of sins and guides to performing righteous deeds. Remembrance of Allah is more remarkable and far superior to anything else. Allah knows everything you do; none of your acts are hidden from Him. He will recompense you for your deeds. If your deeds are good, then the recompense will be good; if they are evil, then the recompense will be evil.

Points of Benefit:

The importance of giving examples (like that of the spider).
There are many types of torment in this life.
Allah is far removed from oppression.
Depending on other than Allah is dependence on the weakest means.
The importance of Prayer in making a believer's conduct upright.
