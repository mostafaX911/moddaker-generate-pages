(٤٦) ۞وَلَا تُجَٰدِلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ إِلَّا بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ مِنۡهُمۡۖ وَقُولُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِٱلَّذِيٓ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡنَا وَأُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكُمۡ وَإِلَٰهُنَا وَإِلَٰهُكُمۡ وَٰحِدٞ وَنَحۡنُ لَهُۥ مُسۡلِمُونَ

O believers, do not discuss or debate with the Jews and Christians to whom the divine books were revealed, except in the best of manners and most exemplary of ways: inviting them by exhortation and establishing clear proofs. As for those who oppress by showing stubbornness and arrogance, fight them until they submit or pay the Jizyah (protection tax paid by non-Muslims living under Muslim rule) from their hands while subdued. And say to the Jews and the Christians: I have believed in whatever Allah has revealed to us in the Quran and what was revealed to you in the Torah and Gospel. Our Lord is one, and so is yours; He has no partner in His Lordship, worship, or perfection, and we submit and humble ourselves to Him alone.

(٤٧) وَكَذَٰلِكَ أَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَۚ فَٱلَّذِينَ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهِۦۖ وَمِنۡ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ مَن يُؤۡمِنُ بِهِۦۚ وَمَا يَجۡحَدُ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ

Just as We revealed the Books upon those before you, We revealed the Quran upon you. Some who read the Torah, such as Abdullah bin Salam, believe in the Quran because they find it described in their books. Some polytheists also believe in this Quran. Only those accustomed to rejection and denial disbelieve in Our signs, even though they are clear.

(٤٨) وَمَا كُنتَ تَتۡلُواْ مِن قَبۡلِهِۦ مِن كِتَٰبٖ وَلَا تَخُطُّهُۥ بِيَمِينِكَۖ إِذٗا لَّٱرۡتَابَ ٱلۡمُبۡطِلُونَ

O Messenger, you had not been reading any book before the [revelation of the] Quran [began], nor did you write anything with your hand because you are unlettered and cannot read or write. If you could read and write, the ignorant people would have doubted your prophethood and used it as an excuse to say you were conveying [verses of the Quran] from the previous books.

(٤٩) بَلۡ هُوَ ءَايَٰتُۢ بَيِّنَٰتٞ فِي صُدُورِ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَۚ وَمَا يَجۡحَدُ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآ إِلَّا ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

Rather, the Quran revealed to you consists of clear verses preserved within the hearts of those believers who have been granted knowledge. Only those who wrong themselves by ascribing partners with Allah and rejecting Him deny Our verses.

(٥٠) وَقَالُواْ لَوۡلَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَٰتٞ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦۚ قُلۡ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡأٓيَٰتُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَإِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ نَذِيرٞ مُّبِينٌ

The polytheists said: Why have no signs been given to Muhammad from his Lord, like those given to the messengers before him? O Messenger, say to them: Indeed signs are only in the control of Allah, may He be Exalted; He grants them whenever He wishes. It is not in my power to bring them; I am only a clear warner to you of the punishment of Allah.

(٥١) أَوَ لَمۡ يَكۡفِهِمۡ أَنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ يُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَرَحۡمَةٗ وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

O Messenger, is it not enough for these people that we have revealed the Quran upon you, which is recited to them? Indeed, the Quran is a mercy and exhortation to people who believe because they are the ones who truly benefit from whatever it contains. Therefore, whatever has been revealed to them is better than sending something similar to what was revealed to the previous messengers as they want.

(٥٢) قُلۡ كَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكُمۡ شَهِيدٗاۖ يَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ وَكَفَرُواْ بِٱللَّهِ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ

O Messenger, say: Allah (may He be Exalted) is enough as a witness to my truthfulness in what I convey and to your rejection of it. He knows everything in the heavens and on Earth; nothing is hidden from Him. As for those who believe in the falsehood of worshipping anything instead of Allah while rejecting the One who alone is worthy of worship, they are the true losers because they chose disbelief over belief.

Points of Benefit:

Debating with the People of the Book should be in the best manner.
Belief in all the messengers and scriptures without distinction is a condition for the validity of faith.
The Quran is the eternal sign and the perpetual evidence attesting to the truthfulness of the Prophet, peace be upon him.
