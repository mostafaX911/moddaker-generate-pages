(٥٣) وَيَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَكَ بِٱلۡعَذَابِ وَلَوۡلَآ أَجَلٞ مُّسَمّٗى لَّجَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُۚ وَلَيَأۡتِيَنَّهُم بَغۡتَةٗ وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

O Messenger, the polytheists want you to bring the punishment you warned them of quickly. Had it not been for Allah already having decreed a fixed time for their punishment, it would have come upon them suddenly while they were unaware.

(٥٤) يَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَكَ بِٱلۡعَذَابِ وَإِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ لَمُحِيطَةُۢ بِٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

They want you to hasten the punishment you warned them about. Indeed, the Hell that Allah warned the disbelievers of will surround them; they will not be able to escape its torment.

(٥٥) يَوۡمَ يَغۡشَىٰهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ مِن فَوۡقِهِمۡ وَمِن تَحۡتِ أَرۡجُلِهِمۡ وَيَقُولُ ذُوقُواْ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

On the Day the punishment will cover them from above and spread under their feet, Allah will rebuke them by saying: Suffer the consequences of the polytheism and sins you used to commit.

(٥٦) يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّ أَرۡضِي وَٰسِعَةٞ فَإِيَّٰيَ فَٱعۡبُدُونِ

O my slaves who believe in Me, leave the land where you cannot worship Me [to somewhere where you can]. Indeed, My land is vast, so worship Me alone and do not ascribe anyone as a partner to Me.

(٥٧) كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ ذَآئِقَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۖ ثُمَّ إِلَيۡنَا تُرۡجَعُونَ

Let not the fear of death prevent you from migrating. Every soul shall taste death, and then to Us alone, you shall return on the Day of Resurrection to be taken to account and recompensed accordingly.

(٥٨) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَنُبَوِّئَنَّهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ غُرَفٗا تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ نِعۡمَ أَجۡرُ ٱلۡعَٰمِلِينَ

Those who have faith in Allah and perform good deeds through which they draw closer to Him, We will accommodate them in raised dwellings under which rivers flow, wherein they shall reside forever. How great is this reward of those who live in obedience to Allah!

(٥٩) ٱلَّذِينَ صَبَرُواْ وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ

How great is the reward for those who obey Allah and remain steadfast on that while refraining from disobeying Him, and they rely upon Him alone in all their affairs?

(٦٠) وَكَأَيِّن مِّن دَآبَّةٖ لَّا تَحۡمِلُ رِزۡقَهَا ٱللَّهُ يَرۡزُقُهَا وَإِيَّاكُمۡۚ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

Every single animal that exists, and there are many, cannot store its provisions; [rather,] Allah sustains them and you. Therefore, not emigrating out of fear of starvation is not a valid excuse for you. He is All-Hearing of your words and Omniscient of your intentions and actions; none of them are hidden from Him, and He shall recompense you accordingly for them.

(٦١) وَلَئِن سَأَلۡتَهُم مَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَسَخَّرَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَ لَيَقُولُنَّ ٱللَّهُۖ فَأَنَّىٰ يُؤۡفَكُونَ

O Messenger, if you ask these polytheists who created the heavens and the Earth and who put the sun and moon into orbit such that they appear one after the other, surely they will say Allah is the one who created the heavens and the Earth and put the sun and moon into constant orbit. How can they turn away from believing in Allah alone to worshiping deities instead of Him that do not benefit or bring harm?

(٦٢) ٱللَّهُ يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ وَيَقۡدِرُ لَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٞ

Allah increases the sustenance for whomever He wishes from His slaves and restricts it for whomever He wishes due to wisdom only He knows. Indeed, Allah knows everything; whatever is good for His slaves is not hidden from Him.

(٦٣) وَلَئِن سَأَلۡتَهُم مَّن نَّزَّلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَحۡيَا بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَوۡتِهَا لَيَقُولُنَّ ٱللَّهُۚ قُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِۚ بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡقِلُونَ

O Messenger, suppose you were to ask the polytheists who causes the rain to descend from the sky and consequently causes the earth to grow after it had been barren. In that case, they will say Allah is the one who causes the rain to fall from the sky and the earth to grow because of it. O Messenger, say [to them]: All praise is for Allah, Who has made the evidence clear against you. However, the crux of the matter is that most of them will not understand because if they were to understand, they would not worship idols that do not benefit or harm them.

Points of Benefit:

The disbeliever's hastening for the punishment is evidence of their folly.
The door of migration for the sake of preserving one's religion is open.
The virtues of patience and reliance on Allah.
Acknowledging the Lordship without acknowledging the Divinity does not secure salvation and belief for its holder.
