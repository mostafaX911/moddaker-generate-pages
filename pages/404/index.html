(٦٤) وَمَا هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ إِلَّا لَهۡوٞ وَلَعِبٞۚ وَإِنَّ ٱلدَّارَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ لَهِيَ ٱلۡحَيَوَانُۚ لَوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ

With all its temptations and enjoyments, this worldly life is only amusement and play for those attached to it; it will quickly perish. Indeed, the Afterlife's abode is the real life because it is eternal. If only they knew, they would not have preferred something temporary over that which is eternal.

Allah recorded the polytheists' contradictions against them, such as their belief in Allah's lordship when asked about the creation of the heavens and the Earth and their disbelief in Allah's absolute divinity when they worship other than Him. Allah records another contradiction when they fully devote themselves to Him alone when they fear drowning and returning back to associating partners with Him when they find safety, stating:

(٦٥) فَإِذَا رَكِبُواْ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ دَعَوُاْ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ فَلَمَّا نَجَّىٰهُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡبَرِّ إِذَا هُمۡ يُشۡرِكُونَ

When the polytheists board ships and sail the ocean, they call out to Allah alone, sincerely supplicating to Him to save them from drowning. After Allah saves them from drowning, they return to their polytheism, calling out to their idols along with Allah.

(٦٦) لِيَكۡفُرُواْ بِمَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمۡ وَلِيَتَمَتَّعُواْۚ فَسَوۡفَ يَعۡلَمُونَ

They return to their polytheism to continue showing ingratitude for Our favors upon them and continue enjoying the beauty of this worldly life. They will soon know the evil punishment that awaits them when they die.

(٦٧) أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّا جَعَلۡنَا حَرَمًا ءَامِنٗا وَيُتَخَطَّفُ ٱلنَّاسُ مِنۡ حَوۡلِهِمۡۚ أَفَبِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ يُؤۡمِنُونَ وَبِنِعۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِ يَكۡفُرُونَ

Do these people who reject the blessings of Allah upon them not see another blessing of His after He saves them from drowning, that He made a sanctuary for them in which their lives and wealth are secure? This is while those around them are raided, killed, looted, and taken captive together with their wives and children. Do they still believe in their false deities and reject the bounties of Allah, not showing Him gratitude for them?

(٦٨) وَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّنِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبًا أَوۡ كَذَّبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ لَمَّا جَآءَهُۥٓۚ أَلَيۡسَ فِي جَهَنَّمَ مَثۡوٗى لِّلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

No one commits more wrong than the one who fabricates a lie regarding Allah by attributing a partner to Him or rejecting the truth that His Messenger brought. Undoubtedly, there is an abode in Hell for the disbelievers and those like them.

(٦٩) وَٱلَّذِينَ جَٰهَدُواْ فِينَا لَنَهۡدِيَنَّهُمۡ سُبُلَنَاۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَمَعَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

And those who exert themselves to seek My pleasure, We will certainly grant them the ability to tread the straight path. Indeed, Allah is with those who do good by helping, assisting, and guiding them.

The Romans (Surah Ar-Rum)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Affirming the uniqueness of Allah in managing affairs and describing His divine established practice in creation.

Explanation:

(١) الٓمٓ

Alif Lām Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) غُلِبَتِ ٱلرُّومُ

The Persians have defeated the Romans.

(٣) فِيٓ أَدۡنَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ غَلَبِهِمۡ سَيَغۡلِبُونَ

In the nearer lands of the Levant extending to Persia. The Romans, after their defeat, will be victorious over them again.

(٤) فِي بِضۡعِ سِنِينَۗ لِلَّهِ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ مِن قَبۡلُ وَمِنۢ بَعۡدُۚ وَيَوۡمَئِذٖ يَفۡرَحُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ

Within a period of three to ten years. To Allah belongs the command before and after the Romans’ victory. And on the day the Romans defeat the Persians, the believers will be happy.

(٥) بِنَصۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ يَنصُرُ مَن يَشَآءُۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

They will be happy that Allah helps the Romans because they are people of the Book. Allah helps whomever He wishes against whomever He wishes; He is the Almighty who cannot be overcome, the Merciful towards His believing slaves.

Points of Benefit:

The polytheists turning to Allah in times of hardship, forgetting their idols, and associating partners with Him in times of prosperity is evidence of their confusion.
Striving in the path of Allah is a means to attaining success in the pursuit of truth.
The Quran's informing about matters that cannot be perceived (i.e., the unseen) is evidence that it is from Allah.
