(٦) وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِۖ لَا يُخۡلِفُ ٱللَّهُ وَعۡدَهُۥ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

This victory was a promise from Allah, and with its fulfillment, the believers will increase in their conviction of Allah's promise of victory being true. As for the majority of people, they do not understand this due to their disbelief.

(٧) يَعۡلَمُونَ ظَٰهِرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ غَٰفِلُونَ

They do not know faith and the rulings of the legislation; they only know of the superficial realities of this worldly life related to earning a livelihood, while they turn away from the real life of the Afterlife, not paying any attention to it.

(٨) أَوَ لَمۡ يَتَفَكَّرُواْ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۗ مَّا خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَأَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۗ وَإِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِلِقَآيِٕ رَبِّهِمۡ لَكَٰفِرُونَ

Do these rejecting polytheists not ponder in themselves as to how Allah has created them and arranged them together in their most complete forms? Allah did not create the heavens and the Earth except for a just cause; He did not create them in vain, and He appointed a fixed time for their existence in the worldly life. Indeed, many people reject the meeting of their Lord on the Day of Resurrection, and that is why they have not prepared for the resurrection by doing good deeds that are acceptable to their Lord.

(٩) أَوَ لَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَيَنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ كَانُوٓاْ أَشَدَّ مِنۡهُمۡ قُوَّةٗ وَأَثَارُواْ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَعَمَرُوهَآ أَكۡثَرَ مِمَّا عَمَرُوهَا وَجَآءَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِۖ فَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيَظۡلِمَهُمۡ وَلَٰكِن كَانُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَظۡلِمُونَ

Do these people not travel the earth so that they can ponder over what the outcome of the nations who rejected Allah before them was? Those nations were much stronger than them; they adapted the land for farming and construction and populated it much more than these people had. Their Messengers brought clear proof and evidence for the Oneness of Allah, but they were rejected. So when Allah punished them, He did not wrong them; instead, they wronged themselves by disbelieving and falling into destruction.

(١٠) ثُمَّ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسَٰٓـُٔواْ ٱلسُّوٓأَىٰٓ أَن كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَكَانُواْ بِهَا يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

Then, the outcome of those who did evil deeds, i.e., polytheism and sins, was utterly terrible. That was because they rejected the signs of Allah and would mock and ridicule them.

(١١) ٱللَّهُ يَبۡدَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَلۡقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُۥ ثُمَّ إِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

Allah creates the creation without any precedent, then He causes it to die, then will resurrect it. Then, to Him alone will you return to be taken to account for and recompensed on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٢) وَيَوۡمَ تَقُومُ ٱلسَّاعَةُ يُبۡلِسُ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ

On the Day when the hour arrives, the criminals shall despair from the mercy of Allah, and their hopes in Allah's mercy will end just as their excuses to disbelieve in Allah end.

(١٣) وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُم مِّن شُرَكَآئِهِمۡ شُفَعَٰٓؤُاْ وَكَانُواْ بِشُرَكَآئِهِمۡ كَٰفِرِينَ

They will not have those they ascribed as partners to Allah (and used to worship in the worldly life) to intercede for them to save them from the torment. Instead, they will reject those partners they ascribed to Allah. They will forsake them when they need them most because they are all equally doomed.

(١٤) وَيَوۡمَ تَقُومُ ٱلسَّاعَةُ يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَتَفَرَّقُونَ

And on the Day the hour arrives, the people will all be divided according to the recompense of their deeds in this world. Some will be elevated to the highest ranks, while others will be demoted to the lowest of the low.

(١٥) فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ فَهُمۡ فِي رَوۡضَةٖ يُحۡبَرُونَ

As for those who believed in Allah and did good deeds pleasing to Him, they will be in Paradise, overjoyed with whatever everlasting bounties they gain therein.

Points of Benefit:

Knowledge of what benefits this world, coupled with neglect of what benefits the Hereafter, is of no avail.
The signs of Allah in the souls and in the horizons are sufficient evidence of His oneness.
Oppression is a cause of the destruction of previous nations.
On the Day of Resurrection, Allah will elevate the believers and debase the disbelievers.
