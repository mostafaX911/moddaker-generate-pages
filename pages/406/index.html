(١٦) وَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَكَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَلِقَآيِٕ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ مُحۡضَرُونَ

As for those who disbelieved in Allah and rejected His signs that were revealed to His Messengers, the resurrection, and the Reckoning, they are the ones who will be in a state of constant punishment.

(١٧) فَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ حِينَ تُمۡسُونَ وَحِينَ تُصۡبِحُونَ

So exalt Allah when night enters, the time of the Maghrib and Isha prayers, and exalt Him when morning enters, the time of the Fajr prayer.

(١٨) وَلَهُ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَعَشِيّٗا وَحِينَ تُظۡهِرُونَ

To Allah (may He be Exalted) belongs all praise; the angels praise Him in the heavens, and His creation praise Him on Earth. Exalt Him when the evening enters, which is the time of the 'Asr prayer, and exalt Him at the time of Zuhr.

(١٩) يُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡحَيَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ وَيُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡمَيِّتَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَيِّ وَيُحۡيِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَاۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ تُخۡرَجُونَ

He brings out life from the dead, like His creation of a human from a drop of semen and a chick from an egg. He also brings out life from the living, like His extracting a drop of semen from a human and an egg from a chicken. He revives the earth after it has become dry by sending down rain and regrowing it. Likewise, you will be resurrected from your graves for the final reckoning and recompense.

(٢٠) وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَنۡ خَلَقَكُم مِّن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ إِذَآ أَنتُم بَشَرٞ تَنتَشِرُونَ

Among Allah's great signs, which prove His omnipotence and oneness, is that He created you, O people, from the Earth when He created your father from clay, and then you soon became many humans, increasing through reproduction and spreading over the Earth's east and west.

(٢١) وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَنۡ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا لِّتَسۡكُنُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيۡنَكُم مَّوَدَّةٗ وَرَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ

Among Allah's great signs, which demonstrate His omnipotence and oneness, is that He created for you, O men, wives from you so that you may find contentment in them due to your unique compatibility. He put love and affection between you. Indeed, the aforementioned are clear proofs and evidence for people who contemplate because they are the ones who benefit by using their intellect.

(٢٢) وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ خَلۡقُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱخۡتِلَٰفُ أَلۡسِنَتِكُمۡ وَأَلۡوَٰنِكُمۡۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّلۡعَٰلِمِينَ

Among Allah's great signs, which demonstrate His omnipotence and oneness, is that He created the heavens and the Earth and your different languages and skin colors. In that, there are proofs and evidence for the people of knowledge and insight.

(٢٣) وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ مَنَامُكُم بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ وَٱبۡتِغَآؤُكُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَسۡمَعُونَ

Among Allah's great signs, which demonstrate His omnipotence and oneness, is your sleeping by night and day so that you may rest from the toil of your work. And among His signs is that He made the day so that you may disperse, seeking sustenance from your Lord. Indeed, these are proofs and evidence for people who listen attentively and willingly.

(٢٤) وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ يُرِيكُمُ ٱلۡبَرۡقَ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعٗا وَيُنَزِّلُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَيُحۡيِۦ بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَآۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ

Among Allah's great signs, which demonstrate His omnipotence and oneness, is that He shows you lightning in the sky, combining within it fear of the thunder and hope of rain. He also sends down rainwater for you from the heavens and revives the earth by the regrowth of plants after it has dried up. Indeed, there are clear proofs and evidence in that for people of understanding so that they may compare that with the resurrection after death for the final reckoning and recompense.

Points of Benefit:

Structuring one's time with prayers and remembrance of Allah is a sign of a favorable outcome.
The evidence for resurrection lies in the renewal of life, as Allah creates the living from the dead and the dead from the living.
The signs of Allah in the souls and in the horizons benefit only those who utilize the sensory and intellectual means of perception that Allah has bestowed upon them.
