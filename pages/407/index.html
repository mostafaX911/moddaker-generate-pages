(٢٥) وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَن تَقُومَ ٱلسَّمَآءُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ بِأَمۡرِهِۦۚ ثُمَّ إِذَا دَعَاكُمۡ دَعۡوَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِذَآ أَنتُمۡ تَخۡرُجُونَ

Among Allah's signs, which prove His omnipotence and oneness, is that by His command, the sky stays up without falling, and the Earth does not collapse in ruins. Then, when He calls you from the Earth by the angel blowing the trumpet, you will suddenly emerge from your graves to give account and be recompensed.

(٢٦) وَلَهُۥ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ كُلّٞ لَّهُۥ قَٰنِتُونَ

And to Allah alone belongs whoever is in the heavens and the Earth; Allah created them, owns them, and has complete control over them. All His creation in the heavens and the Earth obey Him and comply with His command.

(٢٧) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يَبۡدَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَلۡقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُۥ وَهُوَ أَهۡوَنُ عَلَيۡهِۚ وَلَهُ ٱلۡمَثَلُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

He is the One who originates the creation without there being anything like it before. He resurrects it after it withers away. Bringing it back is easier than creating it the first time. However, both are easy for Him because whenever He intends something, He says, 'Be!' and it happens. He (the Mighty and Majestic) has the highest of praise in whatever qualities of majesty and perfection he is praised with. He is the Almighty who cannot be overcome; Wise in His creation and management.

(٢٨) ضَرَبَ لَكُم مَّثَلٗا مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡۖ هَل لَّكُم مِّن مَّا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُكُم مِّن شُرَكَآءَ فِي مَا رَزَقۡنَٰكُمۡ فَأَنتُمۡ فِيهِ سَوَآءٞ تَخَافُونَهُمۡ كَخِيفَتِكُمۡ أَنفُسَكُمۡۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نُفَصِّلُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ

O, polytheists, Allah gives you an example of yourselves: do you have partners from your slaves who are equal to you in everything you own, such that you fear them just as you fear each other? Would you be happy for yourselves with your slaves being such? Undoubtedly, you would not be pleased with such an arrangement, so Allah is more worthy that He does not have a partner from His creation in His kingdom. Similarly, Allah clearly explains different proofs and evidence for people of understanding because they are the ones who benefit from them.

(٢٩) بَلِ ٱتَّبَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوٓاْ أَهۡوَآءَهُم بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖۖ فَمَن يَهۡدِي مَنۡ أَضَلَّ ٱللَّهُۖ وَمَا لَهُم مِّن نَّٰصِرِينَ

Their deviance is not because of any deficiency in proofs or those not being explained; it is only because of following desires and their forefathers, So who is there that is capable of guiding those whom Allah has caused to deviate? No one can do that. They will have no helpers to keep the punishment away from them.

(٣٠) فَأَقِمۡ وَجۡهَكَ لِلدِّينِ حَنِيفٗاۚ فِطۡرَتَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّتِي فَطَرَ ٱلنَّاسَ عَلَيۡهَاۚ لَا تَبۡدِيلَ لِخَلۡقِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلۡقَيِّمُ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

O Messenger, you and those with you should turn your faces towards the true religion which Allah has directed you, the religion of Islam upon which Allah inherently created people, and turn away from all other religions. There is no changing the creation of Allah; it is the correct and upright religion in which there is no deviation, but most people do not know this.

(٣١) ۞مُنِيبِينَ إِلَيۡهِ وَٱتَّقُوهُ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَلَا تَكُونُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

And return to Him (may He be Exalted) by repenting from your sins, fear Him by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, perform prayer in the best way you can, and do not be from the polytheists who go against nature and ascribe partners with Allah by worshipping them.

(٣٢) مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ فَرَّقُواْ دِينَهُمۡ وَكَانُواْ شِيَعٗاۖ كُلُّ حِزۡبِۢ بِمَا لَدَيۡهِمۡ فَرِحُونَ

And do not be from those polytheists who changed their religion, believing in some of it and rejecting other parts of it. They split into groups and sects, each sect happy with whatever falsehood they were on, thinking that they alone were upon the truth and others were on falsehood.

Points of Benefit:

The submission of all creation to Allah, the Exalted, is both through compulsion and choice.
The clear features of the initial creation indicate the resurrection.
Following one's desires leads astray and dominates.
Islam is the religion of sound, innate nature.
