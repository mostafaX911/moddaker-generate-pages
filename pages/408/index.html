(٣٣) وَإِذَا مَسَّ ٱلنَّاسَ ضُرّٞ دَعَوۡاْ رَبَّهُم مُّنِيبِينَ إِلَيۡهِ ثُمَّ إِذَآ أَذَاقَهُم مِّنۡهُ رَحۡمَةً إِذَا فَرِيقٞ مِّنۡهُم بِرَبِّهِمۡ يُشۡرِكُونَ

When the polytheists are afflicted by a difficulty, i.e., poverty or drought, they supplicate only to their Lord (may He be Exalted), returning to Him, humbling themselves, and seeking refuge in Him to remove the affliction from them. Then, when He shows them mercy by removing whatever afflicted them, they return to their ways of idolatry by supplicating to others along with Him.

(٣٤) لِيَكۡفُرُواْ بِمَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمۡۚ فَتَمَتَّعُواْ فَسَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ

They show their ungratefulness for the favors of Allah, and from amongst them is removing their difficulty. So enjoy your ease in this life; you will surely come to know on the Day of Resurrection that you were in clear misguidance.

(٣٥) أَمۡ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ سُلۡطَٰنٗا فَهُوَ يَتَكَلَّمُ بِمَا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يُشۡرِكُونَ

What made them ascribe partners with Allah without evidence supporting their claim? We did not send any evidence to them in the form of any scripture that they could use as proof for their ascribing partners with Allah, nor do they have any scripture in favor of their idolatry that upholds the validity of the disbelief they are on.

(٣٦) وَإِذَآ أَذَقۡنَا ٱلنَّاسَ رَحۡمَةٗ فَرِحُواْ بِهَاۖ وَإِن تُصِبۡهُمۡ سَيِّئَةُۢ بِمَا قَدَّمَتۡ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ إِذَا هُمۡ يَقۡنَطُونَ

And when We give the people a taste of some of Our bounties, i.e., health and wealth, they become happy with it proudly and arrogantly. And if some harm reaches them, i.e., illness or poverty due to their sins, they begin losing hope in the mercy of Allah. They despair from the difficulty ever leaving them.

(٣٧) أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَقۡدِرُۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Do they not see that Allah increases the sustenance for whichever of His slaves He wishes, testing them to see whether they will show gratitude or ingratitude? He restricts sustenance upon whomsoever He wishes, to see whether they are patient or become angry. Indeed, the increasing provision for some and restricting it for others are proofs of the kindness and mercy of Allah.

(٣٨) فَ‍َٔاتِ ذَا ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ حَقَّهُۥ وَٱلۡمِسۡكِينَ وَٱبۡنَ ٱلسَّبِيلِۚ ذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرٞ لِّلَّذِينَ يُرِيدُونَ وَجۡهَ ٱللَّهِۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ

O Muslim, give the relative whatever goodness and kinship he deserves, give the needy whatever he can fulfill his need with, and help the stranger whose path back home has been cut off. Giving in these causes is better for those who seek the pleasure of Allah. Those who give priority to this kind of assistance and rights are the ones who succeed by achieving their objective of Paradise and being granted safety from the feared punishment.

After mentioning what was meant by means of getting closer to Allah through work, Allah mentions what is meant by doing things for other than His sake, referring to petty, worldly gain, stating:

(٣٩) وَمَآ ءَاتَيۡتُم مِّن رِّبٗا لِّيَرۡبُوَاْ فِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِ ٱلنَّاسِ فَلَا يَرۡبُواْ عِندَ ٱللَّهِۖ وَمَآ ءَاتَيۡتُم مِّن زَكَوٰةٖ تُرِيدُونَ وَجۡهَ ٱللَّهِ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُضۡعِفُونَ

Whatever wealth you give to anyone with the desire that they will return it to you with more, the reward of that wealth does not grow with Allah. And whatever wealth you provide to fulfill someone's need, desiring only the pleasure of Allah and not praise or reward from the people, the reward of that deed shall be multiplied for you by Allah.

(٤٠) ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُمۡ ثُمَّ رَزَقَكُمۡ ثُمَّ يُمِيتُكُمۡ ثُمَّ يُحۡيِيكُمۡۖ هَلۡ مِن شُرَكَآئِكُم مَّن يَفۡعَلُ مِن ذَٰلِكُم مِّن شَيۡءٖۚ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥ وَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

Allah alone created you, granted you sustenance, will cause you to die, and will revive you for the resurrection. Are there any of your idols you worship that can do any of that? He is far removed and free of what the polytheists say and believe.

(٤١) ظَهَرَ ٱلۡفَسَادُ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِي ٱلنَّاسِ لِيُذِيقَهُم بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي عَمِلُواْ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ

Corruption has appeared in the land and sea, such as drought, poor rainfall, increased illness, and disease due to the sins they commit. It has appeared so that Allah may give them a taste of some of their evil deeds in the worldly life so that they may repent to Him.

Points of Benefit:

Arrogance in times of blessings and despair in adversity are two characteristics of the disbelievers.
Granting rights to their rightful owners is a cause for success.
Prohibition of usury and the multiplication of rewards for spending in the path of Allah.
The impact of sins in the spread of pandemics and the destruction of the environment is evident.
