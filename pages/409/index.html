(٤٢) قُلۡ سِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَٱنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلُۚ كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّشۡرِكِينَ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: Travel the earth and ponder the outcome of the nations that denied [tawheed] before you. Their outcome was evil; most would ascribe partners with Allah, worshipping others with Him, and because of that, they were destroyed.

(٤٣) فَأَقِمۡ وَجۡهَكَ لِلدِّينِ ٱلۡقَيِّمِ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَ يَوۡمٞ لَّا مَرَدَّ لَهُۥ مِنَ ٱللَّهِۖ يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَصَّدَّعُونَ

O Messenger, turn to the correct religion of Islam, in which there is no deviation before the Day of Resurrection comes. When it comes, there will be no one to prevent it, and on that day, people will be divided: one group shall be blessed in Paradise, while the other shall be punished in the Fire.

(٤٤) مَن كَفَرَ فَعَلَيۡهِ كُفۡرُهُۥۖ وَمَنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَلِأَنفُسِهِمۡ يَمۡهَدُونَ

Whoever disbelieves in Allah will suffer the consequences of his disbelief; an eternity in the Fire. Those who do good deeds seeking Allah's pleasure through them will enter into Paradise and derive enjoyment from whatever is within it, and they shall live in it forever.

(٤٥) لِيَجۡزِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Allah will separate the people and divide them into two groups so that He can reward those who have faith in Allah and perform good deeds that He is pleased with. Indeed, He does not love those who disbelieve in Him and His Messengers. Rather, He hates them severely and will punish them on the Day of Resurrection.

(٤٦) وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَن يُرۡسِلَ ٱلرِّيَاحَ مُبَشِّرَٰتٖ وَلِيُذِيقَكُم مِّن رَّحۡمَتِهِۦ وَلِتَجۡرِيَ ٱلۡفُلۡكُ بِأَمۡرِهِۦ وَلِتَبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

And among Allah's great signs that demonstrate His omnipotence and oneness is that He sends winds that give glad tidings of imminent rain to the slaves, and so that He may give you, O people, a taste of His mercy by the greenery and ease that follows the rainfall. And also so that the ships can sail the sea by His will and you can seek from His grace by trading across the ocean. Perhaps you will show gratitude for the favors of Allah so He may give you more of them.

(٤٧) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ رُسُلًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِمۡ فَجَآءُوهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ فَٱنتَقَمۡنَا مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَجۡرَمُواْۖ وَكَانَ حَقًّا عَلَيۡنَا نَصۡرُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

O Messenger, verily, I have sent many messengers to their people before you, and they brought evidence proving their truthfulness. However, the people rejected whatever their messengers brought. Hence, we took retribution from those who committed evil and destroyed them with Our punishment, saving the messengers and those who believed in them from destruction. Rescuing the believers and helping them is a right that We have obligated upon Ourselves.

(٤٨) ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي يُرۡسِلُ ٱلرِّيَٰحَ فَتُثِيرُ سَحَابٗا فَيَبۡسُطُهُۥ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ كَيۡفَ يَشَآءُ وَيَجۡعَلُهُۥ كِسَفٗا فَتَرَى ٱلۡوَدۡقَ يَخۡرُجُ مِنۡ خِلَٰلِهِۦۖ فَإِذَآ أَصَابَ بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦٓ إِذَا هُمۡ يَسۡتَبۡشِرُونَ

Allah (may He be Exalted) is the One who sends and drives the winds. Those winds form and move the clouds. He extends those clouds, whether many or few, across the sky as he wills and breaks them up. O observer, you can see the rain falling from those clouds. Then, when it rains on whoever He will from His slaves, they are happy with Allah's mercy upon them by sending down rain that brings forth vegetation from the earth, which they and their animals need.

(٤٩) وَإِن كَانُواْ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يُنَزَّلَ عَلَيۡهِم مِّن قَبۡلِهِۦ لَمُبۡلِسِينَ

Previously, they had lost all hope in any rain falling upon them.

(٥٠) فَٱنظُرۡ إِلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِ رَحۡمَتِ ٱللَّهِ كَيۡفَ يُحۡيِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَآۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ لَمُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰۖ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

O Messenger, look at the effects of the rainfall that Allah sends out of mercy for His slaves; how Allah revives the earth with the various plants that grow after it has dried up and become barren. Indeed, He who restored that dry earth is the One who will resurrect the dead back to life. He is Omnipotent over everything; nothing is beyond His capability.

Points of Benefit:

Sending the winds, raining down the water, and the sailing of ships in the sea: blessings that call for expressing gratitude to Allah.
The destruction of the criminals and the triumph of the believers is an established divine practice.
The growth of the earth after its dryness is evidence of the resurrection.
