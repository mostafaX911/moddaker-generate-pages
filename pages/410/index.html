(٥١) وَلَئِنۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا رِيحٗا فَرَأَوۡهُ مُصۡفَرّٗا لَّظَلُّواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ يَكۡفُرُونَ

If We were to send upon their farms and crops a wind which would spoil them, and they would see their crops turn a yellow color after they had previously been green, they would then be ungrateful for the numerous other bounties of Allah they enjoyed the time before that.

(٥٢) فَإِنَّكَ لَا تُسۡمِعُ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَلَا تُسۡمِعُ ٱلصُّمَّ ٱلدُّعَآءَ إِذَا وَلَّوۡاْ مُدۡبِرِينَ

So just as you cannot make the dead and deaf hear, in the same way, after they have distanced themselves from you to make sure they cannot hear you, you cannot guide those similar to them (the dead and the deaf) if they turn away and do not benefit.

(٥٣) وَمَآ أَنتَ بِهَٰدِ ٱلۡعُمۡيِ عَن ضَلَٰلَتِهِمۡۖ إِن تُسۡمِعُ إِلَّا مَن يُؤۡمِنُ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا فَهُم مُّسۡلِمُونَ

You cannot guide those who have deviated from the straight path onto taking the right path. Neither can you make anyone hear in a way that will benefit them, except those who believe in Our signs, because only they are the ones who will benefit from whatever you say and are submissive and compliant to Our command.

(٥٤) ۞ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن ضَعۡفٖ ثُمَّ جَعَلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ضَعۡفٖ قُوَّةٗ ثُمَّ جَعَلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ قُوَّةٖ ضَعۡفٗا وَشَيۡبَةٗۚ يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡقَدِيرُ

O people, Allah is the one who created you from a despicable fluid. He gave you the strength of youth after the weakness of childhood, then the weakness of old age after the strength of youth. Allah creates whatever He wishes of strength and weakness; He is the Omniscient of everything; nothing is hidden from Him. [He is] Omnipotent; nothing is beyond His capability.

(٥٥) وَيَوۡمَ تَقُومُ ٱلسَّاعَةُ يُقۡسِمُ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ مَا لَبِثُواْ غَيۡرَ سَاعَةٖۚ كَذَٰلِكَ كَانُواْ يُؤۡفَكُونَ

When the Day of Resurrection is established, the criminals will swear that they have not been in their graves except for a very short period. Likewise, they were deluded in the worldly life by denying the truth.

(٥٦) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ وَٱلۡإِيمَٰنَ لَقَدۡ لَبِثۡتُمۡ فِي كِتَٰبِ ٱللَّهِ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡبَعۡثِۖ فَهَٰذَا يَوۡمُ ٱلۡبَعۡثِ وَلَٰكِنَّكُمۡ كُنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

Those prophets and angels to whom Allah has granted knowledge will say: You remained for as long as Allah decreed according to His infinite knowledge, until the day of your resurrection, which you would deny, but you did not know that the resurrection was a reality, and so you rejected it.

(٥٧) فَيَوۡمَئِذٖ لَّا يَنفَعُ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ مَعۡذِرَتُهُمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يُسۡتَعۡتَبُونَ

So on the day when Allah shall resurrect all of creation for the reckoning and recompense, all the excuses the wrongdoers make will not benefit them. They will not be asked to please Allah by repenting because the time has long expired.

(٥٨) وَلَقَدۡ ضَرَبۡنَا لِلنَّاسِ فِي هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مِن كُلِّ مَثَلٖۚ وَلَئِن جِئۡتَهُم بِ‍َٔايَةٖ لَّيَقُولَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِنۡ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا مُبۡطِلُونَ

In this Quran, We have mentioned every type of parable for the people so that the truth becomes clearly distinguished to them from falsehood. O Messenger, if you were to bring clear proof of your truthfulness to them, the disbelievers in Allah would say: you are nothing but fabricators of what you brought.

(٥٩) كَذَٰلِكَ يَطۡبَعُ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

Allah seals the hearts of those who purposely reject the signs you bring them. Likewise, Allah seals the hearts of those who do not know that what you brought is the truth.

(٦٠) فَٱصۡبِرۡ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقّٞۖ وَلَا يَسۡتَخِفَّنَّكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُوقِنُونَ

So be patient, O Messenger, with the rejection you face from your people. Indeed, Allah's promise to you of help and empowerment remains. Let not those who do not believe they will be resurrected make you become hasty and impatient.

Points of Benefit:

The disbelievers' despair in the mercy of Allah arises when affliction descends.
Guidance and success are in Allah's Hand, not in the hands of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم.
The stages of life serve as a lesson for those who reflect.
The sealing of hearts is a result of sins.
