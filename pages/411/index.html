Luqman (Surah Luqman)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

The command to follow the wisdom outlined in the Quran and the warning against turning away from it.

Explanation:

(١) الٓمٓ

Alif Lām Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ

O Messenger, these verses revealed to you are verses of the Book that speaks wisdom.

(٣) هُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٗ لِّلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

It is guidance and mercy for those who do good by fulfilling the rights of their Lord and the rights of His slaves.

(٤) ٱلَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَهُم بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ يُوقِنُونَ

Those who perform prayer as best they can and give Zakat of their wealth while being certain of the resurrection, account, and reward in the Afterlife.

(٥) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ عَلَىٰ هُدٗى مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ

Those characterized as such are upon guidance from their Lord, and it is they who are successful by gaining whatever they seek and being distanced from what causes them fear.

After mentioning the traits of those who strive toward excellence, He mentions the traits of the evildoers, stating:

(٦) وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَشۡتَرِي لَهۡوَ ٱلۡحَدِيثِ لِيُضِلَّ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖ وَيَتَّخِذَهَا هُزُوًاۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ مُّهِينٞ

Some people, like An-Nadr Muhammed bin Al-Harith, tell stories to distract people from Allah's religion with ignorance and take Allah's words as a joke, mocking them. Those who possess these characteristics will have a humiliating torment awaiting them in the Afterlife.

(٧) وَإِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَٰتُنَا وَلَّىٰ مُسۡتَكۡبِرٗا كَأَن لَّمۡ يَسۡمَعۡهَا كَأَنَّ فِيٓ أُذُنَيۡهِ وَقۡرٗاۖ فَبَشِّرۡهُ بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ

When you read Our verses to him, he turns away in arrogance, as if he has not heard them, as if his ears are oblivious to sounds. So, O Messenger, warn him of a painful punishment that awaits him.

(٨) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُمۡ جَنَّٰتُ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

Indeed, those who believe and do good deeds will have gardens of Paradise, where they shall live in luxury among everything Allah has prepared for them.

(٩) خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۖ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقّٗاۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

They will remain therein [forever]. Allah has made them a true promise of which there is no doubt. He is the Almighty whom no one can overcome; the Wise in His creation, decree, and legislation.

(١٠) خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ بِغَيۡرِ عَمَدٖ تَرَوۡنَهَاۖ وَأَلۡقَىٰ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَوَٰسِيَ أَن تَمِيدَ بِكُمۡ وَبَثَّ فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ دَآبَّةٖۚ وَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوۡجٖ كَرِيمٍ

Allah (may He be Exalted) created the heavens, which are raised without any pillars, and pitched mountains on the Earth to hold it firmly in place so that it does not shake in convulsion. He dispersed animals throughout the Earth and sent down rainfall from the sky, causing the Earth to grow delightful plants, from which both humans and animals benefit.

(١١) هَٰذَا خَلۡقُ ٱللَّهِ فَأَرُونِي مَاذَا خَلَقَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن دُونِهِۦۚ بَلِ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ

O, polytheists, these all are from Allah's creation, so show Me what those you worship other than Allah have created. Instead, the wrongdoers are in apparent deviance from the truth because they attribute partners to Allah that cannot make anything but are themselves created.

Points of Benefit:

Obedience to Allah leads to success in this world and the Hereafter.
Prohibition of anything that hinders from the straight path, whether in speech or action.
Arrogance prevents one from following the truth.
Allah alone creates and challenges the disbelievers to let their gods create anything.
