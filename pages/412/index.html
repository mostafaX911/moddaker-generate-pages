(١٢) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا لُقۡمَٰنَ ٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ أَنِ ٱشۡكُرۡ لِلَّهِۚ وَمَن يَشۡكُرۡ فَإِنَّمَا يَشۡكُرُ لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَنِيٌّ حَمِيدٞ

Verily, I granted Luqman the understanding of religion and correctness in matters, and I told him: O Luqman, be grateful to your Lord for the blessing He has bestowed upon you of being able to obey Him. Whoever is thankful to his Lord, his gratefulness benefits only himself, for Allah is not dependent upon it. Whoever rejects His blessings, the harm of his rejection afflicts him alone, for his rejection does not hurt Allah in the slightest. Allah is independent of His creation and praiseworthy at all times.

(١٣) وَإِذۡ قَالَ لُقۡمَٰنُ لِٱبۡنِهِۦ وَهُوَ يَعِظُهُۥ يَٰبُنَيَّ لَا تُشۡرِكۡ بِٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّ ٱلشِّرۡكَ لَظُلۡمٌ عَظِيمٞ

O Messenger, remember when Luqman told his son while encouraging him to do good and warning him from evil: O my son, do not worship any of the creation along with Allah. Indeed, worshiping a deity besides Allah is a grave injustice to the self and is a cause for permanently staying in Hell.

(١٤) وَوَصَّيۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ حَمَلَتۡهُ أُمُّهُۥ وَهۡنًا عَلَىٰ وَهۡنٖ وَفِصَٰلُهُۥ فِي عَامَيۡنِ أَنِ ٱشۡكُرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيۡكَ إِلَيَّ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

And We commanded man to obey his parents and do good towards them in matters that do not lead to the disobedience of Allah. His mother carried him in her womb, facing difficulty upon difficulty, weaning him off breastfeeding within two years. We told him: Be grateful to Allah for every blessing He has bestowed upon you, then be thankful to your parents for their care and upbringing. To Me alone is your return, upon which I shall recompense each person according to what he deserves.

(١٥) وَإِن جَٰهَدَاكَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن تُشۡرِكَ بِي مَا لَيۡسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمٞ فَلَا تُطِعۡهُمَاۖ وَصَاحِبۡهُمَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا مَعۡرُوفٗاۖ وَٱتَّبِعۡ سَبِيلَ مَنۡ أَنَابَ إِلَيَّۚ ثُمَّ إِلَيَّ مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ فَأُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

And even if your parents strive to incite you to ascribe partners to Allah according to their ignorant/arbitrary understanding, like what the mother of Sa'd bin Abī Waqqās did, then do not obey them in that because there is no obedience to the creation in the disobedience to the Creator. Instead, live with them in this world kindly, maintaining ties and friendship. And follow the path of those who proclaim My oneness repeatedly and obey Me. To Me alone is your return on the Day of Resurrection so that I may tell you of the actions you used to do in the world and requite you for them.

(١٦) يَٰبُنَيَّ إِنَّهَآ إِن تَكُ مِثۡقَالَ حَبَّةٖ مِّنۡ خَرۡدَلٖ فَتَكُن فِي صَخۡرَةٍ أَوۡ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ أَوۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَأۡتِ بِهَا ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَطِيفٌ خَبِيرٞ

O my son, indeed, evil and good, no matter how small, even if equal to the weight of a mustard seed and in the middle of a rock unbeknown to anyone or any place in the heavens or Earth, Allah will make it known on the Day of Resurrection and recompense the slave. Allah is the Most Subtle, whose Knowledge encompasses all secret and hidden matters. He is Kind to His slaves and Aware of everything.

(١٧) يَٰبُنَيَّ أَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأۡمُرۡ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَٱنۡهَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَآ أَصَابَكَۖ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ

O my son, establish the prayer best you can, enjoin good and forbid evil, and bear patience upon whatever adversity you face. Indeed, my instructions to you are from those Allah has decided you should do, so you have no choice in doing or not doing them.

(١٨) وَلَا تُصَعِّرۡ خَدَّكَ لِلنَّاسِ وَلَا تَمۡشِ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَرَحًاۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخۡتَالٖ فَخُورٖ

Do not turn your face away from the people in arrogance, and do not walk around with pride, amazed with yourself. Indeed, Allah does not like those who walk about arrogantly or are conceited in whatever blessings they have and do not thank Allah for them.

(١٩) وَٱقۡصِدۡ فِي مَشۡيِكَ وَٱغۡضُضۡ مِن صَوۡتِكَۚ إِنَّ أَنكَرَ ٱلۡأَصۡوَٰتِ لَصَوۡتُ ٱلۡحَمِيرِ

Be moderate in walking: not too haste or overly slow, and show dignity. And lower your voice; do not raise it so it annoys others. Indeed, the most hideous of sounds is the braying of a donkey because of how loud it is.

Points of Benefit:

When Allah, the Transcendent, details the hardships of pregnancy and childbirth, it is an indication of His increased blessing upon the mother.
The benefits of obedience and the harm of disobedience are consequences for the servant.
The obligation of children to be committed to upbringing and education.
The comprehensiveness of manners in Islam for individual and collective behavior.
