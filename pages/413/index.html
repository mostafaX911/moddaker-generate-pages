(٢٠) أَلَمۡ تَرَوۡاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَخَّرَ لَكُم مَّا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَأَسۡبَغَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ نِعَمَهُۥ ظَٰهِرَةٗ وَبَاطِنَةٗۗ وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يُجَٰدِلُ فِي ٱللَّهِ بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖ وَلَا هُدٗى وَلَا كِتَٰبٖ مُّنِيرٖ

O people, do you not witness that Allah has made it easy for you to benefit from whatever is in the heavens, such as the sun, moon, and stars? He has also made it easy for you to take advantage of the Earth's livestock, trees, and plants. He has completed His blessings upon you that you see, like a beautiful appearance and pleasant form, and those you do not see, like intelligence and knowledge. Despite all these blessings, some people dispute the Oneness of Allah without having any revealed knowledge from Allah or insightful intellect, nor any book revealed from Allah that clarifies [what they say].

(٢١) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱتَّبِعُواْ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ قَالُواْ بَلۡ نَتَّبِعُ مَا وَجَدۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ ءَابَآءَنَآۚ أَوَلَوۡ كَانَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ يَدۡعُوهُمۡ إِلَىٰ عَذَابِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

When it is said to these people who dispute the Oneness of Allah: Follow the revelation Allah has revealed to His Messenger. They say: We will not follow it; instead, we will follow what we found our forefathers doing and worship our gods. Will they follow their forefathers even though the Devil calls them towards the punishment of a blazing fire on the Day of Resurrection by misguiding them to the worship of idols?

(٢٢) ۞وَمَن يُسۡلِمۡ وَجۡهَهُۥٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَهُوَ مُحۡسِنٞ فَقَدِ ٱسۡتَمۡسَكَ بِٱلۡعُرۡوَةِ ٱلۡوُثۡقَىٰۗ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡأُمُورِ

Whoever turns to Allah sincerely and devotes worship to Him by doing good deeds has held on to the firmest thing that those who hope for salvation can hold on to. He will not fear that whatever he has held on to will be cut off. To Allah alone return all affairs; He will recompense each person as they deserve.

(٢٣) وَمَن كَفَرَ فَلَا يَحۡزُنكَ كُفۡرُهُۥٓۚ إِلَيۡنَا مَرۡجِعُهُمۡ فَنُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا عَمِلُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

O Messenger, whoever disbelieves in Allah, let not their disbelief grieve you. To Us alone, they will return on the Day of Resurrection. We will inform them of the evils they committed and recompense them for their bad deeds. Indeed, Allah knows what is in the hearts; nothing is hidden from Him.

(٢٤) نُمَتِّعُهُمۡ قَلِيلٗا ثُمَّ نَضۡطَرُّهُمۡ إِلَىٰ عَذَابٍ غَلِيظٖ

We will let them enjoy whatever pleasures We have given them in this world for a short while, then compel them to a severe torment on the Day of Resurrection: the punishment of the Fire.

(٢٥) وَلَئِن سَأَلۡتَهُم مَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ لَيَقُولُنَّ ٱللَّهُۚ قُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِۚ بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

O Messenger, if you ask these polytheists: Who created the heavens and the Earth? They will say: Allah created them. Say to them: All praise is for Allah, who has made clear the evidence against you. However, most do not know who deserves all praise because of their ignorance.

(٢٦) لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ

To Allah alone belongs everything in the heavens and on Earth, in terms of being His creation, under His ownership and control. Allah is self-sufficient, not needing his creation, and praiseworthy in this world and the Hereafter.

(٢٧) وَلَوۡ أَنَّمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِن شَجَرَةٍ أَقۡلَٰمٞ وَٱلۡبَحۡرُ يَمُدُّهُۥ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ سَبۡعَةُ أَبۡحُرٖ مَّا نَفِدَتۡ كَلِمَٰتُ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٞ

If all the trees on Earth were to be cut and made into pens, and the seas turned into ink seven times over, the Words of Allah would not be depleted due to their infinite amount. Indeed, Allah is the Almighty whom none can overcome; the Wise in His creation and management.

(٢٨) مَّا خَلۡقُكُمۡ وَلَا بَعۡثُكُمۡ إِلَّا كَنَفۡسٖ وَٰحِدَةٍۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعُۢ بَصِيرٌ

O people, your creation and resurrection for full accountability and recompense on the Day of Resurrection are as easy as creating and resurrecting just one soul. Indeed, Allah hears everything; hearing one sound does not preoccupy Him from hearing another; He also sees everything; seeing one thing does not preoccupy Him from seeing another either. Likewise, creating something and resurrecting it does not preoccupy Him with creating and resurrecting another.

Points of Benefit:

The blessings of Allah are a means to express gratitude and belief in Him, not a means to disbelieve in Him.
The danger of blind-following, especially in matters of belief.
The importance of surrendering to Allah, submitting to Him, and striving for excellence in actions for His pleasure.
The inexhaustibility of the words of Allah.
