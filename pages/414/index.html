(٢٩) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُولِجُ ٱلَّيۡلَ فِي ٱلنَّهَارِ وَيُولِجُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فِي ٱلَّيۡلِ وَسَخَّرَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَۖ كُلّٞ يَجۡرِيٓ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗى وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٞ

Do you not see that Allah shortens the night to lengthen the day and then shortens the day to lengthen the night? He also set the orbits of the sun and the moon so each move in its orbit for a fixed time. Do you also not see that Allah is aware of everything you do; none of your actions are hidden from Him, and He will recompense you for them?

(٣٠) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَأَنَّ مَا يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِ ٱلۡبَٰطِلُ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ

That control and careful measure bear testimony that Allah alone is the truth. He is true in His being, attributes, and actions. It also bears testimony to everything that the polytheists worship, being unfounded falsehood and that Allah is the Exalted in His being and His attributes over all His creation; there is none higher than Him, and He is more significant than everything.

(٣١) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱلۡفُلۡكَ تَجۡرِي فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِنِعۡمَتِ ٱللَّهِ لِيُرِيَكُم مِّنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّكُلِّ صَبَّارٖ شَكُورٖ

O people, do you not see the ships that sail the sea due to His Kindness and Facilitation so that He can show you His signs that prove His Power (may He be Exalted) and Kindness? Indeed, in that are proofs of His Power for every person who is patient upon harms that afflict him and grateful for whatever blessings he receives.

(٣٢) وَإِذَا غَشِيَهُم مَّوۡجٞ كَٱلظُّلَلِ دَعَوُاْ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ فَلَمَّا نَجَّىٰهُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡبَرِّ فَمِنۡهُم مُّقۡتَصِدٞۚ وَمَا يَجۡحَدُ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآ إِلَّا كُلُّ خَتَّارٖ كَفُورٖ

And when a wave the size of mountains and dark clouds surround them from all sides, they call out to Allah, sincerely supplicating and worshipping Him. Then, when Allah accepts their supplication and saves them from drowning, bringing them back to land, some of them are moderate in their praise, falling short of showing due gratitude to Him, while others reject the blessing of Allah. None reject Our signs except every deceitful person ungrateful for Allah's favors upon him, not thanking His Lord for what he has blessed him with, just like this one who promised to be grateful if Allah saved him.

(٣٣) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ وَٱخۡشَوۡاْ يَوۡمٗا لَّا يَجۡزِي وَالِدٌ عَن وَلَدِهِۦ وَلَا مَوۡلُودٌ هُوَ جَازٍ عَن وَالِدِهِۦ شَيۡ‍ًٔاۚ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقّٞۖ فَلَا تَغُرَّنَّكُمُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَلَا يَغُرَّنَّكُم بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡغَرُورُ

O people, be mindful of your Lord by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. Fear His punishment on the day when no father will be able to benefit his child, nor a child benefit its father in the slightest. Indeed, the promise of Allah of requital on the Day of Resurrection is true and will inevitably be fulfilled, so let not the worldly life and whatever desires and amusements it contains deceive you, nor let the Devil deceive you by misunderstanding the forbearance of Allah and His delaying the punishment from you.

(٣٤) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عِندَهُۥ عِلۡمُ ٱلسَّاعَةِ وَيُنَزِّلُ ٱلۡغَيۡثَ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡحَامِۖ وَمَا تَدۡرِي نَفۡسٞ مَّاذَا تَكۡسِبُ غَدٗاۖ وَمَا تَدۡرِي نَفۡسُۢ بِأَيِّ أَرۡضٖ تَمُوتُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمٌ خَبِيرُۢ

Indeed, Allah alone knows of the [last] Hour: He knows when it will occur; He sends the rain whenever He wishes, and He knows what is in the wombs: male or female, successful or wretched. No soul knows what good or bad it will do the next day or what land it will die in. Instead, Allah is the One who knows all of that; He is Knower and Aware of all that; nothing is hidden from Him.

Points of Benefit:

The decrease and increase of night and day, the subjugation of the sun and the moon: signs indicative of the transcendent power of Allah, deserving of gratitude for His blessings.
Patience and gratitude are two means for reflecting upon the signs of Allah.
Fear of the Day of Resurrection protects against being deluded by the worldly life and succumbing to the whispers of the devils.
Allah's complete knowledge encompasses the entirety of the unseen.
