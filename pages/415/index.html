The Prostration (Surah As-Sajdah)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Clarification of the reality of creation and the conditions of humanity in this world and the Hereafter.

Explanation:

(١) الٓمٓ

Alif Lām Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهِ مِن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

This Quran that Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم has brought was revealed to him by the Lord of all the worlds. There is no doubt in it.

(٣) أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ ٱفۡتَرَىٰهُۚ بَلۡ هُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكَ لِتُنذِرَ قَوۡمٗا مَّآ أَتَىٰهُم مِّن نَّذِيرٖ مِّن قَبۡلِكَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَهۡتَدُونَ

These disbelievers say: Indeed, Muhammad has fabricated it and attributed it to his Lord. It is not as they say. Instead, it is the truth in which there is no doubt, revealed from your Lord to you, O Messenger, so that you may warn a nation, to whom no Messenger has come before you, of the punishment of Allah, so that they may be guided to the truth, and follow and practice it.

(٤) ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ مَا لَكُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ مِن وَلِيّٖ وَلَا شَفِيعٍۚ أَفَلَا تَتَذَكَّرُونَ

Allah is the One who created the heavens and the Earth and created everything in between them in six days, while He is capable of making them in less than the time it takes to bat an eyelid. He then rose over His throne in a manner that befits His Majesty. O people, you do not have a guardian who cares for your affairs other than Him or an intercessor who intercedes for you in front of your Lord. Do you not think to worship Allah, who created you, and not to worship others besides Him?

(٥) يُدَبِّرُ ٱلۡأَمۡرَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ثُمَّ يَعۡرُجُ إِلَيۡهِ فِي يَوۡمٖ كَانَ مِقۡدَارُهُۥٓ أَلۡفَ سَنَةٖ مِّمَّا تَعُدُّونَ

Allah, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, disposes of the affair of all creation in the heavens and the Earth. That affair then ascends to Him in a day, the duration of which is like a thousand of your years in this world.

(٦) ذَٰلِكَ عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

The One who organizes all that is aware of everything present and absent; none of it is hidden from Him. He is the Almighty whom none can overcome; who takes retribution from his enemies and is Merciful towards His believing slaves.

(٧) ٱلَّذِيٓ أَحۡسَنَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقَهُۥۖ وَبَدَأَ خَلۡقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ مِن طِينٖ

He is the One who perfected everything He created and created man from clay without any precedent.

(٨) ثُمَّ جَعَلَ نَسۡلَهُۥ مِن سُلَٰلَةٖ مِّن مَّآءٖ مَّهِينٖ

He then created his progeny after him from a delicate fluid: semen.

(٩) ثُمَّ سَوَّىٰهُ وَنَفَخَ فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِهِۦۖ وَجَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَٱلۡأَفۡ‍ِٔدَةَۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَشۡكُرُونَ

Then He completed the creation of man in his complete form, then blew His soul [that He created for him] into him by commanding the angel appointed with blowing the soul, and made for you, O people, ears so that you could hear with them, eyes so that you could see, and hearts so that you could understand. Seldom are you grateful for Allah's bounties, which He bestows upon you.

(١٠) وَقَالُوٓاْ أَءِذَا ضَلَلۡنَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَءِنَّا لَفِي خَلۡقٖ جَدِيدِۢۚ بَلۡ هُم بِلِقَآءِ رَبِّهِمۡ كَٰفِرُونَ

The polytheists who reject the resurrection say: When we die, and our bodies become dust and disappear into the earth, will we be resurrected back to life? That is inconceivable. Instead, they reject the resurrection and do not believe in it.

(١١) ۞قُلۡ يَتَوَفَّىٰكُم مَّلَكُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ ٱلَّذِي وُكِّلَ بِكُمۡ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُمۡ تُرۡجَعُونَ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists who reject the resurrection: The angel of death whom Allah has appointed to seize your souls shall do so, then to Us alone you will return on the Day of Resurrection to give account and to be recompensed.

Points of Benefit:

The wisdom behind sending the messengers is to guide their people to the straight path.
Affirmation of the attribute of istiwā (ascension) for Allah without likening or resemblance.
The dismissal of resurrection by the polytheists despite clear evidence attesting to it.
