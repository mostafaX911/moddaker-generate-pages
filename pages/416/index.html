(١٢) وَلَوۡ تَرَىٰٓ إِذِ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ نَاكِسُواْ رُءُوسِهِمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ رَبَّنَآ أَبۡصَرۡنَا وَسَمِعۡنَا فَٱرۡجِعۡنَا نَعۡمَلۡ صَٰلِحًا إِنَّا مُوقِنُونَ

The criminals will soon emerge on the Day of Resurrection, bowing their heads in humiliation due to their rejection of the resurrection, feeling ashamed, and saying: Our Lord! We have seen the resurrection that we used to reject. Now we hear the confirmation of that which the Messengers brought from You. Therefore, return us to the worldly life so we can do good deeds that will make You pleased with us. Indeed, we are now convinced of the resurrection and the truth of whatever the Messengers brought. If you were to see the criminals in that state, you would see something genuinely profound.

(١٣) وَلَوۡ شِئۡنَا لَأٓتَيۡنَا كُلَّ نَفۡسٍ هُدَىٰهَا وَلَٰكِنۡ حَقَّ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ مِنِّي لَأَمۡلَأَنَّ جَهَنَّمَ مِنَ ٱلۡجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ أَجۡمَعِينَ

And if I wished to give every soul guidance and success, I would have made them [follow the guidance]. However, it was necessary to pass the decree through wisdom and justice that I would fill Hell on the Day of Resurrection with the disbelievers from both assemblies of jinn and humanity because they chose the path of disbelief and error over the path of belief and steadfastness.

(١٤) فَذُوقُواْ بِمَا نَسِيتُمۡ لِقَآءَ يَوۡمِكُمۡ هَٰذَآ إِنَّا نَسِينَٰكُمۡۖ وَذُوقُواْ عَذَابَ ٱلۡخُلۡدِ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

And on the Day of Resurrection, they will be told in rebuke and censure: Now taste the punishment due to your heedlessness of the meeting with Allah on the Day of Resurrection for accountability in the worldly life. I shall leave you in the punishment without caring for how much you suffer from it. Taste the punishment of the everlasting Fire that will not end because of the sins you used to commit in the worldly life.

After mentioning the condition of criminals, Allah mentions the condition of the believers, stating:

(١٥) إِنَّمَا يُؤۡمِنُ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِّرُواْ بِهَا خَرُّواْۤ سُجَّدٗاۤ وَسَبَّحُواْ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ۩

Only those who believe in Our verses revealed to Our Messenger prostrate to Allah when reminded, exalting, and praising Him. They are not arrogant from worshipping Him or prostrating to Him in any condition.

(١٦) تَتَجَافَىٰ جُنُوبُهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡمَضَاجِعِ يَدۡعُونَ رَبَّهُمۡ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعٗا وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ

They stay from their beds to worship Allah, supplicating to Him in their prayers, fearing His punishment, and hoping for His mercy. They also spend the wealth Allah has given them in His path.

(١٧) فَلَا تَعۡلَمُ نَفۡسٞ مَّآ أُخۡفِيَ لَهُم مِّن قُرَّةِ أَعۡيُنٖ جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

So, no one knows what Allah has prepared for them that brings coolness and joy to the eyes as a reward for the good deeds they did in this world. Due to its magnitude, it is a reward that no one except Allah can fully comprehend.

(١٨) أَفَمَن كَانَ مُؤۡمِنٗا كَمَن كَانَ فَاسِقٗاۚ لَّا يَسۡتَوُۥنَ

The one who believes in Allah, acts upon His commands, and avoids His prohibitions is unlike the one who disobeys Him. According to Allah, the two groups are not equal in reward.

(١٩) أَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ فَلَهُمۡ جَنَّٰتُ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ نُزُلَۢا بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

As for those who believe in Allah and do good deeds, the reward prepared for them is gardens in which they shall remain as an honor from Allah to them and a reward for the good deeds they used to do in this world.

(٢٠) وَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ فَسَقُواْ فَمَأۡوَىٰهُمُ ٱلنَّارُۖ كُلَّمَآ أَرَادُوٓاْ أَن يَخۡرُجُواْ مِنۡهَآ أُعِيدُواْ فِيهَا وَقِيلَ لَهُمۡ ذُوقُواْ عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ

As for those who left the obedience of Allah by disbelieving and committing sins, the abode prepared for them on the Day of Resurrection is the Fire in which they shall remain forever. They will be returned to it every time they attempt to come out of it. It will be said to them, rebukingly: Taste the punishment of the Fire you used to deny in the worldly life when your messengers used to warn you.

Points of Benefit:

The belief of the disbelievers on the Day of Resurrection does not benefit them because it is a realm of recompense, not a realm of deeds.
The danger of being heedless about the meeting with Allah on the Day of Resurrection.
Among the guidance for the believers is the establishment of night prayers.
