(٢١) وَلَنُذِيقَنَّهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡأَدۡنَىٰ دُونَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡأَكۡبَرِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ

We will make these disbelievers who left the obedience of their Lord and do not repent suffer trials and afflictions in this worldly life before the major punishment, for perhaps due to those worldly afflictions, they will repent to Allah.

(٢٢) وَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّن ذُكِّرَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِۦ ثُمَّ أَعۡرَضَ عَنۡهَآۚ إِنَّا مِنَ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ مُنتَقِمُونَ

There is no one more wrong than the one who is admonished with the words of Allah but does not take heed and turns away without caring about them. Indeed, We will inevitably take retribution from the criminals because they commit disbelief and sins and turn away from the verses of Allah.

(٢٣) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ فَلَا تَكُن فِي مِرۡيَةٖ مِّن لِّقَآئِهِۦۖ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ هُدٗى لِّبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ

Verily, We gave Moses the Torah, so, O Messenger, do not be in doubt over you having met Moses in the Night Journey and the Ascension. We made the Book revealed to Moses a guide for the Israelites to stop them from falling into misguidance.

(٢٤) وَجَعَلۡنَا مِنۡهُمۡ أَئِمَّةٗ يَهۡدُونَ بِأَمۡرِنَا لَمَّا صَبَرُواْۖ وَكَانُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا يُوقِنُونَ

And We made some Israelites be leaders whom the people would emulate and follow. They became guides to the truth when they were patient in calling others to Allah, complied with the commands of Allah, avoided His prohibitions, and firmly believed in His words revealed to their Messengers.

(٢٥) إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ يَفۡصِلُ بَيۡنَهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فِيمَا كَانُواْ فِيهِ يَخۡتَلِفُونَ

O Messenger, indeed, your Lord is the One who will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection in matters they disputed in this world. He will make the one on truth clear from the one on falsehood and recompense each with what they deserve.

(٢٦) أَوَ لَمۡ يَهۡدِ لَهُمۡ كَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّنَ ٱلۡقُرُونِ يَمۡشُونَ فِي مَسَٰكِنِهِمۡۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٍۚ أَفَلَا يَسۡمَعُونَ

Are these people blind that they cannot see how many of the previous nations We destroyed before them? Here they are, walking past dwellings previously resided in by those who were destroyed, yet they do not take heed of their state. Indeed, the destruction of the previous nations due to their disbelief is a lesson from which the truth of their messengers, who came to them from Allah, can be proven. Will these rejecters of the words of Allah then not listen attentively using their intellects?

(٢٧) أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّا نَسُوقُ ٱلۡمَآءَ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلۡجُرُزِ فَنُخۡرِجُ بِهِۦ زَرۡعٗا تَأۡكُلُ مِنۡهُ أَنۡعَٰمُهُمۡ وَأَنفُسُهُمۡۚ أَفَلَا يُبۡصِرُونَ

Do these resurrection deniers not see that We send rainwater to a barren land where there are no plants and then bring out the crops by that water, from which they and their cattle eat? Do they not see that and understand that whoever causes the barren earth to grow can revive the dead?

(٢٨) وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡفَتۡحُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

The rejectors of resurrection say while seeking the punishment in haste: When is this judgment that you claim between you and us on the Day of Resurrection, such that our abode will be the Fire, and your abode will be Paradise?

(٢٩) قُلۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَتۡحِ لَا يَنفَعُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنُهُمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يُنظَرُونَ

Say to them, O Messenger: This promise is the Day of Resurrection. It is the Day of Judgment between the slaves of Allah when those who disbelieve in Allah in this world will not benefit from believing in it after witnessing the Day of Resurrection, nor will they be given respite to repent to their Lord and turn back to Him.

(٣٠) فَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنۡهُمۡ وَٱنتَظِرۡ إِنَّهُم مُّنتَظِرُونَ

Therefore, O Messenger, turn away from these people once they persist in their deviance and wait for what will befall them. Indeed, they await the punishment that you promised them.

Points of Benefit:

The punishment of the disbeliever in this world serves as a means for their repentance.
The confirmed meeting between our Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم and Moses (peace be upon him) during the Night of Isra and Mi'raj.
Patience and certainty are qualities of the leaders in religion, the people of imamate.
