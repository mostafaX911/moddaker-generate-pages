The Combined Forces (Surah Al-Ahzab)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Declaring Allah's care for His Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم and protecting him and his family.

Explanation:

(١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ ٱتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ وَلَا تُطِعِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمٗا

O Prophet, remain firm, you and those with you, upon piety, through complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, and fear Him alone. Do not obey the disbelievers and hypocrites in what they desire. Allah knows the plans of the disbelievers and hypocrites and is Wise in His creation and management.

(٢) وَٱتَّبِعۡ مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٗا

And follow the revelation your Lord reveals to you. Allah is Aware of what you do. Nothing escapes Him, and He shall reward you for your actions.

(٣) وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ وَكِيلٗا

And rely on Allah alone in all your affairs. He is sufficient as a protector for His slaves who trust Him.

(٤) مَّا جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِرَجُلٖ مِّن قَلۡبَيۡنِ فِي جَوۡفِهِۦۚ وَمَا جَعَلَ أَزۡوَٰجَكُمُ ٱلَّٰٓـِٔي تُظَٰهِرُونَ مِنۡهُنَّ أُمَّهَٰتِكُمۡۚ وَمَا جَعَلَ أَدۡعِيَآءَكُمۡ أَبۡنَآءَكُمۡۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ قَوۡلُكُم بِأَفۡوَٰهِكُمۡۖ وَٱللَّهُ يَقُولُ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَهُوَ يَهۡدِي ٱلسَّبِيلَ

Just as Allah has not placed two hearts in the chest of any man, as some of them believe, He did not make wives like mothers with respect to being forbidden to marry. Likewise, He did not make adopted sons like biological sons because Zihār, which is for a man to make his wife unlawful for himself, and claiming the children of others as one's own are practices of ignorance, which Islam has nullified. That making unlawful through Zihār and taking as sons those whose fathers you are not is a statement you repeat with your mouths, which has no reality. The wife is not a mother, nor is the adopted son rightfully for the claimant. Allah says the truth, so His slaves practice it, and He guides them towards the path of truth.

(٥) ٱدۡعُوهُمۡ لِأٓبَآئِهِمۡ هُوَ أَقۡسَطُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِۚ فَإِن لَّمۡ تَعۡلَمُوٓاْ ءَابَآءَهُمۡ فَإِخۡوَٰنُكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِ وَمَوَٰلِيكُمۡۚ وَلَيۡسَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ جُنَاحٞ فِيمَآ أَخۡطَأۡتُم بِهِۦ وَلَٰكِن مَّا تَعَمَّدَتۡ قُلُوبُكُمۡۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمًا

Attribute those you claim to be your sons towards their natural fathers because attributing them towards them is just in the eyes of Allah. But if you do not know their fathers to whom you can attribute them, then they are your brothers in religion and freed slaves, so call them "O brother" or "O cousin." There is no sin on you if one of you makes a mistake and attributes a claimed son towards his claimant, but you will be sinful if you intentionally say that. Allah is forgiving to those of His slaves who repent and Merciful to them as He did not punish them due to mistakes.

(٦) ٱلنَّبِيُّ أَوۡلَىٰ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ مِنۡ أَنفُسِهِمۡۖ وَأَزۡوَٰجُهُۥٓ أُمَّهَٰتُهُمۡۗ وَأُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَرۡحَامِ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلَىٰ بِبَعۡضٖ فِي كِتَٰبِ ٱللَّهِ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ إِلَّآ أَن تَفۡعَلُوٓاْ إِلَىٰٓ أَوۡلِيَآئِكُم مَّعۡرُوفٗاۚ كَانَ ذَٰلِكَ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مَسۡطُورٗا

The Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم has more right over the believers than themselves in whatever he calls them to, even if they incline towards something else. And his wives are like mothers to all the believers. Hence it is unlawful for any believer to marry any of them after he passes away. Relatives are more deserving of one another in inheritance by Allah's decree than the believers and those who migrated for Allah's cause, who would previously inherit by means of the contractual agreement between themselves at the beginning of Islam. Then Allah abrogated their inheriting from one another after that. Except if you, O believers, do good towards your close associates besides the inheritors by making a bequest for them and being kind to them, then that is your right. That is a decree inscribed in the Preserved Tablet.

Points of Benefit:

No one is exempt from being enjoined with good and forbidden from evil.
The lifting of blame due to unintentional errors for this ummah.
The obligation to prioritize the Prophet's صلى الله عليه وسلم intentions over individual desires.
Clarification of the elevated status of the Prophet's صلى الله عليه وسلم wives and the prohibition of marrying them after his passing, as they are the mothers of the believers.
