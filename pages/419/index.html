(٧) وَإِذۡ أَخَذۡنَا مِنَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ‍ۧنَ مِيثَٰقَهُمۡ وَمِنكَ وَمِن نُّوحٖ وَإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ وَعِيسَى ٱبۡنِ مَرۡيَمَۖ وَأَخَذۡنَا مِنۡهُم مِّيثَٰقًا غَلِيظٗا

O Messenger, remember when We took a firm covenant from the prophets that they will worship Allah alone and not associate any partner with Him, and that they will convey the revelation sent to them. And We have taken it especially from you, and from Noah, Abraham, Moses, and Jesus, son of Mary. We have taken a firm covenant from them to fulfill the conveying of Allah's messages they have been entrusted with.

(٨) لِّيَسۡ‍َٔلَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ عَن صِدۡقِهِمۡۚ وَأَعَدَّ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَذَابًا أَلِيمٗا

Allah took this firm covenant from the prophets to ask the truthful Messengers about their truthfulness to silence the disbelievers. Allah has prepared for those who disbelieve in Him and His Messengers a painful punishment on the Day of Resurrection, which is the Fire.

(٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِذۡ جَآءَتۡكُمۡ جُنُودٞ فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ رِيحٗا وَجُنُودٗا لَّمۡ تَرَوۡهَاۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرًا

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated, remember Allah's favor upon you when the armies of the disbelievers came to Medina, prepared to fight you, and the hypocrites and Jews supported them. Then We sent upon them the eastern winds through which the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم was helped, and We sent armies of angels you did not see. Then the disbelievers turned away, fleeing, unable to do anything. And Allah is Seeing what you do. None of that is hidden from Him, and He shall recompense you for your deeds.

(١٠) إِذۡ جَآءُوكُم مِّن فَوۡقِكُمۡ وَمِنۡ أَسۡفَلَ مِنكُمۡ وَإِذۡ زَاغَتِ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَبَلَغَتِ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ ٱلۡحَنَاجِرَ وَتَظُنُّونَ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلظُّنُونَا۠

That was when the disbelievers came to you from the top and bottom of the valley and the East and West when their eyes were fixed upon the enemy. The hearts reached the throats due to intense fear. You were thinking different thoughts regarding Allah; at times, you thought you would be aided, and other times, you despaired [of His help].

(١١) هُنَالِكَ ٱبۡتُلِيَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَزُلۡزِلُواْ زِلۡزَالٗا شَدِيدٗا

In that situation, in the Battle of the Trench, the believers were tested by what they encountered from the swarming of their enemies against them. And they were severely disturbed by the intensity of fear. This test revealed who was the [true] believer and [exposed] the hypocrite.

(١٢) وَإِذۡ يَقُولُ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٞ مَّا وَعَدَنَا ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥٓ إِلَّا غُرُورٗا

On that Day, the hypocrites and those of weak faith, whose hearts were full of doubts, said: Allah and His Messenger's promise of victory and being established on this land is false and baseless.

(١٣) وَإِذۡ قَالَت طَّآئِفَةٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ يَٰٓأَهۡلَ يَثۡرِبَ لَا مُقَامَ لَكُمۡ فَٱرۡجِعُواْۚ وَيَسۡتَ‍ٔۡذِنُ فَرِيقٞ مِّنۡهُمُ ٱلنَّبِيَّ يَقُولُونَ إِنَّ بُيُوتَنَا عَوۡرَةٞ وَمَا هِيَ بِعَوۡرَةٍۖ إِن يُرِيدُونَ إِلَّا فِرَارٗا

O Messenger, remember when a group of hypocrites said to the people of Medina: O people of Yathrib (the name for Medina before Islam), there is no stability for you next to the trench, so return to your homes. A group was seeking permission from the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم to return to their homes, claiming that their homes were exposed to the enemy. However, they were not exposed as they claimed. They were only seeking to flee from their enemy through this pretext.

(١٤) وَلَوۡ دُخِلَتۡ عَلَيۡهِم مِّنۡ أَقۡطَارِهَا ثُمَّ سُئِلُواْ ٱلۡفِتۡنَةَ لَأٓتَوۡهَا وَمَا تَلَبَّثُواْ بِهَآ إِلَّا يَسِيرٗا

And if the enemy were to enter upon them in Medina from all its sides and ask them to return to disbelieving and associating partners with Allah, they would grant their enemy this. They would not hold themselves back from rebelling and turning back to disbelief except for a few of them.

(١٥) وَلَقَدۡ كَانُواْ عَٰهَدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مِن قَبۡلُ لَا يُوَلُّونَ ٱلۡأَدۡبَٰرَۚ وَكَانَ عَهۡدُ ٱللَّهِ مَسۡ‍ُٔولٗا

And these hypocrites had promised Allah after they fled from fighting on the occasion of Uhud that if Allah were to make them witness another battle, they would definitely fight their enemy and not flee out of fear. However, they broke their promise, and the slave is responsible for the promises he makes to Allah and will soon be questioned about it.

Points of Benefit:

The distinguished status of the messengers, particularly those with firm determination.
Allah's support for His believing servants in times of adversity.
The betrayal of the hypocrites towards the believers during trials.
