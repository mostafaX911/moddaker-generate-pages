(٢٥٣) ۞تِلۡكَ ٱلرُّسُلُ فَضَّلۡنَا بَعۡضَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖۘ مِّنۡهُم مَّن كَلَّمَ ٱللَّهُۖ وَرَفَعَ بَعۡضَهُمۡ دَرَجَٰتٖۚ وَءَاتَيۡنَا عِيسَى ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَأَيَّدۡنَٰهُ بِرُوحِ ٱلۡقُدُسِۗ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مَا ٱقۡتَتَلَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَتۡهُمُ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتُ وَلَٰكِنِ ٱخۡتَلَفُواْ فَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ ءَامَنَ وَمِنۡهُم مَّن كَفَرَۚ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مَا ٱقۡتَتَلُواْ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَفۡعَلُ مَا يُرِيدُ

We favored some of those messengers mentioned to you over others with respect to revelation, followers, and ranks. Some of them Allah spoke to, such as Moses. Others He granted a high rank, such as Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم – who was sent to all of humanity as the last of the prophets and whose nation was preferred over all other nations. We gave Jesus, son of Mary, clear miracles confirming his prophethood, such as bringing the dead to life and curing the leper and the blind. We also supported him with the angel Gabriel to give him strength in fulfilling Allah's instruction. If Allah had willed, those who came after the messengers would not have fought amongst themselves after receiving clear signs, but they disagreed and were divided. Some of them believed in Allah, while others did not. If Allah had willed, they would not have fought with each other, but Allah does as He wills: He guides whomever He wills to believe through His mercy and grace, and He allows whoever He wills to go astray through His justice and wisdom.

(٢٥٤) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَنفِقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَ يَوۡمٞ لَّا بَيۡعٞ فِيهِ وَلَا خُلَّةٞ وَلَا شَفَٰعَةٞۗ وَٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ هُمُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, spend from what We have provided you of various lawful wealth before the Day of Resurrection comes. On that day, there will be no transaction in which a person will gain what benefits them, nor friendship that will help them in times of distress, nor intercession that will ward off harm or bring benefit except after Allah's permission to whoever He wills and is pleased with. The disbelievers are truly the wrongdoers for their disbelief in Allah, the Exalted.

(٢٥٥) ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُۚ لَا تَأۡخُذُهُۥ سِنَةٞ وَلَا نَوۡمٞۚ لَّهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ مَن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يَشۡفَعُ عِندَهُۥٓ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِهِۦۚ يَعۡلَمُ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُمۡۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيۡءٖ مِّنۡ عِلۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّا بِمَا شَآءَۚ وَسِعَ كُرۡسِيُّهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَۖ وَلَا يَ‍ُٔودُهُۥ حِفۡظُهُمَاۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

Allah is the One who alone deserves to be worshipped. He is the One who possesses a perfect life without death or deficiency. He is the Self-Existent and does not need any of His creation. All of creation only exists through Him and is in constant need of Him. Drowsiness or sleep does not come upon Him due to the perfection of His life and existence. He alone is sovereign over the heavens and the earth. No one can intercede before Him without His permission and acceptance. He knows the affairs of His creation in the past and what will happen to them in the future. The creation cannot encompass His knowledge unless He wills to reveal it to them. His Kursi [footstool] encompasses the vastness of the heavens and the earth. It is neither difficult nor overbearing for Him to preserve the heavens and the earth. He is Exalted in His essence, attributes, and control. He is the Greatest in His dominion and authority.

(٢٥٦) لَآ إِكۡرَاهَ فِي ٱلدِّينِۖ قَد تَّبَيَّنَ ٱلرُّشۡدُ مِنَ ٱلۡغَيِّۚ فَمَن يَكۡفُرۡ بِٱلطَّٰغُوتِ وَيُؤۡمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ فَقَدِ ٱسۡتَمۡسَكَ بِٱلۡعُرۡوَةِ ٱلۡوُثۡقَىٰ لَا ٱنفِصَامَ لَهَاۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ

No one is forced to enter the religion of Islam; it is a clear, true religion, and as such, there is no need to compel anyone to believe in it. Guidance stands clear from misguidance. Whoever rejects all that is worshipped besides Allah, freeing oneself from them, and believes in Allah alone has held on to the religion's strongest, unbreakable means for salvation on the Day of Resurrection. Allah is All-Hearing of the words of His slaves, All-Knowing of their deeds, and He will recompense them accordingly.

Points of Benefit:

Allah, the Exalted, has favored some of His Prophets and Messengers over others through His knowledge and wisdom.
Affirming the attribute of Speech for Allah, the Exalted, as it befits His majesty and that He spoke with some of His Messengers, such as Moses and Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم.
Belief, guidance, disbelief, and misguidance are all by Allah's will and decree. He possesses perfect wisdom, and if He had willed, He would have guided all of creation.
Ayat al-Kursi is the greatest verse in the Book of Allah because it encompasses Allah's lordship, divinity, and a description of His attributes.
Following Islam and entering into it should be based on consent and acceptance, as there is no compulsion in the religion of Allah, the Exalted.
Holding fast to the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of His Messenger is the greatest means to happiness in this world and success in the Hereafter.
