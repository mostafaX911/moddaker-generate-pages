(١٦) قُل لَّن يَنفَعَكُمُ ٱلۡفِرَارُ إِن فَرَرۡتُم مِّنَ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ أَوِ ٱلۡقَتۡلِ وَإِذٗا لَّا تُمَتَّعُونَ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

O Messenger, tell these people: Fleeing will never benefit you if you flee from fighting out of fear of death or being killed because the appointed times are fixed. Fighting does not bring it closer, nor does fleeing delay it. And if you do manage to escape and your time has not come, you will only enjoy life for a short while longer.

(١٧) قُلۡ مَن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يَعۡصِمُكُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ إِنۡ أَرَادَ بِكُمۡ سُوٓءًا أَوۡ أَرَادَ بِكُمۡ رَحۡمَةٗۚ وَلَا يَجِدُونَ لَهُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَلِيّٗا وَلَا نَصِيرٗا

O Messenger, tell them: Who can save you from Allah if He wants you to die or be killed, which you hate so much, or if He intends for you the safety and good you hope for? There is nobody to save you from that. These hypocrites will not find for themselves any associate besides Allah to take care of their affairs nor any helper to protect them from Allah punishing them.

(١٨) ۞قَدۡ يَعۡلَمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُعَوِّقِينَ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلۡقَآئِلِينَ لِإِخۡوَٰنِهِمۡ هَلُمَّ إِلَيۡنَاۖ وَلَا يَأۡتُونَ ٱلۡبَأۡسَ إِلَّا قَلِيلًا

Allah knows those of you who hold others back from fighting alongside Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم and those who say to their brothers: Come to us and do not fight alongside him, lest you get killed, because we fear you will get killed. These forsaken people do not come to battle or participate in it except rarely, to remove blame from themselves, not to help Allah and His Messenger.

(١٩) أَشِحَّةً عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ فَإِذَا جَآءَ ٱلۡخَوۡفُ رَأَيۡتَهُمۡ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَيۡكَ تَدُورُ أَعۡيُنُهُمۡ كَٱلَّذِي يُغۡشَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ مِنَ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۖ فَإِذَا ذَهَبَ ٱلۡخَوۡفُ سَلَقُوكُم بِأَلۡسِنَةٍ حِدَادٍ أَشِحَّةً عَلَى ٱلۡخَيۡرِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَمۡ يُؤۡمِنُواْ فَأَحۡبَطَ ٱللَّهُ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡۚ وَكَانَ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرٗا

O believers, they are miserly with their wealth, so they do not help you by spending it; miserly with their souls, therefore they do not fight alongside you; and miserly with their love, thus they do not love you. Then when fear at the time of meeting the enemy comes, you will see them looking at you with their eyes wide open, rolling out of cowardice, just as the eyes of a person suffering the pangs of death roll. But when fear leaves them and they become content, they harm you verbally with abusive, sharp tongues. They are greedy for war booty, which they look for. Those described with these qualities have not truly believed, so Allah nullified the reward of their actions, and this was easy for Allah.

(٢٠) يَحۡسَبُونَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابَ لَمۡ يَذۡهَبُواْۖ وَإِن يَأۡتِ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابُ يَوَدُّواْ لَوۡ أَنَّهُم بَادُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَعۡرَابِ يَسۡ‍َٔلُونَ عَنۡ أَنۢبَآئِكُمۡۖ وَلَوۡ كَانُواْ فِيكُم مَّا قَٰتَلُوٓاْ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

These cowards think the coalition conspiring to fight Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, and the believers will never leave until they destroy them. And suppose it is decreed that the coalition come another time. In that case, these cowardly hypocrites will wish they were not in Medina and with the Bedouins, asking about you: What happened to you after your enemies fought you? Even if they were amongst you, O believers, they would not have fought alongside you except a little, so do not pay attention to them or feel sorry for them.

(٢١) لَّقَدۡ كَانَ لَكُمۡ فِي رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ أُسۡوَةٌ حَسَنَةٞ لِّمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلۡيَوۡمَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَ وَذَكَرَ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا

There has been for you an excellent example in what Allah's Messenger said, carried out, and practiced, for he presented his own noble self and personally engaged in battle, so how after that can you be miserly with your souls over his soul? Only he who is hopeful of the Last Day, works for it, and remembers Allah abundantly will follow Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم. As for the one who is not hopeful of the Last Day and does not remember Allah abundantly, he does not follow His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم.

(٢٢) وَلَمَّا رَءَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابَ قَالُواْ هَٰذَا مَا وَعَدَنَا ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَصَدَقَ ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥۚ وَمَا زَادَهُمۡ إِلَّآ إِيمَٰنٗا وَتَسۡلِيمٗا

When the believers saw the groups that had gathered to fight them, they said: This is the test and help Allah and His Messenger promised us [would happen], and Allah and His Messenger were true in this, for it has occurred. And seeing the groups only increased their conviction and submission to Him.

Points of Benefit:

Fixed are the appointed times; neither combat brings them closer nor fleeing from it pushes them away.
Discouraging from Jihad in the path of Allah is a trait consistently associated with the hypocrites.
The Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم is the exemplar for the believers in his words and actions.
Trust in Allah and submission to Him are qualities of the believers.
