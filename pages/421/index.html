(٢٣) مِّنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رِجَالٞ صَدَقُواْ مَا عَٰهَدُواْ ٱللَّهَ عَلَيۡهِۖ فَمِنۡهُم مَّن قَضَىٰ نَحۡبَهُۥ وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَنتَظِرُۖ وَمَا بَدَّلُواْ تَبۡدِيلٗا

Amongst the believers are men who were faithful to Allah, so they fulfilled what they promised Him: firmness and patience in striving in Allah's way. From them are those who died or were killed in Allah's path, while others await martyrdom in His cause. These believers did not change what they promised Allah as the hypocrites did with their promises.

(٢٤) لِّيَجۡزِيَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ بِصِدۡقِهِمۡ وَيُعَذِّبَ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ إِن شَآءَ أَوۡ يَتُوبَ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا

So that Allah rewards the truthful who fulfilled what they promised Allah, by their honesty and fulfilling their promises, and punishes the hypocrites who broke their pledges if He wishes, by making them die before they can repent from their disbelief or by accepting their repentance by enabling them to repent. And Allah forgives the one who repents for his sins and is Merciful to him.

(٢٥) وَرَدَّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِغَيۡظِهِمۡ لَمۡ يَنَالُواْ خَيۡرٗاۚ وَكَفَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ٱلۡقِتَالَۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ قَوِيًّا عَزِيزٗا

Allah repelled the Quraysh, Ghatafan, and those with them with distress, disappointment, and failure to get what they had hoped for. They were unsuccessful in destroying the believers as they intended, and Allah sufficed the believers [so they did not have to] fight them by sending winds and the angels. Allah is Powerful and Mighty; nobody attempts to overpower Allah except that He will be defeated and disheartened.

(٢٦) وَأَنزَلَ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَٰهَرُوهُم مِّنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِن صَيَاصِيهِمۡ وَقَذَفَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمُ ٱلرُّعۡبَ فَرِيقٗا تَقۡتُلُونَ وَتَأۡسِرُونَ فَرِيقٗا

Allah brought the Jews who helped them down from their fortresses in which they would protect themselves from their enemy, and Allah put fear into their hearts. O believers, you will kill one group and take another captive.

(٢٧) وَأَوۡرَثَكُمۡ أَرۡضَهُمۡ وَدِيَٰرَهُمۡ وَأَمۡوَٰلَهُمۡ وَأَرۡضٗا لَّمۡ تَطَ‍ُٔوهَاۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٗا

After destroying them, Allah gave you possession of their land, along with the crops and date trees they contained. He made you owners of their homes and other wealth as well as owners of the land of Khaybar, which you have yet to step foot on, but you will soon walk on it. This is a promise and glad tidings for the believers. Allah is Omnipotent over everything; nothing is beyond His capability.

(٢٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ قُل لِّأَزۡوَٰجِكَ إِن كُنتُنَّ تُرِدۡنَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَزِينَتَهَا فَتَعَالَيۡنَ أُمَتِّعۡكُنَّ وَأُسَرِّحۡكُنَّ سَرَاحٗا جَمِيلٗا

O Prophet, tell your wives if they seek increased spending [on them], at times you do not have more to spend: If you desire the worldly life and the adornment it contains, let me give you the provisions given to a divorced woman, and I will divorce you in the best of manners without causing you any harm.

(٢٩) وَإِن كُنتُنَّ تُرِدۡنَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَٱلدَّارَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَعَدَّ لِلۡمُحۡسِنَٰتِ مِنكُنَّ أَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا

But if you desire the pleasure of Allah and His Messenger, and you seek Paradise in the Afterlife, remain patient with your circumstances. Allah has prepared an excellent reward for those who do good through patience and fine companionship.

(٣٠) يَٰنِسَآءَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ مَن يَأۡتِ مِنكُنَّ بِفَٰحِشَةٖ مُّبَيِّنَةٖ يُضَٰعَفۡ لَهَا ٱلۡعَذَابُ ضِعۡفَيۡنِۚ وَكَانَ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرٗا

O wives of the Prophet, whoever amongst you commits an open sin, her punishment will be doubled on the Day of Resurrection due to her status and rank, and to protect the honor of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم. And this increase is easy for Allah to do.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's commendation of the companions of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم is a great honor bestowed upon them.
Allah's aid and triumph for His servants comes unexpectedly when they are vigilant in their mindfulness of Him.
The Jews who aided the confederates faced the severe consequences of their betrayal.
The selection of the Prophet's صلى الله عليه وسلم wives in the pure Sunnah is evidence of their strong faith, pleasing to Allah and His Messenger.
