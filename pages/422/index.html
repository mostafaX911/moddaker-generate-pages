(٣١) ۞وَمَن يَقۡنُتۡ مِنكُنَّ لِلَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَتَعۡمَلۡ صَٰلِحٗا نُّؤۡتِهَآ أَجۡرَهَا مَرَّتَيۡنِ وَأَعۡتَدۡنَا لَهَا رِزۡقٗا كَرِيمٗا

Whoever continues to obey Allah and His Messenger from among you and does a righteous deed that pleases Allah, We will give her twice as much reward as other women. We have prepared for her in the Hereafter a generous reward: Paradise.

(٣٢) يَٰنِسَآءَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ لَسۡتُنَّ كَأَحَدٖ مِّنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ إِنِ ٱتَّقَيۡتُنَّۚ فَلَا تَخۡضَعۡنَ بِٱلۡقَوۡلِ فَيَطۡمَعَ ٱلَّذِي فِي قَلۡبِهِۦ مَرَضٞ وَقُلۡنَ قَوۡلٗا مَّعۡرُوفٗا

O wives of the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, you are not like other women in terms of virtue and honor. Rather, you are in a position of virtue and honor that no one else can attain if you comply with Allah's commands and avoid His prohibitions. Therefore, do not soften your tone or beautify your voice if you speak to non-Mahram (unrelated/foreign) men so those who have diseases of hypocrisy and forbidden lust in their hearts will not be tempted. And say something far from questionable by speaking seriously, without jest, as much as possible.

(٣٣) وَقَرۡنَ فِي بُيُوتِكُنَّ وَلَا تَبَرَّجۡنَ تَبَرُّجَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِيَّةِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰۖ وَأَقِمۡنَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتِينَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَطِعۡنَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّمَا يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ لِيُذۡهِبَ عَنكُمُ ٱلرِّجۡسَ أَهۡلَ ٱلۡبَيۡتِ وَيُطَهِّرَكُمۡ تَطۡهِيرٗا

And remain in your homes; do not go out of them unnecessarily. Do not display your charms as women did before Islam in order to seduce men. Perform prayer properly, give the Zakat (obligatory alms) from your wealth, and obey Allah and His Messenger. O wives and relatives of Allah's Messenger, indeed, Allah (may He be Exalted) wants to remove evil and harm from you. He wants to purify your souls by adorning them with the virtues of morality, thoroughly cleansing them from their vices, and leaving no filth behind.

(٣٤) وَٱذۡكُرۡنَ مَا يُتۡلَىٰ فِي بُيُوتِكُنَّ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ لَطِيفًا خَبِيرًا

And mention the verses of Allah recited in your homes revealed to His Messenger and the purified Sunnah (the authentic prophetic tradition) of His Messenger. Allah favored you with kindness when He made you stay in the homes of His Prophet. He was Aware of you when He selected you as wives for His Messenger and chose you as mothers for all the believers of his nation.

(٣٥) إِنَّ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ وَٱلۡمُسۡلِمَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ وَٱلۡقَٰنِتِينَ وَٱلۡقَٰنِتَٰتِ وَٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ وَٱلصَّٰدِقَٰتِ وَٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ وَٱلصَّٰبِرَٰتِ وَٱلۡخَٰشِعِينَ وَٱلۡخَٰشِعَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُتَصَدِّقِينَ وَٱلۡمُتَصَدِّقَٰتِ وَٱلصَّٰٓئِمِينَ وَٱلصَّٰٓئِمَٰتِ وَٱلۡحَٰفِظِينَ فُرُوجَهُمۡ وَٱلۡحَٰفِظَٰتِ وَٱلذَّٰكِرِينَ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا وَٱلذَّٰكِرَٰتِ أَعَدَّ ٱللَّهُ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٗ وَأَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا

Indeed, the males who submit to Allah and the females who submit; the male believers in Allah and the female believers; the males who obey Allah and the females who obey; the males who are truthful in their belief and their statements and the females who are truthful; the males who are patient in obedience, in refraining from sins and during trials and the females who are patient; the males who give in obligatory and optional charity from their wealth and the females who give in charity; the males who keep obligatory and optional fasts and the females who fast; the males who guard their private parts from exposure to those who are forbidden from seeing them and from the immorality of fornication and anything leading to it, and the females who guard their private parts; and the males who remember Allah abundantly in private and in public and the females who remember Allah, Allah has prepared His special forgiveness for them from their sins, and an immense reward on the Day of Resurrection which is Paradise.

Points of Benefit:

Among the Quran's directives to the Muslim woman are the prohibition of speaking softly, the command to stay in the home except for some need, and the prohibition from showing oneself off.
The virtue of the household of the Messenger of Allah صلى الله عليه وسلم, and his wives are from his household.
The principle of equality between men and women applies to actions and recompense, except for what the legislation excludes for either of them.
