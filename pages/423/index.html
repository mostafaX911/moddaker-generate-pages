(٣٦) وَمَا كَانَ لِمُؤۡمِنٖ وَلَا مُؤۡمِنَةٍ إِذَا قَضَى ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥٓ أَمۡرًا أَن يَكُونَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡخِيَرَةُ مِنۡ أَمۡرِهِمۡۗ وَمَن يَعۡصِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَقَدۡ ضَلَّ ضَلَٰلٗا مُّبِينٗا

And it is incorrect for a male or female believer if Allah and His Messenger pass judgment on a matter between them, that they choose between accepting or rejecting it. Whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger has clearly strayed from the straight path.

(٣٧) وَإِذۡ تَقُولُ لِلَّذِيٓ أَنۡعَمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِ وَأَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيۡهِ أَمۡسِكۡ عَلَيۡكَ زَوۡجَكَ وَٱتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ وَتُخۡفِي فِي نَفۡسِكَ مَا ٱللَّهُ مُبۡدِيهِ وَتَخۡشَى ٱلنَّاسَ وَٱللَّهُ أَحَقُّ أَن تَخۡشَىٰهُۖ فَلَمَّا قَضَىٰ زَيۡدٞ مِّنۡهَا وَطَرٗا زَوَّجۡنَٰكَهَا لِكَيۡ لَا يَكُونَ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ حَرَجٞ فِيٓ أَزۡوَٰجِ أَدۡعِيَآئِهِمۡ إِذَا قَضَوۡاْ مِنۡهُنَّ وَطَرٗاۚ وَكَانَ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ مَفۡعُولٗا

And when you say, O Messenger, to whom Allah blessed with Islam, and you granted manumission (referring to Zaid bin Haritha, may Allah be pleased with him, when he came to consult about the matter of divorcing his wife Zainab bint Jahsh), you told him: Hold on to your wife and do not divorce her, and fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding his prohibitions. You hid within yourself, O Messenger, what Allah had revealed to you about your marriage to Zainab, fearing people's reaction when Allah reveals Zaid's divorce and your eventual marriage to her. But Allah is more worthy of fearing regarding this matter. So when Zayd's content heart lost interest in Zainab and divorced her, We married you to her. This was ordained to demonstrate that there is no sin upon the believers to marry the wives of their adopted children if they divorce them and the iddah (waiting period) finishes. Allah's command will always come to pass; nothing can stop or obstruct it.

(٣٨) مَّا كَانَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّ مِنۡ حَرَجٖ فِيمَا فَرَضَ ٱللَّهُ لَهُۥۖ سُنَّةَ ٱللَّهِ فِي ٱلَّذِينَ خَلَوۡاْ مِن قَبۡلُۚ وَكَانَ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ قَدَرٗا مَّقۡدُورًا

There is no sin or restriction on the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم in what Allah permitted of marrying his adopted daughter-in-law. In doing so, he is following the Sunnah (way) of the Prophets before him, and he is no different from the messengers. The Prophet had no choice or opinion in completing this marriage and invalidating the adoption Allah decreed. It was an effective judgment that cannot be reversed.

(٣٩) ٱلَّذِينَ يُبَلِّغُونَ رِسَٰلَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَخۡشَوۡنَهُۥ وَلَا يَخۡشَوۡنَ أَحَدًا إِلَّا ٱللَّهَۗ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ حَسِيبٗا

These prophets who convey the messages of Allah [that were sent to them] to their nations do not fear anyone except Allah (may He be Exalted). Likewise, they ignore what others say when they do whatever Allah has permitted. It is enough for Allah to preserve the deeds of His slaves to hold them accountable for it, and He will recompense them accordingly. If it is good, then with good. If it is bad, then with bad.

(٤٠) مَّا كَانَ مُحَمَّدٌ أَبَآ أَحَدٖ مِّن رِّجَالِكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن رَّسُولَ ٱللَّهِ وَخَاتَمَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ‍ۧنَۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٗا

Muhammad is not the father of any of your men, so he is not the father of Zayd. Therefore, marrying Zayd's wife is not forbidden for him if Zayd divorces her. However, he is Allah's Messenger sent to the people and the Seal of the Prophets, so there is no prophet after him. Allah is the Knower of everything, and nothing is hidden from Him of the affairs of His slaves.

(٤١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ ذِكۡرٗا كَثِيرٗا

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, remember Allah abundantly with your hearts, tongues, and limbs.

(٤٢) وَسَبِّحُوهُ بُكۡرَةٗ وَأَصِيلًا

Exalt Him (may He be Exalted) though the Tasbeeh (praising Allah by saying Subḥan-Allah) and Tahleel (proclaiming His oneness by saying La-ilaha-illallah) at the beginning and end of the day due to their virtue.

(٤٣) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُصَلِّي عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَمَلَٰٓئِكَتُهُۥ لِيُخۡرِجَكُم مِّنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِۚ وَكَانَ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رَحِيمٗا

He is the One who has mercy on you [O believers] and praises you. Moreover, His angels pray for you to bring you out of the darkness of disbelief into the light of faith. Allah is merciful to the believers, so He does not punish them if they obey Him by fulfilling His command and refraining from what He has prohibited.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation for the believer to surrender to the ruling of Allah and submit to it.
Allah knows all that is in the souls.
One of the virtues of the Mother of the Believers, Zainab bint Jahsh, is that Allah gave in marriage from above the seven heavens.
The virtue of making remembrance of Allah, especially in the morning and evening.
