(٤٤) تَحِيَّتُهُمۡ يَوۡمَ يَلۡقَوۡنَهُۥ سَلَٰمٞۚ وَأَعَدَّ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرٗا كَرِيمٗا

The salutation of the believers on the day they meet their Lord is peace and safety from all evil. Moreover, Allah has prepared for them a generous reward, which is His Paradise, as a recompense for their obedience to Him and for staying away from His disobedience.

(٤٥) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ شَٰهِدٗا وَمُبَشِّرٗا وَنَذِيرٗا

O Prophet, indeed, We have sent you to the people as a witness against them that you have conveyed the Message (Islam). Moreover, to bring good tidings to the believers of what Allah has prepared for them (Paradise) and to frighten the disbelievers of what He has prepared for them of His torment.

(٤٦) وَدَاعِيًا إِلَى ٱللَّهِ بِإِذۡنِهِۦ وَسِرَاجٗا مُّنِيرٗا

We also sent you as a caller to Allah's Oneness and obedience to Him by His command. Furthermore, We sent you as an illuminated lamp to enlighten all who want guidance.

(٤٧) وَبَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ بِأَنَّ لَهُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ فَضۡلٗا كَبِيرٗا

Tell the believers in Allah, who do what He has legislated for them, of something that will please them: that they will have a great favor from Allah (may He be Exalted), which includes their victory in this world and their success in the Hereafter by entering Paradise.

(٤٨) وَلَا تُطِعِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ وَدَعۡ أَذَىٰهُمۡ وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ وَكِيلٗا

Do not obey the disbelievers and the hypocrites in their call to prevent people from Allah's religion, but turn away from them. Perhaps this will make it more likely for them to believe in what you have brought them. Also, rely on Allah in all your affairs. Among that is your victory against your enemies. Allah is sufficient as a trustee upon whom the slaves can rely in all their affairs in this world and the Hereafter.

(٤٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا نَكَحۡتُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ ثُمَّ طَلَّقۡتُمُوهُنَّ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن تَمَسُّوهُنَّ فَمَا لَكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِنَّ مِنۡ عِدَّةٖ تَعۡتَدُّونَهَاۖ فَمَتِّعُوهُنَّ وَسَرِّحُوهُنَّ سَرَاحٗا جَمِيلٗا

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, when you tie the knot of marriage with a believing women, and then you divorce them before consummating the marriage with them, they are not required to go through the waiting period for you, regardless of whether it is through menstrual cycles or by counting months. This is because their wombs being free is known because you have not consummated the marriage with them. Also, give them some compensation according to your financial situation to console their broken emotions due to divorce and clear the way for them to return to their families without causing them any inconvenience.

(٥٠) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ إِنَّآ أَحۡلَلۡنَا لَكَ أَزۡوَٰجَكَ ٱلَّٰتِيٓ ءَاتَيۡتَ أُجُورَهُنَّ وَمَا مَلَكَتۡ يَمِينُكَ مِمَّآ أَفَآءَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡكَ وَبَنَاتِ عَمِّكَ وَبَنَاتِ عَمَّٰتِكَ وَبَنَاتِ خَالِكَ وَبَنَاتِ خَٰلَٰتِكَ ٱلَّٰتِي هَاجَرۡنَ مَعَكَ وَٱمۡرَأَةٗ مُّؤۡمِنَةً إِن وَهَبَتۡ نَفۡسَهَا لِلنَّبِيِّ إِنۡ أَرَادَ ٱلنَّبِيُّ أَن يَسۡتَنكِحَهَا خَالِصَةٗ لَّكَ مِن دُونِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَۗ قَدۡ عَلِمۡنَا مَا فَرَضۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ فِيٓ أَزۡوَٰجِهِمۡ وَمَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُهُمۡ لِكَيۡلَا يَكُونَ عَلَيۡكَ حَرَجٞۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا

O Prophet, We have permitted for you your wives whom you gave their dowries, and We have made it lawful for you in what you possess of the slave-girls that Allah has bestowed you from the captives. We also made the marriage of your paternal uncle's daughters, paternal aunts' daughters, maternal uncle's daughters, and maternal aunts' daughters who migrated with you from Mecca to Medina lawful for you. We also made it legal for you to marry a believing woman who gave herself to you without a dowry if you wanted to marry her. This type of marriage, without a dowry, is exclusive to the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم and is not permissible to anyone else from his Nation. Verily, We knew what we have made obligatory upon the believers regarding their wives, where they are not permitted to marry more than four women at once. We also know what we have legislated for them regarding their slave women, as they may enjoy whomever they want without restriction in number. We have also permitted for you from those mentioned earlier what We have not made permissible for others so that there is no distress or hardship upon you. Allah forgives those of His slaves who repent and is Merciful to them.

Points of Benefit:

Patience, when faced with harm, is one of the characteristics of the successful caller to Allah.
It is recommended for the one who divorces his wife before consummating the marriage to give her some money as compensation for her loss.
The permissibility of a woman giving herself in marriage is exclusively for the Prophet, even though he did not do that as the Sunnah indicates.
