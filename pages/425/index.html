(٥١) ۞تُرۡجِي مَن تَشَآءُ مِنۡهُنَّ وَتُ‍ٔۡوِيٓ إِلَيۡكَ مَن تَشَآءُۖ وَمَنِ ٱبۡتَغَيۡتَ مِمَّنۡ عَزَلۡتَ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡكَۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَدۡنَىٰٓ أَن تَقَرَّ أَعۡيُنُهُنَّ وَلَا يَحۡزَنَّ وَيَرۡضَيۡنَ بِمَآ ءَاتَيۡتَهُنَّ كُلُّهُنَّۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي قُلُوبِكُمۡۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلِيمًا حَلِيمٗا

O Messenger, postpone whoever of your wives you want to, so you do not spend the night with her. Likewise, join with whom you want to, so you spend the night with whichever of your wives you wish. Moreover, whomever you ask to spend the night from those you have postponed, there is no sin on you [in that]. This choice and broadening of options [for you] are more likely to make your wives content, comforted, and satisfied with what you have given them all. They know you have not left an obligation or skimped a right. Allah knows what is in your hearts, O men, of the inclination to some women over others. Allah knows His slaves' deeds; nothing is hidden from Him. He is Forbearing and does not hasten their punishment so they may repent to Him.

(٥٢) لَّا يَحِلُّ لَكَ ٱلنِّسَآءُ مِنۢ بَعۡدُ وَلَآ أَن تَبَدَّلَ بِهِنَّ مِنۡ أَزۡوَٰجٖ وَلَوۡ أَعۡجَبَكَ حُسۡنُهُنَّ إِلَّا مَا مَلَكَتۡ يَمِينُكَۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ رَّقِيبٗا

It is not permissible for you, O Messenger, to marry women other than your wives who are already in your custody. Moreover, neither is it permissible for you to divorce them or divorce some of them to marry other women, even if you like the beauty of whom you want to marry from women other than them. However, it is permissible for you to be pleased with what you possess from slave women without limitation to a specific number, and Allah is the guardian of everything. This ruling indicates the virtue of the mothers of the believers, as it was forbidden to divorce them and marry other than them.

(٥٣) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَدۡخُلُواْ بُيُوتَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ إِلَّآ أَن يُؤۡذَنَ لَكُمۡ إِلَىٰ طَعَامٍ غَيۡرَ نَٰظِرِينَ إِنَىٰهُ وَلَٰكِنۡ إِذَا دُعِيتُمۡ فَٱدۡخُلُواْ فَإِذَا طَعِمۡتُمۡ فَٱنتَشِرُواْ وَلَا مُسۡتَ‍ٔۡنِسِينَ لِحَدِيثٍۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكُمۡ كَانَ يُؤۡذِي ٱلنَّبِيَّ فَيَسۡتَحۡيِۦ مِنكُمۡۖ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَسۡتَحۡيِۦ مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّۚ وَإِذَا سَأَلۡتُمُوهُنَّ مَتَٰعٗا فَسۡ‍َٔلُوهُنَّ مِن وَرَآءِ حِجَابٖۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ أَطۡهَرُ لِقُلُوبِكُمۡ وَقُلُوبِهِنَّۚ وَمَا كَانَ لَكُمۡ أَن تُؤۡذُواْ رَسُولَ ٱللَّهِ وَلَآ أَن تَنكِحُوٓاْ أَزۡوَٰجَهُۥ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦٓ أَبَدًاۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكُمۡ كَانَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ عَظِيمًا

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, do not enter the houses of the Prophet except after he permits you to enter them by inviting you to food. Furthermore, do not sit for long waiting for the food to be cooked, but if you are invited to food, then enter, and after you eat, leave. Also, do not remain behind talking and socializing with each other because remaining behind causes inconvenience to the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم, but he feels embarrassed to ask you to leave. However, Allah is not ashamed to command you to do what is right, so He (the Exalted) commanded you to leave so that you do not cause inconvenience to him صلى الله عليه وسلم by staying for too long. If you ask for something from the wives of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم (i.e., utensils, etc.), then ask from behind a curtain, not face to face, in a manner that you cannot see them with your eyes. This is to protect them due to the importance and position of Allah's Messenger. Asking them from behind a curtain, O believers, is purer for your hearts and theirs, so the Devil cannot influence them through whispers and beautifying evil. Likewise, you, O believers, should not harm Allah's Messenger by staying to talk and socialize nor by marrying his wives after his death, for they are the mothers of the believers. It is also not permissible for anyone to marry his mother. Such abuse (including marrying his wives after his death) is forbidden and considered a grave sin by Allah.

(٥٤) إِن تُبۡدُواْ شَيۡ‍ًٔا أَوۡ تُخۡفُوهُ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٗا

If you show something of your deeds or keep it to yourselves secretly, nothing of it will be hidden from Allah. Indeed, Allah knows everything. Nothing of your acts or anything else is hidden from Him, and He will recompense you for your deeds. If good, then good, and if evil, then evil.

Points of Benefit:

The great position the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم has with Allah, which is why He admonished the companions (may Allah be pleased with them) who stayed in his صلى الله عليه وسلم house because that stay harmed him.
Affirming the attributes of Knowledge and Forbearance for Allah, the Exalted.
Modesty is from the manners of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم.
Maintaining the position of the Mothers of the Believers, the wives of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم.
