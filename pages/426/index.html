(٥٥) لَّا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡهِنَّ فِيٓ ءَابَآئِهِنَّ وَلَآ أَبۡنَآئِهِنَّ وَلَآ إِخۡوَٰنِهِنَّ وَلَآ أَبۡنَآءِ إِخۡوَٰنِهِنَّ وَلَآ أَبۡنَآءِ أَخَوَٰتِهِنَّ وَلَا نِسَآئِهِنَّ وَلَا مَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُهُنَّۗ وَٱتَّقِينَ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدًا

There is no sin on the wives of the Messenger if their fathers, sons, brothers, nephews (brothers' sons), and the sons of their sisters by blood or breast-feeding, to see them and talk to them without a curtain. Moreover, there is no sin on them if the believing women or the slaves they possess speak to them without a curtain. Fear Allah, O believing women, in whatever He (may He be Exalted) has commanded you to do and forbade you from, for He is watching what appears from you and what you do.

(٥٦) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَمَلَٰٓئِكَتَهُۥ يُصَلُّونَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّۚ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ صَلُّواْ عَلَيۡهِ وَسَلِّمُواْ تَسۡلِيمًا

Indeed, Allah praises the Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم in the presence of His angels, and His angels pray for him. O you who believe in Allah and do whatever He has legislated for His slaves, pray for the Messenger and greet him with peace abundantly.

When Allah commanded that the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم be venerated and prayed for, He forbade any harm to befall upon him, stating:

(٥٧) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡذُونَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ لَعَنَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَأَعَدَّ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٗا مُّهِينٗا

Indeed, for those who harm Allah and His Messenger through speech or action, Allah has removed and expelled them from the vastness of His mercy in this world and the Hereafter. Moreover, He has prepared a humiliating torment for them in the Hereafter for what they have done by harming His Messenger.

(٥٨) وَٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡذُونَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ بِغَيۡرِ مَا ٱكۡتَسَبُواْ فَقَدِ ٱحۡتَمَلُواْ بُهۡتَٰنٗا وَإِثۡمٗا مُّبِينٗا

Those who harm the believing men and women by speech or actions, without them having committed any wrong or crime that necessitates that harm, carry the burden of openly lying and sinning.

(٥٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ قُل لِّأَزۡوَٰجِكَ وَبَنَاتِكَ وَنِسَآءِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ يُدۡنِينَ عَلَيۡهِنَّ مِن جَلَٰبِيبِهِنَّۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَدۡنَىٰٓ أَن يُعۡرَفۡنَ فَلَا يُؤۡذَيۡنَۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا

O Prophet, say to your wives, daughters, and the women of the believers: Let the outer robes you wear hang loosely over you so that your bodies are not revealed to unrelated men. That is closer to distinguishing them as free women, so they are not subjected to abuse like the slave girls are. Allah forgives the sins of whichever of His slaves repents, and He is merciful to them.

(٦٠) ۞لَّئِن لَّمۡ يَنتَهِ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٞ وَٱلۡمُرۡجِفُونَ فِي ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ لَنُغۡرِيَنَّكَ بِهِمۡ ثُمَّ لَا يُجَاوِرُونَكَ فِيهَآ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

Suppose the hypocrites do not stop their hypocrisy [by harboring disbelief and demonstrating Islam]. And suppose those whose hearts are immoral due to their attachment to their desires and those who bring false news in Medina to divide the believers do not cease. In that case, We will command you, O Messenger, to punish them. Moreover, We will give you power over them. Then they will not reside with you in Medina except for a short period, to destroy or expel them from it because of their corruption on Earth.

(٦١) مَّلۡعُونِينَۖ أَيۡنَمَا ثُقِفُوٓاْ أُخِذُواْ وَقُتِّلُواْ تَقۡتِيلٗا

They would be expelled from the mercy of Allah. Moreover, they would be seized and executed wherever they were found because of their hypocrisy and spreading corruption on Earth.

(٦٢) سُنَّةَ ٱللَّهِ فِي ٱلَّذِينَ خَلَوۡاْ مِن قَبۡلُۖ وَلَن تَجِدَ لِسُنَّةِ ٱللَّهِ تَبۡدِيلٗا

This is Allah's established practice against the hypocrites if they reveal their hypocrisy. Allah's established practice is fixed, and you will never find any change in it.

Points of Benefit:

The high position of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم with Allah and His angels.
The impermissibility of harming the believers without a reason.
Hypocrisy causes the hypocrite to be tormented.
