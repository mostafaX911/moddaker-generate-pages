(٦٣) يَسۡ‍َٔلُكَ ٱلنَّاسُ عَنِ ٱلسَّاعَةِۖ قُلۡ إِنَّمَا عِلۡمُهَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِۚ وَمَا يُدۡرِيكَ لَعَلَّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ تَكُونُ قَرِيبًا

The polytheists ask you, O Messenger, a question of denial and rejection, and the Jews also ask you, regarding the final Hour: When will it occur? Say to them: The knowledge of the Hour is with Allah, so I have no knowledge of it. What could make you, O Messenger, feel that the Hour is close?

(٦٤) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَعَنَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ وَأَعَدَّ لَهُمۡ سَعِيرًا

Indeed, Allah (may He be Exalted) has expelled the disbelievers from His mercy and prepared a flaring fire that awaits them on the Day of Resurrection.

(٦٥) خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدٗاۖ لَّا يَجِدُونَ وَلِيّٗا وَلَا نَصِيرٗا

They will remain in the torment of that fire [prepared for them] forever. They will not find a guardian that benefits them in it, nor a supporter who wards its torment off them.

(٦٦) يَوۡمَ تُقَلَّبُ وُجُوهُهُمۡ فِي ٱلنَّارِ يَقُولُونَ يَٰلَيۡتَنَآ أَطَعۡنَا ٱللَّهَ وَأَطَعۡنَا ٱلرَّسُولَا۠

On the Day of Resurrection, their faces will be turned in the Hellfire. They will say out of severe regret and remorse: If only we obeyed Allah by doing what He commanded us to and avoiding what He prohibited us from, and obeyed the Messenger in what he brought from his Lord.

(٦٧) وَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَآ إِنَّآ أَطَعۡنَا سَادَتَنَا وَكُبَرَآءَنَا فَأَضَلُّونَا ٱلسَّبِيلَا۠

They will bring a flimsy and invalid argument and say: Our Lord, indeed, we obeyed our leaders and the elders of our people, so they misled us from the straight path.

(٦٨) رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِهِمۡ ضِعۡفَيۡنِ مِنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ وَٱلۡعَنۡهُمۡ لَعۡنٗا كَبِيرٗا

O our Lord, give these leaders and elders who misled us from the straight path twice as much torment as You have given us for misleading us. Moreover, expel them out of Your mercy with a great expulsion.

(٦٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّذِينَ ءَاذَوۡاْ مُوسَىٰ فَبَرَّأَهُ ٱللَّهُ مِمَّا قَالُواْۚ وَكَانَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَجِيهٗا

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, do not harm your Messenger. If you do, you would be like those who hurt Moses by saying that he had a defect in his body, so Allah absolved him of what they said, and it became clear to them that he was innocent of what they said about him. Moses is highly ranked according to Allah. His requesting supplications were not rejected, nor were his endeavors disappointed.

(٧٠) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَقُولُواْ قَوۡلٗا سَدِيدٗا

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, be mindful of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding what He has prohibited, and speak rightly and truthfully.

(٧١) يُصۡلِحۡ لَكُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلَكُمۡ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ ذُنُوبَكُمۡۗ وَمَن يُطِعِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَقَدۡ فَازَ فَوۡزًا عَظِيمًا

Indeed, if you are mindful of Allah and speak correctly, Allah will rectify your deeds for you, accept them from you, erase your sins, and not hold you accountable for them. Whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has achieved a great success that no other success can come close to, which is gaining Allah's satisfaction and entering Paradise.

(٧٢) إِنَّا عَرَضۡنَا ٱلۡأَمَانَةَ عَلَى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلۡجِبَالِ فَأَبَيۡنَ أَن يَحۡمِلۡنَهَا وَأَشۡفَقۡنَ مِنۡهَا وَحَمَلَهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ ظَلُومٗا جَهُولٗا

Indeed, We presented the legislated duties and what is kept of money and secrets to the heavens, the Earth, and the mountains. However, they refrained from bearing them and feared consequences, but man carried them. Indeed, he was unjust to himself, ignorant of the consequences of bearing them.

(٧٣) لِّيُعَذِّبَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ وَٱلۡمُشۡرِكَٰتِ وَيَتُوبَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمَۢا

The human carried it as per the decree of Allah. So that Allah may punish the men and women who are hypocrites and polytheists for their hypocrisy and associating partners with Allah and forgive the believing men and women who carried the burden of obligation well. Allah forgives the sins of those who repent of His slaves and is Merciful to them.

Points of Benefit:

Only Allah knows when the final Hour will be.
The followers holding their leaders responsible for their misguidance does not absolve them of responsibility.
The severe prohibition of harming the Prophets through word or action.
The greatness of the trust that mankind bears.
