Sheba (Surah Saba)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing people's different situations regarding blessings and Allah's established practice to change them.

Explanation:

(١) ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَهُ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ

All praise belongs to Allah, to whom belongs everything that is in the heavens and everything that is on Earth, by way of being His creation, in His ownership and under His control. And for Him is all praise in the Hereafter; He is the Wise in His creation and disposing of His affairs, and the Knower of the state of His slaves. Nothing is hidden from Him.

(٢) يَعۡلَمُ مَا يَلِجُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا يَخۡرُجُ مِنۡهَا وَمَا يَنزِلُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا يَعۡرُجُ فِيهَاۚ وَهُوَ ٱلرَّحِيمُ ٱلۡغَفُورُ

He knows everything that enters the earth, i.e., water, seeds, and minerals; He knows whatever comes from it, i.e., plants, etc.; He knows what descends from the heavens, i.e., rain, angels, and sustenance, and He knows whatever ascends to the heavens, i.e., angels, the deeds of His slaves, and their souls. He is merciful to His believing slaves and forgiving of the sins of whoever repents to Him.

(٣) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَا تَأۡتِينَا ٱلسَّاعَةُۖ قُلۡ بَلَىٰ وَرَبِّي لَتَأۡتِيَنَّكُمۡ عَٰلِمِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِۖ لَا يَعۡزُبُ عَنۡهُ مِثۡقَالُ ذَرَّةٖ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَلَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَآ أَصۡغَرُ مِن ذَٰلِكَ وَلَآ أَكۡبَرُ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٖ

Those disbelieving in Allah said: The Hour will never come to us. O Messenger, say to them: No, by Allah, the very Hour you reject will definitely come to you, but its time is only known to Allah. He (may He be Exalted) is the Knower of whatever is hidden, such as the Hour; even the equivalent of the smallest ant is not hidden from His knowledge, nor anything smaller than that in the heavens or on Earth or larger, except that it is recorded in a clear book, which is the Preserved Tablet in which everything that is to happen until the Day of Resurrection is recorded.

(٤) لِّيَجۡزِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٞ وَرِزۡقٞ كَرِيمٞ

Allah has recorded everything He has in the Preserved Tablet to recompense those who believe in Him and do good deeds. They will receive forgiveness from Allah for their sins; He will not take them to account for them, and on the Day of Resurrection, they will receive generous sustenance, His Paradise.

(٥) وَٱلَّذِينَ سَعَوۡ فِيٓ ءَايَٰتِنَا مُعَٰجِزِينَ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ مِّن رِّجۡزٍ أَلِيمٞ

As for those who strived to refute the revealed verses of Allah and said they were sorcery, and claimed that Our Messenger is a fortune-teller, a sorcerer, and a poet, they will receive the most severe and terrible punishment on the Day of Resurrection.

(٦) وَيَرَى ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَ هُوَ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَيَهۡدِيٓ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَمِيدِ

Some scholars of the people of the book testify that the revelation Allah revealed to you is the truth without doubt and that it guides towards the path of Allah, the Mighty, who no one can overpower and who is praiseworthy in this world and the Hereafter.

(٧) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ هَلۡ نَدُلُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ رَجُلٖ يُنَبِّئُكُمۡ إِذَا مُزِّقۡتُمۡ كُلَّ مُمَزَّقٍ إِنَّكُمۡ لَفِي خَلۡقٖ جَدِيدٍ

Those who disbelieved in Allah told one another in astonishment and to mock whatever the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم brought: Shall we lead you to a man who will tell you that when you die and decompose into pieces, you will be brought back to life again?

Points of Benefit:

The extent of Allah's (the Transcendent's) knowledge that encompasses everything.
The virtue of the people of knowledge.
The polytheists rejecting the resurrection of the dead is a denial of the power of Allah, who created them.
