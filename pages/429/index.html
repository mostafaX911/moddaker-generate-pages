(٨) أَفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبًا أَم بِهِۦ جِنَّةُۢۗ بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ وَٱلضَّلَٰلِ ٱلۡبَعِيدِ

And they said: Has this man fabricated and attributed a lie to Allah, claiming that we shall be resurrected after we die, or is he insane, talking nonsense? It is not as these people say; rather, the reality is that those who do not believe in the Hereafter will suffer a severe punishment on the Day of Resurrection and are very far astray.

(٩) أَفَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ إِن نَّشَأۡ نَخۡسِفۡ بِهِمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ أَوۡ نُسۡقِطۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ كِسَفٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّكُلِّ عَبۡدٖ مُّنِيبٖ

Do these rejectors of the resurrection not see the earth [that We have made] in front of them and the sky above them? If We wished the ground beneath their feet to swallow them up, We would have caused it to happen, and if We wished to send down portions/pieces of the punishment from the heavens, We would have done so. Indeed, there is a clear sign in that for every repentant slave through which he witnesses the Power of Allah. The One capable of doing that can resurrect you after your death and the decomposition of your body.

(١٠) ۞وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا دَاوُۥدَ مِنَّا فَضۡلٗاۖ يَٰجِبَالُ أَوِّبِي مَعَهُۥ وَٱلطَّيۡرَۖ وَأَلَنَّا لَهُ ٱلۡحَدِيدَ

And verily, We granted David (peace be upon him) messengership and a kingdom, and said to the mountains: O Mountains, make exaltation together with David. We said the same to the birds. We also made iron soft for him so he could make whatever tools he wanted.

(١١) أَنِ ٱعۡمَلۡ سَٰبِغَٰتٖ وَقَدِّرۡ فِي ٱلسَّرۡدِۖ وَٱعۡمَلُواْ صَٰلِحًاۖ إِنِّي بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٞ

O David, make broad armor which will protect your soldiers from the attacks of their enemies, and make the rivets in proportion to the rings so that they are not too fine such that they do not lodge in them, nor too thick such that they do not fit in them either. And do good deeds; [as] I am watching over your actions, none of them are hidden from Me, and I shall recompense you for them.

(١٢) وَلِسُلَيۡمَٰنَ ٱلرِّيحَ غُدُوُّهَا شَهۡرٞ وَرَوَاحُهَا شَهۡرٞۖ وَأَسَلۡنَا لَهُۥ عَيۡنَ ٱلۡقِطۡرِۖ وَمِنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ مَن يَعۡمَلُ بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهِۦۖ وَمَن يَزِغۡ مِنۡهُمۡ عَنۡ أَمۡرِنَا نُذِقۡهُ مِنۡ عَذَابِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

And We subjugated the wind for Solomon, the son of David. It would blow the distance of a month in the morning and the same distance in the evening. We also made springs of copper flow for him so that he could make whatever he wanted from it, together with employing the Jinn who would work in front of him by the command of his Lord. Whenever any of the Jinn would deviate from whatever We commanded them to do, We would let them taste the punishment of a raging fire.

(١٣) يَعۡمَلُونَ لَهُۥ مَا يَشَآءُ مِن مَّحَٰرِيبَ وَتَمَٰثِيلَ وَجِفَانٖ كَٱلۡجَوَابِ وَقُدُورٖ رَّاسِيَٰتٍۚ ٱعۡمَلُوٓاْ ءَالَ دَاوُۥدَ شُكۡرٗاۚ وَقَلِيلٞ مِّنۡ عِبَادِيَ ٱلشَّكُورُ

These Jinn constructed for him whatever he wanted from places of worship, palaces, and lofty structures. As well as whatever containers he wanted, like giant water basins and fixed cooking pots that were so big they could not move. We said to them: O family of David, show gratitude to Allah for the blessings He has bestowed upon you. Very few of My slaves are grateful to Me for the blessings I grant them.

(١٤) فَلَمَّا قَضَيۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ مَا دَلَّهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَوۡتِهِۦٓ إِلَّا دَآبَّةُ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ تَأۡكُلُ مِنسَأَتَهُۥۖ فَلَمَّا خَرَّ تَبَيَّنَتِ ٱلۡجِنُّ أَن لَّوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ ٱلۡغَيۡبَ مَا لَبِثُواْ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡمُهِينِ

When We decreed for Solomon to die, the Jinn did not realize he had passed away until the woodworms had eaten away at the staff he was leaning on. When he fell, it became clear to the Jinn that they do not know the unseen because if they had known, they would not have remained subdued, continuing to carry out the difficult tasks imposed upon them, which they were doing for Solomon (peace be upon him) thinking that he was watching them.

Points of Benefit:

Allah honored His Prophet David by granting him prophethood and kingship, subjugating the mountains and birds to sing His praises, and making iron soft for him.
Allah honored His Prophet Solomon (peace be upon him) with prophethood and kingship.
One should be grateful for Allah's blessings.
Knowledge of the unseen is exclusively for Allah, so there is no basis for those who claim the Jinn or others know the unseen.
