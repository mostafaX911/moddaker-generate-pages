(٢٥٧) ٱللَّهُ وَلِيُّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ يُخۡرِجُهُم مِّنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَوۡلِيَآؤُهُمُ ٱلطَّٰغُوتُ يُخۡرِجُونَهُم مِّنَ ٱلنُّورِ إِلَى ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِۗ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

Allah takes care of those who believe in Him. He grants them success and victory and takes them out of the darkness of disbelief and ignorance into the light of belief and knowledge. The allies of the disbelievers are the idols, who beautify disbelief to them and take them from the light of belief and knowledge into the darkness of disbelief and ignorance. They are the people of the Fire who will remain therein forever.

After mentioning the two groups, Allah gives two comparisons of the two groups, stating:

(٢٥٨) أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِي حَآجَّ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمَ فِي رَبِّهِۦٓ أَنۡ ءَاتَىٰهُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُلۡكَ إِذۡ قَالَ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمُ رَبِّيَ ٱلَّذِي يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُ قَالَ أَنَا۠ أُحۡيِۦ وَأُمِيتُۖ قَالَ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمُ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأۡتِي بِٱلشَّمۡسِ مِنَ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقِ فَأۡتِ بِهَا مِنَ ٱلۡمَغۡرِبِ فَبُهِتَ ٱلَّذِي كَفَرَۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

O Prophet, have you seen the audacity of the tyrant who argued with Abraham about Allah's Lordship and Oneness? This happened because Allah had given him the power to rule, yet he transgressed. Abraham explained to him the attributes of his Lord by saying: My Lord is the One Who gives life to the creation and causes them to die. The tyrant stubbornly said: I, too, can give life and cause death. I can kill whomever I wish and pardon whomever I want to. Abraham then put forward another stronger argument by saying to him: The Lord I worship is the One Who brings the sun from the direction of the east; so, you try to bring it from the west! The tyrant was overcome by the compelling argument. Allah does not allow wrongdoers to be guided and traverse His Path due to their oppression and tyranny.

(٢٥٩) أَوۡ كَٱلَّذِي مَرَّ عَلَىٰ قَرۡيَةٖ وَهِيَ خَاوِيَةٌ عَلَىٰ عُرُوشِهَا قَالَ أَنَّىٰ يُحۡيِۦ هَٰذِهِ ٱللَّهُ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَاۖ فَأَمَاتَهُ ٱللَّهُ مِاْئَةَ عَامٖ ثُمَّ بَعَثَهُۥۖ قَالَ كَمۡ لَبِثۡتَۖ قَالَ لَبِثۡتُ يَوۡمًا أَوۡ بَعۡضَ يَوۡمٖۖ قَالَ بَل لَّبِثۡتَ مِاْئَةَ عَامٖ فَٱنظُرۡ إِلَىٰ طَعَامِكَ وَشَرَابِكَ لَمۡ يَتَسَنَّهۡۖ وَٱنظُرۡ إِلَىٰ حِمَارِكَ وَلِنَجۡعَلَكَ ءَايَةٗ لِّلنَّاسِۖ وَٱنظُرۡ إِلَى ٱلۡعِظَامِ كَيۡفَ نُنشِزُهَا ثُمَّ نَكۡسُوهَا لَحۡمٗاۚ فَلَمَّا تَبَيَّنَ لَهُۥ قَالَ أَعۡلَمُ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

Or have you seen the example of the one who passed by a town where the roofs had fallen, the walls had collapsed, and the people had died, making it a deserted ruin? Surprised, This man said: How will Allah ever bring these people back to life? Allah then caused him to die for one hundred years, brought him back to life, and asked him: How long were you dead for? He replied: I remained like that for one day or less than a day. Allah then said to him: In fact, you were like this for one hundred years. Look at the food you had with you, which has not gone bad despite this long period of time that has passed! Look at your dead donkey! We want to make you a clear sign to show people the Power We have to bring them back to life. Look at the bones of the donkey that are scattered. We will lift them, put them together, clothe them with flesh, and give them life again. When he saw this, the reality became clear to him, and he recognized Allah's omnipotence and admitted: I know Allah is the Most Capable of everything.

Points of Benefit:

One of the greatest distinctions of the people of belief is that they are guided and have insight from Allah in all their religious and worldly affairs, unlike the people of disbelief.
One of the greatest causes of tyranny is arrogance with power and authority, to the extent that a person becomes blinded to the reality of their condition.
The legitimacy of debating the people of falsehood to clarify the truth and expose their deviation from guidance.
The magnificence of Allah's Omnipotence, the Exalted, nothing can hinder Him, and from that Power is the ability to resurrect the dead.
