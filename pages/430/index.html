After mentioning what He blessed David and his son Solomon (peace be upon them) with, Allah mentions what He blessed the people of Sheba with. The difference is that David and Solomon (peace be upon them) were thankful to Allah, but the people of Sheba were ungrateful to Him. He states:

(١٥) لَقَدۡ كَانَ لِسَبَإٖ فِي مَسۡكَنِهِمۡ ءَايَةٞۖ جَنَّتَانِ عَن يَمِينٖ وَشِمَالٖۖ كُلُواْ مِن رِّزۡقِ رَبِّكُمۡ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لَهُۥۚ بَلۡدَةٞ طَيِّبَةٞ وَرَبٌّ غَفُورٞ

In the dwellings where the tribe of Sheba lived, there was a clear sign of Allah's power and His favor on them, and the clear sign was two gardens: one on the right and the other on the left. We said to them: Eat of the provision of your Lord and thank Him for His favors. This is a pleasant city, and your Lord, Allah, is a forgiving Lord who absolves the sins of those who repent to Him.

(١٦) فَأَعۡرَضُواْ فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ سَيۡلَ ٱلۡعَرِمِ وَبَدَّلۡنَٰهُم بِجَنَّتَيۡهِمۡ جَنَّتَيۡنِ ذَوَاتَيۡ أُكُلٍ خَمۡطٖ وَأَثۡلٖ وَشَيۡءٖ مِّن سِدۡرٖ قَلِيلٖ

But they turned away from thanking Allah and believing in His messengers, so We punished them by turning their favors into difficulties. Then, We sent a strong flood upon them, which destroyed their dam and flooded their fields. Thus, We replaced their two pleasant gardens with two that gave bitter fruit and contained fruitless tamarisk trees and a few lote trees.

(١٧) ذَٰلِكَ جَزَيۡنَٰهُم بِمَا كَفَرُواْۖ وَهَلۡ نُجَٰزِيٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡكَفُورَ

That change occurred to their favors because of their disbelief and turning away from being thankful for those favors. We do not punish so severely except one who rejects Allah's favors.

(١٨) وَجَعَلۡنَا بَيۡنَهُمۡ وَبَيۡنَ ٱلۡقُرَى ٱلَّتِي بَٰرَكۡنَا فِيهَا قُرٗى ظَٰهِرَةٗ وَقَدَّرۡنَا فِيهَا ٱلسَّيۡرَۖ سِيرُواْ فِيهَا لَيَالِيَ وَأَيَّامًا ءَامِنِينَ

We put many neighboring cities between the people of Sheba in Yemen and the cities of the blessed land of Levant, making the distance to travel easier for them so they may pass through these cities and towns, stopping when needed, without any difficulty until they reach the land of Levant. And we announced: Travel through them day and night in complete security from any enemy, hunger, or thirst.

(١٩) فَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَا بَٰعِدۡ بَيۡنَ أَسۡفَارِنَا وَظَلَمُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ أَحَادِيثَ وَمَزَّقۡنَٰهُمۡ كُلَّ مُمَزَّقٍۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّكُلِّ صَبَّارٖ شَكُورٖ

But they arrogantly rejected Allah's favor of facilitating their journey and said: O Our Lord, make our journeys longer by removing those cities in between so that we can taste the exhaustion of traveling and make clear the excellence of our riding mounts. They wronged themselves by arrogantly rejecting Allah's favor and turning away from thanking Him, so We made them into tales which those after them talk of. We separated them in the lands so they could not reach each other. That which has been mentioned, namely favoring the people of Sheba and taking retribution from them, is a lesson for every person who is patient in his obedience to Allah and bearing what he has been afflicted with, and is thankful for Allah's favors on him.

(٢٠) وَلَقَدۡ صَدَّقَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ إِبۡلِيسُ ظَنَّهُۥ فَٱتَّبَعُوهُ إِلَّا فَرِيقٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Iblis confirmed through them what he assumed, i.e., that he is able to lead them astray and misguide them from the truth, as they followed him in disbelief and misguidance except for a group of believers who destroyed his hopes by not following him.

(٢١) وَمَا كَانَ لَهُۥ عَلَيۡهِم مِّن سُلۡطَٰنٍ إِلَّا لِنَعۡلَمَ مَن يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ مِمَّنۡ هُوَ مِنۡهَا فِي شَكّٖۗ وَرَبُّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٍ حَفِيظٞ

Iblis has no power over them, through which he can force them to be misguided. He only beautifies [evil] for them and entices them to lead them astray. We have given him permission to entice so we can make it clear the one who has faith in the Hereafter from the one who is in doubt about the Hereafter. Your Lord, O Messenger, is a Guardian over everything. He guards the actions of His slaves and will reward them for them.

(٢٢) قُلِ ٱدۡعُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ زَعَمۡتُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ لَا يَمۡلِكُونَ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٖ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَلَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا لَهُمۡ فِيهِمَا مِن شِرۡكٖ وَمَا لَهُۥ مِنۡهُم مِّن ظَهِيرٖ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: Call upon those whom you claimed were your gods besides Allah so that they can bring you benefit or remove harm from you. They do not have ownership of anything, not even something as small as a dust particle, nor are they partners with Allah in ownership, nor does Allah have any helpers from amongst them. He is Self-sufficient, in no need of anyone else.

Points of Benefit:

Gratitude preserves blessings, and ingratitude causes them to be taken away.
Security is one of Allah's greatest blessings upon His slaves.
True belief is a protection from following the temptations of the Devil, by Allah's permission.
Showing the falsehood of what leads to polytheism and its gateways, like claiming the idols have some dominion, are partners with Allah, aid Him, or intercede with Him.
