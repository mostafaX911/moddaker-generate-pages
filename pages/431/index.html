(٢٣) وَلَا تَنفَعُ ٱلشَّفَٰعَةُ عِندَهُۥٓ إِلَّا لِمَنۡ أَذِنَ لَهُۥۚ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا فُزِّعَ عَن قُلُوبِهِمۡ قَالُواْ مَاذَا قَالَ رَبُّكُمۡۖ قَالُواْ ٱلۡحَقَّۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ

Intercession does not benefit except the one whom Allah gives permission, and Allah does not permit to intercede except those He is pleased with due to His Greatness. And part of His Greatness is that when He speaks in the heavens, the angels flap their wings in submission to what He says until the fear disappears from their hearts; some of them say to others: What did your Lord just say? The angels reply that He said the truth and is High in His essence, the Great, that everything else is lesser than Him.

(٢٤) ۞قُلۡ مَن يَرۡزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ قُلِ ٱللَّهُۖ وَإِنَّآ أَوۡ إِيَّاكُمۡ لَعَلَىٰ هُدًى أَوۡ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: Who sends provisions to you from the skies by bringing out the crops, plants, and fruits? Then tell them: It is Allah who provides for you. O, polytheists, only one of us can be rightly guided while the other is on clear misguidance. There can be no doubt that the believers are on clear guidance and the polytheists are misguided.

(٢٥) قُل لَّا تُسۡ‍َٔلُونَ عَمَّآ أَجۡرَمۡنَا وَلَا نُسۡ‍َٔلُ عَمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ

O Messenger, say to them: You will not be asked on the Day of Resurrection about the sins we committed, nor will we be asked about what you used to do.

(٢٦) قُلۡ يَجۡمَعُ بَيۡنَنَا رَبُّنَا ثُمَّ يَفۡتَحُ بَيۡنَنَا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَهُوَ ٱلۡفَتَّاحُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

Say to them: Allah will gather you and us on the Day of Resurrection, then He will judge between you and us with justice, then He will manifest the truthful from the liar. He is the Judge who adjudicates with justice, the Omniscient of how to pass judgment.

(٢٧) قُلۡ أَرُونِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَلۡحَقۡتُم بِهِۦ شُرَكَآءَۖ كَلَّاۚ بَلۡ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

O Messenger, say to them: Show me those whom you have made as partners with Allah that you associate with Him in worship. No, indeed, the matter is not as you have thought, i.e., that He has partners, but rather He is Allah, the Mighty whom nobody can overpower, Wise in his creation, decree, and control.

(٢٨) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا كَآفَّةٗ لِّلنَّاسِ بَشِيرٗا وَنَذِيرٗا وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

And We did not send you, O Messenger, except for the sake of all of humanity, to give good news to the pious that for them is Paradise, and to warn the disbelievers and transgressors about the Fire, but most people do not know that. Had they known, they would not have denied you.

(٢٩) وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

And the polytheists say, while hastily seeking the punishment you are warning them of: If you are truthful, tell us when this promise of punishment will occur.

(٣٠) قُل لَّكُم مِّيعَادُ يَوۡمٖ لَّا تَسۡتَ‍ٔۡخِرُونَ عَنۡهُ سَاعَةٗ وَلَا تَسۡتَقۡدِمُونَ

O Messenger, tell these people hastily seeking the punishment: For you is a fixed appointed time. When the time comes upon you, it will not be delayed or preceded by the slightest. And this day is the Day of Resurrection.

(٣١) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَن نُّؤۡمِنَ بِهَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ وَلَا بِٱلَّذِي بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِۗ وَلَوۡ تَرَىٰٓ إِذِ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ مَوۡقُوفُونَ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ يَرۡجِعُ بَعۡضُهُمۡ إِلَىٰ بَعۡضٍ ٱلۡقَوۡلَ يَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتُضۡعِفُواْ لِلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ لَوۡلَآ أَنتُمۡ لَكُنَّا مُؤۡمِنِينَ

Those who disbelieved in Allah said: We will never believe in the Quran, which Muhammad claims is revealed to him, nor will we ever believe in the previous divine books. O Messenger, if you were to see when the wrongdoers are detained before their Lord on the Day of Resurrection for the reckoning. They will dispute with one another, each placing the responsibility and blame on the other. The subdued followers will say to their leaders who oppressed them in this life: If you had not led us astray, we would have been believers in Allah and His messengers.

Points of Benefit:

Being kind to the one being called to Allah so that he does not resort to stubbornness and arrogance.
The one who is guided is raised and elevated by it, and the misguided one is immersed in misguidance and despised.
The message of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم encompasses all of the humans and the Jinn.
