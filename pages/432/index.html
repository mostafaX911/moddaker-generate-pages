(٣٢) قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ لِلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتُضۡعِفُوٓاْ أَنَحۡنُ صَدَدۡنَٰكُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡهُدَىٰ بَعۡدَ إِذۡ جَآءَكُمۖ بَلۡ كُنتُم مُّجۡرِمِينَ

The leaders who were arrogant to the truth will say to the followers they oppressed: Did we stop you from the guidance that Muhammad brought? No, rather, you were the wrongdoers and the people who were corruptly spreading immorality.

(٣٣) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتُضۡعِفُواْ لِلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ بَلۡ مَكۡرُ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذۡ تَأۡمُرُونَنَآ أَن نَّكۡفُرَ بِٱللَّهِ وَنَجۡعَلَ لَهُۥٓ أَندَادٗاۚ وَأَسَرُّواْ ٱلنَّدَامَةَ لَمَّا رَأَوُاْ ٱلۡعَذَابَۚ وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلۡأَغۡلَٰلَ فِيٓ أَعۡنَاقِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْۖ هَلۡ يُجۡزَوۡنَ إِلَّا مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

The followers who were oppressed by their leaders will say to their leaders who were arrogant to the truth: But your deceiving us night and day, when you would order us to disbelieve in Allah and to worship creations besides Him, prevented us from guidance. They will hide their regret over their disbelief in this world when they see the punishment and will know they are going to be punished. And We will put chains on the necks of the disbelievers. They will not be given this recompense except due to what they used to do in the worldly life, namely worshipping others besides Allah and committing sins.

To console the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم after his people rejected him, Allah reminds him that rejection was also the practice of the nations before him, stating:

(٣٤) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا فِي قَرۡيَةٖ مِّن نَّذِيرٍ إِلَّا قَالَ مُتۡرَفُوهَآ إِنَّا بِمَآ أُرۡسِلۡتُم بِهِۦ كَٰفِرُونَ

We have not sent a messenger to any city warning of Allah's punishment except that the affluent people in it amongst those of power, fame, and wealth said: O Messengers, we disbelieve in what you have been sent with.

(٣٥) وَقَالُواْ نَحۡنُ أَكۡثَرُ أَمۡوَٰلٗا وَأَوۡلَٰدٗا وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمُعَذَّبِينَ

And these people of fame said braggingly and arrogantly: We have more wealth and children. And your claim that we will be punished is a lie, as we will not be punished in this world nor the Hereafter.

(٣٦) قُلۡ إِنَّ رَبِّي يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَقۡدِرُ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

O Messenger, say to those who have been deceived by the favors they have been given: My Lord increases the provision for whom He wants to test whether he is grateful or ungrateful, and He restricts it for whom He wants to test whether he is patient or becomes upset. But most people do not know that Allah is Wise. He does not decree a matter except due to complete wisdom known to those who know and which the ignorant are unaware of.

(٣٧) وَمَآ أَمۡوَٰلُكُمۡ وَلَآ أَوۡلَٰدُكُم بِٱلَّتِي تُقَرِّبُكُمۡ عِندَنَا زُلۡفَىٰٓ إِلَّا مَنۡ ءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ جَزَآءُ ٱلضِّعۡفِ بِمَا عَمِلُواْ وَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡغُرُفَٰتِ ءَامِنُونَ

Your wealth and children, which you boast about, do not lead you to Allah's pleasure, but the one who believes in Allah and does good actions has acquired a multiplied reward. His wealth draws him closer to Allah by spending it in Allah's way, and his children are also a means for raising his rank through their supplications for him. So those believers who do good deeds are rewarded multiple times for the good they did, and they will be in the higher ranks of Paradise, safe from whatever they fear: punishment, death, and the end of their bliss.

(٣٨) وَٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡعَوۡنَ فِيٓ ءَايَٰتِنَا مُعَٰجِزِينَ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ مُحۡضَرُونَ

And the disbelievers who make it their utmost effort to turn people away from Our signs and strive hard to achieve their goals are losers in this world and will be punished in the Hereafter.

(٣٩) قُلۡ إِنَّ رَبِّي يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ وَيَقۡدِرُ لَهُۥۚ وَمَآ أَنفَقۡتُم مِّن شَيۡءٖ فَهُوَ يُخۡلِفُهُۥۖ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّٰزِقِينَ

O Messenger, say to them: My Lord increases the provision for whom He wants from His creation and restricts it for whom He wants. And whatever you spend in Allah's way, Allah will replace it for you in this world by giving you what is better than it and in the Hereafter with a great reward. Allah is the best of providers, so whoever seeks provision should seek it from Him.

Points of Benefit:

The leaders and followers disavowing each other does not absolve them of their responsibility.
Luxury keeps one far from deferring to the Truth and submitting to it.
The believer's wealth and children benefit him, but the disbeliever does not benefit from them.
Spending in the path of Allah will lead to the money being replaced in this life and a good reward in the Hereafter.
