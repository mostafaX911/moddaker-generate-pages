(٤٠) وَيَوۡمَ يَحۡشُرُهُمۡ جَمِيعٗا ثُمَّ يَقُولُ لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ أَهَٰٓؤُلَآءِ إِيَّاكُمۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡبُدُونَ

O Messenger, remember the day Allah will gather them all, then Allah will say to the angels to scold the polytheists and rebuke them: Are these the ones who used to worship you in the worldly life besides Allah?

(٤١) قَالُواْ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ أَنتَ وَلِيُّنَا مِن دُونِهِمۖ بَلۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡبُدُونَ ٱلۡجِنَّۖ أَكۡثَرُهُم بِهِم مُّؤۡمِنُونَ

The angels will say: Pure and Exalted are You; You are our guardian, not them, so there is no friendship between them and us, but rather, these polytheists used to worship the devils. They used to appear to them as angels so that they would worship them besides Allah; most were believers in them.

(٤٢) فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ لَا يَمۡلِكُ بَعۡضُكُمۡ لِبَعۡضٖ نَّفۡعٗا وَلَا ضَرّٗا وَنَقُولُ لِلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ ذُوقُواْ عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُم بِهَا تُكَذِّبُونَ

On the Day of Gathering and Reckoning, those worshipped will not be able to benefit those who worshipped them besides Allah in this world, nor will they be able to harm them. And We will say to those who wronged themselves through disbelief and sins: Taste the punishment of the Fire you used to deny in this world.

(٤٣) وَإِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُنَا بَيِّنَٰتٖ قَالُواْ مَا هَٰذَآ إِلَّا رَجُلٞ يُرِيدُ أَن يَصُدَّكُمۡ عَمَّا كَانَ يَعۡبُدُ ءَابَآؤُكُمۡ وَقَالُواْ مَا هَٰذَآ إِلَّآ إِفۡكٞ مُّفۡتَرٗىۚ وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِلۡحَقِّ لَمَّا جَآءَهُمۡ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا سِحۡرٞ مُّبِينٞ

And when you recite Our clear verses revealed to Our Messenger, in which there is no ambiguity, to these denying polytheists, they say: This is a mere human who has brought this message to divert us from the path of our forefathers. They also say: The Quran is nothing but a fabricated lie against Allah. And those who disbelieved in Allah said regarding the Quran when it came to them from Allah: This is only obvious sorcery to separate between a husband and his wife, and a father from his son.

(٤٤) وَمَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُم مِّن كُتُبٖ يَدۡرُسُونَهَاۖ وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِمۡ قَبۡلَكَ مِن نَّذِيرٖ

And We have not given them books they are reading, which may direct them to say the Quran is a lie Muhammad fabricated, nor did We send them, before sending you, O Messenger, a messenger who would warn them of Allah's punishment.

(٤٥) وَكَذَّبَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ وَمَا بَلَغُواْ مِعۡشَارَ مَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمۡ فَكَذَّبُواْ رُسُلِيۖ فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ نَكِيرِ

The previous nations, such as 'Ād, Thamūd, and the people of Lot, were deniers of faith. And the polytheists from your people have not reached even a tenth of the strength, might, and numbers the previous nations reached. Every one of them denied their messenger, so the wealth, strength, and numbers they were given did not benefit them, so My punishment befell them. So, see the outcome of My Reproach and that of My punishment.

(٤٦) ۞قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَعِظُكُم بِوَٰحِدَةٍۖ أَن تَقُومُواْ لِلَّهِ مَثۡنَىٰ وَفُرَٰدَىٰ ثُمَّ تَتَفَكَّرُواْۚ مَا بِصَاحِبِكُم مِّن جِنَّةٍۚ إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا نَذِيرٞ لَّكُم بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ عَذَابٖ شَدِيدٖ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: I only direct you to and advise you of one characteristic, which is that you stand free from desires, for Allah's sake, in pairs and individually, then think about the life of your companion and what you know of his intelligence, truthfulness, and trustworthiness, so that it becomes clear to you that he صلى الله عليه وسلم is not insane. He is only a warner of severe punishment if you do not repent to Allah from associating partners with Him.

(٤٧) قُلۡ مَا سَأَلۡتُكُم مِّنۡ أَجۡرٖ فَهُوَ لَكُمۡۖ إِنۡ أَجۡرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدٞ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists who reject: I do not ask you for any reward or payment for the guidance and good I have brought you; if you are guided, it will be for your benefit. My reward is only with Allah alone; He is a Witness to everything. He witnesses that I have conveyed to you and witnesses your actions, and He will reward you in full for them.

After mentioning the evidence against the people of falsehood and polytheism, Allah the Exalted mentions that this is His established divine practice, stating:

(٤٨) قُلۡ إِنَّ رَبِّي يَقۡذِفُ بِٱلۡحَقِّ عَلَّٰمُ ٱلۡغُيُوبِ

O Messenger, say: My Lord will empower the truth over falsehood, thus negating it, and He knows the unseen. Nothing in the heavens or the Earth is hidden from Him, and [especially] the actions of His slaves are not concealed from Him.

Points of Benefit:

Blind following of forefathers diverts one from true guidance.
Contemplation, free of desires, is a way to reach the correct decision and way of thinking.
The caller to Allah does not expect a reward from the people but expects it from the Lord of all people.
