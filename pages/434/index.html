(٤٩) قُلۡ جَآءَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَمَا يُبۡدِئُ ٱلۡبَٰطِلُ وَمَا يُعِيدُ

O Messenger, tell these rejecting polytheists: The truth, which is Islam, has come, and falsehood no longer has any sway, and its dominance will not return.

(٥٠) قُلۡ إِن ضَلَلۡتُ فَإِنَّمَآ أَضِلُّ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِيۖ وَإِنِ ٱهۡتَدَيۡتُ فَبِمَا يُوحِيٓ إِلَيَّ رَبِّيٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ سَمِيعٞ قَرِيبٞ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists who reject: If I stray away from the truth in what I convey to you, the harm of my misguidance is confined to me. None of it will affect you. And if I am guided to Him, it is due to what my Lord reveals to me. He hears the sayings of His slaves and is Close to me; it is not difficult for Him to hear what I say.

(٥١) وَلَوۡ تَرَىٰٓ إِذۡ فَزِعُواْ فَلَا فَوۡتَ وَأُخِذُواْ مِن مَّكَانٖ قَرِيبٖ

O Messenger, if you were to see when these rejectors will be petrified when they see the punishment on the Day of Resurrection, there will be no place for them to run to, nor any refuge for them to take shelter in. They will be taken from a nearby place that is easy to reach from the beginning. If you were to see that, you would see a strange matter indeed.

(٥٢) وَقَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦ وَأَنَّىٰ لَهُمُ ٱلتَّنَاوُشُ مِن مَّكَانِۢ بَعِيدٖ

And they will say when they see their fate: We believe in the Day of Resurrection. But how will they pursue and acquire faith when the place for accepting faith has gone far from them, by their leaving the worldly abode, the abode of action and no reward, to the home of the Hereafter, the abode of reward and no action?

(٥٣) وَقَدۡ كَفَرُواْ بِهِۦ مِن قَبۡلُۖ وَيَقۡذِفُونَ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ مِن مَّكَانِۢ بَعِيدٖ

How can they attain faith, and how can it be accepted from them when they disbelieved during this worldly life, casting conjecture from a side far from reaching the truth, such as calling the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم a magician, a soothsayer, and a poet?

(٥٤) وَحِيلَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ وَبَيۡنَ مَا يَشۡتَهُونَ كَمَا فُعِلَ بِأَشۡيَاعِهِم مِّن قَبۡلُۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ فِي شَكّٖ مُّرِيبِۢ

And these deniers were prevented from what they desired, from the pleasure of this life and repenting from disbelief, salvation from the Fire, and returning to the worldly life, just as was done with those resembling them from the rejecting nations before them. They doubted what the messengers brought, i.e., believing the Oneness of Allah and faith in the resurrection.

Originator (Surah Fatir)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Demonstrating the utter poverty of slaves before the Creator of the heavens and the earth and His absolute sufficiency from them.

Explanation:

(١) ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ فَاطِرِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَاعِلِ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ رُسُلًا أُوْلِيٓ أَجۡنِحَةٖ مَّثۡنَىٰ وَثُلَٰثَ وَرُبَٰعَۚ يَزِيدُ فِي ٱلۡخَلۡقِ مَا يَشَآءُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

All praise is for Allah, Creator of the heavens and the Earth without precedent, Who appointed messengers from the angels who execute His preordained commands. Some convey the revelation to the Prophets and strengthen them to perform what they were entrusted with. Some of them have two, three, or four wings with which they fly to carry out what they have been ordered. Allah increases His creation in what He wishes of limbs, beauty, or voice. Allah is Omnipotent over everything; nothing is beyond His capability.

(٢) مَّا يَفۡتَحِ ٱللَّهُ لِلنَّاسِ مِن رَّحۡمَةٖ فَلَا مُمۡسِكَ لَهَاۖ وَمَا يُمۡسِكۡ فَلَا مُرۡسِلَ لَهُۥ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

The keys to everything are in Allah's Hand. So whatever provision, guidance, or good fortune He opens up for people, nobody can prevent it. And whatever He stops of this, there is nobody who is able to send it after He has stopped it. And He is the Mighty whom nobody can overpower, Wise in His creating, decreeing, and planning.

(٣) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡۚ هَلۡ مِنۡ خَٰلِقٍ غَيۡرُ ٱللَّهِ يَرۡزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُؤۡفَكُونَ

O people, remember Allah's favor on you through your hearts and tongues, and through your limbs by action. Do you have any creator besides Allah who provides you from the sky with the water He sends to you and who provides you from the earth with the fruits and crops He grows? There is no true deity worthy of worship besides Him. So how now, after this, do you turn away from this truth and fabricate lies against Allah and claim that Allah has partners when He is the One who created all of you and provides for all of you?

Points of Benefit:

The disbelievers' terror on the Day of Resurrection will be a great sight.
The place where belief is beneficial is this life because it is the place for deeds to be done.
The greatness of the creation of the angels shows how great their Creator, the Exalted, is.
