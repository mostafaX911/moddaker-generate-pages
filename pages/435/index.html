(٤) وَإِن يُكَذِّبُوكَ فَقَدۡ كُذِّبَتۡ رُسُلٞ مِّن قَبۡلِكَۚ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ تُرۡجَعُ ٱلۡأُمُورُ

And if your people deny you, O Messenger, be patient because you are not the first messenger whose people denied him, as nations before you had rejected their prophets, such as 'Ād, Thamūd, and the people of Lot. And to Allah alone, all matters are returned, so He will destroy the deniers and help His Messengers and the believers.

(٥) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقّٞۖ فَلَا تَغُرَّنَّكُمُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَلَا يَغُرَّنَّكُم بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡغَرُورُ

O people, the resurrection and recompense on the Day of Resurrection, which Allah has promised you, are true without a doubt, so the pleasures of the worldly life and its temptations should not deceive you from preparing for this day through good actions, nor should the Devil deceive you by his beautifying of falsehood and by inciting you to incline towards the worldly life.

(٦) إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ لَكُمۡ عَدُوّٞ فَٱتَّخِذُوهُ عَدُوًّاۚ إِنَّمَا يَدۡعُواْ حِزۡبَهُۥ لِيَكُونُواْ مِنۡ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

O people, the Devil is your enemy whose enmity is everlasting, so take him as an enemy by being committed to fighting him. The Devil only calls his followers towards disbelief in Allah so that their outcome is to enter the burning fire on the Day of Resurrection.

(٧) ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ شَدِيدٞۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٞ وَأَجۡرٞ كَبِيرٌ

Those who disbelieve in Allah by following the Devil will have a severe punishment. And those who believe in Allah and do good actions will have forgiveness from Allah for their sins and a great reward from Him, which is Paradise.

(٨) أَفَمَن زُيِّنَ لَهُۥ سُوٓءُ عَمَلِهِۦ فَرَءَاهُ حَسَنٗاۖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُضِلُّ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُۖ فَلَا تَذۡهَبۡ نَفۡسُكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَسَرَٰتٍۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمُۢ بِمَا يَصۡنَعُونَ

The one for whom the Devil has embellished his evil actions, such that he thinks them to be good, is unlike the one for whom Allah has beautified the truth, so he believes it to be the truth because Allah leads astray whom He wants and guides whom He desires without anyone to compel Him. So do not harm yourself, O Messenger, out of grief over the misguidance of the misguided. Allah knows what they do. Nothing of their actions is hidden from Him.

(٩) وَٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَرۡسَلَ ٱلرِّيَٰحَ فَتُثِيرُ سَحَابٗا فَسُقۡنَٰهُ إِلَىٰ بَلَدٖ مَّيِّتٖ فَأَحۡيَيۡنَا بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ ٱلنُّشُورُ

Allah is the One who sent the winds; then these winds move clouds, then We lead these clouds to a land where there are no plants, then We give life through its water, causing plants to grow after being dead and dry. So just as We gave life to this land after its death, through the plants We placed in it, the resurrection of the dead will happen on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٠) مَن كَانَ يُرِيدُ ٱلۡعِزَّةَ فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡعِزَّةُ جَمِيعًاۚ إِلَيۡهِ يَصۡعَدُ ٱلۡكَلِمُ ٱلطَّيِّبُ وَٱلۡعَمَلُ ٱلصَّٰلِحُ يَرۡفَعُهُۥۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَمۡكُرُونَ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ شَدِيدٞۖ وَمَكۡرُ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُوَ يَبُورُ

Whoever wants honor in this world or the Hereafter should only seek it from Allah because Allah alone gives honor in both. His pure remembrance is elevated to Him, and the good deeds of His slaves are raised to Him. And those who plot evil plans, such as trying to kill the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, will have a severe punishment. The scheming of these disbelievers will perish, and no objective of theirs will be accomplished.

(١١) وَٱللَّهُ خَلَقَكُم مِّن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ ثُمَّ جَعَلَكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗاۚ وَمَا تَحۡمِلُ مِنۡ أُنثَىٰ وَلَا تَضَعُ إِلَّا بِعِلۡمِهِۦۚ وَمَا يُعَمَّرُ مِن مُّعَمَّرٖ وَلَا يُنقَصُ مِنۡ عُمُرِهِۦٓ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٍۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرٞ

Allah is the One who created your father, Adam, from the soil; then He created you from a sperm-drop; then He made you men and women who marry one another. And no female conceives a fetus or gives birth except with His knowledge. Nothing of that is hidden from Him. And the lifespan of any of His creations is not increased or decreased except that it is written in the Preserved Tablet. All of this, i.e., creating you from the soil, creating you in stages, and writing your lifespans in the Preserved Tablet, is easy for Allah.

Points of Benefit:

Comforting the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم by mentioning the stories of the Messengers with their people.
Being deceived by this life is a reason for turning away from the Truth.
Taking Satan as an enemy by taking the means that help keep him away, like making remembrance of Allah, reciting the Quran, doing acts of obedience, and abandoning acts of disobedience.
Affirming the attribute of Loftiness for Allah, the Exalted.
