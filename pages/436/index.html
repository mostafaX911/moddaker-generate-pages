(١٢) وَمَا يَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡبَحۡرَانِ هَٰذَا عَذۡبٞ فُرَاتٞ سَآئِغٞ شَرَابُهُۥ وَهَٰذَا مِلۡحٌ أُجَاجٞۖ وَمِن كُلّٖ تَأۡكُلُونَ لَحۡمٗا طَرِيّٗا وَتَسۡتَخۡرِجُونَ حِلۡيَةٗ تَلۡبَسُونَهَاۖ وَتَرَى ٱلۡفُلۡكَ فِيهِ مَوَاخِرَ لِتَبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

The two seas are not comparable: One is extremely sweet and palatable due to its sweetness, and the other is salty and bitter, which is not drinkable due to its extreme saltiness. And from each of the abovementioned seas, you eat fresh meat, which is fish, and you extract rubies and coral from them, which you wear as adornment. And you see the ships, splitting the sea as they sail, coming and going, so you can seek the grace of Allah through trade and thank Allah for the many favors He has given you.

(١٣) يُولِجُ ٱلَّيۡلَ فِي ٱلنَّهَارِ وَيُولِجُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فِي ٱلَّيۡلِ وَسَخَّرَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَۖ كُلّٞ يَجۡرِي لِأَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمۡ لَهُ ٱلۡمُلۡكُۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ مَا يَمۡلِكُونَ مِن قِطۡمِيرٍ

Allah slowly extends the night into the day, so it becomes longer, and the day into the night, so it becomes longer. And He made the sun and the moon subservient; each floats in its appointed orbit until a fixed time only Allah knows, which is the Day of Resurrection. Allah, your Lord, sets all of that and carries it out. To Him alone belongs the kingdom. And the idols you worship besides Him do not even possess the thin [flaky] membrane over the datestone, so how do you worship them besides Me?

(١٤) إِن تَدۡعُوهُمۡ لَا يَسۡمَعُواْ دُعَآءَكُمۡ وَلَوۡ سَمِعُواْ مَا ٱسۡتَجَابُواْ لَكُمۡۖ وَيَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ يَكۡفُرُونَ بِشِرۡكِكُمۡۚ وَلَا يُنَبِّئُكَ مِثۡلُ خَبِيرٖ

If you call your gods, they cannot hear your call because they are inanimate objects with no life and cannot hear. And even if they could listen to you calling them, they would not be able to respond to you. And on the Day of Resurrection, they will disassociate themselves from your idolatry and your worshipping them. O Messenger, nobody can inform you more truthfully than Allah.

(١٥) ۞يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ أَنتُمُ ٱلۡفُقَرَآءُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَٱللَّهُ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ

O people, you are the ones in need of Allah in all your affairs and all your situations. Allah is the Self-sufficient, who does not need you for anything. He is the Praiseworthy in this world and the Hereafter for what He decrees for His slaves.

(١٦) إِن يَشَأۡ يُذۡهِبۡكُمۡ وَيَأۡتِ بِخَلۡقٖ جَدِيدٖ

If He wanted to eradicate you by destroying you, He would do so and bring into existence a new creation to take your place who would worship Him and not associate any partners with Him.

(١٧) وَمَا ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ بِعَزِيزٖ

And eliminating you through annihilation and bringing a new creation into your place is not impossible for Allah.

(١٨) وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةٞ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰۚ وَإِن تَدۡعُ مُثۡقَلَةٌ إِلَىٰ حِمۡلِهَا لَا يُحۡمَلۡ مِنۡهُ شَيۡءٞ وَلَوۡ كَانَ ذَا قُرۡبَىٰٓۗ إِنَّمَا تُنذِرُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخۡشَوۡنَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَۚ وَمَن تَزَكَّىٰ فَإِنَّمَا يَتَزَكَّىٰ لِنَفۡسِهِۦۚ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

No soul will carry the sin of another, but rather, every sinning soul will bear the burden of its evil. And if a soul burdened with sins were to ask someone to carry some of its sins, nothing would be taken, even if the one being asked were a close relative. O Messenger, you can only warn of Allah's punishment to those who fear their Lord in private and complete the prayer most perfectly, for they are the ones who will take heed of your warning. Whoever is purified from his sins, the greatest of them being polytheism, then his purification will only benefit himself when he returns to his Lord. Allah does not need His slave's obedience. To Allah is the return on the Day of Resurrection for the reckoning and recompense.

Points of Benefit:

Harnessing the sea, alternating the night and day, and subjugating the sun and moon are among Allah's blessings upon the people. But they become used to these blessings and so ignore them.
The foolishness of the polytheists in calling upon idols that cannot hear or understand anything.
Being in need of Allah is always a characteristic of humans, and being Rich is one of Allah's perfect attributes.
Self-purification goes back to the slave; if he wants, he can maintain it, or if he wants, he can lose it.
