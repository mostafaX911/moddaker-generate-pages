(١٩) وَمَا يَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ وَٱلۡبَصِيرُ

The disbeliever and believer are not the same in status, just as blind people and those who can see are different.

(٢٠) وَلَا ٱلظُّلُمَٰتُ وَلَا ٱلنُّورُ

And disbelief and belief are not the same, just as darkness and light are different.

(٢١) وَلَا ٱلظِّلُّ وَلَا ٱلۡحَرُورُ

And Paradise and Hell do not have the same impact, just as shade and hot winds are different.

(٢٢) وَمَا يَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡأَحۡيَآءُ وَلَا ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰتُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُسۡمِعُ مَن يَشَآءُۖ وَمَآ أَنتَ بِمُسۡمِعٖ مَّن فِي ٱلۡقُبُورِ

And the believers and disbelievers are not the same, just as the living and the dead are different. Allah makes those whom He wants to guide listen attentively. And you, O Messenger, cannot make the disbelievers who are like the dead in the graves hear.

(٢٣) إِنۡ أَنتَ إِلَّا نَذِيرٌ

You are only to them a warner of Allah's punishment.

(٢٤) إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ بَشِيرٗا وَنَذِيرٗاۚ وَإِن مِّنۡ أُمَّةٍ إِلَّا خَلَا فِيهَا نَذِيرٞ

O Messenger, We have sent you with the truth in which there is no doubt to give good news to the believers of the generous reward Allah has in store for them and to warn the disbelievers of the painful punishment He has prepared for them. And there is no nation from those before except that there passed a messenger from Allah to warn them of His punishment.

(٢٥) وَإِن يُكَذِّبُوكَ فَقَدۡ كَذَّبَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ جَآءَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَبِٱلزُّبُرِ وَبِٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُنِيرِ

And if your people reject you, O Messenger, be patient because you are not the first messenger whose people rejected him, as the nations before rejected their messengers, such as 'Ād, Thamūd, and the people of Lot. Their messengers came to them with clear evidence and overwhelming miracles that prove their truthfulness, and their messengers came to them with scrolls and the Scripture, which is enlightening for one who ponders over it and contemplates it.

(٢٦) ثُمَّ أَخَذۡتُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْۖ فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ نَكِيرِ

But despite that, they disbelieved in Allah and His messengers and did not accept [the guidance] they came with from Him, so I destroyed those who disbelieved. So, imagine, O Messenger, how terrible my punishment was when I destroyed them.

(٢٧) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَخۡرَجۡنَا بِهِۦ ثَمَرَٰتٖ مُّخۡتَلِفًا أَلۡوَٰنُهَاۚ وَمِنَ ٱلۡجِبَالِ جُدَدُۢ بِيضٞ وَحُمۡرٞ مُّخۡتَلِفٌ أَلۡوَٰنُهَا وَغَرَابِيبُ سُودٞ

O Messenger, have you not seen that Allah sends rainwater from the sky, then We extract fruits of different colors from that same water, amongst them red, green, yellow, and others, once We have watered their trees with it? And from the mountains are white, red, and pitch-black tracts.

(٢٨) وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَٱلدَّوَآبِّ وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ مُخۡتَلِفٌ أَلۡوَٰنُهُۥ كَذَٰلِكَۗ إِنَّمَا يَخۡشَى ٱللَّهَ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِ ٱلۡعُلَمَٰٓؤُاْۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ غَفُورٌ

And amongst men, animals, and cattle (camels, cows, and sheep) are different colors like that aforementioned. Only those who exalt Allah and have a genuine fear of Him are the knowledgeable ones because they recognize His attributes, His sacred law, and the proof of His power. Allah is Mighty; no one can overpower Him. He forgives the sins of His slaves who repent.

(٢٩) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَتۡلُونَ كِتَٰبَ ٱللَّهِ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَنفَقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ سِرّٗا وَعَلَانِيَةٗ يَرۡجُونَ تِجَٰرَةٗ لَّن تَبُورَ

Those who read the Book of Allah, which We revealed to Our Messenger and practice what it contains, and complete the prayer in the best way, and who spend from what We have provided them, through Zakat and other forms, secretly and openly, seek through those actions a trade with Allah which will never bring them any loss.

(٣٠) لِيُوَفِّيَهُمۡ أُجُورَهُمۡ وَيَزِيدَهُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ غَفُورٞ شَكُورٞ

So that Allah can give them the full reward for their actions and increase them from His grace, as this is His domain. He forgives the sins of those described with these qualities and appreciates their righteous efforts.

Points of Benefit:

Negating equality between Truth and falsehood and their people.
The large number of messengers (peace be upon them) before our Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم is evidence of Allah's mercy and the creation's stubbornness.
Destroying the rejectors is an established divine practice.
The characteristics of belief are a profitable trade, while the attributes of disbelief are a trade at a loss.
