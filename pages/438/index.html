(٣١) وَٱلَّذِيٓ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ مِنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ هُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِعِبَادِهِۦ لَخَبِيرُۢ بَصِيرٞ

The Scripture We have revealed to you, O Messenger, is the truth in which there is no doubt. Allah has revealed it to affirm the precious scriptures. Allah is well-informed about His slaves, and He sees everything. He, therefore, reveals to the messenger of every nation what they need in their time.

(٣٢) ثُمَّ أَوۡرَثۡنَا ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱصۡطَفَيۡنَا مِنۡ عِبَادِنَاۖ فَمِنۡهُمۡ ظَالِمٞ لِّنَفۡسِهِۦ وَمِنۡهُم مُّقۡتَصِدٞ وَمِنۡهُمۡ سَابِقُۢ بِٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَضۡلُ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ

Then We gave the Quran to the nation of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم whom We have chosen over other nations. Some wronged their souls by doing prohibited acts and leaving out their obligations. Some were moderate by doing their duties and leaving out prohibited acts while doing some recommended actions, and some disliked actions. By Allah's leave, some were foremost in good deeds, doing their obligations and recommended acts and leaving out prohibited and disliked actions. The abovementioned choosing of this nation and its being given the Quran is the most excellent favor to which no blessing can come close.

(٣٣) جَنَّٰتُ عَدۡنٖ يَدۡخُلُونَهَا يُحَلَّوۡنَ فِيهَا مِنۡ أَسَاوِرَ مِن ذَهَبٖ وَلُؤۡلُؤٗاۖ وَلِبَاسُهُمۡ فِيهَا حَرِيرٞ

Gardens to settle in, which these chosen ones will enter. In it, they will wear bracelets of gold and pearls, and their garments therein will be silk.

(٣٤) وَقَالُواْ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَذۡهَبَ عَنَّا ٱلۡحَزَنَۖ إِنَّ رَبَّنَا لَغَفُورٞ شَكُورٌ

They will say after they enter Paradise: Praise be to Allah, Who has removed from us all grief because of our fear of entering the Fire. Our Lord forgives the sins of those slaves of His who repent and thanks them for their obedience to Him.

(٣٥) ٱلَّذِيٓ أَحَلَّنَا دَارَ ٱلۡمُقَامَةِ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ لَا يَمَسُّنَا فِيهَا نَصَبٞ وَلَا يَمَسُّنَا فِيهَا لُغُوبٞ

The One who has placed us in the Everlasting Home, out of His bounty, not by our might or strength, where no toil or fatigue will affect us.

After mentioning the reward of His righteous servants, Allah also mentions the punishment for the vile among them, referring to the disbelievers. He states:

(٣٦) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَهُمۡ نَارُ جَهَنَّمَ لَا يُقۡضَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ فَيَمُوتُواْ وَلَا يُخَفَّفُ عَنۡهُم مِّنۡ عَذَابِهَاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي كُلَّ كَفُورٖ

Those who disbelieve in Allah will be in the Hellfire, where they will remain forever. It will not be decreed for them to die so that they can escape the punishment, nor will the punishment of Hell be alleviated for them in any way. In this way, We will recompense every person who denies the favors of His Lord on the Day of Resurrection.

(٣٧) وَهُمۡ يَصۡطَرِخُونَ فِيهَا رَبَّنَآ أَخۡرِجۡنَا نَعۡمَلۡ صَٰلِحًا غَيۡرَ ٱلَّذِي كُنَّا نَعۡمَلُۚ أَوَ لَمۡ نُعَمِّرۡكُم مَّا يَتَذَكَّرُ فِيهِ مَن تَذَكَّرَ وَجَآءَكُمُ ٱلنَّذِيرُۖ فَذُوقُواْ فَمَا لِلظَّٰلِمِينَ مِن نَّصِيرٍ

They will cry out therein as loud as they can, seeking help by saying: Our Lord! Let us out from the Fire, and we will do righteous deeds, different from what we did before, so we can attain Your pleasure and be saved from Your punishment. Allah will respond to them thus: Did We not let you live a life long enough so that anyone who wanted to take heed could do so and do righteous deeds, and the Messenger came to you to warn you of Allah's punishment? Thus, you have no argument or excuses after all of this, so taste the chastisement of the Fire. Those who wrong themselves by disbelief and sin will have no helper to rescue them from Allah's punishment or lighten it.

(٣٨) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَٰلِمُ غَيۡبِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

Allah knows all that is hidden in the heavens and the Earth. Nothing of that passes Him by. He knows all the good and bad His slaves conceal in their chests.

Points of Benefit:

The merit of Muhammad's صلى الله عليه وسلم nation over all other nations.
The different levels of belief of the believers mean different levels in this world and the next.
Time is trust that must be preserved. Whoever wastes it will regret it when regret is of no benefit.
Allah's knowledge encompasses everything.
