(٣٩) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَكُمۡ خَلَٰٓئِفَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ فَمَن كَفَرَ فَعَلَيۡهِ كُفۡرُهُۥۖ وَلَا يَزِيدُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ كُفۡرُهُمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ إِلَّا مَقۡتٗاۖ وَلَا يَزِيدُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ كُفۡرُهُمۡ إِلَّا خَسَارٗا

O people, He made you successors of one another on Earth. Those who disbelieve Allah and what the messengers brought, then the sin and retribution of their disbelief will come back on them. Their disbelief does not harm their Lord. Their disbelief only adds to their Lord's displeasure and hatred for them. Their disbelief adds only to their loss, as they will lose what Allah would have prepared for them in Paradise had they believed.

(٤٠) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ شُرَكَآءَكُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَرُونِي مَاذَا خَلَقُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَمۡ لَهُمۡ شِرۡكٞ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ أَمۡ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمۡ كِتَٰبٗا فَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَيِّنَتٖ مِّنۡهُۚ بَلۡ إِن يَعِدُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ بَعۡضُهُم بَعۡضًا إِلَّا غُرُورًا

O Messenger, tell these polytheists: Tell me about your partners that you worship instead of Allah; what have they created on Earth? Did they make its mountains? Did they create its rivers? Did they create its animals? Or do they have any share with Allah in the creation of the heavens? Or have We given them a book proving the validity of worshipping their idols? Nothing of that is true. Those who wrong themselves by disbelief and sins promise one another nothing but delusion.

(٤١) ۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُمۡسِكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ أَن تَزُولَاۚ وَلَئِن زَالَتَآ إِنۡ أَمۡسَكَهُمَا مِنۡ أَحَدٖ مِّنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ حَلِيمًا غَفُورٗا

Allah (may He be Exalted) holds the heavens and the Earth, preventing them from disappearing, and were they to disappear, hypothetically, there is none after him (may He be Exalted) to keep them from doing so. He is Forbearing and does not rush to punish; He Forgives the sins of those of His slaves who repent.

(٤٢) وَأَقۡسَمُواْ بِٱللَّهِ جَهۡدَ أَيۡمَٰنِهِمۡ لَئِن جَآءَهُمۡ نَذِيرٞ لَّيَكُونُنَّ أَهۡدَىٰ مِنۡ إِحۡدَى ٱلۡأُمَمِۖ فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُمۡ نَذِيرٞ مَّا زَادَهُمۡ إِلَّا نُفُورًا

These rejecting disbelievers swore a solemn and affirmed oath that if a messenger from Allah came to them to warn them of His punishment, they would be more guided and follow the truth more than the Jews, Christians, and others. Yet when Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم came to them after being sent by His Lord and warned them of Allah's punishment, his coming only increased their hatred of the truth and adherence to falsehood. They did not fulfill their solemn oaths to be more guided than those who preceded them.

(٤٣) ٱسۡتِكۡبَارٗا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَكۡرَ ٱلسَّيِّيِٕۚ وَلَا يَحِيقُ ٱلۡمَكۡرُ ٱلسَّيِّئُ إِلَّا بِأَهۡلِهِۦۚ فَهَلۡ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّا سُنَّتَ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَۚ فَلَن تَجِدَ لِسُنَّتِ ٱللَّهِ تَبۡدِيلٗاۖ وَلَن تَجِدَ لِسُنَّتِ ٱللَّهِ تَحۡوِيلًا

The oaths they took in Allah's name were not taken in good faith and with a sound objective. It was only to be arrogant on Earth and to deceive people. An evil plot only rebounds on those who plotted it. So, are these arrogant plotters waiting for anything other than Allah's established practice, which is their destruction as He destroyed their predecessors like them? You will not find any change to Allah's method of destroying arrogant people such that the destruction evades them, nor is it diverted to others besides them because it is an established divine practice.

(٤٤) أَوَ لَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَيَنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ وَكَانُوٓاْ أَشَدَّ مِنۡهُمۡ قُوَّةٗۚ وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيُعۡجِزَهُۥ مِن شَيۡءٖ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَلَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ عَلِيمٗا قَدِيرٗا

Have these deniers not traveled the Earth and reflected on how those nations before them who had denied [the truth] met their end? Did they not meet an evil end by Allah destroying them, although they were superior to these people in strength? Allah is not to be outdone by anything in the heavens or Earth. He is the One who knows about the actions of these deniers. Nothing of their actions is hidden from nor escapes Him. He has the power to destroy them whenever He wills.

Points of Benefit:

Disbelief earns Allah's hatred and is a path to loss and misery.
The polytheists have no evidence, neither intellectual nor textual, to support their polytheism.
The oppressor's plan will destroy him sooner or later.
