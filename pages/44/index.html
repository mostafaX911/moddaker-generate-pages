(٢٦٠) وَإِذۡ قَالَ إِبۡرَٰهِ‍ۧمُ رَبِّ أَرِنِي كَيۡفَ تُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰۖ قَالَ أَوَ لَمۡ تُؤۡمِنۖ قَالَ بَلَىٰ وَلَٰكِن لِّيَطۡمَئِنَّ قَلۡبِيۖ قَالَ فَخُذۡ أَرۡبَعَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلطَّيۡرِ فَصُرۡهُنَّ إِلَيۡكَ ثُمَّ ٱجۡعَلۡ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ جَبَلٖ مِّنۡهُنَّ جُزۡءٗا ثُمَّ ٱدۡعُهُنَّ يَأۡتِينَكَ سَعۡيٗاۚ وَٱعۡلَمۡ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٞ

O Prophet, remember when Abraham said: O My Lord, show me how the dead come back to life. Allah asked him: Do you not believe in this? Abraham replied: I do believe, but I just want to put my heart at ease. So, Allah instructed him: Take four birds and tame them. Then, cut them up into pieces and place a part of each bird on the mountains around you. Then call those birds, and you will see that they will come quickly to you, being brought back to life. Know, O Abraham, that Allah is Mighty in His dominion and Wise in His instruction, legislation, and creation.

(٢٦١) مَّثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَهُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ حَبَّةٍ أَنۢبَتَتۡ سَبۡعَ سَنَابِلَ فِي كُلِّ سُنۢبُلَةٖ مِّاْئَةُ حَبَّةٖۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُضَٰعِفُ لِمَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ

The similitude of the reward of the believers who spend their wealth in Allah's path is like a grain planted by a farmer in fertile ground. This grain then produces seven spikes, each carrying a hundred grains. Allah multiplies the reward for whomever of His slaves He wishes and gives them without anything in return. Allah is Bountiful and Generous, and He knows who deserves their reward multiplied.

(٢٦٢) ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَهُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ لَا يُتۡبِعُونَ مَآ أَنفَقُواْ مَنّٗا وَلَآ أَذٗى لَّهُمۡ أَجۡرُهُمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ وَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

Those who spend their wealth in Allah's obedience and pleasure and do not nullify their reward afterward by reminding people of their favor by their words or actions will receive their reward from their Lord. They will not fear what awaits them, nor will they grieve over the past because of the great bliss they will enjoy.

(٢٦٣) ۞قَوۡلٞ مَّعۡرُوفٞ وَمَغۡفِرَةٌ خَيۡرٞ مِّن صَدَقَةٖ يَتۡبَعُهَآ أَذٗىۗ وَٱللَّهُ غَنِيٌّ حَلِيمٞ

A kind word that makes a believer happy or forgiving the ill-mannered one is better than charity, followed by the harm caused by reminding the person of the favor. Allah does not need His slaves; He is clement as He has not hasten to punish them.

(٢٦٤) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تُبۡطِلُواْ صَدَقَٰتِكُم بِٱلۡمَنِّ وَٱلۡأَذَىٰ كَٱلَّذِي يُنفِقُ مَالَهُۥ رِئَآءَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَا يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۖ فَمَثَلُهُۥ كَمَثَلِ صَفۡوَانٍ عَلَيۡهِ تُرَابٞ فَأَصَابَهُۥ وَابِلٞ فَتَرَكَهُۥ صَلۡدٗاۖ لَّا يَقۡدِرُونَ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٖ مِّمَّا كَسَبُواْۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, do not spoil the reward of your charity by boasting about it and harming the person to whom you gave it. A person who does this is like one who spends to be seen and praised by people and disbelieves in Allah and the reward and punishment of the Day of Resurrection. This is like a smooth stone on which there is some soil: when heavy rain falls onto it, the soil is washed away, and the stone is left smooth with nothing on it. Similarly, the reward of the deeds and charity of those who show off does not remain in the sight of Allah. Allah does not guide disbelievers to what pleases Him and what will benefit them regarding their deeds and charity.

Points of Benefit:

Levels of faith in Allah and the degrees of certainty in Him are diverse without limit. The more the slave reflects on the signs of Allah, whether in the revelation or the universe, the more his faith and certainty increase.
Allah resurrecting the creation after their death clearly indicates the perfection of His Power and the completeness of His Greatness; may He be Transcendent.
The virtue of spending in the way of Allah and the immense reward it carries when done with a sincere intention and not marred by harm or boasting that undermines the deed.
One of the best things a person can offer to others is good character in speech and action, along with pardoning those who wrong him.
