(٤٥) وَلَوۡ يُؤَاخِذُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلنَّاسَ بِمَا كَسَبُواْ مَا تَرَكَ عَلَىٰ ظَهۡرِهَا مِن دَآبَّةٖ وَلَٰكِن يُؤَخِّرُهُمۡ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۖ فَإِذَا جَآءَ أَجَلُهُمۡ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِعِبَادِهِۦ بَصِيرَۢا

If Allah were to rush to punish people for the wrong they have done and the sins they perpetrated, He would have destroyed all the people on Earth at once, together with the animals and wealth they own. However, He (may He be Exalted) gives them respite until a fixed time, the Day of Resurrection. When the Day of Resurrection comes, Allah is fully aware of what His slaves used to do, and nothing remains hidden from Him. He will then recompense them for their actions, whether good or bad.

Ya Sin (Surah Ya-Sin)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Affirming the message, the resurrection, and detailing proof for both.

Explanation:

(١) يسٓ

Yā Sīn – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ

Allah swears by the Quran, whose verses have been perfectly arranged and to which no falsehood approaches it from any direction.

(٣) إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

You, O Messenger, are one of the messengers Allah sent to His slaves, instructing them to declare His Oneness and to worship Him alone.

(٤) عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

Upon a straight methodology and upright law. This straight methodology and upright law are revealed from your Lord, the Mighty, whom no one can challenge. He is Merciful to His slaves who believe in Him.

(٥) تَنزِيلَ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

Upon a straight methodology and upright law. This straight methodology and upright law are revealed from your Lord, the Mighty, whom no one can challenge. He is Merciful to His slaves who believe in Him.

(٦) لِتُنذِرَ قَوۡمٗا مَّآ أُنذِرَ ءَابَآؤُهُمۡ فَهُمۡ غَٰفِلُونَ

We revealed it to you to warn and exhort a nation, the Arabs, to whom no messenger has come to warn them, and as a result, they are heedless of faith and monotheism. Likewise, every nation that has been cut off from the warning needs messengers, scholars, and preachers to remind them.

(٧) لَقَدۡ حَقَّ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ عَلَىٰٓ أَكۡثَرِهِمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

The punishment from Allah is necessary for most of these people after the truth from Allah conveyed to them by His Messenger reached them, and they did not believe in it and remained in their disbelief. They do not believe in Allah nor His Messenger and do not act on the truth that came to them.

(٨) إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَا فِيٓ أَعۡنَٰقِهِمۡ أَغۡلَٰلٗا فَهِيَ إِلَى ٱلۡأَذۡقَانِ فَهُم مُّقۡمَحُونَ

Their example is like those who have shackles that cover their entire neck and whose hands are tied to their necks under their chins. As a result, they are forced to lift their heads to the sky and cannot lower them. These people are shackled from faith in Allah; therefore, they do not submit to it or lower their heads on its account.

(٩) وَجَعَلۡنَا مِنۢ بَيۡنِ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ سَدّٗا وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِمۡ سَدّٗا فَأَغۡشَيۡنَٰهُمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يُبۡصِرُونَ

We placed a barrier to the truth before and behind them. We covered their eyes from the truth so they could not see in a manner that benefited them. This happened to them after their stubbornness and persistence in disbelief became apparent.

(١٠) وَسَوَآءٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَأَنذَرۡتَهُمۡ أَمۡ لَمۡ تُنذِرۡهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

It is all the same to these disbelievers who oppose the truth, whether you, O Muhammad, warn them or you do not warn them. They will not accept what you have brought from Allah.

(١١) إِنَّمَا تُنذِرُ مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَخَشِيَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنَ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِۖ فَبَشِّرۡهُ بِمَغۡفِرَةٖ وَأَجۡرٖ كَرِيمٍ

The person who benefits from your warning is the one who accepts this Quran and follows what is in it and who fears His Lord in solitude where nobody else sees him. So, tell those with these attributes the pleasing news of Allah expiating their sins and forgiving them and of a great reward awaiting them in the Hereafter, which is entering Paradise.

(١٢) إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَنَكۡتُبُ مَا قَدَّمُواْ وَءَاثَٰرَهُمۡۚ وَكُلَّ شَيۡءٍ أَحۡصَيۡنَٰهُ فِيٓ إِمَامٖ مُّبِينٖ

Indeed, We shall bring the dead back to life by resurrecting them to give account on the Day of Resurrection. We record the good and the evil deeds that they send forth in this life to be rewarded for in the next, and we also record the excellent and harmful effects of their actions, such as the good that comes out of charity after they pass away and the evil that results of their misdeeds. We counted everything and recorded it in a clear book, which is the preserved tablet.

Points of Benefit:

Stubbornness stops one from being guided to the Truth.
Acting upon the Quran and revering Allah causes one to enter Paradise.
The virtue of the righteous child, the charity that continues (after death), and the likes for the believing slave.
