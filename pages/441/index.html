(١٣) وَٱضۡرِبۡ لَهُم مَّثَلًا أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِ إِذۡ جَآءَهَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ

Give O Messenger, these stubborn deniers, an example that will be a lesson for them, which is the story of the people of the town when their messengers came to them.

(١٤) إِذۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِمُ ٱثۡنَيۡنِ فَكَذَّبُوهُمَا فَعَزَّزۡنَا بِثَالِثٖ فَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّآ إِلَيۡكُم مُّرۡسَلُونَ

When We first sent them two messengers to call them to Allah's Oneness and His worship, they rejected them. Then we sent as reinforcement for these two a third Messenger with them. The three messengers said to the townspeople: All three of us have been sent to call you to Allah's Oneness and follow His law.

(١٥) قَالُواْ مَآ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا بَشَرٞ مِّثۡلُنَا وَمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ مِن شَيۡءٍ إِنۡ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا تَكۡذِبُونَ

The people of the town said to those who were sent: You are only men like us. You do not have any special status over us. The Merciful has not sent down any revelation to you. You are only lying about Allah in this claim of yours.

(١٦) قَالُواْ رَبُّنَا يَعۡلَمُ إِنَّآ إِلَيۡكُمۡ لَمُرۡسَلُونَ

The three messengers said in response to the denial by the people of the town: Our Lord knows that we have been sent by Him to you, O people of the city. That is enough evidence for us.

(١٧) وَمَا عَلَيۡنَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡبَلَٰغُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

We only must convey what we have been instructed to. We have no control over your guidance.

(١٨) قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا تَطَيَّرۡنَا بِكُمۡۖ لَئِن لَّمۡ تَنتَهُواْ لَنَرۡجُمَنَّكُمۡ وَلَيَمَسَّنَّكُم مِّنَّا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

The people of the town said to the messengers: We see an evil omen in you. If you do not stop calling us to monotheism, we will punish you by stoning you to death, and [in that way] you will receive a painful punishment.

(١٩) قَالُواْ طَٰٓئِرُكُم مَّعَكُمۡ أَئِن ذُكِّرۡتُمۚ بَلۡ أَنتُمۡ قَوۡمٞ مُّسۡرِفُونَ

The messengers responded: Your evil omen is within yourselves because you denied Allah and abandoned following His messengers. Do you see a bad omen if we remind you of Allah? You are a people who have gone too far in the perpetration of disbelief and sins.

(٢٠) وَجَآءَ مِنۡ أَقۡصَا ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ رَجُلٞ يَسۡعَىٰ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱتَّبِعُواْ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

From a far part of the town, a man came running, fearing his people's denial of the messengers and their threatening to kill or harm them. He said: O my people, follow what these messengers have brought.

(٢١) ٱتَّبِعُواْ مَن لَّا يَسۡ‍َٔلُكُمۡ أَجۡرٗا وَهُم مُّهۡتَدُونَ

O people, follow those who do not ask you for any reward for conveying what they brought and are rightly guided in the revelation of Allah that they bring to you. Whoever is like that deserves to be followed.

(٢٢) وَمَا لِيَ لَآ أَعۡبُدُ ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَنِي وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

This man offering sincere advice said: What prevents me from worshipping Allah Who created me? What prevents you from worshipping your Lord Who made you, and you will return to Him when resurrected for the recompense?

(٢٣) ءَأَتَّخِذُ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ ءَالِهَةً إِن يُرِدۡنِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ بِضُرّٖ لَّا تُغۡنِ عَنِّي شَفَٰعَتُهُمۡ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا يُنقِذُونِ

Shall I take other deities to be wrongly worshipped instead of Allah, Who created me? If the Merciful intends harm for me, the intercession of these other deities will not benefit me at all, nor will they be able to save me from the harm Allah intended for me if I die as a disbeliever.

(٢٤) إِنِّيٓ إِذٗا لَّفِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٍ

If I were to take them as deities besides Allah, I would be in clear error as I would have worshipped a being that does not deserve worship and abandoned worshiping the One who deserves it.

(٢٥) إِنِّيٓ ءَامَنتُ بِرَبِّكُمۡ فَٱسۡمَعُونِ

O my people, I have believed in our Lord, so listen to me. I do not care about the threats on my life that you make. But his people insisted on killing him, and Allah admitted him into Paradise.

(٢٦) قِيلَ ٱدۡخُلِ ٱلۡجَنَّةَۖ قَالَ يَٰلَيۡتَ قَوۡمِي يَعۡلَمُونَ

It was said to honor this man after his people killed him: Enter Paradise after you were killed as a martyr. When he entered it and saw the delight therein, he said longingly: If only my people who rejected and killed me knew about the forgiveness of sins that I obtained and what my Lord has honored me with, they would have believed like how I believed, and they would have attained a reward similar to mine.

(٢٧) بِمَا غَفَرَ لِي رَبِّي وَجَعَلَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُكۡرَمِينَ

It was said to honor this man after his people killed him: Enter Paradise after you were killed as a martyr. When he entered it and saw the delight therein, he said longingly: If only my people who rejected and killed me knew about the forgiveness of sins that I obtained and what my Lord has honored me with, they would have believed like how I believed, and they would have attained a reward similar to mine.

Points of Benefit:

The importance of stories when calling to Allah.
Belief in good and bad omens is from the acts of the disbelievers.
Advising the people of Truth is obligatory.
Loving good for the people is a characteristic of the believers.
