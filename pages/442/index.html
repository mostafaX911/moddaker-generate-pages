(٢٨) ۞وَمَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ مِن جُندٖ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا كُنَّا مُنزِلِينَ

And We did not send down an army of angels from heaven to destroy his people who rejected and killed him, nor do We need to send angels upon nations to destroy them. Their matter is easier for Us than that as We have decreed that their decimation would occur simply with a cry from the heavens, without sending down angels of torment.

(٢٩) إِن كَانَتۡ إِلَّا صَيۡحَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ فَإِذَا هُمۡ خَٰمِدُونَ

Thus, the story of the destruction of his people was simply a single cry that We sent upon them, and suddenly, they fell down with nothing remaining. Their example is like a fire that was burning and then extinguished, leaving behind no trace.

(٣٠) يَٰحَسۡرَةً عَلَى ٱلۡعِبَادِۚ مَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

Alas, for the slaves who rejected, how regretful will their situation be on the Day of Resurrection when they see the torment! That is because in this world, whenever any messenger from Allah came to them, they used to mock and ridicule him. Therefore, their outcome will be regret on the Day of Resurrection for neglecting their duty to Allah.

(٣١) أَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ كَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا قَبۡلَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡقُرُونِ أَنَّهُمۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡ لَا يَرۡجِعُونَ

Did these people who reject and ridicule the messengers not see a lesson in the nations who preceded them? They passed away and did not return to this world a second time. Instead, they met the deeds they had sent forth, and Allah will recompense them accordingly for that.

(٣٢) وَإِن كُلّٞ لَّمَّا جَمِيعٞ لَّدَيۡنَا مُحۡضَرُونَ

All nations, without exception, will be brought before Us on the Day of Resurrection after being resurrected so that We may recompense them for their deeds accordingly.

(٣٣) وَءَايَةٞ لَّهُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ ٱلۡمَيۡتَةُ أَحۡيَيۡنَٰهَا وَأَخۡرَجۡنَا مِنۡهَا حَبّٗا فَمِنۡهُ يَأۡكُلُونَ

A sign for those who reject the resurrection that the resurrection is true is this dry and barren land, on which We sent down rain from the sky, and We caused various types of plants and grains to grow therein for people to eat. The One who gave life to this earth by sending down the rain and bringing out the plants can give life to and resurrect the dead.

(٣٤) وَجَعَلۡنَا فِيهَا جَنَّٰتٖ مِّن نَّخِيلٖ وَأَعۡنَٰبٖ وَفَجَّرۡنَا فِيهَا مِنَ ٱلۡعُيُونِ

In this earth, on which We sent down rain, we have placed gardens of date palms and grapes and caused water springs to gush forth to irrigate them.

(٣٥) لِيَأۡكُلُواْ مِن ثَمَرِهِۦ وَمَا عَمِلَتۡهُ أَيۡدِيهِمۡۚ أَفَلَا يَشۡكُرُونَ

So that the people can eat fruits from those gardens that Allah has favored them with without any effort from themselves. Will they then not thank Allah for these favors by worshiping Him alone and believing in His messengers?

(٣٦) سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَزۡوَٰجَ كُلَّهَا مِمَّا تُنۢبِتُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمِنۡ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَمِمَّا لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

Sanctified and Exalted is Allah, Who created various types of plants and trees. He made people into males and females and made other creations of Allah that people do not know of on land, sea, and other places.

(٣٧) وَءَايَةٞ لَّهُمُ ٱلَّيۡلُ نَسۡلَخُ مِنۡهُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فَإِذَا هُم مُّظۡلِمُونَ

A sign that indicates people to Allah's Oneness is that We remove the daylight as the day draws to an end and night comes, and We bring the darkness as the day withdraws from it, then suddenly, the people are in complete darkness.

(٣٨) وَٱلشَّمۡسُ تَجۡرِي لِمُسۡتَقَرّٖ لَّهَاۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَقۡدِيرُ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ

A sign to them that Allah is One is this sun that runs on a fixed course that Allah knows and does not go beyond that. That determination is the determination of the Mighty, who no one overpowers, the Omniscient from Whom nothing of the affairs of His creation is hidden.

(٣٩) وَٱلۡقَمَرَ قَدَّرۡنَٰهُ مَنَازِلَ حَتَّىٰ عَادَ كَٱلۡعُرۡجُونِ ٱلۡقَدِيمِ

A sign for them that indicates His Oneness (may He be Exalted) is this moon for which We have determined phases each night. It starts small, becomes big, and then small again until it becomes similar to a twisted, old date stalk, appearing thin, curved, yellow, and old.

(٤٠) لَا ٱلشَّمۡسُ يَنۢبَغِي لَهَآ أَن تُدۡرِكَ ٱلۡقَمَرَ وَلَا ٱلَّيۡلُ سَابِقُ ٱلنَّهَارِۚ وَكُلّٞ فِي فَلَكٖ يَسۡبَحُونَ

These signs: the sun, moon, night, and day, are predetermined by Allah's divine determination and do not surpass what has been determined for them. The sun cannot catch up with the moon to change its course or take away its light, nor can the night outpace the day and enter upon it before its time ends. All these subservient creations and other creations, such as the stars and galaxies, have their courses as per Allah's determination and protection.

Points of Benefit:

People are lesser to Allah if they disobey Him, and how generous He is to them if they obey Him.
One of the proofs of the resurrection is reviving the lifeless earth with green plants and bringing grains out of it.
Among the evidence of Allah's Oneness is the creation of creatures in the heavens and Earth, managed by Allah's destiny.
