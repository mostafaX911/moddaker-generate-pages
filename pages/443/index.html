(٤١) وَءَايَةٞ لَّهُمۡ أَنَّا حَمَلۡنَا ذُرِّيَّتَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ ٱلۡمَشۡحُونِ

Another sign that indicates to them Allah's Oneness and His favor to His slaves is that We carried those of Adam's progeny (peace be upon Him) who were saved from the flood in the time of Noah (peace be upon Him) in the ship that was filled with Allah's creations. Allah carried therein a pair of every species.

(٤٢) وَخَلَقۡنَا لَهُم مِّن مِّثۡلِهِۦ مَا يَرۡكَبُونَ

A sign that indicates to them Allah's Oneness and His favor to His slaves is that We created conveyances like the ship of Noah for them.

(٤٣) وَإِن نَّشَأۡ نُغۡرِقۡهُمۡ فَلَا صَرِيخَ لَهُمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يُنقَذُونَ

If We wished to drown them, We would have done so. Then there would be no one to help them if We wanted to drown them and no one to rescue them if they drowned by Our command and decree.

(٤٤) إِلَّا رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنَّا وَمَتَٰعًا إِلَىٰ حِينٖ

Except if We show mercy to them by rescuing them from drowning and returning them to enjoy themselves until a specified time that they will not go beyond so that they take a lesson and subsequently believe.

(٤٥) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱتَّقُواْ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ

When these polytheists who turn away from belief are told to beware of what they are heaving towards in the Hereafter and its difficulties and beware of the world behind them, in the hope that Allah will favor them with His mercy, they do not comply with that. Instead, they turn away from it without any care.

(٤٦) وَمَا تَأۡتِيهِم مِّنۡ ءَايَةٖ مِّنۡ ءَايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ إِلَّا كَانُواْ عَنۡهَا مُعۡرِضِينَ

Whenever Allah's signs indicating His Oneness and His deserving being worshipped alone come to these stubborn polytheists, they turn away from it without taking heed.

(٤٧) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمۡ أَنفِقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَنُطۡعِمُ مَن لَّوۡ يَشَآءُ ٱللَّهُ أَطۡعَمَهُۥٓ إِنۡ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ

When these stubborn people are told to assist the poor and the needy with the wealth Allah has provided them with, they disapprovingly reply to the believers: Should we feed those whom Allah could feed if He wanted to?! We do not want to oppose His will. You, O believers, are simply in clear error and far from the truth.

(٤٨) وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

The disbelievers who deny the resurrection say in rejection of it, regarding it as far-fetched: When is this resurrection, O believers, if you are true in your claim that it is to occur?

(٤٩) مَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّا صَيۡحَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ تَأۡخُذُهُمۡ وَهُمۡ يَخِصِّمُونَ

But all these rejectors of resurrection who regard it as far-fetched are only waiting for the first blow when the trumpet will be blown, suddenly overtaking them while they are in their worldly pursuits of buying, selling, irrigating, herding, and other engagements.

(٥٠) فَلَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ تَوۡصِيَةٗ وَلَآ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ

When this cry surprises them, they will not be able to make a bequest for one another and will not be able to return to their homes and families. Instead, they will die while they are in their worldly occupations.

(٥١) وَنُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَإِذَا هُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَجۡدَاثِ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَنسِلُونَ

The trumpet will be blown a second time for the resurrection, and suddenly, they will all come out of their graves, rushing towards their Lord for reckoning and recompense.

(٥٢) قَالُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَا مَنۢ بَعَثَنَا مِن مَّرۡقَدِنَاۜۗ هَٰذَا مَا وَعَدَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وَصَدَقَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ

These disbelievers who reject the resurrection will say in regret: What a loss of ours! Who has resurrected us from our graves? They will be given a response to their question: This is what Allah promised, and it must occur, and the messengers were truthful in respect to what they conveyed from their Lord in this regard.

(٥٣) إِن كَانَتۡ إِلَّا صَيۡحَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ فَإِذَا هُمۡ جَمِيعٞ لَّدَيۡنَا مُحۡضَرُونَ

The resurrection from the graves will only result from a second blowing of the trumpet, and all creation will be presented before Us on the Day of Resurrection for reckoning.

(٥٤) فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ لَا تُظۡلَمُ نَفۡسٞ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا تُجۡزَوۡنَ إِلَّا مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

Judgment will be just on that day, and you, O slaves, will not be wronged in the least by increasing from your bad deeds or decreasing from your good deeds. You will simply be given the full recompense for what you used to do in this worldly life.

Points of Benefit:

One of the techniques of Allah's upbringing of His slaves is that He has placed verses in front of them so they can use them as evidence of what will benefit them in their religion and world.
The Exalted Allah empowered His slaves and gave them the strength with which they were able to do the command and avoid the prohibition. If they left what they were commanded to do, that was a choice on their part.
