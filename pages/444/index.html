(٥٥) إِنَّ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ فِي شُغُلٖ فَٰكِهُونَ

The people of Paradise on the Day of Resurrection will be too preoccupied to think about others because of the permanent bliss and great success they will see. They will be enjoying that happily.

(٥٦) هُمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجُهُمۡ فِي ظِلَٰلٍ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِ مُتَّكِ‍ُٔونَ

They and their spouses will comfortably recline on couches under lush shades in Paradise.

(٥٧) لَهُمۡ فِيهَا فَٰكِهَةٞ وَلَهُم مَّا يَدَّعُونَ

In Paradise, they will have various types of wholesome fruits, such as grapes, figs, pomegranates, and any form of enjoyment or bliss they ask for. Whatever they ask for, they will get.

(٥٨) سَلَٰمٞ قَوۡلٗا مِّن رَّبّٖ رَّحِيمٖ

Over and above this bliss, they will receive peace in the form of a statement from a Lord Who is merciful to them. So when He greets them with peace, they will attain safety in every sense of the word and receive a greeting that no other greeting can surpass.

(٥٩) وَٱمۡتَٰزُواْ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ

The polytheists will be told on the Day of Resurrection: Step away from the believers because it is inappropriate for them to be with you due to the disparity between your recompense and theirs, and your attributes and theirs.

(٦٠) ۞أَلَمۡ أَعۡهَدۡ إِلَيۡكُمۡ يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ أَن لَّا تَعۡبُدُواْ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمۡ عَدُوّٞ مُّبِينٞ

Did I not advise and instruct you on the tongues of My messengers and say to you: O children of Adam! Do not follow the Devil by committing various types of disbelief and acts of disobedience. The Devil is a clear enemy to you. How can any sensible person follow an enemy of his who has made his enmity apparent?

(٦١) وَأَنِ ٱعۡبُدُونِيۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٞ مُّسۡتَقِيمٞ

And I commanded you, O children of Adam, to worship Me alone and not associate anything with Me, for worshiping Me alone and obeying Me is a straight path that leads to My pleasure and entry into Paradise. But, you did not comply with what I advised and commanded you to do.

(٦٢) وَلَقَدۡ أَضَلَّ مِنكُمۡ جِبِلّٗا كَثِيرًاۖ أَفَلَمۡ تَكُونُواْ تَعۡقِلُونَ

The Devil has misled many people from among you. Did you not have intellects that would instruct you to obey your Lord and worship Him alone (may He be Exalted) and warn you from obeying the Devil, your clear enemy?

(٦٣) هَٰذِهِۦ جَهَنَّمُ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ

This is Hell, which you were promised in the worldly life for your disbelief. It was a matter of the ghayb (unseen) to you, but today, you can see it with your own eyes.

(٦٤) ٱصۡلَوۡهَا ٱلۡيَوۡمَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡفُرُونَ

Enter it today and endure its heat because you disbelieved in Allah in this worldly life.

(٦٥) ٱلۡيَوۡمَ نَخۡتِمُ عَلَىٰٓ أَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَتُكَلِّمُنَآ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَتَشۡهَدُ أَرۡجُلُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ

Today, We will seal their mouths, and they will become mute, unable to deny the disbelief and acts of disobedience they committed. Their hands will speak to us about what they did in the world. Their feet will testify about the acts of disobedience they committed and walked towards.

(٦٦) وَلَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَطَمَسۡنَا عَلَىٰٓ أَعۡيُنِهِمۡ فَٱسۡتَبَقُواْ ٱلصِّرَٰطَ فَأَنَّىٰ يُبۡصِرُونَ

If We wished to remove their sight, We could have done so, and they would have been unable to see; they would then have rushed towards the bridge to cross it into Paradise, but it is unlikely that they would cross it while their sight is lost.

(٦٧) وَلَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَمَسَخۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَكَانَتِهِمۡ فَمَا ٱسۡتَطَٰعُواْ مُضِيّٗا وَلَا يَرۡجِعُونَ

If We wished to alter their form and make them sit on their feet, We could have done so, and they would have been unable to move forward or backward.

(٦٨) وَمَن نُّعَمِّرۡهُ نُنَكِّسۡهُ فِي ٱلۡخَلۡقِۚ أَفَلَا يَعۡقِلُونَ

Whoever's life, among humanity, We extend by lengthening his lifespan; We will eventually return him to the stage of weakness. Will they not think with their intellect and understand that this abode is not one of permanence and immortality and that the everlasting abode is that of the Hereafter!?

(٦٩) وَمَا عَلَّمۡنَٰهُ ٱلشِّعۡرَ وَمَا يَنۢبَغِي لَهُۥٓۚ إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرٞ وَقُرۡءَانٞ مُّبِينٞ

We did not teach Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم poetry, nor is that appropriate for him because it is not his nature, nor does his disposition demand it. So, you cannot claim that he is a poet. We have only taught him a reminder and a clear Quran for those who ponder it.

(٧٠) لِّيُنذِرَ مَن كَانَ حَيّٗا وَيَحِقَّ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

To warn those whose hearts are alive and still have illuminated insight, for they are the ones who will benefit from it, and to appoint the punishment against the disbelievers when the evidence was established against them through its revelation and invitation reaching them, leaving them with no excuse.

Points of Benefit:

On the Day of Resurrection, the mercy of their Lord, which wasn't in their minds, will be revealed to the people of faith.
The people of Paradise are pleased with everything the soul desires, the eyes delight, and the wishers wish for it.
The one with a genuine heart is the one who purifies himself with the Qur'an, increases his knowledge, and works from it.
Human organs will bear witness to him [or her] on the Day of Resurrection.
