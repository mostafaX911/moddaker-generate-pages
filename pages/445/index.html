(٧١) أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّا خَلَقۡنَا لَهُم مِّمَّا عَمِلَتۡ أَيۡدِينَآ أَنۡعَٰمٗا فَهُمۡ لَهَا مَٰلِكُونَ

Have they not seen that We have created livestock for them, and they control the affairs of that livestock by doing with it according to their interests?

(٧٢) وَذَلَّلۡنَٰهَا لَهُمۡ فَمِنۡهَا رَكُوبُهُمۡ وَمِنۡهَا يَأۡكُلُونَ

We have subjugated livestock for them and made it submissive to them; they ride on the backs of some, transport their loads on the backs of some, and consume some of its meat.

(٧٣) وَلَهُمۡ فِيهَا مَنَٰفِعُ وَمَشَارِبُۚ أَفَلَا يَشۡكُرُونَ

They also have benefits in the livestock other than just riding and consuming its meat, such as wool, bristles, fur, and value, which they use to make spreads and clothing. They can also drink its milk. So, will they not show gratitude to Allah, who has blessed them with these and other favors?

(٧٤) وَٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ ءَالِهَةٗ لَّعَلَّهُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ

The polytheists have taken other deities instead of Allah, whom they worship, hoping they will help them and save them from Allah's torment.

(٧٥) لَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ نَصۡرَهُمۡ وَهُمۡ لَهُمۡ جُندٞ مُّحۡضَرُونَ

Those deities they have taken cannot help themselves, let alone help those who worship them instead of Allah. The worshippers and their idols will all be brought together to torment but will all protest innocence from one another.

(٧٦) فَلَا يَحۡزُنكَ قَوۡلُهُمۡۘ إِنَّا نَعۡلَمُ مَا يُسِرُّونَ وَمَا يُعۡلِنُونَ

O Messenger, do not let their words, "You are not a messenger," or "You are a poet," and other forms of slander, upset you. Indeed, We know what they say privately and publicly; nothing is hidden from Us, and We will recompense them for it accordingly.

(٧٧) أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ أَنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰهُ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ فَإِذَا هُوَ خَصِيمٞ مُّبِينٞ

Does the human who denies the resurrection after death not ponder that: We created him from semen, then he went through different phases until he was born and developed, turning into a very argumentative and quarreling person? Does he not ponder over that to conclude that resurrection is possible?

(٧٨) وَضَرَبَ لَنَا مَثَلٗا وَنَسِيَ خَلۡقَهُۥۖ قَالَ مَن يُحۡيِ ٱلۡعِظَٰمَ وَهِيَ رَمِيمٞ

This disbeliever is heedless and ignorant when he uses decayed bones to prove the impossibility of resurrection, saying, "Who will resurrect it?" while forgetting his own creation from non-existence.

(٧٩) قُلۡ يُحۡيِيهَا ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَهَآ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ خَلۡقٍ عَلِيمٌ

O Muhammad! Reply to them: Whoever created these bones in the first place will bring them back to life because the One who initially created them is not incapable of returning life to them. He (may He be Exalted) is the knower of everything; nothing is hidden from Him.

(٨٠) ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلشَّجَرِ ٱلۡأَخۡضَرِ نَارٗا فَإِذَآ أَنتُم مِّنۡهُ تُوقِدُونَ

The One who has made for you, O people, lush green trees from which you can ignite a fire. So the One who [can] combine two opposites: the moisture of the green trees and the fire ignited from them, is also capable of reviving the dead.

(٨١) أَوَ لَيۡسَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِقَٰدِرٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يَخۡلُقَ مِثۡلَهُمۚ بَلَىٰ وَهُوَ ٱلۡخَلَّٰقُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

Is the One who created the heavens and the Earth, despite their grandeur, incapable of bringing the dead back to life after causing their death? Of course not! He is capable of it; He is the Creator who created all of creation and is Omniscient of them, and nothing is hidden from Him.

(٨٢) إِنَّمَآ أَمۡرُهُۥٓ إِذَآ أَرَادَ شَيۡ‍ًٔا أَن يَقُولَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ

The command and nature of Allah is such that when He intends to bring something into existence, all He says to it is "Be!" and then the thing that He intends comes into existence. Included in what He intends is to give life, cause death, resurrect, and so forth.

(٨٣) فَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي بِيَدِهِۦ مَلَكُوتُ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

So free and sanctified is Allah of the incapability the polytheists ascribe to Him. He is the One to whom total dominion belongs over everything, and He does as He wishes with it; the keys to everything are in His Hand, and you shall all return to Him in the Hereafter, where He will recompense you for your deeds accordingly.

Points of Benefit:

Among Allah's grace and blessings upon people is providing livestock for them and harnessing it for their various benefits.
The abundance of rational evidence on the Day of Resurrection and the polytheists turning away from it.
One of the attributes of the Exalted Allah is that His knowledge surrounds all His creatures in all their conditions at all times. He knows what the earth reduces in terms of the bodies of the dead and what remains, and He knows the unseen and the seen.
