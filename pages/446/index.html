Those Arranged in Ranks (Surah As-Saffat)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Declaring Allah's exaltedness from what the polytheists ascribe to Him and invalidating their claims regarding the angels and Jinn.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلصَّٰٓفَّٰتِ صَفّٗا

I take an oath by the angels who form compact rows in their worship.

(٢) فَٱلزَّٰجِرَٰتِ زَجۡرٗا

And He swore an oath by the angels who push the rainclouds and send them wherever Allah wills for it to rain.

(٣) فَٱلتَّٰلِيَٰتِ ذِكۡرًا

And I take an oath by the angels who recite the Speech of Allah.

(٤) إِنَّ إِلَٰهَكُمۡ لَوَٰحِدٞ

O people, your true deity is one and has no partner: He is Allah.

(٥) رَّبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا وَرَبُّ ٱلۡمَشَٰرِقِ

The Lord of the heavens, Earth, whatever is between them, and the Lord of the sun in all its rising and setting places throughout the year.

(٦) إِنَّا زَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا بِزِينَةٍ ٱلۡكَوَاكِبِ

We have beautified the heaven nearest to Earth with beautiful decorations: stars that appear like sparkling jewels.

(٧) وَحِفۡظٗا مِّن كُلِّ شَيۡطَٰنٖ مَّارِدٖ

And We have protected the nearest heaven with shooting stars against every rebellious devil who abandons Allah's obedience.

(٨) لَّا يَسَّمَّعُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡمَلَإِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ وَيُقۡذَفُونَ مِن كُلِّ جَانِبٖ

These devils cannot hear the angels in the sky when discussing the sacred law and the divine decree Allah revealed to them. They (devils) are pelted with shooting stars from all sides.

(٩) دُحُورٗاۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ وَاصِبٌ

To repel and drive them away from eavesdropping. And in the Hereafter, they will suffer a painful, everlasting punishment that will not end.

(١٠) إِلَّا مَنۡ خَطِفَ ٱلۡخَطۡفَةَ فَأَتۡبَعَهُۥ شِهَابٞ ثَاقِبٞ

Except for those devils who happen to snatch something quickly, which is a statement that the angels discuss among themselves, which the people of the Earth have yet to receive information of. A shooting star then subsequently goes after him and burns him. Sometimes, the devil is able to pass that statement on to his companion before the shooting star burns him, and it reaches the fortune-tellers, who tell a hundred lies with it.

(١١) فَٱسۡتَفۡتِهِمۡ أَهُمۡ أَشَدُّ خَلۡقًا أَم مَّنۡ خَلَقۡنَآۚ إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰهُم مِّن طِينٖ لَّازِبِۢ

So, O Muhammad, ask the disbelievers who deny the resurrection if they are harder to create and greater in form than the heavens, the Earth, and the angels that We have created. We created them (disbelievers) from sticky clay, so how could they deny the resurrection when they were created from such a weak substance, sticky clay?

(١٢) بَلۡ عَجِبۡتَ وَيَسۡخَرُونَ

Rather you are amazed, O Muhammad, at Allah's power and planning the affairs of His creation; you are amazed at the polytheists rejecting the resurrection. Due to their severe rejection of the resurrection, these polytheists mock what you say about it.

(١٣) وَإِذَا ذُكِّرُواْ لَا يَذۡكُرُونَ

When these polytheists are admonished with any admonishments, they do not take heed nor benefit from it because their hearts are hard.

(١٤) وَإِذَا رَأَوۡاْ ءَايَةٗ يَسۡتَسۡخِرُونَ

And when they see a sign from the signs of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم that proves his truthfulness, they exaggerate in mocking and showing astonishment at it.

(١٥) وَقَالُوٓاْ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا سِحۡرٞ مُّبِينٌ

And they say: This thing that Muhammad has brought is nothing but clear magic.

(١٦) أَءِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابٗا وَعِظَٰمًا أَءِنَّا لَمَبۡعُوثُونَ

[They say:] So when we die and become dust and decomposed crumbling bones, will we be resurrected alive? That is, indeed, far-fetched.

(١٧) أَوَ ءَابَآؤُنَا ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ

[They say:] Will our forefathers who died before us also be resurrected?

(١٨) قُلۡ نَعَمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ دَٰخِرُونَ

O Muhammad! Say to them in reply: Yes, you shall be resurrected after you have turned into dust and decomposed bones, as will your forefathers. You will all be resurrected while you are in a despised and humiliating state.

(١٩) فَإِنَّمَا هِيَ زَجۡرَةٞ وَٰحِدَةٞ فَإِذَا هُمۡ يَنظُرُونَ

It will only be one blow of the trumpet (the second blowing), and suddenly, they will all be looking at the horrors of the Day of Resurrection, awaiting what Allah will do to them.

(٢٠) وَقَالُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَا هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ

The polytheists who reject the resurrection will say: May we be destroyed! This is the Day of Recompense on which Allah will recompense His slaves accordingly for whatever deeds they sent forth in the worldly life!

(٢١) هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ

It will be said to them: This is the Day of Adjudication between the slaves, which you used to deny and reject in the worldly life!

(٢٢) ۞ٱحۡشُرُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ وَأَزۡوَٰجَهُمۡ وَمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡبُدُونَ

On that Day, it will be said to the angels: Gather the polytheists and those like them who did wrong by associating partners with Allah, all those who followed them in rejection, and all the idols they used to worship instead of Allah. Then, show them all the path to the Fire; lead them on to it and drive them towards it because it is their destination.

(٢٣) مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ فَٱهۡدُوهُمۡ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

On that Day, it will be said to the angels: Gather the polytheists and those like them who did wrong by associating partners with Allah, all those who followed them in rejection, and all the idols they used to worship instead of Allah. Then, show them all the path to the Fire; lead them on to it and drive them towards it because it is their destination.

(٢٤) وَقِفُوهُمۡۖ إِنَّهُم مَّسۡ‍ُٔولُونَ

[They will be told:] And hold them back [for a while] before admitting them into the Fire to give account because they are to be questioned. Then, after that, drive them towards the Fire.

Points of Benefit:

Decorating the lower sky with planets has benefits, including obtaining adornment and protecting yourself from the rebellious devil.
Proof of Al Sirat (the path). It is a bridge that stretches across Hell, which the people of Paradise cross, and the feet of the People of the Fire will slip by.
