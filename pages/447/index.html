(٢٥) مَا لَكُمۡ لَا تَنَاصَرُونَ

It will be said to them in rebuke: What is the matter with you that you are not helping each other like you used to in the worldly life, and you used to claim that your idols would help you?

(٢٦) بَلۡ هُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ مُسۡتَسۡلِمُونَ

Instead, Today, they are submissive to the command of Allah and humiliated. They will not help each other because of their incapability to do so and because of their lack of ploys.

(٢٧) وَأَقۡبَلَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ

And they will turn to each other, blaming each other and arguing when blaming and arguing will be useless.

(٢٨) قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّكُمۡ كُنتُمۡ تَأۡتُونَنَا عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ

The followers will say to their leaders: O, our seniors! You used to approach us in matters of religion and truth. You would beautify disbelief, associating partners with Allah and committing acts of disobedience to us while scaring us away from the Truth that the messengers brought from Allah.

(٢٩) قَالُواْ بَل لَّمۡ تَكُونُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ

The leaders will tell the followers: The matter is not as you claim. Rather, you, yourselves, were upon disbelief and were not believers; you were the deniers.

(٣٠) وَمَا كَانَ لَنَا عَلَيۡكُم مِّن سُلۡطَٰنِۢۖ بَلۡ كُنتُمۡ قَوۡمٗا طَٰغِينَ

O followers, we had no power or authority over you to cause you to disbelieve, associate partners with Allah, or commit acts of disobedience. Rather, you were people who transgressed the limits in disbelief and misguidance.

(٣١) فَحَقَّ عَلَيۡنَا قَوۡلُ رَبِّنَآۖ إِنَّا لَذَآئِقُونَ

So the threat of Allah has come to pass against us and you in His statement: "I will surely fill Hell with you and those of them that follow you all together." (Ṣād: 85), and because of that, we will inevitably taste whatever our Lord has promised.

(٣٢) فَأَغۡوَيۡنَٰكُمۡ إِنَّا كُنَّا غَٰوِينَ

So, We invited you towards misguidance and disbelief. We had deviated from the path of guidance.

(٣٣) فَإِنَّهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ مُشۡتَرِكُونَ

So, the followers and the leaders will share in the torment on the Day of Resurrection.

(٣٤) إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَفۡعَلُ بِٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

Just as We did with these people by making them taste the torment, We will do the same with other criminals.

(٣٥) إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُوٓاْ إِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمۡ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ

When these polytheists were told in this world that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah so that they could act upon its requirements and leave everything that contradicted it, they would reject responding and submitting to it purely out of arrogance and disdain towards the truth.

(٣٦) وَيَقُولُونَ أَئِنَّا لَتَارِكُوٓاْ ءَالِهَتِنَا لِشَاعِرٖ مَّجۡنُونِۢ

And they would say, justifying their disbelief: Should we leave the worship of our deities for the words of an insane poet?; referring to Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم.

(٣٧) بَلۡ جَآءَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَصَدَّقَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

Verily, they have fabricated a grave lie. The Messenger of Allah صلى الله عليه وسلم was not insane nor a poet; he brought the Quran, which invites towards the oneness of Allah and following His Messenger. He corroborated the message of monotheism (i.e., Allah's Oneness) and affirmation of the Hereafter, which the previous messengers brought from Allah; he did not contradict them in anything.

(٣٨) إِنَّكُمۡ لَذَآئِقُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡأَلِيمِ

O polytheists, you will surely taste the painful torment on the Day of Resurrection due to your disbelief and rejection of the messengers.

(٣٩) وَمَا تُجۡزَوۡنَ إِلَّا مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

O polytheists, your recompense will only be for the disbelief and acts of disobedience you used to commit in this world.

(٤٠) إِلَّا عِبَادَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ

However, the believing slaves of Allah whom Allah chose for His worship and who sincerely devoted worship to Him will be in a place of safety from this torment.

(٤١) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ رِزۡقٞ مَّعۡلُومٞ

For those sincere slaves, a special provision will be granted to them by Allah, known for its purity, beauty, and eternity.

(٤٢) فَوَٰكِهُ وَهُم مُّكۡرَمُونَ

From this special provision, they will be provided with the purest fruits they have ever eaten and desired; on top of that, they will be honored with their ranks being raised and being able to see the noble Face of Allah.

(٤٣) فِي جَنَّٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

They will get all of that in the gardens of everlasting permanent bliss that will never end or cease to exist.

(٤٤) عَلَىٰ سُرُرٖ مُّتَقَٰبِلِينَ

They will recline on couches, facing one another.

(٤٥) يُطَافُ عَلَيۡهِم بِكَأۡسٖ مِّن مَّعِينِۢ

Glasses filled from rivers flowing with wine that they see before them shall be passed around to them.

(٤٦) بَيۡضَآءَ لَذَّةٖ لِّلشَّٰرِبِينَ

White in color, whoever drinks from it will enjoy it thoroughly.

(٤٧) لَا فِيهَا غَوۡلٞ وَلَا هُمۡ عَنۡهَا يُنزَفُونَ

It will not be like the wine of this world; it will not intoxicate by clouding the minds, nor will its drinkers experience headaches. The bodies and minds of those who drink it will remain healthy.

(٤٨) وَعِندَهُمۡ قَٰصِرَٰتُ ٱلطَّرۡفِ عِينٞ

In Paradise, they will also have chaste wives with beautiful eyes who will not extend their eyesight [longingly] towards anyone other than their husbands.

(٤٩) كَأَنَّهُنَّ بَيۡضٞ مَّكۡنُونٞ

In their white complexion blended with a tinge of yellowness, it will be as if they were bird eggs, protected and untouched by any hand.

(٥٠) فَأَقۡبَلَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ

The people of Paradise will turn to one another, asking about their past and what happened to them.

(٥١) قَالَ قَآئِلٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ إِنِّي كَانَ لِي قَرِينٞ

One of the believers will say: Indeed, I had a companion in the worldly life who would deny the resurrection.

Points of Benefit:

The reason for the torment of the disbelievers is evil deeds, polytheism, and sins.
Among the bliss of the people of Paradise is that they are blessed with gathering and encountering each other. This is the perfection of blessedness.
