(٥٢) يَقُولُ أَءِنَّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُصَدِّقِينَ

He would say to me in denial and mockingly: O my friend! Are you one of those who believe in the resurrection of the dead?

(٥٣) أَءِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابٗا وَعِظَٰمًا أَءِنَّا لَمَدِينُونَ

When we die and turn into dust and decayed bones, will we be resurrected and recompensed for the deeds we did in the world?

(٥٤) قَالَ هَلۡ أَنتُم مُّطَّلِعُونَ

His believing companion will say to his companions of the people of Paradise: Take a look with me; let us see the outcome of that companion of mine who used to deny the resurrection.

(٥٥) فَٱطَّلَعَ فَرَءَاهُ فِي سَوَآءِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

Then, he will look and see his companion in the middle of Hell.

(٥٦) قَالَ تَٱللَّهِ إِن كِدتَّ لَتُرۡدِينِ

He will say: O my companion! By Allah, you were close to dooming me to the Fire by calling me towards disbelief and denying the resurrection!

(٥٧) وَلَوۡلَا نِعۡمَةُ رَبِّي لَكُنتُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُحۡضَرِينَ

Had it not been for Allah favoring me with guidance to belief and the ability to accept it, I would have been one of those brought to the punishment like you.

After finishing speaking to his companion from the people of the Fire, a person will turn to speak to their companions from the people of Paradise, stating:

(٥٨) أَفَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمَيِّتِينَ

We, the people of Paradise, will never die.

(٥٩) إِلَّا مَوۡتَتَنَا ٱلۡأُولَىٰ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمُعَذَّبِينَ

Except our first death on Earth. Then, we will live forever in Paradise and not be tormented as the disbelievers will be.

(٦٠) إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

Indeed, this which our Lord has recompensed us with, such as admission into Paradise, remaining therein eternally, and salvation from the Fire, is the greatest success, and no other success equals it.

(٦١) لِمِثۡلِ هَٰذَا فَلۡيَعۡمَلِ ٱلۡعَٰمِلُونَ

It is for a great reward like this that everyone should strive for because this is a profitable trade.

(٦٢) أَذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرٞ نُّزُلًا أَمۡ شَجَرَةُ ٱلزَّقُّومِ

Is that previously mentioned bliss Allah has prepared for His slaves whom He chose to obey Him, better and more virtuous in rank and honor than the cursed tree of Zaqqum in the Quran, which is the food of the disbelievers and neither nourishes nor satisfies hunger?!

(٦٣) إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰهَا فِتۡنَةٗ لِّلظَّٰلِمِينَ

We have made this tree a trial, with which those who do wrong through disbelief and acts of disobedience are tested when they say: Indeed, fire consumes trees, so it is impossible for it to grow in the Fire.

(٦٤) إِنَّهَا شَجَرَةٞ تَخۡرُجُ فِيٓ أَصۡلِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

Indeed, the tree of Zaqqum is disgusting and comes out of the depths of the Inferno.

(٦٥) طَلۡعُهَا كَأَنَّهُۥ رُءُوسُ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِ

The fruits that come out of it are ugly, like the heads of devils. The ugliness of its appearance indicates the ugliness of its content, which means its fruit tastes disgusting.

(٦٦) فَإِنَّهُمۡ لَأٓكِلُونَ مِنۡهَا فَمَالِ‍ُٔونَ مِنۡهَا ٱلۡبُطُونَ

The disbelievers will eat from its bitter, ugly fruits and fill their empty bellies with them.

(٦٧) ثُمَّ إِنَّ لَهُمۡ عَلَيۡهَا لَشَوۡبٗا مِّنۡ حَمِيمٖ

Then, after having eaten from it, they will have a disgusting, boiling mixture to drink.

(٦٨) ثُمَّ إِنَّ مَرۡجِعَهُمۡ لَإِلَى ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

Then, their return after that will be to the torment of the Inferno; they will move from one torment to another.

(٦٩) إِنَّهُمۡ أَلۡفَوۡاْ ءَابَآءَهُمۡ ضَآلِّينَ

Indeed, these disbelievers found their forefathers astray from the path of guidance and followed them blindly, not based on evidence.

(٧٠) فَهُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِهِمۡ يُهۡرَعُونَ

So they hastily followed the footsteps of their forefathers in misguidance.

(٧١) وَلَقَدۡ ضَلَّ قَبۡلَهُمۡ أَكۡثَرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

And most of the earlier generations also went astray. So, O Messenger, your people are not the first people to go astray.

(٧٢) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا فِيهِم مُّنذِرِينَ

We sent messengers to the previous nations who would warn them of the torment of Allah, but they disbelieved.

(٧٣) فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُنذَرِينَ

O Messenger, see what the consequence was for the nations who did not respond when warned by their messengers! Their outcome was entering the Fire and remaining therein eternally due to their disbelief and rejection of their messengers.

(٧٤) إِلَّا عِبَادَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ

Except those whom Allah chose to believe in Him and His Oneness. They were saved from the torment, which was the outcome, of the disbelieving rejectors.

(٧٥) وَلَقَدۡ نَادَىٰنَا نُوحٞ فَلَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡمُجِيبُونَ

Our Prophet Noah (peace be upon him) supplicated to Us when he supplicated against his people who had rejected him, and how excellent are We in responding! We quickly answered his supplication against his people.

(٧٦) وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَأَهۡلَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلۡكَرۡبِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

And We delivered him, together with members of his household and the believers, from the harm of his people and from drowning in the great flood, which was sent upon the disbelievers from his people.

Points of Benefit:

Gaining the bliss of Paradise is the most significant win. For such giving and favor, the workers should work.
The food of the People of the Fire is Zaqqum (an infernal tree). It's a bitter fruit with an unpleasant taste and smell, difficult to swallow, and painful to eat.
The Exalted Allah answered the supplication of Noah, peace be upon him, to destroy his people. Allah is the best to seek and the best answerer.
