(٧٧) وَجَعَلۡنَا ذُرِّيَّتَهُۥ هُمُ ٱلۡبَاقِينَ

We made the offspring of Noah (peace be upon him) the sole survivors after the destruction of his people.

(٧٨) وَتَرَكۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ

And We left for him a beautiful praise among the later nations with which they would extol him.

(٧٩) سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ نُوحٖ فِي ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Safety and peace be on Noah from being spoken ill of among the later nations. Instead, praise and a favorable mention of him will remain.

(٨٠) إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Just as We rewarded Noah, We will reward those who do good in their worship and obedience to Allah alone.

(٨١) إِنَّهُۥ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Noah was one of Our slaves who believed and acted in obedience to Allah.

(٨٢) ثُمَّ أَغۡرَقۡنَا ٱلۡأٓخَرِينَ

Then We drowned the rest of the people in the flood We sent upon them, and none remained.

(٨٣) ۞وَإِنَّ مِن شِيعَتِهِۦ لَإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ

Abraham was someone of the same religion as Noah, and he also invited to the Oneness of Allah.

(٨٤) إِذۡ جَآءَ رَبَّهُۥ بِقَلۡبٖ سَلِيمٍ

Remember when he came to his Lord with a heart pure from ascribing partners to Allah and from doubts, full of sincerity to Allah, only wanting good for the creation.

(٨٥) إِذۡ قَالَ لِأَبِيهِ وَقَوۡمِهِۦ مَاذَا تَعۡبُدُونَ

When he rebuked his father and his polytheistic people, saying: What is it that you worship instead of Allah?

(٨٦) أَئِفۡكًا ءَالِهَةٗ دُونَ ٱللَّهِ تُرِيدُونَ

Is it false deities you are worshipping instead of Allah?

(٨٧) فَمَا ظَنُّكُم بِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

O my people, what do you think will happen when you meet the Lord of the worlds while you used to worship others instead of Him? What do you think He will do to you?

(٨٨) فَنَظَرَ نَظۡرَةٗ فِي ٱلنُّجُومِ

So Abraham glanced at the stars, hatching a plan to get out of having to go out with his people.

(٨٩) فَقَالَ إِنِّي سَقِيمٞ

He said, presenting an excuse for not going with his people to their festival: I am sick.

(٩٠) فَتَوَلَّوۡاْ عَنۡهُ مُدۡبِرِينَ

So they left him behind and went.

(٩١) فَرَاغَ إِلَىٰٓ ءَالِهَتِهِمۡ فَقَالَ أَلَا تَأۡكُلُونَ

He turned towards their deities [idols] they would worship instead of Allah. He said, mocking them: Will you not eat from the food the polytheists prepared for you?

(٩٢) مَا لَكُمۡ لَا تَنطِقُونَ

What is the matter with you that you do not speak nor answer whoever asks you? Can this type of thing be worshipped instead of Allah?

(٩٣) فَرَاغَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ضَرۡبَۢا بِٱلۡيَمِينِ

Then, Abraham turned to them, hitting them with his right hand to break them.

(٩٤) فَأَقۡبَلُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ يَزِفُّونَ

Then the worshippers of these idols came rushing to him.

(٩٥) قَالَ أَتَعۡبُدُونَ مَا تَنۡحِتُونَ

But Abraham countered them with steadfastness and rebuked them, saying: Do you worship deities instead of Allah, which you carve with your own hands?

(٩٦) وَٱللَّهُ خَلَقَكُمۡ وَمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Allah (may He be Exalted) created you and your deeds; and from among your deeds is the making of these idols, so He is most deserving to be worshipped alone. None should be associated as partners with Him.

(٩٧) قَالُواْ ٱبۡنُواْ لَهُۥ بُنۡيَٰنٗا فَأَلۡقُوهُ فِي ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

When they could not refute him with evidence, they resorted to violence. They consulted with each other about what they should do with Abraham. They said: Build a structure for him, fill it with firewood and set it on fire, then throw him into it.

(٩٨) فَأَرَادُواْ بِهِۦ كَيۡدٗا فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡأَسۡفَلِينَ

So the people of Abraham intended to do evil to him by killing him to be relieved of him, but We made them the losers when We made the fire cool and safe for him.

(٩٩) وَقَالَ إِنِّي ذَاهِبٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّي سَيَهۡدِينِ

Abraham said: I am going to emigrate to my Lord and leave the city of my people so that I can worship Him. My Lord will guide me to whatever is good for me in this world and the Hereafter.

(١٠٠) رَبِّ هَبۡ لِي مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

O my Lord! Grant me a pious child who can help me and be a substitute for my people in exile.

(١٠١) فَبَشَّرۡنَٰهُ بِغُلَٰمٍ حَلِيمٖ

So We responded to his supplication and gave him news which pleased him when We gave him the good news of a child who would grow up and become forbearing. This child was Ishmael (peace be upon him).

(١٠٢) فَلَمَّا بَلَغَ مَعَهُ ٱلسَّعۡيَ قَالَ يَٰبُنَيَّ إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰ فِي ٱلۡمَنَامِ أَنِّيٓ أَذۡبَحُكَ فَٱنظُرۡ مَاذَا تَرَىٰۚ قَالَ يَٰٓأَبَتِ ٱفۡعَلۡ مَا تُؤۡمَرُۖ سَتَجِدُنِيٓ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

When Ishmael grew up and reached the age to work with his father, his father saw a dream. The dreams of prophets are a form of divine revelation, so Abraham told his son, informing him of the meaning of this dream: O my [dear] son! I saw in my sleep that I am slaughtering you, so tell me what you think. Ishmael replied to him, saying: O my father! Do whatever Allah has commanded you to do by slaughtering me. You will find me one of those who are patient and content with the order of Allah.

Points of Benefit:

Among the manifestations of blessings upon Noah were the salvation of Noah and those who believed in him. Allah made his descendants the origins of humanity, ethnicities, and races. Moreover, Allah kept the beautiful remembrance and good praise for him.
Allah created human actions, and the slave does them by choice.
According to the meaning and order of these verses, the Sacrifice is Ishmael, peace be upon him, because good tidings were given to Abraham, peace be upon him, about him first. The good tidings about Isaac were given to Abraham after Ishmael. May peace be upon them all.
Ishmael's saying {You will find me, by Allah's will, among those who are patient} is a reason Allah granted him success to be patient because he made the matter belong to Allah.
