(٢٦٥) وَمَثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَهُمُ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ مَرۡضَاتِ ٱللَّهِ وَتَثۡبِيتٗا مِّنۡ أَنفُسِهِمۡ كَمَثَلِ جَنَّةِۢ بِرَبۡوَةٍ أَصَابَهَا وَابِلٞ فَ‍َٔاتَتۡ أُكُلَهَا ضِعۡفَيۡنِ فَإِن لَّمۡ يُصِبۡهَا وَابِلٞ فَطَلّٞۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ

The example of the believers who spend their wealth seeking Allah's pleasure, with firm conviction in their hearts of Allah's promise, is like that of a garden on high, fertile ground where heavy rain falls and causes it to produce double its crops. In addition, if it does not receive heavy rain, the light rain that falls on it is sufficient because the land is fertile. In the same way, the little that the sincere ones spend is accepted by Allah, and its reward is multiplied. Allah sees what you do: He knows who is sincere and who is not and will reward everyone accordingly.

Then Allah gives an example that depicts the state of one who spends his money to show off, stating:

(٢٦٦) أَيَوَدُّ أَحَدُكُمۡ أَن تَكُونَ لَهُۥ جَنَّةٞ مِّن نَّخِيلٖ وَأَعۡنَابٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ لَهُۥ فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ وَأَصَابَهُ ٱلۡكِبَرُ وَلَهُۥ ذُرِّيَّةٞ ضُعَفَآءُ فَأَصَابَهَآ إِعۡصَارٞ فِيهِ نَارٞ فَٱحۡتَرَقَتۡۗ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَفَكَّرُونَ

Do any of you wish to have a garden with date palms and grapevines through which sweet waters flow, containing all varieties of wholesome fruit? When the owner of such a garden becomes old and is no longer able to work and earn, and he has vulnerable young children who also cannot work, a solid fiery wind strikes this garden, and it burns down completely at a time when he is most in need of it because of his old age and for his young dependable children. The condition of a person who spends his wealth to show people is like that of this man: when he comes before Allah on the Day of Resurrection, he will have no good actions when he most needs them. In this manner, Allah explains what will benefit you in this world and the Hereafter so that you may reflect.

(٢٦٧) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَنفِقُواْ مِن طَيِّبَٰتِ مَا كَسَبۡتُمۡ وَمِمَّآ أَخۡرَجۡنَا لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَلَا تَيَمَّمُواْ ٱلۡخَبِيثَ مِنۡهُ تُنفِقُونَ وَلَسۡتُم بِ‍َٔاخِذِيهِ إِلَّآ أَن تُغۡمِضُواْ فِيهِۚ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَنِيٌّ حَمِيدٌ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, spend from the lawful, pure wealth you have earned and the produce we have taken out for you from the ground. Do not look for what is inferior to spend because if given to you, you would not take it willingly, except with closed eyes, because of its poor quality. How can you be satisfied with offering Allah what you yourselves would not accept? Know that Allah does not need your spending, and He is praised in His essence and actions.

After instructing them to spend their wealth in virtue, Allah warns them about Satan's plots and whispers, stating:

(٢٦٨) ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ يَعِدُكُمُ ٱلۡفَقۡرَ وَيَأۡمُرُكُم بِٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِۖ وَٱللَّهُ يَعِدُكُم مَّغۡفِرَةٗ مِّنۡهُ وَفَضۡلٗاۗ وَٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٞ

The Devil makes you afraid of poverty by encouraging you to be stingy, and he invites you to commit sins. Allah, on the other hand, promises you forgiveness for your sins and vast provision. Allah is very bountiful, and He knows the condition of His slaves.

(٢٦٩) يُؤۡتِي ٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَمَن يُؤۡتَ ٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ فَقَدۡ أُوتِيَ خَيۡرٗا كَثِيرٗاۗ وَمَا يَذَّكَّرُ إِلَّآ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

Allah grants the ability to whoever of His slaves He wishes to be steadfast in speech and deeds. Anyone who is given this ability has been given much good. However, only those with sound minds who seek enlightenment through His light and follow His guidance will pay attention and benefit from the signs of Allah.

Points of Benefit:

Truly, the believers in Allah are confident in Allah's promise and reward. They spend their wealth and exert efforts without fear, grief, or paying attention to the whispers of Satan, such as his intimidating them with poverty and need.
Sincerity is among the greatest factors that bless and enhance deeds.
The people who suffer the greatest loss are those who seek people's approval in their actions, as they have no reward for their deeds except the praise and approval of others.
