(١٠٣) فَلَمَّآ أَسۡلَمَا وَتَلَّهُۥ لِلۡجَبِينِ

So when they submitted to Allah and obeyed Him, Abraham laid his son down on the side of his forehead to carry out the order given to him to slaughter his son.

(١٠٤) وَنَٰدَيۡنَٰهُ أَن يَٰٓإِبۡرَٰهِيمُ

We called out to Abraham while he was about to carry out the order of Allah to slaughter his son, saying: O Abraham!

(١٠٥) قَدۡ صَدَّقۡتَ ٱلرُّءۡيَآۚ إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

You have fulfilled the dream you saw in your sleep by resolving to slaughter your son. Just as We have rewarded you by freeing you of this great trial, We also reward those who do good by saving them from trials and difficulties.

(١٠٦) إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلۡبَلَٰٓؤُاْ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

Indeed, this was a clear test that Abraham passed.

(١٠٧) وَفَدَيۡنَٰهُ بِذِبۡحٍ عَظِيمٖ

And We ransomed Ishmael in exchange for a huge ram to be slaughtered instead of him.

(١٠٨) وَتَرَكۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ

And We made a beautiful praise for Abraham remain among the later nations.

(١٠٩) سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ

As a greeting for Abraham from Allah and a supplication to keep him safe from every harm and difficulty.

(١١٠) كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Just as We recompensed Abraham with this reward for his obedience, We also reward those who do good.

(١١١) إِنَّهُۥ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Abraham was one of Our believing slaves who fulfilled what servitude to Allah requires.

(١١٢) وَبَشَّرۡنَٰهُ بِإِسۡحَٰقَ نَبِيّٗا مِّنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

And We gave him glad tidings of another son, Isaac, who would become a prophet and a pious slave, as a reward for his obedience to Allah by resolving to slaughter his only son, Ishmael.

(١١٣) وَبَٰرَكۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ وَعَلَىٰٓ إِسۡحَٰقَۚ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِهِمَا مُحۡسِنٞ وَظَالِمٞ لِّنَفۡسِهِۦ مُبِينٞ

And We sent down upon him and his son Isaac Our special blessings and gave them both many favors, one of which was an increase in offspring. There were those among their progeny who did good by obeying their Lord and those who wronged themselves by disbelief and committing acts of disobedience.

(١١٤) وَلَقَدۡ مَنَنَّا عَلَىٰ مُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَ

And We favored Moses and his brother Aaron with prophethood.

(١١٥) وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُمَا وَقَوۡمَهُمَا مِنَ ٱلۡكَرۡبِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

We saved them and their people, the Israelites, from Pharaoh enslaving them and from drowning.

(١١٦) وَنَصَرۡنَٰهُمۡ فَكَانُواْ هُمُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبِينَ

We also helped them against Pharaoh and his armies, giving them the upper hand over their enemies.

(١١٧) وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمَا ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَبِينَ

We gave Moses and his brother Aaron the Torah, a clear book from Allah that contained no confusion.

(١١٨) وَهَدَيۡنَٰهُمَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمَ

We also guided them towards the straight path in which there was no deviance. [This straight path] is the path of the religion of Islam that leads to the pleasure of the Creator; may He be Exalted.

(١١٩) وَتَرَكۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمَا فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ

And We left for them a beautiful praise and favorable mention among the later nations.

(١٢٠) سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ مُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَ

They have a beautiful greeting from Allah, praise, and supplication for their safety from every harm.

(١٢١) إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Just as We recompensed Moses and Aaron with this beautiful reward, We also recompense those who do good by obeying their Lord.

(١٢٢) إِنَّهُمَا مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Moses and Aaron were among Our slaves who believed in Allah and acted on what He legislated for them.

(١٢٣) وَإِنَّ إِلۡيَاسَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

Elijah was one of the messengers sent by his Lord. Allah blessed him with prophethood and messengership.

(١٢٤) إِذۡ قَالَ لِقَوۡمِهِۦٓ أَلَا تَتَّقُونَ

Remember when he told his people whom he was sent to from the Israelites: O my people! Do you not fear Allah by fulfilling His commands, which include monotheism, and refraining from what He has prohibited, including associating partners with Him?

(١٢٥) أَتَدۡعُونَ بَعۡلٗا وَتَذَرُونَ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلۡخَٰلِقِينَ

Do you worship your idol Ba'l instead of Allah and leave the worship of Allah, who is the Best of creators?

(١٢٦) ٱللَّهَ رَبَّكُمۡ وَرَبَّ ءَابَآئِكُمُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

Allah is your Lord who created you and your forefathers before you. So, He is the most deserving of worship. No one else from the idols who cannot benefit nor harm is worthy.

Points of Benefit:

His saying, {And when they surrendered} is evidence that Abraham and Ishmael, peace be upon them, were genuinely submissive to the command of Allah the Exalted.
One of the objectives of Sharia law is to free people from human slavery.
Good tribute and pleasing remembrance are among the hastened bliss in the worldly life.
