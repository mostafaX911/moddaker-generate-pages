(١٢٧) فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَإِنَّهُمۡ لَمُحۡضَرُونَ

The only response of his people was that they rejected him, and because of their rejection, they will be brought to the torment.

(١٢٨) إِلَّا عِبَادَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ

Except for those among his people who were believers and devoted in their worship to Allah, they will be saved from being brought to the torment.

(١٢٩) وَتَرَكۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ

And We left for him a beautiful praise and favorable mention among the later nations.

(١٣٠) سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰٓ إِلۡ يَاسِينَ

As a greeting from Allah and praise for Elijah.

(١٣١) إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Just as We recompensed Elijah with this beautiful reward, We will reward those who do good from Our believing slaves.

(١٣٢) إِنَّهُۥ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Indeed, Elijah was one of Our true believing slaves who sincerely believed in Me.

(١٣٣) وَإِنَّ لُوطٗا لَّمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

And Lot was one of the messengers of Allah who were sent to their people as bearers of good news and warners.

(١٣٤) إِذۡ نَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَأَهۡلَهُۥٓ أَجۡمَعِينَ

Remember when We delivered him and all his family from the torment sent upon his people.

(١٣٥) إِلَّا عَجُوزٗا فِي ٱلۡغَٰبِرِينَ

Except for his wife, because she was a woman included in the torment of her people due to her being a disbeliever like them.

(١٣٦) ثُمَّ دَمَّرۡنَا ٱلۡأٓخَرِينَ

Then, We destroyed the rest of his people who rejected him and did not believe in what he had brought.

(١٣٧) وَإِنَّكُمۡ لَتَمُرُّونَ عَلَيۡهِم مُّصۡبِحِينَ

O people of Mecca, you pass their homes in your journeys towards the Levant in the morning.

(١٣٨) وَبِٱلَّيۡلِۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

And likewise, you also pass them at night. So, do you not understand and take heed from the outcome of these people after they rejected, disbelieved, and committed immoral actions, which were unprecedented?

(١٣٩) وَإِنَّ يُونُسَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

And Jonah was one of the messengers of Allah who were sent to their people as bearers of good news and warners.

(١٤٠) إِذۡ أَبَقَ إِلَى ٱلۡفُلۡكِ ٱلۡمَشۡحُونِ

When he fled from his people without His Lord's permission and boarded a ship full of passengers and goods.

(١٤١) فَسَاهَمَ فَكَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُدۡحَضِينَ

The ship was close to sinking because of its load, so the passengers drew lots to throw some of them overboard, fearing that it would sink because of too many passengers. Jonah was one of those that lost, so they threw him into the sea.

(١٤٢) فَٱلۡتَقَمَهُ ٱلۡحُوتُ وَهُوَ مُلِيمٞ

After they threw him into the sea, a whale took and swallowed him. He did that for which he was blameworthy because he went to the sea without His Lord's permission.

(١٤٣) فَلَوۡلَآ أَنَّهُۥ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُسَبِّحِينَ

Had it not been that Jonah was among those who remembered Allah abundantly before what happened to him, and had it not been for his exalting Allah in the belly of the whale,

(١٤٤) لَلَبِثَ فِي بَطۡنِهِۦٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ

He would have remained in the belly of the whale until the Day of Resurrection, with it having become his grave.

(١٤٥) ۞فَنَبَذۡنَٰهُ بِٱلۡعَرَآءِ وَهُوَ سَقِيمٞ

So We cast him from the belly of the whale and placed him on a land with no trees and buildings while he was physically weak because of having remained in the belly of the whale for a while.

(١٤٦) وَأَنۢبَتۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ شَجَرَةٗ مِّن يَقۡطِينٖ

And caused a gourd tree to grow over him on that barren land, which would give him shade and from which he could eat.

(١٤٧) وَأَرۡسَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَىٰ مِاْئَةِ أَلۡفٍ أَوۡ يَزِيدُونَ

And We sent him back to his people, their number being a hundred thousand or more.

(١٤٨) فَ‍َٔامَنُواْ فَمَتَّعۡنَٰهُمۡ إِلَىٰ حِينٖ

They believed and had faith in what He brought, so Allah granted them enjoyment in their worldly lives until their appointed time came.

(١٤٩) فَٱسۡتَفۡتِهِمۡ أَلِرَبِّكَ ٱلۡبَنَاتُ وَلَهُمُ ٱلۡبَنُونَ

So, O Muhammad, ask these polytheists a rebuking question: Do you ascribe daughter to Allah, which you dislike, while ascribing sons to yourselves, which you prefer? What kind of distribution is this?

(١٥٠) أَمۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةَ إِنَٰثٗا وَهُمۡ شَٰهِدُونَ

How could they claim the angels are female when they were not present at their creation and did not witness it?

(١٥١) أَلَآ إِنَّهُم مِّنۡ إِفۡكِهِمۡ لَيَقُولُونَ

Behold! The polytheists, due to their lies and fabrications against Allah, attribute a child to Him while they are liars in this claim of theirs.

(١٥٢) وَلَدَ ٱللَّهُ وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَكَٰذِبُونَ

Behold! The polytheists, due to their lies and fabrications against Allah, attribute a child to Him while they are liars in this claim of theirs.

(١٥٣) أَصۡطَفَى ٱلۡبَنَاتِ عَلَى ٱلۡبَنِينَ

Would Allah prefer daughters for Himself, which you dislike, over sons you prefer? Never!

Points of Benefit:

The established practice of Allah, which does not modify or change, is saving the believers and destroying the unbelievers.
The necessity to take lessons and consider the fate of those who deny the messengers.
According to Sharia, the permissibility of drawing lots. The Exalted Allah says about Jonah peace be upon him: {and he drew lots and was of those who lost.}
