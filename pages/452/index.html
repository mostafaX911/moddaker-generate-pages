(١٥٤) مَا لَكُمۡ كَيۡفَ تَحۡكُمُونَ

O polytheists, what is wrong with you making this unjust judgment where you ascribe daughters to Allah and sons for yourselves?

(١٥٥) أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ

Do you not reflect on the falsity of this corrupt belief you are upon? If you were to reflect, you would not say such words.

(١٥٦) أَمۡ لَكُمۡ سُلۡطَٰنٞ مُّبِينٞ

Or do you have clear proof and plain evidence from a [divine] book or a messenger [from Allah]?

(١٥٧) فَأۡتُواْ بِكِتَٰبِكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

Then bring your book, which bears evidence of this if you are truthful in your claim.

(١٥٨) وَجَعَلُواْ بَيۡنَهُۥ وَبَيۡنَ ٱلۡجِنَّةِ نَسَبٗاۚ وَلَقَدۡ عَلِمَتِ ٱلۡجِنَّةُ إِنَّهُمۡ لَمُحۡضَرُونَ

The polytheists established a lineage between Allah and the invisible angels when they claimed the angels were Allah's daughters. Yet the angels know that Allah will bring the polytheists to be reckoned.

(١٥٩) سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ

Pure is Allah, and sanctified is He from what the polytheists describe Him with that which does not befit Him, such as having a child, partners, etc.

(١٦٠) إِلَّا عِبَادَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ

Except for Allah's chosen slaves, they do not describe Allah except with the attributes of majesty and perfection, which befit Him; may He be Exalted.

(١٦١) فَإِنَّكُمۡ وَمَا تَعۡبُدُونَ

Surely, O polytheists, you and what you worship instead of Allah...

(١٦٢) مَآ أَنتُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ بِفَٰتِنِينَ

Cannot misguide anyone from the true religion.

(١٦٣) إِلَّا مَنۡ هُوَ صَالِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

Except for those whom Allah has decreed to be from the people of the Fire, Allah will fulfill His decree regarding them. They will thus disbelieve and enter the Fire. As for you and your deities, you do not have power over that.

(١٦٤) وَمَا مِنَّآ إِلَّا لَهُۥ مَقَامٞ مَّعۡلُومٞ

And the angels said, stating their servitude to Allah and declaring their innocence from what the polytheists claimed: There is none among us except that he has a known position in worshipping and obeying Allah.

(١٦٥) وَإِنَّا لَنَحۡنُ ٱلصَّآفُّونَ

And we, the angels, stand in rows in Allah's worship and obedience and declare Him free from the attributes and descriptions which do not befit Him.

(١٦٦) وَإِنَّا لَنَحۡنُ ٱلۡمُسَبِّحُونَ

And we, the angels, stand in rows in Allah's worship and obedience and declare Him free from the attributes and descriptions which do not befit Him.

(١٦٧) وَإِن كَانُواْ لَيَقُولُونَ

And the polytheists from the people of Mecca used to say before Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم was sent: If we had a book of the books of the previous people, such as the Torah, for example, We would have worshipped Allah devotedly. But they are lying in that, as Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought them the Quran, but they disbelieved in it. Soon, they will know the severe punishment awaiting them on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٦٨) لَوۡ أَنَّ عِندَنَا ذِكۡرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

And the polytheists from the people of Mecca used to say before Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم was sent: If we had a book of the books of the previous people, such as the Torah, for example, We would have worshipped Allah devotedly. But they are lying in that, as Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought them the Quran, but they disbelieved in it. Soon, they will know the severe punishment awaiting them on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٦٩) لَكُنَّا عِبَادَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ

And the polytheists from the people of Mecca used to say before Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم was sent: If we had a book of the books of the previous people, such as the Torah, for example, We would have worshipped Allah devotedly. But they are lying in that, as Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought them the Quran, but they disbelieved in it. Soon, they will know the severe punishment awaiting them on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٧٠) فَكَفَرُواْ بِهِۦۖ فَسَوۡفَ يَعۡلَمُونَ

And the polytheists from the people of Mecca used to say before Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم was sent: If we had a book of the books of the previous people, such as the Torah, for example, We would have worshipped Allah devotedly. But they are lying in that, as Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought them the Quran, but they disbelieved in it. Soon, they will know the severe punishment awaiting them on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٧١) وَلَقَدۡ سَبَقَتۡ كَلِمَتُنَا لِعِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

Our word, which none can reverse or repel, has already gone forth to Our messengers that they will be victorious against their enemies through what Allah has favored them with of evidence and strength. And Our army, which fights in Allah's path, will have the upper hand so Allah's word will prevail.

(١٧٢) إِنَّهُمۡ لَهُمُ ٱلۡمَنصُورُونَ

Our word, which none can reverse or repel, has already gone forth to Our messengers that they will be victorious against their enemies through what Allah has favored them with of evidence and strength. And Our army, which fights in Allah's path, will have the upper hand so Allah's word will prevail.

(١٧٣) وَإِنَّ جُندَنَا لَهُمُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبُونَ

Our word, which none can reverse or repel, has already gone forth to Our messengers that they will be victorious against their enemies through what Allah has favored them with of evidence and strength. And Our army, which fights in Allah's path, will have the upper hand so Allah's word will prevail.

(١٧٤) فَتَوَلَّ عَنۡهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ حِينٖ

So turn away, O Messenger, from these stubborn polytheists until a period that Allah knows, which is when their torment comes.

(١٧٥) وَأَبۡصِرۡهُمۡ فَسَوۡفَ يُبۡصِرُونَ

And watch them when the torment comes down upon them, for they themselves will see when their sight will be of no benefit.

(١٧٦) أَفَبِعَذَابِنَا يَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَ

Then do these polytheists hastily seek Allah's torment?

(١٧٧) فَإِذَا نَزَلَ بِسَاحَتِهِمۡ فَسَآءَ صَبَاحُ ٱلۡمُنذَرِينَ

Then when Allah's torment comes down upon them, what a bad morning will theirs be!

(١٧٨) وَتَوَلَّ عَنۡهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ حِينٖ

And turn away, O Messenger, from them when Allah decrees their torment.

(١٧٩) وَأَبۡصِرۡ فَسَوۡفَ يُبۡصِرُونَ

And watch, for these people will see the torment and punishment of Allah come upon them.

(١٨٠) سُبۡحَٰنَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ ٱلۡعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ

O Muhammad, free and declare your Lord sanctified, the Lord of might, above the attributes of deficiency the polytheists describe Him with.

(١٨١) وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَى ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ

And Allah's greetings and praise be upon His noble messengers.

(١٨٢) وَٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

And all praise be to Allah, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, as He is deserving of it, and He is Lord of all creation. They have no lord besides Him.

Points of Benefit:

The established practice of Allah is to grant victory to the Messengers and their inheritors with proof and predominance. In the verses, there is great good news that whoever is described as one of Allah's soldiers is a victorious conqueror.
In the verses, there is evidence of the inability of the polytheists and their gods to mislead anyone. Correspondingly, the good news to the sincere slaves of Allah is that Allah, with His power, will save them from the misguidance of the misguided ones.
