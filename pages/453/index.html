The Letter "Saad" (Surah Sad)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Mentioning the issue of debating with falsehood and the consequences of doing so.

Explanation:

(١) صٓۚ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ذِي ٱلذِّكۡرِ

Sād – The discussion on the disconnected letters has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah. I take an oath by the Quran, which contains a reminder for humanity that benefits them in their worldly life and the Hereafter. The matter is not as the polytheists think, namely, that Allah has partners [deities].

(٢) بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فِي عِزَّةٖ وَشِقَاقٖ

But the disbelievers are in [a state of] prejudice and arrogance towards the Oneness of Allah, and in [a state of] opposition and hostility towards Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم.

(٣) كَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّن قَرۡنٖ فَنَادَواْ وَّلَاتَ حِينَ مَنَاصٖ

How many generations who rejected their messengers before these polytheists have We destroyed? They then called out seeking help when the torment came down upon them. Then was not the time for them to be saved from the torment; thus, seeking help from Him was useless.

(٤) وَعَجِبُوٓاْ أَن جَآءَهُم مُّنذِرٞ مِّنۡهُمۡۖ وَقَالَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ هَٰذَا سَٰحِرٞ كَذَّابٌ

And they are astonished when a messenger comes to them from among themselves to warn them of Allah's torment if they continue in disbelief. When the disbelievers saw the proofs of the truthfulness of what Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought, they said: This is a magician who performs magic on people and a liar in claiming that he is a messenger from Allah who receives revelation.

(٥) أَجَعَلَ ٱلۡأٓلِهَةَ إِلَٰهٗا وَٰحِدًاۖ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَشَيۡءٌ عُجَابٞ

Has this man reduced numerous deities into one God, besides whom there is no deity? This act of his is extremely strange.

(٦) وَٱنطَلَقَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ مِنۡهُمۡ أَنِ ٱمۡشُواْ وَٱصۡبِرُواْ عَلَىٰٓ ءَالِهَتِكُمۡۖ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَشَيۡءٞ يُرَادُ

And their nobles and elders went forth, saying to their followers: Carry on with what you are upon and do not enter the religion of Muhammad. Remain firm upon worshipping your deities. That which Muhammad is inviting you towards, namely worshipping one God, is something planned that he wants so that he can reign supreme over us and we become his followers.

(٧) مَا سَمِعۡنَا بِهَٰذَا فِي ٱلۡمِلَّةِ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا ٱخۡتِلَٰقٌ

We have not heard about the Oneness of Allah that Muhammad is inviting us towards in what we found our fathers upon nor in the religion of Jesus (peace be upon him). What we have heard from him is nothing but a lie and fabrication.

(٨) أَءُنزِلَ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلذِّكۡرُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِنَاۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ فِي شَكّٖ مِّن ذِكۡرِيۚ بَل لَّمَّا يَذُوقُواْ عَذَابِ

Is it correct that the Quran is revealed to him out of all of us, he is chosen for it, and it is not revealed to us, whereas we are senior chiefs? But these polytheists doubt the revelation revealed to you, and they have not yet tasted Allah's torment, so they have been deceived due to being given respite. If they were to taste it, they would not have the audacity to disbelieve, associate partners with Allah, and doubt what is revealed to you.

(٩) أَمۡ عِندَهُمۡ خَزَآئِنُ رَحۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡوَهَّابِ

Or is it because these rejecting polytheists possess the treasures of your Lord's favors, the Mighty whom nobody can overpower, who gives what He wants to whom He wants? And from among His treasures of favors is prophethood; He gives it to whom He wishes. It is not up to them to give it to whom they want and prevent it from whom they want.

(١٠) أَمۡ لَهُم مُّلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَاۖ فَلۡيَرۡتَقُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَسۡبَٰبِ

Or is it because the dominion of the heavens, the Earth, and everything in between belongs to them, so they may choose who they give it to or not? If this is their claim, let them adopt the means to go up to the heavens so they can decide what they desire regarding who should be given and who should be held back from. They will not be able to do that!

(١١) جُندٞ مَّا هُنَالِكَ مَهۡزُومٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابِ

These people who reject Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم are a defeated army like those armies who came before them who rejected their messengers.

(١٢) كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٖ وَعَادٞ وَفِرۡعَوۡنُ ذُو ٱلۡأَوۡتَادِ

These people are not the first to reject; instead, the people of Noah, 'Ād, and the Pharaoh, who had stakes with which he punished people, rejected before them.

(١٣) وَثَمُودُ وَقَوۡمُ لُوطٖ وَأَصۡحَٰبُ لۡ‍َٔيۡكَةِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابُ

Also, Thamūd, the people of Lot, and Shuayb's people rejected. Those are the groups who were all united in rejecting their messengers and disbelieving in what they brought.

(١٤) إِن كُلٌّ إِلَّا كَذَّبَ ٱلرُّسُلَ فَحَقَّ عِقَابِ

There is not one of these groups except that they rejected the messengers. So Allah's torment was justified against them, and His punishment befell them, even though it was delayed for a while.

(١٥) وَمَا يَنظُرُ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ إِلَّا صَيۡحَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ مَّا لَهَا مِن فَوَاقٖ

These people who reject Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم are not waiting except for the second blowing of the Trumpet in which there shall be no return. Then, the torment will befall them if they had died while rejecting him.

(١٦) وَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَا عَجِّل لَّنَا قِطَّنَا قَبۡلَ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

And they said mockingly: O our Lord! Hasten for us our share of the torment in the worldly life before the Day of Resurrection.

Points of Benefit:

The Exalted Allah swore by the Great Quran, so it must be received by faith, belief, and devotion to extract its meaning.
The predominance of material standards in the minds of polytheists was due to their desire for revelation to be revealed upon the chiefs and leaders.
The reason for the disbelievers' turning away from faith is arrogance, conceit, and superiority [by rejecting] to follow the truth.
