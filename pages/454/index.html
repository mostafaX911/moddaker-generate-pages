(١٧) ٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَٱذۡكُرۡ عَبۡدَنَا دَاوُۥدَ ذَا ٱلۡأَيۡدِۖ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٌ

O Messenger, be patient with what these rejectors say that does not please you, and remember My slave David, who had the power to fight his enemies and persevere in Allah's obedience. He used to turn to Allah frequently through repentance and deeds which pleased Him.

(١٨) إِنَّا سَخَّرۡنَا ٱلۡجِبَالَ مَعَهُۥ يُسَبِّحۡنَ بِٱلۡعَشِيِّ وَٱلۡإِشۡرَاقِ

We subjugated the mountains with David: they would exalt Allah along with David at the end and the beginning of the day at sunrise.

(١٩) وَٱلطَّيۡرَ مَحۡشُورَةٗۖ كُلّٞ لَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٞ

And We subjugated the birds held in the air. All of them were obedient, exalting Allah, following David.

(٢٠) وَشَدَدۡنَا مُلۡكَهُۥ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَفَصۡلَ ٱلۡخِطَابِ

And We strengthened his kingdom by gifting him with stature, strength, and support against his enemies. And We gave him prophethood, correctness in his affairs, eloquence clarifying his every intent, and decisiveness in speech and judgment.

(٢١) ۞وَهَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ نَبَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَصۡمِ إِذۡ تَسَوَّرُواْ ٱلۡمِحۡرَابَ

O Messenger, has the news of the two disputants reached you when they scaled David's (peace be upon him) place of worship?

(٢٢) إِذۡ دَخَلُواْ عَلَىٰ دَاوُۥدَ فَفَزِعَ مِنۡهُمۡۖ قَالُواْ لَا تَخَفۡۖ خَصۡمَانِ بَغَىٰ بَعۡضُنَا عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ فَٱحۡكُم بَيۡنَنَا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا تُشۡطِطۡ وَٱهۡدِنَآ إِلَىٰ سَوَآءِ ٱلصِّرَٰطِ

When they dropped in on David suddenly, he was startled by the unusual and sudden way they dropped in. When they saw his fear, they said: Do not fear. We are two disputants: one of us has wronged the other, so judge between us justly and do not be unjust when you judge between us, and guide us to the right path, which is the correct path.

(٢٣) إِنَّ هَٰذَآ أَخِي لَهُۥ تِسۡعٞ وَتِسۡعُونَ نَعۡجَةٗ وَلِيَ نَعۡجَةٞ وَٰحِدَةٞ فَقَالَ أَكۡفِلۡنِيهَا وَعَزَّنِي فِي ٱلۡخِطَابِ

One of the disputants said to David (peace be upon him): This man is my brother. He has ninety-nine ewes, and I have one ewe. He asked me to give it to him, and he won the argument against me.

(٢٤) قَالَ لَقَدۡ ظَلَمَكَ بِسُؤَالِ نَعۡجَتِكَ إِلَىٰ نِعَاجِهِۦۖ وَإِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡخُلَطَآءِ لَيَبۡغِي بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٍ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَقَلِيلٞ مَّا هُمۡۗ وَظَنَّ دَاوُۥدُ أَنَّمَا فَتَنَّٰهُ فَٱسۡتَغۡفَرَ رَبَّهُۥ وَخَرَّۤ رَاكِعٗاۤ وَأَنَابَ۩

David eventually judged between them and addressed the claimant, saying: Your brother has wronged you in demanding you to add your ewe to his ewes. And many partners transgress against each other by taking their rights and not being fair, except the believers who do righteous deeds, for they are fair with their partners and do not wrong them. But those attributed with that are few. Then David (peace be upon him) knew that We had only trialed him through this dispute, so he sought forgiveness from his Lord, prostrated to get close to Him, and repented to Him.

(٢٥) فَغَفَرۡنَا لَهُۥ ذَٰلِكَۖ وَإِنَّ لَهُۥ عِندَنَا لَزُلۡفَىٰ وَحُسۡنَ مَ‍َٔابٖ

So We responded to him and forgave him for that. He is among those close to Us, and he has a beautiful destination in the Hereafter.

(٢٦) يَٰدَاوُۥدُ إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰكَ خَلِيفَةٗ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَٱحۡكُم بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا تَتَّبِعِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰ فَيُضِلَّكَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَضِلُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ شَدِيدُۢ بِمَا نَسُواْ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

[Allah said]: O David, We made you a deputy on Earth to execute the worldly and religious rulings and judgments, so judge between people with justice. And do not follow desires in your judgment between people by inclining towards one of the disputants due to a relationship or friendship or by turning away from one of them due to enmity, whereby desires will mislead you from Allah's straight path. Those who go astray from Allah's straight path will receive a severe punishment due to forgetting the Day of Reckoning because if they were to remember it and fear it, they would not be inclined to follow their desires.

Points of Benefit:

An explanation of the virtues of Allah's Prophet David and the verses Allah designated him for.
The prophets, may Allah's prayers and peace be upon them, are infallible in what they convey about the Exalted Allah. The purpose of the message cannot be achieved without this, but they may forget or neglect some rules as human nature. However, Allah takes care of them and initiates His kindness to them.
Some scholars have used as evidence the Exalted Allah's saying: {And indeed, many are associates who oppress one another} as evidence for the legitimacy of a partnership between two or more.
One must adhere to good manners when approaching people of virtue and classiness.
