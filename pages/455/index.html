(٢٧) وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا بَٰطِلٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ ظَنُّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْۚ فَوَيۡلٞ لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنَ ٱلنَّارِ

And We have not created the heavens and the Earth in vain. That is the thinking of those who disbelieve. So, woe to these disbelievers who think this, for they will have the torment of the Fire on the Day of Resurrection when they die disbelieving and having bad thoughts about Allah.

(٢٨) أَمۡ نَجۡعَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ كَٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَمۡ نَجۡعَلُ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ كَٱلۡفُجَّارِ

We will never consider those who believe in Allah, follow His messenger, and do righteous deeds like those who spread corruption throughout the land through disbelief and committing acts of disobedience, nor will We consider those who fear their Lord by fulfilling His orders and avoiding His prohibitions like those who disbelieve and the hypocrites who are engrossed in acts of disobedience. Considering them both equal is an injustice that does not befit Allah. Instead, Allah will recompense the believers who fear Allah by admitting them into Paradise and punish the wretched disbelievers by admitting them into the Fire because they are not equal in Allah's sight. Thus, their reward is also not equal in His sight.

(٢٩) كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَيۡكَ مُبَٰرَكٞ لِّيَدَّبَّرُوٓاْ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَلِيَتَذَكَّرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

This Quran is a book We have revealed to you [Muhammad] which contains much good and benefit. [We have revealed it] so that people ponder over its verses and contemplate its meanings and so that the people of enlightened superior intellect take lessons from it.

(٣٠) وَوَهَبۡنَا لِدَاوُۥدَ سُلَيۡمَٰنَۚ نِعۡمَ ٱلۡعَبۡدُ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٌ

And We granted David his son Solomon, as Our favor and grace to him so that he would be pleased. What an excellent slave Solomon was. He would abundantly repent and return to Allah.

(٣١) إِذۡ عُرِضَ عَلَيۡهِ بِٱلۡعَشِيِّ ٱلصَّٰفِنَٰتُ ٱلۡجِيَادُ

Remember when, at the time of Asr, fast purebred horses were presented to him, standing on three feet with one foot raised. These purebred horses continued to be presented to him until sunset.

(٣٢) فَقَالَ إِنِّيٓ أَحۡبَبۡتُ حُبَّ ٱلۡخَيۡرِ عَن ذِكۡرِ رَبِّي حَتَّىٰ تَوَارَتۡ بِٱلۡحِجَابِ

So Solomon said: I preferred the love of wealth, which includes these horses, over the remembrance of my Lord until sunset, and I delayed the afternoon prayer.

(٣٣) رُدُّوهَا عَلَيَّۖ فَطَفِقَ مَسۡحَۢا بِٱلسُّوقِ وَٱلۡأَعۡنَاقِ

[He then said:] Bring these horses back to me. So they brought them back to him, and he began striking their legs and necks with his sword.

(٣٤) وَلَقَدۡ فَتَنَّا سُلَيۡمَٰنَ وَأَلۡقَيۡنَا عَلَىٰ كُرۡسِيِّهِۦ جَسَدٗا ثُمَّ أَنَابَ

And We tested Solomon and placed on his throne a devil in the form of a human who took control of his kingdom for a short while. Then Allah returned his kingdom to him and gave him authority over the devils.

(٣٥) قَالَ رَبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَهَبۡ لِي مُلۡكٗا لَّا يَنۢبَغِي لِأَحَدٖ مِّنۢ بَعۡدِيٓۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ

Solomon said: O my Lord, forgive me for my sins and grant me an exclusive kingdom, which no person will have after me. O my Lord, You give abundantly, and You are very generous.

(٣٦) فَسَخَّرۡنَا لَهُ ٱلرِّيحَ تَجۡرِي بِأَمۡرِهِۦ رُخَآءً حَيۡثُ أَصَابَ

So We responded to him and subjugated the wind for him. It would submit to his command [and blow] gently. Despite its strength and swift movement, there was no gale-force wind. It would carry him wherever he intended.

(٣٧) وَٱلشَّيَٰطِينَ كُلَّ بَنَّآءٖ وَغَوَّاصٖ

And We subjugated the devils for him, complying with his orders. Some were builders, and others were divers who would dive into the seas and take out pearls from them.

(٣٨) وَءَاخَرِينَ مُقَرَّنِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَصۡفَادِ

And among the devils, some rebellious ones were subservient to him. They were bound together in chains, unable to move.

(٣٩) هَٰذَا عَطَآؤُنَا فَٱمۡنُنۡ أَوۡ أَمۡسِكۡ بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ

O Solomon, this is Our gift, which We gifted you in response to what you asked of Us. So give and withhold from whoever you want, as you will never be held to account for your giving or withholding.

(٤٠) وَإِنَّ لَهُۥ عِندَنَا لَزُلۡفَىٰ وَحُسۡنَ مَ‍َٔابٖ

And Solomon is among the close ones with us. He will receive a beautiful destination, which he will return to: Paradise.

(٤١) وَٱذۡكُرۡ عَبۡدَنَآ أَيُّوبَ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّي مَسَّنِيَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ بِنُصۡبٖ وَعَذَابٍ

O Messenger, remember Our slave Job when he supplicated to Allah, his Lord: The Devil has afflicted me with a tiring and painful condition.

(٤٢) ٱرۡكُضۡ بِرِجۡلِكَۖ هَٰذَا مُغۡتَسَلُۢ بَارِدٞ وَشَرَابٞ

So We then said to him: Strike your foot on the ground. So he struck his foot on the ground, and water sprung forth for him from it, which he would drink and bathe from, and through it, his suffering and pain would disappear.

Points of Benefit:

Encouragement to ponder upon the Quran.
The verses contain evidence that according to the pureness of the heart and the understanding of a person, he [or she] will bear in mind and benefit from the Holy Quran.
The verses contain evidence of the validity of the well-known rule: (Whoever abandons something for Allah, Allah will compensate him with something better than it).
