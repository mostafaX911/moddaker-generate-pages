(٤٣) وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥٓ أَهۡلَهُۥ وَمِثۡلَهُم مَّعَهُمۡ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنَّا وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

So We responded to him and removed the difficulty he was afflicted with: We gave him back his family and added to them a similar number of sons and grandsons as a mercy from Us to him and a recompense for his patience so that the people of superior intellect are reminded that the outcome of patience is relief and reward.

(٤٤) وَخُذۡ بِيَدِكَ ضِغۡثٗا فَٱضۡرِب بِّهِۦ وَلَا تَحۡنَثۡۗ إِنَّا وَجَدۡنَٰهُ صَابِرٗاۚ نِّعۡمَ ٱلۡعَبۡدُ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٞ

When Job became angry with his wife, he swore an oath that he would lash her a hundred times, so We said to him: O Job, take a bunch of twigs in your hand and hit her with them to fulfill your oath. And do not break the oath you made. He then took a bunch of twigs and hit her with them. We found him to be patient in what We tried him with. What an excellent slave he was. He would return to Allah and repent to Him abundantly.

(٤٥) وَٱذۡكُرۡ عِبَٰدَنَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَإِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَ أُوْلِي ٱلۡأَيۡدِي وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرِ

O Messenger, remember Our slaves whom We chose and the messengers whom We sent: Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. They were people firm in obeying Allah and seeking His pleasure, and they were people of real insight into the truth.

(٤٦) إِنَّآ أَخۡلَصۡنَٰهُم بِخَالِصَةٖ ذِكۡرَى ٱلدَّارِ

We blessed them with a special favor for which We selected them, occupying their hearts with the remembrance of the abode of the Hereafter, preparing for it through righteous deeds, and inviting people towards doing deeds to gain it.

(٤٧) وَإِنَّهُمۡ عِندَنَا لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُصۡطَفَيۡنَ ٱلۡأَخۡيَارِ

And they were in Our sight among those whom We chose for Our obedience and worship, and whom We selected to bear Our message and convey it to the people.

(٤٨) وَٱذۡكُرۡ إِسۡمَٰعِيلَ وَٱلۡيَسَعَ وَذَا ٱلۡكِفۡلِۖ وَكُلّٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَخۡيَارِ

O Prophet, remember Ishmael, the son of Abraham, Elisha, and Dhul-Kifl, and praise them well as they deserve it. All these individuals were among the chosen and selected by Allah.

(٤٩) هَٰذَا ذِكۡرٞۚ وَإِنَّ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ لَحُسۡنَ مَ‍َٔابٖ

This is only a reminder for these people of good praise in the Quran. And for those who fear Allah by fulfilling His orders and avoiding His prohibitions, is a beautiful destination in the abode of the Hereafter.

(٥٠) جَنَّٰتِ عَدۡنٖ مُّفَتَّحَةٗ لَّهُمُ ٱلۡأَبۡوَٰبُ

This beautiful destination is the gardens of eternity; they will enter it on the Day of Resurrection through doors that will be opened to welcome them.

(٥١) مُتَّكِ‍ِٔينَ فِيهَا يَدۡعُونَ فِيهَا بِفَٰكِهَةٖ كَثِيرَةٖ وَشَرَابٖ

Reclining on couches that will be adorned for them. They will ask their attendants to bring them the many different types of fruit and drinks they desire, such as wine and other things.

(٥٢) ۞وَعِندَهُمۡ قَٰصِرَٰتُ ٱلطَّرۡفِ أَتۡرَابٌ

And with them will be women whose gaze will be limited only to them, their husbands. They will be all of equal age.

(٥٣) هَٰذَا مَا تُوعَدُونَ لِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

This is the pleasant recompense you are being promised, O you who feared Allah, on the Day of Resurrection for the righteous deeds you used to do in the world.

(٥٤) إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَرِزۡقُنَا مَا لَهُۥ مِن نَّفَادٍ

This recompense that We have mentioned is our provision that We will provide those who fear Allah on the Day of Resurrection. It is a continuous provision that will never pause or end.

(٥٥) هَٰذَاۚ وَإِنَّ لِلطَّٰغِينَ لَشَرَّ مَ‍َٔابٖ

This that We have mentioned is the reward for those who fear Allah. And those who transgress the limits of Allah through disbelief and committing acts of disobedience will have a recompense different from those who fear Allah. They will have the worst destination they shall return to on the Day of Resurrection.

(٥٦) جَهَنَّمَ يَصۡلَوۡنَهَا فَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمِهَادُ

This recompense is Hell, which will surround them, and they will experience its heat and flames. They will have a place to lay down in it, and what an evil place they will have!

(٥٧) هَٰذَا فَلۡيَذُوقُوهُ حَمِيمٞ وَغَسَّاقٞ

This torment is boiling water and pus flowing from the bodies of the people of the Fire being punished in it, so they should drink it as it is their drink. However, it is a drink which does not quench thirst.

(٥٨) وَءَاخَرُ مِن شَكۡلِهِۦٓ أَزۡوَٰجٌ

And they will receive another torment similar to this one. They will receive various torments, which they will be punished with in the Hereafter.

(٥٩) هَٰذَا فَوۡجٞ مُّقۡتَحِمٞ مَّعَكُمۡ لَا مَرۡحَبَۢا بِهِمۡۚ إِنَّهُمۡ صَالُواْ ٱلنَّارِ

And when the people of the Fire enter, they will dispute with one another, cursing and disassociating from one another. Some will say: This is a [new] group that will inhabit the Fire with you. They will respond: They are not welcome; they too will suffer the torment of the Fire like we are suffering.

(٦٠) قَالُواْ بَلۡ أَنتُمۡ لَا مَرۡحَبَۢا بِكُمۡۖ أَنتُمۡ قَدَّمۡتُمُوهُ لَنَاۖ فَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡقَرَارُ

The followers will say to their leaders who were followed: Instead, O leaders who were followed, you are not welcome, as you caused us this painful torment by misguiding and leading us astray. What a terrible settlement this is; the settlement of all of them is Hellfire.

(٦١) قَالُواْ رَبَّنَا مَن قَدَّمَ لَنَا هَٰذَا فَزِدۡهُ عَذَابٗا ضِعۡفٗا فِي ٱلنَّارِ

The followers will say: O our Lord, whoever misguided us from the guidance after it came to us, double his torment in the Fire.

Points of Benefit:

Whoever is patient in the face of harm, the Exalted Allah will cite them with a reward sooner and later, and their prayers will be answered when they call upon Him.
In the verses, there is evidence that the husband has the right to beat his wife as a discipline, but non-severe beating. Job, peace be upon him, swore to hit his wife, so he did.
