(٦٢) وَقَالُواْ مَا لَنَا لَا نَرَىٰ رِجَالٗا كُنَّا نَعُدُّهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَشۡرَارِ

And the arrogant tyrants will say: Why do we not see men with us in the Fire whom we used to consider among the wretched in the world and who deserve punishment?

(٦٣) أَتَّخَذۡنَٰهُمۡ سِخۡرِيًّا أَمۡ زَاغَتۡ عَنۡهُمُ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ

Was mocking them a mistake thus they do not deserve to be punished, or was mocking them right, and they have entered the Fire, but we have not seen them yet?

(٦٤) إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ لَحَقّٞ تَخَاصُمُ أَهۡلِ ٱلنَّارِ

This disputing of the disbelievers on the Day of Resurrection, which We have mentioned to you, is the truth in which there is no doubt.

(٦٥) قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ مُنذِرٞۖ وَمَا مِنۡ إِلَٰهٍ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡوَٰحِدُ ٱلۡقَهَّارُ

O Muhammad, say to the disbelievers from your people: I am only a warner to you of Allah's torment, lest it descends upon you due to disbelief and rejecting His messengers. And there is no deity worthy of worship besides Allah; may He be Exalted, as He is unique in His greatness, attributes, and names, and He is the Subduer who has subdued everything. So everything submits to Him.

(٦٦) رَبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡغَفَّٰرُ

And He is the Lord of the heavens, the Earth, and whatever is between them. And He is the Mighty in His kingdom, with nobody to overpower Him, and Forgiving of the sins of His slaves who repent to Him.

(٦٧) قُلۡ هُوَ نَبَؤٌاْ عَظِيمٌ

O Messenger, tell these rejectors: The Quran is news of great importance.

(٦٨) أَنتُمۡ عَنۡهُ مُعۡرِضُونَ

You are turning away from this news of great importance; you do not turn towards it.

(٦٩) مَا كَانَ لِيَ مِنۡ عِلۡمِۢ بِٱلۡمَلَإِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰٓ إِذۡ يَخۡتَصِمُونَ

I would not have knowledge of the discussion between the angels regarding the creation of Adam if Allah had not revealed it to me and taught me.

(٧٠) إِن يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ إِلَّآ أَنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ نَذِيرٞ مُّبِينٌ

Allah only reveals to me what He reveals, and I am a clear warner to you of His torment.

(٧١) إِذۡ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ إِنِّي خَٰلِقُۢ بَشَرٗا مِّن طِينٖ

Remember when your Lord said to the angels: I am going to create a man from clay, and he is Adam (peace be upon him).

(٧٢) فَإِذَا سَوَّيۡتُهُۥ وَنَفَخۡتُ فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِي فَقَعُواْ لَهُۥ سَٰجِدِينَ

So once I have fashioned his form, perfected his appearance, and blown My spirit into him, then prostrate to him!

(٧٣) فَسَجَدَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ كُلُّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعُونَ

So the angels fulfilled the command of their Lord, and all of them prostrated by way of honor, and none of them remained except that they prostrated to Adam.

(٧٤) إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرَ وَكَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

But not Iblis, who was too arrogant to prostrate. And by being arrogant to the command of his Lord, he became one of the disbelievers.

(٧٥) قَالَ يَٰٓإِبۡلِيسُ مَا مَنَعَكَ أَن تَسۡجُدَ لِمَا خَلَقۡتُ بِيَدَيَّۖ أَسۡتَكۡبَرۡتَ أَمۡ كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡعَالِينَ

Allah said: O Iblis, what prevented you from prostrating to Adam whom I created with My Own Hands? Did arrogance prevent you from prostrating to him, or have you always been arrogant and haughty against your Lord?

(٧٦) قَالَ أَنَا۠ خَيۡرٞ مِّنۡهُ خَلَقۡتَنِي مِن نَّارٖ وَخَلَقۡتَهُۥ مِن طِينٖ

Iblis said: I am better than Adam because You created me from fire while You created him from clay. This was due to his claim that fire was a more noble substance than clay.

(٧٧) قَالَ فَٱخۡرُجۡ مِنۡهَا فَإِنَّكَ رَجِيمٞ

Allah told Iblis: Get out of Paradise because you are accursed and reviled.

(٧٨) وَإِنَّ عَلَيۡكَ لَعۡنَتِيٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ

And you are to be rejected from Paradise until the Day of Recompense, which is the Day of Resurrection.

(٧٩) قَالَ رَبِّ فَأَنظِرۡنِيٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ

Iblis said: Give me respite and do not cause me to die until the day You resurrect Your slaves.

(٨٠) قَالَ فَإِنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُنظَرِينَ

Allah said: You are amongst those given respite.

(٨١) إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡوَقۡتِ ٱلۡمَعۡلُومِ

Until the appointed day for your destruction.

(٨٢) قَالَ فَبِعِزَّتِكَ لَأُغۡوِيَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

Iblis said: I take an oath by Your [attributes of] power and subjugation that I shall misguide all of the children of Adam

(٨٣) إِلَّا عِبَادَكَ مِنۡهُمُ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ

Except those whom You protect from my misguidance and those whom You have chosen to worship You alone.

Points of Benefit:

Qiyas (A standard used for comparison ) or Ijtihad (discretion in passing a judgment) in the presence of an explicit text [the Quran and Sunnah] is a void method.
The disbelief of Iblis (Satan) is a disbelief of stubbornness and arrogance.
Those whom Allah has chosen to worship Him among the creation, Satan has no way on them.
