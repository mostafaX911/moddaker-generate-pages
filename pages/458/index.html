(٨٤) قَالَ فَٱلۡحَقُّ وَٱلۡحَقَّ أَقُولُ

Allah, the Exalted, said: The truth is from Me, and the truth is what I say. I do not say anything besides it.

(٨٥) لَأَمۡلَأَنَّ جَهَنَّمَ مِنكَ وَمِمَّن تَبِعَكَ مِنۡهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

On the Day of Resurrection, I shall fill Hell with you and those from the children of Adam who follow you in your disbelief, every last one of you.

(٨٦) قُلۡ مَآ أَسۡ‍َٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرٖ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُتَكَلِّفِينَ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists: I do not ask you for any reward for the advice I convey to you, and I am not of the pretentious by bringing more than what I have been ordered with.

(٨٧) إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرٞ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

The Quran is only a reminder to those men and jinn who are legally competent.

(٨٨) وَلَتَعۡلَمُنَّ نَبَأَهُۥ بَعۡدَ حِينِۢ

And definitely, after a short time, when you die, you will come to know the news of this Quran and that it is true.

The Troops (Surah Az-Zumar)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Calling to monotheism and sincerity while rejecting polytheism.

Explanation:

(١) تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ

The revelation of the Quran is from Allah, the Mighty whom nobody can overpower, the Wise in His creating, planning, and legislating. No one besides Him revealed it; may He be Exalted.

(٢) إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ فَٱعۡبُدِ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصٗا لَّهُ ٱلدِّينَ

O Messenger, We have revealed the Quran to you, which contains the truth, so all of its information is true, and all of its rulings are just. So worship Allah, declaring Him to be One and sincerely devoted to Him.

(٣) أَلَا لِلَّهِ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلۡخَالِصُۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَ مَا نَعۡبُدُهُمۡ إِلَّا لِيُقَرِّبُونَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ زُلۡفَىٰٓ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَحۡكُمُ بَيۡنَهُمۡ فِي مَا هُمۡ فِيهِ يَخۡتَلِفُونَۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي مَنۡ هُوَ كَٰذِبٞ كَفَّارٞ

Indeed, sincere devotion is due only to Allah, pure of polytheism. And those who have taken guardians besides Allah, such as idols and any false deities which they worship instead of Allah, presenting an excuse for worshipping them by saying that they do not worship these except so that they bring them closer to Allah in status, present their needs to Him and intercede on their behalf with Him; Allah will judge between the monotheistic believers and the polytheistic disbelievers on the Day of Resurrection regarding Allah's Oneness, in which they used to differ. Allah does not inspire with guidance towards the truth those who lie against Him by associating a partner with Him and those who deny Allah's favors.

(٤) لَّوۡ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ أَن يَتَّخِذَ وَلَدٗا لَّٱصۡطَفَىٰ مِمَّا يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥۖ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡوَٰحِدُ ٱلۡقَهَّارُ

If Allah wanted to take a son, He would have chosen whom He wanted from His creation and taken him as a child. Free and sanctified is He, from what these polytheists say. He is One in His essence, attributes, and actions, having no partner in them, the Subduer of all His creation.

(٥) خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ يُكَوِّرُ ٱلَّيۡلَ عَلَى ٱلنَّهَارِ وَيُكَوِّرُ ٱلنَّهَارَ عَلَى ٱلَّيۡلِۖ وَسَخَّرَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَۖ كُلّٞ يَجۡرِي لِأَجَلٖ مُّسَمًّىۗ أَلَا هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡغَفَّٰرُ

He created the heavens and the Earth with complete wisdom, not in vain, as the wrongdoers say. He merges the night into the day and the day into the night, so when one comes, the other disappears. And He has made the sun and the moon subservient; each one orbits for up until an appointed time, which is the end of this life. He (may He be Exalted) is the Mighty who takes retribution from His enemies, and nobody can overpower Him, Forgiving the sins of His slaves who repent.

Points of Benefit:

The one who calls to Allah anticipates reward from Him. He doesn't seek repayment from people for the truth he calls them to.
Affectation is not part of religion.
Supplication to Allah is through His names, attributes, faith, doing good deeds, and nothing else.
