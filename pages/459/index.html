(٦) خَلَقَكُم مِّن نَّفۡسٖ وَٰحِدَةٖ ثُمَّ جَعَلَ مِنۡهَا زَوۡجَهَا وَأَنزَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ ثَمَٰنِيَةَ أَزۡوَٰجٖۚ يَخۡلُقُكُمۡ فِي بُطُونِ أُمَّهَٰتِكُمۡ خَلۡقٗا مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ خَلۡقٖ فِي ظُلُمَٰتٖ ثَلَٰثٖۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمۡ لَهُ ٱلۡمُلۡكُۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُصۡرَفُونَ

O humanity, your Lord created you from one soul, Adam, and then He created from Adam his wife, Eve. He created four pairs of livestock for your benefit: camels, cows, sheep, and goats, from each type, male and female. He creates you in the wombs of your mothers, stage after stage, in three layers of darkness: the stomach, womb, and placenta. He who creates all of that is Allah, your Lord. To Him alone belongs the dominion. There is no deity worthy of worship besides Him, so how are you turned away from His worship to worshipping those who create nothing but are instead created?

(٧) إِن تَكۡفُرُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَنِيٌّ عَنكُمۡۖ وَلَا يَرۡضَىٰ لِعِبَادِهِ ٱلۡكُفۡرَۖ وَإِن تَشۡكُرُواْ يَرۡضَهُ لَكُمۡۗ وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةٞ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰۚ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُم مَّرۡجِعُكُمۡ فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

O humanity, if you disbelieve in your Lord, then Allah does not need your belief. Your disbelief does not harm Him. The harm of your disbelief only affects you. He does not approve for His slaves to disbelieve in Him, nor does He order them to disbelieve, because Allah does not order indecency and wrong. If you show gratitude to Allah for His favors and believe in Him, He will be pleased with your gratitude and reward you for it. No soul will carry the sin of another; instead, every soul will be retained for what it has done. Then, to your Lord alone is your return on the Day of Resurrection, and He will inform you of what you used to do in the world and recompense you for your deeds accordingly. He (may He be Exalted) is Knowing of what is in the hearts of His slaves; nothing of what is in them is hidden from Him.

(٨) ۞وَإِذَا مَسَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ ضُرّٞ دَعَا رَبَّهُۥ مُنِيبًا إِلَيۡهِ ثُمَّ إِذَا خَوَّلَهُۥ نِعۡمَةٗ مِّنۡهُ نَسِيَ مَا كَانَ يَدۡعُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ مِن قَبۡلُ وَجَعَلَ لِلَّهِ أَندَادٗا لِّيُضِلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦۚ قُلۡ تَمَتَّعۡ بِكُفۡرِكَ قَلِيلًا إِنَّكَ مِنۡ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلنَّارِ

And when the disbeliever is afflicted by a difficulty, such as illness, loss of wealth, or fear of drowning, he supplicates to his Lord (may He be Exalted) to remove the difficulty he experiences, turning to Him alone. Then, when Allah favors him by removing the difficulty that afflicted him, he abandons Allah, whom he used to beseech before, and he associates partners with Allah, whom he worships instead of Him. O Messenger, say to a person like this: Enjoy your disbelief for as long as you live, which is a short time, as you are among the people of the Fire who will be inseparable from it on the Day of Resurrection like a companion is inseparable from his friend.

(٩) أَمَّنۡ هُوَ قَٰنِتٌ ءَانَآءَ ٱلَّيۡلِ سَاجِدٗا وَقَآئِمٗا يَحۡذَرُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَيَرۡجُواْ رَحۡمَةَ رَبِّهِۦۗ قُلۡ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِي ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡلَمُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَۗ إِنَّمَا يَتَذَكَّرُ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

Is he who is obedient to Allah, spending the time at night in prostration to his Lord and standing, fearing the torment of the Hereafter and hoping for the mercy of his Lord better, or that disbeliever who worships Allah in hardship but disbelieves in Him in times of ease and associates partners with Him? O Messenger, say: Are those who know what Allah has made obligatory upon them due to their knowledge of Allah and those who do not know any of this equal? Only people of sound intellect recognize the difference between the two.

(١٠) قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمۡۚ لِلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ فِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةٞۗ وَأَرۡضُ ٱللَّهِ وَٰسِعَةٌۗ إِنَّمَا يُوَفَّى ٱلصَّٰبِرُونَ أَجۡرَهُم بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ

O Messenger, say to my slaves who believe in Me and My messengers: Fear your Lord by fulfilling His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. Those among you who do good deeds in this world will receive good in this world through divine assistance, health, and wealth, and in the Hereafter, they will receive Paradise. The land of Allah is vast, so migrate therein until you find a place where you can worship Allah without anything stopping you. The reward given to the patient on the Day of Resurrection will be immeasurable and unlimited due to its abundance and variety.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's care for the human being in their mother's belly.
Affirming the attribute of wealth and the attribute of contentment to the Exalted Allah in a manner befitting Him the Exalted and Transcendent.
The disbelievers' recognition of Allah during hardship and his denial of Him during prosperity is evidence of their uncertainty and confusion.
Fear and hope are two characteristics of people of faith.
