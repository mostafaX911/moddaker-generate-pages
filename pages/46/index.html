(٢٧٠) وَمَآ أَنفَقۡتُم مِّن نَّفَقَةٍ أَوۡ نَذَرۡتُم مِّن نَّذۡرٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُهُۥۗ وَمَا لِلظَّٰلِمِينَ مِنۡ أَنصَارٍ

Whatever you spend, be it a little or a lot, seeking the pleasure of Allah, or any action you have obligated yourselves to do for Allah without having been required to do it, Allah knows of it, and it will not be lost. He will recompense you greatly for it. Those who do wrong by not fulfilling their obligations and transgressing Allah's limits will have no helpers to protect them against the torment on the Day of Resurrection.

(٢٧١) إِن تُبۡدُواْ ٱلصَّدَقَٰتِ فَنِعِمَّا هِيَۖ وَإِن تُخۡفُوهَا وَتُؤۡتُوهَا ٱلۡفُقَرَآءَ فَهُوَ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡۚ وَيُكَفِّرُ عَنكُم مِّن سَيِّ‍َٔاتِكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٞ

It is noble if you openly declare what you spend on charity. But if you conceal it and give it to people experiencing poverty privately, this is better as it indicates sincerity. Charity of the sincere ones is the means of their sins being covered up and forgiven. Allah knows what you do; nothing from your circumstances is hidden.

(٢٧٢) ۞لَّيۡسَ عَلَيۡكَ هُدَىٰهُمۡ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُۗ وَمَا تُنفِقُواْ مِنۡ خَيۡرٖ فَلِأَنفُسِكُمۡۚ وَمَا تُنفِقُونَ إِلَّا ٱبۡتِغَآءَ وَجۡهِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَمَا تُنفِقُواْ مِنۡ خَيۡرٖ يُوَفَّ إِلَيۡكُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تُظۡلَمُونَ

O Prophet, you are not responsible for making them believe and submit to the truth: your duty is to direct them to the truth and explain it to them. Only Allah can guide them to accept the truth, and He guides whomever He wishes. The benefit of any good you spend returns to you; Allah does not need it. So, let your spending be only for Allah's sake: true believers spend only for Allah's pleasure. You will receive the full reward for whatever you spend, no matter how little, because Allah does not wrong anyone.

After calling upon believers to spend their wealth for His sake, Allah defines the causes they should be spending upon, stating:

(٢٧٣) لِلۡفُقَرَآءِ ٱلَّذِينَ أُحۡصِرُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ لَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ ضَرۡبٗا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَحۡسَبُهُمُ ٱلۡجَاهِلُ أَغۡنِيَآءَ مِنَ ٱلتَّعَفُّفِ تَعۡرِفُهُم بِسِيمَٰهُمۡ لَا يَسۡ‍َٔلُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ إِلۡحَافٗاۗ وَمَا تُنفِقُواْ مِنۡ خَيۡرٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِهِۦ عَلِيمٌ

Spend on those poor people who, because they are busy striving in Allah's path, cannot travel to earn a living. Due to their not asking, an uninformed person will think they are not in need. However, the person who knows will recognize them by the apparent signs on their body and clothing. They are unlike other poor people who beg persistently. Allah knows of any wealth you spend and will recompense you greatly.

(٢٧٤) ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَهُم بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ سِرّٗا وَعَلَانِيَةٗ فَلَهُمۡ أَجۡرُهُمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ وَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

Those who spend their wealth for Allah's sake at night and during the day, secretly and openly, without showing off or looking for fame, will be rewarded by their Lord on the Day of Resurrection. As a favor and blessing from Allah, they will not fear what awaits them or grieve over what they missed out on in this world.

Points of Benefit:

If a believer sincerely spends his charity and alms, there is no harm in revealing or concealing his charitable spending according to what is beneficial. However, concealing his charity is greater in reward and virtue because it is closer to sincerity.
The call to believers to be attentive and caring towards those in need who, due to their dignity, refrain from revealing their circumstances and asking people for help.
The legitimacy of spending in the way of Allah at all times and its great reward, as Allah has promised immense rewards in both this world and the Hereafter.
