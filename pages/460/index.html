(١١) قُلۡ إِنِّيٓ أُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أَعۡبُدَ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصٗا لَّهُ ٱلدِّينَ

Say, O Messenger: Allah has instructed me to worship Him alone, being sincerely devoted to Him.

(١٢) وَأُمِرۡتُ لِأَنۡ أَكُونَ أَوَّلَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ

He has instructed me to be the first to surrender and submit to Him from this nation.

(١٣) قُلۡ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ إِنۡ عَصَيۡتُ رَبِّي عَذَابَ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ

Say, O Messenger: I fear, if I disobey Allah and I do not obey Him, the punishment of a great day, which is the Day of Resurrection.

(١٤) قُلِ ٱللَّهَ أَعۡبُدُ مُخۡلِصٗا لَّهُۥ دِينِي

Say, O Messenger: I worship Allah alone, being sincere in my devotion to Him. I do not worship others with Him.

(١٥) فَٱعۡبُدُواْ مَا شِئۡتُم مِّن دُونِهِۦۗ قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَأَهۡلِيهِمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۗ أَلَا ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡخُسۡرَانُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

So, O polytheists, worship whatever idols you wish instead of Him (this instruction is a threat). Say, O Messenger: The true losers are those who will lose themselves and their families, whom they will not meet in the Hereafter due to either being separated from their families when their families enter Paradise without them or their families will enter the Fire with them. Thus, they will never meet. That is indeed, and undoubtedly, the obvious loss.

(١٦) لَهُم مِّن فَوۡقِهِمۡ ظُلَلٞ مِّنَ ٱلنَّارِ وَمِن تَحۡتِهِمۡ ظُلَلٞۚ ذَٰلِكَ يُخَوِّفُ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦ عِبَادَهُۥۚ يَٰعِبَادِ فَٱتَّقُونِ

They will have smoke, flames, and extreme heat above and beneath them. That torment is what Allah warns His slaves of. So, O My slaves, fear Me by complying with My commands and avoiding My prohibitions.

After mentioning the conditions facing the wrongdoers, Allah describes the conditions facing His righteous slaves, stating:

(١٧) وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱجۡتَنَبُواْ ٱلطَّٰغُوتَ أَن يَعۡبُدُوهَا وَأَنَابُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ لَهُمُ ٱلۡبُشۡرَىٰۚ فَبَشِّرۡ عِبَادِ

Those who avoid the worship of idols and any other false gods instead of Allah and return to Allah in repentance will receive glad tidings of Paradise at the time of death, in the grave, and on the Day of Resurrection. So give glad tidings, O Messenger, to My slaves.

(١٨) ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَمِعُونَ ٱلۡقَوۡلَ فَيَتَّبِعُونَ أَحۡسَنَهُۥٓۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ هَدَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمۡ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

Those who attentively listen to what is said, distinguish between the good and evil of it, and then follow the best speech because of its benefit; those who possess these attributes are the ones whom Allah has given guidance, and they are the ones who possess sound intellect.

(١٩) أَفَمَنۡ حَقَّ عَلَيۡهِ كَلِمَةُ ٱلۡعَذَابِ أَفَأَنتَ تُنقِذُ مَن فِي ٱلنَّارِ

Those against whom the decree of torment has been justified due to their persistence in disbelief and misguidance, so, O Messenger, you have no means to guide them; can you, O Messenger, rescue such people from the Fire?

(٢٠) لَٰكِنِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْ رَبَّهُمۡ لَهُمۡ غُرَفٞ مِّن فَوۡقِهَا غُرَفٞ مَّبۡنِيَّةٞ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۖ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ لَا يُخۡلِفُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمِيعَادَ

But those who fear their Lord by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions will have lofty mansions, one above the other, with rivers flowing beneath them. Allah has firmly promised this to them, and Allah does not break His promise.

(٢١) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَسَلَكَهُۥ يَنَٰبِيعَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ثُمَّ يُخۡرِجُ بِهِۦ زَرۡعٗا مُّخۡتَلِفًا أَلۡوَٰنُهُۥ ثُمَّ يَهِيجُ فَتَرَىٰهُ مُصۡفَرّٗا ثُمَّ يَجۡعَلُهُۥ حُطَٰمًاۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَذِكۡرَىٰ لِأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

You are well aware, by having seen, that Allah sends down rainwater from the sky and fills springs and rivers with it. Then, with this water, He produces crops of various colors. Then these crops become dry, and you, witnessing this, see them turning yellow after being green. Then, after they become dry, He makes them into chaff. In what has been mentioned, there is a reminder for those whose hearts are alive.

Points of Benefit:

Sincerity in worship to Allah is a necessity for its acceptance.
Sins are among the causes of Allah's torment and wrath.
Successful guidance to faith is in the Hand of Allah, not in the hands of the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم.
