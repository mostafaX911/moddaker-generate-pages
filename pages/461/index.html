(٢٢) أَفَمَن شَرَحَ ٱللَّهُ صَدۡرَهُۥ لِلۡإِسۡلَٰمِ فَهُوَ عَلَىٰ نُورٖ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦۚ فَوَيۡلٞ لِّلۡقَٰسِيَةِ قُلُوبُهُم مِّن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٍ

Then is he who Allah has opened his chest to Islam and is guided to it with insight from his Lord, like the one whose heart is hardened against Allah's remembrance? They can never be equal! Salvation is for those guided, and loss is for those whose hearts are hardened against Allah's remembrance. Such people are in clear misguidance from the truth.

(٢٣) ٱللَّهُ نَزَّلَ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلۡحَدِيثِ كِتَٰبٗا مُّتَشَٰبِهٗا مَّثَانِيَ تَقۡشَعِرُّ مِنۡهُ جُلُودُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخۡشَوۡنَ رَبَّهُمۡ ثُمَّ تَلِينُ جُلُودُهُمۡ وَقُلُوبُهُمۡ إِلَىٰ ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُدَى ٱللَّهِ يَهۡدِي بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِنۡ هَادٍ

Allah has sent down to His Messenger, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, the Quran, which is the best speech. He sent it down so that part of it resembles other parts in truthfulness, beauty, and consistency without contradiction. It contains numerous stories and rulings, promises and threats, attributes of the people of truth, attributes of the people of falsehood, and so forth. The skin of those in awe of their Lord trembles when they hear the threats and warnings it contains. Then their skin and hearts soften to Allah's remembrance when they hear the hope and glad tidings it contains. What has been mentioned about the Quran and its effect is Allah's guidance by which He guides whoever He wills. Those whom Allah leaves astray and does not guide have no one to guide them.

(٢٤) أَفَمَن يَتَّقِي بِوَجۡهِهِۦ سُوٓءَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۚ وَقِيلَ لِلظَّٰلِمِينَ ذُوقُواْ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡسِبُونَ

Is the person whom Allah guided, blessed in this world, and admitted into Paradise in the Hereafter equal to the person who disbelieved and died as a disbeliever and, as a result, entered the Fire with his hands and feet tied, unable to shield himself against the Fire except by his face that he was pulled upon?! Those who wronged themselves by disbelieving and committing acts of disobedience will be told by way of reproach: Taste the disbelief and acts of disobedience you used to earn, for this is your recompense.

(٢٥) كَذَّبَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ فَأَتَىٰهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

The nations before these polytheists also rejected, and the torment came to them suddenly from where they did not expect. Thus, they could not prepare for it by repenting.

(٢٦) فَأَذَاقَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡخِزۡيَ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَلَعَذَابُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ أَكۡبَرُۚ لَوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ

Allah made them taste through that torment, humiliation, disgrace, and shame in the worldly life. The punishment of the Hereafter that awaits them is greater and more severe, if they only knew.

(٢٧) وَلَقَدۡ ضَرَبۡنَا لِلنَّاسِ فِي هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مِن كُلِّ مَثَلٖ لَّعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ

In this Quran revealed to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, We have given people various parables of good and evil, truth and falsehood, belief and disbelief, and so forth in the hope that they will take a lesson from these parables, act on the truth and abandon falsehood.

(٢٨) قُرۡءَانًا عَرَبِيًّا غَيۡرَ ذِي عِوَجٖ لَّعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَّقُونَ

We have made it an Arabic Quran, without any defect, deviation, or confusion, in the hope that they will fear Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

(٢٩) ضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا رَّجُلٗا فِيهِ شُرَكَآءُ مُتَشَٰكِسُونَ وَرَجُلٗا سَلَمٗا لِّرَجُلٍ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِيَانِ مَثَلًاۚ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِۚ بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

Allah gives an example of a polytheist and a monotheist as that of a man owned by disputing partners - if he pleases some, he angers others, and he is thus confused and disturbed; and of a man who is devoted to one master alone who owns him and whose intentions he knows - he is, therefore, comfortable and calm. These two men are not the same. All praise is to Allah, but most of them do not know, and consequently, they associate others as partners with Allah.

(٣٠) إِنَّكَ مَيِّتٞ وَإِنَّهُم مَّيِّتُونَ

You, O Messenger, will certainly die, and they will die too.

(٣١) ثُمَّ إِنَّكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ عِندَ رَبِّكُمۡ تَخۡتَصِمُونَ

Then, O humanity, on the Day of Resurrection, you will dispute among yourselves before your Lord over that which you disagree with one another, and it will become clear who is on the truth right and who is on falsehood.

Points of Benefit:

The people of faith and heed are those who are humbled when hearing the Quran. On the other hand, the people of sin and letdown are those who do not benefit from it.
Denying what the Messengers brought is the reason for the descent of torment in this world, the Hereafter, or both.
The Quran contains detailed [and general] explanations and proverbs for this world and the Hereafter, so it doesn't leave anything.
