(٣٢) ۞فَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّن كَذَبَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ وَكَذَّبَ بِٱلصِّدۡقِ إِذۡ جَآءَهُۥٓۚ أَلَيۡسَ فِي جَهَنَّمَ مَثۡوٗى لِّلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

No one is more wrong than the person who attributes to Allah what does not befit Him, such as a partner, spouse, or child, and denies the Quran. Also, no one is more wrong than the person who rejects the truth that Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم brought. Is there not in the Fire an eternal abode for those who reject Allah and what His Messenger brought? Indeed, there is a dwelling for them therein.

After mentioning the outright liar, Allah mentions the reliable and truthful, stating:

(٣٣) وَٱلَّذِي جَآءَ بِٱلصِّدۡقِ وَصَدَّقَ بِهِۦٓ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُتَّقُونَ

The prophets and those others who brought truth in speech and action, and those who had faith in it and acted according to it, are the ones who are genuinely Allah-fearing, comply with the commands of their Lord, and avoid His prohibitions.

(٣٤) لَهُم مَّا يَشَآءُونَ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡۚ ذَٰلِكَ جَزَآءُ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

They will have all the everlasting pleasures they wish for from their Lord. That is the reward of those who do good deeds sincerely for their Creator and spread good amongst His slaves.

(٣٥) لِيُكَفِّرَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ أَسۡوَأَ ٱلَّذِي عَمِلُواْ وَيَجۡزِيَهُمۡ أَجۡرَهُم بِأَحۡسَنِ ٱلَّذِي كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

So that Allah may erase from them the worst sins that they used to do in this world, as they had repented from them and turned back to their Lord, and reward them according to the best deeds they used to do.

(٣٦) أَلَيۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِكَافٍ عَبۡدَهُۥۖ وَيُخَوِّفُونَكَ بِٱلَّذِينَ مِن دُونِهِۦۚ وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِنۡ هَادٖ

Is not Allah sufficient for His slave, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, in his religious and worldly matters and to ward off his enemy? Indeed, He is sufficient. They try to intimidate you, O Messenger, because of their ignorance and foolishness, with the idols they worship besides Allah, [claiming] they will bring you harm. Those whom Allah abandons and does not bless with guidance will have no one to guide them.

(٣٧) وَمَن يَهۡدِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن مُّضِلٍّۗ أَلَيۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِعَزِيزٖ ذِي ٱنتِقَامٖ

Whomever Allah blesses with guidance, no one will be able to mislead. Is Allah not Mighty, whom no one can challenge, and the One who takes retribution from those who reject and go against Him? Indeed, He is Mighty and One who takes vengeance.

(٣٨) وَلَئِن سَأَلۡتَهُم مَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ لَيَقُولُنَّ ٱللَّهُۚ قُلۡ أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ إِنۡ أَرَادَنِيَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرٍّ هَلۡ هُنَّ كَٰشِفَٰتُ ضُرِّهِۦٓ أَوۡ أَرَادَنِي بِرَحۡمَةٍ هَلۡ هُنَّ مُمۡسِكَٰتُ رَحۡمَتِهِۦۚ قُلۡ حَسۡبِيَ ٱللَّهُۖ عَلَيۡهِ يَتَوَكَّلُ ٱلۡمُتَوَكِّلُونَ

O Messenger, if you ask these polytheists who created the heavens and who created the Earth, they will say that Allah created them. Say to them, to show them the inability of their gods: Tell me about these idols you worship besides Allah. If Allah willed to harm me, do they have the power to remove that harm from me? Or if my Lord wished to grant me mercy from Himself, can they stop His mercy from reaching me? Say to them: Allah alone is enough for me. It is on Him that I rely in all my affairs. He is the only one relied upon by those who want to entrust their affairs.

(٣٩) قُلۡ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡمَلُواْ عَلَىٰ مَكَانَتِكُمۡ إِنِّي عَٰمِلٞۖ فَسَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ

Say, O Messenger: O my people, continue to act in that state that you have chosen of ascribing partners to Allah. I will continue to act on what my Lord has instructed me by calling towards His Oneness and devoting my worship to Him. Soon, you will come to know the outcome of each path.

(٤٠) مَن يَأۡتِيهِ عَذَابٞ يُخۡزِيهِ وَيَحِلُّ عَلَيۡهِ عَذَابٞ مُّقِيمٌ

Soon you will come to know who will be punished in this world in a way that disgraces and humiliates them and upon whom a perpetual punishment will descend in the Hereafter.

Points of Benefit:

The grave danger of slandering Allah and ascribing something to Him that does not befit Him, may He be Transcendent, or His legislation.
Establishing that Allah protects the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم from being harmed by his enemies.
Only singling out Allah as the Lord without singling Him out in worship will not save the person from the torment of the Fire.
