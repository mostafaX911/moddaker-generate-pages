(٤١) إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ لِلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ فَمَنِ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ فَلِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن ضَلَّ فَإِنَّمَا يَضِلُّ عَلَيۡهَاۖ وَمَآ أَنتَ عَلَيۡهِم بِوَكِيلٍ

O Messenger, We sent you the Quran in truth for the people so you can warn them. Whoever follows the guidance will only benefit from it himself. Allah does not benefit from him following the guidance as He does not need it. Whoever strays, then the harm of him straying is to his own detriment. His straying does not harm Allah. You are not in charge of them to force guidance on them. You are only obliged to convey to them what you have been instructed with.

(٤٢) ٱللَّهُ يَتَوَفَّى ٱلۡأَنفُسَ حِينَ مَوۡتِهَا وَٱلَّتِي لَمۡ تَمُتۡ فِي مَنَامِهَاۖ فَيُمۡسِكُ ٱلَّتِي قَضَىٰ عَلَيۡهَا ٱلۡمَوۡتَ وَيُرۡسِلُ ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰٓ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمًّىۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ

Allah is the One who takes away the souls when their term ends. He also takes the souls whose term has not ended when they sleep. He then withholds those for whom He has decreed death and releases those for whom He has not decreed death until a time fixed in His knowledge (may He be Exalted). In this taking away, release, and giving of death and life, there are signs for people who reflect that the One who does this can resurrect people after they die for reckoning and recompense.

(٤٣) أَمِ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ شُفَعَآءَۚ قُلۡ أَوَلَوۡ كَانُواْ لَا يَمۡلِكُونَ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا يَعۡقِلُونَ

The polytheists took their idols as intercessors, hoping to benefit from them instead of Allah. Say to them, O Messenger: Do you take them as intercessors even though they have no power to do anything for you or for themselves and do not understand? They are lifeless objects that cannot speak, hear, see, benefit, or harm.

(٤٤) قُل لِّلَّهِ ٱلشَّفَٰعَةُ جَمِيعٗاۖ لَّهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ ثُمَّ إِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists: All intercession belongs to Allah alone. No one can intercede before Him except with His permission, and the intercession can only be for those He approves. The control of the heavens and the control of the Earth belongs to Him alone. Then, to Him alone, you will return on the Day of Resurrection for reckoning and recompense, and He will give you the outcome you deserve for your actions.

(٤٥) وَإِذَا ذُكِرَ ٱللَّهُ وَحۡدَهُ ٱشۡمَأَزَّتۡ قُلُوبُ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ وَإِذَا ذُكِرَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ إِذَا هُمۡ يَسۡتَبۡشِرُونَ

Whenever Allah alone is mentioned, the hearts of the polytheists who do not believe in the Hereafter and the resurrection, reckoning, and recompense become filled with hatred. But they become happy when the idols they worship besides Allah are mentioned.

(٤٦) قُلِ ٱللَّهُمَّ فَاطِرَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ عَٰلِمَ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ أَنتَ تَحۡكُمُ بَيۡنَ عِبَادِكَ فِي مَا كَانُواْ فِيهِ يَخۡتَلِفُونَ

Say, O Messenger: O Allah, Creator of the heavens and the Earth without any precedent, Knower of what is absent and what is present. Nothing of that is hidden from You. You alone will decide between Your slaves on the Day of Resurrection what they used to disagree about in this world. It will become clear who is right and who is wrong, and who is fortunate and who is not.

(٤٧) وَلَوۡ أَنَّ لِلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ مَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا وَمِثۡلَهُۥ مَعَهُۥ لَٱفۡتَدَوۡاْ بِهِۦ مِن سُوٓءِ ٱلۡعَذَابِ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۚ وَبَدَا لَهُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَمۡ يَكُونُواْ يَحۡتَسِبُونَ

If those who wronged themselves by worshipping other than Allah and committing sins could have all of the wealth and treasures on this Earth to ransom themselves from the severe punishment on the Day of Resurrection, they would do so. However, even if they did, the ransom would not be accepted from them. Rather, they will face from Allah various types of torment they never used to expect.

Points of Benefit:

Sleeping and waking up are two daily lessons about death and resurrection.
When Allah is mentioned alone in front of the disbelievers, they become distressed and anxious because they remember what He has commanded and forbidden and that they have turned away from all of this.
On the Day of Resurrection, the disbeliever will wish to ransom himself with everything he owned even though he was stingy with it in the worldly life. But that will not be accepted from him.
