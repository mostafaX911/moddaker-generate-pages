(٤٨) وَبَدَا لَهُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتُ مَا كَسَبُواْ وَحَاقَ بِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

The evils of polytheism and the sins they committed will appear to them, and the punishment they were warned of in this world, which they mocked, will engulf them.

(٤٩) فَإِذَا مَسَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ ضُرّٞ دَعَانَا ثُمَّ إِذَا خَوَّلۡنَٰهُ نِعۡمَةٗ مِّنَّا قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أُوتِيتُهُۥ عَلَىٰ عِلۡمِۢۚ بَلۡ هِيَ فِتۡنَةٞ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

When a disbelieving man is afflicted by sickness or poverty, he calls on Us to remove his affliction. Then, when We bless him with health or wealth, the disbeliever says: Allah has only given me this because He knows I deserve it. It is a test and a means of luring him to his destruction. But most disbelievers do not know this and are deceived by the blessings Allah gives them.

(٥٠) قَدۡ قَالَهَا ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ فَمَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ

The disbelievers that came before them made this same statement, but the wealth and status they earned were useless to them.

(٥١) فَأَصَابَهُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتُ مَا كَسَبُواْۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ مِنۡ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ سَيُصِيبُهُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتُ مَا كَسَبُواْ وَمَا هُم بِمُعۡجِزِينَ

The recompense for the evils of polytheism and the sins that they committed caught up with them. Those who wronged themselves by polytheism and sins, from those present [at that time], will also be caught up by the recompense for the evils of what they committed, just like those in the past. They will not escape from Allah nor defeat Him.

(٥٢) أَوَ لَمۡ يَعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَقۡدِرُۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Do these polytheists say this and not know that Allah expands the provision for whomever He wills as a test for him to see if he is grateful or ungrateful? And he restricts it for whomever he wills as a test to see if he is patient or becomes angry with Allah's decree. In what has been mentioned about the increasing and decreasing of provisions, there are signs of Allah's planning for people who believe because they are the ones who benefit from such signs. On the other hand, the disbelievers pass by them without paying any attention to them.

(٥٣) ۞قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسۡرَفُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَا تَقۡنَطُواْ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًاۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Say, O Messenger, to My slaves who have gone beyond the limit against themselves by ascribing partners to Allah and committing sins: Do not lose hope of Allah's mercy and His forgiveness for your sins. Allah forgives all the sins of those who repent to Him. Indeed, He is All-Forgiving of the sins of those who repent and is Merciful towards them.

(٥٤) وَأَنِيبُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُمۡ وَأَسۡلِمُواْ لَهُۥ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَكُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ ثُمَّ لَا تُنصَرُونَ

Return to your Lord through repentance and righteous actions, and submit to Him before the punishment comes to you on the Day of Resurrection. Then, you will not find anyone from your idols and families who can rescue you from the punishment.

(٥٥) وَٱتَّبِعُوٓاْ أَحۡسَنَ مَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكُم مِّن رَّبِّكُم مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَكُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ بَغۡتَةٗ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَشۡعُرُونَ

Follow the Quran, which is the best of what your Lord has revealed to His Messenger. Act on its instructions and avoid its prohibitions before the punishment strikes you suddenly while you are oblivious to it. So prepare for it by always repenting.

(٥٦) أَن تَقُولَ نَفۡسٞ يَٰحَسۡرَتَىٰ عَلَىٰ مَا فَرَّطتُ فِي جَنۢبِ ٱللَّهِ وَإِن كُنتُ لَمِنَ ٱلسَّٰخِرِينَ

Act now to avoid the soul's extreme regret when it declares on the Day of Resurrection: Alas, my grief is over my negligence in my duty to Allah by the disbelief and sins committed and the mocking of the people of belief and obedience in the worldly life.

Points of Benefit:

For the disbeliever, blessings are to draw him into a false sense of security.
The extent of Allah's mercy for His creation.
Regret is only beneficial when it is in the worldly life and followed with sincere repentance.
