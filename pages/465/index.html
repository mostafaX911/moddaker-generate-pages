(٥٧) أَوۡ تَقُولَ لَوۡ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هَدَىٰنِي لَكُنتُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Or it will attempt to justify itself by using fate as an argument and say: If Allah had guided me, I would have been mindful of Him by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

(٥٨) أَوۡ تَقُولَ حِينَ تَرَى ٱلۡعَذَابَ لَوۡ أَنَّ لِي كَرَّةٗ فَأَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Or it will say in hope when it sees the punishment: If only I could return to the world and repent to Allah and be one of those who do good deeds.

(٥٩) بَلَىٰ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكَ ءَايَٰتِي فَكَذَّبۡتَ بِهَا وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرۡتَ وَكُنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

The reality is different from what you now assert by wishing for guidance. My signs came to you, and you arrogantly denied them. You were of those who rejected and disbelieved in Allah, His signs, and His Messengers.

(٦٠) وَيَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ تَرَى ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَبُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ وُجُوهُهُم مُّسۡوَدَّةٌۚ أَلَيۡسَ فِي جَهَنَّمَ مَثۡوٗى لِّلۡمُتَكَبِّرِينَ

On the Day of Resurrection, you will see those who told lies about Allah by attributing a partner and son to Him, with their faces blackened, as a sign of their wretchedness. Is there not a [suitable] place in Hell for those too proud to believe in Allah and His messengers? Indeed, there is a place for them in it.

(٦١) وَيُنَجِّي ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْ بِمَفَازَتِهِمۡ لَا يَمَسُّهُمُ ٱلسُّوٓءُ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

Allah will save those who were mindful of their Lord by complying with His instructions and avoiding His prohibitions from the punishment by entering them into their place of success, Paradise. The punishment will not touch them, nor will they grieve over the worldly pleasures they missed out on.

(٦٢) ٱللَّهُ خَٰلِقُ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖۖ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَكِيلٞ

Allah is the Creator of everything. There is no creator besides Him. He is in charge of everything. He plans His affair and disposes of it as He wills.

(٦٣) لَّهُۥ مَقَالِيدُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ

To Him alone belong the keys to the treasures of all good in the heavens and the Earth. He grants it to whomever He wills and withholds it from whomever He wills. Those who reject Allah's signs are the true losers due to being deprived of faith in this worldly life and entering the Fire to live there forever in the next life.

(٦٤) قُلۡ أَفَغَيۡرَ ٱللَّهِ تَأۡمُرُوٓنِّيٓ أَعۡبُدُ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡجَٰهِلُونَ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists who invite you to worship their idols: Do you instruct me, O you who are ignorant of your Lord, to worship other than Allah? None deserves to be worshipped except Allah alone, so I will never worship other than Him.

(٦٥) وَلَقَدۡ أُوحِيَ إِلَيۡكَ وَإِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكَ لَئِنۡ أَشۡرَكۡتَ لَيَحۡبَطَنَّ عَمَلُكَ وَلَتَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ

Allah has revealed to you, O Messenger, and to the messengers before you: If you worship others along with Allah, the reward of your good deeds will be annulled, and [as a consequence] you will be of the losers in this world due to the loss of your religion, and in the Hereafter due to the punishment.

(٦٦) بَلِ ٱللَّهَ فَٱعۡبُدۡ وَكُن مِّنَ ٱلشَّٰكِرِينَ

Instead, worship Allah alone and do not ascribe a partner to Him and be of those who are grateful to Him for the favors He has given you.

(٦٧) وَمَا قَدَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ حَقَّ قَدۡرِهِۦ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ جَمِيعٗا قَبۡضَتُهُۥ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ وَٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ مَطۡوِيَّٰتُۢ بِيَمِينِهِۦۚ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥ وَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

The polytheists did not show devotion to Allah as He deserved when they ascribed others of His weak and feeble creation as partners to Him. They were heedless of Allah's power, one of the manifestations of which is that the Earth, together with the mountains, trees, rivers, and oceans in it, will be in His grasp on the Day of Resurrection and that the seven heavens will be folded up in His right hand. He is transcendent, pure, and above what the polytheists say and believe.

Points of Benefit:

Pride is a reprehensible, sinister trait that prevents one from finding the Truth.
The blackness of their faces on the Day of Resurrection will be a sign of their misery.
Polytheism nullifies all of the righteous deeds.
Affirming the Grasp and Right Hand of Allah, the Transcendent, without likening Him or making Him resemble the creation.
