(٦٨) وَنُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَصَعِقَ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّا مَن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُۖ ثُمَّ نُفِخَ فِيهِ أُخۡرَىٰ فَإِذَا هُمۡ قِيَامٞ يَنظُرُونَ

On the Day when the angel appointed to blow the trumpet will blow it, everyone in the heavens and the Earth will die except those whom Allah wills to not die. Then the angel will blow it a second time for the resurrection, and they will all stand up alive, looking on to see what Allah will do to them.

(٦٩) وَأَشۡرَقَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ بِنُورِ رَبِّهَا وَوُضِعَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ وَجِاْيٓءَ بِٱلنَّبِيِّ‍ۧنَ وَٱلشُّهَدَآءِ وَقُضِيَ بَيۡنَهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ

The Earth will light up when the Lord of Might appears to judge between the slaves. The records of deeds of all people will be laid open, the prophets will be brought, and the followers of Muhammed صلى الله عليه وسلم will bear witness for the prophets over their people. Allah will judge between them with justice, and they will not be wronged on that day. No person's evil deeds will be increased, nor will his good deeds be decreased.

(٧٠) وَوُفِّيَتۡ كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ مَّا عَمِلَتۡ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يَفۡعَلُونَ

Allah will give each soul its recompense in full, whether its actions were good or evil. Allah knows best what they did. Nothing of their actions, good or bad, is hidden from Him, and He will give them the recompense for their efforts on this day.

(٧١) وَسِيقَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ جَهَنَّمَ زُمَرًاۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءُوهَا فُتِحَتۡ أَبۡوَٰبُهَا وَقَالَ لَهُمۡ خَزَنَتُهَآ أَلَمۡ يَأۡتِكُمۡ رُسُلٞ مِّنكُمۡ يَتۡلُونَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِ رَبِّكُمۡ وَيُنذِرُونَكُمۡ لِقَآءَ يَوۡمِكُمۡ هَٰذَاۚ قَالُواْ بَلَىٰ وَلَٰكِنۡ حَقَّتۡ كَلِمَةُ ٱلۡعَذَابِ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

The angels will drive those who rejected Allah to the Fire in large crowds. When they come to Hell, the angels appointed as its keepers will open its gates and receive them by saying to them rebukingly: Did not messengers from your kind come to you reciting to you the signs of your Lord that were revealed to them, warning you of meeting the Day of Resurrection because of the severe punishment therein? The disbelievers will acknowledge that by saying: Indeed, that took place. However, the word of punishment is binding on the disbelievers, and we were disbelievers.

(٧٢) قِيلَ ٱدۡخُلُوٓاْ أَبۡوَٰبَ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۖ فَبِئۡسَ مَثۡوَى ٱلۡمُتَكَبِّرِينَ

It will be said to them to humiliate them further so they may lose all hope in Allah's mercy and of ever coming out of the Fire: Enter the gates of the Fire to remain therein forever. How terrible the place of those who were proud and arrogant against the truth is.

(٧٣) وَسِيقَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْ رَبَّهُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡجَنَّةِ زُمَرًاۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءُوهَا وَفُتِحَتۡ أَبۡوَٰبُهَا وَقَالَ لَهُمۡ خَزَنَتُهَا سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡ طِبۡتُمۡ فَٱدۡخُلُوهَا خَٰلِدِينَ

The angels will gently lead the believers who feared their Lord by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions to Paradise in large crowds honored [by Allah]. When they come to Paradise, its gates will open for them, and the angels appointed to it will say to them: Peace be on you from every difficulty and from everything you dislike. Your hearts and actions were pure, so enter Paradise to remain there forever.

(٧٤) وَقَالُواْ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي صَدَقَنَا وَعۡدَهُۥ وَأَوۡرَثَنَا ٱلۡأَرۡضَ نَتَبَوَّأُ مِنَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ حَيۡثُ نَشَآءُۖ فَنِعۡمَ أَجۡرُ ٱلۡعَٰمِلِينَ

The believers will say when they enter Paradise: Praise be to Allah, who fulfilled His promise to us on the tongues of His messengers. He had promised to gather us in Paradise. He has given us the land of Paradise, where we can live wherever we want. How excellent the reward of those who worked by doing righteous actions seeking the pleasure of their Lord is.

Points of Benefit:

Affirming the two blows of the horn.
Clarifying the humiliation that the disbelievers will receive and the honor with which the believers will be received.
Affirming the eternal life of the disbelievers in the Inferno and the believers in Paradise.
Good deeds lead to good recompense.
