(٧٥) وَتَرَى ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةَ حَآفِّينَ مِنۡ حَوۡلِ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ يُسَبِّحُونَ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡۚ وَقُضِيَ بَيۡنَهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ وَقِيلَ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

The angels on that witnessed day will surround the throne, declaring Allah to be free from what the disbelievers say that is not befitting. Allah will judge between all creation with justice, honoring those whom He honors and punishing those He punishes. It will be said: Praise be to Allah, the Lord of all created things, for the judgment that He passed of mercy for His believing slaves and punishment for His disbelieving slaves.

The Forgiver (Surah Ghafir)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Detailing the situation of those who dispute Allah's verses and responding to them.

Explanation:

(١) حمٓ

Hā Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ

The revelation of the Quran is from Allah—the Mighty whom none can overpower, the All-Knowing of His slaves' interests—to His Messenger, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم.

(٣) غَافِرِ ٱلذَّنۢبِ وَقَابِلِ ٱلتَّوۡبِ شَدِيدِ ٱلۡعِقَابِ ذِي ٱلطَّوۡلِۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ إِلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

[It is from] The Forgiver of sins of the sinners, Accepter of the repentance of His slaves who turn to Him, Severe in retribution against those who do not repent, Possessor of bounty and grace. There is no being who deserves worship besides Him. To Him alone is the return of the slaves on the Day of Resurrection, and He will recompense them according to what they deserve.

(٤) مَا يُجَٰدِلُ فِيٓ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فَلَا يَغۡرُرۡكَ تَقَلُّبُهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ

No one disputes Allah's signs that indicate His Oneness and the truthfulness of His messengers except those who reject Allah due to their deficient intellects. Do not grieve over them or let the abundant provision and favors they enjoy deceive you. The respite given to them is part of the plan to gradually lure them into a false sense of security.

(٥) كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٖ وَٱلۡأَحۡزَابُ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡۖ وَهَمَّتۡ كُلُّ أُمَّةِۢ بِرَسُولِهِمۡ لِيَأۡخُذُوهُۖ وَجَٰدَلُواْ بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ لِيُدۡحِضُواْ بِهِ ٱلۡحَقَّ فَأَخَذۡتُهُمۡۖ فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عِقَابِ

Before them, the people of Noah rejected [the message], as did the confederates after Noah, including 'Ād, Thamūd, the people of Lot, and the people of Midian. Pharaoh also rejected it. All of them refused to accept [the message]. Every nation plotted against their Messenger to capture and kill him. They argued using falsehood in order to eliminate the truth. But I seized all those nations. So, reflect on how my retribution against them was. It was a severe retribution.

(٦) وَكَذَٰلِكَ حَقَّتۡ كَلِمَتُ رَبِّكَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَنَّهُمۡ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِ

Like how Allah decided to destroy those disbelieving nations, the Word of your Lord, O Messenger, is binding against the disbelievers, that they will be the inmates of the Fire.

(٧) ٱلَّذِينَ يَحۡمِلُونَ ٱلۡعَرۡشَ وَمَنۡ حَوۡلَهُۥ يُسَبِّحُونَ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَيُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهِۦ وَيَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْۖ رَبَّنَا وَسِعۡتَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖ رَّحۡمَةٗ وَعِلۡمٗا فَٱغۡفِرۡ لِلَّذِينَ تَابُواْ وَٱتَّبَعُواْ سَبِيلَكَ وَقِهِمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

The angels who carry the throne of your Lord, O Messenger, and those around it, declare their Lord to be free from all that does not befit Him. They believe in Him and seek forgiveness for those who have faith in Allah, saying in their supplication: O Our Lord, Your knowledge and mercy encompass everything, so forgive those who repent from their sins and follow Your religion, and protect them from being touched by the Fire.

Points of Benefit:

Combining raising awareness of Allah's mercy and building fear from the severity of His punishment is a good approach.
Praising Allah by his singling Him out and by glorifying His praises is from the etiquette of making supplication.
The honor the believer has with Allah is such that He subjugates the angels to seek forgiveness for him.
