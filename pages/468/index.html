(٨) رَبَّنَا وَأَدۡخِلۡهُمۡ جَنَّٰتِ عَدۡنٍ ٱلَّتِي وَعَدتَّهُمۡ وَمَن صَلَحَ مِنۡ ءَابَآئِهِمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجِهِمۡ وَذُرِّيَّٰتِهِمۡۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

The angels will say: O Our Lord, enter the believers into gardens of eternity which You promised, and admit those whose actions are good amongst their fathers, wives, and children. You are the Mighty with nobody to overpower You, Wise in your decreeing and planning.

(٩) وَقِهِمُ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِۚ وَمَن تَقِ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِ يَوۡمَئِذٖ فَقَدۡ رَحِمۡتَهُۥۚ وَذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

And save them from their bad actions, so do not punish them as a result. Whomever You protect from punishment for their evil actions on the Day of Resurrection, then You have been merciful to them. This protection from the punishment and mercy through entry into Paradise is the great success which no success can compare with.

(١٠) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يُنَادَوۡنَ لَمَقۡتُ ٱللَّهِ أَكۡبَرُ مِن مَّقۡتِكُمۡ أَنفُسَكُمۡ إِذۡ تُدۡعَوۡنَ إِلَى ٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ فَتَكۡفُرُونَ

Those who disbelieved in Allah and His messengers will be addressed on the Day of Resurrection when they enter the Fire hating and cursing themselves: Allah's contempt for you is greater than your contempt for yourselves while you were being called in the worldly life to believe in Allah, but you disbelieved in Him and set up gods beside Him [in worship].

(١١) قَالُواْ رَبَّنَآ أَمَتَّنَا ٱثۡنَتَيۡنِ وَأَحۡيَيۡتَنَا ٱثۡنَتَيۡنِ فَٱعۡتَرَفۡنَا بِذُنُوبِنَا فَهَلۡ إِلَىٰ خُرُوجٖ مِّن سَبِيلٖ

The disbelievers will say confessing their sins when their confession will not benefit them: Our Lord, You decreed death for us twice, once before we existed, then You created us. Another time, You decreed death for us after having brought us into existence. Also, You gave us life twice by creating us from nothing and by restoring us for the resurrection. We have acknowledged our sins, so is there any route we can take to exit from the Fire so we can return to life to correct our actions so You become pleased with us?

(١٢) ذَٰلِكُم بِأَنَّهُۥٓ إِذَا دُعِيَ ٱللَّهُ وَحۡدَهُۥ كَفَرۡتُمۡ وَإِن يُشۡرَكۡ بِهِۦ تُؤۡمِنُواْۚ فَٱلۡحُكۡمُ لِلَّهِ ٱلۡعَلِيِّ ٱلۡكَبِيرِ

The punishment you are suffering now is because when Allah alone was called upon, and no partner was associated with Him, you disbelieved in Him and ascribed partners to Him. And when partners were worshipped alongside Allah, you believed. So the judgment is with Allah alone, Lofty with His essence, decree, and dominance, the Great One Who is above all else.

(١٣) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُرِيكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَيُنَزِّلُ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ رِزۡقٗاۚ وَمَا يَتَذَكَّرُ إِلَّا مَن يُنِيبُ

Allah is the One who shows you signs in the horizons (in every direction) and in yourselves to prove His power and oneness to you. He sends down rainwater from the skies for you, so it becomes a cause for the provisions you receive, such as plants, crops, and so forth. And nobody takes lessons from the signs of Allah except one who turns to Him, sincerely repenting.

(١٤) فَٱدۡعُواْ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ وَلَوۡ كَرِهَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ

So call upon Allah sincerely, O believers, in worship and supplication, without associating partners with Him, even if the disbelievers dislike that, and it enrages them.

(١٥) رَفِيعُ ٱلدَّرَجَٰتِ ذُو ٱلۡعَرۡشِ يُلۡقِي ٱلرُّوحَ مِنۡ أَمۡرِهِۦ عَلَىٰ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ لِيُنذِرَ يَوۡمَ ٱلتَّلَاقِ

For He is worthy of sincere supplication and obedience, as He is elevated in ranks, separate from all of His creation. He is the Lord of the Great Throne. He sends revelation upon whomever He wants from His slaves so that they and others become alive. He warns people of the Day of Resurrection, in which the people from the start of time until the end will all meet one another.

(١٦) يَوۡمَ هُم بَٰرِزُونَۖ لَا يَخۡفَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ مِنۡهُمۡ شَيۡءٞۚ لِّمَنِ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۖ لِلَّهِ ٱلۡوَٰحِدِ ٱلۡقَهَّارِ

On the Day when every person will be gathered on a single plane. Nothing of theirs will be hidden from Allah, neither their selves, actions, nor reward. All will be clear to see. He will ask: To whom belongs the kingdom today? There will only be one answer now: The kingdom is for Allah, the One in His essence, attributes, and actions, the Prevailing one who has dominated everything and for Whom everything humbles itself.

Points of Benefit:

The place where repentance is accepted is the worldly life.
Admonition only benefits those who return to their Lord.
The uprightness of the believer is not affected by the attitudes of the disbelievers who reject the religion.
The submission of the tyrannical and oppressive kings to Allah on the Day of Resurrection.
