(١٧) ٱلۡيَوۡمَ تُجۡزَىٰ كُلُّ نَفۡسِۢ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡۚ لَا ظُلۡمَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

Today, every soul will get what it deserves for its actions: if it is good, then the reward will be good, and if it is evil, then likewise, the result will be evil. There will be no injustice today because the judge is Allah, who is Just. Allah is swift in taking account of His slaves due to His All-encompassing knowledge of them.

(١٨) وَأَنذِرۡهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡأٓزِفَةِ إِذِ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ لَدَى ٱلۡحَنَاجِرِ كَٰظِمِينَۚ مَا لِلظَّٰلِمِينَ مِنۡ حَمِيمٖ وَلَا شَفِيعٖ يُطَاعُ

O Messenger, warn them of the Day of Resurrection. This day of standing has drawn near and is soon to come, and anything coming soon is close. When the hearts rise, due to its intense horror, until they reach the throats, they will all be silent, with none of them able to speak except whom [Allah] the All-Merciful allows to speak. Those who wronged themselves through polytheism and sins will not have any friend, relative, or intercessor who will be listened to, even if they could intercede for them.

(١٩) يَعۡلَمُ خَآئِنَةَ ٱلۡأَعۡيُنِ وَمَا تُخۡفِي ٱلصُّدُورُ

He knows what the eyes of the lookers secretly glance at and what the hearts conceal. None of that is hidden from Him.

(٢٠) وَٱللَّهُ يَقۡضِي بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ لَا يَقۡضُونَ بِشَيۡءٍۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡبَصِيرُ

Allah judges with complete justice, so He does not wrong anyone by decreasing His good actions or increasing His evil actions. And those whom the polytheists worship besides Allah cannot judge with anything because they do not possess anything. Allah Hears the words of His slaves and sees their intentions and actions, and He will recompense them for that.

(٢١) ۞أَوَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَيَنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَانُواْ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ كَانُواْ هُمۡ أَشَدَّ مِنۡهُمۡ قُوَّةٗ وَءَاثَارٗا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَأَخَذَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِذُنُوبِهِمۡ وَمَا كَانَ لَهُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ مِن وَاقٖ

Have these polytheists not traveled throughout the Earth so that they can reflect on the outcome of the disbelieving nations before them? Their end was evil. Those [previous] nations were stronger than they are, and they left an impression on Earth with buildings that these people have not done. However, Allah destroyed them because of their sins, and they had nobody to protect them from the punishment of Allah.

(٢٢) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ كَانَت تَّأۡتِيهِمۡ رُسُلُهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ فَكَفَرُواْ فَأَخَذَهُمُ ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ قَوِيّٞ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ

That punishment that befell them only befell them because they disbelieved in Allah and denied His messengers when they used to come to them with clear proofs and spectacular miracles. However, Allah seized them, despite their strength, and destroyed them. He is Powerful and severe in punishing those who disbelieve in Him and reject His messengers.

With the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم facing rejection from his people, Allah mentions the story of Moses and Pharaoh to give him the good news that, ultimately, he will be victorious. He states:

(٢٣) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مُوسَىٰ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٍ

Indeed, We sent Moses with Our clear signs and decisive proof.

(٢٤) إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَهَٰمَٰنَ وَقَٰرُونَ فَقَالُواْ سَٰحِرٞ كَذَّابٞ

[He was sent] To Pharaoh, his minister Haman, and Qarun. They said: Moses is a magician and a liar in his claim to be a messenger.

(٢٥) فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّ مِنۡ عِندِنَا قَالُواْ ٱقۡتُلُوٓاْ أَبۡنَآءَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَعَهُۥ وَٱسۡتَحۡيُواْ نِسَآءَهُمۡۚ وَمَا كَيۡدُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ إِلَّا فِي ضَلَٰلٖ

Then when Moses came to them with the proof to show his truthfulness, Pharaoh said: Kill the sons of those who have believed in Moses and keep their women alive, to disgrace them. And the plot of the disbelievers to attempt to reduce the number of believers will only perish and [ultimately] fail.

Points of Benefit:

Reminders of the Day of Resurrection are one of the greatest deterrents from disobedience.
Allah's knowledge encompasses the actions of His slaves, whether done secretly or openly.
The command to travel the Earth to learn from the situations of the polytheists who were destroyed.
