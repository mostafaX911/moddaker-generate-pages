After encouraging spending for His sake due to the cooperation and solidarity that it builds between Muslims, Allah warns about usury, which nullifies this, stating:

(٢٧٥) ٱلَّذِينَ يَأۡكُلُونَ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْ لَا يَقُومُونَ إِلَّا كَمَا يَقُومُ ٱلَّذِي يَتَخَبَّطُهُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ مِنَ ٱلۡمَسِّۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡبَيۡعُ مِثۡلُ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْۗ وَأَحَلَّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡبَيۡعَ وَحَرَّمَ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْۚ فَمَن جَآءَهُۥ مَوۡعِظَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ فَٱنتَهَىٰ فَلَهُۥ مَا سَلَفَ وَأَمۡرُهُۥٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَمَنۡ عَادَ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

Those who indulge in usury and receive it will rise from their graves on the Day of Resurrection like a person who is afflicted by the Devil: they will stand up from their graves and then abruptly fall like a person who just suffered a seizure. This is because they made usury permissible and made no distinction between usury and that which Allah has permitted in terms of profit earned through trade and sales transactions. They claimed that trade is like usury, meaning both are lawful since both lead to increased wealth. Allah refutes their analogy and explains that He has permitted trade because of the general and specific benefit it contains, and He has prohibited usury because it is oppressive and leads to unjustly eating people's wealth without an exchange [of goods or services]. The person who repents from this sin and stops indulging in usury upon receiving Allah's prohibition and warning against it may keep the money that he received in the past from it, without any sin. Then, whatever he does from then on, Allah will judge it. Whoever returns to dealing with usury after knowing of the prohibition and evidence established against doing so deserves to enter the Fire and remain there eternally. The reference to remaining in the Fire eternally could either be for a person who justifies the indulgence in usury or referring to a long period of time for those who indulge in it while still believing in the Oneness of Allah. Eternal Hell is solely for disbelievers; as for true monotheists (Muslims), they will not remain in the Fire eternally.

After mentioning spending for His sake and engaging in usury, Allah explains the difference between the two in reward, stating:

(٢٧٦) يَمۡحَقُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْ وَيُرۡبِي ٱلصَّدَقَٰتِۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ كَفَّارٍ أَثِيمٍ

Allah destroys usury-based wealth by either physically destroying it or removing its blessing. Yet He increases charity and makes it grow by multiplying its reward by ten times up to seven hundred times or even more and blesses the wealth of those who give charity. Allah does not love a stubborn disbeliever who considers the prohibited as lawful and persists in sins.

(٢٧٧) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتَوُاْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرُهُمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ وَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

Those who believe in Allah, follow His Messenger, do righteous deeds, completely fulfill their prayers, and give Zakat to those deserving will receive their reward from their Lord. They will not fear what awaits them or grieve over what missed them of the world and its pleasures.

(٢٧٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَذَرُواْ مَا بَقِيَ مِنَ ٱلرِّبَوٰٓاْ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, fear Allah by fulfilling His instructions and avoiding His prohibitions. Do not demand any usury owed to you that remains with people if you genuinely believe in Allah and in what He has prohibited you, such as usury.

(٢٧٩) فَإِن لَّمۡ تَفۡعَلُواْ فَأۡذَنُواْ بِحَرۡبٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦۖ وَإِن تُبۡتُمۡ فَلَكُمۡ رُءُوسُ أَمۡوَٰلِكُمۡ لَا تَظۡلِمُونَ وَلَا تُظۡلَمُونَ

If you do not obey the sacred law, be aware and confident of waging a war against Allah and His Messenger. If you repent to Allah and abandon usury, you may keep the initial amount you had given as a loan from your capital in a manner that does not wrong anyone by taking more than your initial capital or receiving less than it.

(٢٨٠) وَإِن كَانَ ذُو عُسۡرَةٖ فَنَظِرَةٌ إِلَىٰ مَيۡسَرَةٖۚ وَأَن تَصَدَّقُواْ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

If the person from whom you demand your debt is going through difficult times and cannot repay the debt, then delay asking until he can obtain money and pay the debt. And to give charity to him by not demanding the debt or giving up a part of it is better for you if you know that virtue in the sight of Allah.

(٢٨١) وَٱتَّقُواْ يَوۡمٗا تُرۡجَعُونَ فِيهِ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ ثُمَّ تُوَفَّىٰ كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ مَّا كَسَبَتۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ

Fear the punishment of a day when you all will be returned to Allah, and you will have to stand before Him. Then, every soul will be recompensed for its good or evil actions. They will not be wronged by receiving less reward than they deserve for their good deeds or more punishment for their evil deeds.

Points of Benefit:

One of the greatest major sins is engaging in usury, and for this reason, Allah Almighty has warned those who consume it with war and promises them destruction in this world and confusion in the Hereafter.
Adhering to the legal rulings in financial transactions brings blessings and growth to them.
The virtue of patience with the debtor and easing their burden by giving charity to them, whether it covers part or the entirety of the debt.
