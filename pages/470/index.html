(٢٦) وَقَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ ذَرُونِيٓ أَقۡتُلۡ مُوسَىٰ وَلۡيَدۡعُ رَبَّهُۥٓۖ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ أَن يُبَدِّلَ دِينَكُمۡ أَوۡ أَن يُظۡهِرَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلۡفَسَادَ

Pharaoh said: Leave me to kill Moses, as a punishment for him, and let him call upon his Lord to save him from me. I do not care if he calls upon His Lord. I fear he will change your religion or that corruption will appear on Earth through killing and destruction.

(٢٧) وَقَالَ مُوسَىٰٓ إِنِّي عُذۡتُ بِرَبِّي وَرَبِّكُم مِّن كُلِّ مُتَكَبِّرٖ لَّا يُؤۡمِنُ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

Moses (peace be upon him) said: I have sought the protection of my Lord and your Lord from every person who is too arrogant to accept and believe in the truth, thus not believing in the resurrection, and being taken into account and punished on it.

(٢٨) وَقَالَ رَجُلٞ مُّؤۡمِنٞ مِّنۡ ءَالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ يَكۡتُمُ إِيمَٰنَهُۥٓ أَتَقۡتُلُونَ رَجُلًا أَن يَقُولَ رَبِّيَ ٱللَّهُ وَقَدۡ جَآءَكُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ مِن رَّبِّكُمۡۖ وَإِن يَكُ كَٰذِبٗا فَعَلَيۡهِ كَذِبُهُۥۖ وَإِن يَكُ صَادِقٗا يُصِبۡكُم بَعۡضُ ٱلَّذِي يَعِدُكُمۡۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي مَنۡ هُوَ مُسۡرِفٞ كَذَّابٞ

A believing man from the family of Pharoah, who was concealing his belief from his people, said, criticizing them for deciding to kill Moses: Are you going to kill a man who has not committed any crime other than him saying: 'My Lord is Allah', despite the fact he brought you miracles and proof to show the truthfulness of his claim to be a messenger from his Lord? If he is a liar, his lying will expose him, but if he is truthful, some of the punishment he promises will soon befall you. Allah does not guide to the truth one who overreaches His limits and lies against Allah and His messengers.

(٢٩) يَٰقَوۡمِ لَكُمُ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ ظَٰهِرِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَمَن يَنصُرُنَا مِنۢ بَأۡسِ ٱللَّهِ إِن جَآءَنَاۚ قَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ مَآ أُرِيكُمۡ إِلَّا مَآ أَرَىٰ وَمَآ أَهۡدِيكُمۡ إِلَّا سَبِيلَ ٱلرَّشَادِ

O people, today you have the kingdom and dominate the land of Egypt. So, who will save us from Allah's punishment if it comes to us due to killing Moses? Pharaoh said: The opinion is my opinion, and the judgment is my judgment. I have decided to kill Moses to remove evil and corruption. I only guide you to what is upright.

(٣٠) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِيٓ ءَامَنَ يَٰقَوۡمِ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ عَلَيۡكُم مِّثۡلَ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابِ

And the one who believed said, advising his people: I fear for you, if you kill Moses out of injustice and transgression, a punishment [will befall you] like the punishment of the groups that united against their prophets previously, then Allah destroyed them.

(٣١) مِثۡلَ دَأۡبِ قَوۡمِ نُوحٖ وَعَادٖ وَثَمُودَ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡۚ وَمَا ٱللَّهُ يُرِيدُ ظُلۡمٗا لِّلۡعِبَادِ

Such is the habit of those who disbelieved and denied the messengers. For instance, the people of Noah, 'Ād, Thamūd, and those who came after them, Allah, destroyed them due to their disbelief and denial of their messengers. And Allah does not want to oppress the slaves. He only punishes them due to their sins.

(٣٢) وَيَٰقَوۡمِ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلتَّنَادِ

O my people, I fear for you the Day of Judgment. People will call one another that day due to their blood relations, status, or thinking they can benefit them during that terrifying time.

(٣٣) يَوۡمَ تُوَلُّونَ مُدۡبِرِينَ مَا لَكُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ مِنۡ عَاصِمٖۗ وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِنۡ هَادٖ

On the Day you will turn away, running out of fear from the Fire, you will not have any protector to protect you from Allah's punishment. whoever Allah abandons and does not place faith in his heart, no one can guide him because a person being able to accept guidance is only in the Hand of Allah.

Points of Benefit:

The believer seeks refuge with his Lord from the plots of his enemies.
The permissibility of concealing one's belief for an overwhelming benefit or to ward off harm.
Offering advice to the people is a characteristic of the believers.
