(٣٤) وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَكُمۡ يُوسُفُ مِن قَبۡلُ بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ فَمَا زِلۡتُمۡ فِي شَكّٖ مِّمَّا جَآءَكُم بِهِۦۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا هَلَكَ قُلۡتُمۡ لَن يَبۡعَثَ ٱللَّهُ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ رَسُولٗاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُضِلُّ ٱللَّهُ مَنۡ هُوَ مُسۡرِفٞ مُّرۡتَابٌ

Joseph came to you before Moses with clear proof of Allah's Oneness. However, you doubted him and remained in denial of what he brought until he died. Then, you only increased in doubt and uncertainty. You said: Allah will never send a messenger after him. Just as you are misguided from the truth, Allah leads astray every person who transgresses the limits of Allah and doubts His oneness.

(٣٥) ٱلَّذِينَ يُجَٰدِلُونَ فِيٓ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ بِغَيۡرِ سُلۡطَٰنٍ أَتَىٰهُمۡۖ كَبُرَ مَقۡتًا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَعِندَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَطۡبَعُ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ قَلۡبِ مُتَكَبِّرٖ جَبَّارٖ

Those who dispute the words of Allah to refute them without any proof or evidence that has come to them, their disputing is ever more despised in the eyes of Allah and those who believe in Allah and His messengers. Just as Allah sealed the hearts of those who argue about Our verses to refute them, Allah seals the hearts of every person who is tyrannical and arrogant to the truth. Thus, he does not find the path to what is correct, nor is he guided to good.

(٣٦) وَقَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ يَٰهَٰمَٰنُ ٱبۡنِ لِي صَرۡحٗا لَّعَلِّيٓ أَبۡلُغُ ٱلۡأَسۡبَٰبَ

Pharaoh said to his minister Haman: O Haman, build a high building for me so that I can attain the means of access.

(٣٧) أَسۡبَٰبَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ فَأَطَّلِعَ إِلَىٰٓ إِلَٰهِ مُوسَىٰ وَإِنِّي لَأَظُنُّهُۥ كَٰذِبٗاۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ زُيِّنَ لِفِرۡعَوۡنَ سُوٓءُ عَمَلِهِۦ وَصُدَّ عَنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِۚ وَمَا كَيۡدُ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِلَّا فِي تَبَابٖ

In the hope that I can access the paths that lead to the skies and see the God of Moses that he claims is the true God. In this way, Pharoah's evil deeds were adorned for him when he requested that from Haman and was turned from the true path to the path of misguidance. The plot of Pharaoh to make the falsehood he believed dominant and to refute the truth Moses brought was a total failure because it was ill-advised, a futile attempt, and led to perpetual damnation.

(٣٨) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِيٓ ءَامَنَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱتَّبِعُونِ أَهۡدِكُمۡ سَبِيلَ ٱلرَّشَادِ

The man from the family of Pharoah, who believed in Allah, said, advising his people and guiding them to the proper path: O people, follow me; I shall show you and guide you to the correct way and guide you to the truth.

(٣٩) يَٰقَوۡمِ إِنَّمَا هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا مَتَٰعٞ وَإِنَّ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ هِيَ دَارُ ٱلۡقَرَارِ

O people, this worldly life is only an enjoyment of fleeting pleasures, so let it not deceive you with its short-lived bliss. [However] With its everlasting pleasure, the abode of the Hereafter is the abode of permanent dwelling, so work for it by obeying Allah, and be wary of becoming preoccupied with your worldly life away from working towards the Hereafter.

(٤٠) مَنۡ عَمِلَ سَيِّئَةٗ فَلَا يُجۡزَىٰٓ إِلَّا مِثۡلَهَاۖ وَمَنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا مِّن ذَكَرٍ أَوۡ أُنثَىٰ وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنٞ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يَدۡخُلُونَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ يُرۡزَقُونَ فِيهَا بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ

Whoever commits an evil action will be recompensed with punishment befitting of what he did, without punishment being added to it. Likewise, whoever does a good action seeking Allah's pleasure through it, whether a male or female, as long as he [or she] is a believer in Allah and His messengers, will enter Paradise on the Day of Resurrection. Allah will grant them therein provisions of fruits and everlasting pleasure without limits.

Points of Benefit:

Arguing to invalidate the Truth and establish falsehood is a reprehensible trait and one of the characteristics of the people of misguidance.
Arrogance prevents one from being guided to the Truth.
The failure of the disbeliever's tricks and their plots to nullify the Truth.
The obligation of preparing for the Hereafter and not to be preoccupied with this world.
