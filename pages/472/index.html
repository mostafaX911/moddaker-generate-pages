(٤١) ۞وَيَٰقَوۡمِ مَا لِيٓ أَدۡعُوكُمۡ إِلَى ٱلنَّجَوٰةِ وَتَدۡعُونَنِيٓ إِلَى ٱلنَّارِ

O my people, why do you think I am calling you towards salvation so you do not lose out in this worldly life and the Hereafter by believing in Allah and doing good actions? Yet, you call me to enter the Fire by asking me to disbelieve in Allah or disobey Him?

(٤٢) تَدۡعُونَنِي لِأَكۡفُرَ بِٱللَّهِ وَأُشۡرِكَ بِهِۦ مَا لَيۡسَ لِي بِهِۦ عِلۡمٞ وَأَنَا۠ أَدۡعُوكُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡغَفَّٰرِ

You are calling me to your falsehood in the hope that I disbelieve in Allah and worship others alongside Him. At the same time, I do not know the validity of doing so. I am calling you to believe in Allah, the Mighty whom nobody can overpower, the Most-Forgiving who has vast forgiveness for His slaves.

(٤٣) لَا جَرَمَ أَنَّمَا تَدۡعُونَنِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ لَيۡسَ لَهُۥ دَعۡوَةٞ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَلَا فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَأَنَّ مَرَدَّنَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَأَنَّ ٱلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ هُمۡ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِ

Truly, what you call me to believe in and obey has no right to be called to in this world or the Hereafter, nor does it respond to whoever calls upon it. The return of us all is to Allah alone, and those who transgress in disbelief and sins are the people of the Fire who will enter it forever on the Day of Resurrection.

(٤٤) فَسَتَذۡكُرُونَ مَآ أَقُولُ لَكُمۡۚ وَأُفَوِّضُ أَمۡرِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَصِيرُۢ بِٱلۡعِبَادِ

But they rejected his advice, so he said: You will remember the advice I gave you, and you will regret not accepting it. I entrust all my affairs to Allah alone. Nothing of Allah's slaves' actions is hidden from Him.

(٤٥) فَوَقَىٰهُ ٱللَّهُ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِ مَا مَكَرُواْۖ وَحَاقَ بِ‍َٔالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ سُوٓءُ ٱلۡعَذَابِ

So Allah protected him from their evil plot when they intended to kill him, and the punishment of drowning surrounded the followers of Pharaoh, as Allah destroyed him and all his armies in this world.

(٤٦) ٱلنَّارُ يُعۡرَضُونَ عَلَيۡهَا غُدُوّٗا وَعَشِيّٗاۚ وَيَوۡمَ تَقُومُ ٱلسَّاعَةُ أَدۡخِلُوٓاْ ءَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ أَشَدَّ ٱلۡعَذَابِ

And after their death, they will be subjected to the Fire at the beginning and end of the day. Then, on the Day of Resurrection, it will be said: Cast the followers of Pharaoh into the most severe and greatest punishment due to their disbelief, denial, and preventing others from Allah's path.

(٤٧) وَإِذۡ يَتَحَآجُّونَ فِي ٱلنَّارِ فَيَقُولُ ٱلضُّعَفَٰٓؤُاْ لِلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُوٓاْ إِنَّا كُنَّا لَكُمۡ تَبَعٗا فَهَلۡ أَنتُم مُّغۡنُونَ عَنَّا نَصِيبٗا مِّنَ ٱلنَّارِ

O Messenger, mention when the people of the Fire who were followers and leaders will argue so that the oppressed followers will say to the arrogant leaders: We were your followers in misguidance in the worldly life, so are you going to relieve us of some of Allah's punishment by bearing it on our behalf?

(٤٨) قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُوٓاْ إِنَّا كُلّٞ فِيهَآ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَدۡ حَكَمَ بَيۡنَ ٱلۡعِبَادِ

The arrogant leaders will say: We are in the Fire, regardless of whether we were followers or leaders. None of us will bear a part of the punishment of the Hereafter. Allah has decided between the slaves, giving everyone the punishment they deserve.

(٤٩) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ فِي ٱلنَّارِ لِخَزَنَةِ جَهَنَّمَ ٱدۡعُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ يُخَفِّفۡ عَنَّا يَوۡمٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ

Those followers and leaders being punished in the Fire will say to the Fire's gatekeepers, once they have lost hope in leaving the Fire and returning to the worldly life to repent: Call upon your Lord to relieve us from one day of this everlasting punishment.

Points of Benefit:

The importance of relying on Allah.
The caller to the Truth is saved from the plots of his enemies.
Affirming the torment of the barzakh (the transitionary life in the grave).
The disbelievers cling to anything that might relieve them from the Fire, even for a little while, but that will never happen.
