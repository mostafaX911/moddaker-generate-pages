(٥٠) قَالُوٓاْ أَوَ لَمۡ تَكُ تَأۡتِيكُمۡ رُسُلُكُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِۖ قَالُواْ بَلَىٰۚ قَالُواْ فَٱدۡعُواْۗ وَمَا دُعَٰٓؤُاْ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ إِلَّا فِي ضَلَٰلٍ

The gatekeepers of Hell will say in response to the disbelievers: Did your messengers not come to you with clear proof and evidence? The disbelievers will say: Indeed, they used to come to us with clear proof and evidence. The keepers will taunt them by saying: Go ahead and implore us, for we will not intercede for the disbelievers. The supplication of the disbelievers is futile and wasted, as it will not be accepted due to their disbelief.

After mentioning the story of Pharaoh and his people and their fate in this life and the Hereafter, Allah mentions the messengers and the faithful believers and the success they will attain in both worlds, stating:

(٥١) إِنَّا لَنَنصُرُ رُسُلَنَا وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَيَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلۡأَشۡهَٰدُ

We will make Our Messengers and those who believe in what they brought victorious by manifesting their proof and supporting them against their enemies. And We shall make them victorious on the Day of Resurrection by entering them into Paradise and punishing their adversaries by casting them into the Fire. This is after the people of truth from the prophets, angels, and believers will all bear witness to the message that has been conveyed and to the denial of the disbelieving nations.

(٥٢) يَوۡمَ لَا يَنفَعُ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ مَعۡذِرَتُهُمۡۖ وَلَهُمُ ٱللَّعۡنَةُ وَلَهُمۡ سُوٓءُ ٱلدَّارِ

On the Day when making excuses will not benefit those who wronged themselves by disbelief and sin, they will be distanced from Allah's mercy and get an evil abode and painful punishment.

(٥٣) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡهُدَىٰ وَأَوۡرَثۡنَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ

And We granted Moses knowledge to guide the Israelites to the truth. We made the Torah an inherited book among them, each generation passing it on to the next.

(٥٤) هُدٗى وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

As a guide to the true path and a reminder to those of sound intellect.

(٥٥) فَٱصۡبِرۡ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقّٞ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لِذَنۢبِكَ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ بِٱلۡعَشِيِّ وَٱلۡإِبۡكَٰرِ

So be patient, O Messenger, with the rejection and harm you face from your people. Allah's promise of help and support to you is undoubtedly true. And seek forgiveness for your sin and exalt the praise of your Lord at the beginning and end of the day.

(٥٦) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُجَٰدِلُونَ فِيٓ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ بِغَيۡرِ سُلۡطَٰنٍ أَتَىٰهُمۡ إِن فِي صُدُورِهِمۡ إِلَّا كِبۡرٞ مَّا هُم بِبَٰلِغِيهِۚ فَٱسۡتَعِذۡ بِٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡبَصِيرُ

Those who dispute Allah's verses to invalidate them without any argument or proof from Him are motivated to do so only by the desire to show arrogance and superiority in the face of truth. They will not achieve the dominance they desire over it. So hold fast, O Messenger, to Allah, for He is All-Hearing of His slaves' words. He is All-Seeing of their deeds; nothing escapes Him, and He will recompense them.

(٥٧) لَخَلۡقُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَكۡبَرُ مِنۡ خَلۡقِ ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

The creation of the heavens and the Earth and their greatness and vastness is greater than the creation of humanity. So, the One who made them, despite their greatness, has the power to bring the dead back to life from their graves, to hold them to account and recompense them. But most people do not know, so they do not consider it proof of the resurrection despite it being so clear.

(٥٨) وَمَا يَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ وَٱلۡبَصِيرُ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَلَا ٱلۡمُسِيٓءُۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَتَذَكَّرُونَ

The blind are not equal to those who can see. In the same way, those who believe in Allah, affirm His messengers, and do good are not the same as those who do evil with corrupt beliefs and sins. Only a few among you reflect. Had you reflected, you would be aware of the difference between the two groups and strived to be amongst those who believed and did good deeds, seeking the pleasure of Allah.

Points of Benefit:

Allah supporting His Messengers and the believers is an established divine practice.
The apologies of the oppressor will be of no benefit on the Day of Resurrection.
The importance of patience when facing falsehood.
The creation of the heavens and the Earth is evidence for the resurrection because the One who created something magnificent is able to bring life back to something less than it.
