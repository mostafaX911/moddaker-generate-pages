(٥٩) إِنَّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ لَأٓتِيَةٞ لَّا رَيۡبَ فِيهَا وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

The Hour in which Allah will raise the dead for reckoning and recompense is coming, but most people do not believe it is coming, so they do not prepare for it.

(٦٠) وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ٱدۡعُونِيٓ أَسۡتَجِبۡ لَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ عَنۡ عِبَادَتِي سَيَدۡخُلُونَ جَهَنَّمَ دَاخِرِينَ

O people, your Lord said: Call upon Me alone, worship Me alone, and do not associate any partner with Me. Those too arrogant to worship Me alone will enter the Fire on the Day of Resurrection, lowly and disgraced.

(٦١) ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلَّيۡلَ لِتَسۡكُنُواْ فِيهِ وَٱلنَّهَارَ مُبۡصِرًاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَذُو فَضۡلٍ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَشۡكُرُونَ

Allah is the One who has made the night dark for you so you can find harmony in it and rest, and He made the day bright and clear so that you can work in it. Allah is full of great bounty to the people when He grants them His apparent and hidden blessings. But most people do not thank Him for what He has blessed them.

(٦٢) ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمۡ خَٰلِقُ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُؤۡفَكُونَ

That Allah, who graced you with His favors, is the Creator of everything; there is no Creator besides Him. There is nothing worthy of worship besides Him. So, how do you turn away from His worship towards worshiping others who possess neither benefit nor harm?

(٦٣) كَذَٰلِكَ يُؤۡفَكُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَانُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ يَجۡحَدُونَ

Just as these people turn away from believing in Allah and worshipping Him alone, those who reject Allah's signs, which prove His oneness, have turned away in every age and place. Thus, they do not find their way to the truth, nor are they rightly guided to it.

(٦٤) ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ قَرَارٗا وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بِنَآءٗ وَصَوَّرَكُمۡ فَأَحۡسَنَ صُوَرَكُمۡ وَرَزَقَكُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمۡۖ فَتَبَارَكَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

O people, Allah is the One who made the Earth a suitable place for you to dwell in. And He firmly formed the sky above you so it cannot collapse. He formed you in the wombs of your mothers, then perfected your form. And He granted you provision from lawful and natural foods. He who granted you these blessings is Allah, your Lord. So, blessed is Allah, Lord of all the creation. There is no lord besides Him.

(٦٥) هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ فَٱدۡعُوهُ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَۗ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

He is the Ever-Living who will never die. There is nothing worthy of worship besides Him. So, call upon him in worship and supplication, seeking His pleasure alone, and do not associate any of His creation as partners with Him. All praise is for Allah, Lord of the creation.

(٦٦) ۞قُلۡ إِنِّي نُهِيتُ أَنۡ أَعۡبُدَ ٱلَّذِينَ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ لَمَّا جَآءَنِيَ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتُ مِن رَّبِّي وَأُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أُسۡلِمَ لِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

O Messenger, say [to them]: Allah has forbidden me from worshipping those you worship besides Him, those which do not benefit nor harm, since clear proof and evidence have come to me regarding the falsehood of worshipping them. Allah has ordered me to submit to Him alone through worship, as He is the Lord of all the creation. There is no lord besides Him for them.

Points of Benefit:

Supplication is inside the concept of the worship that should not be directed to anything besides Allah because supplication is the essence of worship.
The blessings of Allah require the slaves' gratitude.
Affirming the attribute of Life for Allah.
The importance of acting with sincerity.
