(٦٧) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ ثُمَّ مِنۡ عَلَقَةٖ ثُمَّ يُخۡرِجُكُمۡ طِفۡلٗا ثُمَّ لِتَبۡلُغُوٓاْ أَشُدَّكُمۡ ثُمَّ لِتَكُونُواْ شُيُوخٗاۚ وَمِنكُم مَّن يُتَوَفَّىٰ مِن قَبۡلُۖ وَلِتَبۡلُغُوٓاْ أَجَلٗا مُّسَمّٗى وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ

He is the One who created Adam from dust. After that, He created you from a sperm-drop, then after a sperm-drop from clotted blood, and after that, He takes you out of your mothers' wombs as small children so that you reach the peak of your strength and grow older until you become elders. And some of you die before that, reaching a fixed time in the knowledge of Allah, which you cannot decrease or increase. Perhaps you may benefit from this proof and evidence of His power and oneness.

(٦٨) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُۖ فَإِذَا قَضَىٰٓ أَمۡرٗا فَإِنَّمَا يَقُولُ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ

He alone is the One in whose hand is giving life, and He alone is the One who causes death. So when He decides a matter, He only says to that matter: "Be," and it comes into existence.

(٦٩) أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يُجَٰدِلُونَ فِيٓ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ أَنَّىٰ يُصۡرَفُونَ

O Messenger, do you not see those who argue with you about the signs of Allah, denying them despite their clarity, so you may be astonished at their condition while they are turning away from the evident truth?

(٧٠) ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَبِمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا بِهِۦ رُسُلَنَاۖ فَسَوۡفَ يَعۡلَمُونَ

Those who denied the Quran and the truth We sent Our messengers with - these deniers will soon learn the outcome of their denial and they will witness a miserable ending.

(٧١) إِذِ ٱلۡأَغۡلَٰلُ فِيٓ أَعۡنَٰقِهِمۡ وَٱلسَّلَٰسِلُ يُسۡحَبُونَ

They will learn its outcome when the shackles will be on their necks and the chains on their feet, with the angels of Hell dragging them.

(٧٢) فِي ٱلۡحَمِيمِ ثُمَّ فِي ٱلنَّارِ يُسۡجَرُونَ

The angels of punishment will drag them into boiling hot water. Then they will be used as fuel in the Fire.

(٧٣) ثُمَّ قِيلَ لَهُمۡ أَيۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡ تُشۡرِكُونَ

Then it will be said to them, in a scolding and rebuking manner: Where are your so-called gods, that cannot benefit or harm, that you set up as partners by worshipping them besides Allah? The disbelievers will say: They have vanished, so we cannot see them. We did not worship anything that deserved to be worshipped. In the same way, Allah leads the disbelievers astray from the truth in every time and place.

(٧٤) مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۖ قَالُواْ ضَلُّواْ عَنَّا بَل لَّمۡ نَكُن نَّدۡعُواْ مِن قَبۡلُ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُضِلُّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Then it will be said to them, in a scolding and rebuking manner: Where are your so-called gods, that cannot benefit or harm, that you set up as partners by worshipping them besides Allah? The disbelievers will say: They have vanished, so we cannot see them. We did not worship anything that deserved to be worshipped. In the same way, Allah leads the disbelievers astray from the truth in every time and place.

(٧٥) ذَٰلِكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَفۡرَحُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَبِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَمۡرَحُونَ

It will be said to them: The punishment you are suffering is due to your rejoicing over the polytheism you were upon and your excess in rejoicing.

(٧٦) ٱدۡخُلُوٓاْ أَبۡوَٰبَ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۖ فَبِئۡسَ مَثۡوَى ٱلۡمُتَكَبِّرِينَ

Enter the doors of Hell, staying therein forever. How wretched the abode in which those arrogant to the truth will reside is.

When Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم suffered from what his people did to him, Allah instructed him to be patient and consoled him with His promise of victory, stating:

(٧٧) فَٱصۡبِرۡ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقّٞۚ فَإِمَّا نُرِيَنَّكَ بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي نَعِدُهُمۡ أَوۡ نَتَوَفَّيَنَّكَ فَإِلَيۡنَا يُرۡجَعُونَ

O Messenger, be patient with the harm and rejection of your people. The promise of Allah to help you is undoubtedly true. So We will either show you in your lifetime some of the punishment We promised them, as occurred in the Battle of Badr, or We will cause you to pass away before that. To Us alone, they will return on the Day of Resurrection. Thus, We will recompense them for their actions accordingly, casting them into the Fire where they will remain forever.

Points of Benefit:

The progressive stages of creation are an established divine practice from which people can learn to go step-by-step in their lives.
The abomination of being pleased with falsehood.
The importance of patience in people's lives, especially the callers to Allah.
