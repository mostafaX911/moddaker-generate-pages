(٧٨) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا رُسُلٗا مِّن قَبۡلِكَ مِنۡهُم مَّن قَصَصۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ وَمِنۡهُم مَّن لَّمۡ نَقۡصُصۡ عَلَيۡكَۗ وَمَا كَانَ لِرَسُولٍ أَن يَأۡتِيَ بِ‍َٔايَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۚ فَإِذَا جَآءَ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ قُضِيَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَخَسِرَ هُنَالِكَ ٱلۡمُبۡطِلُونَ

O Messenger, We have sent many messengers before you to their people, but they rejected them and caused them harm. The messengers were patient upon the rejection and harm they faced. Among these messengers are those whom We have related their stories to you and those whose stories We have not told. It is incorrect for a messenger to bring a sign to his people from his Lord, except if He, the Exalted, wills it. Thus, for the disbelievers to demand signs is transgression. For when Allah's decree for victory or judgment in favor of the messengers against their nations comes, He will judge between them fairly. The disbelievers will then be destroyed, and the messengers will be saved. At that point, when the judgment is made between the slaves, the people of falsehood will lose out by having put themselves in a position of destruction through their disbelief.

(٧٩) ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمَ لِتَرۡكَبُواْ مِنۡهَا وَمِنۡهَا تَأۡكُلُونَ

Allah is the One who created camels, cows, and livestock for you so that you can ride on some of them and consume the meat of some.

(٨٠) وَلَكُمۡ فِيهَا مَنَٰفِعُ وَلِتَبۡلُغُواْ عَلَيۡهَا حَاجَةٗ فِي صُدُورِكُمۡ وَعَلَيۡهَا وَعَلَى ٱلۡفُلۡكِ تُحۡمَلُونَ

You have many benefits in these creations, which change with every era. You utilize them to acquire whatever necessities you desire to be fulfilled. And from the most apparent of these needs is transportation across the land through camels, among other means, and across the sea through ships.

(٨١) وَيُرِيكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ فَأَيَّ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ تُنكِرُونَ

And Allah shows you many of His signs that point to His power and oneness; so which of Allah's signs do you not acknowledge after it has been established they are His signs?

(٨٢) أَفَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَيَنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ كَانُوٓاْ أَكۡثَرَ مِنۡهُمۡ وَأَشَدَّ قُوَّةٗ وَءَاثَارٗا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَمَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ

Do these rejectors not travel the Earth and ponder the outcome of the nations who rejected before them and take heed from it? Those nations were wealthier, stronger, and more influential upon the world than them, but their actions and strength did not help them when the destructive punishment of Allah came.

(٨٣) فَلَمَّا جَآءَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ فَرِحُواْ بِمَا عِندَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ وَحَاقَ بِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

When their messengers brought them clear proof, they rejected them. They were content with their knowledge that contradicted whatever their messengers came with. So the same punishment they would mock when warned of it by their messengers befell them.

(٨٤) فَلَمَّا رَأَوۡاْ بَأۡسَنَا قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِٱللَّهِ وَحۡدَهُۥ وَكَفَرۡنَا بِمَا كُنَّا بِهِۦ مُشۡرِكِينَ

Then, when they saw Our punishment, they began to confess, at a time when confessing would not benefit them, saying: We believe in Allah alone and reject whatever partners and idols we worshipped instead of Him.

(٨٥) فَلَمۡ يَكُ يَنفَعُهُمۡ إِيمَٰنُهُمۡ لَمَّا رَأَوۡاْ بَأۡسَنَاۖ سُنَّتَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّتِي قَدۡ خَلَتۡ فِي عِبَادِهِۦۖ وَخَسِرَ هُنَالِكَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ

So believing after seeing Our punishment descending upon them will not benefit them. The way of Allah with His slaves has consistently been that believing after witnessing the punishment with their eyes is useless. The disbelievers condemned themselves at the time when the punishment descended by having put themselves in a place of destruction because they disbelieved in Allah and did not repent from that before seeing the punishment.

Points of Benefit:

Allah has messengers whom He did not mention in the Noble Quran and whom we generally believe in.
Allah has messengers He did not mention in the Noble Quran whom we believe in.
The danger of being pleased with falsehood and the evil end of the one who does that.
Belief upon seeing the devastating torment is not valid.
