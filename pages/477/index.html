Explained in Detail (Surah Fussilat)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Mentioning the situation of those who turn away from Allah and detailing their end.

Explanation:

(١) حمٓ

Hā Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) تَنزِيلٞ مِّنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

This Quran is a revelation from Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful.

(٣) كِتَٰبٞ فُصِّلَتۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥ قُرۡءَانًا عَرَبِيّٗا لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ

It is a book in which the verses have been made completely clear. It has been made an Arabic Quran for people who know because they are the ones who will benefit from its meanings and be guided to the truth.

(٤) بَشِيرٗا وَنَذِيرٗا فَأَعۡرَضَ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡمَعُونَ

It gives glad tidings to the believers of the generous reward Allah has prepared for them. It warns the disbelievers of the painful punishment of Allah, but most of them turn away from it and do not listen with an open mind.

(٥) وَقَالُواْ قُلُوبُنَا فِيٓ أَكِنَّةٖ مِّمَّا تَدۡعُونَآ إِلَيۡهِ وَفِيٓ ءَاذَانِنَا وَقۡرٞ وَمِنۢ بَيۡنِنَا وَبَيۡنِكَ حِجَابٞ فَٱعۡمَلۡ إِنَّنَا عَٰمِلُونَ

And they say: Our hearts are covered with multiple layers, so we do not comprehend what you are calling us to. Our ears cannot hear what you are saying. There is a barrier between you and us; none of your speech reaches us, so you carry on doing things your way, and we will do things our way. We will never follow you.

(٦) قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ بَشَرٞ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ أَنَّمَآ إِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهٞ وَٰحِدٞ فَٱسۡتَقِيمُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرُوهُۗ وَوَيۡلٞ لِّلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

O Messenger, tell these opposers: I am only human like you are. I receive revelation from Allah that your true deity is only one deity, who is Allah. So devote your worship to Him alone and seek forgiveness from Him for your sins. Destruction and punishment are for the polytheists who worship others instead of Allah or ascribe partners to Him.

(٧) ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَهُم بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ كَٰفِرُونَ

Those who do not pay Zakat on their wealth and disbelieve in the Hereafter, with its everlasting blessings and painful torment.

(٨) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرٌ غَيۡرُ مَمۡنُونٖ

Indeed, those who believe in Allah and His messengers and do good deeds will have the everlasting reward of Paradise, which will never end.

(٩) ۞قُلۡ أَئِنَّكُمۡ لَتَكۡفُرُونَ بِٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ فِي يَوۡمَيۡنِ وَتَجۡعَلُونَ لَهُۥٓ أَندَادٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists, rebukingly: Why do you disbelieve in Allah, who created the Earth in two days, and attribute equals to Him whom you worship instead of Him? He is the Lord of all creation.

(١٠) وَجَعَلَ فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ مِن فَوۡقِهَا وَبَٰرَكَ فِيهَا وَقَدَّرَ فِيهَآ أَقۡوَٰتَهَا فِيٓ أَرۡبَعَةِ أَيَّامٖ سَوَآءٗ لِّلسَّآئِلِينَ

He created firm mountains on it, which stabilize it and prevent it from shaking. And He blessed it, making it a continuous source of goodness for its people. He determined the sustenance of people and livestock in it for a period of four complete days, for those who wish to ask about that.

(١١) ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰٓ إِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَهِيَ دُخَانٞ فَقَالَ لَهَا وَلِلۡأَرۡضِ ٱئۡتِيَا طَوۡعًا أَوۡ كَرۡهٗا قَالَتَآ أَتَيۡنَا طَآئِعِينَ

Then Allah (may He be Exalted) resolved to create the sky when it was smoke, saying to it and the Earth: Submit to My command willingly or unwillingly. They said: We submit willingly; O our Lord, we have no wish except your wish.

Points of Benefit:

The disbelievers disabling their means of guidance means they will remain upon disbelief.
Clarifying the position of Zakat (obligatory alms) as it is a pillar of Islam.
The universe surrendering to Allah and submitting to His (the Transcendent's) command, along with everything in it.
