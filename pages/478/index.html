(١٢) فَقَضَىٰهُنَّ سَبۡعَ سَمَٰوَاتٖ فِي يَوۡمَيۡنِ وَأَوۡحَىٰ فِي كُلِّ سَمَآءٍ أَمۡرَهَاۚ وَزَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا بِمَصَٰبِيحَ وَحِفۡظٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَقۡدِيرُ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ

Then Allah completed the creation of the heavens in two days. With that, the heavens and the Earth were created in six days. Allah inspired in every heaven whatever He would decree concerning it and whatever obedience and worship He would command. We also decorated the nearest heaven with stars, protecting it from the eavesdropping of the devils. All that is the decree of the Almighty, whom no one can overcome, and the Omniscient of His creation.

(١٣) فَإِنۡ أَعۡرَضُواْ فَقُلۡ أَنذَرۡتُكُمۡ صَٰعِقَةٗ مِّثۡلَ صَٰعِقَةِ عَادٖ وَثَمُودَ

So if these people turn away from believing in what you have brought them, say to them: I have warned you of a punishment that will fall upon you, like the punishment that fell upon 'Ād, the people of Hūd, and Thamūd, the people of Saleh, when they rejected their messengers.

(١٤) إِذۡ جَآءَتۡهُمُ ٱلرُّسُلُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِمۡ أَلَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَۖ قَالُواْ لَوۡ شَآءَ رَبُّنَا لَأَنزَلَ مَلَٰٓئِكَةٗ فَإِنَّا بِمَآ أُرۡسِلۡتُم بِهِۦ كَٰفِرُونَ

When their messengers came to them, each followed the other in bringing one message, instructing them to worship none other than Allah alone. The disbelievers said: If our Lord wished to send down angels as messengers to us, He would have sent them. Therefore, we reject whatever you have been sent with because you are humans like we are.

(١٥) فَأَمَّا عَادٞ فَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَقَالُواْ مَنۡ أَشَدُّ مِنَّا قُوَّةًۖ أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَهُمۡ هُوَ أَشَدُّ مِنۡهُمۡ قُوَّةٗۖ وَكَانُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا يَجۡحَدُونَ

As for the people of Hūd, 'Ād, together with their disbelief in Allah, they displayed excessive arrogance on Earth and oppressed those around them. Having been deceived by their own strength, they said: Who is there that is stronger than us? They assumed that no one was stronger than them, so Allah replied to them: Do these people not know and see that the One who created them and gave them the strength that made them rebellious is stronger than them? They would reject the signs of Allah that Hūd (peace be upon him) brought to them.

(١٦) فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ رِيحٗا صَرۡصَرٗا فِيٓ أَيَّامٖ نَّحِسَاتٖ لِّنُذِيقَهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡخِزۡيِ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَلَعَذَابُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ أَخۡزَىٰۖ وَهُمۡ لَا يُنصَرُونَ

So We sent them a severe wind with a terrifying noise on days that were unfortunate due to the punishment that came to them. So that We may make them suffer humiliation and disgrace in this world, and indeed, the punishment of the Hereafter that awaits them is more humiliating for them. They will not find anyone to help save them from the punishment.

(١٧) وَأَمَّا ثَمُودُ فَهَدَيۡنَٰهُمۡ فَٱسۡتَحَبُّواْ ٱلۡعَمَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡهُدَىٰ فَأَخَذَتۡهُمۡ صَٰعِقَةُ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡهُونِ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ

As for the people of Saleh, the Thamūd, We guided them by making the path of truth clear to them, but they preferred deviance over guidance to the truth. Hence, the blast of humiliating punishment annihilated them because of the disbelief and sins they would commit.

(١٨) وَنَجَّيۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَكَانُواْ يَتَّقُونَ

And We saved those who believed in Allah and His messengers and feared Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. And We rescued them from the punishment that came upon their people.

(١٩) وَيَوۡمَ يُحۡشَرُ أَعۡدَآءُ ٱللَّهِ إِلَى ٱلنَّارِ فَهُمۡ يُوزَعُونَ

And on the Day when the enemies of Allah will be gathered towards the Fire, the appointed angels (of punishment) will thrust every last one of them into it. They will not be able to escape from it.

(٢٠) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا مَا جَآءُوهَا شَهِدَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سَمۡعُهُمۡ وَأَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ وَجُلُودُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

When they come to the Fire that they will be driven toward and deny the actions they did in this world, their own ears, eyes, and skin will testify to their sins and disbelief in the worldly life.

Points of Benefit:

Turning away from the Truth causes destruction in this life and the next.
Arrogance and being deceived by one's power prevent one from submitting to the Truth.
The disbelievers have torment both in this life and the next.
The person's limbs will testify against him on the Day of Resurrection.
