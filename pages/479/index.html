(٢١) وَقَالُواْ لِجُلُودِهِمۡ لِمَ شَهِدتُّمۡ عَلَيۡنَاۖ قَالُوٓاْ أَنطَقَنَا ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنطَقَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖۚ وَهُوَ خَلَقَكُمۡ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

And the disbelievers will ask their skins: Why do you testify against us by stating what we used to do in the worldly life? Their skins will reply: The one who grants everything the ability to speak gave us the ability to speak. He is the one who created you initially when you were in the worldly life, and to Him, you will return in the Hereafter to give account and be recompensed.

(٢٢) وَمَا كُنتُمۡ تَسۡتَتِرُونَ أَن يَشۡهَدَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ سَمۡعُكُمۡ وَلَآ أَبۡصَٰرُكُمۡ وَلَا جُلُودُكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن ظَنَنتُمۡ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَعۡلَمُ كَثِيرٗا مِّمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ

You did not use to hide your sins to the degree that your ears, eyes, and skin cannot testify against you because you did not believe in accountability, punishment, or reward after death. Instead, you thought that Allah (may He be Exalted) was unaware of many things you did and that they remained hidden from Him. Thus, you were deceived.

(٢٣) وَذَٰلِكُمۡ ظَنُّكُمُ ٱلَّذِي ظَنَنتُم بِرَبِّكُمۡ أَرۡدَىٰكُمۡ فَأَصۡبَحۡتُم مِّنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ

And that very evil thought you had concerning your Lord destroyed you, so because of it, you became the ones who lost out in this world and the Hereafter.

(٢٤) فَإِن يَصۡبِرُواْ فَٱلنَّارُ مَثۡوٗى لَّهُمۡۖ وَإِن يَسۡتَعۡتِبُواْ فَمَا هُم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُعۡتَبِينَ

If these people against whom their ears, eyes, and skins testified are to be patient, the Fire will be their abode and dwelling place they will take shelter in. And if they seek to lift the punishment from themselves and gain Allah's pleasure and entry into Paradise, they never achieve either of them.

(٢٥) ۞وَقَيَّضۡنَا لَهُمۡ قُرَنَآءَ فَزَيَّنُواْ لَهُم مَّا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُمۡ وَحَقَّ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ فِيٓ أُمَمٖ قَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِۖ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ خَٰسِرِينَ

We sent upon these disbelievers companions from the devils, who would constantly be close to them, beautifying their evil actions and rejection of the resurrection, so they made them forget it and neglect to work towards it. The punishment is binding upon them as it was upon the nations previously from the humans and Jinn. Indeed, they are the losers who will lose themselves and their families on the Day of Resurrection when they enter the Fire.

(٢٦) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَا تَسۡمَعُواْ لِهَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ وَٱلۡغَوۡاْ فِيهِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَغۡلِبُونَ

When the disbelievers cannot refute the evidence, they urge one another [by saying]: Do not listen to this Quran that Muhammad recites to you, and do not submit to it. Rather, shout and raise your voices when he recites it to you so that perhaps he stops reciting it and calling to it so that you can gain the upper hand over him, and we can have a break from him.

(٢٧) فَلَنُذِيقَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ عَذَابٗا شَدِيدٗا وَلَنَجۡزِيَنَّهُمۡ أَسۡوَأَ ٱلَّذِي كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

So we will make those who disbelieved in Allah and rejected His messengers suffer a severe punishment on the Day of Resurrection. We will recompense them with punishment for the worst of their actions, i.e., ascribing partners with Allah and sins.

(٢٨) ذَٰلِكَ جَزَآءُ أَعۡدَآءِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلنَّارُۖ لَهُمۡ فِيهَا دَارُ ٱلۡخُلۡدِ جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا يَجۡحَدُونَ

That recompense for those who disbelieved in Him and rejected His messengers is the Fire, in which they will live forever, never to come out of it. It is retribution for their rejection of the signs of Allah and not believing in them despite their clarity and depth of evidence.

(٢٩) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ رَبَّنَآ أَرِنَا ٱلَّذَيۡنِ أَضَلَّانَا مِنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِ نَجۡعَلۡهُمَا تَحۡتَ أَقۡدَامِنَا لِيَكُونَا مِنَ ٱلۡأَسۡفَلِينَ

And those who disbelieved in Allah and rejected His messengers will say: O our Lord, show us who from the humans and Jinn led us astray: Iblis who introduced disbelief and inviting towards it and the son of Adam who introduced bloodshed. We will put them in the Fire under our feet so that they are the lowest of the people and the ones most severely punished.

Points of Benefit:

Having a bad opinion of Allah is one of the characteristics of the disbelievers.
Disbelief and disobedience cause the devils to overpower people.
The followers will wish that those they followed receive the most severe torment on the Day of Resurrection.
