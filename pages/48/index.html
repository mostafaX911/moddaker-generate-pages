(٢٨٢) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا تَدَايَنتُم بِدَيۡنٍ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗى فَٱكۡتُبُوهُۚ وَلۡيَكۡتُب بَّيۡنَكُمۡ كَاتِبُۢ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِۚ وَلَا يَأۡبَ كَاتِبٌ أَن يَكۡتُبَ كَمَا عَلَّمَهُ ٱللَّهُۚ فَلۡيَكۡتُبۡ وَلۡيُمۡلِلِ ٱلَّذِي عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَلۡيَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ رَبَّهُۥ وَلَا يَبۡخَسۡ مِنۡهُ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۚ فَإِن كَانَ ٱلَّذِي عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡحَقُّ سَفِيهًا أَوۡ ضَعِيفًا أَوۡ لَا يَسۡتَطِيعُ أَن يُمِلَّ هُوَ فَلۡيُمۡلِلۡ وَلِيُّهُۥ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِۚ وَٱسۡتَشۡهِدُواْ شَهِيدَيۡنِ مِن رِّجَالِكُمۡۖ فَإِن لَّمۡ يَكُونَا رَجُلَيۡنِ فَرَجُلٞ وَٱمۡرَأَتَانِ مِمَّن تَرۡضَوۡنَ مِنَ ٱلشُّهَدَآءِ أَن تَضِلَّ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا فَتُذَكِّرَ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰۚ وَلَا يَأۡبَ ٱلشُّهَدَآءُ إِذَا مَا دُعُواْۚ وَلَا تَسۡ‍َٔمُوٓاْ أَن تَكۡتُبُوهُ صَغِيرًا أَوۡ كَبِيرًا إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلِهِۦۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ أَقۡسَطُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَأَقۡوَمُ لِلشَّهَٰدَةِ وَأَدۡنَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَرۡتَابُوٓاْ إِلَّآ أَن تَكُونَ تِجَٰرَةً حَاضِرَةٗ تُدِيرُونَهَا بَيۡنَكُمۡ فَلَيۡسَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ جُنَاحٌ أَلَّا تَكۡتُبُوهَاۗ وَأَشۡهِدُوٓاْ إِذَا تَبَايَعۡتُمۡۚ وَلَا يُضَآرَّ كَاتِبٞ وَلَا شَهِيدٞۚ وَإِن تَفۡعَلُواْ فَإِنَّهُۥ فُسُوقُۢ بِكُمۡۗ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۖ وَيُعَلِّمُكُمُ ٱللَّهُۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٞ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, when you enter a transaction that involves a debt, such as when you give a loan to one another for a fixed period, then write it down. The person who can write should write it down according to the legislation and should not refuse to justly write down the debt according to the writing Allah has taught him. He should note what is dictated by the person against whom the right [debt] is so that it serves as an acknowledgment from him. He should fear Allah and not reduce anything from the value or description of the debt. In cases where the debtor is not experienced in undertaking transactions or is young, insane, or unable to dictate due to being mute or for some other reason, his authoritative guardian should dictate justly. Also, call two sane and upright men as witnesses. If two men are not found, contact one man and two women whose religion and honesty you are satisfied with so that if one of the women forgets, the other can remind her. The witness should not refuse to be a witness to a debt and must testify if required. Do not be lazy to write down the debt, no matter how small the amount, as this is more just in Allah's law, more reliable as evidence, and more likely to remove any doubt in the type of debt, amount, or period. However, if the transaction is for an item that is present in exchange for a spot price, there is no harm in not writing it down since there is no need to do so. Calling witnesses is legislated so that disputes do not arise. It is unlawful to cause any harm to the witnesses or those who write, nor may they cause any harm to those who request them to write or stand witness. If anyone causes harm, he is going against Allah's sacred law. O believers fear Allah by fulfilling His instructions and avoiding His prohibitions. Allah will teach you what is best for you in this world and the Hereafter. Allah knows everything, and nothing is hidden from Him.

Points of Benefit:

The legitimacy of documenting debts and all financial transactions to avoid disputes and conflicts.
The establishment of guardianship over minors is either due to their inability, lack of mental maturity, or young age.
The legitimacy of witnessing the contracting of debts and rights.
That among the elements of perfection in writing and justice is for the scribe to excel in composition and use appropriate expressions in every transaction according to its nature.
Harming anyone due to documenting and writing rights is not permissible, whether it is from the perspective of the right holders or the one who writes and testifies to it.
