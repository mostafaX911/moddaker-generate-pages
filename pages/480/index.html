After mentioning the recompense awaiting His enemies, Allah mentions the recompense awaiting His allies, stating:

(٣٠) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ تَتَنَزَّلُ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ أَلَّا تَخَافُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَبۡشِرُواْ بِٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ

Indeed, those who say: Our Lord is Allah; we have no Lord besides Him, and they are steadfast in complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, the angels will descend upon them when death nears them, saying: Do not fear death or whatever comes after it and do not grieve over what you will leave behind in this world. Take the glad tidings of Paradise you were promised for your faith in Allah and good deeds.

(٣١) نَحۡنُ أَوۡلِيَآؤُكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ وَلَكُمۡ فِيهَا مَا تَشۡتَهِيٓ أَنفُسُكُمۡ وَلَكُمۡ فِيهَا مَا تَدَّعُونَ

We were your friends in the worldly life; we used to correct you and protect you, and we will also be your friends in the Hereafter, so our friendship with you is continuous. In Paradise, you can fulfill whatever your hearts desire and crave, and you will get whatever you seek.

(٣٢) نُزُلٗا مِّنۡ غَفُورٖ رَّحِيمٖ

It is a provision prepared for you by a merciful Lord forgiving the sins of those of His slaves who repent to Him.

(٣٣) وَمَنۡ أَحۡسَنُ قَوۡلٗا مِّمَّن دَعَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا وَقَالَ إِنَّنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ

There is no one whose speech is better than the one who calls towards Allah's Oneness and acts upon His law while doing good actions that please Him, saying: Indeed, I am one of those who submit and humble myself to Allah. Indeed, whoever does all that is the best in speech.

(٣٤) وَلَا تَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡحَسَنَةُ وَلَا ٱلسَّيِّئَةُۚ ٱدۡفَعۡ بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ فَإِذَا ٱلَّذِي بَيۡنَكَ وَبَيۡنَهُۥ عَدَٰوَةٞ كَأَنَّهُۥ وَلِيٌّ حَمِيمٞ

Doing good deeds in obedience to Allah that please Him and doing evil and committing sins that anger Him are not equal. Respond with conduct that is better to the one who does wrong. If you do so, you will soon find that the previous enmity between you disappears, and it will be as if he is now a close, affectionate friend.

(٣٥) وَمَا يُلَقَّىٰهَآ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ صَبَرُواْ وَمَا يُلَقَّىٰهَآ إِلَّا ذُو حَظٍّ عَظِيمٖ

Only those who are patient with the harm caused to them and the difficulties they face from the people are given the ability to display this praiseworthy character. Only those who are incredibly fortunate are granted the ability to do so because of the abundant goodness and immense benefit there is in it.

(٣٦) وَإِمَّا يَنزَغَنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ نَزۡغٞ فَٱسۡتَعِذۡ بِٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

And if the Devil whispers evil to you at any time, hold fast to Allah and seek refuge in Him. He is All-Hearing of whatever you say and Omniscient of your situation.

(٣٧) وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِ ٱلَّيۡلُ وَٱلنَّهَارُ وَٱلشَّمۡسُ وَٱلۡقَمَرُۚ لَا تَسۡجُدُواْ لِلشَّمۡسِ وَلَا لِلۡقَمَرِ وَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِۤ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَهُنَّ إِن كُنتُمۡ إِيَّاهُ تَعۡبُدُونَ

Among Allah's signs that demonstrate His greatness and oneness are the alternating of the night and the day and the sun and moon. O, people, do not prostrate to the sun nor the moon; rather, prostrate to Allah, who created them all, if you truly worship Him.

(٣٨) فَإِنِ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ فَٱلَّذِينَ عِندَ رَبِّكَ يُسَبِّحُونَ لَهُۥ بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ وَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡ‍َٔمُونَ۩

So if they show arrogance and turn away, not prostrating to Allah the Creator, the angels with Allah exalt and praise Him (may He be Exalted) throughout the day and night anyway, and they do not become bored of their worship of Him.

Points of Benefit:

The position of steadfastness with Allah is a great one.
Allah's honoring of His believing staves and taking care of their affairs and the affairs of those after them.
The position of calling to Allah and that it is the best action.
Patience with harm and repelling evil with what is good are two traits the caller to Allah cannot do without.
