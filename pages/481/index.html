(٣٩) وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَنَّكَ تَرَى ٱلۡأَرۡضَ خَٰشِعَةٗ فَإِذَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهَا ٱلۡمَآءَ ٱهۡتَزَّتۡ وَرَبَتۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَحۡيَاهَا لَمُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ

Among Allah's signs that demonstrate His omnipotence, oneness, and ability to resurrect is that you see the earth without any plants. Then, when We send down rainwater, it begins to move due to the hidden growth of seeds, and it rises. Indeed, the One who revived this barren earth through growth is the One who will revive the dead and resurrect them to take them to account and recompense them. Indeed, He is Omnipotent over everything; reviving the earth after it has become barren is not impossible for Him, nor is reviving the dead and resurrecting them from their graves.

(٤٠) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُلۡحِدُونَ فِيٓ ءَايَٰتِنَا لَا يَخۡفَوۡنَ عَلَيۡنَآۗ أَفَمَن يُلۡقَىٰ فِي ٱلنَّارِ خَيۡرٌ أَم مَّن يَأۡتِيٓ ءَامِنٗا يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۚ ٱعۡمَلُواْ مَا شِئۡتُمۡ إِنَّهُۥ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ

Indeed, those who deviate from Allah's signs, rejecting or distorting them and turning others away, are not hidden from Us. Allah knows who they are. So, is the one who will be thrown into the Fire better than the one who will be safe from the punishment on the Day of Resurrection? O, people, do whatever good or evil you wish, for Allah has made good and evil clear to you, and He sees whatever you do of it. None of your actions are hidden from Him.

(٤١) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِٱلذِّكۡرِ لَمَّا جَآءَهُمۡۖ وَإِنَّهُۥ لَكِتَٰبٌ عَزِيزٞ

Indeed, those who disbelieve in the Quran when it reaches them from Allah will be punished on the Day of Resurrection. Certainly, it is a mighty and impervious book; no one can distort it or change it in any way.

(٤٢) لَّا يَأۡتِيهِ ٱلۡبَٰطِلُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَلَا مِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦۖ تَنزِيلٞ مِّنۡ حَكِيمٍ حَمِيدٖ

Falsehood cannot reach it from any direction by removing, adding, changing, or distorting anything from it. It is a revelation from One who is Wise in His creation, decree, and legislation and praiseworthy in all conditions.

After mentioning those who deny the Scripture (Quran), Allah comforted and consoled His Messenger with what his brothers before him from the messengers suffered of rejection, ridicule, and slander, stating:

(٤٣) مَّا يُقَالُ لَكَ إِلَّا مَا قَدۡ قِيلَ لِلرُّسُلِ مِن قَبۡلِكَۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَذُو مَغۡفِرَةٖ وَذُو عِقَابٍ أَلِيمٖ

O Messenger, the rejection directed towards you is the same that has been directed toward the messengers before you. So be patient because your Lord forgives those of His slaves who repent to Him. However, those who persist upon sins and do not repent, for them is a painful punishment.

(٤٤) وَلَوۡ جَعَلۡنَٰهُ قُرۡءَانًا أَعۡجَمِيّٗا لَّقَالُواْ لَوۡلَا فُصِّلَتۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥٓۖ ءَا۬عۡجَمِيّٞ وَعَرَبِيّٞۗ قُلۡ هُوَ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ هُدٗى وَشِفَآءٞۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ فِيٓ ءَاذَانِهِمۡ وَقۡرٞ وَهُوَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ عَمًىۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يُنَادَوۡنَ مِن مَّكَانِۢ بَعِيدٖ

Had We revealed this Quran in a language other than that of the Arabs, the disbelievers among them would have said: Why are its verses not made clear to us so that we can understand them? Can the Quran be in any language other than Arabic, while the one who brings it is an Arab? O Messenger, tell these people: For those who believe in Allah and His messengers, the Quran is guidance from deviance and a cure for the hearts from ignorance and what it entails. As for those who do not have faith in Allah, their ears are deaf, and they are blinded by it, so they do not understand it. These people are called from a distant place, so how can they hear the voice of the caller?

(٤٥) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ فَٱخۡتُلِفَ فِيهِۚ وَلَوۡلَا كَلِمَةٞ سَبَقَتۡ مِن رَّبِّكَ لَقُضِيَ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَفِي شَكّٖ مِّنۡهُ مُرِيبٖ

Indeed, We gave Moses the Torah, and [people] differed over it. Some believed in it, while others disbelieved. Had Allah not promised that judgment would be passed between the slaves on the Day of Resurrection in matters they disputed over, judgment would already have been passed upon those who disputed over the Torah, and those upon falsehood would have been destroyed. Indeed, the disbelievers are highly doubtful of the authenticity of the Quran.

(٤٦) مَّنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَلِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَنۡ أَسَآءَ فَعَلَيۡهَاۗ وَمَا رَبُّكَ بِظَلَّٰمٖ لِّلۡعَبِيدِ

Whoever does righteous deeds will reap the benefits of his actions because Allah does not benefit from anyone's good deeds. And whoever commits evil deeds, the consequences only affect him, as no sins can harm Allah. Allah will soon recompense each person according to what they deserve. O Messenger, your Lord is not oppressive to His slaves; He will never reduce even a single good deed of theirs nor increase an evil one.

Points of Benefit:

Allah preserves [the verses of] the Quran from being replaced or distorted. He, the Transcendent, takes care of this preservation, unlike His books before it.
The argument of the polytheist Arabs was defeated by revealing the Quran in their language.
Negating oppression from Allah and affirming complete justice for Him.
