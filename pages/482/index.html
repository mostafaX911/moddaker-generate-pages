(٤٧) ۞إِلَيۡهِ يُرَدُّ عِلۡمُ ٱلسَّاعَةِۚ وَمَا تَخۡرُجُ مِن ثَمَرَٰتٖ مِّنۡ أَكۡمَامِهَا وَمَا تَحۡمِلُ مِنۡ أُنثَىٰ وَلَا تَضَعُ إِلَّا بِعِلۡمِهِۦۚ وَيَوۡمَ يُنَادِيهِمۡ أَيۡنَ شُرَكَآءِي قَالُوٓاْ ءَاذَنَّٰكَ مَامِنَّا مِن شَهِيدٖ

To Allah alone, the knowledge of the hour returns. He alone knows when it will occur. No one besides Him knows this. No fruits emerge from their protective sheath, and no female conceives except by His knowledge. Nothing of that escapes Him. On the day when Allah will call the polytheists who used to worship idols together with Him, reproaching them for their worship of those idols: Where are My partners that you used to worship besides Me and claim they are My partners? The polytheists will say: We confess before you. There is not one of us who testifies now that you have a partner.

(٤٨) وَضَلَّ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَدۡعُونَ مِن قَبۡلُۖ وَظَنُّواْ مَا لَهُم مِّن مَّحِيصٖ

The idols that they used to call on will disappear, and they will be certain that they will have no escape from Allah's punishment.

(٤٩) لَّا يَسۡ‍َٔمُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مِن دُعَآءِ ٱلۡخَيۡرِ وَإِن مَّسَّهُ ٱلشَّرُّ فَيَ‍ُٔوسٞ قَنُوطٞ

Man does not tire of asking for health, wealth, children, and other favors. If he is afflicted by poverty, sickness, and the like, then he becomes extremely despondent of Allah's mercy.

(٥٠) وَلَئِنۡ أَذَقۡنَٰهُ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ضَرَّآءَ مَسَّتۡهُ لَيَقُولَنَّ هَٰذَا لِي وَمَآ أَظُنُّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ قَآئِمَةٗ وَلَئِن رُّجِعۡتُ إِلَىٰ رَبِّيٓ إِنَّ لِي عِندَهُۥ لَلۡحُسۡنَىٰۚ فَلَنُنَبِّئَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِمَا عَمِلُواْ وَلَنُذِيقَنَّهُم مِّنۡ عَذَابٍ غَلِيظٖ

If We were to let him taste health, riches, and well-being from Us after difficulty and sickness had afflicted him, he would certainly say: This is mine because I am worthy and deserving of it. I do not think the hour will take place. If it is assumed that the hour could take place, then I would have the very best by Allah. Just as He favored me in this world because I deserved that, He will favor me in the Hereafter. We will certainly tell those who rejected Allah about the disbelief and the sins they did, and We will make them taste a torment of extreme severity.

(٥١) وَإِذَآ أَنۡعَمۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ أَعۡرَضَ وَنَ‍َٔابِجَانِبِهِۦ وَإِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلشَّرُّ فَذُو دُعَآءٍ عَرِيضٖ

When We bless man with the favor of health, well-being, and the like, he becomes unmindful of Allah's remembrance and obedience and turns away in pride. When sickness, poverty, and the like afflict him, he supplicates to Allah plentifully, complaining to Him of what has afflicted him so that He will remove it. He does not thank His Lord when He blesses him and is not patient when He tests him.

(٥٢) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِن كَانَ مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ كَفَرۡتُم بِهِۦ مَنۡ أَضَلُّ مِمَّنۡ هُوَ فِي شِقَاقِۢ بَعِيدٖ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists who reject: Tell me, if this Quran was from Allah and then you rejected and disbelieved in it, what would be your condition? Who is more wrong than the person who is stubborn toward the truth despite it being apparent and despite the clarity and strength of its evidence?

(٥٣) سَنُرِيهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا فِي ٱلۡأٓفَاقِ وَفِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ حَتَّىٰ يَتَبَيَّنَ لَهُمۡ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّۗ أَوَ لَمۡ يَكۡفِ بِرَبِّكَ أَنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدٌ

We will show the disbelievers of the Quraysh Our signs from the lands spread across the Earth that Allah will allow the Muslims to conquer. We will also show them Our signs in themselves through the conquest of Mecca, which will make clear to them and remove all doubt that the Quran is the undeniable truth. Is it not enough for these polytheists that the Quran is true by Allah's testimony that it is from Him? Who can be a greater witness than Allah? If they were seeking the truth, they would have sufficed with the testimony of their Lord.

(٥٤) أَلَآ إِنَّهُمۡ فِي مِرۡيَةٖ مِّن لِّقَآءِ رَبِّهِمۡۗ أَلَآ إِنَّهُۥ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ مُّحِيطُۢ

Indeed, these polytheists doubt the meeting of their Lord on the Day of Resurrection as they deny the resurrection and do not believe in the Afterlife. Therefore, they do not prepare for it with righteous actions. Indeed, Allah's knowledge and power encompass everything.

Points of Benefit:

Only Allah has knowledge of the Hour.
The disbelievers deal with the blessings and curses of Allah with confusion and turmoil.
Allah knows and has power over everything.
