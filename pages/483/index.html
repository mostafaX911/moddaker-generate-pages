The Consultation (Surah Ash-Shuraa)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Clarifying the perfection of Allah's law, emphasizing the obligation to follow it, and warning against violating it.

Explanation:

(١) حمٓ

Hā Mīm - 'Ain Sīn Qāf – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) عٓسٓقٓ

Hā Mīm - 'Ain Sīn Qāf – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٣) كَذَٰلِكَ يُوحِيٓ إِلَيۡكَ وَإِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

This is how this revelation is revealed to you, O Muhammad, and to those before you from Allah's prophets. Allah is The Mighty in taking retribution against His enemies and The Wise in His planning and creation.

(٤) لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

Everything in the heavens and on Earth belongs to Him alone. He created, owns, and controls all of it. He is High in His being and in terms of His status and domination, and Majestic in His being.

(٥) تَكَادُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ يَتَفَطَّرۡنَ مِن فَوۡقِهِنَّۚ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ يُسَبِّحُونَ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَيَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ لِمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَلَآ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

From His Majesty (may He be Exalted) is that the heavens could split apart despite their greatness and height. The angels exalt the praises of their Lord out of submission and in reverence of Him, and they seek forgiveness from Allah for those on Earth. Indeed, Allah is the One who forgives the sins of those slaves of His who repent, and He is merciful to them.

(٦) وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَ ٱللَّهُ حَفِيظٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَمَآ أَنتَ عَلَيۡهِم بِوَكِيلٖ

For those who take idols besides Allah, whom they align with and worship alongside Allah, Allah lies in wait for them. He records their actions, and He will recompense them for that. You, O Messenger, are not in charge of them, and you will not be questioned about their actions. You are only the one who conveys the message.

(٧) وَكَذَٰلِكَ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ قُرۡءَانًا عَرَبِيّٗا لِّتُنذِرَ أُمَّ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ وَمَنۡ حَوۡلَهَا وَتُنذِرَ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡجَمۡعِ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهِۚ فَرِيقٞ فِي ٱلۡجَنَّةِ وَفَرِيقٞ فِي ٱلسَّعِيرِ

Just as We sent revelation to the prophets before you, O Messenger, We have revealed an Arabic Quran to you so that you may warn Mecca and the Arab towns around it and then all humanity and warn people of the Day of Resurrection when Allah will gather the early and the latter peoples on one plain for reckoning and recompense. There is no doubt in the occurrence of that day, on which people will be divided into two groups: One group will be in Paradise, and these are the believers, and one group will be in the Fire, and these are the disbelievers.

(٨) وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَجَعَلَهُمۡ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ وَلَٰكِن يُدۡخِلُ مَن يَشَآءُ فِي رَحۡمَتِهِۦۚ وَٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ مَا لَهُم مِّن وَلِيّٖ وَلَا نَصِيرٍ

If Allah willed to make them one nation on the religion of Islam, He would have done so and entered them all into Paradise. But, His wisdom demanded that He guide whoever He wills into Islam and then admit them into Paradise. Those who wrong themselves by disbelief and sins will have no one to protect them and no helper to rescue them from Allah's punishment.

(٩) أَمِ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَۖ فَٱللَّهُ هُوَ ٱلۡوَلِيُّ وَهُوَ يُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

Instead, these polytheists have taken allies whom they align with besides Allah. Allah is the true Protector. Others besides Him can bring no benefit nor harm. He gives life to the dead by resurrecting them for reckoning and recompense. He has power over all things, and nothing is outside His ability (may He be Exalted).

(١٠) وَمَا ٱخۡتَلَفۡتُمۡ فِيهِ مِن شَيۡءٖ فَحُكۡمُهُۥٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبِّي عَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡتُ وَإِلَيۡهِ أُنِيبُ

Whatever you, O People, differ in about the principle and subsidiary matters of your religion, the judgment for that rests with Allah. His book and the Sunnah of His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم must be referred to. The One who possesses these attributes is my Lord. It is on Him that I rely in all my affairs, and it is to Him that I turn by repenting to Him.

Points of Benefit:

Everything shows the greatness of Allah.
The angels make good supplications for the believers.
The Quran and the Sunnah are references for the believers in all their affairs, especially when differing.
Only warning the people of Mecca and those surrounding it because the response is for them due to them rejecting his صلى الله عليه وسلم message, while he is a messenger to all of mankind, as Allah, the Exalted says: (And We have not sent you except comprehensively to mankind…) [Sheba:28].
