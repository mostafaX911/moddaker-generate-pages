(١١) فَاطِرُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا وَمِنَ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ أَزۡوَٰجٗا يَذۡرَؤُكُمۡ فِيهِۚ لَيۡسَ كَمِثۡلِهِۦ شَيۡءٞۖ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡبَصِيرُ

Allah is the Creator of the heavens and the Earth without any precedent. He made spouses for you from amongst your own kind. He also made for you pairs of camels, cattle, and sheep so that they can multiply for your sake. He creates you through the spouses that He made for you by your marrying one another. He provides a livelihood for you through the cattle that He made for you from their meat and milk. Nothing of His creation resembles Him. He is the One who hears the statements of His slaves and the One who sees their actions. Nothing of that escapes Him, and He will recompense them for their actions: if good, then with good, and if evil, then with bad.

(١٢) لَهُۥ مَقَالِيدُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَقۡدِرُۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٞ

To Him alone belong the keys to the treasures of the heavens and the Earth. He expands the provision for whomever of His slaves He wills as a test to see whether they are grateful or not. He restricts it for whoever of His slaves He wills, as a trial to see whether they are patient or become angry with Allah's decree. He has knowledge of all things. Nothing in which lies the interests of His slaves is hidden from Him.

(١٣) ۞شَرَعَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلدِّينِ مَا وَصَّىٰ بِهِۦ نُوحٗا وَٱلَّذِيٓ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ وَمَا وَصَّيۡنَا بِهِۦٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ وَعِيسَىٰٓۖ أَنۡ أَقِيمُواْ ٱلدِّينَ وَلَا تَتَفَرَّقُواْ فِيهِۚ كَبُرَ عَلَى ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ مَا تَدۡعُوهُمۡ إِلَيۡهِۚ ٱللَّهُ يَجۡتَبِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَهۡدِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ مَن يُنِيبُ

He has ordained for you religious matters similar to what We instructed Noah to convey and act on and what We have revealed to you, O Messenger. He has ordained for you similar to what We instructed Abraham, Moses, and Jesus to convey and act on. In summary: Uphold the religion and leave out division therein. What you call the polytheists towards, such as declaring Allah's Oneness and leaving the worship of others besides Him, is difficult for them. Allah chooses whichever of His slaves He wills and guides them to His worship and obedience, and He guides to Him those of them who return to Him by repenting from their sins.

(١٤) وَمَا تَفَرَّقُوٓاْ إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡعِلۡمُ بَغۡيَۢا بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ وَلَوۡلَا كَلِمَةٞ سَبَقَتۡ مِن رَّبِّكَ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗى لَّقُضِيَ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ وَإِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أُورِثُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ لَفِي شَكّٖ مِّنۡهُ مُرِيبٖ

The disbelievers and the polytheists only became divided after the proof of the mission of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم was established. Their division was only on account of rivalry and oppression. Were it not for what had already been in Allah's knowledge that the punishment would be postponed from them until a time determined in His knowledge, which is the day of resurrection, Allah would have decided between them and punished them quickly on account of their rejection of Allah and denial of His messengers. Those Jews who were made to inherit the Torah, and those Christians who were made to inherit the Gospel after their predecessors, and these polytheists are in doubt about and deny this Quran which Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم has brought.

(١٥) فَلِذَٰلِكَ فَٱدۡعُۖ وَٱسۡتَقِمۡ كَمَآ أُمِرۡتَۖ وَلَا تَتَّبِعۡ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡۖ وَقُلۡ ءَامَنتُ بِمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ مِن كِتَٰبٖۖ وَأُمِرۡتُ لِأَعۡدِلَ بَيۡنَكُمُۖ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّنَا وَرَبُّكُمۡۖ لَنَآ أَعۡمَٰلُنَا وَلَكُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلُكُمۡۖ لَا حُجَّةَ بَيۡنَنَا وَبَيۡنَكُمُۖ ٱللَّهُ يَجۡمَعُ بَيۡنَنَاۖ وَإِلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

Call to this upright religion and remain firm on it as Allah has instructed you. Do not follow their false desires. Say to them when disputing with them: I have faith in Allah and in the scriptures which Allah sent down to His messengers. Allah has instructed me to judge between you with justice. Allah, the One whom I worship, is both our Lord and your Lord. We have our deeds, whether good or bad, and you have your deeds, whether good or bad. There is no quarrel between us after the evidence is apparent and the path is clear. Allah will bring us all together, and to Him is the return on the Day of Resurrection. He will then recompense each one as He deserves, and then it will become clear who is true, who is false, who is right, and who is wrong.

Points of Benefit:

The religion of the Prophets is fundamentally one religion.
The importance of having a united word and the danger of differing about it.
From the elements of the successful call to Allah are: Having sound principles, sticking to them, staying far from those following their desires, being just, focusing on what is common, abandoning fruitless arguments, and reminding of a common final destination.
