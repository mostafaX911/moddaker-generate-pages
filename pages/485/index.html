(١٦) وَٱلَّذِينَ يُحَآجُّونَ فِي ٱللَّهِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا ٱسۡتُجِيبَ لَهُۥ حُجَّتُهُمۡ دَاحِضَةٌ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ وَعَلَيۡهِمۡ غَضَبٞ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ شَدِيدٌ

Those who quarrel with invalid arguments about this religion sent down to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم after people have responded to his call due to the strength of its proof and have entered into it; the argument of these people who quarrel is void and holds no weight with their Lord and with the believers. It has no effect. They have earned Allah's anger due to their disbelief and rejection of the truth, and they will have a severe punishment that awaits them on the Day of Resurrection.

After mentioning the invalidity of the disbeliever's evidence, Allah mentions the origin of the authentic evidence that a Muslim argues with, which is the Quran, stating:

(١٧) ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَٱلۡمِيزَانَۗ وَمَا يُدۡرِيكَ لَعَلَّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ قَرِيبٞ

Allah is the One who sent down the Quran with the truth, in which there is no doubt. In it, He commanded justice so that people judge by it. The hour that these people deny may be close, and it is known that everything that is coming is near.

(١٨) يَسۡتَعۡجِلُ بِهَا ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهَاۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مُشۡفِقُونَ مِنۡهَا وَيَعۡلَمُونَ أَنَّهَا ٱلۡحَقُّۗ أَلَآ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُمَارُونَ فِي ٱلسَّاعَةِ لَفِي ضَلَٰلِۢ بَعِيدٍ

Those who do not believe in it seek to hasten it because they do not believe in reckoning, reward, or punishment. But those who have faith in Allah are fearful of it because they do not know their fate after it, and they certainly know it to be the truth in which there is no doubt. Indeed, those who dispute the Hour and doubt its occurrence are in error, which is far from the truth.

(١٩) ٱللَّهُ لَطِيفُۢ بِعِبَادِهِۦ يَرۡزُقُ مَن يَشَآءُۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡقَوِيُّ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ

Allah is kind towards His slaves. He provides for whomever He wills and expands their provision, and He restricts it for whomever He wills out of mercy for them, even though it may appear otherwise. He is the Strong, Whom none can overpower, and the Mighty, Who takes retribution from His enemies.

(٢٠) مَن كَانَ يُرِيدُ حَرۡثَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ نَزِدۡ لَهُۥ فِي حَرۡثِهِۦۖ وَمَن كَانَ يُرِيدُ حَرۡثَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا نُؤۡتِهِۦ مِنۡهَا وَمَا لَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ مِن نَّصِيبٍ

Whoever wants the rewards of the Hereafter and works for it, We will multiply his reward for him by multiplying the reward of a good deed by ten times up to seven hundred times or even more. Whoever only wants the worldly life, I will give him his allotted share in it, and he will not have any share in the Afterlife as he preferred this world over it.

(٢١) أَمۡ لَهُمۡ شُرَكَٰٓؤُاْ شَرَعُواْ لَهُم مِّنَ ٱلدِّينِ مَا لَمۡ يَأۡذَنۢ بِهِ ٱللَّهُۚ وَلَوۡلَا كَلِمَةُ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ لَقُضِيَ بَيۡنَهُمۡۗ وَإِنَّ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Or do these polytheists have deities besides Allah, who have ordained for them religious matters that Allah did not authorize them to ordain, such as ascribing partners to Allah, declaring unlawful what He made lawful and declaring lawful what He made unlawful? Had it not been for the fixed time that Allah had set to decide between those who differ and that He will postpone them until such time, He would have decided between them. Those who wrong themselves by ascribing partners to Allah and committing sins will have a painful punishment that awaits them on the Day of Resurrection.

(٢٢) تَرَى ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ مُشۡفِقِينَ مِمَّا كَسَبُواْ وَهُوَ وَاقِعُۢ بِهِمۡۗ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ فِي رَوۡضَاتِ ٱلۡجَنَّاتِۖ لَهُم مَّا يَشَآءُونَ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَضۡلُ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ

You, O Messenger, will see those who wrong themselves by ascribing partners to Allah and committing sins, fearing punishment for their wrongdoing. The punishment will inevitably fall on them. Their mere fear that is devoid of repentance will not be of any use to them. Those who have faith in Allah and His messengers and do righteous actions will be in contrast to them. They will be enjoying luxury in the gardens of Paradise. They will have whatever type of endless bliss they wish for with their Lord. That is the great bounty that no other bounty can come close to.

Points of Benefit:

The believer's fear of the horrors of the Day of Resurrection helps him be prepared for it.
The kindness of Allah to His slaves in that He expands the provision of whoever He wills according to what His wisdom demands and restricts it from whoever He wills according to what His wisdom demands.
The danger of preferring this life over the Hereafter.
