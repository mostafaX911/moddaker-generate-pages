(٢٣) ذَٰلِكَ ٱلَّذِي يُبَشِّرُ ٱللَّهُ عِبَادَهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِۗ قُل لَّآ أَسۡ‍َٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ أَجۡرًا إِلَّا ٱلۡمَوَدَّةَ فِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰۗ وَمَن يَقۡتَرِفۡ حَسَنَةٗ نَّزِدۡ لَهُۥ فِيهَا حُسۡنًاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ شَكُورٌ

That is the good news that Allah gives at the hand of His Messenger to those who have faith in Allah and His Messengers and do righteous actions. Say, O Messenger: I do not ask of you any reward for conveying the truth besides one reward, the benefit of which returns to you, which is that you love me because of my blood relationship with you. Whoever does a good deed, We will multiply its reward for him by multiplying the reward of a good deed by ten times. Allah forgives the sins of those of His slaves who repent to Him and is grateful for the righteous actions they do seeking His pleasure.

(٢٤) أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبٗاۖ فَإِن يَشَإِ ٱللَّهُ يَخۡتِمۡ عَلَىٰ قَلۡبِكَۗ وَيَمۡحُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡبَٰطِلَ وَيُحِقُّ ٱلۡحَقَّ بِكَلِمَٰتِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

One of the claims of the polytheists is that Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم made up the Quran and then said it is from his Lord. Allah says in response to them: If you had told yourself to fabricate a lie, I would have sealed off your heart. As this is not the case, this shows that the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم is truthful and receives the revelation from his Lord. Allah eradicates the falsehood of the fabricator and preserves the truth through His words that He sends down to His Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم. He knows what is in the hearts of His slaves. Nothing is hidden from Him.

(٢٥) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يَقۡبَلُ ٱلتَّوۡبَةَ عَنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ وَيَعۡفُواْ عَنِ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا تَفۡعَلُونَ

He (may He be Exalted) is the One who accepts the repentance of His slaves from disbelief and sins when they repent to Him. He overlooks the wrongdoings that they committed. He knows everything that you do. Nothing of your actions is hidden from Him, and He will give you the recompense for them.

(٢٦) وَيَسۡتَجِيبُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَيَزِيدُهُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦۚ وَٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ شَدِيدٞ

He responds to the supplication of those who have faith in Allah and His messengers and gives them even more out of His bounty, which they did not ask for. Those who reject Allah and His Messengers will have a strong punishment awaiting them on the Day of Resurrection.

(٢٧) ۞وَلَوۡ بَسَطَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِعِبَادِهِۦ لَبَغَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَٰكِن يُنَزِّلُ بِقَدَرٖ مَّا يَشَآءُۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِعِبَادِهِۦ خَبِيرُۢ بَصِيرٞ

If Allah had extended the provision for all of His slaves, they would have transgressed on Earth with oppression. But He (may He be Exalted) sends down provision to the extent that He wills to expand or restrict it. He is Well-Aware and Observant of the states of His slaves. He gives for a reason and also withholds for a reason.

(٢٨) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُنَزِّلُ ٱلۡغَيۡثَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا قَنَطُواْ وَيَنشُرُ رَحۡمَتَهُۥۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡوَلِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ

He is the One who sends down the rain to His slaves after they had lost hope of its coming down. He spreads His mercy by causing crops to sprout from the earth after sending down the rain. He is the One Who takes charge of the affairs of His slaves, the One Who deserves praise in every situation.

(٢٩) وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ خَلۡقُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَثَّ فِيهِمَا مِن دَآبَّةٖۚ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ جَمۡعِهِمۡ إِذَا يَشَآءُ قَدِيرٞ

Among Allah's signs that prove His power and oneness is the creation of the heavens and the creation of the Earth and the wondrous creations that He spread throughout them. He is able to bring them all together for the gathering and recompense whenever He wills. He is not incapable of doing this, just as He was not incapable of creating them the first time.

(٣٠) وَمَآ أَصَٰبَكُم مِّن مُّصِيبَةٖ فَبِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَيَعۡفُواْ عَن كَثِيرٖ

Whatever difficulty afflicts you, O people, in yourselves and your wealth, it is because of the sins that your hands earned. Allah overlooks many of them and does not take you to task for them.

(٣١) وَمَآ أَنتُم بِمُعۡجِزِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَمَا لَكُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مِن وَلِيّٖ وَلَا نَصِيرٖ

You are not able to save yourselves from your Lord by running away when He wants to punish you. You do not have anyone besides Him to take charge of your affairs nor anyone to help you by removing the punishment if He intends it for you.

Points of Benefit:

The caller to Allah does not seek any reward from the people.
Expanding and restricting provision is subject to a divine wisdom that is hidden from many people.
Sins and disobedience are from the causes of calamities.
