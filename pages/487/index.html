(٣٢) وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِ ٱلۡجَوَارِ فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ كَٱلۡأَعۡلَٰمِ

Among Allah's signs that prove His omnipotence and oneness are the ships sailing the ocean like mountains in height and elevation.

(٣٣) إِن يَشَأۡ يُسۡكِنِ ٱلرِّيحَ فَيَظۡلَلۡنَ رَوَاكِدَ عَلَىٰ ظَهۡرِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّكُلِّ صَبَّارٖ شَكُورٍ

If Allah wished to make the wind that moves them still He would have done so, and (in that case) they would remain fixed on the ocean motionless. In that which has been mentioned about the creation of ships and the subjugation of the winds, there are clear signs of Allah's power for every person who is patient in difficulty and trials, and who is grateful for Allah's favors upon him.

(٣٤) أَوۡ يُوبِقۡهُنَّ بِمَا كَسَبُواْ وَيَعۡفُ عَن كَثِيرٖ

Or if He (may He be Exalted) willed to destroy those ships by sending stormy winds, He would have done so on account of the sins people earn. He overlooks many of the sins of His slaves and does not punish them for those sins.

(٣٥) وَيَعۡلَمَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُجَٰدِلُونَ فِيٓ ءَايَٰتِنَا مَا لَهُم مِّن مَّحِيصٖ

When those ships are destroyed by the stormy winds sent, those who dispute Allah's signs to refute them will know they cannot escape destruction. So, they will only call on Allah and leave those beside Him.

(٣٦) فَمَآ أُوتِيتُم مِّن شَيۡءٖ فَمَتَٰعُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَمَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرٞ وَأَبۡقَىٰ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ

The wealth, fame, and children you have been given, O people, is the enjoyment of this worldly life that will vanish and come to an end. The everlasting bliss is the bliss of Paradise that Allah has prepared for those who have faith in Allah and His messengers and rely on him alone in all their matters.

(٣٧) وَٱلَّذِينَ يَجۡتَنِبُونَ كَبَٰٓئِرَ ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡفَوَٰحِشَ وَإِذَا مَا غَضِبُواْ هُمۡ يَغۡفِرُونَ

[And for] those who distance themselves from major and vile sins and when they are angry at those who offend them by words or deeds, forgive their mistake and do not punish them for it. This pardon is a grace they offer when there is goodness and benefit in it.

(٣٨) وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَجَابُواْ لِرَبِّهِمۡ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَمۡرُهُمۡ شُورَىٰ بَيۡنَهُمۡ وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ

[And for] Those who respond to their Lord by doing what He instructed and leaving out what He prohibited and complete their ritual prayer in the most perfect manner; those who consult one another in matters of importance to them and who spend from what He has provided for them seeking Allah's pleasure;

(٣٩) وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَآ أَصَابَهُمُ ٱلۡبَغۡيُ هُمۡ يَنتَصِرُونَ

Those who, when afflicted by oppression, defend themselves in honor and respect for themselves if the oppressor is not worthy of pardon. This defense is justified, especially if there is no benefit in pardoning [the oppressor].

(٤٠) وَجَزَٰٓؤُاْ سَيِّئَةٖ سَيِّئَةٞ مِّثۡلُهَاۖ فَمَنۡ عَفَا وَأَصۡلَحَ فَأَجۡرُهُۥ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Whoever wants to take his right is allowed to do so but in equal measure without any excess or transgression. Whoever pardons the one who wrongs him, does not take him to task for that wrong, and sets right the relationship with his brother, then his reward is with Allah. He does not love those who do wrong by oppressing people in their selves, wealth, or honor. Rather, He detests them.

(٤١) وَلَمَنِ ٱنتَصَرَ بَعۡدَ ظُلۡمِهِۦ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ مَا عَلَيۡهِم مِّن سَبِيلٍ

Those who defend themselves will not be blamed as they have taken their right.

(٤٢) إِنَّمَا ٱلسَّبِيلُ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يَظۡلِمُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ وَيَبۡغُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Blame and retribution will only be for those who oppress people and commit sins on Earth. For such people, there will be a painful torment in the Hereafter.

(٤٣) وَلَمَن صَبَرَ وَغَفَرَ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ لَمِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ

As for those who are patient and overlook the harm caused to them by others, such patience will bring good for them and society. This is a praiseworthy thing, and only a person of great fortune is blessed with this.

(٤٤) وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن وَلِيّٖ مِّنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦۗ وَتَرَى ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ لَمَّا رَأَوُاْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ يَقُولُونَ هَلۡ إِلَىٰ مَرَدّٖ مِّن سَبِيلٖ

Those whom Allah does not bless with guidance and lets them stray from the truth will have no one to take charge of their affairs after Him. You will see those who wrong themselves by disbelief and sins when they see the punishment on the Day of Resurrection, longingly saying: If only we could return to the world and repent to Allah.

Points of Benefit:

Patience and gratitude cause one to be guided to consider the signs of Allah.
Consultation has a great status in Islam.
Punishing the oppressor with what is similar to his oppression is permissible, but it is better to pardon him.
