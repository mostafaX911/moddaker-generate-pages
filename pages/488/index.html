(٤٥) وَتَرَىٰهُمۡ يُعۡرَضُونَ عَلَيۡهَا خَٰشِعِينَ مِنَ ٱلذُّلِّ يَنظُرُونَ مِن طَرۡفٍ خَفِيّٖۗ وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَأَهۡلِيهِمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۗ أَلَآ إِنَّ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ فِي عَذَابٖ مُّقِيمٖ

You will see, O Messenger, these wrongdoers when they are exposed to the Fire, while they are humiliated and disgraced, looking at people surreptitiously due to their extreme fear of it. Those who had faith in Allah and His messengers will say: The true losers are those who lost themselves and their families on the Day of Resurrection because of the punishment they received from Allah. Indeed, those who wrong themselves by disbelief and sins will be in a lasting torment that will never end.

(٤٦) وَمَا كَانَ لَهُم مِّنۡ أَوۡلِيَآءَ يَنصُرُونَهُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن سَبِيلٍ

They will have no allies to help them by rescuing them from Allah's punishment on the Day of Resurrection. Those whom Allah does not bless with the truth and lets them go astray will never have any path that leads them to guidance to the truth.

(٤٧) ٱسۡتَجِيبُواْ لِرَبِّكُم مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَ يَوۡمٞ لَّا مَرَدَّ لَهُۥ مِنَ ٱللَّهِۚ مَا لَكُم مِّن مَّلۡجَإٖ يَوۡمَئِذٖ وَمَا لَكُم مِّن نَّكِيرٖ

Respond, O people, to your Lord by rushing to comply with His commands, avoid His prohibitions, and abandon procrastination before the Day of Resurrection comes, which cannot be pushed back when it arrives. You will have no place to take refuge, and you will not be able to deny the sins you have perpetrated in this world.

(٤٨) فَإِنۡ أَعۡرَضُواْ فَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَفِيظًاۖ إِنۡ عَلَيۡكَ إِلَّا ٱلۡبَلَٰغُۗ وَإِنَّآ إِذَآ أَذَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِنَّا رَحۡمَةٗ فَرِحَ بِهَاۖ وَإِن تُصِبۡهُمۡ سَيِّئَةُۢ بِمَا قَدَّمَتۡ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ فَإِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ كَفُورٞ

If they turn away from what you instruct them, then We have not sent you, O Messenger, as a guardian over them to watch their actions. Your duty is only to convey what you have been instructed to convey, and their reckoning is left to Allah. When We give a man a taste of Our mercy of riches, health, etc., he rejoices in it. But when a trial of difficulty befalls them on account of their sins, their nature is to be ungrateful for Allah's favors and become angry at what Allah had in His wisdom decreed.

(٤٩) لِّلَّهِ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ يَهَبُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ إِنَٰثٗا وَيَهَبُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ ٱلذُّكُورَ

To Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and the Earth. He creates whatever He wills and bestows whomever He wills with daughters and whomever He wills with sons, or grants whomever He wills with both sons and daughters and makes whoever He wills barren and childless. He knows what occurs and what will occur in the future. This is on account of the completeness of His knowledge and perfection of His wisdom. Nothing is hidden from Him; nothing is outside His ability.

(٥٠) أَوۡ يُزَوِّجُهُمۡ ذُكۡرَانٗا وَإِنَٰثٗاۖ وَيَجۡعَلُ مَن يَشَآءُ عَقِيمًاۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمٞ قَدِيرٞ

To Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and the Earth. He creates whatever He wills and bestows whomever He wills with daughters and whomever He wills with sons, or grants whomever He wills with both sons and daughters and makes whoever He wills barren and childless. He knows what occurs and what will occur in the future. This is on account of the completeness of His knowledge and perfection of His wisdom. Nothing is hidden from Him; nothing is outside His ability.

(٥١) ۞وَمَا كَانَ لِبَشَرٍ أَن يُكَلِّمَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا وَحۡيًا أَوۡ مِن وَرَآيِٕ حِجَابٍ أَوۡ يُرۡسِلَ رَسُولٗا فَيُوحِيَ بِإِذۡنِهِۦ مَا يَشَآءُۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيٌّ حَكِيمٞ

It is not correct for a human being to be spoken to by Allah except through revelation that He sends through inspiration or by another means, or by speaking directly to him such that he hears His speech but does not see Him, or by sending an angel as a messenger such as Gabriel, who then reveals to the human messenger with Allah's permission what Allah wishes to reveal. He (may He be Exalted) is high in His Being and His Attributes and is wise in His creation, decree, and laws.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation of hastening to comply with Allah's commands and avoid His prohibitions.
The Messenger is responsible for conveying the message, and the results are in Allah's Hand.
Giving males, females, or both together is based on Allah's knowledge of what is best for His slaves. There is no advantage for males over females.
Allah reveals to His Prophets in various ways for a wisdom that He, the Transcendent, knows.
