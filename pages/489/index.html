(٥٢) وَكَذَٰلِكَ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ رُوحٗا مِّنۡ أَمۡرِنَاۚ مَا كُنتَ تَدۡرِي مَا ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ وَلَا ٱلۡإِيمَٰنُ وَلَٰكِن جَعَلۡنَٰهُ نُورٗا نَّهۡدِي بِهِۦ مَن نَّشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَاۚ وَإِنَّكَ لَتَهۡدِيٓ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

Just as We sent revelation to the prophets before you, O Messenger, We revealed a Quran to you from Us. Prior to it, you did not know about the heavenly scriptures revealed to the messengers, and you did not know what faith is. But We revealed this Quran as a light by which We guide whomever of Our slaves We wish. You show people a straight path, which is the religion of Islam.

(٥٣) صِرَٰطِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَلَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ تَصِيرُ ٱلۡأُمُورُ

The path of Allah to Whom belongs whatever is in the heaven and whatever is in the earth. He created, owns, and controls all of it. Definitely, all matters return to Allah alone in their estimation and planning.

The Ornaments of Gold (Surah Az-Zukhruf)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Warning not to be tempted by this world's pleasures as it may lead to polytheism.

Explanation:

(١) حمٓ

Hā Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ

Allah swore by the Quran, which clearly explains the path that guides toward the truth.

(٣) إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰهُ قُرۡءَٰنًا عَرَبِيّٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ

We have revealed it as a Quran in the language of the Arabs in the hope that you, O people whose language it was revealed in, will comprehend and understand its meanings to transmit it to other nations.

(٤) وَإِنَّهُۥ فِيٓ أُمِّ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَدَيۡنَا لَعَلِيٌّ حَكِيمٌ

This Quran is in the Preserved Tablet, which is high, lofty, and contains wisdom. Its verses are wise in what they command and prohibit.

(٥) أَفَنَضۡرِبُ عَنكُمُ ٱلذِّكۡرَ صَفۡحًا أَن كُنتُمۡ قَوۡمٗا مُّسۡرِفِينَ

Should We leave off revealing the Quran unto you just because of the immensity of your polytheism and sins? We won't do that. Instead, We will do the opposite as mercy upon you.

(٦) وَكَمۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن نَّبِيّٖ فِي ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

How many a prophet did We send to the previous nations!

(٧) وَمَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن نَّبِيٍّ إِلَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

Whenever a prophet came from Allah to those previous nations, they would mock him.

(٨) فَأَهۡلَكۡنَآ أَشَدَّ مِنۡهُم بَطۡشٗا وَمَضَىٰ مَثَلُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

We destroyed those who were mightier than those nations, so We are not incapable of destroying those who are weaker than them. The description of the destruction of the previous nations has been mentioned in the Quran, such as that of 'Ād, Thamūd, the nation of Lot, and the people of Midian.

(٩) وَلَئِن سَأَلۡتَهُم مَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ لَيَقُولُنَّ خَلَقَهُنَّ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

O Messenger, if you ask these rejecting polytheists: Who created the heavens, and who created the Earth? They will say in response to your question: The Mighty, who none can overpower, and the Omniscient of everything, created them.

(١٠) ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مَهۡدٗا وَجَعَلَ لَكُمۡ فِيهَا سُبُلٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَهۡتَدُونَ

Allah is the One who made the Earth stable and firm for you. He made paths in it for you through its mountains and valleys in the hope that you will be guided by them in your travels.

Points of Benefit:

The revelation was called a spirit because of its importance in people's lives, so it is like the spirit in the body.
The guidance that the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم can do is directing people to the Truth, not making them accept it.
The polytheists singling out Allah in His Lordship will not benefit them on the Day of Resurrection.
