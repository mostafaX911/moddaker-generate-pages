(٢٨٣) ۞وَإِن كُنتُمۡ عَلَىٰ سَفَرٖ وَلَمۡ تَجِدُواْ كَاتِبٗا فَرِهَٰنٞ مَّقۡبُوضَةٞۖ فَإِنۡ أَمِنَ بَعۡضُكُم بَعۡضٗا فَلۡيُؤَدِّ ٱلَّذِي ٱؤۡتُمِنَ أَمَٰنَتَهُۥ وَلۡيَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ رَبَّهُۥۗ وَلَا تَكۡتُمُواْ ٱلشَّهَٰدَةَۚ وَمَن يَكۡتُمۡهَا فَإِنَّهُۥٓ ءَاثِمٞ قَلۡبُهُۥۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ عَلِيمٞ

If you are traveling and do not find anyone to write down the debt for you, the debtor can give property to the creditor as collateral for his right until the debt is paid. If you trust one another, writing the debt down, calling witnesses, or providing collateral is unnecessary. In such cases, the debt will be the debtor's responsibility, which he must fulfill and fear Allah regarding it by not denying it later. If he later denies it, the person present during the transaction must give evidence and may not hide it. If anyone should hide evidence, it indicates that his heart is sinful. Allah knows what you do; nothing is hidden from Him, and He will recompense you for your deeds accordingly.

(٢٨٤) لِّلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ وَإِن تُبۡدُواْ مَا فِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَوۡ تُخۡفُوهُ يُحَاسِبۡكُم بِهِ ٱللَّهُۖ فَيَغۡفِرُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيُعَذِّبُ مَن يَشَآءُۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ

To Allah belongs everything in the heavens and earth: He created, owns, and controls all of it. Whether you show or hide what is in your heart, Allah knows and will call you to account for it. Then, He will forgive whoever He wishes out of His grace and mercy and punish whoever He wishes out of His wisdom and justice. Allah is the Most Capable of everything.

(٢٨٥) ءَامَنَ ٱلرَّسُولُ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡهِ مِن رَّبِّهِۦ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَۚ كُلٌّ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَمَلَٰٓئِكَتِهِۦ وَكُتُبِهِۦ وَرُسُلِهِۦ لَا نُفَرِّقُ بَيۡنَ أَحَدٖ مِّن رُّسُلِهِۦۚ وَقَالُواْ سَمِعۡنَا وَأَطَعۡنَاۖ غُفۡرَانَكَ رَبَّنَا وَإِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

The Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم believes in everything that was revealed to him by his Lord, as do the believers. All of them believe in Allah, all His angels, all His books revealed to the various prophets, and all His messengers that He sent. They believe in all of them and say: We make no distinction between any of Allah's messengers. They say: We have heard Your instructions and prohibitions, and we obey You by doing what You have instructed us and abandoning what You have prohibited us from. We ask You to forgive us, O our Lord, for our return is to You alone in all our matters.

(٢٨٦) لَا يُكَلِّفُ ٱللَّهُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۚ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتۡ وَعَلَيۡهَا مَا ٱكۡتَسَبَتۡۗ رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذۡنَآ إِن نَّسِينَآ أَوۡ أَخۡطَأۡنَاۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحۡمِلۡ عَلَيۡنَآ إِصۡرٗا كَمَا حَمَلۡتَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِنَاۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلۡنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِۦۖ وَٱعۡفُ عَنَّا وَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَٱرۡحَمۡنَآۚ أَنتَ مَوۡلَىٰنَا فَٱنصُرۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Allah only burdens a soul with deeds that it can do. Allah's religion is based on ease and not difficulty. If any person does good, he will receive the reward for it in full. If any person does evil, he will be punished for his sin, and no one will carry it. The Messenger and the believers said: O Lord, do not punish us if we forget or make a mistake in doing something or saying something unintentionally. O Lord, do not burden us with what is difficult for us and which we cannot do as you had tasked those before us who were punished for their wrongs, such as the Jews. Please do not give us such instructions and prohibitions which are difficult for us and which we cannot fulfill. By Your grace, overlook our sins, forgive us, and have mercy on us. You are our guardian and helper, so give us victory over the disbelievers.

Points of Benefit:

The permissibility of taking collateral to secure rights in case the right cannot be documented, except when the parties involved trust each other.
The prohibition of concealing testimony and the sin of one who conceals it and does not testify.
The perfection of Allah's knowledge, His awareness of His creation, and His complete ability to account for them based on what they have earned from their deeds.
Affirmation of the Pillars of Faith and a clarification of its principles.
This religion is based on ease, and Allah has lifted hardship and difficulty from His servants. Allah does not burden them beyond their capacity, and He does not hold them accountable for what they cannot do.
