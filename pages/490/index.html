(١١) وَٱلَّذِي نَزَّلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءَۢ بِقَدَرٖ فَأَنشَرۡنَا بِهِۦ بَلۡدَةٗ مَّيۡتٗاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ تُخۡرَجُونَ

[He is also] the One who sends down enough water from the heavens that suffices you, your animals, and crops. With that water, We revive barren land in which there is no vegetation. Just as Allah revives that barren land with vegetation, He will revive you for the resurrection.

(١٢) وَٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَزۡوَٰجَ كُلَّهَا وَجَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡفُلۡكِ وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ مَا تَرۡكَبُونَ

He made all the different varieties, such as night and day, male and female, etc. He has made ships and livestock for you, which you ride on your travels, boarding the ships over the sea and riding the livestock over land.

(١٣) لِتَسۡتَوُۥاْ عَلَىٰ ظُهُورِهِۦ ثُمَّ تَذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَةَ رَبِّكُمۡ إِذَا ٱسۡتَوَيۡتُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ وَتَقُولُواْ سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُۥ مُقۡرِنِينَ

He created all that for you, in the hope that you settle yourselves on the backs of your mounts during your travels and then remember the blessing of your Lord over you, in that He has made them subservient to you when you mount them, and say with your tongues: The One who made these mounts subservient to us is pure and Exalted. We control them and would not have been able to do so if Allah had not made them subservient to us.

(١٤) وَإِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ

And indeed, we will certainly return to our Lord alone for reckoning and recompense.

(١٥) وَجَعَلُواْ لَهُۥ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ جُزۡءًاۚ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَكَفُورٞ مُّبِينٌ

The polytheists claimed that some of the creation are the offspring of the Creator (may He be Exalted) when they said: The angels are the daughters of Allah. Indeed, the one who makes these kinds of statements is very ungrateful and deviant.

(١٦) أَمِ ٱتَّخَذَ مِمَّا يَخۡلُقُ بَنَاتٖ وَأَصۡفَىٰكُم بِٱلۡبَنِينَ

O polytheists, do you say Allah has chosen daughters for Himself from among the things He creates while He has privileged you with male offspring?! What an absurd distribution this is, which you claim?!

(١٧) وَإِذَا بُشِّرَ أَحَدُهُم بِمَا ضَرَبَ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ مَثَلٗا ظَلَّ وَجۡهُهُۥ مُسۡوَدّٗا وَهُوَ كَظِيمٌ

When one of them is given the good news of the birth of a female whom he attributes to his Lord, his face becomes gloomy out of severe grief and sadness, and he himself becomes full of anger. So how can he attribute to his Lord the very thing that he himself is saddened by when given the good news of it?

(١٨) أَوَ مَن يُنَشَّؤُاْ فِي ٱلۡحِلۡيَةِ وَهُوَ فِي ٱلۡخِصَامِ غَيۡرُ مُبِينٖ

Do they attribute to their Lord the one who is brought up in adornment and is unclear in speech when arguing due to its femininity?

(١٩) وَجَعَلُواْ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةَ ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عِبَٰدُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ إِنَٰثًاۚ أَشَهِدُواْ خَلۡقَهُمۡۚ سَتُكۡتَبُ شَهَٰدَتُهُمۡ وَيُسۡ‍َٔلُونَ

They also regard the angels as feminine while they are, in reality, the slaves of The Merciful. Were they present at the time Allah created them so that they would know that they are females?! The angels will record this statement of theirs, and they will be questioned regarding it on the Day of Resurrection. Then, they will be punished for it because of their lies.

(٢٠) وَقَالُواْ لَوۡ شَآءَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ مَا عَبَدۡنَٰهُمۗ مَّا لَهُم بِذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عِلۡمٍۖ إِنۡ هُمۡ إِلَّا يَخۡرُصُونَ

And they say, using divine decree as evidence: If Allah had willed for us not to worship the angels, we would not have worshipped them. So because He willed for it to happen, it proves He was happy with it. They make this statement out of ignorance; they are only lying.

(٢١) أَمۡ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمۡ كِتَٰبٗا مِّن قَبۡلِهِۦ فَهُم بِهِۦ مُسۡتَمۡسِكُونَ

Or, have I given these polytheists a book before the Quran that allows them to worship other than Allah, which they are holding fast to and using as evidence?

(٢٢) بَلۡ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا وَجَدۡنَآ ءَابَآءَنَا عَلَىٰٓ أُمَّةٖ وَإِنَّا عَلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِهِم مُّهۡتَدُونَ

No! That has not happened. Rather, they use their blind following as evidence, saying: Indeed, we found our forefathers before us upon a belief, and they used to worship idols. We are continuing in their footsteps in worshipping them.

Points of Benefit:

Every blessing requires gratitude.
The injustice of the polytheists in how they view their Lord by ascribing daughters to Him while they hate that for themselves.
Invalidating using the divine pre-decree as an excuse for sins.
Observation is one of the grounds to establish the truth.
