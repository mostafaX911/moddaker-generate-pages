(٢٣) وَكَذَٰلِكَ مَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ فِي قَرۡيَةٖ مِّن نَّذِيرٍ إِلَّا قَالَ مُتۡرَفُوهَآ إِنَّا وَجَدۡنَآ ءَابَآءَنَا عَلَىٰٓ أُمَّةٖ وَإِنَّا عَلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِهِم مُّقۡتَدُونَ

And just as these people have lied and used the blind following of their forefathers as evidence, We did not send any messenger to any community before you, O Messenger, except that those living in luxury in that village said: Indeed, we found our forefathers upon a belief, and we are following in their footsteps. So your nation is not any different in that regard.

(٢٤) ۞قَٰلَ أَوَلَوۡ جِئۡتُكُم بِأَهۡدَىٰ مِمَّا وَجَدتُّمۡ عَلَيۡهِ ءَابَآءَكُمۡۖ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا بِمَآ أُرۡسِلۡتُم بِهِۦ كَٰفِرُونَ

Their messenger asked them: Will you follow your forefathers even if I brought you something that is better than the belief they were upon? They said: Indeed, we reject whatever you and the messengers before you have been sent with.

(٢٥) فَٱنتَقَمۡنَا مِنۡهُمۡۖ فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

We took retribution from the nations before you that rejected the messengers and destroyed them. So ponder over how the outcome of those who rejected their messengers was because their outcome was painful.

(٢٦) وَإِذۡ قَالَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمُ لِأَبِيهِ وَقَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِنَّنِي بَرَآءٞ مِّمَّا تَعۡبُدُونَ

Remember, O Messenger, when Abraham told his father and his people: Indeed, I am free from the idols you worship instead of Allah.

(٢٧) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَنِي فَإِنَّهُۥ سَيَهۡدِينِ

Except for Allah, who created me, because He will soon guide me to follow His true religion, which will benefit me.

(٢٨) وَجَعَلَهَا كَلِمَةَۢ بَاقِيَةٗ فِي عَقِبِهِۦ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ

Abraham made this monotheistic statement of his (there is none worthy of worship besides Allah) remain in his progeny after him so that some of them would continue worshiping Allah alone and not attribute any partners with Him, hoping that they turn to Allah having repented from ascribing partners with Him and having repented from sins.

(٢٩) بَلۡ مَتَّعۡتُ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ وَءَابَآءَهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَرَسُولٞ مُّبِينٞ

I did not hasten the destruction upon these rejecting polytheists. Rather, I let them enjoy themselves by letting them remain in this world, and I let their forefathers do the same until the Quran and a clear Messenger, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, came to them.

(٣٠) وَلَمَّا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡحَقُّ قَالُواْ هَٰذَا سِحۡرٞ وَإِنَّا بِهِۦ كَٰفِرُونَ

And when this Quran came to them, which was the undoubted truth, they said: This is sorcery which Muhammad intends to bewitch us with. We reject it and will never believe in it.

(٣١) وَقَالُواْ لَوۡلَا نُزِّلَ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ عَلَىٰ رَجُلٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡقَرۡيَتَيۡنِ عَظِيمٍ

The rejecting polytheists say: Why did Allah not reveal this Quran upon one of the two great men of Mecca or Taif instead of revealing it upon Muhammad, the impoverished orphan?

(٣٢) أَهُمۡ يَقۡسِمُونَ رَحۡمَتَ رَبِّكَۚ نَحۡنُ قَسَمۡنَا بَيۡنَهُم مَّعِيشَتَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَرَفَعۡنَا بَعۡضَهُمۡ فَوۡقَ بَعۡضٖ دَرَجَٰتٖ لِّيَتَّخِذَ بَعۡضُهُم بَعۡضٗا سُخۡرِيّٗاۗ وَرَحۡمَتُ رَبِّكَ خَيۡرٞ مِّمَّا يَجۡمَعُونَ

O Messenger, do they distribute the mercy of your Lord, so they can give it to and prevent from it whomsoever they wish, or does Allah do that? We distributed their provisions between them in this world, making some rich and some poor so that they are subservient to each other. The mercy of your Lord to His slaves in the Afterlife is better than the temporary, mortal things of this world that these people hoard.

(٣٣) وَلَوۡلَآ أَن يَكُونَ ٱلنَّاسُ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ لَّجَعَلۡنَا لِمَن يَكۡفُرُ بِٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ لِبُيُوتِهِمۡ سُقُفٗا مِّن فِضَّةٖ وَمَعَارِجَ عَلَيۡهَا يَظۡهَرُونَ

Were it not that humanity would become of one disbelieving creed, We would have made in the houses of those who disbelieved in Allah roofs of silver and silver stairs upon which they could climb.

Points of Benefit:

Imitation was one of the reasons for previous nations being misguided.
It is essential to disown disbelief and its people.
The division of provision is subject to Allah's wisdom.
The insignificance of the worldly life according to Allah, for if he considered it to weigh as much as a mosquito's wing, He would not give the disbeliever a drink of water from it.
