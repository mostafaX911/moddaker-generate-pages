(٣٤) وَلِبُيُوتِهِمۡ أَبۡوَٰبٗا وَسُرُرًا عَلَيۡهَا يَتَّكِ‍ُٔونَ

And We would make doors of silver for their houses and give them elevated seats of silver to recline on as a trial and to lead them into destruction gradually.

(٣٥) وَزُخۡرُفٗاۚ وَإِن كُلُّ ذَٰلِكَ لَمَّا مَتَٰعُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ عِندَ رَبِّكَ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ

We would also give them gold. All that is only the temporary enjoyment of this world; its benefit is little because of its ultimate extinction. However, O Messenger, the bounties of the Afterlife are better according to your Lord for those who are mindful of Him by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

(٣٦) وَمَن يَعۡشُ عَن ذِكۡرِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ نُقَيِّضۡ لَهُۥ شَيۡطَٰنٗا فَهُوَ لَهُۥ قَرِينٞ

Whoever does not look into the Quran, turning away from it, will be punished by a devil set upon him, never leaving him and tempting him more and more.

(٣٧) وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَيَصُدُّونَهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَيَحۡسَبُونَ أَنَّهُم مُّهۡتَدُونَ

Indeed, these devil companions who have been set upon those who turn away from the Quran, prevent them from the religion of Allah. They are unable to fulfill His commands and refrain from things He has not allowed; they think that they are guided to the truth, and so they do not repent from their deviance.

(٣٨) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَنَا قَالَ يَٰلَيۡتَ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكَ بُعۡدَ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقَيۡنِ فَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡقَرِينُ

Until the one who turns away from the remembrance of Allah comes to Us on the Day of Resurrection and says longingly: O companion! If only there had been the distance of the east and west between you and me! You were such a disgraceful companion!

(٣٩) وَلَن يَنفَعَكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ إِذ ظَّلَمۡتُمۡ أَنَّكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ مُشۡتَرِكُونَ

Allah will say to the disbelievers on the Day of Resurrection: After you have oppressed yourselves by committing polytheism and sins, your sharing the punishment will not benefit you today because your partners will not bear anything from your torment.

(٤٠) أَفَأَنتَ تُسۡمِعُ ٱلصُّمَّ أَوۡ تَهۡدِي ٱلۡعُمۡيَ وَمَن كَانَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ

Indeed, these people are deaf from hearing the truth and blind from seeing it. So, are you, O Messenger, capable of making the deaf hear, guiding the blind, or guiding the one clearly astray from the straight path?

(٤١) فَإِمَّا نَذۡهَبَنَّ بِكَ فَإِنَّا مِنۡهُم مُّنتَقِمُونَ

If We were to take you away by causing you to die before We torment them, We would take retribution from them by punishing them in this world and the next.

(٤٢) أَوۡ نُرِيَنَّكَ ٱلَّذِي وَعَدۡنَٰهُمۡ فَإِنَّا عَلَيۡهِم مُّقۡتَدِرُونَ

Or if We were to show you some of the punishment We have prepared for them, indeed, We possess the power over them, and they cannot overpower Us in the slightest.

(٤٣) فَٱسۡتَمۡسِكۡ بِٱلَّذِيٓ أُوحِيَ إِلَيۡكَۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

So, O Messenger, hold fast to whatever your Lord has revealed to you and practice it; you are on the path of truth in which there is no uncertainty.

(٤٤) وَإِنَّهُۥ لَذِكۡرٞ لَّكَ وَلِقَوۡمِكَۖ وَسَوۡفَ تُسۡ‍َٔلُونَ

And indeed, this Quran is an honor for you and your nation. On the Day of Resurrection, you will be questioned regarding having faith in it, following its guidance, and inviting to it.

(٤٥) وَسۡ‍َٔلۡ مَنۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ مِن رُّسُلِنَآ أَجَعَلۡنَا مِن دُونِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ءَالِهَةٗ يُعۡبَدُونَ

O Messenger, ask those messengers We sent before you: Did Allah make deities to be worshipped instead of the Merciful?

(٤٦) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مُوسَىٰ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَمَلَإِيْهِۦ فَقَالَ إِنِّي رَسُولُ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Verily, We sent Moses with Our signs to Pharaoh and the leaders of his people. He told them: Indeed, I am the Messenger of the Lord of all creation.

(٤٧) فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُم بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآ إِذَا هُم مِّنۡهَا يَضۡحَكُونَ

Then, when he brought My signs to them, they began to laugh at them mockingly.

Points of Benefit:

The danger of turning away from the Quran.
The Quran is an honor for the Messenger of Allah صلى الله عليه وسلم and His nation.
All the messages agreed upon the rejection of polytheism.
Mocking the Truth is one of the characteristics of the disbelievers.
