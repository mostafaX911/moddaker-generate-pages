(٤٨) وَمَا نُرِيهِم مِّنۡ ءَايَةٍ إِلَّا هِيَ أَكۡبَرُ مِنۡ أُخۡتِهَاۖ وَأَخَذۡنَٰهُم بِٱلۡعَذَابِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ

We showed Pharaoh and his leaders proof after proof to establish the authenticity of Moses' (peace be upon him) message; each proof was greater than the previous one. I seized them with the torment in this world in the hope that they turn away from their disbelief, but to no avail.

(٤٩) وَقَالُواْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَ ٱلسَّاحِرُ ٱدۡعُ لَنَا رَبَّكَ بِمَا عَهِدَ عِندَكَ إِنَّنَا لَمُهۡتَدُونَ

So when some of the punishment came upon them, they said to Moses (peace be upon him): O sorcerer! Pray to your Lord for us, asking Him to remove this punishment just as He said He would if we believed. If it is removed from us, we will be guided to the truth.

(٥٠) فَلَمَّا كَشَفۡنَا عَنۡهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابَ إِذَا هُمۡ يَنكُثُونَ

Then, when We removed the punishment from them, they began breaking their covenant and did not fulfill it.

(٥١) وَنَادَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنُ فِي قَوۡمِهِۦ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ أَلَيۡسَ لِي مُلۡكُ مِصۡرَ وَهَٰذِهِ ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِيٓۚ أَفَلَا تُبۡصِرُونَ

Pharaoh called out among his people, bragging about his kingdom: O my people! Do I not own the country of Egypt and these rivers that flow beneath my palaces? Do you not see my kingdom and recognize my greatness?

(٥٢) أَمۡ أَنَا۠ خَيۡرٞ مِّنۡ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي هُوَ مَهِينٞ وَلَا يَكَادُ يُبِينُ

And Pharaoh said: Therefore, I am better than Moses, the weak fugitive who cannot even speak well.

(٥٣) فَلَوۡلَآ أُلۡقِيَ عَلَيۡهِ أَسۡوِرَةٞ مِّن ذَهَبٍ أَوۡ جَآءَ مَعَهُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ مُقۡتَرِنِينَ

Why did Allah who sent him not cast down golden bracelets on him so that it becomes clear that he is His Messenger, or why did the angels not come with him, following one another?

(٥٤) فَٱسۡتَخَفَّ قَوۡمَهُۥ فَأَطَاعُوهُۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمٗا فَٰسِقِينَ

So Pharaoh lured his people, and they followed him in deviance. Indeed, they were a nation that went away from the obedience of Allah.

(٥٥) فَلَمَّآ ءَاسَفُونَا ٱنتَقَمۡنَا مِنۡهُمۡ فَأَغۡرَقۡنَٰهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

Then, when they angered Us by continuing upon disbelief, We took retribution from them and drowned them all.

(٥٦) فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ سَلَفٗا وَمَثَلٗا لِّلۡأٓخِرِينَ

So, We made Pharaoh and his people a precursor for the people and the disbelievers from your nation who would follow in their footsteps. We also made them a lesson for those who take heed so they do not do as they did and then are afflicted with what they were afflicted with.

(٥٧) ۞وَلَمَّا ضُرِبَ ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ مَثَلًا إِذَا قَوۡمُكَ مِنۡهُ يَصِدُّونَ

When the polytheists thought that Jesus, whom the Christians worshipped, was included in the statement of Allah, the Exalted: {Indeed, you [disbelievers] and what you worship other than Allah is the firewood of Hell. You will be coming to [enter] it} [Al-Anbiya:98], and Allah had prohibited worshipping him just like He had prohibited the worship of idols; your people, O Messenger, began persisting in argument, saying: We are pleased that our idols are of the same status as Jesus. So Allah revealed, refuting them: {Indeed, those for whom the best [reward] has preceded from Me - they are from it far removed} [Surah Al Anbiya: 101].

(٥٨) وَقَالُوٓاْ ءَأَٰلِهَتُنَا خَيۡرٌ أَمۡ هُوَۚ مَا ضَرَبُوهُ لَكَ إِلَّا جَدَلَۢاۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ قَوۡمٌ خَصِمُونَ

And they say: Are our deities better, or is Jesus better? Ibn Al-Ziba'ra and others did not make this comparison out of the love to attain truth, but rather out of love of argumentation. They are a nation with a quarrelsome nature.

(٥٩) إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا عَبۡدٌ أَنۡعَمۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ مَثَلٗا لِّبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ

Jesus, the son of Mary, is nothing but a slave from Allah's slaves whom He favored with messengership. He made him an example for the Israelites to use as proof of Allah's ability since He created him without a father, just like He created Adam without parents.

(٦٠) وَلَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَجَعَلۡنَا مِنكُم مَّلَٰٓئِكَةٗ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَخۡلُفُونَ

O children of Adam, if We had wished to destroy you, We would have and would have made the angels succeed you on Earth in your place. They would worship Allah and not ascribe partners with Him at all.

Points of Benefit:

Breaking covenants is a trait of the disbelievers.
The wrongdoer is simple-minded. He is belittled by whoever wants to belittle him.
Allah's anger causes loss.
The people of misguidance seek to distort the meanings of the Quranic texts according to their desires.
