(٦١) وَإِنَّهُۥ لَعِلۡمٞ لِّلسَّاعَةِ فَلَا تَمۡتَرُنَّ بِهَا وَٱتَّبِعُونِۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٞ مُّسۡتَقِيمٞ

Indeed, When Jesus descends towards the end of time, he will be a sign from the major signs of the Hour. So do not doubt the Hour's occurrence and follow me in whatever I have brought to you from Allah. What I have brought is the straight path in which there is no deviance.

(٦٢) وَلَا يَصُدَّنَّكُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمۡ عَدُوّٞ مُّبِينٞ

And do not let the Devil turn you away from the straight path through his lure and deception. Indeed, he is an enemy whose enmity is clear.

(٦٣) وَلَمَّا جَآءَ عِيسَىٰ بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ قَالَ قَدۡ جِئۡتُكُم بِٱلۡحِكۡمَةِ وَلِأُبَيِّنَ لَكُم بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي تَخۡتَلِفُونَ فِيهِۖ فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ

After Jesus (peace be upon him) brought clear signs of his messengership to his people, he said to them: I have brought wisdom to you from Allah so that I can make clear to you some matters of your religion you differ in. So be mindful of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, and obey me in whatever I command you with and prohibit you from.

(٦٤) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ رَبِّي وَرَبُّكُمۡ فَٱعۡبُدُوهُۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٞ مُّسۡتَقِيمٞ

Indeed, Allah is my Lord and your Lord; we have no Lord other than Him, so devote your worship to Him alone. This monotheism is the straight path in which there is no deviance.

(٦٥) فَٱخۡتَلَفَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِهِمۡۖ فَوَيۡلٞ لِّلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ مِنۡ عَذَابِ يَوۡمٍ أَلِيمٍ

Then, groups from amongst the Christians disagreed regarding Jesus. Some of them say he is a god, some say he is the son of God, and others say he and his mother are both gods. So woe be to those who oppress themselves by attributing divinity to Jesus, or sonship, or saying that he is one of the Trinity! Woe be to them from a painful punishment that awaits them on the Day of Resurrection.

(٦٦) هَلۡ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّا ٱلسَّاعَةَ أَن تَأۡتِيَهُم بَغۡتَةٗ وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

These disputing parties in regards to Jesus await nothing except the Hour, which will come to them suddenly, while they do not even perceive its arrival. If it comes to them while they are upon disbelief, then their abode is a painful torment.

(٦٧) ٱلۡأَخِلَّآءُ يَوۡمَئِذِۢ بَعۡضُهُمۡ لِبَعۡضٍ عَدُوٌّ إِلَّا ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Those who befriend each other upon disbelief and deviance will be enemies to one another on the Day of Resurrection, except those who are mindful of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. Their friendship is everlasting and will never break.

(٦٨) يَٰعِبَادِ لَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ وَلَآ أَنتُمۡ تَحۡزَنُونَ

Allah will say to them: O, My slaves! There is no fear upon you today in what you will face, nor will you grieve over what you lost out on from the good of the worldly life.

(٦٩) ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا وَكَانُواْ مُسۡلِمِينَ

Those who believed in the Quran that was sent down upon their Messenger and submitted to it, fulfilling its commands and refraining from what it did not allow.

(٧٠) ٱدۡخُلُواْ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ أَنتُمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجُكُمۡ تُحۡبَرُونَ

Enter Paradise; you and those who have faith like you. You will be pleased with the everlasting blessings you will encounter, which shall never finish nor expire.

(٧١) يُطَافُ عَلَيۡهِم بِصِحَافٖ مِّن ذَهَبٖ وَأَكۡوَابٖۖ وَفِيهَا مَا تَشۡتَهِيهِ ٱلۡأَنفُسُ وَتَلَذُّ ٱلۡأَعۡيُنُۖ وَأَنتُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

Their slaves will roam among them with golden plates and handleless cups. In Paradise, there is whatever the hearts desire and whatever delights the eyes. You will reside in it eternally.

(٧٢) وَتِلۡكَ ٱلۡجَنَّةُ ٱلَّتِيٓ أُورِثۡتُمُوهَا بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

That Paradise I have described for you is the one Allah made you inherit from His grace due to your deeds.

(٧٣) لَكُمۡ فِيهَا فَٰكِهَةٞ كَثِيرَةٞ مِّنۡهَا تَأۡكُلُونَ

There are many fruits in it for you that shall never finish, which you can eat from.

Points of Benefit:

The coming down of Jesus is one of the major signs of the Hour.
Friendship based on wrongdoing will end on the Day of Resurrection, but the friendship of the heedful will continue.
Allah gives good news to the believers and reassures them of what they left behind in this life and what awaits them in the Hereafter.
