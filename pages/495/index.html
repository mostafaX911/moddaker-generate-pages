Upon mentioning the reward of the heedful, Allah mentions the recompense awaiting their opposite (i.e., the wrongdoers). He states:

(٧٤) إِنَّ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ فِي عَذَابِ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدُونَ

Indeed, the criminals who commit disbelief and sins will be in the torment of Hell on the Day of Resurrection, wherein they shall remain forever.

(٧٥) لَا يُفَتَّرُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَهُمۡ فِيهِ مُبۡلِسُونَ

The punishment will not be lessened for them. They will lose all hope in the mercy of Allah in it.

(٧٦) وَمَا ظَلَمۡنَٰهُمۡ وَلَٰكِن كَانُواْ هُمُ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

And We did not oppress them by entering them into the Fire; rather, they oppressed themselves by disbelieving.

(٧٧) وَنَادَوۡاْ يَٰمَٰلِكُ لِيَقۡضِ عَلَيۡنَا رَبُّكَۖ قَالَ إِنَّكُم مَّٰكِثُونَ

They will call out to Malik, the gatekeeper of the Fire, saying: O Malik! Our Lord should make us die so that we are relieved of this punishment! Malik will answer them: You are to remain forever in the punishment. You will not die, nor will the punishment stop.

(٧٨) لَقَدۡ جِئۡنَٰكُم بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَكُمۡ لِلۡحَقِّ كَٰرِهُونَ

Indeed, I brought the undisputed truth to you in this world, but the majority of you disliked the truth.

(٧٩) أَمۡ أَبۡرَمُوٓاْ أَمۡرٗا فَإِنَّا مُبۡرِمُونَ

So, if they plot against the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم and hatch an evil plan against him, We will, indeed, hatch a plan against them that will be greater than theirs.

(٨٠) أَمۡ يَحۡسَبُونَ أَنَّا لَا نَسۡمَعُ سِرَّهُمۡ وَنَجۡوَىٰهُمۚ بَلَىٰ وَرُسُلُنَا لَدَيۡهِمۡ يَكۡتُبُونَ

Or do they think that We do not hear the secrets they hide within their hearts or whisper to one another? Nay! We hear them all, and the angels with them write everything they do.

(٨١) قُلۡ إِن كَانَ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ وَلَدٞ فَأَنَا۠ أَوَّلُ ٱلۡعَٰبِدِينَ

Say, O Messenger, to those who attribute daughters to Allah (Allah is Transcendent and far beyond what they say): Allah does not have a child. He is free and sanctified from that. I am the first to worship Him (the Exalted) and declare Him free from anything not befitting Him.

(٨٢) سُبۡحَٰنَ رَبِّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ

The Lord of the heavens, the Earth, and the Throne is pure of the statements of these polytheists who ascribe partners, wives, and children to Him.

(٨٣) فَذَرۡهُمۡ يَخُوضُواْ وَيَلۡعَبُواْ حَتَّىٰ يُلَٰقُواْ يَوۡمَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي يُوعَدُونَ

So, O Messenger, leave them to continue in whatever falsehood they are engrossed in and let them enjoy themselves until they meet the day they have been promised, the Day of Resurrection.

(٨٤) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ إِلَٰهٞ وَفِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَٰهٞۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

He (may He be Exalted) is the true deity in the heavens and the true deity on Earth. He is the most Wise in His creation, decree, and management. He is Omniscient of the conditions of His slaves; nothing is hidden from Him.

(٨٥) وَتَبَارَكَ ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا وَعِندَهُۥ عِلۡمُ ٱلسَّاعَةِ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

Allah's goodness and blessings continue to increase (may He be Exalted). To Him alone belongs the kingdom of the heavens and the Earth and whatever lies between them. And with Him alone is the knowledge of the Hour in which the Day of Resurrection will occur; no one else knows it. You will return to Him alone in the Hereafter for accountability and requital.

(٨٦) وَلَا يَمۡلِكُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِ ٱلشَّفَٰعَةَ إِلَّا مَن شَهِدَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُونَ

Those that the polytheists worship instead of Allah are not able to intercede in front of Allah, except those that testify that there is none worthy of worship except Allah while understanding their testimony, e.g., Jesus, Ezra, and the angels.

(٨٧) وَلَئِن سَأَلۡتَهُم مَّنۡ خَلَقَهُمۡ لَيَقُولُنَّ ٱللَّهُۖ فَأَنَّىٰ يُؤۡفَكُونَ

If you were to ask them: Who created you? They would definitely say: Allah created us. So how can they then turn away from His worship after this acknowledgment?!

(٨٨) وَقِيلِهِۦ يَٰرَبِّ إِنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ قَوۡمٞ لَّا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

With Allah (may He be Exalted) is the knowledge of the complaint of His Messenger when they rejected him and his statement regarding them: O my Lord! Indeed, these people are a nation that does not believe in what you sent me to convey to them.

(٨٩) فَٱصۡفَحۡ عَنۡهُمۡ وَقُلۡ سَلَٰمٞۚ فَسَوۡفَ يَعۡلَمُونَ

So ignore them and say to them whatever repels their evil (this was in Mecca) they will soon realize what punishment they will face.

Points of Benefit:

Disliking the Truth is extremely dangerous.
The plots of the disbelievers always backfire, even if after a while.
The more the slave knows his Lord, the more he trusts Him and submits to His legislation.
Knowledge of the Hour is exclusively for Allah.
