The Smoke (Surah Ad-Dukhan)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Threatening the polytheists by detailing the punishment that awaits them imminently and in the future.

Explanation:

(١) حمٓ

Hā Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ

Allah swore by the Quran, which clearly explains the path that guides toward the truth.

(٣) إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٍۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا مُنذِرِينَ

Indeed, We revealed the Quran on the Night of Decree; it is a night of much goodness. Indeed, We warn through this Quran.

(٤) فِيهَا يُفۡرَقُ كُلُّ أَمۡرٍ حَكِيمٍ

On this night, every precise matter related to provisions, lifespans, etc., is decided which Allah will make happen that year.

(٥) أَمۡرٗا مِّنۡ عِندِنَآۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا مُرۡسِلِينَ

We decide every precise matter of Ours; indeed, We are the One who sends the messengers.

(٦) رَحۡمَةٗ مِّن رَّبِّكَۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

We send the messengers as a mercy from your Lord, O Messenger, for those to whom they are sent. Indeed, He (may He be Exalted) is All-Hearing of His slaves' words and Omniscient of their actions and intentions; nothing is hidden from Him.

(٧) رَبِّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآۖ إِن كُنتُم مُّوقِنِينَ

He is the Lord of the heavens, the Earth, and whatever lies between them. If you are convinced of that, then have faith in My Messenger.

(٨) لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُۖ رَبُّكُمۡ وَرَبُّ ءَابَآئِكُمُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

Nothing is worthy of worship besides Him. He gives life and causes death; no one does that except Him. He is your Lord and the Lord of your forefathers before you.

(٩) بَلۡ هُمۡ فِي شَكّٖ يَلۡعَبُونَ

These polytheists are not convinced of that; rather, they are in doubt about it and distract themselves from it with the falsehood they are in.

(١٠) فَٱرۡتَقِبۡ يَوۡمَ تَأۡتِي ٱلسَّمَآءُ بِدُخَانٖ مُّبِينٖ

So wait, O Messenger, the punishment of your people that will come soon, on a day when the sky will bring forth an apparent smoke, which they will see with their eyes due to severe hunger.

(١١) يَغۡشَى ٱلنَّاسَۖ هَٰذَا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

It will encompass your people and be said to them: This punishment that has afflicted you is a painful torment.

(١٢) رَّبَّنَا ٱكۡشِفۡ عَنَّا ٱلۡعَذَابَ إِنَّا مُؤۡمِنُونَ

They will then beg their Lord, asking him, O our Lord! Turn the punishment that you sent upon us away from us. If you turn it away from us, we will believe in You and Your Messenger.

(١٣) أَنَّىٰ لَهُمُ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ وَقَدۡ جَآءَهُمۡ رَسُولٞ مُّبِينٞ

How can they take heed and repent to their Lord now when a Messenger came to them before with a clear message, and they knew of his truthfulness and honesty?!

(١٤) ثُمَّ تَوَلَّوۡاْ عَنۡهُ وَقَالُواْ مُعَلَّمٞ مَّجۡنُونٌ

But they turned away from believing in him and said about him: He is a pupil taught by others; he is not a messenger. They also said: He is a madman.

(١٥) إِنَّا كَاشِفُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابِ قَلِيلًاۚ إِنَّكُمۡ عَآئِدُونَ

Indeed, when We turn the punishment away from you for a short while, you will return to your disbelief and rejection.

(١٦) يَوۡمَ نَبۡطِشُ ٱلۡبَطۡشَةَ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰٓ إِنَّا مُنتَقِمُونَ

Wait for them, O Messenger, on the day when We will seize the disbelievers of your people with the greatest seizing on the day of Badr. We will take retribution from them for their disbelief in Allah and for rejecting His messengers.

(١٧) ۞وَلَقَدۡ فَتَنَّا قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَجَآءَهُمۡ رَسُولٞ كَرِيمٌ

And verily I tested the people of Pharaoh before them, and their noble Messenger came to them from Allah calling them towards His Oneness and worship. He was Moses (peace be upon him).

(١٨) أَنۡ أَدُّوٓاْ إِلَيَّ عِبَادَ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنِّي لَكُمۡ رَسُولٌ أَمِينٞ

Moses said to Pharaoh and his people: Leave the Israelites to me because they are the slaves of Allah; you have no right to take them as slaves. Indeed, I am a messenger to you from Allah. I am trustworthy in conveying what He has commanded me to convey; I will not take anything away from it nor add anything to it.

Points of Benefit:

Sending down the Quran on the Night of Decree, which contains abundant good, shows it is highly valued.
Sending Messengers and revealing the Quran are manifestations of Allah's mercy to His slaves.
The messages of the Prophets set free those who are oppressed from the grip of the arrogant.
