(١٩) وَأَن لَّا تَعۡلُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۖ إِنِّيٓ ءَاتِيكُم بِسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٖ

And do not show arrogance to Allah by leaving His worship and assuming superiority over His slaves. Indeed, I will bring to you a clear proof.

(٢٠) وَإِنِّي عُذۡتُ بِرَبِّي وَرَبِّكُمۡ أَن تَرۡجُمُونِ

And I have sought protection in my Lord and your Lord from you killing me by stoning.

(٢١) وَإِن لَّمۡ تُؤۡمِنُواْ لِي فَٱعۡتَزِلُونِ

If you do not believe in whatever I have brought, then leave me alone and do not approach me to harm me.

(٢٢) فَدَعَا رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ قَوۡمٞ مُّجۡرِمُونَ

Then Moses supplicated to his Lord: This nation, i.e., Pharaoh and his people, is a criminal nation that deserves a swift punishment.

(٢٣) فَأَسۡرِ بِعِبَادِي لَيۡلًا إِنَّكُم مُّتَّبَعُونَ

So Allah commanded Moses to take his people away at night and informed him that Pharaoh and his people would pursue them.

(٢٤) وَٱتۡرُكِ ٱلۡبَحۡرَ رَهۡوًاۖ إِنَّهُمۡ جُندٞ مُّغۡرَقُونَ

He also commanded him that when they all cross the sea, they leave it standing as it was because Pharaoh and his people were to be destroyed by drowning in it.

(٢٥) كَمۡ تَرَكُواْ مِن جَنَّٰتٖ وَعُيُونٖ

How many gardens and flowing springs did Pharaoh and his people leave behind?!

(٢٦) وَزُرُوعٖ وَمَقَامٖ كَرِيمٖ

And how many farms and beautiful places did they leave behind?!

(٢٧) وَنَعۡمَةٖ كَانُواْ فِيهَا فَٰكِهِينَ

And how many lives of luxuries they used to live did they leave behind!

(٢٨) كَذَٰلِكَۖ وَأَوۡرَثۡنَٰهَا قَوۡمًا ءَاخَرِينَ

Whatever has been described to you happened to them, and we made another nation, i.e., the Israelites inherit their gardens, springs, farms, and structures.

(٢٩) فَمَا بَكَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمَا كَانُواْ مُنظَرِينَ

Neither the heavens nor the Earth cried over Pharaoh and his people when they drowned, nor were they given respite to repent.

(٣٠) وَلَقَدۡ نَجَّيۡنَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ مِنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡمُهِينِ

And verily, We saved the Israelites from the humiliating punishment as Pharaoh and his people would kill their children and spare their women.

(٣١) مِن فِرۡعَوۡنَۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ عَالِيٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ

We saved them from the punishment of Pharaoh. Indeed, he was an arrogant one from those who transgressed the command and religion of Allah.

(٣٢) وَلَقَدِ ٱخۡتَرۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَىٰ عِلۡمٍ عَلَى ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

And verily, We selected the Israelites knowingly over the people of their time because of their many Prophets.

(٣٣) وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ مَا فِيهِ بَلَٰٓؤٞاْ مُّبِينٌ

And We granted them miracles through which We gave strength to Moses, and in which there was an obvious blessing for them, like the Manna and Salwa and other than them.

(٣٤) إِنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ لَيَقُولُونَ

Indeed, these rejecting polytheists will say, denying the resurrection:

(٣٥) إِنۡ هِيَ إِلَّا مَوۡتَتُنَا ٱلۡأُولَىٰ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمُنشَرِينَ

It is only our first death that we will die; there is no life after that, and we will not be resurrected after this death.

(٣٦) فَأۡتُواْ بِ‍َٔابَآئِنَآ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

O Muhammad! If you are truthful in your claim that Allah will resurrect the dead for requital and accountability, then bring our forefathers who have died back to life.

(٣٧) أَهُمۡ خَيۡرٌ أَمۡ قَوۡمُ تُبَّعٖ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَٰهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ مُجۡرِمِينَ

O Messenger, are these denying polytheists stronger and more resistant, or are the people of Tubba' and those after them like 'Ād and Thamūd? We destroyed them all. Indeed, they were criminals.

(٣٨) وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا لَٰعِبِينَ

And We did not create the heavens, the Earth, and everything between it in jest and amusement.

(٣٩) مَا خَلَقۡنَٰهُمَآ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

We only created the heavens and the Earth due to profound wisdom, but most polytheists do not know that.

Points of Benefit:

The believer must seek refuge in his Lord to protect him from the plots of his enemy.
The legitimacy of supplicating against the disbelievers when they do not respond to the call and when they fight the callers to Allah.
The universe does not grieve the death of a disbeliever due to him insulting Allah.
The creation of the heavens and Earth is due to a profound wisdom that the atheists are ignorant of.
