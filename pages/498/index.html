(٤٠) إِنَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ مِيقَٰتُهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ

The Day of Resurrection, when Allah will decide between the slaves, is the appointed time for all of creation in which Allah will gather them.

(٤١) يَوۡمَ لَا يُغۡنِي مَوۡلًى عَن مَّوۡلٗى شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا هُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ

The Day when no relative will benefit his relative or friend benefit his friend nor will they be saved from Allah's punishment, because authority on that day will be for Allah. Nobody will be able to claim it.

(٤٢) إِلَّا مَن رَّحِمَ ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Except the person Allah has mercy on, for he will benefit from the good action he has sent forward. Allah is the Mighty whom nobody can overpower, Wise in His creation, decree, and plan.

After mentioning the Resurrection, Allah explains how people will be divided on that Day according to the nature of their recompense, stating:

(٤٣) إِنَّ شَجَرَتَ ٱلزَّقُّومِ

The tree of Zaqqum that Allah has grown at the bottom of the Inferno.

(٤٤) طَعَامُ ٱلۡأَثِيمِ

Is the food of the extremely sinful who will eat of its horrible fruit!

(٤٥) كَٱلۡمُهۡلِ يَغۡلِي فِي ٱلۡبُطُونِ

This fruit is like black oil, boiling in their stomachs from its intense heat.

(٤٦) كَغَلۡيِ ٱلۡحَمِيمِ

Like the boiling of extremely hot water.

(٤٧) خُذُوهُ فَٱعۡتِلُوهُ إِلَىٰ سَوَآءِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

It will be said to the keepers of the Fire: Grab him and drag him violently and harshly towards the middle of the Inferno.

(٤٨) ثُمَّ صُبُّواْ فَوۡقَ رَأۡسِهِۦ مِنۡ عَذَابِ ٱلۡحَمِيمِ

Then pour hot water over the head of this person being punished, so that the punishment is never separated from him.

(٤٩) ذُقۡ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡكَرِيمُ

It will be said to him mockingly: Taste this painful torment. You are the mighty who is not treated unjustly, noble amongst your people.

(٥٠) إِنَّ هَٰذَا مَا كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَمۡتَرُونَ

This is the punishment you used to doubt would occur on the Day of Resurrection, but now your doubt has been removed by you seeing it.

(٥١) إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي مَقَامٍ أَمِينٖ

Those who are mindful of their Lord through complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions will be in a place of residence that is safe from every misfortune that could afflict them.

(٥٢) فِي جَنَّٰتٖ وَعُيُونٖ

In gardens and flowing springs.

(٥٣) يَلۡبَسُونَ مِن سُندُسٖ وَإِسۡتَبۡرَقٖ مُّتَقَٰبِلِينَ

They will wear fine and thick silk in Paradise. They will be facing one another and not see each other's nape.

(٥٤) كَذَٰلِكَ وَزَوَّجۡنَٰهُم بِحُورٍ عِينٖ

Just as We will honor them with what is mentioned in Paradise, We will marry them to beautiful women with beautiful wide eyes, with the whitest whites and the blackest blacks.

(٥٥) يَدۡعُونَ فِيهَا بِكُلِّ فَٰكِهَةٍ ءَامِنِينَ

They will call their attendants therein to bring them every fruit they wish, safe from their being cut off and from their harm.

(٥٦) لَا يَذُوقُونَ فِيهَا ٱلۡمَوۡتَ إِلَّا ٱلۡمَوۡتَةَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰۖ وَوَقَىٰهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

Living therein forever, they will not taste death therein except the first death in the worldly life, and their Lord will save them from the punishment of the Fire.

(٥٧) فَضۡلٗا مِّن رَّبِّكَۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

As grace and kindness from your Lord to them. That which is mentioned, namely entering them into Paradise and saving them from the Fire, is the great success which no success can compare with.

(٥٨) فَإِنَّمَا يَسَّرۡنَٰهُ بِلِسَانِكَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ

We have made this Quran easy and facilitated it for you by revealing it in your language of Arabic, O Messenger, so that they take heed.

(٥٩) فَٱرۡتَقِبۡ إِنَّهُم مُّرۡتَقِبُونَ

So await your help and their destruction; they are awaiting your destruction.

Points of Benefit:

Both the soul and the body of the disbeliever are tormented.
The great success is being saved from the fire and admitted into Paradise.
Allah makes the Quran easy to pronounce and understand for His slaves.
