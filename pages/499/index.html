The Crouching (Surah Al-Jathiyah)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Detailing the different conditions of the creation from legislative verses and signs in the universe, refuting the arguments of those who reject the resurrection, and alarming them.

Explanation:

(١) حمٓ

Hā Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ

The revelation of the Quran is from Allah, the Mighty whom nobody can overpower, Wise in His creating, decreeing, and planning.

(٣) إِنَّ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

In the heavens and Earth are proof of the power of Allah and His oneness for the believers because they are the ones who take heed of the signs.

(٤) وَفِي خَلۡقِكُمۡ وَمَا يَبُثُّ مِن دَآبَّةٍ ءَايَٰتٞ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُوقِنُونَ

Also, in your own creation, O people, from a drop of semen, then a blood clot, then a piece of flesh, and in the creation of the beast that Allah will send to crawl upon the face of the Earth. All these signs show Allah's oneness for those sure that Allah is the creator.

(٥) وَٱخۡتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ وَمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مِن رِّزۡقٖ فَأَحۡيَا بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَا وَتَصۡرِيفِ ٱلرِّيَٰحِ ءَايَٰتٞ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ

And in the alternation of the night and day, and the rainwater Allah sent down from the sky, then He gave life to the Earth through it by making plants grow in it after it was dead without any plants, and in the alternation of the winds by bringing them from one direction at times and other directions at other times for your benefit, are proof for people who understand. They use them as proof of Allah's oneness, His power to resurrect, and His power over everything.

(٦) تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱللَّهِ نَتۡلُوهَا عَلَيۡكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ فَبِأَيِّ حَدِيثِۢ بَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ وَءَايَٰتِهِۦ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

These verses and proof We read to you in truth, O Messenger. So, if they do not believe in the speech of Allah, which He revealed to His slave, and in His proof, which speech will they believe in after it, and which proof after it will they accept?

(٧) وَيۡلٞ لِّكُلِّ أَفَّاكٍ أَثِيمٖ

Allah's punishment and destruction are for every extremely sinful liar.

(٨) يَسۡمَعُ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ ثُمَّ يُصِرُّ مُسۡتَكۡبِرٗا كَأَن لَّمۡ يَسۡمَعۡهَاۖ فَبَشِّرۡهُ بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ

This disbeliever listens to the verses of Allah in the Quran being recited to him, then continues in the disbelief and sins he is in, arrogant in himself, abstains from following the truth, as though he has not heard these verses being read to him. So inform him, O Messenger, of what he will dislike in the Hereafter, which is a painful punishment that awaits him there.

(٩) وَإِذَا عَلِمَ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا شَيۡ‍ًٔا ٱتَّخَذَهَا هُزُوًاۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ مُّهِينٞ

And when something of the Quran reaches him, he takes it as a joke to mock. Those attributed with this quality of mocking the Quran will have a disgraceful punishment on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٠) مِّن وَرَآئِهِمۡ جَهَنَّمُۖ وَلَا يُغۡنِي عَنۡهُم مَّا كَسَبُواْ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا مَا ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡلِيَآءَۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٌ

In front of them will be the Fire, which awaits them in the Hereafter, and the actions they earned will not help them with Allah at all, nor will the idols they took to worship besides Him remove anything from them. And they will have a great torment on the Day of Resurrection.

(١١) هَٰذَا هُدٗىۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ مِّن رِّجۡزٍ أَلِيمٌ

This book that We revealed to Our Messenger Muhammad is a guide to the true path. And those who disbelieve in the verses of their Lord which were revealed to His Messenger will have a bad, painful punishment.

(١٢) ۞ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡبَحۡرَ لِتَجۡرِيَ ٱلۡفُلۡكُ فِيهِ بِأَمۡرِهِۦ وَلِتَبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

Allah alone is the One who subjugated for you, O people, the sea in which ships sail with His order so that you seek His grace through different lawful trades and are thankful for Allah's favors upon you.

(١٣) وَسَخَّرَ لَكُم مَّا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا مِّنۡهُۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ

And He subjugated whatever is in the heavens, such as the sun, moon, and stars, and whatever is on Earth, such as rivers, trees, mountains, and so forth. In subjugating that for you is proof of Allah's power and His oneness for people who ponder over His signs and take heed from them.

Points of Benefit:

Lying and persisting upon sin, arrogance, and ridiculing the signs of Allah are characteristics of the people of misguidance. Allah threatens those with such traits.
Allah's blessings to His slaves are many. Among them is subjugating what is in the universe for them.
Receiving blessings requires that the slaves thank the deity that gave them.
