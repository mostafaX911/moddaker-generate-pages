(٢٥) وَبَشِّرِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ أَنَّ لَهُمۡ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۖ كُلَّمَا رُزِقُواْ مِنۡهَا مِن ثَمَرَةٖ رِّزۡقٗا قَالُواْ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي رُزِقۡنَا مِن قَبۡلُۖ وَأُتُواْ بِهِۦ مُتَشَٰبِهٗاۖ وَلَهُمۡ فِيهَآ أَزۡوَٰجٞ مُّطَهَّرَةٞۖ وَهُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

As the warnings in the previous verses were for the disbelievers, then give glad tidings -O Prophet- to the believers in Allah who do righteous deeds, of what will please them from gardens with rivers flowing beneath their palaces and trees.Whenever they are given its pure fruits to consume, they will comment on their striking resemblance to the fruits of the earth, saying, 'This is just like what we used to be provided before.' They will be given fruits with the same name and shape, so they recognize and desire them, but they will be different in taste and flavor. In Paradise, they will have spouses free of any unpleasant qualities and traits people have on Earth. They will live there in eternal, everlasting bliss, unlike the temporary and transient bliss of worldly life.

(٢٦) ۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَسۡتَحۡيِۦٓ أَن يَضۡرِبَ مَثَلٗا مَّا بَعُوضَةٗ فَمَا فَوۡقَهَاۚ فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ فَيَعۡلَمُونَ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّهِمۡۖ وَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فَيَقُولُونَ مَاذَآ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ بِهَٰذَا مَثَلٗاۘ يُضِلُّ بِهِۦ كَثِيرٗا وَيَهۡدِي بِهِۦ كَثِيرٗاۚ وَمَا يُضِلُّ بِهِۦٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ

Verily, Allah does not shy away from using whatever example He wishes, whether that be of a gnat or anything bigger or smaller than it. These examples are given to two types of people: believers and disbelievers. As for the believers, they know and trust that there is wisdom in the use of such examples. As for the disbelievers, they mockingly ask one another why Allah uses examples with lowly creatures, such as mosquitos, flies, and spiders. Allah replies that guidance, direction, and a test for people lie in these examples. There are many whom Allah will misguide through these examples because they refuse to ponder them. Likewise, He will guide many along the right path because they take lessons from them. He does not misguide anyone except those who deserve misguidance. They are those who stray far from obedience to Him, such as the hypocrites.

(٢٧) ٱلَّذِينَ يَنقُضُونَ عَهۡدَ ٱللَّهِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مِيثَٰقِهِۦ وَيَقۡطَعُونَ مَآ أَمَرَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦٓ أَن يُوصَلَ وَيُفۡسِدُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ

They are those who break the promise they made with Allah to serve Him alone and follow the Messenger whom other messengers before him foretold of. They are those who cut off what Allah enjoined, such as blood relations. They strive to spread corruption upon the earth with sinful actions. These are the ones who are unfortunate in this world and the Hereafter.

(٢٨) كَيۡفَ تَكۡفُرُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَكُنتُمۡ أَمۡوَٰتٗا فَأَحۡيَٰكُمۡۖ ثُمَّ يُمِيتُكُمۡ ثُمَّ يُحۡيِيكُمۡ ثُمَّ إِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ

Verily, your affair, O disbelievers, is strange! How can you disbelieve in Allah while you witness the signs of His power in your very own existence? You were nonexistent, and He brought you to existence and gave you life. Then He will bring you death and bring you back to life a second time and return you to Him to recompense you for what you did.

(٢٩) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ لَكُم مَّا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰٓ إِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَسَوَّىٰهُنَّ سَبۡعَ سَمَٰوَٰتٖۚ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٞ

It is Allah alone Who created everything on earth for you, such as the countless rivers and trees from which you benefit, enjoying all that he made subservient to you. Then He turned to the sky, making it into seven leveled heavens. And it is He whose knowledge encompasses everything.

Points of Benefit:

From the completion of bliss in Paradise is that its pleasures are not afflicted with any kind of spoiling, nor are they mixed with any harm.
Only the believers benefit from the parables that Allah presents because they want guidance truthfully and seek it truly.
One of the most prominent characteristics of the sinners is that they break their covenants with Allah as well as the creation; they sever that which Allah has commanded to be joined, and they strive to spread corruption on earth.
The origin regarding things is that they are permissible and pure because Allah the Most High favored His servants by creating everything on the earth for them.
