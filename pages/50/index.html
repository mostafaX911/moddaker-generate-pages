Family of 'Imran (Surah Al 'Imran)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Proving that Islam is the truth, in response to the doubts cast by the People of the Book, and strengthening the believers' resolve.

Explanation:

It is a Medinan surah. It is named Al 'Imran due to the mention of the family of 'Imran in the 33rd verse of the surah.

(١) الٓمٓ

(الٓمٓ) Alif Lām Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah. They indicate the inability of the Arabs to bring a Quran like this one even though it is composed of letters similar to the ones at the beginning of this Sūrah, which also form parts of their own speech.

(٢) ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُ

Allah is the One who alone deserves to be worshipped. He is the One Who possesses perfect life without any death or deficiency. He exists by Himself and does not need any of His creation. The creation only exists through Him and is always in need of Him.

(٣) نَزَّلَ عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ وَأَنزَلَ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةَ وَٱلۡإِنجِيلَ

He has revealed to you, O Prophet, the Quran with true stories and just laws that agree with the previous divine books, and there are no contradictions between all these divine books. Before revealing the Quran to you, He revealed the Torah to Moses and the Gospel to Jesus (peace be upon them both). These sacred books are full of guidance and instruction to direct people toward what is good for their religion and worldly lives. Likewise, Allah revealed the Criterion (the Quran) to distinguish between truth and falsehood and guidance from misguidance. Those who disbelieve in the verses of Allah that He revealed to you will receive a painful torment. Allah is the Almighty, and nothing can overcome Him, and He will take revenge on those who reject His messengers and oppose His command.

(٤) مِن قَبۡلُ هُدٗى لِّلنَّاسِ وَأَنزَلَ ٱلۡفُرۡقَانَۗ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ شَدِيدٞۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَزِيزٞ ذُو ٱنتِقَامٍ

He has revealed to you, O Prophet, the Quran with true stories and just laws that agree with the previous divine books, and there are no contradictions between all these divine books. Before revealing the Quran to you, He revealed the Torah to Moses and the Gospel to Jesus (peace be upon them both). These sacred books are full of guidance and instruction to direct people toward what is good for their religion and worldly lives. Likewise, Allah revealed the Criterion (the Quran) to distinguish between truth and falsehood and guidance from misguidance. Those who disbelieve in the verses of Allah that He revealed to you will receive a painful torment. Allah is the Almighty, and nothing can overcome Him, and He will take revenge on those who reject His messengers and oppose His command.

(٥) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَخۡفَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ شَيۡءٞ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ

Nothing on earth or in the sky is hidden from Allah. He knows everything, whether it is apparent or concealed.

(٦) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُصَوِّرُكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡحَامِ كَيۡفَ يَشَآءُۚ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

It is He Who creates you as He wills in different forms inside the wombs of your mothers, either as a male or female, handsome or unattractive, black or white. There is no one worthy of being worshipped besides Him. He is Almighty who cannot be overpowered. He is the All-Wise in His creation, planning, and legislation.

(٧) هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِنۡهُ ءَايَٰتٞ مُّحۡكَمَٰتٌ هُنَّ أُمُّ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَأُخَرُ مُتَشَٰبِهَٰتٞۖ فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمۡ زَيۡغٞ فَيَتَّبِعُونَ مَا تَشَٰبَهَ مِنۡهُ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ ٱلۡفِتۡنَةِ وَٱبۡتِغَآءَ تَأۡوِيلِهِۦۖ وَمَا يَعۡلَمُ تَأۡوِيلَهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُۗ وَٱلرَّٰسِخُونَ فِي ٱلۡعِلۡمِ يَقُولُونَ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦ كُلّٞ مِّنۡ عِندِ رَبِّنَاۗ وَمَا يَذَّكَّرُ إِلَّآ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

O Prophet, He is the One who revealed to you the Quran, which contains verses with clear and unambiguous meaning. These verses are the foundation of the Book, forming the majority of its verses, and are referred to when there is a dispute. However, it also includes other verses that may have multiple meanings unclear to most people. Those who seek other than the truth leave the clear verses and focus on the unclear ones, trying to cause doubts and mislead people. They interpret them in a way that suits their corrupt views. No one knows the actual meanings of such verses except Allah. Those firmly grounded in knowledge say: We believe in the entire Quran because it is all from Allah. They interpret the unclear verses according to the clear ones. Only those who have sound intellects pay attention to this.

(٨) رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغۡ قُلُوبَنَا بَعۡدَ إِذۡ هَدَيۡتَنَا وَهَبۡ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ

Those grounded in knowledge say: O our Lord, do not let our hearts turn away from the truth after you have shown it to us. Save us from what happened to those who deviated and turned away from the truth. Grant us vast mercy, guiding our hearts and protecting us from misguidance. O Our Lord, Indeed you are the Bestower who gives plentifully.

(٩) رَبَّنَآ إِنَّكَ جَامِعُ ٱلنَّاسِ لِيَوۡمٖ لَّا رَيۡبَ فِيهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُخۡلِفُ ٱلۡمِيعَادَ

O our Lord, You will gather all the people back to You: to reckon them on a day in which there is no doubt. O our Lord, indeed, You do not break promises.

Points of Benefit:

Allah established the evidence and removed any excuse for His creation by sending messengers and revealing books that guide to the Truth and warn against falsehood.
The perfect knowledge of Allah and His encompassing of His creation, so nothing escapes Him in the Earth or the heavens, whether apparent or hidden.
One of the foundational principles of the steadfast believers in knowledge is to interpret what is allegorical in the verses with what is clear among them.
The legitimacy of supplicating to Allah, seeking steadfastness in the truth, and seeking guidance in matters, especially during trials and temptations.
