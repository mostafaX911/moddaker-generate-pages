(١٤) قُل لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ يَغۡفِرُواْ لِلَّذِينَ لَا يَرۡجُونَ أَيَّامَ ٱللَّهِ لِيَجۡزِيَ قَوۡمَۢا بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ

O Messenger, say to those who have faith in Allah and accepted His messengers: Forgive those who did bad to you from the disbelievers who do not hope for Allah's reward nor do they fear His punishment, because Allah will reward both the patient believers and the transgressing disbelievers for the actions they used to earn in this world.

(١٥) مَنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَلِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَنۡ أَسَآءَ فَعَلَيۡهَاۖ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُمۡ تُرۡجَعُونَ

Whoever does righteous deeds, the result of those deeds for him; Allah does not need his actions. Whoever commits evil, the result of his evil is that he will be punished for it; his evil does not harm Allah. Then, to Us alone, you will be returned so We can reward everyone with what they deserve.

(١٦) وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحُكۡمَ وَٱلنُّبُوَّةَ وَرَزَقۡنَٰهُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ وَفَضَّلۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَى ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

We granted the Israelites the Torah and the command to decide between people according to its rule. We made most of the prophets amongst them from the children of Abraham (peace be upon him). We provided them with different types of good things and granted them virtue over all those of their time.

(١٧) وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُم بَيِّنَٰتٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِۖ فَمَا ٱخۡتَلَفُوٓاْ إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡعِلۡمُ بَغۡيَۢا بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ يَقۡضِي بَيۡنَهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فِيمَا كَانُواْ فِيهِ يَخۡتَلِفُونَ

We gave them proof that clarifies the truth from falsehood. They did not differ except after the proof was established against them by sending Our Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. Nothing lead them to this difference except some of them oppressing others out of greed for leadership and fame. O Messenger, your Lord will decide between them on the Day of Resurrection what they used to differ about in this world, so He will clearly show who was right and who was wrong.

(١٨) ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَٰكَ عَلَىٰ شَرِيعَةٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ فَٱتَّبِعۡهَا وَلَا تَتَّبِعۡ أَهۡوَآءَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

O Messenger, then We put you on an ordained way regarding the matter of Islam and that which calls them to faith and good actions. So follow this way and do not follow the desires of those who do not know the truth, as their desires lead them away from the truth.

(١٩) إِنَّهُمۡ لَن يُغۡنُواْ عَنكَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۚ وَإِنَّ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ بَعۡضٖۖ وَٱللَّهُ وَلِيُّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Those who do not know the truth will not be able to hold back Allah's punishment from you if you follow their desires. The oppressors from all sects and creeds help and support one another against the believers. And Allah is the helper of those mindful of Him by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

(٢٠) هَٰذَا بَصَٰٓئِرُ لِلنَّاسِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٞ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُوقِنُونَ

This Quran, which is revealed to Our Messenger, is enlightenment through which people can see the truth from falsehood, guidance to the truth, and mercy for people who have conviction because they are the ones who are guided through it to the straight path so that their Lord is pleased with them and then enters them into Paradise and distances them from the Fire.

(٢١) أَمۡ حَسِبَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱجۡتَرَحُواْ ٱلسَّيِّ‍َٔاتِ أَن نَّجۡعَلَهُمۡ كَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ سَوَآءٗ مَّحۡيَاهُمۡ وَمَمَاتُهُمۡۚ سَآءَ مَا يَحۡكُمُونَ

Those who commit disbelief and sins through their limbs think We will make them in reward like those who believed in Allah and did good deeds, making them equal in this life and the Hereafter. Wretched is this judgment of theirs.

(٢٢) وَخَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلِتُجۡزَىٰ كُلُّ نَفۡسِۢ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ

Allah created the heavens and Earth with complete wisdom. He did not create them in vain. He created them so each soul can be rewarded with the good or bad it earned. Allah will not oppress them by decreasing their good deeds or increasing their bad deeds.

Points of Benefit:

Pardoning and overlooking the wrongdoer, if he does not corrupt the Earth or transgress the boundaries of Allah, is a virtuous characteristic that Allah has ordered the believers with if they think it will have a good outcome.
The obligation to follow the legislation and refrain from following human desires.
Just as the traits of the believers and the disbelievers are not equal, their recompense is not equal.
Allah created the heavens and the Earth according to a profound wisdom that atheist materialists are ignorant of.
