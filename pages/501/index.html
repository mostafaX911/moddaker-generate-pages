(٢٣) أَفَرَءَيۡتَ مَنِ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَٰهَهُۥ هَوَىٰهُ وَأَضَلَّهُ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ عِلۡمٖ وَخَتَمَ عَلَىٰ سَمۡعِهِۦ وَقَلۡبِهِۦ وَجَعَلَ عَلَىٰ بَصَرِهِۦ غِشَٰوَةٗ فَمَن يَهۡدِيهِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ٱللَّهِۚ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ

O Messenger, look at the one who followed his desires and made it his object of worship, which he cannot oppose. Then Allah led him astray despite his knowledge because he deserved to be misguided, and Allah sealed his heart so he could not listen in a manner that benefits, and Allah put a veil on his eyes, which stops him from seeing the truth. So who can cause him to accept the truth after Allah has led him astray? Do you not remember the harm of following desires and the benefit of following Allah's sacred law?

(٢٤) وَقَالُواْ مَا هِيَ إِلَّا حَيَاتُنَا ٱلدُّنۡيَا نَمُوتُ وَنَحۡيَا وَمَا يُهۡلِكُنَآ إِلَّا ٱلدَّهۡرُۚ وَمَا لَهُم بِذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عِلۡمٍۖ إِنۡ هُمۡ إِلَّا يَظُنُّونَ

And the disbelievers who denied the resurrection said: There is no life besides this worldly life of ours, so there is no life after it. Generations die never to return, and other generations come to life. Only the alternation of the night and day is what makes us die. They have no knowledge to support their denial of the resurrection. They are only assuming, and assumptions do not benefit the truth.

(٢٥) وَإِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُنَا بَيِّنَٰتٖ مَّا كَانَ حُجَّتَهُمۡ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُواْ ٱئۡتُواْ بِ‍َٔابَآئِنَآ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

When you recite Our clear verses to the polytheists who deny the resurrection, they have no proof which they can use except their saying to the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم and his Companions: Bring our dead fathers back to life if you are true in your claim that we will be resurrected after our death.

(٢٦) قُلِ ٱللَّهُ يُحۡيِيكُمۡ ثُمَّ يُمِيتُكُمۡ ثُمَّ يَجۡمَعُكُمۡ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهِ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

O Messenger, say to them: Allah gives you life by creating you, then He will cause you to die, then He will gather you after your death on the Day of Resurrection for reckoning and recompense; on that day, which is undoubtedly coming. But most people do not know, so they do not prepare for it with righteous actions.

(٢٧) وَلِلَّهِ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَيَوۡمَ تَقُومُ ٱلسَّاعَةُ يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَخۡسَرُ ٱلۡمُبۡطِلُونَ

To Allah alone belongs the kingdom of the heavens and the kingdom of Earth, so there is nobody to be truly worshipped in them besides Him. And on the day the Hour occurs in which Allah will raise the dead for reckoning and recompense, the people of falsehood who used to worship others besides Allah and strive to refute the truth and establish falsehood will lose.

(٢٨) وَتَرَىٰ كُلَّ أُمَّةٖ جَاثِيَةٗۚ كُلُّ أُمَّةٖ تُدۡعَىٰٓ إِلَىٰ كِتَٰبِهَا ٱلۡيَوۡمَ تُجۡزَوۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

And you will see on that day, O Messenger, every nation crouching on its knees, awaiting what will be done with them. Every nation will be called towards its book of deeds, which the guarding angels wrote. Today, you will be rewarded, O people, for the good and bad you used to do in the worldly life.

(٢٩) هَٰذَا كِتَٰبُنَا يَنطِقُ عَلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا نَسۡتَنسِخُ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

This is Our book, in which Our angels used to write your actions, which is speaking against you truthfully, so read it. We used to order the guarding angels to write what you used to do in the worldly life.

(٣٠) فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ فَيُدۡخِلُهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ فِي رَحۡمَتِهِۦۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

As for those who believe and do righteous actions, their Lord will enter them into Paradise through His mercy. That reward Allah granted them is the clear success that no success can compare with.

(٣١) وَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَفَلَمۡ تَكُنۡ ءَايَٰتِي تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فَٱسۡتَكۡبَرۡتُمۡ وَكُنتُمۡ قَوۡمٗا مُّجۡرِمِينَ

As for those who disbelieved in Allah, it will be said to them to rebuke them: Were My verses not recited to you, but you were too proud to believe in them, and you were a transgressing people, gaining disbelief and sins?

(٣٢) وَإِذَا قِيلَ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقّٞ وَٱلسَّاعَةُ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهَا قُلۡتُم مَّا نَدۡرِي مَا ٱلسَّاعَةُ إِن نَّظُنُّ إِلَّا ظَنّٗا وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمُسۡتَيۡقِنِينَ

When it is said to you: Allah's promise, in which He has promised His slaves He will resurrect them and reward them, is true without a doubt, and the Hour is true without a doubt, so work for it, you say: We do not know what this Hour is. We only assume doubtfully that it is coming, and we are not convinced it will come.

Points of Benefit:

Following desires destroys the person and veils the means to guidance from him.
The horror of the Day of Resurrection.
Conjecture is of no avail against the Truth, especially in matters of faith.
