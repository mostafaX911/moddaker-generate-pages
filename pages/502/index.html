(٣٣) وَبَدَا لَهُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتُ مَا عَمِلُواْ وَحَاقَ بِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

The evil consequences of the disbelief and sins they committed in the worldly life will appear to them, and the punishment they used to mock when warned of it will befall them.

(٣٤) وَقِيلَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ نَنسَىٰكُمۡ كَمَا نَسِيتُمۡ لِقَآءَ يَوۡمِكُمۡ هَٰذَا وَمَأۡوَىٰكُمُ ٱلنَّارُ وَمَا لَكُم مِّن نَّٰصِرِينَ

Allah will tell them: Today, I will leave you in the Fire just as you forgot meeting this day of yours, and so did not prepare for it through faith and good actions. Your abode, which you will take refuge in, is the Fire, and you do not have any helpers to repel Allah's punishment from you.

(٣٥) ذَٰلِكُم بِأَنَّكُمُ ٱتَّخَذۡتُمۡ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ هُزُوٗا وَغَرَّتۡكُمُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ لَا يُخۡرَجُونَ مِنۡهَا وَلَا هُمۡ يُسۡتَعۡتَبُونَ

That punishment you are being punished with is because you took the words of Allah as a joke, which you would mock, and life with its pleasures and desires deceived you. So today, these disbelievers who used to mock Allah's words will not be taken out of the Fire. Rather, they will remain in it forever, and neither will they be returned to the worldly life to do good deeds, nor will their Lord be pleased with them.

(٣٦) فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ رَبِّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَرَبِّ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

So for Allah alone is praise, Lord of the heavens and Lord of the Earth, and Lord of all the creation.

(٣٧) وَلَهُ ٱلۡكِبۡرِيَآءُ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

And for Him is grandeur and greatness in the heavens and the Earth, and He is the Mighty whom nobody can overpower, Wise in His creating, decreeing, and planning.

The Wind-Curved Sandhills (Surah Al-Ahqaf)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Affirming humanity's need for the divine message and cautioning those who turn away from it.

Explanation:

(١) حمٓ

Hā Mīm – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah.

(٢) تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ

The revelation of the Quran is from Allah, the Mighty, whom nobody can overpower, Wise in His creating, decreeing, and legislation.

(٣) مَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَأَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ عَمَّآ أُنذِرُواْ مُعۡرِضُونَ

I did not create the heavens and the Earth and what is between them in vain, but rather, I created all of it with truth and complete wisdom. Amongst that is for His slaves to recognize Him through them and so worship Him alone and not associate any partner with Him, and so that they fulfill the demands of their being made deputy on Earth until a fixed time which Allah alone knows. And those who disbelieve in Allah are turning away from the Book of Allah they are being warned through without paying attention to it.

(٤) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَرُونِي مَاذَا خَلَقُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَمۡ لَهُمۡ شِرۡكٞ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِۖ ٱئۡتُونِي بِكِتَٰبٖ مِّن قَبۡلِ هَٰذَآ أَوۡ أَثَٰرَةٖ مِّنۡ عِلۡمٍ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists who turn away from the truth: Tell me about the idols you worship besides Allah: what parts of the earth have they created? Did they create mountains or rivers? Or do they have a partnership and share with Allah in creating the heavens? Bring me a book revealed from Allah before the Quran or some trace of knowledge that the previous people left if you are true in your claim that your idols deserve to be worshipped.

(٥) وَمَنۡ أَضَلُّ مِمَّن يَدۡعُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَن لَّا يَسۡتَجِيبُ لَهُۥٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ وَهُمۡ عَن دُعَآئِهِمۡ غَٰفِلُونَ

No one is more misguided than the one who worships an idol besides Allah that cannot respond to his call until the Day of Resurrection. And these idols, which they worship besides Allah, are oblivious to their worshippers who call upon them, let alone benefiting them or bringing them harm.

Points of Benefit:

Mocking the verses of Allah constitutes disbelief.
The danger of being deluded by the pleasures and desires of this world.
Affirmation of the attribute of Grandeur for Allah, the Exalted.
The answer to supplication is among the most evident proofs of the existence of Allah, the Transcendent, and His deserving of worship.
