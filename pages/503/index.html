(٦) وَإِذَا حُشِرَ ٱلنَّاسُ كَانُواْ لَهُمۡ أَعۡدَآءٗ وَكَانُواْ بِعِبَادَتِهِمۡ كَٰفِرِينَ

As well as not benefiting them in this world, when they are raised on the Day of Judgment, they will be enemies to those who used to worship them and disassociate themselves from them. They will deny having any knowledge of their worship.

(٧) وَإِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُنَا بَيِّنَٰتٖ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِلۡحَقِّ لَمَّا جَآءَهُمۡ هَٰذَا سِحۡرٞ مُّبِينٌ

And when Our verses revealed to Our Messenger are read to them, the disbelievers say regarding the Quran when it came to them at the hands of their Messenger: This is clear magic. It is not a revelation from Allah.

(٨) أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ ٱفۡتَرَىٰهُۖ قُلۡ إِنِ ٱفۡتَرَيۡتُهُۥ فَلَا تَمۡلِكُونَ لِي مِنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡ‍ًٔاۖ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا تُفِيضُونَ فِيهِۚ كَفَىٰ بِهِۦ شَهِيدَۢا بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكُمۡۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Do these polytheists say that Muhammad has made up this Quran and attributed it to Allah? Say to them, O Messenger: If I have made it up on my part, you do not have power over any plan for me if Allah wants to punish me, so how can I put myself at risk of punishment by fabricating against Him? Allah knows best about what you engage in defaming His Quran and slandering me. He (may He be glorified) is sufficient as a witness between me and you. And He forgives the sins of His slaves who repent to Him and is Merciful to them.

(٩) قُلۡ مَا كُنتُ بِدۡعٗا مِّنَ ٱلرُّسُلِ وَمَآ أَدۡرِي مَا يُفۡعَلُ بِي وَلَا بِكُمۡۖ إِنۡ أَتَّبِعُ إِلَّا مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ إِلَّا نَذِيرٞ مُّبِينٞ

O Messenger, tell these polytheists who reject your prophethood: I am not the first Messenger Allah has sent for you to find my call strange, as many messengers came before me. I do not know what Allah will do with me nor what He will do with you in this life. I only follow what Allah reveals to me, so I do not say or do except according to what He reveals. And I am only a warner who clearly warns of His punishment.

(١٠) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِن كَانَ مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ وَكَفَرۡتُم بِهِۦ وَشَهِدَ شَاهِدٞ مِّنۢ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ عَلَىٰ مِثۡلِهِۦ فَ‍َٔامَنَ وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرۡتُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

O Messenger, tell these deniers: Tell me if this Quran is from Allah and you deny it, and a witness from the Israelites testifies that it is from Allah, relying on what is in the Torah, and then he believes in it, while you are too arrogant to believe in it. Are you not then the oppressors? Allah does not enable the oppressors to follow the truth.

(١١) وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَوۡ كَانَ خَيۡرٗا مَّا سَبَقُونَآ إِلَيۡهِۚ وَإِذۡ لَمۡ يَهۡتَدُواْ بِهِۦ فَسَيَقُولُونَ هَٰذَآ إِفۡكٞ قَدِيمٞ

And those who disbelieved in the Quran and what their Messenger brought to them say: If what Muhammad brought was true and it guides towards good, these paupers, slaves, and weak people would not have preceded us to it. And because they are not guided to what their Messenger brought them, they will say: This which he has brought us is an old lie, and we do not follow lies.

(١٢) وَمِن قَبۡلِهِۦ كِتَٰبُ مُوسَىٰٓ إِمَامٗا وَرَحۡمَةٗۚ وَهَٰذَا كِتَٰبٞ مُّصَدِّقٞ لِّسَانًا عَرَبِيّٗا لِّيُنذِرَ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ وَبُشۡرَىٰ لِلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

And before this Quran was the Torah, the book Allah revealed to Moses (peace be upon him) as a guide to be followed in truth and as a mercy for those who believe in it and follow it amongst the Israelites. And this Quran revealed upon Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم is a book that confirms the books before it, in the Arabic language, so that he can warn through it those who wronged themselves through associating partners with Allah and committing sins. It is a glad tiding to the righteous who have perfected their relationship with their Creator and their relationship with His creation.

(١٣) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ فَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

Indeed, those who say: Our Lord is Allah; we have no Lord besides Him, then become firm in faith and good actions, there will be no fear in them in what they face in the Hereafter, nor will they grieve over the fortune of this worlds which escaped them or what they left behind them.

(١٤) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

Those with such qualities are the people of Paradise who will live in it forever as a reward for their good deeds in this world.

Points of Benefit:

Every entity worshipped besides Allah denies its worshippers among the disbelievers.
The Prophet's صلى الله عليه وسلم knowledge of the unseen is limited to what Allah has revealed to him.
The existence of evidence affirming the prophethood of our Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم in the previous scriptures.
Explanation of the virtues of steadfastness and the rewards for those who uphold it.
