(١٥) وَوَصَّيۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ إِحۡسَٰنًاۖ حَمَلَتۡهُ أُمُّهُۥ كُرۡهٗا وَوَضَعَتۡهُ كُرۡهٗاۖ وَحَمۡلُهُۥ وَفِصَٰلُهُۥ ثَلَٰثُونَ شَهۡرًاۚ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا بَلَغَ أَشُدَّهُۥ وَبَلَغَ أَرۡبَعِينَ سَنَةٗ قَالَ رَبِّ أَوۡزِعۡنِيٓ أَنۡ أَشۡكُرَ نِعۡمَتَكَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَيَّ وَأَنۡ أَعۡمَلَ صَٰلِحٗا تَرۡضَىٰهُ وَأَصۡلِحۡ لِي فِي ذُرِّيَّتِيٓۖ إِنِّي تُبۡتُ إِلَيۡكَ وَإِنِّي مِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ

And We commanded man emphatically that he should be kind to his parents in their lifetime and after their death with what does not oppose the legislation. And in particular, his mother, who carried him with difficulty and gave birth to him with hardship. From the time he was carried in the womb until the beginning of his weaning is thirty months. When he completes his mental and physical strength, he says: My Lord, inspire me to thank You for the favor you have blessed my parents with and me and inspire me to do good actions which please you, and accept them from me. And make my children righteous for me. I have repented to You from my sins and am amongst those who submit to Your obedience and surrender to Your commands.

(١٦) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ نَتَقَبَّلُ عَنۡهُمۡ أَحۡسَنَ مَا عَمِلُواْ وَنَتَجَاوَزُ عَن سَيِّ‍َٔاتِهِمۡ فِيٓ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡجَنَّةِۖ وَعۡدَ ٱلصِّدۡقِ ٱلَّذِي كَانُواْ يُوعَدُونَ

Those are the ones from whom We accept the good deeds they did and overlook their sins, so We do not hold them to account due to them. And they are amongst the people of Paradise. This promise they have been given is faithful and will occur.

After giving an example of being dutiful to one's parents and encouraging it, Allah warns against being undutiful to them through another example, stating:

(١٧) وَٱلَّذِي قَالَ لِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ أُفّٖ لَّكُمَآ أَتَعِدَانِنِيٓ أَنۡ أُخۡرَجَ وَقَدۡ خَلَتِ ٱلۡقُرُونُ مِن قَبۡلِي وَهُمَا يَسۡتَغِيثَانِ ٱللَّهَ وَيۡلَكَ ءَامِنۡ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقّٞ فَيَقُولُ مَا هَٰذَآ إِلَّآ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

And the one who said to his parents: Woe unto you. Do you promise I will be taken out of my grave alive after I die, whereas many nations have passed where people died, but none of them were resurrected? And his parents seek help from Allah to guide their son towards faith, and they say to their son: Woe unto you if you do not believe in the Resurrection; so, believe in it. Allah's promise of Resurrection is true without a doubt. But he says, restating his denial of the Resurrection: This which is said regarding the Resurrection is nothing, but something related from the books of the previous people and what they wrote. It is not proven to be from Allah.

(١٨) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ حَقَّ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ فِيٓ أُمَمٖ قَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِۖ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ خَٰسِرِينَ

Those are the ones for whom punishment is a certainty amongst the nations before them from the jinn and men. They are the losers, losing themselves and their families by entering the Fire.

(١٩) وَلِكُلّٖ دَرَجَٰتٞ مِّمَّا عَمِلُواْۖ وَلِيُوَفِّيَهُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ

And each group, the group of Paradise and the group of the Fire will be ranked according to their actions. The ranks of the people of Paradise are high, while the levels of the people of the Fire are low. And so that Allah gives them the full recompense of their actions, and they will not be oppressed on the Day of Judgment by their good deeds being decreased or their evil deeds being increased.

(٢٠) وَيَوۡمَ يُعۡرَضُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ أَذۡهَبۡتُمۡ طَيِّبَٰتِكُمۡ فِي حَيَاتِكُمُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱسۡتَمۡتَعۡتُم بِهَا فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ تُجۡزَوۡنَ عَذَابَ ٱلۡهُونِ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَبِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَفۡسُقُونَ

On the Day when those who rejected Allah and His messengers will be exposed to the Fire to be punished, it will be said to them in rebuke: You have exhausted your share of the pleasures of this life, and you fully enjoyed them. On this day, you will be recompensed with the punishment, which will disgrace and shame you due to your arrogance on earth, which you had no right to do, and your leaving Allah's obedience through disbelief and sins.

Points of Benefit:

Explanation of the elevated status of benevolence towards parents in Islam, particularly with regard to the mother, and the warning against disobedience.
Clarification of the danger of indulging excessively in the comforts of this world, as it distracts from the Hereafter.
Presentation of the severe warning for those who exhibit arrogance and engage in transgression.
