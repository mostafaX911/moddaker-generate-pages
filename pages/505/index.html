(٢١) ۞وَٱذۡكُرۡ أَخَا عَادٍ إِذۡ أَنذَرَ قَوۡمَهُۥ بِٱلۡأَحۡقَافِ وَقَدۡ خَلَتِ ٱلنُّذُرُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦٓ أَلَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ عَذَابَ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ

O Messenger, remember Hūd, the brother of 'Ād in lineage, when he warned his people of Allah's punishment befalling them when they were in their homes in Aḥqāf in the south of the Arabian Peninsula. Messengers had warned their people before Hūd and after him, saying to their people: Do not worship anyone except Allah alone; do not worship anything besides Him. O people, I fear for you the punishment of a great day, which is the Day of Judgment.

(٢٢) قَالُوٓاْ أَجِئۡتَنَا لِتَأۡفِكَنَا عَنۡ ءَالِهَتِنَا فَأۡتِنَا بِمَا تَعِدُنَآ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ

His people told him: Have you come to us to turn us away from worshipping our gods? That will never happen. So, bring us the punishment you threaten us with if you are truthful in your claim.

(٢٣) قَالَ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡعِلۡمُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَأُبَلِّغُكُم مَّآ أُرۡسِلۡتُ بِهِۦ وَلَٰكِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰكُمۡ قَوۡمٗا تَجۡهَلُونَ

He said: The knowledge of the time for punishment is only with Allah, and I do not know it. I am only a messenger who conveys to you what I have been sent with. However, I see you as people who are ignorant of what benefits you, and you abandon it; and what harms you, you embrace it.

(٢٤) فَلَمَّا رَأَوۡهُ عَارِضٗا مُّسۡتَقۡبِلَ أَوۡدِيَتِهِمۡ قَالُواْ هَٰذَا عَارِضٞ مُّمۡطِرُنَاۚ بَلۡ هُوَ مَا ٱسۡتَعۡجَلۡتُم بِهِۦۖ رِيحٞ فِيهَا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Then, when the punishment they asked to befall them quickly came to them, and they saw a cloud appearing in one direction of the sky, heading towards their valleys; they said: This is a cloud which will bring us rain. Hūd told them: The matter is not as you think, i.e., that it is not a raincloud; in fact, it is the punishment you wanted to be hastened. It is a wind that contains a painful punishment.

(٢٥) تُدَمِّرُ كُلَّ شَيۡءِۢ بِأَمۡرِ رَبِّهَا فَأَصۡبَحُواْ لَا يُرَىٰٓ إِلَّا مَسَٰكِنُهُمۡۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

Destroying everything it passed by which Allah ordered to be destroyed, they were killed, and only the houses in which they lived were left visible, bearing witness to their inhabiting them before. With this kind of painful punishment, We punish the transgressors who persist in their disbelief and sins.

(٢٦) وَلَقَدۡ مَكَّنَّٰهُمۡ فِيمَآ إِن مَّكَّنَّٰكُمۡ فِيهِ وَجَعَلۡنَا لَهُمۡ سَمۡعٗا وَأَبۡصَٰرٗا وَأَفۡ‍ِٔدَةٗ فَمَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنۡهُمۡ سَمۡعُهُمۡ وَلَآ أَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ وَلَآ أَفۡ‍ِٔدَتُهُم مِّن شَيۡءٍ إِذۡ كَانُواْ يَجۡحَدُونَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَحَاقَ بِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

And We granted the people of Hūd the means of empowerment which We have not given you, and We gave them hearing to hear with a vision to see with, and hearts to understand with. But their hearing did not benefit them or their imagination or intellect. They did not repel Allah's punishment from them when it came to them, as they used to disbelieve in Allah's signs and the punishment which they used to mock and which their prophet Hūd (peace be upon) used to warn them of, finally befell them.

(٢٧) وَلَقَدۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا مَا حَوۡلَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ وَصَرَّفۡنَا ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ

And We destroyed the cities around you, O people of Mecca; We destroyed 'Ād, Thamūd, the people of Lot, and the people of Midian. We gave them different proof and evidence in the hope they would turn back from their disbelief.

(٢٨) فَلَوۡلَا نَصَرَهُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ قُرۡبَانًا ءَالِهَةَۢۖ بَلۡ ضَلُّواْ عَنۡهُمۡۚ وَذَٰلِكَ إِفۡكُهُمۡ وَمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ

So why did their idols not help them? The idols they took as gods besides Allah, and they used to seek closeness through worship and sacrifice. They did not help them; rather, they disappeared when they needed them the most. And that is the lie and fabrication they invented, i.e., that these idols will benefit and intercede for them with Allah.

Points of Benefit:

The messengers have no knowledge of the unseen except for what their Lord has disclosed to them from Him.
The people of Hūd were deluded when they thought that the impending punishment was merely rain, and they did not repent before it seized them unexpectedly.
The might of the people of 'Ād surpassed that of the Quraysh, yet Allah destroyed them.
The wise person learns from the admonition of others, while the ignorant one is he who takes a lesson from himself.
