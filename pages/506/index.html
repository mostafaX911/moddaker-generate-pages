(٢٩) وَإِذۡ صَرَفۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ نَفَرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ يَسۡتَمِعُونَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ فَلَمَّا حَضَرُوهُ قَالُوٓاْ أَنصِتُواْۖ فَلَمَّا قُضِيَ وَلَّوۡاْ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِم مُّنذِرِينَ

Remember, O Messenger, when I sent you a group of the jinn to listen to the Quran that was revealed to you? When they came to hear it, they said to one another: Be quiet so that we can listen to it. When the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم completed his recitation, they returned to their people to warn them of Allah's punishment if they did not believe in this Quran.

(٣٠) قَالُواْ يَٰقَوۡمَنَآ إِنَّا سَمِعۡنَا كِتَٰبًا أُنزِلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مُوسَىٰ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ يَهۡدِيٓ إِلَى ٱلۡحَقِّ وَإِلَىٰ طَرِيقٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

They said to them: O our people, we have heard a book that Allah has revealed after Moses, confirming the previous scriptures revealed by Allah. This book we heard guides us to the truth and refers to a straight path, which is the path of Islam.

(٣١) يَٰقَوۡمَنَآ أَجِيبُواْ دَاعِيَ ٱللَّهِ وَءَامِنُواْ بِهِۦ يَغۡفِرۡ لَكُم مِّن ذُنُوبِكُمۡ وَيُجِرۡكُم مِّنۡ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ

O our people, respond to the truth that Muhammad calls you towards and believe he is a messenger from his Lord. Allah will forgive your sins and save you from the painful torment that awaits you if you do not respond to the truth that he calls you towards and do not believe that he is a messenger from his Lord.

(٣٢) وَمَن لَّا يُجِبۡ دَاعِيَ ٱللَّهِ فَلَيۡسَ بِمُعۡجِزٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَيۡسَ لَهُۥ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوۡلِيَآءُۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٍ

Those who do not respond to the truth that Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم calls them towards will not be able to escape from Allah by fleeing. They will not have, besides Allah, any protectors to rescue them from the punishment. Such people have deviated from the truth.

(٣٣) أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَلَمۡ يَعۡيَ بِخَلۡقِهِنَّ بِقَٰدِرٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يُحۡـِۧيَ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰۚ بَلَىٰٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

Do these polytheists who deny the Resurrection not understand that Allah, Who created the heavens and the Earth, and did not tire in creating them despite their hugeness and vastness, can give life to the dead for reckoning and recompense? Indeed, He can bring them back to life. He (may He be Exalted) is Omnipotent over everything; He is not hindered from bringing the dead to life.

(٣٤) وَيَوۡمَ يُعۡرَضُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ أَلَيۡسَ هَٰذَا بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ قَالُواْ بَلَىٰ وَرَبِّنَاۚ قَالَ فَذُوقُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡفُرُونَ

On the Day when those who rejected Allah and His messengers will be exposed to the Fire to be punished, it will be said to them in rebuke: Is this punishment that you see not real? Or is it a lie like you used to say in the world? They will say: Indeed, by Our Lord, it is the truth. It will then be said to them: Taste the punishment for disbelieving in Allah.

(٣٥) فَٱصۡبِرۡ كَمَا صَبَرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡعَزۡمِ مِنَ ٱلرُّسُلِ وَلَا تَسۡتَعۡجِل لَّهُمۡۚ كَأَنَّهُمۡ يَوۡمَ يَرَوۡنَ مَا يُوعَدُونَ لَمۡ يَلۡبَثُوٓاْ إِلَّا سَاعَةٗ مِّن نَّهَارِۢۚ بَلَٰغٞۚ فَهَلۡ يُهۡلَكُ إِلَّا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ

So be patient, O Messenger, with the rejection of your people just as the Messengers of Resolve were patient. These were the messengers in addition to our Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم: Noah, Abraham, Moses, and Jesus (peace be upon them). Do not seek to hasten the punishment for them. The deniers from your people, on the day they will see the punishment you warned about in the Hereafter, it will be as if they did not live in this world except for an hour of a day due to the length of their punishment. This Quran revealed to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم is a warning to all people and the jinn, and only those who go against Allah by disbelief and sins will be destroyed by the punishment.

Points of Benefit:

One aspect of good manners is to listen attentively to the speaker and give heed to them.
The swift response of the guided among the jinn to the truth is a compelling message to humankind.
Responding to the truth necessitates hastening in calling others to it.
Patience is the character of the prophets (peace be upon them).
