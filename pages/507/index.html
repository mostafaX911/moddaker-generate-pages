Muhammad (Surah Muhammad)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Inspiring the believers to fight in order to strengthen them and weaken the disbelievers.

Explanation:

(١) ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَصَدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ أَضَلَّ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ

Those who reject Allah and turn people away from Allah's religion, Allah will nullify their deeds.

(٢) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَءَامَنُواْ بِمَا نُزِّلَ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّهِمۡ كَفَّرَ عَنۡهُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِهِمۡ وَأَصۡلَحَ بَالَهُمۡ

And those who believe in Allah, do righteous deeds and believe in what Allah has revealed to His Messenger Muhammad, and what He has revealed to His Messenger Muhammad is the truth from their Lord, Will remit their wrongdoings and won't take them to the task. He will set right their affairs in this world and the Hereafter.

(٣) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ٱتَّبَعُواْ ٱلۡبَٰطِلَ وَأَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّبَعُواْ ٱلۡحَقَّ مِن رَّبِّهِمۡۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ لِلنَّاسِ أَمۡثَٰلَهُمۡ

That mentioned recompense for the two groups is because those who reject Allah follow falsehood, and those who believe in Allah and His Messenger follow the truth from their Lord. Thus, their recompense differs just as their efforts differ. Just as Allah showed His decision about the two groups, believers and disbelievers, Allah gives people their examples so that similarities can be matched.

(٤) فَإِذَا لَقِيتُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فَضَرۡبَ ٱلرِّقَابِ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَثۡخَنتُمُوهُمۡ فَشُدُّواْ ٱلۡوَثَاقَ فَإِمَّا مَنَّۢا بَعۡدُ وَإِمَّا فِدَآءً حَتَّىٰ تَضَعَ ٱلۡحَرۡبُ أَوۡزَارَهَاۚ ذَٰلِكَۖ وَلَوۡ يَشَآءُ ٱللَّهُ لَٱنتَصَرَ مِنۡهُمۡ وَلَٰكِن لِّيَبۡلُوَاْ بَعۡضَكُم بِبَعۡضٖۗ وَٱلَّذِينَ قُتِلُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَلَن يُضِلَّ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ

O believers, when you come face-to-face with those disbelievers who wage war against you, strike their necks with your swords and persevere in fighting them until you subdue them and eradicate their power. Once you have stopped them, tie the chains of the captives. When you capture them, as dictated by common interests [of the Muslims], you can be graceful to them by releasing them for no ransom, ransoming them for wealth or something else, killing them, or enslaving them. Continue fighting and capturing them until the war ends by the disbelievers accepting Islam or by your entering into a treaty with them. The mentioned trial of the believers with the disbelievers, the alternation of fortunes, and the victory of one over the other is Allah's decision. If Allah willed to defeat the disbelievers without a fight, He would have done so, but He ordained striving in His path to test some of you with others. He pushes who of the believers fight and who doesn't. He tries the disbeliever with the believer: If the believer is killed, he enters Paradise; if the believer kills him, he enters the Fire. Those who are killed in Allah's path, Allah will never let their deeds go to waste.

(٥) سَيَهۡدِيهِمۡ وَيُصۡلِحُ بَالَهُمۡ

He will enable them to follow the truth and set their affairs right in their worldly life.

(٦) وَيُدۡخِلُهُمُ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ عَرَّفَهَا لَهُمۡ

He will enter them into Paradise on the day of resurrection, whose description He gave them in the world, so they recognized it and showed them their places in the Hereafter.

(٧) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن تَنصُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ يَنصُرۡكُمۡ وَيُثَبِّتۡ أَقۡدَامَكُمۡ

O you who believe in Allah and act on what He legislated for them, if you help Allah by helping His Prophet and His religion and fighting the disbelievers, He will help you by granting you dominance over them. He will keep your feet firm in battle when you face them.

(٨) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فَتَعۡسٗا لَّهُمۡ وَأَضَلَّ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ

For those who disbelieve in Allah and His Messenger, there will be loss and destruction, and Allah will nullify the reward of their deeds.

(٩) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ كَرِهُواْ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ فَأَحۡبَطَ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ

That punishment will befall them because they detested the Quran Allah revealed to His Prophet. After all, it contained the mention of Allah's Oneness, so Allah destroyed their deeds, and they were losers in this world and the Hereafter.

(١٠) ۞أَفَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَيَنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۖ دَمَّرَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِمۡۖ وَلِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ أَمۡثَٰلُهَا

Have these deniers not traveled the Earth and reflected on the ultimate demise of those who had denied before them? It was a painful end. Allah destroyed their homes above them and killed them and their children and wealth. Disbelievers in every time and place will receive similar punishment.

(١١) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَوۡلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَأَنَّ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ لَا مَوۡلَىٰ لَهُمۡ

That mentioned recompense is for the two groups because Allah helps those who have faith in Him, and the disbelievers have no one to help them.

Points of Benefit:

Expressing disdain for the enemy through lethal means is an exemplary method of subjugating them.
The bestowal of favor, ransom, killing, and enslavement are options in Islam for dealing with captive disbelievers, selecting from them what serves the interest.
The immense virtue of martyrdom in the path of Allah.
Allah's support for the believers is contingent upon their support for His religion.
