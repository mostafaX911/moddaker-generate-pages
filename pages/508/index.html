(١٢) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُدۡخِلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يَتَمَتَّعُونَ وَيَأۡكُلُونَ كَمَا تَأۡكُلُ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمُ وَٱلنَّارُ مَثۡوٗى لَّهُمۡ

Allah will enter those who believe in Allah and His Messenger and who do righteous deeds into gardens beneath whose palaces and trees rivers will flow. Those who reject Allah and His Messenger enjoy the world by pursuing their desires and eating like cattle eat. They have no concerns other than their stomachs and private parts. On the day of resurrection, they will go to the Fire, their final abode.

(١٣) وَكَأَيِّن مِّن قَرۡيَةٍ هِيَ أَشَدُّ قُوَّةٗ مِّن قَرۡيَتِكَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَخۡرَجَتۡكَ أَهۡلَكۡنَٰهُمۡ فَلَا نَاصِرَ لَهُمۡ

We have destroyed many towns of the previous nations with more strength, wealth, and children than Mecca, whose people evicted you from it when they denied their messengers, and they had no helper to rescue them from Allah's punishment when it came to them. Thus, nothing can prevent Us from destroying the people of Mecca if We intend to do so.

(١٤) أَفَمَن كَانَ عَلَىٰ بَيِّنَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ كَمَن زُيِّنَ لَهُۥ سُوٓءُ عَمَلِهِۦ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓاْ أَهۡوَآءَهُم

Are those who have clear proof and glaring evidence from their Lord and therefore worship Him with insight the same as those whom the Devil has beautified their evil actions? They followed what their desires dictated to them by worshipping idols, taking on sin, and denying the messengers.

(١٥) مَّثَلُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلَّتِي وُعِدَ ٱلۡمُتَّقُونَۖ فِيهَآ أَنۡهَٰرٞ مِّن مَّآءٍ غَيۡرِ ءَاسِنٖ وَأَنۡهَٰرٞ مِّن لَّبَنٖ لَّمۡ يَتَغَيَّرۡ طَعۡمُهُۥ وَأَنۡهَٰرٞ مِّنۡ خَمۡرٖ لَّذَّةٖ لِّلشَّٰرِبِينَ وَأَنۡهَٰرٞ مِّنۡ عَسَلٖ مُّصَفّٗىۖ وَلَهُمۡ فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ وَمَغۡفِرَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡۖ كَمَنۡ هُوَ خَٰلِدٞ فِي ٱلنَّارِ وَسُقُواْ مَآءً حَمِيمٗا فَقَطَّعَ أَمۡعَآءَهُمۡ

The description of Paradise that Allah promises those who fear Him by fulfilling His instructions and avoiding His prohibitions is as follows: There are rivers of water whose smell and taste have not changed over time. In it, there are rivers of milk whose taste has not changed. There are rivers of wine that are delicious to those who drink and rivers of honey free from all impurities. Therein, they will get all types of fruit that they wish for. Above all, they will receive an expiation of their sins from Allah, who will not take them to account. Can the person with this recompense be equal to those who will remain in the Fire, never to come out, and who will be given boiling water to drink, which will cut the intestines of their stomachs due to its extreme heat?

(١٦) وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَسۡتَمِعُ إِلَيۡكَ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا خَرَجُواْ مِنۡ عِندِكَ قَالُواْ لِلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ مَاذَا قَالَ ءَانِفًاۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ طَبَعَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓاْ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡ

Some of the hypocrites listen to you, O Messenger, but without acceptance and instead turn away until when they leave your presence, they say to those whom Allah had given knowledge: What did he just say? They pretend to be ignorant and turn away. They are the ones whose hearts Allah has sealed, and thus no good reaches it, and they follow their desires, which have made them blind to the truth.

(١٧) وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱهۡتَدَوۡاْ زَادَهُمۡ هُدٗى وَءَاتَىٰهُمۡ تَقۡوَىٰهُمۡ

Those guided to the path of truth and to follow what the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم brought, their Lord will increase them in guidance and direction to all good, and He inspires them to do that which will protect them from the Fire.

(١٨) فَهَلۡ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّا ٱلسَّاعَةَ أَن تَأۡتِيَهُم بَغۡتَةٗۖ فَقَدۡ جَآءَ أَشۡرَاطُهَاۚ فَأَنَّىٰ لَهُمۡ إِذَا جَآءَتۡهُمۡ ذِكۡرَىٰهُمۡ

Are the disbelievers waiting for anything but the hour to come on them suddenly without them having any prior knowledge of it? Its signs have already arrived, such as the mission of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم and the splitting of the moon. How will they then take heed when the hour comes? It is unlikely that the reminder will be of benefit to them.

(١٩) فَٱعۡلَمۡ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لِذَنۢبِكَ وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مُتَقَلَّبَكُمۡ وَمَثۡوَىٰكُمۡ

Have certainty, O Messenger, that there is no being deserving of worship besides Allah, and seek forgiveness for your sins from Allah. Seek forgiveness from Him for the sins of the believing men and women. Allah knows your movements during the day and your resting place at night. Nothing is hidden from Him.

Points of Benefit:

The sole concern of the disbeliever is limited to indulging in transient pleasures in this worldly life.
The comparison between the reward of the believers and the recompense of the disbelievers illustrates the vast difference between them; thus, the wise chooses to be a believer, while the foolish opts for disbelief.
Manifestation of the discourteous behavior of the hypocrites towards the Messenger of Allah, صلى الله عليه وسلم.
Knowledge precedes speech and action.
