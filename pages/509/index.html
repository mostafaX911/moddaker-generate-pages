(٢٠) وَيَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَوۡلَا نُزِّلَتۡ سُورَةٞۖ فَإِذَآ أُنزِلَتۡ سُورَةٞ مُّحۡكَمَةٞ وَذُكِرَ فِيهَا ٱلۡقِتَالُ رَأَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٞ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَيۡكَ نَظَرَ ٱلۡمَغۡشِيِّ عَلَيۡهِ مِنَ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۖ فَأَوۡلَىٰ لَهُمۡ

Those who believe in Allah say, longing for Allah to reveal to His Messenger a surah containing the instruction for fighting: Why does Allah not reveal a surah in which fighting is mentioned? When Allah reveals a clear surah containing the mention of fighting, you, O Messenger, will see those hypocrites in whose hearts there is doubt looking at you like one unconscious on account of extreme fear and terror. So, woe to them for withdrawing from fighting and being afraid of it.

(٢١) طَاعَةٞ وَقَوۡلٞ مَّعۡرُوفٞۚ فَإِذَا عَزَمَ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ فَلَوۡ صَدَقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَكَانَ خَيۡرٗا لَّهُمۡ

They should follow Allah's instruction and speak only good words that is better for them. So, when fighting is imposed on them, and the matter becomes severe, if they are true to Allah, having faith in Him and following Him, it would be better for them than hypocrisy and going against Allah's instructions.

(٢٢) فَهَلۡ عَسَيۡتُمۡ إِن تَوَلَّيۡتُمۡ أَن تُفۡسِدُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَتُقَطِّعُوٓاْ أَرۡحَامَكُمۡ

If you turn away from believing in Allah and following Him, it is most likely that you will make mischief on Earth by disbelief and sins and cut family ties, just as you used to do pre-Islam.

(٢٣) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَعَنَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ فَأَصَمَّهُمۡ وَأَعۡمَىٰٓ أَبۡصَٰرَهُمۡ

Those who are described as mischief makers on Earth and cutting family ties are the ones whom Allah has distanced from His mercy. He has made their ears deaf to hearing the truth with acceptance and submission and has prevented their sight from seeing it while reflecting on its meanings.

(٢٤) أَفَلَا يَتَدَبَّرُونَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ أَمۡ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبٍ أَقۡفَالُهَآ

Will these people who turn away not ponder the Quran and reflect on what is in it? If they consider it, it will guide them to every good and distance them from evil. Or are there locks on the hearts of these people that are tightly closed so that no advice reaches them and no reminder benefits them?

(٢٥) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱرۡتَدُّواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَدۡبَٰرِهِم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا تَبَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡهُدَى ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ سَوَّلَ لَهُمۡ وَأَمۡلَىٰ لَهُمۡ

Those who turn back from their belief towards disbelief and hypocrisy after the proof has been established against them and the truth of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم has become clear to them; the Devil is the one who has beautified their disbelief and hypocrisy and has raised their vain hopes.

(٢٦) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَالُواْ لِلَّذِينَ كَرِهُواْ مَا نَزَّلَ ٱللَّهُ سَنُطِيعُكُمۡ فِي بَعۡضِ ٱلۡأَمۡرِۖ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ إِسۡرَارَهُمۡ

They became misled because they secretly told the polytheists who hated what Allah had revealed to His Messenger: we will obey you in some matters, such as discouragement from fighting [for the Cause of Allah]. Allah knows everything they hide and their innermost intentions; nothing is hidden from Him. And He exposes what He wishes of their hidden intentions to His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم.

(٢٧) فَكَيۡفَ إِذَا تَوَفَّتۡهُمُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ يَضۡرِبُونَ وُجُوهَهُمۡ وَأَدۡبَٰرَهُمۡ

How horrific do you think their state will be, and the severity of their punishment when the appointed angels remove their souls, striking their faces and backs with steel hammers?

(٢٨) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمُ ٱتَّبَعُواْ مَآ أَسۡخَطَ ٱللَّهَ وَكَرِهُواْ رِضۡوَٰنَهُۥ فَأَحۡبَطَ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ

The severe punishment will occur when their souls are removed because they followed everything that brought Allah's wrath on them, such as disbelief, hypocrisy, and opposition to Allah and His messenger. They hated what would have gotten them closer to their Lord and gained them His pleasure, such as believing in Allah and following His Messenger. Thus, Allah nullify their deeds.

(٢٩) أَمۡ حَسِبَ ٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٌ أَن لَّن يُخۡرِجَ ٱللَّهُ أَضۡغَٰنَهُمۡ

Do those hypocrites who harbor doubts in their hearts think Allah will not bring out and expose their malice? He will undoubtedly bring it out by testing them with trials to distinguish the person who is truthful in belief from the liar. The believer will become apparent, and the hypocrite will be exposed.

Points of Benefit:

The assignment to engage in jihad for the sake of Allah distinguishes the hypocrites from the ranks of the believers.
The significance of reflecting upon the Book of Allah and the peril of turning away from it.
Corruption in the land and severing the ties of kinship are among the reasons for the scarcity of success and distancing oneself from the mercy of Allah.
