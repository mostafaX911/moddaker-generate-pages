(١٠) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَن تُغۡنِيَ عَنۡهُمۡ أَمۡوَٰلُهُمۡ وَلَآ أَوۡلَٰدُهُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمۡ وَقُودُ ٱلنَّارِ

The wealth and children of those disbelieving in Allah and His messengers will not protect them from Allah's torment in this life or the Hereafter. On the contrary, such disbelievers will be the fuel that Hell will be lit with on the Day of Resurrection.

(١١) كَدَأۡبِ ءَالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا فَأَخَذَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِذُنُوبِهِمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ

The matter of these disbelievers is like that of the people of Pharaoh and those who disbelieved in Allah and rejected His signs before them. As a result, Allah tormented and punished them for their sins – their wealth and children did not benefit them. Consequently, Allah severely punishes those disbelieving in Him and rejecting His signs.

(١٢) قُل لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ سَتُغۡلَبُونَ وَتُحۡشَرُونَ إِلَىٰ جَهَنَّمَۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمِهَادُ

O Messenger, say to those who disbelieve, regardless of their religion: The believers will defeat you, and you will die as disbelievers. Allah will then gather you in the Fire, and what an evil resting place it will be for you!

(١٣) قَدۡ كَانَ لَكُمۡ ءَايَةٞ فِي فِئَتَيۡنِ ٱلۡتَقَتَاۖ فِئَةٞ تُقَٰتِلُ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَأُخۡرَىٰ كَافِرَةٞ يَرَوۡنَهُم مِّثۡلَيۡهِمۡ رَأۡيَ ٱلۡعَيۡنِۚ وَٱللَّهُ يُؤَيِّدُ بِنَصۡرِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَعِبۡرَةٗ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرِ

There was a clear sign and lesson in the two groups that met to fight in the Battle of Badr. One group was made up of believers, and this was the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم and his Companions: They were fighting in the path of Allah and to elevate His Word over the word of those who disbelieve. The other group consisted of the disbelievers of Mecca, who had come out in pride and arrogance. The believers saw the disbelievers as double their number, but Allah helps His allies and supports whoever He wishes. In that, there is a lesson and a warning for those who have the insight to realize that the help of Allah can come to the believers even though they may be few in number and that defeat can come to those upon falsehood even though they may be more in number.

(١٤) زُيِّنَ لِلنَّاسِ حُبُّ ٱلشَّهَوَٰتِ مِنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ وَٱلۡبَنِينَ وَٱلۡقَنَٰطِيرِ ٱلۡمُقَنطَرَةِ مِنَ ٱلذَّهَبِ وَٱلۡفِضَّةِ وَٱلۡخَيۡلِ ٱلۡمُسَوَّمَةِ وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ وَٱلۡحَرۡثِۗ ذَٰلِكَ مَتَٰعُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَٱللَّهُ عِندَهُۥ حُسۡنُ ٱلۡمَ‍َٔابِ

Allah mentions that He has made the love of desirable things in this world attractive to people to test them. These desirable things include women, children, abundant gold and silver piled up, beautifully trained horses, livestock such as camels, cows, sheep, and farmlands – all of which are fleeting pleasures of this life lasting only a few short moments. Therefore, a believer should not become attached to these things as Allah alone has the best place of return – Paradise, which is as vast as the heavens and the earth.

Upon mentioning how the desires of this world become cut off, Allah draws attention to what was better, stating:

(١٥) ۞قُلۡ أَؤُنَبِّئُكُم بِخَيۡرٖ مِّن ذَٰلِكُمۡۖ لِلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ جَنَّٰتٞ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا وَأَزۡوَٰجٞ مُّطَهَّرَةٞ وَرِضۡوَٰنٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ بَصِيرُۢ بِٱلۡعِبَادِ

Say (to the people), O Messenger: Should I inform you about something better than those desirable things? Those who fear Allah by obeying Him and avoid disobeying Him will have gardens with trees and palaces beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will live forever. They will also have wives that are void of any defects in their form and character. Moreover, they will gain the pleasure of Allah, and Allah will never be angry with them. Allah sees the conditions of His slaves; nothing is hidden from Him, and He will recompense them for it accordingly.

Points of Benefit:

That the pride of the disbelievers in their wealth and children will not avail them on the Day of Resurrection against the punishment of Allah when it descends upon them.
Victory is a reality not merely contingent on numbers and preparations but on the support and aid of Allah, the Exalted.
Allah, the Exalted, adorned various worldly desires for people to test them and to make it known who among them adheres to His limits and who transgresses them.
All the pleasures and delights of this world are fleeting and limited and not comparable to the immense and everlasting blessings in the Hereafter.
