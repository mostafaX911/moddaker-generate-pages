(٣٠) وَلَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَأَرَيۡنَٰكَهُمۡ فَلَعَرَفۡتَهُم بِسِيمَٰهُمۡۚ وَلَتَعۡرِفَنَّهُمۡ فِي لَحۡنِ ٱلۡقَوۡلِۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ أَعۡمَٰلَكُمۡ

If We wished to inform you of the hypocrites, O Messenger, We would have shown them to you, and you would have recognized them by their marks. You will soon recognize them by their manner of speech. Allah knows your deeds; nothing is hidden from Him, and He will recompense you.

(٣١) وَلَنَبۡلُوَنَّكُمۡ حَتَّىٰ نَعۡلَمَ ٱلۡمُجَٰهِدِينَ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ وَنَبۡلُوَاْ أَخۡبَارَكُمۡ

We will test you, O believers, with striving in Allah's path and fighting and killing the enemy until it becomes apparent, those of you who strive in Allah's path and those who are steadfast in fighting the enemy. We will test you so that We know the truthful person from the liar.

(٣٢) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَصَدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَشَآقُّواْ ٱلرَّسُولَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا تَبَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡهُدَىٰ لَن يَضُرُّواْ ٱللَّهَ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَسَيُحۡبِطُ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ

Those who reject Allah and His messenger, who abstain from Allah's religion themselves and also prevent others from it, and who go against the Messenger and oppose him after it has become clear that he is a prophet, they do not harm Allah. They only harm themselves, and Allah will nullify their deeds.

(٣٣) ۞يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱلرَّسُولَ وَلَا تُبۡطِلُوٓاْ أَعۡمَٰلَكُمۡ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated, follow Allah and follow the Messenger by fulfilling their instructions and avoiding their prohibitions, and do not nullify your deeds by disbelief and ostentation.

(٣٤) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَصَدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ مَاتُواْ وَهُمۡ كُفَّارٞ فَلَن يَغۡفِرَ ٱللَّهُ لَهُمۡ

Those who disbelieve in Allah and turn themselves and people away from Allah's religion and then perish as disbelievers without repenting; Allah will not overlook their sins by covering them. Instead, He will take them to task for it and enter them into the Fire to remain there forever.

(٣٥) فَلَا تَهِنُواْ وَتَدۡعُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱلسَّلۡمِ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَوۡنَ وَٱللَّهُ مَعَكُمۡ وَلَن يَتِرَكُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلَكُمۡ

So do not become weak, O believers, when facing your enemy, and call them to peace before they call you to it when you are dominant and have the upper hand over them. Allah is close to you with His help and support. He will not reduce the reward of your good deeds at all. Instead, He will increase you out of His bounty.

(٣٦) إِنَّمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لَعِبٞ وَلَهۡوٞۚ وَإِن تُؤۡمِنُواْ وَتَتَّقُواْ يُؤۡتِكُمۡ أُجُورَكُمۡ وَلَا يَسۡ‍َٔلۡكُمۡ أَمۡوَٰلَكُمۡ

The life of the world is only idle play and amusement. An intelligent person should not become preoccupied with it away from striving for his Hereafter. If you believe in Allah and His messenger and fear Allah by fulfilling His instructions and avoiding His prohibitions, He will give you the reward of your deeds in full without any reduction, and He will not ask you for all your wealth. He will only ask you for the Zakat.

(٣٧) إِن يَسۡ‍َٔلۡكُمُوهَا فَيُحۡفِكُمۡ تَبۡخَلُواْ وَيُخۡرِجۡ أَضۡغَٰنَكُمۡ

If He had to ask you for all of your wealth and insist on this request from you, you would become miserly with it, and He would bring out the hatred to spend in the way of Allah from your hearts. Thus, He is not asking it from you out of kindness.

(٣٨) هَٰٓأَنتُمۡ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ تُدۡعَوۡنَ لِتُنفِقُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَمِنكُم مَّن يَبۡخَلُۖ وَمَن يَبۡخَلۡ فَإِنَّمَا يَبۡخَلُ عَن نَّفۡسِهِۦۚ وَٱللَّهُ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلۡفُقَرَآءُۚ وَإِن تَتَوَلَّوۡاْ يَسۡتَبۡدِلۡ قَوۡمًا غَيۡرَكُمۡ ثُمَّ لَا يَكُونُوٓاْ أَمۡثَٰلَكُم

Here, you are being called to spend a part of your wealth in Allah's path, and you are not asked to spend it all. Some of you desist from the required spending out of miserliness. Whoever is too miserly to pay a portion of his wealth in Allah's path is being miserly to his own detriment, as he deprives himself of the reward of spending. Allah is Self-sufficient and does not need your spending. You are the ones in need of Him. If you turn back from Islam to disbelief, He will destroy you and bring about another people beside you. Then they will not be like you; rather, they will be obedient to Him.

Points of Benefit:

The secrets and deceit of the hypocrites manifest in their facial features and in the manner of their speech.
Testing is an established divine practice to distinguish the believers from the hypocrites.
Allah's support for His believing servants comes in the form of victory and steadfast guidance.
One aspect of Allah's kindness to His servants is that He does not demand them to spend all their wealth in the path of Allah.
