The Victory (Surah Al-Fath)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Giving the Prophet and the believers glad tidings of triumph and empowerment.

Explanation:

(١) إِنَّا فَتَحۡنَا لَكَ فَتۡحٗا مُّبِينٗا

I have given you, O Messenger, a clear victory by the Treaty of Hudaybiyah.

(٢) لِّيَغۡفِرَ لَكَ ٱللَّهُ مَا تَقَدَّمَ مِن ذَنۢبِكَ وَمَا تَأَخَّرَ وَيُتِمَّ نِعۡمَتَهُۥ عَلَيۡكَ وَيَهۡدِيَكَ صِرَٰطٗا مُّسۡتَقِيمٗا

So that Allah may forgive you for your sins, those that occurred previous to this victory and those that will occur in the future and complete His favor on you by assisting your religion and guiding you to a straight path in which there is no crookedness, which is the straight path of Islam.

(٣) وَيَنصُرَكَ ٱللَّهُ نَصۡرًا عَزِيزًا

Allah will help you against your enemies with formidable support that no one can repel.

(٤) هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ ٱلسَّكِينَةَ فِي قُلُوبِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ لِيَزۡدَادُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنٗا مَّعَ إِيمَٰنِهِمۡۗ وَلِلَّهِ جُنُودُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمٗا

Allah is the One who sent down [the feeling of] stability and tranquility into the hearts of the believers so that they increase their belief. To Allah alone belongs the forces of the heavens and the earth; he supports with them whoever of His slaves He wishes. Allah knows what is in the best interests of His slaves, and He is Wise in the assistance and support He gives.

(٥) لِّيُدۡخِلَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا وَيُكَفِّرَ عَنۡهُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِهِمۡۚ وَكَانَ ذَٰلِكَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ فَوۡزًا عَظِيمٗا

To enter those men and women who believe in Allah and His Messenger into gardens with streams flowing under their palaces and trees and wiping away their wrongdoings from them and not taking them to account for it. What has been mentioned about attaining the objective of Paradise and removing the fear of accountability in the sight of Allah is an unparalleled success.

(٦) وَيُعَذِّبَ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ وَٱلۡمُشۡرِكَٰتِ ٱلظَّآنِّينَ بِٱللَّهِ ظَنَّ ٱلسَّوۡءِۚ عَلَيۡهِمۡ دَآئِرَةُ ٱلسَّوۡءِۖ وَغَضِبَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَعَنَهُمۡ وَأَعَدَّ لَهُمۡ جَهَنَّمَۖ وَسَآءَتۡ مَصِيرٗا

And to punish the hypocrites, male and female, and to punish those men and women who ascribe partners to Allah, and those who think that Allah will not assist His religion and His word will not be raised and that fortune will turn against the believers. Punishment befell them, and Allah became angry with them because of their disbelief and evil thoughts, and He expelled them from His mercy and prepared for them in the Afterlife Hell, which they will enter to remain there forever. What a miserable place of return for them!

(٧) وَلِلَّهِ جُنُودُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَزِيزًا حَكِيمًا

And to Allah belong the soldiers of the heavens and the earth, with which He supports whomever of His slaves He wills, and Allah is All-Mighty, and no one can overcome Him, Most-Wise in His creation, decrees, and administration of affairs.

(٨) إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ شَٰهِدٗا وَمُبَشِّرٗا وَنَذِيرٗا

O Messenger, We have sent you as a witness to give testimony against your nation on the day of resurrection and as one to give good news to the believers of the victory and empowerment Allah has prepared for them in this world and of the bliss He has prepared for them in the Hereafter, and to warn the disbelievers of the humiliation and defeat at the hands of the believers that He has prepared for them in this world, and the painful punishment awaiting them that He has prepared for them in the Hereafter.

(٩) لِّتُؤۡمِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَتُعَزِّرُوهُ وَتُوَقِّرُوهُۚ وَتُسَبِّحُوهُ بُكۡرَةٗ وَأَصِيلًا

In the hope that you will believe in Allah and His Messenger, revere and honor His messenger, and exalt Allah at the beginning and end of the day.

Points of Benefit:

The Treaty of Hudaybiyah marked the beginning of a great triumph for Islam and the Muslims.
Tranquility, one of the effects of belief, instills a sense of peace and steadfastness.
The danger of harboring ill thoughts about Allah, for indeed, Allah deals with people according to their perception of Him, Exalted is He.
The obligation of venerating and honoring the Messenger of Allah, صلى الله عليه وسلم.
