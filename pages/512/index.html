(١٠) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُبَايِعُونَكَ إِنَّمَا يُبَايِعُونَ ٱللَّهَ يَدُ ٱللَّهِ فَوۡقَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡۚ فَمَن نَّكَثَ فَإِنَّمَا يَنكُثُ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَنۡ أَوۡفَىٰ بِمَا عَٰهَدَ عَلَيۡهُ ٱللَّهَ فَسَيُؤۡتِيهِ أَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا

Those of the people of Mecca who pledged allegiance to you, O Messenger, during the Pledge of Satisfaction to fight the Meccan polytheists are pledging allegiance to Allah. Allah is the One who ordered them to fight the polytheists, and He is the One who rewards them. The Hand of Allah is above their hands at the time of giving the pledge, and He is aware of them, and nothing is hidden from Him. So, he who breaches his pledge and fails to fulfill the promise he made to Allah in terms of supporting His religion shall bear the harm of breaking his pledge. Allah is not harmed by this, and whoever fulfills what he promised Allah in terms of supporting His religion, He will give him a great reward, which is Paradise.

(١١) سَيَقُولُ لَكَ ٱلۡمُخَلَّفُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَعۡرَابِ شَغَلَتۡنَآ أَمۡوَٰلُنَا وَأَهۡلُونَا فَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَنَاۚ يَقُولُونَ بِأَلۡسِنَتِهِم مَّا لَيۡسَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمۡۚ قُلۡ فَمَن يَمۡلِكُ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡ‍ًٔا إِنۡ أَرَادَ بِكُمۡ ضَرًّا أَوۡ أَرَادَ بِكُمۡ نَفۡعَۢاۚ بَلۡ كَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرَۢا

Those desert Arabs whom Allah kept behind from accompanying you, O Messenger, on your journey to His House will say to you when you reproach them: Taking care of our wealth and looking after our children kept us busy from going out with you. So, ask for forgiveness from Allah on our behalf for our sins. They ask what is not in their hearts with their tongues by asking the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم to seek forgiveness for them; they have not repented their sins. Say to them: No one can do anything for you against Allah if He intends good or bad for you. Instead, Allah is well aware of what you do. Nothing of your actions is hidden from Him, no matter how much you try to conceal it.

(١٢) بَلۡ ظَنَنتُمۡ أَن لَّن يَنقَلِبَ ٱلرَّسُولُ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِيهِمۡ أَبَدٗا وَزُيِّنَ ذَٰلِكَ فِي قُلُوبِكُمۡ وَظَنَنتُمۡ ظَنَّ ٱلسَّوۡءِ وَكُنتُمۡ قَوۡمَۢا بُورٗا

The reason for your failure to join the Messenger in combat is not the excuse you gave him of being busy caring for your possessions and children. Rather, you thought that the Messenger and his companions would all perish and would not return to their families in Madīnah. The Devil beautified this in your hearts. Your thoughts led you to think wickedly about your Lord: that He would not help His Prophet. You became a doomed people because of your negative thoughts about Allah and because you fell behind from joining His Messenger [in battle].

(١٣) وَمَن لَّمۡ يُؤۡمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ فَإِنَّآ أَعۡتَدۡنَا لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ سَعِيرٗا

The person who does not believe in Allah and His Messenger is a disbeliever. We have prepared on the Day of Resurrection for those who deny Allah a blazing fire by which they will be punished.

(١٤) وَلِلَّهِ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ يَغۡفِرُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيُعَذِّبُ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا

To Allah alone belongs the control of the heavens and the earth. He forgives the sins of those of His slaves whom He wills and enters them into Paradise through His Grace. He torments those of His slaves whom He wills out of His Justice. Allah forgives the sins of those of His slaves who repent to Him, and He is merciful towards them.

(١٥) سَيَقُولُ ٱلۡمُخَلَّفُونَ إِذَا ٱنطَلَقۡتُمۡ إِلَىٰ مَغَانِمَ لِتَأۡخُذُوهَا ذَرُونَا نَتَّبِعۡكُمۡۖ يُرِيدُونَ أَن يُبَدِّلُواْ كَلَٰمَ ٱللَّهِۚ قُل لَّن تَتَّبِعُونَا كَذَٰلِكُمۡ قَالَ ٱللَّهُ مِن قَبۡلُۖ فَسَيَقُولُونَ بَلۡ تَحۡسُدُونَنَاۚ بَلۡ كَانُواْ لَا يَفۡقَهُونَ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

Those whom Allah kept behind, when you, O believers, set off to take the spoils of Khaybar which Allah had promised you after the Treaty of Hudaybiyah, will say: Let us go out with you to get some of it. These people who were left behind want to change, by their request, Allah's promise that He made to the believers after the Treaty of Hudaybiyah to give them alone the spoils of Khaybar. Say to them, O Messenger: You will never follow us to those spoils because Allah has promised us that the seizures of Khaybar are only for those present at Hudaybiyah. They will say: Your preventing us from following you to Khaybar is not an instruction from Allah, but because you are jealous of us. The matter is not as these people who were left behind claim. Instead, they do not understand Allah's instructions and prohibitions except a little, so they have fallen into going against Him.

Points of Benefit:

The status of the Pledge of Ridwan in the sight of Allah is immense, and its participants are among the best people on the face of the earth.
Having negative assumptions about Allah is among the reasons for falling into disobedience and may even lead to disbelief.
Those with weak belief are few in times of distress but numerous when it comes to greed.
