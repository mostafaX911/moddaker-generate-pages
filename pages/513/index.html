(١٦) قُل لِّلۡمُخَلَّفِينَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَعۡرَابِ سَتُدۡعَوۡنَ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمٍ أُوْلِي بَأۡسٖ شَدِيدٖ تُقَٰتِلُونَهُمۡ أَوۡ يُسۡلِمُونَۖ فَإِن تُطِيعُواْ يُؤۡتِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ أَجۡرًا حَسَنٗاۖ وَإِن تَتَوَلَّوۡاْ كَمَا تَوَلَّيۡتُم مِّن قَبۡلُ يُعَذِّبۡكُمۡ عَذَابًا أَلِيمٗا

O Messenger, to test those Bedouins who failed to travel to Mecca with you, say to them: You will soon be called to fight against a strong, courageous group of people whom you shall either have to fight in the path of Allah, or they may enter into Islam without a fight. If you obey Allah in the fighting He is calling you to, He will give you a good reward, i.e., Paradise. If you turn away from His obedience, just like you turned away from traveling with the Messenger to Mecca, He will inflict a painful punishment on you.

(١٧) لَّيۡسَ عَلَى ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ حَرَجٞ وَلَا عَلَى ٱلۡأَعۡرَجِ حَرَجٞ وَلَا عَلَى ٱلۡمَرِيضِ حَرَجٞۗ وَمَن يُطِعِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ يُدۡخِلۡهُ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۖ وَمَن يَتَوَلَّ يُعَذِّبۡهُ عَذَابًا أَلِيمٗا

There is no sin on a person excused due to blindness, disability, or illness if he fails to fight in the path of Allah. Whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger, He will enter him into gardens and trees and palaces of which rivers flow; whoever turns away from their obedience, Allah will inflict a painful punishment on him.

(١٨) ۞لَّقَدۡ رَضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ إِذۡ يُبَايِعُونَكَ تَحۡتَ ٱلشَّجَرَةِ فَعَلِمَ مَا فِي قُلُوبِهِمۡ فَأَنزَلَ ٱلسَّكِينَةَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَأَثَٰبَهُمۡ فَتۡحٗا قَرِيبٗا

Verily, Allah was pleased with the believers when they gave you their oaths at al-Hudaybiyah under the tree. Allah knew of their faith, sincerity, and honesty, bringing tranquility to their hearts. And He rewarded them with a close victory, i.e., the victory over Khaybar, as consolation for them missing out on being able to enter Mecca.

(١٩) وَمَغَانِمَ كَثِيرَةٗ يَأۡخُذُونَهَاۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَزِيزًا حَكِيمٗا

And He also granted them many spoils of war they took from the people of Khaybar. Allah is the Almighty whom no one can overcome; the Wise in His creation, decree, and control.

(٢٠) وَعَدَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ مَغَانِمَ كَثِيرَةٗ تَأۡخُذُونَهَا فَعَجَّلَ لَكُمۡ هَٰذِهِۦ وَكَفَّ أَيۡدِيَ ٱلنَّاسِ عَنكُمۡ وَلِتَكُونَ ءَايَةٗ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَيَهۡدِيَكُمۡ صِرَٰطٗا مُّسۡتَقِيمٗا

O believers, Allah promises you much war booty in future Islamic victories. Still, He gave you the spoils of Khaybar in advance and stopped the hands of the Jews when they intended to capture your families after you. And He has done this so that these advanced spoils become a sign for you of the help of Allah and His support for you. And Allah guides you to a straight path in which there is no deviance.

(٢١) وَأُخۡرَىٰ لَمۡ تَقۡدِرُواْ عَلَيۡهَا قَدۡ أَحَاطَ ٱللَّهُ بِهَاۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٗا

And Allah promises you the spoils of war you have yet to be able to attain. Allah alone can let you acquire them; they are part of the plans and knowledge of Allah. Allah is Omnipotent over everything; nothing is beyond His capability.

(٢٢) وَلَوۡ قَٰتَلَكُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَوَلَّوُاْ ٱلۡأَدۡبَٰرَ ثُمَّ لَا يَجِدُونَ وَلِيّٗا وَلَا نَصِيرٗا

O believers, if those who disbelieve in Allah and His Messenger were to fight you, they would turn their backs, defeated and fleeing. They would not find any guardian who would take care of their affair, nor any helper who would help them fight against you.

(٢٣) سُنَّةَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّتِي قَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلُۖ وَلَن تَجِدَ لِسُنَّةِ ٱللَّهِ تَبۡدِيلٗا

The believers being victorious and then defeating the disbelievers is established in every place and era; Allah's divine practice is prevalent in the nations that have passed before these rejectors. O Messenger! You will never find change in the way of Allah.

Points of Benefit:

The rulings of Islamic law are based on compassion and facilitation.
The Quran's foretelling of future events that later came to pass, such as the Islamic conquests, is a decisive indication that the Quran is from Allah.
The reward for the people of the Pledge of Ridwan includes both immediate benefits and deferred blessings in the Hereafter.
The triumph of the truth and its followers over falsehood and its adherents is an established divine practice.
