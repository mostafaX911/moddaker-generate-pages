(٢٤) وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي كَفَّ أَيۡدِيَهُمۡ عَنكُمۡ وَأَيۡدِيَكُمۡ عَنۡهُم بِبَطۡنِ مَكَّةَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ أَنۡ أَظۡفَرَكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرًا

He is the One who prevented the harm the polytheists from getting to you when approximately 70 men came to Hudaybiyah, intending to cause you harm. He stopped you from them; you did not kill them or cause them any damage. Instead, you freed them after Allah enabled you to capture them. And Allah is watchful over your actions; none of your actions are hidden from Him.

(٢٥) هُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَصَدُّوكُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِ وَٱلۡهَدۡيَ مَعۡكُوفًا أَن يَبۡلُغَ مَحِلَّهُۥۚ وَلَوۡلَا رِجَالٞ مُّؤۡمِنُونَ وَنِسَآءٞ مُّؤۡمِنَٰتٞ لَّمۡ تَعۡلَمُوهُمۡ أَن تَطَ‍ُٔوهُمۡ فَتُصِيبَكُم مِّنۡهُم مَّعَرَّةُۢ بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖۖ لِّيُدۡخِلَ ٱللَّهُ فِي رَحۡمَتِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ لَوۡ تَزَيَّلُواْ لَعَذَّبۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ عَذَابًا أَلِيمًا

They are the ones who disbelieved in Allah and His Messenger and stopped you and the sacrificial animals from the Sacred Mosque. The animals remained barred from reaching the Haram, where they were to be sacrificed. Had it not been for a few believing men whom you did not know were believers, and by killing the disbelievers, you could have killed them also, and incurred sin and would have had to pay blood money unknowingly, Allah would have allowed you to liberate Mecca so that He may enter into His mercy whomever He wished, i.e., the believers in Mecca. If the disbelievers were distinguished from the believers in Mecca, Allah would have punished those who disbelieved in Him and His Messenger with a painful punishment.

(٢٦) إِذۡ جَعَلَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فِي قُلُوبِهِمُ ٱلۡحَمِيَّةَ حَمِيَّةَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِيَّةِ فَأَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ سَكِينَتَهُۥ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِهِۦ وَعَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَأَلۡزَمَهُمۡ كَلِمَةَ ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ وَكَانُوٓاْ أَحَقَّ بِهَا وَأَهۡلَهَاۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٗا

Remember when those who disbelieved in Allah and His Messenger harbored in their hearts the pride of ignorance, an ignorance linked to desires and not the truth? They haughtily rejected the entrance of Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم into Mecca in the year of the treaty of al-Hudaybiyah, fearing and blaming him that he would overpower them and take control over it. Therefore, Allah sent down tranquility from Himself upon His Messenger and the believers so their anger did not lead to them confronting the polytheists with the same actions. Allah made the believers firm upon the statement of truth, i.e., there is none worthy of worship except for Allah, and kept them firm upon fulfilling their rights which they fulfilled. The believers were more worthy of this statement than others and more deserving of it because of the goodness within their hearts that Allah knew of. And Allah is aware of everything; nothing is hidden from Him.

(٢٧) لَّقَدۡ صَدَقَ ٱللَّهُ رَسُولَهُ ٱلرُّءۡيَا بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ لَتَدۡخُلُنَّ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدَ ٱلۡحَرَامَ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ ءَامِنِينَ مُحَلِّقِينَ رُءُوسَكُمۡ وَمُقَصِّرِينَ لَا تَخَافُونَۖ فَعَلِمَ مَا لَمۡ تَعۡلَمُواْ فَجَعَلَ مِن دُونِ ذَٰلِكَ فَتۡحٗا قَرِيبًا

Indeed, Allah made the dream of His Messenger a reality; He showed it to him in his sleep and informed his companions of it. The dream was that he and his companions were entering the sacred house of Allah in safety from their enemies, with some of them having shaved their heads and others having only trimmed their hair, indicating the completion of their acts of pilgrimage. O believers! Allah knows what is in your best interests more than you. So instead of making the dream a reality by letting you enter Mecca that year, He will grant you a victory very soon, i.e., the signing of the treaty of al-Hudaybiyah and the conquest of Khaybar that followed it at the hands of the believers who had witnessed al-Hudaybiyah.

(٢٨) هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَرۡسَلَ رَسُولَهُۥ بِٱلۡهُدَىٰ وَدِينِ ٱلۡحَقِّ لِيُظۡهِرَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلدِّينِ كُلِّهِۦۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ شَهِيدٗا

Allah is the One who sent His Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم with a clear message and a true religion: the religion of Islam, so that He may make it prevail over all other opposing religions. Allah has testified to that, and He is sufficient as a witness.

Points of Benefit:

Turning away from the path of Allah is a crime deserving severe punishment for its perpetrators.
Allah's management for the interests of His slaves transcends beyond their limited knowledge.
A warning against substituting the bonds of religion with tribalism or ignorance.
The emergence of the religion of Islam is an established divine practice and a fulfilled promise.
