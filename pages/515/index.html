(٢٩) مُّحَمَّدٞ رَّسُولُ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ مَعَهُۥٓ أَشِدَّآءُ عَلَى ٱلۡكُفَّارِ رُحَمَآءُ بَيۡنَهُمۡۖ تَرَىٰهُمۡ رُكَّعٗا سُجَّدٗا يَبۡتَغُونَ فَضۡلٗا مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرِضۡوَٰنٗاۖ سِيمَاهُمۡ فِي وُجُوهِهِم مِّنۡ أَثَرِ ٱلسُّجُودِۚ ذَٰلِكَ مَثَلُهُمۡ فِي ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِۚ وَمَثَلُهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡإِنجِيلِ كَزَرۡعٍ أَخۡرَجَ شَطۡ‍َٔهُۥ فَ‍َٔازَرَهُۥ فَٱسۡتَغۡلَظَ فَٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَىٰ سُوقِهِۦ يُعۡجِبُ ٱلزُّرَّاعَ لِيَغِيظَ بِهِمُ ٱلۡكُفَّارَۗ وَعَدَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ مِنۡهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٗ وَأَجۡرًا عَظِيمَۢا

Muhammad is Allah's Messenger, and his companions are strict against the disbelievers that are at war with you and merciful, affectionate, and friendly among themselves. You will see them bowing and prostrating, seeking from Allah that He grace them with forgiveness, a generous reward, and that He becomes pleased with them. Their mark is on their faces due to prostrating in obedience to Allah. That is how the Torah has described them: the book revealed to Moses (peace be upon him). As for their mention in the Gospel, which was shown to Jesus, they are likened in their assisting one another and perfection like crops that have emerged as trim, then strengthened and stood straight up, pleasing the farmers with their strength and perfection. It is so that the disbelievers are angered by the power, steadfastness, and perfection they see within them. And Allah has promised those of the companions who believe in Him and do good deeds forgiveness for their sins; they will not be taken to account over their sins, and the great reward of Paradise.

The Rooms (Surah Al-Hujurat)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

The rectification of one's speech and explaining its impact on an individual's faith and the ethics of society.

Explanation:

(١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تُقَدِّمُواْ بَيۡنَ يَدَيِ ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٞ

O you who believe in Allah and follow whatever He legislates, do not precede Allah or His Messenger in speech or action. Fear Allah by fulfilling His commands and refrain from that which He has prohibited. Indeed, Allah hears your statements and is aware of your actions. He does not miss any of them and will soon recompense you accordingly.

(٢) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَرۡفَعُوٓاْ أَصۡوَٰتَكُمۡ فَوۡقَ صَوۡتِ ٱلنَّبِيِّ وَلَا تَجۡهَرُواْ لَهُۥ بِٱلۡقَوۡلِ كَجَهۡرِ بَعۡضِكُمۡ لِبَعۡضٍ أَن تَحۡبَطَ أَعۡمَٰلُكُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَشۡعُرُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow whatever He legislates, show good manners with His Messenger: do not raise your voices in his presence nor speak loudly to him out of respect for him, and do not call out his name as you do amongst each other. Rather, call him gently, saying, O Prophet, or O Messenger, it is feared that the reward of your deeds become nullified if you do so while you do not even realize it.

(٣) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَغُضُّونَ أَصۡوَٰتَهُمۡ عِندَ رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱمۡتَحَنَ ٱللَّهُ قُلُوبَهُمۡ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰۚ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٞ وَأَجۡرٌ عَظِيمٌ

Indeed, those who lower their voices near Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم are the ones whose hearts Allah has examined for fear of Him and chosen them for it. For them is forgiveness for their sins; they will not be accounted for them, and they will receive a great reward on the Day of Judgment, which will be that Allah enters them into Paradise.

(٤) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُنَادُونَكَ مِن وَرَآءِ ٱلۡحُجُرَٰتِ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡقِلُونَ

O Messenger, indeed, most of the Bedouins who call out to you from behind your wives' houses do not understand.

Points of Benefit:

Mercy is legislated with the believer, while severity is warranted with the combatant disbeliever.
Cohesion and collaboration are among the virtues of the companions of the Prophet, صلى الله عليه وسلم.
Whoever harbors animosity in their heart towards the noble companions is at risk of falling into disbelief.
The obligation of showing respect towards the Messenger of Allah, صلى الله عليه وسلم, his Sunnah, and his inheritors (the scholars).
