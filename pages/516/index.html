(٥) وَلَوۡ أَنَّهُمۡ صَبَرُواْ حَتَّىٰ تَخۡرُجَ إِلَيۡهِمۡ لَكَانَ خَيۡرٗا لَّهُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

O Messenger, if these people who call out to you from behind your wives' houses were to have waited and not called out to you until you came out to them, then talked to you in lowered voices, it would have been better for them than calling out to you from behind the dwellings, because it would have been more dignified and respectful. And Allah Forgives the sins of those who repent and others. He forgives them for their ignorance and is merciful to them.

(٦) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن جَآءَكُمۡ فَاسِقُۢ بِنَبَإٖ فَتَبَيَّنُوٓاْ أَن تُصِيبُواْ قَوۡمَۢا بِجَهَٰلَةٖ فَتُصۡبِحُواْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَعَلۡتُمۡ نَٰدِمِينَ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated, if a wrongdoer brings some news about a group of people to you, verify the truth and do not hasten towards accepting it, in fear that if you take it without verifying it, you could harm a group of people ignorantly. Then, after having hurt them, you will regret it when the false nature of the news becomes clear to you.

(٧) وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ فِيكُمۡ رَسُولَ ٱللَّهِۚ لَوۡ يُطِيعُكُمۡ فِي كَثِيرٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ لَعَنِتُّمۡ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ حَبَّبَ إِلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡإِيمَٰنَ وَزَيَّنَهُۥ فِي قُلُوبِكُمۡ وَكَرَّهَ إِلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡكُفۡرَ وَٱلۡفُسُوقَ وَٱلۡعِصۡيَانَۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلرَّٰشِدُونَ

O believers, know that amongst you is Allah's Messenger upon whom revelation descends, so beware of lying if he receives revelation informing him of your lying. He is better aware of your needs; if he were to obey you in many of the things you suggest, you would surely fall into difficulty, which he would not want for you. However, through His grace, Allah has made belief beloved to you and beautified it in your hearts so you accept it while making you hate disbelief, disobedience, and sin. Those who possess these qualities are the ones who are treading the path of correctness and righteousness.

(٨) فَضۡلٗا مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَنِعۡمَةٗۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٞ

The beautification of goodness and hating of evil that you have achieved is only through the grace of Allah, which He bestowed upon you and favored you. And Allah is aware of those of His slaves who are grateful to Him, and so He gives them success. He is Wise in that He places each thing in its appropriate place.

(٩) وَإِن طَآئِفَتَانِ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ٱقۡتَتَلُواْ فَأَصۡلِحُواْ بَيۡنَهُمَاۖ فَإِنۢ بَغَتۡ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا عَلَى ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰ فَقَٰتِلُواْ ٱلَّتِي تَبۡغِي حَتَّىٰ تَفِيٓءَ إِلَىٰٓ أَمۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ فَإِن فَآءَتۡ فَأَصۡلِحُواْ بَيۡنَهُمَا بِٱلۡعَدۡلِ وَأَقۡسِطُوٓاْۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُقۡسِطِينَ

O believers, if two groups of believers fight each other, then reconcile between them by calling them to let the law of Allah judge between them. Then, if one of the two rejects the reconciliation and transgresses, fight the transgressing group until it returns to accepting the command of Allah. If it returns, reconcile the two groups with fairness and justice. And be fair in your judgment between them. Indeed, Allah loves those who are honest in their judgment.

(١٠) إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ إِخۡوَةٞ فَأَصۡلِحُواْ بَيۡنَ أَخَوَيۡكُمۡۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ

The believers are brothers in Islam, and brotherhood in Islam demands that you, O believers, reconcile between your quarreling brothers. And be mindful of Allah by fulfilling His commands and refraining from that He has prohibited, in the hope that you will be shown mercy.

(١١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا يَسۡخَرۡ قَوۡمٞ مِّن قَوۡمٍ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُونُواْ خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ وَلَا نِسَآءٞ مِّن نِّسَآءٍ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُنَّ خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهُنَّۖ وَلَا تَلۡمِزُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَلَا تَنَابَزُواْ بِٱلۡأَلۡقَٰبِۖ بِئۡسَ ٱلِٱسۡمُ ٱلۡفُسُوقُ بَعۡدَ ٱلۡإِيمَٰنِۚ وَمَن لَّمۡ يَتُبۡ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated, a group of you should not mock another; perhaps those being mocked are better, according to Allah. Proper consideration is only for what Allah considers. And a group of women should not mock another group of women; perhaps the group being mocked is better, according to Allah. Do not taunt the souls of your brothers because they are like your own souls, and do not call others with names they do not like, as was the case of a few of the Anṣār before the arrival of Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم. Whoever does that is a wrongdoer, and that is an evil trait to be tainted with after having believed. Whoever does not repent from these sins wrongs themselves by placing themselves in situations of destruction due to their sins.

Points of Benefit:

It is obligatory to verify the authenticity of news, especially when transmitted by someone accused of misconduct.
The obligation of reconciliation among conflicting Muslims and the legitimacy of fighting against a faction that persists in aggression and rejects reconciliation.
Among the rights of brotherhood in faith is the reconciliation between disputants and refraining from actions that hurt feelings, such as mockery, reproach, and name-calling.
