(١٢) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱجۡتَنِبُواْ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلظَّنِّ إِنَّ بَعۡضَ ٱلظَّنِّ إِثۡمٞۖ وَ لَا تَجَسَّسُواْ وَلَا يَغۡتَب بَّعۡضُكُم بَعۡضًاۚ أَيُحِبُّ أَحَدُكُمۡ أَن يَأۡكُلَ لَحۡمَ أَخِيهِ مَيۡتٗا فَكَرِهۡتُمُوهُۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ تَوَّابٞ رَّحِيمٞ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, avoid most suspicions not based on causes and indications that give them credence because some forms of suspicion are sinful, like suspecting one who is pious. And do not pursue the faults of believers behind their backs, nor mention another in a manner he dislikes, because mentioning him in a way he dislikes is like eating his dead flesh. Would any of you wish to eat the dead flesh of his dead brother? Therefore, hate backbiting him because it is the same thing. And fear Allah by fulfilling His commands and refraining from what He has prohibited. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving of those of His slaves who repent, Ever-Merciful to them.

(١٣) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن ذَكَرٖ وَأُنثَىٰ وَجَعَلۡنَٰكُمۡ شُعُوبٗا وَقَبَآئِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ أَكۡرَمَكُمۡ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَتۡقَىٰكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمٌ خَبِيرٞ

O people, We have indeed created you from one male: your father, Adam, and one female: your mother, Eve. Therefore, your lineage is the same, so some of you should not be boastful of your lineage to others. Then, We made you into many nations and dispersed tribes so that you may recognize one another; not so that you take pride in them because distinction can only be due through having fear of Allah. Hence He said: the noblest from among you is the one who fears Him most. Indeed, Allah is Aware of your conditions, Knowing of what levels of perfection and deficiency you are on; nothing is hidden from Him.

(١٤) ۞قَالَتِ ٱلۡأَعۡرَابُ ءَامَنَّاۖ قُل لَّمۡ تُؤۡمِنُواْ وَلَٰكِن قُولُوٓاْ أَسۡلَمۡنَا وَلَمَّا يَدۡخُلِ ٱلۡإِيمَٰنُ فِي قُلُوبِكُمۡۖ وَإِن تُطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ لَا يَلِتۡكُم مِّنۡ أَعۡمَٰلِكُمۡ شَيۡ‍ًٔاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٌ

When some Bedouins came to the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم, they said, we believe in Allah and His Messenger. O Messenger! Say to them: You have not yet believed; instead, say: we have submitted and obeyed. Faith has not yet entered your hearts; it is hoped that it does enter. O Bedouins! If you obey Allah and His Messenger in faith, in doing good deeds, and in refraining from the unlawful, Allah will not decrease the reward of your deeds in the slightest. Indeed, Allah forgives those of His slaves who repent and is merciful to them.

(١٥) إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَرۡتَابُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَأَنفُسِهِمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلصَّٰدِقُونَ

The believers are only those who believe in Allah and His Messenger and then do not mix their belief with doubts and strive in the way of Allah with their wealth and souls, not holding back on anything. Those are the ones truthful in their belief.

(١٦) قُلۡ أَتُعَلِّمُونَ ٱللَّهَ بِدِينِكُمۡ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٞ

O Messenger, tell these Bedouins: Are you teaching Allah and informing Him about your religion, while Allah knows all that is in the heavens and on Earth? He knows everything, and nothing is hidden from him, so He does not need you to inform Him about your religion.

(١٧) يَمُنُّونَ عَلَيۡكَ أَنۡ أَسۡلَمُواْۖ قُل لَّا تَمُنُّواْ عَلَيَّ إِسۡلَٰمَكُمۖ بَلِ ٱللَّهُ يَمُنُّ عَلَيۡكُمۡ أَنۡ هَدَىٰكُمۡ لِلۡإِيمَٰنِ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

O Messenger, these Bedouins recount their favor upon you by mentioning their Islam. Say to them: Do not recount you're entering the religion of Allah as a favor upon me; its benefit, if gained, will return to you alone. Rather, Allah recounts His favor upon you that He granted you the ability to enter His religion if you are truthful in your claim that you have entered into it.

(١٨) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ غَيۡبَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَٱللَّهُ بَصِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Indeed, Allah knows the unseen of the heavens and the earth; nothing is hidden from him. And Allah is watchful of your actions; none of them are hidden from Him, and He will soon recompense you for your good and bad deeds.

Points of Benefit:

Having negative assumptions about people of goodness is a sin, while caution regarding people of evil is permissible.
The common origin of humanity necessitates rejecting pride based on lineage.
Faith is not merely a verbal expression without corresponding belief; rather, it is belief in the unseen, declaration with the tongue, and action with the limbs.
True guidance and success are solely in the Hand of Allah, and it is a grace from Him, Exalted is He, not a right of anyone.
