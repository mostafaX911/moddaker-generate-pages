The Letter Qaf (Surah Qaf)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Admonishing hearts through the realities of death and resurrection.

Explanation:

(١) قٓۚ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ٱلۡمَجِيدِ

Qāf – This surah begins with disconnected letters, the explanation for which has already preceded in Surat Al-Baqarah. Allah swore by the noble Quran because of its meanings, abundant goodness, and blessings that you shall be resurrected on the Day of Judgment to be taken into account and recompensed accordingly.

(٢) بَلۡ عَجِبُوٓاْ أَن جَآءَهُم مُّنذِرٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ فَقَالَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ هَٰذَا شَيۡءٌ عَجِيبٌ

The cause of their rejection was not that they expected you to lie because they knew your honesty. Instead, they are amazed that a messenger came to warn them from their own kind and not from the angels. They say, in amazement, a human messenger coming to us is bizarre!

(٣) أَءِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابٗاۖ ذَٰلِكَ رَجۡعُۢ بَعِيدٞ

Will we be resurrected after we have died and turned into dust? Resurrection and life returning to our bodies after decomposing is far-fetched; it is impossible.

(٤) قَدۡ عَلِمۡنَا مَا تَنقُصُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ مِنۡهُمۡۖ وَعِندَنَا كِتَٰبٌ حَفِيظُۢ

Indeed, We know how much of their bodies the earth eats away after their death; it is not hidden from Us. We have a book that preserves everything We have decreed for them in their lives and after their deaths.

(٥) بَلۡ كَذَّبُواْ بِٱلۡحَقِّ لَمَّا جَآءَهُمۡ فَهُمۡ فِيٓ أَمۡرٖ مَّرِيجٍ

Rather, these polytheists rejected the Quran when His Messenger brought it to them. They are uncertain about it; they cannot stay firm on anything.

After mentioning the rejection of the Resurrection by polytheists, Allah reminds them of the evidence for its occurrence, stating:

(٦) أَفَلَمۡ يَنظُرُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَوۡقَهُمۡ كَيۡفَ بَنَيۡنَٰهَا وَزَيَّنَّٰهَا وَمَا لَهَا مِن فُرُوجٖ

Do these people who reject the resurrection not ponder over the sky above them: how We created it, made it, and adorned it with the stars We placed in it, and it has no cracks to spoil it? Can the One who created this sky not resurrect the dead back to life?

(٧) وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ مَدَدۡنَٰهَا وَأَلۡقَيۡنَا فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوۡجِۢ بَهِيجٖ

And We spread the earth in a manner that made living on it suitable, placing within it firm mountains so that it does not shake. We also caused all the beautiful plants and trees to grow on it.

(٨) تَبۡصِرَةٗ وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِكُلِّ عَبۡدٖ مُّنِيبٖ

We created all that as a source of enlightenment and a reminder for every slave who turns to his Lord in obedience.

(٩) وَنَزَّلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ مُّبَٰرَكٗا فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا بِهِۦ جَنَّٰتٖ وَحَبَّ ٱلۡحَصِيدِ

And We sent down rainwater from the sky, which contains abundant benefits and goodness. With that water, We then cause gardens and the crops you harvest - such as barley and other than it - to grow.

(١٠) وَٱلنَّخۡلَ بَاسِقَٰتٖ لَّهَا طَلۡعٞ نَّضِيدٞ

We also cause tall date palms to grow with closely arranged minute flowers, one above the other.

(١١) رِّزۡقٗا لِّلۡعِبَادِۖ وَأَحۡيَيۡنَا بِهِۦ بَلۡدَةٗ مَّيۡتٗاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡخُرُوجُ

We cause all that to grow as provision for the slaves so that they can eat from it. We also revive through the rainwater barren lands with no growth. Just as We revive the barren earth with this rainwater, We will also restore the dead who will emerge alive.

(١٢) كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٖ وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلرَّسِّ وَثَمُودُ

O Messenger, before these people who have rejected you, many nations rejected their Prophets. The people of Noah, the people of the Well, and the Thamūd denied their Prophets.

(١٣) وَعَادٞ وَفِرۡعَوۡنُ وَإِخۡوَٰنُ لُوطٖ

The 'Ād, the people of Pharaoh, and the people of Lot all denied their Prophets.

(١٤) وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡأَيۡكَةِ وَقَوۡمُ تُبَّعٖۚ كُلّٞ كَذَّبَ ٱلرُّسُلَ فَحَقَّ وَعِيدِ

The people of Shuayb: the people of the Thicket, also denied their Prophet. The punishment that Allah promised them was then established.

(١٥) أَفَعَيِينَا بِٱلۡخَلۡقِ ٱلۡأَوَّلِۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ فِي لَبۡسٖ مِّنۡ خَلۡقٖ جَدِيدٖ

Were We incapable of creating you in the first instance that We are now incapable of resurrecting you? Rather, they are utterly confused about being created again after already being created.

Points of Benefit:

The polytheists exalt prophethood above humanity and attribute divine qualities to stones!
The creation of the heavens, the creation of the earth, the descent of rain, the sprouting of barren land, and the initial creation—all are evidence of resurrection.
Denying the messengers is a habit of past nations, and the punishment of the deniers is an established divine practice.
