(١٦) وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ وَنَعۡلَمُ مَا تُوَسۡوِسُ بِهِۦ نَفۡسُهُۥۖ وَنَحۡنُ أَقۡرَبُ إِلَيۡهِ مِنۡ حَبۡلِ ٱلۡوَرِيدِ

Verily, We created man and know the thoughts and desires that pass through his mind. We are closer to him than the vein in his neck connected to his heart.

(١٧) إِذۡ يَتَلَقَّى ٱلۡمُتَلَقِّيَانِ عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ وَعَنِ ٱلشِّمَالِ قَعِيدٞ

When the two receiving angels receive his deeds: one sitting on his right, and the other seated on his left.

(١٨) مَّا يَلۡفِظُ مِن قَوۡلٍ إِلَّا لَدَيۡهِ رَقِيبٌ عَتِيدٞ

He does not utter anything except that an angel is present, observing whatever he says.

(١٩) وَجَآءَتۡ سَكۡرَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ ذَٰلِكَ مَا كُنتَ مِنۡهُ تَحِيدُ

The pangs of death are a reality and will come; there is no escaping from them. O, negligent human! That is what you used to turn away from and put off and flee from.

(٢٠) وَنُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِۚ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمُ ٱلۡوَعِيدِ

The angel appointed to blow the trumpet will blow for the second time. That is the Day of Judgment, the day of the punishment the disbelievers and sinners have been warned of.

(٢١) وَجَآءَتۡ كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ مَّعَهَا سَآئِقٞ وَشَهِيدٞ

Each soul will come with an angel dragging it and another angel to testify to the deeds of that soul.

(٢٢) لَّقَدۡ كُنتَ فِي غَفۡلَةٖ مِّنۡ هَٰذَا فَكَشَفۡنَا عَنكَ غِطَآءَكَ فَبَصَرُكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ حَدِيدٞ

It will be said to this human that is being dragged; indeed, you neglected this day because your desires and pleasures deceived you. So, We have removed your heedlessness from you by the punishment and horror you see before you. Your eyesight is sharp today, so now you can see everything you were neglectful of!

(٢٣) وَقَالَ قَرِينُهُۥ هَٰذَا مَا لَدَيَّ عَتِيدٌ

His companion, the angel appointed to him, will say, these are his actions We have with me that We present, without any increase nor decrease.

(٢٤) أَلۡقِيَا فِي جَهَنَّمَ كُلَّ كَفَّارٍ عَنِيدٖ

Allah will say to the two angels: the one dragging him and the testifier, Throw into hell every stubborn rejector of the truth.

(٢٥) مَّنَّاعٖ لِّلۡخَيۡرِ مُعۡتَدٖ مُّرِيبٍ

The one often avoiding the truth which Allah has made obligatory for him, a transgressor of the limits of Allah, doubter in whatever promise or warning he is given.

(٢٦) ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ فَأَلۡقِيَاهُ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلشَّدِيدِ

The one who attributes another deity with Allah and makes him a partner in worship. So throw him into a severe punishment.

(٢٧) ۞قَالَ قَرِينُهُۥ رَبَّنَا مَآ أَطۡغَيۡتُهُۥ وَلَٰكِن كَانَ فِي ضَلَٰلِۢ بَعِيدٖ

His (Devil) companion will say, seeking to distance himself from him, O our Lord! I did not make him transgress, he had already gone far astray himself.

(٢٨) قَالَ لَا تَخۡتَصِمُواْ لَدَيَّ وَقَدۡ قَدَّمۡتُ إِلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡوَعِيدِ

Allah will say: Do not argue in front of Me; there is no benefit in that because I gave the message to you in the world beforehand with severe warnings that My messengers brought to you warning those who disbelieved in Me and disobeyed Me.

(٢٩) مَا يُبَدَّلُ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ لَدَيَّ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ بِظَلَّٰمٖ لِّلۡعَبِيدِ

My word cannot be changed, and I do not turn back on My promise. I do not oppress My slaves by decreasing their good deeds or increasing their evil deeds. Instead, I recompense them for whatever they have done.

(٣٠) يَوۡمَ نَقُولُ لِجَهَنَّمَ هَلِ ٱمۡتَلَأۡتِ وَتَقُولُ هَلۡ مِن مَّزِيدٖ

On the Day, We will say to Hell: Are you full of all the disbelievers and sinners that have been thrown into you? It will reply to its Lord, saying: Are there any more? It will want more, angry for the sake of its Lord.

After mentioning the severe threat to the disbelievers, Allah mentions what He has prepared for His believing slaves, stating:

(٣١) وَأُزۡلِفَتِ ٱلۡجَنَّةُ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ غَيۡرَ بَعِيدٍ

And Paradise will be brought close for those fearing their Lord by fulfilling His commands and refraining from things he did not allow. They will see all the blessings within it without it being far from them.

(٣٢) هَٰذَا مَا تُوعَدُونَ لِكُلِّ أَوَّابٍ حَفِيظٖ

It will be said to them: This is what Allah promised every one of you who frequently repents to his Lord and upholds whatever his Lord made obligatory for him.

(٣٣) مَّنۡ خَشِيَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنَ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَجَآءَ بِقَلۡبٖ مُّنِيبٍ

Whoever fears Allah in private, where none can see him except Allah, meets Allah with a pure heart while frequently turning to Him in [in repentance, etc.].

(٣٤) ٱدۡخُلُوهَا بِسَلَٰمٖۖ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمُ ٱلۡخُلُودِ

It will be said to them: Enter Paradise in total safety from everything you dislike. It is a day of eternity, one which is everlasting.

(٣٥) لَهُم مَّا يَشَآءُونَ فِيهَا وَلَدَيۡنَا مَزِيدٞ

They will have whatever they wish therein, and it will run out, and with Us, there are even more blessings for them that no eye has ever seen, or any ear has ever heard of, or any heart of man has ever imagined. One of them is seeing Allah. May He be glorified.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's knowledge encompasses what crosses the hearts in terms of good and evil.
The peril of being heedless about the Hereafter.
The established attribute of justice for Allah, the Exalted.
