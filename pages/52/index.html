(١٦) ٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَآ إِنَّنَآ ءَامَنَّا فَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ

The people of Paradise are the ones who say in their supplications: O our Lord, indeed we believed in You and in what You revealed to Your messengers, and we have followed Your sacred law. So, forgive the sins we have committed and keep us far away from the torment of the Fire.

(١٧) ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ وَٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ وَٱلۡقَٰنِتِينَ وَٱلۡمُنفِقِينَ وَٱلۡمُسۡتَغۡفِرِينَ بِٱلۡأَسۡحَارِ

They are the ones who show patience while doing good actions, avoiding evil acts, and when calamity befalls them. Those who are truthful in speech and deeds, wholeheartedly fulfill Allah's sacred law, spend their wealth in the path of Allah, and ask for forgiveness in the last part of the night when supplications are more likely to be accepted, and the hearts are void of any distractions.

(١٨) شَهِدَ ٱللَّهُ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَأُوْلُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ قَآئِمَۢا بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۚ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

Allah testifies that He alone is worthy of worship based on the legislative and universal signs indicating His divinity. The angels and people of knowledge also attest to this by declaring and calling to His Oneness. They testified to the most significant thing anyone can testify to: the absolute right of Allah to be worshipped and the perfect justice in His creation and sacred law. This is the greatest thing that anyone can testify to. There is no god but Him, the Almighty, who can never be overpowered, and the One who has complete wisdom regarding His creation, planning, and legislation.

(١٩) إِنَّ ٱلدِّينَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡإِسۡلَٰمُۗ وَمَا ٱخۡتَلَفَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡعِلۡمُ بَغۡيَۢا بَيۡنَهُمۡۗ وَمَن يَكۡفُرۡ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

The religion of Islam is the only religion Allah will accept. It is the surrendering and devotion of all acts of worship to Allah alone, submission to Him in servitude, and belief in all of the messengers, including the final Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم with whom revelations came to an end, and no other legislation other than his will be accepted. Due to envy and greed for worldly things, the Jews and Christians split up into groups and sects after evidence was established against them in the form of knowledge that had come to them. Whoever disbelieves in the verses of Allah revealed to His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, then Allah will be swift in reckoning those who disbelieve in Him and reject His messengers.

(٢٠) فَإِنۡ حَآجُّوكَ فَقُلۡ أَسۡلَمۡتُ وَجۡهِيَ لِلَّهِ وَمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَنِۗ وَقُل لِّلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡأُمِّيِّ‍ۧنَ ءَأَسۡلَمۡتُمۡۚ فَإِنۡ أَسۡلَمُواْ فَقَدِ ٱهۡتَدَواْۖ وَّإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَإِنَّمَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡبَلَٰغُۗ وَٱللَّهُ بَصِيرُۢ بِٱلۡعِبَادِ

If they argue with you, O Messenger, about the truth revealed to you, then say to them: I and the believers who follow me have submitted to Allah. Then, O Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, say to the People of the Book and the polytheists: Do you submit to Allah sincerely, following what I have brought? If they submit to Allah and follow your sacred law, they are on the path of guidance. But, on the other hand, if they turn away from Islam, then you only have to convey your message to them; then their affair is with Allah. He watches over His slaves and will recompense them accordingly for their deeds.

(٢١) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَكۡفُرُونَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَقۡتُلُونَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ‍ۧنَ بِغَيۡرِ حَقّٖ وَيَقۡتُلُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِ مِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ فَبَشِّرۡهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ

Those who disbelieve in Allah's evidence, which He has revealed to them, and unjustly and belligerently kill His prophets and those who command with justice by commanding good and forbidding evil – convey to these murderous disbelievers tidings of a painful torment.

(٢٢) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ حَبِطَتۡ أَعۡمَٰلُهُمۡ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَمَا لَهُم مِّن نَّٰصِرِينَ

The deeds of those doing such things are nullified and will not benefit them, not in this world or the Hereafter. There will be no one to help them repel the torment.

Points of Benefit:

Among the greatest means to expiate sins and safeguard oneself from the Fire's torment is believing in Allah and following what the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم conveyed.
The greatest testimony and reality is the divinity of Allah. For this reason, Allah testified to it for Himself, the angels testified to it, and those with knowledge among His creation also testified to it.
Injustice and envy are among the greatest causes of conflict and deviation from the truth.
