(٣٦) وَكَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا قَبۡلَهُم مِّن قَرۡنٍ هُمۡ أَشَدُّ مِنۡهُم بَطۡشٗا فَنَقَّبُواْ فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ هَلۡ مِن مَّحِيصٍ

How many a nation have We destroyed prior to these denying polytheists amongst the people of Mecca?! They searched in the lands hoping to find a place to run to, but they did not find it.

(٣٧) إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَذِكۡرَىٰ لِمَن كَانَ لَهُۥ قَلۡبٌ أَوۡ أَلۡقَى ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَهُوَ شَهِيدٞ

That destruction of the previous nations mentioned is a reminder and lesson for the one who has the heart to understand, listens attentively with the presence of mind, and is not absentminded.

(٣٨) وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ وَمَا مَسَّنَا مِن لُّغُوبٖ

And We created the heavens, and the earth and whatever is between the sky and the world in six days, despite Our power to make them in one moment, and We were not affected by fatigue as the Jews say.

(٣٩) فَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ قَبۡلَ طُلُوعِ ٱلشَّمۡسِ وَقَبۡلَ ٱلۡغُرُوبِ

So be patient, O Messenger, with what the Jews and others say, and pray the Fajr prayer to your Lord, praising Him before sunrise, and pray 'Asr before sunset.

(٤٠) وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَسَبِّحۡهُ وَأَدۡبَٰرَ ٱلسُّجُودِ

Pray to Him at night, and exalt Him after the prayers.

(٤١) وَٱسۡتَمِعۡ يَوۡمَ يُنَادِ ٱلۡمُنَادِ مِن مَّكَانٖ قَرِيبٖ

O Messenger, listen on the day the angel appointed to blow the trumpet a second time calls from a close place.

(٤٢) يَوۡمَ يَسۡمَعُونَ ٱلصَّيۡحَةَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمُ ٱلۡخُرُوجِ

On that Day, the creation will hear the loud sound of the resurrection, in truth, without any doubt. That day that they will hear the sound will be when the dead come out of their graves to be taken into account and recompensed.

(٤٣) إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نُحۡيِۦ وَنُمِيتُ وَإِلَيۡنَا ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

We alone give life and death. There is nobody to give life and death besides Us. And to Us alone is the return of the slaves on the Day of Judgment.

(٤٤) يَوۡمَ تَشَقَّقُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ عَنۡهُمۡ سِرَاعٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ حَشۡرٌ عَلَيۡنَا يَسِيرٞ

On the Day the earth breaks away from them, they will come out quickly. That is an easy gathering for Us.

(٤٥) نَّحۡنُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يَقُولُونَۖ وَمَآ أَنتَ عَلَيۡهِم بِجَبَّارٖۖ فَذَكِّرۡ بِٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مَن يَخَافُ وَعِيدِ

We know best what these deniers say. And you do not have authority over them, O Messenger, to force them to accept faith. You are only a conveyer of what Allah has ordered you to convey, so remind them with the Quran, the one who fears my warning to the disbelievers and the sinful because the fearful is the one who takes advice and listens to the reminders when given.

The Winnowing Winds (Surah Adh-Dhariyat)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing the jinn and humans by acknowledging that their sustenance is solely from Allah so that they devote their worship exclusively to Him.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلذَّٰرِيَٰتِ ذَرۡوٗا

Allah swears by the wind, which scatters dust.

(٢) فَٱلۡحَٰمِلَٰتِ وِقۡرٗا

And by the clouds that carry plenty of water.

(٣) فَٱلۡجَٰرِيَٰتِ يُسۡرٗا

And the ships which sail in the sea with ease.

(٤) فَٱلۡمُقَسِّمَٰتِ أَمۡرًا

And by the angels which distribute the issues concerning the slaves which Allah has ordered them to distribute.

(٥) إِنَّمَا تُوعَدُونَ لَصَادِقٞ

The reckoning and recompense which Allah promises you are valid without a doubt.

(٦) وَإِنَّ ٱلدِّينَ لَوَٰقِعٞ

And the reckoning of the slaves will occur on the Day of Judgment.

Points of Benefit:

Reflecting upon the events of history is of significance for those with perceptive hearts.
Allah created the universe in six days for reasons known to Him, perhaps among them is an indication of the divine practice of gradual progression.
The discourteous behavior of the Jews in attributing fatigue to Allah after the creation of the heavens and the earth is an act of disbelief in Allah.
