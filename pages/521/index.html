(٧) وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡحُبُكِ

And Allah swears by the sky, which is beautiful and stretched out.

(٨) إِنَّكُمۡ لَفِي قَوۡلٖ مُّخۡتَلِفٖ

O, people of Mecca, you are saying contradictory and conflicting things. Sometimes you say: The Quran is magic; then you say it's poetry. And you say that Muhammad is a magician at times, and at other times you say he is a poet.

(٩) يُؤۡفَكُ عَنۡهُ مَنۡ أُفِكَ

He is diverted away from believing in the Quran and the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم and away from Allah's knowledge due to His knowing that he will not believe, so he will not be guided.

(١٠) قُتِلَ ٱلۡخَرَّٰصُونَ

Cursed are these liars who said regarding the Quran and their Prophet whatever it is that they said.

(١١) ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ فِي غَمۡرَةٖ سَاهُونَ

Those who are ignorant and heedless about the place in the hereafter and care for it not.

(١٢) يَسۡ‍َٔلُونَ أَيَّانَ يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ

They ask when the Day of Recompense is, but they do not work for it.

(١٣) يَوۡمَ هُمۡ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ يُفۡتَنُونَ

So, Allah answers their question by saying the day they will be tormented in the Fire.

(١٤) ذُوقُواْ فِتۡنَتَكُمۡ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَ

It will be said to them: Taste your punishment. This is [the punishment] you were warned about, and to make a mockery of it, you asked for it to befall you quicker.

(١٥) إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّٰتٖ وَعُيُونٍ

Those fearing their Lord by carrying out His commands and refraining from His prohibitions will be in gardens and running springs on the Day of Judgment.

(١٦) ءَاخِذِينَ مَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَبۡلَ ذَٰلِكَ مُحۡسِنِينَ

They are experiencing the generous reward their Lord has given them because, previous to that, they used to do good in the life of this world.

(١٧) كَانُواْ قَلِيلٗا مِّنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ مَا يَهۡجَعُونَ

They used to pray at night, only sleeping a little.

(١٨) وَبِٱلۡأَسۡحَارِ هُمۡ يَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ

And in the morning, they seek forgiveness from Allah for their sins.

(١٩) وَفِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ حَقّٞ لِّلسَّآئِلِ وَٱلۡمَحۡرُومِ

And in their wealth is a right for the beggar amongst people, the one who does not ask out of shyness, and those who are deprived of sustenance for whatever reason.

(٢٠) وَفِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ءَايَٰتٞ لِّلۡمُوقِنِينَ

And the earth and the mountains, seas, rivers, trees, plants, and animals Allah has placed in them are proof of Allah's power for those convinced that Allah is their Creator and the One who fashioned them.

(٢١) وَفِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡۚ أَفَلَا تُبۡصِرُونَ

And in yourselves, O people, are proof of Allah's power; do you not see to take heed?

(٢٢) وَفِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ رِزۡقُكُمۡ وَمَا تُوعَدُونَ

And in the heavens is your worldly and religious provision; in this world is what you have been promised, good and bad.

(٢٣) فَوَرَبِّ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِنَّهُۥ لَحَقّٞ مِّثۡلَ مَآ أَنَّكُمۡ تَنطِقُونَ

By the Lord of the heavens and earth, the resurrection is true without any doubt, just as there is no doubt in your speech when you speak [to each other].

(٢٤) هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ضَيۡفِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ ٱلۡمُكۡرَمِينَ

O Messenger, has the story of the guests of Abraham (peace be upon him) - the angels whom he honored - come to you?

(٢٥) إِذۡ دَخَلُواْ عَلَيۡهِ فَقَالُواْ سَلَٰمٗاۖ قَالَ سَلَٰمٞ قَوۡمٞ مُّنكَرُونَ

When they came to him and said: Peace. Abraham replied: Peace and said: These are people we do not recognize.

(٢٦) فَرَاغَ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ فَجَآءَ بِعِجۡلٖ سَمِينٖ

Then he discreetly went to his family and brought a whole plump calf from them [to eat], thinking they were normal human beings.

(٢٧) فَقَرَّبَهُۥٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡ قَالَ أَلَا تَأۡكُلُونَ

He moved the calf closer to them, saying softly: Will you not eat this food placed before you?

(٢٨) فَأَوۡجَسَ مِنۡهُمۡ خِيفَةٗۖ قَالُواْ لَا تَخَفۡۖ وَبَشَّرُوهُ بِغُلَٰمٍ عَلِيمٖ

When they did not eat, he concealed his fear of them, but they understood this and said to him, to put him at ease: Do not be frightened. We are messengers from Allah. And they informed him of what will please him, i.e., that he will have a son with much knowledge. The one regarding whom glad tidings were given was Isaac (peace be upon him).

(٢٩) فَأَقۡبَلَتِ ٱمۡرَأَتُهُۥ فِي صَرَّةٖ فَصَكَّتۡ وَجۡهَهَا وَقَالَتۡ عَجُوزٌ عَقِيمٞ

When his wife heard the glad tiding, she came screaming out of happiness, slapped her face, and said in surprise: Will an old woman give birth when she is barren?

(٣٠) قَالُواْ كَذَٰلِكِ قَالَ رَبُّكِۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ

The angels told her: What we have informed you of is what your Lord has said. And there is nobody to refute what He said. He is Wise in His creation and decree. He is Omniscient of His creation and what is in their best interests.

Points of Benefit:

Excelling in work and dedicating it sincerely to Allah is a cause for entering Paradise.
The virtue of performing night prayers and how it is among the best acts of worship.
Among the etiquettes of hospitality: responding to greetings with better ones, discreetly preparing the table, being ready for guests before their arrival, not excluding anything from the table, supervising its preparation, hastening with it, bringing it closer to the guests, and addressing them with gentleness.
