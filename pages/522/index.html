(٣١) ۞قَالَ فَمَا خَطۡبُكُمۡ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ

Abraham (peace be upon him) asked the angels: What is your purpose? And what do you intend?

(٣٢) قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّآ أُرۡسِلۡنَآ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمٖ مُّجۡرِمِينَ

The angels replied: Allah has sent us to a nation of criminals who commit despicable sins.

(٣٣) لِنُرۡسِلَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حِجَارَةٗ مِّن طِينٖ

So that We can send stones of hard clay upon them.

(٣٤) مُّسَوَّمَةً عِندَ رَبِّكَ لِلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ

Marked in the presence of your Lord, O Abraham, and sent upon those who transgress the limits of Allah and who go to the extreme in disbelief and commit acts of disobedience.

(٣٥) فَأَخۡرَجۡنَا مَن كَانَ فِيهَا مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

So We took out whoever was in the city from the believers among Lot's people so that the punishment which will befall the criminals will not befall them.

(٣٦) فَمَا وَجَدۡنَا فِيهَا غَيۡرَ بَيۡتٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ

But We only found one house of Muslims in their city, which was the household of Lot (peace be upon him).

(٣٧) وَتَرَكۡنَا فِيهَآ ءَايَةٗ لِّلَّذِينَ يَخَافُونَ ٱلۡعَذَابَ ٱلۡأَلِيمَ

And We left in the city of Lot signs of the torment which will show that the torment befell them so that those who fear the painful torment, which afflicted them, take lessons from it and then not do what they did.

(٣٨) وَفِي مُوسَىٰٓ إِذۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ بِسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٖ

[There is a sign] in [the story of] Moses when We sent him to Pharaoh with clear proofs as a sign for those who fear the painful torment.

(٣٩) فَتَوَلَّىٰ بِرُكۡنِهِۦ وَقَالَ سَٰحِرٌ أَوۡ مَجۡنُونٞ

But Pharaoh turned away from the truth, considering his power and army. He said regarding Moses (peace be upon him): He is a magician who does magic on people or an insane person who says what he does not understand.

(٤٠) فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُ وَجُنُودَهُۥ فَنَبَذۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ وَهُوَ مُلِيمٞ

So We seized him and all his armies and threw them in the sea, so they were drowned and destroyed. Then, Pharaoh will be held accountable for his blameworthy actions: the rejection of the message and his claim of divinity.

(٤١) وَفِي عَادٍ إِذۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلرِّيحَ ٱلۡعَقِيمَ

And in [the story of] 'Ād, the people of Hūd, is a sign for those who fear the painful torment when We sent upon them the wind which did not carry rain or pollinate trees, nor were there any blessings in it.

(٤٢) مَا تَذَرُ مِن شَيۡءٍ أَتَتۡ عَلَيۡهِ إِلَّا جَعَلَتۡهُ كَٱلرَّمِيمِ

It did not spare any soul, property, or anything else it came upon, except that it destroyed it and left it like scattered ruins.

(٤٣) وَفِي ثَمُودَ إِذۡ قِيلَ لَهُمۡ تَمَتَّعُواْ حَتَّىٰ حِينٖ

And in [the story of] Thamūd, the people of Saleh (peace be upon him), is a sign for those who fear the painful torment when it was said to them: Enjoy yourselves before your term of life ends.

(٤٤) فَعَتَوۡاْ عَنۡ أَمۡرِ رَبِّهِمۡ فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلصَّٰعِقَةُ وَهُمۡ يَنظُرُونَ

But they were arrogant towards their Lord's command and haughty towards belief in Him and His obedience, so the thunderbolt of torment seized them while they were waiting for it to descend, as they were promised the torment three days before it came.

(٤٥) فَمَا ٱسۡتَطَٰعُواْ مِن قِيَامٖ وَمَا كَانُواْ مُنتَصِرِينَ

So they could not repel the torment that befell them, nor did they have any power to defend themselves.

(٤٦) وَقَوۡمَ نُوحٖ مِّن قَبۡلُۖ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمٗا فَٰسِقِينَ

And We destroyed the people of Noah by drowning them before these people who have been mentioned. They were people who abandoned Allah's obedience, so they deserved His punishment.

(٤٧) وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بَنَيۡنَٰهَا بِأَيۡيْدٖ وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ

And We constructed the sky and perfected its construction with strength, and We are expanding its edges.

(٤٨) وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ فَرَشۡنَٰهَا فَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡمَٰهِدُونَ

And We made the Earth like a spread for those who live on it. So how excellent are We who prepared it for them!

(٤٩) وَمِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقۡنَا زَوۡجَيۡنِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ

And We created all things in pairs, like the male and female, sky and Earth, and the land and sea, so that you remember the Oneness of Allah who created everything in pairs, and you remember His power.

(٥٠) فَفِرُّوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ إِنِّي لَكُم مِّنۡهُ نَذِيرٞ مُّبِينٞ

So, flee from Allah's punishment towards His reward by obeying Him and not disobeying Him. I am a clear warner to you, O people, of His punishment.

(٥١) وَلَا تَجۡعَلُواْ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَۖ إِنِّي لَكُم مِّنۡهُ نَذِيرٞ مُّبِينٞ

And do not set up alongside Allah another deity to worship instead of Him. I am a clear warner to you.

Points of Benefit:

Eman (Faith) is a higher degree than Islam.
Allah's destruction of nations that denied Him serves as a lesson for all people.
The fear of Allah entails rushing to Him through righteous deeds, not fleeing away from Him.
