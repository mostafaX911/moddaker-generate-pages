(٥٢) كَذَٰلِكَ مَآ أَتَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا قَالُواْ سَاحِرٌ أَوۡ مَجۡنُونٌ

As the people of Mecca rejected, the previous nations rejected. No Messenger from Allah came to them except that they said: He is a magician or insane.

(٥٣) أَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِهِۦۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ قَوۡمٞ طَاغُونَ

Did the predecessors among the disbelievers and those who came after advise one another to reject the messengers? No, instead, their transgression has united them on this.

(٥٤) فَتَوَلَّ عَنۡهُمۡ فَمَآ أَنتَ بِمَلُومٖ

So turn away from these rejectors, O Messenger, for you are not blameworthy, as you have conveyed to them what you were sent with to them.

(٥٥) وَذَكِّرۡ فَإِنَّ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ تَنفَعُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

And do not let their turning away from you prevent you from advising and reminding them. Advise and remind them, as reminding benefits those who believe in Allah.

(٥٦) وَمَا خَلَقۡتُ ٱلۡجِنَّ وَٱلۡإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُونِ

And I did not create jinn and humans except for My worship alone. I did not create them to associate partners with Me.

(٥٧) مَآ أُرِيدُ مِنۡهُم مِّن رِّزۡقٖ وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَن يُطۡعِمُونِ

I do not want any provision from them or want them to feed Me.

(٥٨) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلرَّزَّاقُ ذُو ٱلۡقُوَّةِ ٱلۡمَتِينُ

Allah is the Provider for His slaves; they all need His provision. He is the Possessor of power, the Ever Mighty; nothing can overpower Him. All the jinn and humans submit to His power; may He be Exalted.

(٥٩) فَإِنَّ لِلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ ذَنُوبٗا مِّثۡلَ ذَنُوبِ أَصۡحَٰبِهِمۡ فَلَا يَسۡتَعۡجِلُونِ

Those who have wronged themselves by rejecting you, O Messenger, have a share of the torment, like the share of their companions before them, which has a fixed period. So they should not ask Me to hasten it before its time.

(٦٠) فَوَيۡلٞ لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن يَوۡمِهِمُ ٱلَّذِي يُوعَدُونَ

So destruction and loss are for those who disbelieved in Allah and rejected His messengers on the Day of Resurrection, the day in which they are promised that the punishment will be sent upon them.

Mount Tur (Surah At-Tur)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Arguments and evidences to refute the doubts of the deniers against the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلطُّورِ

Allah swore by the mountain on which He spoke to Moses (peace be upon him).

(٢) وَكِتَٰبٖ مَّسۡطُورٖ

And he swore by the written Book [Quran].

(٣) فِي رَقّٖ مَّنشُورٖ

[Written] on a spread open parchment like the [previously] revealed books.

(٤) وَٱلۡبَيۡتِ ٱلۡمَعۡمُورِ

And He swore by the House, which the angels in the heavens inhabit with the [constant] worship of Allah.

(٥) وَٱلسَّقۡفِ ٱلۡمَرۡفُوعِ

And He swore by the raised sky, which serves as the canopy of the Earth.

(٦) وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ ٱلۡمَسۡجُورِ

And He swore by the sea filled with water.

(٧) إِنَّ عَذَابَ رَبِّكَ لَوَٰقِعٞ

O Messenger, the torment of your Lord against the disbelievers will come to pass.

(٨) مَّا لَهُۥ مِن دَافِعٖ

Nobody can avert it from them and save them from it befalling them.

(٩) يَوۡمَ تَمُورُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ مَوۡرٗا

On the Day in which the sky will move and shake violently, fully announcing the onset of the Day of Resurrection.

(١٠) وَتَسِيرُ ٱلۡجِبَالُ سَيۡرٗا

And the mountains will move away from their places.

(١١) فَوَيۡلٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

So destruction and loss on that Day will be for those who reject Allah's torment, which He promised the disbelievers.

(١٢) ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ فِي خَوۡضٖ يَلۡعَبُونَ

Those who playfully indulge in their vain discourse do not care about resurrection or being brought back to life.

(١٣) يَوۡمَ يُدَعُّونَ إِلَىٰ نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ دَعًّا

On the Day they will be pushed with force and harshness towards the Fire.

(١٤) هَٰذِهِ ٱلنَّارُ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُم بِهَا تُكَذِّبُونَ

And it will be said to them rebukingly: This is the Fire that you used to reject when your messengers used to warn you of it.

Points of Benefit:

Disbelief is one religion, even though its methods, adherents, locations, and times may differ.
Allah's testimony to His Messenger, صلى الله عليه وسلم, regarding the conveyance of the message.
The wisdom behind the creation of jinn and humans is to actualize the worship of Allah in all its aspects.
The conditions of the universe will undergo a change on the Day of Judgment.
