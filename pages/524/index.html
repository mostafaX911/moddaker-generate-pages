(١٥) أَفَسِحۡرٌ هَٰذَآ أَمۡ أَنتُمۡ لَا تُبۡصِرُونَ

Is this torment which you see magic, or are you not seeing it?

(١٦) ٱصۡلَوۡهَا فَٱصۡبِرُوٓاْ أَوۡ لَا تَصۡبِرُواْ سَوَآءٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ إِنَّمَا تُجۡزَوۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

Taste the heat of this Fire and endure it. Be patient in enduring its heat or do not; your patience or impatience is all the same. You will only be recompensed Today for the disbelief and acts of disobedience you used to commit in the worldly life.

After mentioning the recompense of those who reject faith, Allah mentions the recompense of the heedful believers, stating:

(١٧) إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّٰتٖ وَنَعِيمٖ

Those who fear their Lord by fulfilling His commands and abstaining from His prohibitions will be in gardens and great bliss, which will never end.

(١٨) فَٰكِهِينَ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ وَوَقَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

They will be enjoying the pleasures of food, drink, and marriage that Allah will have given them. And their Lord (may He be Exalted) will have protected them from the torment of the Inferno. Hence, they succeeded by achieving their desired pleasures and being saved from trouble.

(١٩) كُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ هَنِيٓ‍َٔۢا بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

It will be said to them: Eat and drink what you desire, in satisfaction without fearing harm or pain from what you eat and drink, as a recompense for your excellent deeds in the worldly life.

(٢٠) مُتَّكِ‍ِٔينَ عَلَىٰ سُرُرٖ مَّصۡفُوفَةٖۖ وَزَوَّجۡنَٰهُم بِحُورٍ عِينٖ

Reclining on adorned couches lined up, We will marry them to fair-skinned women with beautiful wide eyes.

(٢١) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱتَّبَعَتۡهُمۡ ذُرِّيَّتُهُم بِإِيمَٰنٍ أَلۡحَقۡنَا بِهِمۡ ذُرِّيَّتَهُمۡ وَمَآ أَلَتۡنَٰهُم مِّنۡ عَمَلِهِم مِّن شَيۡءٖۚ كُلُّ ٱمۡرِيِٕۢ بِمَا كَسَبَ رَهِينٞ

Those who believed and whose children followed them in belief, We will elevate their children to them, so they are delighted, even though they [children] did not reach their level of deeds. And We will not decrease anything from the reward of their deeds. Every person will be held for the bad deeds he earned; nobody else will bear any of his deeds.

(٢٢) وَأَمۡدَدۡنَٰهُم بِفَٰكِهَةٖ وَلَحۡمٖ مِّمَّا يَشۡتَهُونَ

And We will provide these people of Paradise with different fruits and all the meat they desire.

(٢٣) يَتَنَٰزَعُونَ فِيهَا كَأۡسٗا لَّا لَغۡوٞ فِيهَا وَلَا تَأۡثِيمٞ

They will give cups of wine to one another, bearing no resemblance to the wine of this world, free from false speech and sin which arises from intoxication.

(٢٤) ۞وَيَطُوفُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ غِلۡمَانٞ لَّهُمۡ كَأَنَّهُمۡ لُؤۡلُؤٞ مَّكۡنُونٞ

And youthful slaves, who are like protected pearls due to their clear and fair skin, will serve them.

(٢٥) وَأَقۡبَلَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ

The people of Paradise will turn to one another, asking each other about their conditions in the worldly life.

(٢٦) قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا كُنَّا قَبۡلُ فِيٓ أَهۡلِنَا مُشۡفِقِينَ

They will respond to them: In the worldly life, we used to be among our families, fearful of Allah's torment.

(٢٧) فَمَنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡنَا وَوَقَىٰنَا عَذَابَ ٱلسَّمُومِ

So Allah favored us by guiding us to Islam and protected us from the scorching hot torment.

(٢٨) إِنَّا كُنَّا مِن قَبۡلُ نَدۡعُوهُۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡبَرُّ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

We used to worship Him in the worldly life and supplicate to Him to protect us from the torment of the Fire. He is Kind and Truthful in His promise to His slaves and Merciful to them. Part of His kindness and mercy upon us is that He guided us to believe in Him, admitted us into Paradise, and distanced us from the Fire.

(٢٩) فَذَكِّرۡ فَمَآ أَنتَ بِنِعۡمَتِ رَبِّكَ بِكَاهِنٖ وَلَا مَجۡنُونٍ

So remind with the Quran, O Messenger, for you are not, with the belief and intellect that Allah has favored you, a soothsayer with a jinn, nor are you insane.

(٣٠) أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ شَاعِرٞ نَّتَرَبَّصُ بِهِۦ رَيۡبَ ٱلۡمَنُونِ

Do these polytheists say that Muhammad is not a messenger but a poet for whom we await death to seize so we can be relieved of him?

(٣١) قُلۡ تَرَبَّصُواْ فَإِنِّي مَعَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُتَرَبِّصِينَ

O Messenger, say to them: Await my death, and I will await the torment which will befall you due to rejecting me.

Points of Benefit:

The gathering of parents and children in Paradise holds a unified status, even if some of them fall short in deeds, out of honor for all of them until the joy is complete.
The intoxicants of the Hereafter do not lead to any harm by their consumption.
Whoever fears his Lord in this world will find security with Him in the Hereafter.
