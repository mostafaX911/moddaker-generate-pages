(٣٢) أَمۡ تَأۡمُرُهُمۡ أَحۡلَٰمُهُم بِهَٰذَآۚ أَمۡ هُمۡ قَوۡمٞ طَاغُونَ

Or do their intellects tell them to say that He is a soothsayer and insane? Thus, they combine what cannot be gathered in one person. Rather, they are a people who transgress the bounds; hence, they do not refer to any sacred law or intellect.

(٣٣) أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ تَقَوَّلَهُۥۚ بَل لَّا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

Or do they say that Muhammad fabricated this Quran, and nothing has been revealed to him? He has not fabricated it; instead, they are too arrogant to believe in it, so they say He has fabricated it.

(٣٤) فَلۡيَأۡتُواْ بِحَدِيثٖ مِّثۡلِهِۦٓ إِن كَانُواْ صَٰدِقِينَ

So let them produce a discourse like it, even if it be fabricated, if they are truthful in their claim that he has fabricated it.

(٣٥) أَمۡ خُلِقُواْ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ شَيۡءٍ أَمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقُونَ

Or have they been created without a creator to create them? Or did they create themselves? The existence of a creation without a creator or a creation that creates itself is impossible, so why do they not worship their Creator?

(٣٦) أَمۡ خَلَقُواْ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَۚ بَل لَّا يُوقِنُونَ

Or did they create the heavens and the Earth? Still, they are uncertain that Allah is their Creator; if they had been certain of that, they would have declared His Oneness and believed in His Messenger.

(٣٧) أَمۡ عِندَهُمۡ خَزَآئِنُ رَبِّكَ أَمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡمُصَۜيۡطِرُونَ

Or do they possess the treasures of your Lord's provision, giving it to whom they wish, or do they own prophethood to grant or withhold it from whom they want? Or are they the controllers and disposers according to their wishes?

(٣٨) أَمۡ لَهُمۡ سُلَّمٞ يَسۡتَمِعُونَ فِيهِۖ فَلۡيَأۡتِ مُسۡتَمِعُهُم بِسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٍ

Or do they have a ladder to climb to the sky in which they listen to Allah's revelation, which He sends to tell them they are on the truth? So let the one who listens among them to that revelation bring clear proof to affirm you in your claim that you are on the truth.

(٣٩) أَمۡ لَهُ ٱلۡبَنَٰتُ وَلَكُمُ ٱلۡبَنُونَ

Or does He, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, have daughters which you dislike for yourselves, while you have sons which you prefer?

(٤٠) أَمۡ تَسۡ‍َٔلُهُمۡ أَجۡرٗا فَهُم مِّن مَّغۡرَمٖ مُّثۡقَلُونَ

Or do you, O Messenger, seek reward from them for what you convey to them from their Lord, due to which they are burdened with something they cannot bear?

(٤١) أَمۡ عِندَهُمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبُ فَهُمۡ يَكۡتُبُونَ

Or do they know the unseen so that they write for people what they learn from the unseen and inform them of what they want from there?

(٤٢) أَمۡ يُرِيدُونَ كَيۡدٗاۖ فَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ هُمُ ٱلۡمَكِيدُونَ

Or do these rejectors intend to plot against you and your religion? Trust in Allah, as those who disbelieve in Allah and His Messenger are the ones who will be plotted against, not you.

(٤٣) أَمۡ لَهُمۡ إِلَٰهٌ غَيۡرُ ٱللَّهِۚ سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

Or do they have a deity worthy of worship instead of Allah? Free and sanctified is Allah from the partner they attribute to him. None of what was mentioned has happened, and it is all unthinkable.

(٤٤) وَإِن يَرَوۡاْ كِسۡفٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ سَاقِطٗا يَقُولُواْ سَحَابٞ مَّرۡكُومٞ

And if they were to see a piece of the sky falling, they would say: This is an ordinary pile of clouds. So they do not take heed or believe.

(٤٥) فَذَرۡهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ يُلَٰقُواْ يَوۡمَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي فِيهِ يُصۡعَقُونَ

So leave them, O Messenger, in their stubbornness and denial until they meet their Day in which they will be punished, the Day of Resurrection.

(٤٦) يَوۡمَ لَا يُغۡنِي عَنۡهُمۡ كَيۡدُهُمۡ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا هُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ

The Day when their plot will neither avail them a little or a lot, nor will they be helped by being saved from the torment.

(٤٧) وَإِنَّ لِلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ عَذَابٗا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

And for those who wronged themselves through polytheism and committing acts of disobedience, there is a torment before the torment of the Hereafter: in this life through being killed and imprisoned, and in the barzakh (transitory stage) through the torment of the grave. However, most of them do not know that, so they remain in disbelief.

After mentioning the falsehood that the polytheists are upon, Allah commands His Messenger not to be bothered by them and to be patient in the face of their rejection, stating:

(٤٨) وَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ فَإِنَّكَ بِأَعۡيُنِنَاۖ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ حِينَ تَقُومُ

Be patient, O Messenger, with the decree of your Lord and His divine law, as you are in Our sight and protection. And exalt your Lord with praise when you arise from your sleep.

(٤٩) وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَسَبِّحۡهُ وَإِدۡبَٰرَ ٱلنُّجُومِ

And at night, exalt your Lord and pray to Him, and pray the Fajr (dawn) prayer after the stars have disappeared due to the daylight.

Points of Benefit:

Tyranny is one of the causes of misguidance.
The significance of intellectual debate in proving the truths of religion.
The established existence of the punishment in the Barzakh (intermediate state between death and resurrection).
