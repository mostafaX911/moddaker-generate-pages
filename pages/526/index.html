The Star (Surah An-Najm)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Establishing the authenticity of revelation and that it originates from Allah.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلنَّجۡمِ إِذَا هَوَىٰ

He (may He be Exalted) swore by the star when it fades away.

(٢) مَا ضَلَّ صَاحِبُكُمۡ وَمَا غَوَىٰ

Muhammad, Allah's Messenger, did not deviate from the path of guidance, nor did he go astray. Rather, he is rightly guided.

(٣) وَمَا يَنطِقُ عَنِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰٓ

Nor does he utter this Quran according to his whims.

(٤) إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا وَحۡيٞ يُوحَىٰ

This Quran is only a revelation that Allah revealed to him through Gabriel; peace be upon him.

(٥) عَلَّمَهُۥ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡقُوَىٰ

An angel of great strength, Gabriel, taught him it.

(٦) ذُو مِرَّةٖ فَٱسۡتَوَىٰ

And Gabriel (peace be upon him) is of excellent form, so he rose, appearing to the Prophet in his true form, in which Allah created him.

(٧) وَهُوَ بِٱلۡأُفُقِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ

While Gabriel was on the highest part of the horizon.

(٨) ثُمَّ دَنَا فَتَدَلَّىٰ

Then Gabriel (peace be upon him) came close to the Prophet. Then, he came even closer.

(٩) فَكَانَ قَابَ قَوۡسَيۡنِ أَوۡ أَدۡنَىٰ

So his closeness to him was the distance of two bow lengths, or even closer.

(١٠) فَأَوۡحَىٰٓ إِلَىٰ عَبۡدِهِۦ مَآ أَوۡحَىٰ

So Gabriel (peace be upon him) revealed to Allah's slave, Muhammad, that which He revealed.

(١١) مَا كَذَبَ ٱلۡفُؤَادُ مَا رَأَىٰٓ

Muhammad's heart did not reject what his eyes saw.

(١٢) أَفَتُمَٰرُونَهُۥ عَلَىٰ مَا يَرَىٰ

So how can you, O polytheists, argue with him about what Allah showed him when he was taken on the night journey?

(١٣) وَلَقَدۡ رَءَاهُ نَزۡلَةً أُخۡرَىٰ

And Muhammad had seen Gabriel in his true form another time on the night he was taken on the journey.

(١٤) عِندَ سِدۡرَةِ ٱلۡمُنتَهَىٰ

At the Lote Tree of the utmost boundary, which is a huge tree in the seventh heaven.

(١٥) عِندَهَا جَنَّةُ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰٓ

Near this tree is the Garden of Refuge [Paradise].

(١٦) إِذۡ يَغۡشَى ٱلسِّدۡرَةَ مَا يَغۡشَىٰ

When the Lote Tree was covered by the command of Allah by something great whose true nature only Allah knows.

(١٧) مَا زَاغَ ٱلۡبَصَرُ وَمَا طَغَىٰ

Muhammad's sight did not swerve left or right or go beyond its limit.

(١٨) لَقَدۡ رَأَىٰ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِ رَبِّهِ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰٓ

Muhammad saw the greatest signs of his Lord, which show His power, on the night he ascended to the heavens. He saw Paradise, the Fire, and other things.

(١٩) أَفَرَءَيۡتُمُ ٱللَّٰتَ وَٱلۡعُزَّىٰ

Have you, O polytheists, considered these idols you worship instead of Allah: Lat and Uzza,

(٢٠) وَمَنَوٰةَ ٱلثَّالِثَةَ ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰٓ

And Manat, the third one among your idols? Tell me, do they possess any benefit or harm for you?

(٢١) أَلَكُمُ ٱلذَّكَرُ وَلَهُ ٱلۡأُنثَىٰ

O polytheists, do you have males which you prefer, while He (may He be Exalted) has females which you dislike?

(٢٢) تِلۡكَ إِذٗا قِسۡمَةٞ ضِيزَىٰٓ

This distribution which you made with your desires is unjust.

(٢٣) إِنۡ هِيَ إِلَّآ أَسۡمَآءٞ سَمَّيۡتُمُوهَآ أَنتُمۡ وَءَابَآؤُكُم مَّآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ بِهَا مِن سُلۡطَٰنٍۚ إِن يَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا ٱلظَّنَّ وَمَا تَهۡوَى ٱلۡأَنفُسُۖ وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَهُم مِّن رَّبِّهِمُ ٱلۡهُدَىٰٓ

These idols are mere names without meaning, so they have no share of divine qualities. You and your fathers named them on your own. Allah did not reveal any proof for them. The polytheists do not follow in their beliefs except assumptions and what they desire from what the Devil has made attractive in their hearts. And their Lord's guidance has come to them on the tongue of Muhammad, but they were not guided.

(٢٤) أَمۡ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ مَا تَمَنَّىٰ

Or should man have whatever he desires, such as idols interceding with Allah?

(٢٥) فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ وَٱلۡأُولَىٰ

No, he will not have what he desires. To Allah alone belongs the Hereafter and the first [life]; He grants from them whatever He wishes and withholds whatever He wishes.

(٢٦) ۞وَكَم مِّن مَّلَكٖ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ لَا تُغۡنِي شَفَٰعَتُهُمۡ شَيۡ‍ًٔا إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ أَن يَأۡذَنَ ٱللَّهُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَرۡضَىٰٓ

How many angels are in the heavens whose intercession cannot help if they intend to help someone, except after Allah permits intercession for whom He wishes among them and He is pleased with the one for whom the intercession is made? Allah will never allow one considered a partner [in worship] to intercede, and He will never be pleased with the one for whom the intercession is made if he worships this partner instead of Allah.

Points of Benefit:

The impeccable manners of the Prophet, صلى الله عليه وسلم, as he did not avert his gaze even while in the seventh heaven.
The absurdity of the mentality of the polytheists was that they worshiped something neither harmful nor beneficial, attributed to Allah what they disliked, and selected for themselves what they preferred.
Intercession occurs only under two conditions: permission from the intercessor and the consent of the one for whom intercession is made.
