(٢٧) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ لَيُسَمُّونَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةَ تَسۡمِيَةَ ٱلۡأُنثَىٰ

Those who do not believe in the resurrection in the Hereafter give the angels female names because they believe they are Allah's daughters. Allah is Transcendent and far beyond what they say.

(٢٨) وَمَا لَهُم بِهِۦ مِنۡ عِلۡمٍۖ إِن يَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا ٱلظَّنَّۖ وَإِنَّ ٱلظَّنَّ لَا يُغۡنِي مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا

They have no knowledge to rely on in giving them female names; they merely follow fabrications and delusion. Assumption is not a substitute for the truth and thus cannot take its place.

(٢٩) فَأَعۡرِضۡ عَن مَّن تَوَلَّىٰ عَن ذِكۡرِنَا وَلَمۡ يُرِدۡ إِلَّا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا

So turn away, O Messenger, from whoever turns their back on Allah's remembrance without showing any concern for it, only desiring the life of this world, and therefore do not work for their Hereafter because they do not believe in it.

(٣٠) ذَٰلِكَ مَبۡلَغُهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَنِ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ

What these polytheists say about giving the angels female names is the extent of their knowledge because they are ignorant. They have not reached certainty. Your Lord, O Messenger, knows best who has deviated from the path of truth, and He knows best who is guided to its path. Nothing of that is hidden from Him.

(٣١) وَلِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لِيَجۡزِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسَٰٓـُٔواْ بِمَا عَمِلُواْ وَيَجۡزِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَى

To Allah alone belongs whatever is in the heavens and on Earth. He created, owns, and plans all of it so that He may recompense those who do evil actions in the worldly life with the torment they deserve and recompense the believers who do righteous deeds with Paradise.

(٣٢) ٱلَّذِينَ يَجۡتَنِبُونَ كَبَٰٓئِرَ ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡفَوَٰحِشَ إِلَّا ٱللَّمَمَۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ وَٰسِعُ ٱلۡمَغۡفِرَةِۚ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِكُمۡ إِذۡ أَنشَأَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَإِذۡ أَنتُمۡ أَجِنَّةٞ فِي بُطُونِ أُمَّهَٰتِكُمۡۖ فَلَا تُزَكُّوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡۖ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَنِ ٱتَّقَىٰٓ

Those who avoid major sins and acts of disobedience but fall into minor sins will have their minor sins forgiven if they avoid the major ones and increase in acts of obedience. Your Lord, O Messenger, is of vast forgiveness. He forgives the sins of His slaves when they repent from them. He (may He be Exalted) knew best about their states and affairs when He created their father, Adam, from dust and when you were in the wombs of your mothers, being created in one form after another. Nothing of that is hidden from Him. So do not praise yourselves through commendation and asserting piety. He (may He be Exalted) knows best about those who fear Him by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

(٣٣) أَفَرَءَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِي تَوَلَّىٰ

Have you seen the evil state of the one who turned away from Islam after coming close to it?

(٣٤) وَأَعۡطَىٰ قَلِيلٗا وَأَكۡدَىٰٓ

He gave a little wealth and then stopped because miserliness was his innate quality. Despite this, he asserts himself to be pious.

(٣٥) أَعِندَهُۥ عِلۡمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ فَهُوَ يَرَىٰٓ

Does he know the unseen so that he sees and speaks about it? No! He is, instead, fabricating lies against Allah.

(٣٦) أَمۡ لَمۡ يُنَبَّأۡ بِمَا فِي صُحُفِ مُوسَىٰ

Or has this person, who fabricated lies against Allah, not been informed of what is in the earlier scriptures that Allah had revealed to Moses?

(٣٧) وَإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ ٱلَّذِي وَفَّىٰٓ

And the scriptures of Abraham who fulfilled and completed everything His Lord tasked him with?

(٣٨) أَلَّا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةٞ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰ

That no person will bear the sin of another.

(٣٩) وَأَن لَّيۡسَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ إِلَّا مَا سَعَىٰ

And each person will only receive the reward for the deeds he did.

(٤٠) وَأَنَّ سَعۡيَهُۥ سَوۡفَ يُرَىٰ

And that his deeds will be openly seen on the Day of Resurrection.

(٤١) ثُمَّ يُجۡزَىٰهُ ٱلۡجَزَآءَ ٱلۡأَوۡفَىٰ

Then he will be given the recompense of his deeds in full without reduction.

(٤٢) وَأَنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡمُنتَهَىٰ

And that to your Lord, O Messenger, will be the return and destination of His slaves after they die.

(٤٣) وَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَ أَضۡحَكَ وَأَبۡكَىٰ

And that He makes whoever He wills happy and makes him laugh, and whoever He wills sad and makes him cry.

(٤٤) وَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَ أَمَاتَ وَأَحۡيَا

And He causes the living to die in this world and brings the dead to life by resurrecting them.

Points of Benefit:

The categorization of sins into major and minor.
The peril of speaking about Allah without knowledge.
Prohibition of self-praise.
