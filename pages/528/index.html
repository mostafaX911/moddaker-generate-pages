(٤٥) وَأَنَّهُۥ خَلَقَ ٱلزَّوۡجَيۡنِ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلۡأُنثَىٰ

And He created the pairs: Male and female

(٤٦) مِن نُّطۡفَةٍ إِذَا تُمۡنَىٰ

From a sperm-drop when it is placed in the womb.

(٤٧) وَأَنَّ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلنَّشۡأَةَ ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰ

And that He will undertake to recreate them for the resurrection after they die.

(٤٨) وَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَ أَغۡنَىٰ وَأَقۡنَىٰ

And He enriches whoever of His slaves He wills by making him the owner of wealth, and He gives wealth that people take as possessions that they acquire.

(٤٩) وَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلشِّعۡرَىٰ

And He is the Lord of Sirius, the star worshipped by some polytheists alongside Allah.

(٥٠) وَأَنَّهُۥٓ أَهۡلَكَ عَادًا ٱلۡأُولَىٰ

And He destroyed the first [people of ] 'Ād, who were the people of Hūd when they persisted in their disbelief.

(٥١) وَثَمُودَاْ فَمَآ أَبۡقَىٰ

And He destroyed Thamūd, the people of Saleh, sparing none.

(٥٢) وَقَوۡمَ نُوحٖ مِّن قَبۡلُۖ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ هُمۡ أَظۡلَمَ وَأَطۡغَىٰ

He destroyed the people of Noah before 'Ād and Thamūd. The people of Noah were more unjust and greater in transgression than 'Ād and Thamūd, because Noah lived among them for nine hundred and fifty years, inviting them to Allah's Oneness, but they did not respond to him.

(٥٣) وَٱلۡمُؤۡتَفِكَةَ أَهۡوَىٰ

And He raised the towns of the people of Lot to the sky, then turned them over and dropped them to the ground.

(٥٤) فَغَشَّىٰهَا مَا غَشَّىٰ

He then struck and covered them with stones after they had been raised to the sky and dropped to the ground.

(٥٥) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكَ تَتَمَارَىٰ

So which of your Lord's signs demonstrating His power do you dispute with, O humanity, and not take a lesson from?

(٥٦) هَٰذَا نَذِيرٞ مِّنَ ٱلنُّذُرِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰٓ

This messenger sent to you is like the previous messengers.

(٥٧) أَزِفَتِ ٱلۡأٓزِفَةُ

The Day of Resurrection has drawn near.

(٥٨) لَيۡسَ لَهَا مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ كَاشِفَةٌ

No one can push it back, and no one can disclose it besides Allah.

(٥٩) أَفَمِنۡ هَٰذَا ٱلۡحَدِيثِ تَعۡجَبُونَ

Do you marvel that this Quran recited to you is from Allah?

(٦٠) وَتَضۡحَكُونَ وَلَا تَبۡكُونَ

And you laugh mockingly at it and do not cry when hearing its admonitions?

(٦١) وَأَنتُمۡ سَٰمِدُونَ

While you are neglectful of it, not giving it any attention?

(٦٢) فَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِۤ وَٱعۡبُدُواْ۩

So prostrate to Allah alone and worship Him alone.

The Moon (Surah Al-Qamar)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Reminding about the blessing of making the Quran easy and its verses and admonitions.

Explanation:

(١) ٱقۡتَرَبَتِ ٱلسَّاعَةُ وَٱنشَقَّ ٱلۡقَمَرُ

The coming of the Hour has drawn near, and the moon was split [in two] in the time of the Prophet, the splitting of which was one of the tangible signs of the Prophet.

(٢) وَإِن يَرَوۡاْ ءَايَةٗ يُعۡرِضُواْ وَيَقُولُواْ سِحۡرٞ مُّسۡتَمِرّٞ

When the polytheists see any sign or proof of the Prophet's truthfulness, they turn away from accepting it and say: The evidence and proof we have seen are false magic.

(٣) وَكَذَّبُواْ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓاْ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡۚ وَكُلُّ أَمۡرٖ مُّسۡتَقِرّٞ

They rejected the truth that came to them and followed their desires in this rejection. Whether good or bad, every matter will be settled for the one who deserves it on the Day of Resurrection.

(٤) وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَنۢبَآءِ مَا فِيهِ مُزۡدَجَرٌ

There have come to them the stories of the nations that Allah destroyed because of their disbelief and wrongdoing that were sufficient to deter them from falling into the same.

(٥) حِكۡمَةُۢ بَٰلِغَةٞۖ فَمَا تُغۡنِ ٱلنُّذُرُ

What has come to them is profound wisdom to establish the evidence against them. The warnings do not benefit people who do not believe in Allah and the Last Day.

(٦) فَتَوَلَّ عَنۡهُمۡۘ يَوۡمَ يَدۡعُ ٱلدَّاعِ إِلَىٰ شَيۡءٖ نُّكُرٍ

Since they do not follow the right path, leave them, O Messenger, and turn away from them, waiting for the Day when the angel appointed to blow the trumpet will call towards a horrific matter, the like of which was not known by creation previously.

Points of Benefit:

Not being affected by the Quran is a forewarning of misfortune.
The danger of following one's desires for the self in both this world and the Hereafter.
Not taking heed of the destruction of nations is a characteristic of disbelievers.
