(٧) خُشَّعًا أَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ يَخۡرُجُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَجۡدَاثِ كَأَنَّهُمۡ جَرَادٞ مُّنتَشِرٞ

Their sight will be downcast. They will come out of their graves as if, in their rush towards the place of reckoning, they are swarming locusts.

(٨) مُّهۡطِعِينَ إِلَى ٱلدَّاعِۖ يَقُولُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ هَٰذَا يَوۡمٌ عَسِرٞ

[They will be] rushing to the caller to that place. The disbelievers will say: This is a hard Day because of the severity and horrors therein.

After mentioning the disbelievers turning away from the call of our Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, Allah consoles him by informing him that the previous nations also rejected their messengers. He states:

(٩) ۞كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٖ فَكَذَّبُواْ عَبۡدَنَا وَقَالُواْ مَجۡنُونٞ وَٱزۡدُجِرَ

Before these people rejected your invitation, O Messenger, the people of Noah also rejected. They rejected Our slave, Noah (peace be upon him), when We sent him to them. They said that He was insane, and they rebuked him with various forms of swearing, abuse, and threats if he did not stop inviting them.

(١٠) فَدَعَا رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّي مَغۡلُوبٞ فَٱنتَصِرۡ

So Noah supplicated to His Lord: My people have overwhelmed me, and they did not respond to me. So help me over them with a torment that you send down on them.

(١١) فَفَتَحۡنَآ أَبۡوَٰبَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ بِمَآءٖ مُّنۡهَمِرٖ

So We opened the gates of the sky with continuous pouring water.

(١٢) وَفَجَّرۡنَا ٱلۡأَرۡضَ عُيُونٗا فَٱلۡتَقَى ٱلۡمَآءُ عَلَىٰٓ أَمۡرٖ قَدۡ قُدِرَ

We caused the Earth to burst open, becoming springs from which water gushed. The water coming down from the sky met with the water gushing from the ground for a matter that Allah had determined in eternity. Everyone was drowned besides those whom Allah saved.

(١٣) وَحَمَلۡنَٰهُ عَلَىٰ ذَاتِ أَلۡوَٰحٖ وَدُسُرٖ

We carried Noah on a ship [made] of planks and nails, saving him and those with him from drowning.

(١٤) تَجۡرِي بِأَعۡيُنِنَا جَزَآءٗ لِّمَن كَانَ كُفِرَ

This ship was sailing on the colliding waves of water under Our gaze and protection as a victory for Noah, whose people rejected him and disbelieved in what he brought from Allah.

(١٥) وَلَقَد تَّرَكۡنَٰهَآ ءَايَةٗ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ

We have left this torment that We meted out to them as a lesson and admonition. Is there anyone to take a lesson from that?

(١٦) فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ

Then how was my torment to those who rejected me? And how was my warning that I will destroy them?

(١٧) وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ

We have made the Quran easy to remember and take lessons from, so is there anyone to heed the lessons and admonishments that it contains?

(١٨) كَذَّبَتۡ عَادٞ فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ

Ād rejected their Prophet, Hūd (peace be upon him). So reflect, O people of Mecca, how [dreadful] was my torment to them? And how was my warning to others about their torment?

(١٩) إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ رِيحٗا صَرۡصَرٗا فِي يَوۡمِ نَحۡسٖ مُّسۡتَمِرّٖ

We sent on them a severe, cold, and screaming wind on a day of evil and misfortune that will continue with them until they reach Hell.

(٢٠) تَنزِعُ ٱلنَّاسَ كَأَنَّهُمۡ أَعۡجَازُ نَخۡلٖ مُّنقَعِرٖ

It snatched people up from the ground and threw them on their heads like the trunks of date palms uprooted from their plantations.

(٢١) فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ

So reflect, O people of Mecca, how [dreadful] was my torment to them? And how was my warning to others about their torment?

(٢٢) وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ

We have made the Quran easy to remember and take lessons from, so is there anyone to heed the lessons and admonishments that it contains?

(٢٣) كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ بِٱلنُّذُرِ

Thamūd rejected what their messenger, Saleh (peace be upon him) warned them about.

(٢٤) فَقَالُوٓاْ أَبَشَرٗا مِّنَّا وَٰحِدٗا نَّتَّبِعُهُۥٓ إِنَّآ إِذٗا لَّفِي ضَلَٰلٖ وَسُعُرٍ

They condemningly said: Should we follow a single human being from among us? If we follow him in this state, we will be far from what is correct, misguided, and in difficulty.

(٢٥) أَءُلۡقِيَ ٱلذِّكۡرُ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۢ بَيۡنِنَا بَلۡ هُوَ كَذَّابٌ أَشِرٞ

Has revelation been revealed to him while he is just one person, and has he been chosen for it instead of all of us? No, he is a boastful liar.

(٢٦) سَيَعۡلَمُونَ غَدٗا مَّنِ ٱلۡكَذَّابُ ٱلۡأَشِرُ

On the Day of Resurrection, they will soon come to know who the boastful liar is, Saleh or them?

(٢٧) إِنَّا مُرۡسِلُواْ ٱلنَّاقَةِ فِتۡنَةٗ لَّهُمۡ فَٱرۡتَقِبۡهُمۡ وَٱصۡطَبِرۡ

We will extract a she-camel from the rock and send it as a test to them. So wait, O Saleh, and watch what they do to it and what is done to them, and be patient with their harm.

Points of Benefit:

The legitimacy of supplicating against the persistent disbeliever.
The destruction of the deniers and the salvation of the believers is an established divine practice.
Facilitation of the Quran for memorization, reflection, and admonition.
