(٢٣) أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ نَصِيبٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ يُدۡعَوۡنَ إِلَىٰ كِتَٰبِ ٱللَّهِ لِيَحۡكُمَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ ثُمَّ يَتَوَلَّىٰ فَرِيقٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ وَهُم مُّعۡرِضُونَ

O Prophet, do you not see the state of Jews whom Allah gave a portion of knowledge from the Torah proving your prophethood? Yet, when they were called to refer to the Book of Allah, the Torah, so that it could decide what they differed in, a group of their scholars and leaders turned away, ignoring the judgment of the Torah, because it did not agree with their desires. The right thing for them to have done, having claimed that they were followers of the Torah, was to be the foremost among people to judge according to it.

(٢٤) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَالُواْ لَن تَمَسَّنَا ٱلنَّارُ إِلَّآ أَيَّامٗا مَّعۡدُودَٰتٖۖ وَغَرَّهُمۡ فِي دِينِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ

They turned away from the truth and ignored it because they used to claim that the Fire would only touch them for a few days, and they would then enter Paradise. This ill-conceived notion that they concocted from lies and fabrications deceived them and made them daring against Allah and His religion.

(٢٥) فَكَيۡفَ إِذَا جَمَعۡنَٰهُمۡ لِيَوۡمٖ لَّا رَيۡبَ فِيهِ وَوُفِّيَتۡ كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ مَّا كَسَبَتۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ

What will be their state, and how much will they regret? It will be incredibly terrible when We gather them to give account on the Day of Resurrection, in which there is no doubt. Every soul will be given the reward of its deeds according to what it deserves, without being wronged by its good acts being reduced and its evil actions increased.

(٢٦) قُلِ ٱللَّهُمَّ مَٰلِكَ ٱلۡمُلۡكِ تُؤۡتِي ٱلۡمُلۡكَ مَن تَشَآءُ وَتَنزِعُ ٱلۡمُلۡكَ مِمَّن تَشَآءُ وَتُعِزُّ مَن تَشَآءُ وَتُذِلُّ مَن تَشَآءُۖ بِيَدِكَ ٱلۡخَيۡرُۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

O Messenger, say in praise of your Lord and declaration of His greatness: O Allah, the Lord of the entire dominion in this world and the Hereafter. You give sovereignty to whoever of Your creation You wish and remove it from whoever of Your creation You wish. You honor whomever You wish and disgrace whomever You wish. All of this is done according to Your wisdom and justice. All good lies only in Your hand. Therefore, you are the Most Capable of everything.

(٢٧) تُولِجُ ٱلَّيۡلَ فِي ٱلنَّهَارِ وَتُولِجُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فِي ٱلَّيۡلِۖ وَتُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡحَيَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ وَتُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡمَيِّتَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَيِّۖ وَتَرۡزُقُ مَن تَشَآءُ بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ

A demonstration of Your power is that You cause the day to become longer by extending it into the night, and You cause the night to become longer by extending it into the day. You bring out the living from the dead – as in a believer from a disbeliever and crops from seed – and You bring out the dead from the living – as in a disbeliever from a believer and a chick from an egg. You give provision to whomever You wish, in abundance, without limit or count.

(٢٨) لَّا يَتَّخِذِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ أَوۡلِيَآءَ مِن دُونِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَۖ وَمَن يَفۡعَلۡ ذَٰلِكَ فَلَيۡسَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ فِي شَيۡءٍ إِلَّآ أَن تَتَّقُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ تُقَىٰةٗۗ وَيُحَذِّرُكُمُ ٱللَّهُ نَفۡسَهُۥۗ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

O believers, do not take disbelievers as allies you love and support instead of other believers. Whoever does this is disassociated from Allah, and Allah is disassociated from them. However, suppose you are under their authority and fear for your lives. In that case, there is no sin upon you if you avoid their harm by being nice to them in your speech and actions openly while hating them inside. Allah warns you of Himself, so fear Him and do not become the subject of His anger by committing sins. All slaves will return to Allah alone on the Day of Resurrection to be recompensed for their deeds.

(٢٩) قُلۡ إِن تُخۡفُواْ مَا فِي صُدُورِكُمۡ أَوۡ تُبۡدُوهُ يَعۡلَمۡهُ ٱللَّهُۗ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

Say, O Prophet: Whether you conceal within your hearts or reveal what Allah has forbidden, such as allying with the disbelievers, Allah knows about it. Nothing is hidden from Him. He knows whatever is in the heavens and the earth. He is the Most Capable of everything; nothing can hinder Him.

Points of Benefit:

That success and guidance are from Allah, and knowledge, even if it is abundant and its possessor reaches the highest ranks, if the guidance of Allah does not accompany it, the person will not benefit from it.
That sovereignty belongs to Allah, for He is the Giver, the Withholder, the Honorer, and the Humiliator. In His Hand is all goodness, and all matters return to Him alone. No one is asked about them except Him.
The danger of allying with the disbelievers, as Allah has warned the one who does so with disavowal from Him and reckoning on the Day of Resurrection.
