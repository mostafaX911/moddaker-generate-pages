(٢٨) وَنَبِّئۡهُمۡ أَنَّ ٱلۡمَآءَ قِسۡمَةُۢ بَيۡنَهُمۡۖ كُلُّ شِرۡبٖ مُّحۡتَضَرٞ

And tell them that the water from their well must be divided between them and the she-camel: one day for it and one day for them. Each turn will be attended by its owner alone on the day allocated to him.

(٢٩) فَنَادَوۡاْ صَاحِبَهُمۡ فَتَعَاطَىٰ فَعَقَرَ

So they called their companion to kill the she-camel, and he took a sword and killed it, fulfilling the instruction of his people.

(٣٠) فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ

So reflect, O people of Mecca, how [dreadful] was my torment to them? And how was my warning to others about their torment?

(٣١) إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ صَيۡحَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ فَكَانُواْ كَهَشِيمِ ٱلۡمُحۡتَظِرِ

We sent against them a single scream, which destroyed them, and they became like twigs used by a person who makes a fence for his sheep.

(٣٢) وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ

We have made the Quran easy to remember and take lessons from, so is there anyone to heed the lessons and admonishments that it contains?

(٣٣) كَذَّبَتۡ قَوۡمُ لُوطِۢ بِٱلنُّذُرِ

The people of Lot rejected what their messenger, Lot (peace be upon him), warned them about.

(٣٤) إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَاصِبًا إِلَّآ ءَالَ لُوطٖۖ نَّجَّيۡنَٰهُم بِسَحَرٖ

We sent against them a wind that threw stones on them except the family of Lot (peace be upon him). The torment did not afflict them, as We had rescued them from it as Lot had traveled away with them before the torment came down in the last part of the night.

(٣٥) نِّعۡمَةٗ مِّنۡ عِندِنَاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي مَن شَكَرَ

We rescued them from the torment as a favor from Us upon them. Like this recompense with which We recompensed Lot, We will recompense those who show gratitude to Allah for His favors.

(٣٦) وَلَقَدۡ أَنذَرَهُم بَطۡشَتَنَا فَتَمَارَوۡاْ بِٱلنُّذُرِ

Lot warned them of Our torment, but they disputed with one another about his warning, and they rejected it.

(٣٧) وَلَقَدۡ رَٰوَدُوهُ عَن ضَيۡفِهِۦ فَطَمَسۡنَآ أَعۡيُنَهُمۡ فَذُوقُواْ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ

Lot's people tried to tempt him to give them access to the angels who were his guests, intending to do an indecent act. We obliterated their eyes so they could not see them, and We said to them: Taste my torment and the result of My warning to you.

(٣٨) وَلَقَدۡ صَبَّحَهُم بُكۡرَةً عَذَابٞ مُّسۡتَقِرّٞ

In the morning, a torment came to them, which will stay with them until they reach the Hereafter. Then, the torment of the Hereafter will come to them.

(٣٩) فَذُوقُواْ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ

They will be told: Taste My punishment that I have sent down upon you and the result of Lot's warning.

(٤٠) وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ

We have made the Quran easy to remember and take lessons from, so is there anyone to heed the lessons and admonishments that it contains?

(٤١) وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَ ءَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ ٱلنُّذُرُ

Our warning also came to the people of Pharaoh on the tongue of Moses and Aaron (peace be upon them).

(٤٢) كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا كُلِّهَا فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُمۡ أَخۡذَ عَزِيزٖ مُّقۡتَدِرٍ

They rejected the proofs and evidence that came to them from Us, so We punished them for their rejection thereof with the punishment of the Mighty, whom none can overpower, the Most Powerful, who is not incapable of anything.

(٤٣) أَكُفَّارُكُمۡ خَيۡرٞ مِّنۡ أُوْلَٰٓئِكُمۡ أَمۡ لَكُم بَرَآءَةٞ فِي ٱلزُّبُرِ

Are your disbelievers, O people of Mecca, better than those disbelievers mentioned: The people of Noah, 'Ād, Thamūd, the people of Lot, and Pharaoh and his people? Or do you have immunity from Allah's torment that the heavenly books have brought?

(٤٤) أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ نَحۡنُ جَمِيعٞ مُّنتَصِرٞ

Or do these disbelievers from the people of Mecca say: We are all united and victorious against those who intend evil against us and wish to break up our unity?

(٤٥) سَيُهۡزَمُ ٱلۡجَمۡعُ وَيُوَلُّونَ ٱلدُّبُرَ

Soon, the group of these disbelievers will be defeated, and they will turn their backs on the believers. This occurred on the day of Badr.

(٤٦) بَلِ ٱلسَّاعَةُ مَوۡعِدُهُمۡ وَٱلسَّاعَةُ أَدۡهَىٰ وَأَمَرُّ

In fact, the Hour they reject is their appointed time to be tormented. The Hour is more catastrophic and bitter than the torment of this world that they received on the day of Badr.

(٤٧) إِنَّ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ وَسُعُرٖ

Those guilty of disbelief and sins are misguided from the truth and in torment and difficulty.

(٤٨) يَوۡمَ يُسۡحَبُونَ فِي ٱلنَّارِ عَلَىٰ وُجُوهِهِمۡ ذُوقُواْ مَسَّ سَقَرَ

On the Day when they will be dragged in the Fire on their faces, it will be said to them rebukingly: Taste the torment of the Fire.

(٤٩) إِنَّا كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقۡنَٰهُ بِقَدَرٖ

We have created everything in existence with a prior decree from Us, in accordance with Our knowledge, will, and what We had written in the Preserved Tablet.

Points of Benefit:

The comprehensiveness of punishment, whether immediate for the crime or deferred along with it.
Expressing gratitude to Allah for His blessings is a means of protection from punishment.
The Quran's foretelling of the defeat of the polytheists on the day of Badr before it happened is an indication of the Quran's truthfulness in informing about the unseen.
The obligation of believing in fate (Qadr).
