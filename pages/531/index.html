(٥٠) وَمَآ أَمۡرُنَآ إِلَّا وَٰحِدَةٞ كَلَمۡحِۢ بِٱلۡبَصَرِ

When We intend something, our command is a single word: Be, and what We intend comes into existence quickly, in the blink of an eye.

(٥١) وَلَقَدۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَآ أَشۡيَاعَكُمۡ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ

We have destroyed those who resemble you in disbelief from the previous nations. Can anyone take a lesson from this and desist from disbelief?

(٥٢) وَكُلُّ شَيۡءٖ فَعَلُوهُ فِي ٱلزُّبُرِ

Everything the slaves have done is written in the books of the guardian angels, from whom nothing escapes.

(٥٣) وَكُلُّ صَغِيرٖ وَكَبِيرٖ مُّسۡتَطَرٌ

Every small and big action or statement is written in the books of deeds and the Preserved Tablet, and they will be recompensed for it accordingly.

(٥٤) إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّٰتٖ وَنَهَرٖ

Those who fear their Lord by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions will enjoy themselves in gardens and flowing rivers.

(٥٥) فِي مَقۡعَدِ صِدۡقٍ عِندَ مَلِيكٖ مُّقۡتَدِرِۢ

In a seat of truth, in which there will be no futility or sin, near a Sovereign Who owns everything, the Powerful Who is not incapable of anything. So do not ask about the perpetual bliss they will receive.

The Beneficent (Surah Ar-Rahman)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Reminding the jinn and humans about Allah's hidden and manifest blessings and the impact of His Mercy in this world and the Hereafter.

Explanation:

(١) ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ

The Most Merciful, possessor of vast mercy.

(٢) عَلَّمَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ

He taught the Quran to people by making its memorization easy and facilitating the understanding of its meanings.

(٣) خَلَقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ

He created man perfectly and formed him in the best manner.

(٤) عَلَّمَهُ ٱلۡبَيَانَ

He taught him how to express what is within his mind in speech and writing.

(٥) ٱلشَّمۡسُ وَٱلۡقَمَرُ بِحُسۡبَانٖ

He decreed a perfectly calculated course for the sun and moon so that people could know the number of years and calculations.

(٦) وَٱلنَّجۡمُ وَٱلشَّجَرُ يَسۡجُدَانِ

Plants without stems and trees prostrate to Allah (may He be Exalted) in submission and surrender to Him.

(٧) وَٱلسَّمَآءَ رَفَعَهَا وَوَضَعَ ٱلۡمِيزَانَ

And the sky, He raised it above the Earth to be its canopy. He established justice on Earth and instructed His slaves with it.

(٨) أَلَّا تَطۡغَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡمِيزَانِ

He established justice so that you, O people, are not unjust and deceitful in weighing and measuring.

(٩) وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلۡوَزۡنَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِ وَلَا تُخۡسِرُواْ ٱلۡمِيزَانَ

Weigh justly between yourselves, and do not give a short weight or measure when you measure or weigh for others.

(١٠) وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَضَعَهَا لِلۡأَنَامِ

And the Earth, He prepared it for the creation to settle on.

(١١) فِيهَا فَٰكِهَةٞ وَٱلنَّخۡلُ ذَاتُ ٱلۡأَكۡمَامِ

On it are trees that produce fruit and date palms that have date stalks that split open to reveal [clusters of] dates.

(١٢) وَٱلۡحَبُّ ذُو ٱلۡعَصۡفِ وَٱلرَّيۡحَانُ

On it are grains with husks like wheat, barley, and plants whose fragrances you find pleasant.

(١٣) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(١٤) خَلَقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِن صَلۡصَٰلٖ كَٱلۡفَخَّارِ

He created Adam (peace be upon him) from dried clay with a ringing sound like that of baked clay.

(١٥) وَخَلَقَ ٱلۡجَآنَّ مِن مَّارِجٖ مِّن نَّارٖ

And He created the father of the jinn from a smokeless flame.

(١٦) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(١٧) رَبُّ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقَيۡنِ وَرَبُّ ٱلۡمَغۡرِبَيۡنِ

The Lord of the two points of sunrise and sunset in the winter and summer.

(١٨) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

Points of Benefit:

Recording deeds, both small and large, in the scrolls of actions.
The initiation of the Most Merciful by mentioning His blessings in the Quran is an indication of the Quran's honor and its exalted status among creation.
The significance of justice in Islam.
The blessings of Allah necessitate our acknowledgment and gratitude, not denial and rejection.
