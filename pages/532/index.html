(١٩) مَرَجَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ يَلۡتَقِيَانِ

Allah merged the two seas, the salty and the sweet, that converge according to what the eye sees.

(٢٠) بَيۡنَهُمَا بَرۡزَخٞ لَّا يَبۡغِيَانِ

Between them is a barrier that prevents each one from crossing over to the other, so that the sweet remains sweet and the salty remains salty.

(٢١) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٢٢) يَخۡرُجُ مِنۡهُمَا ٱللُّؤۡلُؤُ وَٱلۡمَرۡجَانُ

From both these seas, pearls and coral emerge.

(٢٣) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٢٤) وَلَهُ ٱلۡجَوَارِ ٱلۡمُنشَ‍َٔاتُ فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ كَٱلۡأَعۡلَٰمِ

He (may He be Exalted) alone has control of the ships that sail in the sea like mountains.

(٢٥) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٢٦) كُلُّ مَنۡ عَلَيۡهَا فَانٖ

Every creation that is on the face of the earth will inevitably perish.

(٢٧) وَيَبۡقَىٰ وَجۡهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو ٱلۡجَلَٰلِ وَٱلۡإِكۡرَامِ

And the face of your Lord, O Messenger, the Possessor of majesty, favor, and bounty over His slaves, will remain and will never perish.

(٢٨) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٢٩) يَسۡ‍َٔلُهُۥ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ كُلَّ يَوۡمٍ هُوَ فِي شَأۡنٖ

The angels in the heavens and the jinn and humans on Earth ask Him for their needs. Every day, he brings about a matter from the affairs of His slaves, such as giving life, death, provision, etc.

(٣٠) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٣١) سَنَفۡرُغُ لَكُمۡ أَيُّهَ ٱلثَّقَلَانِ

We will attend to your reckoning, O humans and jinn, and recompense each one with the reward or punishment he deserves.

(٣٢) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٣٣) يَٰمَعۡشَرَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِ إِنِ ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُمۡ أَن تَنفُذُواْ مِنۡ أَقۡطَارِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَٱنفُذُواْۚ لَا تَنفُذُونَ إِلَّا بِسُلۡطَٰنٖ

Allah will say on the Day of Resurrection, when He gathers the jinn and humans: O assembly of jinn and humans, if you can find an exit from any part of the heavens and Earth, then do so. You will never be able to do so except with power and evidence, and where do you have that?

(٣٤) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٣٥) يُرۡسَلُ عَلَيۡكُمَا شُوَاظٞ مِّن نَّارٖ وَنُحَاسٞ فَلَا تَنتَصِرَانِ

A flame of smokeless fire and smoke without a flame will be sent upon you, O humans and jinn, and you will not be able to protect yourselves from that.

(٣٦) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٣٧) فَإِذَا ٱنشَقَّتِ ٱلسَّمَآءُ فَكَانَتۡ وَرۡدَةٗ كَٱلدِّهَانِ

When the sky splits open for the angels to descend from it, it will become rose-colored like oil.

(٣٨) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٣٩) فَيَوۡمَئِذٖ لَّا يُسۡ‍َٔلُ عَن ذَنۢبِهِۦٓ إِنسٞ وَلَا جَآنّٞ

On that great Day, no human or jinn will be asked about their sins because Allah knows their deeds.

(٤٠) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٤١) يُعۡرَفُ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ بِسِيمَٰهُمۡ فَيُؤۡخَذُ بِٱلنَّوَٰصِي وَٱلۡأَقۡدَامِ

The guilty ones will be recognized on the Day of Resurrection by their marks, which are black faces and blue eyes. Their forelocks will be tied to their feet, and they will be thrown into Hell.

Points of Benefit:

The coexistence of salty and fresh water without them mixing, exemplifying the manifestations of Allah's omnipotence.
The established mortality of all creatures, emphasizing that permanence belongs to Allah alone. It serves as an admonition for His servants to attach themselves to the Eternal, the Exalted, rather than to anything else.
Affirming the attribute of Face for Allah in a manner befitting His majesty, without resemblance or representation.
The diversity of the punishment for the disbeliever.
