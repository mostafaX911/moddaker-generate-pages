(٤٢) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٤٣) هَٰذِهِۦ جَهَنَّمُ ٱلَّتِي يُكَذِّبُ بِهَا ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ

It will be said to them in rebuke: This is the Hell that the guilty ones used to reject in the worldly life, which is now before their eyes and which they cannot deny.

(٤٤) يَطُوفُونَ بَيۡنَهَا وَبَيۡنَ حَمِيمٍ ءَانٖ

They will alternate between it and scalding water.

(٤٥) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٤٦) وَلِمَنۡ خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِۦ جَنَّتَانِ

The one who fears standing before his Lord in the Hereafter, and therefore believed and did righteous deeds, will have two gardens.

(٤٧) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٤٨) ذَوَاتَآ أَفۡنَانٖ

These two gardens will have huge fresh branches producing fruit.

(٤٩) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٥٠) فِيهِمَا عَيۡنَانِ تَجۡرِيَانِ

In both gardens, there will be two springs flowing between them with water.

(٥١) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٥٢) فِيهِمَا مِن كُلِّ فَٰكِهَةٖ زَوۡجَانِ

There will be two types of fruit to enjoy in both of them.

(٥٣) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٥٤) مُتَّكِ‍ِٔينَ عَلَىٰ فُرُشِۢ بَطَآئِنُهَا مِنۡ إِسۡتَبۡرَقٖۚ وَجَنَى ٱلۡجَنَّتَيۡنِ دَانٖ

Reclining on spreads, whose inner lining will be of thick silk, and the fruits they will pluck from the two gardens will be close and within reach of a person standing, sitting, or reclining.

(٥٥) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٥٦) فِيهِنَّ قَٰصِرَٰتُ ٱلطَّرۡفِ لَمۡ يَطۡمِثۡهُنَّ إِنسٞ قَبۡلَهُمۡ وَلَا جَآنّٞ

In them, there will be women who will restrict their gaze to their husbands, whose virginity was not broken by any man or jinn before their husbands.

(٥٧) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٥٨) كَأَنَّهُنَّ ٱلۡيَاقُوتُ وَٱلۡمَرۡجَانُ

As though they are rubies and coral in their beauty and purity.

(٥٩) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٦٠) هَلۡ جَزَآءُ ٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنِ إِلَّا ٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنُ

What is the recompense of those who followed their Lord well, except that Allah should reward them well?

(٦١) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٦٢) وَمِن دُونِهِمَا جَنَّتَانِ

Below those two mentioned gardens are another two gardens.

(٦٣) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٦٤) مُدۡهَآمَّتَانِ

Both of them will be dark green.

(٦٥) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٦٦) فِيهِمَا عَيۡنَانِ نَضَّاخَتَانِ

In both gardens, springs will forcefully gush forth with water. The gushing of their water will not stop.

(٦٧) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

(٦٨) فِيهِمَا فَٰكِهَةٞ وَنَخۡلٞ وَرُمَّانٞ

There will be abundant fruit in both gardens, huge date palms, and pomegranates.

(٦٩) فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ

So which of Allah's numerous favors to you, O assembly of jinn and humans, do you reject?

Points of Benefit:

The significance of fearing Allah and evoking the awe of standing before Him.
Commending the women of Paradise for their chastity is an indication of the virtue of this trait in women.
The recompense is in accordance with the nature of the deed.
