The Inevitable (Surah Al-Waqi'ah)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing the conditions of the servants on the Day of Resurrection.

Explanation:

(١) إِذَا وَقَعَتِ ٱلۡوَاقِعَةُ

When the inevitable [Day of] Resurrection takes place.

(٢) لَيۡسَ لِوَقۡعَتِهَا كَاذِبَةٌ

There will be no soul that will reject it like how it used to reject it in the worldly life

(٣) خَافِضَةٞ رَّافِعَةٌ

It will debase the wicked disbelievers by entering them into the Fire and elevate the believers, who fear Allah, by entering them into Paradise.

(٤) إِذَا رُجَّتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ رَجّٗا

When the Earth is shaken violently,

(٥) وَبُسَّتِ ٱلۡجِبَالُ بَسّٗا

And the mountains are completely crushed into pieces.

(٦) فَكَانَتۡ هَبَآءٗ مُّنۢبَثّٗا

They will become scattered dust due to the crushing.

(٧) وَكُنتُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا ثَلَٰثَةٗ

You will be divided into three categories on that Day.

(٨) فَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ

The people of the right will receive their books in their right hands. How high and great their rank will be!

(٩) وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡ‍َٔمَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡ‍َٔمَةِ

The people of the left will receive their books in their left hands. How despicable and wretched their rank will be!

(١٠) وَٱلسَّٰبِقُونَ ٱلسَّٰبِقُونَ

The Forerunners in doing good deeds in the world will be the Forerunners in the Hereafter to enter Paradise.

(١١) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَ

They are the ones who will be brought close to Allah.

(١٢) فِي جَنَّٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

In gardens of bliss, they will enjoy different types of pleasure.

(١٣) ثُلَّةٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

A group from this nation and from previous nations.

(١٤) وَقَلِيلٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ

A few people at the end of time will be the foremost ones brought close [to Allah]

(١٥) عَلَىٰ سُرُرٖ مَّوۡضُونَةٖ

On couches woven with gold.

(١٦) مُّتَّكِ‍ِٔينَ عَلَيۡهَا مُتَقَٰبِلِينَ

Reclining on these couches facing each other, no one will look at the back of another.

Points of Benefit:

Continual remembrance of Allah's blessings and His signs is a catalyst for magnifying Allah and performing acts of obedience excellently.
The cessation of the denial by the disbelievers upon witnessing the scenes of the Resurrection.
The varying degrees of the inhabitants of paradise correspond to the diversity of their deeds.
