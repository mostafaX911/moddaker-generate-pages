(١٧) يَطُوفُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وِلۡدَٰنٞ مُّخَلَّدُونَ

Youths who never reach old age or die will roam around them to serve them.

(١٨) بِأَكۡوَابٖ وَأَبَارِيقَ وَكَأۡسٖ مِّن مَّعِينٖ

They will roam around them holding handleless jugs, jugs with handles, and glasses of unlimited wine that flow in Paradise.

(١٩) لَّا يُصَدَّعُونَ عَنۡهَا وَلَا يُنزِفُونَ

It is not like the wine of the world; its drinkers will not suffer from headaches, nor will they be intoxicated.

(٢٠) وَفَٰكِهَةٖ مِّمَّا يَتَخَيَّرُونَ

These youths will also roam around them with fruits they may choose from.

(٢١) وَلَحۡمِ طَيۡرٖ مِّمَّا يَشۡتَهُونَ

They will also roam around them with the meat of birds that they desire.

(٢٢) وَحُورٌ عِينٞ

They will also have beautiful, wide-eyed women in Paradise,

(٢٣) كَأَمۡثَٰلِ ٱللُّؤۡلُوِٕ ٱلۡمَكۡنُونِ

Like pearls protected within their shells.

(٢٤) جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

All as a reward for the righteous deeds they used to do in the worldly life.

(٢٥) لَا يَسۡمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغۡوٗا وَلَا تَأۡثِيمًا

They will not hear foul language in Paradise or something leading to sin.

(٢٦) إِلَّا قِيلٗا سَلَٰمٗا سَلَٰمٗا

They will only hear the angels greeting them and the greetings from each other.

(٢٧) وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡيَمِينِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡيَمِينِ

And the people of the right: who are the people of the right? How great their rank and status with Allah is!

(٢٨) فِي سِدۡرٖ مَّخۡضُودٖ

They will be among harmless, thornless lote trees.

(٢٩) وَطَلۡحٖ مَّنضُودٖ

And clusters of bananas neatly arranged in rows with one another.

(٣٠) وَظِلّٖ مَّمۡدُودٖ

And elongated, continuous shade that will never disappear.

(٣١) وَمَآءٖ مَّسۡكُوبٖ

And flowing water that never stops.

(٣٢) وَفَٰكِهَةٖ كَثِيرَةٖ

And abundant unlimited fruit that are.

(٣٣) لَّا مَقۡطُوعَةٖ وَلَا مَمۡنُوعَةٖ

[Their supply] will never be interrupted because they are not seasonal, nor will anything prevent their desire for them at any time.

(٣٤) وَفُرُشٖ مَّرۡفُوعَةٍ

And elevated, high-class spreads placed on the couches.

(٣٥) إِنَّآ أَنشَأۡنَٰهُنَّ إِنشَآءٗ

We created the aforementioned wide-eyed women in an extraordinary way.

(٣٦) فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُنَّ أَبۡكَارًا

We made them virgins, previously untouched.

(٣٧) عُرُبًا أَتۡرَابٗا

They are very loving to their husbands and of equal age.

(٣٨) لِّأَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ

We created them for the people of the right, those who will be taken towards the right as a sign of their success [in the Hereafter].

(٣٩) ثُلَّةٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

They are a group from the nations of the previous prophets.

(٤٠) وَثُلَّةٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ

And a group from the nation of Muhammad, which is the last of all nations.

(٤١) وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلشِّمَالِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلشِّمَالِ

As for the people of the left: who are the people of the left? How bad their condition and destination are!

(٤٢) فِي سَمُومٖ وَحَمِيمٖ

They will be in scalding wind and water.

(٤٣) وَظِلّٖ مِّن يَحۡمُومٖ

And in the shade of black smoke.

(٤٤) لَّا بَارِدٖ وَلَا كَرِيمٍ

Neither will it be pleasant, nor will it be a good sight.

(٤٥) إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَبۡلَ ذَٰلِكَ مُتۡرَفِينَ

Before they came to the torment, they enjoyed the worldly life's luxuries, caring only for their desires.

(٤٦) وَكَانُواْ يُصِرُّونَ عَلَى ٱلۡحِنثِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

They used to persist in disbelieving in Allah and worshipping idols instead of Allah.

(٤٧) وَكَانُواْ يَقُولُونَ أَئِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابٗا وَعِظَٰمًا أَءِنَّا لَمَبۡعُوثُونَ

They also used to deny the resurrection and say, regarding it, mocking it and considering it far-fetched: When we die and become dust and decomposed bones, will we be resurrected after that?

(٤٨) أَوَ ءَابَآؤُنَا ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ

Will our forefathers, who died before us, also be resurrected?

(٤٩) قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ وَٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ

O Messenger, tell these deniers of the resurrection: The earlier and later generations of people from them...

(٥٠) لَمَجۡمُوعُونَ إِلَىٰ مِيقَٰتِ يَوۡمٖ مَّعۡلُومٖ

will soon be inevitably gathered on the Day of Resurrection to be reckoned and recompensed accordingly.

Points of Benefit:

Righteous deeds are a means to attain bliss in the Hereafter.
Indulgence and luxuriating are among the causes of falling into sins.
The danger of persisting in sin.
