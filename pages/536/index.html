(٥١) ثُمَّ إِنَّكُمۡ أَيُّهَا ٱلضَّآلُّونَ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبُونَ

Then, O rejectors of the resurrection and misguided from the straight path, you will, on the Day of Resurrection, eat from the fruits of the tree of Zaqqum, which are the worst and most disgusting of fruits.

(٥٢) لَأٓكِلُونَ مِن شَجَرٖ مِّن زَقُّومٖ

Then, O rejectors of the resurrection and misguided from the straight path, you will, on the Day of Resurrection, eat from the fruits of the tree of Zaqqum, which are the worst and most disgusting of fruits.

(٥٣) فَمَالِ‍ُٔونَ مِنۡهَا ٱلۡبُطُونَ

Filling your hungry bellies with the fruits of that bitter tree.

(٥٤) فَشَٰرِبُونَ عَلَيۡهِ مِنَ ٱلۡحَمِيمِ

Then, on top of that, you will drink scalding water.

(٥٥) فَشَٰرِبُونَ شُرۡبَ ٱلۡهِيمِ

You will drink so much of it, just like a camel suffering from burning thirst drinks.

(٥٦) هَٰذَا نُزُلُهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلدِّينِ

The aforementioned bitter fruit and scalding water are the hospitality you will receive on the Day of Recompense.

(٥٧) نَحۡنُ خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ فَلَوۡلَا تُصَدِّقُونَ

O rejectors, We have created you after you were non-existent, so why do you not believe that We will resurrect you after you have died?

(٥٨) أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تُمۡنُونَ

O people, have you seen the semen you ejaculate into the wombs of your wives?

(٥٩) ءَأَنتُمۡ تَخۡلُقُونَهُۥٓ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقُونَ

Do you create that semen, or is it We who create it?

(٦٠) نَحۡنُ قَدَّرۡنَا بَيۡنَكُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمَسۡبُوقِينَ

We have decreed death among you; each of you has a fixed [amount of] time that he will neither go beyond nor fall short of, and We are not incapable...

(٦١) عَلَىٰٓ أَن نُّبَدِّلَ أَمۡثَٰلَكُمۡ وَنُنشِئَكُمۡ فِي مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

[We are not incapable] of changing the form and appearance you currently know of and recreating you into a form and appearance you do not know.

(٦٢) وَلَقَدۡ عَلِمۡتُمُ ٱلنَّشۡأَةَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ فَلَوۡلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ

You know how We created you initially, so do you not take heed and realize that the One who created you in the first place can resurrect you after you die?

(٦٣) أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تَحۡرُثُونَ

Have you seen the seeds you plant on the Earth?

(٦٤) ءَأَنتُمۡ تَزۡرَعُونَهُۥٓ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلزَّٰرِعُونَ

Do you make that seed grow, or do We make it grow?

(٦٥) لَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ حُطَٰمٗا فَظَلۡتُمۡ تَفَكَّهُونَ

If We wished to turn that crop into debris, We could do so after it was close to ripening and being harvested. You would then remain shocked at what affected it.

(٦٦) إِنَّا لَمُغۡرَمُونَ

You would say: We were tormented by losing what we had spent.

(٦٧) بَلۡ نَحۡنُ مَحۡرُومُونَ

Rather, we have been deprived of provision.

(٦٨) أَفَرَءَيۡتُمُ ٱلۡمَآءَ ٱلَّذِي تَشۡرَبُونَ

Have you seen the water you drink when you are thirsty?

(٦٩) ءَأَنتُمۡ أَنزَلۡتُمُوهُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُزۡنِ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلۡمُنزِلُونَ

Do you cause it to come down from the clouds in the sky, or do We send it down?

(٧٠) لَوۡ نَشَآءُ جَعَلۡنَٰهُ أُجَاجٗا فَلَوۡلَا تَشۡكُرُونَ

If We wished to make that water extremely bitter, such that it would be of no use for drinking nor irrigation, We would have done so. Will you not be grateful to Allah for sending it down as palatable and a mercy for you?

(٧١) أَفَرَءَيۡتُمُ ٱلنَّارَ ٱلَّتِي تُورُونَ

Have you seen the Fire that you kindle for your use?

(٧٢) ءَأَنتُمۡ أَنشَأۡتُمۡ شَجَرَتَهَآ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلۡمُنشِ‍ُٔونَ

Do you make the trees from which it is kindled grow, or do We cause it to grow, being kind to you?

(٧٣) نَحۡنُ جَعَلۡنَٰهَا تَذۡكِرَةٗ وَمَتَٰعٗا لِّلۡمُقۡوِينَ

We have made this fire for you as a reminder of the Fire of the Hereafter, and We have made it a means of benefit for the travelers among you.

(٧٤) فَسَبِّحۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

So, O Messenger, free your Lord, the Greatest, from that which does not befit Him.

(٧٥) ۞فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِمَوَٰقِعِ ٱلنُّجُومِ

Allah swore by the positions and locations of the stars.

(٧٦) وَإِنَّهُۥ لَقَسَمٞ لَّوۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ عَظِيمٌ

And [taking] an oath by these locations is great if only you were to understand its magnificence, as there are unlimited signs and lessons in it.

Points of Benefit:

The ease of resurrection is apparent in the indication of the initial creation.
The descent of water, the growth of the earth, and the fire from which people benefit are blessings that necessitate gratitude to Allah. He has the power to deprive them whenever He wills.
Believing that the planets have an influence on rainfall is a form of disbelief and is a practice from the era of ignorance.
