(٧٧) إِنَّهُۥ لَقُرۡءَانٞ كَرِيمٞ

O people, the Quran that is recited to you is a noble Quran because of its great benefits.

(٧٨) فِي كِتَٰبٖ مَّكۡنُونٖ

It is in a record protected from people's eyes, i.e., the Preserved Tablet.

(٧٩) لَّا يَمَسُّهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡمُطَهَّرُونَ

Only the angels, pure of sin and defects, can touch it.

(٨٠) تَنزِيلٞ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

It is a revelation from the Lord of the creations to His Prophet Muhammad.

(٨١) أَفَبِهَٰذَا ٱلۡحَدِيثِ أَنتُم مُّدۡهِنُونَ

O polytheists, do you then reject this discourse and not believe in it?

(٨٢) وَتَجۡعَلُونَ رِزۡقَكُمۡ أَنَّكُمۡ تُكَذِّبُونَ

And do you show your gratitude to Allah for the favors He has provided you with by rejecting Him and attributing the rain to the setting of stars, by saying: It rained upon us due to the setting of so and so star.

After presenting evidence for the Resurrection, Allah draws attention to His ability to bring the dead back to life. He highlights their inability to avoid death, as the One who can take life can also restore it. Allah states:

(٨٣) فَلَوۡلَآ إِذَا بَلَغَتِ ٱلۡحُلۡقُومَ

Why not when the soul reaches the throat,

(٨٤) وَأَنتُمۡ حِينَئِذٖ تَنظُرُونَ

And at that time, you look at the dying person in front of you,

(٨٥) وَنَحۡنُ أَقۡرَبُ إِلَيۡهِ مِنكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن لَّا تُبۡصِرُونَ

While We, through Our knowledge, power, and Angels, are closer to the dying person than you are, but you do not see these angels,

(٨٦) فَلَوۡلَآ إِن كُنتُمۡ غَيۡرَ مَدِينِينَ

Why not then? If you are not going to be resurrected, as you claim, to be recompensed for your deeds,

(٨٧) تَرۡجِعُونَهَآ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

Return this soul that is leaving the dying person among you, if you are truthful. You cannot do it.

(٨٨) فَأَمَّآ إِن كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبِينَ

So, if the deceased is one of The Forerunners in doing good deeds,

(٨٩) فَرَوۡحٞ وَرَيۡحَانٞ وَجَنَّتُ نَعِيمٖ

Then he will be in such comfort, after which there will be no tiring, and in good provision and mercy. He will have a garden where he will live in luxury, enjoying whatever he desires.

(٩٠) وَأَمَّآ إِن كَانَ مِنۡ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ

And if the deceased is from the people of the right, do not worry about their condition because they will be in peace and security.

(٩١) فَسَلَٰمٞ لَّكَ مِنۡ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ

And if the deceased is from the people of the right, do not worry about their condition because they will be in peace and security.

(٩٢) وَأَمَّآ إِن كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

And if the deceased is from the rejectors of what the Messenger brought and is misguided from the straight path,

(٩٣) فَنُزُلٞ مِّنۡ حَمِيمٖ

Then his hospitable reception, which he will be presented with, is scalding water.

(٩٤) وَتَصۡلِيَةُ جَحِيمٍ

And he will have to burn in the Fire of the Inferno.

(٩٥) إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ حَقُّ ٱلۡيَقِينِ

O Messenger, this which We have narrated to you is convincingly true, in which there is no doubt.

(٩٦) فَسَبِّحۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

So free and sanctify the name of your Lord, the Greatest, from all defects.

The Iron (Surah Al-Hadid)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Elevation of souls through belief and spending in Allah's path.

Explanation:

(١) سَبَّحَ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

Every creation of His in the heavens and the Earth free and sanctify Allah [from any deficiency]. He is the Almighty whom no one can overpower; the Wise in His creation and decree.

(٢) لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُۖ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ

To Him alone belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth. He gives life to whoever He wishes to give life to and causes death to whoever He wishes to cause death to. Allah is Omnipotent over everything; there is nothing that He is incapable of.

(٣) هُوَ ٱلۡأَوَّلُ وَٱلۡأٓخِرُ وَٱلظَّٰهِرُ وَٱلۡبَاطِنُۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٌ

He is the First; nothing existed before Him. He is the Last; nothing will exist after Him. He is the Apparent; there is nothing above Him. He is the Intimate; there is nothing without Him, and He knows everything; nothing escapes His knowledge.

Points of Benefit:

The severity of the pangs of death and the incapacity of humans to avert them.
The default is that humans do not perceive the angels unless Allah wills it for a specific wisdom.
The names of Allah (the First, the Last, the Manifest, the Hidden) necessitate the magnification of Allah and the vigilant observation of Him in both evident and concealed actions.
