(٤) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ يَعۡلَمُ مَا يَلِجُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا يَخۡرُجُ مِنۡهَا وَمَا يَنزِلُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا يَعۡرُجُ فِيهَاۖ وَهُوَ مَعَكُمۡ أَيۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٞ

He is the One who created the heavens and the Earth in six days, starting from Sunday and finishing on Friday, yet He is capable of having created them in less than the duration of the blinking of an eye. He (may He be Exalted) then rose over His Throne as it befits Him, may He be Exalted. He knows everything that enters the Earth, i.e., every drop of rain, seed, etc.; He knows everything that emerges from it, i.e., every plant, mineral, etc.; He knows everything that descends from the sky, i.e., rain, revelation, etc., and He knows everything that ascends to it, i.e., angels, souls, and deeds of the slaves, etc. And He is with you wherever you are, O people, in terms of His knowledge; nothing is hidden from Him, and He sees everything you do. None of your deeds are hidden from Him, and He will recompense you accordingly.

(٥) لَّهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ تُرۡجَعُ ٱلۡأُمُورُ

To Him alone belongs the dominion of the heavens and the Earth, and to Him alone, all affairs return. He will then reckon the creation on the Day of Resurrection and recompense them for their deeds accordingly.

(٦) يُولِجُ ٱلَّيۡلَ فِي ٱلنَّهَارِ وَيُولِجُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فِي ٱلَّيۡلِۚ وَهُوَ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

He enters the night into the day, the darkness arrives, and people sleep; He enters the day into the night, the light arrives, and people go about their work. He is aware of everything that the breasts of His slaves contain; none of it is hidden from Him.

(٧) ءَامِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَأَنفِقُواْ مِمَّا جَعَلَكُم مُّسۡتَخۡلَفِينَ فِيهِۖ فَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنكُمۡ وَأَنفَقُواْ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرٞ كَبِيرٞ

Believe in Allah and His Messenger, and spend from the wealth of which Allah has made you trustees, using it according to what Allah has legislated for you. So those of you who believe in Allah and spend their wealth in His path, for them is a great reward with Him, which is Paradise.

(٨) وَمَا لَكُمۡ لَا تُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلرَّسُولُ يَدۡعُوكُمۡ لِتُؤۡمِنُواْ بِرَبِّكُمۡ وَقَدۡ أَخَذَ مِيثَٰقَكُمۡ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

And what stops you from believing in Allah when the Messenger is inviting you towards Allah, hoping that you believe in your Lord (may He be Exalted) and Allah had taken a covenant from you to believe in Him when He took you out from the loins of your fathers, if you are believers?

(٩) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُنَزِّلُ عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِهِۦٓ ءَايَٰتِۢ بَيِّنَٰتٖ لِّيُخۡرِجَكُم مِّنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِكُمۡ لَرَءُوفٞ رَّحِيمٞ

He is the One who sends down clear verses upon His slave Muhammad to take you out of the darkness of disbelief and ignorance and into the light of belief and knowledge. And indeed, Allah is kind and merciful to you that He sent His Prophet to you as a guide and a bearer of glad tidings.

(١٠) وَمَا لَكُمۡ أَلَّا تُنفِقُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَلِلَّهِ مِيرَٰثُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ لَا يَسۡتَوِي مِنكُم مَّنۡ أَنفَقَ مِن قَبۡلِ ٱلۡفَتۡحِ وَقَٰتَلَۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَعۡظَمُ دَرَجَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنفَقُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدُ وَقَٰتَلُواْۚ وَكُلّٗا وَعَدَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٞ

And what prevents you from spending in the path of Allah when the inheritance of the heavens and the Earth belongs to Allah? O believers! Those of you who spent their wealth in the path of Allah, seeking His pleasure before the conquest of Mecca, and fought the disbelievers in aid of Islam cannot be equal to those who spent their wealth in His path and fought the disbelievers after the conquest of Mecca. Those who spent their wealth before the conquest and fought in the path of Allah are greater in rank in the sight of Allah and higher in status than those who spent their wealth in His path and fought the disbelievers after its conquest. However, Allah has promised both groups Paradise, and He is informed of all that you do; none of your deeds are hidden from Him, and He will soon recompense you for them accordingly.

(١١) مَّن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يُقۡرِضُ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنٗا فَيُضَٰعِفَهُۥ لَهُۥ وَلَهُۥٓ أَجۡرٞ كَرِيمٞ

Who is it that will spend his wealth, happily, for the pleasure of Allah, so that Allah may grant him a multitude of rewards for the wealth he has spent, and he will receive on the Day of Resurrection a noble reward which is Paradise?

Points of Benefit:

Wealth is Allah's wealth, and humans are stewards of it.
The varying degrees of believers are in accordance with their precedence in embracing belief and performing righteous deeds.
Spending in the path of Allah is a cause for the blessing and growth of wealth.
