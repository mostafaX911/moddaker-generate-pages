(١٩) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرُسُلِهِۦٓ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلصِّدِّيقُونَۖ وَٱلشُّهَدَآءُ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرُهُمۡ وَنُورُهُمۡۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَكَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

Those who believe in Allah and His messengers without differentiating between them are the truthful ones. The martyrs have their generous reward prepared for them with Allah, and they will also have a light that shines in front of them and on their right sides on the Day of Resurrection. As for those who disbelieved in Allah and His messengers and rejected Our verses revealed to Our Messenger, they are the people of the Inferno, which they will enter on the Day of Resurrection and remain therein forever, never to come out of it.

(٢٠) ٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لَعِبٞ وَلَهۡوٞ وَزِينَةٞ وَتَفَاخُرُۢ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَتَكَاثُرٞ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰلِ وَٱلۡأَوۡلَٰدِۖ كَمَثَلِ غَيۡثٍ أَعۡجَبَ ٱلۡكُفَّارَ نَبَاتُهُۥ ثُمَّ يَهِيجُ فَتَرَىٰهُ مُصۡفَرّٗا ثُمَّ يَكُونُ حُطَٰمٗاۖ وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ عَذَابٞ شَدِيدٞ وَمَغۡفِرَةٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرِضۡوَٰنٞۚ وَمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ إِلَّا مَتَٰعُ ٱلۡغُرُورِ

Know that the life of this world is only a pastime that the bodies play with; futility that the hearts engage in; beauty that you adorn yourselves with; boasting with one another over possessions and ownership; and taking pride in the abundance of wealth and offspring. It is just like the rainfall that pleases the farmers with the growth it brings, but the green growth does not last long before it begins to dry. Then, O onlooker, you see it after its greenery has turned yellow. Allah then turns it into broken bits falling apart. And in the Hereafter, there will be severe torment for the disbelievers and the hypocrites, and a special forgiveness from Allah for the sins of His believing slaves.

(٢١) سَابِقُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَغۡفِرَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرۡضُهَا كَعَرۡضِ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ أُعِدَّتۡ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرُسُلِهِۦۚ ذَٰلِكَ فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ يُؤۡتِيهِ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو ٱلۡفَضۡلِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

O people, hasten towards righteous deeds such as repentance and other deeds, through which you will achieve forgiveness for your sins. Through them, You will acquire a paradise, the width of which is the width of the heavens and the Earth. Allah has prepared this Paradise for those who believe in Him and His messengers. That reward is Allah's grace that He grants whoever of His slaves He wishes. He (may He be Exalted) is the bestower of great grace on His believing slaves.

(٢٢) مَآ أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن نَّبۡرَأَهَآۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرٞ

No calamity afflicts the people on Earth, such as drought, etc., nor a personal calamity, except that it was recorded in the Preserved Tablet before We created the creation. Indeed, that is easy for Allah.

(٢٣) لِّكَيۡلَا تَأۡسَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَاتَكُمۡ وَلَا تَفۡرَحُواْ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخۡتَالٖ فَخُورٍ

O people, that is so that you do not grieve over anything you miss out on and do not boast about the favors Allah has given you. Allah does not love anyone who is arrogant and boastful over what Allah has granted him.

(٢٤) ٱلَّذِينَ يَبۡخَلُونَ وَيَأۡمُرُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ بِٱلۡبُخۡلِۗ وَمَن يَتَوَلَّ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ

Those who are miserly in spending whatever is obligatory for them to spend and instruct others to be miserly are losers. Whoever turns away from the obedience of Allah will never harm Allah; he only harms himself. Allah is Independent; He does not need the obedience of His slaves, and He is praiseworthy in every state.

Points of Benefit:

Asceticism towards the world and its pleasures and encouragement towards the Hereafter and its everlasting delights assist in treading the straight path.
The obligation of believing in fate (Qadr).
One of the benefits of believing in fate is not grieving over missed opportunities in worldly affairs.
Stinginess and advocating for it are two blameworthy qualities that do not characterize a believer.
