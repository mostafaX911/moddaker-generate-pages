(٢٥) لَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا رُسُلَنَا بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَأَنزَلۡنَا مَعَهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡمِيزَانَ لِيَقُومَ ٱلنَّاسُ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۖ وَأَنزَلۡنَا ٱلۡحَدِيدَ فِيهِ بَأۡسٞ شَدِيدٞ وَمَنَٰفِعُ لِلنَّاسِ وَلِيَعۡلَمَ ٱللَّهُ مَن يَنصُرُهُۥ وَرُسُلَهُۥ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَوِيٌّ عَزِيزٞ

We have sent Our messengers with clear evidence and manifest proofs and sent down books with them and the balance so that people would uphold justice. We also sent down iron, which has great strength; weapons are made from it, and it has many other benefits for the people in their trades and occupations. And so that Allah knows in such a way that is manifest to the people who it is, that supports Him and His messengers without seeing Him. Allah is Strong and Almighty; nothing can overcome His decree, nor is He incapable of doing anything.

(٢٦) وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحٗا وَإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَجَعَلۡنَا فِي ذُرِّيَّتِهِمَا ٱلنُّبُوَّةَ وَٱلۡكِتَٰبَۖ فَمِنۡهُم مُّهۡتَدٖۖ وَكَثِيرٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ فَٰسِقُونَ

And We sent Noah and Abraham (peace be upon them) as messengers. We placed messengerships, and revealed books among their offspring. So from among their offspring are those who were rightly guided to the straight path and are successful, but most of them abandoned Allah's obedience.

(٢٧) ثُمَّ قَفَّيۡنَا عَلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِهِم بِرُسُلِنَا وَقَفَّيۡنَا بِعِيسَى ٱبۡنِ مَرۡيَمَ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلۡإِنجِيلَۖ وَجَعَلۡنَا فِي قُلُوبِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُ رَأۡفَةٗ وَرَحۡمَةٗۚ وَرَهۡبَانِيَّةً ٱبۡتَدَعُوهَا مَا كَتَبۡنَٰهَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ إِلَّا ٱبۡتِغَآءَ رِضۡوَٰنِ ٱللَّهِ فَمَا رَعَوۡهَا حَقَّ رِعَايَتِهَاۖ فَ‍َٔاتَيۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ أَجۡرَهُمۡۖ وَكَثِيرٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ فَٰسِقُونَ

Then We followed them up with Our messengers, sending them one after the other to their nations. We sent Jesus, the son of Mary, after them and gave him the Gospel, placing gentleness and mercy in the hearts of those who believed in and followed him. They were affectionate and merciful to one another but innovated extremism into their religion and abandoned marriage and comforts, which We had made permissible. We did not seek that from them; they obligated it upon themselves and innovated in the religion. We only sought that they follow the pleasure of Allah, which they did not do, and so We granted those who believed among them their reward. However, most abandoned Our obedience by rejecting what Our Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought to them.

(٢٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَءَامِنُواْ بِرَسُولِهِۦ يُؤۡتِكُمۡ كِفۡلَيۡنِ مِن رَّحۡمَتِهِۦ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ نُورٗا تَمۡشُونَ بِهِۦ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, fear Allah by fulfilling His commands and avoiding His prohibitions; He will give you double the reward for believing in Muhammad and the previous messengers. He will also grant you a light through which you will be guided in your worldly life and which you shall use on the Bridge on the Day of Resurrection. He will also forgive your sins by covering them and not tormenting you for them; and Allah (may He be Exalted) is forgiving of the sins of His slaves and merciful towards them.

(٢٩) لِّئَلَّا يَعۡلَمَ أَهۡلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أَلَّا يَقۡدِرُونَ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٖ مِّن فَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَأَنَّ ٱلۡفَضۡلَ بِيَدِ ٱللَّهِ يُؤۡتِيهِ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو ٱلۡفَضۡلِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

O believers, verily, We have clearly explained Our great grace, which We have prepared for you in the form of multiplied rewards, so that the people of the previous Book, i.e., the Jews and Christians, know that they cannot access any of Allah's grace in terms of being able to give it or withhold it from whosoever they wish, and so that they also know that true grace is in Allah's Hand alone, may He be Exalted; He grants it to whosoever He wishes of His slaves. And Allah is the bestower of great grace, which He exclusively gives to whichever of His slaves He wishes.

Points of Benefit:

The truth must have a force to protect and propagate it.
Clarification of the significance of justice in divine laws.
Lineage to the righteous and faithful does not avail a person unless they themselves are believers.
Clarification of the prohibition of innovation in religion.
