The Dispute (Surah Al-Mujadalah)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Demonstrating Allah's comprehensive knowledge and profound understanding to nurture awareness of Him and as a warning against defying Him.

Explanation:

(١) قَدۡ سَمِعَ ٱللَّهُ قَوۡلَ ٱلَّتِي تُجَٰدِلُكَ فِي زَوۡجِهَا وَتَشۡتَكِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَٱللَّهُ يَسۡمَعُ تَحَاوُرَكُمَآۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعُۢ بَصِيرٌ

O Messenger, verily Allah has heard the words of the woman [She was Khawlah bint Tha'labah] who was discussing the issue of her husband with you [who was Aws bin Al-Samit] after he had pronounced dhihar on her. She was complaining to Allah about what her husband had done to her, and Allah was listening to your exchange of words; nothing is hidden from Him. Indeed, He is The Hearing of the speech of His slaves and The Seeing of their actions; none of them are hidden from Him.

(٢) ٱلَّذِينَ يُظَٰهِرُونَ مِنكُم مِّن نِّسَآئِهِم مَّا هُنَّ أُمَّهَٰتِهِمۡۖ إِنۡ أُمَّهَٰتُهُمۡ إِلَّا ٱلَّٰٓـِٔي وَلَدۡنَهُمۡۚ وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَيَقُولُونَ مُنكَرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ وَزُورٗاۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَعَفُوٌّ غَفُورٞ

Those who pronounce dhihar on their women, by one of them saying to his wife: You are like my mother upon me, have lied in this statement of theirs, for their wives are not their mothers. Their mothers are only those who gave birth to them. And indeed, when they say that statement, they say a terrible word and a lie. And, indeed, Allah is surely pardoning and forgiving, for He has legislated expiation for them as a means of ridding them of sin.

(٣) وَٱلَّذِينَ يُظَٰهِرُونَ مِن نِّسَآئِهِمۡ ثُمَّ يَعُودُونَ لِمَا قَالُواْ فَتَحۡرِيرُ رَقَبَةٖ مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَتَمَآسَّاۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ تُوعَظُونَ بِهِۦۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٞ

And those who say this terrible word then want intercourse with those whom they pronounced dhihar on, then they must atone by freeing a slave before they have intercourse with them. You are commanded with that mentioned ruling as a deterrent from dhihar. And Allah is Well-Informed of what you do. Nothing from your deeds is hidden from Him.

(٤) فَمَن لَّمۡ يَجِدۡ فَصِيَامُ شَهۡرَيۡنِ مُتَتَابِعَيۡنِ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يَتَمَآسَّاۖ فَمَن لَّمۡ يَسۡتَطِعۡ فَإِطۡعَامُ سِتِّينَ مِسۡكِينٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ لِتُؤۡمِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦۚ وَتِلۡكَ حُدُودُ ٱللَّهِۗ وَلِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ

So whoever cannot find a slave to free, it is necessary upon him to fast for two months consecutively before he has sexual intercourse with the wife he pronounced dhihar on. Then whoever is incapable of fasting for two months consecutively should feed sixty poor people. This command I have given is so that you believe that Allah is the One who has commanded you with it and so you carry it out. These rulings that We legislate for you are Our (Allah's) limits He has set for His slaves, so do not transgress them. As for those who disbelieve in the commands of Allah and the limits He has set, will face a painful punishment.

(٥) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُحَآدُّونَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ كُبِتُواْ كَمَا كُبِتَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ وَقَدۡ أَنزَلۡنَآ ءَايَٰتِۢ بَيِّنَٰتٖۚ وَلِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَذَابٞ مُّهِينٞ

Indeed, those who oppose Allah and His Messenger have been debased and humiliated just as those who opposed Him from the past nations were debased and humiliated. And we have certainly sent down clear verses. [Prepared] for the disbelievers in Allah, His messengers, and His verses is a humiliating torment.

(٦) يَوۡمَ يَبۡعَثُهُمُ ٱللَّهُ جَمِيعٗا فَيُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا عَمِلُوٓاْۚ أَحۡصَىٰهُ ٱللَّهُ وَنَسُوهُۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدٌ

The Day Allah resurrects them all, leaving out none of them. Then He will inform them of what they did in abhorrent deeds in the worldly life. Allah tallied those deeds against them, so nothing of their actions escaped Him. They forgot them then found them written in their sheets [of deeds], which leave nothing small nor large except that they tallied it. And Allah is Observant over all things; nothing of their deeds is hidden from Him.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's kindness to His oppressed slaves by answering their supplications and aiding them.
From Allah's mercy to His slaves are the different types of expiation for dhihar according to one's ability to relieve the slave from hardship.
Concluding the verses of dhihar by mentioning the disbelievers indicates that it is among their deeds. Then, it was appropriate to cite some of the circumstances of the disbelievers.
