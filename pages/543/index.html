(٧) أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ مَا يَكُونُ مِن نَّجۡوَىٰ ثَلَٰثَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ رَابِعُهُمۡ وَلَا خَمۡسَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ سَادِسُهُمۡ وَلَآ أَدۡنَىٰ مِن ذَٰلِكَ وَلَآ أَكۡثَرَ إِلَّا هُوَ مَعَهُمۡ أَيۡنَ مَا كَانُواْۖ ثُمَّ يُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا عَمِلُواْ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٌ

Have you not perceived, O Messenger, that Allah knows what is in the heavens and the Earth? Nothing within them is hidden from Him. There is no secret conversation between three individuals except that He (may He be Exalted) is their fourth by His knowledge. And there is no secret conversation between five individuals except that He (may He be Exalted) is their sixth by His knowledge, and not less than that number, nor more except that He is with them by His knowledge wherever they are. Nothing is hidden from Him of their talk. Then, on the Day of Resurrection, Allah will inform them of what they did. Indeed, Allah is Omniscient; nothing is hidden from Him.

(٨) أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ نُهُواْ عَنِ ٱلنَّجۡوَىٰ ثُمَّ يَعُودُونَ لِمَا نُهُواْ عَنۡهُ وَيَتَنَٰجَوۡنَ بِٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡعُدۡوَٰنِ وَمَعۡصِيَتِ ٱلرَّسُولِۖ وَإِذَا جَآءُوكَ حَيَّوۡكَ بِمَا لَمۡ يُحَيِّكَ بِهِ ٱللَّهُ وَيَقُولُونَ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَوۡلَا يُعَذِّبُنَا ٱللَّهُ بِمَا نَقُولُۚ حَسۡبُهُمۡ جَهَنَّمُ يَصۡلَوۡنَهَاۖ فَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

Have you not perceived, O Messenger, the Jews who would secretly discuss if they saw a believer? So Allah forbade them from discussing secretly. Then they go back to what Allah forbade them from, and they secretly discuss between themselves with what entails sin, such as backbiting the believers, enmity towards them, and disobedience to the Messenger. And if they come to you, O Messenger, they greet you with a greeting which Allah did not greet you with, saying: As-sammu' alayka (death be upon you),intending [his] demise. And they say in rejection to the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم: Would not Allah torment us for what we say? For if he were truthful in what he claims of being a Prophet, Allah surely would have tormented us for what we say about him!Hell is enough punishment for what they said. They will suffer its heat, so how gruesome the destination that they head toward.

(٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا تَنَٰجَيۡتُمۡ فَلَا تَتَنَٰجَوۡاْ بِٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡعُدۡوَٰنِ وَمَعۡصِيَتِ ٱلرَّسُولِ وَتَنَٰجَوۡاْ بِٱلۡبِرِّ وَٱلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, do not secretly discuss with what entails sin, enmity, or disobedience to the Messenger so that you will not be like the Jews. And secretly discuss what entails obedience to Allah and abstaining from disobeying Him. And take heed of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, for He is whom you will be solely gathered to on the Day of Resurrection for account and recompense.

(١٠) إِنَّمَا ٱلنَّجۡوَىٰ مِنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ لِيَحۡزُنَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَلَيۡسَ بِضَآرِّهِمۡ شَيۡ‍ًٔا إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ

Secret discussion containing sin, enmity, and disobedience to the Messenger is but from the Devil's embellishing and his whispering to his allies to bring sorrow to the believers [by the fact] that they are plotted against. The Devil and his embellishments are not harmful to the believers except by Allah's wish and desire. Let the believers rely upon Allah in all their affairs.

After mentioning the etiquette of speaking, Allah specifies the etiquette of gatherings, stating:

(١١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا قِيلَ لَكُمۡ تَفَسَّحُواْ فِي ٱلۡمَجَٰلِسِ فَٱفۡسَحُواْ يَفۡسَحِ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۖ وَإِذَا قِيلَ ٱنشُزُواْ فَٱنشُزُواْ يَرۡفَعِ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ دَرَجَٰتٖۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٞ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, if it is said to you: make space in a seated gathering, and they make space in them, Allah will make space for you in life in this world and the Afterlife. And if it is said to you: rise from some of the seats so that the people of virtue may sit in them and they rise from them, Allah (may He be Exalted) will raise those who believed amongst you and those who were given knowledge in tremendous degrees. And Allah is Well-Informed of what you do. Nothing of your actions is hidden from Him, and He will recompense you [based] upon them.

Points of Benefit:

Despite Allah being above His slaves in His essence, He is observant of them with His knowledge. Nothing is hidden from Him.
Being that much of creation is sinful with secret discussion, Allah commanded the believers to conduct their private conversations with good conduct and heedfulness.
Among the manners of gatherings is making space for others.
