(١٢) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا نَٰجَيۡتُمُ ٱلرَّسُولَ فَقَدِّمُواْ بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ نَجۡوَىٰكُمۡ صَدَقَةٗۚ ذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ وَأَطۡهَرُۚ فَإِن لَّمۡ تَجِدُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٌ

When the Companions [of the Messenger] were secretly discussing a great deal with the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم, Allah said: O you who have believed, when you want to talk privately with the Messenger, give some charity before you talk in private with him. Giving in charity will be better and purer for you because it is an act of obedience to Allah that purifies the hearts. But if you do not find anything you can give in charity, there is no harm in you talking in private with him because Allah is Forgiving of the sins of His slaves, and He is Merciful towards them by not burdening them except with what they are capable of.

(١٣) ءَأَشۡفَقۡتُمۡ أَن تُقَدِّمُواْ بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ نَجۡوَىٰكُمۡ صَدَقَٰتٖۚ فَإِذۡ لَمۡ تَفۡعَلُواْ وَتَابَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥۚ وَٱللَّهُ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Have you feared poverty from putting forth charity when you secretly discuss with the Messenger?! So if you do not do what Allah commanded from it and He has turned to you in repentance when He permitted you to leave it, then come to prayer in the most complete manner, give zakat (obligatory alms) of your wealth, and obey Allah and his Messenger. Allah is Well-Informed of what you do. Nothing of your actions is hidden from Him, and He will recompense you upon them.

(١٤) ۞أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ تَوَلَّوۡاْ قَوۡمًا غَضِبَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِم مَّا هُم مِّنكُمۡ وَلَا مِنۡهُمۡ وَيَحۡلِفُونَ عَلَى ٱلۡكَذِبِ وَهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُونَ

Have you not perceived, O Messenger, the hypocrites who allied, the Jews whom Allah was angered with due to their disbelief and acts of disobedience? These hypocrites are not from the believers nor the Jews. Rather, they go back and forth, not to them nor to them. They swear that they are Muslims and did not carry the news of the Muslims to the Jews. They are liars in what they swear by.

(١٥) أَعَدَّ ٱللَّهُ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٗا شَدِيدًاۖ إِنَّهُمۡ سَآءَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

Allah has prepared a severe torment for them in the Afterlife when He will enter them into the lowest depths of the Fire. Indeed, what they were upon of disbelieving acts in the worldly life was vile.

(١٦) ٱتَّخَذُوٓاْ أَيۡمَٰنَهُمۡ جُنَّةٗ فَصَدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ مُّهِينٞ

They took their faith, which they swear by as a safeguard from being killed for disbelief, whereby they outwardly showed Islam as a means to protect their blood and wealth. Then they diverted people from the truth by demoralizing and hindering the Muslims. Therefore, for them awaits a humiliating torment. It will debase and disgrace them.

(١٧) لَّن تُغۡنِيَ عَنۡهُمۡ أَمۡوَٰلُهُمۡ وَلَآ أَوۡلَٰدُهُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡ‍ًٔاۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

Neither their wealth nor their children will avail them anything from Allah. Those are the people of the Fire who enter it, remaining in it eternally. The torment will not cease for them.

(١٨) يَوۡمَ يَبۡعَثُهُمُ ٱللَّهُ جَمِيعٗا فَيَحۡلِفُونَ لَهُۥ كَمَا يَحۡلِفُونَ لَكُمۡ وَيَحۡسَبُونَ أَنَّهُمۡ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٍۚ أَلَآ إِنَّهُمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡكَٰذِبُونَ

The Day Allah will resurrect them all, not leaving any of them except that He resurrected them for recompense. Then they will swear to Allah that they were not upon disbelief nor hypocrisy and that they were but believers doing what contented Allah. They swear to Him in the Afterlife just as they would swear to you, O believers, in the worldly life that they are Muslims. They think that with this faith, which they swear by to Allah, that they are upon something of brings benefit to them or fends off harm from them. They are truly the liars in their faith in the worldly life and Afterlife.

(١٩) ٱسۡتَحۡوَذَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ فَأَنسَىٰهُمۡ ذِكۡرَ ٱللَّهِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ حِزۡبُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۚ أَلَآ إِنَّ حِزۡبَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ

The Devil has seized control of them, then made them forget to remember Allah through his whispering, so they did not do what pleases Him. Those described with these attributes are the soldiers of Iblis (Satan) and his followers. Indeed, Iblis's soldiers and followers the losers in the worldly life and Afterlife, for they have sold guidance for misguidance and the Garden [of Paradise] for the Fire.

(٢٠) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُحَآدُّونَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥٓ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ فِي ٱلۡأَذَلِّينَ

Indeed, those who oppose Allah and His Messenger are amongst those whom Allah debases in the worldly life and Afterlife, and they are disgraced from the disbelieving nations.

(٢١) كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَأَغۡلِبَنَّ أَنَا۠ وَرُسُلِيٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَوِيٌّ عَزِيزٞ

Allah decreed in His preceding knowledge: I will undoubtedly triumph, My Messengers and I, over Our enemies in evidence and strength. Indeed, Allah is Powerful in supporting His messengers and Mighty, taking vengeance upon their enemies.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's kindness to His Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم when He trained his companions not to burden him with abundant secret discussions.
Alliance with the Jews is from the matters of the hypocrites.
The defeat of the people of disbelief and the triumph of the people of faith is an established divine practice. It may be delayed, but it will come to pass.
