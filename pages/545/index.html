(٢٢) لَّا تَجِدُ قَوۡمٗا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ يُوَآدُّونَ مَنۡ حَآدَّ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَلَوۡ كَانُوٓاْ ءَابَآءَهُمۡ أَوۡ أَبۡنَآءَهُمۡ أَوۡ إِخۡوَٰنَهُمۡ أَوۡ عَشِيرَتَهُمۡۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ كَتَبَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمُ ٱلۡإِيمَٰنَ وَأَيَّدَهُم بِرُوحٖ مِّنۡهُۖ وَيُدۡخِلُهُمۡ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ رَضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ حِزۡبُ ٱللَّهِۚ أَلَآ إِنَّ حِزۡبَ ٱللَّهِ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ

You will not find, O Messenger, a people believing in Allah and the Day of Resurrection loving and allying those who oppose Allah and His Messenger, even if these enemies to Allah and His Messenger were their fathers, sons, brothers, or close associates whom they ascribe to. That is because faith prevents allying with the enemies of Allah and His Messenger, and because the relationship of faith is superior to all relationships, so it is prioritized when they are conflicting. Those who do not ally with those who oppose Allah and His Messenger, even if they are relatives, are those whom Allah has made faith firm in their hearts so that it does not change. And He strengthened them with clear evidence from Him and light. On the Day of Resurrection, He will enter them into gardens of permanent residence. Rivers flow beneath its castles and trees, remaining in it forever. Its delight does not cease from them, and they do not disappear from it. Allah will be content with them, not ever displeased with them after it. They would be content with Him for what He gave them of the delight that does not run out. From it is seeing Him, may He be Exalted. Those described with what was mentioned are Allah's soldiers, who conform to what He commanded and abstain from what he forbade from. Indeed, Allah's soldiers are the victors by attaining what they seek and what they miss from dread in the worldly life and the Afterlife.

The Banishment (Surah Al-Hashr)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Demonstrating Allah's power and might in weakening the Jews and hypocrites, as well as revealing their disunity, while on the other hand, showing affection for the believers.

Explanation:

(١) سَبَّحَ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

Everything in the heavens and the Earth from creation has exalted Allah and vindicated Him of what does not befit him. He is the Mighty whom none overpowers, the Wise in His creation, legislation, and predetermination.

(٢) هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَخۡرَجَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِن دِيَٰرِهِمۡ لِأَوَّلِ ٱلۡحَشۡرِۚ مَا ظَنَنتُمۡ أَن يَخۡرُجُواْۖ وَظَنُّوٓاْ أَنَّهُم مَّانِعَتُهُمۡ حُصُونُهُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ فَأَتَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَمۡ يَحۡتَسِبُواْۖ وَقَذَفَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمُ ٱلرُّعۡبَۚ يُخۡرِبُونَ بُيُوتَهُم بِأَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَأَيۡدِي ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ فَٱعۡتَبِرُواْ يَٰٓأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرِ

He is the One who expelled [the tribe of] Banu An-Nadeer, who disbelieved in Allah and rejected His Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, from their homes in Medina for their first expulsion from Medina to the Levant. They are from the Jews, people of the Torah. He expelled them for breaking their covenant and being with the polytheists against Him. He expelled them to the Land of the Levant. You did not think, O believers, they would be expelled from their homes due to their might and strength. And they assumed that their forts, which they perfected, would secure them from Allah's punishment and torment. But Allah's retribution came from where they did not expect when His Messenger commanded fighting them and banishing them from their homes. Allah placed intense fear in their hearts. They destroy their homes with their own hands from within so that the Muslims would not benefit from them, and the Muslims destroy them from the outside. So take admonishment, O people of sight, from what occurred to them due to their disbelief. So do not be like them, so you avoid receiving their requital and the punishment that they were punished with.

(٣) وَلَوۡلَآ أَن كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡجَلَآءَ لَعَذَّبَهُمۡ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَلَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ عَذَابُ ٱلنَّارِ

Had it not been that Allah had decreed exile for them, He would have punished them in the world with execution and captivity, as He did with their brothers from Banu Qurayzah. And for them in the Afterlife is the punishment of the Fire that awaits them, wherein they shall live forever.

Points of Benefit:

Love that inhibits the Muslim from disassociating from and hating the disbeliever's religion is prohibited. As for the innate love, such as the Muslim's love for his disbelieving relative, that is permissible.
The bond of faith is the most dependable bond between the believers.
The people of falsehood may rise so high that they think they will not be defeated. Then, their defeat comes from where they do not expect.
From Allah's decree for people is warding off calamities with the befalling of lesser ones.
