(٤) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ شَآقُّواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥۖ وَمَن يُشَآقِّ ٱللَّهَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ

That happened to them because they opposed Allah and His Messenger through disbelief and breaking covenants. And whoever opposes Allah, then Allah is severe in punishment, and His severe punishment will reach him.

(٥) مَا قَطَعۡتُم مِّن لِّينَةٍ أَوۡ تَرَكۡتُمُوهَا قَآئِمَةً عَلَىٰٓ أُصُولِهَا فَبِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِ وَلِيُخۡزِيَ ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ

What you cut, O believers, of date palms to enrage Allah's enemies in the Battle of Banu An-Nadeer or left standing on their trunks to benefit from was by Allah's command and not a cause for corruption on Earth, as they wrongly claim. And Allah will surely debase with it those outside of His obedience among the Jews who broke their covenant and chose the path of treachery over the path of fulfillment.

(٦) وَمَآ أَفَآءَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِهِۦ مِنۡهُمۡ فَمَآ أَوۡجَفۡتُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ خَيۡلٖ وَلَا رِكَابٖ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُسَلِّطُ رُسُلَهُۥ عَلَىٰ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

And as for what Allah gave back to His Messenger from the wealth of Banu An-Nadeer, then you did not hasten your horses nor camels in pursuit of it, and no burden fell upon you from it, but rather, Allah gives His messengers rule over whom He wills. He had given His Messenger rule over Banu An-Nadeer, so he conquered their lands without combat. Allah is Omnipotent over everything; nothing can hinder Him.

(٧) مَّآ أَفَآءَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِهِۦ مِنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ فَلِلَّهِ وَلِلرَّسُولِ وَلِذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينِ وَٱبۡنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ كَيۡ لَا يَكُونَ دُولَةَۢ بَيۡنَ ٱلۡأَغۡنِيَآءِ مِنكُمۡۚ وَمَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمُ ٱلرَّسُولُ فَخُذُوهُ وَمَا نَهَىٰكُمۡ عَنۡهُ فَٱنتَهُواْۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ

The wealth Allah bestowed upon His Messenger from the townspeople without combat belongs to Allah; He designates it to whom He wills. It is for the Messenger, for his relatives from Banu Hashim and Banu Al-Muttalib as compensation to them for what they were forbidden of charity, for the orphans, for the poor, and for the stranger whose travel provision has been exhausted. [That is] so that the circulation of wealth is not limited to the affluent in exclusion of the poor. And the money the Messenger gave you from booty without battle, then take it, O believers, and what he forbade you from, then desist. And heed Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. Indeed, Allah is severe in punishment, so beware of His retribution.

(٨) لِلۡفُقَرَآءِ ٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُخۡرِجُواْ مِن دِيَٰرِهِمۡ وَأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ يَبۡتَغُونَ فَضۡلٗا مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرِضۡوَٰنٗا وَيَنصُرُونَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥٓۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلصَّٰدِقُونَ

A portion of this wealth is for the poor among Al-Muhajireen (the Emigrants) in the path of Allah, who were forced to leave their properties and children. They hope that Allah favors them with provision in the worldly life and contentment in the Afterlife. They support Allah and His Messenger through struggle in Allah's path. The ones described with those attributes are those truly firm in faith.

After praising the Al-Muhajireen (the Emigrants), Allah praises the Al-Ansar (the Supporters) as well, stating:

(٩) وَٱلَّذِينَ تَبَوَّءُو ٱلدَّارَ وَٱلۡإِيمَٰنَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ يُحِبُّونَ مَنۡ هَاجَرَ إِلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا يَجِدُونَ فِي صُدُورِهِمۡ حَاجَةٗ مِّمَّآ أُوتُواْ وَيُؤۡثِرُونَ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَلَوۡ كَانَ بِهِمۡ خَصَاصَةٞۚ وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفۡسِهِۦ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ

And Al-Ansar (the Supporters), who resided in Medina before Al-Muhajireen and chose faith in Allah and His Messenger, love those who emigrated to them from Mecca. They do not find anger nor malice in their hearts against Al-Muhajireen in the path of Allah when they are given anything from the ransom, and they [Al-Ansar] are not given. They put Al-Muhajireen before themselves in terms of the allocations of the worldly life, even if they [Al-Ansar] are considered poor and in need. And whomsoever Allah protects their soul's yearning for wealth such that they spend it in the path of Allah, are the victors by attaining what they hope for and salvation from what they flee from.

Points of Benefit:

Doing what is expected to cause harm to realize a greater benefit does not fall under the category of causing corruption through the earth.
Among the beauties of Islam is tending to those in need of wealth. So, the booty is given to them instead of the wealthy, who already have enough.
Giving preference to others is one of the virtues of Islam. It was exhibited most excellently by Al-Ansar.
