(١٠) وَٱلَّذِينَ جَآءُو مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا ٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَلِإِخۡوَٰنِنَا ٱلَّذِينَ سَبَقُونَا بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ وَلَا تَجۡعَلۡ فِي قُلُوبِنَا غِلّٗا لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ رَبَّنَآ إِنَّكَ رَءُوفٞ رَّحِيمٌ

And the ones who came after these individuals and those who followed them in excellence until the Day of Resurrection say: O Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers in religion who preceded us in faith in Allah and His Messenger, and do not put malevolence and malice in our hearts toward anyone among the believers. Our Lord, indeed, you are Kind with your slaves, and Merciful to them.

(١١) ۞أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ نَافَقُواْ يَقُولُونَ لِإِخۡوَٰنِهِمُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَئِنۡ أُخۡرِجۡتُمۡ لَنَخۡرُجَنَّ مَعَكُمۡ وَلَا نُطِيعُ فِيكُمۡ أَحَدًا أَبَدٗا وَإِن قُوتِلۡتُمۡ لَنَنصُرَنَّكُمۡ وَٱللَّهُ يَشۡهَدُ إِنَّهُمۡ لَكَٰذِبُونَ

Have you not recognized, O Messenger, those who concealed disbelief and outwardly exhibited faith? They say to their brothers in disbelief from the Jews, the followers of the distorted Torah: Stay in your homes, for we will not betray you nor hand you over. Then, if the Muslims expel you from them, we will surely go out united with you, and we will not obey anyone who wants to prevent us from going out with you. And if they fight you, we will certainly help you against them.Allah testifies that, indeed, the hypocrites are surely lying in their claim of going out with the Jews if they are expelled and fighting with them if they are fought.

(١٢) لَئِنۡ أُخۡرِجُواْ لَا يَخۡرُجُونَ مَعَهُمۡ وَلَئِن قُوتِلُواْ لَا يَنصُرُونَهُمۡ وَلَئِن نَّصَرُوهُمۡ لَيُوَلُّنَّ ٱلۡأَدۡبَٰرَ ثُمَّ لَا يُنصَرُونَ

If the Muslims expel the Jews, they will certainly not go out with them. And if they fight them, they will certainly not support or help them. And if they support and help them against the Muslims, they will certainly flee, running away from them. Then the hypocrites will not be supported after that. Rather, Allah will debase and humiliate them.

(١٣) لَأَنتُمۡ أَشَدُّ رَهۡبَةٗ فِي صُدُورِهِم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَوۡمٞ لَّا يَفۡقَهُونَ

You, O believers, are certainly more frightening in the hearts of the hypocrites and Jews than Allah. That is due to the intensity of their fear of you and the weakness of their fear of Allah, because they are people who do not comprehend or understand. If they had comprehended, they would have known that Allah is more deserving to be feared and terrified of, for He is the One who gave you reign over them.

(١٤) لَا يُقَٰتِلُونَكُمۡ جَمِيعًا إِلَّا فِي قُرٗى مُّحَصَّنَةٍ أَوۡ مِن وَرَآءِ جُدُرِۢۚ بَأۡسُهُم بَيۡنَهُمۡ شَدِيدٞۚ تَحۡسَبُهُمۡ جَمِيعٗا وَقُلُوبُهُمۡ شَتَّىٰۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَوۡمٞ لَّا يَعۡقِلُونَ

The gathered Jews do not fight you, O believers, except in towns fortified with defensive walls or behind walls, for they cannot face you out of their cowardice. Their strife between themselves is strong due to the enmity between them. You think they are upon a single word and that their ranks are unified. The reality is that their hearts are divided and differ. That difference and enmity is because, indeed, they are not rational. If they had reason, they would have known the truth, followed it, and not differed regarding it.

(١٥) كَمَثَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ قَرِيبٗاۖ ذَاقُواْ وَبَالَ أَمۡرِهِمۡ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

The example of these Jews in regards to their disbelief and the calamity that befell them is what befell Mecca's polytheists not long ago, for they tasted the evil of their disbelief's consequences. So those who were killed were killed and those who were captured were captured on the day of Badr, and they have a painful torment awaiting them in the Afterlife.

(١٦) كَمَثَلِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ إِذۡ قَالَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ ٱكۡفُرۡ فَلَمَّا كَفَرَ قَالَ إِنِّي بَرِيٓءٞ مِّنكَ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ ٱللَّهَ رَبَّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

The example of those listening to the hypocrites is like the example of the Devil when he adorns disbelief for humans. Then, when a human disbelieves because of the Devil's adornment of disbelief toward him, he [the Devil] says: Indeed, I am innocent of you because of your disbelief. Indeed, I fear Allah, Lord of the creation.

Points of Benefit:

The bond of faith is not affected by the passage of time or changing location.
The friendship of the hypocrites with the Jews is an imaginary one that falls apart in adversities.
The Jews are cowards. They do not face their enemies in battle, and if they fight, they take shelter in their towns and with their weapons.
