(١٧) فَكَانَ عَٰقِبَتَهُمَآ أَنَّهُمَا فِي ٱلنَّارِ خَٰلِدَيۡنِ فِيهَاۚ وَذَٰلِكَ جَزَٰٓؤُاْ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

The conclusion of Satan's matter and the humans who obeyed him is in the Fire on the Day of Resurrection, remaining in it forever. That is the recompense that awaits them. It is the recompense of those who wronged themselves by transgressing Allah's bounds.

(١٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَلۡتَنظُرۡ نَفۡسٞ مَّا قَدَّمَتۡ لِغَدٖۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, take heed of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. And a soul should ponder what it has put forward of righteous work for the Day of Resurrection and take heed of Allah. Indeed, Allah is well-informed of what you do. Nothing of your deeds is hidden from Him, and He will recompense you accordingly.

(١٩) وَلَا تَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّذِينَ نَسُواْ ٱللَّهَ فَأَنسَىٰهُمۡ أَنفُسَهُمۡۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ

And do not be like those who forgot Allah by leaving off complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, so Allah made them forget their souls. Thus, they did not do what would save them from Allah's anger and His punishment. Those who forgot Allah and so did not comply with His commands and did not avoid His prohibitions are the ones outside Allah's obedience.

(٢٠) لَا يَسۡتَوِيٓ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِ وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِۚ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ هُمُ ٱلۡفَآئِزُونَ

The people of the Fire are not equal to the people of Paradise. Rather, their recompense is different, just like their actions in the worldly life are different. The people of Paradise are the victors by attaining what they seek and are saved from what they fear.

(٢١) لَوۡ أَنزَلۡنَا هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ عَلَىٰ جَبَلٖ لَّرَأَيۡتَهُۥ خَٰشِعٗا مُّتَصَدِّعٗا مِّنۡ خَشۡيَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَتِلۡكَ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلُ نَضۡرِبُهَا لِلنَّاسِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ

If we sent down this Quran onto a mountain, you would have seen, O Messenger, that mountain, despite its firmness, lowly and breaking apart from the severity of [its] fear of Allah. [That is] due to what is in the Quran of deterring admonishments and severe threats. We give these examples to the people so they may work their minds and then accept reproval from what its verses contain of admonishments and morals.

(٢٢) هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِۖ هُوَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

He is Allah; there is no rightful object of worship besides Him, Knower of what is absent and what is present; nothing of that is hidden from Him. He is Vastly Merciful in the worldly life and Afterlife and bestows His mercy in both of them to whom He wills. His mercy encompasses all creation. He is the King, the One exalted and pure from any deficiency, free from every defect, the One who affirms the truth of His messengers with astonishing signs, the Observer over the actions of His slaves. He is the Mighty who none defeat, the Dominator who compels everything with His dominance, the Superior. Allah is vindicated and pure from what the polytheists associate with Him of idols and otherwise.

(٢٣) هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡقُدُّوسُ ٱلسَّلَٰمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُ ٱلۡمُهَيۡمِنُ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡجَبَّارُ ٱلۡمُتَكَبِّرُۚ سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ

He is Allah, the Creator who created everything, the one who brings things into existence, the Former of His creations as He wishes. To Him (may He be Exalted) belong the Excellent Names encompassing His lofty attributes. Whatever is in the heavens and the Earth exalts Him from every deficiency. He is the Mighty who none defeat, the Wise in His creation, legislation, and predetermination.

(٢٤) هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقُ ٱلۡبَارِئُ ٱلۡمُصَوِّرُۖ لَهُ ٱلۡأَسۡمَآءُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۚ يُسَبِّحُ لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

He is Allah, the Creator who created everything, the one who brings things into existence, the Former of His creations as He wishes. To Him (may He be Exalted) belong the Excellent Names encompassing His lofty attributes. Whatever is in the heavens and the Earth exalts Him from every deficiency. He is the Mighty who none defeat, the Wise in His creation, legislation, and predetermination.

Points of Benefit:

From the signs of Allah bestowing success upon the believer is that he calls him to account in this world before its reckoning on the Day of Resurrection.
Reminding the slaves of the intensity of the Quran's effect on a great mountain alerts them that they are more deserving of this effect due to their weakness.
The names (the Creator, the Originator, the Former) allude to the stages of completing the creation from his predetermination, [then] bringing him into existence, and [then] making a form specific to him. If any of the names are mentioned in isolation, it encompasses the others.
