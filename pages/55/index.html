(٣٨) هُنَالِكَ دَعَا زَكَرِيَّا رَبَّهُۥۖ قَالَ رَبِّ هَبۡ لِي مِن لَّدُنكَ ذُرِّيَّةٗ طَيِّبَةًۖ إِنَّكَ سَمِيعُ ٱلدُّعَآءِ

When Zechariah saw the provision that Allah miraculously gave to Mary, daughter of 'Imran, he became hopeful that Allah would give him a child despite being very old and his wife being barren. So he said: O my Lord, give me a good child. So indeed, You hear and grant the supplication of the person who supplicates to You.

(٣٩) فَنَادَتۡهُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَهُوَ قَآئِمٞ يُصَلِّي فِي ٱلۡمِحۡرَابِ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُبَشِّرُكَ بِيَحۡيَىٰ مُصَدِّقَۢا بِكَلِمَةٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَسَيِّدٗا وَحَصُورٗا وَنَبِيّٗا مِّنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

The angels called out to him while he stood in prayer in his place of worship, saying: Allah gives you good news of a child to be born to you whose name will be John. He will confirm a word from Allah – which refers to Jesus, son of Mary, as Allah uniquely created him with a word from Him. This child will lead his people in knowledge and worship. He will be innocent, avoiding desires, including approaching women. He will dedicate himself to worshipping His Lord and be a prophet among righteous people.

(٤٠) قَالَ رَبِّ أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لِي غُلَٰمٞ وَقَدۡ بَلَغَنِيَ ٱلۡكِبَرُ وَٱمۡرَأَتِي عَاقِرٞۖ قَالَ كَذَٰلِكَ ٱللَّهُ يَفۡعَلُ مَا يَشَآءُ

When the angels gave him the good news about John, Zechariah said: O my Lord, how can I have a child after I have become old and my wife cannot bear children? Allah replied: The example of John's creation, despite your old age and your wife being barren, is like the creation of anything Allah wishes to create, even though it is out of the ordinary. This is because Allah is the Most Capable of everything and does whatever He wills in His wisdom and knowledge.

(٤١) قَالَ رَبِّ ٱجۡعَل لِّيٓ ءَايَةٗۖ قَالَ ءَايَتُكَ أَلَّا تُكَلِّمَ ٱلنَّاسَ ثَلَٰثَةَ أَيَّامٍ إِلَّا رَمۡزٗاۗ وَٱذۡكُر رَّبَّكَ كَثِيرٗا وَسَبِّحۡ بِٱلۡعَشِيِّ وَٱلۡإِبۡكَٰرِ

Zechariah said: O my Lord, give me a sign that my wife is pregnant. Allah said: The sign you want is that you cannot speak for three whole days, except by using gestures, and this will not be because of any disability. So, remember Allah much and exalt Him at the start and end of every day.

(٤٢) وَإِذۡ قَالَتِ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ يَٰمَرۡيَمُ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱصۡطَفَىٰكِ وَطَهَّرَكِ وَٱصۡطَفَىٰكِ عَلَىٰ نِسَآءِ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Remember, O Messenger, when the angels said to Mary (peace be upon her): Allah has chosen you because of your praiseworthy qualities. He has purified you from all defects and chosen you over all the other women of your time.

(٤٣) يَٰمَرۡيَمُ ٱقۡنُتِي لِرَبِّكِ وَٱسۡجُدِي وَٱرۡكَعِي مَعَ ٱلرَّٰكِعِينَ

O Mary, lengthen your standing in prayer, prostrate to your Lord, and bow down with His righteous slaves who bow down to Him.

(٤٤) ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ أَنۢبَآءِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ نُوحِيهِ إِلَيۡكَۚ وَمَا كُنتَ لَدَيۡهِمۡ إِذۡ يُلۡقُونَ أَقۡلَٰمَهُمۡ أَيُّهُمۡ يَكۡفُلُ مَرۡيَمَ وَمَا كُنتَ لَدَيۡهِمۡ إِذۡ يَخۡتَصِمُونَ

The story of Zechariah and Mary is knowledge of the unseen, which We reveal to you, O Messenger. When they argued about who was most entitled to bring up Mary, you were not there with those scholars and righteous people. So they eventually decided to draw lots by throwing their pens, and the pen of Zechariah (peace be upon him) won.

(٤٥) إِذۡ قَالَتِ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ يَٰمَرۡيَمُ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُبَشِّرُكِ بِكَلِمَةٖ مِّنۡهُ ٱسۡمُهُ ٱلۡمَسِيحُ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ وَجِيهٗا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَمِنَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبِينَ

Remember, O Messenger, when the angels said: O Mary, Allah gives you good news of a child who will be created without a father: merely by a word from Allah, such as 'Be', and he will become a child by Allah's will. This child's name will be the Messiah, Jesus, son of Mary. He will have a high rank in this world and the Hereafter and be one of those brought close to Allah.

Points of Benefit:

The care of Allah, the Exalted, for His allies. Indeed, He, may He be Transcendent, protects them from harm and responds to their supplications.
The virtue of Mary, peace be upon her, as Allah chose her over all the women of creation, purified her from imperfections, and made her blessed.
The greater the blessing of Allah upon a servant, the greater the obligation upon him to express gratitude through devotion, bowing, prostration, and all other forms of worship.
The permissibility of drawing lots when there is a difference in matters without clear evidence or indication to guide the decision.
