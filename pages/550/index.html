(٦) لَقَدۡ كَانَ لَكُمۡ فِيهِمۡ أُسۡوَةٌ حَسَنَةٞ لِّمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلۡيَوۡمَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَۚ وَمَن يَتَوَلَّ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ

This beautiful example is only followed by those who hope for good from Allah in the worldly life and Hereafter. And whosoever turns away from this beautiful example, Allah is indeed free of need of His slaves, not requiring their obedience. He is the one extolled in every circumstance.

(٧) ۞عَسَى ٱللَّهُ أَن يَجۡعَلَ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَبَيۡنَ ٱلَّذِينَ عَادَيۡتُم مِّنۡهُم مَّوَدَّةٗۚ وَٱللَّهُ قَدِيرٞۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

Perhaps Allah will cause love between you, O believers, and those whom you opposed of the disbelievers, where Allah would guide them to Islam so that they would be brothers to you in religion. Allah is able. He can turn their hearts to faith. Allah is forgiving to whoever repents of His slaves; merciful to them.

(٨) لَّا يَنۡهَىٰكُمُ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يُقَٰتِلُوكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِ وَلَمۡ يُخۡرِجُوكُم مِّن دِيَٰرِكُمۡ أَن تَبَرُّوهُمۡ وَتُقۡسِطُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُقۡسِطِينَ

Allah does not prohibit you from those who have not fought you on account of your Islam and who have not expelled you from your home that you would give them fine treatment and be just between them by giving them their rights upon you. Allah loves those who are just to themselves, their families, and what they are in charge of.

(٩) إِنَّمَا يَنۡهَىٰكُمُ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ قَٰتَلُوكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِ وَأَخۡرَجُوكُم مِّن دِيَٰرِكُمۡ وَظَٰهَرُواْ عَلَىٰٓ إِخۡرَاجِكُمۡ أَن تَوَلَّوۡهُمۡۚ وَمَن يَتَوَلَّهُمۡ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

Allah only forbids you from those who fought you because of your faith, expelled you from your homes, and assisted in your expulsion. He forbids you from allying with them. Whoever of you allies with them are those wronging their [own] souls by heading to the roads of destruction because of violating the command of Allah.

(١٠) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا جَآءَكُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتُ مُهَٰجِرَٰتٖ فَٱمۡتَحِنُوهُنَّۖ ٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِإِيمَٰنِهِنَّۖ فَإِنۡ عَلِمۡتُمُوهُنَّ مُؤۡمِنَٰتٖ فَلَا تَرۡجِعُوهُنَّ إِلَى ٱلۡكُفَّارِۖ لَا هُنَّ حِلّٞ لَّهُمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحِلُّونَ لَهُنَّۖ وَءَاتُوهُم مَّآ أَنفَقُواْۚ وَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ أَن تَنكِحُوهُنَّ إِذَآ ءَاتَيۡتُمُوهُنَّ أُجُورَهُنَّۚ وَلَا تُمۡسِكُواْ بِعِصَمِ ٱلۡكَوَافِرِ وَسۡ‍َٔلُواْ مَآ أَنفَقۡتُمۡ وَلۡيَسۡ‍َٔلُواْ مَآ أَنفَقُواْۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ حُكۡمُ ٱللَّهِ يَحۡكُمُ بَيۡنَكُمۡۖ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٞ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He has legislated, if the believing women come to you emigrating from the land of disbelief to the land of Islam, then test them in the truthfulness of their faith. Allah is more knowledgeable of their faith; nothing is hidden from Him from what their hearts envelop. So if you know them to be believers, after the test, according to what is apparent to you of their truthfulness, then do not send them back to their disbelieving husbands. It is not permissible for the believing women to marry disbelievers or the disbelievers to marry the believing women. And give their husbands what they expended for their dowries. There is no sin upon you, O believers, to marry them after their 'iddah (waiting period before a woman becomes permissible for another man) concludes if you give them their dowries. And whoever has a wife who disbelieves or apostates from Islam should not hold on to her due to the severance of their marriage by her disbelief. Ask the disbelievers for what you expended as dowries for your apostating wives, and let them ask what they expended as dowries for their wives who entered Islam. That which is cited, of returning the dowries from your side and their side, is the ruling of Allah. He (may He be Exalted) rules between you with what He wishes. And Allah is Omniscient of the states of His slaves and their actions; nothing of them is hidden from Him, and He is wise in what He legislates for His slaves.

(١١) وَإِن فَاتَكُمۡ شَيۡءٞ مِّنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِكُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡكُفَّارِ فَعَاقَبۡتُمۡ فَ‍َٔاتُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ ذَهَبَتۡ أَزۡوَٰجُهُم مِّثۡلَ مَآ أَنفَقُواْۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنتُم بِهِۦ مُؤۡمِنُونَ

And if it were supposed that some of your women would go out to the disbelievers as apostates and you sought their dowries from the disbelievers and they did not give it, then you were to gain spoils from the disbelievers, then provide the husbands whose wives left as apostates the like of what they expended as dowries. Take heed of Allah, whom you believe in, by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

Points of Benefit:

Allah changing the hearts from enmity to affection and from disbelief to belief is an allusion to the fact that the slaves' hearts are between two of His (the Transcendent's) fingers. So let the slave seek steadfastness upon belief from Him.
The difference in ruling between combatant disbelievers and peaceful ones.
The prohibition of initiating or continuing a marriage to a disbelieving female except one from the People of the Book. The prohibition of initiating or continuing the marriage of a Muslim female to a disbeliever.
