(١٢) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ إِذَا جَآءَكَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتُ يُبَايِعۡنَكَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن لَّا يُشۡرِكۡنَ بِٱللَّهِ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا يَسۡرِقۡنَ وَلَا يَزۡنِينَ وَلَا يَقۡتُلۡنَ أَوۡلَٰدَهُنَّ وَلَا يَأۡتِينَ بِبُهۡتَٰنٖ يَفۡتَرِينَهُۥ بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِنَّ وَأَرۡجُلِهِنَّ وَلَا يَعۡصِينَكَ فِي مَعۡرُوفٖ فَبَايِعۡهُنَّ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُنَّ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

O Prophet, if the believing women come to you giving you their pledge, such as what occurred in the conquest of Mecca, that they would not associate anything as partners with Allah, but rather worship Him alone, and [pledge] that they would not steal, commit zina (unlawful sexual intercourse), kill their children following the custom of the people of the Age of Ignorance, ascribe their children from zina to their husbands, disobey you in what is proper, such as his prohibition from wailing, shaving, and tearing clothes [all in anguish of a death], then exchange the pledge with them and ask Allah for the forgiveness of their sins after pledging to you. Indeed, Allah is forgiving to those who repent from His slaves; merciful to them.

At the beginning of the surah, there was a warning against allying with Allah's enemies, and at the end, there is a warning against it as an affirmation of what preceded. Allah the Exalted states:

(١٣) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَتَوَلَّوۡاْ قَوۡمًا غَضِبَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ قَدۡ يَئِسُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ كَمَا يَئِسَ ٱلۡكُفَّارُ مِنۡ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡقُبُورِ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, do not ally with a people whom Allah was angry with and who do not have certainty in the Afterlife. Rather, they despair of it, just like their despair of their dead returning to them, because they disbelieve in the Resurrection.

The Rank (Surah As-Saff)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Motivating the believers to support the religion.

Explanation:

(١) سَبَّحَ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

All that is in the heavens and the Earth exalt Allah, may He be Exalted and Transcendent, from what does not befit Him. He is the Mighty who none defeat, the Wise in His creation, predetermination, and legislation.

(٢) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لِمَ تَقُولُونَ مَا لَا تَفۡعَلُونَ

O you who believe in Allah, why do you say: We did something, and in reality, you did not do it?! Just as one of them says, he fought with his sword and struck, while he did not fight with his sword nor strike.

(٣) كَبُرَ مَقۡتًا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَن تَقُولُواْ مَا لَا تَفۡعَلُونَ

Allah considers that abhorrent matter to be tremendous, that is, saying what you do not do. So it does not befit a believer that he be dishonest with Allah. His actions should affirm his words.

(٤) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِهِۦ صَفّٗا كَأَنَّهُم بُنۡيَٰنٞ مَّرۡصُوصٞ

Indeed, Allah loves the believers who fight in His path, in pursuit of His contentment, as a row standing side by side as if they are a structure whose parts are fixed to one another.

After mentioning battle and praising the believers who joined forces to fight in His path, Allah mentions the defiance of the companions of Moses and Jesus towards their respective messengers as a warning to the believers against defying their Prophet. He states:

(٥) وَإِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوۡمِهِۦ يَٰقَوۡمِ لِمَ تُؤۡذُونَنِي وَقَد تَّعۡلَمُونَ أَنِّي رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ إِلَيۡكُمۡۖ فَلَمَّا زَاغُوٓاْ أَزَاغَ ٱللَّهُ قُلُوبَهُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ

And remember, O Messenger, when Moses (peace be upon him) said to his people: O my people, why do you harm me by violating my command while you know that I am indeed Allah's Messenger to you?! Then, when they leaned and deviated away from what he brought them of the truth, Allah inclined their hearts away from the truth and uprightness. Allah does not grant success to the truth to the people outside His obedience.

Points of Benefit:

The legitimacy of a pledge to the ruler of listening, obedience, and heedfulness.
The obligation of acting honestly and in accordance with what one says.
Allah clarified the path of good and that of evil. So if the slave chooses deviation and misguidance and does not repent, then Allah will punish him by increasing his deviation and misguidance.
