(٦) وَإِذۡ قَالَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ يَٰبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ إِنِّي رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ إِلَيۡكُم مُّصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيَّ مِنَ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِ وَمُبَشِّرَۢا بِرَسُولٖ يَأۡتِي مِنۢ بَعۡدِي ٱسۡمُهُۥٓ أَحۡمَدُۖ فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ قَالُواْ هَٰذَا سِحۡرٞ مُّبِينٞ

And remember, O Messenger, when Jesus, son of Mary (peace be upon him) said: O Israelites, indeed, I am Allah's Messenger. He sent me to you affirming what was sent down before me of the Torah, so I am not an innovation among the messengers, and [sent me] conveying the good news of a messenger coming after me. His name is Ahmad. Then, when Jesus came with proofs indicating his truthfulness, they said: This is clear magic, so we will not follow him.

(٧) وَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّنِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡكَذِبَ وَهُوَ يُدۡعَىٰٓ إِلَى ٱلۡإِسۡلَٰمِۚ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

And no one is more wrongful than the one who fabricates falsehood about Allah where he would make rivals [with Allah], worshipping them instead of Him, while he is called to Islam, the religion of pure tawheed (singular worship) of Allah. Allah does not grant success to what contains their guidance and virtue to the people wronging themselves with shirk (associating partners) [with Allah] and acts of disobedience.

(٨) يُرِيدُونَ لِيُطۡفِ‍ُٔواْ نُورَ ٱللَّهِ بِأَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَٱللَّهُ مُتِمُّ نُورِهِۦ وَلَوۡ كَرِهَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ

These liars wish to extinguish the light of Allah with what is uttered by them of corrupt words and besmearing the truth. Allah will complete His light against their wishes by making His religion prominent from the east to the west of the Earth and elevating His word.

(٩) هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَرۡسَلَ رَسُولَهُۥ بِٱلۡهُدَىٰ وَدِينِ ٱلۡحَقِّ لِيُظۡهِرَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلدِّينِ كُلِّهِۦ وَلَوۡ كَرِهَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكُونَ

Allah is the One who sent His Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم with the religion of Islam, the religion of guidance and direction to good, beneficial knowledge and righteous action, in order to raise it above all religions despite the wishes of the polytheists who hate that it be established on the Earth.

(١٠) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ هَلۡ أَدُلُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ تِجَٰرَةٖ تُنجِيكُم مِّنۡ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, should I direct and guide you to a profitable trade that would save you from a painful torment?

(١١) تُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَتُجَٰهِدُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ بِأَمۡوَٰلِكُمۡ وَأَنفُسِكُمۡۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

This profitable trade is that you believe in Allah and His Messenger and strive in His path (may He be Exalted) by spending your wealth and expending your souls in pursuit of His contentment. That mentioned action is good for you, if you know, so hasten to it.

(١٢) يَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ ذُنُوبَكُمۡ وَيُدۡخِلۡكُمۡ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ وَمَسَٰكِنَ طَيِّبَةٗ فِي جَنَّٰتِ عَدۡنٖۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

And the profit of this trade is that Allah will forgive you your sins, enter you into gardens in which rivers flow under its castles and trees, and enter you into fine palaces in gardens of settlement, never displaced from them. That mentioned reward is the great victory that no victory comes close to.

(١٣) وَأُخۡرَىٰ تُحِبُّونَهَاۖ نَصۡرٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَفَتۡحٞ قَرِيبٞۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

And from the profit of this trade is another feature you love, and it is given in advance in the worldly life. It is that Allah would support you against your enemy and a conquest near at hand. He will guide you to it, which is the conquest of Mecca and others. Inform, O Messenger, the believers of what pleases them of support in the worldly life and attaining Paradise in the Hereafter.

(١٤) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُونُوٓاْ أَنصَارَ ٱللَّهِ كَمَا قَالَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ لِلۡحَوَارِيِّ‍ۧنَ مَنۡ أَنصَارِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ قَالَ ٱلۡحَوَارِيُّونَ نَحۡنُ أَنصَارُ ٱللَّهِۖ فَ‍َٔامَنَت طَّآئِفَةٞ مِّنۢ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ وَكَفَرَت طَّآئِفَةٞۖ فَأَيَّدۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ عَلَىٰ عَدُوِّهِمۡ فَأَصۡبَحُواْ ظَٰهِرِينَ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, be the supporters of Allah by supporting His religion, which your Messenger came with like the support of the Disciples when Jesus (peace be upon him) said: Who are my supporters to Allah?So they swiftly replied to him: We are the supporters of Allah.So, a group of the Israelites believed in Jesus (peace be upon him), and another group disbelieved in him. Then we aided those who believed in Jesus against those who disbelieved in him, so they became triumphant over them.

Points of Benefit:

The previous messages' glad tidings of our Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم indicate the truth of his prophethood.
Empowering the religion is an established divine practice.
Faith and striving in Allah's path are among the reasons for entering Paradise.
Allah may hasten a believer's recompense in the worldly life, or He may save it for him in the Hereafter. But, He, the Transcendent, never loses it.
