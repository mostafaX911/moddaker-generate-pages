Friday (Surah Al-Jumu'ah)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

The privilege and virtue of this nation due to its Messenger. Enumerating the virtue of Friday.

Explanation:

(١) يُسَبِّحُ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلۡمَلِكِ ٱلۡقُدُّوسِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ

All creation in the heavens and the Earth exalt Allah from everything that does not befit Him from attributes of deficiency and sanctify Him. He alone is the sole King of the dominion, exalted above every deficiency, the Mighty who none defeat, the Wise in His creation, legislation, and predetermination.

(٢) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي بَعَثَ فِي ٱلۡأُمِّيِّ‍ۧنَ رَسُولٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ يَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَيُزَكِّيهِمۡ وَيُعَلِّمُهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَإِن كَانُواْ مِن قَبۡلُ لَفِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ

He is the One who sent among the Arabs who do not read nor write a Messenger of their kind, reciting upon them the verses which He sent down to him, purifying them of disbelief and poor character, and teaching them the Quran and sunnah (the way of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم). And indeed, before he was sent to them, they were in misguidance from the clear truth when they would worship idols, spill blood, and sever kinship ties.

(٣) وَءَاخَرِينَ مِنۡهُمۡ لَمَّا يَلۡحَقُواْ بِهِمۡۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

And He sent this Messenger to other people among the Arabs and others who have not yet come, and they will come. He is the Mighty who none defeat, the Wise in His creation, legislation, and predetermination.

(٤) ذَٰلِكَ فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ يُؤۡتِيهِ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو ٱلۡفَضۡلِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

That which was mentioned of sending the Messenger to the Arabs and others is the favor of Allah; He bestows it to whom He wills. Allah possesses great benevolence, and from His great benevolence is Him sending the Messenger of this nation to all people.

Allah reminds us of the blessings He has bestowed upon us, such as sending the Messenger and revealing the Quran. He then mentions how some of Moses's followers turned away from acting upon the Torah as a warning for us to avoid following their footsteps. Allah states:

(٥) مَثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ حُمِّلُواْ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةَ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَحۡمِلُوهَا كَمَثَلِ ٱلۡحِمَارِ يَحۡمِلُ أَسۡفَارَۢاۚ بِئۡسَ مَثَلُ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

The example of the Jews, who were obliged to uphold what is in the Torah and then deserted what they were obliged with, is like the example of a donkey carrying large books, not knowing what is carried on it: are they books or something else? Atrocious is the example of the people who rejected the verses of Allah. Allah does not grant success in attaining the truth to the unjust people.

(٦) قُلۡ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ هَادُوٓاْ إِن زَعَمۡتُمۡ أَنَّكُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ لِلَّهِ مِن دُونِ ٱلنَّاسِ فَتَمَنَّوُاْ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

Say, O Messenger: O you have remained on Judaism after its alteration, if you claim that you are indeed the allies of Allah, designating you with alliance in exclusion of the rest of the people, then hope for death so that He may hasten for you what He designated you with, according to what you claim, of honor if you are truthful in what you claim about being the allies of Allah in exclusion of the rest of the people.

(٧) وَلَا يَتَمَنَّوۡنَهُۥٓ أَبَدَۢا بِمَا قَدَّمَتۡ أَيۡدِيهِمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

And they will never hope for death. Rather, they hope for eternity in the worldly life because of what they did of disbelief, acts of disobedience, injustice, distorting the Torah, and replacing it. Allah is Omniscient of the unjust. Nothing of their deeds is hidden from Him, and He will recompense them accordingly.

(٨) قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ ٱلَّذِي تَفِرُّونَ مِنۡهُ فَإِنَّهُۥ مُلَٰقِيكُمۡۖ ثُمَّ تُرَدُّونَ إِلَىٰ عَٰلِمِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to those Jews: Indeed, death, which you flee from, will meet you no matter what, whether hastened or delayed. Then on the Day of Resurrection, you will be returned to Allah, Knower of what is present and what is absent; nothing of them is hidden from Him. Then He will inform you of what you used to do in the worldly life and recompense you accordingly.

Points of Benefit:

The immense privilege of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم upon humankind in general, and the Arabs in particular, as they were in ignorance and loss.
Guidance is a favor from Allah alone. It is sought from Him and obtained by obedience to Him.
Denying the Jews' claim that they are the patrons of Allah by challenging them to wish for death if they were truthful in their claim because the patron yearns for his beloved.
