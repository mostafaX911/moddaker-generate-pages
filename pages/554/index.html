(٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا نُودِيَ لِلصَّلَوٰةِ مِن يَوۡمِ ٱلۡجُمُعَةِ فَٱسۡعَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ وَذَرُواْ ٱلۡبَيۡعَۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, if the mu'adhin (caller to prayer) calls to the prayer of Friday after the speaker ascends the pulpit, then hurry to the mosques to attend the sermon and prayer and leave off selling so that it does not busy you from obedience. That command to go [to the sermon and prayer] and leave off selling after the call of the Friday prayer is better for you, O believers, if you knew that. So conform to what Allah commanded you.

(١٠) فَإِذَا قُضِيَتِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةُ فَٱنتَشِرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ

Then, if you finish the Friday prayer, disperse in the land, searching for permissible income and fulfilling your needs. Ask for Allah's favor through permissible income and profit. Remember Allah with much remembrance as you search for provision. Do not let your search for provision cause you to forget Allah's remembrance. [All that] in hopes of attaining what you love and salvation from what you flee.

(١١) وَإِذَا رَأَوۡاْ تِجَٰرَةً أَوۡ لَهۡوًا ٱنفَضُّوٓاْ إِلَيۡهَا وَتَرَكُوكَ قَآئِمٗاۚ قُلۡ مَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهۡوِ وَمِنَ ٱلتِّجَٰرَةِۚ وَٱللَّهُ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّٰزِقِينَ

And if some Muslims eye trade or leisure, they disperse, going out to them and leaving you, O Messenger, standing on the pulpit. Say, O Messenger: What is with Allah of reward for righteous action is better than the trade and leisure you went out to, and Allah is the best of providers.

The Hypocrites (Surah Al-Munafiqoon)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing the reality of hypocrites and warning against them.

Explanation:

(١) إِذَا جَآءَكَ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ قَالُواْ نَشۡهَدُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُهُۥ وَٱللَّهُ يَشۡهَدُ إِنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ لَكَٰذِبُونَ

When the hypocrites, who outwardly profess Islam and inwardly conceal disbelief, come to your gathering, O Messenger, they say: We testify that you are truly Allah's Messenger. Allah testifies that the hypocrites are lying in their claim that they testify from the depth of their hearts that you are His Messenger.

(٢) ٱتَّخَذُوٓاْ أَيۡمَٰنَهُمۡ جُنَّةٗ فَصَدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّهُمۡ سَآءَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

They made their oaths, which they swear by, claiming faith as a screen and protection for them from being killed and captured. They divert people from faith with what they cast of doubt and delving into bad news and tribulations. Indeed, their actions of hypocrisy and false oaths were atrocious.

(٣) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ ءَامَنُواْ ثُمَّ كَفَرُواْ فَطُبِعَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يَفۡقَهُونَ

That is because they believed hypocritically [i.e., concealing disbelief], and faith did not reach their hearts. Then they secretly disbelieved in Allah, so He sealed their hearts because of their disbelief; therefore, faith does not enter them. Then, because of that seal, they do not comprehend what is beneficial and virtuous for them.

(٤) ۞وَإِذَا رَأَيۡتَهُمۡ تُعۡجِبُكَ أَجۡسَامُهُمۡۖ وَإِن يَقُولُواْ تَسۡمَعۡ لِقَوۡلِهِمۡۖ كَأَنَّهُمۡ خُشُبٞ مُّسَنَّدَةٞۖ يَحۡسَبُونَ كُلَّ صَيۡحَةٍ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ هُمُ ٱلۡعَدُوُّ فَٱحۡذَرۡهُمۡۚ قَٰتَلَهُمُ ٱللَّهُۖ أَنَّىٰ يُؤۡفَكُونَ

And if you see them, O observer, their states and appearances impress you because of the beauty and delight they possess. If they speak, you listen to their speech due to the eloquence it has. In your gathering, O Messenger, it is as if they are propped logs, not understanding or grasping anything. They think every voice is targeting them due to the cowardice within them. They are truly the enemy, so take caution of them, O Messenger, that they spread your secrets or plot a scheme against you. Allah cursed them. How do they turn from faith with the clarity of its evidence and clearness of its proofs?!

Points of Benefit:

The obligation of hastening to Friday prayer after the call and the prohibition of anything besides it from the worldly life, except for an excusable matter.
Designating a surah (chapter) specifically for the [issue of] the hypocrites warns of their danger and the hidden nature of their affair.
What matters is righteousness on the inside, not beauty on the outside nor elegant speech.
