(٥) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمۡ تَعَالَوۡاْ يَسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ لَوَّوۡاْ رُءُوسَهُمۡ وَرَأَيۡتَهُمۡ يَصُدُّونَ وَهُم مُّسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ

And if it is said to these hypocrites: Come to Allah's Messenger apologizing for what was uttered from you, and he will request forgiveness for you from Allah for your sins,they would lean their heads in mockery and ridicule, and you would see them avoiding what they were commanded while being too arrogant to accept the truth and submit to it.

(٦) سَوَآءٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَسۡتَغۡفَرۡتَ لَهُمۡ أَمۡ لَمۡ تَسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ لَن يَغۡفِرَ ٱللَّهُ لَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ

O Messenger, it is the same whether you request the forgiveness of their sins or not. Allah will not forgive them for their sins. Indeed, Allah does not grant success to the people outside His obedience, adamant upon his disobedience.

(٧) هُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ لَا تُنفِقُواْ عَلَىٰ مَنۡ عِندَ رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ حَتَّىٰ يَنفَضُّواْۗ وَلِلَّهِ خَزَآئِنُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ لَا يَفۡقَهُونَ

They are the ones who say: Do not spend your wealth on those with Allah's Messenger from the poor and Bedouins around Medina so that they disperse from him. To Allah alone belong the treasures of the heavens and the treasures of the Earth. He provides them for whom He wills among His slaves. However, the hypocrites do not know that the treasures of provision are in His hand; may He be Exalted.

(٨) يَقُولُونَ لَئِن رَّجَعۡنَآ إِلَى ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ لَيُخۡرِجَنَّ ٱلۡأَعَزُّ مِنۡهَا ٱلۡأَذَلَّۚ وَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡعِزَّةُ وَلِرَسُولِهِۦ وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

Their head, Abdullah bin Ubay, says: If we return to Medina, the more mighty, and they are my people and I, shall expel the lowly from it, and they are Muhammad and his companions. Might is only for Allah, His Messenger, and the believers, and it is not for Abdullah bin Ubay and his companions. However, the hypocrites do not know that might is for Allah, His Messenger, and the believers.

After mentioning the efforts of hypocrites to be stingy in spending to prevent people from Allah's path, Allah warns the believers against that and commands them to spend in His cause, stating:

(٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تُلۡهِكُمۡ أَمۡوَٰلُكُمۡ وَلَآ أَوۡلَٰدُكُمۡ عَن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَمَن يَفۡعَلۡ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, let not your wealth nor your children busy you from prayer and other obligations of Islam. Whosoever's wealth and children busy him from what Allah obligated upon him of prayer or otherwise are truly the losers who will have lost their souls and families on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٠) وَأَنفِقُواْ مِن مَّا رَزَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَ أَحَدَكُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ فَيَقُولَ رَبِّ لَوۡلَآ أَخَّرۡتَنِيٓ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ قَرِيبٖ فَأَصَّدَّقَ وَأَكُن مِّنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

And spend from what Allah provided you with of wealth before death comes to one of you, then he would say to his Lord: My Lord, would you not delay me for a short while. Then I would donate from my wealth in the way of Allah and be from the righteous slaves of Allah whose deeds were good.

(١١) وَلَن يُؤَخِّرَ ٱللَّهُ نَفۡسًا إِذَا جَآءَ أَجَلُهَاۚ وَٱللَّهُ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Allah (may He be Exalted) will not delay a soul if its conclusion comes and its lifetime ends. Allah is well-informed of what you do. Nothing of your deeds is hidden from Him, and He will recompense you accordingly. If they are good, then good, and if evil, then evil.

Points of Benefit:

Arrogance and turning away from advice are among the characteristics of the hypocrites.
From the means of the enemies of the religion is the economic blockade of the Muslims.
The danger of wealth and children if they preoccupy one from the remembrance of Allah.
