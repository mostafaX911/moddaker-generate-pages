Mutual Fraud (Surah At-Taghabun)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Warning against matters that will lead to regret and detriment on the Day of Resurrection.

Explanation:

(١) يُسَبِّحُ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ لَهُ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ وَلَهُ ٱلۡحَمۡدُۖ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ

All creation in the heavens and the Earth exalt Allah and declare Him free of what does not befit Him of attributes of deficiency. The dominion is His alone, so there is no king besides Him. The best of praise is due to Him, and He is Omnipotent over everything; nothing hinders Him.

(٢) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُمۡ فَمِنكُمۡ كَافِرٞ وَمِنكُم مُّؤۡمِنٞۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ

He is the One who created you, O people. Then some of you are disbelievers in Him, their destination is the Fire, and some of you are believers in Him, their destination is Paradise. Allah sees what you do; nothing of your deeds is hidden from Him, and He will recompense you accordingly.

(٣) خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَصَوَّرَكُمۡ فَأَحۡسَنَ صُوَرَكُمۡۖ وَإِلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

He created the heavens and the Earth with a true purpose and did not create them in vain. He fashioned you, O people, so He made your forms excellent as benevolence and grace from Him. If He wished, He would have certainly made them atrocious. To Him alone is the return on the Day of Resurrection. Then He will recompense you based on your deeds. If good, then good, and if evil, then evil.

(٤) يَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا تُسِرُّونَ وَمَا تُعۡلِنُونَۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

He knows what is in the heavens and the Earth, and He knows what you hide of deeds and what you disclose. Allah is knowledgeable of what is in the breasts of good or evil. Nothing from that is hidden from Him.

(٥) أَلَمۡ يَأۡتِكُمۡ نَبَؤُاْ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن قَبۡلُ فَذَاقُواْ وَبَالَ أَمۡرِهِمۡ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Has not the news of the rejecting nations before you reached you, O polytheists, such as the people of Noah, 'Ād, Thamūd, and others? They tasted the punishment of the disbelief they were upon in the worldly life, and they have a painful torment in the Hereafter. Nay, that has reached you, so take a lesson from what their matter led to, then repent to Allah before what happened to them happens to you.

(٦) ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُۥ كَانَت تَّأۡتِيهِمۡ رُسُلُهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ فَقَالُوٓاْ أَبَشَرٞ يَهۡدُونَنَا فَكَفَرُواْ وَتَوَلَّواْۖ وَّٱسۡتَغۡنَى ٱللَّهُۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَنِيٌّ حَمِيدٞ

That torment only afflicted them because their messengers would come to them from Allah with clear arguments and manifest proofs, and then they said in objection to the messengers being of humanity: Would humans guide us to the truth?!So they disbelieved and turned away from faith in them, which did not harm Allah at all. He is free of the need for their faith and obedience. That is because their obedience does not increase anything for Him. Allah is self-sufficient, not needing any of His slaves, and praised in His statements and actions. So they disbelieved and turned away from faith in them, then they did not harm Allah at all. He was free of need for their faith and obedience. That is because their obedience does not increase anything for Him. Allah is self-sufficient, not needing any of His slaves, and praised in His statements and actions.

(٧) زَعَمَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَن لَّن يُبۡعَثُواْۚ قُلۡ بَلَىٰ وَرَبِّي لَتُبۡعَثُنَّ ثُمَّ لَتُنَبَّؤُنَّ بِمَا عَمِلۡتُمۡۚ وَذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرٞ

Those who disbelieved in Allah claim that Allah will not resurrect them [to be] living after their death. Say, O Messenger, to these deniers of the Resurrection: Nay, by my Lord, you will surely be resurrected on the Day of Resurrection. Then you will be informed of what you did in the worldly life. That Resurrection is easy for Allah, for He initially created you, so He is able to resurrect you [to be] living after your death for account and recompense.

(٨) فَ‍َٔامِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَٱلنُّورِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلۡنَاۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٞ

So believe, O people, in Allah, His Messenger, and the Quran We sent down upon our Messenger. Allah is aware of what you do. Nothing of your deeds is hidden from Him, and He will recompense accordingly.

(٩) يَوۡمَ يَجۡمَعُكُمۡ لِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡجَمۡعِۖ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمُ ٱلتَّغَابُنِۗ وَمَن يُؤۡمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ وَيَعۡمَلۡ صَٰلِحٗا يُكَفِّرۡ عَنۡهُ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِهِۦ وَيُدۡخِلۡهُ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

Remember, O Messenger, the day when Allah will gather you for the Day of Resurrection to give you the recompense for your actions. That is the day when the loss of the disbelievers will become clear, as the believers will inherit the homes in Paradise of the people of the Fire, and the people of the Fire will inherit the homes in the Fire of the people of Paradise. Those who have faith in Allah and do righteous deeds, Allah will remit their sins from them and enter them into gardens, under the palaces and trees of which rivers flow, to live in them forever and never to come out, the bliss of which will not end. That which they attained is the best form of success, which no other success can come close to.

Points of Benefit:

From Allah's decree is the division of people into wretched and blissful.
From the means that aid in righteous action is remembering the loss of people on the Day of Resurrection.
