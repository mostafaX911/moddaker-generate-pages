(١٠) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَكَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

Those who disbelieve in Allah and reject Our verses, which We sent down to Our Messenger are the people of the Fire, remaining in it eternally. Their destination is an atrocious one.

(١١) مَآ أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَمَن يُؤۡمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ يَهۡدِ قَلۡبَهُۥۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٞ

No calamity befell anyone in their self, wealth or children except by the decree of Allah and His predetermination. And whoever believes in Allah, His decree, and His predetermination, Allah gives their heart success by submission to His command and contentment with His decree. Allah is knowledgeable of everything. Nothing is hidden from Him.

(١٢) وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱلرَّسُولَۚ فَإِن تَوَلَّيۡتُمۡ فَإِنَّمَا عَلَىٰ رَسُولِنَا ٱلۡبَلَٰغُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ

Obey Allah and obey His Messenger. Then if you turn away from what His Messenger came to you with, the sin of that turning away is on you. Nothing is incumbent upon Our Messenger except informing what We commanded him to inform you of, and he has informed you of that.

(١٣) ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ

Allah is the rightful object of worship. There is no rightful object of worship besides Him. Let the believers rely upon Allah alone in all their affairs.

(١٤) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّ مِنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِكُمۡ وَأَوۡلَٰدِكُمۡ عَدُوّٗا لَّكُمۡ فَٱحۡذَرُوهُمۡۚ وَإِن تَعۡفُواْ وَتَصۡفَحُواْ وَتَغۡفِرُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٌ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, indeed, some of your spouses and children are enemies to you because they busy you from the remembrance of Allah and jihad (struggle) in His path, and they deter you. So beware of them affecting you. And if you pardon their blunders and turn away and conceal them, then Allah will forgive your sins and show you mercy. The recompense is according to the type of action.

(١٥) إِنَّمَآ أَمۡوَٰلُكُمۡ وَأَوۡلَٰدُكُمۡ فِتۡنَةٞۚ وَٱللَّهُ عِندَهُۥٓ أَجۡرٌ عَظِيمٞ

Your wealth and children are only a tribulation and test for you, for they may prompt you towards prohibited income and forsaking obedience to Allah. Allah has a great reward for those who give precedence to His obedience over the obedience of children and their preoccupation with wealth. This great reward is Paradise.

(١٦) فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُمۡ وَٱسۡمَعُواْ وَأَطِيعُواْ وَأَنفِقُواْ خَيۡرٗا لِّأَنفُسِكُمۡۗ وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفۡسِهِۦ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ

So take heed of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions as much as you can find a means to obey Him. And listen and obey Allah and His Messenger, and expend your wealth, which Allah provided you, in the avenues of good. Whomsoever Allah protects from his soul's craving are the ones attaining what they seek and saved from what they flee.

(١٧) إِن تُقۡرِضُواْ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنٗا يُضَٰعِفۡهُ لَكُمۡ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ شَكُورٌ حَلِيمٌ

If you loan Allah a fine loan by spending from your wealth in His path, He will multiply your reward by making the good deed [from] ten times its like to seven hundred times to many multiples, and He will overlook your sins. Allah is thankful, giving much reward for little action and forbearing, not hastening punishment.

(١٨) عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

Allah (may He be Exalted) is the knower of what is absent and what is present, nothing of that is hidden from Him, the Mighty who none defeat, the Wise in His creation, legislation, and predetermination.

Points of Benefit:

The mission of the Messengers is to convey from Allah. As for guidance, it is in Allah's Hand.
Faith in predetermination is a cause for serenity and guidance.
Accountability is within the limits of the accountable one's ability.
Multiplication of the reward for the one who spends in the path of Allah.
