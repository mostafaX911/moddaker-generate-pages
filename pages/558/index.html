Divorce (Surah At-Talaq)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Explaining the rulings pertaining to divorce, respecting its boundaries, and the fruits of heedfulness.

Explanation:

(١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ إِذَا طَلَّقۡتُمُ ٱلنِّسَآءَ فَطَلِّقُوهُنَّ لِعِدَّتِهِنَّ وَأَحۡصُواْ ٱلۡعِدَّةَۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ رَبَّكُمۡۖ لَا تُخۡرِجُوهُنَّ مِنۢ بُيُوتِهِنَّ وَلَا يَخۡرُجۡنَ إِلَّآ أَن يَأۡتِينَ بِفَٰحِشَةٖ مُّبَيِّنَةٖۚ وَتِلۡكَ حُدُودُ ٱللَّهِۚ وَمَن يَتَعَدَّ حُدُودَ ٱللَّهِ فَقَدۡ ظَلَمَ نَفۡسَهُۥۚ لَا تَدۡرِي لَعَلَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحۡدِثُ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ أَمۡرٗا

O Prophet, if you or any of the men from your nation intend to divorce your wives, then he should issue a divorce to her, considering her 'iddah (waiting period until she is permissible for another man). That is that the divorce should be issued during a period of purity between two separate menses, in which he has not had sexual relations with her. Also, keep track of the 'iddah, so that you are able to take your wives back within it if you intend to do so. And be mindful of Allah by fulfilling His commands and refraining from the things He has not allowed; do not force your divorced wives out of the homes they live in until their 'iddah finishes, except if they commit an obvious shameful act like adultery. Those are the limits of Allah He has set for His slaves; whoever trespasses the limits of Allah has, indeed, oppressed himself by placing himself in a situation of doom because of disobeying his Lord. O divorcée, you do not know; perhaps Allah may create a longing in the heart of the spouse, and he may take his wife back.

(٢) فَإِذَا بَلَغۡنَ أَجَلَهُنَّ فَأَمۡسِكُوهُنَّ بِمَعۡرُوفٍ أَوۡ فَارِقُوهُنَّ بِمَعۡرُوفٖ وَأَشۡهِدُواْ ذَوَيۡ عَدۡلٖ مِّنكُمۡ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلشَّهَٰدَةَ لِلَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ يُوعَظُ بِهِۦ مَن كَانَ يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۚ وَمَن يَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ يَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ مَخۡرَجٗا

So if they approach the end of their 'iddah then take them back based on desire and excellent companionship or abstain from taking them back until they finish their 'iddah so they would control their own matter while [you are] giving them their rights. And if you wished to take them back or separate from them, then have two just ones among you witness to cut off [any] dispute. And come forth, O witnesses, with the testimony seeking the Face of Allah. He who believes in Allah and the Day of Resurrection is reminded by that which was mentioned of rulings because he is who will benefit by reminder and admonition. Whoever takes heed of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, Allah will make a way out for him for all that he falls into of strain and difficulty.

(٣) وَيَرۡزُقۡهُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَحۡتَسِبُۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسۡبُهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَٰلِغُ أَمۡرِهِۦۚ قَدۡ جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدۡرٗا

And He will provide Him from where he did not imagine and would not be in his deliberation. Whosoever relies on Allah in his affairs, then He suffices him. Indeed, Allah executes his commands. Nothing hinders Him, and nothing escapes Him. Allah has set an interval for everything at which they end. Hence, for adversity, there is an interval, and for ease, there is an interval. Neither is perpetual for the human being.

(٤) وَٱلَّٰٓـِٔي يَئِسۡنَ مِنَ ٱلۡمَحِيضِ مِن نِّسَآئِكُمۡ إِنِ ٱرۡتَبۡتُمۡ فَعِدَّتُهُنَّ ثَلَٰثَةُ أَشۡهُرٖ وَٱلَّٰٓـِٔي لَمۡ يَحِضۡنَۚ وَأُوْلَٰتُ ٱلۡأَحۡمَالِ أَجَلُهُنَّ أَن يَضَعۡنَ حَمۡلَهُنَّۚ وَمَن يَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ يَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ مِنۡ أَمۡرِهِۦ يُسۡرٗا

[Regarding] the divorced women who despair of menstruation due to their old age, if you have doubted the manner of their 'iddah, then their 'iddah is three months. And for those who have not reached the age of menstruation due to their youth, their 'iddah is likewise three months. And [as for] the pregnant women, the end of their 'iddah from divorce or death [of the husband] is when they give birth to what they carry. Whosoever takes heed of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, Allah will facilitate his affairs and make every arduous matter easy for him.

(٥) ذَٰلِكَ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ أَنزَلَهُۥٓ إِلَيۡكُمۡۚ وَمَن يَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ يُكَفِّرۡ عَنۡهُ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِهِۦ وَيُعۡظِمۡ لَهُۥٓ أَجۡرًا

That which was mentioned of the rulings of divorce, taking back [the wife after divorce], and 'iddah is the ruling of Allah. He sent it down to you, O believers, to act upon it. Whoever takes heed of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, He will wipe out his misdeeds, which he carried out, and grant him a great reward in the Hereafter. It is entering Paradise and achieving the delight that is not [ever] exhausted.

Points of Benefit:

What is addressed to the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم is an address to his nation, so long as it is not established that it is particular to him.
The obligation to house and spend on the divorced woman who can be taken back.
The recommendation to have [dealings] witnessed to eliminate matters of disagreement.
The abundant benefits and significance of heedfulness.
