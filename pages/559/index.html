After mentioning the ruling of divorce and raj'ah (taking back the divorcee during her waiting period), Allah legislates the obligation of lodging and expenses for divorced women, stating:

(٦) أَسۡكِنُوهُنَّ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ سَكَنتُم مِّن وُجۡدِكُمۡ وَلَا تُضَآرُّوهُنَّ لِتُضَيِّقُواْ عَلَيۡهِنَّۚ وَإِن كُنَّ أُوْلَٰتِ حَمۡلٖ فَأَنفِقُواْ عَلَيۡهِنَّ حَتَّىٰ يَضَعۡنَ حَمۡلَهُنَّۚ فَإِنۡ أَرۡضَعۡنَ لَكُمۡ فَ‍َٔاتُوهُنَّ أُجُورَهُنَّ وَأۡتَمِرُواْ بَيۡنَكُم بِمَعۡرُوفٖۖ وَإِن تَعَاسَرۡتُمۡ فَسَتُرۡضِعُ لَهُۥٓ أُخۡرَىٰ

Have the divorcées reside, O husbands, where you reside according to your capacity, so Allah does not burden you with other than that. Do not harm them in spending, residence, or otherwise in hopes of straining them. And if the divorced women are pregnant, spend on them until they deliver what they carry. Then, if they nurse your children for you, give them the wage of their nursing and refer to what is customary regarding the wage. Then, if the husband is frugal about what the wife wants of wages and she becomes miserly such that she is not content except with what she wants, then let the father employ a different wet nurse to nurse his child.

(٧) لِيُنفِقۡ ذُو سَعَةٖ مِّن سَعَتِهِۦۖ وَمَن قُدِرَ عَلَيۡهِ رِزۡقُهُۥ فَلۡيُنفِقۡ مِمَّآ ءَاتَىٰهُ ٱللَّهُۚ لَا يُكَلِّفُ ٱللَّهُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا مَآ ءَاتَىٰهَاۚ سَيَجۡعَلُ ٱللَّهُ بَعۡدَ عُسۡرٖ يُسۡرٗا

Let he who has abundance in wealth spend on his divorcée and child from his abundance. And whosoever is constrained in his provision, then let him spend from what Allah gave him of it. Allah does not burden a soul except that which He granted it, so He does not burden beyond it nor more than the soul can bear. After his constrained state and its hardship, Allah will bring about abundance and self-sufficiency.

After mentioning several commands, Allah warns against turning away from those commands and says that the result would be terrible. He states:

(٨) وَكَأَيِّن مِّن قَرۡيَةٍ عَتَتۡ عَنۡ أَمۡرِ رَبِّهَا وَرُسُلِهِۦ فَحَاسَبۡنَٰهَا حِسَابٗا شَدِيدٗا وَعَذَّبۡنَٰهَا عَذَابٗا نُّكۡرٗا

And how many were the towns We punished severely for disobeying their Lord and His messengers (may peace be upon them)! We held them gravely accountable for their wrongdoings and afflicted them with a vicious torment in this world and in the Hereafter.

(٩) فَذَاقَتۡ وَبَالَ أَمۡرِهَا وَكَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ أَمۡرِهَا خُسۡرًا

So they tasted the punishment of their evil deeds, and their end was loss in the worldly life and Hereafter.

(١٠) أَعَدَّ ٱللَّهُ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٗا شَدِيدٗاۖ فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ يَٰٓأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْۚ قَدۡ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَيۡكُمۡ ذِكۡرٗا

Allah prepared an intense torment for them. So take heed of Allah, O people of reason who have believed in Allah and His Messenger, by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions such that what happened to them does not happen to you. Allah has sent down to you a reminder that reminds you of the evil consequence of disobeying Him and the excellent result of obeying Him.

(١١) رَّسُولٗا يَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ مُبَيِّنَٰتٖ لِّيُخۡرِجَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ مِنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِۚ وَمَن يُؤۡمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ وَيَعۡمَلۡ صَٰلِحٗا يُدۡخِلۡهُ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدٗاۖ قَدۡ أَحۡسَنَ ٱللَّهُ لَهُۥ رِزۡقًا

This reminder is a Messenger from Him who recites the clarifying verses of Allah upon you, with no confusion in them, in the hopes of purging those who believed in Allah, affirmed the truth of His Messenger and did righteous actions from the darknesses of misguidance to the light of guidance. And whosoever believes in Allah and does righteous action, Allah will enter him into gardens where rivers flow beneath its palaces and trees, remaining in it forever. Allah has made His provision magnificent, where He has entered him into a garden whose delights do not cease.

(١٢) ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ سَبۡعَ سَمَٰوَٰتٖ وَمِنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِثۡلَهُنَّۖ يَتَنَزَّلُ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ بَيۡنَهُنَّ لِتَعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَدۡ أَحَاطَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عِلۡمَۢا

Allah is the One who created seven heavens and created seven earths like His creation of seven heavens. Allah's command of manifestation and legislation descends between them in the hopes that you know Allah is Omnipotent over everything; nothing is beyond His capability. And that indeed He (may He be Exalted) has encompassed everything in terms of knowledge, so nothing in the heavens nor the Earth is hidden from Him.

Points of Benefit:

The lack of obligation on the pregnant one to nurse if she is divorced.
Accountability is only according to ability.
Belief in Allah's ability and His knowledge encompassing everything is a cause for the contentment and serenity of the heart.
