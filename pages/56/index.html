(٤٦) وَيُكَلِّمُ ٱلنَّاسَ فِي ٱلۡمَهۡدِ وَكَهۡلٗا وَمِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

This child will miraculously speak to people when he is a small baby and speak to them when he grows up and becomes a man. He will tell them what is best for them in their religious and worldly affairs. He will also be one of those who are righteous in speech and deeds.

(٤٧) قَالَتۡ رَبِّ أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لِي وَلَدٞ وَلَمۡ يَمۡسَسۡنِي بَشَرٞۖ قَالَ كَذَٰلِكِ ٱللَّهُ يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ إِذَا قَضَىٰٓ أَمۡرٗا فَإِنَّمَا يَقُولُ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ

Mary was surprised that she was to have a child without a husband and said in astonishment: How can I have a child when no man has come near me lawfully or unlawfully? The angel told her: Just as Allah will create a child for you without a father, He creates whatever He wishes, even though it may be out of the ordinary. When Allah wishes for something, He says 'Be,' and it is. Nothing can render Him incapable.

(٤٨) وَيُعَلِّمُهُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةَ وَٱلۡإِنجِيلَ

Allah will teach him to write and say and do things correctly; He will teach him the Torah that He had revealed to Moses (peace be upon him) and the Gospel that He will reveal to him.

(٤٩) وَرَسُولًا إِلَىٰ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ أَنِّي قَدۡ جِئۡتُكُم بِ‍َٔايَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ أَنِّيٓ أَخۡلُقُ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلطِّينِ كَهَيۡ‍َٔةِ ٱلطَّيۡرِ فَأَنفُخُ فِيهِ فَيَكُونُ طَيۡرَۢا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَأُبۡرِئُ ٱلۡأَكۡمَهَ وَٱلۡأَبۡرَصَ وَأُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَأُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا تَأۡكُلُونَ وَمَا تَدَّخِرُونَ فِي بُيُوتِكُمۡۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Allah will likewise make him a messenger to the Israelites, instructing him to say to them: I am Allah's Messenger to you. I have brought you a sign proving my prophethood: I will fashion a bird using clay, then breathe into it, and it will become a living bird with Allah's permission. I will cure the one who was born blind so that he can see and the leper who will recover from his illness, and I will bring the dead back to life. I will do all of this with Allah's permission. I will tell you about what you consume and hide in your homes. All these extraordinary things I mentioned to you, which human beings cannot do, are a clear sign that I am Allah's Messenger to you if you wish to believe and accept the proof.

(٥٠) وَمُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيَّ مِنَ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِ وَلِأُحِلَّ لَكُم بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي حُرِّمَ عَلَيۡكُمۡۚ وَجِئۡتُكُم بِ‍َٔايَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ

I have come to you to confirm the revelation of the Torah that was before me and to make some of what was unlawful in the past lawful: making things easy for you. I have brought clear proof of the truthfulness of what I say, so fear Allah by fulfilling His instructions and avoiding His prohibitions, and follow that which I invite you to.

(٥١) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ رَبِّي وَرَبُّكُمۡ فَٱعۡبُدُوهُۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٞ مُّسۡتَقِيمٞ

Allah is my Lord and your Lord; He is the only One who deserves to be obeyed and feared. So, worship Him alone. This worship of Allah and fear Him that I instruct you to do is the straight path with no crookedness.

(٥٢) ۞فَلَمَّآ أَحَسَّ عِيسَىٰ مِنۡهُمُ ٱلۡكُفۡرَ قَالَ مَنۡ أَنصَارِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ قَالَ ٱلۡحَوَارِيُّونَ نَحۡنُ أَنصَارُ ٱللَّهِ ءَامَنَّا بِٱللَّهِ وَٱشۡهَدۡ بِأَنَّا مُسۡلِمُونَ

When Jesus realized they would continue in disbelief, he addressed the Israelites: Who will help me in inviting towards Allah? His select followers said: We are the ones who will help Allah's religion. We believe in Allah, and we follow you. Be a witness, O Jesus, that we submit to Allah by accepting His Oneness and obeying Him.

Points of Benefit:

The honor of writing and notation, and the elevated status of both, as Allah, the Exalted, mentioned them before anything else.
One of Allah's divine practices (the Exalted) is to support His messengers with clear signs indicating their truthfulness, which is beyond human capability.
Jesus came with leniency for the Israelites, easing some of the strict laws imposed on them in parts of the Torah. This indicates the occurrence of abrogation between the legislations.
