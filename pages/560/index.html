Prohibition (Surah At-Tahreem)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Call to establish households upon venerating Allah's limits and prioritizing His contentment alone.

Explanation:

(١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ لِمَ تُحَرِّمُ مَآ أَحَلَّ ٱللَّهُ لَكَۖ تَبۡتَغِي مَرۡضَاتَ أَزۡوَٰجِكَۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

O Messenger, why do you make impermissible that which Allah has made permissible for you from seeking pleasure with your slave girl Mariah, wanting to please your wives in doing so after they expressed protective jealousy over her? Allah is Forgiving towards you and is Merciful to you.

(٢) قَدۡ فَرَضَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ تَحِلَّةَ أَيۡمَٰنِكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ مَوۡلَىٰكُمۡۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

Allah has legislated for you the nullification of your oaths through expiation if you find that which is better than them or if you broke them. Allah is your Supporter; He is Omniscient of your conditions and what benefits you, and He is All-Wise in His legislation and predetermination.

(٣) وَإِذۡ أَسَرَّ ٱلنَّبِيُّ إِلَىٰ بَعۡضِ أَزۡوَٰجِهِۦ حَدِيثٗا فَلَمَّا نَبَّأَتۡ بِهِۦ وَأَظۡهَرَهُ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِ عَرَّفَ بَعۡضَهُۥ وَأَعۡرَضَ عَنۢ بَعۡضٖۖ فَلَمَّا نَبَّأَهَا بِهِۦ قَالَتۡ مَنۡ أَنۢبَأَكَ هَٰذَاۖ قَالَ نَبَّأَنِيَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ

And remember when the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم mentioned something only to Hafsah, and that was that he would never again go close to his slave, Maria. So after Hafsah informed Aisha of the news, and Allah informed His Messenger of his secret having been spread, he reprimanded Hafsah and told her part of what she had said, not mentioning the other part. So she asked him: Who informed you of this? He replied: The Omniscient and All-Aware of every hidden thing informed me of it.

(٤) إِن تَتُوبَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ فَقَدۡ صَغَتۡ قُلُوبُكُمَاۖ وَإِن تَظَٰهَرَا عَلَيۡهِ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ مَوۡلَىٰهُ وَجِبۡرِيلُ وَصَٰلِحُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَۖ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ ظَهِيرٌ

It is necessary that you both seek repentance because your hearts had leaned to loving what Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم disdains from avoiding his slave girl and forbidding her from himself. If you insist on resuming your act of ruining relationships for him, then indeed, Allah is his ally and supporter. Likewise, Gabriel and the best believers are his allies and supporters. And the angels, after the support of Allah for him, are his helpers and supporters against whoever hurts him.

(٥) عَسَىٰ رَبُّهُۥٓ إِن طَلَّقَكُنَّ أَن يُبۡدِلَهُۥٓ أَزۡوَٰجًا خَيۡرٗا مِّنكُنَّ مُسۡلِمَٰتٖ مُّؤۡمِنَٰتٖ قَٰنِتَٰتٖ تَٰٓئِبَٰتٍ عَٰبِدَٰتٖ سَٰٓئِحَٰتٖ ثَيِّبَٰتٖ وَأَبۡكَارٗا

Perhaps if His Prophet divorced you, His Lord (may He be Exalted) would exchange him wives better than you, complying with His commands, believers in Him and His Messenger, being obedient to Allah, repentant of their sins, worshipping their Lord, fasting, previously married women and virgins, no one having had intercourse with them before him. However, he did not divorce them.

(٦) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ قُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَأَهۡلِيكُمۡ نَارٗا وَقُودُهَا ٱلنَّاسُ وَٱلۡحِجَارَةُ عَلَيۡهَا مَلَٰٓئِكَةٌ غِلَاظٞ شِدَادٞ لَّا يَعۡصُونَ ٱللَّهَ مَآ أَمَرَهُمۡ وَيَفۡعَلُونَ مَا يُؤۡمَرُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, prepare protection for yourselves and your families from a fire ignited with people and stones. Upon this Fire are angels who are harsh and severe upon whoever enters it. They do not disobey the command of Allah when He commands them, and they do what He commands them without delay or neglect.

(٧) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَا تَعۡتَذِرُواْ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۖ إِنَّمَا تُجۡزَوۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

It is said to the disbelievers on the Day of Resurrection: O you who have disbelieved in Allah, do not present excuses today for what you were upon of disbelief and acts of disobedience, for your excuses will not be accepted. On this Day, you are but recompensed for what you used to do in the worldly life of disbelief in Allah and rejecting His Messengers.

Points of Benefit:

The legitimacy of expiating vows.
Clarifying the Prophet's صلى الله عليه وسلم rank with His Lord and His defense of him.
From the generosity of the Chosen One (i.e., the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم) with his wives was that he would not rebuke to the fullest extent, for he would overlook some mistakes to maintain affection.
The believer's responsibility for himself and his family.
