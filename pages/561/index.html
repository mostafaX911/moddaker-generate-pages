(٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ تُوبُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ تَوۡبَةٗ نَّصُوحًا عَسَىٰ رَبُّكُمۡ أَن يُكَفِّرَ عَنكُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِكُمۡ وَيُدۡخِلَكُمۡ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ يَوۡمَ لَا يُخۡزِي ٱللَّهُ ٱلنَّبِيَّ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَعَهُۥۖ نُورُهُمۡ يَسۡعَىٰ بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَبِأَيۡمَٰنِهِمۡ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَآ أَتۡمِمۡ لَنَا نُورَنَا وَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَآۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

O you who believe in Allah and act by what He legislated for them, repent to Allah from your sins with true repentance. Perhaps your Lord will wipe out your evil deeds from you and enter you into gardens in which rivers flow beneath its palaces on the Day of Resurrection, the Day Allah will not debase the Prophet nor those who believed with Him by entering them into the Fire. Their light goes before them, and on their rights [as they are] on the Sirat (the bridge over the Fire), they will say: O our Lord, make our light complete until we reach Paradise, so we not be like the hypocrites whose lights are extinguished on the Sirat, and forgive us our sins. Indeed, you are Omnipotent over everything, so You are unhindered from completing our light and pardoning our sins.

(٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ جَٰهِدِ ٱلۡكُفَّارَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ وَٱغۡلُظۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ وَمَأۡوَىٰهُمۡ جَهَنَّمُۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

O Messenger, struggle against the disbelievers by sword, and the hypocrites by tongue, and upholding the hudood (legislated punishments). Be severe with them until they hold you in awe. Their refuge that they shelter in on the Day of Resurrection is Hell, and their destination that they return to is a terrible one.

(١٠) ضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ٱمۡرَأَتَ نُوحٖ وَٱمۡرَأَتَ لُوطٖۖ كَانَتَا تَحۡتَ عَبۡدَيۡنِ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا صَٰلِحَيۡنِ فَخَانَتَاهُمَا فَلَمۡ يُغۡنِيَا عَنۡهُمَا مِنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَقِيلَ ٱدۡخُلَا ٱلنَّارَ مَعَ ٱلدَّٰخِلِينَ

Allah gives an example of those who disbelieve in Allah and His messengers, in that their relationship with the believers does not benefit, by giving the example of the case of the wives of two prophets among the prophets of Allah: Noah and Lot, peace be upon them. For they were two wives of two righteous slaves, then they betrayed their husbands by what they were upon of deterring from the way of Allah and supporting the people of disbelief from their communities. So them being the wives to these two righteous slaves did not benefit them, and it was said to them: Enter the Fire along with those entering it of the disbelievers and the wicked.

(١١) وَضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱمۡرَأَتَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِذۡ قَالَتۡ رَبِّ ٱبۡنِ لِي عِندَكَ بَيۡتٗا فِي ٱلۡجَنَّةِ وَنَجِّنِي مِن فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَعَمَلِهِۦ وَنَجِّنِي مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

And Allah gave an example of those who believe in Allah and His messengers, in that their relationship to the disbelievers does not hurt them nor affect them so long as they are upright on the truth, by giving the example of the case of Pharoah's wife when she said: O my Lord, build a house for me near you in Paradise, and save me from the coercion of Pharoah, his authority, and his evil actions. And save me from those wronging themselves by following him in his transgression and wrongdoing.

(١٢) وَمَرۡيَمَ ٱبۡنَتَ عِمۡرَٰنَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَحۡصَنَتۡ فَرۡجَهَا فَنَفَخۡنَا فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِنَا وَصَدَّقَتۡ بِكَلِمَٰتِ رَبِّهَا وَكُتُبِهِۦ وَكَانَتۡ مِنَ ٱلۡقَٰنِتِينَ

And Allah gave an example of those who believe in Allah and His messengers, in the case of Mary, daughter of 'Imran, who guarded her private parts from zina (unlawful sexual intercourse), so Allah commanded Gabriel to blow in them so she became pregnant with Jesus, son of Mary, without a father, by the power of Allah. She affirmed the legislation of Allah, His books sent down upon His messengers, and was among those obedient to Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.

Points of Benefit:

Sincere repentance is a cause for every good.
Pairing striving for knowledge and proof with striving by the sword indicates their importance and that neither is sufficient without the other.
A relationship by cause or lineage does not benefit the holder of that relationship on the Day of Resurrection if the religion has separated them.
Abstinence and avoiding suspicion are among the characteristics of the righteous believing women.
