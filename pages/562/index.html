The Dominion (Surah Al-Mulk)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Illustrating the perfection of Allah's dominion such that it inspires fear and awe of Him and warning of His punishment.

Explanation:

(١) تَبَٰرَكَ ٱلَّذِي بِيَدِهِ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ

Allah's vast goodness, the One who solely possesses the dominion, is magnified and abundant. He is Omnipotent over everything; nothing is beyond His capability.

(٢) ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ وَٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ لِيَبۡلُوَكُمۡ أَيُّكُمۡ أَحۡسَنُ عَمَلٗاۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡغَفُورُ

The One who created death and created life to test which of you, O people, is better in action. He is the Almighty whom no one can defeat, the Forgiver of the sins of those who repented among His slaves.

(٣) ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ سَبۡعَ سَمَٰوَٰتٖ طِبَاقٗاۖ مَّا تَرَىٰ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ مِن تَفَٰوُتٖۖ فَٱرۡجِعِ ٱلۡبَصَرَ هَلۡ تَرَىٰ مِن فُطُورٖ

The One who created seven heavens; each heaven is a layer above the previous one without contact between heaven and heaven. You will not witness, O observer, any discrepancy or unsuitability in what Allah created. So look again: Do you see splitting or fracturing? You will never see that. You will only see a perfected, masterful creation.

(٤) ثُمَّ ٱرۡجِعِ ٱلۡبَصَرَ كَرَّتَيۡنِ يَنقَلِبۡ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡبَصَرُ خَاسِئٗا وَهُوَ حَسِيرٞ

Then, look again, time after time; your eyesight will return lowly, exhausted, and will cease looking, having not seen any defect or fault in the creation of the sky.

(٥) وَلَقَدۡ زَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا بِمَصَٰبِيحَ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهَا رُجُومٗا لِّلشَّيَٰطِينِۖ وَأَعۡتَدۡنَا لَهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

And most verily, We adorned the nearest heaven to the Earth with illuminating stars, and We made those stars flames with which the devils who eavesdrop are repelled such that they burn them. And We have prepared for them the blazing fire in the Hereafter.

(٦) وَلِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِرَبِّهِمۡ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

And for those who disbelieve in their Lord, there is the torment of the kindled fire on the Day of Resurrection. Evil is the place of return that they return to.

(٧) إِذَآ أُلۡقُواْ فِيهَا سَمِعُواْ لَهَا شَهِيقٗا وَهِيَ تَفُورُ

When they are thrown into the Fire, they will hear an ugly, intense sound as it boils, like the boiling of a boiler.

(٨) تَكَادُ تَمَيَّزُ مِنَ ٱلۡغَيۡظِۖ كُلَّمَآ أُلۡقِيَ فِيهَا فَوۡجٞ سَأَلَهُمۡ خَزَنَتُهَآ أَلَمۡ يَأۡتِكُمۡ نَذِيرٞ

Its parts almost separate from each other and become distinct due to the intensity of its fury upon those who enter it. Each time a wave of its disbelieving people is thrown into it, the angels appointed over it will ask them in rebuke: Did not a Messenger come to you in the worldly life, frightening you of the torment of Allah?

(٩) قَالُواْ بَلَىٰ قَدۡ جَآءَنَا نَذِيرٞ فَكَذَّبۡنَا وَقُلۡنَا مَا نَزَّلَ ٱللَّهُ مِن شَيۡءٍ إِنۡ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا فِي ضَلَٰلٖ كَبِيرٖ

The disbelievers will say: Yes, a messenger came to us to frighten us of the torment of Allah, so we rejected him and told him: Allah did not send revelation. You messengers are just in great misguidance from the truth.

(١٠) وَقَالُواْ لَوۡ كُنَّا نَسۡمَعُ أَوۡ نَعۡقِلُ مَا كُنَّا فِيٓ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

And the disbelievers will say: If we had been listening with a listening that is beneficial or reasoning with the reason of who distinguishes the truth from the falsehood, we would not be amongst the people of the Fire. Rather, we would have believed in the messengers, affirmed the truth of what they came with, and been among the companions of Paradise.

(١١) فَٱعۡتَرَفُواْ بِذَنۢبِهِمۡ فَسُحۡقٗا لِّأَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

They confessed against themselves about disbelief and rejection, so they deserved the Fire. So away with the people of the Fire.

After mentioning the traits of the people of disbelief and their recompense, Allah proceeds to mention the traits of the people of belief and their recompense. He states:

(١٢) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخۡشَوۡنَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٞ وَأَجۡرٞ كَبِيرٞ

Indeed, those who fear Allah in private have forgiveness for their sins. And they have a great reward, which is Paradise.

Points of Benefit:

Knowing the wisdom of creating death and life necessitates hastening to righteous action before death.
Hell's fury upon the disbelievers and its rage is out of protective zeal for Allah, the Transcendent.
Obedience to Allah and fear of Him in seclusion are among the causes of forgiveness and entering Paradise.
