(١٣) وَأَسِرُّواْ قَوۡلَكُمۡ أَوِ ٱجۡهَرُواْ بِهِۦٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

O people, speak secretly or openly, for Allah knows it. Indeed, He, Immaculate is He, is knowledgeable of what is in the hearts of His slaves; nothing from that is hidden from Him.

(١٤) أَلَا يَعۡلَمُ مَنۡ خَلَقَ وَهُوَ ٱللَّطِيفُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ

Does the One who created all creation not know what is secret and what is more concealed than what is secret?! He is The Subtle with His slaves, The Aware of their affairs; nothing of them is hidden from Him.

(١٥) هُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ ذَلُولٗا فَٱمۡشُواْ فِي مَنَاكِبِهَا وَكُلُواْ مِن رِّزۡقِهِۦۖ وَإِلَيۡهِ ٱلنُّشُورُ

He is the One who made the Earth soft and easy for you to live on, so traverse its sides and borders and eat from His provision that He prepared for you within it. To Him alone is your resurrection for accountability and requital.

(١٦) ءَأَمِنتُم مَّن فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ أَن يَخۡسِفَ بِكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ فَإِذَا هِيَ تَمُورُ

Do you feel safe from Allah, who is in the sky causing the Earth to split beneath you, just as He caused it to split beneath Qarun after it was easy and subdued for living upon, then it will quake with you after its stillness?!

(١٧) أَمۡ أَمِنتُم مَّن فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ أَن يُرۡسِلَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ حَاصِبٗاۖ فَسَتَعۡلَمُونَ كَيۡفَ نَذِيرِ

Or do you feel safe from Allah, who is in the sky, sending down stones upon you from the sky just like He sent them upon the people of Lot? Then you will know My warning to you when you see My punishment, but you will not benefit from it after seeing the torment.

(١٨) وَلَقَدۡ كَذَّبَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ نَكِيرِ

And most verily, the nations that came before these polytheists denied, so the torment of Allah descended upon them when they insisted on their disbelief and rejection, so how was My reprimand upon them? Verily, it was a severe reprimand.

(١٩) أَوَ لَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ إِلَى ٱلطَّيۡرِ فَوۡقَهُمۡ صَٰٓفَّٰتٖ وَيَقۡبِضۡنَۚ مَا يُمۡسِكُهُنَّ إِلَّا ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءِۢ بَصِيرٌ

Do these rejectors not see how the birds above them as they fly spread their wings in the air at times and close them upon themselves at other times? Nothing keeps them from falling to the Earth except Allah. Indeed, He sees everything; nothing is hidden from Him.

(٢٠) أَمَّنۡ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي هُوَ جُندٞ لَّكُمۡ يَنصُرُكُم مِّن دُونِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِۚ إِنِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ إِلَّا فِي غُرُورٍ

There is no army for you, O disbelievers, that can prevent you from the torment of Allah if He wishes to torment you. The disbelievers are but deluded; the Devil tricked them, so they were deceived by him.

(٢١) أَمَّنۡ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي يَرۡزُقُكُمۡ إِنۡ أَمۡسَكَ رِزۡقَهُۥۚ بَل لَّجُّواْ فِي عُتُوّٖ وَنُفُورٍ

And no one will provide for you if Allah prevents His provision from reaching you. Rather, the crux is that the disbelievers have persisted in argumentativeness, arrogance, and refraining from the truth.

(٢٢) أَفَمَن يَمۡشِي مُكِبًّا عَلَىٰ وَجۡهِهِۦٓ أَهۡدَىٰٓ أَمَّن يَمۡشِي سَوِيًّا عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

So is the one who walks falling on his face, landing upon it, and is a polytheist, more guided, or the believer who walks straight on a straight path?

(٢٣) قُلۡ هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَكُمۡ وَجَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَٱلۡأَفۡ‍ِٔدَةَۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَشۡكُرُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to these rejecting polytheists: Allah is the One who created you and made for you ears you hear with, eyes you see with, and hearts you reason with. Little do you thank Him for the favors He has bestowed upon you.

(٢٤) قُلۡ هُوَ ٱلَّذِي ذَرَأَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ

Say, O Messenger, to these rejecting polytheists: Allah is the One who dispersed you throughout the Earth and spread you in it, not your idols, which do not create anything. To Him alone, on the Day of Resurrection, will you be gathered for account and requital, not to your idols. So fear Him and worship Him alone.

(٢٥) وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

And the rejectors of the resurrection, considering it far-fetched, say: O Muhammad, when will this promise that you and your companions promised us to occur come if you are truthful in your claim that it is coming?!

(٢٦) قُلۡ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡعِلۡمُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَإِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ نَذِيرٞ مُّبِينٞ

Say, O Messenger: The knowledge of the Hour is only with Allah. No one knows when it will happen except Him. I am only a clear warner in my warning to you.

Points of Benefit:

Allah observes all that His slaves' breasts conceal.
Disbelief and acts of disobedience are among the causes of Allah's torment in the worldly life and Afterlife.
Disbelief in Allah is darkness and confusion. Faith in Him is light and guidance.
