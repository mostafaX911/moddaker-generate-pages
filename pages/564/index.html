(٢٧) فَلَمَّا رَأَوۡهُ زُلۡفَةٗ سِيٓ‍َٔتۡ وُجُوهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَقِيلَ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَدَّعُونَ

So when the promise inflicts them, and they see the torment close to them on the Day of Resurrection, the faces of those who disbelieved in Allah will change by blackening. It will be said to them: This is what you used to request and hasten in the worldly life.

(٢٨) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِنۡ أَهۡلَكَنِيَ ٱللَّهُ وَمَن مَّعِيَ أَوۡ رَحِمَنَا فَمَن يُجِيرُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ مِنۡ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ

O Messenger, reprimand these rejecting polytheists by telling them: If Allah took my life and the life of the believers that are with me or had mercy on us by delaying our demise, then who will save the disbelievers from a painful punishment? No one will save them.

(٢٩) قُلۡ هُوَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦ وَعَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡنَاۖ فَسَتَعۡلَمُونَ مَنۡ هُوَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: He is The Vastly Merciful who invites you to worship Him alone. We have believed in Him, and upon Him alone have we relied in our affairs. So you will soon know, inescapably, who is in clear deviance and who is on a straight path.

(٣٠) قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِنۡ أَصۡبَحَ مَآؤُكُمۡ غَوۡرٗا فَمَن يَأۡتِيكُم بِمَآءٖ مَّعِينِۢ

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists: Tell me, if the water you drink from were to become sunken into the Earth, making you unable to reach it, who would bring you abundant flowing water? No one other than Allah.

The Pen (Surah Al-Qalam)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Allah's (may He be Exalted and Transcendent) testimony of the Prophet's صلى الله عليه وسلم exemplary character, defense of him, and making him steadfast.

Explanation:

(١) نٓۚ وَٱلۡقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسۡطُرُونَ

Nūn – The discussion on its counterparts preceded the beginning of Surah Al-Baqarah. Allah swore by the Pen and whatever the people write with their pens.

(٢) مَآ أَنتَ بِنِعۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ بِمَجۡنُونٖ

O Messenger, you are not a madman due to the prophethood Allah has bestowed upon you. Rather, you are free of the madness the polytheists accused you of.

(٣) وَإِنَّ لَكَ لَأَجۡرًا غَيۡرَ مَمۡنُونٖ

You will definitely be rewarded endlessly for what you suffer by carrying this message to the people without reminding them of your favors upon them.

(٤) وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٖ

And indeed, you are certainly upon the great character that the Quran has come with, so you embody what is in it in the most comprehensive manner.

(٥) فَسَتُبۡصِرُ وَيُبۡصِرُونَ

So you will soon see, and these deniers will see.

(٦) بِأَييِّكُمُ ٱلۡمَفۡتُونُ

When the truth is revealed, it will be clear in which of you is the madness.

(٧) إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ

Indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, knows who has strayed from His path, and He is most knowledgeable of the ones guided to it. So He knows that they are indeed the ones who have deviated from it and that you, indeed, are the one who guides to it.

(٨) فَلَا تُطِعِ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

So, O Messenger, do not obey those rejecting what you have brought.

(٩) وَدُّواْ لَوۡ تُدۡهِنُ فَيُدۡهِنُونَ

They wish that you were soft and gentle with them at the cost of the religion, so they are soft and gentle with you.

(١٠) وَلَا تُطِعۡ كُلَّ حَلَّافٖ مَّهِينٍ

And do not obey every lowly person who is abundant in taking oaths with falsehood,

(١١) هَمَّازٖ مَّشَّآءِۢ بِنَمِيمٖ

Abundant in backbiting people, abundant in carrying gossip between them to break up their relations,

(١٢) مَّنَّاعٖ لِّلۡخَيۡرِ مُعۡتَدٍ أَثِيمٍ

Abundant in preventing good, transgressing against the people in their wealth, dignity, and souls; abundant in sins and acts of disobedience.

(١٣) عُتُلِّۢ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ زَنِيمٍ

Harsh, hard, ascribed without relation among his people, adopted,

(١٤) أَن كَانَ ذَا مَالٖ وَبَنِينَ

Because he was a person with wealth and children, he became arrogantly unaccepting of faith in Allah and His Messenger.

(١٥) إِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَٰتُنَا قَالَ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

When Our verses are read to him, he says: This what is written from the fables of the earlier generations.

(١٦) سَنَسِمُهُۥ عَلَى ٱلۡخُرۡطُومِ

We will place a mark on his nose, which will disfigure and remain with him.

Points of Benefit:

Describing the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم with the character of the Quran.
The disbelievers' characteristics are reprehensible. The believer must avoid those characteristics and avoid obeying the people with them.
Whosoever continually swears oaths trivializes the Most Merciful, and their status declines among people.
