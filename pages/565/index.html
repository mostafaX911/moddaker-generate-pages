(١٧) إِنَّا بَلَوۡنَٰهُمۡ كَمَا بَلَوۡنَآ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ إِذۡ أَقۡسَمُواْ لَيَصۡرِمُنَّهَا مُصۡبِحِينَ

Indeed, We have tested these polytheists with drought and hunger, just as We tested the people of the garden when they swore that they would surely harvest its fruits in the morning, hastening so that no poor person would eat from them.

(١٨) وَلَا يَسۡتَثۡنُونَ

And they did not make an exception in their oath by saying: If Allah wills.

(١٩) فَطَافَ عَلَيۡهَا طَآئِفٞ مِّن رَّبِّكَ وَهُمۡ نَآئِمُونَ

So Allah sent a fire to it, and it consumed it while its owners were asleep, unable to fend off the fire from it.

(٢٠) فَأَصۡبَحَتۡ كَٱلصَّرِيمِ

So it became black like a dark night.

(٢١) فَتَنَادَوۡاْ مُصۡبِحِينَ

So they called each other in the morning,

(٢٢) أَنِ ٱغۡدُواْ عَلَىٰ حَرۡثِكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰرِمِينَ

Saying: Leave early to your crop before the destitute arrive if you are to pluck its fruits.

(٢٣) فَٱنطَلَقُواْ وَهُمۡ يَتَخَٰفَتُونَ

So they made their way to their crop quickly, talking to each other in a hushed voice.

(٢٤) أَن لَّا يَدۡخُلَنَّهَا ٱلۡيَوۡمَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّسۡكِينٞ

Saying to each other: No needy person should enter the garden upon you today.

(٢٥) وَغَدَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ حَرۡدٖ قَٰدِرِينَ

And they went at the start of the morning, determined to forbid their fruits, claiming they could do so.

(٢٦) فَلَمَّا رَأَوۡهَا قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا لَضَآلُّونَ

Then, when they saw them burnt, they said to each other: Surely, we have lost the way there.

(٢٧) بَلۡ نَحۡنُ مَحۡرُومُونَ

[No,] rather, we are prohibited from plucking its fruits by what came about from us resolving to prohibit the needy from it.

(٢٨) قَالَ أَوۡسَطُهُمۡ أَلَمۡ أَقُل لَّكُمۡ لَوۡلَا تُسَبِّحُونَ

The best of them said: Did I not tell you, when you resolved on what you resolved upon of prohibiting the needy from it: Will you not exalt Allah and repent to Him?

(٢٩) قَالُواْ سُبۡحَٰنَ رَبِّنَآ إِنَّا كُنَّا ظَٰلِمِينَ

They said: Exalted be our Lord; indeed, we were wronging ourselves when we resolved to prevent the destitute from the fruits of our garden.

(٣٠) فَأَقۡبَلَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ يَتَلَٰوَمُونَ

So they began to go back on their statements in the way of blame.

(٣١) قَالُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَآ إِنَّا كُنَّا طَٰغِينَ

Out of regret, they said: O our loss. Indeed, we were transgressing the limit by prohibiting the destitute from their rights.

(٣٢) عَسَىٰ رَبُّنَآ أَن يُبۡدِلَنَا خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهَآ إِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا رَٰغِبُونَ

Hopefully, our Lord will compensate us with what is better than the garden. Indeed, we are yearning to Allah alone. We hope for pardon from Him and seek goodness from Him.

(٣٣) كَذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡعَذَابُۖ وَلَعَذَابُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ أَكۡبَرُۚ لَوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ

Just like this torment by deprivation of provision, we torment those who disobey Us. Certainly, the punishment of the Hereafter is greater. If only they were to know its severity and permanence.

(٣٤) إِنَّ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ جَنَّٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

Indeed, by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, those who are heedful of Allah have gardens of delight near their Lord, reveling in it. Their delight will never cease.

(٣٥) أَفَنَجۡعَلُ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ كَٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

Should We make the Muslims [those who submit] like the disbelievers in terms of requital, as the polytheists of Mecca claim?!

(٣٦) مَا لَكُمۡ كَيۡفَ تَحۡكُمُونَ

What is wrong with you, O polytheists? How do you conclude this skewed, crooked conclusion?!

(٣٧) أَمۡ لَكُمۡ كِتَٰبٞ فِيهِ تَدۡرُسُونَ

Or do you have a book in which you read that the obedient and disobedient are equal?

(٣٨) إِنَّ لَكُمۡ فِيهِ لَمَا تَخَيَّرُونَ

Indeed, in that case, you would have in that book whatever you choose in the Afterlife. Nay, you do not have that.

(٣٩) أَمۡ لَكُمۡ أَيۡمَٰنٌ عَلَيۡنَا بَٰلِغَةٌ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ إِنَّ لَكُمۡ لَمَا تَحۡكُمُونَ

Or, do you have covenants incumbent upon Us, assured with oaths, that whatever you conclude for yourselves is yours?

(٤٠) سَلۡهُمۡ أَيُّهُم بِذَٰلِكَ زَعِيمٌ

Ask, O Messenger, those saying this statement: Which of them guarantees it?!

(٤١) أَمۡ لَهُمۡ شُرَكَآءُ فَلۡيَأۡتُواْ بِشُرَكَآئِهِمۡ إِن كَانُواْ صَٰدِقِينَ

Or do they have associates other than Allah who make them equal in requital with the believers? Then let them bring forth these associates of theirs if they are truthful in their claim that they have made them equal in requital with the believers.

(٤٢) يَوۡمَ يُكۡشَفُ عَن سَاقٖ وَيُدۡعَوۡنَ إِلَى ٱلسُّجُودِ فَلَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ

Horror will appear on the Day of Resurrection, and our Lord will uncover His Shin. The people will be called to prostrate, so the believers will prostrate, and the disbelievers and hypocrites will remain unable to.

Points of Benefit:

Preventing the rights of the poor leads to destroying one's wealth.
Hastening the punishment in this world is out of desiring good for the slave so that he repents and returns.
The believer and disbeliever do not have the same recompense, just as their characteristics are not the same.
