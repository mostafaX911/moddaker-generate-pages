(٤٣) خَٰشِعَةً أَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ تَرۡهَقُهُمۡ ذِلَّةٞۖ وَقَدۡ كَانُواْ يُدۡعَوۡنَ إِلَى ٱلسُّجُودِ وَهُمۡ سَٰلِمُونَ

Their eyes debased, lowliness and regret enveloping them. In the life of this world, it had been requested from them that they prostrate to Allah while they were free from what they are in today [The Day of Resurrection].

(٤٤) فَذَرۡنِي وَمَن يُكَذِّبُ بِهَٰذَا ٱلۡحَدِيثِۖ سَنَسۡتَدۡرِجُهُم مِّنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ

So, O Messenger, leave Me and whoever denies this Quran that has been revealed to you. We will drive them to the torment, little by little, from where they do not realize that it is a method of leading them on gradually and drawing out their destruction.

(٤٥) وَأُمۡلِي لَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ كَيۡدِي مَتِينٌ

And I give them respite for a period so that they go on in their sin. Indeed, My plan for the people of disbelief and rejection is firm, so they will not escape Me nor be safe from My punishment.

(٤٦) أَمۡ تَسۡ‍َٔلُهُمۡ أَجۡرٗا فَهُم مِّن مَّغۡرَمٖ مُّثۡقَلُونَ

Do you seek, O Messenger, reward for what you invite them towards so they take on a significant undertaking because of that, and hence this is why they shun you? The reality is the opposite, for you do not seek reward from them. So what prevents them from following you?

(٤٧) أَمۡ عِندَهُمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبُ فَهُمۡ يَكۡتُبُونَ

Or do they know the unseen, so they write what appeals to them of arguments that they argue against you with?

(٤٨) فَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تَكُن كَصَاحِبِ ٱلۡحُوتِ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ وَهُوَ مَكۡظُومٞ

So be patient, O Messenger, to what your Lord has decided of drawing out their destruction by [granting them] respite. And do not be like the companion of the whale, Jonah (peace be upon him), in annoyance with his people, when he called his Lord as he was distressed in the darkness of the sea and the darkness of the whale's stomach.

(٤٩) لَّوۡلَآ أَن تَدَٰرَكَهُۥ نِعۡمَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ لَنُبِذَ بِٱلۡعَرَآءِ وَهُوَ مَذۡمُومٞ

Had the mercy of Allah not reached him, the whale would have cast him upon barren land while being blameworthy.

(٥٠) فَٱجۡتَبَٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَجَعَلَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

So his Lord chose him. Thus, He made him one of His righteous slaves.

(٥١) وَإِن يَكَادُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَيُزۡلِقُونَكَ بِأَبۡصَٰرِهِمۡ لَمَّا سَمِعُواْ ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَيَقُولُونَ إِنَّهُۥ لَمَجۡنُونٞ

Indeed, those who disbelieved in Allah and rejected His Messenger could almost drop you with their eyes due to how severe their stares are at you when they hear this Quran revealed to you. And they say, in adherence to their desires and turning away from the truth: Indeed, the Messenger who has brought it is certainly a madman.

(٥٢) وَمَا هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرٞ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

The Quran that has been revealed unto you is but an admonition and a reminder to humans and Jinn.

The Reality (Surah Al-Haqqah)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Affirming that the occurrence of the Resurrection and the recompense on [that day] is true, without a doubt.

Explanation:

(١) ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ

Allah mentions the hour of the resurrection that is incumbent on everyone.

(٢) مَا ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ

Then He magnifies its importance by asking this question: What is al-Haqqah (the Calamity)?

(٣) وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ

And what will inform you what this Al-Haqqah is?

(٤) كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ وَعَادُۢ بِٱلۡقَارِعَةِ

Thamūd, the people of Saleh, and 'Ād, the people of Hūd, denied the Day of Resurrection that will make people's hair fall out due to the severity of its horrors.

(٥) فَأَمَّا ثَمُودُ فَأُهۡلِكُواْ بِٱلطَّاغِيَةِ

So as for Thamūd, Allah certainly destroyed them with the scream that reached the pinnacle of severity and horror.

(٦) وَأَمَّا عَادٞ فَأُهۡلِكُواْ بِرِيحٖ صَرۡصَرٍ عَاتِيَةٖ

And as for 'Ād, Allah destroyed them with a severe, cold wind with a harsh, vexing sound that reached the pinnacle of harshness upon them.

(٧) سَخَّرَهَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ سَبۡعَ لَيَالٖ وَثَمَٰنِيَةَ أَيَّامٍ حُسُومٗاۖ فَتَرَى ٱلۡقَوۡمَ فِيهَا صَرۡعَىٰ كَأَنَّهُمۡ أَعۡجَازُ نَخۡلٍ خَاوِيَةٖ

Allah sent it upon them for seven nights and eight days, destroying every last one of them. So you would see the people destroyed in their homes, thrown onto the ground as if, after their destruction, they were worn-out date palm trunks fallen upon the ground.

(٨) فَهَلۡ تَرَىٰ لَهُم مِّنۢ بَاقِيَةٖ

So do you see any remaining soul from them after what befell them of torment?

Points of Benefit:

Patience is a praiseworthy characteristic incumbent upon the callers [to Allah] and others.
Repentance eradicates that which precedes it and is one of the reasons that Allah selects the slave and makes him among His righteous slaves.
The different types of torment that Allah sends upon the disbelievers and the disobedient show the perfection of His ability and justice.
