(٩) وَجَآءَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ وَمَن قَبۡلَهُۥ وَٱلۡمُؤۡتَفِكَٰتُ بِٱلۡخَاطِئَةِ

And Pharaoh, the nations before him, and the towns that were afflicted by being flipped upside down, the people of Lot, [all] committed the wrongful acts of ascribing partners with Allah and other sins.

(١٠) فَعَصَوۡاْ رَسُولَ رَبِّهِمۡ فَأَخَذَهُمۡ أَخۡذَةٗ رَّابِيَةً

Each of them disobeyed their messenger, who was sent to them, and rejected him, so Allah seized them with a seizure in excess of what would complete their destruction.

(١١) إِنَّا لَمَّا طَغَا ٱلۡمَآءُ حَمَلۡنَٰكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡجَارِيَةِ

Indeed, when the water exceeded its limit in height, We carried those whom you were in their loins [i.e., your ancestors] cruising on the Ark that Noah (peace be upon him) built based on Our command, so it became a means of carrying you.

(١٢) لِنَجۡعَلَهَا لَكُمۡ تَذۡكِرَةٗ وَتَعِيَهَآ أُذُنٞ وَٰعِيَةٞ

So that We make the ark and its story an admonition through which the destruction of the people of disbelief and the salvation of the people of faith could be evidenced, and [so] that retentive ears could retain what they hear.

(١٣) فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ نَفۡخَةٞ وَٰحِدَةٞ

Then, when the appointed angel blows one blowing into the trumpet, it being the second blowing.

(١٤) وَحُمِلَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَٱلۡجِبَالُ فَدُكَّتَا دَكَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ

And the Earth and mountains are raised, then crushed [with] one severe blow that disperses the pieces of the Earth and the pieces of its mountains.

(١٥) فَيَوۡمَئِذٖ وَقَعَتِ ٱلۡوَاقِعَةُ

So on the Day when all that happens, the Resurrection will occur.

(١٦) وَٱنشَقَّتِ ٱلسَّمَآءُ فَهِيَ يَوۡمَئِذٖ وَاهِيَةٞ

And the sky will split on that Day because of the angels descending from it, for on that Day, it will be weak after it had been strong and held together.

(١٧) وَٱلۡمَلَكُ عَلَىٰٓ أَرۡجَآئِهَاۚ وَيَحۡمِلُ عَرۡشَ رَبِّكَ فَوۡقَهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ ثَمَٰنِيَةٞ

The angels will be on its edges and sides. And eight angels close [to Allah] will carry the throne of your Lord on that great Day.

(١٨) يَوۡمَئِذٖ تُعۡرَضُونَ لَا تَخۡفَىٰ مِنكُمۡ خَافِيَةٞ

On that Day, you will be presented, O people, before Allah. No concealed matters of yours are hidden from Allah, whatever they might be. Rather, Allah knows and observes it.

(١٩) فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ هَآؤُمُ ٱقۡرَءُواْ كِتَٰبِيَهۡ

As for the one given the book of his deeds in his right [hand], he will say out of happiness and joy: Take and read the book of my deeds.

(٢٠) إِنِّي ظَنَنتُ أَنِّي مُلَٰقٍ حِسَابِيَهۡ

Indeed, I knew and was convinced in the worldly life that I would be resurrected and meeting my reward.

(٢١) فَهُوَ فِي عِيشَةٖ رَّاضِيَةٖ

So he [will] be in a contented state of life due to what he sees of endless delight.

(٢٢) فِي جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٖ

In a paradise that is elevated in position and rank.

(٢٣) قُطُوفُهَا دَانِيَةٞ

Its fruits are close to whoever grasps them.

(٢٤) كُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ هَنِيٓ‍َٔۢا بِمَآ أَسۡلَفۡتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَيَّامِ ٱلۡخَالِيَةِ

In hospitality, it will be said to them: Eat and drink food and drink containing no harm because of what you put forth of good deeds in the days passed in the worldly life.

(٢٥) وَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِشِمَالِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي لَمۡ أُوتَ كِتَٰبِيَهۡ

And as for the one who is given the book of his deeds in his left [hand], then he will say, from the intensity of regret: O, if only I had not been given the book of my deeds for what it contains of evil deeds warranting my anguish.

(٢٦) وَلَمۡ أَدۡرِ مَا حِسَابِيَهۡ

And O, if only I did not know what my account would be.

(٢٧) يَٰلَيۡتَهَا كَانَتِ ٱلۡقَاضِيَةَ

If only the death which I died was the death after which I would never be revived.

(٢٨) مَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنِّي مَالِيَهۡۜ

My wealth did not protect me at all from Allah's torment.

(٢٩) هَلَكَ عَنِّي سُلۡطَٰنِيَهۡ

My argument has deserted me, as well as what I used to rely on of strength and prestige.

(٣٠) خُذُوهُ فَغُلُّوهُ

And it will be said: Take him, O angels, and shackle his hands to his neck.

(٣١) ثُمَّ ٱلۡجَحِيمَ صَلُّوهُ

Then put him into the Fire so that he suffers its heat.

(٣٢) ثُمَّ فِي سِلۡسِلَةٖ ذَرۡعُهَا سَبۡعُونَ ذِرَاعٗا فَٱسۡلُكُوهُ

Then fasten him with a chain seventy cubits long.

(٣٣) إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

Indeed, he would not believe in Allah the Great.

(٣٤) وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينِ

And he would not encourage others to feed the needy.

(٣٥) فَلَيۡسَ لَهُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ هَٰهُنَا حَمِيمٞ

So on the Day of Resurrection, he will have no relative to defend him from the torment.

Points of Benefit:

The favor upon the parent is a favor upon the child and deserves gratitude.
Feeding the needy and encouraging it are among the causes of protection from the torment of the Fire.
The intensity of the Day of Resurrection's torment necessitates protection from it through belief and righteous deeds.
