(٣٦) وَلَا طَعَامٌ إِلَّا مِنۡ غِسۡلِينٖ

And he will not have any food to eat except the secretions of the bodies of the people of the Fire.

(٣٧) لَّا يَأۡكُلُهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡخَٰطِ‍ُٔونَ

None will eat that food except the people who sin and act disobediently.

(٣٨) فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِمَا تُبۡصِرُونَ

Allah swore by what you see.

(٣٩) وَمَا لَا تُبۡصِرُونَ

And swore by what you do not see.

(٤٠) إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلُ رَسُولٖ كَرِيمٖ

Indeed, the Quran is surely the speech of Allah. His noble Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم recites it upon the people.

(٤١) وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوۡلِ شَاعِرٖۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تُؤۡمِنُونَ

And it is not the saying of a poet because it is not structured as a poem. Little do you believe.

(٤٢) وَلَا بِقَوۡلِ كَاهِنٖۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ

And it is not the saying of a soothsayer, for the speech of the soothsayers is a different matter than this Quran. Little do you realize.

(٤٣) تَنزِيلٞ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Rather, it is sent down by the Lord of all creation.

(٤٤) وَلَوۡ تَقَوَّلَ عَلَيۡنَا بَعۡضَ ٱلۡأَقَاوِيلِ

And if Muhammad fabricated some statements about Us that We did not say,

(٤٥) لَأَخَذۡنَا مِنۡهُ بِٱلۡيَمِينِ

We would have surely taken vengeance upon him and seized him with Our strength and ability.

(٤٦) ثُمَّ لَقَطَعۡنَا مِنۡهُ ٱلۡوَتِينَ

Then we would surely sever the vein connected to his heart.

(٤٧) فَمَا مِنكُم مِّنۡ أَحَدٍ عَنۡهُ حَٰجِزِينَ

And there would not be any among you who could prevent Us from him.

(٤٨) وَإِنَّهُۥ لَتَذۡكِرَةٞ لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ

And indeed, the Quran is surely an admonition for those heedful of their Lord by complying with his commands and avoiding his prohibitions.

(٤٩) وَإِنَّا لَنَعۡلَمُ أَنَّ مِنكُم مُّكَذِّبِينَ

And indeed, We surely know that among you are those who lie about this Quran.

(٥٠) وَإِنَّهُۥ لَحَسۡرَةٌ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

And indeed, the rejection of the Quran is surely a great regret on the Day of Resurrection.

(٥١) وَإِنَّهُۥ لَحَقُّ ٱلۡيَقِينِ

And indeed, the Quran is surely the truth of certainty in which there is no dispute and no doubt that it is from Allah.

(٥٢) فَسَبِّحۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

So, O Messenger, exalt your Lord above what does not befit Him and mention the name of your Great Lord.

The Ascending Stairways (Surah Al-Ma'arij)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing the condition of the creation on the Day of Resurrection and their recompense.

Explanation:

(١) سَأَلَ سَآئِلُۢ بِعَذَابٖ وَاقِعٖ

A supplicator among the polytheists supplicated for a torment upon himself and his people if this torment were to occur. He was mocking, but it will manifest on the Day of Resurrection,

(٢) لِّلۡكَٰفِرِينَ لَيۡسَ لَهُۥ دَافِعٞ

Upon the disbelievers in Allah. There is no one who can repel this torment,

(٣) مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ ذِي ٱلۡمَعَارِجِ

From Allah, the possessor of loftiness, degrees, virtues, and bounties.

(٤) تَعۡرُجُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَٱلرُّوحُ إِلَيۡهِ فِي يَوۡمٖ كَانَ مِقۡدَارُهُۥ خَمۡسِينَ أَلۡفَ سَنَةٖ

The angels and Gabriel rise to Him in those degrees on the Day of Resurrection. And it is a long day. Its length is fifty thousand years.

(٥) فَٱصۡبِرۡ صَبۡرٗا جَمِيلًا

So be patient, O Messenger, with patience exhibiting no distress or complaint.

(٦) إِنَّهُمۡ يَرَوۡنَهُۥ بَعِيدٗا

Indeed, they see this torment as far-fetched and impossible to occur.

(٧) وَنَرَىٰهُ قَرِيبٗا

And We see it close, definitely occurring.

(٨) يَوۡمَ تَكُونُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ كَٱلۡمُهۡلِ

The Day when the sky will be like molten copper, gold, and other [metals].

(٩) وَتَكُونُ ٱلۡجِبَالُ كَٱلۡعِهۡنِ

And the mountains will be like wool in lightness.

(١٠) وَلَا يَسۡ‍َٔلُ حَمِيمٌ حَمِيمٗا

And relatives will not ask each other about their condition because each one will be preoccupied with themselves.

Points of Benefit:

Vindicating the Quran from [being] poetry or soothsaying.
The danger of making up and fabricating about Allah, the Transcendent.
Beautiful patience is that in which Allah's reward is sought and not complained about to anyone other than Him.
