(١١) يُبَصَّرُونَهُمۡۚ يَوَدُّ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُ لَوۡ يَفۡتَدِي مِنۡ عَذَابِ يَوۡمِئِذِۢ بِبَنِيهِ

Every human will witness their relative; they will not be hidden from them. Still, no one will ask anyone [about their condition] because of the horror of the situation. The one deserving of the Fire will wish to offer his children for the torment instead of himself.

(١٢) وَصَٰحِبَتِهِۦ وَأَخِيهِ

And ransom his wife and brother.

(١٣) وَفَصِيلَتِهِ ٱلَّتِي تُ‍ٔۡوِيهِ

And ransom his close associates who stand with him during adversity.

(١٤) وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا ثُمَّ يُنجِيهِ

And ransom all who is on the Earth, of humans, Jinn, or others [so that] then that ransom would save and rescue him from the torment of the Fire.

(١٥) كَلَّآۖ إِنَّهَا لَظَىٰ

The matter is not as this criminal hopes. Indeed, it is the Fire of the Hereafter, flaming and burning.

(١٦) نَزَّاعَةٗ لِّلشَّوَىٰ

It severely splits the skin on the head from the intensity of its heat and flames.

(١٧) تَدۡعُواْ مَنۡ أَدۡبَرَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ

It calls him who turned away from the truth, distanced himself from it, did not believe in it, and did not act,

(١٨) وَجَمَعَ فَأَوۡعَىٰٓ

[Who] collected his wealth and was stingy in spending from it in the way of Allah.

(١٩) ۞إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ خُلِقَ هَلُوعًا

Indeed, the human being was created with great eagerness.

(٢٠) إِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلشَّرُّ جَزُوعٗا

If harm inflicts him from illness or poverty, he is impatient.

(٢١) وَإِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلۡخَيۡرُ مَنُوعًا

And if what is pleasing occurs to him, of luxury and sufficiency, he is very resistant to spending it in the way of Allah.

(٢٢) إِلَّا ٱلۡمُصَلِّينَ

Except for the praying ones, for they are free of those lowly attributes,

(٢٣) ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمۡ دَآئِمُونَ

Those who are consistent with their prayers, not preoccupied from them, and perform them within their specified times.

(٢٤) وَٱلَّذِينَ فِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ حَقّٞ مَّعۡلُومٞ

And those who have an obligatory specified portion in their wealth,

(٢٥) لِّلسَّآئِلِ وَٱلۡمَحۡرُومِ

Which they pay to he who asks them and he who does not ask them from those who have been prevented from provision for any reason.

(٢٦) وَٱلَّذِينَ يُصَدِّقُونَ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ

And those who affirm the Day of Resurrection, the Day Allah will recompense everyone with what they deserve.

(٢٧) وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم مِّنۡ عَذَابِ رَبِّهِم مُّشۡفِقُونَ

And those who fear torment from their Lord, despite what they put forth of righteous deeds.

(٢٨) إِنَّ عَذَابَ رَبِّهِمۡ غَيۡرُ مَأۡمُونٖ

Indeed, the torment of their Lord is frightening. No rational person feels secure from it.

(٢٩) وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِفُرُوجِهِمۡ حَٰفِظُونَ

And those who protect their private parts by covering them and keeping them away from obscenities.

(٣٠) إِلَّا عَلَىٰٓ أَزۡوَٰجِهِمۡ أَوۡ مَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُهُمۡ فَإِنَّهُمۡ غَيۡرُ مَلُومِينَ

Except from their wives or what they own of slave girls, then they are not blameworthy in enjoying them with intercourse and what is less than it.

(٣١) فَمَنِ ٱبۡتَغَىٰ وَرَآءَ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡعَادُونَ

So whoever seeks enjoyment [with their private parts] with other than what was mentioned of wives and slave girls, then those are the ones transgressing the bounds of Allah.

(٣٢) وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِأَمَٰنَٰتِهِمۡ وَعَهۡدِهِمۡ رَٰعُونَ

And those maintaining their pledges that they pledged to people and what they were entrusted with of wealth, secrets, and otherwise. They do not betray [in] their trusts and do not break their pledges.

(٣٣) وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِشَهَٰدَٰتِهِمۡ قَآئِمُونَ

And those who maintain their testimonies in the appropriate manner such that they are not affected by kinship or enmity.

(٣٤) وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمۡ يُحَافِظُونَ

And those who maintain their prayers by performing them in their times in a purified state with tranquility, nothing busying them from it.

(٣٥) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ فِي جَنَّٰتٖ مُّكۡرَمُونَ

Those described with those attributes will be honored in gardens by attaining everlasting bliss and beholding the noble Face of Allah.

(٣٦) فَمَالِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ قِبَلَكَ مُهۡطِعِينَ

What is it, O Messenger, that has led these polytheists within your nation to rush to accuse you of being a liar?

(٣٧) عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ وَعَنِ ٱلشِّمَالِ عِزِينَ

Surrounding you from your right and your left, groups upon groups.

(٣٨) أَيَطۡمَعُ كُلُّ ٱمۡرِيٕٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ أَن يُدۡخَلَ جَنَّةَ نَعِيمٖ

Does every one of them hope that Allah will enter him into the Garden of Delight, relishing in what is in it of permanent delight while he is remaining in his [state of] disbelief?!

(٣٩) كَلَّآۖ إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰهُم مِّمَّا يَعۡلَمُونَ

The matter is not as they envision. Indeed, we created them from what they know, for we have created them from base water, so they are weak, not possessing benefit or harm for themselves. Then how do they become arrogant?!

(٤٠) فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِرَبِّ ٱلۡمَشَٰرِقِ وَٱلۡمَغَٰرِبِ إِنَّا لَقَٰدِرُونَ

Allah the Exalted swore by Himself, the Lord of the East and the West, the Sun and the Moon, and all of the stars: Indeed, We are certainly able,

Points of Benefit:

The severity of the Fire's torment such that the people of the Fire will wish to be saved from it by all the means they used to know from the means of the worldly life.
Prayer is among the greatest expiations of wrongdoings in this world and protects from the Fire in the Hereafter.
Fear of Allah's torment is a motivation for righteous deeds.
