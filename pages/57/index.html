(٥٣) رَبَّنَآ ءَامَنَّا بِمَآ أَنزَلۡتَ وَٱتَّبَعۡنَا ٱلرَّسُولَ فَٱكۡتُبۡنَا مَعَ ٱلشَّٰهِدِينَ

The disciples also said: O our Lord, we have believed in the Gospel that you revealed, and we have followed the Messenger, Jesus (peace be upon him), so make us of those who are witnesses to the truth and who believe in You and Your messengers.

(٥٤) وَمَكَرُواْ وَمَكَرَ ٱللَّهُۖ وَٱللَّهُ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡمَٰكِرِينَ

The disbelievers from the Israelites plotted to kill Jesus (peace be upon him), so Allah plotted against them and left them in their misguidance. He made another person resemble Jesus (peace be upon him) when they tried to kill him. Allah is the best of plotters because nothing can be more severe than His plot against His enemies.

(٥٥) إِذۡ قَالَ ٱللَّهُ يَٰعِيسَىٰٓ إِنِّي مُتَوَفِّيكَ وَرَافِعُكَ إِلَيَّ وَمُطَهِّرُكَ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَجَاعِلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوكَ فَوۡقَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۖ ثُمَّ إِلَيَّ مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ فَأَحۡكُمُ بَيۡنَكُمۡ فِيمَا كُنتُمۡ فِيهِ تَخۡتَلِفُونَ

Allah also plotted against them by saying to Jesus: O Jesus, I will take you away alive, raise your body and soul to Me, rid you of the filth of those who disbelieved you, and distance you from them. I will make those who follow you be upon the true religion, which includes acceptance of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, be above those who disbelieve you until the Day of Resurrection in proof and honor. Then to Me alone will be your return on the Day of Resurrection, and I will pass judgment in truth between you regarding your differences.

(٥٦) فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فَأُعَذِّبُهُمۡ عَذَابٗا شَدِيدٗا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَمَا لَهُم مِّن نَّٰصِرِينَ

As for those who disbelieved in you and the truth you brought to them, I will torment them severely in this life by being killed, imprisoned, and disgraced, and in the Hereafter, I will torment them in the Fire. They will have no one to help them against that torment.

(٥٧) وَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ فَيُوَفِّيهِمۡ أُجُورَهُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

As for those who had faith in you and the truth that you came with and performed righteous deeds (such as prayer, charity, fasting, keeping family relations, etc.), Allah will grant them the reward of their actions in full without any reduction. This verse refers to the Messiah's followers before the advent of the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, whose emergence the Messiah confirmed himself. Allah does not love those who transgress, and among the greatest forms of transgression is to ascribe partners to Allah and to reject His messengers.

(٥٨) ذَٰلِكَ نَتۡلُوهُ عَلَيۡكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ وَٱلذِّكۡرِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ

This account of Jesus We recited to you indicates the authenticity of what was revealed to you. Therefore, it reminds those who fear Allah as it contains no falsehood.

(٥٩) إِنَّ مَثَلَ عِيسَىٰ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ ءَادَمَۖ خَلَقَهُۥ مِن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ قَالَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ

The creation of Jesus (peace be upon him) with Allah is similar to the creation of Adam, who was created from dust without a father or mother. Allah said to him: Be a human, and he came into existence just as Allah willed. How do they then claim that Jesus is a deity because he has no father, while they affirm that Adam is human despite having no father or mother?

(٦٠) ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكَ فَلَا تَكُن مِّنَ ٱلۡمُمۡتَرِينَ

The truth about Jesus, wherein there is no doubt, is revealed to you by your Lord, so do not be one of those who harbor doubt and are skeptical. Instead, be firm on the truth that you are upon.

(٦١) فَمَنۡ حَآجَّكَ فِيهِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ فَقُلۡ تَعَالَوۡاْ نَدۡعُ أَبۡنَآءَنَا وَأَبۡنَآءَكُمۡ وَنِسَآءَنَا وَنِسَآءَكُمۡ وَأَنفُسَنَا وَأَنفُسَكُمۡ ثُمَّ نَبۡتَهِلۡ فَنَجۡعَل لَّعۡنَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ

O Messenger, if any of the Christians from Najran dispute with you regarding the matter of Jesus and claim that he was not Allah's slave after the correct knowledge has come to you, then say to them: Come, let us call all our sons, all of our womenfolk, all of ourselves; and let us gather together in one place and then invoke the curse of Allah upon the liars.

Points of Benefit:

From the perfection of His power, He, the Exalted, is capable of punishing those who plot against His religion and His allies; He plots against them as they plot.
The explanation of the correct belief that is obligatory regarding Jesus (peace be upon him) and the clarification that his creation is not extraordinary, for Adam, created without a father or mother, is more astonishing, and everyone believes he is human.
The legitimacy of Mubahalah (invoking a curse upon the other) between disputants, with its conditions as determined by scholars, based on the description mentioned in the Quranic verse.
