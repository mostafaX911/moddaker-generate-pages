(٤١) عَلَىٰٓ أَن نُّبَدِّلَ خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمَسۡبُوقِينَ

To replace them with others, from those who obey Allah, and destroy them. We are not incapable of that, and we are not defeated when we wish for their destruction and replacement with others.

(٤٢) فَذَرۡهُمۡ يَخُوضُواْ وَيَلۡعَبُواْ حَتَّىٰ يُلَٰقُواْ يَوۡمَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي يُوعَدُونَ

So leave them, O Messenger, to delve into what they are upon of falsehood and misguidance and frolic during their lives until they encounter the Day of Resurrection, which they were promised in the Quran.

(٤٣) يَوۡمَ يَخۡرُجُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَجۡدَاثِ سِرَاعٗا كَأَنَّهُمۡ إِلَىٰ نُصُبٖ يُوفِضُونَ

The Day they will quickly come out of their graves as if racing to a marker.

(٤٤) خَٰشِعَةً أَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ تَرۡهَقُهُمۡ ذِلَّةٞۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمُ ٱلَّذِي كَانُواْ يُوعَدُونَ

Their eyes will be humble, and debasement will shroud them. That is the Day they were promised in the worldly life, and they were unconcerned about it.

Noah (Surah Nuh)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Demonstrating the methodology of calling to Allah through the story of Noah.

Explanation:

(١) إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ أَنۡ أَنذِرۡ قَوۡمَكَ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Indeed, we sent Noah to his people, calling them to alarm them before a painful torment would come to them as a result of what they were upon of shirk (associating partners) with Allah.

(٢) قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ إِنِّي لَكُمۡ نَذِيرٞ مُّبِينٌ

Noah said to his people: O my people, indeed I am a warner to you, clearly warning of a torment awaiting you if you do not repent to Allah.

(٣) أَنِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱتَّقُوهُ وَأَطِيعُونِ

And my warning to you obliges that I tell you: Worship Allah alone, do not associate anything as partners with Him, take heed of Him by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, and obey me in what He commanded you with.

(٤) يَغۡفِرۡ لَكُم مِّن ذُنُوبِكُمۡ وَيُؤَخِّرۡكُمۡ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمًّىۚ إِنَّ أَجَلَ ٱللَّهِ إِذَا جَآءَ لَا يُؤَخَّرُۚ لَوۡ كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

Indeed, if you do so, Allah will forgive from your sins those that do not relate to the rights of the slaves [the creation], and He will extend the life of your nation to a time specified within Allah's knowledge, to dwell upon the Earth as long as you are upright upon that. Indeed, death can not be postponed when it comes. If you knew, you would have rushed to faith in Allah and repentance from what you are upon of shirk and misguidance.

(٥) قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي دَعَوۡتُ قَوۡمِي لَيۡلٗا وَنَهَارٗا

Noah said: O my Lord, indeed, I called my people to Your worship and Your tawheed (exclusion in worship) continuously, night and day.

(٦) فَلَمۡ يَزِدۡهُمۡ دُعَآءِيٓ إِلَّا فِرَارٗا

And my call did not increase them except in fleeing and distance from what I call them to.

(٧) وَإِنِّي كُلَّمَا دَعَوۡتُهُمۡ لِتَغۡفِرَ لَهُمۡ جَعَلُوٓاْ أَصَٰبِعَهُمۡ فِيٓ ءَاذَانِهِمۡ وَٱسۡتَغۡشَوۡاْ ثِيَابَهُمۡ وَأَصَرُّواْ وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ ٱسۡتِكۡبَارٗا

And indeed, each time I call them to will cause the forgiveness of their sins, which is worshipping You alone, obedience to You, and obedience to Your messenger; they close their ears with their fingers to prevent hearing my call, cover their faces with their clothes so as not to see me, persist in the polytheism they are upon, and become arrogant of accepting and yielding to what I call them to.

(٨) ثُمَّ إِنِّي دَعَوۡتُهُمۡ جِهَارٗا

Then, indeed, my Lord, I called them openly.

(٩) ثُمَّ إِنِّيٓ أَعۡلَنتُ لَهُمۡ وَأَسۡرَرۡتُ لَهُمۡ إِسۡرَارٗا

Then, I raised my voice in the call. I also acted in concealed secretness and called them in a low voice, varying the approaches of my call to them.

(١٠) فَقُلۡتُ ٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ غَفَّارٗا

So I told them: O my people, ask for forgiveness from your Lord by repenting to him. Indeed, He (may He be Exalted) is oft-forgiving to the sins of those among his slaves who repent to Him.

Points of Benefit:

The danger of being oblivious to the Hereafter.
Worship and heedfulness of Allah are causes of the forgiveness of sins.
Persistence in calling to Allah and varying its techniques is a right that is incumbent upon the callers.
