(١١) يُرۡسِلِ ٱلسَّمَآءَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّدۡرَارٗا

For indeed, if you do that, Allah will continuously send down the rain upon you whenever you need it, so no drought will befall you.

(١٢) وَيُمۡدِدۡكُم بِأَمۡوَٰلٖ وَبَنِينَ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ جَنَّٰتٖ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ أَنۡهَٰرٗا

And He will give you wealth and children in abundance and produce gardens for you to eat the fruits from and rivers from which you can drink and provide water to your crops and livestock.

(١٣) مَّا لَكُمۡ لَا تَرۡجُونَ لِلَّهِ وَقَارٗا

What is your issue, my people, [such that] you do not fear the greatness of Allah when you disobey Him without any concern?!

(١٤) وَقَدۡ خَلَقَكُمۡ أَطۡوَارًا

While He had created you stage after stage from a drop, then a clot of blood, then a piece of flesh.

(١٥) أَلَمۡ تَرَوۡاْ كَيۡفَ خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ سَبۡعَ سَمَٰوَٰتٖ طِبَاقٗا

Have you not seen how Allah created seven heavens, one above the other?

(١٦) وَجَعَلَ ٱلۡقَمَرَ فِيهِنَّ نُورٗا وَجَعَلَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ سِرَاجٗا

And He made the Moon in the lowest heaven as a light for the people of the Earth, and He made the Sun luminous.

(١٧) وَٱللَّهُ أَنۢبَتَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ نَبَاتٗا

And Allah created you from the Earth by creating your father Adam from dust. Then you are nourished by what it grows for you.

(١٨) ثُمَّ يُعِيدُكُمۡ فِيهَا وَيُخۡرِجُكُمۡ إِخۡرَاجٗا

Then He returns you to it after your death. Then He brings you out of it for the resurrection.

(١٩) وَٱللَّهُ جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِسَاطٗا

And Allah spread the Earth out for you and made it ready to live on.

(٢٠) لِّتَسۡلُكُواْ مِنۡهَا سُبُلٗا فِجَاجٗا

In the hope that you would traverse wide paths through it in pursuit of permissible income.

(٢١) قَالَ نُوحٞ رَّبِّ إِنَّهُمۡ عَصَوۡنِي وَٱتَّبَعُواْ مَن لَّمۡ يَزِدۡهُ مَالُهُۥ وَوَلَدُهُۥٓ إِلَّا خَسَارٗا

Noah said: O my Lord, indeed, my people have disobeyed me in what I commanded them with of your Tawheed and worshipping you alone. The lowly ones among them followed their leaders, whom you have bestowed bounty upon with wealth and progeny, so what you bestowed upon them did not increase them except in misguidance.

(٢٢) وَمَكَرُواْ مَكۡرٗا كُبَّارٗا

And the seniors among them deceitfully plotted a great plot by provoking those under them against Noah.

(٢٣) وَقَالُواْ لَا تَذَرُنَّ ءَالِهَتَكُمۡ وَلَا تَذَرُنَّ وَدّٗا وَلَا سُوَاعٗا وَلَا يَغُوثَ وَيَعُوقَ وَنَسۡرٗا

And they said to their followers: Do not leave the worship of your gods. Do not leave the worship of your idols, Wadd, Suwaa', Yaghuth, Ya'uq, and Nasr.

(٢٤) وَقَدۡ أَضَلُّواْ كَثِيرٗاۖ وَلَا تَزِدِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا ضَلَٰلٗا

They certainly misguided many people with these idols of theirs. Do not, O Lord, increase those wronging themselves by persisting upon disbelief and acts of disobedience, except in misguidance from the truth.

(٢٥) مِّمَّا خَطِيٓـَٰٔتِهِمۡ أُغۡرِقُواْ فَأُدۡخِلُواْ نَارٗا فَلَمۡ يَجِدُواْ لَهُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَنصَارٗا

Because of the misdeeds they undertook, they were drowned by the flood in this world. So they were put into the Fire immediately after their death. Then they did not find in those other than Allah [any] supporters saving them from the drowning or the Fire.

(٢٦) وَقَالَ نُوحٞ رَّبِّ لَا تَذَرۡ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ دَيَّارًا

And when Allah informed him that no one from his people would believe except those who had believed, Noah said: O my Lord, do not leave anyone among the disbelievers [to] go around or move.

(٢٧) إِنَّكَ إِن تَذَرۡهُمۡ يُضِلُّواْ عِبَادَكَ وَلَا يَلِدُوٓاْ إِلَّا فَاجِرٗا كَفَّارٗا

Indeed, our Lord, if You leave them and give them respite, they will misguide Your believing slaves, and they would not give birth except to a person of wickedness who does not obey You, severe in disbelief, not thanking You for Your bounties.

(٢٨) رَّبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيَّ وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيۡتِيَ مُؤۡمِنٗا وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۖ وَلَا تَزِدِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا تَبَارَۢا

My Lord, forgive me my sins, forgive my parents, forgive whoever enters my household as a believer, forgive the male and female believers, and do not increase those wronging themselves with disbelief and acts of disobedience except in destruction and loss.

Points of Benefit:

Seeking forgiveness is a cause for rainfall, as well as abundant wealth and children.
The role of leaders in the misguidance of subordinates is apparent and witnessed.
Sins are a cause of destruction in the worldly life and torment in the Hereafter.
