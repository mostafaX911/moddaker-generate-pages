The Jinn (Surah Al-Jinn)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Invalidating the religion of the polytheists by describing the state of the Jinn and their faith upon hearing the Quran.

Explanation:

(١) قُلۡ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ أَنَّهُ ٱسۡتَمَعَ نَفَرٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا سَمِعۡنَا قُرۡءَانًا عَجَبٗا

Say, O Messenger, to your nation: Allah revealed to me that a group of the Jinn listened to my recitation of the Quran at Batn Nakhlah. So when they returned to their people, they said to them: Indeed, we heard recited speech, impressive in its articulation and eloquence.

(٢) يَهۡدِيٓ إِلَى ٱلرُّشۡدِ فَ‍َٔامَنَّا بِهِۦۖ وَلَن نُّشۡرِكَ بِرَبِّنَآ أَحَدٗا

This speech we heard directs to what is right in creed, word, and action. So we have believed in it and will not associate anyone as partners with our Lord, who sent it down.

(٣) وَأَنَّهُۥ تَعَٰلَىٰ جَدُّ رَبِّنَا مَا ٱتَّخَذَ صَٰحِبَةٗ وَلَا وَلَدٗا

And we have believed that He, exalted be our Lord's greatness and grandeur, did not take a wife nor child as the polytheists say.

(٤) وَأَنَّهُۥ كَانَ يَقُولُ سَفِيهُنَا عَلَى ٱللَّهِ شَطَطٗا

And that it was Iblis who would say a crooked statement about Allah by attributing a wife and child to Him, may He be Exalted.

(٥) وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّآ أَن لَّن تَقُولَ ٱلۡإِنسُ وَٱلۡجِنُّ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبٗا

And that we presumed that the polytheists of the humans and Jinn were not lying when they claimed that He had a wife and child. So we believed what they said, blindly following them.

(٦) وَأَنَّهُۥ كَانَ رِجَالٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡإِنسِ يَعُوذُونَ بِرِجَالٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَزَادُوهُمۡ رَهَقٗا

And that there were men from humans in the Days of Ignorance who would seek protection with men from the Jinn when they would lodge at a frightful place, then one of them would say: I seek refuge with the chief of this valley from the evil of the fools of his people. So the men from the humans increased in fear and terror of the men from the Jinn.

(٧) وَأَنَّهُمۡ ظَنُّواْ كَمَا ظَنَنتُمۡ أَن لَّن يَبۡعَثَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٗا

And that the humans thought as you thought, O Jinn, that Allah would not revive anyone after his death for account and recompense.

(٨) وَأَنَّا لَمَسۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ فَوَجَدۡنَٰهَا مُلِئَتۡ حَرَسٗا شَدِيدٗا وَشُهُبٗا

And that we sought the news of the sky, then found the sky had been filled with strong guards from the angels guarding it from the eavesdropping we used to engage in. And it had been filled with ignited fire that was cast at anyone who came close to it.

(٩) وَأَنَّا كُنَّا نَقۡعُدُ مِنۡهَا مَقَٰعِدَ لِلسَّمۡعِۖ فَمَن يَسۡتَمِعِ ٱلۡأٓنَ يَجِدۡ لَهُۥ شِهَابٗا رَّصَدٗا

And that in the past, we would take stations in the sky, listening from them to what the angels circulate. Then we would inform the soothsayers on Earth of it. The affair has changed. So whoever of us listens now finds an ignited fire prepared for him. If he approaches, it is sent upon him and burns him.

(١٠) وَأَنَّا لَا نَدۡرِيٓ أَشَرٌّ أُرِيدَ بِمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَمۡ أَرَادَ بِهِمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ رَشَدٗا

And that we do not know the reason for this stern defense: Was evil intended for the people of Earth, or was it that Allah wanted good for them? For the news of the sky has been cut off from us.

(١١) وَأَنَّا مِنَّا ٱلصَّٰلِحُونَ وَمِنَّا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَۖ كُنَّا طَرَآئِقَ قِدَدٗا

And that, O community of Jinn, some of us are of the heedful and righteous, and some of us are of the disbelievers and wicked. We were different types and different [in] desires.

(١٢) وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّآ أَن لَّن نُّعۡجِزَ ٱللَّهَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَن نُّعۡجِزَهُۥ هَرَبٗا

And that we have ascertained that we will not escape Allah (may He be Exalted) if He wishes a matter for us, and we will not escape Him by fleeing due to His encompassing grasp of us.

(١٣) وَأَنَّا لَمَّا سَمِعۡنَا ٱلۡهُدَىٰٓ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦۖ فَمَن يُؤۡمِنۢ بِرَبِّهِۦ فَلَا يَخَافُ بَخۡسٗا وَلَا رَهَقٗا

And that when we heard the Quran, which guides to what is most upright, we believed in it. So whoever believes in his Lord will not fear any deficiency in his good deeds nor any sin added to his past sins.

Points of Benefit:

The tremendous effect of the Quran on whoever listens to it with a sound heart.
Seeking help from the Jinn is a type of polytheism. The one who does it will reach the opposite of what he intends in this life.
Invalidating soothsaying with the sending of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم.
From the manners of the believer is not to attribute evil to Allah.
