(١٤) وَأَنَّا مِنَّا ٱلۡمُسۡلِمُونَ وَمِنَّا ٱلۡقَٰسِطُونَۖ فَمَنۡ أَسۡلَمَ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ تَحَرَّوۡاْ رَشَدٗا

And that among us are Muslims (those who submit), resigned to Allah with obedience, and among us are those who are deviant from the right path and uprightness. So whoever humbles himself to Allah with obedience and good work are those who have aimed towards guidance and correctness.

(١٥) وَأَمَّا ٱلۡقَٰسِطُونَ فَكَانُواْ لِجَهَنَّمَ حَطَبٗا

And as for the ones who deviated off the right path and uprightness, they are lumber for Hell, fueling it along with their like from the humans.

(١٦) وَأَلَّوِ ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ عَلَى ٱلطَّرِيقَةِ لَأَسۡقَيۡنَٰهُم مَّآءً غَدَقٗا

And just as He revealed to him [the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم] that a group of the Jinn listened to him, He [also] revealed to him that if the Jinn and humans become upright on the path of Islam and act upon what it contains, Allah would surely provide them much waster to drink and provide them with various bounties.

(١٧) لِّنَفۡتِنَهُمۡ فِيهِۚ وَمَن يُعۡرِضۡ عَن ذِكۡرِ رَبِّهِۦ يَسۡلُكۡهُ عَذَابٗا صَعَدٗا

To test them in it: would they thank Allah's bounty or deny it? And whoever turns away from the Quran and what it contains of admonishments, his Lord will put him into a taxing torment that he will be unable to bear.

(١٨) وَأَنَّ ٱلۡمَسَٰجِدَ لِلَّهِ فَلَا تَدۡعُواْ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ أَحَدٗا

And that the places of prostration (Masājid) are for Him (may He be Exalted) not for other than Him. So do not supplicate in them to anyone alongside Allah, for then you would be like the Jews and Christians in their synagogues and churches.

(١٩) وَأَنَّهُۥ لَمَّا قَامَ عَبۡدُ ٱللَّهِ يَدۡعُوهُ كَادُواْ يَكُونُونَ عَلَيۡهِ لِبَدٗا

And that when the slave of Allah, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, stood to worship his Lord in Batn An-Nakhlah, the Jinn were almost piled upon him due to the severe crowding when they were listening to his recitation of the Quran.

(٢٠) قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَدۡعُواْ رَبِّي وَلَآ أُشۡرِكُ بِهِۦٓ أَحَدٗا

Say, O Messenger, to those polytheists: Indeed, I but supplicate exclusively to my Lord, and I do not associate anyone as a partner with Him in worship, whomever they may be.

(٢١) قُلۡ إِنِّي لَآ أَمۡلِكُ لَكُمۡ ضَرّٗا وَلَا رَشَدٗا

Say to them: Indeed, it is not in my hands to protect you from a harm Allah has preordained for you, nor can I bring about a benefit that Allah prevented you from.

(٢٢) قُلۡ إِنِّي لَن يُجِيرَنِي مِنَ ٱللَّهِ أَحَدٞ وَلَنۡ أَجِدَ مِن دُونِهِۦ مُلۡتَحَدًا

Say to them: No one will save me from Allah if I disobey Him, and I will not find in other than Him a refuge to seek refuge in.

(٢٣) إِلَّا بَلَٰغٗا مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرِسَٰلَٰتِهِۦۚ وَمَن يَعۡصِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَإِنَّ لَهُۥ نَارَ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدًا

But rather, what I can do is convey to you what Allah commanded me to and convey His message, which He sent me with to you. And whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger, then, indeed, his ending will be to enter Hellfire and remain therein eternally. He will never depart it.

(٢٤) حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا رَأَوۡاْ مَا يُوعَدُونَ فَسَيَعۡلَمُونَ مَنۡ أَضۡعَفُ نَاصِرٗا وَأَقَلُّ عَدَدٗا

And the disbelievers [will] remain in their disbelief until they behold, on the Day of Resurrection, what they were promised with, in the worldly life, of torment. Then they will know who has the weakest support and the fewest helpers.

(٢٥) قُلۡ إِنۡ أَدۡرِيٓ أَقَرِيبٞ مَّا تُوعَدُونَ أَمۡ يَجۡعَلُ لَهُۥ رَبِّيٓ أَمَدًا

Say, O Messenger, to these polytheists denying the resurrection: I do not know if the torment you are promised is near or if it has a period that no one knows except Allah.

(٢٦) عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ فَلَا يُظۡهِرُ عَلَىٰ غَيۡبِهِۦٓ أَحَدًا

He (may He be Exalted) is the knower of all the unseen; nothing is concealed from Him, so He does not show His unseen to anyone. Rather, it remains exclusively in His knowledge.

(٢٧) إِلَّا مَنِ ٱرۡتَضَىٰ مِن رَّسُولٖ فَإِنَّهُۥ يَسۡلُكُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦ رَصَدٗا

Except for whom Allah (may He be Exalted) is pleased with as a messenger, acquaints him with whatever He wills, and He sends guardian angels who protect the Messenger from the front and from behind, and no one other than the messenger is made aware of this.

(٢٨) لِّيَعۡلَمَ أَن قَدۡ أَبۡلَغُواْ رِسَٰلَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَأَحَاطَ بِمَا لَدَيۡهِمۡ وَأَحۡصَىٰ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ عَدَدَۢا

In hopes that the Messenger may know that messengers before him had delivered the messages of their Lord, which He commanded them to deliver, due to what care Allah encompassed it with. Allah's knowledge encompasses what the angels and messengers have, so nothing from that is concealed from Him. He enumerated the number of all things, so nothing from them is concealed from Him (may He be Exalted).

Points of Benefit:

Oppression is a cause for entering the Fire.
The importance of uprightness to reach good objectives.
The revelation was protected from the tampering of the devils.
