The Enshrouded One (Surah Al-Muzzammil)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Explaining the factors that help one take on the hardships of calling to Allah.

Explanation:

(١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُزَّمِّلُ

O you who is wrapped in his clothes [meaning the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم].

(٢) قُمِ ٱلَّيۡلَ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

Pray the night except for a little of it.

(٣) نِّصۡفَهُۥٓ أَوِ ٱنقُصۡ مِنۡهُ قَلِيلًا

Pray half of it if you wish, or pray a little less than half such that you pray a third.

(٤) أَوۡ زِدۡ عَلَيۡهِ وَرَتِّلِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَرۡتِيلًا

Or add to it until you reach two-thirds. And make the Quran clear when you recite it, and be unhurried in its recitation.

(٥) إِنَّا سَنُلۡقِي عَلَيۡكَ قَوۡلٗا ثَقِيلًا

O Messenger, indeed, we will send down the Quran to you. It is a heavy word due to what it contains of obligations, limits, rulings, manners, and otherwise.

(٦) إِنَّ نَاشِئَةَ ٱلَّيۡلِ هِيَ أَشَدُّ وَطۡ‍ٔٗا وَأَقۡوَمُ قِيلًا

Indeed, the hours of the night are when the heart is more in line with the recitation and more correct in speech.

(٧) إِنَّ لَكَ فِي ٱلنَّهَارِ سَبۡحٗا طَوِيلٗا

Indeed, during the day, you manage your tasks, so you get busied with them from the recitation of the Quran. So, pray at night.

(٨) وَٱذۡكُرِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ وَتَبَتَّلۡ إِلَيۡهِ تَبۡتِيلٗا

And remember Allah with the various types of remembrances. And dedicate yourself to Him (may He be Exalted) by making worship exclusive to Him.

(٩) رَّبُّ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقِ وَٱلۡمَغۡرِبِ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ فَٱتَّخِذۡهُ وَكِيلٗا

Lord of the East and Lord of the West. There is no rightful object of worship except Him, so take Him as a trustee you rely on in all your affairs.

(١٠) وَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَٱهۡجُرۡهُمۡ هَجۡرٗا جَمِيلٗا

And be patient with what the rejectors say of mockery and profanity. And desert them without causing any harm.

(١١) وَذَرۡنِي وَٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ أُوْلِي ٱلنَّعۡمَةِ وَمَهِّلۡهُمۡ قَلِيلًا

And do not be concerned with the matter of the rejectors, the people of indulgence in the pleasures of the worldly life. Leave them and me, and wait for them for a little while until their end comes.

(١٢) إِنَّ لَدَيۡنَآ أَنكَالٗا وَجَحِيمٗا

Indeed, in the Hereafter, we have heavy restraints and an ignited fire.

(١٣) وَطَعَامٗا ذَا غُصَّةٖ وَعَذَابًا أَلِيمٗا

And food that throats choke on due to its bitterness and painful torment, in addition to what has preceded.

(١٤) يَوۡمَ تَرۡجُفُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَٱلۡجِبَالُ وَكَانَتِ ٱلۡجِبَالُ كَثِيبٗا مَّهِيلًا

That torment will occur to the rejectors on the day the Earth and mountains will quake, and the mountains will be as fluid, dispersed sand due to the severity of its horrors.

(١٥) إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكُمۡ رَسُولٗا شَٰهِدًا عَلَيۡكُمۡ كَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ رَسُولٗا

Indeed, we sent to you a messenger who is a witness of your deeds on the Day of Resurrection, just as we sent a messenger to Pharoah, who was Moses, peace be upon him.

(١٦) فَعَصَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنُ ٱلرَّسُولَ فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُ أَخۡذٗا وَبِيلٗا

Pharoah disobeyed the messenger sent to him from his Lord, so we punished him with a severe punishment in the worldly life with drowning and in the Afterlife with the torment of the Fire. So do not disobey your messenger lest what befell him should befall you.

(١٧) فَكَيۡفَ تَتَّقُونَ إِن كَفَرۡتُمۡ يَوۡمٗا يَجۡعَلُ ٱلۡوِلۡدَٰنَ شِيبًا

So how will you protect yourselves, if you reject Allah and deny His Messenger, from a long, severe day that will make the heads of young children white because of the severity of its horror and its length?

(١٨) ٱلسَّمَآءُ مُنفَطِرُۢ بِهِۦۚ كَانَ وَعۡدُهُۥ مَفۡعُولًا

[When] the sky [is] fractured from its horrors. Allah's promise is inevitable and inescapable.

(١٩) إِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦ تَذۡكِرَةٞۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ سَبِيلًا

Indeed, this admonition, which clarifies what horror and adversity are on the Day of Resurrection, is a reminder. The believers benefit from it. So who wishes to take a road that leads to his Lord takes it.

Points of Benefit:

The importance of the night prayer, reciting the Quran, remembrance of Allah, and patience for the caller to Allah.
Bearing the burdens requires a firm upbringing.
The heart being unoccupied in the night affects memorization and understanding.
Indulgence and excessive gratification obstruct one from the path of Allah.
