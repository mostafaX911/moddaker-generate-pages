(٢٠) ۞إِنَّ رَبَّكَ يَعۡلَمُ أَنَّكَ تَقُومُ أَدۡنَىٰ مِن ثُلُثَيِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَنِصۡفَهُۥ وَثُلُثَهُۥ وَطَآئِفَةٞ مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ مَعَكَۚ وَٱللَّهُ يُقَدِّرُ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَۚ عَلِمَ أَن لَّن تُحۡصُوهُ فَتَابَ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ فَٱقۡرَءُواْ مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِۚ عَلِمَ أَن سَيَكُونُ مِنكُم مَّرۡضَىٰ وَءَاخَرُونَ يَضۡرِبُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَبۡتَغُونَ مِن فَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَءَاخَرُونَ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۖ فَٱقۡرَءُواْ مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنۡهُۚ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَقۡرِضُواْ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنٗاۚ وَمَا تُقَدِّمُواْ لِأَنفُسِكُم مِّنۡ خَيۡرٖ تَجِدُوهُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ هُوَ خَيۡرٗا وَأَعۡظَمَ أَجۡرٗاۚ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ ٱللَّهَۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمُۢ

Indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, knows that you sometimes pray less than two-thirds of the night, sometimes half of it, and sometimes a third of it, and a group of the believers stand with you [in prayer]. And Allah determines the night and the day and enumerates their hours. He (may He be Exalted) knew that you cannot enumerate and match its hours, so it burdens you to stand most of it to ensure what is requested [is done]. So because of that, He has turned to you in repentance, so pray from the night that which is made easy. Allah knew that there would be among you, O believers, ill ones exhausted by disease, others traveling seeking Allah's provision, and others fighting the disbelievers to pursue the contentment of Allah and to make the word of Allah most superior. Standing in prayer burdens these [mentioned], so pray from the night what is made easy for you. And come to the obligatory prayer in the most complete manner, give Zakat (obligatory alms) of your wealth, and spend from your wealth in the path of Allah. And what you put forth for yourselves of good, you will find with Allah on the Day of Resurrection better and greater in reward. And seek forgiveness from Allah. Indeed, Allah is forgiving to those who repent from His slaves, merciful to them.

The Cloaked One (Surah Al-Muddathir)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

The command to give the utmost effort in calling the rejectors and warning them with the Hereafter and the Quran.

Explanation:

(١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُدَّثِّرُ

O you who is enshrouded with his clothes (the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم).

(٢) قُمۡ فَأَنذِرۡ

Get up and instill fear with regards to Allah's punishment.

(٣) وَرَبَّكَ فَكَبِّرۡ

And aggrandize your Lord.

(٤) وَثِيَابَكَ فَطَهِّرۡ

And purify yourself from sins and your clothes from impurities.

(٥) وَٱلرُّجۡزَ فَٱهۡجُرۡ

And stay away from the worship of idols.

(٦) وَلَا تَمۡنُن تَسۡتَكۡثِرُ

And do not vainly recount what you have done for your Lord by considering your good deeds to be much.

(٧) وَلِرَبِّكَ فَٱصۡبِرۡ

And be patient, for Allah's sake, upon what harm you face.

(٨) فَإِذَا نُقِرَ فِي ٱلنَّاقُورِ

So when the second blowing is blown into the horn,

(٩) فَذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَوۡمٌ عَسِيرٌ

Then that day is a difficult one,

(١٠) عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ غَيۡرُ يَسِيرٖ

Not easy upon the disbelievers in Allah and his messengers.

(١١) ذَرۡنِي وَمَنۡ خَلَقۡتُ وَحِيدٗا

Leave me, O Messenger, and whom I created alone in his mother's stomach without wealth or child (referring to al-Waleed bin al-Mughirah).

(١٢) وَجَعَلۡتُ لَهُۥ مَالٗا مَّمۡدُودٗا

I produced abundant wealth for him.

(١٣) وَبَنِينَ شُهُودٗا

I made sons for him, present with him, and witnessing gatherings with him. They do not separate from him for travel due to the abundance of his wealth.

(١٤) وَمَهَّدتُّ لَهُۥ تَمۡهِيدٗا

I extended his life, provision, and offspring for him.

(١٥) ثُمَّ يَطۡمَعُ أَنۡ أَزِيدَ

Then, while disbelieving in Me, he craves that I give him more after what I have given him from all that.

(١٦) كَلَّآۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ لِأٓيَٰتِنَا عَنِيدٗا

The matter is not as he imagined. Indeed, he opposed Our verses revealed to Our Messenger, rejecting them.

(١٧) سَأُرۡهِقُهُۥ صَعُودًا

I will task him with a burden from the torment that he will be unable to bear.

(١٨) إِنَّهُۥ فَكَّرَ وَقَدَّرَ

Indeed, this disbeliever, whom I bestowed upon with those bounties, thought over what he would say about the Quran to invalidate it, and he determined that within himself.

Points of Benefit:

Difficulty brings ease.
The obligation of purification from outer and inner impurities.
Bestowing delight upon the wicked is in prolongation [of his punishment], not in honor [to him].
