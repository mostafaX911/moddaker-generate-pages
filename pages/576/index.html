(١٩) فَقُتِلَ كَيۡفَ قَدَّرَ

So cursed and tormented is he, how he determined.

(٢٠) ثُمَّ قُتِلَ كَيۡفَ قَدَّرَ

Again, cursed and tormented is he, how he determined.

(٢١) ثُمَّ نَظَرَ

Then he reevaluated and pondered what he would say.

(٢٢) ثُمَّ عَبَسَ وَبَسَرَ

Then his face frowned and wrinkled when he did not find what to criticize the Quran with.

(٢٣) ثُمَّ أَدۡبَرَ وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرَ

Then he turned away from faith and became arrogantly stubborn from following the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم.

(٢٤) فَقَالَ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا سِحۡرٞ يُؤۡثَرُ

Then he said: This, what Muhammad brought, is not the speech of Allah. Rather, it is sorcery he narrates from others.

(٢٥) إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا قَوۡلُ ٱلۡبَشَرِ

This is not the speech of Allah. Rather, it is the speech of humans.

(٢٦) سَأُصۡلِيهِ سَقَرَ

I will put this disbeliever into a level from the levels of the Fire, which is Saqar. He will suffer its heat.

(٢٧) وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا سَقَرُ

And what will inform you, O Muhammad, what Saqar is?!

(٢٨) لَا تُبۡقِي وَلَا تَذَرُ

It doesn't leave anything from those tormented in it except that it comes upon it and does not let it go. Then it returns to as it was, then it [Saqar] comes upon it, and so on, again and again.

(٢٩) لَوَّاحَةٞ لِّلۡبَشَرِ

Extreme in burning and altering skin.

(٣٠) عَلَيۡهَا تِسۡعَةَ عَشَرَ

Upon it are nineteen angels, and they are its guards.

(٣١) وَمَا جَعَلۡنَآ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلنَّارِ إِلَّا مَلَٰٓئِكَةٗۖ وَمَا جَعَلۡنَا عِدَّتَهُمۡ إِلَّا فِتۡنَةٗ لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِيَسۡتَيۡقِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَيَزۡدَادَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنٗا وَلَا يَرۡتَابَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَلِيَقُولَ ٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٞ وَٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ مَاذَآ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ بِهَٰذَا مَثَلٗاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُضِلُّ ٱللَّهُ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَمَا يَعۡلَمُ جُنُودَ رَبِّكَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَمَا هِيَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرَىٰ لِلۡبَشَرِ

And We did not make the guards of the Fire except angels. Humans have no power over them. We did not make this many of them except as a test for those who disbelieved in Allah, so that they might say what they said, causing their torment to be multiplied. This is also to show the Jews who were given the Torah and the Christians who were given the Gospel that the Quran was divinely revealed, confirming what was in their books. This is also so that believers would increase in faith when the People of the Book agreed with them. And it would also leave no doubt amongst the Jews, the Christians, and the believers. And this is also so those hesitant in their faith and the unbelievers ask: What did Allah will by this strange number?This is a source of misguidance for those who object to this number and a source of guidance for those who believe in it. Allah misguides whomever He wishes to misguide and guides whomever He wishes to guide. No one knows the abundance of Allah's army except for Him; may He be Exalted. The Fire is nothing more than a source of reminder for humanity. Through it, they know the majesty of Allah (may He be Exalted).

(٣٢) كَلَّا وَٱلۡقَمَرِ

The truth is not as some of the polytheists claim that he would suffice his companions [against] the guards of Hell until he clears them away from it. Allah swore by the Moon.

(٣٣) وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذۡ أَدۡبَرَ

And swore by the night when it retreats.

(٣٤) وَٱلصُّبۡحِ إِذَآ أَسۡفَرَ

And swore by the morning when it illuminates.

(٣٥) إِنَّهَا لَإِحۡدَى ٱلۡكُبَرِ

Indeed, Hellfire is one of the great tribulations.

(٣٦) نَذِيرٗا لِّلۡبَشَرِ

Terrifying and frightening for people.

(٣٧) لِمَن شَآءَ مِنكُمۡ أَن يَتَقَدَّمَ أَوۡ يَتَأَخَّرَ

For whoever of you, O people, wishes to move forth with faith in Allah and righteous action or fall back with disbelief and acts of disobedience.

(٣٨) كُلُّ نَفۡسِۢ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ رَهِينَةٌ

Every soul is requited with what deeds it gained. So either its actions bring disaster upon it, or they rescue and save it from destruction.

(٣٩) إِلَّآ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡيَمِينِ

Except for the believers, for they will rescue themselves from destruction with the good they did from their deeds.

(٤٠) فِي جَنَّٰتٖ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ

They will be in gardens, asking one another on the Day of Resurrection.

(٤١) عَنِ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

About the disbelievers who destroyed themselves by committing acts of disobedience.

(٤٢) مَا سَلَكَكُمۡ فِي سَقَرَ

They will say to them: What made you enter Hell?

(٤٣) قَالُواْ لَمۡ نَكُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُصَلِّينَ

So the disbelievers will answer them saying: We were not among those who would perform the obligatory prayer in the worldly life.

(٤٤) وَلَمۡ نَكُ نُطۡعِمُ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينَ

And we would not feed the needy from what Allah gave us.

(٤٥) وَكُنَّا نَخُوضُ مَعَ ٱلۡخَآئِضِينَ

And we were with the people of falsehood, going with them wherever they would and speaking with the people of misguidance and error.

(٤٦) وَكُنَّا نُكَذِّبُ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ

And we used to reject the Day of Recompense.

(٤٧) حَتَّىٰٓ أَتَىٰنَا ٱلۡيَقِينُ

And we continued with its rejection until death came to us. So it prevented us from repentance.

Points of Benefit:

The danger of arrogance as it turned Al-Waleed bin Al-Mughirah away from faith after the truth had become clear to him.
The human being's responsibility for his deeds in [both] this world and the Hereafter.
Not feeding the needy is one of the causes for entering the Fire.
