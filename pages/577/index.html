(٤٨) فَمَا تَنفَعُهُمۡ شَفَٰعَةُ ٱلشَّٰفِعِينَ

On the Day of Resurrection, the mediation of intercessors from the angels, prophets, and pious people will not benefit them because the requirement for an intercession's acceptance is contingent upon the approval for whom the intercession is made.

(٤٩) فَمَا لَهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلتَّذۡكِرَةِ مُعۡرِضِينَ

What made these polytheists shun the Quran?

(٥٠) كَأَنَّهُمۡ حُمُرٞ مُّسۡتَنفِرَةٞ

As if they are, in their shunning and fleeing from it, wild donkeys strongly running away.

(٥١) فَرَّتۡ مِن قَسۡوَرَةِۢ

That fled from a lion in fear of it.

(٥٢) بَلۡ يُرِيدُ كُلُّ ٱمۡرِيٕٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ أَن يُؤۡتَىٰ صُحُفٗا مُّنَشَّرَةٗ

Rather, each one of these polytheists wishes that an open book appear at his head informing him that Muhammed صلى الله عليه وسلم is a messenger from Allah. The reason for that is not a shortage of evidence and weakness of arguments. It is but obstinance and arrogant stubbornness.

(٥٣) كَلَّاۖ بَل لَّا يَخَافُونَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ

The matter is not so [i.e., they will not each get a book]. Rather, their persistence in their misguidance is because they do not believe in the torment of the Hereafter, so they have remained in their disbelief.

(٥٤) كَلَّآ إِنَّهُۥ تَذۡكِرَةٞ

Is it not that this Quran is an admonishment and reminder?

(٥٥) فَمَن شَآءَ ذَكَرَهُۥ

So whoever wants to read the Quran and be admonished by it will read it and be admonished by it.

(٥٦) وَمَا يَذۡكُرُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ هُوَ أَهۡلُ ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ وَأَهۡلُ ٱلۡمَغۡفِرَةِ

And they will not be admonished except as Allah wishes for them to be admonished. He (may He be Exalted) is befitting to be heeded by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions. And He is befitting to forgive the sins of his slaves when they repent to Him.

The Resurrection (Surah Al-Qiyamah)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Illustrating Allah's ability to resurrect the creation and gather them on the Day of Resurrection.

Explanation:

(١) لَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ

Allah swore by the Day of Resurrection, the Day the people will rise for the Lord of the worlds.

(٢) وَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِٱلنَّفۡسِ ٱللَّوَّامَةِ

And He swore by the decent soul that blames itself for shortcomings in good deeds and for performing evil deeds. He swore by these two matters that He will surely resurrect the people for account and recompense.

(٣) أَيَحۡسَبُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ أَلَّن نَّجۡمَعَ عِظَامَهُۥ

Does the human being think that We will not gather his bones for resurrection after his death?

(٤) بَلَىٰ قَٰدِرِينَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن نُّسَوِّيَ بَنَانَهُۥ

Nay, along with gathering them, we are able to return his fingertips properly created just as they were.

(٥) بَلۡ يُرِيدُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ لِيَفۡجُرَ أَمَامَهُۥ

Rather, in denial of the resurrection, the human wants to continue upon his wickedness in the future without deterrence.

(٦) يَسۡ‍َٔلُ أَيَّانَ يَوۡمُ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ

He asks about the Day of Resurrection as if it is farfetched: When will it occur?

(٧) فَإِذَا بَرِقَ ٱلۡبَصَرُ

So when the vision becomes confused, and he is shocked when he sees what he used to reject.

(٨) وَخَسَفَ ٱلۡقَمَرُ

And the light of the Moon disappears.

(٩) وَجُمِعَ ٱلشَّمۡسُ وَٱلۡقَمَرُ

And the bodies of the Sun and Moon come together.

(١٠) يَقُولُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ يَوۡمَئِذٍ أَيۡنَ ٱلۡمَفَرُّ

The wicked human will say on that Day: Where is the escape?!

(١١) كَلَّا لَا وَزَرَ

There is no escape on that Day, no refuge for the wicked person to seek refuge in, and no protector for him to seek protection with.

(١٢) إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ يَوۡمَئِذٍ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقَرُّ

O Prophet, on that Day, account and recompense will return and be relegated to your Lord.

(١٣) يُنَبَّؤُاْ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ يَوۡمَئِذِۢ بِمَا قَدَّمَ وَأَخَّرَ

On that Day, the human will be informed of what deeds he put forth and what he left behind.

(١٤) بَلِ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِۦ بَصِيرَةٞ

Rather, the human is a witness upon himself, as his limbs bear testimony against him to what sins he committed.

(١٥) وَلَوۡ أَلۡقَىٰ مَعَاذِيرَهُۥ

And if he came with excuses, arguing by them for his own sake as having done no evil, they would not benefit him.

(١٦) لَا تُحَرِّكۡ بِهِۦ لِسَانَكَ لِتَعۡجَلَ بِهِۦٓ

O Messenger, do not move your tongue in haste [for fear] that it may escape from you.

(١٧) إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا جَمۡعَهُۥ وَقُرۡءَانَهُۥ

Indeed, it is upon Us to gather it in your chest and make its recitation firm upon your tongue.

(١٨) فَإِذَا قَرَأۡنَٰهُ فَٱتَّبِعۡ قُرۡءَانَهُۥ

When our messenger Gabriel recites [the Quran] for you [O Prophet], then listen attentively to its recitation.

(١٩) ثُمَّ إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا بَيَانَهُۥ

Then it is upon Us to explain it to you.

Points of Benefit:

The slave's will is subject to Allah's will.
The Messenger's صلى الله عليه وسلم keenness on memorizing whatever is revealed to him from the Quran. Allah took on the onus for him by gathering it in his breast and preserving it in its completeness so he would not forget anything from it.
