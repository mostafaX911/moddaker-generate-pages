(٢٠) كَلَّا بَلۡ تُحِبُّونَ ٱلۡعَاجِلَةَ

Nay, the matter is not as you [disbelievers] have claimed of the impossibility of the Resurrection, for you know that the one able to create you initially is not impeded from reviving you after your death. But the reason for your rejection of the Resurrection is your love for the life of this world, which is quick to finish.

(٢١) وَتَذَرُونَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ

And your desertion of the Hereafter, whose path is performing what Allah commanded you with of acts of obedience and leaving what He forbade you from of prohibitions.

(٢٢) وُجُوهٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ نَّاضِرَةٌ

The faces of the people of faith and happiness on that Day will be beautiful and radiant.

(٢٣) إِلَىٰ رَبِّهَا نَاظِرَةٞ

Looking at their Lord, relishing in that.

(٢٤) وَوُجُوهٞ يَوۡمَئِذِۢ بَاسِرَةٞ

And the faces of the people of disbelief and hardship, on that day, will be frowning.

(٢٥) تَظُنُّ أَن يُفۡعَلَ بِهَا فَاقِرَةٞ

Certain that a great punishment and painful torment will descend upon them.

(٢٦) كَلَّآ إِذَا بَلَغَتِ ٱلتَّرَاقِيَ

The matter is not how the polytheists imagine that they will not be tormented if they die. So when the soul of one of them reaches the top of his chest,

(٢٧) وَقِيلَ مَنۡۜ رَاقٖ

And some people say to each other: Who will recite treatment upon this one so that he is cured?!

(٢٨) وَظَنَّ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡفِرَاقُ

And the one approaching the moment of death of their illness is certain that it is indeed the parting of the worldly life by death,

(٢٩) وَٱلۡتَفَّتِ ٱلسَّاقُ بِٱلسَّاقِ

And the adversities come together at the end of the worldly life and the beginning of the Afterlife,

(٣٠) إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ يَوۡمَئِذٍ ٱلۡمَسَاقُ

When that occurs, the deceased one is driven to his Lord.

(٣١) فَلَا صَدَّقَ وَلَا صَلَّىٰ

So the disbeliever did not affirm what his messenger came with, nor did he pray to Allah, may He be Exalted.

(٣٢) وَلَٰكِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ

Instead, he rejected what his messenger came with and shunned him.

(٣٣) ثُمَّ ذَهَبَ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ يَتَمَطَّىٰٓ

Then this disbeliever went to his family, strutting in his walk out of arrogance.

(٣٤) أَوۡلَىٰ لَكَ فَأَوۡلَىٰ

So Allah threatens the disbeliever that his torment has neared and gotten close to him.

(٣٥) ثُمَّ أَوۡلَىٰ لَكَ فَأَوۡلَىٰٓ

He then repeated the sentence as a form of emphasis and stated: Again, nearer to you [O disbeliever] lies your doom and nearer.

(٣٦) أَيَحۡسَبُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ أَن يُتۡرَكَ سُدًى

Does the human being think Allah will leave him disregarded without obliging him with legislation?

(٣٧) أَلَمۡ يَكُ نُطۡفَةٗ مِّن مَّنِيّٖ يُمۡنَىٰ

Was not this human once a drop of semen poured into the womb?

(٣٨) ثُمَّ كَانَ عَلَقَةٗ فَخَلَقَ فَسَوَّىٰ

Then, after that, was a piece of solid blood, so Allah created him and made his creation proportioned?

(٣٩) فَجَعَلَ مِنۡهُ ٱلزَّوۡجَيۡنِ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلۡأُنثَىٰٓ

Then He made from his kind two types, male and female?!

(٤٠) أَلَيۡسَ ذَٰلِكَ بِقَٰدِرٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يُحۡـِۧيَ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ

Is not the One who created the human from a drop and then a blood clot able to bring life to the dead again for account and recompense?! Nay, rather, He is able.

The Human Being (Surah Al-Insan)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Reminding the human of the origin of his creation and his destination, and describing what Allah has prepared for His allies in Paradise.

Explanation:

(١) هَلۡ أَتَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ حِينٞ مِّنَ ٱلدَّهۡرِ لَمۡ يَكُن شَيۡ‍ٔٗا مَّذۡكُورًا

An extended period had passed upon the human being in which he was non-existent, without mention.

(٢) إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِن نُّطۡفَةٍ أَمۡشَاجٖ نَّبۡتَلِيهِ فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ سَمِيعَۢا بَصِيرًا

Indeed, we created the human being from a drop mixed between the water of the man and the water of the woman. We test him with what we obligate him with of duties. So, We made him hear and see to perform what we assigned him from the legislation.

(٣) إِنَّا هَدَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلسَّبِيلَ إِمَّا شَاكِرٗا وَإِمَّا كَفُورًا

Indeed, We clarified for him the road of guidance upon the tongues of Our messengers, so that has become clear from the road of misguidance. So after that, he is either to be guided to the straight path and then become a believing slave, thankful to Allah, or he is to stray from it and then become a disbelieving slave, denying the signs of Allah.

After mentioning the two categories of guided and misguided, Allah mentions their recompense, stating:

(٤) إِنَّآ أَعۡتَدۡنَا لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ سَلَٰسِلَاْ وَأَغۡلَٰلٗا وَسَعِيرًا

Indeed, we have prepared chains for the disbelievers in Allah and His messengers, with which they will be pulled into the Fire, [and prepared] binds that they will be bound in, and an ignited Fire.

(٥) إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ يَشۡرَبُونَ مِن كَأۡسٖ كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا كَافُورًا

Indeed, the believers obedient to Allah will drink on the Day of Resurrection from a full cup of wine mixed with camphor for its pleasant smell.

Points of Benefit:

The danger of loving this world and turning away from the Hereafter.
Looking upon Allah's noble face is among the greatest of delights.
Affirming mankind's [freedom of] choice, which is from Allah's honoring of him.
