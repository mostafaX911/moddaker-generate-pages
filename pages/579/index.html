(٦) عَيۡنٗا يَشۡرَبُ بِهَا عِبَادُ ٱللَّهِ يُفَجِّرُونَهَا تَفۡجِيرٗا

This drink prepared for the people of obedience is from a spring that is easy to take from, abundant, and never drying up. The slaves of Allah quench their thirst with it. They pour it and make it flow wherever they wish.

(٧) يُوفُونَ بِٱلنَّذۡرِ وَيَخَافُونَ يَوۡمٗا كَانَ شَرُّهُۥ مُسۡتَطِيرٗا

And the attributes of the slaves who drink from it are that they fulfill what they obligate on themselves of acts of obedience. They fear a Day whose evil is widespread and distributed, which is the Day of Resurrection.

(٨) وَيُطۡعِمُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِۦ مِسۡكِينٗا وَيَتِيمٗا وَأَسِيرًا

And they donate food while they are in a state of loving it due to their need for it and craving it. They give it to those in need of the poor, orphans, and prisoners of war.

(٩) إِنَّمَا نُطۡعِمُكُمۡ لِوَجۡهِ ٱللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمۡ جَزَآءٗ وَلَا شُكُورًا

And they secretly say within themselves that they do not feed them except for the Face of Allah, so they do not want reward or praise for feeding them.

(١٠) إِنَّا نَخَافُ مِن رَّبِّنَا يَوۡمًا عَبُوسٗا قَمۡطَرِيرٗا

[They say] Indeed, we fear from our Lord a Day in which the faces of those in hardship will frown due to its severity and horridness.

(١١) فَوَقَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ شَرَّ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمِ وَلَقَّىٰهُمۡ نَضۡرَةٗ وَسُرُورٗا

So Allah, by His favor, protected them from the evil of that great Day. He gave them beauty and light in their faces, in honor to them, and joy in their hearts.

(١٢) وَجَزَىٰهُم بِمَا صَبَرُواْ جَنَّةٗ وَحَرِيرٗا

And because of their patience upon acts of obedience, and with what Allah predetermined, and against acts of disobedience, Allah awarded them a garden in which they delight, and silk they wear.

(١٣) مُّتَّكِ‍ِٔينَ فِيهَا عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِۖ لَا يَرَوۡنَ فِيهَا شَمۡسٗا وَلَا زَمۡهَرِيرٗا

Reclining therein on adorned couches. They do not see, in this garden, any sun whose rays would hurt them, nor any severe cold. Rather, they are in continuous shade, with no heat or cold.

(١٤) وَدَانِيَةً عَلَيۡهِمۡ ظِلَٰلُهَا وَذُلِّلَتۡ قُطُوفُهَا تَذۡلِيلٗا

Its shade is close to them. Its fruits are subdued for whoever takes it, so he takes it comfortably and easily, such that those laying, sitting, and standing can reach it.

(١٥) وَيُطَافُ عَلَيۡهِم بِ‍َٔانِيَةٖ مِّن فِضَّةٖ وَأَكۡوَابٖ كَانَتۡ قَوَارِيرَا۠

And slaves circulate about them with silver vessels and cups, whose color is pure, when they want to drink.

(١٦) قَوَارِيرَاْ مِن فِضَّةٖ قَدَّرُوهَا تَقۡدِيرٗا

They are pure in color, like glass, except they are made of silver. They are sized according to what they want, not more than that or less.

(١٧) وَيُسۡقَوۡنَ فِيهَا كَأۡسٗا كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا زَنجَبِيلًا

And these honored ones are given a cup of wine mixed with ginger to drink.

(١٨) عَيۡنٗا فِيهَا تُسَمَّىٰ سَلۡسَبِيلٗا

They drink from a spring in the Garden named Salsabeel.

(١٩) ۞وَيَطُوفُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وِلۡدَٰنٞ مُّخَلَّدُونَ إِذَا رَأَيۡتَهُمۡ حَسِبۡتَهُمۡ لُؤۡلُؤٗا مَّنثُورٗا

And young adult slaves who remain in their youth circulate about them. If you saw them, you would have thought them scattered pearls due to the beauty of their faces, the splendor of their colors, abundance, and dispersion.

(٢٠) وَإِذَا رَأَيۡتَ ثَمَّ رَأَيۡتَ نَعِيمٗا وَمُلۡكٗا كَبِيرًا

And if you saw what is there in the Garden, you would have seen delight that cannot be described and seen a great kingdom that no kingdom comes close to.

(٢١) عَٰلِيَهُمۡ ثِيَابُ سُندُسٍ خُضۡرٞ وَإِسۡتَبۡرَقٞۖ وَحُلُّوٓاْ أَسَاوِرَ مِن فِضَّةٖ وَسَقَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ شَرَابٗا طَهُورًا

Green elegant clothes having adorned their bodies. They are of fine silk and thick, heavy silk. And they are dressed therein with bracelets of silver. Allah gives them drink with a beverage free from any contaminants.

(٢٢) إِنَّ هَٰذَا كَانَ لَكُمۡ جَزَآءٗ وَكَانَ سَعۡيُكُم مَّشۡكُورًا

And it is said to them, in their honor: Indeed, this delight which We bestowed upon you was a reward for you upon your righteous deeds, and your action was accepted by Allah.

(٢٣) إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَنزِيلٗا

O Messenger, indeed, We sent the Quran down upon you in parts, and We did not send it down upon you all at once.

(٢٤) فَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تُطِعۡ مِنۡهُمۡ ءَاثِمًا أَوۡ كَفُورٗا

So be patient with what Allah determines regarding fate or legislation. And do not obey a sinner with what he calls to of sin, nor a disbeliever in what he calls to of disbelief.

(٢٥) وَٱذۡكُرِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ بُكۡرَةٗ وَأَصِيلٗا

And remember your Lord with the Fajr (dawn) prayer at the beginning of the day and the prayers of Dhuhr (early afternoon) and 'Asr (late afternoon) at the end of it.

Points of Benefit:

Fulfilling vows, feeding the needy, sincerity in action, and fear of Allah are causes for salvation from the Fire and entering Paradise.
If the servants attending to them in Paradise are in such a state of beauty, how would the people of Paradise themselves be?!
