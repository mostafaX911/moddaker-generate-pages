(٦٢) إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلۡقَصَصُ ٱلۡحَقُّۚ وَمَا مِنۡ إِلَٰهٍ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

What We have mentioned to you about Jesus (peace be upon him) is the truth that contains no lie or doubt. Therefore, there is no one worthy of worship besides Allah alone. Indeed, Allah is the Almighty in His dominion—the All-Wise in His planning, command, and regarding His creation.

(٦٣) فَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمُۢ بِٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ

If they turn away from what you have brought and do not follow you, then it is because of their corrupt ways. Allah knows those who cause corruption on earth, and He will recompense them accordingly.

(٦٤) قُلۡ يَٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ تَعَالَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ كَلِمَةٖ سَوَآءِۢ بَيۡنَنَا وَبَيۡنَكُمۡ أَلَّا نَعۡبُدَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَ وَلَا نُشۡرِكَ بِهِۦ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا يَتَّخِذَ بَعۡضُنَا بَعۡضًا أَرۡبَابٗا مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۚ فَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَقُولُواْ ٱشۡهَدُواْ بِأَنَّا مُسۡلِمُونَ

O Messenger, say (to them): O Jews and Christians, People of the Book, let us unite upon a common word in which we are all equal: that we worship Allah alone and we do not worship anyone besides Him, no matter what his rank or how high his status; and that we do not take one another as lords to be worshipped and obeyed instead of Allah. Suppose they turn away from the truth and justice you invite them to. In that case, O believers, say to them: Bear witness that we have surrendered to Allah and submitted through obedience [to Him].

(٦٥) يَٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لِمَ تُحَآجُّونَ فِيٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَمَآ أُنزِلَتِ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةُ وَٱلۡإِنجِيلُ إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦٓۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

O People of the Book, why do you dispute about the religion of Abraham? (You) The Jews claim he was a Jew, and (you) the Christians claim he was a Christian; all the while, you know very well that Judaism and Christianity did not appear until long after him. Can you not see how false your statement is and the error of your claim?

(٦٦) هَٰٓأَنتُمۡ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ حَٰجَجۡتُمۡ فِيمَا لَكُم بِهِۦ عِلۡمٞ فَلِمَ تُحَآجُّونَ فِيمَا لَيۡسَ لَكُم بِهِۦ عِلۡمٞۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

Here you are, O People of the Book, you have disputed with the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم with your knowledge of your religion and what was revealed to you. But why do you dispute regarding Abraham and his religion, which you do not know, as it is not in your Book, and your prophets did not discuss it? Allah knows the reality of matters and what is hidden, and you do not know.

(٦٧) مَا كَانَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمُ يَهُودِيّٗا وَلَا نَصۡرَانِيّٗا وَلَٰكِن كَانَ حَنِيفٗا مُّسۡلِمٗا وَمَا كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

Abraham was not a Jew or Christian in belief, but he was opposed to all false religions and obedient to Allah alone. He was also not one of those who ascribed partners to Allah – contrary to the polytheists of the Arabs, who claimed to follow his religion.

(٦٨) إِنَّ أَوۡلَى ٱلنَّاسِ بِإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ لَلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُ وَهَٰذَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْۗ وَٱللَّهُ وَلِيُّ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

The people most worthy of affiliating themselves to Abraham are those who followed him in his time, as well as this Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) and those who have believed in him from this nation. Thereby, Allah helps and protects the believers.

(٦٩) وَدَّت طَّآئِفَةٞ مِّنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَوۡ يُضِلُّونَكُمۡ وَمَا يُضِلُّونَ إِلَّآ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَمَا يَشۡعُرُونَ

O believers, the scholars of the Jews and the Christians, desire to mislead you from the truth Allah has guided you to. Yet they only deceive themselves because their attempt to mislead the believers only increases their misguidance, and they are not aware of the consequences of their actions.

(٧٠) يَٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لِمَ تَكۡفُرُونَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَأَنتُمۡ تَشۡهَدُونَ

O People of the Book (Jews and Christians), why do you disbelieve in Allah's signs sent down to you that prove the prophethood of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, while you are witness to the fact that it is the truth presented in your (own revealed) books?

Points of Benefit:

All divine messages unanimously agreed on one fundamental principle: singling out Allah, the Exalted, and the prohibition of associating partners with Him.
The importance of knowing history as it could serve as strong evidence to refute the claims of the falsifiers.
The most deserving people of association with Abraham, peace be upon him, are those who follow his path and adhere to his beliefs. Mere claims of affiliation without adherence are of no benefit.
The verses indicate the eagerness of the disbelievers among the People of the Book to mislead the believers from this nation due to their envy.
