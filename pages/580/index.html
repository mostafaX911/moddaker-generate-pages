(٢٦) وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَٱسۡجُدۡ لَهُۥ وَسَبِّحۡهُ لَيۡلٗا طَوِيلًا

And remember Him with the two night prayers, maghrib (sunset) and 'ishaa` (evening). And get up for tahajjud [night prayer after waking up at night] after them.

(٢٧) إِنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ يُحِبُّونَ ٱلۡعَاجِلَةَ وَيَذَرُونَ وَرَآءَهُمۡ يَوۡمٗا ثَقِيلٗا

Indeed, these polytheists love the life of this world and covet it. They leave behind them the Day of Resurrection, which is a heavy day due to what it contains of adversities and tribulations.

(٢٨) نَّحۡنُ خَلَقۡنَٰهُمۡ وَشَدَدۡنَآ أَسۡرَهُمۡۖ وَإِذَا شِئۡنَا بَدَّلۡنَآ أَمۡثَٰلَهُمۡ تَبۡدِيلًا

We created them and strengthened their creation by reinforcing their joints, limbs, and otherwise. If We had willed their destruction and replacement with the likes of them, We would have destroyed and replaced them.

(٢٩) إِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦ تَذۡكِرَةٞۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ سَبِيلٗا

Indeed, this surah [chapter] is an admonishment and reminder. So whoever wishes to take a path that leads him to his Lord's gratification takes it.

(٣٠) وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمٗا

And you do not wish to take a path to Allah's gratification except that Allah wishes that from you, for the matter all goes back to Him. Indeed, Allah is knowledgeable of what befits His slaves and what does not befit them. [He is] wise in His creation, predetermination, and legislation.

(٣١) يُدۡخِلُ مَن يَشَآءُ فِي رَحۡمَتِهِۦۚ وَٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ أَعَدَّ لَهُمۡ عَذَابًا أَلِيمَۢا

He enters whom He wishes of His slaves into His mercy. So He makes them successful by leading them to faith and righteous action. And He prepared for those who wronged themselves with disbelief and acts of disobedience a painful torment in the Afterlife. It is the torment of the Fire.

The Emissaries (Surah Al-Mursalat)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Threatening deniers with destruction on the Day of Resurrection.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلۡمُرۡسَلَٰتِ عُرۡفٗا

Allah swore by the winds, which are continuous like the mane of a horse.

(٢) فَٱلۡعَٰصِفَٰتِ عَصۡفٗا

And swore by the winds of severe gusts.

(٣) وَٱلنَّٰشِرَٰتِ نَشۡرٗا

And swore by the winds that disperse the rain.

(٤) فَٱلۡفَٰرِقَٰتِ فَرۡقٗا

And swore by the angels who descend with what distinguishes between truth and falsehood.

(٥) فَٱلۡمُلۡقِيَٰتِ ذِكۡرًا

And swore by the angels who descend with revelation.

(٦) عُذۡرًا أَوۡ نُذۡرًا

They descend with revelation as an exoneration from Allah to the people and a warning to the people of the torment of Allah.

(٧) إِنَّمَا تُوعَدُونَ لَوَٰقِعٞ

Indeed, what you are promised of resurrection, account, and reward will undoubtedly occur and is inevitable.

(٨) فَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ طُمِسَتۡ

So if the stars' light is erased and their glow disappears.

(٩) وَإِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ فُرِجَتۡ

And the sky fractures due to the descent of the angels from it.

(١٠) وَإِذَا ٱلۡجِبَالُ نُسِفَتۡ

And the mountains are plucked from their places and then crumbled until they become dust.

(١١) وَإِذَا ٱلرُّسُلُ أُقِّتَتۡ

And if the messengers are gathered for a specified time.

(١٢) لِأَيِّ يَوۡمٍ أُجِّلَتۡ

For a great Day. They were postponed for testimony upon their nations.

(١٣) لِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ

For the Day of Separation between the slaves so that the rightful one becomes clear from the wrongful one, and the happy from the wretched.

(١٤) وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ

And what has informed you, O Messenger, what the Day of Separation is?!

(١٥) وَيۡلٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

Destruction, torment, and loss are on that Day for the rejectors who reject what the messengers came with from Allah.

(١٦) أَلَمۡ نُهۡلِكِ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

Did We not destroy the previous nations when they disbelieved in Allah and rejected His messengers?!

(١٧) ثُمَّ نُتۡبِعُهُمُ ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ

Then we make the rejectors of the later ones come after them, so we destroy them as we destroyed them [the previous nations].

(١٨) كَذَٰلِكَ نَفۡعَلُ بِٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ

Just like the destruction of those nations, we destroy the criminals rejecting what Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم came with.

(١٩) وَيۡلٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

Destruction, torment, and loss are on that day for those who reject Allah's threat of the criminal's torment.

Points of Benefit:

The danger of attachment to the world and forgetting the afterlife.
The slave's will is subordinate to Allah's will.
The destruction of the rejecting nations is an established divine practice.
