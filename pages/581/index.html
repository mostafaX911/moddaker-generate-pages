(٢٠) أَلَمۡ نَخۡلُقكُّم مِّن مَّآءٖ مَّهِينٖ

Did We not create you, O people, from a small amount of base water, which is the drop [of semen]?

(٢١) فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ فِي قَرَارٖ مَّكِينٍ

Then we placed that measly water in a secure place, which is the womb of the woman?

(٢٢) إِلَىٰ قَدَرٖ مَّعۡلُومٖ

For a known period, which is the period of pregnancy.

(٢٣) فَقَدَرۡنَا فَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡقَٰدِرُونَ

So we determined the form of the newborn, its size, color, and other than that? So how splendid determiners We are for all that.

(٢٤) وَيۡلٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

Destruction, torment, and loss are on that Day for the rejectors of Allah's ability.

(٢٥) أَلَمۡ نَجۡعَلِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ كِفَاتًا

Did We not make the Earth encompass all people,

(٢٦) أَحۡيَآءٗ وَأَمۡوَٰتٗا

Encompassing their living with residence upon it and its buildings, and their dead with burial within it,

(٢٧) وَجَعَلۡنَا فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ شَٰمِخَٰتٖ وَأَسۡقَيۡنَٰكُم مَّآءٗ فُرَاتٗا

And we made firm, tall mountains in it, preventing it from quaking, and gave you drink, O people, with sweet water? So the one who created that is not impeded from your resurrection.

(٢٨) وَيۡلٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

Destruction, torment, and loss are on that Day for the rejectors of Allah's bounties upon them.

(٢٩) ٱنطَلِقُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَا كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ

And will be said to the rejectors of what their messengers brought: Walk, O rejectors, to what you used to reject of the torment.

(٣٠) ٱنطَلِقُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ ظِلّٖ ذِي ثَلَٰثِ شُعَبٖ

Walk to shade of the Fire's smoke, separated into three bodies.

(٣١) لَّا ظَلِيلٖ وَلَا يُغۡنِي مِنَ ٱللَّهَبِ

There is no coolness of shade in it, and it does not prevent the fervor of the Fire and its heat from passing through to them.

(٣٢) إِنَّهَا تَرۡمِي بِشَرَرٖ كَٱلۡقَصۡرِ

Indeed, the Fire casts sparks. Each spark is like a castle in its greatness.

(٣٣) كَأَنَّهُۥ جِمَٰلَتٞ صُفۡرٞ

As if the sparks it casts, in its blackness and immensity, are like black camels with their colors leaning towards yellow.

(٣٤) وَيۡلٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

Destruction, torment, and loss are on that Day to the rejectors of Allah's torment.

(٣٥) هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ لَا يَنطِقُونَ

This is a Day in which they do not say anything.

(٣٦) وَلَا يُؤۡذَنُ لَهُمۡ فَيَعۡتَذِرُونَ

And it is not permitted for them to give excuses to their Lord for their disbelief and misdeeds such that they could give Him excuses.

(٣٧) وَيۡلٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

Destruction, torment, and loss are on that Day for the rejectors of the news of that Day.

(٣٨) هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَصۡلِۖ جَمَعۡنَٰكُمۡ وَٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

This is the Day of Separation between the creation. We gathered you and the previous nations on one ground.

(٣٩) فَإِن كَانَ لَكُمۡ كَيۡدٞ فَكِيدُونِ

So if you have a trick you can trick with for salvation from the torment of Allah, then trick Me.

(٤٠) وَيۡلٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

Destruction, torment, and loss are on that Day for the rejectors of the Day of Separation.

(٤١) إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي ظِلَٰلٖ وَعُيُونٖ

Indeed, those heedful of their Lord, by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, are amid the extensive shade of the trees of the Garden [of Paradise] and the springs of sweet flowing water.

(٤٢) وَفَوَٰكِهَ مِمَّا يَشۡتَهُونَ

And whatever fruits that they desire to eat.

(٤٣) كُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ هَنِيٓ‍َٔۢا بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ

It will be said to them: Eat from the wholesome things and drink a beverage that is easy to consume with no contaminants because of what you used to do in the worldly life of righteous deeds.

(٤٤) إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Indeed, just like this reward that We awarded you with, We reward the people of excellence for their deeds.

(٤٥) وَيۡلٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

Destruction, torment, and loss are on that Day for the rejectors of what Allah has prepared for the heedful.

(٤٦) كُلُواْ وَتَمَتَّعُواْ قَلِيلًا إِنَّكُم مُّجۡرِمُونَ

And it will be said to the rejectors: Eat and relish the pleasures of life for a short time in this world. Indeed, by disbelieving in Allah and rejecting His messengers, you are criminals.

(٤٧) وَيۡلٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

Destruction, torment, and loss are on that Day for the rejectors of their recompense on the Day of Judgment.

(٤٨) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱرۡكَعُواْ لَا يَرۡكَعُونَ

And if it is said to these rejectors: Pray to Allah, they do not pray to Him.

(٤٩) وَيۡلٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

Destruction, torment, and loss are on that Day for the rejectors who reject what the messengers came with from Allah.

(٥٠) فَبِأَيِّ حَدِيثِۢ بَعۡدَهُۥ يُؤۡمِنُونَ

So if they do not believe in this Quran, which was sent down from their Lord, then which other speech would they believe in?!

Points of Benefit:

Allah's care for the human being in their mother's belly.
The Earth's capacity for the living upon it and the dead within it.
The danger of rejecting the verses of Allah and the severe threat for the one who does that.
