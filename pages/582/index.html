The Tidings (Surah An-Naba)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Explaining the evidence for the ability to resurrect and instilling fear of the outcome.

Explanation:

(١) عَمَّ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ

What are these polytheists asking one another about after Allah has sent His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم to them?

(٢) عَنِ ٱلنَّبَإِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

They are asking one another about the great news, referring to this Quran, which was revealed upon their Messenger and contains news of the Resurrection.

(٣) ٱلَّذِي هُمۡ فِيهِ مُخۡتَلِفُونَ

They differed regarding this Quran, describing it as sorcery, poetry, fortune-telling, or legends of old.

(٤) كَلَّا سَيَعۡلَمُونَ

The matter is not as they claim. These deniers of the Quran will soon know the outcome of their evil denial.

(٥) ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَيَعۡلَمُونَ

Then that will be made certain for them.

(٦) أَلَمۡ نَجۡعَلِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِهَٰدٗا

Did We not spread the earth out for them, suitable for their residence?

(٧) وَٱلۡجِبَالَ أَوۡتَادٗا

And did We not put mountains on it like stakes to stop it from shaking?

(٨) وَخَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا

And We created you, O people, of different types: some of you are males while others are females.

(٩) وَجَعَلۡنَا نَوۡمَكُمۡ سُبَاتٗا

And We made your sleep as a break from your activity so that you may rest.

(١٠) وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلَّيۡلَ لِبَاسٗا

And We made the night a cover for you with its darkness, like the clothing you cover your private areas with.

(١١) وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلنَّهَارَ مَعَاشٗا

And We made the day as a period of earning and searching for a living.

(١٢) وَبَنَيۡنَا فَوۡقَكُمۡ سَبۡعٗا شِدَادٗا

And We made seven firm skies above you, perfected in construction.

(١٣) وَجَعَلۡنَا سِرَاجٗا وَهَّاجٗا

And We made the sun an extremely bright and radiant lamp.

(١٤) وَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلۡمُعۡصِرَٰتِ مَآءٗ ثَجَّاجٗا

And We sent down abundantly flowing water from clouds ready to rain.

(١٥) لِّنُخۡرِجَ بِهِۦ حَبّٗا وَنَبَاتٗا

So that through it, We can extract different types of grains and plants.

(١٦) وَجَنَّٰتٍ أَلۡفَافًا

And extract gardens that entwine with one another due to the severe overlapping of the branches of their trees.

When Allah mentioned these blessings that show His ability, He followed it up by mentioning the Revival and Resurrection because the one who can create these blessings can also resurrect the dead and hold them to account. He says:

(١٧) إِنَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ كَانَ مِيقَٰتٗا

Indeed, the Day of Separation between the creation is a fixed, appointed time that will not be postponed.

(١٨) يَوۡمَ يُنفَخُ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَتَأۡتُونَ أَفۡوَاجٗا

The Day the angel will blow into the horn again, upon which, O people, you shall come in many groups.

(١٩) وَفُتِحَتِ ٱلسَّمَآءُ فَكَانَتۡ أَبۡوَٰبٗا

And the sky will be opened, with crevices and gaps resembling open doors.

(٢٠) وَسُيِّرَتِ ٱلۡجِبَالُ فَكَانَتۡ سَرَابًا

And the mountains will be made to move until they become dispersed dust particles, becoming like a mirage.

(٢١) إِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ كَانَتۡ مِرۡصَادٗا

Indeed, Hell is watching, lying in wait.

(٢٢) لِّلطَّٰغِينَ مَ‍َٔابٗا

[It is] for the unjust a place of return which they will be made to return to.

(٢٣) لَّٰبِثِينَ فِيهَآ أَحۡقَابٗا

Wherein they shall dwell for ages and eons without end.

(٢٤) لَّا يَذُوقُونَ فِيهَا بَرۡدٗا وَلَا شَرَابًا

They will not experience cold breezes in it that cool them from the heat of the blaze, nor will they taste any drink they can enjoy.

(٢٥) إِلَّا حَمِيمٗا وَغَسَّاقٗا

They will only taste scorching hot water and the flowing pus of the people of the Fire.

(٢٦) جَزَآءٗ وِفَاقًا

As a recompense for their disbelief and deviance.

(٢٧) إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ لَا يَرۡجُونَ حِسَابٗا

Indeed, in the world, they did not fear Allah taking them to account in the Afterlife because they did not believe in the Resurrection. So, if they had feared the Resurrection, they would have believed in Allah and acted righteously.

(٢٨) وَكَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا كِذَّابٗا

And they vehemently rejected Our verses We revealed to Our Messenger.

(٢٩) وَكُلَّ شَيۡءٍ أَحۡصَيۡنَٰهُ كِتَٰبٗا

We recorded and counted all their deeds, which are written in their book of deeds.

(٣٠) فَذُوقُواْ فَلَن نَّزِيدَكُمۡ إِلَّا عَذَابًا

Endure this everlasting torment, O transgressors, for We will not increase you in anything except torment upon your anguish.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's control over His creation shows that He can restore it.
Oppression is a reason to enter the Fire.
The multiplying disbelievers' torment.
