(٣١) إِنَّ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ مَفَازًا

Indeed, those mindful of their Lord by following His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, there is a place of triumph they sought to attain: Paradise.

(٣٢) حَدَآئِقَ وَأَعۡنَٰبٗا

It is full of orchards and grapevines,

(٣٣) وَكَوَاعِبَ أَتۡرَابٗا

Full-bosomed women of equal age,

(٣٤) وَكَأۡسٗا دِهَاقٗا

And full glasses of wine.

(٣٥) لَّا يَسۡمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغۡوٗا وَلَا كِذَّٰبٗا

In Paradise, they will not hear any false speech. They will neither hear lies, nor will they lie to one another.

(٣٦) جَزَآءٗ مِّن رَّبِّكَ عَطَآءً حِسَابٗا

All of that will be from what Allah will give them in graciousness and benevolence, which will be sufficient for them.

(٣٧) رَّبِّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِۖ لَا يَمۡلِكُونَ مِنۡهُ خِطَابٗا

[He is] the Lord of the heavens, the earth, and whatever is between them; the Most Merciful of this world and the hereafter. No one on earth or in the heavens can ask Him except those whom He permits.

(٣٨) يَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلرُّوحُ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ صَفّٗاۖ لَّا يَتَكَلَّمُونَ إِلَّا مَنۡ أَذِنَ لَهُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وَقَالَ صَوَابٗا

On the Day when Gabriel and the angels will stand in rows, not speaking to intercede on behalf of anyone except whom the Most Merciful permits them to intercede for and utters a true statement (e.g., the statement proclaiming the oneness of Allah).

(٣٩) ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ مَ‍َٔابًا

That which has been described to you is the day that will come without a doubt. Therefore, whoever wishes salvation from Allah's torment on that day should take the path of performing good deeds that will please his Lord.

(٤٠) إِنَّآ أَنذَرۡنَٰكُمۡ عَذَابٗا قَرِيبٗا يَوۡمَ يَنظُرُ ٱلۡمَرۡءُ مَا قَدَّمَتۡ يَدَاهُ وَيَقُولُ ٱلۡكَافِرُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي كُنتُ تُرَٰبَۢا

O people, I have indeed warned you of an impending torment that will occur on the day when a person sees what deeds he put forth in the world. The disbeliever will say, wishing to be rid of the torment: If only I had become dust like the animals when it is said to them [the animals] on the Day of Resurrection: Become dust.

Those Who Drag Forth (Surah An-Nazi'at)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Reminding of Allah and the Last Day.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلنَّٰزِعَٰتِ غَرۡقٗا

Allah swore by the angels who pull out the souls of the disbelievers harshly and severely.

(٢) وَٱلنَّٰشِطَٰتِ نَشۡطٗا

And He also swore by the angels who gently remove the souls of the believers easily and smoothly.

(٣) وَٱلسَّٰبِحَٰتِ سَبۡحٗا

He also swore by the angels who wade between heaven and earth with Allah's command.

(٤) فَٱلسَّٰبِقَٰتِ سَبۡقٗا

And He swore by the angels who compete with one another to carry out Allah's command.

(٥) فَٱلۡمُدَبِّرَٰتِ أَمۡرٗا

He also swore by the angels who execute Allah's command (i.e., the angels appointed to [record] the deeds of the slaves). He swore by all of those matters that He will certainly resurrect people for reckoning and recompense.

(٦) يَوۡمَ تَرۡجُفُ ٱلرَّاجِفَةُ

On the Day when the earth will tremble upon the first blowing of the horn,

(٧) تَتۡبَعُهَا ٱلرَّادِفَةُ

The second blow will follow it.

(٨) قُلُوبٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ وَاجِفَةٌ

The hearts of disbelievers and the wicked will be fearful on that day.

(٩) أَبۡصَٰرُهَا خَٰشِعَةٞ

The effects of humiliation will appear on their faces.

(١٠) يَقُولُونَ أَءِنَّا لَمَرۡدُودُونَ فِي ٱلۡحَافِرَةِ

They used to say: Will we live again after we die?!

(١١) أَءِذَا كُنَّا عِظَٰمٗا نَّخِرَةٗ

When we become decomposed, empty bones, will we return after that?!

(١٢) قَالُواْ تِلۡكَ إِذٗا كَرَّةٌ خَاسِرَةٞ

They would say: If we return, that return will be a loss, and the person returning will be beguiled.

(١٣) فَإِنَّمَا هِيَ زَجۡرَةٞ وَٰحِدَةٞ

The matter of the Resurrection is easy, for it will only be a single scream from the angel appointed to blow.

(١٤) فَإِذَا هُم بِٱلسَّاهِرَةِ

Then suddenly, everyone will be alive on the face of the earth after they were dead within it.

(١٥) هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ مُوسَىٰٓ

O Messenger, has the news of Moses with his Lord, and his enemy, Pharaoh, come to you?!

(١٦) إِذۡ نَادَىٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ بِٱلۡوَادِ ٱلۡمُقَدَّسِ طُوًى

When his Lord (may He be Exalted) called out to him at the sanctified Valley of Tuwa.

Points of Benefit:

Being heedful is a reason to enter Paradise.
Reminding of the horrors of the [Day of] Resurrection is motivation to do righteous deeds.
The disbeliever's soul is seized forcefully and violently, but the believer's soul is taken gently and softly.
