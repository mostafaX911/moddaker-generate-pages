(١٧) ٱذۡهَبۡ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّهُۥ طَغَىٰ

Among what He told him [was]: Go to Pharaoh; indeed, he has overstepped the limits in injustice and arrogance.

(١٨) فَقُلۡ هَل لَّكَ إِلَىٰٓ أَن تَزَكَّىٰ

So say to him: O Pharaoh, do you want to purify yourself from disbelief and acts of disobedience?

(١٩) وَأَهۡدِيَكَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَتَخۡشَىٰ

And I can direct you to your Lord, who created and nurtured you, so you fear Him, do what makes Him content, and avoid what angers Him.

(٢٠) فَأَرَىٰهُ ٱلۡأٓيَةَ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰ

Moses (peace be upon him) then showed him the great sign indicating he was a messenger from his Lord: the Hand and the Staff.

(٢١) فَكَذَّبَ وَعَصَىٰ

So Pharaoh did nothing except reject this sign and disobeyed what Moses (peace be upon him) commanded him with.

(٢٢) ثُمَّ أَدۡبَرَ يَسۡعَىٰ

Then he turned away from belief in what Moses (peace be upon him) brought, striving to disobey Allah and oppose the truth.

(٢٣) فَحَشَرَ فَنَادَىٰ

Then he gathered his people and followers to overpower Moses (peace be upon him), so he called out, saying:

(٢٤) فَقَالَ أَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ

I am your Lord, the Most High, and there is no obedience incumbent upon you to anyone other than Me.

(٢٥) فَأَخَذَهُ ٱللَّهُ نَكَالَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَٱلۡأُولَىٰٓ

So, Allah seized and punished him in the world by drowning him in the sea, and He will punish him in the Afterlife by entering him into the severest torment.

(٢٦) إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَعِبۡرَةٗ لِّمَن يَخۡشَىٰٓ

Indeed, in Our punishing of Pharaoh in this world and the Afterlife, there is certainly an admonition for the one who fears Allah because he benefits from [such] admonitions.

(٢٧) ءَأَنتُمۡ أَشَدُّ خَلۡقًا أَمِ ٱلسَّمَآءُۚ بَنَىٰهَا

O rejectors of resurrection: Is your origination, or the origination of the heavens that He built, harder for Allah?

(٢٨) رَفَعَ سَمۡكَهَا فَسَوَّىٰهَا

He made the pinnacle of the heavens vertically high and leveled: there are no breaks, cracks, or defects in them.

(٢٩) وَأَغۡطَشَ لَيۡلَهَا وَأَخۡرَجَ ضُحَىٰهَا

And He darkened [the sky's] night when the sun sets and manifested its light when it rises.

(٣٠) وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ دَحَىٰهَآ

As for the earth, after He created the heavens, He spread it out and placed its benefits within it.

(٣١) أَخۡرَجَ مِنۡهَا مَآءَهَا وَمَرۡعَىٰهَا

He extracted water from it as flowing springs and made plants grow on it, which the livestock graze on.

(٣٢) وَٱلۡجِبَالَ أَرۡسَىٰهَا

And He made the mountains firm on the earth.

(٣٣) مَتَٰعٗا لَّكُمۡ وَ لِأَنۡعَٰمِكُمۡ

All of that is for your benefit, O people, and for [the benefit] of your livestock. For the One who created all this is not incapable of recreating you anew.

(٣٤) فَإِذَا جَآءَتِ ٱلطَّآمَّةُ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰ

Alas, the Resurrection will occur when the second blow [of the trumpet] comes, covering everything with its terror.

(٣٥) يَوۡمَ يَتَذَكَّرُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا سَعَىٰ

On the day when it comes, a person will remember the work they put forth, whether good or evil.

(٣٦) وَبُرِّزَتِ ٱلۡجَحِيمُ لِمَن يَرَىٰ

And Hell will be brought and unveiled clearly for all to see.

(٣٧) فَأَمَّا مَن طَغَىٰ

As for the one who transgressed the limits of deviance.

(٣٨) وَءَاثَرَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا

And favored the temporary worldly life over the everlasting Afterlife.

(٣٩) فَإِنَّ ٱلۡجَحِيمَ هِيَ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ

Then indeed, the Fire is his abode he will take shelter in.

(٤٠) وَأَمَّا مَنۡ خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِۦ وَنَهَى ٱلنَّفۡسَ عَنِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰ

And as for the one who feared his standing before his Lord and stopped his soul from following its desires from what Allah prohibited, then Paradise is his abode that he will take shelter in.

(٤١) فَإِنَّ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ هِيَ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ

And as for the one who feared his standing before his Lord and stopped his soul from following its desires from what Allah prohibited, then Paradise is his abode that he will take shelter in.

(٤٢) يَسۡ‍َٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلسَّاعَةِ أَيَّانَ مُرۡسَىٰهَا

O Messenger, these rejectors of Resurrection ask you: When will the Hour occur?

(٤٣) فِيمَ أَنتَ مِن ذِكۡرَىٰهَآ

You do not have any knowledge regarding it such that you can mention it to them, nor is that an affair of yours. Your affair is only to prepare for it.

(٤٤) إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ مُنتَهَىٰهَآ

The total knowledge of the Hour is with your Lord alone.

(٤٥) إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مُنذِرُ مَن يَخۡشَىٰهَا

You are only a warner to the one who fears the Hour because he is the one who benefits from your warning.

(٤٦) كَأَنَّهُمۡ يَوۡمَ يَرَوۡنَهَا لَمۡ يَلۡبَثُوٓاْ إِلَّا عَشِيَّةً أَوۡ ضُحَىٰهَا

It will be as if, on the day they see the Hour, [truly] witnessing [it], that they did not remain in their worldly lives except for a single evening or morning.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation to be gentle when calling someone to Allah.
Fearing Allah and keeping oneself from desires are among the reasons to enter Paradise.
Knowledge of the Hour is from the affairs of the unseen, which nobody knows except Allah.
Allah's explanation of the details of the creation of the Earth and sky.
