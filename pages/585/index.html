He Frowned (Surah 'Abasa)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Reminding the disbelievers who think they do not need their Lord through evidence for the Resurrection.

Explanation:

(١) عَبَسَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ

The Messenger of Allah's face صلى الله عليه وسلم frowned and he turned away.

(٢) أَن جَآءَهُ ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ

Because of the arrival of Abdullah bin Umm Maktoom, who came asking for guidance. He was blind and had come when the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم was busy with the chiefs of the polytheists, hoping they would be guided.

(٣) وَمَا يُدۡرِيكَ لَعَلَّهُۥ يَزَّكَّىٰٓ

O Messenger, how would you know; perhaps this blind man could be purified of his sins!

(٤) أَوۡ يَذَّكَّرُ فَتَنفَعَهُ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰٓ

Or that he may take heed of the admonitions he hears from you so he benefits from them?

(٥) أَمَّا مَنِ ٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰ

As for the one who feels he does not need to believe in what you have come with due to the wealth he possesses,

(٦) فَأَنتَ لَهُۥ تَصَدَّىٰ

You approach and stand before him.

(٧) وَمَا عَلَيۡكَ أَلَّا يَزَّكَّىٰ

What [blame] will there be on you if he is not purified of his sins for failing to repent to Allah?

(٨) وَأَمَّا مَن جَآءَكَ يَسۡعَىٰ

As for the one who hastens toward you, searching for virtue,

(٩) وَهُوَ يَخۡشَىٰ

Fearing his Lord,

(١٠) فَأَنتَ عَنۡهُ تَلَهَّىٰ

You are preoccupied against him with others among the senior polytheists.

(١١) كَلَّآ إِنَّهَا تَذۡكِرَةٞ

The matter is not like that. It (the Quran) is only an admonition and a reminder for whoever accepts it.

(١٢) فَمَن شَآءَ ذَكَرَهُۥ

So, whoever wishes to remember Allah remembers Him and takes heed of what is in this Quran.

(١٣) فِي صُحُفٖ مُّكَرَّمَةٖ

For this Quran is in honorable pages with the angels,

(١٤) مَّرۡفُوعَةٖ مُّطَهَّرَةِۢ

Raised in a high place, pure; no impurity or filth reaches them.

(١٥) بِأَيۡدِي سَفَرَةٖ

And they are in the hands of angelic messengers.

(١٦) كِرَامِۢ بَرَرَةٖ

They are honorable in the eyes of their Lord, frequent performers of goodness and acts of obedience.

(١٧) قُتِلَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَآ أَكۡفَرَهُۥ

The disbelieving human is cursed; how severe is his disbelief in Allah?

(١٨) مِنۡ أَيِّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقَهُۥ

From what did Allah create him such that he be arrogant on earth and disbelieve in Him?

(١٩) مِن نُّطۡفَةٍ خَلَقَهُۥ فَقَدَّرَهُۥ

He created him from a little bit of water, then determined his creation stage after stage.

(٢٠) ثُمَّ ٱلسَّبِيلَ يَسَّرَهُۥ

Then, after these stages, He made the exit from his mother's belly easy for him.

(٢١) ثُمَّ أَمَاتَهُۥ فَأَقۡبَرَهُۥ

Then, after what He decreed of life for him, He caused him to die. Then He made a grave for him, which he remains in until he is resurrected.

(٢٢) ثُمَّ إِذَا شَآءَ أَنشَرَهُۥ

Then, when He wishes, He will resurrect him for reckoning and recompense.

(٢٣) كَلَّا لَمَّا يَقۡضِ مَآ أَمَرَهُۥ

It is not like this disbeliever imagines: that he has fulfilled Allah's rights upon him, for he has not fulfilled the obligations Allah mandated upon him.

(٢٤) فَلۡيَنظُرِ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِلَىٰ طَعَامِهِۦٓ

So, let the human who disbelieves in Allah look at his food which he eats: how did it occur?

(٢٥) أَنَّا صَبَبۡنَا ٱلۡمَآءَ صَبّٗا

It originates from the rain descending from the sky with force and abundance.

(٢٦) ثُمَّ شَقَقۡنَا ٱلۡأَرۡضَ شَقّٗا

Then, We part the earth, splitting it open through [the sprouting of] plants.

(٢٧) فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا حَبّٗا

Then We grow therein grains of wheat, corn, and other [crops].

(٢٨) وَعِنَبٗا وَقَضۡبٗا

And We grow therein fresh grapes and alfalfa as fodder for their livestock.

(٢٩) وَزَيۡتُونٗا وَنَخۡلٗا

And We grow therein olives and date palms.

(٣٠) وَحَدَآئِقَ غُلۡبٗا

And We grow therein orchards of abundant trees.

(٣١) وَفَٰكِهَةٗ وَأَبّٗا

And We grow therein fruits and that which your livestock graze.

(٣٢) مَّتَٰعٗا لَّكُمۡ وَلِأَنۡعَٰمِكُمۡ

For your benefit and the benefit of your livestock.

(٣٣) فَإِذَا جَآءَتِ ٱلصَّآخَّةُ

So, when the great scream comes, that will deafen the ears: the second blowing of the trumpet.

(٣٤) يَوۡمَ يَفِرُّ ٱلۡمَرۡءُ مِنۡ أَخِيهِ

On that Day, man will flee from his brother.

(٣٥) وَأُمِّهِۦ وَأَبِيهِ

And he will flee from his mother and father.

(٣٦) وَصَٰحِبَتِهِۦ وَبَنِيهِ

And he will flee from his wife and children.

(٣٧) لِكُلِّ ٱمۡرِيٕٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ شَأۡنٞ يُغۡنِيهِ

Each one of them will have what distracts them from the other due to the extreme despair that day.

(٣٨) وُجُوهٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ مُّسۡفِرَةٞ

The faces of the blissful on that day will be luminous,

(٣٩) ضَاحِكَةٞ مُّسۡتَبۡشِرَةٞ

Laughing, and delighted with what Allah had prepared for them from His mercy.

(٤٠) وَوُجُوهٞ يَوۡمَئِذٍ عَلَيۡهَا غَبَرَةٞ

And the faces of the wretched will have dust on them on that day.

Points of Benefit:

Allah, the Exalted, admonishing His Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم regarding the issue of Abdullah bin Umm Maktoom (may Allah be pleased with him) shows that the Quran is from Allah.
Taking care of the seeker of knowledge and the seeker of guidance.
The severity of the horrors on the Day of Resurrection as a person will only be concerned with himself. Even the Prophets will say: Myself, myself.
