Darkness will come over them (their faces).

(٤٢) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡكَفَرَةُ ٱلۡفَجَرَةُ

The Overthrowing (Surah At-Takwir)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

The perfection of the Quran in reminding the soul of the disruption of the universe on [the Day of] Resurrection.

Explanation:

(١) إِذَا ٱلشَّمۡسُ كُوِّرَتۡ

When the mass of the sun is gathered, and its light vanishes.

(٢) وَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ ٱنكَدَرَتۡ

And when the stars fall one after the other, and their light is erased.

(٣) وَإِذَا ٱلۡجِبَالُ سُيِّرَتۡ

And when the mountains are moved from their places.

(٤) وَإِذَا ٱلۡعِشَارُ عُطِّلَتۡ

And when the pregnant she-camels whose people once used to compete with one another for will be neglected and abandoned.

(٥) وَإِذَا ٱلۡوُحُوشُ حُشِرَتۡ

And when wild animals are gathered with human beings on one plain.

(٦) وَإِذَا ٱلۡبِحَارُ سُجِّرَتۡ

And when the seas are lit until they become a fire.

(٧) وَإِذَا ٱلنُّفُوسُ زُوِّجَتۡ

And when souls are paired with their like: a sinner will be paired with a sinner, and a pious person will be paired with a pious person.

(٨) وَإِذَا ٱلۡمَوۡءُۥدَةُ سُئِلَتۡ

And when the girl buried alive is asked by Allah.

(٩) بِأَيِّ ذَنۢبٖ قُتِلَتۡ

For what crime were you killed by the person who killed you?

(١٠) وَإِذَا ٱلصُّحُفُ نُشِرَتۡ

And when the slaves' pages of deeds are spread so that each person can read their book of deeds.

(١١) وَإِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ كُشِطَتۡ

And when the sky is peeled like the skin is peeled away from a sheep.

(١٢) وَإِذَا ٱلۡجَحِيمُ سُعِّرَتۡ

And when the Fire is kindled.

(١٣) وَإِذَا ٱلۡجَنَّةُ أُزۡلِفَتۡ

And when Paradise is brought near to the heedful.

(١٤) عَلِمَتۡ نَفۡسٞ مَّآ أَحۡضَرَتۡ

When that occurs, every soul will know what actions it put forth for that day.

(١٥) فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِٱلۡخُنَّسِ

Allah swore by the hidden stars before their emergence at night,

(١٦) ٱلۡجَوَارِ ٱلۡكُنَّسِ

Following their orbits, and disappearing upon the morning's emergence, like an antelope entering its den, i.e., it's home.

(١٧) وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا عَسۡعَسَ

And He swore by the first part of the night when it approaches and the last part when it recedes.

(١٨) وَٱلصُّبۡحِ إِذَا تَنَفَّسَ

And He swore by the morning when its light emerges.

(١٩) إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلُ رَسُولٖ كَرِيمٖ

Indeed, the Quran sent down to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم is Allah's speech that was conveyed by a trustworthy angel, Gabriel (peace be upon him), whom Allah entrusted it to.

(٢٠) ذِي قُوَّةٍ عِندَ ذِي ٱلۡعَرۡشِ مَكِينٖ

One who possesses strength and great rank with the Lord of the Throne, may He be Exalted.

(٢١) مُّطَاعٖ ثَمَّ أَمِينٖ

The inhabitants of the heavens obey him. [He is] entrusted with what he receives from revelation.

(٢٢) وَمَا صَاحِبُكُم بِمَجۡنُونٖ

Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, who is in your company and whose intellect, trustworthiness, and truthfulness you know, is not insane as you slanderously claim.

(٢٣) وَلَقَدۡ رَءَاهُ بِٱلۡأُفُقِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ

Your companion has seen Gabriel in the form that he was created in the clear sky's horizon.

(٢٤) وَمَا هُوَ عَلَى ٱلۡغَيۡبِ بِضَنِينٖ

Your companion is not miserly with you [by] not conveying what he was commanded to convey to you, nor does he take any reward as the fortune-tellers take.

(٢٥) وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوۡلِ شَيۡطَٰنٖ رَّجِيمٖ

This Quran is not the speech of a devil banished from Allah's mercy.

(٢٦) فَأَيۡنَ تَذۡهَبُونَ

So which path will you tread to deny it is from Allah after all these proofs?

(٢٧) إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرٞ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

The Quran is only a reminder and admonition for the Jinn and humanity.

(٢٨) لِمَن شَآءَ مِنكُمۡ أَن يَسۡتَقِيمَ

For whomever of you wishes to be upright on the path of truth.

(٢٩) وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

And you do not wish for uprightness or otherwise except that Allah, the Lord of all creation, wishes that.

Points of Benefit:

The person will be gathered with those like him, good or evil.
If the baby girl buried alive will be questioned, then how about the one who buried her? This is evidence of how great the situation is.
The slave's will is subordinate to Allah's will.
