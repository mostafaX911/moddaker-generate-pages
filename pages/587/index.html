The Cleaving (Surah Al-Infitar)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Warning mankind from being deceived and forgetting the Day of Resurrection.

Explanation:

(١) إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنفَطَرَتۡ

When the sky splits apart because of the angels descending from it.

(٢) وَإِذَا ٱلۡكَوَاكِبُ ٱنتَثَرَتۡ

And when the stars fall one after the other, becoming scattered.

(٣) وَإِذَا ٱلۡبِحَارُ فُجِّرَتۡ

And when the seas open into one another and merge.

(٤) وَإِذَا ٱلۡقُبُورُ بُعۡثِرَتۡ

And when the soil of the graves overturns due to the resurrection of the dead from them.

(٥) عَلِمَتۡ نَفۡسٞ مَّا قَدَّمَتۡ وَأَخَّرَتۡ

Upon that, every soul will know what it put forth of action and what it left behind of unperformed acts.

(٦) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ

O man who has rejected his Lord, what has made you go against the command of your Lord when He gave you respite and did not hasten your punishment out of His grace?

(٧) ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكَ فَسَوَّىٰكَ فَعَدَلَكَ

The One who brought you into existence after you were non-existent and made your limbs balanced and proportional.

(٨) فِيٓ أَيِّ صُورَةٖ مَّا شَآءَ رَكَّبَكَ

He created you in whatever form He wished to create you. He has favored you as He did not create you in the form of a donkey, monkey, dog, or something else.

(٩) كَلَّا بَلۡ تُكَذِّبُونَ بِٱلدِّينِ

The matter is not as you perceive, O deluded ones. Rather, you reject the Day of Recompense, failing to work for it.

(١٠) وَإِنَّ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لَحَٰفِظِينَ

Indeed, upon you are angels recording your deeds.

(١١) كِرَامٗا كَٰتِبِينَ

Noble in Allah's sight, scribes who write down your deeds.

(١٢) يَعۡلَمُونَ مَا تَفۡعَلُونَ

They know of every deed you do, then they write it down.

(١٣) إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ لَفِي نَعِيمٖ

Indeed, those who are abundant in virtuous acts and obedience will certainly be in everlasting bliss on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٤) وَإِنَّ ٱلۡفُجَّارَ لَفِي جَحِيمٖ

And indeed, the people of depravity will certainly be in a fire blazing over them.

(١٥) يَصۡلَوۡنَهَا يَوۡمَ ٱلدِّينِ

They will enter it on the Day of Recompense, suffering from its heat.

(١٦) وَمَا هُمۡ عَنۡهَا بِغَآئِبِينَ

They will never be absent from it. Instead, they will remain in it forever.

(١٧) وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ

What will inform you, O Messenger, what the Day of Judgment is?

(١٨) ثُمَّ مَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ

Then [once again], what will inform you what the Day of Judgment is?

(١٩) يَوۡمَ لَا تَمۡلِكُ نَفۡسٞ لِّنَفۡسٖ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۖ وَٱلۡأَمۡرُ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلَّهِ

The Day when no one will be able to benefit anyone. The entire matter on that day will belong to Allah alone, acting as He wishes and no one else besides Him.

The Defrauders (Surah Al-Mutaffifeen)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Warning the rejecting wrongdoers of the Day of Resurrection and giving the believers glad tidings of it.

Explanation:

(١) وَيۡلٞ لِّلۡمُطَفِّفِينَ

Destruction and loss be upon those who shortchange.

(٢) ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ٱكۡتَالُواْ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ يَسۡتَوۡفُونَ

They are the ones who, when they have something measured by others, take their full right without any deficiency.

(٣) وَإِذَا كَالُوهُمۡ أَو وَّزَنُوهُمۡ يُخۡسِرُونَ

And if they measure or weigh for others, they shortchange in volume and weight. That was the state of Medina's people upon the Prophet's صلى الله عليه وسلم migration to them.

(٤) أَلَا يَظُنُّ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَنَّهُم مَّبۡعُوثُونَ

Do these people who do this evil act not know they will be resurrected before Allah?!

Points of Benefit:

Warning from arrogance, which prevents one from following the Truth.
Greed is a reprehensible characteristic for a trader to have, and only those who fear Allah are safe from it.
Remembering the horrors of the [Day of] Resurrection is one of the greatest deterrents from disobedience.
