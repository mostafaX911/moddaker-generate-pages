(٥) لِيَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ

For reckoning and recompense on a great day, due to the tribulations and horrors it contains.

(٦) يَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلنَّاسُ لِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

The Day when people will stand before the Lord of all creation to face reckoning.

(٧) كَلَّآ إِنَّ كِتَٰبَ ٱلۡفُجَّارِ لَفِي سِجِّينٖ

The matter is not as you imagined that there is no resurrection after death. The book of the people of depravity from among the disbelievers and hypocrites will surely be in loss in the nethermost depth of the earth.

(٨) وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا سِجِّينٞ

What will inform you, O Messenger, what Sijjīn is?

(٩) كِتَٰبٞ مَّرۡقُومٞ

Indeed, their book is written and will not vanish. There will be nothing added or omitted from it.

(١٠) وَيۡلٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

Destruction and loss will be for the rejectors on that day.

(١١) ٱلَّذِينَ يُكَذِّبُونَ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ

Those who reject the Day of Recompense in which Allah requites His slaves for their actions in the world.

(١٢) وَمَا يُكَذِّبُ بِهِۦٓ إِلَّا كُلُّ مُعۡتَدٍ أَثِيمٍ

None rejects that day except every person who transgresses Allah's limits with abundant sins.

(١٣) إِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَٰتُنَا قَالَ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ

When Our verses sent down to Our Messenger are recited to him, he (the rejector) says: They are stories of the earlier nations and are not from Allah.

(١٤) كَلَّاۖ بَلۡۜ رَانَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ

The matter is not as these rejectors envision. Instead, the acts of disobedience they used to gain overtook and shrouded their minds, so they did not see the truth with their hearts.

(١٥) كَلَّآ إِنَّهُمۡ عَن رَّبِّهِمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لَّمَحۡجُوبُونَ

Indeed, they will surely be prevented from seeing their Lord on the Day of Resurrection.

(١٦) ثُمَّ إِنَّهُمۡ لَصَالُواْ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ

Then, indeed, they will enter the Fire, suffering its heat.

(١٧) ثُمَّ يُقَالُ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ

Then on the Day of Resurrection, it will be said to them in rebuke: This torment that you faced is what you used to deny in the world when your Messenger informed you of it.

(١٨) كَلَّآ إِنَّ كِتَٰبَ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارِ لَفِي عِلِّيِّينَ

The matter is not as you perceived in that there will be no reckoning nor recompense. Indeed, the book of obedient people will be in 'Illiyyun.

(١٩) وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا عِلِّيُّونَ

What will inform you, O Messenger, what 'Illiyyun is?

(٢٠) كِتَٰبٞ مَّرۡقُومٞ

Indeed, their book is written and will not vanish. There will be nothing added or omitted from it.

(٢١) يَشۡهَدُهُ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَ

This book is witnessed by the angels near [to Allah] from every heaven.

(٢٢) إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ لَفِي نَعِيمٍ

Indeed, those who do abundant acts of obedience will be in everlasting bliss on the Day of Resurrection.

(٢٣) عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِ يَنظُرُونَ

On adorned couches, looking at their Lord and at everything that delights their souls and makes them happy.

(٢٤) تَعۡرِفُ فِي وُجُوهِهِمۡ نَضۡرَةَ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

If you see them, you will see in their faces the effect of gratification in all its beauty and splendor.

(٢٥) يُسۡقَوۡنَ مِن رَّحِيقٖ مَّخۡتُومٍ

Their slaves will give them drinks of wine whose containers are sealed.

(٢٦) خِتَٰمُهُۥ مِسۡكٞۚ وَفِي ذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَتَنَافَسِ ٱلۡمُتَنَٰفِسُونَ

It will give off the fragrance of musk until its very end. For this noble reward, the competitors must compete by doing what Allah is content with, and leaving what earns His discontent and anger.

(٢٧) وَمِزَاجُهُۥ مِن تَسۡنِيمٍ

This sealed drink will be mixed from the spring of Tasnim.

(٢٨) عَيۡنٗا يَشۡرَبُ بِهَا ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَ

This is a spring in the highest part of Paradise, from which those near [to Allah] will drink, pure and untainted. The rest of the believers will drink from it, [but it will be] mixed with other than it.

(٢٩) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أَجۡرَمُواْ كَانُواْ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ يَضۡحَكُونَ

Indeed, those who committed crimes with what they were upon of disbelief used to laugh mockingly at those who believed.

(٣٠) وَإِذَا مَرُّواْ بِهِمۡ يَتَغَامَزُونَ

When they passed by the believers, they would wink at one another in ridicule and derision.

(٣١) وَإِذَا ٱنقَلَبُوٓاْ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِمُ ٱنقَلَبُواْ فَكِهِينَ

When they would return to their families, they would return happy with what they were upon of disbelief and ridicule of the believers.

(٣٢) وَإِذَا رَأَوۡهُمۡ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ لَضَآلُّونَ

When they watched the Muslims, they would say: Indeed, these people are surely astray from the path of truth, as they abandoned the religion of their forefathers.

(٣٣) وَمَآ أُرۡسِلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَٰفِظِينَ

Allah did not appoint them to preserve their deeds such that they could make this statement of theirs.

Points of Benefit:

The danger of sins to the heart.
The disbelievers being deprived of seeing their Lord on the Day of Resurrection.
Mocking people following the religion is one of the characteristics of the disbelievers.
