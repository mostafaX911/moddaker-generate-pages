(٣٤) فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡكُفَّارِ يَضۡحَكُونَ

So, on the Day of Resurrection, those who believed in Allah will laugh at the disbelievers just as the disbelievers used to laugh at them in the world.

(٣٥) عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِ يَنظُرُونَ

On adorned couches, they will look at what Allah prepared for them of everlasting bliss.

(٣٦) هَلۡ ثُوِّبَ ٱلۡكُفَّارُ مَا كَانُواْ يَفۡعَلُونَ

The disbelievers will have been awarded the humiliating torment for their [evil] deeds in the worldly life.

The Sundering (Surah Al-Inshiqaq)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Reminding the human being of their return to their Lord, demonstrating their weakness and fluctuating situation.

Explanation:

(١) إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنشَقَّتۡ

When the sky ruptures because of the angels descending from it

(٢) وَأَذِنَتۡ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتۡ

And listens submissively to its Lord, as it rightly should.

(٣) وَإِذَا ٱلۡأَرۡضُ مُدَّتۡ

When Allah stretches the earth as leather is stretched.

(٤) وَأَلۡقَتۡ مَا فِيهَا وَتَخَلَّتۡ

And [the earth] throws out the treasures and the dead in them, emptying itself of them.

(٥) وَأَذِنَتۡ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتۡ

And listens submissively to its Lord, as it rightly should.

(٦) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدۡحٗا فَمُلَٰقِيهِ

O human being, verily, you are either doing good or evil and will face it on the Day of Resurrection so that Allah may recompense you for it.

After describing humanity's acts in general, Allah proceeds to provide details about the state of those who performed good deeds on the Day of Resurrection, stating:

(٧) فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ

As for the one given his book of deeds in his right hand.

(٨) فَسَوۡفَ يُحَاسَبُ حِسَابٗا يَسِيرٗا

He will have an easy reckoning with Allah, and his actions will be presented to Him without his being taken into account.

(٩) وَيَنقَلِبُ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ مَسۡرُورٗا

And he will return to his family delighted.

(١٠) وَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ وَرَآءَ ظَهۡرِهِۦ

As for the one who is given his book in his left hand behind his back.

(١١) فَسَوۡفَ يَدۡعُواْ ثُبُورٗا

He will call out for destruction to himself.

(١٢) وَيَصۡلَىٰ سَعِيرًا

And he will enter the Fire and be made to suffer its heat.

(١٣) إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ فِيٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ مَسۡرُورًا

He was amongst his family rejoicing over his acts of disbelief and sins.

(١٤) إِنَّهُۥ ظَنَّ أَن لَّن يَحُورَ

He thought that he would not come back to life after death.

(١٥) بَلَىٰٓۚ إِنَّ رَبَّهُۥ كَانَ بِهِۦ بَصِيرٗا

On the contrary, Allah will bring him back to life just as He created him the first time. Indeed, His Lord was watchful over his condition. Nothing of that was hidden from Him, and He will recompense him for his actions.

(١٦) فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِٱلشَّفَقِ

Allah swore by the redness of the horizon after sunset.

(١٧) وَٱلَّيۡلِ وَمَا وَسَقَ

And He swore by the night and what is gathered therein.

(١٨) وَٱلۡقَمَرِ إِذَا ٱتَّسَقَ

And [by] the moon, when it comes together and is complete, becomes a full moon.

(١٩) لَتَرۡكَبُنَّ طَبَقًا عَن طَبَقٖ

You, O people, will surely mount upon (i.e., pass through) state after state: from a drop, then a clot, then a piece of flesh, then life, then death, and then resurrection.

(٢٠) فَمَا لَهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ

What is the matter with these disbelievers who do not have faith in Allah and the Last Day?!

(٢١) وَإِذَا قُرِئَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ لَا يَسۡجُدُونَۤ۩

And when the Quran is recited to them, they do not prostrate to their Lord?!

(٢٢) بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يُكَذِّبُونَ

Instead, those who disbelieve reject what their messengers brought to them.

(٢٣) وَٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يُوعُونَ

Allah knows best what their hearts contain. None of their deeds are hidden from Him.

(٢٤) فَبَشِّرۡهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ

Inform them, O Messenger, of a painful torment that awaits them.

Points of Benefit:

The submission of the sky and Earth to their Lord.
Every human is seeking something, either good or evil.
A sign of happiness on the Day of Resurrection is taking the book with the right hand, and a sign of misery is taking it with the left hand.
