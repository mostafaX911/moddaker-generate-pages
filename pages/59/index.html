(٧١) يَٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لِمَ تَلۡبِسُونَ ٱلۡحَقَّ بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ وَتَكۡتُمُونَ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَأَنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

O People of the Book, why do you mix the truth revealed in your books with falsehood you have made up yourselves? Why do you hide the truth and guidance in your Book, such as the truthfulness of the prophethood of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, when you know what is the truth from falsehood and what is guidance from misguidance?

(٧٢) وَقَالَت طَّآئِفَةٞ مِّنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ءَامِنُواْ بِٱلَّذِيٓ أُنزِلَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَجۡهَ ٱلنَّهَارِ وَٱكۡفُرُوٓاْ ءَاخِرَهُۥ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ

A group of Jewish scholars said: pretend to accept the Quran, which was revealed to the believers at the start of the day, and then reject it at the end of the day so that, after seeing this, the believers will begin to have doubts about their religion and turn back from it saying: These people know better about Allah's Books, and they have turned their backs.

(٧٣) وَلَا تُؤۡمِنُوٓاْ إِلَّا لِمَن تَبِعَ دِينَكُمۡ قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡهُدَىٰ هُدَى ٱللَّهِ أَن يُؤۡتَىٰٓ أَحَدٞ مِّثۡلَ مَآ أُوتِيتُمۡ أَوۡ يُحَآجُّوكُمۡ عِندَ رَبِّكُمۡۗ قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡفَضۡلَ بِيَدِ ٱللَّهِ يُؤۡتِيهِ مَن يَشَآءُۗ وَٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٞ

They also said: Do not accept anyone unless he follows your religion. Say, O Messenger: Guidance to the truth is the Guidance of Allah, not the rejection and stubbornness that you display, fearing that anyone else could be given a bounty similar to what you were given or that they would argue with you before your Lord if you acknowledged what was revealed to them. Say, O Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم: Bounty is in Allah's Hand, and He gives it to whoever of His slaves He wills: His bounty is not restricted to any one nation. Allah is Bountiful, and He knows who is deserving.

(٧٤) يَخۡتَصُّ بِرَحۡمَتِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۗ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو ٱلۡفَضۡلِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ

He chooses whoever of His creation, bestowing on them His mercy and blessing them with guidance, prophethood, and countless favors. Allah's bounty is vast and has no limit.

(٧٥) ۞وَمِنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مَنۡ إِن تَأۡمَنۡهُ بِقِنطَارٖ يُؤَدِّهِۦٓ إِلَيۡكَ وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ إِن تَأۡمَنۡهُ بِدِينَارٖ لَّا يُؤَدِّهِۦٓ إِلَيۡكَ إِلَّا مَا دُمۡتَ عَلَيۡهِ قَآئِمٗاۗ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَالُواْ لَيۡسَ عَلَيۡنَا فِي ٱلۡأُمِّيِّ‍ۧنَ سَبِيلٞ وَيَقُولُونَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡكَذِبَ وَهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُونَ

There are some People of the Book who, if you entrust them with a large amount of wealth, will honor the trust. On the other hand, there are some among them; if you entrust them with a small amount, they will not return the trust unless you persistently demand it from them. That is because they wrongly believe and say: There is no sin on us for any crime we commit against the Arabs or if we consume the wealth of the Arabs since Allah has permitted that for us. They speak such lies, knowing it to be a fabrication against Allah.

(٧٦) بَلَىٰۚ مَنۡ أَوۡفَىٰ بِعَهۡدِهِۦ وَٱتَّقَىٰ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

It is not as they claimed; rather, they are sinful. However, anyone who fulfills his covenant with Allah by believing in Him and His messengers and fulfills his covenant with people to fulfill their trust and fear Allah by fulfilling His instructions and avoiding His prohibitions. In such a case, Allah loves those who fear Him and will recompense them with the most generous of rewards.

(٧٧) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَشۡتَرُونَ بِعَهۡدِ ٱللَّهِ وَأَيۡمَٰنِهِمۡ ثَمَنٗا قَلِيلًا أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَا خَلَٰقَ لَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَلَا يُكَلِّمُهُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَلَا يَنظُرُ إِلَيۡهِمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ وَلَا يُزَكِّيهِمۡ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Those who take a small amount of this world in exchange for Allah's advice to follow His revelation and His messengers, and in exchange for the oaths they took to fulfill Allah's covenant, will have no share in the reward of the Hereafter. Allah will not speak to them that which will make them happy and will not look at them mercifully on the Day of Resurrection. They will receive a painful torment.

Points of Benefit:

Some scholars among the People of the Book deceive the followers of their religion and do not clarify the truth indicated by their books and brought by their messengers.
Among the means of deception utilized by the disbelievers is their entering into the religion apparently while they sow doubts from inside.
Allah, the Exalted, is the Bestower and the One who Favors. He gives whomever He wants out of His favor and withholds provision from whomever He wills out of His wisdom and justice. His favor is not gained except by obeying Him.
Every alternative to faith in Allah and the fulfillment of His covenant in this life, even if it is great, is lowly in reality and little in front of the reward and status of the Hereafter.
