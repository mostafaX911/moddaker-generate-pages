(٢٥) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرٌ غَيۡرُ مَمۡنُونِۢ

Except for those who believed in Allah and did righteous deeds, for them is a reward that will never cease: Paradise.

The Mansions of the Stars (Surah Al-Buruj)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Explaining Allah's Power, His all-encompassing Knowledge, His support for His allies, and His seizing of His enemies.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡبُرُوجِ

Allah swore by the sky, encompassing the various stages of the sun, moon, and other celestial bodies.

(٢) وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡمَوۡعُودِ

And He swore by the Day of Resurrection in which He promised to gather together all of creation.

(٣) وَشَاهِدٖ وَمَشۡهُودٖ

He then swore by every witness, like the prophet who is a witness over his nation, and everything witnessed, like the nation that is witnessed against by its prophet.

(٤) قُتِلَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡأُخۡدُودِ

Cursed are those who made a great pit in the earth.

(٥) ٱلنَّارِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡوَقُودِ

They lit a fire in the pit and threw the believers into it alive,

(٦) إِذۡ هُمۡ عَلَيۡهَا قُعُودٞ

while they sat around that pit filled with fire.

(٧) وَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَفۡعَلُونَ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ شُهُودٞ

They are witnesses to the torture and exemplary punishment they inflicted upon the believers; they were present during it.

(٨) وَمَا نَقَمُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ إِلَّآ أَن يُؤۡمِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَمِيدِ

The disbelievers did not fault the believers for anything other than their belief in Allah, the Almighty, Whom none can overpower, and the One who is Praised for everything.

(٩) ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدٌ

To Him Alone belong the dominion over the heavens and the earth. He is well aware of everything. No affair of His slaves is hidden from Him.

(١٠) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ فَتَنُواْ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَتُوبُواْ فَلَهُمۡ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابُ ٱلۡحَرِيقِ

Indeed, those who tortured the believing men and women with fire to turn them away from believing in Allah alone, and did not repent afterward to Allah from their sins, will be in the torment of Hell on the Day of Resurrection. They will be punished by the Fire, which will burn them as a recompense for burning the believers with fire.

(١١) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُمۡ جَنَّٰتٞ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ

Those who believed in Allah and did righteous deeds will have gardens with rivers flowing underneath their palaces and trees. This is the reward that has been prepared for them, and this is the ultimate level of success to which no other success comes close.

(١٢) إِنَّ بَطۡشَ رَبِّكَ لَشَدِيدٌ

Your Lord's seizure of the oppressor, O Messenger, is powerful indeed, even if He grants him respite for some time.

(١٣) إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ يُبۡدِئُ وَيُعِيدُ

Indeed, it is He who originates creation and punishment, and it is He who restores them.

(١٤) وَهُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلۡوَدُودُ

He is Oft-Forgiving of the sins of His slaves who repent, and He loves His friends who are mindful of Him.

(١٥) ذُو ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡمَجِيدُ

Owner of the Noble Throne.

(١٦) فَعَّالٞ لِّمَا يُرِيدُ

Doer of what He wills: pardoning the sins of whoever He wills and punishing whoever He wills. None can compel Him; may He be Exalted.

(١٧) هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ٱلۡجُنُودِ

O Messenger, has the news reached you of the armies that set out to battle against the truth and stood against it?

(١٨) فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَثَمُودَ

[They were the armies of] Pharoah and Thamūd, the people of Saleh (peace be upon him).

(١٩) بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فِي تَكۡذِيبٖ

The impediment to these people's belief is not due to news not reaching them about the destruction of nations that rejected faith before them. Rather, they deny what their messenger came with to follow their own desires.

(٢٠) وَٱللَّهُ مِن وَرَآئِهِم مُّحِيطُۢ

Allah encompasses their deeds and keeps a record of them. He misses nothing from their actions, and He will recompense them accordingly.

(٢١) بَلۡ هُوَ قُرۡءَانٞ مَّجِيدٞ

The Quran is neither poetry nor rhyme, as its deniers claim. Rather, it is a noble Quran.

(٢٢) فِي لَوۡحٖ مَّحۡفُوظِۢ

[It is] in a tablet that is preserved against any change, distortion, omission, or addition.

Points of Benefit:

The believer is tested according to his level of belief.
Giving preference to the soundness of faith over physical well-being is one of the signs of salvation on the Day of Resurrection.
Making repentance while fulfilling its conditions destroys the sins that came before it.
