The Nightcommer (Surah At-Tariq)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Explaining Allah's power and His all-encompassing ability to create man and bring him back to life.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلطَّارِقِ

Allah swore by the sky, and He swore by the star that knocks at night.

(٢) وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلطَّارِقُ

What will inform you, O Messenger, of the status of this great star?

(٣) ٱلنَّجۡمُ ٱلثَّاقِبُ

It is the star that pierces the sky with its glimmering light.

(٤) إِن كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ لَّمَّا عَلَيۡهَا حَافِظٞ

There is no soul except that Allah has assigned to it an angel recording its deeds for reckoning on the Day of Resurrection.

(٥) فَلۡيَنظُرِ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مِمَّ خُلِقَ

So let the human ponder what Allah created him from, so that Allah's omnipotence and man's inability become clear to him.

(٦) خُلِقَ مِن مَّآءٖ دَافِقٖ

Allah created him from spurting water that is spilt into the womb.

(٧) يَخۡرُجُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ ٱلصُّلۡبِ وَٱلتَّرَآئِبِ

This water emerges from between the backbone and chest bones of a man.

(٨) إِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ رَجۡعِهِۦ لَقَادِرٞ

Indeed, since He, the Exalted, created him from that ignoble water, He has the ability to resurrect him after death for reckoning and recompense.

(٩) يَوۡمَ تُبۡلَى ٱلسَّرَآئِرُ

On the Day when secrets are tested, the intentions, beliefs, and the like that the hearts used to harbor will be exposed. Because of this, the righteous among them will become distinguished from the corrupt.

(١٠) فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن قُوَّةٖ وَلَا نَاصِرٖ

A person will not have any strength on that day that will prevent them from Allah's torment or an aid to assist them.

(١١) وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلرَّجۡعِ

Allah swore by the sky that contains rain because it comes down from the sky time after time.

(١٢) وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ ذَاتِ ٱلصَّدۡعِ

He swore by the earth that cracks open to reveal the plants, fruit, and trees within it.

(١٣) إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلٞ فَصۡلٞ

Indeed, this Quran that is sent down to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم is a word that separates truth from falsehood, honesty from lying.

(١٤) وَمَا هُوَ بِٱلۡهَزۡلِ

It (The Quran) is not frivolous or falsehood. Rather, it is solemn and true.

(١٥) إِنَّهُمۡ يَكِيدُونَ كَيۡدٗا

Indeed, the rejectors of what their Messenger brought plot many schemes to rebuff and invalidate his call.

(١٦) وَأَكِيدُ كَيۡدٗا

And I plot a scheme to make the religion apparent and nullify falsehood.

(١٧) فَمَهِّلِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ أَمۡهِلۡهُمۡ رُوَيۡدَۢا

So, give respite, O Messenger, to these disbelievers. Give them a brief respite, and do not seek to hasten their torment and destruction.

The Most High (Surah Al-A'la)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Reminding the soul of the Hereafter and freeing it from worldly attachments.

Explanation:

(١) سَبِّحِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡأَعۡلَى

Vindicate your Lord, who is high above His creation, uttering His Name when you remember and revere Him.

(٢) ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ فَسَوَّىٰ

Who created man in due proportion and balanced his stature.

(٣) وَٱلَّذِي قَدَّرَ فَهَدَىٰ

And determined the species, types, and attributes for all creation. He guided every creation to what is appropriate and suitable for it.

(٤) وَٱلَّذِيٓ أَخۡرَجَ ٱلۡمَرۡعَىٰ

Who brought out from the earth what your animals graze on.

(٥) فَجَعَلَهُۥ غُثَآءً أَحۡوَىٰ

Then, He turned it into a dry, blackish straw after it had been green and fresh.

(٦) سَنُقۡرِئُكَ فَلَا تَنسَىٰٓ

We will have you recite, O Messenger, the Quran and gather it within your chest, and you will not forget it. So do not race with Gabriel in recitation out of your eagerness to not forget it.

(٧) إِلَّا مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ يَعۡلَمُ ٱلۡجَهۡرَ وَمَا يَخۡفَىٰ

Except for what Allah wishes you to forget for a wisdom. Indeed, He (may He be Exalted) knows what is exposed and what is hidden. Nothing of that is hidden from Him.

(٨) وَنُيَسِّرُكَ لِلۡيُسۡرَىٰ

We will make it easy for you to do the deeds that content Allah and enter you into Paradise.

(٩) فَذَكِّرۡ إِن نَّفَعَتِ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ

Therefore, advise people with what we revealed to you from the Quran, and remind them so long as the reminder is heard.

(١٠) سَيَذَّكَّرُ مَن يَخۡشَىٰ

The one who fears Allah will pay heed to your admonition because he is the one who benefits from admonition.

Points of Benefit:

The angels protect mankind and record their deeds, good or evil, so that they can be accounted for.
The weakness of the disbelievers' plot when faced with the plot of Allah, the Transcendent.
Awe of Allah causes admonition.
