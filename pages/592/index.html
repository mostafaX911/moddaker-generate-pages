(١١) وَيَتَجَنَّبُهَا ٱلۡأَشۡقَى

The disbeliever will stay away and run from admonition. He will be the most miserable person in the Afterlife because he will enter the Fire.

(١٢) ٱلَّذِي يَصۡلَى ٱلنَّارَ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰ

The one who will enter the Great Fire of the Afterlife suffers and bears its heat forever.

(١٣) ثُمَّ لَا يَمُوتُ فِيهَا وَلَا يَحۡيَىٰ

Then, he will remain eternally in the Fire. He will neither die as a relief to his insufferable torment nor be able to live a good and decent life.

(١٤) قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ

The one who purified himself from shirk (associating partners with Allah) and sins has achieved the objective.

(١٥) وَذَكَرَ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّهِۦ فَصَلَّىٰ

And remembered his Lord with the various types of remembrance that He ordained, and performed the prayer in the manner it is ought to be performed.

(١٦) بَلۡ تُؤۡثِرُونَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا

Rather, you prioritize the worldly life and give preference to it over the Hereafter despite the vast disparity between them.

(١٧) وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرٞ وَأَبۡقَىٰٓ

The Hereafter is certainly better and superior to worldly existence due to what it contains of delights and pleasures, and it is [infinitely] more lasting. That is because what it contains of bliss shall never cease.

(١٨) إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَفِي ٱلصُّحُفِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ

Indeed, these instructions and reports We mentioned to you are certainly in the scriptures revealed before the Quran.

(١٩) صُحُفِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ

They are the scriptures revealed to Abraham and Moses (peace be upon them).

The Overwhelming (Surah Al-Ghashiyah)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Reminding of the Hereafter and its reward and punishment, and considering the evidence for Allah's power.

Explanation:

(١) هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ٱلۡغَٰشِيَةِ

Has word come to you, O Messenger, of the Resurrection that will overwhelm people with its horrors?

(٢) وُجُوهٞ يَوۡمَئِذٍ خَٰشِعَةٌ

For people on the Day of Resurrection will either be wretched or blissful. The faces of the wretched will be humiliated and downcast.

(٣) عَامِلَةٞ نَّاصِبَةٞ

Tired and strained by chains they will be dragged by and the fetters they will be shackled with.

(٤) تَصۡلَىٰ نَارًا حَامِيَةٗ

Those faces will enter a hot fire and suffer its heat.

(٥) تُسۡقَىٰ مِنۡ عَيۡنٍ ءَانِيَةٖ

They will be given to drink from a spring of intensely hot water.

(٦) لَّيۡسَ لَهُمۡ طَعَامٌ إِلَّا مِن ضَرِيعٖ

They will not have any food to nourish them except among the vilest and filthiest of it from a plant called Shibriq. If it dries up, it becomes poisonous.

(٧) لَّا يُسۡمِنُ وَلَا يُغۡنِي مِن جُوعٖ

It neither fattens those who eat it nor does it satisfy their hunger.

(٨) وُجُوهٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ نَّاعِمَةٞ

The faces of the blissful on that day will be full of delight, beauty, and happiness from the bounty they find themselves in.

(٩) لِّسَعۡيِهَا رَاضِيَةٞ

Content with their righteous deeds that they performed in the world, for they will have found the reward of their actions stored for them and multiplied.

(١٠) فِي جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٖ

In a paradise elevated in place and rank.

(١١) لَّا تَسۡمَعُ فِيهَا لَٰغِيَةٗ

In the garden, they will not hear any word of falsehood and frivolity, let alone hear any prohibited word.

(١٢) فِيهَا عَيۡنٞ جَارِيَةٞ

In this paradise, there are flowing springs that they will cause to erupt and direct however they wish.

(١٣) فِيهَا سُرُرٞ مَّرۡفُوعَةٞ

In it (Paradise) are high couches.

(١٤) وَأَكۡوَابٞ مَّوۡضُوعَةٞ

And cups laid out and ready to drink from.

(١٥) وَنَمَارِقُ مَصۡفُوفَةٞ

In it are cushions packed closely next to one another.

(١٦) وَزَرَابِيُّ مَبۡثُوثَةٌ

In it are many carpets spread out everywhere.

Allah highlights the contrast between the wretched and the fortunate in the Hereafter. He then draws the attention of the non-believers towards the beauty of His creation and the power it demonstrates. This serves as evidence for them to believe in Him and gain entry into Paradise, thereby becoming among the blissful. He states:

(١٧) أَفَلَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡإِبِلِ كَيۡفَ خُلِقَتۡ

Do they not look reflecting at the camels, how Allah created them and subjugated them to human beings?!

(١٨) وَإِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ كَيۡفَ رُفِعَتۡ

And look at the sky, how He raised it so that it became a preserved ceiling above them that does not fall on them?!

(١٩) وَإِلَى ٱلۡجِبَالِ كَيۡفَ نُصِبَتۡ

And look at the mountains, how He erected them, stabilizing the earth with them such that it does not shake with the people?!

(٢٠) وَإِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كَيۡفَ سُطِحَتۡ

And look at the earth, how He spread it out, and made it prepared for people to settle down upon?!

After directing them to witness the manifestation of His power, Allah directs His Messenger, stating:

(٢١) فَذَكِّرۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مُذَكِّرٞ

So admonish, O Messenger, these people and instill fear of Allah's torment. You are but a reminder; all that is upon you is to remind them. As for them attaining faith, that is in Allah's hand alone.

(٢٢) لَّسۡتَ عَلَيۡهِم بِمُصَيۡطِرٍ

You do not have the power to compel them to believe.

Points of Benefit:

The importance of purifying the soul from spiritual and physical impurities.
Using the creation as evidence of the Creator and His greatness.
The caller to Allah is responsible for calling to Allah, not to cause the people to be guided because guidance is in the Hand of Allah.
