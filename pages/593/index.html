However, whoever turns away from faith and rejects Allah and His Messenger,

(٢٤) فَيُعَذِّبُهُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡعَذَابَ ٱلۡأَكۡبَرَ

Then Allah will torment him on the Day of Resurrection with the greatest torment by entering him into Hell where they will remain eternally therein.

(٢٥) إِنَّ إِلَيۡنَآ إِيَابَهُمۡ

To Us alone is their return after their death.

(٢٦) ثُمَّ إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا حِسَابَهُم

Then, holding them accountable for their actions is indeed upon Us alone. It is not upon you or anyone else.

The Dawn (Surah Al-Fajr)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Clarifying the end result of the oppressors, the wisdom behind tribulation, and reminding of the Hereafter.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلۡفَجۡرِ

Allah (may He be Exalted) swore by the dawn.

(٢) وَلَيَالٍ عَشۡرٖ

And swore by the first ten nights of Dhul-Hijjah (11th month of the Islamic lunar calendar).

(٣) وَٱلشَّفۡعِ وَٱلۡوَتۡرِ

And swore by the things that come as even and odd.

(٤) وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَسۡرِ

And swore by the night when it arrives, remains, and recedes. The complement of these oaths is: You will certainly be recompensed for your deeds.

(٥) هَلۡ فِي ذَٰلِكَ قَسَمٞ لِّذِي حِجۡرٍ

Is there, in that which was mentioned, an oath that persuades the person of reason?

(٦) أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِعَادٍ

Do you not see, O Messenger, what your Lord did with 'Ād, the people of Hūd, when they rejected their Messenger?!

(٧) إِرَمَ ذَاتِ ٱلۡعِمَادِ

The tribe of 'Ād, which traced its lineage back to its forefather Iram, whose people were lofty in stature.

(٨) ٱلَّتِي لَمۡ يُخۡلَقۡ مِثۡلُهَا فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ

The likes of which Allah never created in the lands.

(٩) وَثَمُودَ ٱلَّذِينَ جَابُواْ ٱلصَّخۡرَ بِٱلۡوَادِ

And [did you not see] how your Lord dealt with Thamūd, the people of Saleh, who would split the stones of the mountains and make homes out of them in the [Valley of] Al-Hijr?

(١٠) وَفِرۡعَوۡنَ ذِي ٱلۡأَوۡتَادِ

And [did you not see] how your Lord dealt with Pharaoh, who had stakes with which he would torment people?

(١١) ٱلَّذِينَ طَغَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ

All these people transgressed the limits of compulsion and injustice; each [nation] transgressed them in their respective land.

(١٢) فَأَكۡثَرُواْ فِيهَا ٱلۡفَسَادَ

So, their increased spreading of disbelief and acts of disobedience caused much corruption within it.

(١٣) فَصَبَّ عَلَيۡهِمۡ رَبُّكَ سَوۡطَ عَذَابٍ

Subsequently, Allah made them suffer His severe torment and eradicated them from the face of the earth.

(١٤) إِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَبِٱلۡمِرۡصَادِ

Indeed, your Lord, O Messenger, watches over and observes the people's actions, so as to recompense the virtuous with Paradise and the malevolent with the Fire.

As nations that Allah destroyed were blessed with power and protection, Allah clarifies that those blessings are not evidence of Allah being pleased with them, stating:

(١٥) فَأَمَّا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِذَا مَا ٱبۡتَلَىٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَأَكۡرَمَهُۥ وَنَعَّمَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّيٓ أَكۡرَمَنِ

So, as for a human being, it is from his nature that when his Lord tests him such that He honors and blesses him with wealth, children, and prestige, he thinks that it is because he is held in esteem with Allah. Thus, He says: My Lord has honored me because I am worthy of being honored by Him.

(١٦) وَأَمَّآ إِذَا مَا ٱبۡتَلَىٰهُ فَقَدَرَ عَلَيۡهِ رِزۡقَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّيٓ أَهَٰنَنِ

And as for when He tests him such that He restricts his provision, then, indeed, he thinks it is because of his insignificance in the sight of his Lord. Thus, he says: My Lord has disgraced me.

(١٧) كَلَّاۖ بَل لَّا تُكۡرِمُونَ ٱلۡيَتِيمَ

Nay, for the matter is not as this human envisions that life's bounty is evidence of Allah's contentment with His slave and that misfortune is evidence of the slave's dishonor in the sight of his Lord. Instead, the reality is that you do not honor the orphan with what Allah has provided you of provision.

(١٨) وَلَا تَحَٰٓضُّونَ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينِ

Nor do you encourage each other to feed the poor, who cannot find anything to sustain themselves with.

(١٩) وَتَأۡكُلُونَ ٱلتُّرَاثَ أَكۡلٗا لَّمّٗا

You fiercely devour the rights of the weak, from women and orphans, without considering whether it is permissible.

(٢٠) وَتُحِبُّونَ ٱلۡمَالَ حُبّٗا جَمّٗا

And you love wealth abundantly, so you act miserly in spending it in the path of Allah, hanging onto it.

(٢١) كَلَّآۖ إِذَا دُكَّتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ دَكّٗا دَكّٗا

These actions are not befitting of you, for remember when the earth will be moved vigorously and shaken.

(٢٢) وَجَآءَ رَبُّكَ وَٱلۡمَلَكُ صَفّٗا صَفّٗا

And your Lord will come, O Messenger, to judge between His slaves, and the angels will also come arranged in rows.

Points of Benefit:

The superiority of the first ten days of Dhul-Hijjah over the rest of the year.
Affirming the coming of Allah, the Exalted, on the Day of Resurrection, in a matter that befits Him without likening that to the creation, making similarities, or negating it.
When the believer is tested, He is patient, and when He receives something, He is thankful.
