(٢٣) وَجِاْيٓءَ يَوۡمَئِذِۢ بِجَهَنَّمَۚ يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَتَذَكَّرُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ وَأَنَّىٰ لَهُ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ

And on that day, Hell will be brought, having seventy thousand reins, each with seventy thousand angels dragging it. On that day, the human being will remember his shortcomings with Allah. But how will the realization on that day benefit him when that will be a day of recompense, not action?

(٢٤) يَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي قَدَّمۡتُ لِحَيَاتِي

He will say, out of intense regret: If only I had put forth good deeds for my Afterlife, which is the true life.

(٢٥) فَيَوۡمَئِذٖ لَّا يُعَذِّبُ عَذَابَهُۥٓ أَحَدٞ

On that Day, no one will inflict punishment like Allah's punishment because it will be more severe and longer lasting.

(٢٦) وَلَا يُوثِقُ وَثَاقَهُۥٓ أَحَدٞ

Neither will anyone bind in chains like His binding of the disbelievers therein.

After mentioning the recompense of the disbelievers, Allah mentions the recompense of the believers, stating:

(٢٧) يَٰٓأَيَّتُهَا ٱلنَّفۡسُ ٱلۡمُطۡمَئِنَّةُ

As for the believer's soul, it will be said to it at the time of death and on the Day of Resurrection: O sound soul, reassured through faith and righteous action,

(٢٨) ٱرۡجِعِيٓ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةٗ مَّرۡضِيَّةٗ

Return to your Lord, pleased with Him because of the abundant reward you will receive, and Him (may He be Exalted) being pleased with you because of the righteous deeds you possess.

(٢٩) فَٱدۡخُلِي فِي عِبَٰدِي

Now enter among My righteous slaves.

(٣٠) وَٱدۡخُلِي جَنَّتِي

And enter with them into My Paradise, which I have prepared for them (i.e., My righteous slaves).

The City (Surah Al-Balad)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing a human being's neediness, constant struggles, and the means to salvation.

Explanation:

(١) لَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِهَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدِ

Allah swore by the sacred land, which is the Honored [city of] Mecca.

(٢) وَأَنتَ حِلُّۢ بِهَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدِ

Permissible is what you do therein, O Messenger, of killing whoever deserves to be killed and capturing whoever deserves to be captured.

(٣) وَوَالِدٖ وَمَا وَلَدَ

And Allah swore by the father of humanity and by all of his descendants.

(٤) لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ فِي كَبَدٍ

Verily, We have created the human being to undergo weariness and distress from suffering worldly adversities.

(٥) أَيَحۡسَبُ أَن لَّن يَقۡدِرَ عَلَيۡهِ أَحَدٞ

Does the human think that when he commits sins, no one has power over him, nor are they able to take retribution from him, even if it is his Lord who created him?

(٦) يَقُولُ أَهۡلَكۡتُ مَالٗا لُّبَدًا

He says: I spent a lot of extensively amassed wealth.

(٧) أَيَحۡسَبُ أَن لَّمۡ يَرَهُۥٓ أَحَدٌ

Does this person who boasts about what he spends think that Allah does not see him and that He will not take him into account regarding his wealth, specifically from where he earned it and on what he spent it?

(٨) أَلَمۡ نَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ عَيۡنَيۡنِ

Did We not create eyes for him with which he sees?

(٩) وَلِسَانٗا وَشَفَتَيۡنِ

And a tongue and lips with which he speaks?

(١٠) وَهَدَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلنَّجۡدَيۡنِ

And We acquainted him with the path of virtue and the path of evil?

(١١) فَلَا ٱقۡتَحَمَ ٱلۡعَقَبَةَ

He is called to traverse the arduous path that separates him from Paradise, so he passes and crosses into it.

(١٢) وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡعَقَبَةُ

And what will inform you, O Messenger, about the arduous path that he must cross to enter Paradise?

(١٣) فَكُّ رَقَبَةٍ

It is to free a slave, male or female.

(١٤) أَوۡ إِطۡعَٰمٞ فِي يَوۡمٖ ذِي مَسۡغَبَةٖ

Or to feed on a day of famine in which food is scarce,

(١٥) يَتِيمٗا ذَا مَقۡرَبَةٍ

A child who has lost his father, whom he is related to,

(١٦) أَوۡ مِسۡكِينٗا ذَا مَتۡرَبَةٖ

Or a poor person who has no possessions.

(١٧) ثُمَّ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلۡمَرۡحَمَةِ

Then, by being from those who believed in Allah and called one another to be patient in acts of obedience, in refraining from sins, and in tribulation, they advised one another to show mercy toward Allah's slaves.

(١٨) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ

Those characterized with those [preceding] traits are the people of the right.

Points of Benefit:

Freeing slaves, feeding the needy at times of hardship, believing in Allah, and enjoining patience and mercy are among the reasons to enter Paradise.
One of the signs of prophethood is that he was informed that Mecca would be permissible for him for an hour of the day.
When Allah restricted the ways of slavery and expanded the ways of emancipation, He made freeing slaves a form of expiation and gaining nearness to Allah.
