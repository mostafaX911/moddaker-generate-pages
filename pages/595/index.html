The Sun (Surah Ash-Shams)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

The longest oath in the Quran emphasizing the importance of purifying the soul through acts of obedience and the severe loss of tainting it through disobedience.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلشَّمۡسِ وَضُحَىٰهَا

Allah swore by the sun, and by the time of sunrise after appearing from its eastern horizon.

(٢) وَٱلۡقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَىٰهَا

And swore by the moon when it follows behind it after [the sun's] setting.

(٣) وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا جَلَّىٰهَا

And swore by the daytime when it reveals whatever is on the face of the earth with its light.

(٤) وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَغۡشَىٰهَا

And swore by the night when it covers the face of the earth, causing it to become dark.

(٥) وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا بَنَىٰهَا

And swore by the sky and its perfect structure.

(٦) وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا طَحَىٰهَا

And swore by the earth and its expansiveness so people can live upon it.

(٧) وَنَفۡسٖ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا

And swore by each soul and by Allah's creation of balance in it.

(٨) فَأَلۡهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقۡوَىٰهَا

Then He made it understand, without teaching, what is evil so it could avoid it and what is good to approach it.

(٩) قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا

The one who has purified his soul by adorning it with virtues and freeing it of atrocities has attained his objective.

(١٠) وَقَدۡ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا

And the one who has secretly buried his soul in sins and acts of disobedience has lost.

After mentioning the loss of the one who corrupts their soul and conceals it with disobedience, He mentions Thamūd as an example, stating:

(١١) كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ بِطَغۡوَىٰهَآ

Thamūd rejected their Prophet Saleh due to their transgressing the limits in committing sins and acts of disobedience.

(١٢) إِذِ ٱنۢبَعَثَ أَشۡقَىٰهَا

When the most wretched among them stood up after his people appointed him for the task.

(١٣) فَقَالَ لَهُمۡ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ نَاقَةَ ٱللَّهِ وَسُقۡيَٰهَا

Allah's messenger Saleh (peace be upon him) said to them: Leave the camel of Allah alone; let it drink on its [assigned] day and do not harm it.

(١٤) فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَعَقَرُوهَا فَدَمۡدَمَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ رَبُّهُم بِذَنۢبِهِمۡ فَسَوَّىٰهَا

Then, they rejected their Messenger regarding the camel. The most wretched among them killed it with the people's approval, and consequently, they all became complicit in the sin. As a result, Allah imposed His torment upon them and destroyed them with a scream for their sins. He equally punished them all with destruction.

(١٥) وَلَا يَخَافُ عُقۡبَٰهَا

Allah inflicted them with the torment that destroyed them; unafraid of its consequences, may He be Exalted.

The Night (Surah Al-Layl)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing the condition of creation regarding belief and spending for Allah's sake and the situation of each group.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَغۡشَىٰ

Allah swore by the night when it covers the face of the earth with its darkness.

(٢) وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا تَجَلَّىٰ

And Allah swore by the day when it reveals itself, becoming apparent.

(٣) وَمَا خَلَقَ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلۡأُنثَىٰٓ

And He swore by His creation of the two genders: male and female.

(٤) إِنَّ سَعۡيَكُمۡ لَشَتَّىٰ

Indeed, your work, O people, is diverse. So among it are good deeds, which are the cause for entering Paradise, and evil deeds, which are the cause of entering the Fire.

(٥) فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أَعۡطَىٰ وَٱتَّقَىٰ

So as for the one who only gives whatever is obligatory, such as Zakat (obligatory alms), mandatory family expenses, and expiation, and is heedful of that which Allah has prohibited him from,

(٦) وَصَدَّقَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ

And believes in the compensation that Allah has promised him,

(٧) فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡيُسۡرَىٰ

Then we will make righteous deeds and spending for Allah's sake easy for him.

(٨) وَأَمَّا مَنۢ بَخِلَ وَٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰ

And as for the one who is miserly with his wealth such that he does not spend it toward his obligations and feels self-sufficient through his wealth from needing Allah, such that he does not ask Allah anything from His bounty,

(٩) وَكَذَّبَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ

And rejects what Allah promised him of compensation and reward for spending his wealth in Allah's path,

Points of Benefit:

The importance of self-purification.
Those who cooperate upon disobedience share in the sin.
Sins cause worldly punishment.
Everyone is led to what they are created for; some are obedient, while others are disobedient.
