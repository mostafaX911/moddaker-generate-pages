(١٠) فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡعُسۡرَىٰ

Then, We will make performing evil deeds easy and doing good difficult for him.

(١١) وَمَا يُغۡنِي عَنۡهُ مَالُهُۥٓ إِذَا تَرَدَّىٰٓ

The wealth that he was miserly with will not benefit him when he perishes and enters the Fire.

(١٢) إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا لَلۡهُدَىٰ

Indeed, it is upon Us to clarify the path of truth from falsehood.

(١٣) وَإِنَّ لَنَا لَلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَٱلۡأُولَىٰ

And indeed, the Afterlife and the worldly life belong to Us; We deal with them as We wish, and that is not for anyone other than Us.

(١٤) فَأَنذَرۡتُكُمۡ نَارٗا تَلَظَّىٰ

So, I warn you, O people, of a kindled fire if you have disobeyed Allah.

(١٥) لَا يَصۡلَىٰهَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡأَشۡقَى

None will suffer the heat of this Fire except the most wretched (i.e., the disbeliever).

(١٦) ٱلَّذِي كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ

The one who rejected what the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم brought and turned away from complying with Allah's command.

(١٧) وَسَيُجَنَّبُهَا ٱلۡأَتۡقَى

The especially heedful one will be distanced from it (i.e., the Fire). Abu Bakr (may Allah be pleased with him) is amongst the first to be intended by this verse and what follows it.

(١٨) ٱلَّذِي يُؤۡتِي مَالَهُۥ يَتَزَكَّىٰ

The one who spends his wealth in avenues of virtue to be purified of sin.

(١٩) وَمَا لِأَحَدٍ عِندَهُۥ مِن نِّعۡمَةٖ تُجۡزَىٰٓ

He does not spend whatever he spends from his wealth to repay any favor done for him by anyone.

(٢٠) إِلَّا ٱبۡتِغَآءَ وَجۡهِ رَبِّهِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ

Through his spending, He only seeks [to behold the] Countenance of his Lord, who is High above His creation.

(٢١) وَلَسَوۡفَ يَرۡضَىٰ

And he will surely be pleased with the generous reward that Allah will grant him.

The Morning Hours (Surah Ad-Duha)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing Allah's concern for His Prophet in all his affairs, from beginning to end.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلضُّحَىٰ

Allah swore by the beginning of the day.

(٢) وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا سَجَىٰ

And He swore by the night when it darkens, and the people's bustling settles.

(٣) مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ

Your Lord has not left you, O Messenger, nor does He hate you, as the polytheists would say when the revelation paused.

(٤) وَلَلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرٞ لَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ

And the abode of the Afterlife is undoubtedly better for you than the worldly life, for it contains everlasting bounties that never cease.

(٥) وَلَسَوۡفَ يُعۡطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرۡضَىٰٓ

Your Lord will grant you and your nation abundant rewards until you become content with what He has blessed you and your nation with.

(٦) أَلَمۡ يَجِدۡكَ يَتِيمٗا فَ‍َٔاوَىٰ

He certainly found you a child having lost your father, so He made a refuge for you with your compassionate grandfather Abdul Muttalib and then your uncle Abu Talib.

(٧) وَوَجَدَكَ ضَآلّٗا فَهَدَىٰ

He found you in a state where you did not know what scripture or faith was, so He taught from that what you did not know.

(٨) وَوَجَدَكَ عَآئِلٗا فَأَغۡنَىٰ

And He found you poor, so He made you self-sufficient.

(٩) فَأَمَّا ٱلۡيَتِيمَ فَلَا تَقۡهَرۡ

Therefore, do not treat the one who lost their father in childhood poorly, nor belittle them.

(١٠) وَأَمَّا ٱلسَّآئِلَ فَلَا تَنۡهَرۡ

And do not rebuke the one asking out of need.

(١١) وَأَمَّا بِنِعۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثۡ

And be grateful for the favors of Allah upon you and talk about them.

The Relief (Surah Ash-Sharh)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

The blessings bestowed upon the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم by giving him perfected intangible blessings.

Explanation:

(١) أَلَمۡ نَشۡرَحۡ لَكَ صَدۡرَكَ

Verily, We have expanded your chest, causing you to love receiving revelation.

(٢) وَوَضَعۡنَا عَنكَ وِزۡرَكَ

And We [Allah] forgave you for all your preceding misdeeds and removed the burden from the Days of Ignorance you lived through.

Points of Benefit:

The status of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم with his Lord is unmatched.
It is Allah's right to be thanked for His blessings upon His slave.
The obligation of being merciful and gentle with the oppressed.
