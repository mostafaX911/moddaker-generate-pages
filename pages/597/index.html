(٣) ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنقَضَ ظَهۡرَكَ

Exhausting you to the extent that it almost broke your back.

(٤) وَرَفَعۡنَا لَكَ ذِكۡرَكَ

And We elevated for you your mention; you are mentioned in the adhan (call to prayer), the iqamah (call to initiate the prayer), and in other than them.

(٥) فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرًا

Indeed, with difficulty and strain, there is ease, facilitation, and alleviation.

(٦) إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرٗا

Indeed, with difficulty and strain, there is much ease, facilitation, and alleviation. When you realize that, the harm from your people will neither intimidate you nor deter you from calling toward Allah.

(٧) فَإِذَا فَرَغۡتَ فَٱنصَبۡ

So, when you finish your work, strive to worship your Lord.

(٨) وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرۡغَب

And make your desire and resolve toward Allah alone.

The Fig (Surah At-Tin)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Allah's blessings upon mankind by establishing their innate nature and form and perfecting the final message.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلتِّينِ وَٱلزَّيۡتُونِ

Allah swore by the fig and the place it grows, and the olive and the place it grows, referring to the land of Palestine in which Jesus (peace be upon him) was sent as [a prophet].

(٢) وَطُورِ سِينِينَ

And He swore by Mount Sinai, where Allah privately conversed with Moses (peace be upon him).

(٣) وَهَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدِ ٱلۡأَمِينِ

And He swore by Mecca, the sacred land which gives sanctuary to whoever enters it, and in which Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم was sent [as a prophet].

(٤) لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ فِيٓ أَحۡسَنِ تَقۡوِيمٖ

We have indeed created the human being with the most proportional form and the best appearance.

(٥) ثُمَّ رَدَدۡنَٰهُ أَسۡفَلَ سَٰفِلِينَ

Then We cause him to regress to extreme old age and senility in this world; he does not benefit from his body, just as he does not benefit from it if he corrupts his natural disposition and heads towards the Fire.

(٦) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ فَلَهُمۡ أَجۡرٌ غَيۡرُ مَمۡنُونٖ

Except for those who have believed in Allah and performed righteous actions, even if they grow extremely old, they have an everlasting reward that never ceases: Paradise. [That is] because they purified their natural disposition.

(٧) فَمَا يُكَذِّبُكَ بَعۡدُ بِٱلدِّينِ

So, O human being, what causes you to reject the Day of Recompense after seeing the many signs of His ability?

(٨) أَلَيۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَحۡكَمِ ٱلۡحَٰكِمِينَ

Is Allah not the most just and fair of judges by appointing the Day of Resurrection for recompense? Is it conceivable that Allah leaves His slaves in vain, without judging between them, such that He would not recompense the virtuous for their good and the sinner for their evil?

The Clot (Surah Al-'Alaq)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Mankind is between being guided through revelation and misguided through arrogance and ignorance.

Explanation:

(١) ٱقۡرَأۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ

Recite, O Messenger, what Allah reveals to you, starting with the name of your Lord, who created everything.

(٢) خَلَقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِنۡ عَلَقٍ

He created the human being from a clot of congealed blood after it had been a drop of semen.

(٣) ٱقۡرَأۡ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلۡأَكۡرَمُ

Recite, O Messenger, what Allah reveals to you. Your Lord is the Most Generous, and none can come close to His generosity, for He is abundant in magnanimity and benevolence.

(٤) ٱلَّذِي عَلَّمَ بِٱلۡقَلَمِ

The One who taught script and writing through the pen.

(٥) عَلَّمَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمۡ يَعۡلَمۡ

He taught the human being that which he did not know.

(٦) كَلَّآ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطۡغَىٰٓ

Truly, a depraved human being like Abu Jahl certainly crosses the limit by overstepping Allah's boundaries.

(٧) أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰٓ

Because he views himself as self-sufficient through his possession of prestige and wealth.

(٨) إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجۡعَىٰٓ

Indeed, to your Lord, O human being, is the return on the Day of Resurrection. So He will requite each person with what they deserve.

(٩) أَرَءَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِي يَنۡهَىٰ

Have you seen anything more astonishing than the matter of Abu Jahl, who prevents our slave Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم when he prays at the Kaabah?

(١٠) عَبۡدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ

Have you seen anything more astonishing than the matter of Abu Jahl, who prevents our slave Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم when he prays at the Kaabah?

(١١) أَرَءَيۡتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلۡهُدَىٰٓ

What if this person [who Abu Jahl] prevented is upon guidance and foresight from his Lord?!

(١٢) أَوۡ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقۡوَىٰٓ

Or [a person who] would instruct the people to be heedful of Allah by complying with His commands and avoiding His prohibitions; is such a person supposed to be prevented?!

Points of Benefit:

Allah honored His Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم by elevating his mention.
Pleasing Allah is the ultimate goal.
The importance of reading and writing in Islam.
The danger of wealth if it leads to arrogance and being far from the Truth.
Forbidding the good is one of the characteristics of the disbelievers.
