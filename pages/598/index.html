(١٣) أَرَءَيۡتَ إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ

What if this preventer rejects what the Messenger has come with and turns away from it; does he not fear Allah?!

(١٤) أَلَمۡ يَعۡلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ

Does the preventer of this slave from prayer not realize that Allah sees whatever he does and that nothing is hidden from Him?

(١٥) كَلَّا لَئِن لَّمۡ يَنتَهِ لَنَسۡفَعَۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ

The matter is not as this ignorant individual perceives. Certainly, if he does not refrain from harming My slave and rejecting him, We will surely seize him, violently dragging him from the front of his head to the Fire.

(١٦) نَاصِيَةٖ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٖ

The person of that forelock is a liar in speech and erroneous in deed.

(١٧) فَلۡيَدۡعُ نَادِيَهُۥ

So, when he is seized by the front of his head towards the Fire, let him call his companions and associates, asking for their help to save him from the torment.

(١٨) سَنَدۡعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ

We will summon the guardians of Hell; fierce angels that do not disobey Allah's commands and do as they are ordered. Let him then see which of the two groups is stronger and more capable.

(١٩) كَلَّا لَا تُطِعۡهُ وَٱسۡجُدۡۤ وَٱقۡتَرِب۩

It is not as this wrongdoer assumes: that he can cause harm to reach you. So do not obey him in command or prohibition. Prostrate to Allah and draw closer to Him through acts of obedience, for they draw [one] closer to Him.

The Power (Surah Al-Qadr)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing the virtues of the Night of Decree.

Explanation:

(١) إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةِ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ

Indeed, We sent down the Quran in its entirety to the lowest heaven, just as We began sending it down to the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم on the Night of Decree of the month of Ramadan.

(٢) وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا لَيۡلَةُ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ

Do you know, O Prophet, what lies in this night of goodness and blessing?

(٣) لَيۡلَةُ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ خَيۡرٞ مِّنۡ أَلۡفِ شَهۡرٖ

This night is a night of great virtue, for it is better than a thousand months for the one who stands [in prayer] with faith and hope for reward.

(٤) تَنَزَّلُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَٱلرُّوحُ فِيهَا بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهِم مِّن كُلِّ أَمۡرٖ

The angels and Gabriel (peace be upon him) descend in it by their Lord's permission (may He be Exalted) with every matter Allah has decreed to happen that year, whether it be provision, death, birth, or otherwise from what Allah decreed.

(٥) سَلَٰمٌ هِيَ حَتَّىٰ مَطۡلَعِ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ

This blessed night is virtuous, all of it, from its beginning until its end with the break of dawn.

The Clear Proof (Surah Al-Bayyinah)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing the perfection and clarity of Muhammad's message.

Explanation:

(١) لَمۡ يَكُنِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ مُنفَكِّينَ حَتَّىٰ تَأۡتِيَهُمُ ٱلۡبَيِّنَةُ

Those from the Jews, Christians, and polytheists who disbelieved will not leave their consensus and agreement upon disbelief until clear evidence and manifest proof come to them.

(٢) رَسُولٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ يَتۡلُواْ صُحُفٗا مُّطَهَّرَةٗ

This clear evidence and manifest proof is the Messenger whom Allah sent to recite the purified pages; only the purified touch it.

(٣) فِيهَا كُتُبٞ قَيِّمَةٞ

In those pages, there are true reports and just rulings, which direct people towards that which ensures their righteousness and guidance.

(٤) وَمَا تَفَرَّقَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَتۡهُمُ ٱلۡبَيِّنَةُ

Neither the Jews who were given the Torah nor the Christians who were given the Gospel differed [regarding the final Prophet] except after Allah sent them His [last] Prophet. Some became Muslim, and others persisted in disbelief despite knowing their Prophet's truthfulness.

(٥) وَمَآ أُمِرُوٓاْ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ حُنَفَآءَ وَيُقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَۚ وَذَٰلِكَ دِينُ ٱلۡقَيِّمَةِ

The criminality and stubbornness of the Jews and the Christians are evident, for they were not commanded in this Quran except with what their scriptures already had of worshipping Allah alone, refraining from ascribing partners [with Him], establishing prayer, and giving zakat (obligatory alms). Hence, they have been commanded with a straight religion devoid of deviance.

Points of Benefit:

The Night of Decree's virtue over all other nights of the year.
Sincerity is one of the conditions for worship to be accepted.
The fundamental agreement in the different legislations is a reason to accept the message.
