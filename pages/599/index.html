This mercy is attained by the one who fears his Lord and so complies with His command and avoids His prohibitions.

The Earthquake (Surah Az-Zalzalah)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Reminding of the terrors of the Day of Resurrection and the precise accounting within it.

Explanation:

(١) إِذَا زُلۡزِلَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ زِلۡزَالَهَا

When the earth will be shaken vigorously, which will happen on the Day of Resurrection.

(٢) وَأَخۡرَجَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ أَثۡقَالَهَا

And the earth will expel what it contains from the dead and other things.

(٣) وَقَالَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا لَهَا

And the human being will say, out of bewilderment:What is the matter with the earth? It is moving and shaking!

(٤) يَوۡمَئِذٖ تُحَدِّثُ أَخۡبَارَهَا

On that great Day, the Earth will report what was committed upon it of good and evil.

(٥) بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوۡحَىٰ لَهَا

Because Allah will have informed it and commanded it to do so.

(٦) يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَصۡدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشۡتَاتٗا لِّيُرَوۡاْ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ

On that great Day in which the earth will quake, people will depart from the place of reckoning in groups to witness the deeds they committed in the world.

(٧) فَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيۡرٗا يَرَهُۥ

So, whoever does a small ant's weight in virtual and benevolent deeds will see it before him.

(٨) وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٖ شَرّٗا يَرَهُۥ

And whoever does a small ant's weight in evil deeds will see it as well.

The Galloping Steeds (Surah Al-'Adiyat)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Warning mankind against ingratitude and greed by reminding them of the Hereafter.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلۡعَٰدِيَٰتِ ضَبۡحٗا

Allah swore by the horses that gallop such that their breathing can be heard due to the intensity of the galloping.

(٢) فَٱلۡمُورِيَٰتِ قَدۡحٗا

And swore by the horses that spark fire with their hooves when they strike stones from the intensity of the strike upon them.

(٣) فَٱلۡمُغِيرَٰتِ صُبۡحٗا

And swore by the horses that charge at the enemy in the morning.

(٤) فَأَثَرۡنَ بِهِۦ نَقۡعٗا

Creating clouds of dust from their galloping.

(٥) فَوَسَطۡنَ بِهِۦ جَمۡعًا

Then, they end up with their riders amidst the enemy group.

Points of Benefit:

Awe of Allah makes Him pleased with His slave.
The Earth's testimony to the humans' deeds.
The disbelievers are the worst of creation, and the believers are the best of them.
