(٣٠) وَإِذۡ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ إِنِّي جَاعِلٞ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ خَلِيفَةٗۖ قَالُوٓاْ أَتَجۡعَلُ فِيهَا مَن يُفۡسِدُ فِيهَا وَيَسۡفِكُ ٱلدِّمَآءَ وَنَحۡنُ نُسَبِّحُ بِحَمۡدِكَ وَنُقَدِّسُ لَكَۖ قَالَ إِنِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

Allah informs the angels that He would put humans on earth who would give birth to other humans and inhabit it to worship Allah. The angels asked their Lord, trying to understand, what the wisdom behind making the children of Adam guardians of the earth was when they would spread ruin within it and unjustly spill blood, saying: while we [the angels] are obedient to You, extol You, praise You, glorify Your Majesty and Perfection without hesitation. Allah replied to their question, stating: I know what you do not know of the incredible wisdom in their creation and the great objectives behind their succession.

(٣١) وَعَلَّمَ ءَادَمَ ٱلۡأَسۡمَآءَ كُلَّهَا ثُمَّ عَرَضَهُمۡ عَلَى ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ فَقَالَ أَنۢبِ‍ُٔونِي بِأَسۡمَآءِ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

To illustrate the status of Adam (peace be upon him), Allah taught him the names of everything: living things and objects, with their pronunciation and meanings. Then he put these things before the angels, asking: Tell me their names if you are truthful in what you say, that you are a nobler and better creation than Adam.

(٣٢) قَالُواْ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ لَا عِلۡمَ لَنَآ إِلَّا مَا عَلَّمۡتَنَآۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ

They acknowledged their shortcomings and attributed all merit to Allah, replying: We exalt and glorify You, O our Lord, without any opposition to Your judgment and law, for we do not know anything except what You have given us knowledge of. Verily, You are the Omniscient Whom nothing is hidden from, and You are the All-Wise Who puts all affairs in their proper place according to Your decree and law.

(٣٣) قَالَ يَٰٓـَٔادَمُ أَنۢبِئۡهُم بِأَسۡمَآئِهِمۡۖ فَلَمَّآ أَنۢبَأَهُم بِأَسۡمَآئِهِمۡ قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُل لَّكُمۡ إِنِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ غَيۡبَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَأَعۡلَمُ مَا تُبۡدُونَ وَمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡتُمُونَ

Then Allah told Adam: Tell them the names of those things. When Adam told the angels what Allah had taught him, Allah told the angels: Did I not inform you that I know everything hidden in the heavens and the earth, and I know everything you reveal from your affairs and what you say within yourselves?

(٣٤) وَإِذۡ قُلۡنَا لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ ٱسۡجُدُواْ لِأٓدَمَ فَسَجَدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ أَبَىٰ وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرَ وَكَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Allah reveals that He commanded the angels to prostrate to Adam out of recognition and respect. They quickly prostrated in their readiness to obedience towards Him, except for Iblis (Satan), who was originally from the Jinn. Iblis refused, objecting to Allah's command to prostrate, and out of arrogance towards Adam, causing him to become among the unfaithful.

(٣٥) وَقُلۡنَا يَٰٓـَٔادَمُ ٱسۡكُنۡ أَنتَ وَزَوۡجُكَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ وَكُلَا مِنۡهَا رَغَدًا حَيۡثُ شِئۡتُمَا وَلَا تَقۡرَبَا هَٰذِهِ ٱلشَّجَرَةَ فَتَكُونَا مِنَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

And We said: O Adam, live with your wife, Eve, in Paradise, and eat from the delightful, vast variety of food from anywhere in Paradise without disturbance. But avoid approaching this tree I forbade you to eat from, lest you become among the wrongdoers by disobeying what I commanded you to do.

(٣٦) فَأَزَلَّهُمَا ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ عَنۡهَا فَأَخۡرَجَهُمَا مِمَّا كَانَا فِيهِۖ وَقُلۡنَا ٱهۡبِطُواْ بَعۡضُكُمۡ لِبَعۡضٍ عَدُوّٞۖ وَلَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مُسۡتَقَرّٞ وَمَتَٰعٌ إِلَىٰ حِينٖ

Satan continued whispering to them to eat from it. He embellished the matter until he caused them to slip and fall into sin by eating from the tree that Allah forbade them from. As a result, Allah took them out of the Paradise they were in and said to them and Satan: Go down to the earth as enemies of each other, and for you on the earth is stability, survival, and enjoyment of the bounties within it until the end of your prescribed lifespan and the establishment of the Hour.

(٣٧) فَتَلَقَّىٰٓ ءَادَمُ مِن رَّبِّهِۦ كَلِمَٰتٖ فَتَابَ عَلَيۡهِۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلتَّوَّابُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Adam then received the words granted to him by Allah and was inspired to supplicate, seeking forgiveness with them, which are mentioned in Sūrat Al-A'rāf, Verse 23: {They said, 'O Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves. If You do not forgive us and have mercy on us, we will certainly be among the losers.'} Allah accepted Adam's repentance and forgave him, for He is Much-Forgiving and Merciful towards His creation.

Points of Benefit:

If Allah's Wisdom behind some of His creation and command is hidden from the believer, it is obligatory for him to submit to Allah regarding it.
The Holy Quran raised the status of knowledge and made it a reason for preference between the creation.
Pride is the head of sins and the foundation of every calamity that befalls creation, and it is the first sin that Allah was disobeyed through.
