(٧٨) وَإِنَّ مِنۡهُمۡ لَفَرِيقٗا يَلۡوُۥنَ أَلۡسِنَتَهُم بِٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لِتَحۡسَبُوهُ مِنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَمَا هُوَ مِنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَيَقُولُونَ هُوَ مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا هُوَ مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَقُولُونَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡكَذِبَ وَهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُونَ

There is a group of Jews who distort the Torah when reciting it by saying words that were not revealed by Allah so that you think that they are reciting from the Torah, whereas it is not from the Torah. Instead, it is their lies and fabrications against Allah. They say: What we recite has been revealed by Allah – whereas Allah did not reveal it. They say lies about Allah when they know they are lying against Allah and His messengers.

(٧٩) مَا كَانَ لِبَشَرٍ أَن يُؤۡتِيَهُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحُكۡمَ وَٱلنُّبُوَّةَ ثُمَّ يَقُولَ لِلنَّاسِ كُونُواْ عِبَادٗا لِّي مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَٰكِن كُونُواْ رَبَّٰنِيِّ‍ۧنَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تُعَلِّمُونَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَبِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَدۡرُسُونَ

It is not befitting for a man whom Allah gives a revealed book, blesses him with knowledge and understanding, and chooses him as a prophet, to say then to people: Become slaves of me instead of Allah. Instead, he should say to them: Be righteous scholars that act upon their knowledge, nurturing people, rectifying their affairs by virtue of your continuous teaching of the revealed Book, and by your constant studying of it by memorizing and understanding it.

(٨٠) وَلَا يَأۡمُرَكُمۡ أَن تَتَّخِذُواْ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةَ وَٱلنَّبِيِّ‍ۧنَ أَرۡبَابًاۗ أَيَأۡمُرُكُم بِٱلۡكُفۡرِ بَعۡدَ إِذۡ أَنتُم مُّسۡلِمُونَ

It is also not befitting for such a man to instruct you to take the angels and the prophets as lords to be worshipped instead of Allah. Is it possible that he would command you to disbelieve after you have become devoted and have surrendered to Him?

(٨١) وَإِذۡ أَخَذَ ٱللَّهُ مِيثَٰقَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ‍ۧنَ لَمَآ ءَاتَيۡتُكُم مِّن كِتَٰبٖ وَحِكۡمَةٖ ثُمَّ جَآءَكُمۡ رَسُولٞ مُّصَدِّقٞ لِّمَا مَعَكُمۡ لَتُؤۡمِنُنَّ بِهِۦ وَلَتَنصُرُنَّهُۥۚ قَالَ ءَأَقۡرَرۡتُمۡ وَأَخَذۡتُمۡ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكُمۡ إِصۡرِيۖ قَالُوٓاْ أَقۡرَرۡنَاۚ قَالَ فَٱشۡهَدُواْ وَأَنَا۠ مَعَكُم مِّنَ ٱلشَّٰهِدِينَ

Remember, O Messenger, when Allah took a firm covenant from the prophets, saying to them: If I give you a revealed book, teach you wisdom and make you reach a high rank and status, and then My Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم comes to you confirming the Book and wisdom that you have, then you must accept what he brings and help him as his followers. Do you, O prophets, accept this and fully commit to Me? They replied: We accept that. So Allah said: Be witnesses over yourselves and your nations, and I will also be a witness over you and them.

(٨٢) فَمَن تَوَلَّىٰ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ

Whoever turns away after this firm covenant witnessed by Allah and His messengers are the ones who abandon the religion of Allah and His obedience.

(٨٣) أَفَغَيۡرَ دِينِ ٱللَّهِ يَبۡغُونَ وَلَهُۥٓ أَسۡلَمَ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ طَوۡعٗا وَكَرۡهٗا وَإِلَيۡهِ يُرۡجَعُونَ

Are those who have abandoned the religion of Allah and obedience to Him looking for something other than Islam that Allah has chosen for His slaves? Every creature in the heavens and earth surrenders to Him, whether willingly like the believers or unwillingly, like the disbelievers. Subsequently, all of creation will return to Him on the Day of Resurrection for the final reckoning and recompense for their deeds.

Points of Benefit:

The misguidance of the scholars of the Jews, their plotting to distort the speech of Allah and their lying to the people about their distortion.
If anyone who claims to follow the religion of a Prophet from the many Islamic Prophets does not believe in Muhammad, then he has broken his covenant with Allah.
The greatest people are the scholars of sound knowledge who have knowledge, act accordingly, and nurture the people upon it.
The greatest of all misguidance is leaving off the religion of Allah, which all of the creation, good and evil, has submitted to.
