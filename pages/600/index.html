The Calamity (Surah Al-Qari'ah)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Thrashing the hearts to evoke the terrors of the [Day of] Resurrection and people's conditions on the scales.

Explanation:

(١) ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ

[It is] the Hour that thrashes people's hearts from the immensity of its horrors.

(٢) مَا ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ

What is this hour that thrashes people's hearts from the immensity of its horrors?

(٣) وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ

And what can make you comprehend, O Messenger, what this Hour that thrashes people's hearts from the immensity of its horrors is? Indeed, it is the Day of Resurrection.

(٤) يَوۡمَ يَكُونُ ٱلنَّاسُ كَٱلۡفَرَاشِ ٱلۡمَبۡثُوثِ

The Day when the people's hearts are struck, they will resemble scattered moths spread all over the place.

(٥) وَتَكُونُ ٱلۡجِبَالُ كَٱلۡعِهۡنِ ٱلۡمَنفُوشِ

And the mountains will be like fluffed wool in their agile movement and motion.

(٦) فَأَمَّا مَن ثَقُلَتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ

So, as for the one whose righteous deeds outweigh his evil deeds,

(٧) فَهُوَ فِي عِيشَةٖ رَّاضِيَةٖ

Then he will be in a pleasant life, which he will attain in Paradise.

(٨) وَأَمَّا مَنۡ خَفَّتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ

And as for the one whose evil deeds outweigh his righteous deeds,

(٩) فَأُمُّهُۥ هَاوِيَةٞ

His abode and permanent residence on the Day of Resurrection will be Hell.

(١٠) وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا هِيَهۡ

And what can make you comprehend, O Messenger, what it is?

(١١) نَارٌ حَامِيَةُۢ

It is a fire of intense heat.

The Rivalry in Worldly Increase (Surah At-Takathur)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Reminding those who hoard wealth and are distracted with this worldly life with the graves and the being called to account.

Explanation:

(١) أَلۡهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ

O people, boasting with one another over wealth and offspring has distracted you from Allah's obedience.

(٢) حَتَّىٰ زُرۡتُمُ ٱلۡمَقَابِرَ

Until you die and enter your graves.

(٣) كَلَّا سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ

You should not have let boasting about these matters distract you from worshipping Allah; you will come to know the consequences of that preoccupation.

(٤) ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ

Then you will surely know its consequences.

(٥) كَلَّا لَوۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ عِلۡمَ ٱلۡيَقِينِ

If you were to truly know with certainty that you are to be resurrected to Allah and that He will requite you for your deeds, you would not have become preoccupied with boasting with one another over wealth and offspring.

(٦) لَتَرَوُنَّ ٱلۡجَحِيمَ

By Allah, you will most surely witness the Fire on the Day of Resurrection.

(٧) ثُمَّ لَتَرَوُنَّهَا عَيۡنَ ٱلۡيَقِينِ

Then, you will most surely truly witness it with undoubtful certainty.

(٨) ثُمَّ لَتُسۡ‍َٔلُنَّ يَوۡمَئِذٍ عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

On the Day of Judgment, Allah will surely inquire about the bounties He granted you, such as your health, independence, and other similar blessings.

Points of Benefit:

It is in man's nature to love wealth.
The danger of boasting and bragging about one's wealth and children.
The grave is a place that people visit briefly on their way to the Hereafter.
On the Day of Resurrection, people will be asked about the blessings Allah bestowed upon them in the worldly life.
