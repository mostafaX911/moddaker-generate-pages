Time (Surah Al-'Asr)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Ways to be saved from loss.

Explanation:

(١) وَٱلۡعَصۡرِ

Allah (may He be Exalted) swore by the time of 'Asr (the late afternoon).

(٢) إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَفِي خُسۡرٍ

Indeed, the human being is surely in loss and destruction.

(٣) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ

Except for those who believed in Allah and His messengers, performed virtuous deeds, and encouraged truth towards one another and stayed patient upon it. Those described with these qualities are saved in their worldly life and the Hereafter.

The Traducer (Surah Al-Humazah)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Warning against mocking the believers due to being deceived by abundant wealth.

Explanation:

(١) وَيۡلٞ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٖ لُّمَزَةٍ

Evil consequences and severe torment are upon the one who frequently backbites and slanders people.

(٢) ٱلَّذِي جَمَعَ مَالٗا وَعَدَّدَهُۥ

Whose concern is to amass wealth and count it; he has no concern other than that.

(٣) يَحۡسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُۥٓ أَخۡلَدَهُۥ

He thinks his amassed wealth will save him from death, remaining immortal in this worldly life.

(٤) كَلَّاۖ لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ فِي ٱلۡحُطَمَةِ

The matter is not as this ignorant person envisions; he will certainly be thrown into Hellfire that crushes and breaks everything thrown into it from its agonizing torment.

(٥) وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡحُطَمَةُ

And what can make you comprehend, O Messenger, this fire that smashes everything thrown into it?

(٦) نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُوقَدَةُ

Indeed, it is the blazing fire of Allah.

(٧) ٱلَّتِي تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلۡأَفۡ‍ِٔدَةِ

That penetrates the bodies of people until it reaches their hearts.

(٨) إِنَّهَا عَلَيۡهِم مُّؤۡصَدَةٞ

Verily, it closes up around those punished within it, sealing them in.

(٩) فِي عَمَدٖ مُّمَدَّدَةِۢ

With towering extended columns, such that they are unable to leave it.

The Elephant (Surah Al-Fil)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing Allah's Omnipotence and His assault on those who invaded His Sacred House.

Explanation:

(١) أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡفِيلِ

O Messenger, do you not know how your Lord dealt with Abraha and his army, known as the People of the Elephant, when they intended to demolish the Kaaba?

(٢) أَلَمۡ يَجۡعَلۡ كَيۡدَهُمۡ فِي تَضۡلِيلٖ

Verily, Allah made their evil plot to destroy it in vain, for they did not achieve what they hoped to try to turn the people away from the Kaaba. They did not accomplish any part of it.

(٣) وَأَرۡسَلَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ طَيۡرًا أَبَابِيلَ

He sent birds upon them that came to them in flocks upon flocks,

(٤) تَرۡمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٖ مِّن سِجِّيلٖ

Striking them with stones of clay.

(٥) فَجَعَلَهُمۡ كَعَصۡفٖ مَّأۡكُولِۢ

So, Allah made them like the leaves of crops eaten and trampled upon by animals.

Points of Benefit:

The loss of those who do not have the characteristics of belief, working righteous deeds, enjoining the Truth, and enjoining patience.
The impermissibility of backbiting and slandering people.
Allah's defense of His Sacred House, and this is part of the security He has provided for it.
