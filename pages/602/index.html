Quraysh (Surah Quraysh)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Noting Allah's blessing upon the Quraysh and Allah's right upon them.

Explanation:

(١) لِإِيلَٰفِ قُرَيۡشٍ

Because of the custom of the Quraysh and their familiarity,

(٢) إِۦلَٰفِهِمۡ رِحۡلَةَ ٱلشِّتَآءِ وَٱلصَّيۡفِ

With the winter journey to Yemen and the summer journey to the Levant in safety,

(٣) فَلۡيَعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡبَيۡتِ

Let them worship Allah alone, the Lord of this Sacred House who made this journey easy for them, and let them not ascribe any partners to Him.

(٤) ٱلَّذِيٓ أَطۡعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعٖ وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنۡ خَوۡفِۢ

Who fed them from hunger and gave them security from fear, through the reverence he placed the hearts of the Arabs for the Sacred Sanctuary and its inhabitants.

The Small Kindnesses (Surah Al-Ma'un)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing the attributes of those who reject Judgment Day.

Explanation:

(١) أَرَءَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِي يُكَذِّبُ بِٱلدِّينِ

Do you know the one who denies recompense on the Day of Resurrection?

(٢) فَذَٰلِكَ ٱلَّذِي يَدُعُّ ٱلۡيَتِيمَ

For he is the one who harshly repulses the orphan from his need.

(٣) وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينِ

And does not enjoin upon himself nor others to feed the pauper.

(٤) فَوَيۡلٞ لِّلۡمُصَلِّينَ

So, destruction and torment be upon those who perform the prayer, who are heedless of their prayer, unconcerned with it to the point that its time expires.

(٥) ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَن صَلَاتِهِمۡ سَاهُونَ

So, destruction and torment be upon those who perform the prayer, who are heedless of their prayer, unconcerned with it to the point that its time expires.

(٦) ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ يُرَآءُونَ

Who show off through their prayers and deeds, failing to perform their virtuous deeds sincerely for Allah.

(٧) وَيَمۡنَعُونَ ٱلۡمَاعُونَ

And they avoid helping others with that which there is no harm [to them] in helping with.

The Abundance (Surah Al-Kawthar)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing Allah's blessing upon His Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم as abundantly good and defending him.

Explanation:

(١) إِنَّآ أَعۡطَيۡنَٰكَ ٱلۡكَوۡثَرَ

Indeed, We have granted you, O Messenger, abundant goodness. Among it is the river of Kawthar in Paradise.

(٢) فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنۡحَرۡ

So give the thanks [due to] Allah for this blessing by praying and slaughtering for His sake alone, as opposed to the offerings of sacrifice the polytheists perform, seeking nearness to their idols.

(٣) إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ ٱلۡأَبۡتَرُ

Indeed, your hater is cut off from every goodness and will be forgotten. If recalled, he will be mentioned negatively.

Points of Benefit:

The importance of security in Islam.
Showing off is one of the diseases of the heart, and it nullifies deeds.
Meeting blessings with gratitude increases them.
The Prophet's صلى الله عليه وسلم dignity, protection, and honor are all upon His Lord in this life and the next.
