The Disbelievers (Surah Al-Kafiroon)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Absolving oneself from disbelief and its people.

Explanation:

(١) قُلۡ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ

Say, O Messenger: O you disbelievers in Allah.

(٢) لَآ أَعۡبُدُ مَا تَعۡبُدُونَ

I will not presently nor in the future worship the idols you worship.

(٣) وَلَآ أَنتُمۡ عَٰبِدُونَ مَآ أَعۡبُدُ

Nor are you worshipping what I worship, and that is Allah alone.

(٤) وَلَآ أَنَا۠ عَابِدٞ مَّا عَبَدتُّمۡ

Nor am I worshipping what you have worshipped of idols.

(٥) وَلَآ أَنتُمۡ عَٰبِدُونَ مَآ أَعۡبُدُ

Nor are you worshipping what I worship, and that is Allah alone.

(٦) لَكُمۡ دِينُكُمۡ وَلِيَ دِينِ

You have your religion, which you have invented for yourselves, and I have my religion, that Allah sent down to me.

The Divine Support (Surah An-Nasr)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Giving the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم the good news of victory and the completion of the mission.

Explanation:

(١) إِذَا جَآءَ نَصۡرُ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡفَتۡحُ

When Allah's victory, O Messenger, arrives for your religion, becoming fortified, and the conquest of Mecca comes to pass.

(٢) وَرَأَيۡتَ ٱلنَّاسَ يَدۡخُلُونَ فِي دِينِ ٱللَّهِ أَفۡوَاجٗا

And you see the people entering Islam, delegation after delegation.

(٣) فَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ تَوَّابَۢا

Then know that is a sign of nearing the end of the duty you were sent with. So, exalt your Lord by praising Him, thanking Him for the blessing of victory and conquest, and seeking His forgiveness. Indeed, He is the Accepter of Repentance, receiving His slaves' contrition and forgiving them.

The Rope of Palm Fiber (Surah Al-Masad)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Describing the loss of Abu Lahab and his wife.

Explanation:

(١) تَبَّتۡ يَدَآ أَبِي لَهَبٖ وَتَبَّ

Both hands of the paternal uncle of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم, Abu Lahab, son of 'Abd al-Muttalib, have perished from his futile deeds, for he used to harm the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم. His efforts have failed.

(٢) مَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنۡهُ مَالُهُۥ وَمَا كَسَبَ

What need did his wealth and children fulfill for him? They did not defend him against torment nor bring him mercy.

(٣) سَيَصۡلَىٰ نَارٗا ذَاتَ لَهَبٖ

He will enter the blazing Fire on the Day of Resurrection and suffer its heat.

(٤) وَٱمۡرَأَتُهُۥ حَمَّالَةَ ٱلۡحَطَبِ

And his wife, Umm Jamil, who used to harm the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم by throwing thorns into his path, will also enter it.

(٥) فِي جِيدِهَا حَبۡلٞ مِّن مَّسَدِۢ

Around her neck will be a tightly bound rope, with which she will be led into the Fire.

Points of Benefit:

Splitting from the disbelievers.
Meeting blessings with gratitude.
Surah Al-Masad is one of the signs of prophethood because it judged that Abu Lahab would die as a disbeliever, and he died ten years after that a disbeliever.
The validity of the disbelievers' marriage.
