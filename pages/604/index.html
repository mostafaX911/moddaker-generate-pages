The Sincerity (Surah Al-Ikhlas)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Singling out Allah with worship and perfection and exalting Him from having a son, father, or partner.

Explanation:

(١) قُلۡ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ

Say, O Messenger: He is Allah, singular in the [attribute] of being worshipped. There is none worthy of worship other than Him.

(٢) ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ

He is the Master to whom belongs complete supremacy in the attributes of perfection and beauty, whom all creation seek for their needs.

(٣) لَمۡ يَلِدۡ وَلَمۡ يُولَدۡ

The One who does not give birth to anyone, nor did anyone give birth to Him. He has neither offspring (may He be Exalted) nor parent.

(٤) وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدُۢ

Nor is there anyone like Him from His creation.

The Daybreak (Surah Al-Falaq)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Encouragement to hold fast to Allah against evil.

Explanation:

(١) قُلۡ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلۡفَلَقِ

Say, O Messenger: I seek refuge in the Lord of the morning, and I seek His protection.

(٢) مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ

From the evil of what harms from [all of] creation.

(٣) وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ

And I seek refuge in Allah from the evils that appear at night of harmful creatures and thieves.

(٤) وَمِن شَرِّ ٱلنَّفَّٰثَٰتِ فِي ٱلۡعُقَدِ

And I seek refuge in Him from the evil of the sorceresses who blow into knots.

(٥) وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ

And I seek refuge in Him from the evil of the jealous one when he acts out of jealousy.

Mankind (Surah An-Nas)

Meccan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Encouragement to seek refuge in Allah from the evil of the Devil and his whisperings.

Explanation:

(١) قُلۡ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ

Say, O Messenger: I seek refuge in the Lord of the people and seek His protection.

(٢) مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ

The Sovereign of humanity, who does whatever He wishes with them. They have no [absolute] ruler other than Him.

(٣) إِلَٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ

He is their rightful deity; they do not have a true god except Him.

(٤) مِن شَرِّ ٱلۡوَسۡوَاسِ ٱلۡخَنَّاسِ

[I seek refuge] from the devil's evil, who casts his whisper upon the human being when he neglects Allah's remembrance and delays it when he does remember.

(٥) ٱلَّذِي يُوَسۡوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ

He casts his whispers into people's hearts.

(٦) مِنَ ٱلۡجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ

And he (the devil) could be from humanity as well as from the jinn.

Points of Benefit:

Affirming the attributes of perfection for Allah and negating the attributes of deficiency.
Confirming magic and the means of treating it.
Treating the Devil's whisperings through Allah's remembrance and seeking refuge from the Devil.
