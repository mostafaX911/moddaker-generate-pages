(٨٤) قُلۡ ءَامَنَّا بِٱللَّهِ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيۡنَا وَمَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلَ وَإِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَ وَٱلۡأَسۡبَاطِ وَمَآ أُوتِيَ مُوسَىٰ وَعِيسَىٰ وَٱلنَّبِيُّونَ مِن رَّبِّهِمۡ لَا نُفَرِّقُ بَيۡنَ أَحَدٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ وَنَحۡنُ لَهُۥ مُسۡلِمُونَ

Say, O Messenger: We have believed in Allah as the one to be worshipped, having obeyed what He commanded. We have believed in the revelation that was sent down to us, and what was revealed to Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, and Jacob, and in what was revealed to the prophets among Jacob's descendants, and in the books and miracles that were given to Moses, Jesus, and all the prophets by their Lord. Also, we make no distinction between them, believing in all of them and that we all submit to Allah alone, surrendering in devotion to Him.

(٨٥) وَمَن يَبۡتَغِ غَيۡرَ ٱلۡإِسۡلَٰمِ دِينٗا فَلَن يُقۡبَلَ مِنۡهُ وَهُوَ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ

Those who seek a religion other than what Allah is pleased with, the religion of Islam, will not have it accepted by Allah. They will be of those who have incurred a great loss to themselves by entering the Fire.

(٨٦) كَيۡفَ يَهۡدِي ٱللَّهُ قَوۡمٗا كَفَرُواْ بَعۡدَ إِيمَٰنِهِمۡ وَشَهِدُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱلرَّسُولَ حَقّٞ وَجَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتُۚ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

How would Allah guide to belief in Him and His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم a people who disbelieved after they had once believed in Him and having witnessed that the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم brought the truth, and after clear proofs of the truth came to them? Allah does not guide wrongdoers to believe in Him and those who choose misguidance over guidance.

(٨٧) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ جَزَآؤُهُمۡ أَنَّ عَلَيۡهِمۡ لَعۡنَةَ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ أَجۡمَعِينَ

The recompense for those wrongdoers who chose falsehood then upon them is the curse of Allah, the angels, and all of humanity; as a result, they will be excluded and far removed from the mercy of Allah.

(٨٨) خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا لَا يُخَفَّفُ عَنۡهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ وَلَا هُمۡ يُنظَرُونَ

[These disbelievers] will live eternally in the Fire, never to leave! Their suffering will not be lightened, and they will not be given more time to repent, ask for forgiveness, or make excuses.

(٨٩) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ تَابُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَ وَأَصۡلَحُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٌ

[This torment is for everyone] except those who repent to Allah, asking for forgiveness after their disbelief and wrongdoing and rectify their deeds. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Compassionate to those from His slaves who repent to Him.

(٩٠) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بَعۡدَ إِيمَٰنِهِمۡ ثُمَّ ٱزۡدَادُواْ كُفۡرٗا لَّن تُقۡبَلَ تَوۡبَتُهُمۡ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلضَّآلُّونَ

Those who disbelieved after believing and remained in this state until death, their repentance will not be accepted from them when they are near their end as it will be too late. Such people are misguided from the straight path that leads to Allah, the Exalted.

(٩١) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَمَاتُواْ وَهُمۡ كُفَّارٞ فَلَن يُقۡبَلَ مِنۡ أَحَدِهِم مِّلۡءُ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ذَهَبٗا وَلَوِ ٱفۡتَدَىٰ بِهِۦٓۗ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ وَمَا لَهُم مِّن نَّٰصِرِينَ

Indeed, for those who disbelieved and died as disbelievers, no ransom will be accepted from any of them, even if they offer the weight of the earth in gold in exchange for their release from the Fire. They will suffer a painful punishment and have no helpers on the Day of Resurrection who can repel the torment from them.

Points of Benefit:

It is obligatory to believe in all of the Prophets whom Allah, the Exalted, sent and in all of the books that were sent to them without differentiating between them.
After sending the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, Allah, the Exalted, accepts no religion from anyone, whatever it may be, except the Islam that he brought.
If one persists in misguidance and continues on its path, then Allah may punish him by not enabling him to seek forgiveness and guidance.
The door of forgiveness is open for the slave until death comes to him or until the sun rises from the west; then, no repentance will be accepted from him.
Nothing will save a person from the punishment of the Fire on the Day of Resurrection except their good deeds. As for wealth, even if it were to fill the Earth, it would not benefit even in the least.
