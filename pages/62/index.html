(٩٢) لَن تَنَالُواْ ٱلۡبِرَّ حَتَّىٰ تُنفِقُواْ مِمَّا تُحِبُّونَۚ وَمَا تُنفِقُواْ مِن شَيۡءٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِهِۦ عَلِيمٞ

O believers, you will not attain the reward of the people of righteousness and their rank until you spend from the wealth you love in the path of Allah. Whatever you spend, a little or a lot, Allah is aware of your intentions and deeds, and He will recompense everyone according to their deeds.

(٩٣) ۞كُلُّ ٱلطَّعَامِ كَانَ حِلّٗا لِّبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ إِلَّا مَا حَرَّمَ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلُ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِۦ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن تُنَزَّلَ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةُۚ قُلۡ فَأۡتُواْ بِٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِ فَٱتۡلُوهَآ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

All pure food was permissible to Israelites, and nothing from it was forbidden to them except what Jacob prohibited for himself before the revelation of the Torah. It is not as Jews allege that the prohibitions were in the Torah. Say to them, O Prophet: Then bring forward the Torah and recite it if you are truthful regarding your claim. Instead, they fabricated lies and did not bring forth the Torah. It is an example demonstrating the false accusations of Jews against the Torah and a distortion of its contents.

(٩٤) فَمَنِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡكَذِبَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

So, whoever fabricates lies against Allah after the appearance of proof that Jacob (peace be upon him) prohibited it for himself without a prohibition from Allah are the ones who oppress themselves by abandoning the truth after the appearance of its proof.

(٩٥) قُلۡ صَدَقَ ٱللَّهُۗ فَٱتَّبِعُواْ مِلَّةَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ حَنِيفٗاۖ وَمَا كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ

Say, O Prophet: Allah has told the truth about what He has informed of from Jacob (peace be upon him) and everything He has revealed and legislated. So, follow the religion of Abraham, peace be upon him. For surely, he was someone who shunned all religions and turned towards Islam. And he never associated partners with Allah.

(٩٦) إِنَّ أَوَّلَ بَيۡتٖ وُضِعَ لِلنَّاسِ لَلَّذِي بِبَكَّةَ مُبَارَكٗا وَهُدٗى لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Indeed, the first house built to worship Allah on earth for all people is the House of Allah, the Holy Sanctuary, which is in Mecca. It is a blessed House holding many religious and worldly benefits. And there is guidance in it for all the worlds.

(٩٧) فِيهِ ءَايَٰتُۢ بَيِّنَٰتٞ مَّقَامُ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَۖ وَمَن دَخَلَهُۥ كَانَ ءَامِنٗاۗ وَلِلَّهِ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ حِجُّ ٱلۡبَيۡتِ مَنِ ٱسۡتَطَاعَ إِلَيۡهِ سَبِيلٗاۚ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَنِيٌّ عَنِ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

There are apparent signs of the nobility and virtue of this House, like the religious rites and rituals performed in its precincts. Among these signs is the Stone upon which Abraham stood when he set about erecting the wall of the Kaaba. Also, among them is that for whoever visits it, fear disappears from him, and harm does not affect him. Allah obligates people to travel to this House in order to fulfill the rites of the pilgrimage, for whoever is able to reach it. And whoever denies the obligation of the pilgrimage, surely Allah is not in need of such a disbeliever, nor is He in need of all the worlds put together.

(٩٨) قُلۡ يَٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لِمَ تَكۡفُرُونَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱللَّهُ شَهِيدٌ عَلَىٰ مَا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Say, O Prophet: "O People of the Book among the Jews and Christians, why do you deny clear proofs of the veracity of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم? And among them are clear proofs presented in the very Torah and Bible?! Allah is well aware of this action of yours. He is a witness of it, and He will requite you for it."

(٩٩) قُلۡ يَٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لِمَ تَصُدُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ مَنۡ ءَامَنَ تَبۡغُونَهَا عِوَجٗا وَأَنتُمۡ شُهَدَآءُۗ وَمَا ٱللَّهُ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Say, O Prophet: "O People of the Book among the Jews and Christians, why do you deter from the religion of Allah he who believes among the people; seeking to pose in the religion of Allah as a means of diversion from truth to falsehood; and to pose as a means of deviation from guidance for its people. All the while, you are witnesses to the fact that this religion is the truth and a confirmation of what is in your own scriptures. Allah is not unaware of what you commit of Disbelief in Him and posing as an obstruction from His path. He will recompense you for it."

(١٠٠) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن تُطِيعُواْ فَرِيقٗا مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ يَرُدُّوكُم بَعۡدَ إِيمَٰنِكُمۡ كَٰفِرِينَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, if you obey a group of the People of the Book among the Jews and Christians in what they say and you accept their thinking in what they allege, they will cause you to return to Disbelief after Belief due to what is in them of jealousy and deviation from guidance.

Points of Benefit:

The Jews falsely claimed that the foods prohibited by Jacob (peace be upon him) were revealed in the Torah.
The Sacred House is the most excellent and most honored place of worship, as it was the first House established for worshiping Allah and has unique characteristics not found anywhere else.
Allah has mentioned the obligation of making Hajj using the most emphatic words to show how vital this obligation is.
Following the whims of the People of the Book leads to misguidance and distancing from the religion of Allah, the Exalted.
