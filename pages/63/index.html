(١٠١) وَكَيۡفَ تَكۡفُرُونَ وَأَنتُمۡ تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡكُمۡ ءَايَٰتُ ٱللَّهِ وَفِيكُمۡ رَسُولُهُۥۗ وَمَن يَعۡتَصِم بِٱللَّهِ فَقَدۡ هُدِيَ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ

How can you disbelieve in Allah after believing in Him while there is accompanying you the greatest reason for affirming belief!? The verses of Allah are being recited to you, and His Messenger Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم is clarifying them for you. Whoever holds fast to the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of His Messenger, then Allah has indeed guided him to a straight path in which there is no crookedness.

(١٠٢) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ حَقَّ تُقَاتِهِۦ وَلَا تَمُوتُنَّ إِلَّا وَأَنتُم مُّسۡلِمُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, fear your Lord as He deserves to be feared. That is by following his commands, staying far away from what He prohibits, thanking Him for His favors, and holding fast to your religion until death arrives while you are in that state.

(١٠٣) وَٱعۡتَصِمُواْ بِحَبۡلِ ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعٗا وَلَا تَفَرَّقُواْۚ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِذۡ كُنتُمۡ أَعۡدَآءٗ فَأَلَّفَ بَيۡنَ قُلُوبِكُمۡ فَأَصۡبَحۡتُم بِنِعۡمَتِهِۦٓ إِخۡوَٰنٗا وَكُنتُمۡ عَلَىٰ شَفَا حُفۡرَةٖ مِّنَ ٱلنَّارِ فَأَنقَذَكُم مِّنۡهَاۗ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَهۡتَدُونَ

Hold fast, O believers, to the Book and the Sunnah. And do not commit actions that will cause you to fall into disunity. Remember the favors of Allah upon you. There was a time when you were enemies before Islam, fighting over the pettiest reasons. Then, He joined your hearts through Islam, and you became brothers in the religion, merciful towards one another and mutually sincere to each other in advice through His grace. Before that, you were on the verge of entering the Fire through your Disbelief. Then, Allah rescued you from it with Islam, and He guided you towards Faith. Just as Allah made this clear to you, He also makes clear to you that which will rectify your conditions in the worldly life and the Afterlife – so that you may be guided to the right path and traverse the straight path.

(١٠٤) وَلۡتَكُن مِّنكُمۡ أُمَّةٞ يَدۡعُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡخَيۡرِ وَيَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَيَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِۚ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ

Let there be from among you, O believers, a group inviting to every good that Allah loves, commanding the good that the law establishes and sound reason promotes while preventing evil that the laws prohibit and sound reason abhors. Those described with this quality are the people of complete victory in the worldly life as well as the Afterlife.

(١٠٥) وَلَا تَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّذِينَ تَفَرَّقُواْ وَٱخۡتَلَفُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتُۚ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٞ

Do not be, O believers, like the People of the Book who divided themselves and became factions and partisans. They differed regarding their religion after clear signs had come to them from Allah, the Exalted. And these ones mentioned have a severe torment from Allah.

(١٠٦) يَوۡمَ تَبۡيَضُّ وُجُوهٞ وَتَسۡوَدُّ وُجُوهٞۚ فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡوَدَّتۡ وُجُوهُهُمۡ أَكَفَرۡتُم بَعۡدَ إِيمَٰنِكُمۡ فَذُوقُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡفُرُونَ

This torment will befall them on the Day of Resurrection when the faces of the Faithful will be illuminated out of joy and happiness. And the faces of disbelievers will be darkened from sadness and grief. As for those whose faces will be darkened during that grievous day, it will be said to them reproachingly: "Did you deny Allah's Oneness and the covenant He accepted on the basis that you do not associate any partners with Him- after your attestation and confirmation of it?! So, taste the torment of Allah, which He promised you because of your disbelief."

(١٠٧) وَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ٱبۡيَضَّتۡ وُجُوهُهُمۡ فَفِي رَحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

As for those whose faces will be illuminated, their position will be in gardens of delight, dwelling in them forever, in a delight that will not diminish nor fade.

(١٠٨) تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱللَّهِ نَتۡلُوهَا عَلَيۡكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۗ وَمَا ٱللَّهُ يُرِيدُ ظُلۡمٗا لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

Those verses, including the promise and threat of Allah, We recite upon you, O Prophet, with truthful information and just rulings. Allah does not wish to oppress anyone in the worlds. Rather, no one will be punished except by that which his own hand has earned.

Points of Benefit:

Holding fast to the Book and the Sunnah and adhering to their guidance is the greatest means to remain steadfast on the truth and to be immune from misguidance and division.
The current-day division and differences among the Muslims concerning the issues of belief also occurred among the People of the Scripture who came before.
The necessity to enjoin what is good and forbid what is evil because this is the way the Muslims will succeed and the means through which they will excel.
