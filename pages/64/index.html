(١٠٩) وَلِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ تُرۡجَعُ ٱلۡأُمُورُ

And Allah (the Transcendent) alone is sovereign over what is in the heavens and earth, in both of creation and command. He (the Exalted) is the one to which every matter of His creation will return. Then, He will recompense all of them according to what each one deserves.

(١١٠) كُنتُمۡ خَيۡرَ أُمَّةٍ أُخۡرِجَتۡ لِلنَّاسِ تَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَتَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَتُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِۗ وَلَوۡ ءَامَنَ أَهۡلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَكَانَ خَيۡرٗا لَّهُمۚ مِّنۡهُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَأَكۡثَرُهُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ

You, O nation of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم are the best nation Allah has brought forward for all humanity in your Faith and your actions. You are the most beneficial of people for all humanity in that you command the good that the law establishes and sound reason promotes. You prohibit the evil which the law forbids and sound reasoning abhors. You believe in Allah with decisive faith, whose actions prove to be true. If the People of the Book, among the Jews and Christians, would believe in Muhammadصلى الله عليه وسلم that would be better for them in terms of their worldly life and their Afterlife. A small number of the People of the Book believe in what Muhammadصلى الله عليه وسلم brought. However, most of them turn away from the religion and legislation of Allah.

(١١١) لَن يَضُرُّوكُمۡ إِلَّآ أَذٗىۖ وَإِن يُقَٰتِلُوكُمۡ يُوَلُّوكُمُ ٱلۡأَدۡبَارَ ثُمَّ لَا يُنصَرُونَ

Among them is an enemy who will not harm you, O believers, in your religion nor yourselves except the injury caused by their tongues in terms of religious defamation, mockery of you, and similar things. If they fight you, they will flee defeated before you and never be helped against you.

(١١٢) ضُرِبَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلذِّلَّةُ أَيۡنَ مَا ثُقِفُوٓاْ إِلَّا بِحَبۡلٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَحَبۡلٖ مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَبَآءُو بِغَضَبٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَضُرِبَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡمَسۡكَنَةُۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ يَكۡفُرُونَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَقۡتُلُونَ ٱلۡأَنۢبِيَآءَ بِغَيۡرِ حَقّٖۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِمَا عَصَواْ وَّكَانُواْ يَعۡتَدُونَ

Disgrace and humiliation are caused to surround the Jews, encompassing them wherever they are found. So, they are not safe except through a covenant or guarantee of security from Allah, the Exalted, or the people. They bring about anger from Allah while need and poverty are caused to surround them. What is brought against them is because of their disbelief in the signs of Allah and their oppressive killing of His Prophets. It is also because of their disobedience and transgression of the limits of Allah. When Allah made clear the prevailing condition of the People of the Book, He also made clear the condition of a group among them who seek to be upright and stand up for the truth. So, He said:

After highlighting the condition of the majority of the People of the Book, Allah mentions a group of believers among them who remain committed to the truth, stating:

(١١٣) ۞لَيۡسُواْ سَوَآءٗۗ مِّنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أُمَّةٞ قَآئِمَةٞ يَتۡلُونَ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ ءَانَآءَ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَهُمۡ يَسۡجُدُونَ

The People of the Book are not all equal in their condition. Rather, there is a group of them who are upright in Allah's religion, upholding Allah's commands and prohibitions, reciting Allah's verses in the hours of the night while praying to Allah. This group existed before the mission of the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. Those among them who came to know of this mission accepted Islam.

(١١٤) يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَيَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَيَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَيُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

They believe in Allah and the Last Day with decisive faith. They command what is right and virtuous and prohibit what is wrong and evil. They take the initiative in good deeds and take advantage of times for acts of obedience. Those described with these qualities are among the slaves of Allah whose intentions and actions are upright.

(١١٥) وَمَا يَفۡعَلُواْ مِنۡ خَيۡرٖ فَلَن يُكۡفَرُوهُۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Whatever good these people do, whether a little or a lot, their repayment will not be lost, nor their reward. Allah knows the fearful who follow His commands and avoid what He prohibits. Nothing of their actions is hidden from Him, and He will recompense them accordingly.

Points of Benefit:

One of the most significant distinguishing features of the Muslims, after belief in Allah, is enjoining what is good and forbidding what is evil.
Allah has decreed humiliation for the People of the Scripture due to their turning away from Allah's religion and not fulfilling their promises.
The People of the Scripture are not all the same. Among them are those who do what Allah instructs, follow the religion, and keep within His limits; they will be significantly rewarded. Such people came before the Prophet Muhammad [صلى الله عليه وسلم] was sent.
