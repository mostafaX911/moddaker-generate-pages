(١١٦) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَن تُغۡنِيَ عَنۡهُمۡ أَمۡوَٰلُهُمۡ وَلَآ أَوۡلَٰدُهُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

For those who disbelieve in Allah and His Messenger, neither their wealth nor their children will defend them at all from Allah- they will not deter His punishment or earn His mercy for them. Rather, [their wealth and children] will only increase their torment and distress. They will be companions of the Fire, remaining in it.

(١١٧) مَثَلُ مَا يُنفِقُونَ فِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا كَمَثَلِ رِيحٖ فِيهَا صِرٌّ أَصَابَتۡ حَرۡثَ قَوۡمٖ ظَلَمُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ فَأَهۡلَكَتۡهُۚ وَمَا ظَلَمَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَلَٰكِنۡ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَظۡلِمُونَ

A parable for what these disbelievers spend in the causes of benevolence and what they expect of rewards for it is like a freezing wind that hits and ruins the crops of a people. So, their crops were ruined when they had expected a great amount of good. Just as this wind ruined the crops and ruined the benefit in them, likewise, disbelief ruins the reward of actions in which they had hope. Allah does not wrong them---He is far above that---rather, they only wrong themselves because of their disbelief in Him and their denial of His Messengers.

(١١٨) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَتَّخِذُواْ بِطَانَةٗ مِّن دُونِكُمۡ لَا يَأۡلُونَكُمۡ خَبَالٗا وَدُّواْ مَا عَنِتُّمۡ قَدۡ بَدَتِ ٱلۡبَغۡضَآءُ مِنۡ أَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَمَا تُخۡفِي صُدُورُهُمۡ أَكۡبَرُۚ قَدۡ بَيَّنَّا لَكُمُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِۖ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, do not take disbelievers as close and true friends, letting them know your secrets and the particulars of your state of affairs. They will not slacken in seeking to harm you and corrupt your state of affairs. They wish for the occurrence of what will harm you and cause difficulty for you. Aversion and hostility have appeared on their tongues through defamation of your religion, incidents between you, and spilling your secrets. The aversion they conceal in their chests is even greater. Evident proofs of what is in your best interest in the worldly life and the Afterlife have been made clear to you, O believers, if only you would realize what your Lord has revealed to you.

(١١٩) هَٰٓأَنتُمۡ أُوْلَآءِ تُحِبُّونَهُمۡ وَلَا يُحِبُّونَكُمۡ وَتُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡكِتَٰبِ كُلِّهِۦ وَإِذَا لَقُوكُمۡ قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا وَإِذَا خَلَوۡاْ عَضُّواْ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡأَنَامِلَ مِنَ ٱلۡغَيۡظِۚ قُلۡ مُوتُواْ بِغَيۡظِكُمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

There you are, O believers, loving these people and wishing them well while they do not love you or wish you well. Rather, they hate you while you believe in all the books, including their books. Yet, they do not believe in the Book that Allah revealed to your Prophet. When they meet you, they say with their tongues: We accept it as true. And when they are away from you with each other, they bite the tips of their fingers out of sorrow and rage because of your unity, your unanimity, the prestige of Islam, and because of their own humiliation. Say, O Prophet, to these people: Remain as you are until you die out of sorrow and rage. Surely, Allah knows what is in the chests of faith and disbelief as well as the good and evil.

(١٢٠) إِن تَمۡسَسۡكُمۡ حَسَنَةٞ تَسُؤۡهُمۡ وَإِن تُصِبۡكُمۡ سَيِّئَةٞ يَفۡرَحُواْ بِهَاۖ وَإِن تَصۡبِرُواْ وَتَتَّقُواْ لَا يَضُرُّكُمۡ كَيۡدُهُمۡ شَيۡ‍ًٔاۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِمَا يَعۡمَلُونَ مُحِيطٞ

If a blessing happens for you, O believers, in the form of victory over an enemy or an increase in wealth and children, worry and sadness befalls them. And if calamity strikes you, such as the victory of an enemy or a decrease in wealth or children, they are happy with that, rejoicing at your misfortune. But, if you are patient with His commands and decrees and shelter yourself from His anger, then their plots and nuisances will not harm you. Surely, Allah is Well-Acquainted with what they scheme. And He will cause them to be unsuccessful.

(١٢١) وَإِذۡ غَدَوۡتَ مِنۡ أَهۡلِكَ تُبَوِّئُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ مَقَٰعِدَ لِلۡقِتَالِۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ

Remember, O Prophet, when you departed from Medina at the beginning of the day to fight the polytheists at Uhud. You were occupied with the believers being accommodated in their places for battle. So, you made each individual's position clear. And Allah hears your statements, and He is knowledgeable of your actions.

Points of Benefit:

The believers are prohibited from becoming close friends with the disbelievers and disclosing their private affairs and secrets.
One form of hostility from the disbelievers is that they are pleased when something terrible happens to the believers, and they are enraged when something good happens to them.
Protection from the plots and schemes of the disbelievers is through patience, not showing fear, and being Mindful while using the means that bring about strength and victory.
