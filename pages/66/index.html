(١٢٢) إِذۡ هَمَّت طَّآئِفَتَانِ مِنكُمۡ أَن تَفۡشَلَا وَٱللَّهُ وَلِيُّهُمَاۗ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ

Remember, O Prophet, what happened between two groups of believers from the tribes of Salamah and Haritha, when they weakened and were on the verge of retreat when the hypocrites withdrew. However, Allah aided them by making them firm in fighting and turning them away from what they were on the verge of doing. So, let the believers rely on Allah alone in every circumstance.

(١٢٣) وَلَقَدۡ نَصَرَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِبَدۡرٖ وَأَنتُمۡ أَذِلَّةٞۖ فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

Allah gave you victory over the polytheists during the Battle of Badr, while you were considered weak due to your small numbers and meager resources. Therefore, be fearful of Allah that perhaps you may be thankful for His blessings granted you.

(١٢٤) إِذۡ تَقُولُ لِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ أَلَن يَكۡفِيَكُمۡ أَن يُمِدَّكُمۡ رَبُّكُم بِثَلَٰثَةِ ءَالَٰفٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ مُنزَلِينَ

Remember, O Prophet, when you said to the believers during the battle of Badr, as reassurance for them after they heard that reinforcements for the polytheists were incoming: Is it not sufficient that Allah will help you with three thousand angels sent down from Him, the Exalted, to reinforce you in battle?

(١٢٥) بَلَىٰٓۚ إِن تَصۡبِرُواْ وَتَتَّقُواْ وَيَأۡتُوكُم مِّن فَوۡرِهِمۡ هَٰذَا يُمۡدِدۡكُمۡ رَبُّكُم بِخَمۡسَةِ ءَالَٰفٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ مُسَوِّمِينَ

Indeed, that should suffice you. There is the good news of other aid from Allah: if you are patient in fighting and fearful of Allah and the reinforcements of your enemies come rushing at you – if that takes place - your Lord will help you with five thousand angels, marking themselves and their horses with apparent signs.

(١٢٦) وَمَا جَعَلَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا بُشۡرَىٰ لَكُمۡ وَلِتَطۡمَئِنَّ قُلُوبُكُم بِهِۦۗ وَمَا ٱلنَّصۡرُ إِلَّا مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ

Allah did not grant you this help and reinforcement with angels except for it to be delightful news to settle your hearts. In fact, the real victory is not through these external means. Rather, true victory is only from Allah, the Almighty, whom none can conquer- the All-Wise in His discretion and sacred laws.

(١٢٧) لِيَقۡطَعَ طَرَفٗا مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَوۡ يَكۡبِتَهُمۡ فَيَنقَلِبُواْ خَآئِبِينَ

The intention behind this victory that materialized for you in the Battle of Badr was so that one group of disbelievers would be destroyed by death, and another group would be disgraced with their defeat, enraging them. Then, they would retreat, fainthearted and humiliated.

(١٢٨) لَيۡسَ لَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ شَيۡءٌ أَوۡ يَتُوبَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَوۡ يُعَذِّبَهُمۡ فَإِنَّهُمۡ ظَٰلِمُونَ

When the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم supplicated for the destruction of the polytheists' leaders after what transpired on their part during the Battle of Uhud, Allah said to him: "Nothing of their affair is for you [to determine]. Rather, all matters are for Allah [to determine]. So, be patient until Allah decides between you: either by guiding them to repentance and the acceptance of Islam; or by their persisting in disbelief. Then, He will punish them. In that case, they would indeed be oppressors deserving of punishment."

(١٢٩) وَلِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ يَغۡفِرُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيُعَذِّبُ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

To Allah belongs the creation and management of all that is in the heavens and the earth. He forgives the sins of whomever He wills among His slaves through His Mercy. And He punishes whomever He wills through his justice. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful to those who repent to Him.

(١٣٠) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَأۡكُلُواْ ٱلرِّبَوٰٓاْ أَضۡعَٰفٗا مُّضَٰعَفَةٗۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, keep far away from receiving usury- increasing and doubling the capital that you advance to others as was done by the people pre-Islam. Be fearful of Allah by following His commands and staying away from what He has prohibited so that perhaps you may attain the good you seek of the worldly life and the Afterlife.

(١٣١) وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱلنَّارَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أُعِدَّتۡ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Place a shield between yourselves and the Fire that Allah has prepared for disbelievers through righteous deeds and leaving behind what is prohibited.

(١٣٢) وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلرَّسُولَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ

Obey Allah and His Messenger by following the commands and staying away from the prohibitions so that perhaps you may receive mercy in the worldly life and the Afterlife.

Points of Benefit:

The permissibility of reminding others of the blessings and trials that people go through so that they may learn lessons from it.
Being constantly mindful of Allah and patient during times of great difficulties are some of the biggest reasons for Allah granting victory to His slaves.
Allah has control of all affairs. He decrees and judges as He wills, and the true believer surrenders wholeheartedly to Allah, conforming to His wisdom.
Sins and usury, in particular, are among the biggest causes of people's ruin, especially during adversities and hardships.
The mention of the prohibition of usury among the verses about the Battle of Uhud reflects the comprehensiveness of Islam in its laws and how they are all interconnected, as it refers to some of these laws while discussing various other matters.
