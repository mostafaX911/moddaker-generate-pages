(١٣٣) ۞وَسَارِعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَغۡفِرَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرۡضُهَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ أُعِدَّتۡ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Take the initiative and compete with one another in performing good deeds and drawing near to Allah through different types of obedient acts. Do this in order to obtain significant forgiveness from Allah and to be admitted into a garden as wide as the heavens and the earth, which Allah has prepared for those among His slaves who are fearful of Him.

(١٣٤) ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ فِي ٱلسَّرَّآءِ وَٱلضَّرَّآءِ وَٱلۡكَٰظِمِينَ ٱلۡغَيۡظَ وَٱلۡعَافِينَ عَنِ ٱلنَّاسِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Those who fear Allah spend generously from their wealth in the path of Allah, in a state of ease and hardship. They resist anger despite the ability to retaliate, and they overlook those who oppress them. Allah loves the benevolent who are described with these elements of good conduct.

(١٣٥) وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا فَعَلُواْ فَٰحِشَةً أَوۡ ظَلَمُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ ذَكَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ فَٱسۡتَغۡفَرُواْ لِذُنُوبِهِمۡ وَمَن يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَلَمۡ يُصِرُّواْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَعَلُواْ وَهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُونَ

They are the ones who, if they were to commit a major sin or wrong themselves by committing a lesser sin, remember Allah, the Exalted, His warning to the disobedient, and His promise to the fearful. Then, they seek from their Lord that He covers their sins and not censure them, remorseful for what they did. This is because no one except Allah alone can forgive sins, and they do not persist in their sins. They know that they are sinners and that Allah can forgive all sins.

(١٣٦) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ جَزَآؤُهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٞ مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡ وَجَنَّٰتٞ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ وَنِعۡمَ أَجۡرُ ٱلۡعَٰمِلِينَ

The reward of those who are described with these praiseworthy characteristics and outstanding qualities is Allah's covering their sins and overlooking them. In the Afterlife, they will have gardens with rivers flowing below their palaces, where they will reside forever. How excellent is that recompense for those who act in obedience to Allah!

(١٣٧) قَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ سُنَنٞ فَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَٱنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ

When the believers were tested with what befell them on the day of Uḥud, Allah said to them as consolation: The established divine practice of the destruction of disbelievers has passed before you. He made the end for the believers after testing them. So, travel through the Earth, carefully consider, and take a lesson from the ultimate destiny of those who deny Allah and His Messengers: their homes are now empty, and their reign has disappeared.

(١٣٨) هَٰذَا بَيَانٞ لِّلنَّاسِ وَهُدٗى وَمَوۡعِظَةٞ لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ

This noble Quran clarifies the truth and warns about falsehood for all people. It shows the path to guidance and restraint for the fearful because they are the ones who can take advantage of the guidance and direction within it.

(١٣٩) وَلَا تَهِنُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَوۡنَ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Do not become weak, O believers, and do not be sad over what befell you on the day of Uḥud. That is not appropriate because you are superior through your faith, superior through Allah's help and your hope of His victory- if you are believers in Allah and His promise to His fearful slaves.

(١٤٠) إِن يَمۡسَسۡكُمۡ قَرۡحٞ فَقَدۡ مَسَّ ٱلۡقَوۡمَ قَرۡحٞ مِّثۡلُهُۥۚ وَتِلۡكَ ٱلۡأَيَّامُ نُدَاوِلُهَا بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَلِيَعۡلَمَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَيَتَّخِذَ مِنكُمۡ شُهَدَآءَۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

If you suffered injuries and deaths, O believers, on the day of Uḥud, then the disbelievers suffered the same injuries and deaths you did. Allah distributes such days between people, their believers, and their disbelievers, giving victory and defeat as He wills for reasons of far-reaching wisdom. Among these reasons is for the believers to be distinguished clearly from the hypocrites. Another reason is for whomever He wills to be honored with martyrdom in His path. Therefore, Allah does not love those who oppress themselves by giving up fighting in His path.

Points of Benefit:

The believer is encouraged to hasten to do good actions, taking advantage of the time left before it is too late.
One thing that makes those who fear Allah worthy of entering Paradise is that they spend their wealth in every situation, control their anger, forgive people, and are kind to creation.
Reflecting on what happened to people of the past is one of the best ways for those who have hearts capable of understanding to learn lessons.
