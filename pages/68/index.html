(١٤١) وَلِيُمَحِّصَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَيَمۡحَقَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

A part of this wisdom is the purification of believers from their sins, the rescue of their ranks from the hypocrites, and the destruction of the disbelievers and their eradication.

(١٤٢) أَمۡ حَسِبۡتُمۡ أَن تَدۡخُلُواْ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ وَلَمَّا يَعۡلَمِ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ جَٰهَدُواْ مِنكُمۡ وَيَعۡلَمَ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

Did you think, O believers, that you would enter Paradise without trials and [demands of] patience by which the true fighters in the path of Allah are distinguished, including those who are patient with the trials that befall them in [that path]?

(١٤٣) وَلَقَدۡ كُنتُمۡ تَمَنَّوۡنَ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن تَلۡقَوۡهُ فَقَدۡ رَأَيۡتُمُوهُ وَأَنتُمۡ تَنظُرُونَ

Indeed, you, O believers, wished to encounter the disbelievers to obtain martyrdom in the path of Allah, as your brothers earned on the day of Badr before you experienced the various means of death and its severity. Now, on the day of Uḥud, you saw what you wished for with your own eyes.

When news spread among the people on the day of Uhud that the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم had been killed, Allah revealed the following as a rebuke to those from among the believers who refrained from fighting further due to this, stating:

(١٤٤) وَمَا مُحَمَّدٌ إِلَّا رَسُولٞ قَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلِهِ ٱلرُّسُلُۚ أَفَإِيْن مَّاتَ أَوۡ قُتِلَ ٱنقَلَبۡتُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَعۡقَٰبِكُمۡۚ وَمَن يَنقَلِبۡ عَلَىٰ عَقِبَيۡهِ فَلَن يَضُرَّ ٱللَّهَ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۗ وَسَيَجۡزِي ٱللَّهُ ٱلشَّٰكِرِينَ

Muhammad is but a messenger like the messengers of Allah who preceded him: they passed away or were killed. If he were to die or be killed, would you apostate from your religion and abandon the fight? Whoever among you apostates from his religion will not harm Allah in any way. He is the Omnipotent, the Almighty. The apostate only harms themself through exposure to the loss in the worldly life and the Afterlife. Allah will abundantly reward those thankful to Him by making them firm in His religion and their fighting in His path.

(١٤٥) وَمَا كَانَ لِنَفۡسٍ أَن تَمُوتَ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِ كِتَٰبٗا مُّؤَجَّلٗاۗ وَمَن يُرِدۡ ثَوَابَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا نُؤۡتِهِۦ مِنۡهَا وَمَن يُرِدۡ ثَوَابَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ نُؤۡتِهِۦ مِنۡهَاۚ وَسَنَجۡزِي ٱلشَّٰكِرِينَ

Not a soul dies except by the Divine Decree of Allah after the time Allah has written for them ends. He has fixed a time that cannot be increased or decreased. Whoever intends worldly gain through his actions, He will grant him in accordance to whatever has been decreed for him of [worldly gain]. There will be no share for him in the Afterlife. Whoever intends by his actions the recompense of Allah in the Afterlife, He will grant its reward. Moreover, He will greatly reward those who are grateful to their Lord.

(١٤٦) وَكَأَيِّن مِّن نَّبِيّٖ قَٰتَلَ مَعَهُۥ رِبِّيُّونَ كَثِيرٞ فَمَا وَهَنُواْ لِمَآ أَصَابَهُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا ضَعُفُواْ وَمَا ٱسۡتَكَانُواْۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

How many a prophet among the prophets of Allah fought while a larger number of followers accompanied him? They did not cower from fighting due to what befell them of death and injuries in the path of Allah. They did not weaken from fighting the enemy or surrender to him. Rather, they were patient and steadfast. Allah loves those who are patient upon hardships and adversities in His path.

(١٤٧) وَمَا كَانَ قَوۡلَهُمۡ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُواْ رَبَّنَا ٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا وَإِسۡرَافَنَا فِيٓ أَمۡرِنَا وَثَبِّتۡ أَقۡدَامَنَا وَٱنصُرۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

When these trials struck them, those who were patient said nothing except: "Our Lord forgive us our sins and our overstepping the limits in our affairs. Plant our feet firmly when we encounter our enemy, and grant us victory against people who disbelieve in You."

(١٤٨) فَ‍َٔاتَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ ثَوَابَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَحُسۡنَ ثَوَابِ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

So, Allah gave them the reward of the worldly life in their victory, making it possible for them. He also gave them the most excellent reward of the Afterlife, in their contentment and residing with eternal bliss in gardens of delight. Allah loves those who hold the highest standards in their worship and transactions.

Points of Benefit:

Subjecting people to trials is the way Allah distinguishes those who honestly and patiently struggle for the sake of Allah from those who do not.
Striving for the sake of Allah and calling to His path should not depend on a particular person, regardless of their position or worth.
Allah has fixed people's lifespan: it will not be made longer by mere hope or acting cowardly, nor made shorter by going into battle or being courageous.
The aspirations and intentions of people differ: some desire the reward of Allah, and some desire this world. Everyone will be rewarded according to their intentions.
