(١٤٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن تُطِيعُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يَرُدُّوكُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَعۡقَٰبِكُمۡ فَتَنقَلِبُواْ خَٰسِرِينَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, if you obey those who disbelieve among the Jews, Christians, and polytheists in the misguidance they order you to commit, then they will return you to what you used to be upon of disbelief after your Belief. Then, you would return to being losers in the worldly life and the Afterlife.

(١٥٠) بَلِ ٱللَّهُ مَوۡلَىٰكُمۡۖ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلنَّٰصِرِينَ

These disbelievers will not stand by you if you obey them. Rather, Allah is your defender against your enemies. So, obey Him, for He, the Exalted, is the best of defenders, and you will not need anyone after Him.

(١٥١) سَنُلۡقِي فِي قُلُوبِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ٱلرُّعۡبَ بِمَآ أَشۡرَكُواْ بِٱللَّهِ مَا لَمۡ يُنَزِّلۡ بِهِۦ سُلۡطَٰنٗاۖ وَمَأۡوَىٰهُمُ ٱلنَّارُۖ وَبِئۡسَ مَثۡوَى ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

We will cast dread into the hearts of those who disbelieve to the point that they are not able to stand firm to fight you. This is because of their association of false gods with Allah. They worshiped them based on their desires when no proof for it was sent down to them. The abode they will return to in the Afterlife will be the Fire. What a miserable abode is the Fire for the oppressors!

(١٥٢) وَلَقَدۡ صَدَقَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَعۡدَهُۥٓ إِذۡ تَحُسُّونَهُم بِإِذۡنِهِۦۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا فَشِلۡتُمۡ وَتَنَٰزَعۡتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡرِ وَعَصَيۡتُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ مَآ أَرَىٰكُم مَّا تُحِبُّونَۚ مِنكُم مَّن يُرِيدُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَمِنكُم مَّن يُرِيدُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَۚ ثُمَّ صَرَفَكُمۡ عَنۡهُمۡ لِيَبۡتَلِيَكُمۡۖ وَلَقَدۡ عَفَا عَنكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو فَضۡلٍ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Allah has fulfilled the victory He promised you over your enemies on the day of Uḥud. You were engaging them in a fierce battle, by Allah's permission, until you cowered and weakened in your resolve to do as the Messenger had commanded you. And you differed about whether to remain at your posts or leave them to gather the spoils of war. You disobeyed the Messenger's command to remain in your positions no matter what happened. You did this after Allah showed you the victory over the enemies you wished for. Among you are those who want the spoils of the worldly life, and they are the ones who left their posts. Also, among you are those who want the reward of the Afterlife, and they were the ones who remained in their positions, obeying the Messenger's command. Then, Allah diverted you from them and gave them authority over you to test you. So, the believer, patient with trials, would be distinguished from the one whose foot slipped and his soul became weak. Allah had indeed pardoned you for what you committed when you went against the command of the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم. Allah possesses great preference for the believers when He guides them to faith, pardons their misdeeds, and rewards them for the calamities they suffer.

(١٥٣) ۞إِذۡ تُصۡعِدُونَ وَلَا تَلۡوُۥنَ عَلَىٰٓ أَحَدٖ وَٱلرَّسُولُ يَدۡعُوكُمۡ فِيٓ أُخۡرَىٰكُمۡ فَأَثَٰبَكُمۡ غَمَّۢا بِغَمّٖ لِّكَيۡلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَاتَكُمۡ وَلَا مَآ أَصَٰبَكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ

Remember, O believers, when you were driven off through the earth as fugitives on the day of Uḥud. It was due to what befell you of weakheartedness because you went against the command of the Messenger. You could not even look at one another, and the Messenger called you as you turned away while he was between you and the polytheists, saying, "Come to me, slaves of Allah! Come to me, slaves of Allah!" Allah repaid you for this with pain and distress in the form of the victory and spoils of war you forfeited. He followed that up with more pain and distress in the form of rumors circulating between you that the Prophet had died. He brought this down on you for you not to grieve over what you forfeited of victory and spoils, what befell you of death and injuries. And after realizing that the Prophet had not been killed then, each calamity and pain was made easy for you to bear. Allah is well aware of everything you do, and nothing about the condition of your hearts is hidden from Him, nor are the actions of your limbs.

Points of Benefit:

There is a warning against obeying the disbelievers and being guided by their desires, leading to loss in this world and the Afterlife.
One way Allah gives the believers victory is by instilling fear into the hearts of the enemies of Allah.
One of the biggest causes of defeat in battle is attachment to this world, the desire for the gains of war, and disobeying the army leader.
An indication of the virtues of the Companions, may Allah be pleased with them, is that Allah follows the mention of their mistake by mentioning forgiveness.
