(٣٨) قُلۡنَا ٱهۡبِطُواْ مِنۡهَا جَمِيعٗاۖ فَإِمَّا يَأۡتِيَنَّكُم مِّنِّي هُدٗى فَمَن تَبِعَ هُدَايَ فَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

We told them: Descend all of you from Paradise to Earth. And when guidance comes to you at the hands of My messengers, then whoever follows it and believes in My messengers will have nothing to fear in the Hereafter, nor will they feel sorrow for what passed them by on earth.

(٣٩) وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَكَذَّبُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَآ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

As for those who disbelieve and deny Our signs, then those are the dwellers of the Fire; they will never leave it.

(٤٠) يَٰبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتِيَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنۡعَمۡتُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَأَوۡفُواْ بِعَهۡدِيٓ أُوفِ بِعَهۡدِكُمۡ وَإِيَّٰيَ فَٱرۡهَبُونِ

O Children of Allah's Prophet Jacob, remember Allah's successive blessings upon you and show gratitude for them. And commit yourselves to fulfill My covenant to you by believing in Me and My messengers and acting in accordance with My laws. If you fulfill your covenant, I will fulfill my covenant of what I promised you all: a good life in this world and a beautiful reward on the Day of Resurrection. Therefore, fear Me alone, and do not break My Covenant.

(٤١) وَءَامِنُواْ بِمَآ أَنزَلۡتُ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا مَعَكُمۡ وَلَا تَكُونُوٓاْ أَوَّلَ كَافِرِۢ بِهِۦۖ وَلَا تَشۡتَرُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِي ثَمَنٗا قَلِيلٗا وَإِيَّٰيَ فَٱتَّقُونِ

Have faith in the Quran, which I revealed to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, confirming what came in the Torah---before its alteration concerning the Oneness of Allah and the prophethood of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم---and beware of becoming the first group to disbelieve him. And do not exchange My verses that I revealed for the small price of position or leadership, and avoid My displeasure and punishment.

(٤٢) وَلَا تَلۡبِسُواْ ٱلۡحَقَّ بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ وَتَكۡتُمُواْ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَأَنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ

Do not conflate the truth I revealed to My messengers with lies that you fabricate. And do not conceal the fact that came in your books regarding the description of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم despite your knowledge and certainty of it.

(٤٣) وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَٱرۡكَعُواْ مَعَ ٱلرَّٰكِعِينَ

And perform the prayer completely with its pillars, obligations, and recommended components. And give Zakat from your wealth, which Allah has put in your hands. And humble yourselves to Allah with those who humble themselves from Muhammad's nation صلى الله عليه وسلم.

(٤٤) ۞أَتَأۡمُرُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ بِٱلۡبِرِّ وَتَنسَوۡنَ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ تَتۡلُونَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ

How shameful it is that you command others to believe and do good, and you yourselves turn away, forgetting yourselves, while you read the Torah and know what it contains regarding the command to follow Allah's religion and to believe His messengers. Do you not benefit from your minds?

(٤٥) وَٱسۡتَعِينُواْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ وَٱلصَّلَوٰةِۚ وَإِنَّهَا لَكَبِيرَةٌ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱلۡخَٰشِعِينَ

Ask for help in all of your religious and worldly affairs through patience and prayer, which bring you close to Allah and allow you to reach Him so that He will help you, protect you, and take away anything harmful from you. Verily, prayer is a hardship except for those who humble themselves before their Lord.

(٤٦) ٱلَّذِينَ يَظُنُّونَ أَنَّهُم مُّلَٰقُواْ رَبِّهِمۡ وَأَنَّهُمۡ إِلَيۡهِ رَٰجِعُونَ

They are the ones who are certain that they will meet their Lord on the Day of Resurrection, that they will return to Him, and will be held accountable for their actions.

(٤٧) يَٰبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتِيَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنۡعَمۡتُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَأَنِّي فَضَّلۡتُكُمۡ عَلَى ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ

O Children of Allah's Prophet Jacob, remember the religious and worldly blessings I bestowed you with. And remember that I favored you over the people of your time who were contemporaries with you in prophethood and dominion.

(٤٨) وَٱتَّقُواْ يَوۡمٗا لَّا تَجۡزِي نَفۡسٌ عَن نَّفۡسٖ شَيۡ‍ٔٗا وَلَا يُقۡبَلُ مِنۡهَا شَفَٰعَةٞ وَلَا يُؤۡخَذُ مِنۡهَا عَدۡلٞ وَلَا هُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ

And place a veil of protection between you and the torment of the Day of Resurrection by obeying commands and avoiding prohibitions. On that day, no soul will be able to help another in any way. No intercession on their behalf will be accepted to benefit them or protect them from harm except with Allah's permission. No ransom will be taken, even if it were to be the earth filled with gold. They will have no helpers on that day. Without appeal, ransom, or helper, where will they turn?

Points of Benefit:

From the greatest disappointments is for a person to command others to be righteous and forget about himself.
Patience and prayer are among the most optimal things that help a servant in all his affairs.
On the Day of Resurrection, no intercessors or ransom will deflect the punishment from a person, and only his good deeds will benefit him.
