(١٥٤) ثُمَّ أَنزَلَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ ٱلۡغَمِّ أَمَنَةٗ نُّعَاسٗا يَغۡشَىٰ طَآئِفَةٗ مِّنكُمۡۖ وَطَآئِفَةٞ قَدۡ أَهَمَّتۡهُمۡ أَنفُسُهُمۡ يَظُنُّونَ بِٱللَّهِ غَيۡرَ ٱلۡحَقِّ ظَنَّ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِيَّةِۖ يَقُولُونَ هَل لَّنَا مِنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ مِن شَيۡءٖۗ قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡأَمۡرَ كُلَّهُۥ لِلَّهِۗ يُخۡفُونَ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِم مَّا لَا يُبۡدُونَ لَكَۖ يَقُولُونَ لَوۡ كَانَ لَنَا مِنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ شَيۡءٞ مَّا قُتِلۡنَا هَٰهُنَاۗ قُل لَّوۡ كُنتُمۡ فِي بُيُوتِكُمۡ لَبَرَزَ ٱلَّذِينَ كُتِبَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقَتۡلُ إِلَىٰ مَضَاجِعِهِمۡۖ وَلِيَبۡتَلِيَ ٱللَّهُ مَا فِي صُدُورِكُمۡ وَلِيُمَحِّصَ مَا فِي قُلُوبِكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

Then He sent down peace and security after the pain and distress, prompting sleep to envelop a group among you who were confident in Allah's promise, resulting in a feeling of safety and serenity in their hearts. Another group, the hypocrites, did not receive a sense of safety or sleepiness. They only worried about their own safety, mired in anxiety and fear. They held negative presumptions about Allah, thinking that He would not grant victory His Prophet victory or support His slaves. They presumed just like the people of the Age of Ignorance who did not assess Allah as He deserved. Due to their ignorance of Allah, these hypocrites say: "We do not have a say about the command to march forth for battle. If we did, we would not have marched forth." Say, O Prophet, in reply to these people: "Truly, the entire matter is for Allah to decide. For He is the one who foreordains whatever He wills, He determines whatever He wills, and He is the One who decreed your marching forth." These hypocrites hide within themselves doubts and negative presumptions that they do not express to you, considering that they say: "If we had a say in the matter of marching forth, we would not have been killed in this location." Tell them, O Prophet, in rebuking them: "Even if you had remained in your homes, far from the locales of killing and death, those among you for whom Allah had written death would surely have departed for wherever their deaths would take place. Allah did not write that except to test the intentions and motives in your chests and to distinguish what they contained of Belief and hypocrisy. Allah knows what is in the chests of His slaves, and nothing in them is hidden from Him.

(١٥٥) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ تَوَلَّوۡاْ مِنكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡتَقَى ٱلۡجَمۡعَانِ إِنَّمَا ٱسۡتَزَلَّهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ بِبَعۡضِ مَا كَسَبُواْۖ وَلَقَدۡ عَفَا ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٌ حَلِيمٞ

Those of you, O Companions of Muhammad, who faced defeat on the day the polytheists encountered the Muslims at Uḥud, it was only because the Devil caused them to slip due to what they earned from disobedience. Nonetheless, Allah has indeed pardoned them and will not take them into account as a favor and mercy on His part. Surely, Allah is Forgiving and Forbearing towards those who repent, and He does not hasten to punish.

(١٥٦) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَقَالُواْ لِإِخۡوَٰنِهِمۡ إِذَا ضَرَبُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَوۡ كَانُواْ غُزّٗى لَّوۡ كَانُواْ عِندَنَا مَا مَاتُواْ وَمَا قُتِلُواْ لِيَجۡعَلَ ٱللَّهُ ذَٰلِكَ حَسۡرَةٗ فِي قُلُوبِهِمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٞ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, do not be like the disbelievers among the hypocrites when others travel to earn a living or go out on a raid, and they die or are killed, [the disbelievers] say to their close relatives: "If only they had stayed near us, not marched forth, and not raided, then they would not have died and been killed." Allah placed this conviction in their hearts to increase their regret and grief. Allah alone is the one who grants life and causes death by His will. Sitting in place does not prevent His Decree, and marching forth does not hasten it. Allah sees everything you do. Your actions are not hidden from Him, and He will requite you based on them.

(١٥٧) وَلَئِن قُتِلۡتُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡ مُتُّمۡ لَمَغۡفِرَةٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرَحۡمَةٌ خَيۡرٞ مِّمَّا يَجۡمَعُونَ

If you are killed or die in Allah's path, O believers, Allah has immense forgiveness for you, and He will bestow mercy upon you. His forgiveness and mercy are better than this whole world and all that its people accumulate in it of its fading pleasures.

Points of Benefit:

Ignorance of Allah and His attributes leads to wrong beliefs and corrupt actions.
People's lifespans are fixed and pre-determined. Being daring and courageous does not shorten it, and being cowardly and careful does not increase it.
From Allah's established practice, the Exalted is to test His slaves to distinguish the good from the bad.
One of the most excellent and noble ranks in the sight of Allah the Exalted is the rank of those martyred in the cause of Allah.
