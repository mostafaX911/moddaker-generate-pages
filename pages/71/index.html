(١٥٨) وَلَئِن مُّتُّمۡ أَوۡ قُتِلۡتُمۡ لَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ

If you die, regardless of your manner of death, or you are killed, you will all return to Allah alone. Truly, He will requite you according to your actions.

(١٥٩) فَبِمَا رَحۡمَةٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ لِنتَ لَهُمۡۖ وَلَوۡ كُنتَ فَظًّا غَلِيظَ ٱلۡقَلۡبِ لَٱنفَضُّواْ مِنۡ حَوۡلِكَۖ فَٱعۡفُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ وَشَاوِرۡهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡرِۖ فَإِذَا عَزَمۡتَ فَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُتَوَكِّلِينَ

Due to Allah's great Mercy, your disposition, O Prophet, is easygoing with your Companions. They would have dispersed from around you if you were hard-hearted and severe in your speech and actions. Therefore, overlook their shortcomings concerning your rights and seek forgiveness for them. Seek out their opinions in whatever requires joint consultation. When you decide on a matter after mutual consultation, proceed and have complete trust in Allah. Allah loves those who fully trust Him so He can grant them success and support.

(١٦٠) إِن يَنصُرۡكُمُ ٱللَّهُ فَلَا غَالِبَ لَكُمۡۖ وَإِن يَخۡذُلۡكُمۡ فَمَن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يَنصُرُكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦۗ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ

If Allah supports you with His aid and help, no one can overcome you, even if everyone on earth gathers against you. If He abandons helping you and entrusts you to yourselves, no one can help you afterward. Help is in His hand alone. So, let the believers rely on Allah and no one else besides Him.

(١٦١) وَمَا كَانَ لِنَبِيٍّ أَن يَغُلَّۚ وَمَن يَغۡلُلۡ يَأۡتِ بِمَا غَلَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۚ ثُمَّ تُوَفَّىٰ كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ مَّا كَسَبَتۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ

It was not for any Prophet among the Prophets to act treacherously by taking anything from the spoils of war other than what Allah assigned explicitly to him. Whoever among you acts treacherously by taking something from the spoils of war will be punished by being disgraced on the Day of Judgment. So, he will come carrying whatever he took in front of all creation. Then, every soul will be given the full requital of what he earned without decrease. They will not be oppressed by any addition to their sins or subtracting from their good deeds.

(١٦٢) أَفَمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ رِضۡوَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ كَمَنۢ بَآءَ بِسَخَطٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَمَأۡوَىٰهُ جَهَنَّمُۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ

Not equal according to Allah is he who observes the Faith and good deeds by which he will obtain the good pleasure of Allah, and he who disbelieves in Allah and commits sins. The latter shall incur Allah's wrath and Fire as their abode. What an evil refuge and abode!

(١٦٣) هُمۡ دَرَجَٰتٌ عِندَ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ بَصِيرُۢ بِمَا يَعۡمَلُونَ

They differ in their ranks in the worldly life and Afterlife, according to Allah. Allah sees what they do. Nothing is hidden from Him. He will recompense every person according to their deeds.

(١٦٤) لَقَدۡ مَنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ إِذۡ بَعَثَ فِيهِمۡ رَسُولٗا مِّنۡ أَنفُسِهِمۡ يَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَيُزَكِّيهِمۡ وَيُعَلِّمُهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَإِن كَانُواْ مِن قَبۡلُ لَفِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٍ

Allah favored the believers and did well for them when He sent them a Messenger from their kind who recited the Quran to them, purified them from polytheism and depravity, and taught them the Quran and Sunnah. Before this Messenger was sent, they had been steeped in clear misguidance, far from guidance and the right path.

(١٦٥) أَوَلَمَّآ أَصَٰبَتۡكُم مُّصِيبَةٞ قَدۡ أَصَبۡتُم مِّثۡلَيۡهَا قُلۡتُمۡ أَنَّىٰ هَٰذَاۖ قُلۡ هُوَ مِنۡ عِندِ أَنفُسِكُمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٞ

When a calamity struck you, O believers, during your defeat at Uḥud, and among you were those who were killed, you had already achieved twice as much from your enemies on the day of Badr in terms of the dead and captured. You said: Where did this calamity come from? We are believers, and the Prophet of Allah is among us! Say, O Prophet: What happened only reached you because of yourselves- you disputed with the Messenger and disobeyed him. Allah is Omnipotent over everything, giving victory to whomever He wills and forsaking whomever He wills.

Points of Benefit:

True victory comes from Allah the Exalted. He is the Omnipotent, who cannot be fought against; the Mighty, Who cannot be overcome.
The state of someone in this world who follows the guidance of Allah and acts according to it is not the same as someone who turns away from it and rejects it. Similarly, they will not be the same rank in the Hereafter.
A person's misfortune and suffering in this world can be because of their sins or a test that will raise them in rank. However, it must be remembered that Allah pardons and overlooks many sins.
