(١٦٦) وَمَآ أَصَٰبَكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡتَقَى ٱلۡجَمۡعَانِ فَبِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِ وَلِيَعۡلَمَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

The death, wounds, and defeat you suffered on the day of Uḥud when your army and the army of the polytheists met was by the permission and decree of Allah, with far-reaching wisdom behind it so that the truthful believers would emerge.

(١٦٧) وَلِيَعۡلَمَ ٱلَّذِينَ نَافَقُواْۚ وَقِيلَ لَهُمۡ تَعَالَوۡاْ قَٰتِلُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ أَوِ ٱدۡفَعُواْۖ قَالُواْ لَوۡ نَعۡلَمُ قِتَالٗا لَّٱتَّبَعۡنَٰكُمۡۗ هُمۡ لِلۡكُفۡرِ يَوۡمَئِذٍ أَقۡرَبُ مِنۡهُمۡ لِلۡإِيمَٰنِۚ يَقُولُونَ بِأَفۡوَٰهِهِم مَّا لَيۡسَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يَكۡتُمُونَ

And so that the hypocrites would emerge who, when it was said to them: 'Fight in Allah's path or defend with your additional men the Muslim masses,' they replied: Had we known that there would be fighting, then we would have followed you. However, we did not think there would be any fighting between you and the people. Their state at that time was closer to what proved their disbelief than what proved their faith, saying with their tongues what was not in their hearts. Allah knows best what they hide in their chests, and He will punish them for it.

(١٦٨) ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ لِإِخۡوَٰنِهِمۡ وَقَعَدُواْ لَوۡ أَطَاعُونَا مَا قُتِلُواْۗ قُلۡ فَٱدۡرَءُواْ عَنۡ أَنفُسِكُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

They are the ones who failed to fight and said to their close relatives who suffered losses on the day of Uḥud: "If [your loved ones] had obeyed us and not marched forth to fight, then they would not have been killed." Say, O Prophet, as a refutation of them: "So ward off death from yourselves when it befalls you if you are truthful in your claim that they would not have been killed if they had obeyed you. And [if you are truthful] that the reason for your escape from death was because you refrained from fighting in Allah's path.

(١٦٩) وَلَا تَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ قُتِلُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ أَمۡوَٰتَۢاۚ بَلۡ أَحۡيَآءٌ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ يُرۡزَقُونَ

Do not think, O Prophet, that those killed fighting in the path of Allah are dead. Rather, they live a special life near their Lord in an abode of His honor, being provided with all types of delights that no one knows of except Allah.

(١٧٠) فَرِحِينَ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَيَسۡتَبۡشِرُونَ بِٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يَلۡحَقُواْ بِهِم مِّنۡ خَلۡفِهِمۡ أَلَّا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

Happiness engulfs them, and joy encompasses them through what Allah has bestowed on them from His favor. They hope for and anticipate meeting their brothers who remained in the worldly life. [They also hope for and anticipate] that [their brothers], if killed during the fighting, will receive the same favor. They fear not in terms of what awaits them from the matters of the Afterlife. Therefore, they are not sorrowful over any worldly prosperity that passed them by.

(١٧١) ۞يَسۡتَبۡشِرُونَ بِنِعۡمَةٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَفَضۡلٖ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُضِيعُ أَجۡرَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

Along with this, they rejoice in the immense reward that awaits them from Allah and the tremendous addition on top of the reward. [They also rejoice] that Allah, the Exalted, does not recant the reward of the believers. Rather, He grants their rewards completely and even adds to them.

(١٧٢) ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَجَابُواْ لِلَّهِ وَٱلرَّسُولِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَآ أَصَابَهُمُ ٱلۡقَرۡحُۚ لِلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ وَٱتَّقَوۡاْ أَجۡرٌ عَظِيمٌ

They are the ones who carried out the command of Allah and His Messenger when they were called to march forth to fight in the path of Allah and meet the polytheists in the battle of Hamra' Al-Asad, which immediately followed the battle of Uḥud. This was after they had suffered injuries on the day of Uhud. Nevertheless, their injuries did not stop them from answering the call of Allah and His Messenger. For those who did well in their efforts and feared Allah by following His commands and keeping away from what He prohibited, there is a tremendous reward from Allah- and it is Paradise.

(١٧٣) ٱلَّذِينَ قَالَ لَهُمُ ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّ ٱلنَّاسَ قَدۡ جَمَعُواْ لَكُمۡ فَٱخۡشَوۡهُمۡ فَزَادَهُمۡ إِيمَٰنٗا وَقَالُواْ حَسۡبُنَا ٱللَّهُ وَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡوَكِيلُ

Some of the polytheists said to them: "The Quraysh, led by Abu Sufyan, had gathered large numbers together to fight and eradicate you. So, beware of them and fear encountering them." These words and the intimidation increased their faith in Allah and the trustworthiness of His promise. So, they marched forth to meet them, saying: "Allah, the Exalted, is sufficient for us, and what an excellent one for us to entrust our affairs.

Points of Benefit:

One of Allah's divine practices is to test His servants to distinguish the real believer from the lying hypocrite.
The greatness of reward in the sight of Allah for those who participate in jihad and martyrdom, wherein Allah will place those on the highest ranks.
The Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) are virtuous and hold an exalted status in this world and the Hereafter because they sacrificed themselves and their wealth for the sake of Allah the Transcendent.
