(١٧٤) فَٱنقَلَبُواْ بِنِعۡمَةٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَفَضۡلٖ لَّمۡ يَمۡسَسۡهُمۡ سُوٓءٞ وَٱتَّبَعُواْ رِضۡوَٰنَ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو فَضۡلٍ عَظِيمٍ

They returned after marching towards the battle of Ḥamrā' Al-Asad with an immense reward from Allah, an increase in their ranks, and security from their enemy. In addition, they did not suffer any death or injury. They followed what pleased Allah by obeying Him and refraining from disobedience. Allah possesses tremendous favor for His believing slaves.

(١٧٥) إِنَّمَا ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ يُخَوِّفُ أَوۡلِيَآءَهُۥ فَلَا تَخَافُوهُمۡ وَخَافُونِ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Only the Devil attempts to frighten you, terrorizing you with his helpers and supporters. Do not cower from them, for they have no power or strength. Rather, fear Allah by obeying Him if you truly believe in Him.

(١٧٦) وَلَا يَحۡزُنكَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡكُفۡرِۚ إِنَّهُمۡ لَن يَضُرُّواْ ٱللَّهَ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۗ يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ أَلَّا يَجۡعَلَ لَهُمۡ حَظّٗا فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٌ

Do not be brought down into sadness, O Messenger, by those among the hypocrites who rush to disbelief, turning on their heels. They cannot cause Allah any harm. Rather, they only harm themselves by distancing themselves from faith and obedience to Allah. Allah intends for them to be defeated, to lack success, and to have no share in the bliss of the Afterlife. They will suffer a severe punishment in the Fire.

(١٧٧) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱشۡتَرَوُاْ ٱلۡكُفۡرَ بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ لَن يَضُرُّواْ ٱللَّهَ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Those who exchange faith for disbelief cannot harm Allah in any way. Instead, they only harm themselves. They will have a painful punishment in the Afterlife.

(١٧٨) وَلَا يَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا نُمۡلِي لَهُمۡ خَيۡرٞ لِّأَنفُسِهِمۡۚ إِنَّمَا نُمۡلِي لَهُمۡ لِيَزۡدَادُوٓاْ إِثۡمٗاۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٞ مُّهِينٞ

Those who disbelieve in their Lord and stubbornly resist His legislation should not presume that their respite and the extension of their lives upon disbelief, just as they are, is better for them. It is not as they presume. We only grant them respite to allow them to increase in sin, with an abundance of disobedience on top of their sin. They will have a humiliating punishment.

(١٧٩) مَّا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيَذَرَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ عَلَىٰ مَآ أَنتُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ حَتَّىٰ يَمِيزَ ٱلۡخَبِيثَ مِنَ ٱلطَّيِّبِۗ وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيُطۡلِعَكُمۡ عَلَى ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَجۡتَبِي مِن رُّسُلِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۖ فَ‍َٔامِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرُسُلِهِۦۚ وَإِن تُؤۡمِنُواْ وَتَتَّقُواْ فَلَكُمۡ أَجۡرٌ عَظِيمٞ

It is not from Allah's wisdom to leave you, O believers, as you are, mixed with the hypocrites with no distinction between you, without making clear who truly are the believers. He will distinguish you through different responsibilities and trials to differentiate the pure believers from the filthy hypocrites. It is also not from Allah's wisdom to inform you of all the Unseen so you can distinguish between the believer and the hypocrite. Rather, Allah chooses whomever He wills among His Messengers and informs them about some of the Unseen, just as He informed His Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم about the circumstances of the hypocrites. Therefore, realize your faith in Allah and His Messenger, genuinely believe in and fear Allah by following His commands and staying away from what He has prohibited. Thus, you shall attain an immense reward from Allah.

(١٨٠) وَلَا يَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَبۡخَلُونَ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ هُوَ خَيۡرٗا لَّهُمۖ بَلۡ هُوَ شَرّٞ لَّهُمۡۖ سَيُطَوَّقُونَ مَا بَخِلُواْ بِهِۦ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۗ وَلِلَّهِ مِيرَٰثُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٞ

Some are miserly with the comforts Allah has bestowed upon them as a favor from Him, and thus, they withhold the rights of Allah therein. They should not presume that it is a good intended for them. Rather, it is an evil intended for them because that in which they are miserly will become a collar wrapped around their necks, tormenting them on the Day of Resurrection. All that is in the heavens and earth must return to Allah alone. He is the Ever-Living after all His creation has perished. Allah knows the tiniest details of everything you do and will recompense you accordingly.

Points of Benefit:

The believers should ignore Satan's intimidation through his assistants and helpers from among the disbelievers because all affairs are under Allah's control.
A slave of Allah should not be complacent because Allah gives him respite; instead, he should rush to repent to Allah, asking for His forgiveness in the time given to him before it passes.
The miser who withholds the bounty Allah has given him only harms himself by depriving himself of rewards and gains from Allah, the Generous, the Giving, and exposing himself to the consequences of such behavior on the Day of Judgment.
