(١٨١) لَّقَدۡ سَمِعَ ٱللَّهُ قَوۡلَ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ فَقِيرٞ وَنَحۡنُ أَغۡنِيَآءُۘ سَنَكۡتُبُ مَا قَالُواْ وَقَتۡلَهُمُ ٱلۡأَنۢبِيَآءَ بِغَيۡرِ حَقّٖ وَنَقُولُ ذُوقُواْ عَذَابَ ٱلۡحَرِيقِ

Allah certainly heard the Jews when they said: Indeed, Allah is poor since He asks us for a 'loan,' while we are rich because of the wealth we have. Allah will record this slander and lie they have uttered against their Lord and their killing the prophets of Allah without right. We will tell them: Taste the torment of burning in the Fire.

(١٨٢) ذَٰلِكَ بِمَا قَدَّمَتۡ أَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَيۡسَ بِظَلَّامٖ لِّلۡعَبِيدِ

That punishment is because of disobedient and disgraceful acts you, O Jews, committed by your own hands. Allah does not oppress a single one of His slaves.

(١٨٣) ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَهِدَ إِلَيۡنَآ أَلَّا نُؤۡمِنَ لِرَسُولٍ حَتَّىٰ يَأۡتِيَنَا بِقُرۡبَانٖ تَأۡكُلُهُ ٱلنَّارُۗ قُلۡ قَدۡ جَآءَكُمۡ رُسُلٞ مِّن قَبۡلِي بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَبِٱلَّذِي قُلۡتُمۡ فَلِمَ قَتَلۡتُمُوهُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ

They are the ones who spoke lies and slanders, saying: Allah has instructed us in His books and on the tongues of His prophets to not believe in a Messenger unless he comes to us with something to verify his claim. Moreover, he will seek a means to Allah through an offering which would be consumed by a fire sent down from heaven. So, they lied against Allah in attributing such instructions to Him and restricting proofs of the truthfulness of the Messengers to what they mentioned. Due to this, Allah ordered His Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم to ask them: Messengers have come to you before with clear proofs of their truthfulness, and even with what you mentioned of an offering being burned by fire from heaven- so, why did you deny them and kill them if you were truthful in what you said?

(١٨٤) فَإِن كَذَّبُوكَ فَقَدۡ كُذِّبَ رُسُلٞ مِّن قَبۡلِكَ جَآءُو بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَٱلزُّبُرِ وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُنِيرِ

If they deny you, O Prophet, do not be sad because it is normal for disbelievers. Many Messengers who came with clear evidence were denied before you, books containing exhortations and heart-softening accounts, and the Book guiding people with legal rulings and legislation.

(١٨٥) كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ ذَآئِقَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۗ وَإِنَّمَا تُوَفَّوۡنَ أُجُورَكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۖ فَمَن زُحۡزِحَ عَنِ ٱلنَّارِ وَأُدۡخِلَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ فَقَدۡ فَازَۗ وَمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ إِلَّا مَتَٰعُ ٱلۡغُرُورِ

Every soul, no matter what, must taste death. No created being should be deceived by this worldly life. On the Day of Judgment, you will be granted the reward for your actions in full, without omission. Whoever Allah keeps far from the Fire and admits into Paradise has achieved all that he could hope for of goodness and has been rescued from all that he could fear of evil. The worldly life is nothing but fading possessions. Only those who are deceived are attached to them.

(١٨٦) ۞لَتُبۡلَوُنَّ فِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِكُمۡ وَأَنفُسِكُمۡ وَلَتَسۡمَعُنَّ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ وَمِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَشۡرَكُوٓاْ أَذٗى كَثِيرٗاۚ وَإِن تَصۡبِرُواْ وَتَتَّقُواْ فَإِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ

You will undoubtedly be tested, O believers, in fulfilling the rights your wealth requires and in calamities sent down along with it. You will certainly be tested in your souls with regard to fulfilling the responsibilities of Divine legislation and in different types of trials sent down to you. You will undoubtedly hear many things from those who were given the books before you and those who associate partners with Allah, things that will hurt you, criticizing you and your religion. If you have patience with the calamities and trials that afflict you, are fearful of Allah by doing what He commands and staying away from what He has prohibited, then that is from among the matters that require resolve and only the genuine contenders [for Allah's favor] will compete for it.

Points of Benefit:

Some of the evil actions of the Jews include their attacks on the prophets of Allah, rejecting and killing them.
Every success in this world is deficient, and complete victory is only possible in the Hereafter by being saved from the Fire and entering Paradise.
One way a believer is tested is through what harm comes to them from the People of the Scripture and the idolaters, and when this happens, it is necessary to bear it with patience and to practice piety.
