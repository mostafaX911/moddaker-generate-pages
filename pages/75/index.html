(١٨٧) وَإِذۡ أَخَذَ ٱللَّهُ مِيثَٰقَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ لَتُبَيِّنُنَّهُۥ لِلنَّاسِ وَلَا تَكۡتُمُونَهُۥ فَنَبَذُوهُ وَرَآءَ ظُهُورِهِمۡ وَٱشۡتَرَوۡاْ بِهِۦ ثَمَنٗا قَلِيلٗاۖ فَبِئۡسَ مَا يَشۡتَرُونَ

Remember, O Prophet, when Allah took a firm covenant from the scholars among the Jews and Christians to clarify the Book of Allah for people, not to hide what it contains of guidance and what proves the prophethood of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. No one was among them except they cast off and did not observe the covenant. They concealed the truth and expressed falsehood. They traded the covenant of Allah for a trifling price, like for the prestige and wealth they had obtained. How terrible is this price for which they traded Allah's covenant!

(١٨٨) لَا تَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَفۡرَحُونَ بِمَآ أَتَواْ وَّيُحِبُّونَ أَن يُحۡمَدُواْ بِمَا لَمۡ يَفۡعَلُواْ فَلَا تَحۡسَبَنَّهُم بِمَفَازَةٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ

Do not assume, O Prophet, that those who are happy with the repugnant actions they have committed and who love that people praise them for the good they did not perform- do not assume that they are rescued and safe from punishment. Instead, Jahannam is their final abode, where they will be in excruciating torment.

(١٨٩) وَلِلَّهِ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ

To Allah alone, and no one else, belongs the dominion of the heavens and earth and everything in them concerning their creation and management. Allah is Omnipotent over everything.

(١٩٠) إِنَّ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱخۡتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

Indeed, for those of sound intellect, there are clear proofs in the generation of the heavens and the earth from nothing without a previous example, the alternation of the night and day, and their varying between long and short. These proofs are evidence of the Creator of the universe, who alone deserves acts of worship.

(١٩١) ٱلَّذِينَ يَذۡكُرُونَ ٱللَّهَ قِيَٰمٗا وَقُعُودٗا وَعَلَىٰ جُنُوبِهِمۡ وَيَتَفَكَّرُونَ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَبَّنَا مَا خَلَقۡتَ هَٰذَا بَٰطِلٗا سُبۡحَٰنَكَ فَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ

They are the ones who remember Allah in all circumstances- while standing, sitting, and lying on their sides. They reflect on the creation of the heavens and earth, saying: "O Lord! You did not generate this wonderful creation in vain. You are far from frivolity. So, spare us from the punishment of Fire by enabling us to do righteous deeds and preserving us from evil deeds.

(١٩٢) رَبَّنَآ إِنَّكَ مَن تُدۡخِلِ ٱلنَّارَ فَقَدۡ أَخۡزَيۡتَهُۥۖ وَمَا لِلظَّٰلِمِينَ مِنۡ أَنصَارٖ

For indeed You, Our Lord, have humiliated and disgraced those among Your creation that You introduce into the Fire. On the Day of Judgment, wrongdoers will have no assistants to prevent Allah's torment and punishment of them.

(١٩٣) رَّبَّنَآ إِنَّنَا سَمِعۡنَا مُنَادِيٗا يُنَادِي لِلۡإِيمَٰنِ أَنۡ ءَامِنُواْ بِرَبِّكُمۡ فَ‍َٔامَنَّاۚ رَبَّنَا فَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا وَكَفِّرۡ عَنَّا سَيِّ‍َٔاتِنَا وَتَوَفَّنَا مَعَ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارِ

O our Lord, we have heard someone inviting us to faith, and he is Your Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم saying: "Believe in your Lord who is One God." Then, we believed in what he invited us to and followed his legislation. So, cover over our sins, do not expose us, overlook our evil deeds, and do not take us to account for them. Let our souls be taken while accompanying the righteous by guiding us to do good deeds and abandoning evil deeds.

(١٩٤) رَبَّنَا وَءَاتِنَا مَا وَعَدتَّنَا عَلَىٰ رُسُلِكَ وَلَا تُخۡزِنَا يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۖ إِنَّكَ لَا تُخۡلِفُ ٱلۡمِيعَادَ

O our Lord, grant us the guidance and help in the worldly life that You promised us upon the tongues of Your messengers. Do not disgrace us on the Day of Judgment by placing us in the Fire. Indeed, You, O Lord, are generous and do not break Your promise.

Points of Benefit:

Among the characteristics of evil scholars among the People of the Scripture is their concealing of knowledge, following desires, and pleasure at being praised by others despite their secrets and (detestable) actions.
Thinking about the creation of Allah leads to certainty in the greatness of Allah and complete humility towards Him, the Mighty and Majestic.
Calling on Allah and having humility in the heart towards Him are among the most perfect expressions of worship.
