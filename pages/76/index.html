(١٩٥) فَٱسۡتَجَابَ لَهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ أَنِّي لَآ أُضِيعُ عَمَلَ عَٰمِلٖ مِّنكُم مِّن ذَكَرٍ أَوۡ أُنثَىٰۖ بَعۡضُكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡضٖۖ فَٱلَّذِينَ هَاجَرُواْ وَأُخۡرِجُواْ مِن دِيَٰرِهِمۡ وَأُوذُواْ فِي سَبِيلِي وَقَٰتَلُواْ وَقُتِلُواْ لَأُكَفِّرَنَّ عَنۡهُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِهِمۡ وَلَأُدۡخِلَنَّهُمۡ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ ثَوَابٗا مِّنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱللَّهُ عِندَهُۥ حُسۡنُ ٱلثَّوَابِ

Their Lord responded to their supplication: "I do not let perish the reward of your deeds, great or small, whether the doer is male or female. So, the principle concerning one another is the same in religion- a person is not given more for being male, and a person is not given less for being female. Those who emigrated in the path of Allah, forced from their homes by disbelievers, suffered harm to obey their Lord. They fought in the path of Allah and were killed so that the Word of Allah would be the highest. I will undoubtedly forgive and overlook their sins on the Day of Judgment and admit them into gardens with rivers flowing under their palaces as a reward from Allah. The best recompense which has no equal is with Allah.

(١٩٦) لَا يَغُرَّنَّكَ تَقَلُّبُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ

The movement of the disbelievers in the land, their command of it, and the abundance of their trade and livelihood should not deceive you, O Prophet, to the point that you feel anxiety and grief at their circumstances.

(١٩٧) مَتَٰعٞ قَلِيلٞ ثُمَّ مَأۡوَىٰهُمۡ جَهَنَّمُۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمِهَادُ

This worldly life is a small commodity that has no perpetuity. After that, the fate they will resort to on the Day of Judgment will be Hell. What a terrible bed is the Fire for them!

(١٩٨) لَٰكِنِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْ رَبَّهُمۡ لَهُمۡ جَنَّٰتٞ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا نُزُلٗا مِّنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَمَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرٞ لِّلۡأَبۡرَارِ

However, those who fear their Lord by following His commands and staying away from what He has prohibited will receive gardens with rivers flowing under their palaces, dwelling in them forever as recompense prepared for them by Allah, the Exalted. What Allah has prepared for the righteous among His slaves is better and more superior than any worldly pleasures the disbelievers may be wallowing in.

(١٩٩) وَإِنَّ مِنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَمَن يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكُمۡ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡهِمۡ خَٰشِعِينَ لِلَّهِ لَا يَشۡتَرُونَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ ثَمَنٗا قَلِيلًاۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرُهُمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ

The People of the Book are not all the same. A group among them believes in Allah, the truth, and the guidance He revealed to you. They also believe in what was revealed to them in their books, and they do not make distinctions between the messengers of Allah, humbling themselves, submissive to Allah, and wishing for what is with Him. They do not trade the verses of Allah for a small profit in the wares of worldly life. The ones described with these qualities shall have a tremendous reward with their Lord. Indeed, Allah is swift in taking deeds into account and swift in requital for them.

(٢٠٠) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱصۡبِرُواْ وَصَابِرُواْ وَرَابِطُواْ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, be patient with responsibilities under the Divine law and the worldly calamities that strike. Defeat the disbelievers through patience, for they are not more patient than you. Take care to fight in the path of Allah. Fear Allah by following His commands and staying away from what He has prohibited so that you may obtain what you seek of safety from the Fire and enter Paradise.

Points of Benefit:

The harm that a believer endures for the sake of Allah when forced to emigrate from his home because of his faith is one of the greatest means by which sins are forgiven and rewards are multiplied.
What the disbeliever may be given regarding wealth and enjoyment, even if significant, is unimportant because it is fleeting. The only important thing is the reality of what happens to them in the Hereafter in the abode of eternity.
Among the People of the Book are those who bear witness to the truth in their books: they have faith in what was revealed to them and the believers. Therefore, they will get double the reward.
Patience upon the truth and struggling against those who reject it while striving for its sake is the path to success in the Hereafter.
