The Women (Surah An-Nisa)

Medinan Surah

Surah Objective(s):

Organizing the Muslim community, building its relationships, preserving rights, encouraging armed struggle, and invalidating the claim that Christ was killed.

Explanation:

It was named as such due to the mention of women and the details of many rulings pertaining to them.

(١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن نَّفۡسٖ وَٰحِدَةٖ وَخَلَقَ مِنۡهَا زَوۡجَهَا وَبَثَّ مِنۡهُمَا رِجَالٗا كَثِيرٗا وَنِسَآءٗۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِي تَسَآءَلُونَ بِهِۦ وَٱلۡأَرۡحَامَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ رَقِيبٗا

O people, fear your Lord, for He created you from a single soul- your father, Adam. From Adam, He created his spouse, Eve, your mother. From the two of them, He dispersed a great multitude of people, both male and female, throughout the regions of the earth. Be fearful of Allah when asking one another by invoking Him, such as: I ask you, by Allah, to do such-and-such.Also, be fearful of severing ties of kinship, which link you together. Allah keeps a close watch over you, and not a thing from your actions escapes Him. Rather, He calculates them and recompenses you based on that.

(٢) وَءَاتُواْ ٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰٓ أَمۡوَٰلَهُمۡۖ وَلَا تَتَبَدَّلُواْ ٱلۡخَبِيثَ بِٱلطَّيِّبِۖ وَلَا تَأۡكُلُوٓاْ أَمۡوَٰلَهُمۡ إِلَىٰٓ أَمۡوَٰلِكُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ حُوبٗا كَبِيرٗا

O guardians, for those whose fathers have died and have not yet reached puberty, i.e., orphans, give them their wealth in full when they reach puberty. Do not turn what is permitted into what is prohibited by taking what has an excellent value from the property of orphans and exchanging it for something inferior or of no value from your property. Do not take the property of orphans and combine it with your own – that is a great crime, according to Allah.

(٣) وَإِنۡ خِفۡتُمۡ أَلَّا تُقۡسِطُواْ فِي ٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ فَٱنكِحُواْ مَا طَابَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ مَثۡنَىٰ وَثُلَٰثَ وَرُبَٰعَۖ فَإِنۡ خِفۡتُمۡ أَلَّا تَعۡدِلُواْ فَوَٰحِدَةً أَوۡ مَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُكُمۡۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَدۡنَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَعُولُواْ

If you fear that you will not be just when marrying female orphans under your guardianship – either by reducing their dowry from what their status demands or by mistreating them – then leave them alone and marry who is agreeable among other women. If you wish, marry two, three, or four. However, if you fear that you will not be equitable between them, restrict yourself to just one, or enjoy what you possess of slave women since their rights are not the same as the rights of wives. That which is mentioned in the verse concerning orphans and restricting oneself to marrying a single woman or enjoying slave women is the closest to not being unjust and being biased.

(٤) وَءَاتُواْ ٱلنِّسَآءَ صَدُقَٰتِهِنَّ نِحۡلَةٗۚ فَإِن طِبۡنَ لَكُمۡ عَن شَيۡءٖ مِّنۡهُ نَفۡسٗا فَكُلُوهُ هَنِيٓ‍ٔٗا مَّرِيٓ‍ٔٗا

Give women their rightful dowries as required. Yet, if they gladly relinquish anything from the dowry to you without being forced to, use it as permitted and do not be bothered about it.

(٥) وَلَا تُؤۡتُواْ ٱلسُّفَهَآءَ أَمۡوَٰلَكُمُ ٱلَّتِي جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ قِيَٰمٗا وَٱرۡزُقُوهُمۡ فِيهَا وَٱكۡسُوهُمۡ وَقُولُواْ لَهُمۡ قَوۡلٗا مَّعۡرُوفٗا

O guardians, do not give property to those not proficient in disposing of it. Allah made this property a way to provide for the welfare and living expenses of slaves, and such individuals are not qualified to manage and protect property. So, spend on them and clothe them from that property. Speak to them in a good way. Prepare them well so you can give them their property when they reach puberty and become proficient at managing it.

(٦) وَٱبۡتَلُواْ ٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا بَلَغُواْ ٱلنِّكَاحَ فَإِنۡ ءَانَسۡتُم مِّنۡهُمۡ رُشۡدٗا فَٱدۡفَعُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِمۡ أَمۡوَٰلَهُمۡۖ وَلَا تَأۡكُلُوهَآ إِسۡرَافٗا وَبِدَارًا أَن يَكۡبَرُواْۚ وَمَن كَانَ غَنِيّٗا فَلۡيَسۡتَعۡفِفۡۖ وَمَن كَانَ فَقِيرٗا فَلۡيَأۡكُلۡ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِۚ فَإِذَا دَفَعۡتُمۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡ أَمۡوَٰلَهُمۡ فَأَشۡهِدُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ حَسِيبٗا

O guardians, test orphans when they reach the age of puberty by giving them a part of their wealth to manage. If they dispose of it well, and their maturity is evident to you, hand over their property in full without holding it back from them. Do not consume their wealth when the need arises, overstepping the limit Allah has permitted you. Do not rush to use it up for fear that they will take it when they reach maturity. Those with enough wealth to suffice them should refrain from taking the wealth of orphans. He among you who is poor and has no wealth should only use it proportionately to his need. When you hand over the property after they reach puberty and their maturity is apparent, there should be a witness to that hand-over to protect the rights involved and to prevent causes for disagreement. Allah is sufficient as a witness to that and as an accountant for slaves regarding their actions.

Points of Benefit:

All humans have the same origin. So they must be mindful of their Lord Who created them and should be merciful to each other.
Allah commands that kindness be shown to those who are weak among women and orphans so that their treatment should be just and generous.
It is permissible for a Muslim man to have up to four wives, with the condition of being just between them and being able to fulfill his duties towards them.
The legitimacy of placing an inept person who does not behave correctly under guardianship for his own good and to protect him from wasting his wealth, which is used in his best interests.
