(٧) لِّلرِّجَالِ نَصِيبٞ مِّمَّا تَرَكَ ٱلۡوَٰلِدَانِ وَٱلۡأَقۡرَبُونَ وَلِلنِّسَآءِ نَصِيبٞ مِّمَّا تَرَكَ ٱلۡوَٰلِدَانِ وَٱلۡأَقۡرَبُونَ مِمَّا قَلَّ مِنۡهُ أَوۡ كَثُرَۚ نَصِيبٗا مَّفۡرُوضٗا

Men are to have a share of what is left behind after death by their parents or relatives, such as brothers and uncles, whether it is a large or small sum. Women are also to have a share of what their relatives leave behind, contrary to the Age of Ignorance, when women and children, were left out of the inheritance. As Allah obligates, this share is a right, and its amount is clearly determined.

(٨) وَإِذَا حَضَرَ ٱلۡقِسۡمَةَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينُ فَٱرۡزُقُوهُم مِّنۡهُ وَقُولُواْ لَهُمۡ قَوۡلٗا مَّعۡرُوفٗا

If relatives, orphans, and the poor, who do not inherit, are present at the time the inheritance is divided, then give them, on the basis it is a recommendation, some of the wealth before it is divided. Give whatever your souls find agreeable since they may be hoping for a share, and it came to you without any hardship. Also, kindly speak to them without any meanness.

(٩) وَلۡيَخۡشَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَوۡ تَرَكُواْ مِنۡ خَلۡفِهِمۡ ذُرِّيَّةٗ ضِعَٰفًا خَافُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ فَلۡيَتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَلۡيَقُولُواْ قَوۡلٗا سَدِيدًا

Those who would leave behind young, weak children if they died should fear they would be left in ruins. They should fear Allah regarding orphans under their guardianship by avoiding unfairness towards them. This is so that Allah will facilitate for them after death someone who will treat their children as well as they did. They should conduct themselves excellently concerning the rights of children present at his last will by telling them statements that correspond to the truth. This is so that his heirs are not wronged in his last will after he is gone. Nor should he deprive himself of this good deed by not leaving a last will.

(١٠) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَأۡكُلُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَ ٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ ظُلۡمًا إِنَّمَا يَأۡكُلُونَ فِي بُطُونِهِمۡ نَارٗاۖ وَسَيَصۡلَوۡنَ سَعِيرٗا

Those who take the property of orphans and spend it wrongly and unjustly only consume a Fire into their stomachs that sets fire to them. The Fire will incinerate them completely on the Day of Judgment.

(١١) يُوصِيكُمُ ٱللَّهُ فِيٓ أَوۡلَٰدِكُمۡۖ لِلذَّكَرِ مِثۡلُ حَظِّ ٱلۡأُنثَيَيۡنِۚ فَإِن كُنَّ نِسَآءٗ فَوۡقَ ٱثۡنَتَيۡنِ فَلَهُنَّ ثُلُثَا مَا تَرَكَۖ وَإِن كَانَتۡ وَٰحِدَةٗ فَلَهَا ٱلنِّصۡفُۚ وَلِأَبَوَيۡهِ لِكُلِّ وَٰحِدٖ مِّنۡهُمَا ٱلسُّدُسُ مِمَّا تَرَكَ إِن كَانَ لَهُۥ وَلَدٞۚ فَإِن لَّمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ وَلَدٞ وَوَرِثَهُۥٓ أَبَوَاهُ فَلِأُمِّهِ ٱلثُّلُثُۚ فَإِن كَانَ لَهُۥٓ إِخۡوَةٞ فَلِأُمِّهِ ٱلسُّدُسُۚ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ وَصِيَّةٖ يُوصِي بِهَآ أَوۡ دَيۡنٍۗ ءَابَآؤُكُمۡ وَأَبۡنَآؤُكُمۡ لَا تَدۡرُونَ أَيُّهُمۡ أَقۡرَبُ لَكُمۡ نَفۡعٗاۚ فَرِيضَةٗ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمٗا

Allah entrusts and orders you concerning your children's inheritance to divide it between them as follows: a son gets a share equal to the share of two daughters. If the deceased left two daughters or more without a son, they get two-thirds of what is left behind. If there is just one daughter, she gets half of what is left behind. Each of the deceased's parents gets a sixth of what is left behind if he has a male or female child. If he has no children or heir except for his parents, the mother gets a third, and the father gets the rest as an unfixed share. If the deceased has two or more siblings, male or female, full siblings or half-siblings, his mother gets a sixth share as a fixed share, and the father gets the rest as an unfixed share. There is nothing for the siblings. This division of the inheritance is after execution of the bequests that the deceased ordered on the stipulation that his bequests cannot be more than a third of his total wealth and his debts have been repaid. Allah has divided inheritance like this because you do not know who among your parents and children are most beneficial to you in the worldly life and Afterlife. The deceased may presume the best of one of his heirs and leave everything to him or presume the worst of someone and forbid him from receiving anything. Then, it turns out that the circumstances were contrary to that. The one who is aware of all of that is Allah. He is the one from whom nothing is hidden. For this reason, the division of inheritance was made clear, and He made it an obligation for His slaves. Allah knows everything, and nothing is hidden from Him concerning the welfare of His slaves. He is All-Wise in His legislation and management.

Points of Benefit:

The inheritance laws of Islam give men and women their rights, ensuring justice between them.
The sanctity of the wealth of orphans is firmly established, and violating its limits or wasting it in any way is forbidden.
With wealth being one of the biggest causes of disputes between people, Allah Himself has set out how to divide it through the laws of inheritance.
