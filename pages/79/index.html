(١٢) ۞وَلَكُمۡ نِصۡفُ مَا تَرَكَ أَزۡوَٰجُكُمۡ إِن لَّمۡ يَكُن لَّهُنَّ وَلَدٞۚ فَإِن كَانَ لَهُنَّ وَلَدٞ فَلَكُمُ ٱلرُّبُعُ مِمَّا تَرَكۡنَۚ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ وَصِيَّةٖ يُوصِينَ بِهَآ أَوۡ دَيۡنٖۚ وَلَهُنَّ ٱلرُّبُعُ مِمَّا تَرَكۡتُمۡ إِن لَّمۡ يَكُن لَّكُمۡ وَلَدٞۚ فَإِن كَانَ لَكُمۡ وَلَدٞ فَلَهُنَّ ٱلثُّمُنُ مِمَّا تَرَكۡتُمۚ مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ وَصِيَّةٖ تُوصُونَ بِهَآ أَوۡ دَيۡنٖۗ وَإِن كَانَ رَجُلٞ يُورَثُ كَلَٰلَةً أَوِ ٱمۡرَأَةٞ وَلَهُۥٓ أَخٌ أَوۡ أُخۡتٞ فَلِكُلِّ وَٰحِدٖ مِّنۡهُمَا ٱلسُّدُسُۚ فَإِن كَانُوٓاْ أَكۡثَرَ مِن ذَٰلِكَ فَهُمۡ شُرَكَآءُ فِي ٱلثُّلُثِۚ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ وَصِيَّةٖ يُوصَىٰ بِهَآ أَوۡ دَيۡنٍ غَيۡرَ مُضَآرّٖۚ وَصِيَّةٗ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَلِيمٞ

Half of what your wives leave behind is for you, O husbands, if they have no male or female children with you or someone else. If they have male or female children, you get a fourth of the wealth they leave behind. You receive your share after the execution of her bequests and the repayment of her debts. Wives get a quarter of what you leave behind, O husbands if you have no male or female children with them or another woman. If you have male or female children, wives receive an eighth of what you leave behind, to be divided after the execution of your bequests and the repayment of your debts. If a man or woman dies without any parents or children to inherit from them, and the deceased has a brother or sister from the same mother, then each receives a fixed sixth share. If they have more than one male or female sibling from the same mother, they share a third fixed share, with males and females receiving an equal share. They only take their share after the execution of the deceased's bequests and the repayment of his debts, stipulating that the bequest does not involve harming the inheritors, such as it being more than a third of his wealth. The rulings contained in the verses are a trust from Allah to you, which He has obligated. Allah knows what is in the best interest of His slaves in the worldly life and Afterlife. He is forbearing and does not rush to punish the disobedient.

(١٣) تِلۡكَ حُدُودُ ٱللَّهِۚ وَمَن يُطِعِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ يُدۡخِلۡهُ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ وَذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ

The rulings mentioned concerning orphans and others are the laws of Allah that Allah has legislated for His slaves to act upon. Whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger by following His commands and staying away from what He has prohibited, Allah will admit him into gardens with rivers flowing beneath their palaces, residing where destruction cannot catch up with them. That divine reward is the great success that no other success comes near to.

(١٤) وَمَن يَعۡصِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَيَتَعَدَّ حُدُودَهُۥ يُدۡخِلۡهُ نَارًا خَٰلِدٗا فِيهَا وَلَهُۥ عَذَابٞ مُّهِينٞ

Whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger by denying His rulings, neglecting to act upon them, or doubting them and overstepping the limits He has legislated, He will insert him into a fire. There, he will dwell in humiliating torment.

Points of Benefit:

Wealth from inheritance is not divided between the inheritors until the deceased person's debts have been settled and their lawful bequests taken from it, which cannot constitute more than a third of the total wealth.
There is a warning against being careless about the division of the inheritance because it is Allah's decree and His commandment to His believing servants, so it is forbidden to be negligent or disregard it.
The characteristics of faith include following Allah's orders, respecting what He has prohibited, and stopping at His established limits.
A manifestation of Allah's justice and wisdom is that He promises the greatest reward to those who obey Him and the severest punishment to those who oppose Him and overstep His limits.
