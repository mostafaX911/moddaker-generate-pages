(٤٩) وَإِذۡ نَجَّيۡنَٰكُم مِّنۡ ءَالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ يَسُومُونَكُمۡ سُوٓءَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ يُذَبِّحُونَ أَبۡنَآءَكُمۡ وَيَسۡتَحۡيُونَ نِسَآءَكُمۡۚ وَفِي ذَٰلِكُم بَلَآءٞ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ عَظِيمٞ

O Israelites, remember the time We saved you from the followers of Pharaoh who would make you taste various forms of punishment, slaughtering your sons so you would not survive and letting your women live so they could serve them, taking great pains to humiliate and insult you. And in your salvation from the onslaught of Pharoah and his followers and his people was a great test for you from your Lord, that perhaps you may be thankful.

(٥٠) وَإِذۡ فَرَقۡنَا بِكُمُ ٱلۡبَحۡرَ فَأَنجَيۡنَٰكُمۡ وَأَغۡرَقۡنَآ ءَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَأَنتُمۡ تَنظُرُونَ

Remember Our blessing upon you when We parted the sea, making it a dry path for you to cross through. We saved you while drowning your enemies, Pharaoh and his followers, before your very eyes as you looked on at them.

(٥١) وَإِذۡ وَٰعَدۡنَا مُوسَىٰٓ أَرۡبَعِينَ لَيۡلَةٗ ثُمَّ ٱتَّخَذۡتُمُ ٱلۡعِجۡلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ وَأَنتُمۡ ظَٰلِمُونَ

And remember that the forty nights appointed to Moses were from Allah's blessings, in which the revelation of the Torah, with its light and guidance, was completed. Yet you went and worshipped the calf during that period, becoming transgressors through your actions.

(٥٢) ثُمَّ عَفَوۡنَا عَنكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

Then We forgave after your repentance and did not take you to account for it so that you might be thankful to Allah by worshipping Him well and obeying him.

(٥٣) وَإِذۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡفُرۡقَانَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَهۡتَدُونَ

Remember that among Our blessings upon you was when we gave Moses (peace be upon him) the Torah as a criterion between truth and falsehood. It distinguished between guidance and misguidance so that you might be guided to the truth through it.

(٥٤) وَإِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوۡمِهِۦ يَٰقَوۡمِ إِنَّكُمۡ ظَلَمۡتُمۡ أَنفُسَكُم بِٱتِّخَاذِكُمُ ٱلۡعِجۡلَ فَتُوبُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ بَارِئِكُمۡ فَٱقۡتُلُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡ عِندَ بَارِئِكُمۡ فَتَابَ عَلَيۡكُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلتَّوَّابُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

And remember from these blessings was Allah granting you the ability to repent after worshipping the calf, when Moses (peace be upon him) told you: You have wronged yourselves by taking the calf as a deity and worshipping it. So repent and return to your Creator and Originator by some of you killing others (those that transgressed). Repenting to Him in this way is better for you than persisting in disbelief, which would lead to an eternity in the Fire. So, you did this with Allah's guidance and aid, and He accepted your repentance, for He is Oft-Forgiving and Merciful toward His slaves.

(٥٥) وَإِذۡ قُلۡتُمۡ يَٰمُوسَىٰ لَن نُّؤۡمِنَ لَكَ حَتَّىٰ نَرَى ٱللَّهَ جَهۡرَةٗ فَأَخَذَتۡكُمُ ٱلصَّٰعِقَةُ وَأَنتُمۡ تَنظُرُونَ

And remember the time you brazenly addressed Moses (peace be upon him), proclaiming: We will not believe you [O Moses] until we see Allah with your own eyes, unveiled and before us. As a result, a burning fire took and killed you as you looked at one another.

(٥٦) ثُمَّ بَعَثۡنَٰكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ مَوۡتِكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ

Then, We brought you back to life after death so that you might be thankful to Him for the blessing of life that He granted you.

(٥٧) وَظَلَّلۡنَا عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡغَمَامَ وَأَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡمَنَّ وَٱلسَّلۡوَىٰۖ كُلُواْ مِن طَيِّبَٰتِ مَا رَزَقۡنَٰكُمۡۚ وَمَا ظَلَمُونَا وَلَٰكِن كَانُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَظۡلِمُونَ

And from Our blessing upon you was sending clouds to shade you from the sun's heat when you were wandering the earth. And We sent down from our bounties a sweet drink resembling honey (called Manna) and a small bird with good meat resembling that of quails (called Salwa). We said to you: Consume the good things We have provided for you. We [Allah] lost nothing through their denial and rejection of these blessings. Instead, they only wronged themselves by losing their share of the reward and exposing themselves to suffering.

Points of Benefit:

The greatness and abundance of Allah's blessings upon the Israelites, yet it only increased them in arrogance and stubbornness.
Allah's abundant forbearance and mercy toward His servants, even if their sins are immense.
Revelation is the decisive factor between truth and falsehood.
