(٢٠) وَإِنۡ أَرَدتُّمُ ٱسۡتِبۡدَالَ زَوۡجٖ مَّكَانَ زَوۡجٖ وَءَاتَيۡتُمۡ إِحۡدَىٰهُنَّ قِنطَارٗا فَلَا تَأۡخُذُواْ مِنۡهُ شَيۡ‍ًٔاۚ أَتَأۡخُذُونَهُۥ بُهۡتَٰنٗا وَإِثۡمٗا مُّبِينٗا

If you, O husbands, want to divorce a woman and replace her with another, then there is no harm. However, if you gave the one you intend to divorce a great deal of wealth as a dowry, you are not allowed to take anything back from it. Returning what you gave them would be considered open defamation and a clear sin.

(٢١) وَكَيۡفَ تَأۡخُذُونَهُۥ وَقَدۡ أَفۡضَىٰ بَعۡضُكُمۡ إِلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ وَأَخَذۡنَ مِنكُم مِّيثَٰقًا غَلِيظٗا

How could you take what you gave to them as a dowry after what occurred between you of connection, friendship, enjoyment, and sharing secrets? Greed for the wealth they possess, despite all this, is detestable and shameful. They took a covenant from you that was strongly attested by which they became lawful to you through the Word of Allah, the Exalted, and His legislation.

(٢٢) وَلَا تَنكِحُواْ مَا نَكَحَ ءَابَآؤُكُم مِّنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ إِلَّا مَا قَدۡ سَلَفَۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ فَٰحِشَةٗ وَمَقۡتٗا وَسَآءَ سَبِيلًا

Do not marry women whom your fathers married. That is forbidden except for what happened before Islam, in which case you will not be taken to account for it. Sons marrying their fathers' wives is a practice of immense repulsiveness, a cause for earning Allah's anger for its perpetrator, and an evil path for whoever traverses it.

(٢٣) حُرِّمَتۡ عَلَيۡكُمۡ أُمَّهَٰتُكُمۡ وَبَنَاتُكُمۡ وَأَخَوَٰتُكُمۡ وَعَمَّٰتُكُمۡ وَخَٰلَٰتُكُمۡ وَبَنَاتُ ٱلۡأَخِ وَبَنَاتُ ٱلۡأُخۡتِ وَأُمَّهَٰتُكُمُ ٱلَّٰتِيٓ أَرۡضَعۡنَكُمۡ وَأَخَوَٰتُكُم مِّنَ ٱلرَّضَٰعَةِ وَأُمَّهَٰتُ نِسَآئِكُمۡ وَرَبَٰٓئِبُكُمُ ٱلَّٰتِي فِي حُجُورِكُم مِّن نِّسَآئِكُمُ ٱلَّٰتِي دَخَلۡتُم بِهِنَّ فَإِن لَّمۡ تَكُونُواْ دَخَلۡتُم بِهِنَّ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَحَلَٰٓئِلُ أَبۡنَآئِكُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ مِنۡ أَصۡلَٰبِكُمۡ وَأَن تَجۡمَعُواْ بَيۡنَ ٱلۡأُخۡتَيۡنِ إِلَّا مَا قَدۡ سَلَفَۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا

Allah has prohibited for you to marry the following: your mothers, even if far removed through an ascended relationship, in other words, your grandmother and great-grandmother, whether paternal or maternal; your daughters, even if far removed through descended relationship, in other words, your granddaughters and great-granddaughters; likewise, the daughters of your son and daughters of daughters, even if far removed through descended relationship; your full sisters and half-sisters; your paternal aunts and great-aunts of your fathers and mothers, even if far removed through ascendant relationship; your maternal aunts and maternal great-aunts of your father and mothers, even if far removed through ascendant relationship; the daughters of your full brother and full sister, and their children, even if far removed through descendant relationship; your mothers through breastfeeding and your sisters through breastfeeding; the mothers of your wives, whether you have consummated the marriage or not; and the daughters of your wives raised mostly in your home whom she had with someone else, if you have consummated the marriage. However, if you have not consummated the marriage, then there is no harm in marrying their daughters. You are also forbidden to marry your sons' wives from your loins, even if they did not consummate the marriage. Included in this ruling are the wives of your sons through breastfeeding. It is prohibited for you to marry two sisters at the same time who are related by blood or breastfeeding, except if this happened in the Age of Ignorance. In this case, Allah has pardoned it. Allah is forgiving and compassionate to His slaves who are repentant towards Him. In the authentic examples set by the Prophet, the prohibition of simultaneously marrying a woman and her paternal or maternal aunt is also confirmed.

Points of Benefit:

If a man has sexual relations with his wife, the dowry is hers to keep. He is not, then, allowed to touch it or wish for it, even if he wishes to separate from her and divorce her.
Allah has prohibited marriage to the ex-wives of one's father because it is an evil that anyone in their right mind with a wholesome nature detests.
Allah details which women are allowed to be married and which are prohibited from being married to, whether due to one's family relationship with them or association by marriage or wet-nursing. This is done to honor and protect them from transgression.
