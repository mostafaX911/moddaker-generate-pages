(٢٤) ۞وَٱلۡمُحۡصَنَٰتُ مِنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ إِلَّا مَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُكُمۡۖ كِتَٰبَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡۚ وَأُحِلَّ لَكُم مَّا وَرَآءَ ذَٰلِكُمۡ أَن تَبۡتَغُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِكُم مُّحۡصِنِينَ غَيۡرَ مُسَٰفِحِينَۚ فَمَا ٱسۡتَمۡتَعۡتُم بِهِۦ مِنۡهُنَّ فَ‍َٔاتُوهُنَّ أُجُورَهُنَّ فَرِيضَةٗۚ وَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فِيمَا تَرَٰضَيۡتُم بِهِۦ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ٱلۡفَرِيضَةِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمٗا

Those who are already married are prohibited to you, except those whom you have captured while fighting in Allah's path: intimate relations with them are allowed to you after making sure they are not pregnant by the arrival of a menstruation cycle – this is an emphatic obligation from Allah. Aside from that, Allah has permitted you to seek to protect your chastity and theirs with your wealth in a lawful way without pursuing fornication. Give those you enjoy in marriage their dowry, which Allah has made an obligation for you. However, there is nothing wrong if you mutually agree, after the obligation of the dowry has been set, to increase it or to give up part of it. Allah knows His creation, and nothing of them is hidden from Him. He is All-Wise in His management and His legislation.

(٢٥) وَمَن لَّمۡ يَسۡتَطِعۡ مِنكُمۡ طَوۡلًا أَن يَنكِحَ ٱلۡمُحۡصَنَٰتِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ فَمِن مَّا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُكُم مِّن فَتَيَٰتِكُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۚ وَٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِإِيمَٰنِكُمۚ بَعۡضُكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡضٖۚ فَٱنكِحُوهُنَّ بِإِذۡنِ أَهۡلِهِنَّ وَءَاتُوهُنَّ أُجُورَهُنَّ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ مُحۡصَنَٰتٍ غَيۡرَ مُسَٰفِحَٰتٖ وَلَا مُتَّخِذَٰتِ أَخۡدَانٖۚ فَإِذَآ أُحۡصِنَّ فَإِنۡ أَتَيۡنَ بِفَٰحِشَةٖ فَعَلَيۡهِنَّ نِصۡفُ مَا عَلَى ٱلۡمُحۡصَنَٰتِ مِنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِۚ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَنۡ خَشِيَ ٱلۡعَنَتَ مِنكُمۡۚ وَأَن تَصۡبِرُواْ خَيۡرٞ لَّكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٞ رَّحِيمٞ

O men, those of you who cannot marry free women due to a lack of resources are allowed to marry a slave owned by someone else- if they appear to be believers. Allah knows best the reality of your faith and the innermost aspect of your circumstances. You are equal to them in religion and humanity, so do not be too proud to marry them. Marry them with the permission of their owners and give them their dowries without reducing the amount or delaying it. This is if they are chaste and not open fornicators. Do not be among those who have intimate relationships to commit fornication with women in secret. If they get married and then commit the disgraceful act of adultery, their legal punishment is half the punishment for a free woman: fifty lashes and not stoning, contrary to the punishment for women previously married. The permissibility of marriage to chaste, believing slave women is a dispensation for those who fear falling into fornication and are unable to marry free women. Patience is preferable to marrying slave women, so that the children do not become slaves. Allah is Forgiving and Compassionate to those of His slaves who repent. From His mercy, He legislated marriage to slaves for those who fear fornication in the case of not being able to marry free women.

(٢٦) يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ لِيُبَيِّنَ لَكُمۡ وَيَهۡدِيَكُمۡ سُنَنَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ وَيَتُوبَ عَلَيۡكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٞ

By legislating these rulings, Allah (may He be Exalted) wishes to clarify His laws and religion and what is in your best interests in the worldly life and Afterlife. He also wishes to guide you to the ways of the previous prophets in allowing and prohibiting matters, their noble qualities, and praiseworthy lives so that you can follow them. He wishes to bring you from disobedience to His obedience. Allah is Omniscient about what is in the best interests of His slaves, and He legislates accordingly. He is All-Wise in His legislation and in managing their affairs.

Points of Benefit:

The prohibition of marrying married women: It is impermissible to marry free or slave women until their waiting period (Iddah) is completed, regardless of the reason for the waiting period.
That the dowry of a woman is due after consummation, and it is permissible for her to waive part of her dowry willingly.
The permissibility of marrying believing slave women when unable to marry free women if one fears falling into adultery.
One of the purposes of the Sharia is to clarify guidance and misguidance and to guide people to the ways of guidance that lead them to Allah, the Exalted.
