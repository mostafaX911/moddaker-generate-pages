(٢٧) وَٱللَّهُ يُرِيدُ أَن يَتُوبَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَيُرِيدُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَتَّبِعُونَ ٱلشَّهَوَٰتِ أَن تَمِيلُواْ مَيۡلًا عَظِيمٗا

Allah wants to pardon you and forgive your sins, while those who march forward in life behind their own pleasures want you to become distant from the straight path.

(٢٨) يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ أَن يُخَفِّفَ عَنكُمۡۚ وَخُلِقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ ضَعِيفٗا

Allah wants to reduce the burden of what He legislates for you. Therefore, He does not burden you with what you cannot bear because He knows the weakness of human beings in their nature and disposition.

(٢٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَأۡكُلُوٓاْ أَمۡوَٰلَكُم بَيۡنَكُم بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ إِلَّآ أَن تَكُونَ تِجَٰرَةً عَن تَرَاضٖ مِّنكُمۡۚ وَلَا تَقۡتُلُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِكُمۡ رَحِيمٗا

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, do not take the money of others falsely, by force, theft, bribery, or other unjust means. Instead, money should come from trade through mutual agreement and goodwill between the contracting parties. In this way, it will be lawful for you to consume and dispose of such money. Do not kill one another, do not kill yourselves, and do not cast yourselves into ruin. Allah is Merciful with you and made your blood, wealth, and honor inviolable out of mercy.

(٣٠) وَمَن يَفۡعَلۡ ذَٰلِكَ عُدۡوَٰنٗا وَظُلۡمٗا فَسَوۡفَ نُصۡلِيهِ نَارٗاۚ وَكَانَ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرًا

Whoever does the things Allah has prohibited – consuming the wealth of others or wronging them by killing and the like, purposely exceeding [limits], not out of ignorance or forgetfulness – Allah will admit them to a tremendous fire on the Day of Judgment. There, they will suffer its burning heat and torment. That is insignificant for Allah because He is All-Powerful, and nothing is impossible for Him.

(٣١) إِن تَجۡتَنِبُواْ كَبَآئِرَ مَا تُنۡهَوۡنَ عَنۡهُ نُكَفِّرۡ عَنكُمۡ سَيِّ‍َٔاتِكُمۡ وَنُدۡخِلۡكُم مُّدۡخَلٗا كَرِيمٗا

O believers, if you avoid acts of major disobedience like joining partners with Allah, ingratitude towards your parents, murder, and receiving interest on loans, then Allah will overlook your lesser sins by wiping them out, erasing them. And We will admit you into a noble place near Him: Paradise.

(٣٢) وَلَا تَتَمَنَّوۡاْ مَا فَضَّلَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦ بَعۡضَكُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖۚ لِّلرِّجَالِ نَصِيبٞ مِّمَّا ٱكۡتَسَبُواْۖ وَلِلنِّسَآءِ نَصِيبٞ مِّمَّا ٱكۡتَسَبۡنَۚ وَسۡ‍َٔلُواْ ٱللَّهَ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٗا

O believers, do not wish for that by which Allah favors some of you over others, lest that leads to resentment and envy. Women should not wish for what Allah has given to men in particular. Indeed, both genders have a share of acts that suit them. Call upon Allah to increase what He grants you. Allah knows everything, so He gives each of them what is consistent with their actions.

(٣٣) وَلِكُلّٖ جَعَلۡنَا مَوَٰلِيَ مِمَّا تَرَكَ ٱلۡوَٰلِدَانِ وَٱلۡأَقۡرَبُونَۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ عَقَدَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُكُمۡ فَ‍َٔاتُوهُمۡ نَصِيبَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدًا

For every one of you, We have made relations to inherit from what parents and relatives leave in their inheritance. For those with whom you have solemnly sworn to be an ally and helper, give them their share of the inheritance. Indeed, Allah is a witness to everything, and His witness is over your oaths and covenants. Granting inheritance utilizing oaths took place at the beginning of Islam, then it was abrogated.

Points of Benefit:

The vast mercy of Allah towards His servants; indeed, He, the Exalted, loves repentance from them and easing for them. As for the people of desires, they only seek to lead them astray from guidance.
The Sharia has preserved the rights of people; it prohibits aggression against lives, wealth, and honor and has established the severest penalties for such transgressions.
Keeping away from major sins is a cause for entering Paradise and forgiveness for minor sins.
Satisfaction with what Allah has decreed and refraining from coveting what is in the hands of people helps one avoid envy and resentment towards the divine decree of Allah, the Exalted.
