(٣٤) ٱلرِّجَالُ قَوَّٰمُونَ عَلَى ٱلنِّسَآءِ بِمَا فَضَّلَ ٱللَّهُ بَعۡضَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ وَبِمَآ أَنفَقُواْ مِنۡ أَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡۚ فَٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتُ قَٰنِتَٰتٌ حَٰفِظَٰتٞ لِّلۡغَيۡبِ بِمَا حَفِظَ ٱللَّهُۚ وَٱلَّٰتِي تَخَافُونَ نُشُوزَهُنَّ فَعِظُوهُنَّ وَٱهۡجُرُوهُنَّ فِي ٱلۡمَضَاجِعِ وَٱضۡرِبُوهُنَّۖ فَإِنۡ أَطَعۡنَكُمۡ فَلَا تَبۡغُواْ عَلَيۡهِنَّ سَبِيلًاۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيّٗا كَبِيرٗا

Men take care of women and manage their affairs because of the favor over [women] by which Allah has distinguished [men]. It is also because He has obligated [men] to spend on and look after them. Righteous women are obedient to their Lord, obedient to their husbands, and protectors of their honor for [men] in their absence through Allah granting them success. Those [women] you fear are too proud to obey their husbands, in words or actions, begin, O husbands, by reminding them and urging them to fear Allah. If they do not respond favorably, then forsake them in bed by turning his back to her and not being intimate with her. If they still do not respond favorably, then strike them non-violently. If they return to obedience, do not transgress against them with injustice or reproach. Indeed, Allah possesses loftiness over everything and is tremendous in His Divine Self and Attributes. So, fear Him.

(٣٥) وَإِنۡ خِفۡتُمۡ شِقَاقَ بَيۡنِهِمَا فَٱبۡعَثُواْ حَكَمٗا مِّنۡ أَهۡلِهِۦ وَحَكَمٗا مِّنۡ أَهۡلِهَآ إِن يُرِيدَآ إِصۡلَٰحٗا يُوَفِّقِ ٱللَّهُ بَيۡنَهُمَآۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا خَبِيرٗا

If you fear, O appointed representative of the two spouses, that the disagreement between the two of them will lead to enmity and mutual hostility, then send a just man from the family of the husband and a just man from the family of the wife to determine the advantages of separation or reconciliation - which is more preferred and worthier. If the two judges want reconciliation and they both pursue the best methods for it, Allah will reconcile the two spouses and remove the disagreement between them. Nothing about His slaves is hidden from Allah, and He is All-Knowledgeable of the minute things they conceal in their hearts.

(٣٦) ۞وَٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَلَا تُشۡرِكُواْ بِهِۦ شَيۡ‍ٔٗاۖ وَبِٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ إِحۡسَٰنٗا وَبِذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينِ وَٱلۡجَارِ ذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡجَارِ ٱلۡجُنُبِ وَٱلصَّاحِبِ بِٱلۡجَنۢبِ وَٱبۡنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَمَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُكُمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ مَن كَانَ مُخۡتَالٗا فَخُورًا

Worship Allah alone by submission to Him, and do not worship any others with Him. Be good to your parents by honoring and treating them with benevolence. Be good to relatives, orphans, and the poor. Be good to the neighbor with whom you share kinship and the neighbor with whom you do not. Be good to the friend who accompanies you. Be good to the traveler who is a stranger and has no means to complete their journey. Be good to those you own [slaves]. Allah does not like the ones who are amazed by themselves, arrogant with His slaves, and given to praising himself by boasting to others.

(٣٧) ٱلَّذِينَ يَبۡخَلُونَ وَيَأۡمُرُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ بِٱلۡبُخۡلِ وَيَكۡتُمُونَ مَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦۗ وَأَعۡتَدۡنَا لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَذَابٗا مُّهِينٗا

Allah does not love those who refuse to spend from His bounty that He has given them as obligated. And yet, they order others with speech and actions to do just that while hiding Allah's favors in terms of bounty, knowledge, and other things Allah has given them. Instead of clarifying the truth for people, they hide it and manifest falsehood. These are among the traits of disbelief, and We have prepared a humiliating punishment for disbelievers.

Points of Benefit:

The establishment of men's authority over women is due to Allah's granting them specific responsibilities and rights, foremost among them being the obligation to provide financial support for their wives.
Warning against injustice and oppression towards women in discipline by reminding the servant of Allah's power and His exalted status.
Warning against reprehensible moral traits, such as arrogance, boastfulness, stinginess, concealing knowledge, and not elucidating it for people.
