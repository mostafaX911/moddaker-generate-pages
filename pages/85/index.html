(٣٨) وَٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَهُمۡ رِئَآءَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَلَا بِٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۗ وَمَن يَكُنِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ لَهُۥ قَرِينٗا فَسَآءَ قَرِينٗا

We have likewise prepared punishment for those who spend their wealth so that people notice them and praise them. They do not believe in Allah and the Day of Judgment. We have prepared that shameful punishment for them. It is through following the Devil that they have gone astray. Whoever has the Devil as an inseparable friend, then what an evil companion he has.

(٣٩) وَمَاذَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ لَوۡ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَأَنفَقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقَهُمُ ٱللَّهُۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِمۡ عَلِيمًا

What harm would it have done these people if they had faith in Allah and the Day of Judgment and spent their wealth in the path of Allah, being sincere to Him? Rather, that is the sum of all things good. Allah is All-Knowledgeable about them. Nothing about their condition is hidden from Him, and everyone will be requited according to their actions.

(٤٠) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَظۡلِمُ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٖۖ وَإِن تَكُ حَسَنَةٗ يُضَٰعِفۡهَا وَيُؤۡتِ مِن لَّدُنۡهُ أَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا

Allah is Just and does not wrong His slaves in any way. He does not decrease their good deeds by the amount of a tiny ant or add to their sins at all. If a good deed is worth an atom's weight, He multiplies its reward as a favor from Him. And along with the multiplication, He grants an immense reward.

(٤١) فَكَيۡفَ إِذَا جِئۡنَا مِن كُلِّ أُمَّةِۢ بِشَهِيدٖ وَجِئۡنَا بِكَ عَلَىٰ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ شَهِيدٗا

How will it be on the Day of Judgment, when We bring a prophet for each nation to bear witness for what they did, and We bring you, O Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, as a witness for your nation?

(٤٢) يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَوَدُّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَعَصَوُاْ ٱلرَّسُولَ لَوۡ تُسَوَّىٰ بِهِمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَلَا يَكۡتُمُونَ ٱللَّهَ حَدِيثٗا

On that momentous Day, those who disbelieved in Allah and disobeyed His Messenger will wish they could become dirt so they could be equal to the earth. They will not be able to hide from Allah anything they did because Allah will seal their mouths so they cannot speak, and He will allow their limbs to bear witness to their actions against them.

(٤٣) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَقۡرَبُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَنتُمۡ سُكَٰرَىٰ حَتَّىٰ تَعۡلَمُواْ مَا تَقُولُونَ وَلَا جُنُبًا إِلَّا عَابِرِي سَبِيلٍ حَتَّىٰ تَغۡتَسِلُواْۚ وَإِن كُنتُم مَّرۡضَىٰٓ أَوۡ عَلَىٰ سَفَرٍ أَوۡ جَآءَ أَحَدٞ مِّنكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡغَآئِطِ أَوۡ لَٰمَسۡتُمُ ٱلنِّسَآءَ فَلَمۡ تَجِدُواْ مَآءٗ فَتَيَمَّمُواْ صَعِيدٗا طَيِّبٗا فَٱمۡسَحُواْ بِوُجُوهِكُمۡ وَأَيۡدِيكُمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَفُوًّا غَفُورًا

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, do not pray while intoxicated until you recover and can distinguish what you are saying- this was before intoxicants were forbidden entirely. Do not pray when you are in a state of post-sexual impurity. Do not enter mosques in that state, except just passing through without remaining in them, until you have performed the major ritual bath. If you fall ill and cannot use water, are traveling and have emitted a minor impurity, or have had sexual relations with a woman and you cannot find water - then find clean earth and wipe your faces and hands with it. Allah pardons your shortcomings and is Forgiving towards you.

(٤٤) أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ نَصِيبٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ يَشۡتَرُونَ ٱلضَّلَٰلَةَ وَيُرِيدُونَ أَن تَضِلُّواْ ٱلسَّبِيلَ

O Messenger, do you not know about the Jews to whom Allah gave a portion of knowledge in the Torah, and they exchanged guidance for misguidance? They persist in misleading you, O believers, from the straight path with which the Messenger came so you can traverse their crooked path.

Points of Benefit:

From the perfection of His justice and the completeness of His mercy is that He does not wrong His servants in the slightest, no matter how insignificant, and He favors them by multiplying their good deeds.
Due to the intense horror of the Day of Judgment and the enormity of what awaits the disbeliever, they would wish to be mere dust.
Major ritual impurity (janabah) prevents one from performing prayers and staying in the mosque. However, passing through the mosque without lingering is permissible.
Allah has facilitated for His servants the permissibility of Tayammum (dry ablution) in the absence of water or when unable to use it.
