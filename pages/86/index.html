(٤٥) وَٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِأَعۡدَآئِكُمۡۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ وَلِيّٗا وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ نَصِيرٗا

Allah, the Lofty and Sublime, knows your enemies better than you do, O believers. So, He informs you of them and clarifies their hostility towards you. Allah is sufficient as an ally, keeping you safe from their harm. And Allah is enough as a helper, defending you from their plots and harassment while granting you victory over them.

(٤٦) مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ هَادُواْ يُحَرِّفُونَ ٱلۡكَلِمَ عَن مَّوَاضِعِهِۦ وَيَقُولُونَ سَمِعۡنَا وَعَصَيۡنَا وَٱسۡمَعۡ غَيۡرَ مُسۡمَعٖ وَرَٰعِنَا لَيَّۢا بِأَلۡسِنَتِهِمۡ وَطَعۡنٗا فِي ٱلدِّينِۚ وَلَوۡ أَنَّهُمۡ قَالُواْ سَمِعۡنَا وَأَطَعۡنَا وَٱسۡمَعۡ وَٱنظُرۡنَا لَكَانَ خَيۡرٗا لَّهُمۡ وَأَقۡوَمَ وَلَٰكِن لَّعَنَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِكُفۡرِهِمۡ فَلَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

Among the Jews are evil people who alter the speech Allah has sent down, misconstruing it. They say to the Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم when he commands them in something: We hear what you said, and we disobey your command. They also mockingly say: Listen to what we say. You did not hear [properly]. [Muslims] received a false impression when [Jews] said, "Rā`inā" thinking that they meant "lend us your ear," when they intended imprudence by twisting [the meaning] in their language, intending to supplicate against the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم. They intended to insult the religion. If they had said, "We hear your statements and obey your commands," instead of "We hear your statements and disobey your commands"; "Listen," instead of "Listen to us, you did not hear"; and "Wait for us to understand what you say," that would have been better and more just for them than what they said at first. In [saying what is better and more just], there is excellent etiquette befitting the honor of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم. However, Allah cursed them and removed them from His mercy due to their disbelief, for they do not believe in a way that benefits them.

(٤٧) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ ءَامِنُواْ بِمَا نَزَّلۡنَا مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا مَعَكُم مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن نَّطۡمِسَ وُجُوهٗا فَنَرُدَّهَا عَلَىٰٓ أَدۡبَارِهَآ أَوۡ نَلۡعَنَهُمۡ كَمَا لَعَنَّآ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلسَّبۡتِۚ وَكَانَ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ مَفۡعُولًا

O Jews and Christians who were given the Book, believe in what We sent down to Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم which came as a confirmation for what remains with you from the revelations to Moses and Jesus before We obliterate all [enjoyment] of the senses in the faces [of disbelievers] and We make the locus of [their senses] behind them, or We expel them from the mercy of Allah just like We expelled those who violated the Sabbath by hunting after We forbid it for them. Thus, Allah transformed them into apes. Allah's command and decree are taking place unavoidably.

(٤٨) إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَغۡفِرُ أَن يُشۡرَكَ بِهِۦ وَيَغۡفِرُ مَا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَن يَشَآءُۚ وَمَن يُشۡرِكۡ بِٱللَّهِ فَقَدِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰٓ إِثۡمًا عَظِيمًا

Allah does not forgive that anything from His creation should be joined as partners with Him. Through His favor, He will overlook anything besides polytheism and disbelief in terms of acts of disobedience for whomever He wills. Or through His justice, He will punish those He wills among them according to their sins. Whoever joins others as partners with Allah has committed a terrible sin that is not forgiven for the one who dies in that state.

(٤٩) أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يُزَكُّونَ أَنفُسَهُمۚ بَلِ ٱللَّهُ يُزَكِّي مَن يَشَآءُ وَلَا يُظۡلَمُونَ فَتِيلًا

O Messenger, do you not know the affair of those full of praise for themselves and their actions? Allah alone praises and declares as righteous whomever He wills among His slaves. He is All-Aware of what is hidden in the hearts, and He will not decrease the reward of their actions at all, even if the amount of a thread on a date stone.

(٥٠) ٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ يَفۡتَرُونَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡكَذِبَۖ وَكَفَىٰ بِهِۦٓ إِثۡمٗا مُّبِينًا

Look, O Messenger, how they fabricate lies against Allah through praise for themselves. That suffices as a clear sin for their misguidance.

(٥١) أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ نَصِيبٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡجِبۡتِ وَٱلطَّٰغُوتِ وَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ أَهۡدَىٰ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ سَبِيلًا

O Messenger, do you not know and wonder at the condition of the Jews whom Allah gave a portion of knowledge? They believe in the things they worship beside Allah and flatter the polytheists by saying that they are more guided than the Companions of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم.

Points of Benefit:

Allah's sufficiency for the believers and His support render them self-sufficient from all others.
Explanation of the crimes of the Jews, such as their distortion of the words of Allah, their ill-mannered behavior towards the Prophet (peace be upon him), and their adherence to laws other than those prescribed by Allah, the Exalted.
Clarification of the danger of associating partners with Allah and disbelief, emphasizing that forgiveness is not granted to the one who dies upon them. As for sins lesser than that, they are under the will of Allah, the Exalted.
