(٥٢) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَعَنَهُمُ ٱللَّهُۖ وَمَن يَلۡعَنِ ٱللَّهُ فَلَن تَجِدَ لَهُۥ نَصِيرًا

Allah has removed from His mercy those who hold onto these corrupt beliefs. And whoever Allah removes from it will find no helper to protect him.

(٥٣) أَمۡ لَهُمۡ نَصِيبٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُلۡكِ فَإِذٗا لَّا يُؤۡتُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ نَقِيرًا

They do not have any share in the dominion. And if they did, they would not give anyone anything from it, not even the amount of a speck on the surface of a date stone.

(٥٤) أَمۡ يَحۡسُدُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ عَلَىٰ مَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦۖ فَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَآ ءَالَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُم مُّلۡكًا عَظِيمٗا

Rather, they envy Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم and his Companions because of the prophethood, faith, and strength in the land Allah has given them. Why do they envy them when previously Allah gave the offspring of Abraham the revealed Book and what We revealed to them aside from the Book? Why, when We gave them vast power over people?

(٥٥) فَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ ءَامَنَ بِهِۦ وَمِنۡهُم مَّن صَدَّ عَنۡهُۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِجَهَنَّمَ سَعِيرًا

Some People of the Book believed in what Allah revealed to Abraham صلى الله عليه وسلم and the Prophets among his offspring. Some of them turned away from belief in that. This is also their position with regard to what was sent down to the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. The Fire is a sufficient punishment for whoever disbelieves among them.

(٥٦) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِنَا سَوۡفَ نُصۡلِيهِمۡ نَارٗا كُلَّمَا نَضِجَتۡ جُلُودُهُم بَدَّلۡنَٰهُمۡ جُلُودًا غَيۡرَهَا لِيَذُوقُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابَۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَزِيزًا حَكِيمٗا

On the Day of Judgment, We will enter those who disbelieve in Our verses into a fire that will completely encircle them. Whenever their skins have been charred, We will replace them with another so that the punishment is perpetual for them. Allah is All-Mighty, and nothing can contend with Him. He is All-Wise in what He arranges and determines.

(٥٧) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ سَنُدۡخِلُهُمۡ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدٗاۖ لَّهُمۡ فِيهَآ أَزۡوَٰجٞ مُّطَهَّرَةٞۖ وَنُدۡخِلُهُمۡ ظِلّٗا ظَلِيلًا

On the Day of Judgment, We will admit into gardens with rivers flowing beneath their palaces those who believe in Allah, follow His messengers, and perform acts of obedience. They will reside in them forever. In these gardens, they will have spouses purified from all defilement, and We will admit them into expansive refreshing shade wherein there is neither heat nor cold.

(٥٨) ۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأۡمُرُكُمۡ أَن تُؤَدُّواْ ٱلۡأَمَٰنَٰتِ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهَا وَإِذَا حَكَمۡتُم بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِ أَن تَحۡكُمُواْ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ نِعِمَّا يَعِظُكُم بِهِۦٓۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ سَمِيعَۢا بَصِيرٗا

Allah commands you, O believers, to reunite everything entrusted to you with their owners. He commands that you be just when judging between people and not be biased and oppressive regarding Allah's laws. How excellent is that with which Allah reminds you and that to which He guides you in all of your conditions! Allah is All-Hearing concerning your statements and All-Seeing about your actions.

(٥٩) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱلرَّسُولَ وَأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَمۡرِ مِنكُمۡۖ فَإِن تَنَٰزَعۡتُمۡ فِي شَيۡءٖ فَرُدُّوهُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَٱلرَّسُولِ إِن كُنتُمۡ تُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۚ ذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرٞ وَأَحۡسَنُ تَأۡوِيلًا

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, obey Allah and obey His Messenger by doing what he commands and staying away from what he prohibits. Obey those entrusted with your affairs as long as they do not order you to commit disobedience [of Islamic law]. If you disagree about something, refer it to Allah's Book and the Sunnah of His Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم if you believe in Allah and the Last Day. Referring to the Book and the Sunnah is better than persisting in disagreements and holding onto mere opinions. They will also bring about the best result for you.

Points of Benefit:

One of the greatest reasons for the disbelief of the People of the Book is their envy of the believers for the blessings bestowed upon them by Allah, such as prophethood and dominance in the land.
The command to uphold noble moral virtues includes safeguarding entrusted things and ruling with justice.
The obligation of obeying the rulers as long as they do not command disobedience and resorting to the judgment of Allah and His Messenger (peace be upon him) in case of disputes as an implementation of the meaning of faith.
