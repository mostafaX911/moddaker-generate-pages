(٦٠) أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يَزۡعُمُونَ أَنَّهُمۡ ءَامَنُواْ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ مِن قَبۡلِكَ يُرِيدُونَ أَن يَتَحَاكَمُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱلطَّٰغُوتِ وَقَدۡ أُمِرُوٓاْ أَن يَكۡفُرُواْ بِهِۦۖ وَيُرِيدُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ أَن يُضِلَّهُمۡ ضَلَٰلَۢا بَعِيدٗا

Have you not seen, O Messenger, the hypocrites among the Jews contradicting one another, falsely claiming that they believe in what was sent down to you and what was sent down to the Messengers before you? They want to judge their disputes not by what Allah has legislated but by what man has fabricated. They had already been commanded to disbelieve in such things. The Devil wants to lead them far away from the truth and that they not be guided along with it.

(٦١) وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمۡ تَعَالَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ وَإِلَى ٱلرَّسُولِ رَأَيۡتَ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ يَصُدُّونَ عَنكَ صُدُودٗا

If these hypocrites are told, 'Come to the law that Allah has sent down in His Book, and to the Messenger to judge between you in your claims against one another,' you will see them, O Messenger, turning completely away from you for someone else to judge.

(٦٢) فَكَيۡفَ إِذَآ أَصَٰبَتۡهُم مُّصِيبَةُۢ بِمَا قَدَّمَتۡ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ ثُمَّ جَآءُوكَ يَحۡلِفُونَ بِٱللَّهِ إِنۡ أَرَدۡنَآ إِلَّآ إِحۡسَٰنٗا وَتَوۡفِيقًا

How will the hypocrites' situation be when calamities befall them because of the sins they committed? Then, they will come to you, O Messenger, making excuses to you, swearing by Allah: We did not intend by choosing someone else to judge between us except to bring about the best result and reconciliation for the feuding parties. They are lying about that. The best result is in resorting to the judgment of Allah's law between His slaves.

(٦٣) أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡلَمُ ٱللَّهُ مَا فِي قُلُوبِهِمۡ فَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنۡهُمۡ وَعِظۡهُمۡ وَقُل لَّهُمۡ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ قَوۡلَۢا بَلِيغٗا

Allah knows what hypocrisy and ulterior motives these people hide in their hearts. So, leave them alone, O Messenger, turn away from them, and make clear the laws of Allah through exhortation and admonishment. Tell them deeply profound, grave words that will penetrate their souls.

(٦٤) وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا لِيُطَاعَ بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَلَوۡ أَنَّهُمۡ إِذ ظَّلَمُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ جَآءُوكَ فَٱسۡتَغۡفَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱسۡتَغۡفَرَ لَهُمُ ٱلرَّسُولُ لَوَجَدُواْ ٱللَّهَ تَوَّابٗا رَّحِيمٗا

We have not sent a Messenger except for him to be followed in what he commands by Allah's will and His discretion. When they wronged themselves through disobedience, if they had come to you, O Messenger, during your lifetime, confessing what they had done, regretful, repentant, and seeking forgiveness from Allah, and you had asked for forgiveness for them, they would have found Allah forgiving and merciful with them.

(٦٥) فَلَا وَرَبِّكَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ حَتَّىٰ يُحَكِّمُوكَ فِيمَا شَجَرَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ ثُمَّ لَا يَجِدُواْ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ حَرَجٗا مِّمَّا قَضَيۡتَ وَيُسَلِّمُواْ تَسۡلِيمٗا

The situation is not as these hypocrites claim. Allah swore by His Self, may He be exalted and Exalted, that they will not truly believe until they refer judgment in every disagreement between them to the Messenger during his lifetime and to his legislation after his death. Then, they are happy with the Messenger's ruling without feeling anxiety or doubt about it, and they submit entirely to it, with external and internal submission.

Points of Benefit:

Referring to sources other than the divine law of Allah and being content with them is contradictory to faith in Allah the Exalted. Complete faith is realized only through adherence to the divine law, with the heart's contentment and outward and inward submission to what the law decrees.
One of the prominent traits of the hypocrites is their dissatisfaction with the divine law and prioritizing the rule of tyrants over the rule of Allah, the Exalted.
Encouraging the avoidance of ignorant people and deviant ideologies while emphasizing sincere advice to them and warning them of the consequences with the fear of Allah, the Exalted.
