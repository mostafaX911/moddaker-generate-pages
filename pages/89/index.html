(٦٦) وَلَوۡ أَنَّا كَتَبۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَنِ ٱقۡتُلُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡ أَوِ ٱخۡرُجُواْ مِن دِيَٰرِكُم مَّا فَعَلُوهُ إِلَّا قَلِيلٞ مِّنۡهُمۡۖ وَلَوۡ أَنَّهُمۡ فَعَلُواْ مَا يُوعَظُونَ بِهِۦ لَكَانَ خَيۡرٗا لَّهُمۡ وَأَشَدَّ تَثۡبِيتٗا

If We had obligated them to kill each other or to depart from their homes, no one would have obeyed Our command except a small number. So, they should be grateful to Allah for not imposing what would be difficult for them. If they had done what was mentioned out of obedience to Allah, it would have been better than the contrary and stronger in firming up their faith. We would have granted them an immense reward and guided them to the path that leads to Allah and His Paradise.

(٦٩) وَمَن يُطِعِ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلرَّسُولَ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنۡعَمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِم مِّنَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ‍ۧنَ وَٱلصِّدِّيقِينَ وَٱلشُّهَدَآءِ وَٱلصَّٰلِحِينَۚ وَحَسُنَ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ رَفِيقٗا

Those who obey Allah and the Messenger will be with the Prophets and truthful ones whom Allah has blessed with entry into Paradise. They are the ones who had complete faith in what the Messengers came with, and they acted upon it. They will also be with the martyrs who were killed in Allah's path and the righteous who rectified their external and internal states and, thus, their actions were righteous. What excellent companions these are in Paradise!

(٧٠) ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَضۡلُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ عَلِيمٗا

That reward mentioned is Allah's favor upon His slaves. Allah is All-Knowing regarding their circumstances and will reward each of them according to his actions.

(٧١) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ خُذُواْ حِذۡرَكُمۡ فَٱنفِرُواْ ثُبَاتٍ أَوِ ٱنفِرُواْ جَمِيعٗا

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, be on guard against your enemies by adopting the means to help you fight them. Go out to fight them one group after another and go out altogether. Each manner of going out should be done according to what is in your best interests and whatever entails defeat for your enemies.

(٧٢) وَإِنَّ مِنكُمۡ لَمَن لَّيُبَطِّئَنَّ فَإِنۡ أَصَٰبَتۡكُم مُّصِيبَةٞ قَالَ قَدۡ أَنۡعَمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيَّ إِذۡ لَمۡ أَكُن مَّعَهُمۡ شَهِيدٗا

There are among you, O Muslims, some people who procrastinate going out to fight your enemies because of their cowardice. They also try to delay others. They are hypocrites and weak in faith. If death or defeat happens among you, one of them says while happy that he is safe: Allah has favored me! Thus, I was not there to fight alongside them. Otherwise, what befell them would have happened to me.

(٧٣) وَلَئِنۡ أَصَٰبَكُمۡ فَضۡلٞ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ لَيَقُولَنَّ كَأَن لَّمۡ تَكُنۢ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَبَيۡنَهُۥ مَوَدَّةٞ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي كُنتُ مَعَهُمۡ فَأَفُوزَ فَوۡزًا عَظِيمٗا

If Allah's favor through victory and spoils of war happen to you, O Muslims, this person who stayed behind from fighting will say, as if not one of you and as if there was neither friendship nor affinity between you and him: If only I were with them in that fighting! Then, I would have had the great victory that they enjoyed.

(٧٤) ۞فَلۡيُقَٰتِلۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَشۡرُونَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ وَمَن يُقَٰتِلۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَيُقۡتَلۡ أَوۡ يَغۡلِبۡ فَسَوۡفَ نُؤۡتِيهِ أَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا

Let the truthful believers – who sell the life of this world in exchange for the Afterlife, desiring the Afterlife more than the goods of this world – fight in the path of Allah so that Allah's Word is supreme. Anyone who fights in the path of Allah so that His Word is supreme, whether he is killed as a martyr or defeats His enemy and is victorious, Allah will give him a great reward: Paradise and the pleasure of Allah.

Points of Benefit:

The performance of acts of obedience is one of the most important reasons for steadfastness in religion.
Exercising caution and vigilance by employing all means that assist in confronting the enemy, not by sitting idle or showing reluctance.
Beware of hesitating in engaging in jihad and discouraging people from it, for jihad is one of the greatest means of upholding the dignity of Muslims and preventing the dominance of the enemy over them.
