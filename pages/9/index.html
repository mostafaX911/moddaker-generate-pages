(٥٨) وَإِذۡ قُلۡنَا ٱدۡخُلُواْ هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةَ فَكُلُواْ مِنۡهَا حَيۡثُ شِئۡتُمۡ رَغَدٗا وَٱدۡخُلُواْ ٱلۡبَابَ سُجَّدٗا وَقُولُواْ حِطَّةٞ نَّغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ خَطَٰيَٰكُمۡۚ وَسَنَزِيدُ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Remember from Allah's blessings upon you: when We [Allah] told you: Enter Jerusalem and eat abundantly of the good things within from wherever you wish. And bow in humility to Allah when you enter Jerusalem, saying, 'O Our Lord, relieve us of our sins,' so that We may answer you. And We will increase the reward of those who excel in their deeds for their excellence.

(٥٩) فَبَدَّلَ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ قَوۡلًا غَيۡرَ ٱلَّذِي قِيلَ لَهُمۡ فَأَنزَلۡنَا عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ رِجۡزٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡسُقُونَ

The wrongdoers among them distorted what they were commanded to do and say. They entered dragging themselves on their backs, proclaiming: 'A grain in a hair,' in an attempt to mock Allah's command, may He be Exalted. Allah sent down punishment from the sky upon these wrongdoers for transgressing the limits of the law and violating the command.

(٦٠) ۞وَإِذِ ٱسۡتَسۡقَىٰ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوۡمِهِۦ فَقُلۡنَا ٱضۡرِب بِّعَصَاكَ ٱلۡحَجَرَۖ فَٱنفَجَرَتۡ مِنۡهُ ٱثۡنَتَا عَشۡرَةَ عَيۡنٗاۖ قَدۡ عَلِمَ كُلُّ أُنَاسٖ مَّشۡرَبَهُمۡۖ كُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ مِن رِّزۡقِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَا تَعۡثَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مُفۡسِدِينَ

Remember from Allah's blessings upon you when you were in the desert and afflicted with extreme thirst. So Moses (peace be upon him) prayed to his Lord and asked that He give you all water. Thus, We commanded him to hit the rock with his staff. When he hit it, twelve springs burst forth from it, corresponding with the number of your tribes. Water gushed from them, and We specified the place of drink designated for each tribe so that there would be no conflict between you all. And We told you: Eat and drink from what Allah provided and brought for you, without any effort or toil on your part, and do not attempt to spread corruption upon the earth.

(٦١) وَإِذۡ قُلۡتُمۡ يَٰمُوسَىٰ لَن نَّصۡبِرَ عَلَىٰ طَعَامٖ وَٰحِدٖ فَٱدۡعُ لَنَا رَبَّكَ يُخۡرِجۡ لَنَا مِمَّا تُنۢبِتُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ مِنۢ بَقۡلِهَا وَقِثَّآئِهَا وَفُومِهَا وَعَدَسِهَا وَبَصَلِهَاۖ قَالَ أَتَسۡتَبۡدِلُونَ ٱلَّذِي هُوَ أَدۡنَىٰ بِٱلَّذِي هُوَ خَيۡرٌۚ ٱهۡبِطُواْ مِصۡرٗا فَإِنَّ لَكُم مَّا سَأَلۡتُمۡۗ وَضُرِبَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلذِّلَّةُ وَٱلۡمَسۡكَنَةُ وَبَآءُو بِغَضَبٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِۗ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ يَكۡفُرُونَ بِ‍َٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَقۡتُلُونَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ‍ۧنَ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّۗ ذَٰلِكَ بِمَا عَصَواْ وَّكَانُواْ يَعۡتَدُونَ

And remember the time you expressed ungratefulness for the blessings of your Lord, becoming bored of the Manna and Salwa Allah sent down to you, declaring: We will not continue to endure eating the one type of unchanging food. So you asked Moses (peace be upon him) to ask Allah to produce plants from the earth to eat consisting of herbs, vegetables, cucumbers (specifically referring to the Armenian cucumber/snake melon), grains, lentils, and onions. Moses condemned their request, telling them: Would you exchange the nobler and better Manna and Salwa which came to you without effort or toil, for that which is inferior and worse? Go down from this land to any town, and you will find what you are asking for within its fields and markets. By following their whims and repeatedly turning away from what Allah chose for them, they were afflicted with constant disgrace, poverty, and misery. They earned Allah's wrath because they turned away from His religion, disbelieved in His signs, and unjustly and brutally killed His prophets – all because they rebelled against Allah and kept transgressing His limits.

Points of Benefit:

Anyone who tampers with the texts of the legislation and distorts them has a resemblance to the Jews, and he is threatened with punishment from Allah, the Exalted.
The greatness of Allah's favor upon the Israelites, and in contrast to that, the intensity of their rejection, stubbornness, and turning away from Allah and His legislation.
From the misfortune of sin and transgressing the limits of Allah, the Exalted, is the humiliation and degradation that befall a person and the dominance of enemies over him.
