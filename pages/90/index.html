(٧٥) وَمَا لَكُمۡ لَا تُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡمُسۡتَضۡعَفِينَ مِنَ ٱلرِّجَالِ وَٱلنِّسَآءِ وَٱلۡوِلۡدَٰنِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَآ أَخۡرِجۡنَا مِنۡ هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِ ٱلظَّالِمِ أَهۡلُهَا وَٱجۡعَل لَّنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ وَلِيّٗا وَٱجۡعَل لَّنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ نَصِيرًا

What is preventing you, O believers, from fighting in the path of Allah to raise His Word high and rescue the weak men, women, and children who call to Allah, saying: O Lord of ours! Take us out of Mecca because of the oppression of its people who associate partners with Allah and are hostile toward His slaves. Appoint us someone near to You with whom we can entrust leadership and protection of our affairs and appoint a helper to defend us from harm.

(٧٦) ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱلطَّٰغُوتِ فَقَٰتِلُوٓاْ أَوۡلِيَآءَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۖ إِنَّ كَيۡدَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ كَانَ ضَعِيفًا

The truthful believers fight in the path of Allah to raise His Word high. On the other hand, the disbelievers fight in the path and way of their deities. So, fight the supporters of the Devil. If you fight them, you will defeat them because the Devil's strategy is weak, and it cannot harm those who place their complete trust in Allah, the Exalted.

(٧٧) أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ قِيلَ لَهُمۡ كُفُّوٓاْ أَيۡدِيَكُمۡ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ فَلَمَّا كُتِبَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقِتَالُ إِذَا فَرِيقٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ يَخۡشَوۡنَ ٱلنَّاسَ كَخَشۡيَةِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡ أَشَدَّ خَشۡيَةٗۚ وَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَا لِمَ كَتَبۡتَ عَلَيۡنَا ٱلۡقِتَالَ لَوۡلَآ أَخَّرۡتَنَآ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ قَرِيبٖۗ قُلۡ مَتَٰعُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا قَلِيلٞ وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرٞ لِّمَنِ ٱتَّقَىٰ وَلَا تُظۡلَمُونَ فَتِيلًا

O Messenger, do you not know about the condition of some of your Companions who asked that striving in Allah's path be ordained for them, but they were told: Hold back from fighting, establish the prayer, and give Zakat (obligatory alms). That was before fighting in Allah's path was obligated. Islam became strong when they migrated to Medina, and fighting was obligated. Some of them found it difficult and began fearing people as they should fear Allah or even more so. They said: O our Lord! Why did You obligate fighting for us? Why did You not delay it for a while so we could enjoy the worldly life? Say to them, O Messenger: The enjoyment of the world, no matter how much, is insignificant and vanishing. Meanwhile, the Hereafter is better for the one who fears Allah, the Exalted, because its delights are everlasting. Your righteous deeds will not be reduced, not even the amount of the thread on a date stone.

(٧٨) أَيۡنَمَا تَكُونُواْ يُدۡرِككُّمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ وَلَوۡ كُنتُمۡ فِي بُرُوجٖ مُّشَيَّدَةٖۗ وَإِن تُصِبۡهُمۡ حَسَنَةٞ يَقُولُواْ هَٰذِهِۦ مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَإِن تُصِبۡهُمۡ سَيِّئَةٞ يَقُولُواْ هَٰذِهِۦ مِنۡ عِندِكَۚ قُلۡ كُلّٞ مِّنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِۖ فَمَالِ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ لَا يَكَادُونَ يَفۡقَهُونَ حَدِيثٗا

At any time and place, death will surely come to you when you reach your prescribed life term, even if you are in guarded palaces far from battle. And when these hypocrites receive something pleasing, such as a child or wealth, they say: This is from Allah. Yet when they face difficulty concerning their children or wealth, they attribute a bad sign and misfortune to the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم and say: This evil is because of you. Say, O Messenger, in response to these people: Everything, good or evil, occurs by Allah's decree and decision. What is the matter with these people who say such things and hardly understand anything you tell them?

(٧٩) مَّآ أَصَابَكَ مِنۡ حَسَنَةٖ فَمِنَ ٱللَّهِۖ وَمَآ أَصَابَكَ مِن سَيِّئَةٖ فَمِن نَّفۡسِكَۚ وَأَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ لِلنَّاسِ رَسُولٗاۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ شَهِيدٗا

O child of Adam: Any good that reaches you, regarding your wealth and children, is from Allah's favor upon you. Whatever evil reaches you, regarding your wealth and children, is from yourself because of the disobedient acts you committed. We have sent you, O Prophet, as a messenger from Allah to all people to communicate the message of your Lord. Allah is enough of a witness to the truth of what you communicate from Him because of the evidence and clear proofs He has given you.

Points of Benefit:

The obligation of fighting to uphold the word of Allah and support the oppressed, and the condemnation of fear, cowardice, and resistance against the judgments of Allah.
The Hereafter is better than this world and all its possessions and pleasures for those who fear Allah and act in obedience to Him.
All good and evil is by the decree of Allah, and Allah may test His servants with some adversity in this world for reasons, including their sins and transgressions.
