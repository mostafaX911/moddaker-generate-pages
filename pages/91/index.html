(٨٠) مَّن يُطِعِ ٱلرَّسُولَ فَقَدۡ أَطَاعَ ٱللَّهَۖ وَمَن تَوَلَّىٰ فَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَفِيظٗا

Whoever obeys the Messenger by complying with his commands and avoiding what he prohibits has fulfilled Allah's command. Whoever turns away from obeying you, O Messenger, do not feel sad about him because I have not sent you as a sentinel to watch over his actions. We are the Ones Who will watch his actions and take him into account.

(٨١) وَيَقُولُونَ طَاعَةٞ فَإِذَا بَرَزُواْ مِنۡ عِندِكَ بَيَّتَ طَآئِفَةٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ غَيۡرَ ٱلَّذِي تَقُولُۖ وَٱللَّهُ يَكۡتُبُ مَا يُبَيِّتُونَۖ فَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنۡهُمۡ وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ وَكِيلًا

The hypocrites say to you with their mouths: We will obey your commands and comply with them. Then, when they leave you, they secretly plan the opposite of what they said to you. Allah knows what they are planning, and He will requite them for their deception. So, do not pay any attention to them, as they will not harm you at all. Consign your affairs to Allah and rely on Him alone. Allah suffices you as one to entrust and rely upon.

(٨٢) أَفَلَا يَتَدَبَّرُونَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَۚ وَلَوۡ كَانَ مِنۡ عِندِ غَيۡرِ ٱللَّهِ لَوَجَدُواْ فِيهِ ٱخۡتِلَٰفٗا كَثِيرٗا

Why do these people not reflect on the Quran and study it until it is proven to them that no contradictions or inconsistency can be found in it!? They should do so until they know the truth with which you arrived. Had it been from anyone other than Allah, the Exalted, indeed, they would have found inconsistencies in its rulings and many contradictions in its meanings.

(٨٣) وَإِذَا جَآءَهُمۡ أَمۡرٞ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡنِ أَوِ ٱلۡخَوۡفِ أَذَاعُواْ بِهِۦۖ وَلَوۡ رَدُّوهُ إِلَى ٱلرَّسُولِ وَإِلَىٰٓ أُوْلِي ٱلۡأَمۡرِ مِنۡهُمۡ لَعَلِمَهُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَنۢبِطُونَهُۥ مِنۡهُمۡۗ وَلَوۡلَا فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَرَحۡمَتُهُۥ لَٱتَّبَعۡتُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا

Whenever these hypocrites happen upon a matter dealing with the safety of Muslims, their private affairs, or issues of fear and sadness, they rush to spread it. If they had waited and referred the matter to Allah's Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم and to the possessors of critical thinking, knowledge, and counsel, they would have understood and investigated what should be done concerning spreading such news or concealing it. If it were not for Allah's favoring you with Islam and His having mercy on you with the Quran, O believers, and thus, protecting you from what these hypocrites were afflicted with, all except a few of you would have followed the whispers of the Devil.

(٨٤) فَقَٰتِلۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُكَلَّفُ إِلَّا نَفۡسَكَۚ وَحَرِّضِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَۖ عَسَى ٱللَّهُ أَن يَكُفَّ بَأۡسَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْۚ وَٱللَّهُ أَشَدُّ بَأۡسٗا وَأَشَدُّ تَنكِيلٗا

Fight, O Prophet, in Allah's path to raise His Word high. You will not be questioned about or held accountable for others. You are only responsible for prompting yourself to fight, exhorting, and urging the believers to do so. Perhaps Allah will repel the might of the disbelievers by your fighting. Allah is stronger in might and more severe in punishment.

(٨٥) مَّن يَشۡفَعۡ شَفَٰعَةً حَسَنَةٗ يَكُن لَّهُۥ نَصِيبٞ مِّنۡهَاۖ وَمَن يَشۡفَعۡ شَفَٰعَةٗ سَيِّئَةٗ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كِفۡلٞ مِّنۡهَاۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ مُّقِيتٗا

Whoever strives to direct another person towards some evil will receive a share of the sin. Allah is a witness to everything a person does, and He will requite them for it. So, whoever among you is a vehicle for achieving some good will receive a share and a portion of it. Whoever is a vehicle for achieving evil, some part of it will reach him.

(٨٦) وَإِذَا حُيِّيتُم بِتَحِيَّةٖ فَحَيُّواْ بِأَحۡسَنَ مِنۡهَآ أَوۡ رُدُّوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٍ حَسِيبًا

When anyone offers a greeting of peace to you, then respond to him with something better than what he offered or return a similar greeting. To respond with something better is more virtuous. Allah keeps watch over what you do, and He will requite each person for his actions.

Points of Benefit:

Reflecting upon the Quran instills certainty that it is a revelation from Allah, as evidenced by its flawless composition, and it reveals the profound wisdom contained in its commandments.
It is not permissible to disseminate news that disturbs the security of the believers or spreads fear among their ranks.
Speaking about the issues of Muslims and public affairs related to them should be done by people of knowledge and those in authority among them.
The permissibility of righteous intercession that involves no sin or violation of people's rights and the prohibition of any intercession that involves sin or transgression.
