(٨٧) ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ لَيَجۡمَعَنَّكُمۡ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهِۗ وَمَنۡ أَصۡدَقُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ حَدِيثٗا

Allah—there is nothing rightfully worshiped except Him. Verily, He will gather together on the Day of Resurrection, the first and last of you, a day about which there is no doubt. This is to recompense you for your actions. There is no one more truthful in speech than Allah.

(٨٨) ۞فَمَا لَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ فِئَتَيۡنِ وَٱللَّهُ أَرۡكَسَهُم بِمَا كَسَبُوٓاْۚ أَتُرِيدُونَ أَن تَهۡدُواْ مَنۡ أَضَلَّ ٱللَّهُۖ وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَلَن تَجِدَ لَهُۥ سَبِيلٗا

What is the matter with you, O believers, that you have become two groups differing over how to deal with the hypocrites? One group wants to fight against them because of their disbelief, and another group wants to leave them because of their belief. You should not differ over them, for Allah has returned them to disbelief and misguidance because of their actions. Do you wish to guide those Allah has not guided to the truth? Whoever Allah misguides, you will never find a road toward guidance for him.

(٨٩) وَدُّواْ لَوۡ تَكۡفُرُونَ كَمَا كَفَرُواْ فَتَكُونُونَ سَوَآءٗۖ فَلَا تَتَّخِذُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءَ حَتَّىٰ يُهَاجِرُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ فَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَخُذُوهُمۡ وَٱقۡتُلُوهُمۡ حَيۡثُ وَجَدتُّمُوهُمۡۖ وَلَا تَتَّخِذُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ وَلِيّٗا وَلَا نَصِيرًا

The hypocrites wish that you would disbelieve in what was sent down to you as they have disbelieved. They want you to be equal with them in disbelief. Due to their animosity, do not take them as allies until they migrate in Allah's path from the land of polytheism to the land of Islam- as proof of their belief. If they turn away and persist upon their condition, seize and kill them wherever you find them. Do not take any allies from among them to support you with your affairs, nor anyone to assist you against your enemies.

(٩٠) إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ يَصِلُونَ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِۢ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَبَيۡنَهُم مِّيثَٰقٌ أَوۡ جَآءُوكُمۡ حَصِرَتۡ صُدُورُهُمۡ أَن يُقَٰتِلُوكُمۡ أَوۡ يُقَٰتِلُواْ قَوۡمَهُمۡۚ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَسَلَّطَهُمۡ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فَلَقَٰتَلُوكُمۡۚ فَإِنِ ٱعۡتَزَلُوكُمۡ فَلَمۡ يُقَٰتِلُوكُمۡ وَأَلۡقَوۡاْ إِلَيۡكُمُ ٱلسَّلَمَ فَمَا جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سَبِيلٗا

Except for those who reach people with whom you have a peace treaty or those who come to you reluctant to fight against you or their people. If Allah wanted, He could have made them stronger than you, and they would have fought you. Therefore, accept Allah's protection and do not set out to kill or capture them. If they withdraw without fighting and yield to you peacefully, giving up the fight against you, then Allah has not provided you a way to fight or take them as prisoners.

(٩١) سَتَجِدُونَ ءَاخَرِينَ يُرِيدُونَ أَن يَأۡمَنُوكُمۡ وَيَأۡمَنُواْ قَوۡمَهُمۡ كُلَّ مَا رُدُّوٓاْ إِلَى ٱلۡفِتۡنَةِ أُرۡكِسُواْ فِيهَاۚ فَإِن لَّمۡ يَعۡتَزِلُوكُمۡ وَيُلۡقُوٓاْ إِلَيۡكُمُ ٱلسَّلَمَ وَيَكُفُّوٓاْ أَيۡدِيَهُمۡ فَخُذُوهُمۡ وَٱقۡتُلُوهُمۡ حَيۡثُ ثَقِفۡتُمُوهُمۡۚ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكُمۡ جَعَلۡنَا لَكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سُلۡطَٰنٗا مُّبِينٗا

You, O believers, will find another group of hypocrites exhibiting faith to you to protect themselves. When they return to their people who are disbelievers, they exhibit disbelief to them in order to be safe from them as well. They fall head first into it whenever they are invited to disbelief in Allah and polytheism. If these people do not give up fighting you and come to you peacefully, restraining themselves, then seize and kill them wherever you find them. We have provided you with clear proof for seizing and fighting the people fitting this description because of their betrayal and deception.

Points of Benefit:

The concealment of the true nature of some hypocrites has led to disagreements among the believers regarding how to deal with them.
Clarifying the manner of dealing with hypocrites according to their conditions and the dictates of benefit in handling them.
Islam's justice lies in withholding harm from those who have not committed transgressive harm, especially from the hypocrites.
Jihad in the path of Allah exposes the hypocrites due to their reluctance and the burden of their excuses.
