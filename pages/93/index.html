(٩٢) وَمَا كَانَ لِمُؤۡمِنٍ أَن يَقۡتُلَ مُؤۡمِنًا إِلَّا خَطَ‍ٔٗاۚ وَمَن قَتَلَ مُؤۡمِنًا خَطَ‍ٔٗا فَتَحۡرِيرُ رَقَبَةٖ مُّؤۡمِنَةٖ وَدِيَةٞ مُّسَلَّمَةٌ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن يَصَّدَّقُواْۚ فَإِن كَانَ مِن قَوۡمٍ عَدُوّٖ لَّكُمۡ وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنٞ فَتَحۡرِيرُ رَقَبَةٖ مُّؤۡمِنَةٖۖ وَإِن كَانَ مِن قَوۡمِۢ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَبَيۡنَهُم مِّيثَٰقٞ فَدِيَةٞ مُّسَلَّمَةٌ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ وَتَحۡرِيرُ رَقَبَةٖ مُّؤۡمِنَةٖۖ فَمَن لَّمۡ يَجِدۡ فَصِيَامُ شَهۡرَيۡنِ مُتَتَابِعَيۡنِ تَوۡبَةٗ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمٗا

A believer ought not to kill another believer unless by mistake. Whoever kills a believer by mistake must free a believing slave as an expiation for his actions. The inheritors of the killer are responsible for paying blood money to the inheritors of the victim unless they forgo that right. If the victim was a believer and belonged to a people at war with you, the killer must free a believing slave, and no blood money is owed. Suppose the victim was not a believer and he belonged to a people living in an Islamic state under a covenant of protection. In that case, the inheritors of the killer are responsible for paying blood money to the inheritors of the victim, and the killer must free a believing slave as an expiation for his action. If he cannot find anyone to free or afford to pay the cost, he must fast continuously for two consecutive months so that Allah may forgive him for what he has done. Allah is All-Knowing about the actions and intentions of His slaves, and He is All-Wise in His legislation and planning.

(٩٣) وَمَن يَقۡتُلۡ مُؤۡمِنٗا مُّتَعَمِّدٗا فَجَزَآؤُهُۥ جَهَنَّمُ خَٰلِدٗا فِيهَا وَغَضِبَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِ وَلَعَنَهُۥ وَأَعَدَّ لَهُۥ عَذَابًا عَظِيمٗا

Whoever intentionally kills a believer without right, his requital is to enter Hell eternally. Allah's anger is upon him. He removes His mercy from him and prepares a severe punishment for him because of his commission of a heinous crime.

(٩٤) يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا ضَرَبۡتُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَتَبَيَّنُواْ وَلَا تَقُولُواْ لِمَنۡ أَلۡقَىٰٓ إِلَيۡكُمُ ٱلسَّلَٰمَ لَسۡتَ مُؤۡمِنٗا تَبۡتَغُونَ عَرَضَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا فَعِندَ ٱللَّهِ مَغَانِمُ كَثِيرَةٞۚ كَذَٰلِكَ كُنتُم مِّن قَبۡلُ فَمَنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فَتَبَيَّنُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٗا

O you who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger, when you go out to fight in Allah's path, be sure about the affair of those you fight. Do not tell someone who proves he is a believer: You are not a believer! You only displayed Islam out of fear for your blood and wealth. Then, you kill him in pursuit of worldly life's trivial, material goods, such as the spoils of war. Allah possesses abundant spoils that are greater than this. In the past, you were like this person who hid his faith from his people. Then Allah favored you with Islam and protected your blood. So be sure, as none of your actions are hidden from Allah. Even if it is minuscule, He will recompense you for it.

Points of Benefit:

The Quran emphasizes the sanctity of a believer's life, prohibiting its violation and stipulating the most severe punishments for such transgressions.
It is a belief of the people of Sunnah and the community that a believer who commits a major sin leading to punishment in the Fire will not remain there forever. Instead, they will be punished for a prolonged period and then be taken out by the mercy of Allah, the Exalted.
The obligation of verification and clarification in jihad and the avoidance of hastiness in passing judgment on people so as not to wrong the innocent.
