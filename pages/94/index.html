(٩٥) لَّا يَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡقَٰعِدُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ غَيۡرُ أُوْلِي ٱلضَّرَرِ وَٱلۡمُجَٰهِدُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ بِأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَأَنفُسِهِمۡۚ فَضَّلَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُجَٰهِدِينَ بِأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَأَنفُسِهِمۡ عَلَى ٱلۡقَٰعِدِينَ دَرَجَةٗۚ وَكُلّٗا وَعَدَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۚ وَفَضَّلَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُجَٰهِدِينَ عَلَى ٱلۡقَٰعِدِينَ أَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا

Those believers who remain behind from fighting in Allah's path – besides those who have an excuse, such as sickness and the blind – are not equal to those who fight in Allah's path, giving their wealth and lives. Allah has favored those who fight in His path a rank above those who remain behind. Those who fight and stay behind with a valid excuse shall receive the reward they deserve. Allah has preferred those who fight over those who stay behind by giving them a greater reward.

(٩٦) دَرَجَٰتٖ مِّنۡهُ وَمَغۡفِرَةٗ وَرَحۡمَةٗۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمًا

This reward is in levels, some above others, with forgiveness of their sins and mercy. Allah is Ever-Forgiving and Merciful with them.

(٩٧) إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ تَوَفَّىٰهُمُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ ظَالِمِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ قَالُواْ فِيمَ كُنتُمۡۖ قَالُواْ كُنَّا مُسۡتَضۡعَفِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضُۚ قَالُوٓاْ أَلَمۡ تَكُنۡ أَرۡضُ ٱللَّهِ وَٰسِعَةٗ فَتُهَاجِرُواْ فِيهَاۚ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ مَأۡوَىٰهُمۡ جَهَنَّمُۖ وَسَآءَتۡ مَصِيرًا

When angels take the souls of those who wronged themselves by not migrating from the land of disbelief to the land of Islam, at the time of grasping hold of their souls, the angels will say in rebuke of them: What condition were you in and in what way were you distinguished from the polytheists? They will reply, presenting the excuse: We were weak, having no power nor might to defend ourselves. The angels will again rebuke them, saying: Were the lands of Allah not spacious enough for you to depart for in order to secure your religion and lives from humiliation and domination? The Fire is the abode in which those who do not migrate will settle down. What an evil place of return it will be for them!

(٩٨) إِلَّا ٱلۡمُسۡتَضۡعَفِينَ مِنَ ٱلرِّجَالِ وَٱلنِّسَآءِ وَٱلۡوِلۡدَٰنِ لَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ حِيلَةٗ وَلَا يَهۡتَدُونَ سَبِيلٗا

Exempted from this warning are the weak men, women, and children with an excuse. They had no power to defend themselves from oppression and subjugation. They could not discover a path to free themselves from their subjugation. Perhaps Allah will pardon such people through His mercy and benevolence. Allah is Ever-Pardoning of His slaves and Oft-Forgiving of whoever repents among them.

(٩٩) فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ عَسَى ٱللَّهُ أَن يَعۡفُوَ عَنۡهُمۡۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَفُوًّا غَفُورٗا

Exempted from this warning are the weak men, women, and children with an excuse. They had no power to defend themselves from oppression and subjugation. They could not discover a path to free themselves from their subjugation. Perhaps Allah will pardon such people through His mercy and benevolence. Allah is Ever-Pardoning of His slaves and Oft-Forgiving of whoever repents among them.

After mentioning the threat of punishment for abandoning the hijra (emigration from Mecca to Medina) despite having the capability to do so, Allah encourages them to do it, stating:

(١٠٠) ۞وَمَن يُهَاجِرۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ يَجِدۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مُرَٰغَمٗا كَثِيرٗا وَسَعَةٗۚ وَمَن يَخۡرُجۡ مِنۢ بَيۡتِهِۦ مُهَاجِرًا إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ ثُمَّ يُدۡرِكۡهُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ فَقَدۡ وَقَعَ أَجۡرُهُۥ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا

Whoever emigrates from the land of disbelief to the land of Islam, seeking Allah's pleasure, will find a place altered and different than the land they left behind. In this new land, they will acquire honor and ample provision. Whoever leaves his home as a migrant to Allah and His Messenger and death arrives before reaching the land he was migrating to, his reward is certain with Allah. It will not be held against him if he was unable to reach the land he was emigrating to. Allah is Ever-Forgiving and Merciful to whoever repents among His slaves.

(١٠١) وَإِذَا ضَرَبۡتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَلَيۡسَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ جُنَاحٌ أَن تَقۡصُرُواْ مِنَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ إِنۡ خِفۡتُمۡ أَن يَفۡتِنَكُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ كَانُواْ لَكُمۡ عَدُوّٗا مُّبِينٗا

When you travel in the land, there is no sin in shortening the four-unit prayer to a two-unit prayer if you fear harm from disbelievers will befall you. Indeed, the disbelievers have open, apparent animosity towards you. The permissibility of shortening the prayer while traveling in a state of safety is established by authentic Sunnah.

Points of Benefit:

The virtue of jihad in the path of Allah and the immense reward for the Mujahideen, as Allah has promised them high ranks in Paradise that no others will attain.
Those with valid excuses are exempted from the obligation of jihad, though they may still receive rewards if their intentions are sincere.
The virtue of emigrating to the lands of Islam and its obligation upon the capable, especially if they fear for their religion in their own country.
The permissibility of shortening the prayer during travel.
