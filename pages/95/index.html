(١٠٢) وَإِذَا كُنتَ فِيهِمۡ فَأَقَمۡتَ لَهُمُ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ فَلۡتَقُمۡ طَآئِفَةٞ مِّنۡهُم مَّعَكَ وَلۡيَأۡخُذُوٓاْ أَسۡلِحَتَهُمۡۖ فَإِذَا سَجَدُواْ فَلۡيَكُونُواْ مِن وَرَآئِكُمۡ وَلۡتَأۡتِ طَآئِفَةٌ أُخۡرَىٰ لَمۡ يُصَلُّواْ فَلۡيُصَلُّواْ مَعَكَ وَلۡيَأۡخُذُواْ حِذۡرَهُمۡ وَأَسۡلِحَتَهُمۡۗ وَدَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَوۡ تَغۡفُلُونَ عَنۡ أَسۡلِحَتِكُمۡ وَأَمۡتِعَتِكُمۡ فَيَمِيلُونَ عَلَيۡكُم مَّيۡلَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗۚ وَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِن كَانَ بِكُمۡ أَذٗى مِّن مَّطَرٍ أَوۡ كُنتُم مَّرۡضَىٰٓ أَن تَضَعُوٓاْ أَسۡلِحَتَكُمۡۖ وَخُذُواْ حِذۡرَكُمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَعَدَّ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَذَابٗا مُّهِينٗا

When you, O Messenger, are in the army at the time of fighting the enemy, and you wish to pray with them, then divide the army into two groups: one group should stand and pray with you, keeping their weapons with them during the prayer; while the other group guards you. When the first group has completed one unit of prayer with the leader, they must complete the rest on their own. So, when they finish praying, they will be behind you, facing the enemy. After the first group finishes one unit of prayer, the group keeping guard who has not prayed should come and pray one unit of prayer with the leader. When the leader completes the prayer by sending peace and mercy, they should complete the remainder of it. They should take precautions with their enemy and keep their weapons with them. The disbelievers wish that you become unmindful of your weapons and supplies when you pray, so they can all attack you at once while you are heedless. There is no sin on you to not carry your weapons when harm strikes because of heavy rain, illness, and the like. However, be cautious with the enemy as much as possible. Allah has prepared for disbelievers a humiliating punishment.

(١٠٣) فَإِذَا قَضَيۡتُمُ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ فَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ قِيَٰمٗا وَقُعُودٗا وَعَلَىٰ جُنُوبِكُمۡۚ فَإِذَا ٱطۡمَأۡنَنتُمۡ فَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَۚ إِنَّ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ كَانَتۡ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ كِتَٰبٗا مَّوۡقُوتٗا

When you, O believers, have finished the prayer, remember Allah in all circumstances by exalting Him, thanking Him, and declaring His Oneness while standing, sitting, or lying on your sides. When fear subsides, and you are safe, establish the prayer with its pillars, obligations, and recommended acts, as commanded of you. Prayer is an obligation for believers restricted to particular times, and delaying them without an excuse is not permissible. This applies while at home. However, you are allowed to combine and shorten prayers while traveling.

(١٠٤) وَلَا تَهِنُواْ فِي ٱبۡتِغَآءِ ٱلۡقَوۡمِۖ إِن تَكُونُواْ تَأۡلَمُونَ فَإِنَّهُمۡ يَأۡلَمُونَ كَمَا تَأۡلَمُونَۖ وَتَرۡجُونَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا يَرۡجُونَۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمًا

Do not slacken or become idle, O believers, in pursuit of your enemy among the Disbelievers. If you suffer because of the death and injuries that afflict you, then they, too, are suffering just like you, and what afflicts you also afflicts them. Their patience cannot be greater than yours because you have hope in Allah's reward, help, and approval, which they have no hope for. Allah is All-Knowing about the conditions of His slaves and All-Wise in His planning and legislation.

(١٠٥) إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ لِتَحۡكُمَ بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِمَآ أَرَىٰكَ ٱللَّهُۚ وَلَا تَكُن لِّلۡخَآئِنِينَ خَصِيمٗا

I have sent down the Quran to you, O Messenger, containing the truth so that you can settle all matters between the people following what Allah has taught and inspired you with, not with your inclinations and thoughts. Do not defend those who betray their own selves and their promises, deterring someone from seeking his rights from them.

Points of Benefit:

The recommendation of the (Prayer of Fear), along with an explanation of its rulings and description.
Taking means is required in all circumstances, even when a person is engaged in worship.
The legitimacy of constantly remembering Allah in all situations, as it is the life of the hearts and a means to their tranquility.
The prohibition of weakness and laziness in times of confronting the enemy, and the command to persevere in fighting against them.
