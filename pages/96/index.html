(١٠٦) وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرِ ٱللَّهَۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا

Seek forgiveness and pardon from Allah. Allah is Oft-Forgiving and Merciful towards His slaves who repent to Him.

(١٠٧) وَلَا تُجَٰدِلۡ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخۡتَانُونَ أَنفُسَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ مَن كَانَ خَوَّانًا أَثِيمٗا

Do not argue on behalf of a person who betrays and exaggerates in concealing his deception. Allah does not like these deceitful liars.

(١٠٨) يَسۡتَخۡفُونَ مِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَا يَسۡتَخۡفُونَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ وَهُوَ مَعَهُمۡ إِذۡ يُبَيِّتُونَ مَا لَا يَرۡضَىٰ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِمَا يَعۡمَلُونَ مُحِيطًا

When perpetrating acts of disobedience, they hide from people out of fear and shame, but they cannot hide from Allah. He is with them through His knowledge of them. Nothing about them is hidden from Him when they secretly plan to say things He is not pleased with, such as defending a sinner or falsely accusing an innocent person. Allah is well aware of what they do secretly and openly. Nothing is hidden from Him, and He will requite them for their actions.

(١٠٩) هَٰٓأَنتُمۡ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ جَٰدَلۡتُمۡ عَنۡهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا فَمَن يُجَٰدِلُ ٱللَّهَ عَنۡهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ أَم مَّن يَكُونُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَكِيلٗا

Here you are, O people concerned with these people who commit an offense, arguing on their behalf regarding worldly life to prove their innocence and defend them from punishment. Who will argue on their behalf with Allah on the Day of Resurrection while He knows their true status? Who will be their representative on that day? Without a doubt, no one will be able to do that.

(١١٠) وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ سُوٓءًا أَوۡ يَظۡلِمۡ نَفۡسَهُۥ ثُمَّ يَسۡتَغۡفِرِ ٱللَّهَ يَجِدِ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا

Whoever commits an evil act or wrongs himself by perpetrating disobedience then seeks forgiveness from Allah, admitting his sin, remorseful over it, abandoning it- he will find Allah Ever-Forgiving towards his sins and merciful with him.

(١١١) وَمَن يَكۡسِبۡ إِثۡمٗا فَإِنَّمَا يَكۡسِبُهُۥ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِۦۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمٗا

The punishment for whoever commits a minor or major sin is on him alone. He cannot transfer it to someone else. Allah is All-Knowing about the slaves' actions and All-Wise in His planning and legislation.

(١١٢) وَمَن يَكۡسِبۡ خَطِيٓ‍َٔةً أَوۡ إِثۡمٗا ثُمَّ يَرۡمِ بِهِۦ بَرِيٓ‍ٔٗا فَقَدِ ٱحۡتَمَلَ بُهۡتَٰنٗا وَإِثۡمٗا مُّبِينٗا

If a person makes a mistake or commits a sin intentionally, then accuses an innocent person of that sin, he has taken a grave lie and a clear sin upon himself.

(١١٣) وَلَوۡلَا فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكَ وَرَحۡمَتُهُۥ لَهَمَّت طَّآئِفَةٞ مِّنۡهُمۡ أَن يُضِلُّوكَ وَمَا يُضِلُّونَ إِلَّآ أَنفُسَهُمۡۖ وَمَا يَضُرُّونَكَ مِن شَيۡءٖۚ وَأَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَعَلَّمَكَ مَا لَمۡ تَكُن تَعۡلَمُۚ وَكَانَ فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكَ عَظِيمٗا

If not for Allah's favor of protecting you, O Messenger, from this group of people who deceive themselves and their determination to mislead you about the truth, you would have passed an unfair judgment. In reality, they only mislead themselves. The punishment for what they perpetrated in attempting to misdirect [blame] is returning against them. Allah sent down the Quran and Sunna to you, and He taught you the right guidance and light, which you did not know beforehand. Allah's favor upon you, in the form of prophethood and protection, is tremendous.

Points of Benefit:

The prohibition of defending and supporting those engaged in falsehood, as it constitutes collaboration in sin and aggression.
It is incumbent upon the true believer that their fear, reverence, and modesty towards Allah surpass all others among the people.
The vastness of Allah's mercy and forgiveness for those who have wronged themselves, no matter the extent of their wrongdoing, if they sincerely repent and turn away from their sins.
Warning against accusing the innocent and falsely attributing to them what they did not do; the one who engages in such actions falls into the gravest falsehood and sin.
