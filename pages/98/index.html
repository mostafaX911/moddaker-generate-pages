After mentioning the recompense for those who followed the Devil, Allah notes the reward of those who followed the messengers, stating:

(١٢٢) وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ سَنُدۡخِلُهُمۡ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدٗاۖ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقّٗاۚ وَمَنۡ أَصۡدَقُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ قِيلٗا

We will admit those who have faith in Allah and do righteous acts that bring them close to Him into gardens with streams flowing beneath its palaces, where they will live eternally. This is a promise from Allah, and His promise is true. He does not break promises, and no one is truer than Allah in terms of speech.

(١٢٣) لَّيۡسَ بِأَمَانِيِّكُمۡ وَلَآ أَمَانِيِّ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِۗ مَن يَعۡمَلۡ سُوٓءٗا يُجۡزَ بِهِۦ وَلَا يَجِدۡ لَهُۥ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَلِيّٗا وَلَا نَصِيرٗا

The issue of salvation and success, O Muslims, is not dependent on what you or the People of the Book hope and wish for. Rather, it is dependent on actions. Whoever among you does an evil act will be repaid for it on the Day of Judgment. He will not find any protector to bring any benefit nor any helper to repel harm from him.

(١٢٤) وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ مِن ذَكَرٍ أَوۡ أُنثَىٰ وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنٞ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يَدۡخُلُونَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ وَلَا يُظۡلَمُونَ نَقِيرٗا

Any person, male or female, who does righteous actions and is a true believer in Allah, the Exalted, thereby combining faith and action, will enter Paradise. The reward of their efforts will not be reduced in the least, not even to the extent of a speck on a date stone.

(١٢٥) وَمَنۡ أَحۡسَنُ دِينٗا مِّمَّنۡ أَسۡلَمَ وَجۡهَهُۥ لِلَّهِ وَهُوَ مُحۡسِنٞ وَٱتَّبَعَ مِلَّةَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ حَنِيفٗاۗ وَٱتَّخَذَ ٱللَّهُ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ خَلِيلٗا

No one is more excellent in religion than the one who surrenders to Allah outwardly and inwardly, sincere in his intentions for His sake, excelling in his actions by following what He legislated and following the religion of Abraham, which forms the basis of the religion of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. He turns away from polytheism and disbelief towards monotheism and faith. Allah chose His Prophet Abraham (peace be upon him) for complete love from among all of His creation.

(١٢٦) وَلِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ مُّحِيطٗا

To Allah alone belongs the dominion of everything in the heavens and the earth. With His knowledge, power, and planning, Allah surrounds everything in His creation.

(١٢٧) وَيَسۡتَفۡتُونَكَ فِي ٱلنِّسَآءِۖ قُلِ ٱللَّهُ يُفۡتِيكُمۡ فِيهِنَّ وَمَا يُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ فِي يَتَٰمَى ٱلنِّسَآءِ ٱلَّٰتِي لَا تُؤۡتُونَهُنَّ مَا كُتِبَ لَهُنَّ وَتَرۡغَبُونَ أَن تَنكِحُوهُنَّ وَٱلۡمُسۡتَضۡعَفِينَ مِنَ ٱلۡوِلۡدَٰنِ وَأَن تَقُومُواْ لِلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۚ وَمَا تَفۡعَلُواْ مِنۡ خَيۡرٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِهِۦ عَلِيمٗا

They ask you, O Messenger, about the rights and duties of women. Say: Allah clarifies what you have asked about and what is recited to you in the Quran concerning orphan girls in your care, to whom you do not give their dowry and inheritance as Allah has obligated. You do not wish to marry them, yet you prevent them from marrying because of your greed for their wealth. He also clarifies that your obligation concerning helpless minor children is to give them their due right from the inheritance and not wrong them by confiscating their wealth. He clarifies your obligation of being just towards orphans and looking after their best interests in terms of the worldly and Afterlife. Allah knows of any good you do for orphans and others, and He will repay you for it.

Points of Benefit:

The rewards with Allah are not attained merely through wishes and claims; rather, belief and righteous deeds are necessary.
The recompense is of the same kind as the deed; whoever does evil will be rewarded accordingly, and whoever does good will be rewarded with what is better.
Sincerity and adherence are the measures of the acceptance of deeds by Allah, the Exalted.
Islam has elevated the rights of vulnerable groups, including women and children. It prohibits aggression against them and mandates the safeguarding of their interests in accordance with divine legislation.
