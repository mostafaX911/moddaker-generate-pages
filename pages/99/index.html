(١٢٨) وَإِنِ ٱمۡرَأَةٌ خَافَتۡ مِنۢ بَعۡلِهَا نُشُوزًا أَوۡ إِعۡرَاضٗا فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيۡهِمَآ أَن يُصۡلِحَا بَيۡنَهُمَا صُلۡحٗاۚ وَٱلصُّلۡحُ خَيۡرٞۗ وَأُحۡضِرَتِ ٱلۡأَنفُسُ ٱلشُّحَّۚ وَإِن تُحۡسِنُواْ وَتَتَّقُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٗا

If a woman fears disdain and a loss of desire from her husband, there is no sin in reaching a compromise where the woman gives up some of the rights owed to her, such as maintenance and spending the night together. Reconciliation is better for both of them than divorce. People have a natural tendency towards greed and miserliness. So, they are not keen on giving up rights owed to them. Therefore, spouses should cure this behavior by cultivating tolerance and benevolence. If you achieve excellent conduct in all your affairs and fear Allah by fulfilling His commands and avoiding what He prohibits, then Allah is All-Aware of what you do. Nothing is hidden from Him, and He will repay you for it.

(١٢٩) وَلَن تَسۡتَطِيعُوٓاْ أَن تَعۡدِلُواْ بَيۡنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ وَلَوۡ حَرَصۡتُمۡۖ فَلَا تَمِيلُواْ كُلَّ ٱلۡمَيۡلِ فَتَذَرُوهَا كَٱلۡمُعَلَّقَةِۚ وَإِن تُصۡلِحُواْ وَتَتَّقُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا

You, O husbands, will not be able to be entirely just towards your wives concerning inclinations of the heart, even if you aspire to that. This may be due to circumstances and factors beyond your control. So do not disincline too far away from the one you do not love and leave her hanging suspended to the point where she does not have a husband to fulfill her rights, nor is she unmarried so that she may marry someone else. If you make right what is between you by pushing yourselves to not insist on having things your way, giving the wife her due rights, and fearing Allah concerning her, then Allah is Ever-Forgiving and Merciful with you.

(١٣٠) وَإِن يَتَفَرَّقَا يُغۡنِ ٱللَّهُ كُلّٗا مِّن سَعَتِهِۦۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعًا حَكِيمٗا

If a couple should separate by divorce or annulment, then Allah will suffice both through His vast Bounty: He will suffice the man with a wife better than her, and He will suffice the woman with a husband better than him. Allah's favor and mercy are vast. He is All-Wise in His planning and assessment.

(١٣١) وَلِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ وَلَقَدۡ وَصَّيۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ وَإِيَّاكُمۡ أَنِ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ وَإِن تَكۡفُرُواْ فَإِنَّ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَنِيًّا حَمِيدٗا

To Allah alone belongs the dominion of everything in the heavens, the earth, and all that is between them. We struck a covenant with the People of the Book among the Jews and Christians, and We struck a covenant with you to fulfill Allah's commands and avoid what He has prohibited. If you denied this covenant, you would only harm yourselves. Allah does not need your obedience because He has dominion over everything in the heavens and earth. He is in no need of any of His creation. He is worthy of praise for all His attributes and actions.

(١٣٢) وَلِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ وَكِيلًا

To Allah alone belongs the dominion of everything in the heavens and the earth, and He deserves to be obeyed. Allah is sufficient for arranging all the affairs of His creation.

(١٣٣) إِن يَشَأۡ يُذۡهِبۡكُمۡ أَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ وَيَأۡتِ بِ‍َٔاخَرِينَۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ قَدِيرٗا

If He wills, He can destroy you, O people, and bring others besides you who will obey Allah and not disobey Him. Allah is fully capable of doing that.

(١٣٤) مَّن كَانَ يُرِيدُ ثَوَابَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا فَعِندَ ٱللَّهِ ثَوَابُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ سَمِيعَۢا بَصِيرٗا

Whoever among you, O people, only wishes to gain the reward of this worldly life by his actions should know that with Allah is the reward of the worldly life and Afterlife. He should seek the reward of both from Him. Allah is All-Hearing concerning your statements and All-Seeing concerning your actions. He will repay you for them.

Points of Benefit:

The recommendation of reconciliation between spouses in times of dispute and giving precedence to the interest of maintaining the marital contract by compromising some rights.
Allah, the Exalted, has mandated justice between wives, especially in financial matters that are within the capability of the husbands. The Sharia allows leniency when it is difficult to establish justice in matters related to emotions, such as love and emotional inclination.
There is no blame on the spouses for separation if living together becomes difficult for them.
The comprehensive advice for all of creation, from the first to the last, is to command the fear of Allah by obeying His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.
